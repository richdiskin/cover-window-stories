Part 1: Handicap Recap

Thursday night put two games on the board, and neither one stayed close to the number once the second half got going. Southern Miss +13.5 was covering at the half in Hattiesburg, still covering by half a point after three quarters, and then James Madison ran away and the dog tickets burned. Florida Atlantic +7 never needed a sweat in Boca Raton, and the Under 72.5 was already cooked by halftime in a 89-point track meet. James Madison covered 13.5 by 17.5 and the Over 58.5 cleared it by 18.5. Florida Atlantic covered 7 by 38 and the Under went over by 16.5.

In The Trenches had no side and no total in either game. Letdown Watch made no picks Thursday. Follow The Money had no side and no total in UTSA vs Florida Atlantic, and no total in James Madison vs Southern Miss. None of those are graded.

James Madison 54, Southern Miss 23

Southern Miss +13.5 looked like a cash for a long stretch. Suarez opened it with a 32-yard field goal, Evans ran in from 3 yards out, and James Madison led 10-0. White cut it to 10-7 from 3 yards out, Suarez made it 13-7 from 31, and then Robinson punched in from 2 yards out with 37 seconds left in the half for a 14-13 Southern Miss lead. Suarez answered from 40 at the gun, and it was James Madison 16, Southern Miss 14 at the break. Southern Miss +13.5 was covering by 11.5, and the total sat at 30, 28.5 short of the Over 58.5.

The third quarter still belonged to the dog tickets. Evans ran 16 yards for a touchdown and 2 more later, and it was 30-14. Southern Miss +13.5 was still covering by half a point with a quarter to go. The Over sat at 44 and needed 14.5 more.

Then the fourth quarter tore up every Southern Miss +13.5 ticket on the board. Evans found Cromwell for 16 yards and a touchdown at 13:41, and the margin jumped to 23. Southern Miss +13.5 was dead by 9.5 before the next snap. McCarter ran 30 yards for another touchdown at 11:33, and it was 44-14. 58 points, and Over 58.5 tickets were half a point short. White hit Sanders for 34 yards and a touchdown at 9:10, the two-point try failed, and it was 44-20. 64 points, Over 58.5 cashed by 5.5, and the Southern Miss side was already buried. Coleman found Scott for 26 more, Wilbanks hit from 29, Suarez hit from 34, and the final was James Madison 54, Southern Miss 23. Southern Miss +13.5 lost by 17.5 after covering for three quarters. The Over cleared 58.5 by 18.5.

No pick: Follow The Money on the total; In The Trenches on the side or the total.

UTSA 29, Florida Atlantic 60

Florida Atlantic +7 never needed a sweat. Veltkamp hit Messer from 6 yards out for a 7-0 lead, McCown answered with Anderson from 20, then Messer threw a 14-yard touchdown to Johnson and Veltkamp hit Messer again from 15, and Florida Atlantic led 21-7 after one. Florida Atlantic +7 was already covering by 21.

The first half kept pouring it on. Peterson ran 52 yards for a touchdown, and the kick failed. Johnson ran in from 4. Anderson caught one from 45, and the two-point try failed. Farrow caught an 18-yard touchdown with 50 seconds left in the half, Petro hit a 54-yard field goal at the gun, and it was Florida Atlantic 35, UTSA 22 at the break. 57 points already, and Under 72.5 tickets needed the second half to stay under 15.5 combined. Florida Atlantic +7 was covering by 20.

The second half finished both tickets. Johnson ran in from 4 again, and the two-point try made it 43-22. McCown found Butler-Tanner from 26, then Farrow ripped off a 59-yard touchdown run, and it was 50-29. Rogers had a 28-yard field goal blocked, and Veltkamp hit Messer from 6 on the next snap anyway. Tennison got sacked, fumbled, and Florida Atlantic recovered. Grace hit from 37, and the final was Florida Atlantic 60, UTSA 29. Florida Atlantic +7 cashed by 38. The Under went over by 16.5, and the second half alone scored 32.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side or the total; In The Trenches on the side or the total.
