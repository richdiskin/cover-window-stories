Monday Morning Quarterback: College Wednesday night review, Nov 4, 2026

College Wednesday night review: Ball State at UMass, Toledo at Sacramento State and Central Michigan vs Eastern Michigan at Ford Field (Nov. 4). Then the rest of the college week.

Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026.

ShareXFacebook

Part 1: Handicap Recap

Wednesday night gave the MAC window three games, and one of them took overtime to settle every ticket on it. UMass −2.5 had first-and-goal at the Ball State 2 with a 4-point lead and came away with a field goal, Ball State tied it with 25 seconds left, and overtime came down to a missed extra point and a walk-off run. UMass won by 1 and lost the number by a point and a half. The same overtime turned a half-point Over sweat into a 12.5-point cash. Central Michigan vs Eastern Michigan was 35-0 until Eastern Michigan scored with 10 seconds left, and the Over 44.5 died 2.5 points short. Toledo vs Sacramento State never came near the 25.5.

Follow The Money had no side in UMass vs Ball State or Toledo vs Sacramento State, and no total in the Michigan game. In The Trenches called the trenches even in Toledo vs Sacramento State, so it had no side there, and it reads sides only, so it had no total in any of the three. Letdown Watch made no picks Wednesday. None of those are graded.

Ball State 30, UMass 31

UMass −2.5 was one touchdown from a cash with 4:48 to go. Watson hit Dia-Johnson for 47 yards to the Ball State 18, Kimmons ran 16 more to the 2, and UMass led 21-17 with first-and-goal. Kimmons got stuffed for no gain. Watson got stuffed for no gain. Then Watson fumbled, fell on it himself and lost 2 yards. Morris kicked a 23-yard field goal at 2:14 for a 24-17 lead. A touchdown there makes it 28-17. Instead UMass −2.5 was covering by 4.5, and the total sat at 41, 7.5 short of the Over 48.5, with Ball State getting the ball back.

Ball State went 75 yards in 1:49. Luster moved it to the UMass 24, and on a deep ball for Hamilton with 38 seconds left Magee got flagged for interference, a 15-yard gift down to the 9. Luster ran it in from 2 yards out with 25 seconds left, and Boehm's kick tied it at 24. UMass knelt on it. Regulation ended 24-24, the UMass side was back to needing a 3-point win, and the total sat at 48. Over 48.5 tickets were half a point short, Under 48.5 tickets were half a point from cashing, and in overtime the first points on the board would settle the total.

Ball State had the ball first. Luster found Hoover for 20 yards to the UMass 2, and Bonds got in from a yard out on the third snap from the 2. 30-24, 54 points, Over 48.5 cashed and Under 48.5 torn up. Then Boehm's extra point failed. Down 6, the most UMass could win by on its own possession was 2, with a touchdown and a two-point try, so the 2.5 was gone before UMass took a snap. Watson ran for 12, then 13 more for the touchdown, Morris hit the kick, and UMass won 31-30. UMass −2.5 lost by a point and a half, one goal-to-go series short of a cash.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side; In The Trenches on the total.

Central Michigan 35, Eastern Michigan 7

Eastern Michigan +3 was finished by halftime. Glasser hit Williams from 8 yards out for a 7-0 Central Michigan lead, Woodford ran in from 11 and from 2 yards out, and it was 21-0 at the break. Woodford added a 1-yard run in the third, and Eastern Michigan +3 holders were down 28 with a quarter to go.

The Over 44.5 was the real sweat, and it needed Eastern Michigan to show up. Central Michigan made it 35-0 at 11:07 of the fourth after Horace got flagged for interference to put the ball at the 2, Eastern Michigan drew an unsportsmanlike flag and Blue ran it in from a yard out. 35 points with 11 minutes left, and the Over needed 10 more. Eastern Michigan drove to the Central Michigan 35, and Mattord got stopped for no gain on fourth down at 6:07.

Eastern Michigan got one more crack with under four minutes left and went 85 yards in 15 plays. Stuckey found Devereaux for what was ruled a 20-yard touchdown with 29 seconds left, and Central Michigan challenged and got it overturned to an incompletion. Eastern Michigan ran 14 yards to the 6, and Stuckey hit Mack from 6 yards out with 10 seconds left. 42 points, and Over 44.5 tickets needed 3 more with 10 seconds on the clock. Eastern Michigan kicked it deep for a touchback, Central Michigan took a knee, and the Over died 2.5 points short. The Eastern Michigan +3 side lost by 25.

Called it: Follow The Money on Central Michigan −2.5, covered by 25.5 points. In The Trenches on Central Michigan −3, covered by 25 points.

No pick: Follow The Money on the total; In The Trenches on the total.

Toledo 20, Sacramento State 17

Sacramento State +25.5 never needed a sweat. Sacramento State led 10-7 at the half after Sharman hit Coleman from 2 yards out. Toledo went up 17-10 on Ison's 19-yard run late in the third and 20-10 on Griffith's 40-yard field goal at 7:51 of the fourth, and Curtis cut it to 20-17 from 3 yards out with 2:20 left. Sacramento State got the ball back at its own 16 with 55 seconds left, Lambert-Pipkin sacked Salazar for 10 yards, and the last throw, for Smith Jr., got broken up by Petite on fourth down. Sacramento State +25.5 cashed by 22.5 and finished 3 points from winning the game outright.

The Over 52.5 was dead long before that. 17 points at the half, 37 at the end, and the Over came up 15.5 short. Follow The Money's Under 52.5 was the one total on the board Wednesday that never had a scare.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side; In The Trenches on the side or the total.
