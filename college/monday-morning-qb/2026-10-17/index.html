20, 2026. Weekend review. College Saturday (Oct. 17) and all of NFL Week 6, Monday night included. Then next weekend, with the new numbers.

Tuesday, Oct.

Part 1: The misses

Every pick below is graded against the line printed on its card when the game kicked off, not the closing line. A push counts as neither a hit nor a miss.

Florida 27, Texas 24

The Under was alive until 4:21 was left. Cam Coleman's 9-yard touchdown catch from Manning made it 51 points, half a point over.

The Citadel 0, Texas A&M 41

Texas A&M led 34-0 after three quarters and needed to win by 47. Aaron Gregory's 12-yard touchdown catch from Brady Hart with 5:47 left made it 41-0, and that was the last score. The Citadel missed a 47-yard field goal with 1:13 left.

Auburn 27, Georgia 24

Auburn led 24-14 after three quarters and won outright. Georgia's last real chance ended when Gunner Stockton fumbled near midfield with 58 seconds left; C. Sigler forced it and S. Smith-Reed recovered for Auburn.

The Under held until the last play. It was 24-24, a 48-point game, when Georgia was flagged for roughing the passer on G. Harris Jr. with 15 seconds left, 15 yards to the Georgia 19. Alex McPherson's 34-yard field goal on the final play made it 51.

Arkansas 31, Vanderbilt 21

Arkansas led 28-21 after Cam Settles' 3-yard run with 9 seconds left in the third. Vanderbilt's best drive of the fourth went 63 yards in 14 plays and ended with an interception by M. Mitchell.

The total was 49 with 24 seconds left. Max Gilbert's 39-yard field goal made it 52.

Kentucky 21, Oklahoma 13

Kentucky won outright. Willie Rodriguez's 12-yard touchdown catch from Kenny Minchey put Kentucky ahead 14-13 with 12:53 left, and Jordan Castell returned an interception 41 yards for a touchdown with 1:17 left.

Kansas 24, Kansas State 35

Kansas led 24-18 in the third. Two fourth-quarter plays hurt it most. Isaiah Marshall was intercepted at the goal line by L. Bartley with 10:02 left, and Kansas State scored on its next possession, Jaron Tibbs' 9-yard catch from Avery Johnson for 32-24, a drive helped by a Kansas face-mask penalty. Then Marshall broke a 46-yard touchdown run, and another Kansas face-mask penalty took it off the board. That touchdown would have made it 32-30 before the extra point.

Arizona State 25, Texas Tech 53

Texas Tech led 43-10 at the half. It started with Ben Roberts' 24-yard interception return, and J'Koby Williams added touchdown runs of 62 and 38 yards.

The Under rested on Texas Tech's defense. The defense scored, and the offense scored 46 more. The total passed 49.5 before halftime, on Coy Eakin's 11-yard catch from Will Hammond with 31 seconds left in the second quarter. The final total was 78.

Notre Dame 24, BYU 28

Lyons' touchdown also took the total from 45 to 52.

Oklahoma State 32, Houston 35

Houston led 35-25 after Koby Young's 48-yard catch with 9:48 left. Drew Mestemaker's 8-yard touchdown pass to Justin Bowick with 4:54 left cut it to 3, and nobody scored after that.

The Under stood at 44 points with 1:21 left. DeAndre Moore Jr.'s 2-yard touchdown catch from Isaac Wilson made it 51.

North Carolina 24, Duke 20

Duke rallied from 17-0 and led 20-17 after Nate Sheppard's 7-yard catch from Walker Eget with 4:39 left. Jaylen McGill's 24-yard touchdown run with 1:53 left put North Carolina back in front, and K. Dopson III intercepted Eget at the North Carolina 15 with 19 seconds left.

Florida State 35, Miami 43 (2 overtimes)

Florida State led 21-14 after three quarters and did not go away. Duce Robinson's 75-yard touchdown catch from Ashton Daniels tied it 28-28 with 2:39 left. Miami won in the second overtime on Malachi Toney's 3-yard touchdown catch, and Toney threw the two-point pass himself.

Stanford led 34-0 after Micah Ford's 81-yard run early in the fourth, and the extra point failed. That left Stanford exactly 34 points ahead, half a point short. Elon's Kamerin McDowell-Moore then scored on an 18-yard run, and Stanford's last scoring chance ended with a fumble at the Elon 1 that went through the end zone for a touchback.
