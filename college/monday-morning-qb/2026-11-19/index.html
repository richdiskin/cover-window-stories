Monday Morning Quarterback: College Thursday night review, Nov 19, 2026

College Thursday night review: Rice at Temple (Nov. 19). Then the rest of the college week.

Friday, Nov. 20, 2026.

XFacebook

Part 1: Handicap Recap

Thursday night put one American game on the board, Rice at Temple at Lincoln Financial Field, and every ticket on it came down to the last six seconds. Temple led 27-17 with 5:18 left. Rice scored twice in the final 1:55, the second on a 30-yard throw with six seconds on the clock, and won 31-27. That one throw flipped Temple −2.5 from a half-point winner into a 6.5-point loser and dragged the total from 51 to 58, cashing the Over 51.5.

Follow The Money stayed off the side but jumped on the Over 51.5 at Thursday morning's Lock It Up after the total moved a full point. In The Trenches was the lone voice on Rice +2.5 and had no total. The Formula, Next Man Up and Letdown Watch all took Temple −2.5 and the Over.

Rice 31, Temple 27

Temple −2.5 backers grabbed the lead early, and the first half turned into a giveaway contest. Smolik found Chase from 10 yards out on the opening drive. Rice drove to the Temple 15 and came away empty when Powell broke up a fourth-down throw. Then May knocked the ball loose from Smith at the Rice 29, and Rice recovered. Ochoa answered with a 33-yard strike to Matthews early in the second quarter. 7-7.

Then came the weirdest swing of the night. Akui intercepted Smolik, ran it back 23 yards and fumbled it right back to Temple at midfield. Temple turned that gift into a 21-yard Hardin field goal, and Rice turned it over on downs at the Temple 42 for the second time. Temple 10, Rice 7 at the half. Temple −2.5 was covering by half a point, and the total sat at 17, a mile from the Over 51.5.

The third quarter turned into a track meet. Hardeman ran 46 yards for a Rice touchdown. 14-10 Rice. Smolik hit Freeman from 2 yards out, Rice went three-and-out, and Young went 79 yards on Temple's second snap. Temple 24, Rice 14 with 6:44 left in the third, Temple −2.5 covering by 7.5, and 38 points on the board, 13.5 shy of the Over.

Rice answered with a 20-play drive that ate 11:43, and Temple backers caught the break of the night on it. Leahey's run to the Temple 8 was wiped out by a Rice chop block that pushed the ball back to the 27, and Rice settled for a 37-yard Bauchiero field goal. 24-17. Mason broke a 35-yard run, and that drive ended in a 35-yard Hardin field goal at 5:18. Temple 27, Rice 17. Temple −2.5 was covering by 7.5 with five minutes to play, and Over backers were stuck on 44, needing 8 more points.

Rice wasn't dead. Brown was flagged for intentional grounding on the second snap, and then Crayton hit Mosey for 47 yards. Ochoa found Mosey from 9 yards out with 1:55 left. Temple 27, Rice 24. Temple −2.5 was now covering by half a point, and the total sat at 51, half a point short of the Over 51.5. One game, two tickets, and both of them hanging on the same half-point.

Temple needed one first down to finish it. It got 4 yards on three runs while Rice burned two timeouts, and Atton punted to the Rice 42 with 52 seconds left. Crayton threw three straight incompletions. On fourth-and-10, he hit Clark for 18 yards to the Temple 40. Temple jumped offside on the next snap, a 5-yard gift, Clark caught another for a first down at the 30, and with nine seconds left Crayton threw deep left to Brown, the same Brown flagged for grounding five minutes earlier, for a 30-yard touchdown with six seconds on the clock.

Rice 31, Temple 27. One throw took Temple −2.5 from a half-point winner to a 6.5-point loser, and the same throw took the total from 51 to 58 and cashed the Over 51.5 by 6.5. Temple's kickoff return went through three laterals and died at its own 1. Rice had been 1-9. Temple −2.5 was six seconds from cashing. Over backers who had been reaching for the remote got the best six seconds of their week, and Temple backers got the worst.

Called it: In The Trenches on Rice +2.5, covered by 6.5 points. The Formula, Next Man Up, Letdown Watch and Follow The Money on the Over 51.5, cleared it by 6.5 points.
