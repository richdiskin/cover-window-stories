Monday Morning Quarterback: College Tuesday night review, Nov 17, 2026

College Tuesday night review: Eastern Michigan at Western Michigan, Ball State at Ohio and Miami (OH) at Kent State (Nov. 17). Then the rest of the college week.

Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026.

XFacebook

Part 1: Handicap Recap

Tuesday put three MAC games on the board, and all three are final. The one that hurts most came out of Kalamazoo. Western Michigan led Eastern Michigan 31-7 at halftime, Western Michigan −15.5 was covering by 8.5, and the Eagles scored 20 of the last 23 points to beat the number by 8.5. In Athens, Ball State tied Ohio 28-28 on a touchdown and a two-point conversion with 2:23 left, which cashed the Over 49.5 and buried Ohio −11.5 for good. In Kent, Miami (OH) missed an extra point and a 30-yard field goal and still kept Kent State +9.5 half a point short at halftime before pulling away, 20-3.

The Formula flipped to Eastern Michigan +15.5 when the Kalamazoo line moved Tuesday morning, and Follow The Money jumped on Western Michigan −15.5 at the same Lock It Up. That was the only ticket Follow The Money wrote all night. In The Trenches took Eastern Michigan +15.5 and Kent State +9.5, sat out Athens and had no totals. Next Man Up and Letdown Watch made picks on all three games.

Eastern Michigan 27, Western Michigan 34

Western Michigan −15.5 looked like the easiest ticket of the night for 30 minutes. Buckley went 72 yards for a touchdown on Western Michigan's first snap and added a 1-yard run for 14-0. Eastern Michigan's only first-half points came after Gouldsby muffed a punt at the Western Michigan 7, and Blue walked in from 7 yards out on the next snap. Western Michigan added a 36-yard field goal and a 2-yard Cabana run with 40 seconds left in the half, and then Moore intercepted an Eastern Michigan throw and went 50 yards for a touchdown with two seconds on the clock. Western Michigan 31, Eastern Michigan 7 at the break. Western Michigan −15.5 was covering by 8.5, and the total sat at 38, 8.5 under the 46.5.

Then the second half turned every Western Michigan ticket upside down. Blue ran 41 yards for a touchdown on Eastern Michigan's first drive of the third quarter, and the two-point try failed. 31-13. An interference flag on Western Michigan moved the ball from the 14 to the 2, and McCaughey found Long from a yard out at 5:53. 31-20. Western Michigan −15.5 was 4.5 short, and the Under 46.5 was dead at 51 points with almost 21 minutes still to play.

Western Michigan answered with a 14-play, eight-minute drive that stalled at the Eastern Michigan 9 and settled for a 27-yard field goal early in the fourth. 34-20, a 14-point lead, 1.5 short of the number and one more score from life. It never came. Eastern Michigan went 65 yards in 11 plays, and Blue scored his third touchdown from a yard out at 7:36. 34-27. Western Michigan's last two drives ended in punts, and Eastern Michigan's last one died on a 17-yard sack on fourth down with 30 seconds left.

Western Michigan won by 7 and lost the ticket by 8.5. Every Western Michigan −15.5 and Under 46.5 ticket went down together, and Eastern Michigan +15.5 backers cashed a game they trailed by 24 at halftime.

Called it: The Formula and In The Trenches on Eastern Michigan +15.5, covered by 8.5 points. The Formula on the Over 46.5, cleared it by 14.5 points.

No pick: Follow The Money and In The Trenches on the total.

Ball State 28, Ohio 35

Ohio −11.5 backers were in trouble before halftime. Brune scored from a yard out on the opening drive, then Fiene made a 24-yard field goal and Luster hit Hamilton for a 10-yard touchdown. Scandrett picked off Poulos in the end zone, and Luster found Hohlt for an 80-yard touchdown on the second play of the next drive. Ball State 17, Ohio 7. Poulos ran in from 8 yards out at 1:09, and Fiene banged a 43-yard field goal as the half ended. Ball State 20, Ohio 14 at the break. Ohio −11.5 was down 6, and the total sat at 34.

Nobody scored in the third quarter. Walker forced and recovered a Horton fumble at the Ohio 36 early in the half, Ohio went three-and-out, and later Ohio burned more than nine minutes on a 13-play drive that ended in a punt. The Over 49.5 went to the fourth quarter at 34 points, needing 16 in 15 minutes.

Then the fourth quarter paid the Over in full. James-Hamilton picked off Luster and went 34 yards for a touchdown 41 seconds in. 21-20 Ohio, 41 points. Poulos ran 12 yards for a touchdown at 7:15. 28-20 Ohio, 48 points, 1.5 short of the Over 49.5, and Ohio −11.5 needed four more points of margin. It never got them. Ball State went 68 yards in 10 plays, an interference flag on Ohio moved the ball from the 13 to the 2, and Horton scored from 2 yards out at 2:23 with Hamilton catching the two-point try. 28-28. The Over 49.5 cashed on a two-point conversion, and Ohio −11.5 needed to win by 12 in a tie game with 2:23 left.

Ohio got the ball back, went 66 yards in six plays, and Poulos hit Dorwart for a 29-yard touchdown at 1:16. Ohio 35, Ball State 28. Ball State's last throw, deep into the end zone with five seconds left, was broken up by James-Hamilton. Ohio won by 7 and missed the number by 4.5, and the Over 49.5 cleared by 13.5.

No pick: Follow The Money and In The Trenches on the side or the total.

Miami (OH) 20, Kent State 3

Kent State +9.5 spent the entire second quarter half a point from the window. Jones scored from a yard out on the opening drive, Newcomb answered with a 39-yard field goal, and Jones scored again from a yard out with 22 seconds left in the first quarter. The extra point failed, so it was 13-3, and Kent State +9.5 was losing by half a point instead of 1.5.

Then the swings. Knighten picked off DeShields at the Miami 11, and that one stung. Stidham then missed a 30-yard field goal at 6:38 of the second quarter, and that one helped. Miami (OH) 13, Kent State 3 at halftime. One Kent State field goal would have put Kent State +9.5 back in the window.

It never came. Bellisari ran 23 yards for a touchdown at 9:46 of the third. 20-3, and Kent State +9.5 was buried. Miami (OH) even turned it over on downs at the Kent State 8 in the fourth after Jones fumbled and fell on his own ball, and it didn't matter. Kent State's offense never scored after the first quarter.

The Over 44.5 never had a chance. The teams had 16 points at halftime and 23 at the end, and every Over 44.5 ticket on this game fell 21.5 short.

Called it: The Formula and Letdown Watch on Miami (OH) −9.5, covered by 7.5 points.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side or the total; In The Trenches on the total.
