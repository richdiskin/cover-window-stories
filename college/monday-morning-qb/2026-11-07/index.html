Part 1: Handicap Recap

Saturday put 50 college games with lines on the board, and all 50 are final and graded here. It was a night for broken hearts. Texas A&M +7.5 was covering until a fourth-down sack and a 41-yard touchdown run with 79 seconds left. Kansas −3 was covering until UCF scored on the final snap. Every game below is graded against the line printed on the card at kickoff, using ESPN finals and play-by-play, and a push counts as neither a hit nor a miss. Merrimack at Wake Forest had no line on the card and isn't graded. The heartbreakers go first, then the escapes, then Friday's two late finishes, then the rest of the card.
