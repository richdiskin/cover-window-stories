Monday Morning Quarterback: College Friday night review, Nov 20, 2026

College Friday night review: Iowa State at UCF, Clemson at Duke, Bowling Green at Toledo, Oregon at Michigan State, UTEP at Air Force and New Mexico at Wyoming (Nov. 20). Then the rest of the college week.

Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026.

XFacebook

Part 1: Handicap Recap

Friday morning's Lock It Up had a busy room after the lines moved. Follow The Money went from no pick to a pick on eight tickets and sat out New Mexico at Wyoming completely. The Formula and Next Man Up flipped to UCF +1.5 when Iowa State became the favorite, Next Man Up also flipped to Clemson +13.5, Letdown Watch flipped its Orlando total to the Over 48.5, and The Formula flipped UTEP at Air Force to the Under 47.5. In The Trenches stayed put, with UCF, Toledo, Michigan State and Wyoming on the side, no side at Duke or Air Force, and no totals.

New Mexico 24, Wyoming 17

New Mexico −7.5 backers owned this one for three quarters. Moga hit Keith from 5 yards out in the first quarter, Booker blocked a 49-yard Sandvik field goal try and recovered it himself, and Glasgow hit from 48. Sibley's 5-yard touchdown run got overturned on review with the ball at the 1, and Moga ran it in from a yard out at 6:09. 17-0. Sims ran 2 yards for a Wyoming touchdown with 1:53 left in the half, and Sibley scored from a yard out late in the third. New Mexico 24, Wyoming 7 after three, New Mexico −7.5 covering by 9.5, and the total sat at 31, 6.5 short of the Over 37.5.

Then the fourth quarter fell apart one flag at a time. Bogar forced a Fa'avae fumble at the Wyoming 40, Sims found J. Holman for 37 yards, and Sandvik kicked a 29-yard field goal at 8:12. 24-10, 34 points. New Mexico answered with the craziest sequence of the night. A Wyoming interference flag put the Lobos at the Wyoming 30, a holding call wiped out the next run, and a personal foul shoved them all the way back to their own 45. Glasgow missed from 53 at 5:53.

Wyoming went the other way, and New Mexico helped again. Sims got sacked for 5, and an unsportsmanlike conduct flag on New Mexico handed Wyoming 15 yards and a first down. Five snaps later, Sims threw deep right to Frendt for a 34-yard touchdown at 2:57. New Mexico 24, Wyoming 17. That one throw took the total from 34 to 41 and cashed the Over 37.5, and it turned New Mexico −7.5 from a 6.5-point winner into a half-point loser.

Called it: In The Trenches on Wyoming +7.5, covered by 0.5 points. The Formula, Next Man Up and Letdown Watch on the Over 37.5, cleared it by 3.5 points.

Bowling Green 0, Toledo 19

Toledo −16.5 backers had the easy part figured out early. Bowling Green never scored. The hard part was getting Toledo to 17. Griffith opened with a 46-yard field goal, Chandler recovered an Amachree fumble on the very next snap, and Toledo came away empty when Griffith missed from 43. Griffith hit from 29 and from 39. 9-0, and Toledo kept settling for three.

Bowling Green had its one real chance in the second quarter, and it ended with a 32-yard Kenner field goal attempt that missed. Then, with 46 seconds left in the half, Richter threw deep left to Dabbs for a 35-yard touchdown. Toledo 16, Bowling Green 0. Remember that number. Toledo −16.5 was losing by half a point, and the total sat at 16, so Over 47.5 backers were already reaching for the remote.

After the break, Toledo's offense went to sleep for most of the second half, and a half-point sweat set in. Toledo punted on its first four drives of the half, one of them a three-and-out that went backward 22 yards. Bowling Green drove to the Toledo 34 early in the fourth quarter and turned it over on downs when White got thrown for a 5-yard loss. Richter got sacked and fumbled on the next series, and he fell on it himself. Bowling Green got a free 15 yards on a Toledo roughing-the-passer flag and still punted.

So with nine minutes left, Toledo −16.5 backers were sitting on a 16-0 shutout and losing. Toledo finally moved, with Walendzak catching one for 15 yards and running for 15 more, and the drive stalled at the Bowling Green 24. Toledo called timeout, and Griffith came out for his fifth field goal try of the night. He hit from 42 with 3:54 left. Toledo 19, Bowling Green 0, and Toledo −16.5 went from a half-point loser to a 2.5-point winner on one swing of the leg.

Bowling Green got one more crack at the backdoor. Novosad hit Saffold for 29 yards to the Toledo 46, and then Toledo sacked him twice in four snaps, the second one on fourth down at 2:25. Toledo ran the clock out from the Bowling Green 33. Toledo −16.5 cashed by 2.5. The Under 47.5 cashed by 28.5, and every Over ticket on this game was dead long before the end.

Called it: Next Man Up, Letdown Watch, Follow The Money and In The Trenches on Toledo −16.5, covered by 2.5 points. Follow The Money on the Under 47.5, stayed under by 28.5 points.

Iowa State 17, UCF 48

Iowa State −1.5 backers were finished by halftime. The Over 48.5 was a whole different story. It was tied 3-3 after one, and then UCF went off. Barnett hit Derry for 21 yards and for 65 more, UCF recovered a fumble on the next Iowa State snap, and Barnett ran 15 yards for a touchdown eight seconds later. UCF 24, Iowa State 3 in the second quarter, and Iowa State −1.5 was down 22.5 and going nowhere.

That turned this into an Over game, and the Over took the long way home. Barnett found Derry for a third touchdown, from 24 yards out, early in the third, and Flora answered with a 62-yard run at 9:55. UCF 31, Iowa State 17. That's 48 points with almost 25 minutes to play, half a point short of the Over 48.5. And then nobody scored for more than 10 minutes.

UCF had the Over on its foot late in the third, and Stone missed a 29-yard field goal. Then Iowa State handed it right back. Two snaps later, Johnson knocked the ball loose from Polite after a 20-yard catch, and Williams recovered at the Iowa State 44. Barnett hit Hornes for 34 yards to the 2, and Thomas ran it in from 2 yards out at 14:25 of the fourth. UCF 38, Iowa State 17, 55 points, and the Over 48.5 was finally home. Stone added a field goal from 38. Jackson picked off Raynor, and Barnett threw a 5-yard touchdown to Maiava at 6:35. UCF 48, Iowa State 17. The Over cleared by 16.5, and UCF +1.5 cashed by 32.5.

Called it: The Formula, Next Man Up and In The Trenches on UCF +1.5, covered by 32.5 points. The Formula, Next Man Up and Letdown Watch on the Over 48.5, cleared it by 16.5 points.

Clemson 20, Duke 10

Duke −13.5 backers knew by halftime they had a problem. Clemson led 3-0 at the break on a 36-yard Hauser field goal with 17 seconds left, and the Under was cruising in Durham. Pearman ran 9 yards for a Clemson touchdown in the third, Sheppard answered with a 55-yard Duke touchdown run 49 seconds later, and that was as close as Duke got. Strange missed a 46-yard field goal early in the fourth with Duke down 10-7. Denson ran 7 yards for a Clemson touchdown at 8:52, Strange hit from 46, Hauser hit from 39, and Myrick picked off Eget with 20 seconds left. Clemson 20, Duke 10. The 13.5-point dog won outright, and the Under 41.5 cashed by 11.5 without ever being in danger.

Called it: The Formula and Next Man Up on Clemson +13.5, covered by 23.5 points. Next Man Up on the Under 41.5, stayed under by 11.5 points.

Oregon 45, Michigan State 7

Michigan State +17.5 backers got a missed 47-yard Boyd field goal in the second quarter and not much else. Smith scored from 5 yards out in the first quarter, and Hurych kicked a 40-yarder. Oregon punched in a touchdown run from 15 yards out with two seconds left in the half. Oregon 17, Michigan State 0 at halftime. Oregon scored twice more in the third, Michigan State got a 1-yard Patterson run in between, and it was 31-7. That's 38 points, 6.5 short of the Over 44.5. Uiagalelei picked off Coffman on the second play of the fourth. Raiola hit Moore from 31 yards out for a touchdown on the third play of the next drive. 38-7, the Over was home, and Michigan State +17.5 was losing by 13.5. Price's 1-yard run at 3:56 made it 45-7. Oregon −17.5 cashed by 20.5, and the Over cleared by 7.5.

Called it: Follow The Money on Oregon −17.5, covered by 20.5 points. The Formula, Next Man Up and Letdown Watch on the Over 44.5, cleared it by 7.5 points.

UTEP 3, Air Force 52

UTEP +21.5 backers lasted about nine minutes. LaDue ran in from 3 yards out, Perez forced a fumble on UTEP's next snap, and Medders hit Hrynciw for a 31-yard touchdown three plays later. Air Force 14, UTEP 0. Arreola's 42-yard field goal was UTEP's only points of the night. Perez picked off Moore late in the half, and Long ran 13 yards for a touchdown with 19 seconds left. Medders hit Dawson for a 77-yard touchdown on Air Force's second snap of the third quarter. Air Force 35, UTEP 3, and the side was over. The Over 47.5 had to wait. Cremascoli's 19-yard field goal made it 41 points, and Morales ran 29 yards for a touchdown with 1:02 left in the third. 45-3, 48 points, Over home. Martin hit Quin for a 35-yard touchdown in the fourth to make it 52-3. Air Force −21.5 cashed by 27.5, and the Over cleared by 7.5.

Called it: Next Man Up and Letdown Watch on Air Force −21.5, covered by 27.5 points. Next Man Up and Letdown Watch on the Over 47.5, cleared it by 7.5 points.
