Monday Morning Quarterback: College Wednesday night review, Nov 18, 2026

College Wednesday night review: Akron at UMass and Central Michigan at Buffalo (Nov. 18). Then the rest of the college week.

Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026.

XFacebook

Part 1: Handicap Recap

Wednesday put two more MAC games on the board, and both are final. The one that hurts came out of Amherst. UMass led Akron 26-7 in the second quarter with UMass −3.5 covering by 15.5, and Akron outscored UMass 27-7 the rest of the way, winning 34-33 on a 28-yard field goal with 5:34 left. The Over 46.5 in that game was cashed 13 seconds into the second half on a 100-yard kickoff return. In Buffalo, the Bulls trailed Central Michigan 14-13 with 39 seconds left in the half and then outscored the Chippewas 27-7, covering −2.5 by 16.5 and burying the Under 45.5 before the fourth quarter.

Letdown Watch flipped its Buffalo total from Over 43.5 to Under 45.5 at Wednesday morning's Lock It Up after the total moved, and Follow The Money jumped on Buffalo −2.5 at the same session after the spread moved a full point. That was the only ticket Follow The Money wrote all night. In The Trenches took UMass −3.5, sat out Buffalo and had no totals. The Formula, Next Man Up and Letdown Watch made picks on both games.

Akron 34, UMass 33

UMass −3.5 backers had this one in the bank by the middle of the second quarter. Faulkner ran 52 yards for a touchdown on the opening drive, and Morris added a 49-yard field goal. Gant answered with a 73-yard touchdown run for Akron, and Inagawa found Ogunniyi from 3 yards out with 51 seconds left in the first quarter. Reed then picked off Broughton and ran it back 68 yards to the Akron 2, which became a 23-yard Morris field goal. Inagawa hit Dowling from 2 yards out at 6:29 of the second quarter, and the two-point throw failed. UMass 26, Akron 7. UMass −3.5 was covering by 15.5.

Then it began to leak. A UMass unsportsmanlike conduct flag on the kickoff handed Akron the ball at its own 40, and Broughton hit Burris for a 36-yard touchdown on the third snap. 26-14. On the very next UMass snap, Frazier intercepted Inagawa, and Stire kicked a 23-yard field goal. 26-17. Then Morris missed from 50 yards with 59 seconds left in the half. UMass 26, Akron 17 at the break. UMass −3.5 was still covering by 5.5, and the total sat at 43, 3.5 short of the Over 46.5.

The Over didn't need long. Hills took the second-half kickoff 100 yards for a touchdown. 26-24, 50 points, and the Over 46.5 was cashed 13 seconds into the third quarter. UMass −3.5, meanwhile, was 1.5 short. UMass answered with a 13-play drive that covered 75 yards, an Akron unnecessary roughness flag moved the ball from the Akron 25 to the 12, and Dowling caught his second touchdown, from 11 yards, at 7:30. 33-24, covering by 5.5 again. It didn't last. A UMass roughing the passer flag kept Akron's next drive moving, a UMass personal foul moved the ball from the 6 to the 3, and Gant scored from a yard out at 2:15. 33-31.

Akron took over at its own 17 early in the fourth, a UMass interference flag moved the ball from the 19 to the 34, Mason caught a 41-yard throw, and the drive ran 7:49 off the clock before Stire hit a 28-yard field goal at 5:34. Akron 34, UMass 33. From that moment, even a game-winning field goal would have lost the ticket. UMass −3.5 needed a touchdown. UMass got to the Akron 40, then lost 3 yards on a Kimmons run and more when Inagawa fumbled and fell on his own ball, and fourth down fell incomplete with 58 seconds left. After an Akron punt, UMass got one last chance. An Akron interference flag moved the ball to the UMass 32, Hobson broke up one deep throw, Branch broke up the next, and Inagawa was sacked at 0:11. The clock ran out from there.

Here's the part that will keep UMass backers up tonight. The failed two-point try and the 50-yard miss cost UMass exactly five points. Put them back on the board and the 33 is a 38, a four-point win and a cashed ticket. Instead UMass lost by 1, missed the number by 4.5, and Akron +3.5 cashed with an outright win. The Over 46.5 cleared by 20.5.

Called it: The Formula, Next Man Up and Letdown Watch on the Over 46.5, cleared it by 20.5 points.

Central Michigan 21, Buffalo 40

Buffalo −2.5 backers spent most of the first half sweating. Glasser hit Naves for a 32-yard touchdown on Central Michigan's opening drive. Hautanen answered with a 27-yard field goal, Davis picked off Glasser on the next snap, and Hautanen added a 26-yarder. Davis then forced a Woodford fumble on the very next play, and Burch ran 10 yards for a touchdown. 13-7 Buffalo.

Then the game stalled, and the sweat came back. Central Michigan turned it over on downs at the Buffalo 42, Buffalo punted, and Duley answered with an 86-yard punt that pinned Buffalo at its own 1. Buffalo went three-and-out, and Glasser hit Peters for a 5-yard touchdown with 39 seconds left in the half. Central Michigan 14, Buffalo 13, and Buffalo −2.5 was losing by 3.5. Buffalo needed 29 seconds to flip it. Wright found Andrews for 49 yards, and then hit Morrow for a 17-yard touchdown with four seconds left. Buffalo 20, Central Michigan 14 at halftime. Buffalo −2.5 was covering by 3.5, and the total sat at 34, 11.5 under the 45.5.

The Under 45.5 needed a quiet second half, and it got the opposite. Buffalo opened the third quarter by going 75 yards in nine plays, and Wright scored on a 1-yard run at 10:46, with his two-point throw failing. 26-14, 40 points. A Buffalo face mask flag added 15 yards to a Central Michigan run, and that drive ended with Glasser hitting Townsend from 2 yards out at 3:55. 26-21, 47 points, and the Under 45.5 was dead with 18:55 still to play. Nine seconds later, Burch ran 75 yards for a touchdown on Buffalo's first snap. 33-21, 54 points.

Shelton's 2-yard run at 10:45 of the fourth made it 40-21. Greer intercepted Glasser, and Hautanen missed from 39 yards at 5:31. Central Michigan's last drive reached the Buffalo 12 when the clock ran out. Buffalo won by 19, Buffalo −2.5 cashed by 16.5, and the 61 points beat the Under 45.5 by 15.5.

Called it: The Formula, Next Man Up, Letdown Watch and Follow The Money on Buffalo −2.5, covered by 16.5 points. The Formula on the Over 45.5, cleared it by 15.5 points.

Part 2: Looking forward

The college week doesn't wait for Saturday. Thursday night, Rice (1-9) visits Temple (2-8) at Lincoln Financial Field at 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN.

Friday brings six more. Iowa State (5-5) is at UCF (5-5) at 6 p.m. ET on FS1. At 7:30 p.m. ET, Clemson (6-4) visits Duke (7-3) on ESPN, and Bowling Green (3-7) visits Toledo (8-2) on ESPN2. At 8 p.m. ET, Oregon (7-3) is at Michigan State (3-7) on Fox, and UTEP (1-9) is at Air Force (8-2) on The CW. New Mexico (8-2) closes the night at Wyoming (7-3) at 9 p.m. ET on FS1.
