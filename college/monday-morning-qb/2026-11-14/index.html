Part 1: Handicap Recap

Saturday put 55 college games on the card, and it was a night for the 1.5-point heartbreak. Four different Overs missed by exactly 1.5: Temple's offense turned it over on downs at the UAB 17 instead of kicking a field goal, Oregon kicked a field goal from the Michigan 3 instead of scoring a touchdown, Auburn and Mississippi State sat at 50 points for the last 13:54, and a pick-six in overtime at Missouri State ended the game at 48. Louisville −3.5 took two knees at the North Carolina 12 and won by two. Miami −14 died on a backdoor touchdown with 21 seconds left, and NC State +3 turned into a push with 48 seconds left. After midnight, the Over 43.5 at San Diego State missed by half a point, and Utah −1.5 took two knees at the Arizona 12 with a one-point lead and missed by half a point too.

On the other side of the window, Texas +4.5 cashed on a touchdown with 3 seconds left, Northwestern +14 and the Over 45.5 cashed on the same touchdown with no time on the clock, Alabama −14 survived a missed Vanderbilt field goal and a fourth-down stop to cash by one point, and North Dakota State −7 cashed by one point on a pick-six in Honolulu.

Every game below is graded against the line printed on the card at kickoff, using ESPN finals and play-by-play, and a push counts as neither a hit nor a miss. Saturday's Lock It Up line moves count at their final lines. The heartbreakers go first, then the escapes, then Friday's two late finishes, Illinois at UCLA and Houston at Colorado, then the rest of the card.

Follow The Money had a side in 21 Saturday games and a total in 33, and In The Trenches had a side in 28 and no totals. Games without a pick aren't graded for them.
