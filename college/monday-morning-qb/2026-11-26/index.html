Monday Morning Quarterback: College Thursday night review, Nov 26, 2026

College Thursday night review: TCU at Texas Tech (Nov. 26). Then the rest of the college week.

Friday, Nov. 27, 2026.

XFacebook

Part 1: Handicap Recap

Thanksgiving night had one college game, and it went final in Lubbock. TCU backers carried a 10-point lead and 15.5 points into the second quarter, then watched Texas Tech score 21 points in the last 2:08 of the half, with a fumble on the first snap after a kickoff and a 79-yard punt return in the middle of it. Texas Tech won 48-27 and covered the −15.5 by 5.5, and two TCU touchdowns in the final nine minutes only made the TCU side lose by less. The total stopped being a contest early in the third quarter. It went past 46.5 with almost 24 minutes left and finished at 75.

Two cards moved at the Thursday morning line check after the spread climbed to Texas Tech −15.5. Letdown Watch flipped off Texas Tech −13.5 and onto TCU +15.5, and Follow The Money went from pending to Texas Tech −15.5 and the Under 46.5. In The Trenches took TCU and stayed off the total. The Formula and Next Man Up both had TCU, and they split on the total.

TCU 27, Texas Tech 48

For one quarter, TCU +15.5 looked like the easiest money of the holiday. The Frogs marched 12 plays on the opening drive and came away empty when Schobel got stuffed for no gain on fourth down at the Texas Tech 26, but they got the ball right back when Texas Tech muffed a punt and Haley fell on it at the Texas Tech 33. A TCU interference flag wiped out a 4-yard catch and backed them up 10 yards, so Lemmermann settled for a 33-yard field goal at 4:48. Then Schobel hit Walker for 42 yards and found Small for 3 yards and a touchdown with 19 seconds left in the quarter. TCU 10, Texas Tech 0. The Frogs were up 10 with 15.5 in their pocket, and the Over 46.5 crowd was already checking the clock.

The second quarter crept along for 12 minutes and then caved in. Harrington hit from 54 yards at 12:52. TCU drove to the Texas Tech 37, and Schobel got sacked and coughed it up, with Trick recovering at the Texas Tech 40 at 8:28. Harrington added a 39-yarder at 5:54. TCU 10, Texas Tech 6, and TCU +15.5 was still covering by 19.5.

Then Texas Tech scored 21 points in 75 seconds and ended the night for every TCU ticket in the country. A TCU interference flag turned a second-down incompletion into 15 free yards, Castellanos hit Simmons for 30, and Castellanos found Johnson for a 19-yard touchdown at 2:08. On the very next snap after the kickoff, Payne fumbled and Morgan recovered at the TCU 21. One play later, Castellanos hit Williams for a 21-yard touchdown at 1:51. TCU went three and out, and Simmons took the punt 79 yards to the house at 0:53. Texas Tech 27, TCU 10 at halftime. Texas Tech −15.5 was covering by 1.5, and the total was at 37, 9.5 away from the 46.5.

The third quarter turned the Over into a formality. Castellanos hit Simmons for a 32-yard touchdown at 12:47. A TCU clipping flag on its own punt moved the Red Raiders from the 20 to the 35, a TCU interference flag added 15 more, and Williams caught a 57-yarder down to the TCU 3. Carter ran it in from 3 yards out at 8:59. Texas Tech 41, TCU 10. That's 51 points, and the Over 46.5 cashed with almost 24 minutes left to play. The Under was buried, and Texas Tech −15.5 was ahead of the number by 15.5.

TCU answered with an 18-play drive that ate the rest of the quarter and stalled at the 9, where a third-down throw got knocked away in the end zone. A TCU flag before the next snap backed it up 5 yards, and Lemmermann hit from 32 to close the third. Dickey then ran 16 yards for a touchdown at 11:20 of the fourth. Texas Tech 48, TCU 13, a 35-point margin.

Then the backdoor creaked open. Everhart ripped off 49 yards, and Schobel hit Small for a 4-yard touchdown at 8:57. 48-20. TCU got the ball back at its 12, Everhart broke a 36-yard run to the Texas Tech 15, and Schobel hit Hayes for an 11-yard touchdown with 1:07 left. 48-27, a 21-point game. One more touchdown and TCU +15.5 cashes. TCU kicked it deep for a touchback, Texas Tech ran it twice, and the clock hit zero. Texas Tech won by 21, Texas Tech −15.5 covered by 5.5, and the 75 points cleared the 46.5 by 28.5.

Called it: Follow The Money on Texas Tech −15.5, covered by 5.5 points. The Formula and Letdown Watch on the Over 46.5, cleared it by 28.5 points.
