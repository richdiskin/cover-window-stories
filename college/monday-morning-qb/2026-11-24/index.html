Monday Morning Quarterback: College Tuesday night review, Nov 24, 2026

College Tuesday night review: Kent State at Eastern Michigan and Miami (OH) at Western Michigan (Nov. 24). Then the rest of the college week.

Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026.

XFacebook

Part 1: Handicap Recap

Thanksgiving week opened with two MAC games on Tuesday night, and both are final. The gut punch came from Ypsilanti. Eastern Michigan led Kent State 14-12 early in the fourth quarter when a 40-yard touchdown throw that would have put Eastern Michigan −6.5 tickets in front got wiped out by an ineligible-man-downfield flag. The Eagles won 17-12 on a late field goal and missed the number by 1.5, and the Over 46.5 never had a prayer. In Kalamazoo, Western Michigan −4.5 never got in front of the number, Miami (OH) won 28-24, and the total sat at 45 points, half a point under 45.5, until Miami scored with 5:40 left.

Letdown Watch flipped its Ypsilanti total from Over 45.5 to Under 46.5 early Tuesday morning after the total moved, and nobody changed a pick at Tuesday's Lock It Up. At the late-afternoon line check, Follow The Money jumped on Eastern Michigan −6.5 and Miami (OH) +4.5 after both spreads moved, and The Formula flipped from Miami (OH) +6.5 to Western Michigan −4.5 when the Kalamazoo line dropped two points. In The Trenches sat out both games entirely. Next Man Up made picks on both games.

Kent State 12, Eastern Michigan 17

Eastern Michigan −6.5 backers got paid in their heads 46 seconds into this game. Blue went 61 yards up the middle on the second snap, and it was 7-0 before most people found their seats. Kent State answered by going 90 yards in eight plays, capped by a 17-yard Thanthanavong throw to Smith, and the try after it failed. 7-6. Kessinger then missed from 52 yards early in the second quarter. A Kent State personal foul moved the ball from the Kent State 31 to the 16, and Johnson ran it in from 7 yards out at 2:26. Eastern Michigan 14, Kent State 6. Eastern Michigan −6.5 was covering by 1.5.

It lasted 14 seconds. Smith took the ensuing kickoff 100 yards to the house, and the two-point throw failed. 14-12. Llewellyn picked off Thanthanavong with six seconds left in the half, and the teams went in with Eastern Michigan up 2. Eastern Michigan −6.5 was 4.5 short, and the total sat at 26. The Over 46.5 needed 21 more points in the second half. It got three.

The third quarter was a field-position grind, and Kent State came away empty from the Eastern Michigan 23 when fourth down fell incomplete at 6:30. Then came the play Eagles backers will be replaying all week. Early in the fourth, McCaughey hit Mack for 40 yards and a touchdown, and the flag came out for an ineligible man downfield. That score puts Eastern Michigan up 20-12 before the kick and covering. Instead, the drive died on fourth down at the Kent State 36 at 11:22. Kent State then drove inside the Eastern Michigan 30, and Hopperton fumbled at the 29 with Eastern Michigan falling on it.

Eastern Michigan finally got the break it needed. Llewellyn picked off Thanthanavong again and returned it 35 yards to the Kent State 11 with 2:42 left. First-and-10 at the 11, up 2. A touchdown there covers the number and the bank is open. Blue lost 3, Bennett gained 4, Blue was stuffed for no gain, and Kessinger kicked a 28-yard field goal at 1:57. 17-12. Eastern Michigan −6.5 was 1.5 short with the game basically over.

Kent State still made everybody sweat the result. A 32-yard catch by Harris and an Eastern Michigan interference flag put the ball at the 15, and on fourth down with five seconds left Thanthanavong found Harris in the end zone. It went to review and came back incomplete. Kent State +6.5 tickets were already cashed either way. Eastern Michigan won by 5, lost the number by 1.5, and the 29 total points left the Over 46.5 dead by 17.5.

Called it: Letdown Watch on the Under 46.5, stayed under by 17.5 points.

Miami (OH) 28, Western Michigan 24

Western Michigan −4.5 never covered for one second of this game. Bellisari hit Smith for 31 yards and a touchdown on the opening drive. Western Michigan answered with a short field goal, and then a Western Michigan interference flag moved Miami from the Broncos 30 to the 15, and Bellisari found Loftus for a 14-yard touchdown at 2:46 of the first quarter. 14-3 RedHawks.

Western Michigan coughed up its chances in the second quarter. The Broncos turned it over on downs at the Miami 27, and McCain picked off Lowry at the Miami 12 at 6:52. On the other end, a Western Michigan illegal use of hands flag gave Miami a fresh set at the 11, and McLaughlin missed a 29-yard field goal with five seconds left. Miami 14, Western Michigan 3 at halftime. Western Michigan −4.5 needed a 16-point swing, and the total sat at 17, 28.5 short of the 45.5.

Then the points came in a flood, and the total took over the night. Buckley ripped off 44 yards and finished the drive from 3 yards out at 10:54. Bellisari answered with an 18-yard touchdown run at 7:27. Lowry hit Chenault for a 13-yard touchdown at 4:39, and Miami kept extending the two-point try with three interference flags before Western Michigan finally failed on it. 21-16 after three, 37 points. Then Buckley ran 23 yards for a touchdown at 8:02 of the fourth, and Lowry hit Cabana for the two-pointer. Western Michigan 24, Miami 21. That's 45 points, half a point under the 45.5, with eight minutes to play. Western Michigan led for the first time all night and was still 1.5 short of covering.

Miami didn't let anybody hold that half point for long. A Western Michigan interference flag gave the RedHawks 15 free yards, Jones broke a 25-yard run to the 12, and Bellisari ran it in from 12 yards out at 5:40. 28-24, 52 points. The Over 45.5 cashed and the Under died right there, and Western Michigan −4.5 now needed more than a touchdown.

The Broncos made one last run anyway. A Miami interference flag moved the ball from the 33 to the 18, Buckley got it to the 7, and Western Michigan had first-and-goal. Two runs later, Lowry threw it to Knighten in the end zone with 20 seconds left. Even a touchdown there wins the game by 3 and misses the number. Miami won by 4, Miami +4.5 covered by 8.5, and the 52 points cleared the 45.5 by 6.5.

Called it: Follow The Money on Miami (OH) +4.5, covered by 8.5 points. The Formula and Letdown Watch on the Over 45.5, cleared it by 6.5 points.
