Part 1: Handicap Recap

This is college football Week 9 against the spread, graded, and it was a weekend for bad nerves. Kansas State vs Colorado went to overtime and split the tickets down the middle on the last snap. Kansas vs TCU landed dead on the spread. Western Michigan vs Bowling Green and North Dakota State vs UTEP each missed the number by half a point, Boise State vs Texas State flipped a 25-point halftime lead into a one-point win and a lost cover, and the over/under board broke hearts from Durham to Fresno. Florida vs Georgia and Ohio State vs USC went the wrong way early and stayed there.

All 56 games on the card from Tuesday through Saturday are graded below against the line printed on the card at kickoff, with ESPN finals and play-by-play. A push counts as neither a hit nor a miss. The heartbreakers go first, then the escapes, then the rest of Saturday, then the weeknight games.

Follow The Money and In The Trenches sit out when a game doesn't fit them: Follow The Money had no side in 48 games and no total in 35, and In The Trenches had no side in 34 games and no total in 51. Those games aren't graded for them. Letdown Watch made no picks this week.
