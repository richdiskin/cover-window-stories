Part 1: Handicap Recap

Tuesday put three MAC games on the board, and all three are final. The one that hurts ended on a failed two-point try in Bowling Green. Amachree's 48-yard touchdown run with 1:51 left put Bowling Green up 5, the conversion failed, and Bowling Green −5.5 lost by half a point after leading by 13 in the fourth quarter. In Oxford, the Over 46.5 sat at 37 points with under four minutes to go and got home on a fumble and a 58-yard pick-six in the final two minutes. Western Michigan −14 rolled in Akron, and the Over 48.5 cleared on an Akron touchdown.

Follow The Money moved four times at Tuesday's Lock It Up and is graded on its final numbers: Western Michigan −15.5 and the Over 49.5 in Akron, the Under 43.5 in Bowling Green, and the Over 47.5 in Oxford, with no side in the last two. In The Trenches took Akron +14 and Ohio −1.5, had no side in Bowling Green and had no totals. The Formula, Next Man Up and Letdown Watch made picks on all three games.

Kent State 28, Bowling Green 33

Bowling Green −5.5 was the ticket you could have framed after three quarters. Novosad found Amachree for a 70-yard touchdown in the first quarter, Kenner banged a 46-yard field goal with two seconds left in the half, and it was 17-14 Bowling Green at the break. Novosad hit Cleaver from a yard out and Kenner added a 38-yarder for a 27-14 lead. Bowling Green −5.5 was covering by 7.5, and the total sat at 41, 3.5 under the 44.5.

Then the fourth quarter took a wrecking ball to every ticket on the board. Kent State marched to the Bowling Green 7, and Andrews picked off DeShields at the 2. Big sigh of relief. It lasted about five minutes. DeShields found Harris for a 13-yard touchdown at 6:37, 27-21, and that one score did double duty. It sent the total to 48 and tore up every Under 44.5 and Under 43.5 ticket in the building, and it left Bowling Green −5.5 covering by half a point.

Bowling Green had Kent State backed up and punting at 3:31, and a roughing-the-kicker flag handed the ball right back. DeShields hit Clark for 28 to the Bowling Green 25, then ran 25 yards for the go-ahead touchdown with two minutes left. Kent State 28, Bowling Green 27. Now Bowling Green needed a touchdown just to have a chance at covering.

Nine seconds. A 34-yard kickoff return, a 16-yard catch by Saffold, and Amachree ripped off a 48-yard touchdown run at 1:51. Bowling Green 33, Kent State 28. Bowling Green −5.5 needed the two-point try to get home, Novosad kept it, and the try failed. Bowling Green won by 5 and lost the ticket by half a point. Sawyer picked off DeShields in the end zone at 0:54 to end the last Kent State drive, Bowling Green knelt twice, and the Bowling Green faithful walked out happy while every Bowling Green −5.5 ticket went in the trash.

Called it: The Formula and Letdown Watch on the Over 44.5, cleared it by 16.5 points.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side; In The Trenches on the side or the total.

Ohio 17, Miami (OH) 34

Ohio −1.5 backers were counting their money before halftime. Poulos hit Neer for an 8-yard touchdown, Brune punched one in from a yard out, and Peacock made a 22-yard field goal for a 17-0 lead. Smith found Huggins for 15 yards and a Miami (OH) touchdown with 1:02 left in the half. Ohio −1.5 was covering by 8.5, Miami (OH) +1.5 was dead in the water, and the total sat at 24, 22.5 short of the Over 46.5.

Ohio never scored again. Jones ran in from a yard out in the third, and Stidham tied it 17-17 with a 36-yard field goal early in the fourth. Miami (OH) +1.5 was covering by 1.5. Stidham then made it 20-17 from 25 yards out at 3:55. The Miami ticket looked good. The Over 46.5 looked hopeless at 37 points, needing 9.5 more with under four minutes to play. That's two scores, from two offenses that had managed one touchdown between them since halftime.

Then the bottom fell out for Ohio, and the Over got a gift. Poulos fumbled at his own 48, and McCain forced it and recovered it. On the very next snap, Jones went 48 yards for a touchdown at 1:45. 27-17, 44 points, 2.5 shy of the Over 46.5 and 3.5 shy of the Over 47.5. Ohio got a break when it held onto a muffed kickoff, then Knighten picked off Poulos and went 58 yards the other way for a touchdown at 1:25. Miami (OH) 34, Ohio 17. The Over 46.5 cleared by 4.5, the Over 47.5 cleared by 3.5, and both landed in the final two minutes on a fumble and a pick-six. Miami (OH) +1.5 covered by 18.5 after trailing by 17.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side; In The Trenches on the total.

Western Michigan 42, Akron 14

Western Michigan −14 never really sweated, but the total went down to a late fourth-quarter touchdown. Western Michigan opened with two touchdowns, a 3-yard catch by Levicki and an 82-yard Cabana run. Broughton found Williams for 35 yards to get Akron on the board, Buckley went in from a yard out late in the half, and it was 21-7 at the break. Western Michigan −14 was sitting exactly on the number, and the total was at 28, 20.5 short of 48.5.

Buckley broke a 35-yard touchdown run in the third, and Lowry hit Boone-Nelson from 20 yards out early in the fourth for 35-7. That's 42 points with 13 minutes left. Then Akron did the Over a favor. Broughton found Williams again, this time for 39 yards at 11:44, 35-14, and the total hit 49. The Over 48.5 cleared on an Akron touchdown in a game Akron was losing by three scores. The Over 49.5 still needed a field goal.

Reynolds took care of that, finding Levicki for a 10-yard touchdown at 6:53, 42-14. Western Michigan −14 covered by 14, −15.5 covered by 12.5, and the Over 48.5 and Over 49.5 got home with room to spare. Akron +14 lost by 14, and the Under 48.5 crowd learned that a 28-point blowout can still beat you on the total.

No pick: In The Trenches on the total.
