Monday Morning Quarterback (on Sunday): College Tuesday night review, Nov 3, 2026

4, 2026. College Tuesday night review: Ohio at Akron and Buffalo at Miami (OH) (Nov. 3). Then the rest of the college week.

Wednesday, Nov.

ShareXFacebook

Part 1: Handicap Recap

This is the Tuesday night MAC doubleheader against the spread, graded, and both games came down to the last few minutes for somebody's money. Buffalo spotted Miami (OH) a 15-point lead in the fourth quarter, came all the way back and won in overtime, which tore up every Miami −6.5 ticket on the board. Ohio vs Akron was never close to the 13.5, but the Over 51.5 sat 4 points away for the last 1:56 and never got there.

Both games are graded below against the line printed on the card at kickoff, with ESPN finals and play-by-play. The cards first went up at Akron +12.5 with a total of 50.5, and Miami (OH) −6.5 with a total of 44.5. The pre-kickoff refresh moved them to Akron +13.5 and 51.5, and Miami (OH) −6.5 and 45.5, and that's the number graded here. Every result lands the same at either number. A push counts as neither a hit nor a miss. The heartbreaker goes first.

Follow The Money had no side in Buffalo vs Miami (OH) because its number was still pending when the card went up, and In The Trenches reads sides only, so it had no total in either game. Those aren't graded. Letdown Watch made no picks Tuesday.

Buffalo 34, Miami (OH) 31

If you laid the 6.5 with Miami (OH), you had this one cashed with 12 minutes left. Miami led 17-10 at the half and 24-10 early in the third, and Buffalo kept handing it back. Wright hit Morrow for 39 yards down to the Miami 5, then got picked off by Coleman Jr. at the 1 on the very next snap. Three minutes later Young picked him again. When Buffalo finally got in, Hautanen missed the extra point, and Smith ran it in from 6 yards out at 12:01 of the fourth for a 31-16 lead. Miami −6.5 was covering by 8.5, and the Over 45.5 was already cashed at 47 points.

Then Buffalo stopped giving it away. Wright and Morrow went 75 yards in 11 plays, finished it with a 4-yard touchdown and hooked up again on the two-point try at 8:56. 31-24, and the 6.5 was down to half a point of cushion. Miami picked up two first downs and stalled, Clark sacked Heavner for 6 yards, and Miami punted it away.

Buffalo's last drive lived on Miami flags. Yharbrough got flagged for interference on a deep ball at 3:58, and from the 11 with 1:54 left Coppess got flagged for it too, putting the ball at the 2. Two incompletions later, Wright ran it in from 2 yards out. 31-31 with 1:35 left, and the Over was already 16.5 points clear.

Miami −6.5 still had one breath going into overtime: hold Buffalo scoreless, score a touchdown, kick the extra point and win by 7. Buffalo killed that on the first possession when Hautanen hit from 27 yards for a 34-31 lead. Miami got the ball, a flag for moving early backed it up 5 yards, and McLaughlin's 45-yard field goal try missed. Buffalo +6.5 cashed with an outright win, and 65 points left the Over 45.5 nearly 20 points clear. Anybody holding Miami −6.5 watched a 15-point fourth-quarter lead turn into a loss on the scoreboard and at the window.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side; In The Trenches on the total.

Ohio 28, Akron 20

Akron +13.5 was never in trouble. Ohio's biggest lead all night was 11. DJ Walker took an interception 45 yards for an Ohio touchdown in the first quarter, Peacock missed a 41-yard field goal at the end of the half, and Akron actually led 17-14 after Gant scored from a yard out and Akron converted the two-point try with 9:35 left. Ohio answered 13 seconds later when Poulos hit Dorwart for a 60-yard touchdown. Akron backers never had to sweat the number.

The Over 51.5 was the sweat. At 21-17 Akron had fourth down at the Ohio 9 with 4:11 left, and Broughton's throw for Burris fell incomplete. On the very next snap Brune went 91 yards for a touchdown. 28-17, 45 points, and the Over needed one more touchdown to get home.

Akron gave it that touchdown chance. Hale got flagged for roughing the passer to put the ball at the Ohio 25, then three straight throws fell incomplete and Akron settled for Stire's 43-yard field goal at 1:56. 48 points. The onside kick went 9 yards and Ohio took over, and Ohio's last snap was a fourth-down deep ball from Poulos toward Dorwart with 4 seconds left that fell incomplete. Final 28-20, and the Over 51.5 died 3.5 points short. Akron +13.5 cashed by 5.5 and never needed that last drive.

No pick: In The Trenches on the total.
