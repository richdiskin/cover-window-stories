Part 1: Handicap Recap

Saturday put 58 games on the card, and 53 of them are final and graded below. Five late games are still being played: Utah at TCU, San Diego State at Boise State, Oklahoma State at Arizona State, Utah State at Oregon State and Hawai'i at Nevada. They stay pending, and they get graded here in the next update once ESPN has the finals.

It was a night for the half-point and the last-second score. The Over 49.5 at Penn State died at 49 when Rutgers won 25-24 on a two-point play with no time left. SMU +20.5 lost by half a point because Notre Dame ran out the clock with kneel-downs. The Under 47.5 at Texas missed by half a point at 48, and the Under 54.5 at Marshall missed by half a point when Georgia State scored with 2 seconds left. Southern Miss +4.5 lost by half a point after its two-point try failed, Old Dominion scored with 1 second left to take 5.5 points off UConn −13.5, and Navy scored with 15 seconds left to beat Memphis −2.5 outright.

The other side of the window had its moments too. Kansas State +7.5 cashed by half a point in overtime, Maryland +17.5 and the Under 56.5 both cashed with the last point scored at 56, and Chattanooga +48.5 cashed when Alabama stopped at 47. Wake Forest −21 landed exactly on the number, and five tickets got their money back. BYU at Kansas went three overtimes and UCLA at Michigan went three overtimes, and Colorado State climbed back from 22 down to cash +20.5.

Every game below is graded against the line printed on the card at kickoff, using ESPN finals and play-by-play, and a push counts as neither a hit nor a miss. Saturday's cards were refreshed on game day, and Lock It Up line moves count at their final lines. The heartbreakers go first, then the nail-biters, then the rest of the card, then the five games still being played.

Follow The Money had a side in 14 of the graded games and a total in 25, and In The Trenches had a side in 27 and no totals. Games without a pick aren't graded for them.
