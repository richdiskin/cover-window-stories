Part 1: Handicap Recap

Friday put five college games on the board, and all five are final and graded here. The one that will keep South Florida backers up at night ended on a 55-yard field goal as time ran out in Greenville. It turned South Florida +1.5 from a cash into a loss on the last snap and left the Over 39.5 stuck at 37. SMU −5.5 and the Over 56.5 cruised, and Illinois +4.5 and the Under 52.5 never needed a sweat in Champaign. Late in Tucson, Arizona needed a muffed kickoff and a field goal with 38 seconds left just to win by one, and Arizona −6.5 lost anyway. In Reno, New Mexico won by 28, and Nevada +10.5 was never close.

Follow The Money had a side only in Tucson, on TCU +6.5, and took the Over at SMU, in Champaign and in Tucson. In The Trenches had a side only in Tucson, on Arizona −6.5, and made no total picks. Letdown Watch made no picks Friday. Games without a pick aren't graded for them.

South Florida 17, East Carolina 20

South Florida +1.5 was the kind of ticket you could hold with a straight face for 59 minutes and 58 seconds. Kromenhoek hit Rodgers for a 26-yard touchdown on the opening drive, Perez answered with a 44-yard field goal, Gramatica made it 10-3 from 30, and Williams found Sides from 15 yards out with 31 seconds left in the half. It was 10-10 at the break. South Florida +1.5 was covering by 1.5, and the total sat at 20, with the Over 39.5 needing 19.5 more.

The third quarter is where both tickets began to sweat. South Florida turned it over on downs at the East Carolina 32, and Williams hit Sides again from 8 yards out for a 17-10 East Carolina lead. Collins Jr. punched it in from a yard out at 9:42 of the fourth, it was 17-17, and South Florida +1.5 was right back to covering by 1.5. The total sat at 34. The Over 39.5 needed a touchdown, because a field goal would still leave it 2.5 short.

Then came nine and a half minutes of punts, sacks and flags. A South Florida holding call wiped out a breakup and handed East Carolina a fresh set of downs, and East Carolina punted anyway. South Florida punted it right back with under two minutes left and the game tied. On the last drive, an interference flag on South Florida moved East Carolina 10 yards to its own 49, Williams hit Gray for 18 to the South Florida 40, spiked it, and found Gray for 3 more to the 37. South Florida called timeout with two seconds left, and Andrew Conrad drilled a 55-yard field goal as time ran out. East Carolina 20, South Florida 17. South Florida +1.5 lost by 1.5 on the final snap, and the morning number of +2.5 would have lost by half a point. The Over needed a touchdown from somebody, got a field goal, and died at 37.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side or the total; In The Trenches on the side or the total.

Virginia Tech 28, SMU 51

SMU −5.5 never sweated, and the Over 56.5 got home with nine minutes to spare. Green ran 12 yards for a touchdown on the opening drive, Baird hit from 47, and Grunkemeyer snuck one in for Virginia Tech. Then came the turnover that tilted it. Grunkemeyer got sacked and stripped by White at the Virginia Tech 46, and Jennings hit Knight for a 46-yard touchdown on the very next snap. Overton ran 22 yards for a Virginia Tech touchdown, Baird made a 50-yarder with three seconds left in the half, and SMU led 23-14 at the break. SMU −5.5 was covering by 3.5, and the total sat at 37, 19.5 short of the Over.

Raphael ran in from 3 yards out, Virginia Tech turned it over on downs at the SMU 25, and Knight caught a 13-yard touchdown. It was 37-14 after three, SMU −5.5 was covering by 17.5, and the Over needed 5.5 more with a full quarter to play.

Knight's third touchdown, a 5-yarder from Jennings at 9:15 of the fourth, made it 44-14 and 58 points, and every Under 56.5 ticket in the building was torn up right there. Virginia Tech piled on backdoor points anyway, Overton from 2 with a two-point conversion and Grunkemeyer to Brown from 3, but Jennings answered with a 50-yard strike to Cooper and the SMU side never felt a breeze. SMU −5.5 covered by 17.5, and the Over cleared by 22.5.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side; In The Trenches on the side or the total.

Nebraska 13, Illinois 24

Illinois +4.5 and the Under 52.5 were cashing from the first quarter on, and Nebraska's kicker made sure of it. Hohl missed from 45 and again from 54 just before the half. In between, Nelson ran 15 yards for a Nebraska touchdown and Houser found Dixon from 3, and it was 7-7 at the break. Illinois +4.5 was covering by 4.5, and the total sat at 14, a mile under 52.5.

Houser hit Anderson from 5 yards out, Nebraska turned it over on downs late in the third, and Houser found Dixon again from 6 early in the fourth. It was 21-7 Illinois, and the Illinois ticket was covering by 18.5.

Colandrea hit Barney for a 51-yard touchdown, and the two-point try failed. Moczulski answered with a 21-yard field goal, 24-13. Nebraska turned it over on downs at 2:17 and again in the last minute, and that was it. Illinois +4.5 covered by 15.5, the Under stayed under by 15.5, and Nebraska −4.5 backers can send their complaints to the two missed kicks.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side; In The Trenches on the side or the total.

New Mexico 31, Nevada 3

Nevada +10.5 and the Over 38.5 were in trouble from the first quarter. Humphrey scored from 2 yards out, Sibley ran 31 yards for a touchdown, and New Mexico led 14-3 at the half after Fernandez's 32-yard field goal. Humphrey's second touchdown and Fa'avae's 25-yard throw to Keith made it 28-3, and Romero's 37-yard field goal capped it. New Mexico 31, Nevada 3. Nevada +10.5 lost by 17.5, and the Over 38.5 fell 4.5 points short with Nevada scoring a single field goal.

Called it: Next Man Up on New Mexico −10.5, covered by 17.5 points.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side or the total; In The Trenches on the side or the total.

TCU 28, Arizona 29

Arizona −6.5 looked like the right ticket for a quarter. Fifita hit Mizell for a 20-yard touchdown, Salgado-Medina added a 43-yard field goal, and Arizona led 10-0. Then Jaden Craig ran 7 yards for a TCU touchdown, and Hooper returned an interception 93 yards for another. TCU took a 14-10 lead into the half, Arizona −6.5 was losing by 10.5, and the total sat at 24, with the Under 46.5 holding 22.5 points of room.

Fifita ran in from 9 yards out, and Dwyer caught a 6-yard touchdown from Jaden Craig, so it was 21-17 TCU after three. Salgado-Medina's 23-yard field goal early in the fourth made it 21-20. Then Jaden Craig ran in from 2 yards out with 6:35 left, TCU led 28-20, and the total hit 48. The Under 46.5 was gone with six and a half minutes to play.

Arizona still needed a touchdown and a field goal just to win, and more than that to cover. Arizona's Quincy Craig fumbled at the TCU 9, and Fifita recovered at the 15. Fifita found Quincy Craig for a 15-yard touchdown with 1:45 left, and the two-point throw failed. TCU's Ed Small muffed a 35-yard kickoff, and Arizona's Zuri Watson recovered at the TCU 26. Salgado-Medina hit a 37-yard field goal with 38 seconds left, and Boss intercepted Jaden Craig on TCU's last drive. Arizona 29, TCU 28. Arizona −6.5 won the game and lost the ticket by 5.5, and the Under 46.5 went over by 10.5.

Called it: Follow The Money on TCU +6.5, covered by 5.5 points. Follow The Money on the Over 46.5, cleared it by 10.5 points.

No pick: In The Trenches on the total.
