College Thursday night review: Memphis at South Florida and Louisiana at UL Monroe (Nov. 12). Then the rest of the college week.

Friday, Nov. 13, 2026.

Part 1: Handicap Recap

Thursday put two more college games on the board, and both are final. The one that hurts came in Tampa. South Florida +5.5 climbed out of a 21-0 hole, was covering by 1.5 with under four minutes left, came up empty on fourth down at the Memphis 4, and then watched Carter run 26 yards for a Memphis touchdown with 44 seconds left. Memphis won by 11, and the Bulls ticket lost by 5.5. In Monroe, Louisiana −11.5 needed two lost UL Monroe fumbles and a late Blanco touchdown to get home, and the Over 48.5 cashed in both games. Memphis and South Florida combined for 93 points against a 48.5 total.

Memphis 52, South Florida 41

South Florida +5.5 was the ticket that came back from the dead and then got buried twice in the last four minutes. Memphis opened like it wanted to cash everybody's Memphis −5.5 before the popcorn was gone. Stokes ran 53 yards for a touchdown, Isaac fumbled on the next USF snap and Ishmael recovered at the USF 21, and Stokes ran in from 8 yards out. 21-0 in the first quarter. South Florida then marched 13 plays to the Memphis 23 and walked away empty when Kinsler sacked Kromenhoek on fourth down. The Bulls ticket looked like a donation.

The Over 48.5 crowd never had that problem. Memphis missed a 60-yard field goal try, but Kromenhoek found Reuben twice for touchdowns, the second from 19 yards with 49 seconds left in the half, and it was 28-17 at the break with 45 points already on the board. Collins Jr. caught a 13-yard touchdown early in the third, 28-24, and the Over 48.5 was cashed with more than 24 minutes still to play. The rest was gravy for Over holders: Collins Jr. went 75 yards on a single carry ten seconds of clock after a Memphis score, and Kromenhoek's 23-yard touchdown run tied it at 38.

Now the South Florida +5.5 tickets were alive and kicking. Memphis turned it over on downs at the USF 32 to open the fourth, Gramatica's 38-yard field goal put the Bulls up 41-38 at 8:04, and on the second snap after the kickoff, Stokes hit Davis for 76 yards and a touchdown. Even then, Memphis 45, South Florida 41 had the Bulls covering by 1.5. Reuben caught a 44-yarder, Kromenhoek ran 13 yards to the Memphis 12, and South Florida was knocking on the door with a chance to win outright. Fourth down from the 4, Kromenhoek threw for Rodgers, and it fell incomplete at 3:38. Still covering. Memphis went three-and-out from deep in its own end, and Tuck punted it 51 yards. Still covering.

Then the floor gave out. Tapp sacked Kromenhoek twice on the next series, the fourth-down heave for Reuben fell incomplete, and Memphis took over at the USF 30 with 57 seconds left, up 4. The South Florida +5.5 crowd only needed Memphis to run some clock. Carter ran 26 yards for a touchdown with 44 seconds left. Memphis 52, South Florida 41. An 11-point margin, and South Florida +5.5 lost by 5.5 after covering for most of the fourth quarter. In The Trenches on Memphis −5.5 was losing that ticket for most of the fourth quarter and cashed it with 44 seconds left, and the Over 48.5 cleared by 44.5.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side or the total; In The Trenches on the total.

Louisiana 42, UL Monroe 21

Louisiana −11.5 and the Over 48.5 both cashed, and both made their backers earn it. The first half was a coin flip that nobody holding Louisiana −11.5 wanted. Carlisle ran in from a yard out to put UL Monroe up first, Winfield found Sampson Jr. for a 13-yard touchdown, Carlisle threw one to Tucker, and Wade's 8-yard run with 40 seconds left made it 14-14. UL Monroe drove to midfield before the break, and Jackson picked off Carlisle at the Louisiana 13 with five seconds left. Tied at the half, Louisiana −11.5 was 11.5 points from home, and the total sat at 28, 20.5 short of the Over 48.5.

Then the Warhawks began handing the ball to the other team. Jacob punched it out of Carlisle's arms on the first UL Monroe snap of the third quarter, and Johnson scooped it and ran 18 yards for a touchdown. 21-14. UL Monroe turned it over on downs at the Louisiana 40, then Eleam forced a fumble from Robinson, Johnson recovered again at the UL Monroe 19, and Garner ran it in from 19 yards on the very next snap. 28-14, and Louisiana −11.5 was covering by 2.5. Garner went 18 yards for another one at 11:32 of the fourth. Louisiana 35, UL Monroe 14: 49 points, and the Over 48.5 cashed right there by half a point with 11 minutes to spare.

Louisiana −11.5 still had a sweat coming. UL Monroe went 75 yards in 15 plays, and Chaney's 6-yard touchdown run with 5:52 left made it 35-21. A 14-point game, Louisiana −11.5 covering by 2.5, and two Warhawks scores away from a backdoor cover. UL Monroe tried the onside kick, and it went 15 yards to midfield. Louisiana kept it on the ground. Blanco ran 9 yards into the end zone, and a Louisiana procedure flag took it off the board. Two plays later Blanco scored from 2 yards out with 1:57 left. Louisiana 42, UL Monroe 21. Louisiana −11.5 covered by 9.5, and the Over 48.5 cleared by 14.5.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side or the total; In The Trenches on the side or the total.
