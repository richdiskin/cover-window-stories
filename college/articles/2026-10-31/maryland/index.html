Maryland vs Illinois: Terps Fall 20-17 on Final-Play Kick

Final: Illinois (5-3) 20, Maryland (2-6) 17, Sat., Oct. 31, SECU Stadium, College Park. Next: at Purdue, Sat., Nov. 7, 12:30 PM ET, Ross-Ade Stadium, West Lafayette

XFacebook

Illinois 20, Maryland 17, and it ended on a kick with no time left. SECU Stadium hosted a home game on Halloween for the first time since 1992, and the Terps came out in red helmets, black jerseys and black pants, a look not worn since Nov. 9, 1991, at Baltimore Memorial Stadium. Back in black, back in it, and still on the wrong end. That one stings like a crab pinch.

This was the first game under interim head coach Aazaar Abdul-Rahim, and the effort was real. Messiah Delhomme intercepted Illinois on the opening drive, his fourth career interception, and set us up at the Illinois 12. We gave it back with a fumble at the 1. Then Malik Washington ran 21 yards to get us back in the red zone and scored on an 8-yard keeper. Maryland 7-0.

Illinois tied it before halftime. Our offense opened the third quarter with a seven-play, 65-yard drive. Washington went 5 for 5 for 44 yards on it, and DeJuan Williams ran in from 17. 14-7 Terps. Illinois answered with a 45-yard field goal, and then Javari Barnett broke a 64-yard touchdown run on the first snap of the fourth.

Sean O'Haire answered with a season-long 46-yard field goal to tie it 17-17 with 10:16 left. That was his 30th field goal as a Terp. Then Illinois took the ball at its own 26 with 5:45 left, ran 13 plays for 67 yards, drained every second and kicked a 26-yarder on the final play.

Lavain Scruggs led the defense with 10 tackles. Chris Durr Jr. caught six balls for 58 yards. Washington threw for 190 yards and ran for a score. We drew one penalty all day for five yards, our fewest since 2022. That's clean, disciplined football. A student also won a free semester of tuition on the field in the fourth quarter, so somebody in College Park left a winner.

We're 2-6 and 0-5 in the Big Ten. Purdue is next on the road, Saturday, Nov. 7, at 12:30 PM ET in Ross-Ade Stadium. Pack the Old Bay. Fear the Turtle.
