Arizona vs Texas Tech: Fifita's 417 Not Enough in Lubbock 34-26

Final: Texas Tech (7-1) 34, Arizona (6-2) 26, Sat., Oct. 31, Galaxy Stadium, Lubbock. Next: vs. TCU, Fri., Nov. 6, Casino Del Sol Stadium, Tucson

Texas Tech 34, Arizona 26, and the five-game winning streak is over in Lubbock. Back home on Saturday, 40 Arizona high school bands filled Casino Del Sol Stadium for the 73rd U of A Band Day with the Pride of Arizona. Tucson played loud. We needed that kind of noise on the road and only got it in bursts.

The bursts were big. Michael Salgado-Medina opened the scoring from 35 yards. In the second quarter Noah Fifita hit Isaiah Mizell for 94 yards, the kind of throw that makes a whole desert stand up, and Kedrick Reescano punched in a 5-yard run. We went to the half up 17-10 in their building.

Then the ground game dried up like an August wash. We ran 30 times for 12 yards. Twelve. Fifita carried the whole offense on his arm, 33 of 45 for 417 yards and a touchdown with zero interceptions, and we had zero turnovers. Mizell finished with seven catches for 182 yards, his third straight 100-yard game, and Giovanni Richardson added six for 90.

Texas Tech answered on the ground. Thomas Castellanos ran it in from 8 to tie it 17-17. Reescano scored again from a yard out, but the extra point got blocked, and that missing point hung over the rest of the night. Castellanos found Kenny Johnson for 27 yards on fourth-and-2, and we trailed 24-23. Their kicker made it 27-23 from 42 early in the fourth.

We had our shot. Fourth-and-1 at the Tech 18, and the throw sailed. Later Dwight Bootle II jumped on a Cameron Dickey fumble, the one gift of the night. Then J'Koby Williams broke a 64-yard run with 2:05 left, and that was the dagger. Salgado-Medina hit from 22 with 22 seconds left, Texas Tech recovered the onside kick, and we were done.

Credit where it's due. Tech ran for 328 yards, and Williams had 175 of them on 14 carries. Malcolm Hartzog Jr. made 10 tackles trying to plug it. A saguaro takes decades to grow an arm. This team grew a passing game in one afternoon. Now it needs legs.

We are 6-2 and 3-2 in the Big 12. TCU comes to Casino Del Sol Stadium on Friday, Nov. 6, at 10:15 PM ET. Home crowd, Friday night, back to work. Bear Down.
