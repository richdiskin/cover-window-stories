Ohio State vs USC: Field Goals Can't Save Buckeyes 30-18

Final: USC (7-2) 30, Ohio State (6-2) 18, Sat., Oct. 31, Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum, Los Angeles. Next: vs. Oregon, Sat., Nov. 7, Ohio Stadium, Columbus

XFacebook

USC 30, Ohio State 18, and this one stings all the way back to High Street. Columbus already did its trick-or-treat on Thursday, and on Saturday our hockey team hosted That Place at Value City Arena at 5 p.m. Not saying the name. No buckeye candy fine tonight. Out at the Coliseum, the football team walked straight into a haunted house.

The fright started on the second snap. Jayden Maiava hit Trent Mosley for 69 yards and a touchdown 39 seconds in. Connor Hawkins answered with a 39-yarder, then our punt team got torched: Mosley returned one 44 yards, a personal foul on us tacked on 15 more, and Waymond Jordan ran in from 9. 14-3.

We kept moving the ball and kept kicking. Hawkins hit from 34, Ryon Sayeri answered from 54, and then Kenyatta Jackson Jr. punched the ball out of King Miller's arms and Earl Little Jr. fell on it at our 42. We drove 13 plays to their 1, a false start shoved us back, and Hawkins kicked from 23. Caden Chittenden's 36-yarder with one second left made it 20-9 at the half.

The back-breaker came in the third. We were at their 31 when Isaiah West caught a short throw, Desman Stephens II knocked it loose, and Floyd Boucard fell on it. Mark Bowman's 70-yard catch set up Miller's 1-yard run, 27-9. Hawkins went four for four from 47, and Luke Fahey capped a 13-play, 80-yard drive with a 1-yard touchdown throw to Jeremiah Smith with 2:12 left. The two-point try failed, USC grabbed the onside kick, and Chittenden kicked from 32.

One last shot. Fahey found Chris Henry Jr. for 39, Bo Jackson for 15 and Smith for 20 to reach their 12 with a minute to go. Four straight throws into the end zone fell incomplete, and the ball went over on downs with 36 seconds left.

My Route Tree segment on this one writes itself, and it's ugly. We outgained USC 464 yards to 338, held the ball 39:28 and piled up 25 first downs. Jackson ran 21 times for 121 yards, Smith caught 10 for 83, and Fahey went 21 of 38 for 260. But 11 penalties for 71 yards, 7 of 16 on third down, a fumble that turned into seven points, and four field goals where we needed touchdowns. You can't trade threes for sevens with a team that scores in two snaps.

We're 6-2 and 4-1 in the Big Ten. No time to sulk: Oregon comes to Ohio Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7. The press box hallway is open, and I'm running routes until this tape makes sense. Go Bucks.
