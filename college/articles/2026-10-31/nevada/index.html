Nevada vs UCLA: Wolf Pack Shut Out at the Rose Bowl, Fall 27-0

Final: UCLA (8-0) 27, Nevada (3-5) 0, Sat., Oct. 31, Rose Bowl, Pasadena. Next: vs. New Mexico, Fri., Nov. 6, Mackay Stadium, Reno

ShareXFacebook

UCLA 27, Nevada 0, at the Rose Bowl, and this was a powder day that went sideways before the first chair loaded. Back home in Reno, Reno Public Market on Plumb Lane opened its food hall to trick-or-treaters from 4 to 7 on Halloween, kids in costume working the vendor stalls. The Pack went trick-or-treating in Pasadena and came home with an empty bag.

On our first snap of the day, Scooter Jackson intercepted Luke Duncan at our 30, and UCLA kicked a 26-yard field goal. Our next drive reached the UCLA 16 before Jalen Woods intercepted Duncan there. One drive later, Derrick Barksdale intercepted Duncan and ran it back 26 yards for a touchdown. Three interceptions, and 7:33 still left in the first quarter. 10-0.

Credit the defense for digging in after that. UCLA's next three drives ended in punts. Ty Dieffenbach finally ran it in from the 4 with 1:20 left in the half, and Mateen Bhaghani missed from 51 at the gun. 17-0.

The third quarter is where we needed a turn and never got it. UCLA went 13 plays and 75 yards, Dieffenbach throwing 12 yards to Wayne Knight. We answered with a 10-play drive, and Joe McFadden missed from 48. Bhaghani hit from 38 in the fourth, and Ty Lee intercepted Reeve Slone with 45 seconds left.

The defense kept us upright. EJ Smith made nine tackles, Larry Turner-Gooden had eight and broke up two throws, and Dylan LaBarbera shared a sack and broke up two more. UCLA finished with 303 yards. Ewan Baulch punted five times for a 44.4 average. But we threw four interceptions, ran 35 times for 65 yards and finished with 176 yards and 10 first downs. Marshaun Brown caught three balls for 51 yards, and AJ Bianco went 6 of 13 for 71.

Some days the mountain wins. You pack it in, wax the board and come back meaner.

We're 3-5, 2-2 in the Mountain West. New Mexico comes to Mackay Stadium on Friday night, Nov. 6, and Reno gets its own team back. Go Pack.
