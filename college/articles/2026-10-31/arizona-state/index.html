Arizona State vs BYU: Sun Devils Rally, Fall Late in Provo 27-20

Final: BYU (7-1) 27, Arizona State (4-4) 20, Sat., Oct. 31, LaVell Edwards Stadium, Provo. Next: vs. Colorado, Sat., Nov. 7, Mountain America Stadium, Tempe

ShareXFacebook

BYU 27, Arizona State 20, and the Sun Devils dragged No. 8 BYU into a fourth quarter it did not want. Back in Tempe, the trombones, tubas and euphoniums of the Happy How-Low-Ween Concert were booked for Nelson Fine Arts Plaza at 5 p.m., and I wanted that brass playing a victory song. It played a sad one instead, and it played it loud.

Jake Fette made his first career start in Provo, and the first quarter was rough. He fumbled on the third snap of our opening drive, and BYU scored when Sione Moa scooped up a Cougar fumble at the goal and took it in. In the second quarter, Faletau Satuala blocked a field goal, we got new life on a Cougar personal foul, and still settled for a 24-yarder from Carson Smith. Down 17-3 at the half, six sacks taken by the end of the day. Ouch.

Then the third quarter happened, and it was all maroon and gold. Fette led a 13-play, 81-yard march and hit Omarion Miller from 20 yards out. Owen Long punched the ball out of Kyler Kasper's hands, Adrian Wilson fell on it at the BYU 39, and we converted two fourth downs on the way to Fette's 1-yard touchdown throw to Anthony Miller. Seventeen all, with 1:15 left in the third, and 63,332 people in Provo got very quiet.

Cardae Mack was the engine all day: 123 rushing yards on 23 carries plus five catches for 47 more, 170 yards of pure Tempe toughness. Long finished with 10 tackles, and Montana Warren intercepted Bear Bachmeier right before the half. Smith hit both of his field goals, including a 44-yarder with 57 seconds left that pulled us within seven.

The honest film session hurts. BYU went 95 yards in 11 plays to put it away, Bachmeier found Legend Glasker for 53 on the big one, and Moa scored from a yard out with 2:09 left. Fette went 22 of 42 for 166 yards and two touchdowns with no interceptions, but we were 6 of 17 on third down, and that is the whole story.

So, a quick sip of water, a long exhale, and the truth: we won the third quarter 14-0 on the road against a top-10 team. That is a group with fight in it.

We're 4-4 and 2-3 in the Big 12. Colorado comes to Mountain America Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7. Forks up.
