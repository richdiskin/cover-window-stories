Rutgers vs Michigan: Knights Rally, Fall Late at SHI 28-21

Final: Michigan (5-3) 28, Rutgers (2-6) 21, Sat., Oct. 31, SHI Stadium, Piscataway. Next: at Wisconsin, Sat., Nov. 7, Camp Randall Stadium, Madison

XFacebook

Michigan 28, Rutgers 21, and it hurts because we had them tied with 11:43 to play. Halloween On the Banks turned the Rutgers Boardwalk into a trick-or-treat route, with a costume contest, a mechanical pumpkin and a haunted halftime show from the Marching Scarlet Knights. The scary part came after halftime.

Listen to me. For three quarters this team looked like it wanted a fight with a big Michigan program, and I loved every minute. Dane Pizzaro hit from 46 yards to get us on the board. Then AJ Surace launched one deep to Jourdin Houston, and Houston was gone for an 80-yard touchdown, the first of his career. Pizzaro added a 32-yarder before the half, and we trailed only 14-13. Down here we invented college football, and that throw looked like a first edition.

Michigan went up 21-13 in the third. Then the break we needed came early in the fourth. A kick-catch interference flag on Michigan gave us the ball back at our 37, and three snaps later Antwan Raymond ripped off a 54-yard run to the 3. Clay Thevenin ran it in from a yard out, Surace found Ben Black III for the two-point try, and it was 21-21. I bought a six-foot sub on the spot to celebrate. Big mistake.

Michigan took over at its own 2 and ground out 11 plays and 75 yards over 7:19, finishing with Tommy Carr's 9-yard run at 4:24. We got the ball back and went three-and-out. That was the ballgame.

The honest box score: we had nine first downs and 249 yards, and Surace completed three of 12 throws, even if those three went for 123 yards. Michigan converted 12 of 16 third downs and held the ball for 39:06. Our defense was out there forever. JJ Conyers and Moses Walker each made nine tackles, Devonte Anderson and Sean Allison made eight apiece, and Sekou Kromah got a sack. Raymond finished with 85 yards on 11 carries.

We are 2-6 and 1-4 in the Big Ten, and nobody on the Turnpike is hiding that. Next week we go to Wisconsin at Camp Randall Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7. Bring that fourth-quarter fight for all four quarters, Scarlet Knights. The sub can wait.
