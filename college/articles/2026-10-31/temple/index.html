Temple vs East Carolina: Owls Grind but Fall 17-9 in Greenville

Final: East Carolina (4-4) 17, Temple (2-6) 9, Sat., Oct. 31, Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium, Greenville. Next: at Navy, Sat., Nov. 7, 3:30 PM ET, Navy-Marine Corps Memorial Stadium, Annapolis

ShareXFacebook

East Carolina 17, Temple 9. On Halloween night back on North Broad, Temple Theaters ran Alice by Heart at the Randall Theater on 13th Street, a story about kids pushing through the London Blitz on grit and imagination. Down in Greenville, our Owls spent the afternoon doing the same thing on a football field. Grit we had. Finishing we didn't.

Real talk from the gym floor: the defense did its reps. ECU went 77 yards on 13 plays for a 1-yard score late in the first quarter, and after that the Owls dug in. We stuffed a fourth-down try in the red zone in the first half, stopped a fake punt in the third, and held the Pirates to 4 of 12 on third down. Curly Ordonez made 14 tackles. Jayvant Brown and Saboor Karriem each made nine.

Carl Hardin carried the scoreboard by himself. He hit from 41 before halftime, from 46 late in the third and from 41 again with 10:12 left, a career-high three field goals. That cut a 17-3 hole to 17-9, one possession. That's Temple Made: you get knocked down, you get back on the bar.

Then came the part that keeps me up at night. Right after the fake punt stop, an interception on the next snap gave it right back. Late in the fourth, Karriem gave us a lifeline with his first career interception at our 21. Two snaps later, we threw another one. Three interceptions in all. You can't leave three reps on the floor and expect to win.

Ajani Sheppard ran for 69 yards on 15 carries and threw for 118. Keveun Mason added 51 on eight carries, and Temple has now run for 100-plus yards in all eight games. JoJo Bermudez caught three balls for 48 yards and broke a 33-yard punt return.

We're 2-6 and 1-3 in the American. Navy is next, on the road Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:30 PM ET at Navy-Marine Corps Memorial Stadium in Annapolis. Chalk up, Owls. Temple Made.
