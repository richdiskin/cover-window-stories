Washington vs Nebraska: McLaughlin Seals Halloween Win 17-10

Final: Washington (5-3) 17, Nebraska (5-3) 10, Sat., Oct. 31, Memorial Stadium (Lincoln, NE), Lincoln. Next: vs. Penn State, Sat., Nov. 7, Husky Stadium, Seattle

XFacebook

Washington 17, Nebraska 10, and I am rowing home from Lincoln on pure adrenaline. Back in Seattle, U Village merchants spent Halloween Saturday handing out treats to trick-or-treaters. In Memorial Stadium, wrapped in black for Nebraska's Blackout game, the Huskies took the treat with 1:30 left and then grabbed a second helping 15 seconds later.

Let me be honest about the first three quarters, because this boat doesn't float on fibs. Our offense ran 19 times for 32 yards. Elijah Brown went 19 of 30 for 136 yards and got sacked twice, which no coxswain would allow. Nebraska went 95 yards on a 10-play drive in the first quarter and Anthony Colandrea hit Carter Nelson for a 24-yard touchdown. Tyler Robles drilled a 50-yard field goal with 41 seconds left in the half, and it sat 7-3 until deep in the fourth.

The defense kept us in the water all night. Rylon Dillard-Allen intercepted Colandrea at our 8 on Nebraska's opening drive and ran it back 20 yards. On Nebraska's next drive, Emmanuel Karnley intercepted him again at our 30. Early in the fourth quarter, Nebraska had first-and-goal at our 5, then a delay of game, two incompletions and Derek Colman-Brusa's 8-yard sack. The 36-yard field goal try missed. Zaydrius Rainey-Sale finished with 10 tackles and a sack.

Then Demond Williams Jr. took the oar. Starting from our 14 after a false start, he ran for 17 yards, found Chris Lawson for 22, and shook off two Blackshirt sacks in four snaps. With 1:30 left, he hit Lawson for a 25-yard touchdown. 10-7, Washington. Lawson caught 12 balls for 155 yards. That man earned a seat in the lead boat.

On Nebraska's next snap after an 11-yard catch, Alex McLaughlin jumped a Colandrea throw at the Nebraska 48 and ran it all the way back. Forty-eight yards, touchdown, 17-7 with 1:15 to go. A 42-yard field goal from John Hohl made it 17-10, and that was it. Hunter Green punted eight times for a 48.9-yard average with five inside the 20, the quiet hero of a long, muddy row.

Zero turnovers for us, three interceptions for Nebraska, and a Halloween win in a sea of black. We're 5-3, 2-3 in the Big Ten and 2-1 on the road. Penn State comes to Husky Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7, and I'm sailing in. Bow Down to Washington!
