Louisiana Tech vs South Alabama: Bulldogs Roll in Mobile 35-20

Final: Louisiana Tech (6-2) 35, South Alabama (4-4) 20, Sat., Oct. 31, Hancock Whitney Stadium, Mobile. Next: at Troy, Sat., Nov. 7, Veterans Memorial Stadium, Troy

XFacebook

Louisiana Tech 35, South Alabama 20, and we're 4-0 in the Sun Belt. Halloween morning back home started with the Depot Dash, a costumed 5K through downtown Ruston and along the Greenway. By 2 o'clock in Mobile our Bulldogs were running their own race, and they finished it out front with nobody gaining in the second half.

It started rough. Our first drive went backward with Blake Baker sacked twice, and South Alabama went up 7-0 on a 10-yard Jared Hollins throw to Anthony Eager. We answered in four plays. Baker hit Rayshun Hurt on a short throw that turned into a 67-yard catch-and-run to the Jaguars 4, and Andrew Burnette punched it in from there.

South Alabama went back up 14-7 with a 14-play drive that ate 7:33. Then we hung 21 on them in the second quarter. Hurt broke a 30-yard run and Baker found Marcus Calwise Jr. for a 22-yard touchdown. After a fourth-down stop, Trey Kukuk threw 27 yards to Jalen Mickens for the lead. The Jaguars answered with an 11-yard Keenan Phillips run but missed the extra point, and Baker hit Calwise again from 35. 28-20 at the half.

The second half belonged to the defense. South Alabama had seven drives after the break and scored on none of them. MJ Moultrie II knocked the ball away from Phillips at the Jaguars 6, Alonzo Jackson Jr. recovered, and two plays later Baker found Luke Watkins for a 4-yard touchdown. The Jaguars came up empty on fourth down twice, and Janard Lockhardt intercepted Hollins at our 5 with 1:40 left.

Baker went 10 of 16 for 167 yards and three touchdowns, and we didn't turn it over once. Jackson led us with nine tackles and the fumble recovery, Taeden Rogers had seven tackles and a sack, and Emmanuel Oguns added another. South Alabama got flagged 12 times for 100 yards.

Out at the farm we grade peaches by feel, and this one's firm all the way through. Peachy!

We're 6-2 and unbeaten in league play. Next is a trip to Troy and Veterans Memorial Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7. Go Dawgs.
