Colorado State vs Utah State: Rams Fall Behind Early 31-14

Final: Utah State (3-5) 31, Colorado State (3-5) 14, Sat., Oct. 31, Maverik Stadium, Logan. Next: vs. Boise State, Sat., Nov. 7, Canvas Stadium, Fort Collins

Utah State 31, Colorado State 14, and we were down 24-0 before we scored a point. On Friday morning, costumed preschoolers in Fort Collins trick-or-treated through Old Town Square for Tiny Tot Halloween, collecting candy from nearly 100 downtown businesses. Those little ones moved through more ground before lunch than our offense did in the first half in Logan.

I run the foothills trails every morning, and I know what it feels like when the climb beats you. This was that. Utah State opened with a six-play, 75-yard drive and a 31-yard touchdown run by Nehemiah Washington. Kian Afrookhteh added a field goal. Then the Aggies ran a throwback trick play, Anthony Garcia hitting McCae Hillstead for 44 yards, and a few snaps later Hillstead ran in an 18-yard score. It was 17-0 at halftime, and we had nothing on the board.

Hillstead, making his first start since the season opener, carved us up: 17 of 22 for 173 yards and a touchdown, plus 79 rushing yards. Garcia caught 11 balls for 121 yards. Utah State ran for 247 yards and held the ball for 36:08.

The fight showed up late, I will give the boys that. Durell Robinson ran in a 6-yard touchdown at the end of the third quarter, and Hauss Hejny hit Cedric Ross for a 21-yard score and then hit the two-point try with 2:31 left. But we fumbled twice in Aggie territory. Hejny got stripped on a sack at the Utah State 30, and Robinson coughed it up at the 29 in the fourth. Those are points left on the mountain.

Hejny finished 19 of 30 for 186 yards. Terrence Smith Jr. and RJ Vick each caught five balls, and Jayden Fox returned five kickoffs for 126 yards. Oumar Diomande made nine tackles, and Tre'Shawn Moore and Robby Harrison each made eight. Nathan Sutcliffe averaged 45 yards a punt.

We are 3-5 and 1-3 in conference play. Boise State comes to Canvas Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7, and that is a home climb we have to make. Lace up, Rams. Ram tough.
