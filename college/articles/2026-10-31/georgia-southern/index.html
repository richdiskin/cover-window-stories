Georgia Southern vs App State: White Out Defense Wins 19-3

Final: Georgia Southern (4-4) 19, App State (5-3) 3, Sat., Oct. 31, Allen E. Paulson Stadium, Statesboro. Next: vs. Marshall, Sat., Nov. 7, Allen E. Paulson Stadium, Statesboro (Homecoming)

ShareXFacebook

Georgia Southern 19, App State 3, on Erk Russell's 100th birthday celebration at a White Out Paulson Stadium, and it was Erk's kind of night. The Eagles unveiled Eagle Legends busts of Tracy Ham, Adrian Peterson and Paul Johnson at Parrish-Scoggins Plaza at 3 p.m., the "One More Time" exhibit sat on the Bishop Fieldhouse Patio, and the costume contest had a Best Dressed as Erk Russell category. Then our defense played the kind of football Erk built this place on.

It started on App's third snap. Brandon Tyson hurried Malachi Singleton, and Chace Calicut intercepted him at our 32. Max Johnson and the offense went to work with a 14-play, 73-yard drive. Weston Bryan converted a fourth-and-1 at the App 6, and Tripp Bryant kicked a 25-yarder. Next time out, Bryan threw 7 yards to Josh Dallas on fourth-and-1, and Taeo Todd ran it in from 5, 10-0.

App got a 51-yard kickoff return and a missed 35-yarder, and James London hit from 33 at the end of the half, 10-3. That was all the Mountaineers got. Bryant kicked a 35-yarder for 13-3, then a 34-yarder at the end of a 14-play drive that burned 7:25 off the clock, 16-3.

Then the defense took over the barn. Nigel Eldridge Jr. shared a sack, and Ethan Johnson sacked Singleton for 10 yards on the very next snap. Our offense came up empty on fourth down at the App 34, so the defense went and got the ball back itself: Anthony Bynum jumped on a Singleton fumble at the App 29, and Bryant drilled a 48-yarder, 19-3. App drove to our 2 with three minutes left and walked away with nothing when Singleton got intercepted in the end zone, with MJ Stroud in his face.

Johnson completed 36 of 47 throws for 318 yards with no interceptions. Alex Taylor caught 10 balls for 93 yards, Dallas caught seven for 86, and Jeremiah Ware had a 50-yarder among his three catches. Bryant went 4 for 4 and scored 13 points. Bynum had a sack, two tackles for loss and the fumble recovery. We held App to 222 yards and 6 on the ground, owned the ball for 43:54 and didn't turn it over once. Honest report from the creek: 8 of 17 on third down and four field goals means we left touchdowns out there.

We're 4-4, 3-1 in the Sun Belt and sitting first in the East. Marshall comes to Paulson for Homecoming on Saturday, Nov. 7. GATA, and Hail Southern.
