Minnesota vs Indiana: Gophers Drop OT Kicking Duel 23-16

Final/OT: Indiana (8-1) 23, Minnesota (5-3) 16, Sat., Oct. 31, Memorial Stadium (Bloomington, IN), Bloomington. Next: vs. UCLA, Sat., Nov. 7, Huntington Bank Stadium, Minneapolis

XFacebook

Indiana 23, Minnesota 16, in overtime in Bloomington, and I'll keep my voice down, because that's how we do it up north. Back home on Halloween night, BareBones Puppet Theatre ran its 33rd Halloween Extravaganza at Powderhorn Park, giant puppets and fire under the theme "Hum, Honk, Howl: As Above, So Below," with folks bringing tarps to sit on the cold ground. Grandpa calls that ice-house training.

We had this one. Luke Drzewiecki hit from 37 to open the scoring. A Drake Lindsey fumble at our 26 handed Indiana a short field and a 9-yard touchdown, but Lindsey answered in the second quarter with a 39-yard strike to Roman Voss. Jaxon Howard intercepted Josh Hoover, and we went to the half up 10-7.

Indiana tied it in the third. Drzewiecki made it 13-10 from 51 early in the fourth, and after Indiana tied it again with 3:05 left, Darius Taylor broke a 19-yard run to get us into Hoosier territory. Drzewiecki stepped up from 60 with 19 seconds left and drilled it. 16-13. Cold enough for ya, Bloomington?

Then 19 seconds turned into a long winter. Grant Wilson, who took over at quarterback for Indiana in the second half, hit Charlie Becker for 33 yards, and Paddy McAteer matched our kicker with a 60-yarder of his own as time ran out. In overtime, Wilson found Becker from 25 on the first snap.

Our turn. Lindsey hit Noah Jennings for 17 yards to the Indiana 8 and ran it to the 3 himself. Then Jeff Utzinger and Tobi Osunsanmi got home on back-to-back sacks, and it ended at the Indiana 9.

Lindsey went 20 of 37 for 120 yards and the Voss touchdown. TJ Thomas caught six balls for 54 yards, and Javon Tracy had seven for 40. Maverick Baranowski made 10 tackles, Kerry Brown seven, and TJ Bush Jr. got a sack. Tom Weston punted seven times for a 48.6 average. We had 215 yards and 10 first downs and still had Indiana beat with 19 seconds left. Drzewiecki went 3 for 3 with a 60-yarder. That man owes nobody a thing.

Some days on the lake you sit eight hours and catch nothing. This was one of those days, except we had a fish on the hook with 19 seconds left. I'll get over it by Thursday, maybe.

We're 5-3, 3-2 in the Big Ten. UCLA comes to Huntington Bank Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7, and they're bringing an unbeaten record into November in Minneapolis. Bring the good coat. Ski-U-Mah.
