Wake Forest vs Virginia: Deacons Roll the Cavaliers 51-7

Final: Wake Forest (7-1) 51, Virginia (4-5) 7, Sat., Oct. 31, Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium, Winston-Salem. Next: vs. Merrimack, Sat., Nov. 7, Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium, Winston-Salem

XFacebook

Wake Forest 51, Virginia 7. DEACTOWN dressed up for Halloween, with a pumpkin patch at Gate 5, the Golden Knights on the parachute schedule for the anthem and Thriller on the halftime bill for the Spirit of the Old Gold and Black. Then the Demon Deacons went out and scared the Cavaliers right out of Winston-Salem. Roll the Quad!

Resolved: this was the most complete game of the season. I will argue the affirmative. Exhibit A is the opening drive, set up by a 30-yard punt return from Kam Shanks and a 33-yard strike from Gio Lopez to Carlos Hernandez, finished by a 2-yard Ty Clark III touchdown. Exhibit B is Braylon Johnson's interception on a Virginia fourth down, which led to a 45-yard field goal from Connor Calvert. The defense rests? Hardly.

Clark scored three touchdowns, all on short runs. Lopez went 13 of 21 for 229 yards, hit Hernandez for a 10-yard score after a 64-yard Shanks punt return, and found Drayden Dickmann for 47 on another drive. Hernandez finished with eight catches for 136 yards. Calvert went 3 for 3 on field goals and 6 for 6 on extra points, good for 15 points by himself.

Exhibit C is the third quarter, when we outscored Virginia 24-7. Jamar Searcy broke a 55-yard run and finished the drive with a 7-yard score. Kyndall McCaleb added a 27-yard touchdown run in the fourth. We piled up 538 yards, ran for 309, went 9 of 14 on third down and did not turn it over once.

Rebuttal time, because a fair debater admits the other side's best point. Virginia's Da'Shawn Martin beat us for a 47-yard touchdown late in the third quarter, and that cost us the shutout. Still, our defense stood up on fourth-and-1 at our own 6, where Zach Lohavichan stuffed the run, and again on fourth-and-goal in the fourth quarter. Camden Hardy and Dallas Afalava each got a sack, and Virginia finished with 16 rushing yards.

Closing argument: that is five straight wins, and the second-biggest margin we have ever put on Virginia in this series. I move that we keep winning. Motion carries.

We're 7-1. Merrimack comes to Winston-Salem on Saturday, Nov. 7. Go Deacs.
