Nebraska vs Washington: Late Huskies Strike Spoils Blackout 17-10

Final: Washington (5-3) 17, Nebraska (5-3) 10, Sat., Oct. 31, Memorial Stadium (Lincoln, NE), Lincoln. Next: at Illinois, Fri., Nov. 6, Gies Memorial Stadium, Champaign

Washington 17, Nebraska 10, and that one is going to sit in my stomach longer than the Halloween candy they handed kids at the Cornhusker Kickoff in the Hawks Center. It was our Blackout game, the first Halloween game in Lincoln since 1998, and Memorial Stadium in black watched a 7-3 lead hold into the final two minutes before it got away.

Credit first, because a farm girl pays her debts. The Blackshirts were nasty. Washington ran 19 times for 32 yards. Kade Pietrzak dropped Elijah Brown for a 10-yard loss, Jahsear Whittington and Mason Goldman each had a sack, and Riley Van Poppel and Vincent Shavers Jr. split another. Owen Chambliss led us with seven tackles. Washington's first three quarters added up to one 50-yard field goal.

Our only touchdown was a beauty. Backed up at our own 5, we went 95 yards in 10 plays. Jamal Rule ripped off a 16-yarder, Mekhi Nelson ran for 19, and Anthony Colandrea dropped a 24-yard touchdown throw on Carter Nelson with 1:40 left in the first quarter. Rule finished with 72 yards on 13 carries, and Jacory Barney Jr. was everywhere: six catches for 65 yards, four punt returns for 46 and a 37-yard kick return.

Now the part I'd rather spike into the floor. Colandrea was intercepted on each of our first two drives, at the Washington 8 and the Washington 30. Early in the fourth, Barney's 31-yard catch put us at their 5. A delay of game, two incompletions and an 8-yard sack later, the 36-yard field goal try missed. That was the night, right there.

Washington went 81 yards in 12 plays, and even after our rush sacked Demond Williams Jr. twice in four snaps, he found Chris Lawson for a 25-yard touchdown with 1:30 left. Two snaps into our answer, Alex McLaughlin intercepted Colandrea and ran it back 48 yards. John Hohl's 42-yard field goal made it 17-10 with 31 seconds left. We outgained them 387 yards to 267 and turned it over three times to their zero. That math doesn't win Big Ten games.

We're 5-3 and 2-3 in the Big Ten, and three of our losses have come by a combined 13 points. Friday, Nov. 6, we're at Illinois. Those people in black deserved better tonight. Now go win one for them. Go Big Red!
