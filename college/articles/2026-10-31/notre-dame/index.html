Notre Dame vs Navy: Irish Score 28 Straight to Sink Navy 35-13

Final: Notre Dame (7-1) 35, Navy (2-6) 13, Sat., Oct. 31, Gillette Stadium, Foxborough. Next: vs. Miami, Sat., Nov. 7, 7:30 PM ET, Notre Dame Stadium, Notre Dame

XFacebook

Notre Dame 35, Navy 13. Back home, the Library Lawn is set for HalloweenFest tonight, with a custom haunted house, carnival rides and food trucks, and the Fighting Irish are bringing the campus a fitting Halloween story: a first half that gave us a real scare and a second half that buried it. I would posit, for the record, that the scare was earned.

Proposition one: Navy's option is not a gimmick, it is a clock-eating machine. The Midshipmen held the ball for 23:33 of the first half and scored on their first three trips. Jackson Gutierrez carried 28 times for 110 yards, Nolan Bartley hit field goals from 38 and 26, and Gutierrez's 2-yard run put Navy up 13-7 with 1:52 left in the half. Our offense had the ball for 6:27 before the break. Six minutes. You cannot grade an offense that never gets to the exam.

Proposition two: when Nolan James Jr. gets a crease, the argument is over. With 55 seconds left in the half he broke a 52-yard touchdown run, and we went to the locker room up 14-13. James finished with 107 yards and two scores on nine carries. Nine.

Proposition three: the second half was the thesis. We took the opening kickoff 78 yards in 11 plays, and Jonaz Walton punched it in from 3. James went 23 yards for 28-13. Navy went for it on fourth down at its own 43 and came up short, and two plays later CJ Carr found Jerome Bettis Jr. for a 31-yard touchdown on the first snap of the fourth quarter. That's 28 unanswered. Navy held the ball for 9:07 after halftime and never scored again.

Carr went 19 of 29 for 210 yards and two touchdowns, with an 11-yard strike to Micah Gilbert for our first score. Bettis caught six balls for 78 yards. We had zero turnovers and two penalties. Luke Talich made 10 tackles, and Jaiden Ausberry added eight.

Peer review, because honesty is the whole discipline: Navy ran for 212 yards on 48 carries, the most anyone has run on this defense all season. The defense adjusted, but the first half needs a footnote in red ink.

That's nine straight wins over Navy, and we're 7-1. Next is Miami at Notre Dame Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:30 PM ET. Defense rests. Go Irish.
