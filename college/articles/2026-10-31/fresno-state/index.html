Fresno State vs Oregon State: Mandal Spooks the Beavers 38-22

Final: Fresno State (5-3) 38, Oregon State (5-3) 22, Sat., Oct. 31, Valley Children's Stadium, Fresno. Next: at Utah State, Sat., Nov. 7, Maverik Stadium, Logan

XFacebook

Fresno State 38, Oregon State 22, and the Valley is buzzing like a raisin shed at harvest. The Red & White Lot turned into a Trunk or Treat for Halloween night, the Bulldogs went with a Blackout theme, and the visitors from Corvallis rolled in on a five-game win streak. They rolled out without it.

It didn't start pretty. Jayden Mandal got intercepted on our second snap, and Oregon State drove inside our 10. Our defense held, a false start wiped out a Beaver touchdown, and they turned it over on downs. After their 54-yard field goal, Mandal went to work: eight plays, 75 yards, and a 15-yard touchdown run. 7-3.

Bryson Donelson made the next statement. After Rayshon Luke ripped off 12 yards, Donelson broke loose for a 63-yard touchdown, 45 seconds after an Oregon State field goal. He finished with 108 yards on 16 carries. Mandal then hit Josiah Freeman for a 26-yard score, and with 18 seconds left in the half, Dylan Hampsten strip-sacked Braden Atkinson and Mordecai Hines fell on it at our 17. 21-9 at the break.

The third quarter was a full harvest: 17 points. Manaki Watanabe hit a 30-yard field goal. On the very next snap, Martin Owusu strip-sacked Atkinson and Maverick Noonan recovered at the Oregon State 19. Mandal ran it in from 3. After a Beaver touchdown, Mandal found Freeman again from 31, and it was 38-16.

Mandal finished 11 of 17 for 202 yards with two touchdowns and ran for two more. Freeman caught six balls for 88 yards and both scores, and Jahlil McClain had a 48-yard grab. Hampsten had two sacks and seven tackles. Ethan Tierney and Deijon Laffitte added back-to-back sacks, Owusu got one, and the defense finished with five. Tytus Khajavi led us with eight tackles.

Honest note: Atkinson threw for 373 yards on 59 attempts, and Oregon State piled up 450 yards to our 392. But the Beavers ran for 77, went 2 for 5 on fourth down, and we recovered the late onside kick. We're 5-3, 2-2 in the Pac-12 and 3-1 at home. Next up is Utah State in Logan on Nov. 7. Go 'Dogs!
