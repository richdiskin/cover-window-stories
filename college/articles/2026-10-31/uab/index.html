UAB vs South Florida: Blazers Go Cold in Tampa, Fall 30-10

Final: South Florida (6-2) 30, UAB (4-4) 10, Sat., Oct. 31, Raymond James Stadium, Tampa. Next: vs. Charlotte, Sat., Nov. 7, Protective Stadium, Birmingham

XFacebook

South Florida 30, UAB 10, and it stings worse because we were up 10-0. Back home on Friday night, Sloss Furnaces opened the Historic No. 2 Casting Shed for Bowl Night: Molten & Mysterious, a cast iron bowl class plus a night tour of the old blast furnace. Iron goes in hot and comes out hard. Our Blazers went in hot at Raymond James Stadium and cooled off way too fast.

The start was pure Birmingham. We took the opening kickoff and went 67 yards in seven plays, with Ryder Burton hitting CJ Smith for 41 yards to the USF 3 and Rod Robinson II running it in from 5. Next time out we reached the USF 8, and Jonah Delange kicked a 30-yard field goal. 10-0 in the first quarter.

Then Delange missed from 41 early in the second, USF lost its starting quarterback to injury, and Luke Kromenhoek came in and flipped the afternoon. He broke tackles for 42 yards to set up a field goal, then ran for 25 more to set up Jason Collins Jr.'s 6-yard touchdown. 10-10 with 47 seconds left. On our very next snap, C.J. Hicks sacked Burton at our 17, the ball came out, and Asani Redwood scooped it and scored. Fourteen points in 10 seconds. 17-10 at the half.

It happened again early in the fourth. Hicks hit Burton on third-and-10 at our 19, the ball popped loose, and Grayson Howard took it 13 yards for a touchdown. Hicks finished with four sacks, tying a South Florida record. Burton went down six times and ended the day with minus-37 rushing yards.

Honest numbers: 33 carries for 17 yards, 4 of 16 on third down, 10 first downs, 177 yards of offense. After those first two drives we never scored again. A 16-play, 61-yard march in the fourth ended with Delange missing from 32. Burton finished 13 of 27 for 149.

The defense deserved a better ending. Jeremiah Jordan and Jo'Tavion Pierce made 11 tackles apiece, Marlin Dean had 2.5 sacks, and USF threw for just 46 yards all day. Oliver Straw boomed four punts at 49.8 yards a pop.

My granddaddy spent his working life around Birmingham steel, and he had one rule for a bad shift: you clock back in. Forged, not given.

We're 4-4, 2-2 in the American and 0-4 on the road. Charlotte comes to Protective Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7, and Blaze better be breathing fire. Go Blazers.
