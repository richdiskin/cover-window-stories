Syracuse vs SMU: Orange Stun No. 19 Mustangs Late 30-27

Final: Syracuse (4-4) 30, SMU (6-2) 27, Sat., Oct. 31, JMA Wireless Dome, Syracuse. Next: vs. Clemson (Homecoming), Sat., Nov. 7, JMA Wireless Dome, Syracuse

XFacebook

Syracuse 30, SMU 27, and the ranked visitors from Dallas got tricked on Halloween. Before kickoff, kids in costumes walked the same Quad route our team walks into the JMA Wireless Dome, the suites handed out candy, and for the first time ever the Orange wore helmets with Otto on the side. After today, that helmet belongs in a trophy case.

Steve Angeli set the tone on the opening drive with a 26-yard touchdown run, a full foot of fresh snow before lunch. SMU answered with a field goal and a Kevin Jennings touchdown throw, and we trailed 10-7 after one. Then Demetres Samuel Jr. punched the ball out of Jalen Hale's hands, Davien Kerr scooped it up, and Tripp Woody kept splitting the uprights. Ahmad Miller plunged in from a yard out with 22 seconds left in the half, and we went in up 20-10.

The fourth quarter tested every one of us. Pinned at our own 8, we gave up a safety, Kendrick Raphael scored, the two-point try went in, and we were down 27-20 with 9:04 to play. That felt like the plow truck sliding into the ditch on Erie Boulevard.

This team does not sit in the ditch. Angeli moved us into range and Woody hit from 26 yards, his third field goal of the day. Tunmise Adeleye sacked Jennings to kill the next SMU drive. Then Angeli went to work from our 32 with 3:35 left, hitting Umari Hatcher, David Clement, Calvin Russell III and Daunte Bacheyie, and on the 11th play he flipped a 2-yard touchdown to Noah Meyers with 17 seconds left. Woody's kick made it 30-27. Chase Geter fell on one more SMU fumble on the final play.

Angeli finished 21 of 34 for 200 yards with no interceptions. Hatcher caught six balls for 65 yards. Samuel made 10 tackles, Keyshawn Johnson had two sacks, and Jimmy Gregg boomed four punts for a 52.8-yard average. We had zero turnovers, and we went 4 for 4 on fourth down.

That's a two-foot lake-effect dump of a WIN, and I am celebrating with a whole bag of salt potatoes. We are 4-4 and 2-4 in the ACC. Clemson comes to the Dome for Homecoming on Nov. 7. Pack it again, Orange Nation.
