Air Force vs UConn: Hodges Runs for 4 Scores 35-26

Final: Air Force (6-2) 35, UConn (4-4) 26, Sat., Oct. 31, Falcon Stadium, Colorado Springs. Next: at Army, Sat., Nov. 7, Michie Stadium, West Point

ShareXFacebook

Air Force 35, UConn 26, and the Falcons did not complete a single throw. Did not need one. It was Space Force Day at Falcon Stadium, a Black Out with the team in its Space Force uniforms to honor the USSF, and the Falcons flew one ground mission after another: 74 carries, 366 rushing yards, five rushing touchdowns.

Call sign for today: Kemper Hodges, Workhorse. Thirty-five carries, 200 yards, four touchdowns. He scored from 7 yards and then broke a 62-yarder in the first quarter, punched in a 3-yarder in the second, and closed the show from a yard out with 3:09 left. Owen Allen added 70 yards on 18 carries, and Gage LaDue scored from 4 yards in the third quarter.

I logged a lot of hours as an instructor pilot, and I know a long sortie when I see one. UConn cut it to 28-26 on Kenji Christian's 46-yard run on the first play of the fourth quarter. Then our offense took the ball for 19 plays, 75 yards and 11:37 of clock. That is a full mission profile, briefed, flown and debriefed, while UConn's offense sat and watched. Air Force held the ball for 41:49 and went 5 for 6 on fourth down.

The defense earned its wings at the end. After Hodges' last score, UConn threw on every snap. Blake Fletcher and Roger Jones Jr. broke up throws, the Huskies turned it over on downs, and Isaac Hubert and Jake Kepler shared a sack on the final snap. Max Mustell led the team with eight tackles, and Fletcher and Hubert each got a sack.

Honest debrief: Kalieb Osborne hit us for 263 yards and two long scoring strikes, and the defense has to clean that up. Will Duncan also averaged 56.3 yards on three punts. That is altitude.

Air Force is 6-2 and bowl eligible for the first time since 2023. Next we go to West Point to face Army at Michie Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7. Fly, fight, win.
