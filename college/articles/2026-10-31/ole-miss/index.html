Ole Miss vs Auburn: Rebels Pull Away on Halloween, Win 45-15

Final: Ole Miss (6-2) 45, Auburn (4-4) 15, Sat., Oct. 31, Vaught-Hemingway Stadium, Oxford. Next: vs. Georgia, Sat., Nov. 7, Vaught-Hemingway Stadium, Oxford

ShareXFacebook

Ole Miss 45, Auburn 15. On Halloween, the Party in the Sip called for powder blue, and 67,041 of us filled Vaught-Hemingway Stadium to watch the Rebels write a fourth quarter that Mr. Faulkner would have needed three hundred pages to finish. Auburn hung around into the late third quarter. The ending belonged to Oxford.

Every great novel opens slow. Our first drive went 16 plays and ran out of quarter at the Auburn 1. On the first snap of the second, Will Echoles plowed in for the first rushing touchdown of his career, capping a 17-play, 89-yard march that was our longest drive of the season.

The middle chapters had twists. Jehiem Oatis blocked Auburn's extra point, and Jaylon Braxton carried it 92 yards to the Auburn 5. Chambliss was intercepted on the first play of our next drive, but Jamarious Brown answered two plays later with the first fumble recovery of his career, and Shekai Mills-Knight finished an 86-yard drive from a yard out. Auburn crept to 17-15 late in the third. A false start pushed its two-point try back five yards, and our defense slammed the door.

Then came the finale, 28 straight points. Trinidad Chambliss ran 26 yards for a touchdown, his seventh rushing score of the season. In the fourth quarter he threw 6 yards to Caleb Cunningham and 28 yards to Horatio Fields, and Deuce Knight closed it with a 17-yard run. Chambliss finished 22 of 34 for 263 yards and two touchdown throws, plus 65 rushing yards and the score on the ground. He went over 6,000 career passing yards at Ole Miss and has now thrown a touchdown in 21 straight games, one shy of the school record.

The defense was the plot. A season-high 10 tackles for loss. Suntarine Perkins and Tah'j Butler had two sacks each, and Perkins climbed to fourth on the Ole Miss career list with 22. Keaton Thomas led us with eight tackles, Anthony Robinson III forced a late fumble and Dante Core fell on it. Auburn ran for 63 yards all day, and we converted 13 of 16 third downs.

I delivered my victory monologue under the nearest chandelier. It ran three paragraphs. No regrets.

That is 16 straight home wins, tied for the second-longest active streak in FBS, and three straight over Auburn. We're 6-2 and 4-2 in the SEC. Georgia comes to the Vaught on Saturday, Nov. 7. Hotty Toddy.
