Purdue vs Penn State: Boilers Tie It, Then Fade at Beaver 31-17

Final: Penn State (4-4) 31, Purdue (3-5) 17, Sat., Oct. 31, Beaver Stadium, University Park. Next: vs. Maryland, Sat., Nov. 7, Ross-Ade Stadium, West Lafayette

ShareXFacebook

Penn State 31, Purdue 17. West Lafayette set its trick-or-treat hours for 6 to 8 p.m. on Halloween, and by the end of this one I was ready to hand out every piece of candy in the house just to think about something else. The Boilermakers hung with Penn State for a half in Beaver Stadium, then ran out of fuel.

Let me open the Blueprint, because the first half had real engineering in it. After Penn State scored on its opening drive, Ryan Browne hit Asaad Waseem for 38 yards and then Xavier Townsend for a 33-yard touchdown. Four plays, 75 yards, tied 7-7. In the second quarter, Bisi Owens blocked a Penn State punt and Jalil Hall scooped it up and ran 31 yards for the score that tied it 14-14. That was the best special teams play we have made all year.

Then the design flaw showed up. Penn State ran 51 times for 269 yards and held the ball for 43:27. Its 15-play drive before the half ended with a 1-yard touchdown on fourth down, and in the third quarter Jamison Patton intercepted Browne to set up a field goal. Cam Wallace finished with 135 rushing yards. You cannot build a machine that only runs for 16 minutes.

Our defense spent all night on the field and still fought. Justin Denson made 15 tackles, Jojo Hayden made 10, and Curt Neal had two sacks. Jacobo Echeverria Lozano hit a 49-yarder in the fourth quarter after Browne converted a fourth down with a 24-yard throw.

The honest numbers are ugly. We finished with 175 yards, nine first downs and 33 rushing yards. Browne went 10 of 29 for 142 yards with a touchdown and two interceptions. Penn State has now won 12 straight in this series, and we have not beaten them since 2004.

My model Boilermaker Special has a cracked wheel tonight. I'll fix it in the garage this week, same as this team needs to fix its run game.

We're 3-5 and 2-3 in the Big Ten. Maryland comes to Ross-Ade Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7. Boiler up!
