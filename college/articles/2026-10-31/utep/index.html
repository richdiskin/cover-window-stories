UTEP vs North Dakota State: Miners Strike First, Fall 31-7

Final: North Dakota State (7-2) 31, UTEP (1-7) 7, Sat., Oct. 31, Fargodome, Fargo. Next: vs. Hawai'i, Sat., Nov. 7, 9:30 PM ET, Sun Bowl, El Paso

North Dakota State 31, UTEP 7. Back home on Halloween morning, folks were hiking the Franklin Mountains in costume at the state park's Halloween Fest, and the rest of us were packing up the Hatch-hot salsa and heading north in spirit. First trip to a dome since 2022, first game ever against the Bison. For one play, it felt like a party.

That one play was a beauty. Raymond Moore III took his first snap at quarterback, found a huge hole and went 57 yards to the house. 7-0 Miners, early in the first quarter. Kaleb Miles had already intercepted Nathan Hayes on the Bison's opening drive. We had Fargo quiet.

Then the Bison went to work, and we didn't score again. NDSU ground out an 83-yard drive for a field goal, then punched in a fourth-and-goal from the 1 for a 10-7 lead. A 3-yard touchdown throw to Reis Kessel late in the third made it 17-7. Two scores in the final two minutes made it 31-7.

The killers were turnovers. We came to Fargo with back-to-back games without a giveaway, and we left with three. EJ Colson Jr. threw two interceptions, the last one under heavy pressure on third down with about six minutes left. Then a Jaden Smith fumble went 46 yards the other way. That's how a 17-7 fight turns ugly.

Give the defense its due against the top defense in the Mountain West. Esteban Guillory made 12 tackles, double his old career best. Kode Lowe made 10, and Justin Content made nine with two tackles for loss. We ran for 147 yards. Moore had 67 of them on three carries.

We're 1-7 and 0-4 in the Mountain West. Hawai'i comes to the Sun Bowl on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 9:30 PM ET, and our last Halloween win was in 1998, against those same Warriors. Bring the green chile. Axes up, Miners.
