Arkansas vs Missouri: Hogs Give It Away on Halloween, 34-10

Final: Missouri (6-2) 34, Arkansas (3-5) 10, Sat., Oct. 31, Donald W. Reynolds Razorback Stadium, Fayetteville. Next: at Auburn, Sat., Nov. 7, Jordan-Hare Stadium, Auburn

ShareXFacebook

Missouri 34, Arkansas 10, and it hurts to write it on the first Halloween on the Hill since 2015. Woooo, Pig. The 105th Homecoming Week ended with trunk-or-treating in the tailgate lots, a Hog Walk at Maple and Razorback, a trick-or-treat trailgating parade at 5:45 and a halftime laser show. The Hill brought the party. We brought four turnovers.

Hog Call, then the hard stat: four giveaways, 24 Missouri points. KJ Jackson got intercepted by Trajen Greco on our third drive, and Missouri kicked a field goal. Next time out, a snap got away from Cam Settles, rolled into our end zone, and Missouri's Darris Smith fell on it, 10-0. After a 53-yard field goal made it 13-0, Max Gilbert's 52-yard try came up empty.

We found some fight before the half. Jackson capped an eight-play, 52-yard drive with a 2-yard keeper with 41 seconds left, 13-7, and the place was rocking. Then Missouri went 75 yards to open the third, and on our very next snap, Jackson got stripped and Missouri recovered at our 45. Jamal Roberts scored from 2 three plays later, 27-7.

The drive I'll tell my science class about Monday: 20 plays, 62 yards, 9:51. Antonio Jordan caught a 25-yard touchdown that came back on a penalty, and Settles scored from the 1 before a chop block wiped that off too. We settled for a 32-yarder from Gilbert, 27-10. Bradley Shaw recovered a Roberts fumble at our 16, and we gave it right back two plays later when Jordan was stripped at our 33. Missouri scored four plays after that, 34-10.

Jackson completed 20 of 34 throws for 229 yards and ran for our only touchdown. Jordan caught seven balls for 106 yards with a long of 37, and CJ Brown caught four for 37. Shaw made nine tackles and broke up two throws, Joker Johnson made eight with a tackle for loss, and Christian Harrison made seven. Our defense held Missouri to 179 yards through the air.

Honest lab report: 59 rushing yards on 36 carries, 8 of 17 on third down, a turnover on downs late, and seven penalties for 85 yards. We're 3-5 and 1-4 in the SEC, and we head to Auburn's Jordan-Hare Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7. Call the Hogs anyway. Woooo, Pig, Sooie.
