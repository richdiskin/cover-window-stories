Georgia State vs Coastal Carolina: Panthers Rally Late, Fall 31-20

Final: Coastal Carolina (3-5) 31, Georgia State (4-4) 20, Sat., Oct. 31, Center Parc Stadium, Atlanta. Next: at App State, Sat., Nov. 7, Kidd Brewer Stadium, Boone

XFacebook

Coastal Carolina 31, Georgia State 20. Halloween at Center Parc Stadium had everything we asked for: a wear-black call, a pumpkin carving station at the Panther Pregame, kids trick-or-treating all over the stadium. The one thing missing was a team that showed up before the fourth quarter.

We got on the board first. Samer Layous hit a 32-yard field goal late in the first. Then traffic stopped dead. Coastal scored 17 straight in the second quarter, two touchdowns from Deuce Bailey and a 53-yard field goal. Layous kicked another 32-yarder as time ran out, and we went in down 17-6. Two more Coastal touchdowns in the third and it was 31-6. That's the Downtown Connector at 5 p.m. on a Friday.

The defense did get its hands on the ball. We intercepted Bailey twice. Alex Mullings took the second one to the Coastal 1, where Bailey knocked it loose, and it went for a touchback. We got the ball back and handed it right back to them.

The fourth quarter was the express lane. Ayden Pereira hit Grant Hollier for 9 yards and then found Qwantavius Wiggins for two. Antonio Wesley recovered a Bailey fumble at the Coastal 18, and Pereira finished the drive with an 18-yard touchdown to DJ Riles. 31-20 with 7:38 left. The two-point throw failed. Then we turned it over on downs at 3:12 and took a sack on the last snap.

Real numbers. Pereira went 20 of 37 for 200 yards and two touchdowns. Wiggins ran 11 times for 96 yards with a 58-yard burst. We ran for 160 yards. We drew nine penalties for 96 yards and went 5 of 15 on third down. Coby Owens made nine tackles with two for loss, and Tyson McCrary added eight.

Three straight losses. We are 4-4 and 1-3 in the Sun Belt.

Next is App State at Kidd Brewer Stadium in Boone on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 2:30 PM ET. Long drive up the mountain. Start the engine early this time. Let's go, Panthers.
