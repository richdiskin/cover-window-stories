Marshall vs Old Dominion: Herd Ruin ODU Homecoming 36-23

Final: Marshall (5-3) 36, Old Dominion (2-7) 23, Sat., Oct. 31, S.B. Ballard Stadium, Norfolk. Next: at Georgia Southern, Sat., Nov. 7, 3:30 PM ET, Allen E. Paulson Stadium, Statesboro

XFacebook

Marshall 36, Old Dominion 23, and we crashed somebody else's Homecoming on Halloween. Back in Huntington, Healing Halloween ran free kids activities from 4 p.m. and a recovery fundraiser on Vernon Street that night. As a nurse, I call that real medicine for a town that knows hard times. Down in Norfolk, the Herd was handing out its own treatment. We Are.

Triage came fast. On the second snap of the game, Kenyatta Hardge knocked the ball loose, and Cannon Lewis recovered at the ODU 26. Four plays later, TJ Lester scored from 4 yards. Then Lester burst 49 yards off the left side, and it was 14-0 before the Monarchs had a pulse. Hardge finished with seven tackles and two sacks.

Now the part I'll be telling every patient on my floor this week. With four seconds left in the half, ODU lined up for a field goal, and Xayvion Turner-Bradshaw took it back 100 yards for a touchdown. It was the first return of a field goal try in Marshall history and only the fifth 100-yard score ever by the Herd. That made it 27-10 at halftime instead of a one-score game.

Jorge Diaz Nicolas made the record book on his own. He hit field goals from 35, 30, 34, 30 and 24 yards and became the first Marshall player ever with five in a game. Judah Price led a 196-yard ground game with 82 yards, and Lester added 76 and two scores.

The defense took four turnovers. Charles McCartherens intercepted one in the end zone and tipped another that Caleb Clark-Glover caught. Daytione Smith added a fourth-quarter interception. Brayden Hall made 11 tackles. I'll be honest on the chart: we threw only 8 of 18 for 142 yards, gave up an interception return for a score and drew 12 penalties for 94 yards. That needs treatment before Statesboro.

We're 5-3 and 2-2 in the Sun Belt, and 11-1 all-time against Old Dominion. Next is Georgia Southern on the road, Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:30 PM ET at Allen E. Paulson Stadium. Scrub in. We Are Marshall.
