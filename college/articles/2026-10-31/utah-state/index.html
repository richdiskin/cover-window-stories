Utah State vs Colorado State: Hillstead Leads Aggies 31-14

Final: Utah State (3-5) 31, Colorado State (3-5) 14, Sat., Oct. 31, Maverik Stadium, Logan. Next: vs. Fresno State, Sat., Nov. 7, Maverik Stadium, Logan

XFacebook

Utah State 31, Colorado State 14, and the Aggies led 24-0 before the Rams found the end zone in Logan. Friday night the Taggart Student Center and Fieldhouse hosted the annual Howl, the USU Halloween bash with a live haunted house, laser tag, karaoke and a dance floor that sold out. Saturday the haunted house moved to Maverik Stadium, and Colorado State got lost in it.

McCae Hillstead got his first start since the season opener, and he played like a man who had been waiting all fall for the keys. He was 17 of 22 for 173 yards and a touchdown, and he ran 11 times for 79 yards and another score. Anthony Garcia was his go-to target: 11 catches for 121 yards. Then Garcia threw a 44-yard throwback right back to Hillstead, and a few snaps later Hillstead ran in from 18 yards. I have milked cows at 4 a.m. for 20 years, and I have never seen a better play in Cache Valley.

Nehemiah Washington broke a 31-yard touchdown run on the opening drive. Kian Afrookhteh made a 25-yard field goal. Javen Jacobs scored from 12 yards in the third, and Hillstead hit Kaina Watson for a 7-yard touchdown in the fourth. Utah State ran for 247 yards and had zero turnovers.

The defense was the real story. David Kabongo strip-sacked Hauss Hejny at our 30 in the third quarter, and Carson Tujague fell on it. Early in the fourth, Tevita Tuha punched the ball loose from Durell Robinson and James Jennette recovered at our 29. Kabongo made seven tackles with two tackles for loss, and Tujague also got a sack. Colorado State finished with 263 yards.

The honest note: the Rams scored 14 points late, and Afrookhteh missed one field goal. That is cleanup work, not a crisis.

We are 3-5 and 2-2 in conference play, and that makes three wins at Maverik Stadium. Fresno State comes to Logan on Saturday, Nov. 7. I am buying a double scoop of Aggie Ice Cream tonight, and I am not sharing.
