Clemson vs Florida State: Tigers Take Four Turnovers, Win 36-24

Final: Clemson (5-3) 36, Florida State (3-5) 24, Sat., Oct. 31, Doak Campbell Stadium, Tallahassee. Next: at Syracuse, Sat., Nov. 7, JMA Wireless Dome, Syracuse, N.Y.

XFacebook

Clemson 36, Florida State 24, and well, hey there, y'all, the Tigers celebrated a birthday in Tallahassee. Clemson played its very first football game on Oct. 31, 1896, a win over Furman in Greenville, back when Professor Walter Riggs coached a bunch of cadets who had barely seen a football. One hundred thirty years later to the day, on Halloween night, we won again. Some traditions keep.

This one was won by a kicker and a secondary, and I have the choreography notes to prove it. Nolan Hauser went 5 for 5 on field goals for 18 points, from 35, 35, 50, 46 and 49 yards. The 50-yarder came with two seconds left in the first half and sent us to the locker room up 23-10. My orange rain boots Darlene did a full high step in the living room.

Now the defense, my kind of routine. Jerome Carter III intercepted Ashton Daniels and brought it back 26 yards, and he had a sack too. Late in the half, Sammy Brown hurried Daniels into a throw that Elliot Washington II intercepted. In the third, Jahiem Lawson jumped on a Daniels fumble at our 16 to end a 75-yard Seminole drive. Then, with Florida State driving late, Washington intercepted Daniels again in the end zone. Four takeaways, zero giveaways. I'm naming that formation the Halloween Hold.

Tait Reynolds steered it all. He went 18 of 32 for 189 yards with touchdown throws to Christian Bentancur from 18 and Olsen Patt-Henry from 4. Then, with Florida State back within 29-24, he kept it and ran 79 yards for the touchdown that ended the argument with 3:17 left. Tyler Brown caught five balls for 74 yards, and Corey Myrick made 10 tackles. Will Heldt had a sack and 2.5 tackles for loss.

I'll say it plainly, because I don't decorate facts. We were 4 of 17 on third down, and we let a 29-10 lead shrink to five in the fourth quarter. Field goals look great in the box score only when they don't keep the other team in it. We have to finish drives.

We are 5-3 and 3-2 in the ACC. Next is Syracuse at the JMA Wireless Dome on Saturday, Nov. 7, at noon ET. Orange is ready for Orange. Go Tigers.
