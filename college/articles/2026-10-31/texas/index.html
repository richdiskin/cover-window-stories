Texas vs Mississippi State: Biles and the Defense Rule 31-3

Final: Texas (6-2) 31, Mississippi State (4-4) 3, Sat., Oct. 31, DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium, Austin. Next: at Missouri, Sat., Nov. 7, Memorial Stadium, Columbia

XFacebook

Texas 31, Mississippi State 3. As forecast. Austin shut Sixth Street to cars for Halloween night and Pumpkin Nights ran its final night in town, but the best show in the city was at DKR, where our defense ran the Bulldog offense through a hostile takeover.

The first acquisition closed fast. On third-and-9, Arch Manning hit Cam Coleman for 47 yards, then found him again for a 14-yard touchdown. Four plays, 62 yards, 7-0.

Then the defense took over the boardroom. Mississippi State drove 12 plays to our 18 and walked away with a missed 38-yarder. Gianni Spetic drilled a 45-yarder for 10-0. With 1:13 left in the half, Rasheem Biles sacked AJ Swann at our 39, the ball came loose, and Maraad Watson scooped it up and ran 61 yards for a touchdown. 17-0 at the break. That is how you merge two departments into one end zone.

The second half had one messy page of paperwork. Manning got sacked twice on one series and lost the ball at our 28, and the Bulldogs turned it into a 30-yard field goal. Our response: seven plays, 75 yards, a 37-yard strike to open it and a 4-yard touchdown throw to Raleek Brown, 24-3. Early in the fourth, the defense stopped a fourth-and-2 at our 40. Then on third-and-1, Manning hit Emmett Mosley V deep for a 42-yard touchdown. 31-3. Close the books.

Caro Bucks report. Biles: three sacks, four tackles for loss, eight tackles, top of the portfolio. Watson: a defensive touchdown, and he just earned a corner office. Manning went 16 of 20 for 220 yards and three touchdowns, Mosley caught four balls for 98 yards, and Jelani McDonald led us with nine tackles. Seven sacks as a team, and the Bulldogs finished with 187 yards and 68 on the ground. The audit flags are real: 60 rushing yards on 32 carries, five sacks allowed and a lost fumble. That's tomorrow's board-meeting agenda.

We're 6-2, 3-2 in the SEC and 5-1 at DKR. Next up, a trip to Missouri on Saturday, Nov. 7. Hook 'em.
