Colorado vs Kansas State: Buffs Win Halloween OT Thriller 27-24

Final (OT): Colorado (3-5) 27, Kansas State (4-4) 24, Sat., Oct. 31, Folsom Field, Boulder. Next: at Arizona State, Sat., Nov. 7, Mountain America Stadium, Tempe

ShareXFacebook

Colorado 27, Kansas State 24 in overtime, and the five-game skid is dead. Down on Pearl Street, Halloween meant Munchkin Masquerade, kids 12 and under in costume working downtown shops from 3 to 6. Up at Folsom Field, the Buffs handed out the best treat in Boulder: our first Big 12 win of the year.

Richard Young was the engine. He scored from 2 in the first quarter, his first touchdown as a Buff, and finished with 120 yards on 24 carries. JaQuail Smith ripped off a 47-yard touchdown run in the third, and our ground game piled up 222 yards and three scores.

The best drive started late in the third. Pinned at our own 4, we went 96 yards in 10 plays. Isaac Wilson hit Joseph Williams for 38, and Young cut left and ran 22 yards for the touchdown. 21-14 with 12:27 left. K-State then drove deep and missed a 43-yard field goal at 6:52.

Then the gut punch. Blake Barnett went 62 yards in 53 seconds, and his 8-yard throw to Garrett Oakley with six seconds left tied it 21-21. I stared at the Flatirons and did some quiet math on entropy.

Overtime flipped it back. K-State got to our 7, and Cree Thomas and Boo Carter broke up back-to-back throws in the end zone to force a 25-yard field goal. We took the ball, Young ran for 9 and 4, and on third down Wilson found Charlie Williams from 11 yards out. Ralphie's running, folks.

Wilson finished 14 of 21 for 185 yards with the winner, and Williams caught five balls for 89. Gideon Lampron was everywhere with 15 tackles, two sacks and four tackles for loss. Yamil Talib added two more sacks, and Damon Greaves pinned three punts inside the 20. We did cough one up when DeKalon Taylor fumbled through the end zone at the end of a 30-yard run in the second quarter, and that was our only turnover all day.

I celebrated the only way a physicist from Boulder knows how: a cold kombucha in the hammock and one very loud yell.

We're 3-5 and 1-4 in the Big 12. Next is Arizona State at Mountain America Stadium in Tempe on Saturday, Nov. 7. Sko Buffs.
