App State vs Georgia Southern: Mountaineers Stuck at 3, 19-3

Final: Georgia Southern (4-4) 19, App State (5-3) 3, Sat., Oct. 31, Allen E. Paulson Stadium, Statesboro. Next: vs. Georgia State, Sat., Nov. 7, Kidd Brewer Stadium, Boone

ShareXFacebook

Georgia Southern 19, App State 3, and Halloween in Statesboro turned into a long, cold climb with no summit. Back home, Boone BOO! filled King Street from 4 to 6:30 p.m., with the costume contest at 5 in front of the Jones House. Kickoff came at 6:30, right as the last kids headed home. Downtown Boone had the better night.

It went sideways on the third snap. Malachi Singleton got hurried by Brandon Tyson and Chace Calicut intercepted him at the Georgia Southern 32. Sam Pickett III ripped off a 45-yard catch on the next drive, but we punted. The Eagles answered with a 14-play, 73-yard grind that ended in a Tripp Bryant field goal, then a 12-play, 81-yard march that ended with a Taeo Todd 5-yard run, 10-0.

We had our chances. Ja'Quez Cross brought a kickoff back 51 yards to the Georgia Southern 40, and James London missed from 35. Before the half, Singleton converted a fourth-and-1 to Dillon Galloway, found Pickett for 10, and London hit from 33 with three seconds left, 10-3. That was the last time we scored.

The second half was a slow whittle down to nothing. Bryant kicked from 35 to make it 13-3 and from 34 to make it 16-3, and that second kick capped a 14-play drive that ate 7:25. On our next series, Singleton got sacked on back-to-back snaps, the second by Ethan Johnson for 10 yards. Then Singleton coughed up the ball at our 29, Anthony Bynum fell on it, and Bryant hit from 48, 19-3. We drove 75 yards in five plays to the Georgia Southern 2 and watched Singleton get intercepted in the end zone at 3:02.

Singleton went 15 of 28 for 215 yards with two interceptions and finished with minus-13 rushing yards. Pickett caught four balls for 86 yards, and Chris Lofton caught five for 82. Colton Phares made 12 tackles, and Juan Berchal had 10 with two throws broken up. Honest trail report: we ran for 6 yards on 16 carries, had the ball for 16:06 against 43:54, went 2 of 9 on third down and turned it over three times.

We're 5-3 and 2-2 in the Sun Belt. Georgia State comes to Kidd Brewer Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7. Come home to the mountain and get loud. Go Apps.
