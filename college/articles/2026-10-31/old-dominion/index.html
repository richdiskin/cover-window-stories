Old Dominion vs Marshall: Homecoming Spoiled in 36-23 Loss

Final: Marshall (5-3) 36, Old Dominion (2-7) 23, Sat., Oct. 31, S.B. Ballard Stadium, Norfolk. Next: at Coastal Carolina, Sat., Nov. 7, 1:00 PM ET, Brooks Stadium, Conway

XFacebook

Marshall 36, Old Dominion 23, and the royal court has to report a Homecoming gone wrong. It was Halloween in Norfolk, and the palace rules at S.B. Ballard Stadium were clear: costumes welcome, face paint welcome, masks and toy weapons banned. The Monarchs came dressed for a party. Marshall came dressed as a wrecking crew.

The opening ceremony collapsed on the second snap. Jaxon Potter was stripped, Marshall recovered at our 26, and four plays later it was 7-0. A 49-yard run made it 14-0. Maurki James restored some dignity with a 6-yard touchdown run, and Andrew Nelms hit a 21-yard field goal to make it 17-10.

Then came the moment that will stay in the court records. With four seconds left in the half, we tried a field goal, and Marshall returned it 100 yards for a touchdown. 27-10 at the break. A crown dropped on the ballroom floor.

Brandon Crutchfield answered for the crown early in the third. He intercepted Marshall at its own 44 and took it 44 yards to the end zone, and it was 27-17. Jackson Forrest led the defense with 12 tackles, and Lucas Struck added nine. Mario Thompson, Ahmaad Foster and Malachi Harris each recorded a sack, and the defense held Marshall to 2 of 14 on third down and 142 passing yards. But the Herd kept kicking. Their kicker made five field goals, and our offense threw three interceptions and turned it over on downs twice.

Potter threw for 329 yards on 23 of 44, and Josh Rodriguez was the brightest jewel in the crown with nine catches for 143 yards. Kendall Harris added four for 79. James punched in a 14-yard score late for his second touchdown of the day.

We're 2-7 and 1-5 in the Sun Belt. The court travels to Coastal Carolina on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 1:00 PM ET at Brooks Stadium. Long live the Monarchs.
