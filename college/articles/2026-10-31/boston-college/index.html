Boston College vs Duke: Eagles Buried in Durham 47-2

Final: Duke (6-2) 47, Boston College (4-4) 2, Sat., Oct. 31, Wallace Wade Stadium, Durham. Next: vs. Florida State, Sat., Nov. 7, Alumni Stadium, Chestnut Hill

Duke 47, Boston College 2, and the court will note for the record that our only points came on a 100-yard sprint by Carter Davis after a failed Duke extra point. Back home in Newton, nearly 500 kids had spent last weekend painting Halloween scenes on more than 150 storefront windows across seven village centers. Those windows came out sharper than anything we drew up in Durham.

I have argued losing cases in moot court. I have never argued one this hopeless. Duke punted on its first drive, we punted on ours, and then Nate Sheppard went to work. He scored from 1 yard, then 2 yards, then 54 yards. Jeremiah Hasley caught a 5-yard touchdown from Walker Eget with 2:14 left in the half, and we went to the locker room down 27-2. Sheppard scored twice more in the third quarter for five rushing touchdowns, a Duke school record set on our watch.

Exhibit A for the prosecution: we went 2 for 13 on third down and 0 for 4 on fourth down, and we were flagged 15 times for 129 yards. Fifteen. I could not get fifteen objections sustained in a full semester. Duke sacked our quarterbacks six times. Mason McKenzie was 9 of 17 for 87 yards and ran 13 times for 24 more, and we lost two fumbles. We finished with 213 yards.

Exhibit B for the defense, because there is one. Carter Davis was everywhere. He took that extra point the length of the field, he intercepted Dan Mahan in the third quarter, and he had two tackles for loss. Anthony Palano made nine tackles. Marcus Upton made eight more and returned four kicks for 81 yards. Bryce LaFollette punted six times for a 42.8-yard average, and Jackson Wade hauled in a 36-yard grab. That defense spent way too long on the field and still kept swinging.

My verdict is guilty on all counts, and I am sentencing myself to a double order at the Court of Iced Coffee before the bus gets home. We are 4-4 and 2-3 in the ACC, and a .500 record is not a closing argument. Florida State comes to Alumni Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7. Gavel down. Fix it by then, Eagles.
