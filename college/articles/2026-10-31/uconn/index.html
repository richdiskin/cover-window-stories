UConn vs Air Force: Huskies Ground Down by Falcons 35-26

Final: Air Force (6-2) 35, UConn (4-4) 26, Sat., Oct. 31, Falcon Stadium, Colorado Springs. Next: vs. North Carolina, Sat., Nov. 7, Pratt & Whitney Stadium at Rentschler Field, East Hartford

Air Force 35, UConn 26, and the Falcons ran the ball 74 times to get it done. Back in Storrs, WHUS Radio was throwing its Mischief After Dark Halloween concert in the Student Union Ballroom, with Ovlov and Whisper Doll on stage and a costume contest. The real mischief happened at 7,000 feet, and we were on the wrong end of it.

I count possessions. I am a point guard, it is what we do. Air Force held the ball for 41:49. We had it for 18:06. You do not win a game where the other team gets the ball for more than two-thirds of the clock, and that is the whole story.

Kemper Hodges ran 35 times for 200 yards and four touchdowns, including a 62-yarder in the first quarter. We were down 14-0 before we touched the end zone. Then this offense showed real fight. Trey Cornist scored from a yard out, Kalieb Osborne hit Ryder Treadway for a 2-yard score before the half, and Osborne found Alex Godavitarne for a 55-yard touchdown in the third. Kenji Christian broke a 46-yard touchdown run on the first play of the fourth quarter, and we were down 28-26.

That is where it slipped away. The extra point after Godavitarne's touchdown failed, and the two-point try after Christian's run failed too. Then Air Force took the ball and went 19 plays and 75 yards over 11:37. Nineteen plays. The Falcons went 5 for 6 on fourth down, and Hodges finished the drive from a yard out with 3:09 left. We got one more shot, and it died on a fourth-down incompletion.

The credit list is real. Osborne threw for 263 yards and two touchdowns with no interceptions, and Christian averaged 13.6 yards a carry. John Lista made 15 tackles, Tahjae Mullix had 12, and Hayden Pegg got a sack. Our defense was on the field all day and never quit, and we had zero turnovers.

We are 4-4, and the big part of the schedule is right here. North Carolina comes to Pratt & Whitney Stadium at Rentschler Field on Saturday, Nov. 7. Get the ball, keep the ball, Huskies. Every possession counts.
