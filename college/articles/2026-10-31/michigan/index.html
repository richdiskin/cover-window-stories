Michigan vs Rutgers: Carr Runs in Late Winner 28-21

Final: Michigan (5-3) 28, Rutgers (2-6) 21, Sat., Oct. 31, SHI Stadium, Piscataway. Next: vs. Michigan State, Sat., Nov. 7, Michigan Stadium, Ann Arbor

ShareXFacebook

Michigan 28, Rutgers 21, and Tommy Carr played the whole song on Halloween in Piscataway. Back in Ann Arbor, the University Symphony and Philharmonia Orchestras packed Hill Auditorium for the annual Halloween Concert, with Danse Macabre and the William Tell Overture on the program. Carr gave us our own overture, and he finished it with the trumpets blaring.

I logged this one in the maize sweater with the VICTORS mug at my elbow, and my data labels tell the story. Carr went 22 of 27 for 266 yards, ran 12 times for 54 yards and scored three touchdowns. He hit JJ Buchanan for a 6-yard score on a 92-yard first drive. Jordan Marshall punched in a 2-yard run early in the second quarter, and Carr ran one in from 15 yards in the third. Andrew Marsh caught seven balls, Buchanan five for 63, and Salesi Moa grabbed a 44-yarder.

Now the part that made the mug shake. Rutgers hit an 80-yard touchdown throw in the second quarter, and Dane Pizzaro made two field goals. Trey Butkowski missed both of ours. Early in the fourth, a kick-catch interference flag wiped out a muffed punt we had recovered, Antwan Raymond broke a 54-yard run on the very next drive, and Rutgers tied it 21-21 with 11:43 left.

Then came the best chart I have made all year. Michigan took the ball at our 2 and went 75 yards in 11 plays, chewing 7:19 off the clock. Hogan Hansen caught throws of 16 and 14 yards, Savion Hiter pounded inside, and Carr finished it himself from 9 yards out with 4:24 left. The defense forced a three-and-out, and Carr took a knee to end it.

The numbers back the eye test. We converted 12 of 16 third downs, held the ball for 39:06 and had zero turnovers. Rutgers completed three of 12 throws and managed nine first downs. Benny Patterson III made seven tackles, and Chris Bracy and Enow Etta each had a sack.

We are 5-3 and 2-3 in the Big Ten, and the two missed kicks go in red ink on the chart. Next week is the one we circle every year: Michigan State at Michigan Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7. Hail to the Victors. Fill the mug.
