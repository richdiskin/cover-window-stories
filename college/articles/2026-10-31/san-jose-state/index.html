San José State vs New Mexico: Spartans Fall Short 21-16

Final: New Mexico (7-1) 21, San José State (4-5) 16, Sat., Oct. 31, CEFCU Stadium, San José. Next: vs. Northern Illinois, Sat., Nov. 7, CEFCU Stadium, San José

XFacebook

New Mexico 21, San José State 16, and our last drive died in the end zone with 11 seconds left. CEFCU Stadium did Halloween right: Trick or Treat with the Spartans and Youth Spirit Day, the Spartan Boo Bash with trick-or-treat stations and pumpkin decorating in the Simpkins Stadium Center, and local youth dance and cheer groups on the field at halftime. The kids shipped a clean release. The football team shipped a bug.

Let me file the incident report. Our defense was ready. Trajan Sinatra hit field goals from 25, 24 and 28 yards, and we trailed only 14-9 at the half after two Scottre Humphrey touchdown runs. New Mexico pushed it to 21-9 in the third on a 20-yard throw to Cade Keith.

Then Tuli Tagovailoa-Amosa pushed the hotfix. He intercepted Toa Fa'avae and ran it back 89 yards for a touchdown with 10:47 left. 21-16. Justin Stearns blocked a 51-yard field goal try later in the fourth. Dylan Lee and Brian Dukes Jr. also intercepted Fa'avae. Three takeaways, and Lee led us with seven tackles.

Here is the bug. Our offense ran 35 times for 48 yards, and you cannot ship a product with that kind of latency. Luke Weaver threw two interceptions, the first on our series right after that interception return. Late in the fourth we drove into New Mexico territory and failed on fourth down at 1:23. We got the ball back, Weaver hit Anthony Ivey for 39 yards to the New Mexico 34 with 16 seconds left, and the next throw got intercepted in the end zone. Rollback.

Weaver finished 16 of 23 for 190 yards. Carson Clark caught eight balls for 91 yards, and Jabari Bates carried 22 times for 63. New Mexico ran for 231 yards, and its first win in San José since 1969 came against us. That stings.

We are 4-5 and 2-3 in the Mountain West. Northern Illinois comes to CEFCU Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7. Patch the run game, run the tests, and ship it to prod, Spartans.
