Cincinnati vs Utah: Bearcats Buried Early on Halloween, 49-25

Final: Utah (8-0) 49, Cincinnati (5-3) 25, Sat., Oct. 31, Nippert Stadium, Cincinnati. Next: at Houston, Sat., Nov. 7, TDECU Stadium, Houston

XFacebook

Utah 49, Cincinnati 25, at Nippert Stadium, and this one goes down as plain spaghetti. No chili, no cheese, no onions. The program asked us to wear black for Breast Cancer Awareness and invited the kids to come in Halloween costumes, and Clifton showed up dressed for it. The stands looked right. The scoreboard did not.

We hung with them early. Stephen Rusnak hit from 47 to make it 7-3. Then Utah ran 13 yards for a score, and the second quarter got ugly: Gi'Bran Payne lost a fumble that Utah returned 51 yards for a touchdown, Rusnak missed from 35, and Daniel Bray ran 30 yards for another.

We caught a break when Bray fumbled the kickoff after Rusnak's 39-yarder, but Utah fell on it and went 95 yards anyway. Then JC French IV was intercepted and Elijah Davis took it 39 yards for six. 42-6 at the half, a week after we beat Texas Tech on our 100th Homecoming.

The third quarter was at least a three-way. Jacob Finley jumped a Devon Dampier throw at our 21 and ran 79 yards for a touchdown. Cole Tabb ripped off a 48-yard touchdown run. French ran in from the 2 early in the fourth. We won the second half, and nobody on Calhoun Street is handing out trophies for that.

Tabb ran 14 times for 131 yards. French went 16 of 30 for 263 yards. JV Gibson caught five balls for 76, and Malachi Henry had a 63-yard grab. Finley made 10 tackles on top of his touchdown, Antwan Peek Jr. had eight, and Patrick Bauer forced and recovered a fumble in the fourth. We put up 456 yards. Utah put up 534 and scored twice on defense.

Utah is 8-0 for a reason. Credit to them, and now we get back to work.

We're 5-3, 2-3 in the Big 12. Next is a trip to Houston on Saturday, Nov. 7, at TDECU Stadium. Five-way or nothing. Go Bearcats.
