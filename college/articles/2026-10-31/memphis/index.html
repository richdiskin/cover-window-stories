Memphis vs Army: Stokes Throws Four, Tigers Roll 49-22

Final: Memphis (7-2) 49, Army (5-3) 22, Sat., Oct. 31, Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium, Memphis. Next: at South Florida, Thu., Nov. 12, Raymond James Stadium, Tampa

XFacebook

Memphis 49, Army 22, and Homecoming at Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium got the full band treatment. Hero Day honored the military, veterans and first responders, the crowd was asked to wear black, and Tiger Lane ran a Tailgate and Treat before kickoff. Our Tigers honored the cadets the right way: by playing their very best.

Marcus Stokes was the lead guitar all night. He completed 29 of 35 throws for 371 yards and four touchdowns, no interceptions. Jermane Hayes caught nine balls for 86 yards and scored twice from 13 and 5. Jemyri Davis caught touchdowns of 15 and 26. Hunter Tipton grabbed five for 86, including a 51-yarder. I hummed a riff after every score until my neighbor asked me to take a set break.

Now, I won't play loose with the sour notes. Davis lost a fumble in the second quarter, and Army tied it 14-14 four plays later. Their option ran for 320 yards, Godspower Nwawuihe went for 100, and Zach Mundell broke a 68-yarder that set up a score to make it 35-22. That defense needs to tune a few strings.

But listen to the groove we found once it was tied. Jaylin Carter punched in a 1-yard run, Stokes found Davis with 35 seconds left in the half, and Davis scored again in the third. Three straight touchdowns, 35-14. We converted 11 of 15 third downs and gained 554 yards with just two penalties.

And the fourth quarter had the strangest solo of the night. Stokes kept it from the 1, fumbled at the 4, grabbed his own ball and still got in for a touchdown and a 42-22 lead. Right after, Chauncey Logan Jr. intercepted Cale Hellums, and Carter's 3-yard run made it 49-22. Ellis Ellis Jr. intercepted the last throw at our 2 to close the show. Mike Montgomery led us with nine tackles.

On my soul scale, Army's alma mater gets a full respectful bow. The scoreboard gets an amen.

We're 7-2, 4-1 in the American and 5-0 at home. Next up, we head to South Florida on Thursday, Nov. 12, at Raymond James Stadium. Go Tigers Go!
