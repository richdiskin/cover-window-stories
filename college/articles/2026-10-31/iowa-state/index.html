Iowa State vs Oklahoma State: Konrardy Wins It at 0:00, 51-49

Final: Iowa State (4-4) 51, Oklahoma State (5-3) 49, Sat., Oct. 31, Jack Trice Stadium, Ames. Next: at Baylor, Sat., Nov. 7, McLane Stadium, Waco

Iowa State 51, Oklahoma State 49, and the forecast for Halloween night in Ames verified right at 0:00: Kyle Konrardy from 34 yards, good, ballgame. The Cyclones wore black, the athletics department told all of us to Pack the Jack and Wear Black, and Jack Trice Stadium looked like one giant storm cell on the radar from the opening kick. Costumes were welcome, masks were not, so every face in the place got to show exactly how loud it was.

Aiden Flora was the low-pressure system nobody could escape. He opened the scoring with a 29-yard run on our first drive and finished with 221 yards on 29 carries. His biggest gust came with 11:08 left: Oklahoma State had just pulled to 38-35, and Flora went 75 yards on the very next snap, 45-35.

Jaylen Raynor completed 18 of 28 throws for 309 yards and three touchdowns and ran in another from 8 yards out. Jamal Polite Jr. caught six balls for 171 yards, including a 43-yard score in the second quarter just 79 seconds after Raynor's 2-yard touchdown throw tied it 14-14. Cameron Pettaway hauled in a 40-yarder late in the third, 38-28.

The defense didn't have a clean night, but it brought the hail when it counted. Beau Goodwin intercepted Drew Mestemaker at the Oklahoma State 35 with 4:35 left in a 45-42 game, and Konrardy turned it into a 29-yarder, 48-42. Before the half, Caleb Schmidt jumped on a muffed punt at the Oklahoma State 36, and Konrardy hit from 39 with seven seconds left for a 24-21 lead. Sullivan Schlimgen led us with eight tackles and broke up two throws.

Then the front stalled right over Ames. Oklahoma State went 75 yards in 12 plays and scored with 53 seconds left, 49-48. A 15-yard unsportsmanlike flag on the Cowboys pushed their kickoff back, Raynor found Polite deep for 33 yards to the Oklahoma State 30, and Flora carried three straight times to the 16. Two seconds left. Konrardy, 34 yards, straight through. He went 3 for 3 and scored 15 points.

Honest radar check: we allowed 533 yards, Mestemaker threw five touchdowns, and Caleb Hawkins ran for 194. We answered with 581 yards, 272 rushing, held the ball for 34:47 and turned it over once.

We're 4-4 and 2-3 in the Big 12. Next up is Baylor at McLane Stadium in Waco on Saturday, Nov. 7. Seven-day outlook for Ames: sunshine in every kitchen. Go Cyclones.
