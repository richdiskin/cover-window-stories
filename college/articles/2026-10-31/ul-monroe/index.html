UL Monroe vs Southern Miss: Late Rally Falls Short 35-21

Final: Southern Miss (3-5) 35, UL Monroe (1-7) 21, Sat., Oct. 31, Malone Stadium, Monroe. Next: at Arkansas State, Sat., Nov. 7, Centennial Bank Stadium, Jonesboro

Southern Miss 35, UL Monroe 21. It was Halloween in Monroe, the Black Out Breast Cancer game, and fans were asked to wear black for Breast Cancer Awareness Month. It's also the first season under the FMOL Health St. Francis Stadium name, with an installation underway to honor Coach James Malone. The Warhawks gave the folks in black a fourth quarter worth staying for, just not a win.

Late-night radio, Delta style, optimism counter on the desk. We start at zero, because the first half was ugly. Southern Miss scored on a 33-yard run and a field goal, and we trailed 10-0 at the break. Abram Murray missed his only field-goal try.

Click one on the counter: Aidan Armenta hit Marcellus Jackson for 83 yards and a touchdown early in the third. 10-7, house alive. Then the defense gave up a 21-yard run, a 21-yard touchdown throw and a field goal, and it was 27-7.

Click two, click three. Armenta ran it in from 1 early in the fourth. Then he found Nic Trujillo from 2 with 6:47 left. 27-21, and that rally was real. Then Jamarice Wilder went 32 yards with 1:52 left, and the conversion made it 35-21.

Straight talk for the night shift. Armenta went 19 of 34 for 202 yards with two touchdowns and ran for another. We lost two fumbles and went zero for three on fourth down. Southern Miss ran for 249 yards on us. Don Chaney led us with 54 rushing yards. Noah Flemmings made 10 tackles with a half sack, and Taijh Overton added nine.

We are 1-7 and 1-3 in the Sun Belt, still hunting the first home win. Next is Arkansas State at Centennial Bank Stadium in Jonesboro on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3 PM ET. Counter reads three. Keep the radio on. Go Hawks.
