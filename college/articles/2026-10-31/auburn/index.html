Auburn vs Ole Miss: Tigers Fade Late on Halloween, Fall 45-15

Final: Ole Miss (6-2) 45, Auburn (4-4) 15, Sat., Oct. 31, Vaught-Hemingway Stadium, Oxford. Next: vs. Arkansas, Sat., Nov. 7, Jordan-Hare Stadium, Auburn

XFacebook

Ole Miss 45, Auburn 15, on Halloween afternoon in Oxford. Back home on Friday, the 38th Annual Pumpkin Carve lit up Dudley Courtyard with 200 pumpkins, and Aubie worked the crowd until the leftovers were auctioned off at 8:30. By Saturday evening, the only thing glowing on the Plains was my phone, full of texts I refuse to answer.

For a half, this was a fight. Byrum Brown led a 14-play, 75-yard drive and found Chas Nimrod for a 21-yard touchdown, then Ole Miss blocked the extra point and ran it back 92 yards to our 5. AnQuon Fegans intercepted Trinidad Chambliss on the first play of the next Rebel drive. That was the good news. Two plays later we fumbled it right back, and Ole Miss went 86 yards to make it 14-6. Alex McPherson hit from 29 with five seconds left, and we went in down 14-9.

Coming out of the break, Brown did it with his legs, capping a 10-play, 75-yard drive with a 7-yard touchdown run. We trailed 17-15 with 6:20 left in the third, and the whole Plains was on its feet. Then a false start pushed the two-point try back five yards, the try came up empty, and the afternoon went sideways.

From there Ole Miss scored the last 28 points. Chambliss ran 26 yards for one score and threw two more touchdowns in the fourth quarter, and Deuce Knight added a 17-yard run with 3:27 left. Sam Turner lost a fumble after that. The Rebels converted 13 of 16 third downs, and I need to lie down just typing it.

Honest film session: Brown went 25 of 38 for 195 yards and a touchdown, but his 13 carries netted 5 yards, and as a team we ran for 63 yards on 27 tries. Ole Miss piled up 10 tackles for loss, four of them sacks, and rolled up 487 yards. Champ Anthony, Bryce Deas and Kaleb Harris made eight tackles apiece, and Nimrod and DeShawn Spencer each caught four balls for 62 yards. We were 7 of 14 on third down. That part I will defend at any tailgate.

Bless their hearts, the Rebels earned it. I am sipping my lemonade with every ounce of dignity I have left, and I promise you it is not much.

We're 4-4, 2-4 in the SEC and 1-2 on the road. Arkansas comes to Jordan-Hare Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7, and Toomer's Corner has waited long enough for its next roll. War Eagle.
