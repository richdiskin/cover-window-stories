Duke vs Boston College: Sheppard Scores Five in Rout 47-2

Final: Duke (6-2) 47, Boston College (4-4) 2, Sat., Oct. 31, Wallace Wade Stadium, Durham. Next: at NC State, Sat., Nov. 7, Carter-Finley Stadium, Raleigh

ShareXFacebook

Duke 47, Boston College 2, and Nate Sheppard just rewrote the Duke record book on Halloween. Wallace Wade Stadium went full Blue Devil Halloween, with $10.31 tickets for Durham residents, trick-or-treating on the concourse, a halftime costume celebration and candy buckets for the first 750 kids in costume. Sheppard handed out touchdowns the same way.

Allow me to cite my sources. Footnote one: a 10-play, 80-yard drive capped by a 1-yard Sheppard plunge, with Javen Nicholas grabbing a 44-yard throw on third-and-5. Footnote two: Kobe Smith sacked the quarterback, KD Cotton returned the punt 18 yards, and Sheppard scored from 2 yards. Footnote three: a 54-yard Sheppard run, the longest of his career. Footnote four: Walker Eget's 5-yard touchdown throw to tight end Jeremiah Hasley with 2:14 left in the half. That was 27-2 at the break, peer-reviewed.

The third quarter added two more footnotes. Sheppard scored from 4 yards and then from 1 yard on the first two drives of the half. Five rushing touchdowns, a Duke single-game record, on 20 carries for 97 yards. Gavin Strange closed it with two field goals in the fourth quarter, including a 41-yarder, the longest of his career.

Now the defense, which deserves its own dissertation. Boston College got 213 yards. The Eagles went 0 for 6 on third down in the first half, 0 for 4 on fourth down all day, and they lost two fumbles. Duke sacked them six times, with Bradley Gompers, Bryce Davis, Kobe Smith and Owen Wafle each getting a full one. Nick Morris Jr. and Kobe Smith each fell on a fumble. The two points the Eagles scored came on a returned extra point. Their offense never found the scoreboard. That is the fewest points by a Duke conference opponent since 1989.

My one grade of incomplete: the failed extra point Carter Davis took 100 yards the other way, and an interception off Dan Mahan late in the third. Minor revisions. The rest of the paper gets an A.

Duke is 6-2 and 3-2 in the ACC, and bowl eligible for the fifth straight season. Pre-bowl, pre-November, pre-everything. Next we go to NC State at Carter-Finley Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7. Pack the pens. We are writing more.
