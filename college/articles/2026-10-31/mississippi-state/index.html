Mississippi State vs Texas: Bulldogs Sacked Into Silence 31-3

Final: Texas (6-2) 31, Mississippi State (4-4) 3, Sat., Oct. 31, DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium, Austin. Next: vs. Vanderbilt, Sat., Nov. 7, Davis Wade Stadium, Starkville

XFacebook

Texas 31, Mississippi State 3, and the cowbell went in the sock before halftime. Back home, the Starkville Community Trick-or-Treat Map wrapped up on Halloween, with Bulldog Burger, Mugshots and Bluto's among the six stops handing out free candy. Starkville gave away treats all day. Austin gave us seven sacks.

It went sideways on the fourth snap. On third-and-9, Arch Manning found Cam Coleman for 47 yards, then hit him for a 14-yard touchdown. Four plays, 62 yards, 7-0 with 12:59 still left in the first quarter.

We had our chances in the first half and let them walk out of the hen house. A 12-play drive reached their 18, Anthony Evans III lost six yards on a run, and Kyle Ferrie missed from 38. Gianni Spetic made his from 45. Then, with 1:13 left before the break, Rasheem Biles sacked AJ Swann at the Texas 39, the ball squirted loose, and Maraad Watson scooped it and ran 61 yards for a touchdown. 17-0. I wrote that one on a receipt and folded it very small.

Our defense gave us a pulse in the third. Jaray Bledsoe sacked Manning, and Zakari Tillman fell on the loose ball at the Texas 28. Biles then dropped Evans for a 15-yard loss, and we settled for Ferrie's 30-yarder, 17-3. Texas answered with a seven-play, 75-yard drive capped by Manning's 4-yard touchdown throw to Raleek Brown. Early in the fourth we went for it on fourth-and-2 at their 40, and Parker Puckett's throw to Evans fell incomplete. Manning hit Emmett Mosley V for a 42-yard touchdown on third-and-1, 31-3.

Here's what the feed-store receipt says. Our defense held Texas to 60 rushing yards on 32 carries and sacked Manning five times. Tillman had a sack, 2.5 tackles for loss and that fumble recovery, and Fatt Forrest made seven tackles. We held the ball 32:28. Now the honest half of the receipt: 187 yards of offense, 68 rushing yards on 41 carries, 6 of 18 on third down, seven sacks allowed. Swann went 12 of 15 for 97 yards, Puckett 3 of 10 for 22, and Fluff Bothwell ran 17 times for 49. Marquis Johnson caught six balls for 61 yards. You can't grow a crop on 187 yards.

We're 4-4 and 2-4 in the SEC. Vanderbilt comes to Davis Wade Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7, and the cowbell stays in the sock until then. Hail State.
