Miami vs North Carolina: Canes Escape Chapel Hill 38-35

Final: Miami (8-0) 38, North Carolina (4-4) 35, Sat., Oct. 31, Kenan Stadium, Chapel Hill. Next: at Notre Dame, Sat., Nov. 7, Notre Dame Stadium, Notre Dame, Ind.

XFacebook

Miami 38, North Carolina 35, and we are 8-0. Back home, the Miami Canes packed Lost Boy Dry Goods on East Flagler Street downtown for the official game watch in orange and green, and I promise that room was louder than Kenan Stadium every time Carolina tied it or took the lead. That happened four times. On Halloween. Rude.

Let me be honest, because The Breeze does not do spin. This was a street fight. Carolina's quarterback Billy Edwards Jr. went 28 of 34 for 341 yards and four touchdowns, and the Tar Heels went 8 of 11 on third down. We trailed 21-17 in the third and 28-24 early in the fourth after Demon June punched one in from a yard out.

Then the grown-ups took over. Down 28-24 with 11:46 to go, Darian Mensah hit Malachi Toney for 31 yards on a no-huddle march, and Mark Fletcher Jr. bulled in from 2 for his second rushing score. 31-28. Next series, Marquise Lightfoot sacked Edwards, the ball came loose, and Justin Scott jumped on it at the Carolina 37. Six plays later Mensah found Toney from 9 yards out. 38-28. Lightfoot, se acabó, the quarterback hit the ground, adiós!

Carolina scored with 1:38 left to make it 38-35, then tried three onside kicks. Two got wiped out by offside flags, and Ezekiel Marcelin Jr. grabbed the third. Mensah knelt, and the sunglasses stayed in my pocket.

The numbers. Mensah went 22 of 29 for 274 yards, three touchdowns and zero interceptions. Toney caught seven balls for 75 yards and two scores, Cooper Barkate added four for 71, and Fletcher scored three times, two on the ground and once on a 31-yard catch. Lightfoot had two sacks. Chase Smith, JJ Dunnigan and Mohamed Toure each made nine tackles. We turned it over zero times and went 6 of 11 on third down.

Now the part I won't hide. We let them throw for 341 yards and gave up 35 points to a 4-4 team. The defense has to tighten up, because the road gets harder.

We are 8-0, 6-0 in the ACC and 4-0 away from home. Next is Notre Dame at Notre Dame Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:30 PM ET. Arms up, make the U. Swag!
