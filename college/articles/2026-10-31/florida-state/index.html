Florida State vs Clemson: Four Turnovers Sink Seminoles 36-24

Final: Clemson (5-3) 36, Florida State (3-5) 24, Sat., Oct. 31, Doak Campbell Stadium, Tallahassee. Next: at Boston College, Sat., Nov. 7, Alumni Stadium (Chestnut Hill, MA), Chestnut Hill

ShareXFacebook

Clemson 36, Florida State 24. The record will show that Doak Campbell Stadium did its part. Florida State rolled out Operation Garnet Storm for Halloween night, with festivities on Langford Green from 3 p.m., a salute to Seminoles legend Peter Warrick, the program's first-ever drone show and a stadium light show at the start of the fourth quarter. The lights came on, and so did our offense. Too late, but it came.

Let the minutes reflect the first three quarters. We trailed 29-10 after Nolan Hauser's fifth field goal early in the fourth. Ashton Daniels threw three interceptions, two to Elliot Washington II and one to Jerome Carter III, and fumbled at the Clemson 20 after a 10-yard run in the third quarter. Four turnovers. We went 1 of 10 on third down and ran for 65 yards. That bill dies in committee every time.

Then the floor opened. Daniels ran in from 13 and threw for the two-point conversion, 29-18. Next series, he found Jasen Lopez for 41 yards and a touchdown, and it was 29-24 with 5:30 left. Doak was shaking.

Motion to reconsider: denied. Tait Reynolds ran 79 yards for a touchdown on Clemson's next drive, and our last push ended when Washington intercepted Daniels in the end zone with 1:03 to go.

For the record, the playmakers showed up. Daniels threw for 350 yards on 21 of 33 with two touchdowns, and he ran for one. Micahi Danzy caught four balls for 108 yards, including a 46-yard touchdown in the first quarter. Jayvan Boggs had two catches for 64. CJ Richard Jr. made 13 tackles, Chris Jones made 10, and Ashlynd Barker added nine. We out-gained Clemson 415 yards to 351. The turnovers decided it.

We are 3-5 and 1-4 in the ACC. Next is Boston College at Alumni Stadium in Chestnut Hill on Saturday, Nov. 7, at noon ET. My amendment for Chestnut Hill is simple: protect the football. All in favor? The ayes have it.
