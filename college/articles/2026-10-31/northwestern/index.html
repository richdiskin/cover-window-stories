Northwestern vs Oregon: Wildcats Blow 20-6 Lead, Fall 34-26

Final: Oregon (5-3) 34, Northwestern (6-2) 26, Sat., Oct. 31, Autzen Stadium, Eugene. Next: vs. Iowa, Sat., Nov. 7, Ryan Field, Evanston

Oregon 34, Northwestern 26. Back in Evanston, New Ryan Field threw open its gates Saturday afternoon for a free Family Halloween Festival, with candy, costumes and the Northwestern Wildcats at Oregon on the big screen. Those kids watched us own Autzen Stadium for a half and then watched the Ducks take it back. Footnote: we were in control. Footnote to the footnote: control is not a final score.

On our first trip to Eugene, we scored on our first four drives. Aidan Chiles found Griffin Wilde behind the whole secondary for a 64-yard touchdown, Jackson Kleather hit from 37 twice, and Chiles kept it on a 5-yard bootleg for a 20-6 lead in the second quarter. Wilde finished with five catches for a season-high 146 yards. Chiles went 22 of 38 for 281 yards and ran 14 times for a career-high 93.

Here is the investigative finding, and I filed it with a heavy heart. We kept knocking on the door and kicking field goals. Raiola hit Dakorien Moore for 10 yards before halftime, and it was 20-13. Braydon Brus ripped the ball from Moore on the first drive of the third, Damon Walters fell on it, and we got only a 49-yard Kleather kick out of it. Then Oregon went 80 yards for one touchdown and 76 for another, and we trailed 27-23.

Kleather drilled another 49-yarder to make it 27-26. One snap later, Dylan Raiola hit Moore for 75 yards. Kleather's 42-yard try with 5:14 left missed, his only miss in five, and the Ducks closed it out. Oregon outscored us 21-6 after halftime.

The data on the defense is not all bad. Alijah Jones made eight tackles, and Brus, Montae Pate and Kobie McKinzie had six each. We had zero turnovers and held the ball for 35:54. But Raiola threw for 336 yards and three touchdowns, and that stat sits in the margin and hurts.

We're 6-2 and 3-2 in the Big Ten, and the correction desk opens Monday. Iowa comes to Ryan Field on Saturday, Nov. 7, and that game matters in the standings. Go 'Cats.
