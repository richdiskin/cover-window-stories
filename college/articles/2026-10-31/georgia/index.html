Georgia vs Florida: Dawgs Defense Takes Six Straight, Wins 27-8

Final: Georgia (7-1) 27, Florida (5-3) 8, Sat., Oct. 31, Mercedes-Benz Stadium, Atlanta. Next: at Ole Miss, Sat., Nov. 7, Vaught-Hemingway Stadium, Oxford

XFacebook

Georgia 27, Florida 8, on Halloween in Atlanta, and that's six straight in this series for the Dawgs. Back in Athens, the 18th Wild Rumpus Parade stepped off at 8 p.m. outside Creature Comforts on W. Hancock Ave., and downtown partied into the night. So did our defense, about four hours earlier.

First time this rivalry ever came to Atlanta, and the set list opened with a skipped track: Peyton Woodring missed from 43 on our first drive. Then the defense dropped the needle. Ellis Robinson IV intercepted Aaron Philo's underhand flip, a roughing penalty on our punt handed us new life, and Nate Frazier went in from the 1 for a 7-0 lead.

Demello Jones intercepted Philo and brought it back 23 yards, and Woodring hit from 28. Bo Walker capped a 10-play, 81-yard drive with a 1-yard run. Then the headliner: Quintavius Johnson sacked Tramell Jones Jr., the ball came out, and Chris Cole ran it 66 yards to the house with 29 seconds left in the half. 24-0. That's the closer you save for the encore, and we played it before intermission.

The second half was a B-side, I'll admit it. Talyn Taylor lost a fumble, and Florida turned it into eight points. But Braylon Conley intercepted Jones in the end zone with 4:50 left in the third to keep Florida off the board, and Woodring added a 21-yarder with 3:34 left to close it at 27-8.

Our ground game ran 42 times for 217 yards. Frazier had 67 and a touchdown, Dwight Phillips Jr. had 67 with a 26-yard burst, and Gunner Stockton went 13 of 23 for 101 while running for 45 more. Cole led us with nine tackles and added a sack on top of the touchdown. Raylen Wilson had eight. Four takeaways turned into 17 points, and Florida ran for 48 yards on 28 carries.

Album review: four takeaways, four sacks, one fumble-return encore. Ten out of ten. Play it loud on the drive home.

We're 7-1, 5-1 in the SEC. Next up is Ole Miss on Saturday, Nov. 7, at Vaught-Hemingway Stadium in Oxford. Pack the headphones and bring the defense. Go Dawgs.
