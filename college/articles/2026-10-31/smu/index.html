SMU vs Syracuse: Mustangs Lose on Last-Minute Score 30-27

Final: Syracuse (4-4) 30, SMU (6-2) 27, Sat., Oct. 31, JMA Wireless Dome, Syracuse. Next: vs. Virginia Tech, Fri., Nov. 6, Gerald J. Ford Stadium, Dallas

XFacebook

Syracuse 30, SMU 27. Back home in Dallas, NorthPark Center's Pumpkin Patch was in its final weekend in Center Park, gnome village and mythical maze included, and I had my Monday photo shoot planned around it. Up in the JMA Wireless Dome, the Syracuse Orange wore Otto on their helmets for the first time ever, and with 17 seconds left on Halloween, that big orange pumpkin of a mascot got the last laugh.

I am not hiding it. We had this one. Kevin Jennings was close to flawless, 29 of 36 for 351 yards, two touchdowns and no interceptions. He hit Jalen Hale from 9 yards out at the end of the first quarter, found Yamir Knight for a 14-yard score in the third, and Knight finished with six catches for 80 yards. Yannick Smith caught five for 95. That is a throwing game you frame and hang in the shop window.

Then the fourth quarter got loud. Wade McSparron's punt pinned Syracuse at its own 8, their drive went backward, and we took a safety, 20-19. Kendrick Raphael ran it in from 13 yards, Jennings threw the two-point try to Derrick McFall, and we led 27-20 with 9:04 left. I was reaching for the confetti.

Put it back in the box. Syracuse kicked a field goal, our next drive went three plays backward with a sack in the middle of it, and Steve Angeli marched the Orange 11 plays down the field. His 2-yard throw to Noah Meyers with 17 seconds left was the dagger. Our last gasp ended in a fumble.

The honest fitting-room mirror: two lost fumbles, nine penalties for 90 yards and 70 rushing yards on 31 carries. Hale's fumble in the second quarter, forced by Demetres Samuel Jr., set up a Syracuse field goal, and Jennings got sacked four times. Syracuse went 4 for 4 on fourth down. We outgained them 421 to 338 and still flew home with the loss. Sael Reyes made 10 tackles and the defense pinned Syracuse deep all day. They deserved better.

So yes, I am wearing the biggest sunglasses I own on the flight home. We are 6-2 and 4-2 in the ACC, and nothing is lost that a Friday night on the Boulevard cannot fix. Virginia Tech comes to Gerald J. Ford Stadium on Friday, Nov. 6. Pony up.
