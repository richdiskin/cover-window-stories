San Diego State vs Washington State: Kinnard Runs Aztecs 26-20

Final: San Diego State (4-5) 26, Washington State (1-8) 20, Sat., Oct. 31, Snapdragon Stadium, San Diego. Next: vs. Utah State, Sat., Nov. 14, Snapdragon Stadium, San Diego

ShareXFacebook

San Diego State 26, Washington State 20, on Halloween at Snapdragon Stadium, and the Aztecs gave the whole River Park a treat. The River Park Block Party opened at 11 a.m., kids paraded around the Yellow Lot right after the Warrior Walk, our student-athletes handed out candy, and the students got the green light to wear costumes to the game. Then Javion Kinnard put on his work boots.

Kinnard set the tone early. After our defense stuffed the Cougars on fourth-and-1 at our 21, he capped an eight-play, 79-yard drive with a 9-yard touchdown run. Washington State tied it, and Kinnard went right back to work, finishing an 11-play, 79-yard march from 10 yards out, 14-7. The Cougars tied it again, so Cooper DiLeva drilled a 36-yarder. Before the half, Sione Hala hurried Owen Eshelman and Kai Wallin jumped the throw at our 28. Gnarly. 17-14 at the break.

The third quarter turned into a big swell for the defense. Stone Saunders got intercepted on our first series, and the Cougars had a short field. A Cougar personal foul pushed them back 15 yards, they gained six on four plays, Djibril Rahman hurried Eshelman on fourth-and-4, and the throw fell incomplete. DiLeva banged home a 45-yarder, 20-14. Krishna Clay then dropped Eshelman for a 14-yard loss, and the 53-yard try that followed sailed no good.

Here's the wave I'll be talking about at Ocean Beach for a month. Fourth-and-goal at the Cougar 2, a 15-play drive that ate 7:11, and Bert Emanuel Jr. took the ball and threw a touchdown to Jackson Ford, 26-14. Washington State answered, missed the extra point, and got one last shot. On fourth-and-10 from their 48 with 38 seconds left, Mike Lindsay broke up the deep throw. Paddle in, dudes.

Lucky Sutton ran 18 times for 115 yards, with a 46-yard burst. Kinnard added 87 yards and both rushing scores on 17 carries. We ran for 202 yards on 37 tries. Tainoa Lave made nine tackles, Clay had 1.5 sacks, and the defense sacked Eshelman three times and stopped all three Cougar fourth-down tries. Honest surf report: Saunders went 12 of 21 for 102 yards with an interception, Eshelman threw for 299 on us, and the Cougars converted 9 of 14 third downs. That water gets cleaned up.

We're 4-5, 3-2 in the Pac-12 and 4-1 at Snapdragon. After the open week, Utah State comes to Snapdragon Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14. Wax the board. Go Aztecs.
