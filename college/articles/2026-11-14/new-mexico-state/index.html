New Mexico State vs Missouri State: 253 Rushing Yards and a Heartbreak in Overtime

Final: Missouri State Bears (4-6) 27, New Mexico State Aggies (3-7) 21, Sat., Nov. 14, Robert W. Plaster Stadium, Springfield. Next: vs Delaware, Sat., Nov. 21, 3:00 PM ET, Aggie Memorial Stadium, Las Cruces

By Esperanza Lujan-Tafoya, The New Mexico State Fanatic

XFacebook

Aggies, I've had a lot of hard nights following this team, and this one goes near the top of the list. Missouri State 27, New Mexico State 21. It took overtime at Robert W. Plaster Stadium, and we're 3-7. We had it. We had it right there.

The ground game was beautiful. James Jones ran 25 times for 150 yards and a 1-yard touchdown early in the fourth that gave us a 21-14 lead. TJ Washington Jr. added 107 yards on 15 carries and caught a 14-yard touchdown from Trey Hedden in the third quarter to tie it. We ran for 253 yards and held the ball for more than 33 minutes. That's Aggie football, the way I like it, green chile tough.

Hedden also found Brodie Malone-Bradford for a 17-yard touchdown in the second quarter. The defense stood up when it counted, too: Tory Gethers had two and a half tackles for loss, De'Marcus Peters picked off a throw in the third quarter and made a fourth-down stop, and we stuffed them on fourth down again in the fourth. Nick Session made six tackles.

But the mistakes. Oh, the mistakes. Missouri State's first touchdown was an interception return. A fumble in the third quarter killed a drive at their 40. Twelve penalties for 115 yards. And with 2:51 left, up seven, we missed a 22-yard field goal that would have just about sealed it. They went 80 yards and tied it with 56 seconds left.

Then in overtime, a throw got picked off and returned 88 yards. It was finished before I could even stand up.

I'm proud of the way this team ran the football. I'm heartbroken about the rest. Las Cruces, we'll be back.

Home cooking. Let's go, Aggies. Pistols up! The New Mexico State Aggies host Delaware at Aggie Memorial Stadium, Nov. 21, 3 PM ET.
