San Diego State vs Utah State: Cooper DiLeva Kicks Three and Throws One in a 30-13 Win

Final: San Diego State Aztecs (5-5) 30, Utah State Aggies (4-6) 13, Sat., Nov. 14, Snapdragon Stadium, San Diego. Next: at Boise State, Sat., Nov. 21, 9:30 PM ET, Albertsons Stadium, Boise

By Kai Delgado-Sorensen, The San Diego State Fanatic

XFacebook

Aztec fans, our kicker threw a touchdown. Let me say that again for the folks in the back at Snapdragon Stadium: our kicker threw a touchdown. San Diego State 30, Utah State 13 on Saturday night, and we're 5-5 with a 4-2 conference mark.

It did not begin pretty. Utah State scored first on a fumble that their own guy fell on in the end zone, and it was 7-0 midway through the first quarter. Cooper DiLeva got us on the board with a 31-yard field goal, and late in the half Lucky Sutton punched in a 4-yard run. 10-7 at the break.

Then came the play I'll be telling my grandkids about. Midway through the third quarter, DiLeva set up for what looked like a 38-yard try. The holder flicked the ball behind his back to DiLeva, he rolled right, and he floated it to linebacker Kai Wallin, who was so wide open he could have stopped for a fish taco. Twenty yards, touchdown, 17-7. A linebacker scoring on a throw from a kicker. That's Aztec football, baby.

DiLeva wasn't done. He hit from 42 yards before the third quarter ended and from 34 early in the fourth. Three field goals, one touchdown throw, three extra points. Twelve points from one guy. Somebody get that man a parking spot.

The ground game carried the rest. Javion Kinnard ran 19 times for 113 yards and added 47 yards on punt returns. Sutton had 96 yards on 21 carries and sealed it with a 2-yard score with 2:12 left. Nathan Acevedo caught eight balls for 92 yards. Stone Saunders threw one interception, but we outgained them 410 to 200.

And the defense was nasty. Utah State managed 61 rushing yards. Sione Hala had a sack and two tackles for loss, and his sack ended a fourth-down try in our territory. Tano Letuli made eight tackles. Three of the last four are wins for us.

Big one next. The Aztecs head to Boise to face Boise State at Albertsons Stadium, Saturday, Nov. 21, 9:30 PM ET. Let's go steal one on the blue turf, fellas.
