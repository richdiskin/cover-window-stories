Cal vs Virginia: Golden Bears Give Away Two Touchdowns in a 36-7 Loss

Final: Virginia Cavaliers (5-5) 36, California Golden Bears (3-7) 7, Sat., Nov. 14, Scott Stadium, Charlottesville. Next: vs Stanford, Sat., Nov. 21, 7:30 PM ET, California Memorial Stadium, Berkeley

By Quentin Oyelaran-Fitch, The California Fanatic

XFacebook

Golden Bears, it was a long way to fly for that. Virginia 36, California 7. That was Saturday at Scott Stadium in Charlottesville. It leaves us at 3-7. That's four straight losses. I'm writing this from a hotel lobby with a cup of tea that's gone cold, and that feels about right.

The game turned on one play near halftime. We were down 9-0 and driving with 2:11 left, and we tried a trick play, a throw from M. Mini. Virginia picked it off in its own end zone and ran it back 100 yards. Instead of a two-point game, it was 16-0. I put my head in my hands, and I left it there for a while.

We did answer. Adam Mohammed punched in a 5-yard touchdown with 14 seconds left in the half, and it was 16-7 at the break. Mohammed ran hard all day, and Mark Hamper caught six balls for 66 yards.

But the second half didn't go our way. Jaron-Keawe Sagapolutele threw two interceptions, and the second one came back 33 yards for a Virginia touchdown with four seconds left in the third quarter. That made it 26-7. We ran for 42 yards and had 201 for the game. It's hard to win when you can't get anything going on the ground.

The defense hung in longer than the final score says. AJ Tuitele made 11 tackles. Kingston Lopa had eight. Cam Sidney broke up a throw. Virginia went 7 of 16 on third down, and we kept a couple of drives to field goals. When your offense gives up 14 points on its own, though, the defense can only do so much.

I'm not giving up on these kids. Justin Wilcox's teams always fight, and the biggest game of the year is next.

It's Big Game week. Forget the record. Beat Stanford. Go Bears! The California Golden Bears host Stanford at California Memorial Stadium, Nov. 21, 7:30 PM ET.
