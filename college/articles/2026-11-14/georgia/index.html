Georgia vs Missouri: Five Straight Scores and a 48-21 Rout Between the Hedges

Final: Georgia Bulldogs (9-1) 48, Missouri Tigers (6-4) 21, Sat., Nov. 14, Sanford Stadium, Athens. Next: at South Carolina, Sat., Nov. 21, 7:30 PM ET, Williams-Brice Stadium, Columbia

By Thaddeus 'Thad' Okafor-Brandt, The Georgia Fanatic

XFacebook

Dawg Nation, we're back on top and we celebrated like it. Georgia 48, Missouri 21 on Saturday at Sanford Stadium, and we're 9-1. The last time we got to No. 1, we tripped on Auburn the very next week. This time, the Dawgs came out angry.

Five possessions, five scores. We got the ball first and Nate Frazier ran it in from 4 yards. Then Missouri's first snap got picked off by Raylen Wilson, who brought it back 30 yards to their 7, and Gunner Stockton ran it in from 8. Frazier scored again from 3, Peyton Woodring kicked a 41-yarder and Stockton hit Isiah Canion for a 12-yard touchdown. 31-0, and the second quarter wasn't even half done. I hugged the man next to me, then his wife, then a stranger in a red sweater.

Stockton was in complete control: 18 of 24 for 252 yards and a touchdown, plus that rushing score. Craig Dandridge caught six balls for 85 yards. Talyn Taylor had three for 63. Dwight Phillips Jr. ran seven times for 66. Frazier finished with three short touchdown runs, and Chauncey Bowens added a fourth-quarter score.

The defense set the tone, and Kirby will be happy with how it held up while it mattered. Justin Williams made 10 tackles. Rasean Dinkins and Chris Cole each had a tackle for loss. Missouri went 2 of 11 on third down and had 286 yards, a lot of it after the outcome was decided.

Woodring hit from 41 and 40 and made all six extra points. That man is automatic.

Kirby said this team will be measured by November and the months after. I'm already planning my trips.

Night game, road crowd. Bring it on. Go Dawgs! The Georgia Bulldogs travel to face South Carolina in Columbia, Nov. 21, 7:30 PM ET.
