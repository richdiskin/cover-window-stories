Michigan vs Oregon: Savion Hiter's 70-Yard Run Is the Lone Bright Spot

Final: Oregon Ducks (7-3) 31, Michigan Wolverines (5-5) 7, Sat., Nov. 14, Autzen Stadium, Eugene. Next: vs UCLA, Sat., Nov. 21, time TBA, Michigan Stadium, Ann Arbor

By Augustin 'Gus' Hartwell, The Michigan Fanatic

XFacebook

Wolverines, I flew out to Eugene hoping for a turnaround. I got a long flight home. Oregon 31, Michigan 7 on Saturday at Autzen Stadium, and we're 5-5. One more win gets us bowl eligible in Kyle Whittingham's first season, and right now that's what I'm holding onto.

The week was rough before we even got on the plane. After last week's 15-6 loss to Michigan State, the offensive coordinator was let go. Then on Saturday, Oregon hit a 60-yard touchdown on its opening drive, and we were chasing all day.

There was one moment I'll keep. Early in the second quarter, down 14-0, Savion Hiter took a handoff and went 70 yards for a touchdown. I was the only one standing in my row, and I didn't care. Hiter finished with 107 yards on 13 carries. Bryson Kuzdzal ran for 46 and Tommy Carr for 43. We ran for 215 yards. That part of the plan worked.

The rest didn't. We completed 6 of 20 throws for 71 yards between Carr and Bryce Underwood. We went 0 for 3 on fourth down and lost two fumbles in the fourth quarter. Oregon threw for 341 yards, and a 59-yard touchdown with six seconds left in the first half made it 21-7. That one hurt.

Chris Bracy had a sack and eight tackles. Zeke Berry made eight stops, and Troy Bowles had seven. J. Hill broke up a fourth-down throw in the second quarter. The defense kept swinging even when the offense couldn't help.

This is a hard season. It's not the Michigan I grew up with. But the Big House is still the Big House, and Saturday is a chance to send this team into a bowl.

Go Blue. The Michigan Wolverines host UCLA at Michigan Stadium, Nov. 21, time TBA.
