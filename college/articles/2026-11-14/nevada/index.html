Nevada vs Northern Illinois: Herschel Turner Runs for 153 and the Pack Rolls in DeKalb

Final: Nevada Wolf Pack (4-6) 28, Northern Illinois Huskies (0-10) 7, Sat., Nov. 14, Huskie Stadium, Dekalb. Next: vs Hawai'i, Sat., Nov. 21, 10:30 PM ET, Mackay Stadium, Reno

By Ridge Corbell, The Nevada Fanatic

XFacebook

Wolf Pack, that's how you take a road trip. Nevada 28, Northern Illinois 7 on Saturday at Huskie Stadium in DeKalb, and we're 4-6. I traded the Sierra for the cornfields for a weekend, and the boys made it worth every mile.

Herschel Turner was a man on a mission. Thirteen carries, 153 yards, almost 12 yards every time he touched it. He opened the scoring with a 6-yard run in the first quarter and then broke a 29-yarder early in the second. We ran for 308 yards as a team, and AJ Bianco added 90 of them on 13 carries. When the ground game works like that, everything works.

And Trace Estes? Two catches. Two touchdowns. A 51-yarder from Bianco in the second quarter that made it 21-0, and a 22-yarder in the third that made it 28-0. Efficiency, my friends. Bianco only needed 13 throws all afternoon.

The defense had Northern Illinois in a headlock. Joshua Scott picked off a throw on the Huskies' very first drive. E. Smith forced a fumble early in the second quarter that I. Jordan recovered. David Paine Jr. was a terror with two sacks. Jeremy Naborne-Owens had a tackle and a half for loss and broke up a throw, and Aiden Walker made seven tackles. NIU ran for 68 yards and didn't score until 4:16 left. Brandon Amaniampong recovered a fumble in the final minute to finish it off.

We did leave some points out there. We came up short on fourth down twice and threw an interception in the end zone late. But a 21-0 halftime lead on the road is something I'll gladly take.

This team keeps getting better, and Reno should be excited about what's coming next.

Let's pack Mackay and keep this rolling. Go Pack! The Nevada Wolf Pack host Hawai'i at Mackay Stadium, Nov. 21, 10:30 PM ET.
