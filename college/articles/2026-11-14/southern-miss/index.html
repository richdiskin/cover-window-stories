Southern Miss vs Louisiana Tech: Mario Sanders Shines on a Rough Night in Ruston

Final: Louisiana Tech Bulldogs (8-2) 63, Southern Miss Golden Eagles (3-7) 31, Sat., Nov. 14, Joe Aillet Stadium, Ruston. Next: vs South Alabama, Sat., Nov. 21, 4:00 PM ET, M. M. Roberts Stadium, Hattiesburg

By Delphine 'Dee' Broussard, The Southern Miss Fanatic

XFacebook

Golden Eagle fans, I'm going to be honest with you. Louisiana Tech 63, Southern Miss 31. That was Saturday night at Joe Aillet Stadium. It leaves us at 3-7. We threw an interception on the first play of the game, and the night never really got better from there.

It was 21-3 after one quarter. Then, in a two-minute stretch of the second, Louisiana Tech returned two interceptions for touchdowns, one for 36 yards and one for 89. By halftime it was 42-10. We threw four interceptions in all, and two of them went the other way for scores. You can't win like that against anybody, let alone a team that's 8-2.

There were moments worth cheering, and I cheered every one. Jamarice Wilder broke a 53-yard touchdown run early in the second quarter. Mario Sanders was outstanding, with eight catches for 119 yards, including a 41-yard touchdown in the fourth quarter. Grant Page caught a 7-yard touchdown in the third. Steven Robinson ran for 94 yards, and Creighton Wilbanks hit a 44-yard field goal. And late in the fourth, MaLik Caswell intercepted a throw and took it 32 yards to the house. That one felt good.

Wesley Edison was everywhere on defense with 10 tackles and two throws broken up. Mathis Haygood made seven stops, and Landon Sylvie added five. We ran for 186 yards and finished with 406. That's not nothing. It just doesn't matter much when you hand the other team the ball five times.

Three and seven hurts. I've been coming to games since I was a kid, and I've seen this program climb out of tough spots before. But Hattiesburg is home, and home is where we fix this.

Fill The Rock. To the Top! The Southern Miss Golden Eagles host South Alabama at M. M. Roberts Stadium, Nov. 21, 4 PM ET.
