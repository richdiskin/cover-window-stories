Kennesaw State vs Sam Houston: Cam Miller Goes 88 Yards and the Owls Win a Third Straight

Final: Kennesaw State Owls (4-6) 26, Sam Houston Bearkats (1-9) 7, Sat., Nov. 14, Elliott T. Bowers Stadium, Huntsville. Next: vs Florida International, Sat., Nov. 21, 3:00 PM ET, Walens Family Field at Fifth Third Stadium, Kennesaw

By Ximena Holcombe-Reyes, The Kennesaw State Fanatic

XFacebook

Owls, three in a row! Kennesaw State 26, Sam Houston 7 on Saturday at Elliott T. Bowers Stadium in Huntsville, Texas, and we're 4-6 with three straight Conference USA wins. We had never beaten the Bearkats. We were 0-3 against them, all close losses. Not anymore.

The play of the day, maybe the season, belongs to Cam Miller. Late in the third quarter, Sam Houston had fourth-and-3 in the red zone, and Miller jumped the throw across the middle at our 12 and ran 88 yards the other way untouched. I screamed so loud the family next to me moved down a row. 26-7, and that settled it.

Boogie Woods III was a workhorse again, 17 carries for 101 yards, and he caught a 19-yard touchdown from Landon Varnes in the first quarter to make it 10-0. Woods came in leading the conference in rushing touchdowns, and now he's beating teams through the air, too. Brayden Munroe got one carry all day and turned it into a 6-yard touchdown. Talk about efficient.

Varnes was cool and careful, 22 of 35 for 181 yards with no turnovers. Davis Bryson caught six balls for 56 yards. Daniel Kinney hit field goals from 47 and 33 yards.

And the defense? Sam Houston had 184 yards, and after its touchdown early in the second quarter it never scored again. Marcus Patterson got a sack. Josh Anglin made six tackles. Tywon Christopher and Baron Hopson each had a tackle for loss. The Bearkats missed a 45-yard field goal in the third, and then came Miller's interception. Defense wins, y'all.

Remember when we beat Middle Tennessee 44-28 on the road and Delaware 42-40 at home? This streak is real, and the Owls are playing their best football right now.

Let's make it four. Hooty hoo! The Kennesaw State Owls host Florida International at Walens Family Field at Fifth Third Stadium, Nov. 21, 3 PM ET.
