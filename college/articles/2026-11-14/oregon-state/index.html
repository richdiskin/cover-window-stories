Oregon State vs Boise State: Maalik Murphy Throws for 396, and the Beavers Push the Broncos to the End

Final: Boise State Broncos (9-1) 46, Oregon State Beavers (5-5) 41, Sat., Nov. 14, Albertsons Stadium, Boise. Next: vs Utah State, Sat., Nov. 21, 10:30 PM ET, Reser Stadium, Corvallis

By Juniper Ostrowski, The Oregon State Fanatic

XFacebook

Beaver Nation, I'm proud of this team, and I'm heartbroken at the same time. Boise State 46, Oregon State 41. That was Saturday night at Albertsons Stadium. It leaves us at 5-5. We went into the loudest place in the league, with College GameDay in town, and traded punches with a 9-1 team for four quarters.

Maalik Murphy was phenomenal. He completed 31 of 39 throws for 396 yards and three touchdowns. Jesse Legree caught 12 balls for 140 yards and a 15-yard touchdown in the first quarter. Adonis McDaniel had a 40-yard catch. Eric Olsen caught a 20-yard touchdown with 4:25 left to make it 46-41, and Cynai Thomas grabbed a 12-yarder in the fourth quarter. Every time Boise State scored, we came right back.

Jake Reichle punched in two short touchdowns, from 1 and 5 yards. Cornell Hatcher Jr. ran in from 2 yards. We converted 6 of 9 on third down and finished with 506 yards. That's an offense that can play with anybody.

There were a few that got away. Late in the first quarter, Boise State returned an interception 58 yards for a touchdown and added a two-point conversion to go up 15-7. Caleb Ojeda missed a field goal earlier in that quarter. And Rasean Jones caught 11 balls for 230 yards against us. We just couldn't stop that guy.

Aiden Sullivan was a monster on defense, with 12 tackles and a sack. Jaheim Patterson and Gyriece Goodman each made seven tackles. Drake Vickers broke up two throws. After Olsen's touchdown, Boise State got the ball back and ran out the clock, and we never got another chance.

Five and five. This team is close to something good.

Bring the orange to Corvallis. Go Beavs! The Oregon State Beavers host Utah State at Reser Stadium, Nov. 21, 10:30 PM ET.
