UTEP vs Wyoming: Raymond Moore III Runs Wild, but the Miners Fall 27-15

Final: Wyoming Cowboys (7-3) 27, UTEP Miners (1-9) 15, Sat., Nov. 14, Sun Bowl, El Paso. Next: at Air Force, Fri., Nov. 20, 8:00 PM ET, Falcon Stadium, USAF Academy

By Ernesto 'Neto' Galindo, The UTEP Fanatic

XFacebook

Miner fans, I'm going to start with the good, because there was good. Raymond Moore III took off on an 80-yard touchdown run about seven minutes into Saturday night's game at the Sun Bowl, and for a minute, El Paso was flying. Then the rest of the night happened. Wyoming 27, UTEP 15. We're 1-9.

Moore was the best player on the field for us. He ran 20 times for 164 yards and completed 11 of 19 throws. When he got loose, the whole stadium came to its feet. He's a gamer, and he keeps getting up.

But the first half got away. Wyoming went 93 yards for a touchdown to take a 7-6 lead, and then a bad snap got past our punter, Brady Braun, at our 21. One play later, it was 14-6. They added a touchdown run and a field goal before the half, and it was 24-6 at the break. Braun, for the record, still averaged 53 yards a punt. The man has a leg.

The defense kept fighting, and I want them to hear it. Kode Lowe forced a fumble that Leander Parrish recovered early in the second quarter. Jayden Wilson knocked another one loose late in the third, and Justin Content fell on it. Shakaun Bowser forced a third one in the fourth, and Parrish scooped it up again and returned it 11 yards. Garrett Hawkins made 10 tackles. Wilson had nine.

Carlos Arreola nailed a 55-yard field goal at the end of the third quarter, and Elijah McCoy powered in from a yard out to make it 24-15 with 12:00 left. We missed the two-point try, missed a field goal later, and came up short on a fourth down. Close, but not enough.

Eight straight losses is hard to type. But this team keeps showing up, and so will I.

Next up: the Miners travel to Colorado Springs to face Air Force at Falcon Stadium, Friday, Nov. 20, 8 PM ET. Keep swinging, Miners.
