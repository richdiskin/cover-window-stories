NC State vs Syracuse: Wolfpack Let a 15-Point Lead Slip in the Home Finale

Final: Syracuse Orange (5-5) 34, NC State Wolfpack (4-6) 31, Sat., Nov. 14, Carter-Finley Stadium, Raleigh. Next: at Florida State, Sat., Nov. 21, 3:30 PM ET, Doak Campbell Stadium, Tallahassee

By Rutherford 'Ruff' Pegram, The NC State Fanatic

XFacebook

Pack, I don't even know where to start. Syracuse 34, NC State 31 on Saturday at Carter-Finley Stadium, our home finale, and we're 4-6. A day after the school told us Philip Rivers is coming home to coach next season, this was supposed to be a party. For three quarters, it was.

That third quarter was some of the most fun I've had in Raleigh all year. CJ Bailey hit Chance Robinson for a 12-yard score to start it. Davion Gause punched in a 2-yarder. Then Jayden Scott broke loose for a 32-yard touchdown run, and we were up 28-13 heading to the fourth. Scott finished with 127 yards on 14 carries, and every time he touched it, the student section stood up.

Bailey was good, too. He ran for a 9-yard touchdown in the second quarter and finished with 56 rushing yards to go with 211 through the air. Davion Dozier caught one ball all day, and it went for 69 yards. That's how you make a catch count.

King Mack and Jordan Moreta made 10 tackles each, and Brody Barnhardt had nine. Kanoah Vinesett kicked a 21-yard field goal with 6:55 left to make it 31-20, and I thought we were home free.

We weren't. On the next Syracuse drive, a throw got picked off and returned 18 yards for a touchdown. Then the Orange marched down, converted a fourth down off a ball that bounced their way, and scored with 48 seconds left, plus the two-point try. Our final try ended on a fumble at midfield. They had a receiver catch 17 balls, and our secondary is going to see Calvin Russell III in its sleep for a while.

Interim coach Todd Goebbel said a lot of people are hurting, and he's right. I'm one of them. But the seniors who played their last game in this building deserve our thanks, and this program has better days coming soon.

Wolfpack, let's go finish the season with our heads up. The NC State Wolfpack travel to face Florida State in Tallahassee, Nov. 21, 3:30 PM ET.
