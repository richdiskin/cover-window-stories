Middle Tennessee vs Liberty: Blue Raiders Push the Unbeaten Flames to the Wire

Final: Liberty Flames (9-1) 28, Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders (5-5) 23, Sat., Nov. 14, Williams Stadium (VA), Lynchburg. Next: at Sam Houston, Sat., Nov. 21, 2:00 PM ET, Elliott T. Bowers Stadium, Huntsville

By Opal Stonebriar, The Middle Tennessee Fanatic

XFacebook

Blue Raider fans, I'm frustrated, I'm proud and I'm hoarse. Liberty 28, Middle Tennessee 23. That was Saturday at Williams Stadium in Lynchburg. It leaves us at 5-5. Liberty came in unbeaten in the league and just clinched a spot in the title game, and we had them sweating with six minutes left.

Roman Gagliano was terrific. Twenty-four of 34 for 202 yards and two touchdowns, plus 47 yards on the ground. Cam'ron Lacy caught 10 balls for 97 yards and was open every time I looked up. Ziyon Butler made a 23-yard touchdown catch in the third quarter that cut it to 21-17. And Neo Clifton, my goodness, Neo Clifton. With 6:18 left, he reached out one hand around a defender and snagged a 14-yard touchdown. That catch belongs on a poster in Murfreesboro.

Dominic Bourgeois hit a 45-yard field goal, and Antonio Martin punched in an 8-yard run in the second quarter to give us a 10-7 lead. We were right in it the whole way.

The defense made some big plays. Korey Smith was everywhere, with 16 tackles. Reggie Johnson sacked the quarterback in the first quarter and knocked the ball loose, and Ashton Taylor recovered it. We stopped Liberty on fourth down twice and turned both of those stops into touchdowns.

But Liberty ran for 370 yards. You can't give up that much on the ground and win on the road. And after Clifton's catch, the two-point try failed and we never got the ball back with a real chance. Liberty held it for more than 36 minutes, and that was the game.

We're 5-5. One more and we're bowl eligible. That's in front of us, and this team plays hard every week.

Let's go get number six, Blue Raiders. The Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders travel to face Sam Houston in Huntsville, Nov. 21, 2 PM ET.
