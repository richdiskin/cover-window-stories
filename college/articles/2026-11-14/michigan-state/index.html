Michigan State vs Washington: Tied Late, Then a Tough Finish in East Lansing

Final: Washington Huskies (6-4) 21, Michigan State Spartans (3-7) 14, Sat., Nov. 14, Spartan Stadium, East Lansing. Next: vs Oregon, Fri., Nov. 20, 8:00 PM ET, Spartan Stadium, East Lansing

By Shay Kowalczyk, The Michigan State Fanatic

XFacebook

Spartan fans, this one hurts. Washington 21, Michigan State 14. That was Saturday night at Spartan Stadium. It leaves us at 3-7. It was tied 14-14 from late in the second quarter all the way to 2:24 left. We had our chances, and we just couldn't grab one.

The start was great. Carson Gulker ran it in from 1 yard in the first quarter, and the student section was bouncing. Washington tied it late in the first quarter on a 58-yard throw, then went ahead in the second. We answered before halftime when Cam Fancher found Bryson Williams for a 2-yard touchdown. 14-14 at the break, and the place was loud.

Brandon Tullis ran 21 times for 82 yards. Fredrick Moore caught four balls for 66 yards, and Williams caught four for 63. Alessio Milivojevic came in and went 7 of 12 for 108 yards. We converted all three of our fourth-down tries, too. We moved the ball, and we held it for more than 32 minutes.

But the second half slipped away a little at a time. We threw an interception in the third quarter. Liam Boyd missed a field goal in the fourth. Then Washington drove 78 yards and scored with 2:24 left. We got one last drive into their territory, and with 35 seconds left, the ball came loose after a catch, and Washington recovered. That was it.

Nikai Martinez made five tackles, and Aveon Grose, Michael Richard and Dion Crawford each had a piece of a tackle for loss. The defense held Washington to 44 rushing yards. That deserved better.

Three and seven in Pat Fitzgerald's first season is not what any of us wanted. A bowl is off the table now. But I'm not going anywhere, and neither are the people sitting next to me.

A Friday night at home. Let's make some noise, Spartans! The Michigan State Spartans host Oregon at Spartan Stadium, Friday, Nov. 20, 8 PM ET.
