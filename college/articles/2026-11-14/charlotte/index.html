Charlotte vs East Carolina: Aiden Bui Kicks Three, Niners Fall 17-16

Final: East Carolina Pirates (6-4) 17, Charlotte 49ers (1-9) 16, Sat., Nov. 14, Jerry Richardson Stadium, Charlotte. Next: at Tulsa, Sat., Nov. 21, 2:00 PM ET, H. A. Chapman Stadium, Tulsa

By Priya Halloran, The Charlotte Fanatic

XFacebook

Niner Nation, one point. One single point. East Carolina 17, Charlotte 16 on Saturday at Jerry Richardson Stadium, and we're 1-9. I've had a lot of tough Saturdays this fall, but this one is going to sit with me on the drive down Highway 29.

The first half was the best football we've played in weeks. Aiden Bui opened the scoring with a 50-yard field goal. Conner Harrell hit Jaden Barnes for an 11-yard touchdown in the second quarter. Then Bui hit from 27 with 1:09 left and from 37 on the final play of the half. Three for three, and we led 16-7. The student section was bouncing.

And the defense was taking the ball away. Jonathan Patterson picked off a throw in the first quarter and broke up a fourth-down try. Dy'Lon Womack intercepted one at our 11, then recovered a fumble at our 24 right before the half after Gavin Willis knocked it loose. Willis was a beast all afternoon with 13 tackles and two and a half for loss. Kadin Schmitz made 11 tackles, and Collin Gill had a sack.

Derrick Eley gave us some fireworks with four catches for 96 yards, including a 33-yarder. Harrell threw for 161.

But the second half just died on us. We had 22 yards of offense after halftime, and minus-17 on the ground. ECU scored on a direct-snap plunge in the third, kicked a 43-yarder in the fourth, and we never found a first down when it mattered.

Then came the cruelest moment. ECU missed a 31-yarder with 2:12 left, and we got the ball at our 20 with a chance to win. On the very next snap, a tipped throw was picked off. I just sat down and stared at the field.

I'm still coming back. The defense deserves a crowd, and Bui deserves every bit of applause we've got.

Keep swinging, Niners. The Charlotte 49ers travel to face Tulsa in Tulsa, Nov. 21, 2 PM ET.
