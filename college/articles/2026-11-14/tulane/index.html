Tulane vs Rice: A 2 a.m. Bus Ride and a Bredell Richardson Grab Win It

Final: Tulane Green Wave (5-5) 20, Rice Owls (1-9) 17, Sat., Nov. 14, First Community Stadium, Houston. Next: vs North Texas, Sat., Nov. 21, time TBA, Yulman Stadium, New Orleans

By Antoine 'Twan' Ducros-Labat, The Tulane Fanatic

XFacebook

Wave fans, what a weekend for this team. Tulane 20, Rice 17 on Saturday night at First Community Stadium in Houston, and we're 5-5. Friday's nationwide air traffic ground stop forced a change of plans, so the team rode a bus and rolled into Houston around 2 a.m. Saturday. Then they went out and won a football game. I'm so proud I could burst.

It wasn't pretty for a while. Rice led 17-6 at halftime after Jamir Benjamin intercepted Trace Johnson and ran it back 62 yards with 31 seconds left in the half. I sat in the stands thinking about that bus ride and wondering how much these guys had left.

Turns out they had plenty. DJ Dugar punched it in from 1 yard late in the third quarter to make it 17-13. Early in the fourth, J. Moore knocked the ball loose from Tyvonn Byars, and Dickson Agu recovered at the Rice 34. Three plays later, Johnson lofted one to the left pylon, and Bredell Richardson pulled it down for a 24-yard touchdown. 20-17 with about 10 minutes left. I screamed so loud the people around me turned and stared.

Then the defense had to hold, with a stiff crosswind blowing. Rice ground out a drive of more than seven minutes to our 20, and on fourth-and-1, Makai Williams stopped Gael Ochoa for no gain. Williams finished with 12 tackles. Agu had 10 and a fumble recovery. Anthony Rogers made two tackles for loss, and Jah'rie Garner had a sack. Rice got the ball back once more and never reached midfield.

Maurice Turner ran for 90 yards, including a 45-yard burst. Cooper Helmke made field goals from 35 and 41 yards. We converted 3 of 4 on fourth down too.

Five and five, with a long night's sleep coming. These guys earned it.

Roll Wave! The Tulane Green Wave host North Texas at Yulman Stadium, Nov. 21, time TBA.
