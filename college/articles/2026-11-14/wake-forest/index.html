Wake Forest vs SMU: 42-0 at the Half, Seven Straight, and a Message for the Committee

Final: Wake Forest Demon Deacons (9-1) 49, SMU Mustangs (7-3) 7, Sat., Nov. 14, Gerald J. Ford Stadium, Dallas. Next: at Georgia Tech, Sat., Nov. 21, time TBA, Bobby Dodd Stadium, Atlanta

By Eloise Brightman-Rowe, The Wake Forest Fanatic

XFacebook

Deacs, are they paying attention now? Wake Forest 49, SMU 7 on Saturday at Gerald J. Ford Stadium in Dallas. That's seven straight wins, we're 9-1 and 5-0 on the road, and the Mustangs had never lost an ACC game at home until we showed up. I'd roll the Quad tonight if I weren't stuck in Texas.

It was 42-0 at halftime. Let me say that again for the folks in the back: 42-0, at halftime, against a ranked team, in its building. Ty Clark III started it with a 27-yard touchdown run on third-and-18, and four of our first-half touchdowns came on third down. Jamar Searcy ripped off a 38-yard run on fourth-and-1 that set up another. Carlos Hernandez caught a 44-yard touchdown from Gio Lopez. We were converting everything.

Gio was terrific. He went 14 of 25 for 201 yards and a score and ran for two more touchdowns, both from 5 yards out. We finished 10 of 15 on third down and held the ball for almost 37 minutes. Steele Pizzella came in and added a 25-yard touchdown run in the fourth for good measure.

And the defense? Aiden Hall picked off Kevin Jennings in the first quarter. We stuffed SMU on fourth down at its own 26. D. Afalava and K. Dembele each recovered fumbles. SMU ran for 66 yards and converted 3 of 14 third downs. The Mustangs didn't score until late in the third quarter, and by then the Ford Stadium crowd was heading for the exits.

Jake Dickert said this week that eventually people are going to have to say we're good. Two days after signing his extension, his team made that case about as loudly as a team can. The only loss on our schedule is to Miami, and I would love a second crack at them in Charlotte.

Keep stacking them, Deacs. Go Deacs! The Wake Forest Demon Deacons travel to face Georgia Tech in Atlanta, Nov. 21, time TBA.
