James Madison vs UConn: Dukes Can't Slow Osborne in a 46-22 Loss

Final: UConn Huskies (5-5) 46, James Madison Dukes (8-3) 22, Sat., Nov. 14, Pratt & Whitney Stadium, East Hartford. Next: vs Coastal Carolina, Sat., Nov. 28, time TBA, Bridgeforth Stadium, Harrisonburg

By Lila Shenandoah, The James Madison Fanatic

XFacebook

Dukes, I'm not going to dress this up. UConn 46, James Madison 22 on Saturday at Pratt & Whitney Stadium in East Hartford, and we're 8-3. I drove up from Harrisonburg with my purple scarf and a lot of hope, and Connecticut sent me home with a long drive to think.

It started fine. After UConn's opening field goal, JC Evans punched in a 1-yard touchdown run, and we led 6-3. Then, on the very next play, the kickoff went 100 yards the wrong way. That's the kind of thing you can't give a team at home.

We came back, though. Late in the second quarter, Evans hit Braeden Wisloski for a 16-yard touchdown, and we were up 13-10. For about 30 seconds, all was right in the Valley. Then UConn hit a 67-yard touchdown three snaps later, kicked a field goal at the end of the half and never looked back.

Evans battled all day. He threw for 236 yards, ran for two scores and took some hits along the way. Wisloski had seven catches for 75 yards. Cole Keller and Corey Scott made some big grabs, and Morgan Suarez hit a 36-yard field goal in the fourth. On defense, Seth Gathers made 10 tackles and Nakian Jackson had nine with a sack.

But the penalties killed us. Thirteen of them for 130 yards. You can't play like that on the road against a quarterback who completed 16 of 20 throws. UConn put up 504 yards, and we didn't force a single turnover.

This one's going to sting through the open week. But eight wins is eight wins, and this program still has a lot to play for. The Dukes don't stay down long.

Two weeks to get right, Harrisonburg. Go Dukes! The James Madison Dukes host Coastal Carolina at Bridgeforth Stadium, Nov. 28, time TBA.
