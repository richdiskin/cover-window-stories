Fresno State vs Texas State: A 35-31 Lead Slips Away in San Marcos

Final: Texas State Bobcats (6-4) 37, Fresno State Bulldogs (5-5) 35, Sat., Nov. 14, UFCU Stadium, San Marcos. Next: vs Colorado State, Sat., Nov. 21, 7:30 PM ET, Valley Children's Stadium, Fresno

By Rosalind 'Roz' Ayala, The Fresno State Fanatic

XFacebook

Bulldog fans, I flew to San Marcos believing we were going to steal this one. For a long stretch in the fourth quarter, we had. Texas State 37, Fresno State 35 on Saturday at UFCU Stadium, and we're 5-5. We led 35-31 with 10:33 left, and we couldn't finish it.

The afternoon started with a jolt. After Texas State scored first, Rayshon Luke took the kickoff 100 yards to the house, and it was 7-7 just like that. I was still standing when he reached the end zone. Jayden Mandal then found Brock Lium for a 2-yard touchdown in the second quarter, and Bryson Donelson punched in scores from 1 yard and, later, from 2 yards to give us that 35-31 lead. Ryan Kobzeff's 19-yard run late in the third got us within 31-28. Mandal finished 20 of 34 for 250 yards.

Josiah Freeman caught six balls for 69 yards. The defense, led by Gerayas Grimes and his 14 tackles, hung in there too. Croix Stewart forced a fumble at our goal in the fourth quarter, and Grimes recovered it in the end zone for a touchback. K'vion Thunderbird had a sack. For a minute, it felt like we had this game in our hands.

Then it got away. Michael Patterson intercepted Mandal with 3:39 left, and Texas State drove 53 yards and scored with a minute to go. We got the ball back, but Mandal lost the football on a scramble, and Texas State recovered at our 4 with 48 seconds left. That was the end of it. We also missed a 51-yard field goal in the first quarter. Those are the snaps you want back.

Five and five. This one is going to sit with me all week.

Come home, Dogs. Let's answer this. The Fresno State Bulldogs host Colorado State at Valley Children's Stadium, Nov. 21, 7:30 PM ET.
