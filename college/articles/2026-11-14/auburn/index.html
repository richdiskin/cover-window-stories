Auburn vs Mississippi State: Four Recovered Fumbles, Six Wins, and a Loud Road Trip

Final: Auburn Tigers (6-4) 41, Mississippi State Bulldogs (4-6) 9, Sat., Nov. 14, Davis Wade Stadium, Starkville. Next: vs Samford, Sat., Nov. 21, 3:30 PM ET, Jordan-Hare Stadium, Auburn

By Lacey Bellamy, The Auburn Fanatic

XFacebook

Tiger fans, I made the drive to Starkville, and I am still hoarse. Auburn 41, Mississippi State 9 on Saturday at Davis Wade Stadium, and we're 6-4. That's six wins, and we can go to a bowl for the first time since 2023. I didn't care that it was a road game. I sang War Eagle anyway.

The defense set the table all afternoon. We recovered four fumbles, and those takeaways led to 24 points. Midway through the second quarter, Shadarius Toodle strip-sacked AJ Swann, Darrion Smith scooped it and ran to the 1, and Byrum Brown walked it in on the next snap. Nate Johnson then sacked Parker Puckett and forced another fumble that J. Smith recovered. R. Pleasant fell on one more. When we hit the quarterback, everything else opened up.

Brown completed 19 of 26 throws for 232 yards and three touchdowns. He found Keshaun Singleton for 26 yards in the first quarter and 42 yards in the third, and he hit Chas Nimrod for 35 yards in between. Singleton finished with seven catches for 103 yards. Jeremiah Cobb ran 10 yards for a score, and Alex McPherson made field goals of 37 and 31 yards. We had 371 yards and never trailed.

Mississippi State got a 34-yard touchdown throw to Sanfrisco Magee early in the second quarter and a safety in the fourth when we were tackled in our own end zone. That was it. Toodle finished with 10 tackles, a sack and two for loss. Da'Shawn Womack and Eric Winters each made eight stops.

Coach Alex Golesh said six wins means we get to keep practicing, and he also said the standard at Auburn is competing for championships. I agree with both. This was a big step. It is not the last one.

I'll be in my orange, yelling. War Eagle! The Auburn Tigers host Samford at Jordan-Hare Stadium, Nov. 21, 3:30 PM ET.
