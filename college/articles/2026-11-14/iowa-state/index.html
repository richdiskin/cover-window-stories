Iowa State vs Cincinnati: Aiden Flora Runs Wild Before Ames Finishes Brunch

Final: Iowa State Cyclones (5-5) 41, Cincinnati Bearcats (5-5) 13, Sat., Nov. 14, Jack Trice Stadium, Ames. Next: at UCF, Fri., Nov. 20, 6:00 PM ET, Acrisure Bounce House, Orlando

By Twyla Hemmingsen, The Iowa State Fanatic

XFacebook

Cyclone Nation, the 11 a.m. kickoff was worth every early alarm. Iowa State 41, Cincinnati 13 on Saturday at Jack Trice Stadium, and we're 5-5 and 5-1 at home. I barely had time to finish my breakfast casserole before Aiden Flora had three touchdowns.

Three touchdowns. In the first quarter. Flora ran it in from 11 yards, then broke a 77-yarder that had the whole east side on its feet, then added a 5-yarder before the first quarter was even done. He also caught a couple, threw one for 37 yards, and by the end of the day he had 216 rushing yards on 28 carries. The former walk-on from right here in our program has now gone for 200 three times this season. Somebody check that young man's legs for springs.

Jaylen Raynor ran the show beautifully. He hit Cameron Pettaway for a 70-yard touchdown on the first play of the second quarter, and it was 28-7 before most of Ames had found its seats. Raynor finished with 229 yards through the air. Jamal Polite Jr. and Vince Benetti each had big grabs, and Arnold Barnes III put a bow on it with a 32-yard touchdown run with 1:40 left.

The defense bent but didn't let Cincinnati back in. The Bearcats ran for 273 yards, and that number made me grumpy, but they went 2 of 10 on third down and 1 of 5 on fourth. Sullivan Schlimgen and Beau Goodwin had nine tackles each, Drew Surges made the stop on fourth down before halftime, and Z. Hawk scooped up a fumble in the fourth quarter. After their touchdown late in the second quarter, Cincinnati didn't score again.

Our offense piled up 551 yards, the fourth time this year we've cleared 500. Kyle Konrardy missed a couple of field goals, but he still hit from 22 and 24 and every extra point, so we'll forgive him and buy him a pork tenderloin.

Jimmy Rogers talked after the game about finding different ways to get Flora the ball. Mission accomplished, Coach.

Pack the sunscreen, Clones. Bowl eligibility is one win away. The Iowa State Cyclones travel to face UCF in Orlando, Friday, Nov. 20, 6 PM ET.
