Kansas vs West Virginia: Isaiah Marshall Runs Wild in Morgantown

Final: Kansas Jayhawks (4-6) 37, West Virginia Mountaineers (5-5) 14, Sat., Nov. 14, Milan Puskar Stadium, Morgantown. Next: vs BYU, Sat., Nov. 21, time TBA, David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium, Lawrence

By Fitz Calloway, The Kansas Fanatic

XFacebook

Rock Chalk, Jayhawk! Kansas 37, West Virginia 14 on Saturday at Milan Puskar Stadium in Morgantown, and we're 4-6 with a pulse. I sang the alma mater in the visitors' section so loud a Mountaineer fan offered me a cough drop.

Isaiah Marshall, ladies and gentlemen. The sophomore ran 19 times for a career-high 164 yards, including a 50-yard burst, and five of his runs went for 10 or more. He completed his first nine throws and finished 15 of 18 for 152 yards. He even shook off a first-half knock and kept going. He punched in a 2-yarder with 45 seconds left in the half that made it 30-7, the biggest halftime hole West Virginia has been in all year.

And the guy who got it all started? Trey Lathan, a former Mountaineer, picked off a throw on West Virginia's second snap of the game. Leroy Harris III got his hand on it first. Talk about a homecoming. We kicked a 24-yard field goal, then Khijohnn Cummings-Coleman scored from 5 and from 26, and we were rolling. Chase Belcher added two more touchdown runs, from 2 and 8 yards.

We ran for 296 yards as a team and held the ball for nearly 37 minutes. That's Kansas football I want framed on my wall.

The defense was terrific too. West Virginia ran for 35 yards. Corey Gordon made 11 tackles, 10 of them solo. Harris had a sack and two tackles for loss, and Blake Herold got another sack. Q. Davis stuffed a fourth-down run early in the fourth quarter, and J. Conde broke up the Mountaineers' last fourth-down try in the final minute.

We still have hope for a bowl. Faint, sure, but hope. And after a day like this, I'll ride that hope all the way home on I-70.

Wave the wheat, Lawrence. The Kansas Jayhawks host BYU at David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium, Nov. 21, time TBA.
