Buffalo vs Ball State: Messiah Burch Runs for 209 and the Bulls Go to 7-3

Final: Buffalo Bulls (7-3) 28, Ball State Cardinals (3-7) 7, Wed., Nov. 11, Scheumann Stadium, Muncie. Next: vs Central Michigan, Wed., Nov. 18, 7:30 PM ET, Broadview Stadium, Buffalo

By Kendra Woolcott, The Buffalo Fanatic

XFacebook

Pack a lunch, Muncie. Buffalo 28, Ball State 7 on Wednesday night at Scheumann Stadium, and the Bulls are 7-3 and 5-1 in the MAC. We ran the ball 52 times for 302 yards. I counted every carry from the visitors' side, and my mittens are done for the season.

The night opened crooked. Ball State blocked one of our punts late in the first quarter, and Gabe Aramboles fell on it in the end zone. 7-0 Cardinals. Fine. We do our best work from behind.

Jason Wright answered with an 11-play, 80-yard drive in the second quarter and finished it with a 14-yard throw to Chance Morrow. 7-7. Wright completed five throws all night, and he didn't need more. This team knows exactly who it is.

Who it is, mostly, is Messiah Burch. He carried 31 times for 209 yards and two scores. His 2-yard run capped a 12-play, 79-yard drive in the third for a 14-7 lead. Then, with 4:04 left, he found a crease up the middle and went 67 yards. 21-7.

And then the coaches got bold. Oliver Hautanen popped an onside kick, we came up with it at the Ball State 29, and Terrance Shelton Jr. went 29 yards on the very next snap. Two touchdowns in six seconds of game clock. 28-7. Shelton finished with 92 yards on 10 carries.

The defense deserves its own parade down Main Street. Ball State came away empty on fourth down three times, twice inside our 25 in the fourth quarter, and three Cardinals field goal tries, from 47, 44 and 39 yards, did not go through. SaVeon Brown and Zach Bergmann had nine tackles each, Mikey Crawford had eight, and Miles Greer chipped in a tackle for loss. Zero turnovers for us, too.

Next up is Central Michigan at Broadview Stadium on Wednesday, Nov. 18, at 7:30 PM ET. Bring the hand warmers. We're bringing the run game.
