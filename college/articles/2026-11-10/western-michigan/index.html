Western Michigan vs Akron: 360 Rushing Yards and a 42-14 Night in the Rubber City

Final: Western Michigan Broncos (7-3) 42, Akron Zips (1-9) 14, Tue., Nov. 10, InfoCision Stadium, Akron. Next: vs Eastern Michigan, Tue., Nov. 17, 7:00 PM ET, Waldo Stadium, Kalamazoo

By Corwin Dahlquist, The Western Michigan Fanatic

XFacebook

I brought film to Akron, and the film was right. Western Michigan 42, Akron 14, Tuesday night at InfoCision Stadium, and the Broncos are 7-3 and 5-1 in the MAC. Write that down in pen.

Begin with the clock. We held the ball for 42:46. The Zips had it for 17:14. We converted 13 of 17 third downs and ran it 56 times for 360 yards. Every guy up front gets a game ball, and I'd like the record to show I diagrammed this exact plan in the parking lot.

The first drive scared me. Broc Lowry took a fourth-down sack and a hard hit, and Mason Reynolds came in. Reynolds completed his first career throw, per WMU Athletics, ran 35 yards down to the Akron 4, and then found Nate Levicki in the flat for a 3-yard score. Cool as Kalamazoo in November.

Then Cole Cabana did it again. For the second straight game, WMU Athletics says, he went 82 yards for a touchdown, and this one finished a 98-yard drive in four plays. He ended with 107 yards on nine carries, his second straight 100-yard game.

After Akron answered, we put together the drive that broke them: 17 plays, 75 yards, 9:19 off the clock, finished by Jalen Buckley from a yard out for a 21-7 halftime lead. Buckley added a 35-yarder in the third on fourth-and-1, bouncing it outside and down the right sideline. That's 121 yards and two scores for him.

Lowry came back for the second half and didn't miss. Not once. He went 15 for 15 for 149 yards, and his 20-yard touchdown to Keivin Boone-Nelson II was the first touchdown catch of Boone-Nelson's career. Reynolds finished 7 of 8 with two scores, both to Levicki. Baylin Brooks caught a career-high 8 balls for 78 yards.

My one gripe: we went 1 for 4 on fourth down, and the Zips hit two long ones to Marcel Williams. Fix the coverage, fellas. Two throws were their whole night.

Next Tuesday, Eastern Michigan comes to Waldo Stadium, and the AP says a win gives us our third Michigan MAC Trophy in four years. Kickoff is 7:00 PM ET. Bring the blocking diagrams. I'll bring extras.
