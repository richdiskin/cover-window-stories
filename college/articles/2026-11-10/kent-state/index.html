Kent State vs Bowling Green: A Fourth-Quarter Comeback That Lasted Nine Seconds

Final: Kent State Golden Flashes (3-7) 28, Bowling Green Falcons (3-7) 33, Tue., Nov. 10, Doyt L. Perry Stadium, Bowling Green. Next: vs Miami (OH), Tue., Nov. 17, 7:00 PM ET, Zoeller Field at Dix Stadium, Kent

By Ozzie Brannigan, The Kent State Fanatic

XFacebook

I expected the worst. I got a more creative version. Kent State came back from 13 points down in the fourth quarter at Doyt L. Perry Stadium, took a 28-27 lead with 2:00 left, and lost 33-28 anyway. The lead lasted nine seconds. We're 3-7, 2-4 in the MAC, and the Anniversary Award rides back to Bowling Green.

Let me be fair, because Dru DeShields earned it. He threw for 332 yards and two touchdowns and ran for two more, including the 25-yard run that put us ahead. I was on my feet. I don't stand up for much.

Wayne Harris was the best player on the field. Eight catches, 143 yards, a 39-yard score in the second quarter for a 14-7 lead and a 13-yarder in the fourth to begin the comeback. Da'Realyst Clark added 107 yards on five catches. Those two can play with anybody in this conference.

Here's where my mood went. Early in the fourth we drove 67 yards, and DeShields was intercepted by Josira Andrews at the Bowling Green 2. Points, gone. Then there's Myles Bradley, who ESPN credits with five sacks against us. Five. We converted 5 of 17 third downs. You can't live like that.

After our go-ahead score, Bowling Green needed two plays: a 16-yard catch by Joseph Saffold and a 48-yard run by Nakai Amachree. We stopped the two-point try, which I mention only because I like to be accurate. Then DeShields got us to the Bowling Green 35, took a sack, and threw it into the end zone, where Nick Sawyer caught it. Touchback. That's the season in one sentence.

Some good news, since I'm required to look for it. Jaihien Roy had a sack and 6 tackles, Darian Blachewicz had a sack and a half, and Hayden Dawahare made 9 tackles. The defense held Bowling Green to 118 rushing yards and 6 of 14 on third down.

Next Tuesday, Nov. 17, Miami (OH) comes to Dix Stadium for a 7:00 PM ET kickoff. We haven't won on the road this year, so a home game is welcome news. I expect rain. I expect it every week.
