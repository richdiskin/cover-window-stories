Miami (OH) vs Kent State: 58 Carries, 301 Yards and a 20-3 Win for the RedHawks

Final: Miami (OH) RedHawks (7-4) 20, Kent State Golden Flashes (3-8) 3, Tue., Nov. 17, Zoeller Field at Dix Stadium, Kent. Next: at Western Michigan, Tue., Nov. 24, 7:00 PM ET, Waldo Stadium, Kalamazoo

By Cordelia Van Doren, The Miami (OH) Fanatic

XFacebook

Miami (OH) 20, Kent State 3, and I'd like everyone in Oxford to write down the date. Freshman Drew Bellisari ran the show from the opening snap for the first time Tuesday night at Dix Stadium, the RedHawks are 7-4 and 5-2 in the MAC, and Chuck Martin now has a winning record at Miami: seventy-nine wins, seventy-eight losses. The Cradle of Coaches has a new page to add.

We ran the ball 58 times for 301 yards. Fifty-eight! Our athletics office says the last time Miami ran that many times in a game was Nov. 8, 1997, with Hall of Famer Travis Prentice in the backfield. I'm adding a footnote to my binder tonight.

D'Shawntae Jones ran like a bulldozer, with a career-high 29 carries for 121 yards and two 1-yard touchdowns. Rodney Nelson set up the first one with a 47-yard catch on a shovel throw, and Jones finished the seven-play march for 7-0. After Kent kicked a field goal, Jones scored again on a fourth-down direct snap with 22 seconds left in the first quarter. The extra point missed, so it was 13-3. Nelson added 90 yards on 16 carries.

Bellisari kept it simple and smart. He went 7 of 12 for 124 yards, and in the third quarter Scoop Smith set him up with an 11-yard catch and a 25-yard run. Four plays later, Bellisari kept it himself and ran 23 yards up the middle for a touchdown. 20-3.

Now let's talk about the defense. Kent State gained 170 yards, the fewest by any Miami opponent this season, and just 31 on the ground. Amariyun Knighten intercepted Dru DeShields at our 11 to end their best chance, Malcolm McCain made nine tackles, and Grant Lyons, Devin Bell and OJ Alexander each had a sack. The Flashes went the final 51 minutes without a point.

We did leave a few chances behind, with a missed 30-yard field goal and a fourth-down stop at their 2, so I'll keep my applause polite. We held the ball for 38:36. I'll take it.

Next up: at Western Michigan, Waldo Stadium, Tuesday, Nov. 24, at 7:00 PM ET. Love and Honor, RedHawks.
