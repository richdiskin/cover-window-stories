Ohio vs Ball State: Dorwart's Late Catch Makes It 18 Straight at Peden, 35-28

Final: Ohio Bobcats (8-3) 35, Ball State Cardinals (3-8) 28, Tue., Nov. 17, Peden Stadium, Athens. Next: vs Toledo, Fri., Nov. 27, 12:00 PM ET, Peden Stadium, Athens

By Penny Trautman, The Ohio Fanatic

XFacebook

Ohio 35, Ball State 28, and the black-shirted crowd at Peden Stadium got its money's worth Tuesday night. It was Wear Black night in Athens, and the Bobcats spent three quarters in the dark before Darren James-Hamilton and Dom Dorwart turned the lights back on. We're 8-3, 6-1 in the MAC, and that's 18 straight wins at home, still the longest home streak in the country.

It began the right way. Duncan Brune plunged in from the 1 on our first drive, 7-0. Then Ball State got rolling: a field goal, a 10-yard throw to Donovan Hamilton, and, right after Nick Poulos got intercepted in their end zone, an 80-yard catch-and-run by Eli Hohlt. 17-7. Poulos ran in from 8 to make it 17-14, and the Cardinals kicked a 43-yarder at the horn. 20-14 at the half, and my black boots were nervous.

The third quarter was a grind. DJ Walker forced a fumble and recovered it on Ball State's first drive, and we built a 13-play drive that ate 9:23 of clock before a sack pushed us back and we punted. Nobody scored. I walked a lap around my seat.

Then James-Hamilton happened. Early in the fourth, he intercepted Keldric Luster and went 34 yards the other way. 21-20, and Peden shook. After a Ball State three-and-out, Poulos capped an eight-play drive by running in from 12 yards, 28-20 with 7:15 left.

Ball State tied it with 2:23 to go on a TJ Horton touchdown and a two-point try to Hamilton. 28-28. So Poulos went to work. Riley Neer caught a 14-yarder, and on the next play Poulos threw deep to Dorwart, who came down with it for a 29-yard touchdown with 1:16 left. The review upheld it. 35-28. Ty Neubert and Evan Herrmann shared a late sack, and James-Hamilton knocked away the final heave into the end zone.

Poulos went 9 of 13 for 155 yards, ran for 58 more and had a hand in three touchdowns. Neer caught five for 92. Michael Molnar made 12 tackles, Walker made 11, and our defense allowed just 122 yards after halftime.

Next up: Toledo comes to Peden Stadium on Black Friday, Nov. 27, at 12:00 PM ET. Keep the black shirts handy, Bobcat fans. Nineteen has a nice ring to it.
