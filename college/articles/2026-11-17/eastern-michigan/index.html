Eastern Michigan vs Western Michigan: Blue's 160 Yards Fuel a Rally, 34-27

Final: Western Michigan Broncos (8-3) 34, Eastern Michigan Eagles (4-7) 27, Tue., Nov. 17, Waldo Stadium, Kalamazoo. Next: vs Kent State, Tue., Nov. 24, 7:00 PM ET, Rynearson Stadium, Ypsilanti

By Imogen Tremblay, The Eastern Michigan Fanatic

XFacebook

Western Michigan 34, Eastern Michigan 27, and if you left Waldo Stadium at halftime Tuesday night, I feel bad for you. The Eagles trailed 31-7 at the break in Kalamazoo, clawed back to one score, and had the ball at the Broncos' 22 in the final minute. We're 4-7, 3-4 in the MAC, and I'm worn out in the proudest way.

We came in after a 13-day break with the Michigan MAC Trophy already staying in Kalamazoo, so this one was for pride, and the first half didn't give us much. Jalen Buckley ran 72 yards on their first snap, then capped a 93-yard drive, and it was 14-0. Our bright spot came off a Daniel Hull punt, when their returner muffed it and Donmiel Rogers fell on it. On the very next play, Amareon Blue ran it in from 7 yards. 14-7.

We stopped them on fourth down at our 3 early in the second quarter, and then it got away. A field goal, a 2-yard Cole Cabana touchdown with 40 seconds left, and a 50-yard interception return by Dillon Moore with two seconds on the clock. 31-7.

Then Brogan McCaughey came in at quarterback, and the Eagles woke up. We scored on our first three drives of the second half. Blue broke a 41-yard touchdown run, and the two-point try didn't make it, 31-13. McCaughey hit Joshua Long from 1 yard out to finish an 81-yard drive, 31-20. After their field goal, Blue scored from the 1 with 7:36 left. 34-27. McCaughey went 12 of 16 for 135 yards.

Blue was the whole show. He's listed at 5-foot-6, and he ran for a career-high 160 yards on 27 carries with three touchdowns, the first 100-yard game of his career. He caught six balls, too. Nick Devereaux added four catches for 62 yards.

Our defense forced two straight punts and gave us one last chance from our 23 with 1:51 to go. Blue ran for 10, Long made a 21-yard grab, a face mask flag moved us to their 25, and we reached the 22. On fourth down, McCaughey got sacked. I sat down on a cold bleacher and stayed there a while. Eleven flags for 125 yards didn't help us, either.

One game left, and it's at home. Next up: Kent State at Rynearson Stadium, Tuesday, Nov. 24, at 7:00 PM ET. Bring the green, Ypsi. Go Eagles!
