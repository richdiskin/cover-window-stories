Jacksonville State vs New Mexico State: Gamecocks Blank Aggies 24-0

Final: Jacksonville State (6-2) 24, New Mexico State (3-5) 0, Wed., Oct. 28, Aggie Memorial Stadium, Las Cruces. Next: vs. Sam Houston, Sat., Nov. 7, 7:00 PM ET, AmFirst Stadium, Jacksonville

ShareXFacebook

Jacksonville State 24, New Mexico State 0, and somebody back on the square in Jacksonville, Alabama, ought to ring a bell. The Gamecocks flew all the way to Las Cruces, out where the Organ Mountains rise above the desert, on the Wednesday before Halloween, and the only thing that got spooked was the home scoreboard. A road shutout. I am framing this one.

Caden Creel was the whole show. He ran 21 times for 211 yards, including an 84-yard burst, and his 3-yard touchdown run early in the second quarter put up the only points of the first half. He threw for 109 more, and his 29-yard touchdown strike to Luke Gilbert with 4:48 left made it 24-0. Gilbert caught five balls for 67 yards.

As a team, we ran for 324 yards at 7.0 a carry and rolled up 433 yards of offense. Garrison Rippa hit a 49-yard field goal in the third quarter, and Jalen Likely punched in a 1-yard touchdown late in the third to make it 17-0. We held the ball for 35:18, which is a polite way of saying the Aggies spent most of the night watching.

Now let me brag on the defense, because a zero on the road in Conference USA does not happen by accident. New Mexico State completed six of 19 throws for 29 yards. Caleb Nix made nine tackles, eight of them on his own. Brayden Matthews got home for a sack, Marc Woods came up with an interception, and Jonavan Carr broke up two throws.

In fairness to our hosts, James Jones ran hard for them, 112 yards on 20 carries, and Creel threw two interceptions, so we left points in the desert. The Aggies also missed two field goal tries, and I will not be sending a sympathy card about that.

Sam Houston Is Next

That is five straight wins, and the Gamecocks are 6-2 overall and 4-0 in Conference USA. After an open week, we come home to AmFirst Stadium in Jacksonville to face Sam Houston on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:00 p.m. ET. Happy Halloween from Gamecock country.
