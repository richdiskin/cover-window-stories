Kennesaw State vs Middle Tennessee: Owls Snap Skid With 44-28 Win

Final: Kennesaw State (2-6) 44, Middle Tennessee (4-4) 28, Wed., Oct. 28, Johnny "Red" Floyd Stadium, Murfreesboro. Next: vs. Delaware, Sat., Nov. 7, 3:00 PM ET, Walens Family Field at Fifth Third Stadium, Kennesaw

Kennesaw State 44, Middle Tennessee 28, and the Owls finally have a reason to hoot again. Back home, the General sits in the Southern Museum, the old locomotive taken from Big Shanty, the original name of our town, to kick off the Great Locomotive Chase. On Wednesday night in Murfreesboro, our Owls went on a chase of their own and ran down a six-game losing streak.

Rickie Collins was close to perfect. He completed 16 of 19 throws for 206 yards and three touchdowns. Dylan Gary caught only two balls, and both went for scores, from 31 and 28 yards out. Davis Bryson grabbed five for 46 yards, including a 23-yard touchdown, and he added 71 yards on kickoff returns.

Boogie Woods III opened the scoring with a 12-yard touchdown run and closed our touchdown parade with a 2-yard plunge with 5:15 left that made it 41-20. He ran 12 times for 67 yards, and he even caught the two-point conversion after the play that flipped this whole night.

That play belongs to Tywon Christopher. Early in the third quarter, with us clinging to a 19-17 lead, he jumped in front of a Roman Gagliano throw and took it 59 yards to the house. That made it 27-17, and the Blue Raiders never got closer than seven again. Isaac Paul added an interception of his own and brought it back 41 yards. Ugonna Nwoha had two sacks, and Tay'vion Lawson and Baron Hopson made nine tackles apiece.

Now, my mama raised me to tell the truth. Middle Tennessee held the ball for 40:19 and went 11 of 20 on third down. Antonio Martin ran for 106 yards. We trailed 14-7 in the second quarter, got flagged nine times for 72 yards, missed an extra point and gave up a 100-yard kickoff return late. Every one of those made me grab my hat.

Daniel Kinney bounced back from that missed kick with a 44-yard field goal with 1:55 left to make it 44-20, and Brayden Munroe ripped off 61 yards on two punt returns. When the Owls make more big plays than mistakes, the Owls win.

Delaware Comes to Kennesaw

We are 2-6 and 1-3 in Conference USA, and that first league win tastes as sweet as Sunday pie after church. After an open week, the Owls come home to face Delaware on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:00 p.m. ET at Walens Family Field at Fifth Third Stadium. Hoot hoot, y'all.
