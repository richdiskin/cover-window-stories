UCLA vs Illinois: Scooter Jackson, Aidan Mizell and a Rain-Soaked 10-0

Final: UCLA Bruins (10-0) 22, Illinois Fighting Illini (6-4) 14, Fri., Nov. 13, Rose Bowl, Pasadena. Next: at Michigan, Sat., Nov. 21, time TBA, Michigan Stadium, Ann Arbor

By Kensington 'Kenzie' Rhee, The UCLA Fanatic

XFacebook

Ten and oh. Say it slowly, Westwood. UCLA 22, Illinois 14 on Friday night at the Rose Bowl, and the Bruins are 10-0 for the third time in program history. It rained in Pasadena, which basically never happens, and I stood in the Arroyo with a soggy blue poncho and the biggest grin in Southern California.

Let's start with Aidan Mizell, because the man refuses to be slowed by anything, rain included. Six catches, 176 yards. On our opening drive he hauled in a 54-yard touchdown from Ty Dieffenbach, and the whole stadium knew right away this was going to be his night.

Then came the scare. Dieffenbach went down on that elbow late in the second quarter and headed to the locker room, and I swear you could hear 45,800 people hold their breath at once. Madden Iamaleava kept the ship upright for three series. When Ty jogged back onto the field with 55 seconds left in the third, the noise was something I will tell my grandkids about. His first drive back went 75 yards in eight plays and ended with Semaj Morgan catching a 26-yarder in the end zone. 19-7, Bruins. Dieffenbach finished 13 of 20 for 232 yards and two scores. Warrior stuff.

But the best part of this whole wet night was the defense. Scooter Jackson picked off two throws, including the one with 2:50 left that he took back 41 yards to the Illinois 19. Derrick Barksdale grabbed another. Aiden Gobaira sacked Katin Houser in the end zone for a safety in the third quarter, and Sammy Omosigho led us with seven tackles. Illinois managed 240 yards and 64 on the ground. Seven straight opponents held to 20 points or fewer. Pasadena, that's a wall.

Mateen Bhaghani was perfect, too: a 20-yarder before halftime and a 37-yarder with 1:56 left that made it a two-score game. Curtis Gerrand dropped three punts inside the 20. Special teams win wet games.

Illinois never quit, I'll give the Illini that. They cut it to 19-14 with 7:14 to go, and I was not breathing again for a while. But this group just keeps finding a way.

Big House, big stage, 10-0 Bruins. Pack a sweater, Westwood. We're going to need it. The UCLA Bruins travel to face Michigan in Ann Arbor, Nov. 21, time TBA.
