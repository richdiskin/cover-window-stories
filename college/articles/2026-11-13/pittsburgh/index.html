Pittsburgh vs Florida State: Panthers Defense Battles, Falls 16-10 at Home

Final: Florida State Seminoles (5-5) 16, Pittsburgh Panthers (6-4) 10, Fri., Nov. 13, Acrisure Stadium, Pittsburgh. Next: at Louisville, Sat., Nov. 21, time TBA, L&N Federal Credit Union Stadium, Louisville

By Dominic 'Yinz' Bertolucci, The Pittsburgh Fanatic

XFacebook

Yinz, I'm not gonna sugarcoat it. Florida State 16, Pittsburgh 10 on Friday night at Acrisure Stadium, and the Panthers are 6-4. There were 45,734 of us bundled up on the North Shore, and we deserved a happier walk home across the river.

The defense showed up, I'll give 'em that. Florida State managed 260 yards, ran for just 97 on 33 carries and converted 3 of 13 third downs. Cruce Brookins was everywhere with 10 tackles. Braylan Lovelace forced a fumble and had a tackle and a half for loss. Early in the fourth, Alex Sanford Jr. knocked the ball loose from Ashton Daniels and Shawn Lee Jr. fell on it at our 6 to keep it a 13-10 game.

Our lone touchdown was a beauty. Corey Dailey moved us 85 yards in 10 plays late in the second quarter, hit Malik Knight for 28 deep down the left side and found Ja'Kyrian Turner for an 11-yard score to put us up 7-6 at the half. Turner finished with seven catches.

Then came the play that stung. On our second snap of the third quarter, a throw was intercepted at our 39, and five plays later Florida State was in the end zone. Antonio Chadha's 34-yard field goal pulled us to 13-10, but their kicker answered from 47 with 6:27 left.

We had our chances. Three times we went for it on fourth down and came up short, including a sack near midfield with 8:44 left. After a 64-yard punt pinned us at our own 2 with 1:49 to go, Dailey took off for 24 yards on the first snap and got us to their 48, but the last fourth-down throw fell incomplete with 43 seconds left. Dailey ran for 58 yards on nine carries and battled every snap.

Pittsburgh doesn't do quit. We're still 6-4, still 5-2 at home, and cold November football is our kind of weather. Three rivers, one city, and a whole lot of fight left in this bunch.

Next: at Louisville, Saturday, Nov. 21, kickoff time to be announced. Wear the blue and gold, make some noise on the road, and let's bring one home. Hail to Pitt.
