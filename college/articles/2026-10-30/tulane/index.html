Tulane vs Charlotte: Green Wave Rally to Win 38-31 in Overtime

Final: Tulane (4-4) 38, Charlotte (1-7) 31 (OT), Fri., Oct. 30, Jerry Richardson Stadium, Charlotte. Next: vs. Tulsa (Homecoming), Sat., Nov. 7, 12:00 PM ET, Yulman Stadium, New Orleans

XFacebook

Tulane 38, Charlotte 31, in overtime. Back Uptown on Friday night, the Lavin-Bernick Center set up a free Halloween-eve horror movie night in the Rat. Fine idea, but the scariest show of the night was on ESPN2, live from Jerry Richardson Stadium, and this one had a happy ending for the Green Wave.

Let me be honest about the first three acts, because we have watched enough film to know a rough plot when we see one. Charlotte led 24-14 at halftime. Conner Harrell threw two touchdowns before the break, Henry Rutledge ran 22 times for 121 yards, and the 49ers held the ball for 33:47 without a single turnover. When Derrick Eley punched in a 1-yard run with 10:52 left, it was 31-21, and I was ready to hide behind the couch.

Then Trace Johnson grew up in about 11 minutes. He finished 28 of 36 for 284 yards, and he was surgical when it mattered. Johnnie Daniels ran 13 times for 99 yards and two scores, and Cooper Helmke's 27-yard field goal made it a one-score game. Up 31-24 with 1:52 left, Charlotte went for it on fourth down at our 42. Garrett Mmahat knocked the ball loose, it squirted out of bounds short of the sticks, and we had it back.

What came next goes in the scrapbook. Daniels ripped off 33 yards up the middle on third down, Johnson ran for a first down at the 11, and with 10 seconds left he found Cameron Roberts in the end zone. Helmke's kick tied it at 31-31.

In overtime, Destyn Hill caught two throws to set it up, and Dagan Bruno plowed in from a yard out. Then Charlotte got first-and-goal at our 4, and the defense stood up four straight times. TJ Smith broke up one throw to Jaden Barnes, and E'zaiah Shine knocked away the last one. Game over.

TJ Smith also returned a kickoff 95 yards for a touchdown in the second quarter, the kind of play kids will dress up as next Halloween. Dickson Agu made 14 tackles, and Kevin Adams III added 10.

We are 4-4 and 2-2 in the American, and after last week's loss to UTSA at Yulman, this bounce-back felt like a second wind off the river. Homecoming is next, with Tulsa coming to Yulman Stadium on Nov. 7. Roll Wave.
