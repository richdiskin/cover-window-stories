Sacramento State vs Kent State: Hornets Grab First MAC Win 31-10

Final: Sacramento State (2-7) 31, Kent State (3-6) 10, Fri., Oct. 30, Hornet Stadium, Sacramento. Next: vs. Toledo, Wed., Nov. 4, Hornet Stadium, Sacramento

Sacramento State 31, Kent State 10. Friday was Healthcare Appreciation and AANHPI Heritage Night at Hornet Stadium, and the postgame fireworks came back. This time they went up over the first Mid-American Conference win in program history, in front of 9,931 fans. Plate it up, chef. This one goes in the cookbook.

Kent State hit us early. Dru DeShields escaped trouble in his own end zone and found Wayne Harris for 92 yards, and the Golden Flashes led 7-0 in the first quarter. Grant Meadors kept us close with field goals from 42 and 28 yards, but Kent State kicked one at the horn, and we went in down 10-6. Back to the kitchen? Not this time.

The second half was 25-0, all Hornets, and the defense ran the kitchen. Five takeaways, our most since five at Incarnate Word on Sept. 7, 2017. Fata Puloka forced two fumbles and Xavier Williams forced another on a sack. Miles Bailey recovered two, and Boogsie Silvera recovered one to go with a game-high 10 tackles. On third-and-5 in the fourth quarter, Donovan Turner jumped a throw and ran it back 38 yards for a touchdown, his first trip to the end zone since his senior year at Long Beach Poly in 2021. Malikai Nichols added an interception in the end zone in the final two minutes.

Isaiah Ene carried the main course. He ran 19 times for a career-high 107 yards and two touchdowns. His 35-yard run late in the third put us ahead to stay, and he ran in the two-point try himself to make it 17-10. His 5-yard run with 2:55 left closed it out. Meadors went 3 of 3 on field goals, adding a 36-yarder, and Hamish Adams averaged 45.3 yards on six punts. We ran for 161 yards on 42 carries, and Jackson Sharman went 14 of 25 for 117 yards with no interceptions.

Kitchen cleanup: 10 penalties for 89 yards, and Kent State outgained us 302 to 278. We will sharpen the knives on that. But this win tastes like the first plate off a brand-new menu.

We're 2-7 and 1-5 in the MAC. Toledo comes to Hornet Stadium on Wednesday, Nov. 4, for Senior Night. Fire up the grill. Go Hornets!
