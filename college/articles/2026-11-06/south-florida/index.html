South Florida vs East Carolina: One Kick From Greenville Stings 17-20

Final: South Florida Bulls (6-3) 17, East Carolina Pirates (5-4) 20, Fri., Nov. 6, Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium, Greenville. Next: vs Memphis, Thu., Nov. 12, 8:00 PM ET, Raymond James Stadium, Tampa

By Reina Valdés-Kowalski, The South Florida Fanatic

XFacebook

South Florida Bulls 17, East Carolina Pirates 20. Final score 20-17. A 55-yard kick at the gun in Greenville, and we walk out of Dowdy-Ficklen with a gut punch. Six and three. Two and three in the American. We had this game tied with under 10 minutes left and still flew home empty-handed after a long Friday night.

Luke Kromenhoek did his job in place of the usual starter. Seventeen of 21 for 179 yards and a touchdown, the 26-yarder to Bryson Rodgers that opened the scoring at 7-0. D.J. Crowther ran hard for 111 yards on 17 carries and added three catches for 42. Jason Collins Jr. punched in from a yard out to tie it 17-17 in the fourth. Nico Gramatica was good from 30. We put up 329 yards and never threw an interception. Zero turnovers. On paper that should travel and win you a road game in November.

Here is what killed us. One of 10 on third down. Seven punts. Emory Williams found Landon Sides twice for scores, and Sides finished with 14 catches for 126. Ashton Gray ran for 101. We led 10-3 late in the half, gave up a 15-yard Sides score before the break, then watched an 8-yarder in the third put them ahead 17-10. Grayson Howard led us with 13 tackles and half a sack. The defense kept answering until it could not answer a 55-yarder from Andrew Conrad.

Conrad's kick with two seconds left is going to live rent-free for a while. We had the ball game knotted and the clock on our side of the argument, then one swing of a leg flipped the airport mood. That is a road loss in November, not a fluke of the schedule, and it drops us in the American race.

Memphis comes to Raymond James Stadium on Thursday, Nov. 12, at 8 PM ET. Home night under the lights. Fix third down, finish drives, and make Tampa loud again. Bulls up.
