Nevada vs New Mexico: Blanked After Halftime in a 3-31 Home Loss

Final: New Mexico Lobos (8-1) 31, Nevada Wolf Pack (3-6) 3, Fri., Nov. 6, Mackay Stadium, Reno. Next: at Northern Illinois, Sat., Nov. 14, 3:30 PM ET, Huskie Stadium, DeKalb

By Ridge Corbell, The Nevada Fanatic

XFacebook

Nevada Wolf Pack 3, New Mexico Lobos 31. Final score 31-3 at Mackay Stadium, and it was a long Friday night in Reno. Three and six. Two and three in the Mountain West. We hosted a ranked Lobo team and never got the second half started the way this crowd wanted.

Jacob Fernandez hit a 32-yard field goal late in the first half for our only points, cutting it to 14-3 for a minute. That was the highlight. AJ Bianco went 14 of 31 for 106 yards and lost a fumble. We rushed for minus-9 yards as a team. Six first downs. Two of 15 on third down. Ten punts from Ewan Baulch. Ninety-seven yards of offense against a New Mexico defense that sat on us for 38 minutes. Dylan LaBarbera led us with nine tackles and a sack. The effort was there. The yards were not.

Scottre Humphrey ran for 107 and two scores. Kiefer Sibley hit us for a 31-yard touchdown run. Toa Fa'avae found Cade Keith from 25 in the fourth. Nick Romero added a 37-yard field goal. They put up 374 yards and never turned it over. We turned it over once and spent the night in third-and-long. That is how you get blanked after the half on your own field.

The first half at least had a pulse when Fernandez drilled the 32-yarder. After the break it was Humphrey from 1, Keith from 25, Romero from 37, and the student section checking the exits. Hard night for a home crowd that showed up ready for a fight and got a clinic instead.

Northern Illinois on the road Saturday, Nov. 14, at 3:30 PM ET. Huskie Stadium in DeKalb. Fix the run game, win third down, and put points on the board before this season slips further. Pack up.
