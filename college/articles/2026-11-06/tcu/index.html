TCU vs Arizona: Hooper's 93-Yard Return Not Enough, 29-28

Final: TCU Horned Frogs (4-5) 28, Arizona Wildcats (7-2) 29, Fri., Nov. 6, Casino Del Sol Stadium, Tucson. Next: vs Kansas State, Sat., Nov. 14, Amon G. Carter Stadium, Fort Worth

XFacebook

TCU Horned Frogs 28, Arizona Wildcats 29. That 29-28 final from Casino Del Sol Stadium is going to ride around in my truck all week. We led Arizona 28-20 with 6:35 to play in Tucson on Friday night and still came home 4-5, 2-4 in the Big 12. Back in Fort Worth, Frog Fountain spent Friday afternoon hosting Frogchella 2.0, the First Gen Day party for TCU's first-generation students, with food, games and music. Campus spent the day honoring firsts. Our football team spent the night 38 seconds away from beating a ranked Big 12 team on the road.

Hudson Hooper was the best player on that field for long stretches. Twelve tackles, and with Arizona knocking on our door late in the second quarter, he jumped a throw to the flat and ran it back 93 yards for a touchdown. That flipped a 10-0 hole into a 14-10 halftime lead, and I nearly threw my hat into the rafters of my living room. Jaden Craig ducked a sack and scrambled 7 yards for our first score, hit Jordan Dwyer from 6 in the third quarter, and bulled in from 2 in the fourth. Craig finished 28 of 41 for 232 yards. Kari Ashley caught 14 balls for 95. Dwyer added nine grabs for 86.

Then the floor gave way. Arizona cut it to 28-26 with 1:45 left, the two-point try failed, and for a few seconds all of Frog Nation exhaled. Then Ian French floated an onside pooch kick, Ed Small could not come up with it, and Zuri Watson fell on the ball at our 26. Four plays later Michael Salgado-Medina hit from 37 with 38 seconds showing. Tyrese Boss intercepted Craig on our last try, and the desert went home happy.

The fix list writes itself. Kyle Lemmermann missed both of his field goal tries, and that is six points left in the cactus. We ran for 49 yards on 21 carries. Arizona piled up 441 yards to our 281, with Giovanni Richardson catching 11 balls for 171. Eight flags for 55 yards. I would love to blame Dallas for every one of those, and I will try, but the film is the film.

Kansas State comes to Amon G. Carter Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14, at 2 PM ET, and Utah follows a week later. Get home, get the run game fixed, and give Fort Worth a Saturday that ends with my hat in the air. Riff ram, Frogs.
