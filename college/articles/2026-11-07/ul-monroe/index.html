UL Monroe vs Arkansas State: Warhawks Keep Rice Bowl, 19-17

Final: UL Monroe (2-7) 19, Arkansas State (4-5) 17, Sat., Nov. 7, Centennial Bank Stadium, Jonesboro. Next: vs. Louisiana, Thu., Nov. 12, Malone Stadium, Monroe

ShareXFacebook

UL Monroe 19, Arkansas State 17, and I KNEW IT. I knew it at 2 a.m., I knew it at kickoff, and I knew it when Gavin Howard came down with that ball with a minute left. Saturday morning back home, folks gathered at The Grove on our own ULM campus for the Monroe/West Monroe Walk to End Alzheimer's and planted the Promise Garden. By afternoon the Warhawks gave Monroe one more thing to hold onto: our first win in Jonesboro since 2001.

We kept the Rice Bowl, too. Two meetings, two trophies, and the optimism counter on my desk is pinned at 100 and smoking a little.

Aidan Armenta opened it with a 9-yard touchdown run to finish a 79-yard drive, and after we trailed 14-6 he took us 75 yards to start the third and ran it in from 2 for a 16-14 lead. Austin Carlisle did the heavy lifting the rest of the way: 111 rushing yards on 19 carries and 4 for 4 throwing. Don Chaney added 68 yards, including a 36-yard burst on our first drive. As a team we ran for 268 yards and held the ball for 35:32.

The defense wrote the happy ending. Armoni Rue intercepted Trey Owens late in the half, and Abram Murray kicked a 23-yarder as time ran out. In the third, Kevontay Wells sacked Owens on fourth down. Noah Flemmings led us with eight tackles and a sack. Then Arkansas State kicked a 41-yarder to go up 17-16 with 5:42 left, and we answered with seven plays, nearly four minutes off the clock and a 40-yard Murray field goal with 1:49 left.

One more stand. The Red Wolves crossed midfield, and on third down Howard, who had already forced an Owens fumble in the first half, made the first interception of his career at our 20. Carlisle got the first down, we knelt, and somewhere in Ouachita Parish a man on the overnight shift turned his radio all the way up.

That's two wins in a row on the road in this league. We are 2-7, 2-3 in the Sun Belt, and Louisiana comes to Monroe on Thursday night, Nov. 12. Keep the faith, y'all. It's working.
