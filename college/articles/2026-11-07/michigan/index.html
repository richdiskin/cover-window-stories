Michigan vs Michigan State: Wolverines Lose Paul Bunyan 15-6

Final: Michigan State (3-6) 15, Michigan (5-4) 6, Sat., Nov. 7, Michigan Stadium, Ann Arbor. Next: at Oregon, Sat., Nov. 14, Autzen Stadium, Eugene

ShareXFacebook

Michigan State 15, Michigan 6, and I have taken off the maize sweater and folded it very carefully, the way you fold a flag. It was a Blue Out and our Military Appreciation Game at the Big House, 111,172 people in the stands for the Paul Bunyan Trophy, and the trophy is riding home to East Lansing tonight.

I keep a ledger for every game, and this column is all red. We had four turnovers. Tommy Carr was intercepted twice, Bryson Kuzdzal fumbled at the end of the third quarter, and Andrew Marsh had the ball knocked loose after a catch in the fourth. Jacob Baggett hit the left upright on a 26-yard try in the second quarter. The AP says it was our third straight home game without a point in the first half.

Meanwhile the Spartans just kept kicking. Liam Boyd made all five of his field goals, and one of those drives went 79 yards in 17 plays from their own 1 and took nearly 11 minutes. Carr got sacked five times. We ran for 54 yards on 26 carries, and Carr finished 14 of 26 for 138 yards.

Savion Hiter finally ran one in from a yard out with 1:35 left, which the AP says kept us from being shut out for the first time since 2014. Then Carr was sacked on the two-point try. That is not a column I ever wanted to fill in.

There were good entries. Troy Bowles made 15 tackles, Chris Bracy had 10, and John Henry Daley had two sacks and two and a half tackles for loss. Our defense held Michigan State to 2.6 yards per carry and kept us close for three quarters. Kuzdzal ran for 49 yards on nine carries.

We are 5-4 and 2-4 in the Big Ten, and next Saturday, Nov. 14, we go to Oregon at Autzen Stadium. Hail to the Victors, even on the nights the ledger won't balance. I'll have the sweater back on by Wednesday.
