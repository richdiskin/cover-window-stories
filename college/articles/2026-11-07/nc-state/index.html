NC State vs Duke: Wolfpack Shut Out of End Zone, Fall 41-3

Final: Duke (7-2) 41, NC State (4-5) 3, Sat., Nov. 7, Carter-Finley Stadium, Raleigh. Next: vs. Syracuse, Sat., Nov. 14, Carter-Finley Stadium, Raleigh

ShareXFacebook

Duke 41, NC State 3. It was our Annual Military Appreciation game, and before kickoff the Lowes Food Fan Zone between the Lenovo Center and Carter-Finley Stadium collected paper towels, tissues and supplies for veterans and their families through Support U.S. Armed Forces. That was the warmest part of my grill all day.

The thermometer told the story early. Duke's Nate Sheppard took his first carry 69 yards for a touchdown. We answered with a 39-yard Kanoah Vinesett field goal, and that was the last time we touched the scoreboard. Sheppard scored again from 8, Gavin Strange hit a 25-yarder, and Walker Eget found Jaivon Solomon for an 18-yard touchdown with 26 seconds left in the half.

Then came the moment that sent the whole cooker up in smoke. We tried a 50-yard field goal on the last play of the half. It came up short, Andrew Pellicciotta caught it in the back of the end zone and ran it 100 yards the other way. That turned 24-3 into 31-3, and 56,919 people went quiet in a hurry.

I promised not to cook the numbers, so here they are, raw. We ran 25 times for 32 yards. We gained 217 yards in all, the same number Sheppard ran for by himself. We converted 3 of 14 third downs. CJ Bailey completed 15 of 24 for 123 yards and took hits all afternoon. Amiri Acker caught two balls for 54 yards, Hunter Provience caught five for 51, and King Mack had six tackles and three kickoff returns. Popo Aguirre led us with eight stops.

This was our first game without a touchdown since a 24-3 loss at Duke in 2023, and interim coach Todd Goebbel is now 1-1. We are 4-5 and 2-4 in the ACC, still 4-2 at home. Syracuse comes to Carter-Finley next Saturday, Nov. 14. I'll be at the same lot, same grill, and I'll start the coals earlier.
