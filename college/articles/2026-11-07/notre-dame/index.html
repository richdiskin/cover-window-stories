Notre Dame vs Miami: Carr and Talich Topple No. 1 Canes 31-24

Final: Notre Dame Fighting Irish (8-1) 31, Miami Hurricanes (8-1) 24, Sat., Nov. 7, Notre Dame Stadium, Notre Dame. Next: vs. Boston College, Sat., Nov. 14, 3:30 PM ET, Notre Dame Stadium, Notre Dame

XFacebook

Notre Dame 31, Miami 24, and I would posit that 77,622 people have rarely been this loud in Notre Dame Stadium. The night honored the late Lou Holtz: captains from his teams, among them Tony Rice, Byron Spruell and Ricky Watters, presented the flags, and the band saluted him at halftime and again during the 1812 Overture. His 1988 Irish beat No. 1 Miami 31-30 in this building. Thirty-eight years later, the Irish took down the top-ranked Hurricanes again with 31.

Tonight's thesis: CJ Carr was the best quarterback in the stadium. Exhibit A is 29 of 40 for 319 yards, three touchdowns and zero interceptions. He found Jordan Faison for a toe-tapping 27-yard score in the right corner, hit Micah Gilbert on an 18-yard slant with 29 seconds left in the half, and found Gilbert again from 20 two plays after Faison's 56-yard punt return. Gilbert finished with nine catches for 98 yards. It was 24-3 early in the third.

Then Miami scored twice in a hurry, it was 24-17, and my sweater vest needed air. Enter Luke Talich, who undercut a deep out and intercepted Darian Mensah at our 49. On fourth-and-2 at the Miami 43, Carr found Nolan James Jr. split wide for 36 yards, and James finished it from a yard out. That was the deciding possession, and I will defend that claim before any committee.

Spencer Porath's 53-yard try came up short, Miami drove to our 22, and on fourth down Mensah threw to the end zone. Elija Lofton had it with two feet down. Talich ripped it out. Motion carried. James then ran six straight times to burn the Hurricanes' timeouts, Jasper Scaife punted 50 yards, and Boubacar Traore and Bryce Young shared the sack that sealed it.

Footnotes for the defense: the second-quarter stand at our own 1, where Jaiden Ausberry and Traore dropped Mensah for a 3-yard loss and held Miami to a field goal. Kyngstonn Viliamu-Asa added a sack and two tackles for loss. Ausberry and Adon Shuler made nine tackles apiece, and Christian Gray broke up two throws. No turnovers on our side, two on theirs.

That is the program's 10th win against a No. 1 team, per Notre Dame Athletics. We're 8-1. Boston College visits Notre Dame Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14, at 3:30 PM ET. The defense rests, and Coach Holtz got his tribute the right way.
