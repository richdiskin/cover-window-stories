UTEP vs Hawai'i: Miners Come Up Three Short, 14-17

Final: Hawai'i Rainbow Warriors (3-6) 17, UTEP Miners (1-8) 14, Sat., Nov. 7, Sun Bowl, El Paso. Next: vs. Wyoming, Sat., Nov. 14, 10:00 PM ET, Sun Bowl, El Paso

XFacebook

Hawai'i 17, UTEP 14. Mild salsa, mi gente, and I can't stand mild. Leis were the giveaway at the Sun Bowl, El Paso Children's Hospital sponsored the night, and it was the first time we'd hosted Hawai'i since 2004. Women's basketball took on Incarnate Word at 1 p.m., and a hoops ticket got you into the football game free. A whole Saturday of Miners, and we came up three points short.

The effort grade? That's ore worth keeping. Jayden Wilson made 11 tackles and forced a DeVon Rice fumble that Garrett Hawkins recovered. Xavier Smith made nine tackles and intercepted Micah Alejado in the end zone in the final seconds of the half. Kode Lowe had a sack among his seven tackles, and Justin Content made nine stops.

Our first touchdown came fast, three plays and 65 yards in 36 seconds in the second quarter. Carver Cheeks ran for 22, Lamar Sperling ran for 23, and Sperling finished it from the 5. 10-7, and the orange in the stands woke up.

In the fourth, after Hawai'i went 98 yards, Raymond Moore III answered with a seven-play, 79-yard drive and a 15-yard touchdown throw to Judah Ezinwa. 17-14 with 6:57 left. The crowd of 8,581 found its voice.

Then Hawai'i held the ball for the last 6:57, and we never touched it again. That one burns worse than a ghost pepper. Earlier, a 26-yard Kam Thomas run ended in a fumble, and a fourth-down try at the Hawai'i 32 came up short just before halftime. Those are the plays I'll be chewing on all week.

Sperling ran for 63 yards on 11 carries. Moore ran for 41 and threw for 109 and the score. Cheeks caught two for 46. We ran for 179 yards at 5.8 a carry, which tells you the front came to work. Brady Braun punted five times for a 41.6 average, two inside the 20.

We're 1-8 and 0-5 in the Mountain West. Next is Senior Night against Wyoming at the Sun Bowl on Saturday, Nov. 14, at 10 PM ET. Bring the green chile, El Paso. Let's make it Hatch-hot.
