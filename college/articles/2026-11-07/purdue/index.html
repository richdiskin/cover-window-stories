Purdue vs Maryland: Five Field Goals, No Homecoming Win, 29-34

Final: Maryland Terrapins (3-6) 34, Purdue Boilermakers (3-6) 29, Sat., Nov. 7, Ross-Ade Stadium, West Lafayette. Next: at Iowa, Sat., Nov. 14, 7:30 PM ET, Kinnick Stadium, Iowa City

XFacebook

Purdue Boilermakers 29, Maryland Terrapins 34. Final score 34-29 at Ross-Ade Stadium on our 104th Homecoming, and the blueprint had a crack in it right down the middle. Three and six. Two and four in the Big Ten. Third straight loss. David Bell, our 2021 All-American receiver, was back as honorary captain, and 51,929 came home to see a win that never got built.

The first half went almost to spec. Jacobo Echeverria Lozano hit from 47, 26, 24 and 36, and we led 12-7 at the half while holding Maryland to 108 first-half yards, per the AP. Later he added a 22-yarder, five for five on the day, and that gave him 21 field goals this season, a school record for a freshman by five, per the AP. The kid is a precision instrument.

Here is the engineering failure report. Four red zone trips ended in field goals, per the AP. One of them came after a bad snap on third-and-goal inside the Maryland 1. Ryan Browne fumbled at our own 2 in the third quarter, and four plays later Maryland was in the end zone. Xavier Townsend ripped off a 30-yard touchdown run to pull us to 21-19, and on the very next snap DeJuan Williams went 75 the other way. Then Chris Durr Jr. broke a tackle at the 11 and ran 87 yards. Three Maryland touchdowns of 44 yards or longer.

Browne threw for 296 yards on 24 of 38. Tra'Mar Harris caught three for 76. Antonio Harris ran for 51 on five carries. Fame Ijeboi punched in from 2 with 3:50 left to make it 34-29, and we got the ball back and reached the Maryland 11 with five seconds to go. The last play was a sack. Jojo Hayden led us with eight tackles, Trey Smith intercepted Malik Washington, and CJ Madden got a sack. We had 26 first downs to their 12 and two penalties all day. The numbers say we built the better machine. The scoreboard says otherwise.

Next up: Iowa, Saturday, Nov. 14, at 7:30 PM ET at Kinnick Stadium. Back to the drafting table. Boiler up.
