South Carolina vs Texas A&M: Sellers' Ankle, Sellers' Run, 17-9

Final: Texas A&M Aggies (4-5) 9, South Carolina Gamecocks (6-3) 17, Sat., Nov. 7, Williams-Brice Stadium, Columbia. Next: at Arkansas, Sat., Nov. 14, 7:45 PM ET, Donald W. Reynolds Razorback Stadium, Fayetteville

XFacebook

South Carolina Gamecocks 17, Texas A&M Aggies 9. Cue the music, because the final score is 17-9 and Williams-Brice just watched LaNorris Sellers limp, then sprint, into Gamecock legend on Homecoming weekend. Six and three. Four and three in the SEC. Four straight wins after opening league play oh-and-three, and bowl eligible.

What a stage it was. Steve Spurrier and the back-to-back national champion baseball teams from 2010 and 2011 were recognized at halftime as part of the new Athletics Hall of Fame class, in front of the 22nd straight sellout, 78,687 strong in Columbia. And while the Head Ball Coach was taking a bow, our quarterback was in the locker room getting his ankle X-rayed. Spotlight, please. He came back out.

This was a grind. Sellers hit Mazeo Bennett Jr. from 1 yard for our only first-half touchdown, after Bennett had already ripped off a 64-yard catch. Texas A&M answered with three field goals and led 9-7 into the fourth. Our offense sputtered, and the Aggies ran it right at us for 206 yards and held the ball for 34 minutes.

Then the defense stole the show. Fred Johnson made 11 tackles with a sack and two tackles for loss. Anthony Addison added eight. We stuffed the Aggies twice on fourth down. Malik Orsan drilled a 43-yarder with 8:14 left to put us up 10-9, and Mason Love kept pinning them back with a 51.4-yard average.

And the finale? One play after Anthony Addison and Justin Okoronkwo sacked Marcel Reed on fourth down, Sellers, bad ankle and all, broke loose for 41 yards and the touchdown with 1:19 left. Texas A&M still got to our 3 on its final drive, and the last throw sailed high. Lights up. Curtain call. Sellers finished 20 of 31 for 251 yards, and Nyck Harbor caught four for 70.

Next stop: Arkansas, Saturday, Nov. 14, at 7:45 PM ET in Fayetteville. Pack the speakers. Forever to thee.
