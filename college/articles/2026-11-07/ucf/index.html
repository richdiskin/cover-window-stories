UCF vs Kansas: Piening at the Pylon, Road Streak Ends 33-32

Final: UCF Knights (5-4) 33, Kansas Jayhawks (3-6) 32, Sat., Nov. 7, David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium, Lawrence. Next: vs Arizona State, Sat., Nov. 14, Acrisure Bounce House, Orlando

ShareXFacebook

UCF Knights 33, Kansas Jayhawks 32. I am typing this 33-32 win with the knight's helmet on, visor down, because that was the wildest ride Lawrence has run all year and I designed rides for a living. Five and four. Three and three in the Big 12. And the 12-game road losing streak? Closed for permanent maintenance.

Ride Check, first hill: after an early interception set Kansas up at our 3, Isaiah Marshall punched it in and we trailed 7-0. Then we climbed. Will Stone hit from 37 and 23, Duane Thomas Jr. ran one in from 10, and we held the Jayhawks to 61 yards in the first half. Out of the break, Taevion Swint scored from 1 and Alonza Barnett III hit Josh Derry for 44. That made it 26-7. Smooth track, full speed.

Then the drop nobody posted a warning sign for. Kansas scored 25 straight points, and Dylan Edwards took a 32-26 lead into our faces with 1:06 left.

Here is where my gauge broke. Barnett, in his first full game back from a hamstring injury, went 10 plays and 73 yards in less than a minute, with 65 of his yards on that drive, and found Caden Piening at the left pylon from 6 with one second showing. Replay review. Call stands. Somewhere in Lawrence a seismograph twitched. Barnett finished 18 of 31 for 251 yards and two scores, Derry caught eight for 143, and Curtis Tackett made 11 tackles. We were perfect on five red-zone trips and drew zero penalties. Zero. That ride clears inspection.

Back home that night, the UCF Percussion Ensemble and Steel Band played Beyond Drums at a church on East Robinson Street, part of the Milk District Salon Concert Series. Their whole point is that percussion is more than drums. Our point today: a road trip can be more than a loss. Arizona State visits the Bounce House on Saturday, Nov. 14. Keep your hands and feet inside the stadium, and Charge On.
