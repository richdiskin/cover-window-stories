New Mexico State vs Liberty: Williams Shines, Aggies Fall 14-30

Final: Liberty Flames (8-1) 30, New Mexico State Aggies (3-6) 14, Sat., Nov. 7, Aggie Memorial Stadium, Las Cruces. Next: at Missouri State, Sat., Nov. 14, 3:00 PM ET, Robert W. Plaster Stadium, Springfield

XFacebook

Liberty 30, New Mexico State 14. ¡Ay, Aggies! I'll give this one a chile grade of mild with a little char, and I mean that kindly. Downtown that morning, Las Cruces held its 30th annual Veterans Day Parade, with a cannon blast to kick it off and a theme of honoring 250 years of military service to the nation. The Organ Mountains had a busy day watching.

And our opening drive? Hatch hot. Trey Hedden, back in the No. 1 job, took us 75 yards in seven plays and hit AJ Williams III for a 24-yard touchdown. 7-0 Aggies, and Liberty had not trailed in conference play all season.

Then Liberty kicked three field goals, and a 15-yard touchdown with 18 seconds left in the half made it 16-7. After a scoreless third quarter, we cooked up a 12-play, 90-yard drive, and Hedden found Williams again from 5 yards out. 16-14 early in the fourth, and Aggie Memorial was roasting.

Williams was the whole chile field tonight: eight catches for 150 yards and two touchdowns, with a long of 44. Hedden finished 19 of 29 for 256 yards. On defense, Tory Gethers made 10 tackles with a sack and two tackles for loss, Nick Session also had 10, and Chance Barron had eight. Sone Aupiu got a sack too. Brody Richter averaged 49.3 a punt, with a 58-yarder that I'd grade Christmas-style.

The hard part: Liberty answered with a five-play, 78-yard drive, our fourth-down try came up short on the next series, and the Flames scored again. We ran for only 44 yards, and James Jones was held to 51 on 15 carries. Three turnovers, too, including an interception in the final minute.

We're 3-6 and 2-3 in Conference USA, and 2-3 at home. Next is a trip to Missouri State on Saturday, Nov. 14, at 3 PM ET at Robert W. Plaster Stadium in Springfield. Roast it, peel it, keep going. Aggies up.
