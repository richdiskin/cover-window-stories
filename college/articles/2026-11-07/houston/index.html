Houston vs Cincinnati: Sanford's Dash Wins Homecoming 31-24

Final: Houston Cougars (7-2) 31, Cincinnati Bearcats (5-4) 24, Sat., Nov. 7, TDECU Stadium, Houston. Next: at Colorado, Fri., Nov. 13, 10:15 PM ET, Folsom Field, Boulder

ShareXFacebook

Houston 31, Cincinnati 24, and Homecoming at TDECU Stadium has a happy landing. The Cougars came off the bye to host the Bearcats in front of 32,372, and we got the program's first win against Cincinnati since 2016, plus its first home win against them since 2015. Mission control, you may exhale now.

Liftoff was instant. Conner Weigman found Amare Thomas for an 8-yard score, then for 31 more, and we were up 14-0 before the first quarter was half gone. Thomas finished with six catches for 78 yards and both touchdowns. Weigman went 17 of 24 for 227 yards with no interceptions and added a 2-yard keeper in the second quarter, 21-10 at the break.

Then the Bearcats got their thrusters working. JC French IV threw two scores and ran in another, and with 6:21 left it was 24-24. Houston, we had a problem. That's when Re'Shaun Sanford II hit the hole, slipped between two defenders and outran the entire secondary for a 51-yard touchdown with 5:10 to go. Sanford finished with 126 yards on 15 carries, and Makhi Hughes added 90 on 12. We ran for 241 yards at 6.9 a pop. That is escape velocity.

The final stage separation came on replay. Cincinnati reached our 42, French threw a swing toss that officials first ruled incomplete, then a review called it a backward toss, and Christian Brathwaite was credited with the recovery with 2:11 left. From there the offense ran out the clock, and Homecoming was ours.

Honest flight review: the defense bent plenty. Cincinnati ran 71 plays, piled up 30 first downs and converted 9 of 14 third downs. Jaden Yates led us with nine tackles, Ashton Porter had six, and Jalen Mayo, Kentrell Webb and Jordan Allen each broke up a throw. Allen was also ejected for targeting on the fourth-quarter hit that sent Cincinnati's JV Gibson off on a cart, and we are praying for JV's recovery.

We're 7-2 and 4-2 in the Big 12, and the title chase is still on. Next is a Friday night trip to Colorado, Nov. 13, at 10:15 PM ET at Folsom Field in Boulder. Pack the oxygen. Go Coogs.
