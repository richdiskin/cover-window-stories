Maryland vs Purdue: Washington's Brace, Skid Snapped, 34-29

Final: Maryland Terrapins (3-6) 34, Purdue Boilermakers (3-6) 29, Sat., Nov. 7, Ross-Ade Stadium, West Lafayette. Next: vs Wisconsin, Sat., Nov. 14, 12:00 PM ET, SECU Stadium, College Park

XFacebook

Maryland Terrapins 34, Purdue Boilermakers 29. Final score 34-29 at Ross-Ade Stadium, and somebody hand me the Old Bay, because the six-game losing streak is finished and so is the 13-game Big Ten skid. Three and six. One and five in the league. The players stormed the field and gave interim coach Aazaar Abdul-Rahim a Gatorade bath, then carried him off on their shoulders. I may have cried into a crab mallet.

Back in College Park on Saturday night, the UMD Wind Orchestra played Parody and Parity at the Clarice, and the program included Ernst Krenek's Three Merry Marches. Fitting, because our third quarter was three merry marches of its own. Malik Washington hit Na'eem Abdul-Rahim Gladding on a screen that went 44 yards for a 14-12 lead. Four plays after Purdue fumbled at its own 2, Washington plunged in from a yard out. Then, right after a Purdue score, DeJuan Williams scooted 75 yards to make it 28-19.

The first half was a crab with no meat: five first downs and 108 yards, per the AP, and we trailed 12-7 at the break, with Washington's 15-yard touchdown run our only score. Whatever got said in that locker room, I want it bottled and sold at every seafood shack on Route 1.

Washington was the story. He wore a brace on his left knee all second half and still went 13 of 18 for 253 yards, two touchdown throws, two touchdown runs. The dagger came early in the fourth: Chris Durr Jr. caught a short one at the 11, broke a tackle, ran across the whole field and went 87 yards for a 34-22 lead. Durr finished with four catches for 127. Williams ran for 99 on 14 carries.

The defense bent but held the door. Nahsir Taylor made 11 tackles with a sack and two and a half tackles for loss, and Lavain Scruggs got a sack too. Purdue reached our 11 with five seconds left, and the final play was a sack of Ryan Browne. Honest note: nine penalties for 107 yards is not seasoning anybody wants.

Wisconsin comes to SECU Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14, at noon ET. Bring the crabs, bring the noise, and let's make it two in a row. Fear the Turtle.
