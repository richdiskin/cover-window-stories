Clemson vs Syracuse: Lawson Scoop, Hauser Kicks Win It 18-13

Final: Clemson Tigers (6-3) 18, Syracuse Orange (4-5) 13, Sat., Nov. 7, JMA Wireless Dome, Syracuse. Next: vs. Georgia Tech, Sat., Nov. 14, 12:30 PM ET, Memorial Stadium, Clemson

XFacebook

Clemson 18, Syracuse 13, and y'all, that's a win that took some choreography. An hour before kickoff, the Clemson University Symphony Orchestra was playing Peter and the Wolf on the green at Patrick Square, every character with its own instrument. Up in the Dome, our defense played the wolf, and Steve Angeli spent the afternoon hearing footsteps.

Nolan Hauser was our whole offense for a while, and I mean that with love. He hit from 32, 36 and 37 in the first half, missed from 57, and we went to the break up 9-6. In the third he drilled a 54-yarder for 12-6, four field goals on the day. He also punted twice for a 54.5 average. Somebody give that young man a second locker.

Then came the step-slide-scoop I'll be teaching the flag corps for years. Angeli tried a backward toss, our defensive tackle jarred it loose, and Jahiem Lawson scooped it up and ran 22 yards to the end zone. 18-6 with 6:39 left in the third. The two-point try failed, and I didn't care one bit.

Syracuse kept coming, and the defense kept answering. On third-and-goal from our 4 with a little more than 10 minutes left, Ashton Hampton intercepted Angeli in the end zone. Hampton finished with eight tackles and two breakups, Sammy Brown made eight stops, and we sacked Angeli three times while he went 18 of 40. At one stretch he missed 11 straight throws. When Syracuse finally scored on a 33-yarder with 3:12 left, our offense took the ball and never gave it back.

Honest film session: no offensive touchdowns and 4 of 16 on third down is not a dance anybody wants to repeat. Tait Reynolds went 17 of 28 for 161 yards with an interception and ran for 61. Jarvis Green broke a 53-yard run and finished with 85. Bryant Wesco Jr. caught six balls for 100 yards and even threw one for 36, though he also lost a fumble.

We're 6-3 and 4-2 in the ACC, and we've won two in a row for the first time in more than a month. Georgia Tech comes to Memorial Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14, at 12:30 PM ET. Orange you glad we're headed home?
