Utah State vs Fresno State: Aggies Hold the Fort in Logan, 17-10

Final: Utah State Aggies (4-5) 17, Fresno State Bulldogs (5-4) 10, Sat., Nov. 7, Maverik Stadium, Logan. Next: at San Diego State, Sat., Nov. 14, 9:30 PM ET, Snapdragon Stadium, San Diego

XFacebook

Utah State 17, Fresno State 10. Well, neighbors, that one was worth staying up for. It was Salute to Service night at Maverik Stadium, Military Appreciation, with Operation Hat Trick gear raising support for the Rocky Mountain Veterans Center. And Cache Valley's first responders had free seats, the same folks who fought the Canyon Road Fire south of campus this summer.

Our defense gave them a proper thank-you. Fresno State ran 27 times for 16 yards. That's it. Ronnie Mageo had 1.5 sacks and three tackles for loss. Asher Cunningham had eight tackles, two for loss and three quarterback hurries. Brevin Hamblin made nine stops. And Bronson Olevao Jr., who went down to thank the Logan police and firefighters in person back in September, dropped Josiah Freeman for a 10-yard loss late in the fourth.

The offense waited out a scoreless first quarter the way a cow waits on the morning milking, then went 80 yards in three plays across the break. McCae Hillstead hit Anthony Garcia for 37 and ran for 12, Fresno State kept drawing flags, and Sesi Vailahi scored from the 2. Hillstead scored from the 3 at 9:47 of the second, and it was 14-0 at halftime.

The second half was patience. Fresno State drove 66 yards for a field goal, 14-3. Early in the fourth they reached our 18, and our front stuffed Julius Gillick on fourth down. Kian Afrookhteh hit a 43-yard field goal with 4:21 left to make it 17-3. Fresno scored late, Kaina Watson fell on the onside kick, and Hillstead took a knee.

Hillstead went 9 of 14 for 117 yards without an interception and ran 12 times for 56 and a score. Javen Jacobs ran for 36 and returned three punts for 30. Will Evans punted five times and pinned three of them inside the 20. Zero turnovers for the good guys.

We're 4-5 and 3-2 in the Pac-12, 4-1 at home. San Diego State and Snapdragon Stadium are next, 9:30 PM ET on Nov. 14. I'll have my double scoop of Aggie Ice Cream either way, but this week I'm asking for extra fudge. Udderly earned.
