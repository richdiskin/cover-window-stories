Georgia Tech vs Louisville: Jackets Grounded at Home, 17-44

Final: Louisville Cardinals (6-3) 44, Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets (2-7) 17, Sat., Nov. 7, Bobby Dodd Stadium, Atlanta. Next: at Clemson, Sat., Nov. 14, 12:30 PM ET, Memorial Stadium, Clemson

XFacebook

Louisville 44, Georgia Tech 17. Jackets fans, I've re-run this simulation a few times now, and the outputs don't change. It was Military Appreciation Day at Bobby Dodd, and 44,680 of us came out to Midtown to honor those who served. That part of the night went exactly to plan. The football did not.

We actually opened fine. Aidan Birr hit a 32-yard field goal to tie it 3-3 in the first quarter. Then Louisville scored on an 8-yard throw, a 4-yard throw and a 52-yard end-around, and the curve bent away from us fast. 24-3 in the second quarter is a steep slope. Louisville averaged 7.0 yards a carry, which by my math is a first down every carry and a half.

The staff tried a new variable in the first half, alternating Alberto Mendoza and Grady Adamson drive by drive. Mendoza found Isaiah Fuhrmann for a 10-yard touchdown with 10 seconds left before the half, and Adamson hit Chris Corbo for an 11-yard score in the fourth. Mendoza went 13 of 25 for 148, and Adamson 12 of 19 for 93 with an interception.

The good data points: Justice Haynes ran 12 times for 87 yards, 7.3 a carry, with a 33-yard burst. Dalen Penson caught five balls for 66 yards, and Corbo caught six. Tae Harris and Taje McCoy made eight tackles each, and Ronnie Thomas III recovered a fumble. Alex Bacchetta punted four times for a 43.3 average, and graph paper says those were gorgeous parabolas.

The bug report is short and painful: 4 of 17 on third down, 251 rushing yards allowed, and a third straight loss. Both quarterbacks deserve credit for staying in rhythm while swapping in and out all half. I'll keep debugging, and I'll keep believing.

We're 2-7 and 1-5 in the ACC. Next is a trip to Clemson on Saturday, Nov. 14, at 12:30 PM ET at Memorial Stadium. Recalibrate and launch again, Jackets.
