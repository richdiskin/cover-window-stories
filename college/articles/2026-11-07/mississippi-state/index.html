Mississippi State vs Vanderbilt: Homecoming Rally Falls Short 25-23

Final: Vanderbilt (4-5) 25, Mississippi State (4-5) 23, Sat., Nov. 7, Davis Wade Stadium, Starkville. Next: vs. Auburn, Sat., Nov. 14, Davis Wade Stadium, Starkville

XFacebook

Vanderbilt 25, Mississippi State 23, on Homecoming, and the sock is on the cowbell. The whole week was "There's No Place Like State," from the True Maroon Trek through campus to Will Clark and Rafael Palmeiro running teams at the Diamond Dawg Classic on Friday night. Davis Wade Stadium was packed with 48,904 people in maroon on Saturday. We just couldn't give them the ending.

I keep my feed-store receipts, and the first half reads like a bad month. Vanderbilt held the ball for an 18-play drive, kicked three field goals and led 16-0 at halftime. We didn't make a first down on four straight drives in the first half, and AJ Swann was intercepted before the break.

Then the barn caught fire in a good way. Swann hit Sanfrisco Magee for a 65-yard touchdown on our first drive of the third quarter, and on the very next Vanderbilt possession, Tyshun Willis sacked Jared Curtis, knocked the ball loose, and Will Whitson scooped it up and scored from 14 yards. Two touchdowns in 52 seconds, and it was 16-14. Every cowbell in the place was ringing.

But their kicker never missed. Brock Taylor went 6 for 6, including a 55-yarder with 1:46 left that made it 25-17. We had already gotten to their 8 and settled for Kyle Ferrie's 26-yard field goal. Swann drove us down and hit Magee for 30 yards with 32 seconds left. The two-point try failed, and Vanderbilt got the onside kick.

Swann completed 23 of 36 for 254 yards and two touchdowns, and Magee caught two balls for 95 yards and both scores. Jardin Gilbert made 13 tackles, and Fatt Forrest had 11 with two tackles for loss. We are 4-5 and 2-5 in the SEC. Auburn comes to Davis Wade Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14. I'm taking the sock off for that one.
