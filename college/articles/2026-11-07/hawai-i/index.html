Hawai'i vs UTEP: Rainbow Warriors Ride It Out, 17-14

Final: Hawai'i Rainbow Warriors (3-6) 17, UTEP Miners (1-8) 14, Sat., Nov. 7, Sun Bowl, El Paso. Next: vs. North Dakota State, Sat., Nov. 14, 11:00 PM ET, Clarence T.C. Ching Athletics Complex, Honolulu

XFacebook

Hawai'i 17, UTEP 14. Shoots! Time-zone math first, because somebody has to do it: kickoff at the Sun Bowl was 7:30 in El Paso, 4:30 in the afternoon back home in Honolulu. And Saturday night at Bankoh Arena at Stan Sheriff Center, the Rainbow Wahine swept Nevada in front of 6,306 and clinched at least a share of the Mountain West regular-season title. Miliana Sylvester had 13 kills and six blocks. Big day for the islands.

Our guys did their part on the mainland. Micah Alejado threw for 303 yards, and Titan Lacaden was everywhere: 11 catches, 109 yards and a 20-yard touchdown in the first quarter, both career highs. Audric Harris added four catches for 101.

The drive I'll be surfing in my sleep came early in the fourth. We were pinned at our own 2 and went 98 yards in 12 plays. Sitani Mikaele ripped off 23, Lacaden caught one for 18 and another for 23 to the 6, and Mikaele scored from the 6 at 10:08. 17-7.

It didn't come easy, though. Sean Olvera-Harle missed from 42 before hitting a 20-yarder to finish an 18-play, 91-yard march for a 10-0 lead. A throw in the final seconds of the half got intercepted in the end zone, and DeVon Rice lost a fumble in the third. UTEP answered our big drive with a 79-yard march of its own, and it was 17-14 with 6:57 to go.

Then came the closer. Thirteen plays, 40 yards, all 6:57 of clock. Alejado kept the chains moving with short throws to Lacaden and Kainoa Carvalho, Mikaele and Cam Barfield ground out the rest, UTEP spent its timeouts, and Alejado took three knees. Island time, finally working for us.

The defense earned its plate lunch. Jamih Otis made 10 tackles with 1.5 for loss. Elijah Palmer made nine and forced two fumbles, and Matagi Thompson recovered the first one, which set up Lacaden's score. UTEP went 2 of 10 on third down.

We're 3-6 and 2-4 in the Mountain West. North Dakota State comes to the Clarence T.C. Ching Athletics Complex next, with an 11 PM ET kickoff on Nov. 14. Home break, Warriors. Paddle out early.
