Vanderbilt vs Mississippi State: Taylor Kicks Six in 25-23 Win

Final: Vanderbilt (4-5) 25, Mississippi State (4-5) 23, Sat., Nov. 7, Davis Wade Stadium, Starkville. Next: vs. Alabama, Sat., Nov. 14, FirstBank Stadium, Nashville

XFacebook

Vanderbilt 25, Mississippi State 23, and somebody hand me my guitar, because this song just went up a key. While the Nashville Farmers' Market threw its 28th Turnip Green Festival on Saturday morning, with Smiley's cast iron cauldron of braised greens and catfish and cornbread plates, the Commodores went down to Mississippi and brought home the first SEC win of the year.

Verse one was all Brock Taylor. He kicked a 38-yarder in the first quarter, and then we put together an 18-play, 87-yard drive that ate 10 minutes and 19 seconds, finished by Sedrick Alexander on a 2-yard run. Jordan Matthews intercepted AJ Swann with 1:25 left in the half, and Taylor kicked two more. Up 16-0 at halftime. I was humming.

Then the bridge got rough. The Bulldogs hit a 65-yard touchdown, and 52 seconds later Jared Curtis got sacked and fumbled, and they ran it back for a score. Sixteen to 14. My heart did a little breakup ballad. But Taylor came back out and kicked from 54, then from 34, then from 55 with 1:46 left. Six field goals, six makes, 19 points. Somebody write that man a song. I'm volunteering.

State scored with 32 seconds left to pull within two, but the two-point try failed, and we recovered the onside kick to end it. Curtis completed 17 of 27 for 168 yards, Makhilyn Young ran for 63 and Alexander for 59, and we held the ball for 39 minutes and 55 seconds. Issa Ouattara had two sacks, and Mason Lewis made 10 tackles.

That snaps a five-game losing streak. The AP says it's our first win against Mississippi State since 2004 and our first win on their home field since 1971. We are 4-5 and 1-5 in the SEC, and Alabama comes to FirstBank Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14. Final chorus, all together now.
