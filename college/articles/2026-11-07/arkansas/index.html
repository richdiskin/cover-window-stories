Arkansas vs Auburn: Hogs Rally to Tie, Lose on Late Kick 23-26

Final: Auburn Tigers (5-4) 26, Arkansas Razorbacks (3-6) 23, Sat., Nov. 7, Jordan-Hare Stadium, Auburn. Next: vs. South Carolina, Sat., Nov. 14, 7:45 PM ET, Donald W. Reynolds Razorback Stadium, Fayetteville

ShareXFacebook

Auburn 26, Arkansas 23. Woooo, Pig. Here comes the stat, and it hurts: 33 seconds. That's how much time was left when Auburn's kicker split it from 43. Back home, Fayetteville's Veterans Day Parade was honoring Northwest Arkansas veterans and their families from 11 to 1, right as our Hogs were kicking off on the Plains. Those folks deserved the salute. We deserved a better last minute.

Science class, first lesson: the run game came back to life. We came into Jordan-Hare last in the SEC in rushing yards per game, and we ran for 172. Cam Settles carried 16 times for 100 yards, including a 46-yard burst. Sutton Smith ripped off a 65-yarder. Braylen Russell punched in a 2-yard touchdown, Max Gilbert hit from 38, and we led 10-0 in the second quarter. 10-3 at the half.

Second lesson: field goals keep you alive, touchdowns win. Gilbert made three of his five tries, from 38, 37 and 46, and the 46-yarder tied it 16-16 with 9:32 left. Then Auburn went ahead 23-16 with 3:56 to go, and KJ Jackson answered like a grown man. He drove us down and found Antonio Jordan for a 23-yard touchdown with 1:54 left. 23-23. Hog Call, louder than I've done it all year.

It wasn't enough time on the clock for us, and it was plenty for them. Auburn moved into range and kicked the 43-yarder with 33 seconds left. Jackson finished 14 of 30 for 180 yards with the touchdown and an interception. He also got sacked hard, losing 27 net yards on the ground, mostly to a freshman linebacker who made three sacks.

The defense kept us in it. Khmori House made nine tackles with two for loss, Miguel Mitchell had eight and two for loss, Joker Johnson broke up two throws, and Carlon Jones got a sack. We held the ball for 33:16 and converted 8 of 18 third downs. Chris Marshall caught five balls for 65 yards. Every one of those players fought for 60 minutes.

We're 3-6 and 1-5 in the SEC. South Carolina comes to Fayetteville on Saturday, Nov. 14, at 7:45 PM ET. Lights on, Razorback Stadium loud, and a whole lesson plan built on that run game. Woooo, Pig. Sooie.
