North Texas vs Rice: Six Straight, Mean Green Roll 52-23

Final: North Texas Mean Green (7-2) 52, Rice Owls (1-8) 23, Sat., Nov. 7, DATCU Stadium, Denton. Next: at UTSA, Sat., Nov. 14, 9:00 PM ET, Alamodome, San Antonio

XFacebook

North Texas 52, Rice 23, and that's six straight wins, Mean Green. Six! The record is spinning and nobody's lifting the needle. Denton had a full Saturday, too: the UNT College of Music had its Afro-Cuban, Brazilian and Latin Jazz Concert at Voertman Concert Hall at 6:30 that night. Somewhere in that building, a drummer was playing in a meter almost as tricky as our second half.

Because the first half was a slow intro. Dominic De Freitas opened with a 37-yard field goal, Jahiem White scored from 3, and Rice had us at 10-7 at the break. Then they even took a run at us. Tyvonn Byars scored, and it was 17-14 midway through the third quarter.

And then the band kicked in. North Texas scored a touchdown on all six of its second-half possessions. White scored from a yard for 24-14. On the very next snap, Zakye Barker forced a fumble and Bryce Garza fell on it at the Rice 31. Five plays later, Tayven Jackson hit Dalton Carnes for 19 yards and a touchdown. Then White went 60 yards to the end zone on the first play of the fourth quarter. That's the hook you hum all week.

The liner notes: White ran 21 times for 139 yards and three touchdowns. Jackson threw for 303 yards and also ran in two scores. Justin Stevenson caught five balls for 117 yards, Max Reese had 72 and Baron Tipton 52. Brendon Haygood added a 4-yard score late, and De Freitas went seven for seven on kicks after touchdowns.

Barker led the defense with 10 tackles, Brandon Jones had eight, and Peyton Taylor came up with an interception and a 41-yard return. Rice did run for 261 yards, so the rhythm section has some rehearsing to do. Fair enough. We still never gave the ball away.

We're 7-2, 5-0 at home and 5-0 in the American, a game clear of Memphis and UTSA. Next is the road gig of the season: at UTSA, Saturday, Nov. 14, at 9 PM ET at the Alamodome in San Antonio. Turn it up.
