FIU vs Missouri State: Lyons Runs for 216, Walk-Off Kick 23-20

Final: Missouri State Bears (3-6) 20, Florida International Panthers (4-5) 23, Sat., Nov. 7, Pitbull Stadium, Miami. Next: at Delaware, Sat., Nov. 14, 3:00 PM ET, Delaware Stadium, Newark

XFacebook

Florida International Panthers 23, Missouri State Bears 20. ¡Qué noche! A 23-20 walk-off on Homecoming at Pitbull Stadium, a Military Appreciation night too, the first time these two ever met in Miami, and five minutes from campus my abuela is slapping a domino on the table so hard the creamer cups jumped. Four and five. Two and three in Conference USA. Four and one at home. Three-game skid, gone.

Let me set up the dominoes. Devonte Lyons, our redshirt freshman, ran for 216 yards on 24 carries with touchdowns from 6 and 17. FIU Athletics says that is the third-most rushing yards in a game in program history. We ran for 276 as a team, and the defense made a fourth-down stop at our own 8 before we marched 92 yards to go up 17-6. Shamir Sterlin had an interception, and Elisha Baldridge led us with seven tackles.

Then the Bears punched back, because nobody sleeps on anybody in this league. A strip sack, a 51-yard scoop and score, a two-point run, and it was 17-17. Ay, my heart.

Robert Czeremcha hit from 42 with 1:55 left. Missouri State tied it with a field goal. And then, mi gente, JJ Kohl got the ball with seconds left and went to work: 15 yards to Kyle McNeal, 20 to Tyson Carter, then Kohl himself took off for 27 to put us at the 13. Clock it. Then Czeremcha from 35. Good as the clock hit zero. FIU Athletics says it is our first game-winning field goal since Sept. 8, 2012, against Akron, and it is our first back-to-back Homecoming wins since the 2017 to 2019 run.

Kohl finished 18 of 26 for 218 yards, and McNeal and Carter combined for nine catches. Somebody make me a colada, because I am not sleeping tonight anyway.

Next we go north to Delaware on Saturday, Nov. 14, at 3 PM ET. Pack a coat, Panthers. ¡Vamos, FIU!
