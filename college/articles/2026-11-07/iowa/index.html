Iowa vs Northwestern: Hawkeyes Win in Evanston Again, 24-10

Final: Iowa (7-2) 24, Northwestern (6-3) 10, Sat., Nov. 7, Ryan Field, Evanston. Next: vs. Purdue, Sat., Nov. 14, Kinnick Stadium, Iowa City

ShareXFacebook

Iowa 24, Northwestern 10, and I'm sitting here in my one black-and-gold sweater grinning like a man who just got the corn in before the rain. Saturday morning in Iowa City, folks hauled their leftover jack-o'-lanterns to Chauncey Swan Park for Jack SPLAT!, watching them dropped off the parking ramp and splattered for the compost pile. By Saturday night our defense was doing the same thing to the Northwestern offense in Evanston.

Hank Brown started it like a man with somewhere to be. He went 4 for 4 on the opening drive and then ran a quarterback draw 21 yards for the touchdown. Our next score was a 97-yard drive, helped along by a 45-yard L.J. Phillips Jr. run, and Brown capped it with a 24-yard throw to Tony Diaz early in the second quarter. That was 14-0 and plenty of breathing room.

Now let me talk about the part of football I love most. Northwestern finally kicked a 45-yard field goal late in the third, and Braeden Jackson took the very next kickoff 96 yards to the house. That is his second kickoff return touchdown this season, and I nearly sent the lamp flying. Caden Buhr followed with a 52-yard field goal, and it was 24-3.

The defense is the reason I still sleep at night. Northwestern converted 1 of 11 third downs and 1 of 4 fourth downs, ran for 2.8 yards a carry, and did not get inside our 26 until the fourth quarter. Cam Buffington made 12 tackles, Jayden Montgomery had nine with a sack, and Bryce Hawthorne got a sack of his own.

Phillips had his best day in black and gold, 128 yards on 21 carries. Brown finished 16 of 21 for 126 yards with a touchdown, a score on the ground and one interception. We lost a fumble too, so the coaches will have plenty to say on Monday. Fair enough.

That is five straight wins against Northwestern and the first loss anybody has handed the Wildcats in their new stadium. We are 7-2 and 4-2 in the Big Ten, and Purdue comes to Kinnick Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14. I'll be wearing the sweater. My wife has stopped asking.
