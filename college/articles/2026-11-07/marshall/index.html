Marshall vs Georgia Southern: Herd Run Hard, Fall 13-49

Final: Georgia Southern Eagles (5-4) 49, Marshall Thundering Herd (5-4) 13, Sat., Nov. 7, Allen E. Paulson Stadium, Statesboro. Next: vs. App State, Sat., Nov. 14, 6:00 PM ET, Joan C. Edwards Stadium, Huntington

ShareXFacebook

Georgia Southern 49, Marshall 13. Friends, I'll give it to you straight, the way I'd give it to a patient's family: this one was rough from early on. Back home, the November First Saturday Art Market filled the old factory on Vernon Street with Appalachian makers from 11 to 4, and I'd have traded my seat in Statesboro for a zine workshop by the second quarter.

The turn came in seven seconds. Georgia Southern scored on a 23-yard throw with 3:11 left in the first quarter, and on our first snap after the kickoff a Zion Turner throw was intercepted and returned 24 yards for another touchdown. Down 14-0 that fast. Our answer came right back, and it was a beauty: Jo'Shon Barbie took a handoff 45 yards to the end zone. 14-7. That's the Herd I know.

Then Max Johnson found Alex Taylor for 70 yards on a one-play drive in the second quarter, and the Eagles opened the second half by scoring on four straight possessions. Johnson finished with four touchdown throws and ran for another. Jeremiah Ware and Taylor each had 94 receiving yards.

The parts I'll hold onto: we ran for 250 yards at 6.4 a carry. Barbie went for 83 on 12 carries. Khalil Wilkins broke a 66-yard touchdown run late and completed both of his throws. We put up 412 yards, Owen Sweeney caught four balls for 46, and Cooper Heimbach punted seven times for a 45.4 average without a single complaint. Jalen Marshall made seven tackles and Javae Gilmore six.

The parts that sting: 4 of 13 on third down, two interceptions, and a Georgia Southern offense that went 21 of 30 through the air. Turner finished 11 of 26 for 141 yards. That isn't a knock on any young man in green. It's just the truth on the chart, and the truth is how we heal.

We're 5-4 and 2-3 in the Sun Belt. App State comes to Joan C. Edwards Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14, at 6 PM ET, and Huntington will be ready to hold this team close. We Are... and always will be.
