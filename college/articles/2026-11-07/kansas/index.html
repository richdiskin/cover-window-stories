Kansas vs UCF: 25 Straight Points, One Second Short, 32-33

Final: UCF Knights (5-4) 33, Kansas Jayhawks (3-6) 32, Sat., Nov. 7, David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium, Lawrence. Next: at West Virginia, Sat., Nov. 14, Milan Puskar Stadium, Morgantown

ShareXFacebook

Kansas Jayhawks 32, UCF Knights 33. One point, one second, one 33-32 loss at the Booth, and I have a basketball mind, so let me say it the way my brain processes it: we lost on a buzzer-beater. Three and six. One and five in the Big 12. Somebody take the remote from me before I rewatch it again.

The opening was a fast break. Trey Lathan intercepted a throw on UCF's first snap and returned it to the 3, and Isaiah Marshall scored from 1. Then we went cold from the field. UCF led 12-7 at the half, held us to 61 first-half yards, and pushed it to 26-7 early in the third. In my math, that's a 19-point deficit in football, which is roughly a 19-point deficit in basketball. I did the math myself. It's the same number.

And then, Allen Fieldhouse energy in a football stadium. Marshall ran 30 yards for a score. Khijohnn Cummings-Coleman powered in from 1. Martin Connington made a 34-yard field goal. Dylan Edwards scored from 2 with 1:06 left, and Marshall threw the two-point try to Nik McMillan. Twenty-five straight points. That's eight threes and a free throw, by my count, and 31,008 of us were up on our feet.

Marshall was terrific: 125 yards rushing on 13 carries with two touchdowns, and 21 of 32 for 210 through the air. Keaton Kubecka caught five for 71.

Then UCF went 73 yards in less than a minute and scored from 6 with one second on the clock. Replay upheld it. In hoops you'd foul there. Football never gives you that option.

Back home in Lawrence on Saturday, the Lawrence Arts Center ran Napoleon's Dynamite Musical Parody at 2 and 7 p.m. A comedy about a guy who keeps trying, keeps coming up a little short, and keeps dancing anyway. I relate. We go to West Virginia on Saturday, Nov. 14. Rock Chalk.
