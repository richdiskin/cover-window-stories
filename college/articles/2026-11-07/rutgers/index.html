Rutgers vs Wisconsin: Tied at the Half, Shut Out After, 10-31

Final: Rutgers Scarlet Knights (2-7) 10, Wisconsin Badgers (6-3) 31, Sat., Nov. 7, Camp Randall Stadium, Madison. Next: vs Nebraska, Sat., Nov. 14, 12:30 PM ET, SHI Stadium, Piscataway

XFacebook

Rutgers Scarlet Knights 10, Wisconsin Badgers 31. A 31-10 loss in Madison, and if you're asking where my mood is tonight, take the Turnpike south past Exit 7A and keep going until the road turns to gravel. Two and seven. One and five in the Big Ten.

Let me be fair to these kids first, because they earned it. We went to Camp Randall without KJ Duff, without Dylan Lonergan, and without offensive linemen Tyler Needham and Kwabena Asamoah. AJ Surace stepped in at quarterback and gave it all a young man can give: 14 of 24 for 161 yards and no turnovers. His 30-yard touchdown to Dyzier Carter tied it 10-10 late in the second quarter. Jack Scullion drilled one from 45. Ben Black III caught six for 70. At that point, I was cruising around Exit 11 with the windows down.

Then the boardwalk arm-wrestle flipped. Wisconsin kicked a field goal with 24 seconds left in the half, then won the second half 18-0. Their freshman quarterback, Ryan Hopkins, opening a game for the first time, finished 18 of 24 for 241 and three touchdowns, and their 6-foot-6 tight end Jacob Harris went 66 yards for the last one. The Badgers sacked Surace five times. We ran 36 times for 94 yards, and Antwan Raymond got 56 of those on 15 tough carries. A couple of flags hurt us, too, including one that erased an interception by JJ Conyers.

I won't sugarcoat it. Seven losses means a second straight losing season. That stings, and I'm not going to pretend otherwise.

Back home on Saturday morning, the Rutgers farmers market was open on Jones Avenue in New Brunswick, selling fresh produce at half off for families using nutrition benefits. That's Jersey at its best: show up, feed your neighbors, no excuses. That's what I want from the last stretch of this season.

Nebraska comes to SHI Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14, at 12:30 PM ET. Exit 9, folks. Bring your voice. RU-RAH-RAH.
