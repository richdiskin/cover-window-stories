Coastal Carolina vs Old Dominion: Turnovers Sink Homecoming 8-21

Final: Old Dominion Monarchs (3-7) 21, Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (3-6) 8, Sat., Nov. 7, Brooks Stadium (SC), Conway. Next: vs Arkansas State, Sat., Nov. 14, 1:00 PM ET, Brooks Stadium (SC), Conway

XFacebook

Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 8, Old Dominion Monarchs 21. Final score 21-8 at Brooks Stadium, and Homecoming in Conway went out with the tide. Three and six. Two and three in the Sun Belt. Six turnovers on your own field, in front of 16,092 who came home for the weekend, makes for a hard afternoon to write about. I will do it anyway, because that is the job.

Low tide notes, first quarter: Deuce Bailey lost a fumble, and Mario Thompson took it 42 yards for a touchdown just 37 seconds after Old Dominion's first score. Late in the half another fumble went the wrong way at our 6, and the Monarchs scored on the next snap. Fourteen of their 21 points came off our mistakes. Nico Cavanillas Alti did his part, hitting from 30 and from 44 as the half expired, and it was 21-6 at the break.

Then the tide came back in for our defense. Old Dominion did not score again. Marcellius Pulliam made 22 tackles, which is not a typo, and he and Nolan Korzeniowski tackled Maurki James in the end zone for a safety in the fourth quarter. Jesse Powell II added 11 tackles. Xzavier Booker had eight with a sack and three tackles for loss. We held the Monarchs to 69 rushing yards and 264 in all, and Roland Podesta averaged 46.6 a punt.

The offense never found its current. We drove 86 yards in the third quarter and Ja'Mez Drummer intercepted us at the 1. Ja'Marion Wayne gave us life with a 35-yard interception return to the Old Dominion 14 with 7:51 left, and we came up empty on downs. Four interceptions in all, three in the fourth quarter. Twenty rushing yards. Two of 15 on third down. Bailey threw for 233, and Karmello English caught five for 57.

Arkansas State comes to Brooks Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14, at 1 PM ET for the home finale. One more Saturday on the teal turf. Let's make the last one sing. Teal forever.
