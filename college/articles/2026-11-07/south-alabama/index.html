South Alabama vs Louisiana: Jags Rally, Fall Short 26-28

Final: Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns (6-3) 28, South Alabama Jaguars (4-5) 26, Sat., Nov. 7, Our Lady of Lourdes Stadium, Lafayette. Next: vs. Troy, Sat., Nov. 14, 7:00 PM ET, Hancock Whitney Stadium, Mobile

ShareXFacebook

Louisiana 28, South Alabama 26. Y'all, I had my towel spinning over my head by the fourth quarter, and I still don't know if I was cheering or begging. Back home on the Avenue, the Historic Avenue Cultural Center threw its third annual Block Party from 11 to 3, with music, films and a sneak peek at the Ace Theatre restoration that will be the future home of the Excelsior Band. Mobile knows how to throw a party. Our Jaguars nearly crashed one in Lafayette.

It opened rough: JJ Garner took the opening kickoff 100 yards. Our answer was pure Gulf Coast grit, a 14-play, 63-yard drive that Bishop Davenport finished with a 1-yard run. After Garner broke a 65-yarder to open the second quarter, Jaylen Moson jumped a throw and returned it 72 yards for a touchdown. 14-14. I may have hollered at a stranger.

Dylan Freebury hit a 44-yard field goal before the half to get us to 21-17. Down 28-17 in the third, Davenport took off on a 45-yard touchdown run. The two-point try came up short, but Freebury drilled a 50-yarder early in the fourth, and it was 28-26.

Then our defense got the ball back with four minutes to go after a Louisiana field goal try missed. We had our chance. Davenport was sacked at midfield, and his next throw was intercepted with less than two minutes left. That's the one I'll be drawing up on a napkin all week, trying to fix it.

Davenport was terrific: 16 of 22 for 210 yards, plus two rushing touchdowns. Brendan Jenkins caught six balls for 101 yards. Keenan Phillips returned five kickoffs for 120. Seidrion Langston led us with eight tackles, Xavious Anderson Jr. had seven, and E'Mauri Smiley got a sack. We went 2 for 2 on fourth down. The part we have to fix is the run defense, which gave up 312 yards.

We're 4-5 and 2-3 in the Sun Belt. Troy comes to Hancock Whitney Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14, at 7 PM ET. Pull up a lawn chair, Mobile, and bring the noise. Go Jags.
