Florida State vs Boston College: Fake FG Sparks 31-24 Win

Final: Florida State (4-5) 31, Boston College (4-5) 24, Sat., Nov. 7, Alumni Stadium (Chestnut Hill, MA). Next: at Pittsburgh, Fri., Nov. 13, Acrisure Stadium, Pittsburgh

ShareXFacebook

Florida State 31, Boston College 24, and the motion carries. While the Anhinga Music & Art Festival filled Lewis and Bloxham Parks in downtown Tallahassee with zydeco, food trucks and free admission on Saturday afternoon, our Seminoles were up in Chestnut Hill voting through the most important bill of the season: a road win, the first since Nov. 25, 2023.

Let the record show this was interim coach Rick Stockstill's first game, and the man who once played quarterback for Bobby Bowden called a game with some Bowden in it. Down 17-14 late in the half, we lined up for a short field goal. Holder Carter Jula flipped the ball back to kicker Gabe Panikowski, who caught it in stride and scored from a yard out. Motion to reconsider the scoreboard: granted, 21-17.

The opening session was rough. Boston College scored on its first drive and led 10-0, then 17-7. Ashton Daniels answered with a 4-yard touchdown run, and Amari Thomas ripped off a 20-yard score to get us within three. Nehemiah Chandler intercepted Mason McKenzie in the final seconds of the half and returned it 30 yards, and Ja'Bril Rawls intercepted another throw in the third.

Floor debate got tense after Boston College ran a spinning trick play for a 2-yard touchdown and a 24-21 lead. Panikowski tied it with a 22-yard field goal early in the fourth. Then came the vote that mattered: 90 yards in 14 plays, and Thomas bounced outside for an 11-yard touchdown with 2:29 left. Our defense held from there.

The committee report: Thomas ran 19 times for 98 yards and two scores. Daniels completed 15 of 26 for 178 yards with an interception and ran for 38. Micahi Danzy caught seven balls for 86 yards. Ashlynd Barker made 10 tackles and Chris Jones eight. We converted 8 of 14 third downs and ran for 202 yards.

We are 4-5 and 2-4 in the ACC, and bowl eligibility is still on the table. Next up is Pittsburgh at Acrisure Stadium on Friday night, Nov. 13. Gavel down. This session is adjourned with a win.
