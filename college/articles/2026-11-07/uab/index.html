UAB vs Charlotte: Blazers Win Children's Harbor Game, 23-12

Final: UAB (5-4) 23, Charlotte (1-8) 12, Sat., Nov. 7, Protective Stadium, Birmingham. Next: at Temple, Sat., Nov. 14, Lincoln Financial Field, Philadelphia

ShareXFacebook

UAB 23, Charlotte 12, and forged, not given, folks. Saturday was the 10th annual Children's Harbor Game at Protective Stadium, and our Blazers wore gray jerseys with the names of 99 children served by Children's Harbor stitched across their backs. Kids Takeover filled Blazer Village with games and inflatables. Every one of those names went home a winner.

Act one was a hard watch, I'll be honest. Ryder Burton was intercepted three times, Rod Robinson II lost a fumble, and Charlotte kicked two field goals off those mistakes. We were tied 3-3 at the half only because Jonah Delange hit a 42-yarder with two seconds left. Down 6-3 in the third, this stage needed a new lead, and I mean that in every theater sense of the word.

Enter the defense. Marlin Dean sacked Conner Harrell three times. Tylin Jackson was in the backfield all day with half a sack, a tackle and a half for loss and three quarterback hurries. Jo'Tavion Pierce made eight tackles and Muaaz Byard seven. Charlotte ran 31 times for 59 yards, converted 4 of 17 third downs and finished with 234 yards. That is steel.

Then the final act. Delange tied it with a 46-yarder late in the third and put us ahead 9-6 with a 36-yarder to open the fourth. Ja'Vin Simpkins scored from a yard out with about eight minutes left and from 5 yards with 2:07 to go. Robinson ran 14 times for 101 yards. Bam McReynolds added 54, and EJ Reid caught five balls for 57. Delange went 3 for 3 and made both extra points.

That makes us 5-0 at home, which UAB has not done since 2019, plus 5-4 overall and 3-2 in the American. Next Saturday, Nov. 14, we head to Temple at Lincoln Financial Field. Curtain down on a good one. Take a bow, Blazers, and give those jerseys to the kids.
