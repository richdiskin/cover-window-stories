Middle Tennessee vs Western Kentucky: Homecoming Thriller 51-42

Final: Middle Tennessee (5-4) 51, Western Kentucky (3-6) 42, Sat., Nov. 7, Johnny "Red" Floyd Stadium, Murfreesboro. Next: at Liberty, Sat., Nov. 14, Williams Stadium (VA), Lynchburg

XFacebook

Middle Tennessee 51, Western Kentucky 42, on Homecoming in Murfreesboro, and I'm going to need a second pan of biscuits. Our alumni came home to Floyd Stadium, the youth sports teams got their moment on the video board, and the Blue Raiders gave everybody a finish that will be told at every Thanksgiving table in Rutherford County.

We started like a hot oven. Roman Gagliano threw a 25-yard touchdown to Neo Clifton, then found Ziyon Butler for 65 yards a minute later, and it was 14-0. Western Kentucky clawed back to 14-13, so Gagliano hit Clifton again from a yard out with 29 seconds left in the half. On the first snap of the third, Antonio Martin ran 75 yards for a touchdown. Sit down, I'm not done.

Martin added a 2-yard score and finished with 125 rushing yards on 16 carries. Gagliano completed 30 of 41 for 312 yards and four touchdowns, the last a 4-yarder to Koraun Perdue for a 42-27 lead early in the fourth. Butler caught six balls for 129 yards, and Cam'ron Lacy caught nine. Korey Smith led the defense with seven tackles, and Zeion Simpson-Smith got a sack.

Then the Hilltoppers scored 15 straight and tied it 42-42 with 31 seconds left. I put my hand on my heart. Gagliano did not blink. He hit Lacy for 23 yards, a holding penalty helped, and Dominic Bourgeois kicked a 48-yard field goal with one second on the clock. Western Kentucky tried lateral after lateral on the kickoff, the ball came loose, and Aidan Singleton scooped it up and scored as time ran out.

That ends a seven-game losing streak to Western Kentucky in the 100 Miles of Hate, and I never once stopped believing it would happen. We are 5-4, 3-2 in Conference USA and 4-1 at home. Next Saturday, Nov. 14, we go to Liberty at Williams Stadium. Pack a lunch, boys. I'll have one waiting when you get back.
