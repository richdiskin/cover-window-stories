Baylor vs Iowa State: Lagway's Legs Power a Homecoming 42-31

Final: Iowa State Cyclones (4-5) 31, Baylor Bears (6-3) 42, Sat., Nov. 7, McLane Stadium, Waco. Next: at BYU, Sat., Nov. 14, LaVell Edwards Stadium, Provo

ShareXFacebook

Baylor Bears 42, Iowa State Cyclones 31. Final score 42-31 at McLane Stadium on Homecoming, and Waco did it the Baylor way from sunrise on. The oldest and largest collegiate homecoming parade in the country rolled down Fifth Street to campus Saturday morning, floats and bands and all, and by early afternoon the Bears had built a comeback worth putting on a float of its own. Six and three. Four and two in the Big 12.

I will be honest about the before photos. Iowa State led 17-7 in the second quarter after Aiden Flora broke a 40-yard run and Jaylen Raynor found Cameron Pettaway for 50. That room needed a full gut job.

Then DJ Lagway grabbed the hammer. He took off for a 54-yard touchdown run, and in the final minute of the half, a loose ball in the end zone ended up with offensive lineman Koltin Sieracki on top of it for the go-ahead score. Ugly demo, pretty finish, 21-17 at the half. Out of the break, Lagway lofted a 62-yarder to Taz Williams Jr., and Ryelan Morris ran 33 yards for another score. Four straight touchdowns. Shiplap on every wall, 35-17.

Lagway finished 16 of 28 for 229 yards and two touchdowns, the first a 19-yarder to Tony Livingston, and ran 13 times for a career-high 119 yards. Morris added an 11-yard score late to make it 42-23. Gavin Freeman caught six balls for 60. Micah Gifford led us with nine tackles, and Travion Barnes and Devin Turner each got a sack.

The detail work was gorgeous: zero turnovers and one penalty, for 15 yards, all afternoon. Flora still ran for 182 against us, and Iowa State held the ball for 36 minutes, so the run defense goes on the punch list. But 39,229 came home for Homecoming and left happy, and we are 2-0 with interim coach Joe Klanderman in charge.

Next job site: BYU, Saturday, Nov. 14, at LaVell Edwards Stadium in Provo, kickoff time still to be set. Pack the tape measure. Sic 'em.
