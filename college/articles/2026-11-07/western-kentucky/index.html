Western Kentucky vs Middle Tennessee: Tops Fall at Buzzer, 51-42

Final: Middle Tennessee (5-4) 51, Western Kentucky (3-6) 42, Sat., Nov. 7, Johnny "Red" Floyd Stadium, Murfreesboro. Next: vs. Jacksonville State, Sat., Nov. 14, Houchens Industries-L.T. Smith Stadium, Bowling Green

XFacebook

Middle Tennessee 51, Western Kentucky 42, and I have had to set the clippers down twice just to catch my breath. While the Kentucky Museum on our campus threw Sonic Fest on Saturday afternoon, with Government Cheese and Tommy Womack celebrating a year of the Sonic Landscape exhibit, our Hilltoppers were 100 miles down the road writing a song nobody in Bowling Green wanted to hear the last verse of.

Pull up a chair, this one needs a long haircut. We fell behind 14-0 in the first quarter after two quick Roman Gagliano touchdown throws. Then the Tops started trimming. Karl Campbell kicked a 35-yarder, Brock Glenn ran one in from 2, and Campbell hit from 42. We trailed 21-13 at the half, and Glenn found Moussa Barry for 21 yards in the third to cut it to 28-20.

Ajay Allen was the best thing in the shop all day: 116 yards on 15 carries and an 8-yard touchdown run. Jonathan Stafford Jr. caught eight balls for 90 yards. Jaylen Wester made 13 tackles and got a sack. We ran for 238 yards as a team and gained 497 in all.

Down 42-27 in the fourth, Rodney Tisdale Jr. came in and nearly wrote a legend. He hit Quincy Burroughs for an 8-yard touchdown, threw the two-point try to Lofton Howard, and then ran 5 yards for the tying score with 31 seconds left. Forty-two all. Every chair in the shop would have spun.

Then 30 seconds was too much time. Middle Tennessee moved into range, Dominic Bourgeois hit a 48-yarder with 1 second left, and our lateral try on the kickoff ended with the ball bouncing loose and a Blue Raider running it in. That ended our seven-game winning streak in this rivalry.

We are 3-6 and 2-3 in Conference USA. Jacksonville State comes to Houchens Industries-L.T. Smith Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 14. Stand up and cheer anyway, Tops. The next customer is already in the chair.
