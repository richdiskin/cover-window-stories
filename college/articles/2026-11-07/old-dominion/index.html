Old Dominion vs Coastal Carolina: Six Takeaways, Skid Ends 21-8

Final: Old Dominion Monarchs (3-7) 21, Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (3-6) 8, Sat., Nov. 7, Brooks Stadium (SC), Conway. Next: at UConn, Sat., Nov. 21, 3:30 PM ET, Pratt & Whitney Stadium, East Hartford

XFacebook

Hear ye, hear ye: Old Dominion Monarchs 21, Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 8. Final score 21-8 at Brooks Stadium in Conway, and the three-game losing streak has been formally banished from the realm. Three and seven. Two and five in the Sun Belt. We crashed their Homecoming and walked out with six takeaways in the royal treasury.

Back home in Norfolk on Saturday night, the Barry Art Museum's Helios festival lit up 43rd Street with Luke Jerram's giant inflatable Sun, lit from the inside, after Friday's night got washed out by weather. A fitting backdrop. Our defense was the brightest light in two states.

The coronation came early. Quinn Henicle ran in from 2 with 5:05 left in the first quarter, and 37 seconds later Mario Thompson scooped up a Deuce Bailey fumble and carried it 42 yards for a 14-0 lead. With 27 seconds left in the half, we recovered another fumble at the Coastal 6, and on the very next snap Jaxon Potter found Leon Haughton Jr. for a touchdown. Twenty-one points, and two of those touchdowns came gift-wrapped.

Then the second half belonged to the secondary. Four interceptions after the break, from four different Monarchs: Ja'Mez Drummer, Zion Frink, Brandon Crutchfield and Tavares Murray Jr. Drummer's came at our 1 to end an 86-yard Coastal march, and he added two sacks for good measure. Knight him. Ian Brandt punted eight times and dropped four inside the 20, and we held the ball for the final 7:34.

Honest court record: we were blanked in the second half, ran for just 69 yards on 35 carries, and took a safety early in the fourth after an interception left us pinned deep. Potter went 17 of 30 for 150. Haughton caught six for 67, and Josh Rodriguez caught six for 53. Coastal held us to 264 yards. Defense won this one, and the crown fits it.

Now an open week to rest, then a trip north to UConn on Saturday, Nov. 21, at 3:30 PM ET. Polish the crown. Long live the Monarchs.
