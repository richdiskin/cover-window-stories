Minnesota vs Wisconsin: The Axe Goes Back to Madison, 5-28

Final: Wisconsin Badgers (8-4) 28, Minnesota Golden Gophers (6-6) 5, Fri., Nov. 27, Camp Randall Stadium, Madison. Regular season complete

By Torvald 'Tor' Engebretsen, The Minnesota Fanatic

XFacebook

Minnesota lost 28-5 at Wisconsin on Friday night, and Paul Bunyan's Axe goes back to Madison. I'm writing this from a very quiet corner of the Twin Cities.

It began with promise. On the Badgers' opening drive, Naiim Parrish jumped a Colton Joseph throw and returned it 18 yards, and Luke Drzewiecki banged home a 53-yard field goal. Minnesota 3, Wisconsin 0, and for about 10 minutes of game time I believed.

From there, Wisconsin scored touchdowns on three straight drives while we went three-and-out three straight times. At halftime it was 21-3, and the Badgers had 16 first downs to our one. One.

The final numbers are brutal. We were outgained 482-115 and outrushed 265-11. We had six first downs all night. Drake Lindsey completed 14 of 28 throws for 104 yards behind a front that couldn't hold up. Joseph ran for 101 yards and had a hand in three touchdowns against us. Our only other points came on a safety when Wisconsin was flagged for holding in its own end zone.

A few Gophers deserve a salute. Joey Gerlach made 12 tackles. Maverick Baranowski made seven and grabbed an interception. Parrish made the play that gave us the early lead. We didn't lose a fumble or throw an interception, and still couldn't keep pace. When you run for 11 yards on 14 carries, the defense ends up on the field for 38:54, and no defense in this league survives that.

The series is now tied at 64-64-8, and Wisconsin has beaten us just twice in six years. That's a stat I'll cling to until next November.

We're 6-6, 4-5 in the Big Ten, bowl eligible, and waiting on a destination. One more game to send this group out the right way, and a full offseason to figure out how to block Wisconsin. Ski-U-Mah.
