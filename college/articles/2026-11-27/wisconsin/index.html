Wisconsin vs Minnesota: The Axe Comes Home to Madison, 28-5

Final: Wisconsin Badgers (8-4) 28, Minnesota Golden Gophers (6-6) 5, Fri., Nov. 27, Camp Randall Stadium, Madison. Regular season complete

By Lars 'Brat' Ellefson, The Wisconsin Fanatic

XFacebook

Paul Bunyan's Axe is back in Madison where it belongs. Wisconsin flattened Minnesota 28-5 on Black Friday night in front of 72,651 at Camp Randall, and the most-played rivalry in the FBS is now dead even at 64-64-8.

Colton Joseph was a one-man wrecking crew. He completed 12 of 18 throws for 217 yards, ran for 101 more on 20 carries, and scored three touchdowns. On third-and-13 on the first play of the second quarter, he found Tyrell Henry for a 27-yard score to put us ahead for good. He ran one in from 7 yards before the half, and from the 1 late in the third. Along the way he set the single-season rushing record for a Wisconsin quarterback, now at 659 yards.

The numbers look like a typo. We outgained the Gophers 482-115. We outrushed them 265-11. At halftime it was 21-3, and we had 16 first downs to their one. Bryan Jackson II added a 1-yard touchdown, and Darrion Dupree carried 23 times for 80 yards.

Minnesota got its points from a 53-yard field goal after an interception on our opening drive, and a holding flag in our end zone for a safety. The Gophers finished with six first downs. Cooper Catalano made seven tackles, and Tyreese Fearbry got a sack.

This defense has been a fortress at home all season. We allowed 44 points in six games at Camp Randall and never gave up more than 10 in any of them. AP says the last time a Wisconsin team did that was 1951.

We're 8-4, 6-3 in the Big Ten, and we doubled last season's number of wins. Joseph threw two interceptions, one off a receiver's hands, and we'll fix that before the bowl. Tonight, though, the Axe is ours, and it looks perfect in red. Somebody grab a saw. On, Wisconsin.
