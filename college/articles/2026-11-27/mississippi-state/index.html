Mississippi State vs Ole Miss: Three Points Away in Oxford, 17-41

Final: Ole Miss Rebels (8-4) 41, Mississippi State Bulldogs (5-7) 17, Fri., Nov. 27, Vaught-Hemingway Stadium, Oxford. Regular season complete

By Wade Pettus, The Mississippi State Fanatic

XFacebook

For one half in Oxford, Mississippi State owned the Egg Bowl. We went to the locker room up 10-6, Fluff Bothwell was running through the Rebels like they owed him money, and Kyle Ferrie had just drilled a 42-yard field goal with 1:48 left in the half. The final says Ole Miss 41-17. I'd like a word with the third quarter.

Bothwell was the best player on that field. He went 16 yards for our first touchdown, finished with 129 yards on 18 carries, and caught three balls, including the 12-yard touchdown from Parker Puckett on the first play of the fourth quarter that cut it to 20-17. Puckett was sharp all night, 22 of 27 for 201 yards, and Marquis Johnson hauled in five catches for 96 yards, one of them a 45-yarder.

We had the Rebels sweating. Down three, driving, momentum on our side. Then Blake Purchase jumped a Puckett throw and ran it back 58 yards to our 1. Trinidad Chambliss walked in on the next snap, and it went from 20-17 to 27-17 in about the time it takes to say Egg Bowl.

From there, Ole Miss scored twice more, and the 35-7 second half makes this look like a blowout it wasn't for 30 minutes. We held the ball for 33:22. Jardin Gilbert made nine tackles and a sack. Fatt Forrest added eight tackles and Kendel Dolby seven.

But you can't give a team like that 10 penalties for 84 yards and expect to survive Oxford, and a 3-of-11 night on third down will not get it done either.

We finish 5-7, 2-7 in the SEC, and the season ends with a trophy we wanted sitting on the wrong side of the state. One more win and we'd be packing for a bowl. That's the part that stings tonight.

Here's the part I'm keeping: the first half, Bothwell's legs and Puckett's poise. Our cowbells will be loud again next November. Hail State.
