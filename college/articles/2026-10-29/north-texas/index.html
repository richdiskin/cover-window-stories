North Texas vs Florida Atlantic: Mean Green Survive 48-41 Shootout

Final: Florida Atlantic (5-3) 41, North Texas (6-2) 48, Thu., Oct. 29, DATCU Stadium, Denton. Next: vs. Rice, Sat., Nov. 7, 4:00 PM ET, DATCU Stadium, Denton

XFacebook

North Texas 48, Florida Atlantic 41. Five days after Denton's Day of the Dead Festival sent coffins racing downtown and lanterns glowing through the Twilight Lantern and Costume Parade, North Texas vs Florida Atlantic turned DATCU Stadium into its own haunted house on a wet Thursday night. The Owls kept rising from the grave. The Mean Green kept burying them anyway, and now we are 6-2, 4-0 in the American and bowl eligible for a third straight season.

Start with the quarterback, because Tayven Jackson earned the first paragraph and then some. He went 33 of 46 for 404 yards, four touchdown throws and zero interceptions, then ran five times for 41 more. That is not a game manager. That is a conductor. Our offense piled up 644 yards and 31 first downs, went 8 of 14 on third down and held the ball for 35:05. The Owls' offense spent a lot of the night watching from the bench.

Jahiem White ran 26 times for 193 yards and two scores, and his 59-yard sprint late in the second quarter put us back in front, 24-17. Justin Stevenson caught nine balls for 119 yards and a score. Kyle Koch matched him with nine grabs, went for 78 yards and scored twice, including the 2-yard dagger with 4:02 left that made it 48-38. Max Reese added seven catches for 85 yards, and Dalton Carnes turned three catches into 66.

Now the honest part, because a real fan does not hide the film. White put the ball on the turf twice, and Florida Atlantic recovered both. Easton Messer caught 16 balls for 189 yards and three touchdowns, and he caught every throw sent his way. Caden Veltkamp threw for 452 yards. Our secondary spent the night chasing a receiver who apparently cannot be tackled by rain, by brackets or by prayer, though I tried that last one.

But the defense made the two plays that decided it. Brayden Knox stripped Veltkamp on a sack in the third quarter, and we turned that into a 34-24 cushion. Peyton Taylor then intercepted Veltkamp near our goal in the fourth quarter and added seven tackles. Curlee Thomas IV got home for a sack too. Dominic De Freitas went 2 for 2 on field goals, including a 48-yarder, and the late onside kick did not go Florida Atlantic's way.

Five straight wins. A 4-0 league start for the first time since 2004, when we played in the Sun Belt. Rice comes to Denton for Homecoming on Nov. 7, and the coffins can stay in the garage. We are not done yet.
