Troy vs James Madison: Trojans Stun Dukes 24-21 in Overtime

Final: Troy (4-4) 24, James Madison (7-2) 21 (OT), Thu., Oct. 29, Bridgeforth Stadium, Harrisonburg. Next: vs. Louisiana Tech, Sat., Nov. 7, 4:00 PM ET, Veterans Memorial Stadium, Troy

Troy 24, James Madison 21, in overtime. On Tuesday night, TROYAPALOOZA brought the Janice Hawkins Cultural Arts Park a costume contest, a concert and fried Oreos. Two nights later, Troy vs James Madison gave us the best costume change of the week. The Trojans walked into Bridgeforth Stadium dressed as a team down 21-7 at halftime and walked out as the team that ended the Dukes' 11-game home winning streak.

Let me introduce Justin Powe to the rest of the country. He intercepted three throws, which ties Ronnie Shelley's single-game school record from 1968. The biggest came on the second play of the second half, when he took it back 37 yards for a touchdown. Just like that it was 21-14, and you could feel the whole stadium tighten up.

Then the defense took over. After the Dukes scored on their first three drives, they did not score again for the final 38:41. Troy forced five turnovers. Keno Jones made 13 tackles, recovered a fumble and, in overtime, stuffed Seth Cromwell on fourth-and-1 from the 5. Donnie Smith got his seventh sack of the season. That is how you avenge last year's Sun Belt Championship Game on the other team's own field.

On offense, we pounded the ground the way Troy football should. We ran it 45 times for 170 yards. Jaheim Merriweather broke a 28-yard touchdown run in the third quarter to tie it at 21-21, and he finished with 84 yards on 13 carries. Gavin Griffin opened our scoring with a 1-yard run on the first play of the second quarter, then gained 8 yards on third-and-6 in overtime to set up the kick. Tucker Kilcrease made his first start of the season and went 15 of 31 for 179 yards without an interception.

I will be fair to my own team. We went 0 for 3 on fourth down, and Hayden Wolfe missed from 49 yards late in regulation. But the kid came back in overtime and put a 27-yarder straight down the middle. It was his first field goal at Troy and our first walk-off kick in the final minute or overtime since 2020. Evan Crenshaw also averaged 45.6 yards on five punts.

We are 4-4 and 2-2 in the Sun Belt, and The Vet gets Homecoming against Louisiana Tech on Nov. 7. Save me a fried Oreo.
