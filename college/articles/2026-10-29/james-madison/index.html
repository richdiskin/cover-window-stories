James Madison vs Troy: Dukes Fall 24-21 in Overtime at Home

Final: Troy (4-4) 24, James Madison (7-2) 21 (OT), Thu., Oct. 29, Bridgeforth Stadium, Harrisonburg. Next: at Southern Miss, Thu., Nov. 5, 8:00 PM ET, M. M. Roberts Stadium, Hattiesburg

ShareXFacebook

Troy 24, James Madison 21, in overtime. James Madison vs Troy was our Brighten the Lights of Madison game, a white out named for a lyric from our own fight song, with white pompoms waiting on the general admission seats at Bridgeforth Stadium. For one half the lights were blinding. Then the Trojans flipped the switch, and the Dukes lost at home for the first time in 12 games.

The first half was everything we came for. Camden Coleman found Corey Scott for a 2-yard touchdown, then hit Braeden Wisloski for 31 more, and it was 14-0 after one quarter. Seth Cromwell finished a third straight scoring drive with a 14-yard run, and we went to the locker room up 21-7. Cromwell ended with 135 yards on 23 carries, his first 100-yard game as a Duke, and he earned every inch of it.

Then came the part I would rather skip, but a real fan reads the whole box score. We turned it over five times, three interceptions and two lost fumbles, and drew 10 flags for 71 yards. The second play of the second half was an interception that Justin Powe returned 37 yards for a touchdown. A strip later set up Troy's tying run. After that 21-7 halftime lead, we did not score again for the final 38:41.

The defense deserves better than this headline. Amar Thomas had two sacks and six tackles. Nakian Jackson, TJ McGill and Rasheed Lovelace each made seven stops. Troy went 0 for 3 on fourth down and missed a 49-yarder late in regulation. Coleman finished 14 of 25 for 175 yards and two touchdowns, and Michael Scott caught four balls for 68 yards. We outgained them 408 yards to 349. That should win at home. Not with five giveaways.

Overtime hurt the most. We got one first down, then got stopped on fourth-and-1 from the 5, and Troy kicked a 27-yarder to end it. That is two straight overtime losses, and the 11-game home streak is gone.

Here is the good news. We are 7-2, 4-2 in the Sun Belt and still first in the East. We go to Southern Miss on Thursday, Nov. 5, for another night game. Pack the white jerseys away for now. Start wearing purple again.
