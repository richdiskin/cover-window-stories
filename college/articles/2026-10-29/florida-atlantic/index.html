Florida Atlantic vs North Texas: Owls Fall 48-41 Despite Records

Final: Florida Atlantic (5-3) 41, North Texas (6-2) 48, Thu., Oct. 29, DATCU Stadium, Denton. Next: vs. UTSA, Thu., Nov. 5, 7:30 PM ET, Flagler Credit Union Stadium, Boca Raton

North Texas 48, Florida Atlantic 41. Florida Atlantic vs North Texas was our first game on ESPN since the 2020 Montgomery Bowl, and the Owls flew into Denton five days after the Rice shootout like they owned the whole sky. We did not get the win. We did get a night the record book will remember, and Boca Raton is not handing the sky back.

Easton Messer caught 16 balls for 189 yards and three touchdowns. Sixteen catches is the program record and tied for the most by any FBS player this season, and the man caught every throw that came his way. His 12-yard grab on fourth-and-4 with 39 seconds left in the half, slipping between two defenders, tied it 24-24. His 34-yarder with 8:26 to go pulled us within three. On our campus the burrowing owls dig in and refuse to move, and Messer played like one of them all night.

Caden Veltkamp went 37 of 56 for 452 yards and five touchdown throws, which ties the program record he has now matched three times. He also became the first Owl quarterback with more than one 400-yard game. Dominique Henry caught 10 balls for 116 yards, a career high, and Kaden Shields-Dutton touched it four times for 53 yards on one drive and finished it on a 22-yard shovel throw for a touchdown. Collin Rogers hit both field goals, including a 43-yarder with 1:56 left.

Here is the honest film session. Two turnovers flipped it. A third-quarter strip-sack set up the North Texas score that made it 34-24, and an interception near their goal in the fourth quarter cost us points we badly needed. The defense allowed 644 yards and 31 first downs, and North Texas held the ball for 35:05. Jahiem White ran for 193 yards. Joseph Sipp Jr. made eight tackles trying to plug it all by himself. When the onside kick bounced the wrong way, the clock did the rest.

We still went 10 of 18 on third down and 2 of 4 on fourth down, on the road, against the top team in the American. That is not a team that folds.

Now the Owls come home at 5-3. UTSA visits Flagler Credit Union Stadium on Thursday, Nov. 5, for Paradise Appreciation and Vice Night, our first Thursday home game since 2006 and the first ever at Flagler Credit Union Stadium. Bring the noise. Messer will bring the hands.
