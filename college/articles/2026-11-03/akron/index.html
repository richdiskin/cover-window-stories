Akron vs Ohio: Zips Outgain the MAC Leaders, Lose to Big Plays

Final: Ohio Bobcats (7-2) 28, Akron Zips (1-8) 20, Tue., Nov. 3, InfoCision Stadium, Akron. Next: vs Western Michigan, Tue., Nov. 10, 7:00 PM ET, InfoCision Stadium, Akron

By Hollister 'Zip' Grubbs, The Akron Fanatic

XFacebook

Ohio 28, Akron 20. We had the MAC leaders on the ropes Tuesday night at InfoCision Stadium, and three big plays knocked us back down. We're 1-8 and 0-5 in the league, and this one stings worse than most of them.

Look at the sheet. We ran 79 plays, piled up 392 yards, had 26 first downs to Ohio's 12 and held the ball for 35:46. Cibastian Broughton made his first start since getting hurt at Minnesota on Sept. 19 and went 22 of 34 for 232 yards. Jordan Gant ran for 112 yards on 16 carries. That's a team that came to fight.

And then the tires blew. DJ Walker jumped a Broughton throw and went 45 yards for a score with nine seconds left in the first quarter. That was Ohio's only first-half touchdown, and our offense gave it to them.

The defense kept us alive. Donelius Johnson recovered a Nick Poulos fumble at the Ohio 32, and Aidan Stire turned it into a 24-yard field goal for a 9-7 lead. Stire was 4 of 5 on the night, good from 42, 47, 24 and 43.

The fourth quarter is where this one hurts. Down 14-9, we went 77 yards in 12 plays, Gant punched it in from a yard out and Broughton hit Marcel Williams for two. Akron 17-14 with 9:35 left. The place was rocking. Thirteen seconds later Poulos hit Dom Dorwart for 60 yards, and it was 21-17 Ohio.

We answered again, 57 yards to the Ohio 9. Fourth down came up short, turnover on downs at 4:11. Next snap, Duncan Brune went 91 yards. Two plays, two drives, two gut punches. 28-17.

Game ball to Gant, who keeps running hard every week. Johnson had 9 tackles and 2 tackles for loss and set up points with that fumble recovery. Goat: the big play, again. Ohio scored on plays of 45, 60 and 91 yards. Take those away and we're talking about something else this morning.

Next: Western Michigan comes to InfoCision Stadium on Tuesday, Nov. 10, 7 PM ET on CBS Sports Network. Same plan, fewer leaks. Check the tread and roll.
