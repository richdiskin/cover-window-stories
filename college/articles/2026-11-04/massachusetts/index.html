Massachusetts vs Ball State: Pop Watson Runs It In and the Skid Is Dead

Final: Ball State Cardinals (3-6) 30, Massachusetts Minutemen (5-4) 31 (OT), Wed., Nov. 4, Warren McGuirk Alumni Stadium, Amherst. Next: at Toledo, Wed., Nov. 11, time TBA, Glass Bowl, Toledo

By Abner Kettleworth, The Massachusetts Fanatic

XFacebook

Thirteen yards. That's how far William Watson III had to go in overtime Wednesday night, and he went all of it. Derek Morris put the kick through, and UMass beat Ball State 31-30 at McGuirk. Four straight losses, buried. We're 5-4.

Count the holes we climbed out of. Down 7-0 after one quarter. Down 10-7 with 33 seconds left in the half. Down 17-14 in the third after we coughed up the ball at their end of the field. Every time, this team got back up off the mat. Three deficits, three answers.

The answer before halftime was the best one. Ball State kicks a field goal, Kezion Dia-Johnson brings the kickoff back 28 yards, and two snaps later Watson drops a 64-yarder on Jabari Lewis. Fourteen seconds, 64 yards, 14-10 at the break. That's how you walk into a locker room.

Then the third quarter. Watson hit Harding for 46 and Dia-Johnson for 47 on back-to-back plays, then ran it in himself from the 1. 21-17. Watson finished 17 of 25 for 302 yards, a career high per UMass Athletics. Dia-Johnson had 3 catches for 97. T.Y. Harding caught 6 for 70.

Now the part I'm not happy about. Harding put the ball on the ground twice, and the second one turned into a 42-yard Jalen Bonds touchdown run. Morris missed from 36 on our first drive. And we went up 24-17 with 2:14 left and still let Keldric Luster go 75 yards to tie it with 25 seconds on the clock. Close the door, fellas.

Overtime, though. Ball State scored first and missed the extra point, wide left. That's the opening. Watson took it 12 yards, then 13 more, and the place went nuts. Curtis Kimmons IV gave us 85 hard yards on 19 carries all night, Zeraun Daniel made 10 tackles, and Joshua Iseah sacked Luster twice.

UMass Athletics says five wins is the most for this program since 2011, and the most since the move to FBS. Read that again. Five.

Next Wednesday it's Toledo at the Glass Bowl, and those guys are 7-2. Hold onto the football, make the kicks, and let Pop cook.
