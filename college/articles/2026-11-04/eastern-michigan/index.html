Eastern Michigan vs Central Michigan: Eagles Get Steamrolled 35-7 at Ford Field

Final: Central Michigan Chippewas (4-5) 35, Eastern Michigan Eagles (4-6) 7, Wed., Nov. 4, Ford Field, Detroit. Next: at Western Michigan, Tue., Nov. 17, 7:00 PM ET, Waldo Stadium, Kalamazoo

By Imogen Tremblay, The Eastern Michigan Fanatic

ShareXFacebook

I'm not going to dress this one up. Central Michigan 35, Eastern Michigan 7, Wednesday night at Ford Field, in a game where we were the home team. We did the Eagle Walk at Brush Street and Adams Avenue, we brought the buses down from Ypsilanti, we tried to turn Detroit green. Then the Chippewas took the ball and kept it.

Here's the damage, straight off the stat sheet. Nine first downs. 177 yards of offense. 74 yards rushing on 21 carries. We went 1-10 on third down. We had the ball for 19:04 while they had it for 40:56. Mitten Football noted we didn't get our second first down until about 10 minutes were left in the game. That's not a slump. That's a stall.

Brogan McCaughey took the first snap and went 12 of 17 for 59 yards with an interception. Amareon Blue had 42 yards on 8 carries. Nobody could get going, and the guys up front got whipped all night.

Meanwhile CMU's Nick Woodford ran for three scores, and their third-quarter drive went 16 plays and 90 yards and ate 8:39. I watched our defense stand on that field so long they needed a change of address.

Credit where it's due. Bryce Llewellyn fought like crazy with 10 tackles, a sack and a tackle for loss. Bryce Eliuk made 10 tackles too. Daniel Hull boomed five punts for a 49.2 average, which tells you how often he was out there. Harold Mack caught a 6-yard touchdown with 10 seconds left to wreck the shutout, and good for him.

But we fell to 4-6, 3-3 in the MAC, and the math is simple now. Two games left, at Western Michigan and home against Kent State. Win both, get to six wins, go bowling. Lose either, and it's wait till next year.

Kalamazoo, Tuesday, Nov. 17, 7 PM ET. It's Western. If that doesn't light a fire in this locker room, we have bigger problems.
