Toledo vs Sacramento State: Miller and Ison Grind Out 20-17 and Keep the MAC Race Alive

Final: Toledo Rockets (7-2) 20, Sacramento State Hornets (2-8) 17, Wed., Nov. 4, Hornet Stadium, Sacramento. Next: vs Massachusetts, Wed., Nov. 11, time TBA, Glass Bowl, Toledo

By Rocco Szczepanski, The Toledo Fanatic

XFacebook

Eight yards. Sacramento State ran the ball 31 times Wednesday night and gained eight yards. Toledo Athletics says that's the fewest any Rocket opponent has managed since 2015. That's the story of Toledo 20, Sacramento State 17, and it's why we're flying home from California at 7-2 and 4-1 in the MAC.

Don't let the final fool you. This was not pretty. It wasn't. We trailed 10-7 at the half. John Alan Richter threw for 60 yards all night and threw an interception. Ugly. We went 5-15 on third down. Ugly again.

So we did what Toledo does. We handed it to CJ Miller and Autavius Ison and told them to go get it. Miller carried 27 times for 135 yards. Ison went for 101 on 15 carries, and his 19-yard run late in the third put us up 17-10 for good. Two backs, two hundreds, 230 yards on the ground. That's grown-man football.

The defense set the tone from the first series. Andrew Zock strip-sacked Jackson Sharman, Nathaniel Chandler fell on it at their 32, and five plays later Richter found Anthony Simpson for an 18-yard score. Toledo Athletics counted 12 tackles for loss and five sacks. Isaac Zay made 11 tackles. Chandler had a sack, two tackles for loss and the recovery.

Special teams pulled their weight too. Markus Meincke pinned them inside their own 5 to open the second half, we got the ball back at midfield, and Reice Griffith tied it from 34. He added a 40-yarder for 20-10 with 7:51 left.

I'll admit the last two minutes made me sweat. The Hornets went 75 yards for a touchdown with 2:20 left, then got it back at their own 16. Then our guys sacked Salazar, and on fourth-and-14 the throw got knocked down. Ballgame.

Toledo Athletics says we're tied with Western Michigan and Buffalo for second in the MAC with three to play. Next Wednesday, UMass comes into the Glass Bowl. Same recipe: feed the backs, squeeze the throat, go home happy.
