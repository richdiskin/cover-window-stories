Sacramento State vs Toledo: Hornets Push MAC Contender to the Final Snap, Fall 20-17

Final: Toledo Rockets (7-2) 20, Sacramento State Hornets (2-8) 17, Wed., Nov. 4, Hornet Stadium, Sacramento. Next: at Central Michigan, Wed., Nov. 11, 7:30 PM ET, Mount Pleasant

By Mateo Villaseñor, The Sacramento State Fanatic

XFacebook

Fourth-and-14, 55 seconds to work with, the ball in Brian Salazar Jr.'s hands, and it got knocked to the turf. That's how the home season ended for the Hornets. Toledo 20, Sacramento State 17. We're 2-8, 1-6 in the MAC, and I'm proud and ticked off in equal parts.

Proud first. Toledo is 7-2 and tied for second in this league, and we had them down 10-7 at halftime. Jackson Sharman went 8 of 15 for 113 and hit Matt Coleman from 2 yards out to cap a 10-play drive. Raiden Brown went up and got a 59-yard ball that bounced off a Toledo defender, which set up Grant Meadors from 38. Brown finished with 5 catches for 108.

The defense hit people too. Boogsie Silvera intercepted John Alan Richter in the first quarter, and Toledo threw for 60 yards the whole night. Tim Thomas had 7 tackles, a sack and two tackles for loss. Fata Puloka made 8 stops.

Now the ticked-off part. We ran it 31 times for eight yards. Eight. You can't win a football game like that against anybody, let alone a team that rams the ball down your throat for 230. Their two backs both topped 100. We gave Toledo a short field on our first possession with a strip-sack at our own 32, and they scored five plays later.

And the guys still didn't quit. Down 20-10 with 7:51 left, Jamar Curtis capped a 12-play, 75-yard drive from the 3 with 2:20 to go. The defense got the ball back. We just ran out of room from our own 16.

Credit to the seniors who played their last game in Sacramento, and to the new uniforms and hand-painted helmets made with the Wilton Rancheria tribe for Native American Heritage Month. Sharp look, and the effort matched it.

Next Wednesday it's a flight across the country to Mount Pleasant to face Central Michigan, 7:30 PM ET. Find a running game somewhere between here and Michigan, boys.
