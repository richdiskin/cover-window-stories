Ball State vs Massachusetts: One Missed Kick, a 31-30 Loss and a Long Ride Home

Final: Ball State Cardinals (3-6) 30, Massachusetts Minutemen (5-4) 31 (OT), Wed., Nov. 4, Warren McGuirk Alumni Stadium, Amherst. Next: vs Buffalo, Wed., Nov. 11, 7:30 PM ET, Scheumann Stadium, Muncie

By Gus Ponder, The Ball State Fanatic

XFacebook

One kick. We came into Amherst, punched UMass in the mouth for four quarters, scored first in overtime, and then the extra point sailed wide left. Watson ran 13 yards on the other end, they made their kick, and Ball State lost 31-30. We're 3-6, 2-3 in the MAC. I'm still steaming.

Here's what kills me. This was a game we should have won going away. Our guys forced two fumbles, Gabe Aramboles got one and Mikeah Webster got the other, and we didn't give it away once. Zero giveaways on the road and we still lost. Explain that one.

Explanation: flags. Ten penalties for 95 yards, and Ball State Athletics counted eight of them before halftime, including three straight on one UMass drive that handed them 30 yards and the tying score. Then Brody Boehm hits a 22-yarder to put us up 10-7 with 33 seconds left, and our defense gives up 64 yards in two snaps. Fourteen seconds. Brutal.

Now the good stuff, because there was plenty. Jalen Bonds was a grown man out there. 132 yards on 17 carries, a 42-yard touchdown run in the third and the overtime score from the 1. Ball State Athletics says it's the first 100-yard game of his career. It won't be the last.

And Keldric Luster, down 24-17 in the fourth with 2:14 left, takes us 75 yards in nine plays and walks in from the 2 with 25 seconds to go. That's a quarterback. He finished 17 of 27 for 182 and a touchdown to Jamarion McDougle, the opener from 20 yards out. Sterling Smith had 9 tackles. Webster had 7 and the strip.

But UMass threw for 302, outgained us 455 to 350, and we went 6-14 on third down. That's the game right there, plus one kick.

Buffalo comes to Scheumann Stadium next Wednesday, Nov. 11, 7:30 PM ET. Fill the place. Keep the flags in the pocket, and nobody blinks on the extra point.
