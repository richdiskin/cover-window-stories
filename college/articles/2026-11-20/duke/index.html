Duke vs Clemson: Nate Sheppard's 55-Yard Burst Not Enough as the Blue Devils Fall, 20-10

Final: Clemson Tigers (7-4) 20, Duke Blue Devils (7-4) 10, Fri., Nov. 20, Wallace Wade Stadium, Durham. Next: at Wake Forest, Sat., Nov. 28, time TBA, Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium, Winston-Salem

By Harrison Wexley-Moore IV, The Duke Fanatic

XFacebook

We had a drone show. We had a Heisman campaign. We had Nate Sheppard ripping off a 55-yard touchdown that made Wallace Wade Stadium shake. What we didn't have, sadly, was enough offense, and Clemson left Durham with a 20-10 win on Friday night. Duke is 7-4, 4-4 in the ACC.

First, our guy, because he deserves it. Sheppard came into the night leading the country in all-purpose yards, and he ran 19 times for 116 yards. The big one came in the third quarter, right after Clemson went up 10-0. He burst through the right side, dodged a diving tackle at the sideline, and was gone. 10-7, and the place came alive. Folks, that is a Heisman run, and I'll argue with anybody about it.

The defense gave us every chance. Andrew Pellicciotta was everywhere with 12 tackles, DaShawn Stone had eight and broke up two throws, and Kevin O'Connor made two tackles for loss. Evan Smith grabbed an interception in the first quarter, and we fell on a Clemson fumble in the second. Nobody scored until a Clemson field goal with 17 seconds left in the half, and our guys kept the Tigers out of the end zone for nearly 39 minutes.

The trouble was on our side of the ball. Walker Eget went 16 of 38 for 149 yards, and we went 6 for 16 on third down. Javen Nicholas did his part with six catches for 87 yards, and Jeremiah Hasley caught six for 55. After Clemson went up 17-7, Gavin Strange drilled a 46-yard field goal to make it a one-score game at 17-10. We got the ball back with a chance, but a blitzer came free on fourth down with 3:34 left and knocked the throw down.

Clemson owned the clock, 37:15 to 22:45, and ran 51 times. That's a lot of snaps for any defense to handle.

Here's the good news. Seven wins is seven wins, and this team still has a lot to play for.

Next up: at Wake Forest, Saturday, Nov. 28, to close the regular season against an old in-state foe ranked No. 10. Get on the road to Winston-Salem, Duke fans, and bring the noise. Go Duke!
