North Texas vs Tulane: Jahiem White and Brendon Haygood Run for 251, but the Mean Green Fall 28-24 in New Orleans

Final: Tulane Green Wave (6-5) 28, North Texas Mean Green (7-4) 24, Sat., Nov. 21, Yulman Stadium, New Orleans. Next: vs UAB, Sat., Nov. 28, 4:00 PM ET, DATCU Stadium, Denton

By Grady Mayhew-Ochoa, The North Texas Fanatic

XFacebook

Three quarterbacks. That's how many North Texas used on Saturday night in New Orleans, and a wide receiver was the last one standing. We still almost won. Tulane 28, Mean Green 24, and I don't know whether to be furious or proud. Both, I think.

Here's how it went down. Chris Jimerson Jr. made his first career start with Tayven Jackson hurt from last week. He was 8 of 16 for 124 yards before he went down with a non-contact injury early in the third quarter and didn't return. A freshman came in briefly. Then wide receiver James Tyre, a former high school quarterback, took the snaps, with some direct snaps to Brendon Haygood mixed in. Tyre went 4 of 7 for 63 yards. Wild.

And through all that chaos, we kept running the ball down Tulane's throat. Fifty-nine carries for 302 yards. Jahiem White ran 28 times for 148 yards. Haygood ran for 103 and two scores, including a 46-yard burst in the first quarter that put us up 14-0 and a 2-yarder in the fourth that made it 24-14. We held the ball for 37:27 and went 11 of 20 on third down. That's a team that refuses to quit.

Jimerson opened the scoring with a 6-yard run. Dominic De Freitas hit a 23-yard field goal for a 17-14 lead in the third. Max Reese caught six balls for 73 yards. Peyton Taylor picked off a throw and brought it back 27 yards. CJ Coombes made seven tackles.

Then Tulane hit two deep strikes in the final seven minutes, and that was that. With an emergency quarterback, we couldn't answer.

That's two straight losses after a 35-16 loss at UTSA last week snapped our six-game win streak. We're 7-4, 5-2 in league play, and we started the day sharing first place. The title picture is cloudy now, and I hate it.

But here's the thing. This team just played a road game with its fourth-string guy and a receiver taking the snaps and nearly stole it. That's heart. UAB comes to DATCU Stadium next Saturday for the finale, and the Mean Green faithful need to pack the place. Let's go, Mean Green.
