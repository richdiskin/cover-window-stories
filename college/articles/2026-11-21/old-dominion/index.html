Old Dominion vs UConn: Leon Haughton Jr. Scores Twice, Monarchs Fall 29-21

Final: UConn Huskies (6-5) 29, Old Dominion Monarchs (3-8) 21, Sat., Nov. 21, Pratt & Whitney Stadium, East Hartford. Next: vs Southern Miss, Sat., Nov. 28, 1:00 PM ET, S.B. Ballard Stadium, Norfolk

By Winifred 'Winnie' Pocklington, The Old Dominion Fanatic

XFacebook

Down 14-0 after one quarter in East Hartford, and my Monarchs kept swinging all the way to the final second. It ended 29-21, Huskies, and we're 3-8.

The second quarter was ours. Quinn Henicle hit Leon Haughton Jr. for a 17-yard touchdown on the first play of the quarter, and later he found Devin Roche for 33 yards and another score. Two touchdown throws, a 17-14 game, and we were right back in it. Henicle went 9 of 15 for 99 yards with those two scores.

Then Jaxon Potter came in and put on a clinic in his own right. He went 23 of 29 for 240 yards and kept moving the chains. With one second left, he hit Haughton from 6 yards out for the final touchdown. Haughton finished with seven catches, 62 yards and two scores.

Roche was terrific all night. Seven catches for 83 yards, that 33-yard touchdown, and 40 more on the ground. Josh Rodriguez had seven catches for 78, and Kendall Harris added four for 69. As a group, our quarterbacks went 32 of 45 for 339 yards. And we didn't give the ball away once.

So what went wrong? The ground game. We ran it 22 times for 53 yards, and UConn ran for 246. Every time we got close, their kicker made us pay. Three field goals from that guy. In an eight-point loss, that's the difference.

DeAndre Lynch was a wrecking ball on defense, with seven tackles and three and a half for loss. Zion Frink grabbed an interception. Daevon Iles led us with eight tackles. Mario Easterly had seven and two hurries.

I know 3-8 isn't what anybody in Norfolk wanted. But this team keeps playing hard, and that counts for plenty.

Next up: Southern Miss at S.B. Ballard Stadium, Saturday, Nov. 28, at 1:00 PM ET. Last game of the year, at home. Let's send the seniors out with a win. Go Monarchs!
