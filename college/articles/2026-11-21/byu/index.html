BYU vs Kansas: Bear Bachmeier Finds Jojo Phillips in the Third Overtime, Cougars Survive 31-29

Final: BYU Cougars (9-2) 31, Kansas Jayhawks (4-7) 29, Sat., Nov. 21, David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium, Lawrence. Next: vs Cincinnati, Sat., Nov. 28, 3:30 PM ET, LaVell Edwards Stadium, Provo

By Bryce Halvorsen-Tuilagi, The BYU Fanatic

XFacebook

Three overtimes in Lawrence. My heart rate is still somewhere back on I-70. Final: BYU 31, Kansas 29, and the Cougars are 9-2 with the playoff dream still alive.

It came down to one snap. Third overtime, both teams down to two-point tries, and Bear Bachmeier rolled around until he found Jojo Phillips in the back of the end zone. Then the Kansas try came up empty, and I let out a yell my neighbors will be talking about at church.

Let's be honest about how we got there. Regulation should have been ours. We led 16-6 going into the fourth after Bachmeier bulled in from 7 yards out, and the Jayhawks ripped off 10 straight points, the tying score coming on fourth down with four minutes left. Matthias Dunn then missed a 37-yarder as time ran out. Coach Kalani Sitake called it a mishit, and I'll leave it there, because Dunn also hit from 52, 36 and 26 and kept us alive all afternoon.

LJ Martin was the workhorse. Twenty-five carries, 127 yards and the touchdown that opened the second overtime. Bachmeier went 15 of 26 for 173 yards and ran for two scores, including the 2-yard answer in the first overtime after Kansas had punched first. Walker Lyons hauled in a 41-yarder, and Legend Glasker led the group with five grabs.

The defense deserves a statue. Cade Uluave piled up 10 tackles. Tausili Akana and Raider Damuni each came away with an interception off Isaiah Marshall, and the second one set up our first touchdown of the day. Jake Clifton had two tackles for loss. Kansas converted third downs and fourth downs all day, and our guys kept getting off the field when it counted most.

This was the first time in four tries that we beat the Jayhawks, and yes, I remember 2024 in Provo. Consider that one paid back, with interest and three extra periods.

Was it pretty? Not even close. Two lost fumbles, a missed kick at the gun and a whole lot of gray hair for this fan base. But Sitake said it best afterward: they just kept fighting back. So did we.

Cincinnati comes to Provo next Saturday for the regular-season finale. Bring the noise to LaVell Edwards Stadium, because a team chasing 10 wins deserves a full house. Rise and Shout.
