Sam Houston vs Middle Tennessee: Mason Holtzclaw Throws Three, Bearkats Come Up Just Short, 31-28

Final: Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders (6-5) 31, Sam Houston Bearkats (1-10) 28, Sat., Nov. 21, Elliott T. Bowers Stadium, Huntsville. Next: at Florida International, Sat., Nov. 28, 2:00 PM ET, Pitbull Stadium, Miami

By Lem Coxwell, The Sam Houston Fanatic

XFacebook

Our last drive ran out of room at the Middle Tennessee 36 with 14 seconds to go, and that's how close Sam Houston came on Saturday at Elliott T. Bowers Stadium. The Blue Raiders held on, 31-28. We're 1-10, 0-7 in Conference USA, and that's eight losses in a row.

Here's what I want everybody in Huntsville to know: this team didn't quit. We were down 21-7 in the second quarter and fought back to tie it 21-21. Mason Holtzclaw was sharp, 16 of 25 for 191 yards and three touchdowns, to Chris Reed from 15, Darrick Bledsoe III from 24 and Tim Burns Jr. from 30.

The defense took the ball away four times. Ishmel Atkins and Railyn Adams each intercepted Roman Gagliano, and we recovered two fumbles. Four takeaways in one afternoon! Dean Ford led us with seven tackles, and Caesar Magee broke up two throws.

Brett Holloway got us started with a 3-yard touchdown run in the first quarter. Reed's catch came with 10 seconds left in the half, and Bledsoe's tied it midway through the third. Grady O'Neill caught seven balls for 65 yards, and Alton McCaskill ran 15 times for 65.

What hurt was one play. Right after we tied it, Terrence Brown went 75 yards untouched. Then we came up short on fourth down at our own 34, and they kicked a field goal. Holtzclaw found Burns from 30 to make it 31-28 with 5:35 left, and we got the ball back with a chance. It ended on downs.

We converted 8 of 16 third downs. We didn't lose a fumble. A lot went right. One play and one fourth down went wrong, and that's football.

One more game. We're at Florida International on Saturday, Nov. 28, at 2:00 PM ET at Pitbull Stadium in Miami to close the season. Go get win number two, Bearkats. Eat 'Em Up, Kats!
