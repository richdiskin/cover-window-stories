UCLA vs Michigan: Bruins Fall in Triple Overtime, First Loss at 10-1

Final: Michigan Wolverines (6-5) 18, UCLA Bruins (10-1) 16, Sat., Nov. 21, Michigan Stadium, Ann Arbor. Next: vs USC, Sat., Nov. 28, 3:30 PM ET, Rose Bowl, Pasadena

By Kensington 'Kenzie' Rhee, The UCLA Fanatic

XFacebook

Bruin fans, the perfect season is done, and it took Michigan three overtimes to do it. Michigan 18, UCLA 16 on Saturday in Ann Arbor. We're 10-1 and 7-1 in the Big Ten, and I need a minute.

Okay. Minute's up. Here's what happened.

We spent ten games winning the turnover battle. That's been our whole identity in Coach Chesney's first year. In Ann Arbor we coughed it up three times and took it away once. An interception at the Michigan 11 in the first quarter. A sack-fumble right before halftime. Another fumble at the end of the third. You can't hand a team in its own stadium extra chances and expect to walk away a winner.

And yet we almost did. Down 10-3 with the clock dying, we went to the bag of tricks. Anthony Woods took a direct snap, ran at the pile and flipped a 1-yard jump throw to Brayden Loftin with 10 seconds left. Tied 10-10. I thought we'd just stolen a win in Ann Arbor.

Mateen Bhaghani was perfect. Three for three, from 44, 38 and 38 yards, and the last two came in overtime with the whole game riding on them. That kid has ice in his veins.

The hardest part was watching Ty Dieffenbach get carted off in overtime after getting hurt on a run. He'd gone 19 of 30 for 188 yards. Madden Iamaleava came in cold, and his brother Nico was our No. 1 quarterback until he got hurt last month. This team has been through a lot. Get well, Ty.

The defense deserves better than a loss. Malik Hartford and Donavyn Pellot each made nine tackles. Aiden Gobaira had seven tackles, a sack and two and a half tackles for loss. Michigan finished with 247 yards and converted 5 of 15 third downs. Our guys did their job.

In the third overtime, Michigan stopped our two-point try and hit theirs. That's football. It stings, but one loss doesn't erase ten wins.

And now the best possible way to bounce back. Next up: USC at the Rose Bowl, Saturday, Nov. 28, at 3:30 PM ET. Rivalry week, at home in Pasadena. Let's make it loud. Go Bruins!
