Houston vs West Virginia: Freshman Keisean Henderson and Jonathan Dimas Win It at the Horn, 34-31

Final: Houston Cougars (8-3) 34, West Virginia Mountaineers (5-6) 31, Sat., Nov. 21, Milan Puskar Stadium, Morgantown. Next: vs Baylor, Sat., Nov. 28, 9:30 PM ET, TDECU Stadium, Houston

By Yolanda 'Yo' Pacheco, The Houston Fanatic

XFacebook

Coogs, the kid has arrived. Keisean Henderson made his first start on Saturday in Morgantown, filling in for an injured Conner Weigman, and he walked out of Milan Puskar Stadium a winner when Jonathan Dimas drilled a 23-yard field goal as time ran out. Houston 34, West Virginia 31. We're 8-3, 5-3 in the Big 12, and my voice is gone.

Henderson hadn't played in two months. His very first throw, on our third snap, went 68 yards to a wide-open Amare Thomas for a touchdown. Hello, Big 12. He finished 18 of 28 for 268 yards without a turnover, and the freshman never once looked rattled in a loud road stadium.

Then there's Makhi Hughes. Nineteen carries, 158 yards, three touchdowns, a season high, with a 71-yard burst mixed in. He scored from 21, from 2 and from 3, and that last one is the one I'll be telling people about at Thanksgiving.

Because this one got scary. West Virginia ran right at us for 329 yards and led 31-21 early in the third quarter. Dimas got us a 34-yard field goal late in the third to make it 31-24. Then Henderson took off for 8 yards and then 9 more himself, and Hughes punched it in from the 3. Tied 31-31 with 5:25 left.

That's when our defense found its feet. On its last five drives, West Virginia missed a 42-yard field goal and punted four times. Jaden Yates was everywhere with 16 tackles and two and a half for loss, and Sione Fotu chipped in nine. On the last of those punts, Trent Walker brought it back 22 yards to the West Virginia 38. We pushed it inside the 5, and Dimas did the rest.

We went 9 of 19 on third down, never gave the ball away and bounced right back from that double-digit loss at Colorado last week. That's a grown-up road win, led by a freshman making his first start.

Baylor comes to TDECU Stadium for the regular-season finale on Saturday, Nov. 28, at 9:30 PM ET. Third Ward, bring the noise for the freshman. Go Coogs!
