Illinois vs Iowa: Katin Houser Throws for 318 Through a Broken Hand, Illini Fall, 34-17

Final: Iowa Hawkeyes (9-2) 34, Illinois Fighting Illini (6-5) 17, Sat., Nov. 21, Gies Memorial Stadium, Champaign. Next: at Northwestern, Sat., Nov. 28, 12:30 PM ET, Ryan Field, Evanston

By Margery Vandermolen, The Illinois Fanatic

XFacebook

If you want to know what toughness looks like, watch Katin Houser. Playing through a broken hand and a broken nose, he threw for 318 yards and two touchdowns against the best scoring defense in the country. It still wasn't enough, and Iowa beat Illinois 34-17 at Gies Memorial Stadium. We're 6-5, 4-4 in the Big Ten.

For a half, we had the Hawkeyes rattled. Brayden Trimble caught a short one, tiptoed down the sideline, got sprung by an Eddie Kasper block and went 75 yards to tie it 10-10. Then Houser found Davin Stoffel from 11 with seven seconds left before halftime, and it was 17-17. That was more points than any of Iowa's previous four opponents had managed in an entire game.

Collin Dixon was terrific, eight catches for 142 yards. Trimble finished with four for 121. When Houser had a second to breathe, he made plays.

But we ran the ball 17 times for 28 yards, and you can't live like that against Iowa. This offense is missing a lot of people. Hudson Clement is out for the season, and so are Ca'Lil Valentine and Javari Barnett. In the second half we punted three times in the third quarter, got stopped on fourth down twice and threw an interception that Jaylen Watson ran back 40 yards for a touchdown.

Iowa ground out a 19-play, 91-yard drive that ate 10 minutes, and that was the ballgame. Our defense still fought. Matthew Bailey made 11 tackles, nine of them solo, Ben Clawson and James Kreutz had eight each, and Ismael Kante broke up two throws. Ethan Moczulski hit a 39-yard field goal in the first quarter.

That's two straight losses after a four-game winning streak, and it stings. But we're 6-5 with one more Saturday to play.

We're at Northwestern on Saturday, Nov. 28, at 12:30 PM ET at Ryan Field, and the Land of Lincoln Trophy is on the table. Go take it home to Champaign. Oskee-wow-wow!
