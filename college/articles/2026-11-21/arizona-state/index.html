Arizona State vs Oklahoma State: Harris and Fette Build a 24-7 Lead, Sun Devils Fall in Overtime 30-27

Final: Oklahoma State Cowboys (7-4) 30, Arizona State Sun Devils (6-5) 27, Sat., Nov. 21, Mountain America Stadium, Tempe. Next: at Arizona, Sat., Nov. 28, 9:00 PM ET, Casino Del Sol Stadium, Tucson

By Skylar Vanterpool, The Arizona State Fanatic

XFacebook

That one hurts because we had it. The Arizona State Sun Devils led the Oklahoma State Cowboys 24-7 at halftime on Saturday night at Mountain America Stadium, and we lost 30-27 in overtime. Our record sits at 6-5 and 4-4 in league play, and a night that felt like a launch turned into a long walk to the parking lot.

The first half was everything I wanted. After William Mason's 13-yard run put the Cowboys up 7-0, Jake Fette found Reed Harris for a 1-yard touchdown, kept it himself from a yard out, and hit Harris on a 60-yard scoring throw. Smith drilled a 51-yard field goal as time expired in the second quarter, and it was 24-7. Harris caught 11 balls for 172 yards and two scores. Fette went 17 of 28 for 232 yards and those two touchdown throws. We converted all five of our fourth downs. Martell Hughes picked off Drew Mestemaker. This was our building.

We just couldn't put it away. Caleb Hawkins scored from 9 yards, Sam Keltner made a 25-yarder, and Hawkins punched in a 7-yard run with 9:11 left, and just like that it was 24-24. Smith put us back in front 27-24 from 38 yards at 3:12, which is the kick I wanted in that moment. Keltner tied it again from 43 at 1:11.

In overtime, Keltner made a 35-yard field goal, 30-27. We got the ball, and Hammonds came down with an interception at the Oklahoma State 21. Cardae Mack had run for 66 yards on 16 carries and given us tough yards all night. The last snap went the other way. That's a cruel way to end it.

Coach Kenny Dillingham said of Fette, "He thinks that game's on him and it's not on him at all. The kid played really, really good football. There's so many things that could have changed the outcome of that game, not just the final play." I'll second that. This loss is on more than one throw.

Owen Long and Hughes each made 12 tackles. We did enough good work to win. We didn't do it for 60 minutes, and then we didn't do it in overtime either.

Next up: at Arizona, Saturday, Nov. 28, at 9:00 PM ET at Casino Del Sol Stadium in Tucson. The Territorial Cup is sitting there. Forks up. Let's go get that one.
