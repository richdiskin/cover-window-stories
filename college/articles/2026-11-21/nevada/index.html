Nevada vs Hawai'i: Fernandez Kicks Three, Bianco Scores, Wolf Pack Holds On 16-8

Final: Nevada Wolf Pack (5-6) 16, Hawai'i Rainbow Warriors (3-8) 8, Sat., Nov. 21, Mackay Stadium, Reno. Next: at UNLV, Sat., Nov. 28, 9:00 PM ET, Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas

By Ridge Corbell, The Nevada Fanatic

XFacebook

The score was ugly, and I loved every bit of it. The Nevada Wolf Pack beat the Hawai'i Rainbow Warriors 16-8 at Mackay Stadium in Reno, and we're 5-6, 4-3 in the Mountain West, one win from being bowl eligible. If you wanted pretty, you came to the wrong high-desert Saturday.

Jacob Fernandez did the early work. He made a 24-yard field goal in the second quarter after a long drive stalled, then hit a 45-yarder with 12 seconds left in the half for a 6-0 lead. Hawai'i's Sean Olvera-Harle missed from 49 yards twice. That's the kind of swing you feel in your seat.

We opened the third quarter the way I wanted. Herschel Turner ripped off a 48-yard run, and five plays later AJ Bianco scored from 5 yards to make it 13-0. Turner ran 13 times for 73 yards. Bianco went 8 of 18 for 106 yards and added 55 yards on 14 carries. We finished with 311 yards. They had 182. The Wolf Pack ran it, and they couldn't stop us from doing it.

We had a scare in the third. Gerick Robinson muffed a punt, and Sitani Mikaele recovered it at our 13. Hawai'i got down close and still couldn't finish the drive at 5:40 of the third. I started breathing again. Isaiah Jordan later intercepted Bjorn Jurgensen, and Fernandez added a 38-yarder with 2:25 left, 16-0. Fernandez was 3 for 3.

Then it got sweaty. Titan Lacaden returned the next kickoff 53 yards, and Jurgensen found him from 14 yards with 1:38 to go. The two-point throw to Tama Uiliata made it 16-8. Their onside kick went 9 yards, we fell on it, and we knelt it out. I'll take a win that makes me sweat in November. Those are the ones you remember at Mackay.

Next up: at UNLV, Saturday, Nov. 28, at 9:00 PM ET at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas. The Cannon is sitting there, and a bowl berth is sitting there with it. Win that one. Go Pack!
