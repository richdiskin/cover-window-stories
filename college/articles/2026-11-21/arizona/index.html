Arizona vs Kansas State: Noah Fifita Wins It in Overtime in the Manhattan Rain, 35-28

Final: Arizona Wildcats (8-3) 35, Kansas State Wildcats (4-7) 28, Sat., Nov. 21, Bill Snyder Family Stadium, Manhattan. Next: vs Arizona State, Sat., Nov. 28, 9:00 PM ET, Casino Del Sol Stadium, Tucson

By Inez Paloverde, The Arizona Fanatic

XFacebook

Desert kids don't know rain, they said. Tell that to Noah Fifita. In a cold, driving downpour at Bill Snyder Family Stadium, Arizona came back from 11 points down in the last 11 minutes and won it in overtime. Arizona 35, Kansas State 28. Arizona sits at 8-3, 5-3 in Big 12 play, and I'm still wringing out my socks.

It looked bleak. With 10:55 left, Fifita couldn't handle a snap at our 2 after a great Kansas State punt, and Wendell Gregory recovered it for a touchdown. 28-17, and their whole Senior Day crowd was roaring in the rain. My buddy started checking flight times.

Then Fifita went to work. Michael Salgado-Medina knocked in a 26-yard field goal to make it 28-20. We got the ball right back, and with 1:52 left Fifita found Isaiah Mizell for a 35-yard touchdown. Then he hit Giovanni Richardson for the two-point try, and it was 28-28. I hugged a guy from Tucson I'd never met.

In overtime, Fifita and Richardson did it again, a 25-yard strike, and then our defense stood up Kansas State's quarterback on fourth down at the 3. That's the ballgame. Fifita finished 29 of 42 for 323 yards and two touchdowns, plus an 8-yard touchdown run and 58 rushing yards. Richardson caught 12 balls for 88 yards. Mizell had five catches for 122. Kedrick Reescano scored from a yard out in the second quarter.

The defense kept us alive on a miserable night. Mays Pese and Taye Brown made nine tackles apiece, Cam Chapa came up with an interception, and Leroy Palu got a sack. We also blocked a punt early in the third quarter that set up Salgado-Medina's 33-yarder. Our three losses all came against Utah, Texas Tech and BYU, the three teams ahead of us in the Big 12. Nobody else has beaten us.

Eight wins and the Territorial Cup coming to Tucson. Bear Down, and dry your shoes. We've got Arizona State to handle next Saturday night.
