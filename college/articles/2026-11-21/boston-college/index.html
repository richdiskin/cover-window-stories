Boston College vs Syracuse: Bo MacCormack III Runs for 236 Against the School He Spurned, Eagles Win 27-15

Final: Boston College Eagles (5-6) 27, Syracuse Orange (5-6) 15, Sat., Nov. 21, Alumni Stadium (Chestnut Hill, MA), Chestnut Hill. Next: at Miami, Sat., Nov. 28, 12:00 PM ET, Hard Rock Stadium, Miami Gardens

By Siobhan Dunleavy, The Boston College Fanatic

XFacebook

Bo MacCormack III once committed to Syracuse. Then he flipped to Boston College in November of his senior year of high school. On Saturday, on senior day at Alumni Stadium, he ran for 236 yards against the Orange, and the Eagles won 27-15. Best call that kid ever made, if you ask me.

Thirty carries. Two hundred thirty-six yards. And the dagger came in the third quarter, with us clinging to a 13-9 lead, when Bo split the middle and went 56 yards with three Orange defenders chasing his jersey. Coach Bill O'Brien said Bo is what this place is all about, one of the most improved guys on the team. You won't get an argument from me.

Mason McKenzie was steady. He went 14 of 24 for 131 yards and two scores without a turnover. He rolled left on our opening drive, an 81-yard march, and flipped a touchdown to tight end Zeke Moore. In the fourth, he found Nedrick Boldin Jr. from 22 yards out to make it 27-15. Luca Lombardo drilled field goals from 46 and 32, the second one at the halftime gun.

Our defense did what it had to. Syracuse threw for 406 yards and got deep into our end again and again, and we kept them to field goals. Charlie Comella had nine tackles and two for loss. Marcus Upton added 1.5 tackles for loss and broke up two throws. Zero turnovers on our side, too.

Comella wore No. 31 on Saturday, the jersey BC hands out every senior day in memory of Jay McGillis, the defensive back who died of leukemia in 1992 at age 21. That's the kind of thing that makes this place special, and Comella honored it.

We snapped a three-game skid after a rough day at Notre Dame last week. We're 5-6 and 3-5 in the ACC. Bowl hopes are still alive, barely.

One more to get there: a trip to Miami next Saturday at noon. It'll take a big upset in Hard Rock Stadium. Bo, start stretching now. Ever to Excel, Eagles.
