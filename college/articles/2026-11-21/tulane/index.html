Tulane vs North Texas: Trace Johnson Throws Four Scores, Freshman Cameron Roberts Wins It Late, Green Wave Go Bowling 28-24

Final: Tulane Green Wave (6-5) 28, North Texas Mean Green (7-4) 24, Sat., Nov. 21, Yulman Stadium, New Orleans. Next: at South Florida, Sat., Nov. 28, 3:30 PM ET, Raymond James Stadium, Tampa

By Antoine 'Twan' Ducros-Labat, The Tulane Fanatic

XFacebook

Tulane didn't lead for one second of this game until there were 2:13 left on the clock. That's when Trace Johnson dropped a back-shoulder throw into the hands of Cameron Roberts at the front of the end zone, Yulman Stadium lost its mind, and the Green Wave beat North Texas 28-24 to become bowl eligible. Roll Wave, baby!

Let me tell you about Cameron Roberts. Freshman tight end. Came into Saturday with five catches all season. Left with four catches for 81 yards and two touchdowns, the 6-yarder that tied it at halftime and the 28-yard winner. Somebody get that kid a po'boy and a parade.

That winning drive was four plays and 87 yards. Johnson hit Roberts for 11, found Oliver Mitchell Jr. for 12, then went deep to Roberts for 36, and then the dagger. Four plays! I barely had time to stand up.

Johnson was terrific all night: 17 of 21 for 283 yards and four touchdowns. He hit Jaylin Lucas for a 69-yard strike in the first quarter to get us on the board after we fell behind 14-0, and he found Destyn Hill for 37 yards to cut a 24-14 deficit to three with 6:52 left. Hill had two catches for 44, and Lucas had three for 79.

North Texas pounded the ball, no doubt. They ran it 59 times for 302 yards and held it for more than 37 minutes. But our defense came up with the big ones. Joshua Moore and E'zaiah Shine each intercepted a throw. Makai Williams had two sacks. Dickson Agu was everywhere, with 13 tackles. North Texas went through three quarterbacks before the night was done, and our guys kept swarming.

Tulane football: down two touchdowns early, down 10 in the fourth, and still finding a way. That's the kind of team that's fun to follow in November.

We're 6-5 and 4-3 in the American. Bowl eligible. Next Saturday we head to Tampa to face South Florida to close the regular season. Take care of business in Tampa and we'll be choosing a nice December destination. Roll on, Wave.
