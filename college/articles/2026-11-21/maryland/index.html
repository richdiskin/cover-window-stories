Maryland vs USC: DeJuan Williams Scores Twice, but Terps Fall at the Coliseum, 35-21

Final: USC Trojans (8-3) 35, Maryland Terrapins (4-7) 21, Sat., Nov. 21, Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum, Los Angeles. Next: vs Penn State, Sat., Nov. 28, 3:30 PM ET, SECU Stadium, College Park

By Abigail 'Abby' Chesapeake, The Maryland Fanatic

XFacebook

Terps, I flew home from Los Angeles with my red shell jacket zipped all the way up and my pride mostly intact. For one half, Maryland was the better team in the Coliseum. We went into the locker room leading 14-13, and the home crowd was booing their own team. The final says USC 35, Maryland 21, and we're 4-7, 2-6 in the Big Ten. But I saw a team that believes.

DeJuan Williams set the tone. On the first snap of the second quarter he busted a 52-yard touchdown run, and the handful of us in red went nuts. Later in the quarter we went 15 plays and Williams finished it from a yard out with 26 seconds left in the half. He ran 17 times for 83 yards and two scores.

Malik Washington was back running the show after missing last week, and he threw for 251 yards on 24 completions. Kaleb Webb and Preston Howard caught six balls apiece. And in the third quarter, Washington hit Chris Durr Jr. for a 67-yard touchdown that should have made it a two-score game. Flag. Illegal formation. Touchdown gone. USC drove for a score, and the whole night tilted.

That's when it got ugly. A tipped ball got intercepted, and USC scored. Then another interception on the very next snap, and another USC touchdown. In about five minutes, a one-point lead turned into 35-14. We threw three interceptions on the night, and you can't do that against a ranked team in its own building.

Lavain Scruggs was everywhere on defense with 12 tackles, nine solo and two for loss. Carlton Smith and Daniel Wingate each made seven. And Washington, who took a hit to his knee in the fourth, came back and scored on a 4-yard run himself late. That's toughness.

This team had won two straight before this trip, and nobody quit out there. Penn State comes to College Park next, and I want SECU Stadium packed and loud for the seniors. Fear the Turtle, today and always.
