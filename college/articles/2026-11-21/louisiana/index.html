Louisiana vs Coastal Carolina: Micah Johnson's Scoop-and-Score and Lunch Winfield's Four Touchdowns, 38-24

Final: Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns (8-3) 38, Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (4-7) 24, Sat., Nov. 21, Our Lady of Lourdes Stadium, Lafayette. Next: at Georgia State, Sat., Nov. 28, 2:00 PM ET, Center Parc Stadium, Atlanta

By Amélie 'Mimi' Fontenot, The Louisiana Fanatic

XFacebook

Micah Johnson didn't wait around. On the second snap of the game at Our Lady of Lourdes Stadium, he scooped up a Coastal Carolina fumble and rumbled 24 yards for a touchdown, and Louisiana never trailed. Ragin' Cajuns 38, Coastal Carolina 24, and we're 8-3, 5-2 in the Sun Belt.

That's three straight wins, and Lunch Winfield is the reason we pulled away. He went 14 of 20 for 229 yards and three touchdowns, and he ran for another from 6 yards out. Fourteen completions, four touchdowns. Efficient doesn't begin to describe it.

Winfield found a different guy every time: a 1-yard touchdown to Caden Jensen in the second quarter, a 27-yarder to KeDarius Wade in the third and a 4-yarder to Brock Chappell late in the fourth. Wade had three catches for 79 yards. JJ Garner ran 11 times for 90 yards, including a 55-yard burst that had me out of my seat.

The defense came to eat. Harrison Kidder had a sack, an interception and eight tackles. Avery Demery intercepted another one. Johnson added a sack to his touchdown, and Collin Jacob and Maurion Eleam each got after the quarterback too. Coastal Carolina gave it away three times, and we only gave it back once.

We led 21-10 at halftime and 38-17 with about five minutes left. Tony Sterner made all five extra points and a 36-yard field goal. Shelton Sampson Jr. had two catches for 50 yards. We needed only 14 first downs to put up 38, because when this offense hits, it hits big.

And we're perfect at home this year, 6-0 in Lafayette. Nobody walks into our place and leaves happy, and 19,367 of our people were in the house to see this one.

Next up: at Georgia State on Saturday, Nov. 28, at 2:00 PM ET at Center Parc Stadium in Atlanta. Take this show on the road and bring home number nine. Geaux Cajuns!
