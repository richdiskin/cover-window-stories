UNLV vs San José State: Jackson Arnold Does It Both Ways, Rebels Hang On, 24-22

Final: UNLV Rebels (7-4) 24, San José State Spartans (5-7) 22, Sat., Nov. 21, CEFCU Stadium, San Jose. Next: vs Nevada, Sat., Nov. 28, 9:00 PM ET, Allegiant Stadium

XFacebook

Forty-five seconds. That's how long I held my breath on Saturday night in San Jose. San José State had just scored to pull within two, their quarterback dropped back for the two-point try, and the ball hit the turf. UNLV 24, San José State 22. We're 7-4, 5-2 in the Mountain West, and my heart rate is finally coming back down.

Jackson Arnold carried this team. He threw for 197 yards on 17 of 27, including a 38-yard rocket to Keyan Burnett in the second quarter that put us back in front 14-10. He led us in rushing too, with 66 yards on 16 carries, and he finished the job himself with a 1-yard plunge with 16 seconds left in the third quarter. That made it 24-13, and it turned out we needed every point.

Jai'Den Thomas got us started with a 9-yard touchdown run in the first quarter. Taeshaun Lyons was Arnold's go-to target all night and caught nine balls for 85 yards. Ramon Villela drilled a 32-yard field goal with 34 seconds left in the half, and we went to the locker room up 17-13.

The defense made the plays that won it. Andre Porter intercepted Luke Weaver and also got a piece of a sack. Jake Pope made nine tackles, and Cam Santee had seven with a tackle and a half for loss. San José State ran for just 56 yards on 23 tries. When their kicker kept trotting out for field goals instead of touchdowns, I knew we were in decent shape.

Was it pretty at the end? No. Weaver hit Carson Clark for a 30-yarder with 45 seconds left, and I aged five years. But a road win is a road win, and we held the ball for more than 34 minutes.

Now it's Nevada at home with the Fremont Cannon up for grabs, in our house. Seven wins and counting. Paint that cannon red, Rebels.
