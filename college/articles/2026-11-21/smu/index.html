SMU vs Notre Dame: Kendrick Raphael Breaks a 70-Yarder, Mustangs Stall in South Bend, 24-3

Final: Notre Dame Fighting Irish (10-1) 24, SMU Mustangs (7-4) 3, Sat., Nov. 21, Notre Dame Stadium, Notre Dame. Next: at Stanford, Sat., Nov. 28, time TBA, Stanford Stadium, Stanford

By Tatum Delacroix-Price, The SMU Fanatic

XFacebook

Pony fans, I've watched this team score on just about everybody for years. Then came Saturday night in South Bend. Notre Dame 24, SMU 3. Three points. It's the fewest we've scored in a game since 2016, and we're 7-4.

Here's the part that makes me want to throw my hat across the room. We moved the ball. Kendrick Raphael broke a 70-yard run and finished with 100 yards on just 12 carries. Yamir Knight caught nine balls for 115 yards, including a 41-yarder. Six of our drives died in Notre Dame territory. All we got out of the whole night was one Brendan Baird field goal from 40 yards.

Three times we got stopped on fourth down, at the Irish 19, the 3 and the 2. The 2! Late in the second quarter we were at their 24 and Kevin Jennings threw one into the end zone that their safety went way up and took away. That's 14 points, maybe more, left on that field. Jennings finished 24 of 45 for 216 yards, and he took some big hits.

Our defense did its job for a long stretch. Alexander Kilgore made 12 tackles. Jayvon Thomas intercepted CJ Carr, and Sael Reyes recovered a fumble. After a 21-yard touchdown catch gave Notre Dame a 14-3 lead in the second quarter, we held the Irish to 10 more points. Against a top-10 team, on their field, that's a respectable night for the defense. The offense just couldn't cash a single trip inside the 20.

That's two straight weeks with fewer than 10 points, and this team is better than that. Way better. We've got one more regular-season game, at Stanford next Saturday, and I want to see the old SMU show up out there.

Eight wins is still on the board. Pony Up, Mustangs. Go take it.
