Western Kentucky vs Liberty: Brock Glenn Keeps Swinging, but the Bowl Streak Ends in Lynchburg, 45-14

Final: Liberty Flames (10-1) 45, Western Kentucky Hilltoppers (4-7) 14, Sat., Nov. 21, Williams Stadium (VA), Lynchburg. Next: vs Kennesaw State, Sat., Nov. 28, 1:00 PM ET, Houchens Industries-L.T. Smith Stadium, Bowling Green

By Thurman 'Tops' Whitsitt, The Western Kentucky Fanatic

XFacebook

Seven straight seasons of bowl games with Tyson Helton in charge, and that run ended on Saturday in Lynchburg. Liberty beat Western Kentucky 45-14 at Williams Stadium, and at 4-7, 3-4 in Conference USA, the Hilltoppers can't reach six wins this year. I'm not going to pretend that doesn't sting.

The game was decided early. Liberty scored on its first offensive play, an 81-yard throw, and led 21-0 after one quarter and 28-0 at halftime. The Flames converted 9 of 12 third downs, and we just couldn't get them off the field when it still mattered.

But I'm proud of how this group kept swinging. Brock Glenn threw 47 times, completed 24 for 273 yards and didn't throw an interception, even while getting sacked three times. On the last play of the third quarter, he found Coleson Arends for a 25-yard touchdown to get us on the board. Then in the fourth he ran one in himself from 16 yards out.

Zion Taylor led us with six catches for 68 yards. Jyziah Rockwell caught three for 64, with a 37-yarder, and Moussa Barry had three for 55. We went 3 for 6 on fourth down because we weren't about to stop trying.

The defense took the ball away twice, recovering two Liberty fumbles. Jaylen Wester was everywhere with 11 tackles and two for loss, and Jaylen Pollard added nine. We didn't give it away once.

Let's be honest about the hard part. We ran 29 times for 54 yards. You can't live on one side of the offense against a team that's won 10 straight. A week ago we beat Jacksonville State, so we know what this team looks like when it's rolling.

One more Saturday on the Hill. Kennesaw State comes to Houchens Industries-L.T. Smith Stadium on Nov. 28 at 1:00 PM ET for the finale. Send the red towels flying for this group one last time. Go Tops!
