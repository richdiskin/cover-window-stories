Notre Dame vs SMU: Jordan Faison's One-Hander and a Brick-Wall Defense, Irish Win 24-3

Final: Notre Dame Fighting Irish (10-1) 24, SMU Mustangs (7-4) 3, Sat., Nov. 21, Notre Dame Stadium, Notre Dame. Next: at Syracuse, Sat., Nov. 28, 12:00 PM ET, JMA Wireless Dome, Syracuse

By Declan Moriarty-Chen, The Notre Dame Fanatic

XFacebook

Six SMU drives died in our territory on Saturday night, every one of them stuffed by a Notre Dame defense that was flat-out mean. Notre Dame 24, SMU 3 in South Bend, and the Irish are 10-1 and perfect at home. Touchdown Jesus approves.

Three of those stands came on fourth down, at our 19, our 3 and our 2. You want a defense that plays with an attitude? That's it. SMU hadn't scored this few points since 2016. Boubacar Traore was in their backfield all night with two and a half tackles for loss. Adon Shuler got a sack. Joey O'Brien made seven tackles. And late in the second quarter, with SMU at our 24, Jayden Sanders went up in the end zone and came down with a leaping interception. Beautiful.

CJ Carr was cool as ever. He went 19 of 29 for 312 yards, and his best throw of the night found Jordan Faison in the end zone from 21 yards out. Faison reached out with one hand and reeled it in. I screamed so loud the guy in front of me turned around. That made it 14-3. Faison finished with seven catches for 109 yards, and Jaden Greathouse hauled in a 62-yarder.

Cooper Flanagan got us on the board with a 2-yard run in the first quarter. Spencer Porath kicked a 32-yard field goal with two seconds left in the half for a 17-3 lead. Nolan James Jr. ran 15 times for 98 yards and put the final points up with a 4-yard run early in the third.

Was it a perfect night? No. We put the ball on the ground once and threw an interception. But when your defense plays like this, you can afford a mistake or two.

Ten wins. Syracuse next Saturday, and the Irish can clinch a College Football Playoff spot with one more. Cheer, cheer for old Notre Dame. Let's finish the job in the Dome.
