Oklahoma vs Texas A&M: John Mateer Accounts for Four Scores in Home Finale, 28-7

Final: Oklahoma Sooners (7-4) 28, Texas A&M Aggies (4-7) 7, Sat., Nov. 21, Memorial Stadium (Norman, OK), Norman. Next: at Missouri, Sat., Nov. 28, 4:15 PM ET, Memorial Stadium, Columbia

By Savannah Redbird-Lowe, The Oklahoma Fanatic

XFacebook

John Mateer's last game in Norman ended with him hurdling a guy at the 7 and landing in the end zone. If that's not a proper sendoff, I don't know what is. Oklahoma 28, Texas A&M 7 on Saturday at Owen Field, our third straight win. We're 7-4 and 5-3 in the SEC. Boomer!

Now, I won't pretend the first half was pretty. A&M went 96 yards for a touchdown early in the second quarter, and Mateer threw an interception and lost a fumble on our next two trips. We trailed 7-0 inside the final three minutes of the half, and the crowd was grumbling.

Then Mateer flipped the switch. He took us 92 yards, completed seven of eight throws on the drive, and hit our tight end for a 4-yard touchdown with 22 seconds left. Tied at 7, and you could feel the place exhale.

The fourth quarter was all Sooners. Tory Blaylock caught a 5-yarder to put us ahead. Then Courtland Guillory picked off Brady Hart and ran it back 20 yards, and on the very next snap Mateer found Isaiah Sategna III wide open for 25 yards. Then came the hurdle. Twenty-eight straight points to finish it.

Mateer went 23 of 34 for 269 yards and three touchdowns, and ran 14 times for 66 more. Sategna caught six for 94. Parker Livingstone had four for 47.

And our defense? Nasty. A&M finished with 185 yards and 10 first downs and ran for 54 yards on 32 carries. That's 1.7 a pop. The Aggies punted eight times. David Stone had a sack and a half. Owen Heinecke made eight tackles with two for loss. Kip Lewis had eight too.

Eight straight home wins against A&M now. The Aggies used to be Big 12 neighbors. Now they're just a regular visitor we send home sad.

Next up: at Missouri, Saturday, Nov. 28, at 4:15 PM ET at Memorial Stadium in Columbia. Win number eight is waiting in Mizzou country. Boomer Sooner!
