Alabama vs Chattanooga: Austin Mack Throws Four Touchdowns in His First Start, 47-0

Final: Alabama Crimson Tide (9-2) 47, Chattanooga Mocs (7-5) 0, Sat., Nov. 21, Bryant-Denny Stadium, Tuscaloosa. Next: vs Auburn, Sat., Nov. 28, time TBA, Bryant-Denny Stadium, Tuscaloosa

By Mary-Kathryn 'MK' Strickland, The Alabama Fanatic

XFacebook

Austin Mack waited years for a Saturday like this one, and he didn't waste a snap of it. In his first college start, with Keelon Russell held out to protect the ankle he hurt against LSU, Mack threw four touchdowns and ran for another as Alabama blanked Chattanooga 47-0 at Bryant-Denny Stadium. We're 9-2, 6-2 in the SEC, and the Iron Bowl is next.

Mack has been with Kalen DeBoer since 2023 at Washington, and he's spent his time here backing up the guys ahead of him and waiting his turn. On Saturday he went 20 of 25 for 254 yards. He found Ryan Coleman-Williams from 8, Lotzeir Brooks from 3, Rico Scott from 29 at the end of a two-minute drive before halftime and Noah Rogers from 8 in the third quarter. Four different guys in the end zone. That's a quarterback reading the field, not forcing it.

He scored first, too, on a 1-yard run in the opening quarter. Then the defense got a safety, Conor Talty hit a 24-yard field goal, and it snowballed. It was 33-0 at halftime and 40-0 when the regulars took a seat in the third quarter.

Now, about that defense. Chattanooga finished with 86 yards. Eighty-six! Five first downs, 27 rushing yards and 1 of 9 on third down. Yhonzae Pierre had a sack and two tackles for loss, QB Reese had 1 1/2 tackles for loss, and Isaia Faga was in the backfield too. Chattanooga never had room to breathe.

We ran for 263 yards and put up 553 in all. Daniel Hill went for 75 on 10 carries, Trae'shawn Brown for 70 on six, and Kevin Riley added 59 on the ground and a 15-yard touchdown catch in the fourth. Kaleb Edwards caught three balls for 63.

The coaches agreed to shorten the fourth quarter to 10 minutes, which tells you how this one went. Credit to the Mocs for making the trip and playing it out. But this day belonged to the 98,552 who got to watch a patient quarterback make the most of his first start.

Auburn comes to Bryant-Denny on Saturday, Nov. 28, with the kickoff time still to be announced. The Tide just found out it has two quarterbacks who can win in this building. Roll Tide, and beat Auburn!
