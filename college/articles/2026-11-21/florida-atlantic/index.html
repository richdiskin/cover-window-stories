Florida Atlantic vs South Florida: Caden Veltkamp Throws Four Through the Weather Delays, 35-14

Final: Florida Atlantic Owls (7-4) 35, South Florida Bulls (6-5) 14, Sat., Nov. 21, Flagler Credit Union Stadium, Boca Raton. Next: at East Carolina, Sat., Nov. 28, 1:00 PM ET, Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium, Greenville

By Kaia Brightwater, The Florida Atlantic Fanatic

XFacebook

The skies above Boca Raton kept stopping this game, and Caden Veltkamp kept starting it right back up. Florida Atlantic beat South Florida 35-14 on Saturday night at Flagler Credit Union Stadium, in a game that sat through multiple weather delays. We're sitting at 7-4 and 4-3 in league play, and the Owls are flying.

Veltkamp was surgical: 30 of 38 for 386 yards and four touchdowns. That's seven straight games with multiple touchdown throws, and he's up to 33 for the season. He came in second in the nation in touchdown throws, and nobody's slowing him down now.

Easton Messer caught two of them, from 8 and 24 yards in the third quarter, and he has 15 touchdown catches this year. Kelby Valsin was a machine, 12 catches for 112 yards, a 16-yard touchdown and a two-point catch. AJ Johnson got us started with a 36-yard score in the first quarter, and Dominique Henry had six catches for 106 yards, with a 41-yarder.

Collin Rogers drilled field goals from 48 and 28, the second with three seconds left in the half, and it was 20-0 at the break. By the end of the third quarter it was 35-7. South Florida never had an answer.

And the defense? The Bulls ran for 45 yards. Forty-five, on 27 carries. Khmari Johnson had a sack and a half, Tyson Rooks made 10 tackles, and Josh Roberts came up with an interception. South Florida threw it 49 times trying to catch up, and it never mattered. We also went 7 of 11 on third down, which is just showing off.

Beating your in-state rival at home, on a night that wouldn't stop getting interrupted, with your quarterback throwing darts? I'll sit through every delay for that.

Next up: at East Carolina on Saturday, Nov. 28, at 1:00 PM ET at Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium in Greenville. Eight wins sounds about right to me. Go Owls!
