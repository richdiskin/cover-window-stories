Fresno State vs Colorado State: Rayshon Luke Scores Three, Bulldogs Reach Six Wins, 49-42

Final: Fresno State Bulldogs (6-5) 49, Colorado State Rams (3-8) 42, Sat., Nov. 21, Valley Children's Stadium, Fresno.

By Rosalind 'Roz' Ayala, The Fresno State Fanatic

XFacebook

Sixty-eight seconds. That's how long it took Simeon Harris to light up Valley Children's Stadium on Saturday night, jumping a Colorado State throw and taking it 35 yards to the house. Fresno State 49, Colorado State 42, and the Bulldogs are 6-5. Six wins. Bowl eligible. The Valley is fired up.

Rayshon Luke was everywhere. He ran for touchdowns from 18 and 3 yards, caught a 6-yard score, and racked up 119 yards on four kickoff returns, including a 59-yarder. Three touchdowns and a special-teams highlight reel. Give that young man a raise in his meal plan.

Jayden Mandal was almost perfect. Fifteen of 17 for 191 yards and three touchdowns. Josiah Freeman caught two of them, from 5 and 21 yards, and finished with five catches for 104 yards, including a 60-yard catch-and-run that had the place standing. Bryson Donelson ran 16 times for 165 yards, and his 61-yard touchdown at the start of the third quarter made it 35-17. As a team we ran for 265 yards, more than eight a carry. That's bully ball, Bulldog style.

Now, about the ending. We were up 49-27 with almost the whole fourth quarter to play, and I was already planning my bowl road trip. Colorado State scored with 1:48 left, then again with 26 seconds left, added a two-point try, and suddenly it was a one-score game. My nerves are fried. But we closed it out, and that's what counts.

Harris had two tackles for loss to go with the interception return. Tytus Khajavi made eight tackles, and Tommy Nix had six, all solo. Their quarterback threw for 413 yards, so there's film to study, but our defense made the first big play of the night and it set the tone.

Six and five, and the Red Wave gets to keep this ride going into the postseason. Go Dogs, and tell your friends from Clovis to Kerman.
