Vanderbilt vs Florida: Brock Taylor Goes 4 for 4 From Deep, but a Fluky Bounce Sinks the Commodores 34-27

Final: Florida Gators (7-4) 34, Vanderbilt Commodores (4-7) 27, Sat., Nov. 21, Ben Hill Griffin Stadium, Gainesville. Next: vs Tennessee, Sat., Nov. 28, time TBA, FirstBank Stadium, Nashville

By Penelope 'Pippa' Ashcroft, The Vanderbilt Fanatic

XFacebook

For one half in Gainesville on Saturday, the Commodores had a Swamp crowd of 88,025 booing its own team. That's what I'll remember, even after the final read Gators 34, Vandy 27.

We led 24-17 at halftime, and we earned it. Brock Taylor drilled field goals from 53, 52 and 42 yards in the first half. That's three kicks most teams wouldn't even try. Then Jared Curtis went to work: a 9-yard touchdown throw to Sedrick Alexander, a two-point conversion to Cole Spence, and with 39 seconds left before the break, a 75-yard bomb to Junior Sherrill. Florida's crowd let its team have it.

So what happened? A few things, and none of them easy to swallow. Early in the third, Curtis picked up a fourth-and-1 at our 36, but his helmet came off on the run, and the officials reversed it and ruled him short. Florida took the short field and tied it. Then, with the game tied at 27, a Gator throw sailed high, got tipped, and dropped right into a receiver's arms for 37 yards. They scored the winner a few plays later.

Coach Clark Lea said we left too many plays on the field, and he's right. But I won't let one bounce erase what these guys did.

Taylor finished 4 for 4, adding a 51-yarder to tie it at 27 in the fourth. Sherrill caught six balls for 149 yards. Alexander ran for 129 yards on 15 carries with a 51-yard burst. CJ Heard made 11 tackles and played like a man who didn't want his senior year to end. Curtis hurt his left wrist late and came back anyway to give us one more chance. A fourth-down sack at the Florida 45 ended it.

We're 4-7, 1-7 in the league. No bowl this year, the first time we've missed one since 2023, and that hurts. But Curtis is the future, and Saturdays like this show what he can be.

One game left, and it's the one everybody in Nashville circles: Tennessee at FirstBank Stadium. Let's send Sherrill and Heard out with a win against the Vols. Anchor Down.
