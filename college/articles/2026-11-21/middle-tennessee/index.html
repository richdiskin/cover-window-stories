Middle Tennessee vs Sam Houston: Terrence Brown's 75-Yard Run Makes the Blue Raiders Bowl Eligible, 31-28

Final: Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders (6-5) 31, Sam Houston Bearkats (1-10) 28, Sat., Nov. 21, Elliott T. Bowers Stadium, Huntsville. Next: vs New Mexico State, Sat., Nov. 28, 2:00 PM ET, Johnny "Red" Floyd Stadium, Murfreesboro

By Opal Stonebriar, The Middle Tennessee Fanatic

XFacebook

Bowl season has a seat saved for Murfreesboro. Middle Tennessee beat Sam Houston 31-28 at Elliott T. Bowers Stadium in Huntsville on Saturday, and the Blue Raiders are bowl eligible for the first time since 2022. We're 6-5, 4-3 in Conference USA.

The play of the day belongs to Terrence Brown. Tied 21-21 in the third quarter, he took a handoff 75 yards through the middle and down the left side, and nobody laid a finger on him. That's six carries for 108 yards on the day, and one run I'll be replaying until Christmas.

We came out swinging. Antonio Martin scored from 10 on our opening drive, Roman Gagliano hit AJ Jones for a 6-yard touchdown, and Gagliano ran one in from 21 himself. It was 21-7 in the second quarter, and it felt comfortable.

Comfortable is a dangerous word. We gave it away four times, two fumbles and two interceptions, and Sam Houston climbed back to tie it. After Brown's run, the defense stuffed the Bearkats on fourth down at their own 34, and Dominic Bourgeois hit a 31-yard field goal for a 31-21 lead heading into the fourth.

It got tight again when Sam Houston scored with 5:35 left to make it 31-28. But the defense stood up one more time, and the Bearkats' last drive died on downs at our 36 with 14 seconds left. Korey Smith was everywhere, with 10 tackles and an interception. Christofer Noble added six tackles, and Reggie Johnson got a tackle for loss and two hurries.

Gagliano finished 18 of 29 for 189 yards and ran for 64 more. Cam'ron Lacy caught six for 73, Neo Clifton four for 52. We ran for 244 yards as a team and won a road game while giving the ball away four times. That's a tough team.

New Mexico State comes to Johnny "Red" Floyd Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 28, at 2:00 PM ET. Let's make it seven at home and give the bowl folks something to think about. Blue Raider proud!
