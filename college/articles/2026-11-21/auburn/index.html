Auburn vs Samford: Tristan Ti'a Goes 17 of 18, Tigers Hit Seven Wins for First Time Since 2019

Final: Auburn Tigers (7-4) 59, Samford Bulldogs (4-8) 17, Sat., Nov. 21, Jordan-Hare Stadium, Auburn. Next: at Alabama, Sat., Nov. 28, time TBA, Bryant-Denny Stadium, Tuscaloosa

By Lacey Bellamy, The Auburn Fanatic

XFacebook

Two plays. That's all it took. Jeremiah Cobb went 62 yards on our first snap, Byrum Brown hit Chas Nimrod for 63 on our second, and Jordan-Hare was already shaking. Auburn 59, Samford 17 on Saturday, and the Tigers are 7-4. That's seven wins for the first time since 2019. War Eagle, y'all.

Now let's talk about Tristan Ti'a, because what that young man did in the second quarter belongs in a museum. He went 17 of 18 for 341 yards and four touchdowns, and he did all of it in the first half. Seventeen of eighteen! He threw three touchdowns to Keshaun Singleton, from 35, 33 and 28 yards, hit Cobb for a 47-yarder, and ran one in himself from a yard out. We scored 38 points in the second quarter alone and went to the locker room up 52-3.

Alex McPherson capped the half with a 40-yard field goal at the horn, and he was perfect on all eight extra points. The man never missed a chance to add on.

Auburn set a school record with 480 yards through the air, and we piled up 672 yards of offense. Nimrod caught seven balls for 157 yards. Singleton finished with six for 150 and three scores. Cobb ran 10 times for 120 yards and caught two for 52. Rhys Brush came in late and threw a 6-yard touchdown to Jonathan Echols, and you could hear the whole sideline cheer for him.

Credit the defense too. Jaquez Wilkes had six tackles and two tackles for loss. Jourdin Crawford and Michael Matthews-Canty each got home for sacks. Samford ran 32 times for 90 yards.

I know Samford is an FCS team, and I know the second half was mostly the backups. I don't care. Coach Golesh has this program believing again, and seven wins tastes a lot better than the last few Novembers.

Bowl eligible, seven wins, and now the game every Auburn fan circles first.

Next up: at Alabama, Saturday, Nov. 28, at Bryant-Denny Stadium, time to be announced. It's Iron Bowl week. Let's go to Tuscaloosa and make some noise. War Eagle!
