Colorado vs Cincinnati: Isaac Wilson Throws for 312 and Three Scores, Buffs Fall 35-28 After a Travel Nightmare

Final: Cincinnati Bearcats (6-5) 35, Colorado Buffaloes (4-7) 28, Sat., Nov. 21, Nippert Stadium, Cincinnati. Next: vs UCF, Sat., Nov. 28, 2:00 PM ET, Folsom Field, Boulder

By Moss Feldstein, The Colorado Fanatic

XFacebook

Our bus broke down on the way to the airport. Then the flights got delayed. Then we didn't land in Cincinnati until late Friday. And then, on Saturday, we coughed up the ball four times and lost 35-28. That's the whole week in four sentences, Buffs fans.

Coach Deion Sanders didn't make excuses. He said early in the game we were still on the tarmac, and that the early turnovers crippled us. Then he said all of it's on him. I respect that. I also respect my blood pressure, which is a mess.

Here's what kills me. Cincinnati intercepted us on our opening drive and recovered a fumble on the next one, and we were down 14-0 before we'd blinked. You can't spot a bowl-hungry home team two touchdowns in a building like Nippert.

But this team swung back. Isaac Wilson hit Ernest Campbell for 36 yards on fourth-and-1 to get us on the board. Richard Young plowed in from 4 to tie it at 14 late in the half. Then in the third, Wilson went big. A 54-yarder to DeAndre Moore Jr. And then a deep ball down the right sideline to Joseph Williams, who made a one-handed grab near the 40 and outran everybody for a 70-yard score. That might be the catch of the year in Boulder, and it came in Ohio.

Wilson finished with 312 yards and three touchdowns. Williams had four catches for 112. Moore caught five for 91.

Defensively, Randon Fontenette was a one-man wrecking crew: 14 tackles, a sack, and 2.5 stops in the backfield. Liona Lefau added 10 stops and a sack. The trouble was Cole Tabb, who did a little of everything, and those screens. Sanders said it himself: they knew it was coming and still couldn't stop it.

We're 4-7. No bowl again, and that's a gut punch for a program that expected more. Sanders said there's no true consistency, and he's right.

UCF comes to Folsom Field next Saturday for the finale. Send the seniors off right, play a clean four quarters for once, and make Boulder loud. Sko Buffs.
