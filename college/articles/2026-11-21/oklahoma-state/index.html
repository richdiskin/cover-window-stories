Oklahoma State vs Arizona State: Keltner Hits in Overtime, Hammonds Ends It, Cowboys Rally 30-27

Final: Oklahoma State Cowboys (7-4) 30, Arizona State Sun Devils (6-5) 27, Sat., Nov. 21, Mountain America Stadium, Tempe. Next: vs Kansas, Sat., Nov. 28, 6:15 PM ET, Boone Pickens Stadium, Stillwater

By Dusty Lockridge, The Oklahoma State Fanatic

XFacebook

I had my head in my hands at halftime, and I'm not ashamed of it. The Oklahoma State Cowboys trailed the Arizona State Sun Devils 24-7 in Tempe on Saturday night, and then we chipped, and chipped, and found a 30-27 win in overtime at Mountain America Stadium. We're 7-4, 5-3 in the Big 12, and this is the kind of night you tell people about in Stillwater for years.

It started fine. William Mason ran 13 yards for a touchdown on the opening drive, and I thought, here we go. Then Jake Fette and Reed Harris took the building. Fette hit Harris for a 1-yard score, ran one in from a yard, and found Harris again from 60 yards. Smith knocked through a 51-yard field goal as the half expired, and it was 24-7. Drew Mestemaker, playing after a right shoulder injury last week, threw an interception to Martell Hughes. We looked stuck.

The second half was a different team. Caleb Hawkins scored from 9 yards in the third, Sam Keltner made a 25-yard field goal, and Hawkins ran in from 7 yards at 9:11 of the fourth to tie it 24-24. Hawkins carried 22 times for 98 yards and those two scores. Mestemaker finished 30 of 40 for 294 yards. Justin Bowick caught seven balls for 95.

Smith put Arizona State back in front 27-24 with a 38-yard field goal at 3:12. Keltner answered from 43 yards at 1:11, and we went to overtime tied at 27. Keltner, who was 3 for 3, opened overtime with a 35-yarder to make it 30-27. On the next snap, Quinton Hammonds came down with an interception at our 21. That was the ballgame. Hammonds also made eight tackles. Jerry Lawson had two sacks.

Coach Eric Morris said, "We were so flat in the first half. We battled back one play at a time in the second half and found a way to win it." That's exactly how it felt from my seat.

Next up: Kansas at Boone Pickens Stadium, Saturday, Nov. 28, at 6:15 PM ET. Get to Stillwater early and make it loud. Go Pokes!
