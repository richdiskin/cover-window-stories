Georgia Southern vs Troy: Max Johnson Throws Three, Eagles Smother Trojans 45-7

Final: Georgia Southern Eagles (7-4) 45, Troy Trojans (4-7) 7, Sat., Nov. 21, Veterans Memorial Stadium (AL), Troy. Next: vs Louisiana Tech, Sat., Nov. 28, 12:00 PM ET, Allen E. Paulson Stadium, Statesboro

By Royce Ogeechee, The Georgia Southern Fanatic

XFacebook

First snap of the game, Brandon Tyson jumps the route, steals it and races 26 yards to the Troy 6. That set the tone for the whole afternoon. Georgia Southern 45, Troy 7 on Saturday, and the Eagles are 7-4 and 6-1 in the Sun Belt. Hail Southern!

Max Johnson needed one drive to cash in. Even after a holding flag backed us up, he found Alex Taylor from 12 yards out for a 7-0 lead. Later in the first quarter he dropped a 45-yard beauty into Andre Greene Jr.'s hands. Right before halftime he hit Jeremiah Ware from 22. Three touchdown throws, a 21-0 lead at the break, and Johnson finished 22 of 26 for 227 yards. Twenty-two of twenty-six. Surgical.

And then there's our defense, which I'd like to have bronzed. Troy ran the ball 21 times for minus-7 yards. Minus seven! MJ Stroud had two sacks and recovered a fumble. Jeremiah Ticket fell on another one late. We took the ball away three times and didn't give it away once. Troy had 127 yards of offense all day.

The second half was a victory lap. Tripp Bryant knocked through a 35-yarder and went 6 for 6 on extra points. Weston Bryan came in and hit Greene for a 22-yard touchdown, Greene's second of the day. Taeo Todd broke a 19-yard touchdown run and had a 41-yard catch, and Ty Cummings punched in an 8-yarder to finish it.

Taylor caught six balls for 70 yards. Greene had four for 97. Todd ran eight times for 66. Everybody ate.

This team has found a gear in November, and it's fun to watch. Seven wins, a great Sun Belt record, and a defense playing nasty.

Now let's finish it at home. Next up: Louisiana Tech at Paulson Stadium, Saturday, Nov. 28, at noon ET. Statesboro, bring the noise and bring the eagle. Hail Southern!
