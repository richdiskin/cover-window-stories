Georgia Tech vs Wake Forest: Grady Adamson Runs for 143, but a 29-0 Hole Sinks the Jackets 50-29

Final: Wake Forest Demon Deacons (10-1) 50, Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets (3-8) 29, Sat., Nov. 21, Bobby Dodd Stadium, Atlanta. Next: at Georgia, Sat., Nov. 28, time TBA, Sanford Stadium, Athens

By Wendell Quist, The Georgia Tech Fanatic

XFacebook

Bobby Dodd Stadium had 45,296 people in it on Saturday, and for about 20 minutes most of them were glaring at the scoreboard like it owed them money. The final was 50-29, Wake. We're 3-8.

The start was brutal, and I'm not going to pretend otherwise. Wake scored on its first four drives and led 29-0 early in the second quarter. By halftime it was 36-7. Gio Lopez had all day, Carlos Hernandez was running free, and we coughed up the ball three times on the afternoon. You can't do that against a team headed to the ACC title game.

But I'm going to tell you what I saw after that, because it matters. This team won the second half 22-14. Nobody quit. Grady Adamson, one half of our two-quarterback plan, ran wild, 143 yards on 16 carries with a 43-yard burst and a 12-yard touchdown. He also went 6 of 8 throwing. That kid plays with his hair on fire.

Justice Haynes scored from 4 yards out for our first points and then ran in a two-point try. Trelain Maddox ran for 79 yards and a score. Graham Knowles punched in a 7-yarder late. As a team we piled up 289 rushing yards at 6.7 a carry and 26 first downs. That's real football, folks, even on a day when the scoreboard disagreed.

Cayman Spaulding made 11 tackles and never stopped chasing. E.J. Lightsey had a tackle for loss. Aidan Birr was perfect on three extra points.

Look, a week ago we were running through Death Valley and beating Clemson. This week a top-10 team showed us how far we still have to climb. Both things are true. The Jackets are building something, and Saturdays like this are part of the bill.

Now comes the big one: a trip to Athens to face No. 1 Georgia in the regular-season finale. Nobody gives us a prayer, which is exactly how I like it. Beat the Dawgs, and Go Jackets.
