Akron vs Massachusetts: Hills' 100-Yard Return and Stire's Kick Snap the Skid, 34-33

Final: Akron Zips (2-9) 34, Massachusetts Minutemen (5-6) 33, Wed., Nov. 18, Warren McGuirk Alumni Stadium, Amherst. Next: vs Buffalo, Fri., Nov. 27, 12:00 PM ET, InfoCision Stadium, Akron

By Hollister 'Zip' Grubbs, The Akron Fanatic

XFacebook

Akron 34, Massachusetts 33, and the eight-game skid is dead. Wednesday night at McGuirk Alumni Stadium, the Zips trailed 26-7 in the second quarter, didn't lead until the final six minutes, and still came home with the win. We're 2-9, 1-6 in the MAC, and it's our first victory since September. I yelled myself hoarse in Amherst and regret not one second of it.

The first half was rough. UMass scored on a 52-yard Elijah Faulkner run on its first drive, and Isaiah Reed intercepted Cibastian Broughton twice. The second one came back 68 yards to our 2. The best moment of the half? Our defense pushed them backward from the 2, and they had to take a field goal. A short touchdown catch and a failed two-point try later, it was 26-7.

Jordan Gant had already given us a spark with a 73-yard touchdown run in the first quarter, the longest of his career. Before the break, Broughton hit Miles Burris for a 36-yard touchdown, Dahvon Frazier intercepted Logan Inagawa, and Aidan Stire knocked through a 23-yard field goal. UMass missed from 50, and it was 26-17 at the half.

Then Brandon Hills took the second-half kickoff 100 yards. Thirteen seconds into the third quarter, it was 26-24. That's the fourth 100-yard kickoff return in program history and the first since 2003. UMass answered with an 11-yard Max Dowling catch for 33-24, and we answered right back with a 10-play, 72-yard drive. Gant finished it from the 1 with 2:15 left in the third. 33-31.

The winning drive went 13 plays and 73 yards and ate 7:49 of clock. Kyan Mason made a 41-yard catch to get us to the UMass 19, and Stire hit a 28-yard field goal with 5:34 left. 34-33, our first lead of the night.

Then the defense finished it. UMass reached our 46, and on fourth down with 58 seconds left, Mehki Flowers and the rush hurried Inagawa into an incompletion. The last UMass series, from its own 17, ended with Frazier in on a sack.

Gant finished with 151 yards on 21 carries and two touchdowns. Hills piled up 157 yards on three kick returns. Flowers and Donelius Johnson made 12 tackles apiece, and Stire went 2 for 2 on field goals.

Next up: Buffalo comes to InfoCision Stadium on Friday, Nov. 27, at 12:00 PM ET, and we send off the seniors. Bring the blue and gold, Akron. Go Zips!
