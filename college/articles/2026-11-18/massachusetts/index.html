UMass vs Akron: A 26-7 Lead Slips Away on Senior Night, 34-33

Final: Akron Zips (2-9) 34, Massachusetts Minutemen (5-6) 33, Wed., Nov. 18, Warren McGuirk Alumni Stadium, Amherst. Next: at Bowling Green, Sat., Nov. 28, 3:30 PM ET, Doyt L. Perry Stadium, Bowling Green

By Abner Kettleworth, The Massachusetts Fanatic

XFacebook

Akron 34, Massachusetts 33, and I'm still in my seat at McGuirk Alumni Stadium trying to figure out how. It was Senior Night and Fan Appreciation in Amherst on Wednesday, we led 26-7 in the second quarter, and we didn't lead at the end. The Minutemen are 5-6, 2-5 in the MAC.

The first half went exactly the way I wanted. Elijah Faulkner went 52 yards for a touchdown on our opening drive. Isaiah Reed intercepted Cibastian Broughton, and Derek Morris drilled a 49-yard field goal. Akron answered with a 73-yard Jordan Gant run, but Logan Inagawa hit Matt Ogunniyi from 3 yards out, and it was 17-7 after one quarter.

Reed got his second interception and returned it 68 yards to the Akron 2. We came away with a 23-yard Morris field goal from there, and that still bugs me. Max Dowling caught a 2-yard touchdown, the two-point try failed, and it was 26-7.

Akron chipped back. Miles Burris caught a 36-yard touchdown, an interception set up a field goal, and Morris missed from 50 right before the break. 26-17.

Then the second-half kickoff. Brandon Hills took it 100 yards, and it was 26-24 just like that. Our guys answered with a 13-play, 75-yard drive, and Inagawa found Dowling from 11 yards for 33-24. Akron came back with Gant from the 1 to make it 33-31, then went 13 plays for a 28-yard Aidan Stire field goal with 5:34 left. 34-33.

We had our chances. Our last real drive went 11 plays to the Akron 46, and on fourth down with 58 seconds left, the throw to Dowling fell incomplete. A final series from our own 17 ended with a sack.

There's plenty to be proud of. Faulkner ran for 105 yards on 13 carries. Inagawa threw three touchdowns and ran for 64 yards. Reed's two interceptions for 68 return yards deserve a plaque, and Tyler Martin had seven tackles and a sack. We ran for 269 yards and held the ball for 35:57.

To the seniors: thank you. One more Saturday. Next up: at Bowling Green, Saturday, Nov. 28, at 3:30 PM ET, Doyt L. Perry Stadium. Go Minutemen!
