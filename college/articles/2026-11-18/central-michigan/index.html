Central Michigan vs Buffalo: Glasser Throws Three, and It All Comes Down to Muncie, 40-21

Final: Buffalo Bulls (8-3) 40, Central Michigan Chippewas (5-6) 21, Wed., Nov. 18, Broadview Stadium, Buffalo. Next: at Ball State, Sat., Nov. 28, 12:00 PM ET, Scheumann Stadium, Muncie

By Brynn Haverkamp, The Central Michigan Fanatic

XFacebook

Buffalo 40, Central Michigan 21, and I've had plenty of time to stew since the final whistle Wednesday night at Broadview Stadium. The Chippewas are 5-6, 3-4 in the MAC, and it comes down to one game: win at Ball State on Nov. 28, and we're bowl eligible.

The first drive went exactly how I'd hoped. Our guys went 11 plays and 75 yards, and Jadyn Glasser found Braylen Naves for a 32-yard touchdown. 7-0. Then came the turnovers: an interception on the first play of one drive and a fumble on the first play of the next, and Buffalo turned them into 10 points.

Then Declan Duley did what no Chippewa had ever done. He boomed an 86-yard punt that was downed at the Buffalo 1, a school record. We forced a three-and-out, got the ball back at the Buffalo 45, and Glasser hit Myles Peters from 5 yards out with 39 seconds left in the half. 14-13, Chippewas!

And then 35 seconds of heartbreak. Buffalo hit a 49-yard throw, and Chance Morrow made a 17-yard touchdown catch with four seconds left. 20-14 at the half.

We didn't fold. Buffalo went up 26-14 early in the third after its two-point try failed, and our guys put together a 12-play, 75-yard march that chewed up 6:51. Glasser found Brock Townsend from 2 yards out with 3:55 left in the third. 26-21. Then Messiah Burch ran 75 yards on Buffalo's next snap, and Terrance Shelton Jr. scored from 2 yards out early in the fourth. 40-21.

Glasser threw a career-high three touchdowns, going 10 of 19 for 101 yards. Jayden Clerveaux ran for 70 yards on 15 carries, Nick Woodford added 57, and Mayson Hitchens led the defense with seven tackles. Coach Matt Drinkall put it plainly: "Very good team. They made plays and we made too many mistakes." Three turnovers against a team like that is too many, and everybody in maroon and gold knows it.

Next up: at Ball State, Saturday, Nov. 28, at 12:00 PM ET, Scheumann Stadium in Muncie. One win for a bowl. Fire Up Chips!
