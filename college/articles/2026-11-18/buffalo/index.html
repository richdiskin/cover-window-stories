Buffalo vs Central Michigan: Burch Hits 200 Again as the Bulls Win Six Straight, 40-21

Final: Buffalo Bulls (8-3) 40, Central Michigan Chippewas (5-6) 21, Wed., Nov. 18, Broadview Stadium, Buffalo. Next: at Akron, Fri., Nov. 27, 12:00 PM ET, InfoCision Stadium, Akron

By Kendra Woolcott, The Buffalo Fanatic

XFacebook

Buffalo 40, Central Michigan 21, and that's six straight. Wednesday was the home finale at Broadview Stadium, and the Bulls sent everybody home happy. We're 8-3, 6-1 in the MAC, sharing first place, and this is our longest winning streak since the seven-game run in 2013. We finished 5-1 at home, too, matching the best home record since the Bulls joined the FBS.

Messiah Burch, again. He ran for exactly 200 yards and two touchdowns, the second straight game he has hit 200 and scored twice. That gives him 1,310 yards this season, and I'd like the rest of the MAC to know he isn't tired.

It began a little shaky. Central drove 75 yards on its first possession, and Jadyn Glasser hit Braylen Naves for a 32-yard touchdown. 7-0. Oliver Hautanen got us on the board from 27. Then Ronnell Davis went to work. He intercepted Glasser on the very next snap, and Hautanen kicked a 26-yarder. On Central's next play, Davis forced a fumble, SaVeon Brown fell on it at the CMU 26, and Burch scored from 10 yards out. 13-7.

Central went ahead 14-13 on a 5-yard Myles Peters catch with 39 seconds left in the half. Jason Wright needed 29 seconds to fix that. He hit Jaylen Andrews for 49 yards, and after two incompletions, Chance Morrow made a falling catch in the end zone from 17 yards out with four seconds left. 20-14. That was Morrow's 10th touchdown catch of the season.

Wright opened the third by running in from the 1 to finish a 75-yard drive. The two-point try failed, 26-14. Central answered with a 12-play drive and a 2-yard Brock Townsend catch, 26-21, and my stomach dropped. For one snap. Burch went straight up the middle for 75 yards on the very next play. 33-21. Terrance Shelton Jr. added a 2-yard run early in the fourth, 40-21.

Davis finished with 11 tackles to go with that interception and forced fumble, and Miles Greer also made 11 tackles and came up with an interception of his own. Shelton ran for 81 yards. As a team we ran for 283 yards, piled up 435 yards of offense, and didn't give the ball away once.

Next up: at Akron, Friday, Nov. 27, at 12:00 PM ET, InfoCision Stadium. Seven in a row sounds good to me. Go Bulls!
