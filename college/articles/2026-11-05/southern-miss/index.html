Southern Miss vs James Madison: A 54-23 Night That Got Away at The Rock

Final: James Madison Dukes (8-2) 54, Southern Miss Golden Eagles (3-6) 23, Thu., Nov. 5, M. M. Roberts Stadium, Hattiesburg. Next: at Louisiana Tech, Sat., Nov. 14, 5:00 PM ET, Joe Aillet Stadium, Ruston

By Delphine 'Dee' Broussard, The Southern Miss Fanatic

ShareXFacebook

Southern Miss Golden Eagles 23, James Madison Dukes 54. Final score 54-23. Primetime at The Rock, and we got steamrolled after hanging around for a half. We're 3-6, 2-3 in the Sun Belt, and Main Street UMC's Family Fall Festival hits Hattiesburg this weekend while this one sits in the stomach like a bad meal.

For two quarters, this was a real fight. John White scored from 3 early in the second to cut it to 10-7. Steven Robinson punched in from the 2 with 37 seconds left before the half, and we actually led 14-13. Then Morgan Suarez drilled a 40-yarder at the gun, and the whole building flipped. We never led again. Never even sniffed it.

JC Evans, back at quarterback for James Madison after missing eight starts, ran for three touchdowns and threw for another. He went 19 of 26 for 251. Jackson McCarter ripped a 30-yarder. Camden Coleman checked in and hit Michael Scott from 26. They put up 54 and 498 yards. JT Hooten sacked us three times. That's the difference between a home dogfight and a home demolition.

Our bright spots? John White went 22 of 35 for 266 yards with a scoring throw to Mario Sanders and that early rushing score. Kaden Saunders caught six for 122. Mathis Haygood flew around for 13 tackles. Creighton Wilbanks hit a 29-yard field goal late. We just went 3 of 14 on third down, managed 88 rushing yards and watched Evans own the whole second half. Zero turnovers and we still lost by 31. Explain that without pointing at the third-down chart.

Louisiana Tech on Nov. 14 at Joe Aillet Stadium in Ruston, 5 PM ET. Fix the run defense, win third down and stop letting one quarterback own a whole half of football. Southern Miss. Get it right.
