Florida Atlantic vs UTSA: Veltkamp, Messer and a 60-29 Bowl Ticket

Final: UTSA Roadrunners (7-2) 29, Florida Atlantic Owls (6-3) 60, Thu., Nov. 5, Flagler Credit Union Stadium, Boca Raton. Next: at Tulsa, Sat., Nov. 14, 3:30 PM ET, H. A. Chapman Stadium, Tulsa

By Kaia Brightwater, The Florida Atlantic Fanatic

ShareXFacebook

Sixty to twenty-nine. Write it on the wall at Flagler Credit Union Stadium. Florida Atlantic Owls 60, UTSA Roadrunners 29, and we're bowl eligible. Six and three. Three and two in the American. That's the night Boca Raton has been waiting on.

Before kickoff, SportsCenter on Campus rolled into Publix Paradise Plaza outside the gates, free for anybody who wanted to show up at 5. Then the Owls went out and made the American Conference leader look ordinary. Caden Veltkamp went 17 of 25 for 210 yards and four touchdowns. Easton Messer caught three of them and finished with seven grabs for 85 yards. Messer even flipped a 14-yard score to AJ Johnson himself in the first quarter. Versatile is an understatement.

UTSA answered once early when Owen McCown hit Mekhi Anderson from 20, tying it 7-7. After that, this building belonged to the Owls. Veltkamp found Messer again from 15. AJ Johnson punched in from 4. Veltkamp hit Leonard Farrow from 18 before the half. Out of the break, Johnson scored again from 4 and Veltkamp converted the two-point try. Then Farrow ripped off a 59-yard touchdown run late in the third to make it 50-29. He finished with 126 yards on 12 carries. Ethan Ervin added 92 on 15. Kelby Valsin caught six for 77.

On defense, Jonathan Bax wrecked the pocket with 2.5 sacks. Leon Hart Jr. led us with eight tackles. Zero turnovers. 563 yards of offense. 339 on the ground. Collin Rogers was perfect on seven extra points, and Alex Grace drilled a 37-yarder in the fourth. We scored in every quarter and never trailed after that early trade.

First conference loss of the year for the team that walked in at 4-0 in league play. We just punched the ticket the hard way, against the club sitting alone atop the American. Tulsa on Nov. 14 at Chapman Stadium. Bring the same energy. The Owls are flying.
