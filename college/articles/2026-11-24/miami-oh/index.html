Miami (OH) vs Western Michigan: Bellisari Scores Four, Wins 28-24

Final: Miami (OH) RedHawks (8-4) 28, Western Michigan Broncos (8-4) 24, Tue., Nov. 24, Waldo Stadium, Kalamazoo. Regular season complete

By Cordelia Van Doren, The Miami (OH) Fanatic

XFacebook

Miami (OH) 28, Western Michigan 24, and true freshman Drew Bellisari had a hand in all four of our touchdowns on a 32-degree night in Kalamazoo. He threw two, ran for two, and Amariyun Knighten intercepted Broc Lowry in the end zone with 20 seconds left to finish it. We're 8-4, 6-2 in the MAC, and our title-game hopes are still alive.

In Oxford, Goggin Ice Center opens its Thanksgiving Holiday Skate on Wednesday at 3:15, eight bucks with free skate rental. I'm taking laps on that rink with my arms up. Western Michigan can keep the free tickets. We took the win.

Bellisari didn't play like a freshman for one second. He hit Maleek Huggins for 33 and Scoop Smith for a 31-yard touchdown on our first drive. Next time out he ran for 31 and found Brendan Loftus from 14. 14-3 after one quarter. Malcolm McCain intercepted Lowry at our 12 in the second, and we took the 11-point margin to the locker room.

Western Michigan came out of the half running hard. Jalen Buckley broke a 44-yarder and scored from the 3, 14-10. Bellisari answered on third-and-19, hitting Huggins for 31, then kept it himself for an 18-yard touchdown, 21-10. The Broncos scored again, and later Pierse Stainton turned a punt into a 19-yard run. That's a coaching staff that trusts its guys.

The fourth got tense. Cole Cabana broke a 41-yarder, Buckley scored from 23, and the two-point try made it 24-21 Broncos with 8:02 left. Our answer took four plays. An interference flag got us 15, D'Shawntae Jones ran for 25 on third-and-5, and Bellisari scored from 12 at 5:40. Brady Stidham's kick made it 28-24.

Then the defense had to hold up for 14 plays. Western Michigan ran for 297 yards and got to our 5. Zhaiylen Scott, who made 10 tackles, helped stuff Buckley for no gain on second down. On the next snap, Knighten caught Lowry's throw in the end zone.

Bellisari threw for 214 yards and ran for 93. Huggins caught four for 77. We had zero turnovers and took two away. Bring on Wednesday at Goggin.
