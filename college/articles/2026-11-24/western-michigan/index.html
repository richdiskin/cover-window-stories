Western Michigan vs Miami (OH): Free Seats, Five Yards Short, 28-24

Final: Miami (OH) RedHawks (8-4) 28, Western Michigan Broncos (8-4) 24, Tue., Nov. 24, Waldo Stadium, Kalamazoo. Regular season complete

By Corwin Dahlquist, The Western Michigan Fanatic

XFacebook

Western Michigan lost 28-24 to Miami (OH) at Waldo Stadium on Tuesday night, and the free-ticket crowd of 16,512 deserved a better last scene. Greenleaf Trust, Greenleaf Hospitality and Ziegler Auto Group gave Kalamazoo free seats for the regular-season finale, and the Broncos gave those seats 297 rushing yards, a fourth-quarter lead and a final drive that died 5 yards from the end zone. We finish 8-4, 6-2 in the MAC.

The first quarter was ugly. Drew Bellisari hit Maleek Huggins for 33 and Scoop Smith for a 31-yard touchdown on Miami's first drive, then ran for 31 and found Brendan Loftus from 14 out. 14-3 before I had my gloves on. Our first half was a 33-yard field goal, a fourth-down stop at the Miami 27 and a Broc Lowry throw that Malcolm McCain intercepted at the Miami 12.

Then the Broncos remembered who they are. Jalen Buckley ripped off a 44-yard run early in the third and powered in from the 3, 14-10. Bellisari answered with an 18-yard touchdown run, so Lowry hit Baylin Brooks for 38 and Aveion Chenault for a 13-yard score. The two-point try failed. 21-16. Miami even ran a fake punt, Pierse Stainton taking off for 19 yards, and our defense still stopped them on fourth down at the 25 to open the fourth quarter.

Cole Cabana broke a 41-yarder to the Miami 23, and Buckley went the rest of the way. Lowry found Cabana for two. 24-21 with 8:02 left, and Waldo was as loud as I've heard it all year.

It lasted 2:22. An interference flag handed Miami 15 yards, D'Shawntae Jones ran for 25 on third down, and Bellisari scored from 12 out at 5:40. 28-24.

So we did what we do. Fourteen plays, 66 yards, Buckley and Lowry carrying it, a Miami interference flag to the 18, Buckley to the 7, Lowry to the 5. Third down, 26 seconds left, and Lowry's throw in the end zone ended up in Amariyun Knighten's hands. That one's going to sit with me through the turkey.

Lowry ran for 100 yards. Buckley ran for 126 and two scores, and that moved him past LeVante Bellamy into fourth on Western Michigan's career rushing list. Joshua Franklin had a sack and 62 yards on kickoff returns. The ticket was free. Losing to Miami at home in November costs plenty.
