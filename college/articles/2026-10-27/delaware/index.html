Delaware vs Western Kentucky: Nick Minicucci Throws for 414 in a 35-21 Blue Hens Win

Final: Delaware (4-4) 35, Western Kentucky (3-5) 21, Tue., Oct. 27, Houchens Industries-L.T. Smith Stadium, Bowling Green, Ky. Next: at Kennesaw State, Sat., Nov. 7, 3:00 PM ET, Walens Family Field at Fifth Third Stadium, Kennesaw, Ga.

ShareXFacebook

Delaware 35, Western Kentucky 21, and I want it read out loud on Main Street in Newark. The Blue Hens went to Bowling Green, Kentucky, on a Tuesday night and came home with a road win, which this program had not done in seven straight trips. That road streak is finished. Nobody in my house is allowed to mention it again.

Start with Nick Minicucci, because the young man was a surgeon. He completed his first 17 throws, a Delaware record for straight completions to open a game, and finished 32 of 38 for 414 yards and four touchdowns. He also climbed to fourth in program history with 6,459 career yards. A week after he tied the school record with five touchdown throws, he went out and made more history on a weeknight.

Sean Wilson was the guy Minicucci kept looking for, and who could blame him? Nine catches. 183 yards. Touchdowns of 21 and 40 yards. That 40-yarder with 4:20 left in the third quarter made it 35-21, and nobody scored again. Kaderris Roberts caught seven balls and opened our scoring with a 17-yard touchdown, and Jo Silver grabbed an 8-yard score with 59 seconds left in the half to give us our first lead at 28-21.

Now, honesty is a family value in my house. The Hilltoppers jumped us 7-0 and 14-7, and Brock Glenn ran for 129 yards on 17 carries. Western Kentucky piled up 232 rushing yards. My blood pressure would like a word with our run defense.

But this defense found a second gear after halftime and held the Hilltoppers scoreless for the final 30 minutes. Kevin Moore picked off a throw late in the first quarter, and AJ Graham got his first career interception on the first play of the fourth, deep in our own end, which shut the door for good. Gavin Moul led us with seven tackles, and Dominick Brogna got home for a sack.

The Blue Hen is our state bird, and tonight every one of them in the First State is crowing. We are 4-4 and 2-2 in Conference USA, right back in the middle of things. After an open week, the Blue Hens head to Kennesaw State on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:00 p.m. ET. Bring the whole flock.
