Sam Houston vs Missouri State: Bearkats Fall 24-3 in Springfield

Final: Missouri State (3-5) 24, Sam Houston (1-7) 3, Tue., Oct. 27, Robert W. Plaster Stadium, Springfield, Mo.. Next: at Jacksonville State, Sat., Nov. 7, 7:00 PM ET, AmFirst Stadium, Jacksonville, Ala.

Missouri State 24, Sam Houston 3. Back home in Huntsville, the big statue of General Sam Houston stands 67 feet tall off the interstate, and on Tuesday night in Springfield I could have used a little of that height and a lot of that patience.

Here is the part that should not get lost. For three quarters this was a fistfight. Sam Carpenter drilled a 33-yard field goal with 31 seconds left in the first quarter, and our defense held the Bears to a single field goal the rest of the first half. It was 10-3 heading into the fourth. Josh Alexander-Felton played like a man who had heard every bad thing anybody ever said about this season. Seven tackles, two sacks and three tackles for loss. Brian Bates Jr. added seven tackles of his own.

And Cal Joseph, bless his right leg. Five punts, a 50.2 average, a 70-yard boomer, and two pinned inside the 20. When your punter is one of the best players on the field, you know it was a long night, but that young man earned his supper.

DJ Bailey kept slinging it, too. He went 20 of 35 for 258 yards with no interceptions. Grady O'Neill caught six balls for 74 yards, Chris Reed had four for 64, and Tim Burns Jr. hauled in a 46-yarder.

Now the hard part. We ran 25 times for 26 yards. You cannot live on that. We were flagged 10 times for 84 yards, we went 0 for 2 on fourth down, and the fourth quarter got away fast. Henry Belin IV hit Jmariyae Robinson for a 37-yard touchdown on the first play of the fourth to make it 17-3, a fumbled punt return set up Connor Lair's 1-yard run with 3:44 left, and our last chance ended on a fourth-down throw that got knocked away with 1:08 to go.

So we are 1-7 and 0-4 in Conference USA, and I am not going anywhere. Neither is the statue. The Bearkats get an open week, then go to Jacksonville State on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:00 p.m. ET at AmFirst Stadium. Eat Em Up, Kats.
