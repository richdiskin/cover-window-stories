A Proud First Start, a Tough Harvest: Kansas State Falls at Arizona State, 28-17

Arizona State 28, Kansas State 17 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Mountain America Stadium

By Hattie Brandvold, The Kansas State Fanatic

XFacebook

Well, howdy, Wildcat neighbors. Hattie Brandvold here, on the porch with coffee, a soil probe and a sky going orange over the Flint Hills. Arizona State 28, Kansas State 17, in Tempe. Not every harvest comes in full. But I'll tell you, there was a lot of good grain in this field.

Start with Jacob Knuth. A fifth-year senior, he'd waited a long time for his first career start, and he got it with Avery Johnson sidelined. On our first drive, Knuth threw 7 yards to Jaron Tibbs for a touchdown. In the third quarter, he ran one in from 10 yards out, and we led 17-14. He said afterward it was a moment he'd been waiting for a long time. Coach Collin Klein said he was very proud of him. So am I, son. So am I.

Then the weather turned. On the first play of the fourth quarter, Arizona State freshman Cardae Mack ran 12 yards for the lead. With 6:23 left, he went 11 more for his fourth touchdown. And when we drove late, the Sun Devils sacked Knuth on fourth down. Klein said we lost the battle up front on both sides, and a farmer knows you can't grow much without good ground.

Hero: Knuth, 26 of 41 for 194 yards, a touchdown pass, a touchdown run, and zero turnovers in his first start. Tibbs caught nine passes, Derrick Salley Jr. had six for 63, and Josh Manning caught seven. Jordan Allen had two sacks, and Logan Bartley made 11 tackles. The goat, said over a second cup: fourth downs, where we went 0 for 4. That's four rows left unplanted.

What it means: Kansas State is 4-3 and 1-3 in the Big 12. The Arizona State quarterback carousel spun three times, and we still couldn't catch the ring. But the AP said Knuth performed creditably and protected the ball, and you can build on that.

Next up: at Colorado on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 2 p.m. ET on TNT, at Folsom Field. Halloween in Boulder, with the mountains watching. I'll pack a sweater and the soil probe.

Every Man a Wildcat. Go Cats.
