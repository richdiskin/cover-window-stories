A Lotta Yikes, a Little N'at: Pitt Runs Into No. 1 Miami, Falls 52-23

Miami 52, Pittsburgh 23 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Hard Rock Stadium

By Dominic 'Yinz' Bertolucci, The Pittsburgh Fanatic

How's it goin', yinz? Dominic "Yinz" Bertolucci, back on my stool down the Strip, whistle around my neck, pierogi going cold. Miami 52, Pitt 23. We played the No. 1 team in the country in its house, and the house won. Coach Narduzzi called it a buzz saw. I'd call it a buzz saw with a jet engine on it.

Let me blow the whistle on how it got away. Miami scored on seven of its first eight possessions. That's a yikes. Malachi Toney caught 16 balls, a Miami school record. That's a double yikes with a side of fries. The real dagger came in the second quarter. Damon Ferguson Jr. ran in a 5-yard touchdown to make it 17-7, and I was up off the stool yellin'. Then Toney caught a 53-yard touchdown, and Miami added another score with 39 seconds left. Thirty-one to seven at the half. Stool, meet seat.

Now the yinz. Holden Geriner got the start at quarterback and threw for 268 yards. He found Blue Hicks, a Miami native, for a 36-yard touchdown in the third quarter. Blue finished with five catches for 149 yards in his hometown, which is a full-on, mill-town, lunch-pail yinz. Geriner also hit Hicks for 53 yards at the end of the half, but Blue got dragged down at the Miami 1 as time ran out. One yard, n'at.

More yinz: Jaeden Moore had two sacks. Cruce Brookins made nine tackles. Corey Dailey came in late and hit Cam Sapp for a 49-yard touchdown with 46 seconds left, because Pitt folks don't leave early. And we got a safety, two points, and I'll take two points.

The goat, and I say it with love like I'm talkin' to my brother-in-law: the run game. Sixty-nine yards on the ground. Gotta get that going, or we're throwin' into the wind all year.

What it means: Pitt is 5-3 and 2-3 in the ACC, losers of three straight. The AP notes Pitt is now 0-14-1 all-time against No. 1, so we've got company in our misery. No shame losing to this Miami team, but the time to turn it around is right now.

Next up: Georgia Tech comes to Acrisure Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN2. Halloween under the lights at home. Wear gold, yinz.

Hail to Pitt. Whistle's dryin' out for next week.
