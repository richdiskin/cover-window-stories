The Pivot Worked: Davis Warren Ships Four Touchdowns and Stanford Holds Off NC State 31-28

Stanford 31, NC State 28 (final), Fri., Oct. 23, Stanford Stadium

By Ignatius 'Iggy' Yamamoto-Ross, The Stanford Fanatic

ShareXFacebook

Greetings, stakeholders. Ignatius "Iggy" Yamamoto-Ross here, founder of three failed apps and, as of Friday night, one wildly successful product launch. Last week my deck said, "Let's pivot to the run game." Stanford pivoted to everything. Stanford 31, NC State 28. Please hold applause until the end of the slide.

The demo opened hot. Davis Warren threw touchdown passes on each of the first two drives, to Nico Brown and Sedrick Irvin, and Stanford racked up 234 yards in the first quarter, its most in an opening quarter since 2017. My houseplant social network gained 234 users in its entire lifetime, and most of them were my mother.

NC State tied it at 14, so Warren ran the two-minute drill like a seed round closing at midnight: 90 yards, capped by a 26-yard touchdown to Caden High with 25 seconds left in the half. Then, after a Wolfpack touchdown came off the board on an offensive pass interference flag, Stanford marched 90 more yards, and Warren hit Brown for 5 yards on fourth-and-4. Going for it on fourth down is what we in the Valley call disruption. NC State scored with 1:20 left to make it 31-28, tried the onside kick, and Stanford recovered. A stranger hugged me. I allowed it. Culture matters.

Hero: Warren, 22 of 37 for 303 yards, four touchdowns and no interceptions. Co-founder: Micah Ford, 150 rushing yards on 22 carries, plus 58 receiving. Caden High caught nine for 103. Affectionate goat: the fourth quarter, when we let the Wolfpack score twice. Consider it a bug. We'll patch it.

What it means: Stanford is 4-4 and 2-3 in the ACC, with four wins in five home games. The road is the problem. The AP notes Stanford is 0-3 away from home, and the Cardinal must win at least one of their next three road games to have a shot at the program's first bowl since 2018.

Next up: at Louisville on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 3:15 p.m. ET on ACC Network. I'm building a deck called "The Farm Travels Well." Slide one is a suitcase.

Go Card, stakeholders. Meeting adjourned.
