Happy 130th, Pack: Luke Duncan Throws Two Touchdowns, Marshaun Brown Throws One, and Nevada Beats San José State on Its Birthday, 28-13

Nevada 28, San José State 13 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, Mackay Stadium

By Ridge Corbell, The Nevada Fanatic

Yo, Pack nation. Ridge Corbell here, on the tailgate of my truck outside Reno, board waxed and birthday cake in the cooler. On Saturday, Nevada football turned 130, and the Wolf Pack celebrated at Mackay Stadium by beating San José State 28-13. Fresh powder, baby. Nobody lost at their own party.

Here's the run, top to bottom.

The first turn: Luke Duncan hit Marshaun Brown for a 4-yard touchdown in the first quarter. Then Duncan threw a 25-yard touchdown pass to Trace Estes in the third. Fourteen to 3.

The trick: early in the fourth, Brown flipped the script and threw a 32-yard touchdown pass to Herschel Turner. A receiver touchdown pass on your 130th birthday? That's a backflip off the big kicker, dude.

The landing: SJSU scored to make it 21-10. Then K. Woods took the kickoff 90 yards to the SJSU 8, and after a penalty, Dominic Kelley ran in from the 4 on the next play. Twenty-eight to 10. Send it.

My hero is Marshaun Brown, with a touchdown catch, a touchdown pass and five catches. Duncan completed 18 of 29 for 178 yards and two scores. Kelley ran 24 times for 82 yards. Dylan LaBarbera had two sacks, and our defense held San José State to one third-down conversion in 14 tries. We also held the ball for over 38 minutes.

My affectionate goat: the second-quarter pass by Reeve Slone that got intercepted. Not every backflip lands, bro.

What it means: Nevada is 3-4 and 2-2 in the Mountain West. Mackay Stadium's field is at 4,610 feet, and on Saturday, the Pack were flying even higher than that.

Next up, Nevada visits UCLA at the Rose Bowl on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 4:15 p.m. ET on BTN. A Halloween trip to Pasadena, a big stage and a chance at a big run. Light the candles. Send it.
