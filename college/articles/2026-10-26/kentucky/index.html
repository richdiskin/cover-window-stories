Alone at the Top of the SEC! No. 12 Kentucky Rallies Past Vanderbilt, 34-17

Kentucky 34, Vanderbilt 17 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Kroger Field

By Rhett Abernathy-Coyle, The Kentucky Fanatic

Good evening, friends, and welcome back to the paddock. Rhett Abernathy-Coyle here, on my fence rail off Versailles Road, in my lucky seersucker for the eighth straight week, because I do not mess with a streak. And friends? Kentucky 34, Vanderbilt 17. We are 7-1. And per the AP, we are alone in first place in the Southeastern Conference. I read that out loud to my mare, Lady Bluegrass, and she whinnied. I'm counting it as agreement.

It did not start like a coronation. Vanderbilt led 10-0, and per the AP, we had 14 yards in the first quarter. Fourteen! Lady Bluegrass travels farther than that on her way to breakfast. But then, the second quarter. Kenny Minchey found DJ Miller for a 9-yard touchdown. And with 21 seconds left in the half, Willie Rodriguez ran it in from a yard out. Fourteen to ten. Back in stride.

The third quarter, Brennen Ward ran 10 yards for a touchdown. Vandy answered with a 34-yard touchdown pass to make it 21-17, and my seersucker started to sweat. Then Adam Zouagui made a 47-yard field goal and a 32-yarder, and Minchey ran one in from a yard out with 5:11 left. Thirteen straight points to finish it. That's a horse that knows how to close.

Hero: Minchey, 19 of 26 for 203 yards with a touchdown pass and a touchdown run. Rodriguez caught six passes for 67 yards and scored. Kenny Darby had a 44-yard catch. Jovantae Barnes ran for 68 yards. And the defense held Vandy to 15 rushing yards, with an interception from Ty Bryant, a fumble recovery from Tavion Gadson and a sack from Mi'Quise Humphrey-Grace. Zouagui was perfect. The goat, with a gentle pat on the nose: that first quarter. Shake it off, fellas.

What it means: Kentucky clinched a winning SEC record for the third time since 1977, and the AP says we haven't been this close to an SEC title since 1976. We jumped to No. 9 in the AP poll released Oct. 25. Coach Will Stein said this was one of our best games, and he's proud of the team's toughness and fight.

Next up: a well-earned week off on Oct. 31, then at Tennessee on Saturday, Nov. 7, time to be announced. Rest the legs, friends. The stretch run is coming.

Go Big Blue! See you in the paddock.
