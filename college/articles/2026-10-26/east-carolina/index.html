Scuttled in Overtime: The Pirates Lead by 14, Tie It With 90 Seconds Left, and Still Sink in Memphis, 41-34

Memphis 41, East Carolina 34 (final, OT), Thu. Oct. 22, Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium

By Bartholomew 'Bart' Quidley, The East Carolina Fanatic

Ahoy, mateys. Bart Quidley here, and the purple tricorn hat is back in its box with a tear stain on the brim. I wore it to Memphis on Thursday night. For three quarters, it was the luckiest hat in Pitt County. Then Memphis beat East Carolina 41-34 in overtime, and now it's just a hat.

Here's how the ship went down.

First, the plunder. We led 16-3 at halftime. Noah Perez kicked four field goals on the night, including a 49-yarder as the first half ended, after D. Speer forced a punt-return fumble and B. Shirley recovered it. Early in the third, TJ Engleman Jr. ran it in from 4 yards out, and Mitch Griffis hit Landon Sides for two. Pirates 24, Tigers 10. I was ordering the grog.

Second, the mutiny. Marcus Stokes threw three touchdown passes and ran for two more after halftime. Memphis took a 34-27 lead with 3:31 left.

Third, the last broadside. Griffis drove us down the field and threw a 20-yard touchdown pass to Landon Sides with 1:30 left. Thirty-four all! I nearly threw the tricorn into the Mississippi. But in overtime, Memphis scored in two plays. We drove to the Memphis 4, and Ellis Ellis Jr. intercepted Griffis in the end zone. Down to Davy Jones' locker.

My hero is Landon Sides: seven catches, 130 yards, the tying touchdown and a two-point catch. Noah Perez went 4-for-4 on field goals and scored 14 points. Engleman ran for 92 yards.

My affectionate goat: the second half. We gave up 38 points after the break. Matey, that's not a defense, that's a plank.

What it means: the Pirates are 3-4 and 1-2 in the American after back-to-back losses, both on the road and both in overtime. Two heartbreakers in a row is a rough voyage, but Greenville has weathered worse storms. Dowdy-Ficklen holds 51,000, and it's time to fill it.

Next up, Temple comes to Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 3 p.m. ET on ESPN+. Halloween at home. Wear your best pirate costume, which in Greenville is just regular clothes. Hoist the colors, mateys. The tricorn is coming back out.
