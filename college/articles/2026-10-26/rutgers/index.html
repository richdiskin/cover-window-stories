Back Past Exit 8A: Rutgers Loses Its Quarterback Early and Falls at Northwestern, 42-14

Northwestern 42, Rutgers 14 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Ryan Field

By Vinny Scolari, The Rutgers Fanatic

Yo. Vinny here.

Last week I was at Exit 9, windows down, joy in the cupholder. This week? I'm back at Exit 8A, where the anxiety lives, sitting in traffic behind a truck full of mattresses. Northwestern 42, Rutgers 14, in Evanston. Ugh.

The big one first, and it ain't a play. Dylan Lonergan, our starting quarterback, took a blow to the head on a scramble 6 minutes and 25 seconds into the game and didn't come back. Get well, kid. That's the only score I care about there.

After that, the turnpike got bumpy. Surace came in and drove us to a 43-yard Dane Pizzaro field goal. Seven to three, fine. Then the sack-fumble at our own 8, Northwestern recovered, and the Wildcats scored on the next snap. Twenty-one to three after one quarter. That's a toll booth with no E-ZPass lane.

Play that still bugs me: late in the third, Clay Thevenin broke a 43-yard run and Antwan Raymond added 31 more to get us to the Northwestern 7. Two incompletions and a fourth-down sack later, nothin'. You can't leave a drive like that in the breakdown lane.

Hero, and I mean it: Antwan Raymond, 150 yards on 18 carries and a 46-yard touchdown run late in the third. Ashenfelder ran in the two-pointer after it. Thevenin added 116 rushing yards. We ran for 244 yards as a team, with backup and third-string quarterbacks in there. That's Jersey grit. Rondo Porter had three tackles for loss and a sack, and DK Gilley intercepted Aidan Chiles, his first interception of the year. Pizzaro hit both field goals.

The goat, said with love like I'm yellin' at my cousin on the Parkway: the passing game, 100 yards and two interceptions. Not the backups' fault. They got thrown in the deep end.

What it means: Rutgers is 2-5 and 1-3 in the Big Ten. The bowl road just got longer and there's construction on every lane. But at home, we can still make some noise.

Next up: Michigan comes to SHI Stadium in Piscataway on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 12:30 p.m. ET on BTN. Halloween at home against the Wolverines. Wear scarlet. Bring a costume. Bring a voice.

Exit 9 is still out there. See ya on the Turnpike.
