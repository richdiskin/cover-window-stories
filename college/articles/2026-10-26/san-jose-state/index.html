Release Notes: Third Downs Broken, Kickoff Coverage Broken, and San José State Falls at Nevada, 28-13

Nevada 28, San José State 13 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, Mackay Stadium

By Tessa Nakagawa-Ruiz, The San José State Fanatic

Hey, team. Tessa Nakagawa-Ruiz here, at my standing desk in downtown San Jose, staring at a whiteboard full of red tickets. On Saturday at Mackay Stadium, Nevada beat San José State 28-13. This release did not pass QA.

Here's the retro.

Bug one: third downs. We converted one of 14. One! Nevada held the ball for more than 38 minutes, and our offense managed 204 yards. That's a blocker, team. Priority zero.

Bug two: the trick play. Down 14-3 early in the fourth, Nevada's Marshaun Brown, who'd already caught a touchdown pass, threw a 32-yard touchdown to Herschel Turner. Twenty-one to 3. Nobody had that one in the spec.

Bug three: kickoff coverage. Luke Weaver hit Dominique McKenzie for 47 yards, and Viliami Teu ran in an 11-yard touchdown. Twenty-one to 10, and we had a little momentum. Then K. Woods returned the kickoff 90 yards to our 8, and Nevada scored on the next play. That's what we call a regression.

What shipped: Trajan Sinatra made field goals of 38 and 41 yards. Jayden Robertson intercepted a pass, and Solomone Langi and Etu Finau had sacks. Tuli Tagovailoa-Amosa made 12 tackles, and Naseri Danielson made 11. Our offense also didn't turn it over.

My hero is Tuli Tagovailoa-Amosa, who also stopped Nevada on fourth down near midfield before halftime. Viliami Teu ran for our touchdown.

My affectionate goat: the penalty flags. Eight for 74 yards. Team, please stop filing bugs against yourselves.

What it means: San José State is 4-4 and 2-2 in the Mountain West. Our sprint velocity is down, but the roadmap still works. San Jose was California's first state capital. We know how to get things started.

Next up, New Mexico comes to CEFCU Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 4 p.m. ET on MW+. Halloween at home: costume optional, loud required. Ship it to prod.
