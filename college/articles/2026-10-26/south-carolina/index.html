Walk-Off at Williams-Brice: Sellers Stretches, Stewart Strips, Orsan Drills It as South Carolina Stuns No. 11 Tennessee

South Carolina 27, Tennessee 24 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Williams-Brice Stadium

By Beaumont 'Beau' Cheraw, The South Carolina Fanatic

Bum. Bum. Bum. BUMMMMM. Beau Cheraw here, former drumline snare, and my sticks are still vibrating. South Carolina 27, No. 11 Tennessee 24, on a 48-yard Malik Orsan field goal as time ran out. Fans poured onto the field. I poured onto the field. I may still be on the field.

Let's play it back, measure by measure. The intro was gorgeous: LaNorris Sellers threw a 17-yard touchdown to Mazeo Bennett Jr. and ran 31 yards for another. Fourteen to nothing, first quarter. Then the tempo dragged. Tennessee scored 21 in the second quarter, added a field goal, and led 24-17 in the fourth.

The bridge: Tennessee went for it on fourth-and-1 at our 39 with 3:35 left, and our defense stuffed MacIntyre. I hit a rimshot on my car's dashboard.

The crescendo: third-and-goal, 1:17 left. Sellers took on two defenders, stayed on his feet and stretched the ball to the pylon. Review upheld it. Tied 24-24. I lost a drumstick in the stands. Then, with 33 seconds left, Dylan Stewart came around the corner and stripped MacIntyre, and Tomiwa Durojaiye fell on it. We ran the clock to three seconds, and Orsan drilled it. BUMMMMM.

Hero: Sellers, 18 of 32 for 210 yards and a touchdown, plus 96 rushing yards and two scores. Stewart and Durojaiye for the play of the year. Orsan, 2 for 2 with the winner. Fred Johnson made 14 tackles, and Justin Okoronkwo added 10. Bennett caught six passes for 88 yards. The goat, with a gentle cymbal crash: the middle two quarters, when Tennessee outscored us 21-3. We'll fix the tempo in rehearsal.

The sweetest note: per the AP, one of coach Shane Beamer's first moves after the game was hugging his father, Frank Beamer, who just turned 80. Happy birthday, Coach. Hope you liked the present.

What it means: South Carolina is 4-3 and 2-3 in the SEC, and the AP notes this follows a win at then-No. 16 Florida two weeks ago. After a three-game skid, the Gamecocks have their swagger back, and earned votes in the AP poll released Oct. 25.

Next up: at Oklahoma on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 4:15 p.m. ET on SEC Network. Halloween in Norman. I'll bring the snare.

Forever to Thee, and go Gamecocks! Bum. Bum. Bum. BUMMMMM.
