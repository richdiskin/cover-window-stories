Another Medal for the Sash: A'Marion Peterson Scores Twice, Owen Pewee Gets Three Sacks, and UTSA Wins a Fifth Straight, 42-19 at Tulane

UTSA 42, Tulane 19 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, Yulman Stadium

By Marisol Ybarra, The UTSA Fanatic

Birds up! Marisol Ybarra here, on a River Walk patio, with a sash so heavy I may need a third ribbon. On Saturday at Yulman Stadium, UTSA beat Tulane 42-19. That's five straight wins, Roadrunners. Somebody get me a needle and thread.

Here's how the medals got earned.

Medal one: the bounce back. Tulane's Anthony Rogers intercepted our very first pass and ran it back 22 yards for a touchdown. Not the start I wanted, mi gente. But A'Marion Peterson ran in from the 1 and then from the 16, and Owen McCown ran in a 4-yard touchdown. Twenty-one to 7.

Medal two: the defense, which forced six turnovers. J. Buxton forced a fumble at our 1 that went out the end zone for a touchback. Willie Nelson III and Zach Morris each intercepted Trace Johnson. Our defense recovered four fumbles. And Owen Pewee had three sacks.

Medal three: the knockout. Up 21-13 at halftime, Will Henderson III ran in a 9-yard touchdown, and Brandon High Jr. ran in from the 5. Thirty-five to 13. Mark Wiepert added a 1-yard touchdown late.

My hero is Owen Pewee, with three sacks. Henderson ran for 102 yards, and Peterson scored twice. We converted 12 of 16 third downs and held the ball for over 35 minutes.

My affectionate goat: our own turnovers. McCown threw two interceptions, and Peterson fumbled at the Tulane 3. Three giveaways in a blowout? Ay, Roadrunners, don't make me take a medal back.

What it means: UTSA is 7-1 and 4-0 in the American, and the Roadrunners are bowl eligible. San Antonio has held Fiesta every April since 1891, and right now it feels like Fiesta in October.

Next up, UTSA visits Florida Atlantic at Flagler Credit Union Stadium on Thursday, Nov. 5, at 8 p.m. ET. TV is still to be announced. A week off first, then a Thursday night under the lights. Birds up!
