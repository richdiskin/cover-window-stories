Lid Opened Too Early: NC State Rallies Late but Comes Up Three Short at Stanford

Stanford 31, NC State 28 (final), Fri., Oct. 23, Stanford Stadium

By Rutherford 'Ruff' Pegram, The NC State Fanatic

XFacebook

Well, howdy, Pack. Rutherford "Ruff" Pegram here, writing from a cold grill. Not a bye-week cold grill. A "we flew all the way to the Farm and came home three points light" cold grill. Stanford 31, NC State 28. Somebody opened the smoker lid too early.

First rule of the pit: don't let the fire get away from you early. Davis Warren threw touchdowns on Stanford's first two drives, and it was 14-0 before most of us back home found the channel. But this team adjusts the vents. CJ Bailey dropped a beauty to Deandre Desinor for a 58-yard touchdown, Will Wilson plowed in from 2 yards, and it was 14-14. I howled. That's my one howl. Discipline.

Then the flare-ups. We let Stanford go 90 yards in the last two minutes of the half for a 21-14 lead. Our opening drive of the third quarter ended in a touchdown, until a flag for offensive pass interference on Victor Snow took it away and we punted. Stanford went 90 more and scored on fourth-and-4. Wilson answered with his second 2-yard touchdown, and Bailey hit Keenan Jackson for 16 yards with 1:20 left to make it 31-28. Onside kick. Stanford recovered. Fire out.

Hero platter: Bailey, 17 of 30 for 256 yards, two touchdowns, no interceptions and 59 rushing yards. Ronnie Royal III made 10 tackles. We didn't turn it over once and went 8 of 14 on third down. That's temperature control. The goat, with love: the pass defense, which allowed 303 yards and four touchdown throws. When the other pitmaster serves four touchdowns, check your thermometer.

And credit to the Cardinal. The AP says this was only NC State's second game on the West Coast in 66 seasons, and our boys practiced late at night to fight the time change. Didn't quite take.

What it means: 3-4, 1-3 in the ACC, and the margin in the conference race is gone. But two of our four losses came by four points or fewer. Close fires can be fixed.

Next up: California comes to Carter-Finley on Saturday, Oct. 31, at noon ET on ACC Network. Halloween in Raleigh. Grill lit by 7 a.m.

Light the fire, Pack. We're not done cooking.
