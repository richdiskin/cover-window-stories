The Court Rules for the Ground Game: Boston College Runs for 399 Yards in a 49-27 Rout at Georgia Tech

Boston College 49, Georgia Tech 27 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Bobby Dodd Stadium

By Siobhan Dunleavy, The Boston College Fanatic

All rise. The Court of Siobhan Dunleavy is now in session, and I find the defendant, Georgia Tech, guilty on all counts of being run over. Boston College 49, Georgia Tech 27, in Atlanta. Wicked good. Case closed. Actually, no, I love the sound of my own gavel. Let's review the evidence.

Exhibit A: the slow start. Georgia Tech scored on its first two possessions and led 10-0. The Court was displeased. For the second straight week, BC fell behind by double digits early, then rallied, just like the 27-21 win over Pitt. I'd like the record to show I am too old for this.

Exhibit B: the third quarter. Trailing 13-12 at halftime, the Eagles scored touchdowns on their first two possessions after the break. Mason McKenzie faked the handoff up the middle and ran 40 yards right down the Georgia Tech sideline. Bo MacCormack III punched in a 2-yarder to take the lead for good. Then Charlie Comella intercepted a deflected pass, and McKenzie ran it in from the 6. Objection overruled, Atlanta.

Exhibit C: the dagger. Up 35-20 in the fourth, Comella intercepted his second pass of the day and returned it 47 yards for a touchdown. The jury gasped. The jury is me. I gasped.

Hero: the ground game. Three hundred ninety-nine rushing yards. McKenzie ran for 170 and two touchdowns and threw a 25-yard touchdown to Nedrick Boldin Jr. MacCormack ran for 155 yards and three scores, which, per the AP, is more than he had in his previous 13 career games combined. He filled in for Evan Dickens, who was hurt against Pitt and is reportedly out for the season. Get well, Evan. And Comella, two interceptions and a touchdown. Anthony Palano made nine tackles.

The goat, sentenced to community service: the kicking game. One missed field goal and two missed extra points. Practice your PATs, gentlemen. That's a court order.

What it means: BC is 4-3 and 2-2 in the ACC, already doubling the two wins from last year's 2-10 season, and two wins from bowl eligibility. The Court finds this program officially un-dismissed.

Next up: at Duke on Saturday, Oct. 31, at noon ET on The CW. Halloween in Durham. I'll be wearing my robe, which is also my costume.

Court adjourned. Ever to Excel, and go Eagles!
