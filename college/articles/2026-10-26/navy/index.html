Logbook Entry: 506 Yards, 228 From Jackson Gutierrez, and a Comeback That Fell Three Points Short as North Texas Beats Navy, 49-39

North Texas 49, Navy 39 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, Navy-Marine Corps Memorial Stadium

By Cmdr. (Ret.) Annabel Merriweather, The Navy Fanatic

Good morning, shipmates. Commander Annabel Merriweather, retired, reporting from the seawall in Annapolis. I've been keeping my logbook for a long time, and I'll record Saturday honestly: North Texas beat Navy 49-39 at Navy-Marine Corps Memorial Stadium. We ran for 363 yards and still lost. The logbook has a few water stains this morning.

Entry one: the leaks. Our defense couldn't stop the deep passes. Justin Stevenson caught 209 yards worth of them. Worse, two special-teams plays sank us. A fumbled snap led to a punt, and Dalton Carnes returned it 76 yards for a touchdown, and Zakye Barker returned a fumble 63 yards for a touchdown in the third.

Entry two: the counterattack. Jackson Gutierrez ran for an 18-yard touchdown, a 1-yarder and a 43-yarder, then threw a two-point conversion pass to Jake Norris. Irabonoise Oniha intercepted Tayven Jackson, and Gutierrez hit Michael Barrow for a 15-yard touchdown pass late in the third. Barrow ran in from the 4 early in the fourth. Forty-two to 39. I was on my feet on the seawall. Full speed ahead.

Entry three: the torpedo. On North Texas' very next drive, Jahiem White ran 59 yards for a touchdown. Then they held the ball for the last 6:32. We never got it back.

My hero is Jackson Gutierrez: 26 carries, 228 yards, three rushing touchdowns and a touchdown pass. Barrow ran for 92 yards and scored twice, and the team committed only one penalty all day. That's a disciplined crew.

My affectionate goat: the deep pass defense, which let Stevenson catch passes of 45, 46 and 53 yards. Fellas, we need more sailors in the deep water.

What it means: Navy is 2-5 and 1-4 in the American, with two straight losses in shootouts. George Washington resigned his commission in Annapolis, but this team doesn't quit.

Next up, Navy plays Notre Dame at Gillette Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at noon ET on ABC. One of college football's oldest series, on Halloween, on national TV. Brace for heavy seas. Steady as she goes.
