Mission Accomplished at the Gun: Kloska Blocks a Punt, Hellums Scores Three Times, and Dawson Jones Drills a 50-Yarder to Beat Tulsa, 38-35

Army 38, Tulsa 35 (final), Fri. Oct. 23, H. A. Chapman Stadium

By Capt. (Ret.) Thaddeus Rourke, The Army Fanatic

Attention on deck. Capt. Thaddeus Rourke, retired, reporting from my recliner in a state of high alert. My laminated clipboard has a dent in it, and that dent is the exact shape of my fist. Friday night at H. A. Chapman Stadium, Army beat Tulsa 38-35 on a 50-yard field goal as time expired. Field report follows.

Phase one: the setback. Tulsa scored three straight touchdowns, including a 59-yard run by Trequan Jones, and took a 35-28 lead early in the fourth quarter. Mission in jeopardy. My coffee went cold, against regulations.

Phase two: special operations. Tulsa missed a 40-yard field goal. Then, with 3:52 left, T. Kloska blocked a Tulsa punt and recovered it at the Tulsa 13. Five plays later, Cale Hellums pushed in from the 1 on third down. Thirty-five all. Hooah!

Phase three: the final objective. Our defense forced a three-and-out. Julian Porter broke up a pass, and K. Harris-Miller and J. Bousum sacked Baylor Hayes. Army got the ball back at our 37 with 45 seconds left and moved 27 yards in seven plays. Then Dawson Jones stepped up and hit a 50-yarder as the clock struck zero. Target neutralized.

My heroes: Dawson Jones went 3-for-3 on field goals, from 51, 34 and 50 yards. In a game decided by three points, that's the whole margin. Cale Hellums ran 30 times for 121 yards and three touchdowns. Godspower Nwawuihe ran 12 times for 135 yards, including a 66-yard touchdown. Kloska gets a medal for the block.

My affectionate goat: the run defense in the third quarter. We gave up two long touchdown runs in four minutes. Soldiers, we will be running drills.

What it means: Army is 5-2 and 4-1 in the American after three straight wins. Last year, Tulsa beat us by one point at West Point. This year, we returned the favor on their field. Michie Stadium has stood since 1924, and the Corps has been settling accounts much longer than that.

Next up, Army visits Memphis at Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 8 p.m. ET on ESPNU. Thank God for every man and woman who serves, and pack a jacket. Copy that, and beat Navy.
