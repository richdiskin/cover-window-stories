Four Punts, Four Dad Jokes, One Big Win: Keldric Luster Accounts for Three Touchdowns as Ball State Beats Sac State, 34-17

Ball State 34, Sacramento State 17 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, Scheumann Stadium

By Gus Ponder, The Ball State Fanatic

Gus Ponder here, coming to you from the garage, where the whiteboard is full of arrows and the punt chart says four. On Saturday at Scheumann Stadium, Ball State beat Sacramento State 34-17. That's two wins in a row, folks. I'm so happy I alphabetized my socket set.

Rules are rules, though. Four punts, four dad jokes. Here we go.

Joke one: Why did the Cardinal sit in the garage? He was waiting for a big play. Well, he didn't have to wait long. Down 3-0 in the second, Keldric Luster threw a 65-yard touchdown pass to Isaiah Thacker.

Joke two: What do you call a bird that won't give up? A comeback Cardinal. Sac State answered with a 65-yard touchdown pass, and less than a minute later, Luster found Jamarion McDougle for 55 yards and a score. Thirteen to 10. Yes, we missed the extra point. I'm not ready to talk about it.

Joke three: Why was the quarterback so calm? Because he had his sacks in order. With 38 seconds left in the half, Kaleb Stewart sacked Jackson Sharman and forced a fumble, and B. Marsh recovered. TJ Horton scored from the 1. Twenty to 10.

Joke four: How does Garfield like his football? Lasagna-style, in layers. Luster ran in from the 6 early in the third, and Horton added a 6-yard touchdown run in the fourth. Thirty-four to 17. Jim Davis studied at Ball State, by the way. I tell everyone.

My hero is Keldric Luster: two touchdown passes, a rushing touchdown and 234 passing yards. Jalen Bonds ran for 77 yards, and Cole Stumbaugh averaged 51.8 yards on his four punts, with a long of 70.

My affectionate goat: interceptions. Luster threw two, and both went to Boogsie Silvera. Keldric, buddy, he's not on our team.

What it means: Ball State is 3-5 and 2-2 in the MAC. The Ball brothers built a glass factory here in 1888, and this season, things are starting to look clearer.

Next up, Ball State visits UMass at Warren McGuirk Alumni Stadium on Wednesday, Nov. 4. Kickoff time and TV are still to be announced. Here's the garage plan:

1. Run the ball. Then run it again. 2. Clean tackling. 3. Keep Stumbaugh rested. Fewer punts, fewer jokes. I'll make that sacrifice.
