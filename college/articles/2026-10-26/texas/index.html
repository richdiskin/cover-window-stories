Hook 'Em, Hold On: Arch Manning Runs for Two Scores as Texas Survives No. 7 Ole Miss, 34-27

Texas 34, Ole Miss 27 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium

By Caro Whitfield, The Texas Fanatic

XFacebook

Well, hello, y'all. Caro Whitfield, from the Forty Acres, where the Tower is glowing burnt orange, and darling, I needed to see that glow. Texas 34, No. 7 Ole Miss 27. Did we make it harder than it needed to be? Honey, we made it a whole quarterly report. But a win is a win, and this one ended a two-game skid.

The first half was a board meeting where every slide landed. Arch Manning ran one in from 2 yards out. Then he hit Ryan Wingo for a 50-yard touchdown. Michael Terry III ran in from 2 more. And just before halftime, our defense stopped Ole Miss at the goal. Texas 24, Ole Miss 3. I was mentally drafting the press release.

Then the third quarter happened, and the press release went in the shredder. On the first possession of the half, Manning got hit from the blind side, and Ole Miss recovered in the end zone. Then the Rebels threw two touchdowns to Horatio Fields, and it was 24-24 early in the fourth. Per the AP, we set season highs with 14 penalties for 134 yards. Coach Steve Sarkisian said we weren't perfect, and he could recite the penalty yards. So could I.

And then, the comeback within the comeback. Raleek Brown broke a 47-yard run, and Manning faked the handoff and strolled around the end untouched from a yard out. Thirty-one to twenty-four. Gianni Spetic made it 34-24 with a 33-yarder. Ole Miss kicked a field goal and recovered an onside kick, which made my blood pressure a key metric. But on the final play, Jelani McDonald intercepted the pass in the end zone. Hook 'em!

Hero: Brown, 157 rushing yards, and Manning, a touchdown pass and his first two rushing scores of the season. Wingo caught seven passes for 111 yards. McDonald made 12 tackles plus the game-ending interception, and Justin Cryer and Rasheem Biles also intercepted passes. Spetic was perfect. The goat, with my most diplomatic smile: penalties and special teams. We'll address that in the Q4 review.

What it means: Texas is 5-2 and 2-2 in the SEC, back on track after two losses, and jumped to No. 11 in the AP poll released Oct. 25.

Next up: Mississippi State visits DKR on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN, the last of three straight home games. Halloween night in Austin. I'm dressed as a rodeo queen. It's not a costume.

Hook 'em, Horns! The Eyes of Texas are upon you.
