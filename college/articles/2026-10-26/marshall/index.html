We Are... Clutch: Judah Price Punches It In With 1:49 Left, and the Herd Holds On at the Joan

Marshall 20, South Alabama 13 (final), Tue. Oct. 20, Joan C. Edwards Stadium

By Mercy Holderby, The Marshall Fanatic

How am I? We are. Mercy Holderby here, green ribbon on the microphone and a voice that is officially out of commission after Tuesday night. Marshall beat South Alabama 20-13 at Joan C. Edwards Stadium, and the night-shift nurses on my floor will have to communicate with me by sign language until Thursday.

Let me chart the vitals.

Vital sign one: patience. It was 6-6 at the half. Jorge Diaz Nicolas hit field goals from 47 and 48 yards, and our defense kept South Alabama's offense in triage.

Vital sign two: the takeaway. In the third quarter, Cole Funderburk intercepted Jared Hollins at midfield. Soon after, Zion Turner threw a 38-yard dart to Adrian Norton, and then Turner ran it in from 3 yards out. Thirteen to six, and the Joan was rocking.

Vital sign three: the code. South Alabama tied it at 13 with 4:38 left, and my heart rate did something I'd chart as "concerning." Then the Herd went 75 yards in eight plays. Khalil Wilkins ripped off 14 yards to the 9, and Judah Price bulled in from the 5 with 1:49 left. On South Alabama's last chance, B. Ward hurried Hollins into a fourth-down incompletion. Patient stable. Discharge papers signed.

My hero is Zion Turner: 21 of 33 for 181 yards, a rushing touchdown, and zero turnovers. After six turnovers against Coastal earlier this month, a clean chart is beautiful. Funderburk and Ward are co-heroes. The defense held the Jaguars to 4-of-18 on third down.

My affectionate goat: the kicking game, which also missed from 49 and 27 yards. Jorge, honey, I love you. Next time, aim for the middle.

What it means: Marshall is 4-3 and 1-2 in the Sun Belt, and that sour taste from the Coastal loss is finally gone. We needed a win at home, and the Herd brought one.

Next up, Marshall visits Old Dominion at S.B. Ballard Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 1 p.m. ET on ESPN+. Halloween in Norfolk. I'm going as a nurse, which is not a costume, it's a lifestyle. Herd up, friends. How are we? We are... Marshall!
