Seven-Eight Time in Annapolis: Justin Stevenson Catches 209 Yards, Jahiem White Seals It, and North Texas Outlasts Navy, 49-39

North Texas 49, Navy 39 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, Navy-Marine Corps Memorial Stadium

By Grady Mayhew-Ochoa, The North Texas Fanatic

Mean Green, baby. Grady Mayhew-Ochoa here, sticks in my back pocket and my heart still doing a drum solo. Saturday at Navy-Marine Corps Memorial Stadium, North Texas beat Navy 49-39. That wasn't a football game. That was a 60-minute jam session where both bands refused to stop playing.

Here's the setlist.

Track one, the big hits. Tayven Jackson found Justin Stevenson for a 45-yard touchdown on our first drive and a 53-yarder in the third. Stevenson caught eight passes for 209 yards. That's a solo, baby. Crash cymbal on every catch.

Track two, the special guests. Dalton Carnes returned a punt 76 yards for a touchdown in the second quarter. Then, in the third, Zakye Barker scooped up a Navy fumble and ran 63 yards for another score. When your defense and special teams both take a solo, you know the band is cooking.

Track three, the closer. Navy kept coming, and after Michael Barrow's 4-yard run with 10:49 left, it was 42-39. My hi-hat was shaking. On our very next drive, Jahiem White ran 59 yards to the end zone. Forty-nine to 39. Then we held the ball for the final 6:32 with a 12-play drive. That's a fade-out, baby. The crowd goes home.

My hero is Justin Stevenson, but Jahiem White deserves an encore: 174 rushing yards, two touchdowns and four catches. Jackson threw for 305 yards and three touchdowns on 17-of-23 passing.

My affectionate goat: the run defense. Navy ran 65 times for 363 yards. Jackson Gutierrez alone ran for 228. My drummer brain needs a nap.

What it means: North Texas is 5-2 and 3-0 in the American after four straight wins. Denton's College of Music was the first school to offer a jazz studies degree, and Saturday, this team played like it graduated with honors.

Next up, Florida Atlantic comes to DATCU Stadium on Thursday, Oct. 29, at 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN. Thursday night, national TV, home crowd. Count it off. Seven-eight time, baby.
