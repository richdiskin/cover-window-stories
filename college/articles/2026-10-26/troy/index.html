Upside Down and Inside Out: Troy Leads 14-3 With 6:16 Left, Then Gives Up 18 Points as ULM Steals One, 21-14

UL Monroe 21, Troy 14 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, Veterans Memorial Stadium (AL)

By Marlo Pickens, The Troy Fanatic

Hey there, buddy. Marlo Pickens, former Trojan long snapper, peanut farmer's son, and a man staring at the world upside down. On Saturday at Veterans Memorial Stadium, UL Monroe beat Troy 21-14. I flipped that game over every way I know, and it still looks bad. ULM hadn't won a game all year.

Here's the row-by-row count.

The planting: our defense was stout for 57 minutes. Gavin Griffin caught a 5-yard touchdown pass from Tucker Kilcrease in the first quarter and ran in from the 1 with 6:16 left. Fourteen to 3. I started counting my peanuts before they were boiled.

The weeds: Aidan Armenta threw a 12-yard touchdown with 2:34 left. We went three-and-out, and Kilcrease got sacked. Then, with 1:40 left, ULM drove 86 yards, helped by our 15-yard personal foul, and Armenta threw a 16-yard touchdown with 15 seconds left. Fifteen to 14.

The hailstorm: on the final play, we fumbled, and Billy Pullen fell on it in the end zone. Twenty-one to 14. I sat on the tailgate for an hour.

Earlier, we wasted a gift. We recovered a muffed punt at the ULM 21 in the second quarter, and moments later, our pass was intercepted in the end zone. That's a whole row of peanuts left in the field.

My hero is Jordan Lovett, who ran 17 times for 70 yards. Keno Jones had 12 tackles, and Ty Clemons and Donnie Smith had sacks.

My affectionate goat: the passing game. Forty-seven yards, fellas. I've thrown farther than that... well, backwards, between my legs.

What it means: Troy is 3-4 and 1-2 in the Sun Belt, with its first home loss of the season. Troy used to be called Deer Stand Hill, and right now, we're sitting in that deer stand waiting for something good to walk by.

Next up, Troy visits James Madison at Bridgeforth Stadium on Thursday, Oct. 29, at 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN2. A short week, a tough road trip and a national TV crowd. Come early, buddy. Bring a sack of boiled peanuts. Bring two. I'll bring the puns.
