Darlings, What a Finish: D'Shawntae Jones Scores With 1:41 Left, and Miami Steals One in Mount Pleasant, 28-27

Miami (OH) 28, Central Michigan 27 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, Kelly/Shorts Stadium

By Cordelia Van Doren, The Miami (OH) Fanatic

Hello, darlings. Cordelia Van Doren here, back on my bench in Uptown Oxford, tea gone cold, red scarf wrapped twice around my neck for emotional support. Saturday at Kelly/Shorts Stadium, Miami beat Central Michigan 28-27. One point, darlings. I aged a decade, and I'm wearing it beautifully.

Here's the drama in three acts.

Act one: the plot twist. Early in the third, Justin Taylor intercepted Caleb Heavner and returned it 46 yards for a touchdown. Central Michigan led 17-14. I clutched my pearls so hard one popped off. Heavner answered with a 27-yard touchdown run. Very Cradle of Coaches of him.

Act two: the deficit. Central Michigan scored a touchdown and a field goal, and with 4:17 left, we trailed 27-21. I put my tea down. Things were serious.

Act three: the triumph. Our offense drove 75 yards in eight plays. Heavner threw a 27-yard pass to Maleek Huggins at the CMU 10. Then the Chippewas were flagged for pass interference twice in a row, and D'Shawntae Jones bulled in from the 2 with 1:41 left. Twenty-eight to 27. Then our defense held on downs, Jones picked up a first down, and we knelt. Curtain. Roses. Bows.

My hero is Maleek Huggins: seven catches for 122 yards, including the big one on the winning drive. Heavner ran for a touchdown and threw for 221 yards. Roosevelt Andrews III had a sack and a half.

My affectionate goat: interceptions. Heavner threw two, and one went the other way for a touchdown. Caleb, darling, we pass to the red jerseys. It's in the brochure.

What it means: Miami is 5-3 and 3-1 in the MAC after three straight wins, one game behind Ohio. And we've now beaten Central Michigan six straight times. Paul Brown, Woody Hayes, Bo Schembechler, Ara Parseghian and Sean McVay all came through Miami. Saturday, we saw why this place makes winners.

Next up, Buffalo comes to Yager Stadium on Tuesday, Nov. 3, at 7 p.m. ET. A Tuesday night, darlings. Bring a blanket and your best red. Ta-ta, darlings. Love and Hawks.
