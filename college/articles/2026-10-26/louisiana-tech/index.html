Two Shutouts, One Orchard: Blake Baker Completes 28 of 34, the Defense Sacks ODU Six Times, and Louisiana Tech Wins 26-0

Louisiana Tech 26, Old Dominion 0 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, Joe Aillet Stadium

By Booker 'Champ' Tullos, The Louisiana Tech Fanatic

ShareXFacebook

Well, howdy, and welcome to the orchard. Champ Tullos here, former fullback and full-time peach man. On Saturday at Joe Aillet Stadium, Louisiana Tech beat Old Dominion 26-0. That's two straight shutouts, folks. Our defense hasn't allowed a point in eight quarters. These peaches are ripe.

Here's how we picked the crop. Well, harvested it. You know what I mean.

First, the fast start. We scored two touchdowns in the first quarter. Blake Baker ran in from the 4, and Trey Kukuk ran in from the 5. That's my kind of football, right up the middle, fullback style.

Second, the air show. Baker completed 28 of 34 passes for 303 yards. Eli Finley caught 10 passes for 128 yards, including a 2-yard touchdown in the third quarter. Jalen Mickens caught eight for 110, including a 36-yarder to set up a Hunter Rioux 49-yard field goal.

Third, the brick wall. Our defense sacked Jaxon Potter six times. Jadon Mayfield had two, and Alonzo Jackson Jr. and Justice Williams had one and a half each. Old Dominion ran for minus-13 yards. Amari Butler intercepted Potter in the fourth quarter, and we stopped them on fourth down twice.

My hero is the whole defense, but Jadon Mayfield gets the first peach. Baker gets the second, for throwing and running for touchdowns. And Kaegan Kent added a 25-yard field goal.

My affectionate goat: penalties. Nine for 75 yards. And we got stuffed on a fourth down at the 10 before halftime. A fullback would've scored there, I'm just saying.

What it means: Louisiana Tech is 5-2 and 3-0 in the Sun Belt, in our first year in the league. We've played football since 1901, and Saturday felt like one of the good ones. Ruston has a Peach Festival every June, but we're celebrating early this year.

Next up, Louisiana Tech visits South Alabama at Hancock Whitney Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 3 p.m. ET on ESPN+. A Halloween trip to Mobile. Come early, bring a lawn chair, and if anybody offers you a peach, say yes ma'am.
