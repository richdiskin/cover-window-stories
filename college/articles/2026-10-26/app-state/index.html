The View Was Worth the Climb: James London Splits It in Overtime, and App State Knocks Off Unbeaten JMU in Overtime

App State 34, James Madison 31 (final, OT), Thu. Oct. 22, Kidd Brewer Stadium

By Zebulon 'Zeb' Coldiron, The App State Fanatic

Mornin' from the porch. Zeb Coldiron here, notebook open, coffee long gone cold, and voice gone hoarse from Thursday night. Kidd Brewer Stadium, 33,322 strong, wind off the ridge, and a 7-0 James Madison team came up our mountain. They left with a loss. App State won 34-31 in overtime. Let's take this hike one switchback at a time.

Switchback one: the takeaway. It was 24-24 with about six minutes left when Colton Phares picked off Camden Coleman and returned it 21 yards to the JMU 14. Moments later, Henry Hasselbeck hit Luke Douglas for a 4-yard touchdown. Hasselbeck also threw the earlier touchdown pass to Chris Lofton that, with Malachi Singleton's two-point pass to Sam Pickett III, tied it at 21. Not bad for a backup.

Switchback two: the loose gravel. JMU drove the length of the field and scored with three seconds left. Thirty-one all. I said a word on the porch my mama wouldn't have approved of.

Switchback three: the summit. JMU had the ball first in overtime and lost 11 yards on a run. Their 47-yard kick missed. Then we ran two plays, set it up in the middle, and James London split it from 40.

My hero is James London. Four field goals on four tries, including the winner, plus two extra points. That's 14 points from a kicker. Malachi Singleton deserves a nod too: 26 of 40 for 271 yards and two touchdowns. And the defense blocked a field goal and held JMU to 2-of-15 on third down.

My affectionate goat: the late lead we let slip. Up seven with under six minutes left, and we gave up a tying drive. That one made my blood pressure climb faster than the road up from Blowing Rock.

What it means: App State is 5-2 and 2-1 in the Sun Belt after its second straight win, and we just beat the conference's last unbeaten team. Boone sits at 3,333 feet, and the view from up here looks mighty fine.

Next up, App State visits Georgia Southern at Allen E. Paulson Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 6:30 p.m. ET on ESPN+. Halloween in Statesboro. Bring a jacket, Mountaineers. Slow down, the view's worth the wait.
