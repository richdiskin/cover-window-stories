Seven Index Cards, Seven W's: UCLA Shakes Off a Slow Open to Beat Michigan State 28-10

UCLA 28, Michigan State 10 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Rose Bowl

By Kensington 'Kenzie' Rhee, The UCLA Fanatic

XFacebook

Okay, everybody, settle in, phones on silent. Thank you for taking this meeting. Kenzie Rhee here, and I have news for the corkboard: card number seven says W. UCLA 28, Michigan State 10, at the Rose Bowl. The Bruins are 7-0 for the first time since 2005, per the AP. My landlord has described the corkboard as "a lot." It is now "a lot, plus one."

Act one was a rough cut. We fumbled, Michigan State's Devin Vaught scooped it up and ran 56 yards, and the Spartans scored on a 14-yard pass to go up 7-0. First time we'd trailed in two weeks. I put my head in my hands. My index cards fluttered.

Then the montage. Jaivian Thomas ran 7 yards to tie it. Wayne Knight plunged in from the 1 for his 12th rushing touchdown of the season, per the AP. Fourteen to seven at the half.

The showstopper opened the third quarter: Ty Dieffenbach launched a 63-yard touchdown to Brian Rowe Jr. to finish a four-play, 75-yard drive. Then Anthony Woods punched one in from 4 yards out. Twenty-eight to seven. Cue the strings. In the second and third quarters, UCLA outgained Michigan State 280 to 13. That's not a game, that's a montage.

Hero: Dieffenbach, 198 passing yards in only his second start since Nico Iamaleava went down with a season-ending knee injury. Woods ran for 112 yards on 14 carries. Semaj Morgan caught seven passes. Defense? Award-season worthy. Sammy Omosigho had two sacks, Jalen Woods had nine tackles and a sack, and Donavyn Pellot and Malik Hartford each intercepted a pass. Michigan State finished with 156 yards of offense.

The goat, gently, from the director's chair: ball security, with two lost fumbles at home. Coach Bob Chesney called it sloppy, and he's right. Also, we wish Knight well after he left with a hit to his side.

What it means: UCLA is 7-0 and 5-0 in the Big Ten and climbed from No. 9 to No. 7 in the AP poll released Oct. 25. Every frame is still a W. Please, nobody touch the corkboard.

Next up: Nevada visits the Rose Bowl on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 4:15 p.m. ET on BTN. Halloween in Pasadena. Costumes encouraged. Blue and gold mandatory.

That's a wrap. Eight clap, and go Bruins!
