Counter: 1,000! ULM Scores 18 Points in the Final 2:34, Billy Pullen Scores on the Last Play, and the Warhawks Get Their First Win at Troy, 21-14

UL Monroe 21, Troy 14 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, Veterans Memorial Stadium (AL)

By Darnell Vidrine, The UL Monroe Fanatic

XFacebook

Good evening, night owls, and welcome back to the overnight show. Darnell Vidrine here, and friends, I need you to turn the radio up. On Saturday at Veterans Memorial Stadium, UL Monroe beat Troy 21-14. That's our first win of the season. The optimism counter just broke. It's at 1,000. I might need a new counter.

Here's how it went down.

For most of the night, it looked like another reset. Troy led 7-3 at halftime and 14-3 after Gavin Griffin's 1-yard touchdown with 6:16 left. But DJ Jester intercepted a pass in the end zone in the second quarter, right after Troy recovered our muffed punt at our 21. Hope was still on the air.

First hit: Aidan Armenta threw a 12-yard touchdown pass to Bryce Anderson with 2:34 left. Fourteen to 9. Our defense forced a three-and-out, and James Smith sacked Tucker Kilcrease. A holding penalty put us at our 14 with 1:40 left.

Second hit: 86 yards in six plays. Zach Palmer-Smith ran 14 yards, and a Troy personal foul added 15 more. Armenta hit P. Fulghum for 19 yards, then Darrion Gilliam for a 16-yard touchdown with 15 seconds left. Fifteen to 14!

Third hit: on the final play, the ball came loose, and Billy Pullen fell on it in the end zone. Twenty-one to 14. Friends, I spilled my coffee on the soundboard. Worth it.

My hero is Aidan Armenta: 13 of 22 for 147 yards and two late touchdowns. Billy Pullen had the winning touchdown, Kevontay Wells had a sack and a half, and Noah Flemmings had 15 tackles. Our defense held Troy to 47 passing yards.

My affectionate goat: two failed two-point tries. They made my heart skip beats. Our hearts are fine now. Mostly.

What it means: UL Monroe is 1-6 and 1-2 in the Sun Belt. Monroe is where the crop-dusting company that became Delta got started. Tonight, the Warhawks took off.

Next up, Southern Miss comes to Malone Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 3:30 p.m. ET on ESPN+. Halloween at home. Counter: reset to 100. Keep the faith, Warhawks.
