Sweet Tea and Sweeter Finishes: Steven Robinson Scores Three Times, Jamarice Wilder Wins It Late, and Southern Miss Tops Louisiana, 35-31

Southern Miss 35, Louisiana 31 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, Renasant Stadium

By Delphine 'Dee' Broussard, The Southern Miss Fanatic

XFacebook

Well, hey there, sugar. Dee Broussard, on my porch in the Pine Belt, and this week, my sweet tea and my disposition are equally sweet. On Saturday in Hattiesburg, Southern Miss beat Louisiana 35-31. That's our first win since the season opener, sugar, and it was worth every gray hair.

Here's the tea.

Pour one: Steven Robinson ran for three touchdowns in the first half, from 8, 4 and 10 yards. He carried 22 times for 145 yards. Bless that young man's legs.

Pour two: we trailed 28-21 heading into the fourth. Then Kolby Peavy intercepted Lunch Winfield and scored. Tied, 28-28. I spilled my tea all over my bow. Worth it.

Pour three: Louisiana kicked a field goal with 2:51 left to take a 31-28 lead. Our offense drove 75 yards in eight plays, and Jamarice Wilder ran 28 yards and then 16 yards for the touchdown with 58 seconds left. Then our defense forced three straight incompletions, and Landry Lyddy took a knee. Sugar, I hugged a stranger.

My hero is Jamarice Wilder: 13 carries, 154 yards and the winning touchdown. Robinson ran for three scores, and we ran for 286 yards as a team. Michael Robinson III had 12 tackles and a sack.

My affectionate goat: the kicking game. Creighton Wilbanks missed his only field goal try, a 41-yarder. Also, our fourth-down offense went four for seven, with three stops. Sometimes a lady has to punt, sugar.

What it means: Southern Miss is 2-5 and 1-2 in the Sun Belt, and that ended a five-game losing streak. The Golden Eagles won conference titles in 1996, 1997, 1999, 2006 and 2011. We're not there yet, but Saturday felt like the start.

Next up, Southern Miss visits UL Monroe at Malone Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 3:30 p.m. ET on ESPN+. A Halloween road trip, a fresh win and a new bow. To the top, sugar. We're just climbing from the bottom step.
