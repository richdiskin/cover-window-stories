Let It Rip: Trey Owens Throws Two Touchdowns, Runs for Two More, and Arkansas State Holds Off Georgia State, 38-32

Arkansas State 38, Georgia State 32 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, Centennial Bank Stadium

By Ty Doucet-Harmon, The Arkansas State Fanatic

Quaaack. Ty Doucet-Harmon here, Delta duck guide and Red Wolves lifer, with my duck call around my neck and my voice mostly gone. On Saturday at Centennial Bank Stadium, Arkansas State beat Georgia State 38-32. It came down to the final seconds, folks. I let it rip.

Here's the hunt.

Setting the decoys: Trey Owens threw a 12-yard touchdown pass to Jaylen Bonelli in the first quarter and ran in a 2-yard score in the second. Then early in the third, Brandon Greil intercepted Ayden Pereira, and on the next play Owens hit Nana Burris for a 54-yard touchdown. Twenty-one to 14.

The flock lands: Georgia State tied it on a 77-yard run. Then Owens hit Bonelli for 35 yards and ran in from the 1. Timothy Carter ran in a 2-yard touchdown to start the fourth, and Owens hit Bonelli for 38 yards to set up Clune Van Andel's 26-yard field goal. Thirty-eight to 21. Quaaack.

The last shot: Georgia State pulled within 38-32 and drove into our territory in the final minute. With 15 seconds left, Makai Shahid intercepted Ayden Pereira at our 6. Shahid had an interception called back by a roughing penalty just one play earlier. Second chance, good shot. Dinner's in the cooler, folks.

My hero is Trey Owens: 21 of 32 for 309 yards, two touchdown passes and two rushing touchdowns. Bonelli caught seven passes for 150, and Burris caught six for 114. Joe Drew made 10 tackles, and Tre Stevens and Shakai Woods had sacks. We didn't turn it over.

My affectionate goat: field goals. Van Andel missed from 44 and 48 yards before hitting from 26. And we went two for 10 on third downs.

What it means: Arkansas State is 4-4 and 2-2 in the Sun Belt. We've been the Red Wolves since 2008, and this pack knows how to hunt.

Next up, UL Monroe comes to Centennial Bank Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3 p.m. ET. TV is still to be announced. That means a week off first to rest the calls. Get in the blind early, keep your voice low until it counts, and when it counts, let it rip. Quaaack.
