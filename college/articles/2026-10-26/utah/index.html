Send It! Devon Dampier Runs for 150 as No. 10 Utah Rallies Past No. 14 Houston, Stays Perfect

Utah 21, Houston 13 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Rice-Eccles Stadium

By Aspen Mortensen, The Utah Fanatic

Hey, Utes. Aspen Mortensen here, boots clicked into my old race skis in the living room, because I'm still too fired up to sit down. Utah 21, No. 14 Houston 13. We are 7-0. That's 13 straight wins, the longest active streak in college football, per the AP. Send it, no fear!

But friends, the first half was a course full of ruts. Houston stuffed our run game. Our best drive ended at halftime with a missed field goal after a sloppy snap and hold. Houston led 3-0 at the break, then opened the third with a 15-play touchdown drive. Ten to nothing. Per the AP, Dampier told coach Morgan Scalley not to worry. And then he went out and skied the cleanest run of the night.

Dampier ran for a 22-yard touchdown, accounting for every yard of the drive. Houston kicked a field goal to make it 13-7, but early in the fourth, Wayshawn Parker took a direct snap and bolted 5 yards for the touchdown. Utah 14, Houston 13! First lead of the night.

Then the gate that decided the race. With 2:15 left, Jackson Bennee blitzed and sacked Conner Weigman, the ball popped loose, and Jireh Moe recovered at the Houston 9. On the very next play, Dampier ran it in. Twenty-one to thirteen! Houston drove to our 23, but threw four straight incompletions. Clean through the final gate!

Hero: Dampier. 150 rushing yards, a season high, and two touchdowns, plus 204 passing yards. He threw two interceptions, and that's the goat, with love: a couple of hooked tips. But he crossed the finish first. Bennee had a sack and a forced fumble, Moe recovered it and had a sack of his own, and Trey Reynolds made nine tackles. Creed Whittemore caught four passes for 53 yards.

What it means: Utah is 7-0 and 4-0 in the Big 12, did it in front of its 100th straight sellout, per the AP, and jumped to No. 6 in the AP poll released Oct. 25.

Next up: at Cincinnati on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 3:45 p.m. ET on ESPN, at Nippert Stadium. Halloween on the road. Wax the skis.

Go Utes! Send it, no fear.
