Hydrate and Dominate: Freshman Cardae Mack Runs for Four Touchdowns as Arizona State Beats Kansas State, 28-17

Arizona State 28, Kansas State 17 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Mountain America Stadium

By Skylar Vanterpool, The Arizona State Fanatic

Hi, Sun Devils! Skylar Vanterpool here, on a pool float on Tempe Town Lake in spirit, with my 40-ounce water bottle. Take a sip. Okay. Arizona State 28, Kansas State 17! Freshman Cardae Mack ran for four touchdowns, and I ran four laps around my living room, one per touchdown. That's called cross-training.

Here's the workout. Mikey Keene, making his first start, hit Reed Harris for 51 yards on our very first play. Mack scored from 9 yards out to tie it, then from a yard out for a 14-7 lead. Then Keene got hurt in the final minute of the first half and was helped off. We're sending him all the ice packs and good vibes.

The third quarter was a cramp. Kansas State took a 17-14 lead. So coach Kenny Dillingham went to freshman Jake Fette, our third quarterback of the day, who'd thrown two passes all season. Dillingham said the team wanted some energy, and Fette brought it. First play of the fourth quarter: Mack, 12 yards, touchdown. Sun Devils lead. Hydrate! Then, with 6:23 left, Mack took a direct snap and went 11 yards for his fourth touchdown. Twenty-eight to seventeen. And when Kansas State threatened late, another freshman, Julian Hugo, sacked the quarterback on fourth down. Freshmen everywhere! It's like a first-day-of-school photo, but with tackles.

Hero: Mack, 26 carries for 159 yards and four touchdowns. Harris caught six passes for 147 yards. Martell Hughes made 14 tackles, and Owen Long added 10. Per the AP, after giving up 50-plus points in each of its last two Big 12 games, the defense held Kansas State to 84 rushing yards. That's what we call recovery. The goat, with a gentle stretch: the third quarter, when the offense went flat. Nothing a cold water bottle can't fix.

What it means: Arizona State is 4-3 and 2-2 in the Big 12 after a rough week in Lubbock. Dillingham said Fette will start if Keene can't play. Young legs, big hearts.

Next up: at BYU on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 3:30 p.m. ET on Fox, at LaVell Edwards Stadium. Halloween in Provo, at altitude, so pack the electrolytes. Take a sip. I'm serious.

Forks up, Sun Devils! Hydrate and dominate!
