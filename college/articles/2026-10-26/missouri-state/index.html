Rapids Ahead: Isaiah Myers Goes for 242, but the Bears Flip the Canoe in Newark

Delaware 61, Missouri State 38 (final), Tue. Oct. 20, Delaware Stadium

By Clementine Bilyeu, The Missouri State Fanatic

Howdy from the Ozarks. Clementine Bilyeu here, wringing out my socks on the bank of the James River. I guide float trips for a living, and Tuesday night in Newark was one of those floats where the first hour is glorious and then you meet a rock with your name on it. Delaware beat us 61-38.

The glorious part was real. J.J. O'Neal grabbed a Delaware pass and took it back 47 yards for a touchdown, and we led 14-7 after one quarter. Henry Belin IV found Isaiah Myers twice for touchdowns, the second with 14 seconds left in the half, and the Bears went to the locker room up 28-24. Connor Lair ran for two touchdowns, and we were still ahead 35-31 midway through the third.

Then came the rock. Down 38-35, Belin got sacked by Jawarren Corbin, the ball came loose, and B. Gorham returned it 59 yards to our 4. Delaware scored on the next play. After that, Corbin intercepted Belin, George Mullins intercepted Belin, and Delaware rattled off 30 straight points. That's not a rapid, friends. That's a waterfall.

My hero is Isaiah Myers: 11 catches, 242 yards, two touchdowns. He was a downstream current all night, and I'd let him steer my canoe anytime. Belin threw for 337 yards and kept chucking it, God bless him.

My affectionate goat is the yellow flag: 12 penalties for 100 yards. You can't paddle upstream with the anchor dragging, kids.

What it means: the Bears are 2-5 and 1-2 in Conference USA. That's a bumpy float, but there's a lot of river left, and the next stretch is calm water at home.

Next up, Sam Houston comes to Plaster Stadium on Tuesday, Oct. 27, at 8 p.m. ET on ESPN2. Plaster's been around since 1941, built as a WPA project, so it's seen worse nights than this one, and better ones coming. Pack a snack and a dry bag, Bears fans. We're putting back in.
