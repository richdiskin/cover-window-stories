Up the Mountain, Down Again: The Dukes' Perfect Season Slips Away in Overtime in Boone, 34-31

App State 34, James Madison 31 (final, OT), Thu. Oct. 22, Kidd Brewer Stadium

By Lila Shenandoah, The James Madison Fanatic

Ready? Okay... actually, give me a second. Lila Shenandoah here, pom-poms drooping, glitter going everywhere, and my voice left somewhere on the side of Howard's Knob. Thursday night at Kidd Brewer Stadium, App State beat James Madison 34-31 in overtime. The zero in our loss column is gone, y'all. I'm going to cheer anyway, because that's what cheerleaders do.

Here are the three plays that broke my heart.

Play one: the interception. It was 24-24 with just over six minutes left. Colton Phares picked off Camden Coleman and returned it 21 yards to our 14. Moments later, Henry Hasselbeck threw a 4-yard touchdown pass to Luke Douglas. Thirty-one to 24, and 33,322 Mountaineer fans made a noise I'll hear in my dreams.

Play two: the comeback. Dukes don't quit, honey. Camden drove us down the field, and with three seconds left, he ran it in from 6 yards out himself. Thirty-one all! I did a toe-touch so high I may have entered North Carolina airspace.

Play three: overtime. We had the ball first, lost 11 yards on a run, and Morgan Suarez's 47-yard kick missed. Then James London kicked a 40-yarder for App State, and that was that.

My hero is Braeden Wisloski, who caught a 70-yard touchdown pass from JC Evans that had me shrieking. Camden also deserves a cartwheel: 232 passing yards, a touchdown pass and the tying score.

My affectionate goat is the kicking game. Suarez went 1-for-3 and had one blocked. Morgan, sweetie, it's okay. We still love you. We also went 2-for-15 on third down, which is not a cheer, it's a cry for help.

What it means: the Dukes are 7-1 and 4-1 in the Sun Belt. One loss, on the road, in overtime, on a mountain. That's a stumble, not a fall. Harrisonburg has the best food in Virginia, and we'll eat our feelings downtown this weekend.

Next up, Troy comes to Bridgeforth Stadium on Thursday, Oct. 29, at 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN2. Home sweet home. Ready? Okay! "Purple! Reign! Shake it off and win again!"
