Floyd Walks Away: Iowa Ties It Late but Falls at Minnesota on a Final-Play Field Goal, 13-10

Minnesota 13, Iowa 10 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Huntington Bank Stadium

By Orrin Kalvelage, The Iowa Fanatic

Pull up a chair on the porch. The one with the cushion. Coffee's in the pot, and I'm going to need a second cup. Orrin Kalvelage here, same black-and-gold sweater as always. Minnesota 13, Iowa 10, on a field goal as time expired, and Floyd of Rosedale is headed north. That bronze pig always did like a road trip. I just wish he'd stayed home.

It got away from us one turnover at a time. Iowa came in with three turnovers all season. Saturday, we threw three interceptions and lost a fumble, and gave up a blocked punt right off the bat. Coach Kirk Ferentz said you can't expect to win a conference game like that, and nobody on this porch is arguing.

Then a penalty stung. In the third quarter, Iowa stopped Minnesota on fourth down, but an offside flag gave the Gophers another chance, and Darius Taylor scored on the next play. Ten to three.

Now, the part I'll tell the grandkids. Down seven, Hank Brown went 98 yards in four plays, and hit Reece Vander Zee for a 37-yard touchdown with 3:46 left. Tied, 10-10. I stood up and the dog barked. Then Minnesota hit a 39-yard pass on third down, and Luke Drzewiecki made it from 46 with no time left. The dog lay back down. So did I.

Hero: the defense, which held Minnesota to 209 yards of offense, the Gophers' season low. Zach Lutmer made 11 tackles, Cam Buffington had 10, Bryce Hawthorne had three tackles for loss, and Deshaun Lee intercepted a pass. Caden Buhr made his field goal. Vander Zee caught four for 69 yards, and Tony Diaz broke a 57-yard catch. The goat, said gently: the run game, 90 yards. And we wish Kamari Moulton well. He left in the second quarter with an injury.

What it means: Iowa is 5-2 and 2-2 in the Big Ten and dropped out of the AP poll released Oct. 25. The quarterback question isn't settled, and the AP says the Playoff hopes took a hit. But I've been on this porch through worse harvests.

Next up: Wisconsin comes to Kinnick Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at noon ET on Fox. We'll turn around and wave to the kids at the children's hospital after the first quarter, like always. That never loses.

Go Hawks. Same sweater next week.
