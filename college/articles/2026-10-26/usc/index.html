A Box-Office Bust in Madison: No. 16 USC Falls at Wisconsin, 24-7

Wisconsin 24, USC 7 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Camp Randall Stadium

By Sterling Ashworth-Vance, The USC Fanatic

In a world... where 71,000 people in red jumped around... one team in cardinal and gold... lost the plot.

Good evening. Sterling Ashworth-Vance here, film school graduate, and I'm afraid this week's feature is a disappointment. Wisconsin 24, USC 7, at Camp Randall. Night game, and the critics were not kind. Neither was I. One clapperboard. Generously.

Act One had promise. Down 3-0, Jayden Maiava threw a 4-yard touchdown to Taniela Tupou in the second quarter. USC 7, Wisconsin 3. I cued the swelling strings. Then the plot twist nobody in our writers' room wanted: with less than two minutes left in the first half, Bryan Jackson II, who spent the last two seasons at USC, ran 5 yards for a Wisconsin touchdown. Against us. That's not a twist. That's a sequel nobody ordered.

Act Two dragged. Maiava threw two interceptions in the first half, after coming into the game with three all season, per the AP. In the third quarter, Wisconsin scored a touchdown on fourth-and-2, and then blocked our field goal. Then a 1-yard run in the fourth made it 24-7. Roll credits.

The goat, and I say this with love: the run game. Minus-17 rushing yards. Minus! Coach Lincoln Riley said the team didn't play well enough on any of the three sides of the ball, and didn't coach well enough either. He owned it. I respect a director who owns the edit.

Hero: Tupou, six catches and the touchdown. Maiava threw for 258 yards, and Trent Mosley, Tanook Hines and Kayden Dixon-Wyatt each caught at least four passes. On defense, Desman Stephens II made eight tackles, Luke Wafle had a sack, and Kameryn Crawford had two tackles for loss. They kept us within range for a long time.

What it means: USC dropped out of the AP poll released Oct. 25. A tough review. But every great franchise has a bad installment, and the next one is a blockbuster.

Next up: No. 2 Ohio State comes to the Coliseum on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 7:30 p.m. ET on NBC. Halloween night, prime time, the biggest stage in Los Angeles. If there's ever a night for a comeback story, that's it.

Fight On, Trojans. Fade to cardinal and gold.
