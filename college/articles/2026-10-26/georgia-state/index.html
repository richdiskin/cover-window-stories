Missed the Last Train: Qwantavius Wiggins Runs 77 Yards for a Touchdown, but Georgia State Falls at Arkansas State, 38-32

Arkansas State 38, Georgia State 32 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, Centennial Bank Stadium

By Malik Overstreet, The Georgia State Fanatic

Next stop, heartbreak. Malik Overstreet here, MARTA card in one pocket, third-down chart in the other, riding the train home from the station of sadness. On Saturday in Jonesboro, Arkansas State beat Georgia State 38-32. We were one drive from making the connection. We missed it.

Here's the route map.

Stop one: Qwantavius Wiggins. He caught a 33-yard touchdown pass from Ayden Pereira with 26 seconds left in the first half to tie it 14-14. Then in the third quarter, right after Arkansas State took the lead, he ran 77 yards for a touchdown. Twenty-one to 21. That's express service.

Stop two: the delay. Arkansas State scored 17 straight points, with two touchdowns and a field goal. Thirty-eight to 21. That's the Downtown Connector at 5:15 on a Friday.

Stop three: the comeback. Pereira threw a 27-yard touchdown to Rodshaun Dorsey and found Derrick Avery Jr. for two. Then Pereira hit Dorsey for 37 yards, and Samer Layous kicked a 33-yard field goal. Thirty-eight to 32 with 4:25 left. We got the ball back at our 13 with 1:49 left and drove into Arkansas State territory. Then Makai Shahid intercepted Pereira at the 6 with 15 seconds left. Doors closing.

My hero is Qwantavius Wiggins: 99 rushing yards, five catches and two touchdowns. Dorsey caught three passes for 85 yards. Cameran Brown threw a touchdown to DJ Riles. Jaydon Hood made 10 tackles, and Tre Mittman and Coby Owens each had sacks.

My affectionate goat: the two interceptions. And three-for-12 on third down. You know I keep the chart, Panthers.

What it means: Georgia State is 4-3 and 1-2 in the Sun Belt. Center Parc Stadium was built for the 1996 Olympics, and we still have time to finish the race.

Next up, Coastal Carolina comes to Center Parc Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 2 p.m. ET on ESPN+. Halloween at home, Panthers. Take the train. Next stop, Center Parc. Mind the gap, and mind the Chanticleers.
