Dry Well at the Buzzer: Tulsa Strikes Oil With a 35-28 Lead, Then Army Caps the Gusher With a Last-Second 50-Yarder, 38-35

Army 38, Tulsa 35 (final), Fri. Oct. 23, H. A. Chapman Stadium

By Wiley Pettibone, The Tulsa Fanatic

Howdy from the Oil Capital of the World. Wiley Pettibone here, petroleum geology grad, and the only man in Tulsa who reads a box score like a well log. Friday night's log at Chapman Stadium starts with a gusher and ends with a dry hole. Army beat Tulsa 38-35 on a 50-yard field goal as time expired.

Let me walk you through the core samples.

Sample one: the gusher. Down 28-14 in the third quarter, we struck oil. David Wells Jr. ran in from 11 yards out. Then Trequan Jones broke a 59-yard touchdown run. Early in the fourth, Baylor Hayes hit Jimmy Calloway for 13 yards and a touchdown. Thirty-five to 28, Tulsa. I was pricing derricks.

Sample two: the pressure drop. Owen Carey missed a 40-yard field goal that would've put us up 10. Then, with 3:52 left, Army blocked our punt and recovered it at our 13. Army tied it from the 1 with 1:50 left.

Sample three: the dry hole. We went three-and-out, with a broken-up pass and a sack. Army got the ball at its 37 with 45 seconds left, moved 27 yards, and Dawson Jones made a 50-yarder at the gun.

My hero is Jimmy Calloway, who caught 10 passes for 88 yards and a touchdown. Baylor Hayes went 23 of 34 for 262 yards and two touchdowns, and Javon Ross scored twice, one rushing and one receiving. Trequan Jones ran five times for 66 yards. That's high yield per barrel.

My affectionate goat: the punt team. A blocked punt with 3:52 left is a blowout preventer that blew out. We also gave up 275 rushing yards to Army, which, to be fair, is what Army does.

What it means: Tulsa is 4-4 and 1-3 in the American after its third loss in four games. That stings, especially after we won at West Point last season. But the Golden Driller has stood 76 feet tall since 1966, and he's seen plenty of dry wells before the next gusher.

Next up, Tulsa gets a week off, then visits Tulane at Yulman Stadium on Saturday, Nov. 7. The kickoff time and TV are still to be announced. Wave at the Golden Driller on your way out of town. He can wait one more week for a win.
