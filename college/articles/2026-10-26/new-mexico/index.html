Riding the Thermal: Austin Brawley's 56-Yard Interception Return Lifts New Mexico Past North Dakota State, 21-16, and the Lobos Are 6-1

New Mexico 21, North Dakota State 16 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, University Stadium (NM)

By Paloma Begaye-Ortiz, The New Mexico Fanatic

XFacebook

Buenos días, Lobo nation. Paloma Begaye-Ortiz here, tailgate down, breakfast burrito in hand, red and green. On Saturday night at University Stadium, New Mexico beat North Dakota State 21-16. We read the wind, we caught the thermal, and we landed it. Six and one, Lobos!

Here's the flight log.

Liftoff: Kiefer Sibley ran 29 yards for a touchdown in the first quarter. Then CJ McBean intercepted Nathan Hayes and returned it 20 yards to the NDSU 30. Seven to nothing, and the sky was blue.

Steady altitude: just before halftime, M. Beltran Jr. returned a punt 44 yards, and Scottre Humphrey ran in from the 2 with 10 seconds left. Fourteen to 3 at the break.

The gust: North Dakota State pulled within 14-9. Then early in the fourth, Austin Brawley intercepted Hayes and returned it 56 yards for a touchdown. Twenty-one to 9! I nearly let go of the burner. NDSU scored to make it 21-16, but on their last drive, D. Agu and F. Edwards dropped Myles Mitchell for a 5-yard loss, and Hayes' fourth-down pass fell incomplete. Fa'avae took three knees. Soft landing.

My hero is Austin Brawley, for the play of the night. Sibley ran for 91 yards, and we ran for 187 as a team against one of the best defenses in the country. Mercury Swaim made 11 tackles, and Brett Karhu had a sack. We forced two turnovers and gave up only one.

My affectionate goat: field goals. Grant Glasgow missed from 54 and 42 yards. And our passing game managed 92 yards. Some flights, you just have to trust the wind.

What it means: New Mexico is 6-1 and 3-0 in the Mountain West, and we're bowl eligible. Albuquerque hosts the largest balloon festival in the world, and right now, the Lobos are floating high above all of it.

Next up, New Mexico visits San José State at CEFCU Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 4 p.m. ET on MW+. Halloween in the Bay Area. Bring a jacket. Red or green? Both. Go Lobos.
