A Tough Switchback: No. 2 BYU Falls at UCF in Double Overtime, 39-31

UCF 39, BYU 31 (final, 2OT), Sat., Oct. 24, Acrisure Bounce House

By Bryce Halvorsen-Tuilagi, The BYU Fanatic

Oh my heck, Cougar Nation. Bryce Halvorsen-Tuilagi here, on the trail up Y Mountain, where the view is fantastic and the Wi-Fi is not, and this week, neither is my mood. UCF 39, BYU 31, in double overtime. Our first loss of the season. Every great ride has a nasty switchback, and Saturday in Orlando was the steepest one.

Bear Bachmeier and Kyler Kasper had us climbing early. Bachmeier hit Kasper for a 27-yard touchdown in the first quarter, and Kasper finished with six catches for 129 yards. Bachmeier ran one in too, and Sione Moa punched in a third-quarter score. When UCF went ahead 24-21, Matthias Dunn drilled a 47-yarder to tie it in the fourth.

Then the ending, which the UCF coach called the weirdest he'd ever seen. Our defense stopped a fourth-and-goal sneak at the 1. Then, after a replay overturned a sack and a flag moved UCF into range, our special teams blocked a 47-yard field goal and time ran out. Overtime! I nearly rode off the trail.

In the first overtime, Bachmeier found Legend Glasker for 28 yards and the lead. UCF tied it on one play. In the second, UCF scored and converted the two. We needed eight and drove to the UCF 4, but the fourth-down pass to Walker Lyons fell incomplete. Brake pads, fully worn.

Hero: Kasper, the big-play man. Bachmeier threw for 257 yards and two touchdowns. Cade Uluave made 10 tackles, and Justin Kirkland had a sack. The goat, offered with a fist bump: the defense on third down, where UCF converted nine times, and coach Kalani Sitake said UCF put us on our heels. He gave them full credit. That's how we do it in Provo.

What it means: BYU is 6-1 and 3-1 in the Big 12 and slid from No. 2 to No. 8 in the AP poll released Oct. 25. But here's the good news, from the AP: a Big 12 title and a Playoff spot are still within reach. Kasper said it's still right there if we win out. Why did the Cougar bring a map to practice? Because the path is still clear. That's a dad joke, and it counts double.

Next up: Arizona State at LaVell Edwards Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 3:30 p.m. ET on Fox, then a trip to Utah on Nov. 7. Holy War season is coming.

Rise and shout, the Cougars are out!
