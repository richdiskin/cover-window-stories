Oh, Sugar: North Carolina Falls to Syracuse 31-30 on an Overtime Two-Pointer

Syracuse 31, North Carolina 30 (final, OT), Sat., Oct. 24, Kenan Stadium

By Ainsley Pemberton, The North Carolina Fanatic

Well, hello, darlings. Ainsley Pemberton here, pearls slightly askew, kinesiology PhD in hand, and a heart that is, I confess, a little bruised. Syracuse 31, North Carolina 30, in overtime at Kenan. One point, sugar. One two-point conversion. Let me pour us both a sweet tea.

We started like a debutante's first waltz. Billy Edwards Jr. completed his first 11 passes, flawless footwork, hips square to the target. Jordan Shipp caught a 5-yard touchdown, Rece Verhoff kicked a field goal, and it was 10-0 after one quarter. In the third, on fourth-and-1, Demon June ran 25 yards for a touchdown, the longest run of our season, per the AP. Twenty to ten. I was already picturing the after-party.

And then the fourth quarter. Syracuse hit a 76-yard touchdown pass. We drove deep into their territory but settled for a 22-yard field goal. Then Syracuse scored with 1:54 left, but missed the extra point. Tied at 23. Overtime. Fine. We can do overtime.

We scored first, too! Edwards to June, 3 yards. Touchdown, Tar Heels! But Syracuse answered with a touchdown and then went for two and the win. The pass was complete. Oh, sugar.

Hero: Edwards, a season-high 271 yards and two touchdowns, and Shipp, eight catches for 102 yards. Jelani Thurman added seven catches for 77. Verhoff made all three field goals and every extra point. Derek McDonald made 16 tackles, and Peyton Seelmann had 11 and a sack. And we didn't turn it over once. The goat, with a gentle pat: the red zone in the fourth quarter. A touchdown there and we'd be having a very different tea party.

What it means: North Carolina is 4-3 and 2-2 in the ACC. Coach Bill Belichick said Syracuse made a couple more plays, and that was the difference. Darlings, he's right. One play.

Next up: No. 1 Miami visits Kenan Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 7:30 p.m. ET on ABC. Halloween night, prime time, and the best team in the country. If we want to make a statement, there's no grander ballroom.

Go Heels! And, as always, a gentle word for Duke: hush.
