Hotdish Left in the Oven Too Long: Nathan Hayes Throws Two Touchdowns, but an Interception Return Sinks North Dakota State at New Mexico, 21-16

New Mexico 21, North Dakota State 16 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, University Stadium (NM)

By Ingeborg 'Inga' Halvorsen, The North Dakota State Fanatic

Well, hi there. Inga Halvorsen here, in my kitchen in south Fargo, scraping the burnt bits off a tater tot hotdish. On Saturday night in Albuquerque, New Mexico beat North Dakota State 21-16. Oh, fer cryin' out loud. We were right there, dontcha know.

Here's the potluck report.

The first dish: New Mexico scored first, and then Nathan Hayes threw an interception. But Ray James Jr. intercepted Toa Fa'avae right back, and Jackson Williams caught a 41-yard pass to set up a Drew Klein field goal. Seven to 3.

The second dish: Hayes threw a 15-yard touchdown to Reis Kessel in the third quarter, and we trailed 14-9 after the extra point was blocked. Our defense was holding strong, and New Mexico missed field goals from 54 and 42 yards. I had my oven mitts on, ready to serve.

The dish that burned: early in the fourth, Austin Brawley intercepted Hayes and returned it 56 yards for a touchdown. Twenty-one to 9. Hayes threw a 4-yard touchdown to Mekhi Collins to make it 21-16 with 6:42 left. We got the ball back at our 9 with 2:10 left, but a 5-yard loss and a fourth-down incompletion ended it.

My hero is Ray James Jr.: 11 tackles, an interception and a sack. Gavin Sell had 10 tackles and a sack. Our defense held New Mexico to 279 yards and 92 passing. Williams caught four passes for 71 yards.

My affectionate goat: the run game. Fifty-three yards on 28 carries. That's like Jell-O salad without the Jell-O. And the two interceptions, ope.

What it means: North Dakota State is 6-2. Fargo has seen colder nights than this. The Bison have won 10 national championships, so we know a thing or two about bouncing back.

Next up, UTEP comes to the Fargodome on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 3:30 p.m. ET on MW+. Halloween indoors, where it's warm. Bring a hotdish. Oh, for cute. Go Bison.
