Water Damage, Again: Baylor's Late Field Goal Blocked in a 25-24 Loss at Kansas

Kansas 25, Baylor 24 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium

By Maggie Whitlock, The Baylor Fanatic

Hey, y'all. Maggie Whitlock here, in the studio with my tape measure, a stack of paint swatches and a "Sic 'Em" throw pillow I'm currently hugging for emotional support. Kansas 25, Baylor 24, in Lawrence. We'd beaten the Jayhawks 14 straight times, per the AP. That streak just came down to the studs.

The final walkthrough, room by room. The foyer looked lovely: down 21-10 going into the fourth, the house was staged and ready to sell. Then Kansas hit a 34-yard touchdown pass on the first play of the quarter and a field goal. Rhett Armstrong gave us a 24-19 lead with a 42-yarder with 2:40 left. Fine. Curtains up.

Then the kitchen flooded. Kansas went 71 yards, and Isaiah Marshall ran it in from 15 yards out on fourth-and-6 with 1:13 left. Kansas went 5 for 8 on fourth down all day. That's a leak we never patched.

And the master bath: DJ Lagway got us to the Kansas 27 in the final minute. Our 45-yard field goal try got blocked by Blake Herold. Per the AP, Kansas had tipped two earlier kicks. Third one got through the drywall. I set the pillow down. I picked it back up.

The good bones, because there are good bones: Devon Jordan stepped in front of a pass to open the second half and returned it 42 yards for a touchdown. Gorgeous. Micah Gifford intercepted one too. Our defense got five sacks, Kyland Reed had one, and Travion Barnes made 13 tackles. Gavin Freeman caught nine passes for 123 yards. Lagway hit Jayden McGowan for an 8-yard touchdown, and McGowan's 51-yard kickoff return set up our first score.

The goat, with a gentle hand on the shoulder: the penalties. Twelve for 116 yards. You can't keep tracking mud across a white rug and expect it to look new.

What it means: Baylor is 4-3 and 2-2 in the Big 12. The Big 12 race is still open, but we've got some repairs to make, and fast.

Next up: a short week. Baylor plays at UCF on Friday, Oct. 30, at 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN, at the Acrisure Bounce House in Orlando. I'm bringing a fresh swatch of green and gold for Halloween eve.

Sic 'em, Bears. We'll fix the leak.
