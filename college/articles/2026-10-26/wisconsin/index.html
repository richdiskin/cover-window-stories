Oom-Pah Upset! Bryan Jackson Scores Against His Old Team as Wisconsin Stuns No. 16 USC, 24-7

Wisconsin 24, USC 7 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Camp Randall Stadium

By Lars 'Brat' Ellefson, The Wisconsin Fanatic

Oom-pah, oom-pah, oom-pah-pah!

That's the polka break, folks, and this week I did about 40 of them. Lars "Brat" Ellefson here, with a brat on the grill and my knees fully loosened. Wisconsin 24, No. 16 USC 7, under the lights at Camp Randall! That's our second win over a ranked team this year, after the one at Penn State in September. And now the Badgers are ranked, too.

The story writes itself, and it's a good one. Bryan Jackson II spent the last two seasons at USC. Saturday night, against his old team, with less than two minutes left in the first half, he ran 5 yards for a touchdown. His first as a Badger! Wisconsin 10, USC 7. Oom-pah!

Then the dagger, served with mustard. Third quarter, fourth-and-2, and Colton Joseph threw a 16-yard touchdown to Chris Brooks Jr. Seventeen to seven. Late in the third, Junior Poyser blocked a USC field goal. And in the fourth, Ryan Schwendeman plunged in from a yard out. Twenty-four to seven. I polka'd so hard I knocked the brat tongs into the cooler.

Hero: the defense. All of it. USC finished with minus-17 rushing yards. Minus! Sebastian Cheeks had two and a half sacks, Mason Posa had one and a half, and Cooper Catalano made 10 tackles. Cairo Skanes and Bryce West each intercepted a pass in the first half, and Matthew Jung broke up three passes. Per the AP, the defense held USC to 92 yards in the first half. Gavin Lahm made a 33-yard field goal and every extra point. Abu Sama III ran for 71 yards. The goat, with a friendly elbow: our passing game, 58 yards. But Joseph completed 12 of 14, so it was efficient, like a good bratwurst.

What it means: Wisconsin is 5-2 and 3-1 in the Big Ten, and the Badgers entered the AP poll at No. 25 on Oct. 25. Coach Luke Fickell said Jackson showed something special. So did the whole stadium.

Next up: at Iowa on Saturday, Oct. 31, at noon ET on Fox, at Kinnick Stadium. Halloween at noon, with the Heartland Trophy on the mind. I'll pack extra brats.

Oom-pah-pah! On, Wisconsin!
