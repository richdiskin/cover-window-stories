A Five-Way Afternoon: Cole Tabb Runs Wild as Cincinnati Stuns No. 8 Texas Tech 37-24

Cincinnati 37, Texas Tech 24 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Nippert Stadium

By Lionel 'Skyline' Abernathy, The Cincinnati Fanatic

XFacebook

Bearcats, it's Skyline, and I'm ordering a five-way. Everything on it. Extra cheese. Onions and beans. Cincinnati 37, No. 8 Texas Tech 24, at Nippert, on a Saturday that made the whole Clifton neighborhood shake. The AP says it's our first home win over a top-10 team since Nov. 18, 2006, when we beat No. 7 Rutgers. I've charted a lot of games. I've never charted a bigger bowl of chili.

Start with the spaghetti, the foundation: our second quarter. Down 6-3, JC French IV threw three touchdown passes in that quarter alone. Cole Tabb caught a 4-yarder. Malachi Henry caught a 5-yarder with 1:37 left. Then MJ Cannon intercepted Will Hammond, Tabb ripped off a 51-yard run, and French hit tight end Lyviel Waters for 2 yards with five seconds left. Seventy-five yards in 27 seconds. That's a three-way served before you can grab a napkin.

Then the cheese. Tech landed a punch in the third quarter, a 53-yard interception return for a touchdown, to make it 24-15. Nippert got nervous. So the Bearcats answered with a nine-play, 80-yard drive, and French hit Larenzo Fenner for 17 yards and his fourth touchdown pass, a career high. Onions on top.

And the beans: with about six minutes left, Cole Tabb ran 58 yards for a touchdown. Ballgame. Tabb finished with a career-best 173 rushing yards on 16 carries. Five-way, all day.

Hero: Tabb, obviously, and French, 19 of 30 with four touchdowns. Defense gets a big ladle too: Jasper Beeler had 11 tackles, Simeon Coleman had 10 with a sack, and we recovered two fumbles. The goat, lovingly: French's two interceptions, including the one Tech returned for a score. Nobody's perfect. Even chili drips on your shirt sometimes.

The engineer in me loves this note from the AP: we came in averaging 231 rushing yards, had minus-6 in the first quarter thanks to a couple of sacks, and still finished north of 250. That's called correcting the recipe mid-pot.

What it means: Cincinnati is 5-2 and 2-2 in the Big 12, and we earned votes in the AP poll released Oct. 25. A seat at the conference table again.

Next up: No. 6 Utah comes to Nippert on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 3:45 p.m. ET on ESPN. Another ranked guest. I'll have the cheese grater ready.

Go Bearcats. Pass the oyster crackers.
