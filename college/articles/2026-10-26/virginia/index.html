A Fresh Nib for a Hard Entry: Virginia Falls to Duke's Late Trick Play at Scott Stadium, 19-16

Duke 19, Virginia 16 (final), Fri., Oct. 23, Scott Stadium

By Prescott Ashby-Lowell, The Virginia Fanatic

XFacebook

Good evening, and welcome to the Grounds. Prescott Ashby-Lowell, fourth-generation Wahoo, with a fresh nib in the fountain pen and a heavy heart in the waistcoat. Duke 19, Virginia 16, Friday night at Scott Stadium. Our first home loss of the season. Mr. Jefferson charted the weather at Monticello through storms, and so shall I.

The ledger entry reads as follows. With three minutes left, Will Bettridge kicked a 30-yard field goal, and Virginia led 16-13. I underlined it twice. Then, with 1:14 left, Duke handed the ball to Nate Sheppard, the ACC's leading rusher, and Sheppard turned and threw 28 yards to a wide-open Brody Keefe. Linebacker Kam Robinson said afterward, in essence, that the defense had played wonderfully until that one busted snap. Coach Tony Elliott pointed to a similar look the play before. I drew a small, sad sketch of it in the margin.

Our last chance ended when Beau Pribula's final pass was intercepted. I set the pen down. I lifted it again. A ledger must be complete.

And there is much to record with pride. Jekail Middlebrook ran 24 times for 117 yards and a touchdown, a 1-yard plunge in the first quarter. Bettridge kicked three field goals, including a 32-yarder as the first half ended to give us a 13-7 lead. Kam Robinson intercepted a pass. Christian Ellis made six tackles, and Maddox Marcellus had two tackles for loss. We outgained Duke 353 to 327 and held the ball for 38:47. John Rogers caught five passes for 65 yards.

The goat, recorded in the gentlest ink: the red zone and the scoreboard. Per the AP, the offense gained yards but couldn't turn them into points, and three field goals plus one touchdown will rarely be enough. That, and one trick play.

What it means: Virginia is 4-4 and 2-3 in the ACC, with three losses in four games, per the AP. Duke, which beat us in the ACC title game last season, has now done it again in October. Rivalries in the ledger tend to have long memories.

Next up: at Wake Forest on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 3:45 p.m. ET on ESPN2, at Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium. Halloween in Winston-Salem. I'll be in a seersucker costume, which is just my clothing.

Wahoowa, and the Good Old Song, always.
