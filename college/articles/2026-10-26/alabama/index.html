Back in the Saddle: Keelon Russell Throws Three Touchdowns as Alabama Ends Its Skid, 31-20 Over Texas A&M

Alabama 31, Texas A&M 20 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Bryant-Denny Stadium

By Mary-Kathryn 'MK' Strickland, The Alabama Fanatic

Well, hello, darlings. Napkins in laps, please. Mary-Kathryn Strickland here, MK to my friends, and Mama is smiling again. Alabama 31, Texas A&M 20, at Bryant-Denny, and our two-game skid is over. I told you a horse teaches you to get right back on. Saturday, the Tide got back on, and rode.

We came out of the gate in style. Less than four minutes in, Dijon Lee Jr. intercepted Marcel Reed and returned it 39 yards for a touchdown. I spilled my sweet tea, and I do not spill sweet tea. But then the Aggies, bless them, came back. Reed ran one in, A&M kicked two field goals, and they led 20-17 heading into the fourth. Mama started fanning herself.

Then came the turn. Texas A&M went for it on fourth-and-1 and Reed lost the ball on a sneak. We took over at midfield. Three plays later, Keelon Russell hit Rico Scott for 38 yards to the A&M 5, and then found Marshall Pritchett for the touchdown. We never trailed again. When the Aggies threatened, Fatutoa Henry, who per the AP was nursing an injury earlier in the week, got home for our first sack of the day. Then Russell found Kaleb Edwards from 8 yards out. Thirty-one to twenty. Pass the pecan pie.

Hero: Russell, 19 of 30 for 311 yards and three touchdowns, two of them in the fourth quarter. And Scott, six catches for 156 yards; coach Kalen DeBoer said Rico came up in a big way. Lee, of course, for that interception return, darling. Chuck McDonald III also got his first career interception, Caleb Woodson made 10 tackles, and the defense held A&M to 278 yards. The goat, with a pat on the hand: Conor Talty, one for three on field goals. Even the best-bred horse stumbles.

What it means: Alabama is 6-2 and 4-2 in the SEC and climbed to No. 10 in the AP poll released Oct. 25. Per the AP, the Tide avoided its first three-game losing streak since 2007, and a Playoff spot is well within reach if we keep winning.

Next up: an open date, and then the big one, at LSU on Saturday, Nov. 7, time and TV to be announced. Death Valley at night, I hope. Mama will bring her pearls.

Roll Tide, darlings. Roll Tide.
