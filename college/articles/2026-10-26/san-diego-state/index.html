Big Waves, Wipeout Ending: Stone Saunders Throws for 369 Yards, but San Diego State Falls at Colorado State, 56-49

Colorado State 56, San Diego State 49 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, Canvas Stadium

By Kai Delgado-Sorensen, The San Diego State Fanatic

XFacebook

What's up from Pacific Beach, Aztec nation. Kai Delgado-Sorensen here, wetsuit peeled down, fish taco half-eaten, still processing. On Saturday at Canvas Stadium, Colorado State beat San Diego State 56-49. Dude. That was a 105-point swell. We rode a lot of waves. We just wiped out on a few too many.

Here's the surf report.

Set one: Stone Saunders was carving. He threw for 369 yards, and Jaylon Hawkins caught six passes for 164, including a 43-yard touchdown. Javion Kinnard ran for touchdowns of 19 and 32 yards. It was 24-21 us in the second quarter. Glassy conditions, bro.

Set two: then the closeout. Colorado State scored twice late in the half, and the second one was a 38-yard interception return by Tre'Shawn Moore. Thirty-five to 24 at halftime. That's getting caught inside, dude.

Set three: the comeback swell. Down 56-42, Saunders threw an interception that D'Mon Brinson returned to our 3. But on the very next play, G. Lightfoot forced and recovered a Hauss Hejny fumble at our 1. Then our offense went 99 yards in 16 plays, and Lucky Sutton ran in from the 7 with 1:57 left. Fifty-six to 49! But Colorado State got the ball back and ran out the clock.

My hero is G. Lightfoot, for that fumble that gave us one last wave. Hawkins was a freak, Sutton ran for two scores, and Saunders also threw a 24-yard touchdown to Jordan Napier and a two-point conversion to Bert Emanuel Jr. Josh Hunter made 13 tackles.

My affectionate goat: the defense. Five hundred forty-nine yards allowed. Fellas, that's not a defense, that's a beach break with no lifeguard.

What it means: San Diego State is 3-5 and 2-2 in the Pac-12. San Diego is the Birthplace of California, and we can still be born again this season.

Next up, Washington State comes to Snapdragon Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 7 p.m. ET on CBSSN. Halloween by the ocean. Costumes encouraged, wetsuits optional. I'll be watching from the beach, then heading in when the sun drops. Gnarly, dude. Go Aztecs.
