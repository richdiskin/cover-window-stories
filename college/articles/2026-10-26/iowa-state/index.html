Storm Warning Canceled: Iowa State's Run Game Stalls in a 39-3 Loss at No. 25 Arizona

Arizona 39, Iowa State 3 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Casino Del Sol Stadium

By Twyla Hemmingsen, The Iowa State Fanatic

Good morning, Cyclone Nation, and welcome to the Ames weather center. Here's the honest forecast for last Saturday night in Tucson: clear skies for the home team, and for us, a dry spell. Twyla Hemmingsen here, with my tiny anemometer for emotional support. Arizona 39, Iowa State 3. The anemometer did not spin.

The front came in fast. On the second play of the game, Arizona hit a 71-yard touchdown pass. Next drive, a 6-yard touchdown run. Fifteen to nothing before most folks had found their seats. Kyle Konrardy hit a 40-yard field goal late in the first quarter, and I logged it as a pressure system forming. It did not form. Arizona led 25-3 at halftime.

The key moments came in the first half, too. Per the AP, we crossed Arizona's 40 three times and got only the field goal. We were stopped on a fourth-and-3 at the Wildcats' 22, and we lost a fumble on a pitch while trying to avoid a sack. In the second half, Connor Moberly came in at quarterback, and coach Jimmy Rogers said they were just looking for a spark. Per the AP, we didn't cross midfield after halftime.

Hero: linebacker Sullivan Schlimgen, 15 tackles, a sack and two tackles for loss. That young man was a one-man storm cell. Carson Willich made 10 tackles, and Keyon Washington added eight. Konrardy was perfect. Jaylen Raynor completed eight of 10 passes, and Vince Benetti caught three for 42 yards. The goat, said gently, like a light drizzle: the run game. We'd run for 100 yards in 23 straight games, and Arizona held us to 52. Aiden Flora, one of the nation's top rushers, had 36. Rogers said it was a collective effort, and nobody's pointing fingers. Neither am I.

What it means: Iowa State is 3-4 and 1-3 in the Big 12. That's a tough barometer reading in a first season for a new coach, and I won't pretend otherwise. But storms pass, and fronts change.

Next up: Oklahoma State comes to Jack Trice Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 7 p.m. ET on FS1. Halloween night in Ames. The forecast: cool, crisp, and loud, if we make it that way.

Cyclone Power, everybody. Go State!
