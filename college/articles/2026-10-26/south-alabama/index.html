Float Stalled at the Judges' Stand: South Alabama Ties It Late, Then Watches Marshall Roll Past 20-13

Marshall 20, South Alabama 13 (final), Tue. Oct. 20, Joan C. Edwards Stadium

By Azalea Fairhope-Grice, The South Alabama Fanatic

Well, hey, y'all. Azalea Fairhope-Grice here, beads still around my neck and glue gun unplugged in mourning. I grade football like a Mardi Gras float: Is it bold? Does it move? Did the crowd scream? Tuesday night in Huntington, our float was gorgeous for three and a half quarters, then lost a wheel in front of the judges. Marshall won 20-13.

Here's how the parade went sideways.

First, the long stall. It was 6-6 at the half on four field goals, and our offense went 4-for-18 on third down. Jared Hollins completed 12 of 33. That's a float stuck at the corner of Government and Royal while the crowd checks its watch.

Second, the one bead we threw to the wrong side of the street. In the third quarter, Cole Funderburk intercepted Hollins at midfield, Marshall hit a 38-yard pass, and Zion Turner ran it in from the 3. Then Davis Little missed a 45-yarder.

Third, the gut punch. PJ Martin, bless him, ran 19 yards for the tying touchdown with 4:38 left, and I screamed so loud a krewe captain in Mobile turned around. Marshall answered with eight plays and 75 yards, and Judah Price scored from the 5 with 1:49 to go. Our last drive ended on a fourth-down incompletion at our own 34.

My hero is PJ Martin, plus a defense that held Marshall to 328 yards while Marshall's kicker missed from 49 and 27. And we went 5-for-6 on fourth down. That's a float with guts.

My affectionate goat is third down. Four for 18, darlin'. My volleyball girls convert more than that serving underhanded.

What it means: the Jaguars are 4-3 and still 2-1 in the Sun Belt. One bad parade doesn't cancel Carnival, and Mobile invented Carnival in this country, back in 1703. We know how to come back next year. Or next week.

Next up, Louisiana Tech comes to Hancock Whitney Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 3 p.m. ET on ESPN+. Halloween at home. Dress up as a first down, y'all. Throw me 20.
