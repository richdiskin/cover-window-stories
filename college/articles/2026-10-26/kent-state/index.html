I Expected This (Honestly, I Didn't): Dru DeShields Throws Four Touchdowns, Wayne Harris Catches Three, and the Wagon Wheel Stays in Kent

Kent State 35, Akron 20 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, Zoeller Field at Dix Stadium

By Ozzie Brannigan, The Kent State Fanatic

Hello. It's Ozzie Brannigan, on my bench near Brady's Leap. Saturday at Dix Stadium, Kent State beat Akron 35-20 and kept the Wagon Wheel. I expected this. I always expect this. That's not entirely true. After last week's 29-2 loss, I expected a blizzard of sadness. Instead, we scored 35 points. I've adjusted my scarf to an 8 out of 10.

Here's what happened, as calmly as I can manage.

First, the comeback. We trailed 7-0, 14-7 and 17-14. Each time, Dru DeShields answered. He hit Wayne Harris for a 1-yard touchdown before halftime, then for 55 yards in the third. Then Brandon White caught a 52-yard pass, and Hunter Hopperton caught a 2-yard touchdown. Twenty-one to 17, Flashes. I expected... no. I didn't.

Second, the interception. Early in the fourth, Akron was driving to take the lead. Norey Johnson intercepted Brayden Roggow at our 15 and ran it back 37 yards. I stood up. Nobody on my bench stood up. I'm usually alone on my bench.

Third, the knockout. Akron tried a fourth-down fake at its own 22 and came up short. Three plays later, DeShields hit Harris for a 19-yard touchdown. Then we got the ball right back at the Akron 13, and DeShields ran it in himself. Thirty-five to 17. That's 14 points in about two minutes. Good.

My hero is Dru DeShields: 294 passing yards, four touchdown passes, a rushing touchdown and no turnovers. Wayne Harris caught nine passes for 156 yards and three scores. We also committed only three penalties.

My affectionate goat: the run defense, which let Jordan Gant run for 162 yards. Also Tommy Newcomb's missed 32-yard field goal. I expected that one. Sorry, Tommy.

What it means: Kent State is 3-5 and 2-2 in the MAC, and we've won the Wagon Wheel two years running. Kent is called the Tree City, and this team just grew a new branch.

Next up, Kent State visits Sacramento State at Hornet Stadium on Friday, Oct. 30, at 10:30 p.m. ET on ESPN2. Do I think we'll win? I expected you to ask that. Here's the honest answer: I'm staying up to find out.
