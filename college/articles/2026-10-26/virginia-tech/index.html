Hup! Hup! HUP! Virginia Tech Wins at Clemson on Luke Reynolds' Late Touchdown, 20-16

Virginia Tech 20, Clemson 16 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Memorial Stadium (Clemson, SC)

By Ruthanne Dellinger, The Virginia Tech Fanatic

Attention, Hokie Nation! Ruthanne Dellinger reporting, former Corps of Cadets drum major, mace in hand, posture perfect, and knees, frankly, exhausted. Virginia Tech 20, Clemson 16, in Clemson, on Saturday night, in front of more than 80,000 people. Every time our defense made a stop, I jumped and said "hup!" My neighbors in Blacksburg filed a noise report. I filed it in the Lunch Pail.

The march went like this. John Love kicked a 37-yarder in the first quarter. Then, with 1:24 left in the half, Ethan Grunkemeyer found Luke Reynolds from 6 yards out. Ten to three at the half. Hup!

The third quarter was out of step. Clemson kicked a 49-yard field goal, Tait Reynolds ran 16 yards for a touchdown, and Nolan Hauser added a 36-yarder with three seconds left in the quarter. Clemson 16, Tech 10. My mace drooped. Not my posture, though. Never my posture.

Then the fourth quarter, and the band struck up again. Love drilled a 47-yarder with 11:52 left. Sixteen to thirteen. And then, with 1:44 left, Grunkemeyer found Luke Reynolds again, this time from 5 yards out. Twenty to sixteen, Hokies. The defense held on, and I jumped so high I nearly cleared the hedge.

Hero: Luke Reynolds, seven catches and two touchdowns, and he even completed a pass himself, 8 yards. A two-way Reynolds! Grunkemeyer was 20 of 32 for 156 yards and two touchdowns with no interceptions. Que'Sean Brown caught five for 69 yards. And the Lunch Pail defense, no names on the backs, but I'll name a few: Jaquez White made 11 tackles and a sack, Kaleb Spencer had nine with two tackles for loss, and Noah Chambers added two more. Zero turnovers for us all night. The goat, given with a respectful salute: the run game, 70 yards. We'll drill it at reveille.

What it means: Virginia Tech is 6-2 with a fourth-quarter comeback win on the road at Clemson, and the Hokies received a vote in the AP poll released Oct. 25. One vote! I'm framing it.

Next up: an open date, then at SMU on Friday, Nov. 6, at 7 p.m. ET on ESPN, at Gerald J. Ford Stadium. Friday night lights in Dallas. I'll bring the mace.

Let's go, Hokies! Hup!
