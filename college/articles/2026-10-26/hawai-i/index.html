Worth Setting the Alarm For: Micah Alejado Throws Three Touchdowns, DeVon Rice Runs for Three, and Hawai'i Rolls in DeKalb, 56-21

Hawai'i 56, Northern Illinois 21 (final), Sat. Oct. 24, Huskie Stadium

By Kainoa Akana-Baird, The Hawai'i Fanatic

Aloha kakou. Kainoa Akana-Baird here, on the lanai with a plate lunch and a whiteboard full of time-zone math. Saturday's kickoff in DeKalb was 9:30 a.m. in Honolulu. By the time I'd finished my second cup of coffee, Hawai'i had beaten Northern Illinois 56-21. Best breakfast of the year.

Here's how the morning went.

First, the swell builds. The game was tied 14-14 early in the second quarter. Then DeVon Rice broke a 33-yard touchdown run, and the waves started rolling. Micah Alejado hit Cam Barfield on a 62-yard catch-and-run to the NIU 3, and Barfield punched it in from the 1 soon after. After Jeremiah Hughes intercepted Taron Dickens, Alejado found Audric Harris for 44 yards and then Frank Abreu for a 12-yard touchdown with 23 seconds left in the half. Thirty-five to 14.

Second, the wave doesn't stop. Elijah Palmer recovered a fumble early in the third, and Rice scored again from the 5. Then Alejado hit Josiah Fetui for a 44-yard touchdown. That made it 35 straight points. Shaka, brah.

Third, the clean finish. Kydel Stone intercepted Dickens late, and TJ Fo'ilefutu added a 6-yard touchdown. Northern Illinois turned it over five times.

My hero is Micah Alejado, who threw for 323 yards and three touchdowns. DeVon Rice ran for three scores, and Frank Abreu caught two touchdown passes.

My affectionate goat: Alejado's fourth-quarter interception. Brah, we were up 35. You can take the rest of the morning off.

What it means: Hawai'i is 2-6 and 1-4 in the Mountain West, and it snapped a four-game losing streak. We scored 56 points, which is more than our previous two games combined. Hawai'i is the westernmost state, but on Saturday, the Rainbow Warriors made themselves right at home in the Midwest.

Next up, Hawai'i visits UTEP at the Sun Bowl on Saturday, Nov. 7. Kickoff time and TV are still to be announced, so the whiteboard is ready and the marker is uncapped. Island time, but on schedule.
