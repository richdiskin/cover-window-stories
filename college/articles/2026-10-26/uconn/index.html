Fast Break, Helmets On: Osborne Scores Three Ways, Bowers Goes 70, and UConn Runs Away From UMass, 35-16

UConn 35, Massachusetts 16 (final), Fri. Oct. 23, Pratt & Whitney Stadium

By Jo Ferrante, The UConn Fanatic

Listen up, Husky Nation. Jo Ferrante here, still wearing my whistle from practice, still grinning from Friday night. UConn beat UMass 35-16 at Pratt & Whitney Stadium in front of 32,106 people. And if you tell me "nobody watches UConn football," I've got a stadium full of people who'd like a word.

Here's the game film.

Possession one: the two-minute flurry. Late in the second quarter, Kalieb Osborne threw a 20-yard touchdown pass to JD Willoughby. Thirty-two seconds later, Cyncir Bowers took a handoff 70 yards to the house. That's a fast break, folks. Get the stop, push the ball, finish at the rim. Twenty-one to 3 at halftime.

Possession two: the hustle play. In the third quarter, we blocked a UMass punt, and Shamar Porter scooped it up in the end zone. That's diving for a loose ball on the hardwood. Porter also caught a 5-yard touchdown pass in the first quarter. The man is everywhere.

Possession three: the dagger. Osborne ran it in from 13 yards out with 3:04 left. Thirty-five to 9. Ballgame.

My hero is Kalieb Osborne: 14 of 19 for 173 yards, two touchdown passes, a rushing touchdown and no turnovers. If my high school quarterbacks completed that many passes, I'd buy pizza for the whole team. Bowers ran for 131 yards on 15 carries. The run game piled up 265 yards.

My affectionate goat is the penalty flag. Eleven of them for 100 yards. Fellas, that's a lot of free throws for the other team. We also missed a 47-yard field goal. Coach Jo wants those back.

What it means: UConn is 4-3 with two straight wins, and the Huskies own New England bragging rights again. The stadium sits on Pratt & Whitney's old airfield, and the Huskies took flight on Friday night.

Next up, UConn visits Air Force at Falcon Stadium on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 3 p.m. ET on MW+. Halloween at altitude. Push the pace, protect the ball, and stop the penalties, Huskies. Let's go to work.
