Please Remain Seated... Never Mind: UCF Knocks Off No. 2 BYU in Double Overtime, 39-31

UCF 39, BYU 31 (final, 2OT), Sat., Oct. 24, Acrisure Bounce House

By Knox Brevard-Stiles, The UCF Fanatic

Welcome to the Bounce House, everybody! Knox Brevard-Stiles, theme-park ride designer, and my ride-safety gauge just filed a formal complaint. UCF 39, No. 2 BYU 31, in double overtime. Per the AP, it's the first win over a top-5 team in school history. The stands moved. The goalpost moved, too, straight off its base, as the fans stormed the field. Please keep your hands and arms inside the... oh, forget it.

This ride had more loops than anything I've designed. Five lead changes in regulation. Late in the fourth, tied 24-24, we got stopped on a fourth-and-goal sneak at the 1. Then the defense forced a three-and-out, and we got the ball back with 42 seconds left. Keyone Jenkins lost the ball on what was ruled a sack, but replay turned it into an incomplete pass, and BYU drew an unsportsmanlike flag. Then BYU blocked Will Stone's 47-yarder, Stone's helmet came off on the play, and the clock ran out. Coach Scott Frost called it the weirdest ending he'd ever seen. As a professional, I'd have to agree.

Overtime, loop one: BYU scored on a 28-yard pass. Jenkins answered on a single play, 25 yards to Josh Derry. Loop two: Jenkins found tight end Grayson Brousseau for the touchdown, then hit Tyren Hornes for two. BYU needed eight and drove to our 4, but Bear Bachmeier's fourth-down pass fell incomplete. Ride complete. Exit through the gift shop.

Hero: Jenkins, 25 of 41 for 293 yards, three touchdowns and a rushing score in his fifth start filling in for the injured Alonza Barnett III. Frost said our backup is not a backup, and I'd put that on a T-shirt. Hornes caught six passes for 137 yards. Duke Watson ran for 94 yards. Tackett Curtis intercepted a pass, and Jayden Williams made eight tackles. The goat, said gently: that fourth-and-goal sneak, which Frost said should have won it in regulation. But then we wouldn't have had the bonus loops.

What it means: UCF is 4-3 and 2-2 in the Big 12. Per the AP, this is Frost's signature win of his second stint, and our first win over a ranked team since 2023. BYU dropped from No. 2 to No. 8.

Next up: Baylor at the Bounce House on Friday, Oct. 30, at 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN. Friday night under the lights. Bring earplugs.

Charge On, and Go Knights!
