Floyd Comes Home: Luke Drzewiecki's Walk-Off Kick Beats No. 15 Iowa, 13-10

Minnesota 13, Iowa 10 (final), Sat., Oct. 24, Huntington Bank Stadium

By Torvald 'Tor' Engebretsen, The Minnesota Fanatic

Well, hi there. Come on in, take your boots off, the rug's new and so is the pig. Tor Engebretsen here, hotdish in the oven, and Floyd of Rosedale is coming home to Minnesota. Gophers 13, No. 15 Iowa 10, on a 46-yard field goal as time expired. First time we've had Floyd since 2023, per the AP. Grandpa would've driven the truck straight onto the lake to celebrate, after checking the ice, of course.

Here's how the ice held. Minnesota blocked a punt early, and Luke Drzewiecki hit a 31-yard field goal in the first quarter. Then, in the third quarter, Iowa thought it had stopped us on fourth down, but the Hawkeyes jumped offside. Next play, Darius Taylor ran it in from a yard out. Ten to three.

Iowa tied it with 3:46 left on a 37-yard touchdown pass. My coffee went cold in the thermos. Then the play: third down, 39 seconds left, at our own 33. Drake Lindsey threw deep, Jalen Smith went up and got it for 39 yards. Then Drzewiecki, who joined us in August from New Mexico, lined up from 46 and drilled it. Twelve for 12 on the year. That young man is as reliable as a good ice auger.

Hero: the defense. Coach P.J. Fleck called it one of the best defensive performances he's been around here. Joey Gerlach, TJ Bush Jr. and Matt Kingsbury each intercepted a pass. Zack Harden made 12 tackles and a sack, and John Nestor had 11. Iowa came in averaging 194 rushing yards and got 90. Through three quarters, the Hawkeyes had just 163 yards of offense. The goat, said kindly over a second helping: our offense, 209 yards, a season low. But the hotdish doesn't have to be fancy if it fills you up.

What it means: Minnesota is 5-2 and 2-2 in the Big Ten, and earned votes in the AP poll released Oct. 25. And now, folks, gather close: per the AP, the Gophers hold Floyd of Rosedale, the Little Brown Jug and Paul Bunyan's Axe at the same time for the first time since 1978. The trophy case is full. Grandpa would need a bigger shelf. And no, it is not a pitcher.

Next up: at No. 4 Indiana on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 3:30 p.m. ET on CBS. Halloween in Bloomington. Bundle up, and bring Floyd's good luck along.

Ski-U-Mah, and Rah, rah, rah for Minnesota!
