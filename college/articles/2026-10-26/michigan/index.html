Column D Stays Empty: Michigan Holds Indiana to 13 but Falls in Overtime at the Big House

Indiana 13, Michigan 6 (final, OT), Sat., Oct. 24, Michigan Stadium

By Augustin 'Gus' Hartwell, The Michigan Fanatic

I keep a spreadsheet, and every column is a lyric from "The Victors." Column D is "the champions." Saturday afternoon, after Indiana beat us 13-6 in overtime at the Big House, Column D stayed blank, and I spent a long time staring at an empty cell. Gus Hartwell here, maize sweater, heavy heart.

The defense deserves a standing ovation from all 111,276 of us. It held the No. 6 team in the country to two field goals in regulation. Chris Bracy made 13 tackles. Zeke Berry intercepted Josh Hoover and returned it 37 yards, setting up a Trey Butkowski field goal. Smith Snowden got a sack. That's engineering-grade work.

The offense? I ran the numbers twice, hoping for a rounding error. Michigan gained 224 yards and ran for 57. Tommy Carr, starting over Bryce Underwood, lost a fumble on a pitch in the first quarter and threw an interception in the second half. Sacks pushed us out of field-goal range twice, including once after we'd reached the Indiana 1, and we missed two kicks. In the final minute of regulation we got to the Indiana 3, Carr was sacked, and Butkowski tied it 6-6 with a 26-yarder as time expired.

Overtime: Indiana scored on a 4-yard Hoover run. We reached the 13, and Carr's last throw landed in front of Salesi Moa just outside the end zone. Spreadsheet closed. Coffee cold.

Hero: Bracy and the whole defense. Hall of fame effort in a loss. Goat, with all the love an engineer can compute: the red zone. Two trips that deep should equal at least one touchdown. The formula's broken. To his credit, Carr took the blame for both turnovers, and the AP notes he's the grandson of Lloyd Carr. That young man will have better days in this stadium.

What it means: Michigan is 4-3 and 1-3 in the Big Ten. Per the AP, every game this year except UTEP has come down to the last possession. Our cardiologist has asked for season tickets.

Next up: at Rutgers on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 12:30 p.m. ET on BTN. Halloween in Piscataway. I'm dressing as a touchdown so at least one shows up.

Hail to the Victors, even this week. Especially this week.
