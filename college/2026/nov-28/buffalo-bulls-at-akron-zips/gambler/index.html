Buffalo vs Akron Pick: Bulls −7.5, Over 50.5

Buffalo Bulls (8-3) at Akron Zips (2-9)

12:00 PM ET Fri Nov 27 · InfoCision Stadium · CBSSN

XFacebook

The Zips honor their seniors at noon on Black Friday, and Buffalo rolls into Akron needing a win to keep its ticket to Detroit in play. My Buffalo vs Akron prediction for InfoCision Stadium is Buffalo Bulls −7.5 and the Over 50.5, and the Bulls have a six-game winning run behind them.

Kendra Woolcott has done the math on a napkin. "Win Friday and we're still alive for Ford Field. Lose and we're done. Hollister, I need your seniors to have a nice ceremony and then a quiet afternoon."

Hollister "Zip" Grubbs is unbothered. "We beat you last year, Kendra. Twenty-four to sixteen. And we just won at UMass. That's a winning run too. It's one game long, but it counts."

The Bulls are 8-3 and 6-1 in the MAC, and they win by running the ball until the other team quits. Messiah Burch has 1,310 rushing yards and 9 touchdowns, and he went for 200 against Central Michigan last week in a 40-21 win. Jason Wright only threw 13 times in that game. He didn't need more, but he has Chance Morrow when he does, with 710 receiving yards and 10 touchdowns.

That ground game is aimed right at Akron's soft spot. The Zips are 2-9 and allow 33.2 points a game. Western Michigan scored 42 on them in Akron two weeks ago, Central Michigan put up 41, and Miami (OH) scored 34. Akron has to stop a back like Burch from getting to the second level, and it hasn't shown that it can.

The Zips can score some. Jordan Gant has 942 rushing yards and 9 touchdowns and ran for 151 at UMass, and Cibastian Broughton has handled the throws the last three weeks. Donelius Johnson has 101 tackles and will be in on plenty more Friday. That's enough to push the total past the posted number, not enough to stay within a touchdown.

DraftKings opened Buffalo at 10 and the total at 49.5. I'm laying the shorter price.

Buffalo Bulls 31, Akron Zips 21 is a 10-point win, two and a half clear of the spread, with 52 total points, a point and a half past 50.5.

The cards are split right down the middle on the side. The Formula has Buffalo 30-21 with Buffalo Bulls −7.5 and the Over 50.5. Letdown Watch likes the Bulls laying it and has 35-15, but takes the Under. Next Man Up has 31-24, takes Akron Zips +7.5 and likes the Over. In The Trenches has 30-25, likes Akron plus the points and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Next Man Up: "Letdown Watch thinks Akron scores fifteen. On Senior Day. Somebody check that card for frostbite."

Letdown Watch: "I watched Akron play this year. You watched the highlights."

The parade lights up South Main Street that evening and the fireworks go off downtown. By then the Bulls should know whether the Tuesday and Friday scores sent them to Detroit. They'll have done their part.

Buffalo Bulls 31, Akron Zips 21. Buffalo Bulls cover the 7.5. Over 50.5.
