LSU vs Arkansas Pick: Razorbacks +14.5, Over 49.5

LSU Tigers (6-5) at Arkansas Razorbacks (4-7)

12:45 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Donald W. Reynolds Razorback Stadium · SEC Network

XFacebook

Lane Kiffin's first LSU season has gone sideways in November, and now the Tigers climb The Hill in Fayetteville for the Golden Boot on Arkansas's Senior Day. For LSU vs Arkansas, the spread and total I'm playing are Arkansas Razorbacks +14.5 and the Over 49.5.

Cece Thibodeaux has stopped apologizing for the month. "Alabama, Texas by one, Tennessee. Fine. We still beat Clemson 51-10 and Texas A&M 35-6. Bubba, the Boot's been ours four years running. Pack it gently."

Bubba Fayette-Dorsey is one week removed from a 45-3 loss in Austin and still talking. "We beat South Carolina 34-6 two weeks ago. Coach Silverfield's got these boys playing hard at home. You come up here at lunchtime with a three-game skid. Good luck with that."

LSU has the talent. Sam Leavitt has 3,019 yards and 20 touchdowns, Jackson Harris has 871 receiving yards, and Princewill Umanmielen has 9.5 sacks. The defense allows 18.9 points a game. That's a team that should win this game by double digits.

The problem is the road and the turnovers. LSU is 1-3 away from home this year, and Leavitt has thrown 12 interceptions, three of them in the one-point loss to Texas. The LSU offense hasn't topped 21 points in any of its last three games. It's hard to lay two touchdowns with a team that hasn't scored three in a month.

Arkansas isn't good, but it's better at home. KJ Jackson has thrown for 2,231 yards, and Chris Marshall has 867 receiving yards and caught nine balls for 106 in Austin. The Razorbacks lost by seven to Tennessee and by three at Auburn. This is a senior class that will want one more good afternoon.

The total opened at 47.5 at DraftKings and has gone up two points. LSU's season scoring of 33.5 points a game says the Tigers have it in them, and Arkansas gives up 27.1. If LSU's offense wakes up, the Hogs still find enough on the other end to push it over.

LSU Tigers 32, Arkansas Razorbacks 19 is a 13-point win, a point and a half short of the spread, and 51 total points, a point and a half past 49.5.

The room is together on the side and split on the total. The Formula has LSU 31-18 and takes Arkansas +14.5 with the Under 49.5. Next Man Up has 31-19 and takes Arkansas +14.5 with the Over 49.5. Letdown Watch has 30-19 and takes Arkansas +14.5 with the Under 49.5. In The Trenches has no pick in this game. Follow The Money has no pick.

Letdown Watch: "A team that lost three straight playing on the road at lunchtime? I've been waiting all year for this one."

Next Man Up: "You've been waiting all year for every one of them. That's your whole personality."

The Tigers keep the Boot, Bubba gets a fourth quarter he can live with, and the Hogs hang inside two touchdowns for their seniors.

LSU Tigers 32, Arkansas Razorbacks 19. Arkansas Razorbacks cover the 14.5. Over 49.5.
