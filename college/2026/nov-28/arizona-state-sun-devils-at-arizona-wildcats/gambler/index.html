Arizona State vs Arizona Pick: Wildcats −8.5 and the Under 54.5

Arizona State Sun Devils (6-5) at Arizona Wildcats (8-3)

9:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Casino Del Sol Stadium · Fox

XFacebook

Saturday night in Tucson is the hundredth meeting in the Territorial Cup, and Arizona comes in 8-3 after winning in overtime at Kansas State while Arizona State is 6-5 after losing in overtime to Oklahoma State. My Arizona State vs Arizona prediction goes with the home team to win by more than a touchdown, Arizona Wildcats −8.5, and stays low with the Under 54.5.

Inez Paloverde has a cup of prickly-pear lemonade and a plan. "Seven o'clock kickoff. Sun's down. I can finally watch a game without squinting."

Skylar Vanterpool, up from Tempe, has a plan too. "Keep it close, steal it late, take the Cup home. Our defense has been good for a month."

The side starts with Noah Fifita. He has 3,473 passing yards, 26 touchdowns and just 6 interceptions, and he threw for 323 yards and ran for a score in the overtime win at Kansas State. Isaiah Mizell has 990 receiving yards and 10 touchdowns. Arizona is 5-1 at home, and the only loss came 40-39 to Utah.

Arizona State's offense has been a moving target. Cutter Boley, Mikey Keene and Jake Fette have all led the team in attempts, and Fette has been the guy for the last four games. The Sun Devils scored 28 or fewer in nine of their last ten games. Reed Harris is the threat, with 1,159 receiving yards and 11 touchdowns, including 11 catches for 172 yards against Oklahoma State. On the road, they lost at Texas A&M, Texas Tech and BYU, and won at UCF.

The defense is the reason Skylar is hopeful. Owen Long has 110 tackles and Jalen Thompson has 8 sacks, and the Sun Devils held Colorado to 10 and UCF to 13. That keeps the score down, but it also makes eight and a half hard to get.

The market moved toward Arizona. DraftKings opened Arizona −4.5 and 52.5, per ESPN. Our card has Arizona −8.5. Arizona Wildcats 34, Arizona State Sun Devils 20 is a 14-point win, comfortably clear.

The Under is Arizona State's offense. Four of the Sun Devils' last five games finished under this total, and the only one that didn't needed overtime. Kedrick Reescano has 9 rushing touchdowns for Arizona, and a lead lets the Wildcats run the clock. I have 54.

The room is all Arizona on the side. The Formula has 32-22, takes Arizona Wildcats −8.5 and goes Under 54.5. Next Man Up has 35-21, likes Arizona and goes Over 54.5. Letdown Watch has 34-19, likes Arizona and goes Under. In The Trenches has no score this week and takes neither side nor total. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Next Man Up: "Thirty-five to twenty-one. Fifita at home, on Fox, in a rivalry. He's going to throw it all over the place."

The Formula: "Thirty-two to twenty-two. They win by ten, and it's not as wild as you think."

My final is Arizona by 14 with 54 points, half a point under. Fifita makes the throws, the defense handles Arizona State, and the Cup stays in Tucson.

Arizona Wildcats 34, Arizona State Sun Devils 20. Arizona Wildcats cover the 8.5. Under 54.5.
