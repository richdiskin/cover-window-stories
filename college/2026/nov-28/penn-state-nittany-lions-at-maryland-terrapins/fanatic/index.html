Penn State vs Maryland: Senior Day for the Terps in College Park

Penn State Nittany Lions (6-5) at Maryland Terrapins (4-7)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · SECU Stadium · FS1

XFacebook

Abby Chesapeake has a clipboard, a seating chart for her section and a list of names with check marks next to them, because she works the halls in Annapolis for a living and she runs Senior Day like a floor vote. "I need everyone loud on the opening kickoff," she says. "I've got commitments from three rows. The fourth row is undecided."

Penn State Nittany Lions vs Maryland Terrapins is the 49th meeting between the two schools, and it's Maryland's home finale at SECU Stadium. The Terps will honor their seniors before the game. Abby will be counting heads during it.

Luann Kessler played volleyball at Penn State, and she still sets everything up for somebody else. "Here's your opening," she tells Abby. "You're 4-7. Go ahead."

"Two wins in our last four," Abby says. "Under an interim coach who took over in October. I'll take that to any committee in the state."

Abby's Terps have had a strange season. Maryland opened with wins over Hampton and UConn, then lost five straight, including a 54-3 loss to UCLA that Abby refuses to discuss. The school made a coaching change, and Aazaar Abdul-Rahim took over as the interim. Then came a 34-29 win at Purdue and a 24-21 win over Wisconsin. With Malik Washington hurt for that one, Cardell Williams made his first start for the Terps and completed 19 throws in a row, a program record.

"Nineteen in a row," Abby says. "Ask me about it. I'll tell you every one."

Washington came back at USC and threw for 251 yards in a 35-21 loss. He has 2,309 yards this season. Chris Durr Jr. has 74 catches for 848 yards, DeJuan Williams has 592 yards on the ground, and Zion Elee leads the defense with 6 sacks.

Luann has a sore spot of her own. Penn State had won three straight, and then last Saturday happened. Senior Day at Beaver Stadium. A 24-10 lead in the third quarter. Rutgers drove down the field, scored on the last play and went for two. They got it, and won 25-24.

"I've watched that two-point try maybe forty times," Luann says. "It doesn't get better. I keep hoping somebody falls down."

What Luann does like is that the offense showed up. Rocco Becht went 14 of 21 for 233 yards and ran for a score. Chase Sowell caught 6 for 123. Cam Wallace broke a 56-yard touchdown run on the third play of the game. Tony Rojas made 15 tackles. Becht has 19 touchdown throws and only 5 interceptions this season, and Sowell has 758 receiving yards.

"We're 6-5," Luann says. "That's a bowl team. And a bowl team doesn't lose twice in a row to teams it beats every year."

Penn State has won almost every one of these lately. The Nittany Lions won 44-7 in 2024, the last time these two played, and 51-15 the year before. The one Maryland win in that run came in 2020, 35-19. These two had played every season for 11 years before skipping last year.

Here's how I see it. Abby's section is loud on the opening kickoff, all four rows of it. Maryland moves the ball early, and Durr makes a couple of big catches. But Penn State gets to Washington, and an interception sets up a Becht touchdown throw to Sowell. Wallace breaks another long run before halftime. Maryland keeps it close into the third quarter with a DeJuan Williams touchdown, and then Penn State pulls away late.

Abby checks off every name on her list on the way out and says the vote was close, but not close enough. Luann tells her Penn State had the votes the whole time. Abby says she'll be calling her back next year.

My call: Penn State Nittany Lions 32, Maryland Terrapins 21.
