Penn State vs Maryland Pick: Nittany Lions −7, Over 50.5

Penn State Nittany Lions (6-5) at Maryland Terrapins (4-7)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · SECU Stadium · FS1

XFacebook

Penn State lost to Rutgers on the final play last week, and the Nittany Lions now close the regular season at SECU Stadium against a Maryland team playing its home finale on Senior Day. My Penn State vs Maryland prediction is Penn State Nittany Lions −7 with the Over 50.5.

Luann Kessler is still mad about Saturday. "Rutgers. On the last play. With a two-point try. I'm not over it and I'm not getting over it until we score on the first drive in College Park."

Abby Chesapeake is all business. "We've won two of our last four under the interim. Everyone keeps telling me we're 4-7. I keep telling them we're playing at home."

Penn State is 6-5, and the loss to Rutgers had little to do with the offense. Rocco Becht went 14 of 21 for 233 yards, Chase Sowell caught 6 for 123, and Cam Wallace ran for 95. The Nittany Lions scored 29.6 points a game and allowed 19.4 this season. Tony Rojas leads the defense with 86 tackles and 6.5 sacks.

Maryland is 4-7 and has allowed 30.7 points a game. The Terps have the better story lately. Interim coach Aazaar Abdul-Rahim won at Purdue 34-29 and beat Wisconsin 24-21, before a 35-21 loss at USC. Malik Washington is back at quarterback with 2,309 yards, but he has thrown 10 interceptions, two of them at USC. Chris Durr Jr. has 74 catches for 848 yards.

The series has been lopsided. Penn State won 44-7 the last time these teams met, in 2024, and 51-15 the year before. The only Maryland win in the run was 35-19 in 2020.

I'm pricing Washington at quarterback for the Terps and Becht for the Nittany Lions. Penn State's front gets to Washington, he throws a couple he wants back, and the Nittany Lions turn those into points.

The spread opened at 7.5 at DraftKings and has dropped to 7. The total opened at 49.5 and climbed to 50.5. The total moving up fits the Over.

Penn State Nittany Lions 32, Maryland Terrapins 21 covers 7 by four points, and 53 total points clears 50.5.

The Formula has Penn State 31-21, likes Penn State Nittany Lions −7 and takes the Over. Letdown Watch is the loudest on Penn State at 34-17, likes the Nittany Lions to cover and takes the Over. Next Man Up has 30-24, takes Maryland with the points and likes the Over. In The Trenches sees the two fronts as even and has no score and no side this week. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Next Man Up: "Washington makes the throws that keep Maryland in it. Seven is too many for a team coming off a loss like that."

Letdown Watch: "Coming off a loss like that is exactly why they win by double digits."

Penn State gets its seventh win, Luann gets her first-drive touchdown, and Abby says a Senior Day loss still counts as a nice afternoon in College Park.

Penn State Nittany Lions 32, Maryland Terrapins 21. Penn State Nittany Lions cover the 7. Over 50.5.
