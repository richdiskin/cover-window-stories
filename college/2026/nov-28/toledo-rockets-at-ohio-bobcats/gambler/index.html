Toledo vs Ohio Pick: Rockets −3 and the Over 44.5

Toledo Rockets (9-2) at Ohio Bobcats (8-3)

12:00 PM ET Fri Nov 27 · Peden Stadium · ESPNU

XFacebook

Ohio wants Peden Stadium green from one end zone to the other for Friday's Fan Appreciation game, the Bobcats' last home date of the year, and Toledo shows up with back-to-back shutouts and the same 6-1 MAC mark as the home team. My Toledo vs Ohio prediction lays the short spread with Toledo Rockets −3 and takes the Over 44.5 on the over/under.

Penny Trautman has a green flannel picked out. "Green socks too, Rocco. I'm going to be the first car into the lot."

Rocco Szczepanski shrugs. "Wear the whole rainbow, Penny. My defense hasn't given up a point in two games."

True, and also the least useful number in this matchup. Toledo shut out Massachusetts 24-0 and Bowling Green 19-0. When the Rockets have played teams that can score, the games have gone up and down: 49-48 in overtime against Temple, 45-38 against Western Michigan. John Alan Richter threw for 429 yards in the first of those and 391 in the second, and he has 20 touchdowns against 3 interceptions this year. Adjatay Dabbs has 8 receiving touchdowns and CJ Miller has run for 862 yards.

Ohio's last two games produced 51 and 63 points. The Bobcats scored 35 on Ball State and allow 23.4 a game. Nick Poulos has been the main quarterback since October with 9 touchdowns and 2 interceptions, and Riley Neer has 717 receiving yards, but last week Poulos threw only 13 times. Ohio's offense runs through Duncan Brune, and it scored 17 at Miami earlier this month.

The side comes down to Toledo's front. Andrew Zock has 12.5 sacks and Julian Fox has 89 tackles, and the Rockets allow 20.0 a game. Ohio has Michael Molnar, who leads the team in tackles and sacks, but Toledo's two losses came at Michigan State and against Buffalo, and nothing on Ohio's schedule looks like that kind of trouble for Richter.

The price is friendlier than it was. DraftKings opened Toledo −4.5 and 48.5, per ESPN. As of 6:11 AM ET Friday it's Toledo Rockets −3 and 44.5, with the total down five points and rain in the Athens forecast. Toledo Rockets 28, Ohio Bobcats 23 is a five-point win, two clear of the number.

The cards with a score are lined up on this one. The Formula has 28-24 with Toledo Rockets −3 and likes the Over 44.5. Next Man Up has 29-25 and likes the Over too. Letdown Watch sees 28-22, likes the Rockets and goes Over. In The Trenches has no score this week, calls the fronts even and takes neither side nor total. Follow The Money has no side pick and takes the Under 44.5.

Letdown Watch: "Ohio's home finale, Fan Appreciation, everybody in green. That's exactly the setting where a road team gets punched in the mouth. I still have Toledo by six."

Next Man Up: "Richter with 3 interceptions all year. I'll take the quarterback who doesn't hand the ball away."

My final is Toledo by five with 51 points, six and a half over even after the drop. Peden goes green, the scoreboard stays busy, and the Rockets head north with the win.

Toledo Rockets 28, Ohio Bobcats 23. Toledo Rockets cover the 3. Over 44.5.
