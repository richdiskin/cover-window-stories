Toledo Rockets vs Ohio Bobcats: Peden Goes Green on Black Friday

Toledo Rockets (9-2) at Ohio Bobcats (8-3)

12:00 PM ET Fri Nov 27 · Peden Stadium · ESPNU

XFacebook

Toledo Rockets vs Ohio Bobcats is Fan Appreciation Day at Peden Stadium on Black Friday, and Ohio has asked everybody in Athens to fill the place with green for the home finale. Penny Trautman has traded last week's black boots for a green flannel and green socks, and Rocco Szczepanski is rolling up Richland Avenue across the Hocking River with the Toledo flag still zip-tied to his antenna and a cooler full of Tony Packo's hot dogs that he promises are for sharing.

The lots open at 7 a.m. Rocco is idling at the gate before the attendant has his coffee.

"No charcoal grills," Penny says, reading off her phone like a hall monitor. "Says so right here. Tailgating in the grass next to your spot only."

"I'm not grilling," Rocco says. "These are already cooked. I drove them in from Toledo in an insulated bag. They traveled better than your offense does."

Penny lets that one go, because Penny knows what Friday is. These two teams are both 6-1 in the MAC. Toledo is 9-2. Ohio is 8-3. Whatever happens in Athens is going to hang over the whole conference for a week.

"You know the last time you were in this stadium?" Penny says. "Twenty twenty-four. We beat you 24-7. I still have the program."

"And you know what happened in that championship game two years before that? Seventeen to seven. Our way. I don't keep a program. I keep a grudge."

"That's not very Thanksgiving of you."

"It's Friday. Thanksgiving was yesterday."

Rocco is feeling good because his defense has stopped allowing points altogether. The Rockets beat Massachusetts 24-0 and Bowling Green 19-0, and Andrew Zock has 12.5 sacks from the end of that front, the kind of season that turns a quarterback's Saturday into a long afternoon of looking over his shoulder. Julian Fox has 89 tackles at linebacker. Toledo gives up 20 points a game, and lately it's been giving up nothing.

"Zock is coming," Rocco says. "You can wear green. You can wear orange. He's still coming."

"Michael Molnar has 106 tackles and seven sacks," Penny says. "He's not a linebacker, he's a weather system."

That's fair. Molnar is the heart of the Ohio defense, and the Bobcats have won eight games with a quarterback room that has changed hands more than once. Matt Vezza took most of the September snaps, Nick Poulos has carried most of it since October, and Riley Neer has 52 catches for 717 yards no matter who's throwing. Duncan Brune runs hard with 563 yards and six scores. Ohio scored 35 on Ball State last week, and the Bobcats beat Jacksonville State in overtime back in September, so Penny has seen this team survive a little chaos.

"We like it messy," Penny says. "We've been messy all year. Messy works."

"Messy didn't work at Miami."

Penny takes a long breath. The 34-17 loss in Oxford, Ohio, earlier this month is the one stain on the Bobcats' conference season, and she doesn't want to talk about it in a green flannel.

Toledo's offense is the reason Rocco keeps grinning. John Alan Richter has thrown for 2,692 yards with 20 touchdowns and three interceptions, and he threw for 429 yards in that wild 49-48 overtime win over Temple in September. Adjatay Dabbs has eight touchdown catches and CJ Miller has 862 rushing yards. The only teams to beat Toledo were Michigan State in the opener and Buffalo in October.

"He doesn't throw it to the other team," Rocco says. "Three interceptions all year. You could count them on one glove."

"You could count your manners on one glove too."

Here's how it plays out. The green crowd is loud from the first snap, and Ohio comes out running Brune right at the Toledo front, drawing first blood on a long drive that has Penny waving her socks over her head. Richter answers with Dabbs over the middle, and the two teams trade scores into halftime. Molnar gets to Richter once in the third quarter and the Peden crowd nearly comes over the rail. But Zock takes over late, Miller grinds out the clock, and Toledo kicks a field goal with a few minutes left to stretch the lead. Ohio scores once more, Neer making a catch that will be on every Athens phone by sundown, and it isn't enough.

Penny will unzip her green flannel in the parking lot. Rocco will hand her a Packo's dog, still warm, and tell her it's a peace offering until next year.

My call: Toledo Rockets 28, Ohio Bobcats 23.
