Tulsa Golden Hurricane vs UTSA Roadrunners: First Trip to the Dome

Tulsa Golden Hurricane (7-4) at UTSA Roadrunners (9-2)

12:00 PM ET Fri Nov 27 · Alamodome · ESPN

XFacebook

San Antonio is going to be busy on Black Friday, with UTSA's Senior Day at 11 in the morning and the 45th Ford Holiday River Parade floating down the River Walk at 6 that night. Tulsa Golden Hurricane vs UTSA Roadrunners squeezes into the middle of all that, and it's the first time Tulsa football has ever come to San Antonio, which Marisol Ybarra treats as a personal invitation and Wiley Pettibone treats as a road trip with unfinished business.

"Welcome to the Alamodome," Marisol says. "Park early. Downtown is going to be wall to wall by lunch, and the seniors get one more walk out of that tunnel before you even find your seats."

"I'll find mine," Wiley says. "I've been waiting two years to sit in them."

Two years ago in Tulsa, the Golden Hurricane rallied and beat UTSA 46-45, and Wiley still describes that afternoon like a man who struck oil in his own backyard. UTSA won the first meeting back in 2013, 34-15, but nobody in Oklahoma is bringing that one up this week.

Here's why Wiley drove down I-35 smiling. Baylor Hayes is playing the best football of anybody in this game. He threw four touchdowns against Charlotte last Saturday, and Oran Singleton Jr. caught three of them for 116 yards. Singleton has 70 catches and 10 touchdowns on the year, and he's the guy Marisol keeps circling on her program. Tulsa has won three in a row, including a 60-10 demolition at Tulane, and Trequan Jones ran for 109 yards on ten carries last week just to make sure the Charlotte defense knew it had to worry about the ground, too.

"That's three gushers in a row," Wiley says. "Hayes finds oil every time he drops back."

"Then he can drop back in front of a dome full of people who want him to stop," Marisol says.

Marisol's case is the building. UTSA hasn't lost at the Alamodome all year, and under Jeff Traylor, now in his seventh season, the Roadrunners are 38-5 at home and 28-1 against league opponents there. They sit first in the American at 6-1 with a chance to finish the job in front of their own people. That kind of home record makes visiting teams nervous before they've even unloaded the bus.

The way UTSA has been winning lately won't win any beauty contests. The Roadrunners beat North Texas 35-16 and then ground out a 19-17 win at UAB last week, with Will Henderson III carrying 23 times for 93 yards. Owen McCown has thrown for more than two thousand yards this year, but the last two weeks he's handed off, managed the game and let the defense do the heavy lifting. Darion White leads that defense with 75 tackles. Owen Pewee has 4 sacks. They don't make highlight reels every week. They make the other team's quarterback uncomfortable.

"Ugly wins count the same," Marisol says. "I've got a medal for every one of them."

"You've got a drawer full of medals," Wiley says.

"And room for more."

Wiley's worry is Tulsa's own history in close ones this year. The Golden Hurricane lost to North Texas in overtime, 45-44, and dropped a 38-35 game to Army, and both times the offense had the ball with a chance late. Hayes can throw it with anybody, but Tulsa's defense needs to stand up when McCown has to make a play on third down, and Kyran Duhon, with 8 sacks, is the guy who has to make that happen.

Here's how the morning goes. The seniors get their moment, the dome gets loud, and Tulsa answers with a quick touchdown to Singleton that quiets it for a few minutes. Henderson grinds, McCown hits one deep shot to remind everybody he can, and the game turns into a back-and-forth that has nobody leaving early for the River Walk. Tulsa gets the ball late with a chance. UTSA's front gets home on third down, and the Roadrunners run out the clock.

Marisol heads downtown to stake out a spot on the River Walk with confetti in her pocket. Wiley heads to his truck, checks the clock, and figures he can be north of Austin before the first float hits the water.

My call: UTSA Roadrunners 29, Tulsa Golden Hurricane 27.
