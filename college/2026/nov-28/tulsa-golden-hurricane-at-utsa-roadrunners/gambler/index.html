Tulsa vs UTSA Pick: Roadrunners −1.5 and the Under 58.5

Tulsa Golden Hurricane (7-4) at UTSA Roadrunners (9-2)

12:00 PM ET Fri Nov 27 · Alamodome · ESPN

XFacebook

Tulsa has never played a football game in San Antonio, and its first trip lands on UTSA's Senior Day, an 11 a.m. local kickoff at the Alamodome on Black Friday. My Tulsa vs UTSA pick is UTSA Roadrunners −1.5 and the Under 58.5, because the Roadrunners haven't lost in that building this year and the last month of their football has been a grind, not a track meet.

Marisol Ybarra has her day planned. "Seniors at eleven, River Parade at six. I'll be on the River Walk with a medal around my neck for every senior who walks out of that tunnel."

Wiley Pettibone came down from Tulsa ready to talk about 2024. "Forty-six to forty-five, Marisol. We came back on you. Baylor Hayes is throwing better than anybody you've seen this month."

He isn't wrong about Hayes. The Tulsa quarterback has 2,275 yards with 20 touchdowns and 4 interceptions, and he threw four scores against Charlotte last week in a 42-14 win. Oran Singleton Jr. has 70 catches, 891 yards and 10 touchdowns, three of them on Saturday. Tulsa is 7-4, scoring 34.4 points a game, and it has won three straight by a combined 140-45.

So why the Roadrunners? Because the building matters and so does the shape of their season. UTSA is 9-2, 6-1 in the American, and unbeaten at the Alamodome. The Roadrunners have also won their last two by grinding: 35-16 over North Texas and 19-17 at UAB, with Owen McCown throwing for 85 yards in one and 112 in the other. Will Henderson III carried 23 times at UAB. That's a team that wants to shorten the game. The number only asks UTSA to win by two, and a team that wins close games at home is the one I'll take at a short price.

That's also the Under case. McCown has 2,213 yards and 13 touchdowns, and he can throw it around when he has to, but UTSA has chosen not to lately. Tulsa's defense has allowed 10, 21 and 14 points in its last three games, and Kyran Duhon has 8 sacks for a front that will make McCown hold the ball. I have 56, two and a half under the number.

DraftKings opened UTSA Roadrunners −2.5 with a 58.5 total, and as of Monday the side is down to −1.5 with the total unchanged.

Most of the room is with the home team, and all of it that makes a call is on the Under. The Formula has Tulsa 28-27, likes the Golden Hurricane +1.5 and likes the Under 58.5. Next Man Up has UTSA 30-27 and goes Under. Letdown Watch has UTSA 29-27 and likes the Under. In The Trenches also has UTSA 29-27, likes the Roadrunners and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Letdown Watch: "You went against a 6-1 team on Senior Day, The Formula. Do you even know what a senior is?"

The Formula: "I know what a one-point game looks like. You'll be the one sweating the hook."

My final is UTSA Roadrunners 29, Tulsa Golden Hurricane 27. Marisol makes the six o'clock parade. Wiley drives home composing a long text about 2024.

UTSA Roadrunners 29, Tulsa Golden Hurricane 27. UTSA Roadrunners cover the 1.5. Under 58.5.
