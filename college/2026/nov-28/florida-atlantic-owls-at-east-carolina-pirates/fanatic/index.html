Florida Atlantic Owls vs East Carolina Pirates: Senior Day Shootout

Florida Atlantic Owls (7-4) at East Carolina Pirates (7-4)

1:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Senior Day at Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium closes East Carolina's home schedule, and Florida Atlantic Owls vs East Carolina Pirates gives Greenville a 1 p.m. send-off against a team that throws the ball like it's trying to empty the building. Bart Quidley has a purple flag flying from his truck bed and a speech ready for every senior, and Kaia Brightwater has come up from Boca Raton with sunscreen in her bag out of habit and an Owls team that is already going bowling for the first time since 2020.

"Bowl team," Kaia says, before she's even sat down. "Bowl. Team. I'm going to keep saying it until it feels normal."

"Say it all you want," Bart says. "We've beaten you three straight. Twenty-two to seven. Forty-nine to fourteen. Forty-two to three last year. You've scored twenty-four points in three games against us, Kaia."

"That was before Veltkamp."

That's the name Kaia has been saying all season like it's a weather forecast for sunshine. Caden Veltkamp has thrown for 3,472 yards with 33 touchdowns and just six interceptions. Last week he went 30 for 38 for 386 yards and four scores against South Florida. Easton Messer has 90 catches, 1,097 yards and 15 touchdowns. Under Zach Kittley, the Owls score 38 points a game and treat the clock like a suggestion.

"We don't run plays," Kaia says. "We run sprints. The other team has to chase us around for three hours."

Bart nods slowly. "And then what happened at Florida?"

Kaia's smile drops a few degrees. "Sixty-six," she says quietly. "We don't talk about it."

"And Army?"

"We definitely don't talk about Army."

Bart has reason to be smug. Florida Atlantic gives up 33.4 points a game, and on the road the Owls have been shaky, losing 56-24 at Army, 48-41 at North Texas and 38-21 at Tulsa. East Carolina, meanwhile, is 7-4, second in the American, and riding four straight wins. They just haven't been pretty. The Pirates beat Temple 17-9, South Florida 20-17, Charlotte 17-16 and then Army 10-6 at West Point last week.

"Ten to six," Kaia says. "Ten. That's not a score. That's a phone number missing some digits."

"It's a win," Bart says. "Our defense held Army to six points at West Point. You gave Army fifty-six."

He has her there, and he knows it. Crews Law leads the Pirates with 74 tackles from linebacker, and Kendrick DuJour is the guy who brings the pressure. TJ Engleman Jr. carries the offense with 808 rushing yards, and he ran 22 times for 113 yards against Army. Emory Williams handles the throwing, and Landon Sides is his top target. It's a team built to shorten the game and squeeze the other side.

"We're a pirate ship," Bart says. "We don't race. We board."

"That's a terrible metaphor."

"It's the only kind of metaphor we have in Greenville."

There's a real question underneath all the jokes. East Carolina's offense hasn't topped 20 points in a month. To beat this team, the Pirates are going to have to score like they did a year ago, when they put up 42 on the Owls. Kaia thinks they can't. Bart thinks Senior Day is the day they will.

Here's how Saturday goes. The seniors walk out to a purple roar, and Florida Atlantic wastes no time spoiling the mood, with Veltkamp hitting Messer for a long touchdown on the first drive. East Carolina answers with Engleman, carry after carry, and the Pirates tie it before the first quarter ends. The two teams trade scores all afternoon, the Owls through the air and the Pirates on the ground, and it's tied going into the fourth. Then Law makes the play of the day, a third-down stop that forces a field goal, and East Carolina marches down for one more score. Veltkamp gets the ball back with a chance, but DuJour gets to him on the last drive, and the Pirates hang on.

Bart will climb into his truck bed and wave the purple flag until the last senior leaves the field. Kaia will stand under it, shake her head and say, "Bowl team," one more time anyway.

My call: East Carolina Pirates 30, Florida Atlantic Owls 27.
