Michigan State vs Rutgers Pick: Scarlet Knights +3.5, Over 47.5

Michigan State Spartans (3-8) at Rutgers Scarlet Knights (3-8)

4:15 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · SHI Stadium · BTN

XFacebook

A week ago Rutgers won at Beaver Stadium on a lateral as time ran out and a two-point throw. Now the Scarlet Knights come home to SHI Stadium to send off their seniors against a Michigan State team that has lost two straight. My Michigan State vs Rutgers prediction is Rutgers Scarlet Knights +3.5 with the Over 47.5.

Vinny Scolari has watched the ending more times than he'll admit. "Surace to Raymond, Raymond to Black, and then the two. I'm getting it framed. I'm getting the two-point try framed separately."

Shay Kowalczyk isn't interested in the replay. "We beat Michigan in the Big House this month. Nobody in New Jersey wants to talk about that."

These two teams are both 3-8, and the numbers say it should be close. Michigan State scores 17.7 points a game and allows 25.3. Rutgers scores 23.4 and allows 31.5. Michigan State's quarterback situation has been a mess. Alessio Milivojevic took most of the throws this year, but Kayd Coffman started the 45-7 loss to Oregon. I'm pricing Coffman for the Spartans and AJ Surace for Rutgers. Surace went 24 of 39 at Penn State.

Rutgers has a runner. Antwan Raymond has 911 yards and 8 touchdowns, and he ran for 101 and two scores at Penn State. KJ Duff has 774 receiving yards and 9 touchdowns. That's the Over case. Michigan State gave up 45 to Oregon and 21 to Washington in its last two games.

The Spartans' defense isn't the problem. Dion Crawford has 65 tackles and Anelu Lafaele has 4.5 sacks. The offense is. Michigan State has scored 7, 14 and 15 points in its last three games.

The spread opened at Michigan State −3.5 at DraftKings and hasn't moved. The total opened at 48.5 and dropped to 47.5. I'm on the other side of that move.

Rutgers Scarlet Knights 26, Michigan State Spartans 25 is an outright home win, so Rutgers Scarlet Knights +3.5 covers easily, and 51 total points clears 47.5.

All four cards take the Knights with the points, and three of them have the game within a field goal. The Formula has Michigan State 26-23 and takes Rutgers +3.5 with the Over. Next Man Up has Michigan State 27-26, likes Rutgers with the points and likes the Over. Letdown Watch has Michigan State 25-24, likes the Knights to cover and takes the Over. In The Trenches is the only card with Rutgers winning, 26-24, and it likes the Knights plus the points and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Letdown Watch: "Rutgers spent all week watching that lateral. The comedown is real."

In The Trenches: "Rutgers spent all week running the ball down Penn State's throat. That doesn't come down."

Rutgers wins on a late Raymond touchdown, Vinny gets another frame for the wall, and Shay heads back to East Lansing saying the Michigan game still counts double.

Rutgers Scarlet Knights 26, Michigan State Spartans 25. Rutgers Scarlet Knights cover the 3.5. Over 47.5.
