Michigan State vs Rutgers: After the Lateral, Senior Day in Piscataway

Michigan State Spartans (3-8) at Rutgers Scarlet Knights (3-8)

4:15 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · SHI Stadium · BTN

XFacebook

Shay Kowalczyk keeps a whiteboard on her refrigerator in East Lansing, and this season it has a list of Michigan State quarterbacks on it. Milivojevic. Hannan. Fancher. Coffman. She's left room at the bottom for one more. Michigan State Spartans vs Rutgers Scarlet Knights is the season finale for both teams at SHI Stadium, and Shay says she'll fill in the last name when she sees who runs out for warmups.

Vinny Scolari has his own wall decoration now. It's a still photo of Antwan Raymond lateraling the ball to Ben Black III as the clock hit zero at Beaver Stadium last Saturday. Vinny printed it at the drugstore, put it in a frame from the dollar store and hung it over his television.

"I'm getting a second one," Vinny says. "For the two-point try. Surace to Thevenin. Separate frame."

It was a game Rutgers fans will be talking about for years. The Scarlet Knights were down 16-0 early and 24-10 in the fourth quarter at Penn State. They put together a 19-play, 75-yard touchdown drive that ate almost ten minutes. Then, with six seconds left and the ball two yards away, AJ Surace threw to Raymond, Raymond pitched it to Black, and Black walked in. Greg Schiano went for two. Surace found Clay Thevenin on a swing throw, and Rutgers won 25-24. It was the school's first win over Penn State since 1988, in front of 106,200 people.

"Thirty-eight years," Vinny says. "I was waiting thirty-eight years for that. I was ready to wait forty."

Shay isn't impressed. She has her own win over a blue-blood to talk about. Michigan State went into Michigan Stadium on Nov. 7 and won 15-6.

"Fifteen to six in the Big House," Shay says. "Nobody in New Jersey brings that up. That's fine. I bring it up enough for everybody."

Both teams are 3-8, and both have had a rough fall. The Spartans opened 2-0 and then lost six of their next seven, including a 38-37 overtime loss to Illinois and a 24-21 loss to Northwestern. Since beating Michigan, they've lost to Washington and Oregon, scoring 14 and 7. Alessio Milivojevic has thrown for 1,559 yards this year, but Kayd Coffman started against Oregon. Jaziun Patterson leads the team with 6 rushing touchdowns, and Chrishon McCray has 42 catches. On defense, Dion Crawford has 65 tackles and Anelu Lafaele has 4.5 sacks.

Rutgers has been up and down too. The season opened with a loss to UMass. There was a 58-7 win over Howard, a 37-30 win at Maryland and a 31-0 loss to Nebraska. Then Penn State happened. Raymond has 911 yards and 8 touchdowns on the ground, and he ran for 101 and two scores in State College. KJ Duff has 774 receiving yards and 9 touchdowns. Sean Allison made 12 tackles against Penn State, and Sekou Kromah had two sacks.

The Scarlet Knights will honor their seniors on the Scarlet Walk before the game. Vinny says he's going early so he can clap for every one of them by name.

"These guys beat Penn State," Vinny says. "They get the Scarlet Walk and they get a parade if I'm in charge."

"You're not in charge," Shay says.

"Not yet."

Here's how I see it. Rutgers comes out flying in front of the home crowd and Raymond scores early. Michigan State answers with a short Patterson touchdown run, and the game goes back and forth all afternoon. McCray makes a big catch to give the Spartans a lead in the fourth quarter. Then Surace leads one more late drive, and Raymond punches it in from close range.

Vinny says the wall over his television is getting crowded and he'll have to move a lamp. Shay writes the last quarterback's name on her whiteboard on the drive home, and then she erases the whole thing and says next year starts clean.

My call: Rutgers Scarlet Knights 26, Michigan State Spartans 25.
