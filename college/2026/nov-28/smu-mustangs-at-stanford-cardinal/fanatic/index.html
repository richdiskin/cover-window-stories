SMU Mustangs vs Stanford Cardinal: FansGiving on The Farm

SMU Mustangs (7-4) at Stanford Cardinal (6-5)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Stanford Stadium · ACC Network

XFacebook

Stanford is giving away a football ticket for every ticket it sells this weekend. That's the deal for FansGiving, the Cardinal's fan appreciation game against SMU: buy a seat, and the school donates another one to an underserved community group, so somebody who's never been on The Farm gets to see a college football game on the last home Saturday of the year. There's also a food drive for Second Harvest of Silicon Valley, photos with Santa Claus at Fan Fest, and the seniors get one more walk out of the tunnel at Stanford Stadium.

Iggy Yamamoto-Ross has turned the whole thing into a pitch deck.

"It's a two-sided marketplace," he says. "Fans buy a ticket, the community gets a ticket. Growth on both ends. Honestly, I should've built this app years ago."

Tatum Delacroix-Price, who flew in from Dallas in a red coat she says is "aggressively Mustang," is not interested in the pitch deck.

"Iggy, it's a nice thing your school's doing. I respect it. But my team is coming out there to fix a bad two weeks, and I don't care how many Santas are at Fan Fest."

The bad two weeks are real. SMU was 7-2 and sitting pretty in the ACC in early November, then Wake Forest came to Dallas and won 49-7. Last week the Mustangs went to South Bend and lost 24-3 to Notre Dame. Kevin Jennings threw it 45 times in that one and never found the end zone.

"I graded the Wake Forest game an F," Tatum says. "Not the outfits. The football. The outfits were fine."

Iggy doesn't let it go. "So SMU's in a down round."

"SMU is 7-4 and has the best quarterback on that field. Write that in your deck."

She's got numbers to back her up. Jennings has thrown for 3,189 yards and 26 touchdowns with only 8 interceptions this year. He threw for 430 yards at Florida State in the opener and 439 against Missouri State. Kendrick Raphael has 909 yards on the ground and ran for 100 at Notre Dame even in a loss, and Yamir Knight caught nine balls for 115 yards in that same game. Marques White has 8.5 sacks. This is a team that beat Virginia Tech 51-28 and Cal 59-20 a month ago.

"We just need one good week," Tatum says. "One. Then I'll sit on The Farm and eat a three-dollar ice cream like a lady."

Iggy's case is that his team is finally shipping. Stanford is 6-5, already bowl-eligible, and has won two in a row on the road, 40-22 at Virginia Tech and 44-28 at Cal last week. Micah Ford has 1,051 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns, and he ran for 151 yards and two scores in Berkeley. Davis Warren has 2,620 yards and has thrown just 3 interceptions all season, which is the kind of number Iggy puts on slide one.

"We pivoted to the run game," Iggy says. "Product-market fit. Micah Ford is the killer feature."

"Micah Ford is a very good running back," Tatum says. "Your defense is a beta."

That hurts because it's true. Stanford gives up 35.1 points a game. Miami put 45 on the Cardinal at home. Wake Forest scored 57 in Winston-Salem. Notre Dame scored 58 and Louisville scored 52. When Stanford has played the best teams on its schedule, the defense hasn't held up, and SMU, on a normal day, is one of those teams.

The two programs haven't played much. SMU won 40-10 in 2024 and 34-10 last year, both after the Mustangs joined the ACC. Iggy calls those early versions of the product. Tatum calls them receipts.

"Two meetings, two wins," she says. "Both blowouts. And you want to talk to me about scaling?"

Stanford does have a few things working for it Saturday. It's home. It's the seniors' last game in that building. It's an early kickoff in California for a team that flew in from Dallas, and the Cardinal has a back who can keep SMU's offense standing on the sideline. If Ford gets 25 carries and the Stanford defense holds Jennings to field goals, this game gets tight.

Here's what I see, though. SMU is tired of hearing about the last two weeks. Jennings gets loose against a secondary that's been leaking all year. Ford runs hard and gets his, and Stanford's seniors make it a game for a while. But Raphael grinds out the second half, White gets home a couple of times, and the Mustangs pull away late.

Tatum will give the team an A-minus. She'll give Iggy's red sneakers a C. Iggy will thank everybody for coming and call it a learning round.

My call: SMU Mustangs 32, Stanford Cardinal 24.
