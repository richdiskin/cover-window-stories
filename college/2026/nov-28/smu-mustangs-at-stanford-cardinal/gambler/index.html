SMU vs Stanford Pick: Mustangs −7.5 and the Under 60.5

SMU Mustangs (7-4) at Stanford Cardinal (6-5)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Stanford Stadium · ACC Network

XFacebook

SMU has scored 10 points in its last two games, a 49-7 home loss to Wake Forest and a 24-3 loss at Notre Dame, and now it flies to Stanford for a 12:30 kickoff Pacific time against a Cardinal team that's won two straight. My SMU vs Stanford pick is SMU Mustangs −7.5 and the Under 60.5, because the Mustangs are still the better team and this game doesn't look like a shootout to me.

Tatum Delacroix-Price grades the week before anybody asks. "Two losses, two bad outfits. We're changing clothes on the plane. The Boulevard does not lose three in a row."

Iggy Yamamoto-Ross is already pitching. "Stanford's six and five, bowl-eligible, and scaling. We pivoted to the run game and the product finally shipped."

He's got a point about the product. Micah Ford has 1,051 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns, and he went for 151 and two scores in the 44-28 win at Cal last week. The Cardinal won 40-22 at Virginia Tech the week before that. Davis Warren has thrown only 3 interceptions all season.

But Stanford's defense gives up 35.1 a game. It allowed 45 to Miami, 57 at Wake Forest, 58 at Notre Dame and 52 at Louisville. The good teams on this schedule have rolled it.

SMU is one of those teams on a normal day. Kevin Jennings has 3,189 yards with 26 touchdowns and 8 interceptions, and Kendrick Raphael has 909 rushing yards. The Mustangs beat Cal 59-20 and Virginia Tech 51-28, and they score 31.2 a game. Marques White has 8.5 sacks and should live in the Stanford backfield. The last two weeks were ugly, but Notre Dame and Wake Forest are better defenses than anything Stanford puts out there.

SMU has won both meetings since joining the ACC, 40-10 in 2024 and 34-10 last year.

My margin is nine. That clears the 7.5 by a point and a half, and I'm fine with that.

On the total, I have 55. SMU's offense has gone cold, and Stanford wants to run Ford and keep the clock moving. DraftKings opened SMU Mustangs −10 with a 60.5 total. As of 6:31 AM ET Saturday it's SMU Mustangs −7.5 and 60.5.

The room is together on both. The Formula has SMU 33-23 and takes SMU −7.5 with the Under 60.5. Next Man Up has 34-25 and takes SMU −7.5 with the Under 60.5. Letdown Watch has 34-21 and takes SMU −7.5 with the Under 60.5. In The Trenches has SMU 28-26 and takes Stanford +7.5, with no over/under call. Follow The Money has no pick.

Letdown Watch: "SMU's had its letdown. Two of them. In The Trenches is the one still sleeping."

In The Trenches: "Two points, Letdown Watch. My front says two points. Come see me in the fourth quarter."

My final is SMU Mustangs 32, Stanford Cardinal 23. Tatum gives the Mustangs an A-minus for the travel suits. Iggy calls it a learning round.

SMU Mustangs 32, Stanford Cardinal 23. SMU Mustangs cover the 7.5. Under 60.5.
