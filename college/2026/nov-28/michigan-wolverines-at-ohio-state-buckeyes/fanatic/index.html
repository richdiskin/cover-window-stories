Michigan vs Ohio State: No Senior Day, Just The Game

Michigan Wolverines (6-5) at Ohio State Buckeyes (7-4)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Ohio Stadium · Fox

XFacebook

Michigan Wolverines vs Ohio State Buckeyes in Columbus has no Senior Day this year, because Ohio State moved its seniors' big moment all the way to the September opener against Ball State so that nothing, not even a farewell, would get in the way of The Game. Buck Holloway calls that focus, Gus Hartwell calls it nerves, and the two of them have been arguing about it since the Skull Session at St. John Arena let out Saturday morning.

"You moved Senior Day," Gus says. "You moved Senior Day because of us. I want that framed."

"We moved it so the seniors could enjoy it," Buck says. "This Saturday isn't for enjoying. This Saturday is for winning."

That's the thing about this rivalry. Everybody knows the history, and everybody uses it differently. Gus will remind you that Michigan won four straight meetings before last year, including two right here at Ohio Stadium, 45-23 in 2022 and 13-10 in 2024. Buck will remind you that Ohio State won 27-9 in Ann Arbor last November and handed out gold pants for it, a tradition the Buckeyes have kept since 1934.

"Gold pants," Buck says, pretending to polish a tiny pair on his keychain. "Ninety-two years and they still shine."

"You've had to polish them a lot less lately," Gus says.

Both teams arrive with bruises. Ohio State is 7-4 and has lost three of its last four, including a 31-28 heartbreaker at Nebraska last week. Michigan is 6-5 and needed overtime to beat UCLA 18-16 in Ann Arbor. Neither side is where it expected to be in August, and that might make this one meaner than usual.

Michigan's case is its defense, and Gus will walk you through every piece of it. The Wolverines give up 17.5 points a game. John Henry Daley has eight sacks off the end, Troy Bowles has 89 tackles, and Chris Bracy has been everywhere in the secondary, with two interceptions and three sacks of his own.

"You want to throw on us?" Gus says. "Go ahead. Ask UCLA how that went."

"We're not throwing at you," Buck says. "We're throwing to Jeremiah."

And that's Ohio State's case in one name. Jeremiah Smith has 86 catches for 1,482 yards and 17 touchdowns this season. He caught six balls for 165 yards and two scores at Nebraska. According to Ohio State's own notes, he needs one touchdown, nine catches and 125 yards on Saturday to break all three school single-season records at once.

"In The Game," Buck says. "He's going to do it in The Game. You can't write that. Well, you can. I'm going to."

There's one stat Michigan's notes put right at the top this week, and Gus has it memorized. In 24 straight meetings, the team that won the rushing battle won the game. Savion Hiter has 542 yards for Michigan and carried 26 times for 104 against UCLA. Ohio State's Bo Jackson has 851 yards and nine touchdowns.

"Win the ground, win the day," Gus says. "Savion's going to carry it thirty times if he has to."

"Then Bo carries it thirty-one," Buck says.

The quarterbacks are the wild card. Bryce Underwood took most of Michigan's throws against UCLA after Tommy Carr handled the job from late October into mid-November, and Underwood has had a hard year throwing the ball. For Ohio State, Luke Fahey has taken over most of the throws since Halloween, and his job is simple: find Jeremiah Smith, and then find him again.

Here's how Saturday goes. Michigan's defense makes the first quarter a street fight, and Hiter grinds out a long drive that ends in a field goal. Ohio State answers when Fahey finds Smith down the sideline for a long touchdown, and Ohio Stadium shakes. Michigan hangs in until halftime. But in the second half Jackson starts breaking tackles, the Buckeyes win the ground game by a little, and Smith takes care of the rest, scoring again in the fourth quarter to grab one of those school records in front of a full Horseshoe.

When it's over, Buck pulls out his keychain and holds up the tiny gold pants, and Gus, to his credit, shakes his hand and says the same thing he says every year, win or lose: see you in Ann Arbor, and bring a coat.

My call: Ohio State Buckeyes 30, Michigan Wolverines 13.
