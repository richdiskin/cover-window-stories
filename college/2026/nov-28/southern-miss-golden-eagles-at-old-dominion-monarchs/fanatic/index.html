Southern Miss Golden Eagles vs Old Dominion Monarchs: Norfolk's Cocoa War Finale

Southern Miss Golden Eagles (3-8) at Old Dominion Monarchs (3-8)

1:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · S.B. Ballard Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

The cups are already out on Granby Street, and the marshmallows in downtown Norfolk are about the size of a fist. The Great Hot Cocoa War ends Saturday, the same day Southern Miss Golden Eagles vs Old Dominion Monarchs closes the season at S.B. Ballard Stadium, and Winnie Pocklington has told Dee Broussard that she has to choose a side in both.

"It says it right on the sign," Winnie says. "Get your cup and choose your side."

"Easy," Dee says. "I choose the side that scores."

Winnie lets that one go, because it's Senior Day and Military Appreciation at Old Dominion, and in a Navy town that matters more than a hot chocolate argument. The Monarchs will honor their seniors before kickoff and the service members in the stands all afternoon, and Winnie plans to spend the first half thanking people and the second half yelling at the officials, which she considers a balanced day.

Both teams are 3-8. Both have had the kind of season that makes a fan count the days to spring practice. But the two records came about in totally different ways, and Dee has been explaining the difference since she arrived from Hattiesburg.

"Our problem is defense," she says. "Yours is everything else."

Southern Miss allows 36.8 points a game, and that's how a team that scores 27.6 ends up 3-8. The Golden Eagles lost 63-31 at Louisiana Tech, 54-23 to James Madison and 55-34 at Troy. Last week they scored 35 against South Alabama on Senior Day at The Rock and still lost by five. John White threw for 294 yards and three touchdowns in that game, and Mario Sanders caught seven balls for 143 yards and two scores.

"Mario can play for anybody," Dee says. "Put him in a crowd and he's the one that comes out with the ball."

Sanders has 960 receiving yards and 9 touchdowns. Steven Robinson has run for 958 yards and 11 touchdowns. Mathis Haygood leads the defense with 97 tackles, and Jameer Lewis has 4.5 sacks. When the offense gets rolling, Southern Miss can keep up with anyone in the Sun Belt. The trouble is, so can the other team.

Winnie's team is the reverse. Old Dominion allows 26.1 points a game, which is respectable, and scores 17.2, which is not. The Monarchs went to Conway two weeks ago and beat Coastal Carolina 21-8, and Winnie talks about that game the way some people talk about their wedding day. They also lost 26-0 at Louisiana Tech and 42-10 at Georgia State.

"We don't score," Winnie says. "I know. You know. Everybody on the Elizabeth River knows. But Jaxon Potter just went 23 for 29 at UConn. He's figuring it out."

Potter has taken the throws for four straight weeks. Josh Rodriguez is the top receiver with 514 yards, and Devin Roche caught seven balls and a touchdown at UConn on top of leading the backs. On defense, Lucas Struck has 77 tackles from safety, and Javen Gibson has 5.5 sacks.

"Our defense is going to be the best defense Mario Sanders sees all year," Winnie says.

"He's seen Auburn," Dee says.

"Then the second best."

Here's how I see Saturday. ODU comes out of the pregame ceremony fired up and scores on its first drive, with Potter hitting Rodriguez on a third down that wakes up the whole stadium. Southern Miss answers right away when White finds Sanders deep. That sets the tone, and the game turns into exactly what Winnie was afraid of: two defenses getting tired. Robinson runs for a couple of scores, Roche gets in from close range, and the Monarchs take the lead for good on a field goal and a late touchdown run. Sanders gets one more score in the final minutes to make the seniors sweat, and ODU holds on.

Dee and Winnie walk back downtown afterward, because the Cocoa War is still on until the end of the night. Dee buys two cups from the stand Winnie hates, and makes her drink one on the way to the car.

My call: Old Dominion Monarchs 29, Southern Miss Golden Eagles 24.
