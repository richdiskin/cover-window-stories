Southern Miss vs Old Dominion Pick: Golden Eagles +5.5, Over 49.5

Southern Miss Golden Eagles (3-8) at Old Dominion Monarchs (3-8)

1:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · S.B. Ballard Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Downtown Norfolk's Great Hot Cocoa War ends Saturday, the same afternoon Old Dominion hosts Southern Miss for Senior Day and Military Appreciation at S.B. Ballard Stadium. Two 3-8 teams, one last game, and a Southern Miss vs Old Dominion prediction that takes Southern Miss Golden Eagles +5.5 and the Over 49.5.

Winnie Pocklington has her cup and her side picked. "We went to Conway and held Coastal to 8 points. We play defense in this town, Dee. Ask anybody at the shipyard."

Dee Broussard is not impressed. "Winnie, your team scored 17 points a game this year. We scored 35 last week and lost. Our problem is the other end. Yours is every end."

Dee has the stronger argument. The two teams have the same record, but they got there in very different ways. Southern Miss scores 27.6 points a game and allows 36.8. Old Dominion scores 17.2 and allows 26.1. Only one of these offenses can win a shootout, and it isn't the home team.

John White has taken the throws for Southern Miss the last three weeks, and he threw for 294 yards and three touchdowns against South Alabama. Mario Sanders caught seven of those for 143 yards and two scores, and he has 960 receiving yards with 9 touchdowns on the season. Steven Robinson has 958 rushing yards and 11 touchdowns.

Old Dominion has been stuck in neutral. Jaxon Potter has taken the throws the last four games and has two touchdown throws on the season against five interceptions. Devin Roche leads the backs with 481 yards. The Monarchs were shut out at Louisiana Tech and scored 10 at Georgia State. Their defense, led by Lucas Struck with 77 tackles and Javen Gibson with 5.5 sacks, has carried them.

DraftKings opened Old Dominion Monarchs −4.5 with a 50.5 total. As of 6:31 AM ET Saturday it's Old Dominion Monarchs −5.5 and 49.5. The side has crept up, and I'll take the extra point with the better offense.

On the total, the case is simple. Southern Miss games pile up points because of that defense, and the Golden Eagles have allowed 40 or more four times this year. Even a sleepy ODU offense should get into the high 20s against that group.

Old Dominion Monarchs 28, Southern Miss Golden Eagles 24 is a 4-point win, a point and a half short of the spread, with 52 total points, two and a half past 49.5.

The room is together on both. The Formula has Old Dominion 28-25 and takes Southern Miss +5.5 with the Over 49.5. Next Man Up has 28-23 and takes Southern Miss +5.5 with the Over 49.5. Letdown Watch has 29-24 and takes Southern Miss +5.5 with the Over 49.5. In The Trenches has no pick in this game. Follow The Money has no side pick and takes the Under 49.5.

Letdown Watch: "Three of us on the same side. Somebody check if we accidentally copied The Formula's homework."

The Formula: "You did. You're welcome."

ODU's seniors win their last home game, Dee drinks her cocoa on the losing side, and Southern Miss keeps it to a field goal and change.

Old Dominion Monarchs 28, Southern Miss Golden Eagles 24. Southern Miss Golden Eagles cover the 5.5. Over 49.5.
