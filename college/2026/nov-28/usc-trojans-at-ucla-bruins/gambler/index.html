USC vs UCLA Pick: Bruins +6.5 and the Over 46.5

USC Trojans (8-3) at UCLA Bruins (10-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Rose Bowl · CBS

XFacebook

A 10-1 UCLA team is getting points at home against an 8-3 USC team, with the Victory Bell and a trip to Indianapolis on the table. This USC vs UCLA prediction backs UCLA Bruins +6.5 as the home underdog, and with the total down nine points since the open, my over/under call is Over 46.5.

Kensington "Kenzie" Rhee is at the Rose Bowl early. "Win and we're in the Big Ten title game. Simple."

Sterling Ashworth-Vance doesn't look up from his phone. "Win with who, Kenzie? Your quarterback got carted off in Ann Arbor."

That's the story of the week. Ty Dieffenbach was carted off after a big hit in the first overtime of the 18-16 loss at Michigan, and third-stringer Madden Iamaleava finished the game. Who starts Saturday is the question of the week, and I won't guess.

Here's what I know. UCLA is 6-0 at the Rose Bowl, and only Purdue scored more than 14 there. The Bruins allow 14.5 points a game. Wayne Knight has 875 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns, which gives any quarterback a running game to hand off to. Sammy Omosigho has 5.5 sacks and Jalen Woods has 75 tackles.

USC is a different team on the road. The Trojans won at Rutgers and Penn State, then scored 7 at Wisconsin and 13 at Indiana. Jayden Maiava has 3,072 yards and 23 touchdowns, but he threw 3 interceptions at Indiana. Trent Mosley has 720 receiving yards and 8 touchdowns.

The market has moved hard. DraftKings opened USC −11.5 with a total of 55.5, per ESPN, and it's USC −6.5 and 46.5 now. I'm still on the home team. UCLA Bruins 27, USC Trojans 24 is an outright win for the Bruins.

The Over is the drop. A total that opened at 55.5 now sits at 46.5, and USC has scored 25 or more in nine of eleven games. Seven of USC's eleven games finished above this number, including the 35-21 win over Maryland last week. I have 51.

The room is all on one side. The Formula has UCLA 26-25, takes UCLA Bruins +6.5 and likes the Over 46.5. Next Man Up has UCLA 29-23, takes UCLA and the Over. Letdown Watch has UCLA 25-24 with UCLA and the Over. In The Trenches has no score this week and takes neither side nor total. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Next Man Up: "Twenty-nine to twenty-three. Knight runs it, the defense holds, and the Bell turns blue and gold."

The Formula: "Twenty-six to twenty-five. A one-point game. Take the six and a half and don't overthink it."

My number has UCLA by three and 51 points. USC gets its yards, UCLA's defense makes the big stop, and the Bruins head to Indianapolis.

UCLA Bruins 27, USC Trojans 24. UCLA Bruins cover the 6.5. Over 46.5.
