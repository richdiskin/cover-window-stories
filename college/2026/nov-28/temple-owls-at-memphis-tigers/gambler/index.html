Temple vs Memphis Pick: Owls +17.5, Under 64.5

Temple Owls (2-9) at Memphis Tigers (8-3)

3:30 PM ET Fri Nov 27 · Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium · ESPN

XFacebook

Charles Huff sends out his first Memphis senior class on Black Friday afternoon at the remodeled Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium, and the Tigers are coming off a 38-34 loss at Navy that still stings on Tiger Lane. My Temple vs Memphis prediction for the 3:30 PM ET kickoff is Temple Owls +17.5 and the Under 64.5.

Otis Delaney-Crum wants this one loud. "Eight and three, the seniors walk, and Jaylin Carter runs through somebody. Teddy, I'll even let your guys keep the ball for a while. It won't matter."

Teddy Fiorella is from South Philly and has heard worse. "Otis, you gave up 38 to Navy. Navy. We lost to them by a touchdown on the road. Don't tell me about blowouts."

Memphis has the better team by a lot. The Tigers are 8-3, score 38.5 points a game and have Marcus Stokes at 3,047 yards with 28 touchdowns. Carter has 1,267 rushing yards and 15 touchdowns, and he ran for 229 yards and three scores in Annapolis. Elijah Kinsler has 8.5 sacks.

The question at 17.5 is whether Temple stays in the frame, and the Owls have done that more than a 2-9 record says. They lost 49-48 in overtime at Toledo, 21-17 to Army, 38-31 to Charlotte, 42-35 at Navy and 31-27 to Rice last week. That's five losses by a touchdown or less. Curly Ordonez has 111 tackles, Keveun Mason has 751 rushing yards, and the quarterback job has moved between Ajani Sheppard and Jaxon Smolik, who took the throws against Rice. I'm pricing Smolik.

The Memphis defense has also given up 26.5 a game, which is how a 17-point spread gets crowded late. And the Under fits how Memphis played last week. Carter carried 23 times in Annapolis and Stokes threw just 33. A team that relies on the run with a lead burns clock, and Temple has scored 10 or fewer three times.

DraftKings opened this at 17.5 and 64.5 and hasn't moved either number.

Memphis Tigers 38, Temple Owls 22 is a 16-point win, a point and a half short of the spread, with 60 total points, four and a half under 64.5.

The handicappers split two and two on the side. The Formula has Memphis 36-20 with Temple Owls +17.5 and likes the Under 64.5. In The Trenches has 33-28, likes Temple with the points and makes no over/under call. Letdown Watch likes Memphis Tigers −17.5 at 40-17 and likes the Under. Next Man Up has 43-25 with the Tigers laying it and likes the Over. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

In The Trenches: "Next Man Up thinks Memphis scores 43. After Navy ran the ball down their throat for a whole afternoon. Bold arithmetic."

Next Man Up: "Temple scored ten against UAB. Your card thinks they score 28. Pick a lane."

Huff's first class leaves a winner and Carter piles up another big afternoon, but Temple keeps swinging into the fourth quarter and finishes inside the number.

Memphis Tigers 38, Temple Owls 22. Temple Owls cover the 17.5. Under 64.5.
