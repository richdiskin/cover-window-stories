Baylor vs Houston Pick: Bears +3.5 and the Under 56.5

Baylor Bears (6-5) at Houston Cougars (8-3)

9:30 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · TDECU Stadium · ESPN2

XFacebook

One of the last games on the board this weekend is a 9:30 Eastern kickoff in Houston, and the Cougars may not have their senior quarterback for his own Senior Night. Conner Weigman got hurt at Colorado and missed last week's 34-31 win at West Virginia, where freshman Keisean Henderson threw for 268 yards in his first start. My Baylor vs Houston pick is Baylor Bears +3.5 and the Under 56.5, because I don't trust a freshman quarterback to win this by a touchdown against a defense this good.

Yo Pacheco is already on the countdown. "Houston, we have a quarterback. Two of them, maybe. Henderson went to Morgantown and stuck the landing. Five-and-oh at home, and we're not scrubbing the launch on Senior Night."

Maggie Whitlock opens her binder. "We had a rough two weeks, Yo. We're taking it down to the studs. But you know what doesn't need renovating? Our defense."

Maggie's right about the defense. Baylor gives up just 18.3 points a game, and it held Colorado to 13 and UCF to 9. Garrick Ponder has 4.5 sacks and Travion Barnes has 80 tackles. The problem has been the offense lately. Baylor lost 28-7 at BYU and 20-10 to Texas Tech, and DJ Lagway threw it 48 times in that last one for 236 yards. Gavin Freeman has 81 catches for 901 yards, but Ryelan Morris leads the team with only 381 rushing yards.

Houston scores 35.0 a game, but a lot of that came with Weigman, who has 25 touchdown throws and just 3 interceptions. Makhi Hughes ran for 158 yards and three touchdowns at West Virginia and has 870 on the year, and Amare Thomas has 10 touchdown catches. The Cougars give up 24.1 a game, and they lost 42-32 at Colorado and 21-13 at Utah.

These two have played close games. Houston won 25-24 in 2023 and 31-24 last year, and Baylor won 20-10 in 2024.

My margin is two. That keeps Baylor inside the 3.5 with room to breathe.

On the total, I have 50. Baylor's defense keeps games in the 40s, and Houston will ride Hughes if Henderson plays. DraftKings opened Houston Cougars −4.5 with a 54.5 total. As of 6:31 AM ET Saturday it's Houston Cougars −3.5 and 56.5.

The room is split on the side and together on the total. The Formula has Houston 27-24 and takes Baylor +3.5 with the Under 56.5. Next Man Up has 29-22 and takes Houston −3.5 with the Under 56.5. Letdown Watch has Baylor 26-25 and takes Baylor +3.5 with the Under 56.5. In The Trenches has no pick in this game. Follow The Money takes Baylor Bears +3.5 and has no total pick.

Letdown Watch: "Senior Night, freshman quarterback, last game of the year. Houston's the one I'm watching."

Next Man Up: "Watch Henderson then. He's the next man up. Literally my whole name."

My final is Houston Cougars 26, Baylor Bears 24. Yo says Houston, we have a win. Maggie claps once and says the reveal comes next year.

Houston Cougars 26, Baylor Bears 24. Baylor Bears cover the 3.5. Under 56.5.
