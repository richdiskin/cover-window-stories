Marshall vs UL Monroe Pick: Herd −4.5 and the Over 51.5

Marshall Thundering Herd (6-5) at UL Monroe Warhawks (2-9)

1:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Malone Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

The Herd's first-ever trip to Malone Stadium in Monroe, Louisiana, closes out a Marshall regular season that already includes six wins. My Marshall vs UL Monroe pick is Marshall Thundering Herd −4.5 and the Over 51.5: the better team on paper wins by more than a field goal, and two defenses that give up 30-plus a game keep the scoreboard moving.

Mercy Holderby keeps the green ribbon on her microphone and the history in her pocket. "First time in Monroe. We'll be polite. We'll also leave with a seventh win."

Darnell Vidrine has his optimism counter reset to a hundred, as always. "Y'all lost to us once with a title on the line, Mercy. We're 2-9, but we took App State to overtime last week."

He's right about App State. UL Monroe lost 17-16 in overtime in Boone, and Austin Carlisle went 16 of 22 for 167 yards with a touchdown and ran for another. Carlisle leads the team in rushing with 524 yards, and Aidan Armenta has thrown for 1,490 this year, so the Warhawks have a little juggling act at quarterback. Noah Flemmings has 95 tackles for a defense that allows 33.8 points a game.

That defense is why I'm laying the points. Marshall isn't a great offense, scoring 22.3 a game, but it has found something lately. Carlos Del Rio-Wilson threw three touchdowns in the 28-27 win over Georgia State last week, including a 94-yard catch-and-run by Chris Culliver, and he has 1,412 yards with 9 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Xayvion Turner-Bradshaw leads the receivers with 443 yards. Against a defense that has allowed 42 or more four times, the Herd should find its 29.

The margin is the part I've thought hardest about. Marshall's last two wins were by 13 and by one. But the Warhawks have lost three of their last four, and the Herd beat them 28-23 in Huntington in 2024. My margin is five, a half point past the number.

The Over is where I feel best. Marshall's games have gone over 51.5 in 7 of 11, and the Herd's defense gives up 30.4 points a game. I have 53. DraftKings opened Marshall Thundering Herd −4 with a 49.5 total, and both have crept up, to −4.5 and 51.5.

The room splits on the side and agrees on the total. The Formula has Marshall 28-24, takes UL Monroe Warhawks +4.5 and goes Over 51.5. Next Man Up has 27-25, likes the Warhawks and goes Over. Letdown Watch has 29-23, takes the Herd and goes Over. In The Trenches has no score this week, sees the fronts as split, and takes neither side nor total. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Letdown Watch: "Two cards on a 2-9 team. Next Man Up, I didn't know you were a Warhawks fan."

Next Man Up: "I'm a fan of points. Two of them, specifically. Enjoy your hook, Letdown Watch."

My final is Marshall Thundering Herd 29, UL Monroe Warhawks 24. Mercy says "We are" at the final whistle. Darnell resets the counter.

Marshall Thundering Herd 29, UL Monroe Warhawks 24. Marshall Thundering Herd cover the 4.5. Over 51.5.
