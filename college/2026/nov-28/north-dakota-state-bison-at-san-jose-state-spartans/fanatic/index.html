North Dakota State Bison vs San José State Spartans: Black Friday Deals

North Dakota State Bison (9-2) at San José State Spartans (5-7)

3:30 PM ET Fri Nov 27 · CEFCU Stadium · CBSSN

XFacebook

Black Friday deals at a football game are the hook for North Dakota State Bison vs San José State Spartans, a Senior Day and Fan Appreciation finale at CEFCU Stadium that San José State says will offer the best deals of the entire year. Tessa Nakagawa-Ruiz came with a shopping list and a credit card already warm from a morning at the outlets, and Inga Halvorsen flew in from Fargo with one carry-on and one goal: watch the Bison spoil the party and still be home for Sunday church.

"Hoodie, beanie, scarf for my mom," Tessa says, tapping her phone. "And whatever lands on the clearance rack at halftime. It's the end of a seven-game home slate. You don't get these prices in December."

"In Fargo we don't shop on game day," Inga says. "We shovel on game day."

"Nobody here owns a shovel."

"I noticed. It feels like cheating."

That's as nasty as Inga gets, and she's in a cheerful mood anyway. The Bison are 9-2, second in the Mountain West, and they bring the stingiest defense either woman will see all day. North Dakota State gives up 13.2 points a game. Kelton McCaslin has 7.5 sacks off the end, and Donovan Woolen leads the team with 80 tackles. Wyoming scored zero against that group in October. Fordham scored zero in September.

"Our defense doesn't rent," Inga says. "It owns."

Tessa's Spartans have been the hard-luck story of the South Bay this fall. San José State is 5-7, and three of those losses came by a combined ten points right here at CEFCU Stadium. Wyoming won 16-13 in overtime on homecoming night. New Mexico won 21-16 on Halloween. UNLV won 24-22 last Saturday, and Tessa took that one personally.

"We're not bad," she says. "We're unlucky. There's a difference, and I built a chart that proves it."

"You build a chart for everything."

"I live in San Jose. We have charts for our charts."

The Spartans have pieces worth the drive. Luke Weaver has thrown for 2,198 yards and 13 touchdowns, and he put up 250 yards and two scores against UNLV. Carson Clark has 75 catches for 848 yards and is the guy Weaver looks for when a drive gets tight. Jabari Bates carries the load on the ground with 716 yards. On defense, Dylan Lee has piled up 110 tackles, and Jayland McGlothen has seven sacks.

"Dylan Lee is everywhere," Tessa says. "He's like my phone. You put it down, and somehow it's in your hand again."

Inga has her own quarterback to brag on. Nathan Hayes threw four touchdowns against Northern Illinois last week in a 31-25 win, and he has 18 on the year. Jackson Williams has 726 receiving yards and caught six balls for 104 in that game. DJ Scott leads the backfield. The Bison have scored 31 or more seven times.

What keeps Inga honest is the road. North Dakota State lost 13-7 at UNLV and 21-16 at New Mexico, and the 21-13 win at Hawai'i was a grind. When the Bison leave the Fargodome, the scoreboard gets smaller.

"We don't love airplanes," she admits. "Too much sitting. Bison like to stand."

"You're about to sit through four quarters of us."

Here's how Friday goes. The seniors get their moment before kickoff, the crowd is loud and happy and carrying shopping bags, and San José State comes out swinging. Weaver finds Clark early, and the Spartans take a lead into the second quarter that has Tessa texting her whole office. Then the Bison settle down. McCaslin gets to Weaver, Hayes hits Williams on a long one, and North Dakota State pulls ahead by halftime. The Spartans keep coming, with Bates churning out yards and Lee making tackles in every corner of the field, and a late touchdown makes it a one-score game. The Bison hold on with their defense on the field, the way they've won all year.

Tessa will buy Inga a Spartans beanie at the Black Friday price on the way out. Inga will wear it all the way back to Fargo and tell everyone she found it on sale.

My call: North Dakota State Bison 25, San José State Spartans 18.
