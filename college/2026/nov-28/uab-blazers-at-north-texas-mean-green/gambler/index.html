UAB vs North Texas Pick: Mean Green −3.5, Over 47.5

UAB Blazers (6-5) at North Texas Mean Green (7-4)

4:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · DATCU Stadium · ESPNU

XFacebook

UAB vs North Texas comes with a quarterback problem in Denton. Tayven Jackson, who has thrown for 3,005 yards and 23 touchdowns, is out. His backup, Chris Jimerson Jr., left the Tulane game hurt early in the second half, and North Texas finished that game with a wide receiver taking snaps. My UAB vs North Texas pick is North Texas Mean Green −3.5 with the Over 47.5.

Grady Mayhew-Ochoa isn't panicking. "We ran it twenty-nine straight times at Tulane and still had them beat until the kick coverage fell apart. We don't need a quarterback. We need a handoff."

Ozella Birchfield has heard that before. "Then hand it off into the best secondary in the country. Oh wait, you won't be throwing anyway. Good luck."

Grady's point is real. North Texas ran for over three hundred yards at Tulane without a real quarterback and led by ten with under eight minutes left. Jahiem White has 1,366 yards and 16 touchdowns, and he went for 148 at Tulane. North Texas runs for 5.1 a carry against a UAB defense that allows 4.3, and Brendon Haygood added a hundred more and two scores last week. Even a one-dimensional North Texas scores; the Mean Green score 40.2 points a game.

The UAB offense is the other half of the side. Ryder Burton has six touchdown throws and nine interceptions, and he went 10 of 25 for 118 yards against UTSA. UAB scores 22.6 a game, and it runs for 4.0 a carry against a North Texas defense that allows 4.5. The Blazers will need more than that on the road.

The Over is the cleanest number on the card. Every North Texas game this season has gone over this total, all eleven of them. The lowest was 50 combined, against UNLV. North Texas allows 31.0 points a game, so UAB will get its share, too.

DraftKings opened North Texas Mean Green −4.5 with a 48.5 total. Both have come down a point since, so the side and the total got a little cheaper.

The room agrees on everything here. The Formula has 34-21 and takes North Texas Mean Green −3.5 with the Over 47.5. Next Man Up has 39-24 with North Texas and the Over. Letdown Watch has 34-24 with North Texas and the Over. In The Trenches has no pick in this game. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Letdown Watch: "Next Man Up, thirty-nine points with a wide receiver at quarterback? What's he going to do, throw it to himself?"

Next Man Up: "He doesn't have to throw it. Thirty-nine is what happens when White carries it forty times. You'd know that if you watched the second half last week."

My final is North Texas Mean Green 36, UAB Blazers 23. North Texas wins by thirteen, well past the three and a half, and 59 combined clears the total. The Mean Green run it, run it again and wear down a UAB defense that's built to stop the throw.

North Texas Mean Green 36, UAB Blazers 23. North Texas Mean Green cover the 3.5. Over 47.5.
