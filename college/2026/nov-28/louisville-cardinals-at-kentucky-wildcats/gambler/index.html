Louisville vs Kentucky Pick: Cardinals −3, Over 54.5

Louisville Cardinals (8-3) at Kentucky Wildcats (7-4)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Kroger Field · ESPN2

XFacebook

Louisville vs Kentucky for the Governor's Cup lands in the middle of Thanksgiving break, with students scattered for the holiday until Big Blue Nation comes back for a Blue Out and Senior Day at noon. My Louisville vs Kentucky pick is Louisville Cardinals −3 with the Over 54.5, and the trend that matters most is how each team has played in November.

Bianca Thornberry has made the drive east with a smile she hasn't wiped off since last week. "Five touchdown throws against Pitt. Lincoln Kienholz has 20 on the year and 4 picks. Bring your Blue Out. We'll bring the scoreboard operator a snack. He's going to be busy."

Rhett Abernathy-Coyle is not in the mood. "We've lost three straight. I know. But this is the Governor's Cup, on Senior Day, at our place. Records go out the window."

They don't, really. Louisville is 8-3 and has won five straight. Kentucky is 7-4 and has lost three in a row, scoring 6 at Tennessee, 14 against Florida and 14 at Missouri. Kenny Minchey threw 43 times at Missouri and came away with no touchdowns and 2 interceptions. Kentucky's offense has gone cold at the worst possible time.

Louisville's offense hasn't. The Cardinals score 38.6 a game. Isaac Brown has 834 rushing yards and 8 touchdowns, Tre Richardson has 822 receiving yards, and Louisville runs for 5.7 yards a carry against a Kentucky defense that allows 3.9. Clev Lubin has 9 sacks on the other side.

DraftKings opened Kentucky Wildcats −1.5 with a 54.5 total. The side has swung all the way to Louisville Cardinals −3 since, which tells you how the market reads the last three weeks.

The Over is where Louisville lives. Nine of the Cardinals' eleven games went past 54.5, including 52-24 over Stanford and 44-17 at Georgia Tech. Kentucky can still score when it's right. CJ Baxter has 8 rushing touchdowns, Kenny Darby has 711 receiving yards, and the Wildcats put up 35 in overtime wins over South Carolina and LSU. The series has seen 41-14 and 41-0 the last two years, both Louisville.

The room splits on the side and the total. The Formula has 30-26 and takes Louisville Cardinals −3 with the Over 54.5. Next Man Up has 31-29, takes Kentucky Wildcats +3 and the Over. Letdown Watch has 32-22, takes Louisville and the Under 54.5. In The Trenches has no pick in this game. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Next Man Up: "Two points, Letdown Watch. That's what a rivalry game on Senior Day is worth to me. You've got Kentucky scoring 22 and acting like that's generous."

Letdown Watch: "Kentucky scored 14 last week, Next Man Up. Twenty-two is generous. I'm practically handing out pie."

My final is Louisville Cardinals 31, Kentucky Wildcats 26. Five points clears the 3, and 57 combined gets past the total by two and a half. Kentucky's seniors get a fight to remember, and the Governor's Cup rides back to Louisville with Bianca.

Louisville Cardinals 31, Kentucky Wildcats 26. Louisville Cardinals cover the 3. Over 54.5.
