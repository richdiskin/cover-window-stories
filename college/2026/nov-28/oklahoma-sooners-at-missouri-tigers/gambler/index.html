Oklahoma vs Missouri Pick: Tigers −1.5 and the Over 41.5

Oklahoma Sooners (7-4) at Missouri Tigers (7-4)

4:15 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Memorial Stadium · SEC Network

XFacebook

Missouri has lost only once at Memorial Stadium this year, and Saturday's Senior Day finale against No. 22 Oklahoma is the last chance for this group to finish with a winning SEC record. My Oklahoma vs Missouri pick is Missouri Tigers −1.5 and the Over 41.5, because the Tigers are tough at home and Austin Simmons is the best quarterback in this game.

Delphine Arceneaux-Moss hits the ticker sound. "Breaking news: Missouri is good at home. Film at eleven. Also film at three-fifteen, on Faurot Field."

Savannah Redbird-Lowe doesn't flinch. "Boomer!" She waits. Nobody answers. "Three straight wins, Delphine. We roll the Schooner into Columbia and roll it right back out."

Savannah has the hot team. Oklahoma has won three in a row, 23-7 at Florida, 24-17 over Ole Miss and 28-7 over Texas A&M, and the Sooners give up just 17.9 points a game. John Mateer threw for 269 yards and three touchdowns against the Aggies. Owen Heinecke has 90 tackles.

But Oklahoma's offense comes and goes. The Sooners score 22.8 a game, lost 21-13 at home to Kentucky and 42-25 to South Carolina, and Mateer has thrown 8 interceptions. Lloyd Avant leads the team with only 469 rushing yards.

Missouri's offense is steadier. Simmons has 2,693 yards with 25 touchdowns and just 4 interceptions, and he went 17 of 22 for 314 yards and four scores in the 28-14 win over Kentucky last week. Jamal Roberts has 1,200 rushing yards. Cayden Lee has 940 receiving yards. Daeden Hopkins has 9 sacks. The Tigers beat Florida 45-17 and Texas A&M 27-6 in this building and score 29.2 a game.

These two have split the last two meetings. Missouri won 30-23 at home in 2024. Oklahoma won 17-6 last year.

My margin is two. That's half a point past the 1.5, so there's no room to spare here.

On the total, I have 44. Oklahoma's defense is real, but Simmons has thrown 25 touchdowns, and Missouri has scored 20 or more in ten of its eleven games. DraftKings opened Missouri Tigers −1.5 with a 42.5 total, and the side hasn't moved while the total has come down a point to 41.5.

The room is split everywhere. The Formula has Missouri 24-23 but takes Oklahoma +1.5 and likes the Over 41.5. Next Man Up has Missouri 21-18, goes Missouri −1.5 and likes the Under. Letdown Watch has Missouri 23-21, goes Missouri and likes the Over. In The Trenches has no score this week, sees the fronts as split, and takes neither side nor total. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

The Formula: "My score has Missouri winning, Next Man Up. My side says it won't be by two. That's not a contradiction, that's math."

Next Man Up: "It's a contradiction with a calculator."

My final is Missouri Tigers 23, Oklahoma Sooners 21. Delphine breaks the news that Missouri won. Savannah says they'll roll the Schooner again.

Missouri Tigers 23, Oklahoma Sooners 21. Missouri Tigers cover the 1.5. Over 41.5.
