Tennessee vs Vanderbilt: Senior Day Under the Lights in Nashville

Tennessee Volunteers (9-2) at Vanderbilt Commodores (4-7)

7:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · FirstBank Stadium · ESPN

XFacebook

Pippa Ashcroft teaches English at a school in Nashville, and she grades everything with a red pen, including the game program. By kickoff on Saturday night, she'll have circled every typo in it and written "see me" next to the Tennessee roster. Tennessee Volunteers vs Vanderbilt Commodores is the regular-season finale at FirstBank Stadium and Senior Day for the Commodores, and Pippa says the seniors deserve a clean program, so she's bringing an extra one to hand in.

Earl "Rocky Top" Haskew drove in from Knoxville on I-40, and he's wearing so much orange that Pippa says he looks like a construction zone. "That's on purpose," Earl says. "Everybody slows down when they see me coming."

Earl has a lot to be happy about. Tennessee is 9-2 and ranked No. 9 in the country. The Volunteers have beaten Alabama, Auburn, Arkansas, Kentucky, Texas A&M and LSU. The two losses were 20-17 to Texas and 27-24 at South Carolina. The defense has allowed 14.2 points a game.

"We beat LSU by two touchdowns last week," Earl says. "We held Texas A&M to seven. I'm not worried about anybody."

The Volunteers win with the ground game and the defense. DeSean Bishop has 1,215 yards and 13 touchdowns. George MacIntyre has been the quarterback since October, and Faizon Brandon ran for 112 yards and two scores against LSU. Mike Matthews has 608 receiving yards. Tyree Weathersby has 7 sacks, and Arion Carter has 75 tackles.

Pippa listens, takes notes and circles one number. "Seventy-nine yards, Earl. That's what your quarterback threw for against LSU. I'd give that a C-minus."

"We didn't need to throw it."

"That's what everyone says right before they need to throw it."

Pippa's Commodores are 4-7, and it's been a hard year after last fall, when Vanderbilt went undefeated at home for the first time at FirstBank Stadium. Clark Lea was the SEC Coach of the Year two seasons running, and this year the wins have been harder to find. Vanderbilt beat Austin Peay, Delaware, NC State and Mississippi State. The losses include Georgia, Ole Miss, Arkansas, Kentucky, Alabama and a 34-27 game at Florida last week.

But Pippa can still point to her offense. Jared Curtis has thrown for 2,080 yards and 10 touchdowns. Junior Sherrill has 69 catches for 996 yards. Sherrill had 149 yards at Florida, and Sedrick Alexander ran for 129 in the same game. CJ Heard leads the defense with 83 tackles.

"Sherrill needs four yards to get to a thousand," Pippa says. "He'll get them on the first drive. I've already written the sentence."

Pippa also keeps a short list of Vanderbilt wins over Tennessee taped inside her grade book. Three in a row from 2016 to 2018, including a 45-34 game in 2016. Earl keeps a longer list. Tennessee won every meeting from 2019 through 2024, including 56-0 in 2022 and 36-23 in 2024.

"Your list is longer," Pippa says. "Mine is better written."

Here's how I see it. The seniors are honored before the game, Pippa hands in her program, and Vanderbilt scores first on a long throw from Curtis to Sherrill. Sherrill goes over a thousand yards on the play. Tennessee answers with Bishop, and the Volunteers' defense takes over in the second quarter. Brandon breaks a long run, Bishop scores again, and Tennessee goes up by two touchdowns in the third. Vanderbilt keeps throwing and gets a late touchdown to make it respectable.

Earl heads for I-40 with his orange on and his windows down, singing. Pippa marks the final score in red and writes "acceptable" next to it, which she says is the highest grade she has ever given Tennessee.

My call: Tennessee Volunteers 30, Vanderbilt Commodores 19.
