Tennessee vs Vanderbilt Pick: Commodores +13.5, Over 43.5

Tennessee Volunteers (9-2) at Vanderbilt Commodores (4-7)

7:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · FirstBank Stadium · ESPN

XFacebook

Tennessee is No. 9 in the country and 9-2, and Vanderbilt is playing for nothing but pride and its seniors in the regular-season finale at FirstBank Stadium. My Tennessee vs Vanderbilt prediction is Vanderbilt Commodores +13.5 with the Over 43.5.

Earl Haskew drove in on Interstate 40 at dawn. "We've beaten Alabama, Kentucky, Texas A&M and LSU. Our defense gives up 14.2 a game. Pippa, I'm being polite by showing up."

Pippa Ashcroft doesn't look up from her notes. "You beat LSU with 79 yards through the air, Earl. You're running the ball because you have to."

Pippa has a point. Tennessee wins with defense and the ground game. DeSean Bishop has 1,215 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns. George MacIntyre has taken the throws since October, and against LSU he went 12 of 21 for 79 yards. Faizon Brandon ran for 112 yards and two scores in that game. The defense is the real thing, with Tyree Weathersby at 7 sacks, though the SEC availability report lists him out, and Arion Carter at 75 tackles.

That's the problem with laying 13.5. A team that throws for 79 yards doesn't pull away from many opponents. Tennessee won 22-7 at Texas A&M and 27-13 over LSU. Those are wins by 15 and 14. Both barely clear the spread, and both were games Tennessee controlled.

Vanderbilt is 4-7, but the Commodores can move the ball through the air. Jared Curtis has 2,080 yards, and Junior Sherrill has 69 catches for 996 yards. Sherrill had 149 yards at Florida in a 34-27 loss. Sedrick Alexander ran for 129 in that game. The problem is defense, at 29.1 points allowed per game.

I'm pricing MacIntyre for Tennessee and Curtis for Vanderbilt. Vanderbilt gets a couple of big plays to Sherrill, Tennessee runs the ball, and the Volunteers win without pulling away.

The Over is the other half. Tennessee has scored 31.0 points a game, and Vanderbilt gives up 29.1. Vanderbilt scored 27 at Florida. A total of 43.5 is low for two teams that combine for more than 50 points a game on offense.

Tennessee Volunteers 30, Vanderbilt Commodores 19 is an 11-point Tennessee win, so Vanderbilt Commodores +13.5 covers by two and a half, and 49 total points clears 43.5.

The room is split on the side and together on the total. The Formula has Tennessee 31-18 and takes Vanderbilt +13.5 with the Over 43.5. Next Man Up has 29-17 and takes Vanderbilt +13.5 with the Over 43.5. Letdown Watch has 32-18 and takes Tennessee −13.5 with the Over 43.5. In The Trenches has Tennessee 27-22 and takes Vanderbilt +13.5, with no over/under call. Follow The Money has no pick.

Letdown Watch: "Tennessee wants to make a statement for the committee. Big win, big margin."

In The Trenches: "Statements are hard to make with 79 throwing yards."

Tennessee wins, Earl drives home happy on I-40, and Pippa says her seniors made the Volunteers earn every yard.

Tennessee Volunteers 30, Vanderbilt Commodores 19. Vanderbilt Commodores cover the 13.5. Over 43.5.
