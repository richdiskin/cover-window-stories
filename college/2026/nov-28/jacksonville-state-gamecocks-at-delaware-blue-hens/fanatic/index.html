Jacksonville State Gamecocks vs Delaware Blue Hens: Newark Finale

Jacksonville State Gamecocks (8-3) at Delaware Blue Hens (6-5)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Delaware Stadium · CBSSN

XFacebook

The last time Jacksonville State played a football game in Newark, it was 1978, and Henrietta "Hen" Lofland has spent all week telling people at the Delaware Stadium gate that her uncle was there and is still mad about the parking. On Saturday at 3:30, Jacksonville State Gamecocks vs Delaware Blue Hens closes the home schedule in an 18,500-seat stadium, kids get discounted tickets, students get in free, and Burl Ashcraft-Neeley has driven up from Alabama with a cooler full of sandwiches and a very relaxed attitude.

"We already clinched," Burl says. "Title game. Liberty. December fourth. I'm here on vacation."

"You drove all the way from Alabama for a vacation?"

"I drove all the way from Alabama to watch Caden Creel. Vacation's a bonus."

Hen has heard plenty about Caden Creel this week, and she'll admit he's earned it. Last Saturday, the Gamecocks trailed Missouri State 20-3 going into the fourth quarter, and ESPN's win numbers gave them barely more than a one-in-a-hundred chance. Then Creel threw touchdown passes of 59 and 21 yards, ran for a 22-yard score, and Jacksonville State won 25-20 on Senior Day.

"One in a hundred," Burl says, holding up a finger. "My uncle has a better shot at the lottery, and he doesn't even play."

"Your uncle and my uncle should meet."

Creel leads the team in passing and rushing, with 2,417 yards and 19 touchdowns through the air and 892 on the ground. Deondre Johnson is the big-play receiver with 651 yards, and kicker Garrison Rippa set the school record with his 51st career field goal last week. On defense, safety Caleb Nix has 73 tackles and Tae Floyd has five sacks.

Hen's answer is Nick Minicucci, and she says the name like it's a family member. He set Delaware's career record for touchdown passes in the 42-30 win at New Mexico State, throwing for 398 yards and five scores, and the Blue Hens are bowl eligible for the second straight year. He has 23 touchdown throws against four interceptions. Sean Wilson is his top target with 782 yards, and tight end Scott Moore caught three touchdowns last week alone.

"Twenty-three to four," Hen says. "You know how many interceptions that is? Barely any. I've lost more umbrellas."

"You've lost more umbrellas in a month."

"In a week, Burl. I'm not proud of it."

Gavin Moul leads the Delaware defense with 84 tackles and Blake Matthews has five sacks. Hen is less excited about the rest of the defensive numbers. The Blue Hens give up 28 points a game, they lost 42-3 at Virginia in September, and Liberty came into Newark in October and won 30-14.

"Liberty," Burl says. "The same Liberty we play next week. You're a practice round."

"We're a final exam."

The two teams met last year, and Jacksonville State won 38-25. Hen remembers the score and refuses to say it out loud. She just points at the scoreboard and makes a face.

Here's how Saturday goes. The students fill the stands early, the kids wave homemade signs, and Minicucci comes out throwing, hitting Wilson for a long touchdown on the first drive. Creel answers by running it in himself. Delaware's offense keeps moving the ball, but Rippa gets the Gamecocks points every time they stall. Late in the fourth, with Delaware up by two, Creel scrambles out of trouble, finds Johnson down the sideline, and sets up the go-ahead field goal. Minicucci gets one last chance and comes up just short near midfield.

Burl will pack up his cooler, shake Hen's hand and promise to come back before another forty-eight years go by. Hen will tell him to bring his uncle.

My call: Jacksonville State Gamecocks 27, Delaware Blue Hens 26.
