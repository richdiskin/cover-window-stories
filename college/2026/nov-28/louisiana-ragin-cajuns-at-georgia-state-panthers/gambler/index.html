Louisiana vs Georgia State Pick: Panthers +4.5, Over 55.5

Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns (8-3) at Georgia State Panthers (5-6)

2:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Center Parc Stadium · ESPN+

A can of food or a new toy gets you into Center Parc Credit Union Stadium for free on Saturday, because Georgia State is calling its finale the Giveback Game, and the Panthers need one more win to reach six. I'm taking Georgia State Panthers +4.5 against the spread in this Louisiana vs Georgia State prediction, with the over/under going Over 55.5.

Malik Overstreet already dropped off two bags of canned beans. "Senior Day, Mimi, plus a good deed. Atlanta is undefeated today."

Mimi Fontenot laughs. "Your team lost by one at Marshall, Malik. Undefeated is a strong word."

That 27-28 loss in Huntington is part of why I like the points. Georgia State has scored 27 or more in seven games, and Ayden Pereira has been the quarterback since late October, throwing three touchdowns at Marshall. Rodshaun Dorsey has 10 receiving touchdowns. The defense has Xavier Esqillion, who has 10.5 sacks, and he is the type of player who changes a game in one snap.

Louisiana is 8-3 and the better team, but it hasn't been dominant away from Lafayette. The Ragin' Cajuns lost 31-32 at Louisiana Tech and 31-35 at Southern Miss, and beat Charlotte and UL Monroe on the road. Lunch Winfield threw for 229 yards and three scores against Coastal Carolina last week. JJ Garner has 8 rushing touchdowns and Jake St. Andre has 83 tackles.

The market moved a point and a half toward the visitors. DraftKings opened Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns −3 with a 56.5 total. As of 6:31 AM ET Saturday it's Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns −4.5 and 55.5. Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns 30, Georgia State Panthers 27 is a three-point road win, a point and a half short of that. Louisiana won three of the four meetings since 2018, but three of the four were decided by four points or fewer.

The total has come down a point to 55.5, and I'm on the high side. Louisiana's last two games, 42-21 at UL Monroe and 38-24 over Coastal Carolina, both cleared it. Georgia State scores 32.2 and allows 27.1. Esqillion can wreck a drive, but neither defense has been able to keep a determined offense out of the end zone for four quarters. I have this at 57.

The room splits on the side and the total. The Formula has Louisiana 29-26 and takes Georgia State +4.5 with the Under 55.5. Next Man Up has Georgia State 31-30 and takes Georgia State +4.5 with the Over 55.5. Letdown Watch has Louisiana 30-25 and takes Louisiana −4.5 with the Under 55.5. In The Trenches has no pick in this game. Follow The Money takes Georgia State Panthers +4.5 and has no total pick.

Next Man Up: "Georgia State by one. Senior Day, six wins on the table. The home team has every reason to play its best game."

My final is Louisiana by three with 57 points, a point and a half over. The food drive does its job, and the Panthers stay within reach.

Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns 30, Georgia State Panthers 27. Georgia State Panthers cover the 4.5. Over 55.5.
