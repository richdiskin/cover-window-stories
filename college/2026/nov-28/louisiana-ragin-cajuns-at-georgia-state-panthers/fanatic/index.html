Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns vs Georgia State Panthers: Giveback Game

Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns (8-3) at Georgia State Panthers (5-6)

2:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Center Parc Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Bring a can of soup or a new, unwrapped toy to the box office at Center Parc Credit Union Stadium on Saturday and you get into Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns vs Georgia State Panthers for free, because Georgia State's finale is the Giveback Game and Senior Day rolled into one. Malik Overstreet rode MARTA in with two grocery bags full of canned beans, and Mimi Fontenot drove in from Lafayette with a toy fire truck under one arm and a Ragin' Cajuns flag under the other, ready to be generous with everything except the scoreboard.

"Look at this," Malik says, holding up a bag. "Black beans, green beans, baked beans. A whole bean portfolio. That's my ticket."

"I brought a fire truck," Mimi says. "It has a ladder and a siren. You know what your ticket is? Beans."

"Beans feed a family, Mimi."

"Then the family's going to need dessert. I'll bring a pie next year."

That's how it goes with these two, generous and competitive in the same breath. The donations go to people in need around Atlanta, and both of them feel good about that. The game is another story.

Mimi's Ragin' Cajuns are 8-3 and rolling, with three straight wins over South Alabama, UL Monroe and Coastal Carolina. Lunch Winfield threw for 229 yards and three touchdowns against Coastal last week, and JJ Garner has run for 635 yards and eight scores. Jake St. Andre leads the defense with 83 tackles, and Maxie Baudoin IV is the man up front.

"We eat well, we run hard and we dance after," Mimi says. "That's the whole plan. It's worked eight times."

"It didn't work in Ruston," Malik says.

Mimi sets the fire truck down. "Thirty-two to thirty-one. You had to go there."

"Or Hattiesburg."

"You're not getting a slice of that pie, Malik."

Malik's Panthers are 5-6, and a win gets them to six, which means Senior Day could end with a bowl conversation. Last week was a heartbreaker, a 28-27 loss at Marshall, with Ayden Pereira throwing three touchdowns and Qwantavius Wiggins running for 114 yards. Pereira took over the offense in late October, and Rodshaun Dorsey has been his go-to target in the end zone, with 10 touchdown catches on the year.

The Panthers' best player might be on defense. Xavier Esqillion has 10.5 sacks from linebacker, and Malik talks about him the way people in Atlanta talk about traffic, like a force of nature you plan your whole day around.

"Esqillion is coming," Malik says. "He's like the Connector at five o'clock. You can't avoid him, and he'll ruin your afternoon."

"Lunch Winfield runs around people," Mimi says. "He's a quarterback named Lunch. You think he's scared of a linebacker? He's never missed a meal in his life."

The history here is close, too. Georgia State won 20-17 the last time these two met. Louisiana won 21-17 and 34-31 before that. Malik says every game in this series feels like it's decided by whoever brought the most snacks.

Here's how Saturday goes. The seniors are honored before kickoff, and the stadium is full of people who paid their way in with groceries. Georgia State comes out fired up, and Pereira hits Dorsey for a touchdown on the first drive. Louisiana answers with Garner, carry after carry, and Winfield finds a receiver over the middle to tie it. The game swings back and forth, with Esqillion getting to Winfield twice and the home crowd roaring each time. Late in the fourth, Louisiana leads by three, and Georgia State drives into field-goal range. Then St. Andre makes a big stop on third down, the Panthers have to settle for a long try that sails wide, and the Ragin' Cajuns run out the clock.

Malik will drop the last can of beans in the donation bin on the way out, because a good deed doesn't depend on a final score. Mimi will slip her fire truck in right next to it.

My call: Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns 30, Georgia State Panthers 27.
