San Diego State Aztecs vs Washington State Cougars: The Rematch in Pullman

San Diego State Aztecs (6-5) at Washington State Cougars (2-9)

6:30 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Martin Stadium · CW

XFacebook

Hank Palouse has a cassette tape on his kitchen table with "OCT 31" written on the label in black marker. He recorded the radio call of Washington State's 26-20 loss in San Diego, the one where the Cougars drove for the go-ahead score in the final minute and got turned away, and he's been listening to it in the combine all month. Now the Cougars get the Aztecs again, in Pullman, on Senior Day, and Hank has already hung Ol' Crimson out the front window.

"Second chance," Hank says. "You don't get many of those in life. I'm not wasting this one."

Kai Delgado-Sorensen flew up from San Diego with a beanie, two sweatshirts and a very relaxed attitude about the whole thing.

"Hank, my man, respect. But this is a flex game. It doesn't count. We beat Boise on the blue turf last week, and we play them again for the whole thing on December 4. This one's a small wave. We're just out here keeping our arms loose."

That's the setup for Saturday. The rebuilt Pac-12 added a flex week this year, and the results don't count in the league standings. San Diego State is 6-5 overall and 5-2 in the conference, and its 22-20 win at Boise State last week clinched a spot in the championship game. Washington State is 2-9 and 1-6 in league play, sitting in seventh place. The league paired them up for one more game, and Pullman gets a home date with a team that has its mind on next week.

"They're looking past us," Hank says. "Good. Let them look."

Kai doesn't argue with that so much as wave it off. "We don't look past anybody. We just look at the forecast."

The Aztecs have done it with defense and a running game. Javion Kinnard has 812 rushing yards. Sione Hala has 101 tackles, and Djibril Rahman has 5 sacks. Stone Saunders took over at quarterback in October and the Aztecs have won four of five since, including the win over the Cougars in San Diego, but the throwing part is still a work in progress. He has 3 touchdowns and 5 interceptions this year. San Diego State has also given up 31.1 points per game, and it lost 66-14 at Oregon State and 56-49 at Colorado State.

"Wipeouts," Kai says. "Everybody wipes out. You paddle back out."

Hank's case is the quarterback on his side. Owen Eshelman threw for 299 yards against the Aztecs in October and ran for two touchdowns, and he went 13 for 14 when the Cougars put up 73 on Colorado State two weeks ago. Branden Ganashamoorthy has 597 receiving yards. Kirby Vorhees has 8 rushing touchdowns. Keith Brown has 89 tackles.

Last week wasn't pretty. Washington State lost 42-14 at Texas State, and Hank told a 20-minute story on the drive home about a wheat harvest years back that went sideways, which is what he does after a bad loss.

"Cougs never quit," Hank says. "You heard me say it after Texas State. I'll say it again on Saturday, but louder."

The Cougars do have recent history in their corner. They're 3-2 all-time against San Diego State and won the only previous meeting in Pullman, 36-13 last season. They also won a 29-26 game in 2024. Kai calls those different eras. Hank has the tapes of both.

"Of course you do," Kai says.

The Cougars will honor their seniors before the 3:30 local kickoff. It's the last game in Martin Stadium for a group that's been through a lot, and Hank wants them to go out on a high note. The Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation are presenting the game, and Hank says he'll bring the Ol' Crimson flag for the senior walk.

Here's where I come out. San Diego State is the better team on paper, but the Aztecs have a title game in six days, and they'll be careful with the guys they need. Washington State has nothing left to save. Eshelman throws it around Martin Stadium like it's October again, Vorhees punches one in, and this time the Cougars get the stop at the end.

Hank will wave Ol' Crimson until his arms hurt. Kai will say "wipeout, dude," pull his beanie down and fly home to get ready for Boise.

My call: Washington State Cougars 27, San Diego State Aztecs 23.
