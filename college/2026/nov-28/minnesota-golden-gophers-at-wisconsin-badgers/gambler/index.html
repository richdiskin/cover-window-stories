Minnesota vs Wisconsin Pick: Badgers −7.5, Over 37.5

Minnesota Golden Gophers (6-5) at Wisconsin Badgers (7-4)

7:30 PM ET Fri Nov 27 · Camp Randall Stadium · NBC

XFacebook

Minnesota has carried Paul Bunyan's Axe out of this rivalry two years running, and on Black Friday night at Camp Randall the Badgers get their shot to bring it home. My Minnesota vs Wisconsin prediction: Wisconsin Badgers −7.5 and the Over 37.5, with the rivalry under the lights at 7:30 PM ET.

Lars "Brat" Ellefson has the Purdue tape on a loop. "Three hundred eighty-one rushing yards, Tor. Colton Joseph ran for 187 of them. We didn't throw it because we didn't have to."

Tor Engebretsen counters with a kicker. "Drzewiecki from forty-four with no time left to beat Northwestern. Same distance he made to beat Iowa. We win close games, Lars. You know that better than anybody."

Wisconsin's defense is the reason this game is a full score and more. The Badgers allow 16.5 points a game. They held USC to 7, Rutgers to 10, Michigan State to 3 and Purdue to 10. Cooper Catalano has 103 tackles and Sebastian Cheeks has 7 sacks. With Joseph back taking snaps after Ryan Hopkins started two games, the offense also has its run threat again.

Minnesota got to 6-5 and bowl eligibility on that field goal, but the offense is in the middle of a reset. P.J. Fleck fired offensive coordinator Greg Harbaugh Jr. on Nov. 15 and tight ends coach Eric Koehler called plays against Northwestern. Drake Lindsey threw for 224 yards and two touchdowns that day, his best game in a while. Before that, the Gophers scored 14 at Penn State, 20 against UCLA and 16 at Indiana.

Those Minnesota losses weren't close, either. Purdue won 41-13, Mississippi State won 38-13 and Penn State won 42-14. When the Gophers fall behind, they don't have the offense to catch up.

The total is low for a reason, but it's low enough. DraftKings opened Wisconsin at 6 and the total at 38.5, and the side has moved toward the Badgers. If Wisconsin runs like it did at Purdue, it gets to the high 20s by itself, and Minnesota scores just enough to pull the game past 37.5.

Wisconsin Badgers 28, Minnesota Golden Gophers 15 is a 13-point win, five and a half clear of the spread, with 43 total points, five and a half over 37.5.

All four cards with a score on them are with the Badgers. The Formula has Wisconsin 28-18, likes Wisconsin Badgers −7.5 and likes the Over. Letdown Watch has 28-13 and likes Wisconsin and the Over. In The Trenches has 32-15, likes the Badgers and makes no over/under call. Next Man Up likes Wisconsin at 24-12 but takes the Under 37.5. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Next Man Up: "Thirty-six points. That's this game. You three are dreaming of a shootout in a rivalry that's been played in a phone booth for ten years."

The Formula: "You took the Under on a team that just ran for nearly four hundred. Bundle up."

The Axe goes back to Madison, the Badgers run through the cold, and Minnesota scores just enough late to get the total home.

Wisconsin Badgers 28, Minnesota Golden Gophers 15. Wisconsin Badgers cover the 7.5. Over 37.5.
