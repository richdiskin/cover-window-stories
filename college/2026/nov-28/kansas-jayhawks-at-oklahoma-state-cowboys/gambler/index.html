Kansas vs Oklahoma State Pick: Jayhawks +6, Under 64.5

Kansas Jayhawks (4-7) at Oklahoma State Cowboys (7-4)

6:15 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Boone Pickens Stadium · ESPN2

XFacebook

Kansas vs Oklahoma State is a matchup of a 4-7 team that keeps losing close and a 7-4 team that keeps starting slow. The Jayhawks have dropped four one-score games, the last one a two-point loss to BYU in triple overtime. The Cowboys have trailed or been tied at halftime in seven of their eight Big 12 games. My Kansas vs Oklahoma State pick is Kansas Jayhawks +6 with the Under 64.5.

Dusty Ridgeway has heard enough about close losses. "Close only counts in horseshoes, Fitz. You're 4-7. We're going to a bowl."

Fitz Calloway has the scar tissue and the receipts. "We've won two of the last three in this series, Dusty. Last year it was 38-21. Your guys like to wake up around halftime. We'll be up by then."

That's the side. Kansas lost by seven to Arizona State, six at TCU, one to UCF and two to BYU. The Jayhawks don't get blown out much. Isaiah Marshall leads them in rushing with 782 yards and in passing with 2,054. Oklahoma State won 30-27 in overtime at Arizona State last week after coming from behind, which is exactly the kind of game a seven-point spread doesn't love.

The Under comes from the Kansas side of the ledger. Not one of Kansas's eleven games this season reached 66 points. The Jayhawks allow 24.4 a game, and their run defense allows 4.0 a carry against an Oklahoma State offense that runs for 5.1. Leroy Harris III has 8.5 sacks and will be in Drew Mestemaker's face all night.

Mestemaker is the reason anyone takes the Over. He has 3,533 yards, 30 touchdowns and 11 interceptions, and Oklahoma State scores 41.1 a game. Caleb Hawkins has 1,270 rushing yards. Justin Bowick has 963 receiving yards and 10 scores. But Mestemaker threw no touchdowns at Arizona State, and Texas Tech held the Cowboys to 27 the week before.

DraftKings opened Oklahoma State Cowboys −6.5 with a 67.5 total. As of 6:31 AM ET Saturday it's Oklahoma State Cowboys −6 and 64.5. The side has come down half a point, and the total has dropped three points.

The room splits on the side and the total. The Formula has Oklahoma State 31-26 and takes Kansas +6 with the Under 64.5. Next Man Up has 38-30 and takes Oklahoma State −6 with the Over 64.5. Letdown Watch has Kansas 30-29 and takes Kansas +6 with the Under 64.5. In The Trenches has no pick in this game. Follow The Money has no side pick and takes the Under 64.5.

Next Man Up: "Letdown Watch has Kansas winning in Stillwater. Has anybody told him what year it is?"

Letdown Watch: "It's the year the Cowboys can't play a first half. Check the tape before you check the calendar."

My final is Oklahoma State Cowboys 34, Kansas Jayhawks 29. Oklahoma State wins by five, inside the six, and 63 combined stays under the total. The Cowboys find their legs in the second half again, but the Jayhawks make it close one more time.

Oklahoma State Cowboys 34, Kansas Jayhawks 29. Kansas Jayhawks cover the 6. Under 64.5.
