Troy Trojans vs Arkansas State Red Wolves: One Last Howl in Jonesboro

Troy Trojans (4-7) at Arkansas State Red Wolves (4-7)

3:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Centennial Bank Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Somebody else will coach Arkansas State next fall, and the Red Wolves found that out a week before their last game. The school announced last Tuesday that Butch Jones won't be back in 2027, the change took effect right away, and Jalen Ortiz, the associate head coach who runs the cornerbacks, has the whole program for one Saturday against Troy. Troy Trojans vs Arkansas State Red Wolves is the season finale for both teams, and Ty Doucet-Harmon has been sitting in the duck blind behind his desk since Tuesday afternoon.

"Six years," Ty says, and gives one long, low duck call. "Three bowl trips in a row. Two bowl wins. Thirty wins. That's a man who did right by this town, and now we send him out with Fan Appreciation Day."

Marlo Pickens doesn't wait long. "Ty, I'm sorry about your coach. I mean that. But Troy didn't drive up here to be a going-away party. We came to crack some shells."

Here's the thing for Red Wolves fans. Jones won the 68 Ventures Bowl in 2024 and the Xbox Bowl last year, and this season fell apart anyway. Arkansas State is 4-7 and 2-5 in the Sun Belt. It's lost three in a row. The 19-17 loss at home to UL Monroe still hurts, the 28-21 loss at Coastal Carolina hurt, and then last week in Ruston was a 48-3 beating at Louisiana Tech that nobody in Jonesboro wants to talk about.

"We don't talk about Ruston," Ty says.

"I'll talk about Ruston," Marlo says.

"Then I'll talk about Statesboro."

Fair enough. Troy got crushed too, 45-7 at home by Georgia Southern, so neither one of these fan bases gets to walk in with its chest out. Troy is also 4-7 and 2-5 in conference. It's dropped three straight too, 41-17 to Louisiana Tech, 27-14 at South Alabama and then the Georgia Southern game.

"See, we're even," Marlo says. "Same record, same mood, same long week. It's a peanut pile, and you got to dig to the bottom."

Ty's case is the quarterback. Trey Owens has thrown for 2,559 yards and 12 touchdowns this year, and on a good night he's a problem. He threw for 418 yards and four touchdowns against South Alabama in a 56-49 shootout and threw for 325 at Memphis in the opener. Jaylen Bonelli has 43 catches for 612 yards, and Joe Drew has 103 tackles from his safety spot, which tells you how often the ball has gotten past the front seven.

"Owens throws it thirty times, and Bonelli catches it everywhere," Ty says. "That's mallards coming in over the decoys. You just wait and let them land."

Marlo's case is the stuff that doesn't show up on a highlight. Troy won 24-21 over Sam Houston in September and won 24-21 in overtime at James Madison in late October, and the Trojans have a habit of making a game messy and staying in it. Goose Crowder has 1,527 yards and 11 touchdown throws, and he was nearly perfect against Southern Miss, 18 of 21 for 225 yards in a 55-34 win. Donnie Smith has 9 sacks, and Keno Jones has 85 tackles in the middle.

"Donnie Smith is the peanut butter," Marlo says. "He's in everything. You can't get him off the knife."

The series history doesn't settle the argument either. Since 2016 these two have played eight times and split them four apiece. Troy won 48-19 in 2022 and 37-3 in 2023. Arkansas State answered with a 34-31 win in 2024 and a 23-10 win last year.

Saturday is also Senior Day in Jonesboro, so the Red Wolves seniors get their walk before the 2 p.m. local kickoff at Centennial Bank Stadium. The school is calling it Fan Appreciation Day, with concession specials for the crowd and a holiday food drive for the A-State Food Bank, and Ty is bringing two bags of canned goods.

There's also a real football reason to think the home team shows up. Players playing for an interim coach in the final game usually play hard for him, and Ortiz knows the secondary better than anybody on that staff. If he can get the Red Wolves defense to tackle in space for four quarters, Owens has enough to win a close one. Arkansas State is 3-2 at home and 1-5 on the road, and this is the right building for a last stand.

Troy will run Gavin Griffin, who has 512 yards on the ground, and try to keep Owens on the sideline. If Crowder takes care of the ball, Troy hangs around all afternoon. If he throws one up for grabs late, Joe Drew will be there.

Here's how I see it. Owens gets his yards. Crowder makes it close. The seniors get one last win in front of the home folks, the food bank gets its cans, and Ty blows that duck call so hard the press box hears it.

Marlo cracks a peanut, shakes his head and says, "Close enough to taste."

My call: Arkansas State Red Wolves 26, Troy Trojans 23.
