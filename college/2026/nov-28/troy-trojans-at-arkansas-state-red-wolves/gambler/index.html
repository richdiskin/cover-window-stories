Troy vs Arkansas State Pick: Trojans +4 and the Over 46.5

Troy Trojans (4-7) at Arkansas State Red Wolves (4-7)

3:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Centennial Bank Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Arkansas State announced last Tuesday that Butch Jones won't be back, put associate head coach Jalen Ortiz in charge for the rest of the year, and now plays its final game against a Troy team that's lost three straight just like the Red Wolves have. My Troy vs Arkansas State pick is Troy Trojans +4 and the Over 46.5, because I don't trust a home team in a coaching search to pull away from anybody.

Marlo Pickens has the bag open already. "Two 4-7 teams, one shell to crack. Troy's not shelling out four points to anybody. That's just nuts."

Ty Doucet-Harmon blows a duck call right in his ear. "Interim coach, Senior Day, Fan Appreciation Day. Every bird in Jonesboro is coming in low for this one, Marlo."

Both teams are 4-7 and 2-5 in the Sun Belt, and both got blown out last week. Troy lost 45-7 to Georgia Southern at home. Arkansas State lost 48-3 at Louisiana Tech. Neither one is going to a bowl, so this is about pride and about what the film looks like in December.

The Red Wolves have the better passer. Trey Owens has 2,559 yards with 12 touchdowns and 10 interceptions, and he threw for 418 against South Alabama in a 56-49 loss. Jaylen Bonelli leads them with 612 receiving yards. The problem is the defense. Arkansas State gives up 28.5 a game, and it lost 19-17 at home to UL Monroe and 28-21 at Coastal Carolina before the mess in Ruston.

Troy scores only 20.2 and gives up 28.0, and Goose Crowder has 11 touchdown throws and 6 interceptions on 1,527 yards. But Troy has been in plenty of tight games. It beat Sam Houston 24-21 and won 24-21 in overtime at James Madison, and the Trojans have shown they can hang around when the game stays ugly.

The series is dead even. Since 2016 these two have split eight meetings, 4-4, and Arkansas State won 34-31 in 2024 and 23-10 last year.

My margin is three. That keeps Troy inside the 4 with a little room.

On the total, I have 49. Arkansas State scores 24.6 a game and gives up 28.5, which adds up past 46.5 by itself, and Owens will throw it 30-plus times. DraftKings opened Arkansas State Red Wolves −3.5 with a 46.5 total, and the side has moved half a point to −4 while the total hasn't budged.

The room splits on the side. The Formula has Arkansas State 27-24, takes Troy +4 and likes the Over 46.5. Next Man Up has Arkansas State 26-20 and goes Arkansas State −4 and the Under. Letdown Watch has Arkansas State 26-23, takes Troy +4 and likes the Over. In The Trenches has Troy 26-25 and likes Troy +4, with no total. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Next Man Up: "Interim coaches win the first one. Everybody knows that, Letdown Watch."

Letdown Watch: "Everybody knows a 48-3 loss doesn't wash off in a week. Look it up."

My final is Arkansas State Red Wolves 26, Troy Trojans 23. Ty gets his win. Marlo gets his four points and calls it peanuts.

Arkansas State Red Wolves 26, Troy Trojans 23. Troy Trojans cover the 4. Over 46.5.
