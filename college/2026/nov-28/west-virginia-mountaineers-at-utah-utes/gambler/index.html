West Virginia vs Utah Pick: Utes −22.5 and the Under 68.5

West Virginia Mountaineers (5-6) at Utah Utes (11-0)

9:00 PM ET Fri Nov 27 · Rice-Eccles Stadium · Fox

XFacebook

After back-to-back years spent on the road the day after Thanksgiving, Utah finally gets a Black Friday game at Rice-Eccles Stadium, and it's Senior Night for an 11-0 team ranked No. 2. My West Virginia vs Utah prediction lays the big spread with Utah Utes −22.5 and takes the Under 68.5 on the over/under.

Aspen Mortensen has been waiting for this. "Home on Black Friday, unbeaten, seniors on the field. Two Thanksgivings I spent watching this game from somebody else's stadium."

Jolene Buckhannon hums a few notes of Country Roads. "Enjoy the ceremony, Aspen. We've got a few folks who'd like to ruin it."

They'll need more than a few. West Virginia allows 34.7 points a game and has given up 37 or more six times: 41 to Oklahoma State, 45 at Iowa State, 52 to Arizona, 42 to Cincinnati, 53 at Texas Tech and 37 to Kansas. Utah scored 48 on this defense last September. The best comparison is Kansas: the Utes beat the Jayhawks 41-24 in this building, and the Jayhawks went to Morgantown and won 37-14.

Devon Dampier runs the Utah offense with 24 touchdown throws against 8 interceptions, and he threw for 278 yards and ran for two scores at TCU last week. Wayshawn Parker and Braden Pegan each have 9 touchdowns. The Utes score 37.9 a game. West Virginia has dropped three in a row, 48-53, 14-37 and 31-34, and needs a sixth win to save its season.

The market has climbed toward the Utes. DraftKings opened Utah −20.5, per ESPN. It now reads Utah Utes −22.5. Utah Utes 44, West Virginia Mountaineers 20 is a 24-point win, a point and a half past it.

The Under rests on Utah's defense at home. The Utes allow 18.5 a game, and four of six visitors to Rice-Eccles scored 14 or fewer. Only the 66-14 opener against Idaho topped this total. West Virginia runs through Cam Cook, who has 243 carries for 1,215 yards, and a ground game keeps the clock moving.

This room is all over the place. Letdown Watch is closest to me at 45-16, with Utah Utes −22.5 and the Under 68.5. Next Man Up also takes Utah, at 49-24, but likes the Over. The Formula goes the other way on the side, 38-21 with West Virginia Mountaineers +22.5, and joins me on the Under. In The Trenches calls the fronts split, has no score this week, and takes no side or total. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

The Formula: "Utah beat Houston by eight and BYU by eight in this stadium. Twenty-three is a lot of points to give."

Letdown Watch: "West Virginia lost by 23 at home to Kansas. A Senior Night crowd and a team that's dropped three straight. That isn't a trap. That's a send-off."

My number is Utah by 24 with 64 points, four and a half under. The seniors get their night, and the Mountaineers get a quiet flight east.

Utah Utes 44, West Virginia Mountaineers 20. Utah Utes cover the 22.5. Under 68.5.
