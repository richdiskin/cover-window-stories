UCF Knights vs Colorado Buffaloes: A First Trip to Boulder

UCF Knights (6-5) at Colorado Buffaloes (4-7)

2:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Folsom Field · TNT

XFacebook

UCF Knights vs Colorado Buffaloes is the first football game UCF has ever played in Boulder, and it falls on November 28, the same date that Scott Frost, now the Knights' coach, ran in a touchdown as Nebraska's quarterback here 29 years ago. Knox Brevard-Stiles is treating the whole trip like the opening day of a new ride, while Moss Feldstein, who has watched every visitor show up at Folsom Field gasping for air, is waiting with a thermos of hot chocolate and a grin.

"First time in Boulder," Knox says, staring up at the Flatirons. "Look at that. That's a postcard. That's a screensaver."

"That's a mile of altitude," Moss says. "Enjoy the view in the first quarter. In the third quarter you're going to be looking at the ground."

"I'm from Orlando, Moss. I've waited two hours for a roller coaster in August. I can handle a little less oxygen."

That isn't the kind of brag Moss is used to hearing, and he takes a second to respect it. Then he goes right back to work, because he has a home game to defend. Colorado is 4-7 and the bowl dream died last week in a 35-28 loss at Cincinnati, but this is still the Buffaloes' last game of the season, and nobody in Boulder wants to send the team into winter on a losing note.

"We're playing for pride," Moss says. "Pride is a powerful thing at altitude. It's lighter up here, so you can carry more of it."

Knox has more than pride on his side. UCF is 6-5 after a 48-17 thumping of Iowa State last week, which makes the Knights bowl eligible. Alonza Barnett III threw four touchdowns in that game, three of them to Josh Derry, and Duke Watson ran for 113 yards. Derry has 833 receiving yards on the year. Tackett Curtis leads the defense with 69 tackles, and Isaiah Nixon brings the pressure off the end. UCF also knocked off BYU in double overtime back in October, a win Knox has framed in his living room.

"We beat a top-two team," Knox says. "Twice overtime. My heart rate still hasn't recovered."

"And then you lost to Baylor by thirty-two," Moss says.

"We don't ride that ride anymore. It's closed for maintenance."

Moss's best case is his top receiver. Joseph Williams has 67 catches for 1,045 yards and eight touchdowns, and he went for 112 yards against Cincinnati. Isaac Wilson threw for 312 yards in that game, though he also threw three interceptions, and Moss admits the turnovers keep him up at night. Wilson has 12 touchdown throws and 12 interceptions. JaQuail Smith leads the backfield.

"Isaac is a gunslinger," Moss says. "Sometimes the gun slings back."

The two programs have met once, in Orlando two years ago, and Colorado won that one 48-21. Moss reminds Knox about it twice before kickoff. Knox reminds Moss that UCF has a different quarterback, a different coach and, as of last week, a bowl game to look forward to.

There's also the Frost connection, which Moss finds a little personal. Twenty-nine years ago today, the man on UCF's sideline ran 19 yards for a touchdown in a 27-24 Nebraska win on this field. In 2019, as Nebraska's coach, he watched Colorado come back from 17 points down and win in overtime.

"So he's one and one in Boulder," Moss says. "Today's the rubber match."

"Rubber match," Knox says. "I like the sound of that."

Here's how Saturday goes. Colorado comes out fired up, and Wilson finds Williams deep for a touchdown on the first possession, sending Folsom Field into a frenzy. UCF answers with Watson grinding up the middle, and Barnett hits Derry on a long one before halftime. The teams trade scores in the third quarter, and Knox starts breathing a little harder, though he swears it's nerves and not the altitude. Late in the fourth, with the score tied, Wilson tries to force one more throw, and the Knights intercept it near midfield. Barnett moves UCF into range, and the Knights kick the go-ahead field goal. Colorado gets one last shot and comes up just short.

Knox will take one last photo of the Flatirons on the way out. Moss will tell him to come back in the spring, when the air is easier and the Buffaloes are, too.

My call: UCF Knights 26, Colorado Buffaloes 25.
