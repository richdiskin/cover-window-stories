Nevada vs UNLV Pick: Wolf Pack +5.5, Over 44.5

Nevada Wolf Pack (5-6) at UNLV Rebels (7-4)

9:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Allegiant Stadium · CBSSN

XFacebook

Nevada vs UNLV has the Fremont Cannon on the line, but the Wolf Pack are playing for something bigger. Nevada is 5-6, and a win gets it to six and its first bowl trip in five years. UNLV is 7-4 and already going bowling. My Nevada vs UNLV pick is Nevada Wolf Pack +5.5 with the Over 44.5.

Ridge Corbell drove down from Reno with a can of blue paint in the trunk. "Just in case. You never know when a cannon needs touching up."

Monroe-Kalani hasn't even looked at him. "That cannon's been red for four years, and it's staying red. The paint stays in the trunk."

The side comes down to how close UNLV has been. The Rebels beat Northern Illinois by seven, won at New Mexico by four and won at San José State by two. They lost at home to Wyoming. They don't blow teams away. Nevada, meanwhile, has won two straight, and its defense allows 20.6 points a game. A team playing for its first bowl in five years doesn't need to win this one big. It needs to hang in.

In The Trenches likes the Pack's ground game here. UNLV allows 5.0 yards a carry, and Herschel Turner has 746 rushing yards and six touchdowns. Nevada runs for 4.5 a carry, and the Pack completed just six passes in a 28-7 win at Northern Illinois. They know who they are.

The Over rides on UNLV. Eight of UNLV's eleven games went over this total. The Rebels score 25.5 a game and allow 24.5. Jackson Arnold has 18 touchdown throws and seven picks, and Kayden McGee gets over twenty yards a catch, with 568 yards on 25 grabs. Jai'Den Thomas has 817 rushing yards. Nevada can't throw much, with AJ Bianco at four touchdowns and four picks, but it doesn't need many points to help get this over.

DraftKings opened UNLV Rebels −4.5 with a 45.5 total. As of 6:31 AM ET Saturday it's UNLV Rebels −5.5 and 44.5. Our card uses five and a half on the side.

The room splits on the side and the total. The Formula has UNLV 26-21 and takes Nevada +5.5 with the Over 44.5. Next Man Up has 23-18 and takes Nevada +5.5 with the Under 44.5. Letdown Watch has 27-20 and takes UNLV −5.5 with the Over 44.5. In The Trenches has Nevada 27-26 and takes Nevada +5.5, with no over/under call. Follow The Money has no pick.

Letdown Watch: "Trenches has Nevada winning. A team that completed six passes in a game. Six."

In The Trenches: "And won by three touchdowns. You want to complete a bunch of passes, go watch the Rebels lose to Wyoming."

My final is UNLV Rebels 25, Nevada Wolf Pack 21. UNLV wins by four, inside the five and a half, and 46 combined gets over the total by a point and a half. The cannon stays red for another year, but the Pack make Monroe-Kalani sweat for it.

UNLV Rebels 25, Nevada Wolf Pack 21. Nevada Wolf Pack cover the 5.5. Over 44.5.
