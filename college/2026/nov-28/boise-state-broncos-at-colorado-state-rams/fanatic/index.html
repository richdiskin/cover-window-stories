Boise State Broncos vs Colorado State Rams: Fort Collins Flex

Boise State Broncos (9-2) at Colorado State Rams (3-8)

6:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Canvas Stadium · USA Net

XFacebook

Colorado State and Boise State already played this season, three weeks ago, in this same stadium, and the Broncos won 64-38. Now the Pac-12's first-ever Flex Week sends them back to Canvas Stadium for a Saturday 4 p.m. kickoff that doesn't count in the conference race, and Ansel Rimrock is treating Boise State Broncos vs Colorado State Rams like a second chance at a first impression.

"Rematch," Ansel says, zipping his jacket up to his chin. "Nobody gets a rematch in November. We got one. That's a gift."

Thatcher Bluestem drove down from Boise with two thermoses and a very calm look on his face.

"It's not a gift, Ansel. It's a scheduling quirk. We play San Diego State for the title on December fourth. This is a tune-up."

"A tune-up? You scored 64 here."

"That's what a tune-up looks like when it goes well."

Ansel doesn't love that answer, but he can't argue the numbers. Boise State's 64 points in that first game were the most the Broncos had scored in a road game since 1952, and they piled up 669 yards. Max Cutforth threw for 317 yards and four touchdowns that day, and Thatcher has a photo of the scoreboard he's been using as his computer background.

"Change it," Ansel says.

"Never."

The Broncos are 9-2, but they lost 22-20 to San Diego State last Saturday on Senior Night, losing it in the final seconds, and Thatcher is still a little sore about it. He spent the drive down replaying the last minute in his head and arguing with it. Dylan Riley has 1,360 rushing yards and 11 touchdowns. Maddux Madsen has 20 touchdown passes against just three interceptions, and Rasean Jones leads the team with 940 receiving yards.

"We lost by two to the team we play next," Thatcher says. "So I'm not worried. I'm just annoyed."

Ansel's Rams are 3-8, and he's not going to pretend this has been a fun fall. They gave up 73 at Washington State two weeks ago. They lost 49-42 at Fresno State last week. But Hauss Hejny threw for 413 yards in that Fresno game and ran for two touchdowns, and Tommy Maher had 111 receiving yards. Oumar Diomande has made 103 tackles, which Ansel says is because he's the only guy who never gets tired.

"Our offense is fine," Ansel says. "Our defense is a work in progress."

"Your defense gave up 64 to us."

"Like I said. Progress."

Colorado State has scored 35 or more six times this year, including the 56-49 win over San Diego State in October. That's the game Ansel brings up whenever anyone from Boise is in earshot.

"We beat the team that beat you," he says.

"And we beat you by 26."

"Football's weird, Thatcher."

Here's how it goes Saturday. Boise State comes out with Riley carrying it over and over, because nobody wants to get hurt a week before the title game. The Broncos take a two-score lead, but Hejny keeps throwing, and Maher catches a long one to keep the Rams in it at halftime. In the second half, Boise turns the game into a long run of handoffs and short throws, and the clock moves fast. Colorado State scores late to make the final look respectable, and Ansel celebrates the touchdown like it's the Rams who just clinched a trip somewhere.

Thatcher will shake his hand and tell him the Rams looked better than last time. Ansel will say it couldn't have looked worse, and then he'll ask Thatcher to bring the second thermos back next year, full this time, because a rematch deserves a rematch.

My call: Boise State Broncos 45, Colorado State Rams 26.
