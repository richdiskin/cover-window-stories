Texas vs Texas A&M Pick: Aggies +10.5 and the Over 40.5

Texas Longhorns (9-2) at Texas A&M Aggies (4-7)

7:30 PM ET Fri Nov 27 · Kyle Field · ABC

XFacebook

Friday night is the Maroon Out Game at Kyle Field, the student-led tradition born the night a sea of maroon shirts helped Texas A&M upset second-ranked Nebraska. My Texas vs Texas A&M pick is Texas A&M Aggies +10.5 and the Over 40.5: the No. 5 Longhorns win the rivalry game, but 10.5 points is too many to lay on the road against a full house of maroon.

Caro Whitfield has priced the Aggie roster and isn't impressed. "Four and seven, no bowl, and three different quarterbacks on the board. I'm pricing that offense at a nickel."

Colt Wendeborn answers by explaining the tradition, then explaining why it matters. "Maroon Out started with an upset of the No. 2 team in the country. We don't wear the shirts for decoration, Caro. We wear them because it worked."

Texas has every reason to play hard. A Texas loss ends its SEC title-game hopes in every scenario, and a win sends the Longhorns to Atlanta unless four other games break against them. Arch Manning has 2,485 yards, 21 touchdowns and 10 interceptions, and he went 21 of 25 for 287 yards in last week's 45-3 win over Arkansas. Colin Simmons, listed questionable, has 10.5 sacks for a defense that gives up 16.5 points a game.

So why take the Aggies? Because Texas doesn't blow teams out on the road in this conference. The Longhorns won 20-17 at Tennessee, 34-20 at Missouri and 22-21 at LSU. Their two losses were both by three. Steve Sarkisian's team is very good and still plays close games, and a rivalry night in College Station is where they happen.

The Texas A&M side of it is ugly, I'll admit. The Aggies have lost four straight, scored 9, 7 and 7 points in their last three, and Mike Elko hasn't said whether Marcel Reed, Brady Hart or Helaman Casuga takes the first snap. Hart went 8 of 20 for 131 yards at Oklahoma. Reed has 1,712 yards with 8 touchdowns and 9 interceptions. I'm pricing this as a backup-quarterback game either way, and it still comes out inside the number because the A&M defense, allowing 19.8 points a game, keeps it from getting silly.

The Over is about Texas. The Longhorns score 31.5 a game, and I have them at 26 by themselves, which leaves the Aggies needing 15 to get us there. I have 44. DraftKings opened Texas Longhorns −2.5 with a 53.5 total, and as of 6:13 AM ET Friday it sits at −10.5 and 40.5.

The scoring cards split two and two. The Formula's 29-18 Texas win clears the 10.5, so it moves to Texas Longhorns −10.5 and likes the Over 40.5. Letdown Watch is with it at 29-16, takes the Longhorns and likes the Over. Next Man Up has Texas 23-15, likes the Aggies and likes the Under. In The Trenches has A&M winning 24-22, likes the Aggies and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money has no pick.

Letdown Watch: "In The Trenches has A&M winning outright. Somebody take the maroon shirt off that card."

In The Trenches: "You've never seen a Thanksgiving weekend you didn't think was a letdown, Letdown Watch."

My final is Texas Longhorns 26, Texas A&M Aggies 18. Caro calls it as forecast.

Texas Longhorns 26, Texas A&M Aggies 18. Texas A&M Aggies cover the 10.5. Over 40.5.
