Texas Longhorns vs Texas A&M Aggies: Maroon Out at Kyle Field

Texas Longhorns (9-2) at Texas A&M Aggies (4-7)

7:30 PM ET Fri Nov 27 · Kyle Field · ABC

XFacebook

Colt Wendeborn will be wearing maroon on Friday night, and so will most of the people around him, because Texas A&M has made its rivalry game with Texas the annual Maroon Out Game at Kyle Field. Texas Longhorns vs Texas A&M Aggies is a 4-7 team with no bowl to play for against a No. 5 team with Atlanta in its sights, and Caro Whitfield has flown in from Austin in burnt orange specifically to stand out.

"Every year somebody tells me about the shirts," Caro says. "I have never once lost a football game to a shirt."

"Let me explain the shirts," Colt says, because he always explains. "Nineteen ninety-eight. Nebraska comes in ranked second in the country. Tens of thousands of Aggies show up in maroon to wash out the red. A&M wins 28-21. Afterward, the Daily Nebraskan wrote that the maroon-out 'proved to be lights out for Nebraska.' Their own paper said it."

"Great story," Caro says. "Wrong decade."

She has a point about the decade. This has been a hard year in College Station. The Aggies have lost four in a row, scored seven points in each of the last two games, and been outscored 58-0 in the fourth quarter over their last four. Mike Elko hasn't said who plays quarterback on Friday. It could be Marcel Reed, who has taken most of the snaps this year, or Brady Hart, who got the call at Oklahoma, or Helaman Casuga. Colt has quit guessing and gone to praying.

"Whoever it is, Mario Craver is the guy," Colt says. "He's played through everything this year. Get him the ball and get out of the way."

Caro, meanwhile, has done her pricing. She values the Texas roster in what she calls Caro Bucks, and this week she has every number in her head. Arch Manning has 2,485 yards and 21 touchdown throws, and he was close to perfect against Arkansas last week, 21 of 25 for 287 yards in a 45-3 win. Ryan Wingo has 659 receiving yards. Emmett Mosley V caught eight balls for 106 yards against the Razorbacks. And the defense is the kind that makes a quarterback controversy worse: Colin Simmons has 10.5 sacks, Jelani McDonald has 84 tackles, and the Longhorns give up 16.5 points a game.

"Here's the math," Caro says. "We win, we're going to Atlanta unless four other games all go wrong on Saturday. We lose, we're done. I don't need a shirt. I need a defense, and I have one."

That is the whole reason Colt thinks Friday is interesting. Texas has to win, and Texas has spent all year winning close. The Longhorns beat Tennessee by three in Knoxville, LSU by one in Baton Rouge, and Ohio State by one at home. Both of their losses came by three points. This is a great team that hasn't made a habit of running anybody off the field on the road.

"We don't need to be better than you for four quarters," Colt says. "We need to be better than you for about three, and then not do what we've done in every fourth quarter this month."

"That's a big 'then,'" Caro says.

The Aggie defense is why this has a chance to stay tight. Ray Coney leads the team with 73 tackles, and the group has held most teams in check even while the offense sputtered. They gave up 22 to Tennessee and 17 to South Carolina, and they'll have Kyle Field screaming behind them on every third down.

Colt does what Colt does. He stands up, explains the next yell to the visitors' section, and then does it at full volume anyway.

Here's how Friday goes. The place is a wall of maroon at kickoff, and the Aggies play like it. Texas moves the ball and settles for field goals early while A&M's defense gets two big stops and the crowd gets louder than it's been all year. Then Manning finds Wingo in the third quarter, Simmons gets home on a third down, and the Longhorns grind out the rest of it. A&M scores late to make Caro put her phone away for a minute, but Texas punches its ticket toward Atlanta.

Caro raises one eyebrow on the way out and says "As forecast," to nobody in particular. Colt keeps yelling at the empty field for ten more minutes, because the shirt deserved better.

My call: Texas Longhorns 26, Texas A&M Aggies 18.
