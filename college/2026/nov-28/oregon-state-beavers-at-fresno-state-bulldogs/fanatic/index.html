Oregon State Beavers vs Fresno State Bulldogs: Flex Week Rematch

Oregon State Beavers (6-5) at Fresno State Bulldogs (6-5)

10:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Valley Children's Stadium · CW

XFacebook

Oregon State and Fresno State have already played once this fall, on Halloween, and Fresno State won 38-22 at Valley Children's Stadium. This time it's the conference's brand-new Flex Week that brings them back to the same field for a 7 p.m. kickoff on The CW, a nonconference game with both teams sitting at 6-5 and both trying to get to seven wins. Oregon State Beavers vs Fresno State Bulldogs, take two.

Juniper Ostrowski drove down from Corvallis in a beat-up hatchback and calls this trip "the rematch nobody asked for but everybody needed." Roz Ayala, who has lived in Fresno her whole life and owns more red than any person should, calls it "a second helping."

"A second helping of what?" Juniper asks.

"Of us beating you."

"We were a different team on Halloween."

"You were dressed up as a team that loses by sixteen."

Juniper takes that one on the chin and moves on. The Beavers are bowl eligible after beating Utah State 31-24 last week, when Reichle scored three touchdowns. Maalik Murphy has thrown the most passes in the last two games, including 396 yards and three touchdowns in a 46-41 loss at Boise State that Juniper still thinks they should've won. Jesse Legree is the headline, though, with 1,418 receiving yards and 15 touchdowns.

"Fifteen touchdowns," Juniper says. "Fifteen. He catches everything. He'd catch your car keys if you threw them from the top row."

"Don't give me ideas."

"You'd miss. He wouldn't."

Roz shakes her head, but she's smiling. Fresno State has been through it this year, too. The Bulldogs lost 39-0 at USC to open the season, then dropped close ones to Boise State, San Diego State, Utah State and Texas State, all by a touchdown or less. Last week they beat Colorado State 49-42, and Jayden Mandal was nearly perfect, going 15 for 17 for 191 yards and three scores. Bryson Donelson ran for 165 yards, and Josiah Freeman caught two touchdowns.

"Mandal threw two incompletions," Roz says. "Two. One of them was on purpose."

"How do you throw an incompletion on purpose?"

"You throw it away, Juniper. It's called being smart."

On defense, Fresno has K'vion Thunderbird with 84 tackles and Dylan Hampsten with six sacks. Oregon State counters with Takari Hickle, who has 7.5 sacks, and Aiden Sullivan, who has 89 tackles. Both teams have given up big numbers in the past few weeks, and Roz says that's because both defenses have been "busy."

"Busy doing what?" Juniper asks.

"Chasing people."

The two teams also played last September, and Fresno State won that one 36-27. Juniper reminds Roz that Oregon State won the meeting before that, 35-32 in 2022. Roz reminds Juniper that 2022 was a long time ago.

Here's how it goes. The stadium is loud for a night game, the Fresno crowd is in red, and the Bulldogs come out running Donelson right at the Beaver defense. Oregon State answers with Murphy finding Legree for a long touchdown, and it's tight at halftime. The second half goes back and forth, with Mandal hitting Freeman for a score and Legree catching another. Late in the fourth, Fresno kicks a field goal to go up two, and Oregon State gets the ball back with a minute left. Hickle has already sacked Mandal twice, and Roz has been yelling at the refs for most of the quarter, but the Beavers run out of clock in Fresno territory.

Roz will hand Juniper a Bulldogs foam finger on the way out as a souvenir. Juniper will toss it in the back seat, and it will still be there in March.

My call: Fresno State Bulldogs 29, Oregon State Beavers 27.
