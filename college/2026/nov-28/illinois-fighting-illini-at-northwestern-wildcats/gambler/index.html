Illinois vs Northwestern Pick: Wildcats −3.5 and the Under 48.5

Illinois Fighting Illini (6-5) at Northwestern Wildcats (6-5)

12:30 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Ryan Field · BTN

XFacebook

Illinois has never played at Northwestern's new Ryan Field, and its first trip there on Saturday comes with the Land of Lincoln Trophy and Northwestern's Senior Day on the line. My Illinois vs Northwestern pick is Northwestern Wildcats −3.5 and the Under 48.5, because the market has already swung from one side to the other and I think it swung the right way.

Ezra Lindqvist has prepared remarks, with footnotes. "Four straight losses, yes. Footnote one: two came down to one score. Footnote two: we're 5-1 at home."

Margery Vandermolen brought her slide rule and a correction. "Ezra, we've won two straight in this series. We'd like a third, and we'd like it in your new house."

Margery has the trophy and the receiver. Collin Dixon has 80 catches for 1,088 yards and 13 touchdowns, and he caught eight for 142 against Iowa last week. Katin Houser has 2,449 yards and 20 touchdowns, though the Big Ten availability report lists him out. He also has 10 interceptions, and five of them came in the last two games, losses to UCLA and Iowa by a combined 25 points.

Northwestern has its own question at quarterback. Nicco Marchiol has taken the most throws in each of the last three games, and he went 13 of 23 for 169 yards with a touchdown at Minnesota while tying the game with a late run. They lost on a walk-off field goal, but the ground game showed up: Caleb Komolafe ran for 109 yards on 19 carries in that one and has 806 on the year. Griffin Wilde has 920 receiving yards. Braydon Brus leads the defense with 86 tackles and 4 sacks, and the Wildcats allow 20.2 points a game.

Why Northwestern? Because Komolafe can run on this Illinois defense and Houser has been throwing the ball to the other team. Senior Day, a new stadium, a four-game slide and a rivalry trophy all point the same way. My margin is seven, double the number.

The Under follows. Northwestern's last three games produced 34, 47 and 37 combined points, and a team running Komolafe 20-plus times while protecting Marchiol shortens the game. I have 47, a point and a half under. DraftKings opened Illinois Fighting Illini −3.5 with a 52.5 total. As of 6:31 AM ET Saturday it's Northwestern Wildcats −3.5 and 48.5.

The room is together on the side and split on the total. The Formula has Northwestern 28-21 and takes Northwestern −3.5 with the Over 48.5. Next Man Up has 29-18 and takes Northwestern −3.5 with the Under 48.5. Letdown Watch has 28-20 and takes Northwestern −3.5 with the Under 48.5. In The Trenches has Northwestern 28-26 and takes Illinois +3.5, with no over/under call. Follow The Money has no side pick and takes the Under 48.5.

Next Man Up: "In The Trenches, you're the only one on the Illini. Even the trophy's packing for Evanston."

In The Trenches: "I've watched Illinois win the line of scrimmage two years running. Call me when you've watched a lineman."

My final is Northwestern Wildcats 27, Illinois Fighting Illini 20. Ezra publishes a correction. Margery recalculates on the drive home.

Northwestern Wildcats 27, Illinois Fighting Illini 20. Northwestern Wildcats cover the 3.5. Under 48.5.
