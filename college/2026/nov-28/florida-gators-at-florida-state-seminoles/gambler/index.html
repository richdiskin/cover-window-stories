Florida vs Florida State Pick: Seminoles +6.5, Over 53.5

Florida Gators (7-4) at Florida State Seminoles (5-6)

3:30 PM ET Fri Nov 27 · Doak Campbell Stadium · ABC

XFacebook

Florida State's whole season comes down to Friday afternoon at Doak Campbell Stadium: it's Senior Day, the Seminoles are 5-6, and only a win over No. 24 Florida makes them bowl-eligible. My Florida vs Florida State pick is Florida State Seminoles +6.5 and the Over 53.5, with the Gators winning the game but not by enough to clear the number.

Gianna Ravello has a motion on the floor. "Senior Day, a bowl on the table, and an interim coach who wants one more home win before the transition. I move that the Seminoles keep this within a field goal, and I have the votes."

Dax Marchetti answers like he's narrating a nature film. "Observe the Gator in late November. Patient. Rested. Winner of its last two. It does not care about your motion."

Interim coach Rick Stockstill has a coaching transition coming after the season, and he said Monday that Ashton Daniels stays his quarterback. Daniels is the reason this game stays close and the reason it scores. He has 2,515 yards and 14 touchdowns, and he also has 14 interceptions, including two in last week's 28-10 loss to NC State. Micahi Danzy, with 584 receiving yards, is the deep threat. Stockstill also said he isn't confident his injured running backs will be back Friday, which pushes even more of this onto Daniels' arm.

Florida is 7-4 and back on track with Aaron Philo under center. The Gators beat Kentucky 21-14 and Vanderbilt 34-27, and Philo went 20 of 26 for 241 yards against the Commodores. Jadan Baugh has 980 yards and 16 rushing touchdowns, and he's the best player on the field. Last year in Gainesville, the Gators won 40-21.

So why take the points? A desperate home team on Senior Day with a bowl trip on the line doesn't fold in the second half. Florida's last two wins were one-score games, and six and a half points on the road in a rivalry is a lot to ask. My margin is three, which keeps Florida State inside the number even if the Gators make the last big play.

The Over comes from the turnover math. Daniels throws it to both teams, and Baugh turns short fields into touchdowns. Florida has gone over 53.5 in 7 of 11 games, and Florida State's defense is giving up 26.6 points a game. I have 55. DraftKings opened Florida −4.5 with a 52.5 total, and as of 6:13 AM ET Friday the Gators are up to −6.5 with the total at 53.5.

Three cards take Florida State. The Formula has Florida 29-25 with Florida State Seminoles +6.5 and the Over. Next Man Up has 30-26, takes the Seminoles and likes the Over 53.5. Letdown Watch has 27-26 with Florida State, and its 53 now goes Under 53.5. In The Trenches has no score and no pick. Follow The Money has no pick.

Next Man Up: "Three cards on the same side. Letdown Watch, you finally read the room instead of the bus schedule."

Letdown Watch: "Twenty-seven to twenty-six, Next Man Up. Mine's the one that lands."

My final is Florida Gators 29, Florida State Seminoles 26. Dax gets his chomp. Gianna files a complaint with the replay booth.

Florida Gators 29, Florida State Seminoles 26. Florida State Seminoles cover the 6.5. Over 53.5.
