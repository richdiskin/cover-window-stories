Florida Gators vs Florida State Seminoles: Six Wins or Bust

Florida Gators (7-4) at Florida State Seminoles (5-6)

3:30 PM ET Fri Nov 27 · Doak Campbell Stadium · ABC

XFacebook

Florida State's seniors walk out at Doak Campbell Stadium on Friday afternoon knowing that if they lose to the Gators, their season ends right there with no bowl game. Florida Gators vs Florida State Seminoles is Senior Day in Tallahassee, the Seminoles are sitting at five wins, and Gianna Ravello has been saying "six" out loud all week like she's trying to make it happen by repetition, while Dax Marchetti rolls up from Gainesville sounding like a man who'd love to spoil a party.

"Six," Gianna says. "Six wins, a bowl, and our first winning season in three years. Write it down. Frame it. Hang it in the Capitol."

"I've seen this before," Dax says. "The garnet-and-gold creature, cornered in its own den, makes a lot of noise. Then the Gator eats."

There's more going on in Tallahassee than a bowl chase. Rick Stockstill is the interim coach, there's a coaching transition coming after the season, and Friday is a chance for him and the staff to go out with a rivalry win in front of the home crowd. Stockstill spent his first press conference of the week telling the younger players exactly how much this game means to the people in this town, in case anybody on the roster hadn't figured it out yet.

There's a twist, too. Florida's offensive coordinator, Buster Faulkner, used to work for Stockstill at Middle Tennessee, and Stockstill called him a brilliant offensive mind on Monday. So the old boss is trying to stop the guy he helped bring up.

"That's a family reunion," Dax says. "Somebody's leaving the table hungry."

"Coach knows every trick in that playbook because he taught half of them," Gianna says. "Read the fine print, Dax."

The Gators come in hot. They've won two straight, 21-14 at Kentucky and 34-27 over Vanderbilt, and Aaron Philo looked sharp against the Commodores, going 20 of 26 for 241 yards. Jadan Baugh is the problem for any defense in the state. He has 980 yards and 16 rushing touchdowns, and he runs like he's personally offended by arm tackles. Eric Singleton Jr. caught six balls for 117 yards against Vanderbilt, and Myles Graham leads the defense with 86 tackles. Jayden Woods has 7 sacks.

"Baugh is an alligator snapping turtle," Dax says. "Slow to get going, then he takes your whole arm."

Florida State has to answer with Ashton Daniels, and Stockstill made it clear on Monday that Daniels is his guy. Daniels has 2,515 yards and 14 touchdown throws, and he's had big days: 377 yards at Alabama, 350 against Clemson, four touchdowns at Miami. He's also thrown 14 interceptions, and last week's 28-10 loss to NC State had two of them. Micahi Danzy, with 584 yards, is the guy he'll look for when it matters. Chris Jones has 85 tackles at linebacker, and Rylan Kennedy has 6 sacks up front.

"When Ashton is right, nobody in this state throws it prettier," Gianna says.

"When he's wrong, my secondary says thank you," Dax says.

The running game is where Gianna gets quiet. Stockstill said Monday it's too early to tell on his injured running backs and that he doesn't feel good about them being back for Friday. That puts a lot on Daniels and a lot on a group of senior linemen who'd love to go out pushing people around.

Last year in Gainesville, Florida won 40-21, and Dax brings it up roughly every six minutes. Gianna counters with 2022, a 45-38 Seminoles win in a shootout, and 2023, when they won again. Either way, this one has been swinging back and forth for years, and nobody on either side expects a quiet afternoon.

Here's how it goes. Doak is loud from the opening kick, Daniels hits Danzy deep in the first quarter, and the seniors play like they've been told this is their last home game, because it is. Baugh answers. Then he answers again. It's a back-and-forth game where every Seminole mistake turns into Florida points and every Florida State big play gets the place shaking. Florida gets the last real drive, Baugh grinds out the clock, and Florida State's season ends three points short with the seniors still swinging on the final snap.

Gianna sits in her seat long after the band stops, already drafting a speech about next year. Dax does a full chomp in the parking lot, then remembers where he is and does a smaller one.

My call: Florida Gators 29, Florida State Seminoles 26.
