Pitt vs Cal Pick: Panthers −9.5 and the Over 47.5

Pittsburgh Panthers (6-5) at California Golden Bears (3-8)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · California Memorial Stadium · ACC Network

XFacebook

Pitt doesn't know who its quarterback is for Saturday, and it's still laying 9.5 on the road. Holden Geriner started at Louisville and got pulled in the third quarter of a 42-10 loss, Corey Dailey started the Florida State game before that, and Mason Heintschel has been out since he tore his ACL at Virginia Tech in October. My Pitt vs Cal pick is Pittsburgh Panthers −9.5 and the Over 47.5, because whoever plays quarterback for Pitt is going against the worst defense it's seen in weeks.

Dominic "Yinz" Bertolucci blows the homemade whistle. "Louisville was a yikes. Big yikes. But yinz seen Cal's defense? That's a yinz week if I ever saw one."

Quentin Oyelaran-Fitch has a sign ready. It reads RESPECT THE BEAR. "Pittsburgh has lost five of its last six. You're asking a team in crisis to travel to Berkeley and dominate. I reject the premise."

Quentin can reject it, but the numbers are rough for Cal. The Bears are 3-8, have lost five straight, and gave up 44 to Stanford at home last week. Cal allows 33.1 points a game and scores 21.1. Over the five-game skid, it's given up 41, 59, 31, 36 and 44. Jaron-Keawe Sagapolutele has 2,019 yards and 12 touchdowns, and Adam Mohammed ran for 90 yards against Stanford, but Cal hasn't scored more than 28 in any of those five losses.

Pitt is 6-5 and already bowl-eligible, and it still scores 27.9 a game. Damon Ferguson Jr. has 740 rushing yards, Cataurus Hicks has 750 receiving yards, and the defense gives up 21.5 a game. Cruce Brookins has 80 tackles. Pitt also beat Georgia Tech 34-11 with Geriner at quarterback in late October.

These two met in 2024, and Pitt won 17-15.

My margin is 12. That clears the 9.5 by two and a half, which leaves room for one Cal touchdown late.

On the total, I have 50. Cal's defense has given up 31 or more in five straight games, and Pitt only needs a normal day on offense to get into the 30s. DraftKings opened the Pittsburgh Panthers −7.5 with a 47.5 total, and the side has moved two points to −9.5 while the total hasn't moved.

Every card in the room is on Pitt. The Formula has Pitt 31-21 and goes Pitt −9.5 and likes the Over 47.5. Next Man Up has 31-20, goes Pitt and likes the Over. Letdown Watch has 31-20, goes Pitt and likes the Over. In The Trenches has Pitt 31-16 and likes Pittsburgh −9.5, with no total. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Next Man Up: "I wrote 31 without knowing who the quarterback is. That's the job."

Letdown Watch: "You wrote 31 because we all wrote 31. That's copying."

My final is Pittsburgh Panthers 31, California Golden Bears 19. Yinz yells it so loud the board op flinches. Quentin organizes a peaceful protest against the punter.

Pittsburgh Panthers 31, California Golden Bears 19. Pittsburgh Panthers cover the 9.5. Over 47.5.
