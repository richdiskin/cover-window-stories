Boston College Eagles vs Miami Hurricanes: Canes 4 Life Day

Boston College Eagles (5-6) at Miami Hurricanes (10-1)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Hard Rock Stadium · ESPN

XFacebook

Miami's seniors get one more walk onto the grass at Hard Rock Stadium on Saturday, and the school has named the day Canes 4 Life Day. Boston College Eagles vs Miami Hurricanes pits a team that has already punched its ticket to the ACC title game against a team that needs one more win just to see December, and Javi "The Breeze" Castillo is calling it a farewell party while Siobhan Dunleavy calls it a hearing.

"Court is in session," Siobhan says, and sets a toy gavel on the armrest. "The Eagles are 5-6. Win, and we're bowl-eligible. Lose, and we go home. I'd say we have the more motivated client."

"Motivated is nice," Javi says. "Seniors in the sun, Charlotte when we're done. You can be motivated from the visitors' bench."

Miami clinched its trip to Charlotte last week with a 28-20 win over Virginia Tech, and Mark Fletcher Jr. ran for three touchdowns to make sure of it. Wake Forest is waiting on Dec. 5. That leaves Saturday as a send-off for the seniors on a team that went 8-0 in the league, with its only loss coming at Notre Dame.

"This team is the best thing I've watched since I hung up my own cleats," Javi says. "And I was a defensive back, so I watched a lot of guys run past me."

His case is mostly one man. Darian Mensah has 3,317 yards with 29 touchdown throws and only 4 interceptions, and he has the kind of accuracy that makes defensive coordinators stare at the ceiling at night. He went 27 of 30 for 401 yards at Stanford in the opener and never really slowed down. Malachi Toney is the guy on the other end: 73 catches, 1,027 yards, 13 touchdowns. Javian Mallory has 624 rushing yards and 9 scores. On defense, Marquise Lightfoot has 7 sacks and Mohamed Toure has 76 tackles.

Siobhan has heard all of it. She has a rebuttal, and his name is Bo MacCormack III.

"Exhibit A," she says. "Saturday against Syracuse. Thirty carries. Two hundred thirty-six yards. A 47-yard run on our first drive and a 56-yard touchdown in the third quarter. That kid ran like he was late for the T."

That was a wicked good afternoon in Chestnut Hill. Mason McKenzie threw touchdowns to Zeke Moore, his first as an Eagle, and to Nedrick Boldin Jr. on a screen that went 22 yards. Luca Lombardo hit field goals from 46 and 32. Charlie Comella had a career-high nine tackles. BC won 27-15 and walked off the field with a chance.

McKenzie is the engine of this offense in every way. He leads the Eagles in throwing yards and in rushing yards, 718 on the ground, which tells you how much this team asks of him. Anthony Palano has 93 tackles at linebacker. Demetrius Ballard has 4.5 sacks.

"Now here's the part where I object to myself," Siobhan says. "We lost at Duke. We lost at Notre Dame. Those weren't close, and I'm not going to pretend they were."

"I appreciate the honesty," Javi says. "It's very Boston of you."

"It's very law school of me."

The last time these two met, Miami won 45-20. The time before that, in 2018, Boston College won 27-14, and Siobhan has a printout of that box score she keeps in the same folder as her diploma. She brought it to Florida. She will not be showing it to anyone in the parking lot.

Here's how Saturday goes. The seniors get their moment, the stadium gets loud, and BC tries to do exactly what it did to Syracuse, handing it to MacCormack and grinding the clock. It works for one drive. Then Mensah finds Toney on a deep ball, Mallory punches one in, and the Hurricanes start pulling away before halftime. BC gets a field goal out of a long drive and a late touchdown, and MacCormack gets his yards, but the Eagles never get close enough to make Javi take his sunglasses off.

Siobhan bangs the gavel one last time on the walk out and rules that next season is now in session. Javi does the U with both arms in the tunnel and rhymes something about Charlotte that nobody can hear over the band.

My call: Miami Hurricanes 42, Boston College Eagles 13.
