Boston College vs Miami Pick: Hurricanes −25.5, Over 53.5

Boston College Eagles (5-6) at Miami Hurricanes (10-1)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Hard Rock Stadium · ESPN

XFacebook

Saturday is Canes 4 Life Day at Hard Rock Stadium, Miami's Senior Day, and the Hurricanes walk their seniors out already holding a ticket to the ACC title game. My Boston College vs Miami pick is Miami Hurricanes −25.5 and the Over 53.5, because the Eagles need this game more than anybody in the building and still don't have the horses to stay close.

Javi "The Breeze" Castillo has his sunglasses on before noon. "Seniors in the sun, Charlotte when we're done. Darian Mensah throws it, and Boston College runs."

Siobhan Dunleavy opens her case anyway. "Objection, Javi. We're 5-6. One win and we're in a bowl. You've got nothing to play for but a parade. We've got everything."

She's right about the stakes. Boston College has to win to get to six, and the Eagles came in hot after a 27-15 win over Syracuse in which Bo MacCormack III ran 30 times for 236 yards. That's the BC plan: run it, shorten it, and hope. Mason McKenzie can help that plan with his legs. He leads the team in passing yards at 1,798 and in rushing yards at 718.

The problem is what happens when that plan meets a real team on the road. Boston College lost 47-2 at Duke and 41-7 at Notre Dame. The Eagles score 21.0 points a game and give up 27.2. Miami is 10-1, 8-0 in the ACC, scoring 42.6 a game. Mensah has 3,317 yards with 29 touchdowns and 4 interceptions, and Malachi Toney has 73 catches for 1,027 yards and 13 scores.

Here's my worry, and I'll say it out loud. Miami has clinched. Wake Forest is waiting in Charlotte, and a coaching staff with a title game next week might pull its starters early. But this is the seniors' last home game, Miami's last two wins over Duke and Virginia Tech were tighter than the program likes, and Mensah threw two interceptions against the Hokies. A team that wants to look sharp heading into a title game scores early and often against a defense like this one. My margin of 29 assumes Miami is up big at halftime and coasts the rest.

The Over needs Miami to do most of the work, and it can. The Hurricanes have scored 52 or more three times at home. I have 55, a point and a half past the number. DraftKings opened Miami Hurricanes −26.5 with a 53.5 total, and the side has come down a point.

The room is mostly on Miami. The Formula has Miami 40-14 with the Hurricanes −25.5 and the Over 53.5. Next Man Up has 44-13 and likes the Canes and the Over. Letdown Watch has 41-12, likes Miami −25.5, and goes Under. In The Trenches has no score this week, sees the fronts as split, and takes neither side nor total. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Next Man Up: "Letdown Watch, a team clinches and you still take its side. Did you lose your own playbook?"

Letdown Watch: "I'm with you on Miami. I just think they get bored in the fourth quarter."

My final is Miami Hurricanes 42, Boston College Eagles 13. Javi does the U. Siobhan files an appeal on the flight home.

Miami Hurricanes 42, Boston College Eagles 13. Miami Hurricanes cover the 25.5. Over 53.5.
