Kennesaw State vs WKU Pick: Hilltoppers +3.5, Under 54.5

Kennesaw State Owls (5-6) at Western Kentucky Hilltoppers (4-7)

1:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Houchens Industries-L.T. Smith Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Kennesaw State vs Western Kentucky closes the Hilltoppers' season with Senior Day and Big Red, White and Blue Day in Bowling Green, a red-shirted crowd and discounted seats for veterans and military families, and Kennesaw State needs the win to reach six. My Kennesaw State vs WKU pick is Western Kentucky Hilltoppers +3.5 with the Under 54.5.

Tops Whitsitt has heard the same complaint from every chair in his barber shop. "Four and seven. I know. But we're 3-1 at home, and that's the only record I'm cutting today."

Ximena Holcombe-Reyes has the hot hand and knows it. "Four straight wins. Landon Varnes took over and hasn't lost. We're one win from six, Tops, and we're not here to admire your band."

She has the momentum. Kennesaw State beat Middle Tennessee, Delaware, Sam Houston and FIU in a row, and Varnes was 21 for 27 for 219 yards and 2 touchdowns last week. Boogie Woods III has 649 rushing yards and 10 touchdowns and ran for 100 and two scores against FIU.

So why the home team? Because Kennesaw State doesn't blow people out, and it hasn't been the same team on the road. The Owls are 2-3 away from home, and their three conference losses came by 3, 1 and 3. The 42-40 win over Delaware needed every point. A field goal and a hook is plenty of cushion.

Western Kentucky has had a rough year, 4-7 and allowing 33.5 a game, and the 45-14 loss at Liberty last week was ugly. But Brock Glenn is a tough runner with 642 yards and 9 touchdowns on the ground, and the Hilltoppers beat Missouri State 34-13 and Jacksonville State 34-24 at home. The last time these two met, in 2024, WKU won 31-14.

The Under comes from Kennesaw State's style. Eight of the Owls' eleven games finished under 54.5. They run the ball with Woods, they don't throw wild, and they've allowed 7 and 24 the last two weeks. DraftKings opened Kennesaw State Owls −1.5 with a 53.5 total. As of 6:31 AM ET Saturday it's Kennesaw State Owls −3.5 and 54.5.

The room is together on the side and split on the total. The Formula has Kennesaw State 27-25 and takes Western Kentucky +3.5 with the Under 54.5. Next Man Up has Western Kentucky 29-28 and takes Western Kentucky +3.5 with the Over 54.5. Letdown Watch has Kennesaw State 27-26 and takes Western Kentucky +3.5 with the Under 54.5. In The Trenches has Kennesaw State 27-25 and takes Western Kentucky +3.5, with no over/under call. Follow The Money has no side pick and takes the Over 54.5.

Next Man Up: "Four cards on Western Kentucky and I'm the only one brave enough to say they win it. The rest of you want the points and none of the credit."

In The Trenches: "You've got a 4-7 team scoring 29, Next Man Up. That's not bravery. That's a coin flip in a nice shirt."

My final is Kennesaw State Owls 27, Western Kentucky Hilltoppers 26. One point is inside the 3.5, and 53 combined slips a point and a half under. Kennesaw State gets its six, and the Hilltoppers' seniors make it a game to the last snap.

Kennesaw State Owls 27, Western Kentucky Hilltoppers 26. Western Kentucky Hilltoppers cover the 3.5. Under 54.5.
