Kennesaw State vs Western Kentucky: Red, White and Blue Day

Kennesaw State Owls (5-6) at Western Kentucky Hilltoppers (4-7)

1:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Houchens Industries-L.T. Smith Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Kennesaw State Owls vs Western Kentucky Hilltoppers is Senior Day and Big Red, White and Blue Day in Bowling Green, part of the country's 250th birthday celebration, with the whole crowd asked to wear red and the Big Red Marching Band leading the team down Avenue of Champions for one last Topper Walk. Tops Whitsitt closed his barber shop early to be there, and Ximena Holcombe-Reyes rode up from Kennesaw with a four-game winning streak and a plan to make it five.

"Red, white and blue," Ximena says. "I'm wearing two of the three. I'll let you figure out which one's missing."

"Red's the important one," Tops says. "Red's what we wear. Big Red's what we hug. The rest is just decoration."

Tops has seen a lot of this season from his barber chair. Western Kentucky is 4-7, and every customer since September has had a theory about what went wrong. A brutal early stretch didn't help: the Hilltoppers played at Nevada, at Georgia and at Indiana in the first three weeks. Then came a long, uneven fall, with a big win over Missouri State and a 34-24 Homecoming win over Jacksonville State sprinkled in among the losses.

"I tell everybody the same thing," Tops says. "Keep your head still and let me finish. It's good advice for a haircut and a football season."

The seniors are what Tops came for. This is their final regular-season home game, and he plans to clap for every name. The Hilltoppers are 3-1 at home this year, and he'd like to make it 4-1 before anybody gets to the parking lot.

Ximena's team is on a different road entirely. Kennesaw State started 1-6, lost three straight one-score games in late September and October, and looked like a team that would be done by Halloween. Then something clicked. The Owls won at Middle Tennessee, beat Delaware 42-40, won at Sam Houston 26-7 and handled FIU 35-24 last week. A win in Bowling Green gets them to six.

"We're still building this program," Ximena says. "Brick by brick. Every year, we add a new floor. This year we might add a whole bowl game on top."

The guy holding the bricks is Landon Varnes. He took over most of the throwing in early November, and the Owls haven't lost since. Last week he went 21 for 27 for 219 yards and two touchdowns. Boogie Woods III is the engine, with 649 rushing yards and 10 touchdowns on the ground, and he ran for 100 yards and two scores against FIU. Dylan Gary leads the Owls in receiving yards.

"Boogie," Ximena says. "You can't stop a guy named Boogie. It's not allowed."

Tops has his own answer in Brock Glenn, who leads Western Kentucky in both throwing and running. He has 642 rushing yards and nine touchdowns on the ground. When a play breaks down, he's the most dangerous Hilltopper on the field. Jonathan Stafford Jr. has 61 catches and is the receiver Glenn trusts on third down. Jaylen Lewis leads the defense in tackles.

"Brock runs like he's late for his own wedding," Tops says. "You don't want to be standing in the aisle."

The only time these two programs have met, in 2024, Western Kentucky won 31-14. Tops mentions it once, politely, and Ximena points out that this is a different Kennesaw State team. Both of them are right.

Here's how Saturday goes. Kennesaw State comes out running, and Woods pounds out an early score. Glenn answers with his legs, breaking a long run up the middle that gets the red in the stands on its feet. It's tight all afternoon, a game of long drives and field goals. Varnes hits Gary for a touchdown in the third quarter, and Western Kentucky answers again. With a couple of minutes left, the Hilltoppers get one last chance, but the Owls' defense holds near midfield, and Kennesaw State runs out the clock.

When it's over, Tops claps for the seniors as they walk off Feix Field for the last time, and then he walks over to Ximena and tells her he'd give her a free haircut if she ever needs one, but she'll have to come to Bowling Green and sit in a red chair to get it.

My call: Kennesaw State Owls 28, Western Kentucky Hilltoppers 26.
