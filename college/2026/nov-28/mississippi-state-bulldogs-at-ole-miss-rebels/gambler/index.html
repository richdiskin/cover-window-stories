Mississippi State vs Ole Miss Pick: Bulldogs +17.5, Under 56.5

Mississippi State Bulldogs (5-6) at Ole Miss Rebels (7-4)

12:00 PM ET Fri Nov 27 · Vaught-Hemingway Stadium · ABC

XFacebook

The Egg Bowl is back on Black Friday with an eleven o'clock kickoff in Oxford, and Ole Miss is chasing a fourth straight win in this rivalry, something it hasn't pulled off since the game was played in Jackson. My Mississippi State vs Ole Miss prediction takes the points on the spread, Mississippi State Bulldogs +17.5, and the over/under pick is Under 56.5.

Langston Fairbanks has been practicing a speech since Sunday. "Last year it was 38-19, Wade. I'd like the scoreboard to look familiar."

Wade Pettus doesn't look up from his cowbell. "Your quarterback missed one throw last week, Langston. Against Wofford. Frame it."

Trinidad Chambliss went 19 for 20 for 367 yards and five touchdowns against Wofford, which is a poor scouting report. The two SEC games before it say more: 7 points against Georgia and 17 at Oklahoma, with 170 and 127 throwing yards. Ole Miss scores 34.5 a game and allows 25.0. I won't lay three touchdowns behind a defense that gives up 25.

Mississippi State's issue is the quarterback room. Kamario Taylor threw 17 touchdowns against 4 interceptions in the first five games, then the job went to Parker Puckett and AJ Swann. Puckett was 17 for 25 for 232 yards and two touchdowns in the 34-16 win over Tennessee Tech, and Anthony Evans III has 73 catches for 929 yards no matter who is throwing. I'm pricing the Puckett version, and it still finds three scores. Taylor is out, Swann is doubtful and Ole Miss rusher Kewan Lacy is out, per the SEC report.

The market went the other way. DraftKings opened Ole Miss −14.5 with a total of 57.5. As of 6:11 AM ET it sits at Ole Miss Rebels −17.5 and 56.5. That's three points of movement toward the home team in a rivalry the Rebels have won the last three years by 19, 12 and 10. Ole Miss Rebels 35, Mississippi State Bulldogs 21 leaves the Bulldogs three and a half to spare.

The Under comes from the Bulldogs' SEC tape. They put up 0 at LSU, 15 against Oklahoma, 3 at Texas, 23 against Vanderbilt and 9 against Auburn. The 2024 and 2023 meetings finished with 40 and 24 combined points. Ole Miss can get to 35 without this game sailing over.

The room mostly agrees on the side. The Formula is with me on both, 34-20, Mississippi State Bulldogs +17.5 and the Under 56.5. Next Man Up has Ole Miss 40-24, takes the Bulldogs with the points and goes Over. Letdown Watch is the only vote for Ole Miss Rebels −17.5, at 38-17, and it likes the Under. In The Trenches has a 26-23 game, takes Mississippi State, and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money takes Mississippi State Bulldogs +17.5 and has no total pick.

Next Man Up: "Forty points. Chambliss just went 19 for 20. I'm not going to pretend I didn't see it."

Letdown Watch: "And I'm the only one here who thinks the Rebels pull away. They've won three of these in a row."

My number is Ole Miss by 14 with 56 combined points, half a point under. The Rebels can keep the bragging rights for another year. The Bulldogs keep this one inside three scores.

Ole Miss Rebels 35, Mississippi State Bulldogs 21. Mississippi State Bulldogs cover the 17.5. Under 56.5.
