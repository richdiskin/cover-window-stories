Kansas State vs Iowa State: Farmageddon, Maybe the Last One

Kansas State Wildcats (4-7) at Iowa State Cyclones (5-6)

2:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Jack Trice Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Kansas State Wildcats vs Iowa State Cyclones is Farmageddon, and this year it comes with a lump in the throat. These two have played every season since 1917, and the Big 12 schedule doesn't have them meeting in 2027, so Saturday at Jack Trice Stadium could be the last one for a while. Hattie Brandvold drove up from Manhattan for it, and Twyla Hemmingsen has been in Ames all week, getting more nervous every day.

"Since 1917," Twyla says. "My great-grandpa could've gone to the first one. He didn't. He had cows. But he could have."

"Mine went to every one he could," Hattie says. "He'd be furious about this. He'd write a letter. To somebody. In pencil."

Neither of them can believe it. This rivalry is two farm towns and two fan bases that know exactly what the other one had for breakfast. And now nobody knows when it comes back.

"So we have to make this one count," Twyla says. "And I need it to count for us, because we're 5-6."

That's the other part of the story. Iowa State needs this win to reach six and go to a bowl game in Jimmy Rogers' first year as head coach. The Cyclones have been up and down all fall. They beat West Virginia 45-42 and Oklahoma State 51-49 at home, then lost by double digits on the road at BYU, Arizona and UCF. The loss in Orlando last week was ugly, 48-17.

"We're a different team at home," Twyla says. "At home, we're the team I raised. On the road, we're a teenager."

Hattie's team can't get to six. Kansas State is 4-7 in Collin Klein's first year, and the Wildcats have lost five straight. Some of those hurt more than others. Last week in the rain in Manhattan, Kansas State had an 11-point lead in the fourth quarter against Arizona and lost 35-28 in overtime. The week before that it was 10-20 at TCU. Before that, the Wildcats took Colorado to overtime and lost by three.

"We've been close so many times I should get a punch card," Hattie says. "Ten close ones, and you get a free win. We're due."

And that's what makes her dangerous on Saturday. Kansas State has nothing to lose and everything to spoil. Nothing would make Hattie happier than to drive home knowing the Cyclones are spending December at home.

"That's my bowl game," she says. "You not having one."

The man Twyla is counting on is Aiden Flora. The running back has 1,506 rushing yards and 14 touchdowns, and he went for 171 yards and two scores even in the loss at UCF. When he gets to the second level, he's gone.

"Fifteen hundred yards," Twyla says. "In the Big 12. And nobody outside of Ames talks about him. Good. Keep it quiet. Let him surprise people."

Kansas State has a runner of its own. Rodney Fields Jr. had his best day of the year against Arizona, 144 yards on 28 carries, and he has 751 on the season. The Wildcats have used several quarterbacks this fall, and Dillon Duff took most of the throws the last two weeks. Hattie wants the ball in Fields' hands until his arms get tired.

"Run it at them," she says. "Run it at them, run it again, and run it some more. That's what my granddad would've said, if he weren't busy writing his letter."

Iowa State has won three straight in this series, including the opener in Dublin, Ireland, to start last season, 24-21. Twyla brings up Ireland a lot. Hattie mentions that the Wildcats won 10-9 in 2022, which in her mind was the finest football game ever played.

Here's how Saturday goes. Both teams come out running, and neither defense can stop it for very long. Flora breaks a long one in the first quarter, and Fields answers with a touchdown drive that eats half the second quarter. It's a back-and-forth game all afternoon, the kind this rivalry has always been. Iowa State takes a late lead on a Flora run, and Kansas State gets one last chance but comes up short near midfield. The Cyclones get to six.

When it's over, the two of them stand at the rail longer than everybody else, and neither one wants to be the first to leave. Hattie finally says it: see you whenever they let us do this again. Twyla says she'll be here, and that she'll save Hattie a seat.

My call: Iowa State Cyclones 30, Kansas State Wildcats 27.
