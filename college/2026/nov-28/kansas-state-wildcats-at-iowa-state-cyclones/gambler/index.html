Kansas State vs Iowa State Pick: Wildcats +3.5, Over 54.5

Kansas State Wildcats (4-7) at Iowa State Cyclones (5-6)

2:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Jack Trice Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Kansas State vs Iowa State might be the last Farmageddon for a while. These two have played every season for more than a century, and the Big 12's schedule doesn't have them meeting next year, so Jack Trice Stadium could be closing out one of the longest unbroken series in the sport. My Kansas State vs Iowa State pick is Kansas State Wildcats +3.5 with the Over 54.5.

Twyla Hemmingsen has the bigger stakes and isn't hiding it. "We're 5-6. Win, and we're going bowling in Jimmy Rogers' first year. Lose, and I'm spending December staring at the cornfield."

Hattie Brandvold has nothing to play for except the thing that matters most. "We can't get to six. Fine. We can keep you from getting there. That's a bowl game for Manhattan."

That spoiler energy is real, and so is the history of this rivalry being close. Iowa State has won the last three meetings by 7, 8 and 3, and the year before that Kansas State won 10-9. Kansas State is 4-7 but has lost close a lot: 24-27 in overtime at Colorado, 10-20 at TCU, and 28-35 in overtime to Arizona last week after leading late. This isn't a team that quits.

The run game is the case for both the points and the Over. Kansas State runs for 5.0 yards a carry against an Iowa State defense that allows 4.9. Rodney Fields Jr. had 144 yards on 28 carries against Arizona and has 751 on the year. Iowa State runs for 5.1 against a Kansas State defense that allows 4.3, and Aiden Flora is a problem for anybody. He has 1,506 rushing yards and 14 touchdowns, and he ran for 171 at UCF.

Both defenses give up yards on the ground, and both teams can score. Iowa State scores 29.2 a game and allows 29.2. Kansas State scores 29.1 and allows 26.9. The Cyclones' home games against West Virginia and Oklahoma State finished 45-42 and 51-49. The Kansas State quarterback spot has moved around, and Dillon Duff has taken the most throws the last two weeks, but Fields and the ground game don't need him to win it.

DraftKings opened Iowa State Cyclones −5.5 with a 54.5 total, and the side has dropped two points since.

The room splits down the middle on the side. The Formula has 29-26 and takes Kansas State Wildcats +3.5 with the Over 54.5. Next Man Up has 32-28, takes Iowa State Cyclones −3.5 and the Over. Letdown Watch has 30-25 with Iowa State and the Over. In The Trenches has 29-27 and takes Kansas State Wildcats +3.5, with no over/under call. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

In The Trenches: "Two-point game. Rivalry. Probably the last one for a while. And Next Man Up wants four. Go sit in a cornfield and think about it."

Next Man Up: "I'll take the cornfield, Trenches. At least it has more running room than Kansas State's front."

My final is Iowa State Cyclones 30, Kansas State Wildcats 27. Iowa State wins by three, inside the 3.5, and 57 combined clears the total by two and a half. Flora gets the Cyclones to six, and Hattie makes them sweat every snap of it.

Iowa State Cyclones 30, Kansas State Wildcats 27. Kansas State Wildcats cover the 3.5. Over 54.5.
