Georgia Tech vs Georgia Pick: Yellow Jackets +35.5, Under 55.5

Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets (3-8) at Georgia Bulldogs (10-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Sanford Stadium · ABC

XFacebook

The last time Georgia Tech came to Sanford Stadium, it took eight overtimes for the Bulldogs to get out alive. Saturday is Clean, Old-Fashioned Hate again, Georgia is No. 1 and already headed to the SEC Championship Game, and my Georgia Tech vs Georgia prediction is Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets +35.5 with the Under 55.5.

Thad Okafor-Brandt has the numbers ready. "Eight straight in this series, Wendell. Nine would be the longest run either school has ever had. We're 10-1 and we gave up six points in Columbia last week."

Wendell Quist has his own numbers. "And the last three were all one-score games. Including the one in your house that went eight overtimes. Five touchdowns is a lot of touchdowns, Thad, even for you."

Wendell is right about the price. Georgia is great. The Bulldogs score 41.1 points a game, allow 13.9, and Gunner Stockton has 22 touchdowns against 4 interceptions. Chris Cole leads the defense with 71 tackles and 5.5 sacks. But a spread this big asks Georgia to keep its foot down for four full quarters with a title game a week away.

Kirby Smart's team has blown teams out, 70-20 over Western Kentucky and 63-3 over Tennessee State. Against real conference teams, though, the margins have been 28 at South Carolina, 35 at Ole Miss and 27 against Missouri. None of those cleared five touchdowns.

Georgia Tech is 3-8 and has lost five of six, but this game means everything to Brent Key. "We have the biggest game of the year coming up," he said after the 50-29 loss to Wake Forest. The quarterback spot is unsettled. Alberto Mendoza took the throws most of the year, and Grady Adamson took over against Wake and ran for 143 yards. I'm pricing Adamson, and a running quarterback who keeps the ball on the ground also keeps the clock moving.

That's the Under case, too. Georgia's defense keeps games short, and a Tech offense that runs the quarterback eats time. The total opened at 55.5 at DraftKings, and the spread opened at 35.5. Neither has moved.

Georgia Bulldogs 43, Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets 11 lands three and a half points short of the spread, and the 54 total points come in a point and a half under 55.5.

The room is together on the side and split on the total. The Formula has Georgia 41-13 and takes Georgia Tech +35.5 with the Under 55.5. Next Man Up has 47-12 and takes Georgia Tech +35.5 with the Over 55.5. Letdown Watch has 44-9 and takes Georgia Tech +35.5 with the Under 55.5. In The Trenches has no pick in this game. Follow The Money has no pick.

Letdown Watch: "Georgia's got Atlanta on Dec. 5 circled in red. Nobody's running up the score with a title game on deck."

Next Man Up: "You said that about South Carolina, and they still won by four touchdowns."

Georgia wins a ninth straight, Thad finally gets to sit down in peace, and Tech scores late enough to stay inside five touchdowns.

Georgia Bulldogs 43, Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets 11. Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets cover the 35.5. Under 55.5.
