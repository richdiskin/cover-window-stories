App State vs South Alabama Pick: Jaguars −3.5, Under 56.5

App State Mountaineers (7-4) at South Alabama Jaguars (6-5)

3:00 PM ET Fri Nov 27 · Hancock Whitney Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

App State vs South Alabama got moved off its Saturday and onto Black Friday, so Hancock Whitney Stadium holds its Senior Day the day after Thanksgiving, with the city buses parked for the holiday and everybody in Mobile driving themselves to the game. My App State vs South Alabama pick is South Alabama Jaguars −3.5 with the Under 56.5, and the two halves of that ticket come from different teams.

Azalea Fairhope-Grice wants to talk about the seniors. "These boys won two straight to get us to six wins. They get to walk out on their own field on a holiday. I'll be crying before the coin toss."

Zeb Coldiron wants to talk about the last two weeks in Boone, slowly. "We won at Marshall 20-16. We beat Monroe 17-16 in overtime. We don't win pretty. We just keep doing it."

That's the Under in two sentences. App State's last two games combined for 36 and 33 points, and six of its eleven finished below 56.5. The Mountaineers allow 22.9 a game, Caleb Sandstrom has 7 sacks and Colton Phares has 90 tackles. Malachi Singleton has 13 touchdown throws against 7 interceptions and doesn't take many wild shots. Its games have a way of turning into long, slow afternoons.

South Alabama is the reason the total sits this high. The Jaguars score 32.6 a game and allow 30.8, and they've played in shootouts all year: 56-49 at Arkansas State, 52-35 over UL Monroe, 40-35 at Southern Miss last week. App State's defense is a different animal, and it gives up 3.8 yards a carry.

The side goes to the home team because of the backfield. PJ Martin has 767 yards and 9 touchdowns, and he ran for 128 and two scores at Southern Miss. South Alabama runs for 4.8 a carry. Bishop Davenport threw for 267 yards in that win with no interceptions, and Brendan Jenkins has 607 receiving yards. App State's own run game manages 3.8 a carry against a Jaguars defense that allows 5.2, and that gap is exactly why the room isn't unanimous. The last meeting, in 2024, went to South Alabama 48-14.

DraftKings opened South Alabama Jaguars −2.5 with a 55.5 total. The side has moved a point toward the Jaguars since.

The Formula has 29-25 with South Alabama Jaguars −3.5 and the Under 56.5. Next Man Up has 33-27 with the Jaguars and the Over. Letdown Watch has 31-24 with the Jaguars and the Under. In The Trenches has 28-25 and takes App State Mountaineers +3.5, with no over/under call. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

In The Trenches: "A three-point game, and Next Man Up wants sixty points. App State's last two games didn't reach forty combined."

Next Man Up: "And you've got South Alabama winning by three, Trenches. Write a check for half a point and see how far it gets you."

My final is South Alabama Jaguars 30, App State Mountaineers 25. Five points clears the 3.5, and 55 combined stays a point and a half under. Martin gets his carries, the Jaguars get a seventh win, and Zeb gets a long holiday drive back to Boone.

South Alabama Jaguars 30, App State Mountaineers 25. South Alabama Jaguars cover the 3.5. Under 56.5.
