Nebraska vs Iowa Pick: Hawkeyes −3.5, Over 40.5

Nebraska Cornhuskers (7-4) at Iowa Hawkeyes (9-2)

12:00 PM ET Fri Nov 27 · Kinnick Stadium · CBS

XFacebook

Nebraska vs Iowa is the Heroes Game, and on Black Friday at Kinnick Stadium the winner carries home a trophy with two ordinary citizens' names inscribed on it, one from each state, honored on the field before a noon Eastern kickoff. My Nebraska vs Iowa pick is Iowa Hawkeyes −3.5 with the Over 40.5, and the hardest part of the case is that Nebraska just beat Ohio State.

Darla Rasmussen hasn't come down yet. "Thirty-one to twenty-eight over the Buckeyes. Anthony Colandrea threw three touchdowns and no picks. I'm still wearing the same red sweatshirt. I'll wash it in December."

Orrin Kalvelage is unbothered, which is the most Iowa thing about him. "Nice win. Now come play a defense that gives up 12.2 a game, on Senior Day, after a six-day week."

He's right about the week. Nebraska spent everything it had last Saturday, and now it travels to Iowa City on a Friday. The Huskers lost four straight in the middle of the season by 3, 3, 7 and 11. Iowa's defense is better than any of them. The Hawkeyes have three shutouts, held Purdue to 7 and Northwestern to 10, and Cam Buffington has 97 tackles. The latest Big Ten availability report also has Nebraska without its sack leader, Jahsear Whittington, and receiver Larry Miles.

The series backs it up. Iowa has won nine of the last ten meetings, including 40-16 a year ago. The lone Nebraska win in that stretch was 24-17 in 2022.

Iowa's offense isn't pretty, but it gets there. L.J. Phillips Jr. ran for 154 yards on 26 carries in the 34-17 win at Illinois, and Iowa runs for 5.3 yards a carry against a Nebraska defense that allows 3.1. Hank Brown doesn't need big throwing days. He has 10 touchdown throws and 4 interceptions, and he hands off well.

The Over is the part that might surprise people who still picture these games ending 13-10. Nebraska scores 30.4 a game and Iowa scores 28.4. Six of Nebraska's eleven games went past 40.5, and Kwazi Gilmer has 8 touchdown catches. DraftKings opened Iowa Hawkeyes −6.5 with a 38.5 total, and as of 6:11 AM ET Friday the side has come down to 3.5 and the total has climbed to 40.5.

The room is split. The Formula has 24-21 and takes Nebraska Cornhuskers +3.5 with the Over 40.5. Next Man Up has 21-15, takes Iowa Hawkeyes −3.5 and goes Under 40.5. Letdown Watch has 26-16 with Iowa and the Over. In The Trenches has no pick in this game. Follow The Money takes Nebraska Cornhuskers +3.5 and has no total pick.

Letdown Watch: "Nebraska beat Ohio State, threw a party, and has to show up in Iowa City on Friday. The Formula has them losing by three. The Formula has never been to a party."

The Formula: "And you've got a team that just hung 31 on the Buckeyes scoring 16. I've been to parties. I just leave before I start making calls like that."

My final is Iowa Hawkeyes 24, Nebraska Cornhuskers 18. Six points clears the 3.5, and 42 combined gets over 40.5 without needing a shootout. The Swarm comes out, the seniors get their send-off, and the Heroes Trophy stays in Iowa City another year.

Iowa Hawkeyes 24, Nebraska Cornhuskers 18. Iowa Hawkeyes cover the 3.5. Over 40.5.
