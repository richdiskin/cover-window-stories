Duke vs Wake Forest: Senior Day, and a Four-Year Itch to Scratch

Duke Blue Devils (7-4) at Wake Forest Demon Deacons (10-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium · ESPN

XFacebook

Duke Blue Devils vs Wake Forest Demon Deacons is the 106th time these two have met, and Eloise Brightman-Rowe has a problem with the last four. Duke won all of them. The last time Wake beat the Blue Devils was a 45-7 win in 2021, and she's done waiting. It's Senior Day in Winston-Salem, and she wants this class to walk off the field with that one checked off.

"Ten wins, a spot in Charlotte, and I'm still mad about Duke," Eloise says. "That's how you know it's a rivalry."

Harrison Wexley-Moore IV drove over from Durham and parked as close to the stadium as he could, which wasn't close. He's wearing a Duke scarf his grandfather wore to this game, and he wants everybody to notice.

"Four straight," Harrison says. "I don't even need to say anything else. I'll just stand here."

He's going to have to stand there a while, because Wake Forest is having a season. The Deacons are 10-1. They've won eight straight. They beat Louisville on the road, crushed SMU 49-7, and went into Atlanta last week and put up 50 on Georgia Tech. That win clinched a spot in the ACC title game against Miami in Jake Dickert's second year.

"I've been to Charlotte for a lot of things," Eloise says. "Never for this. I'm going to bring a sign. Maybe two."

Gio Lopez has been the whole show. He has 2,701 yards, 21 touchdowns and one interception. One. All season. Carlos Hernandez has 75 catches for 1,184 yards, and he had 145 in Atlanta last week.

"Gio doesn't throw it to the other team," Eloise says. "He throws it to Carlos. Sometimes he throws it to Carlos and three other guys at once. It's art."

Harrison has a runner he loves even more than his scarf. Nate Sheppard has 1,436 rushing yards and 20 touchdowns. He's carried Duke all year, and he went for 116 yards against Clemson last week even in a loss.

"Nate's the best running back in the ACC," Harrison says. "Ask Clemson. Ask anybody who tried to tackle him."

The problem for Duke is that the last two weeks haven't gone their way. The Blue Devils lost 36-25 at Miami and 20-10 to Clemson. Walker Eget threw it 38 times against Clemson and completed 16. Harrison doesn't want to talk about that part.

"The ball was cold," he says.

"The ball was fine," Eloise says.

And here's what has Eloise a little nervous: Duke's defense is the real deal. The Blue Devils give up 15.7 points a game. And Wake has to play Miami a week after this, the only team that beat them all season. She doesn't want her guys looking ahead.

"If anybody on that sideline is thinking about Charlotte before the fourth quarter, I'll be thinking about them," she says.

But she also thinks the Deacons are too good to let this one go. Wake's defense gives up 16.3 points a game, and Duke's run game is going to hit a wall. The Deacons have been tough to run on all fall, and Eloise expects Sheppard to have to work for every inch.

Here's how Saturday goes. The seniors get a big roar before kickoff, and the Deacons come out sharp. Lopez finds Hernandez deep on the opening drive, and Wake scores. Duke hangs around in the first half behind Sheppard, who gets loose for a touchdown in the second quarter. But Wake pulls away in the third, and Lopez throws two more scores. Duke gets a late one to make it look closer, and the Deacons finally break the Blue Devils' run in the series.

Afterward, Eloise finds Harrison by his car, still wearing the scarf. She tells him it looks better on a loser. He says he'll wear it next year too, and she'd better get used to it.

My call: Wake Forest Demon Deacons 32, Duke Blue Devils 20.
