Coastal Carolina vs James Madison: Royal Dukes Alumni Come Home

Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (4-7) at James Madison Dukes (8-3)

2:00 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · Bridgeforth Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

The Marching Royal Dukes alumni are coming back to Harrisonburg this weekend, and Lila Shenandoah has already told three of them where to sit. Coastal Carolina Chanticleers vs James Madison Dukes is Senior Day, the MRD Alumni Game and Letterwinner Weekend all at once at Bridgeforth Stadium, and Marigold "Goldie" Seabrook has driven up from the Grand Strand to watch the whole purple-and-gold reunion.

"Every old trumpet player in Virginia is going to be in the stands," Lila says. "We're going to be loud, and we're going to be on key."

"I've heard about the on-key part," Goldie says. "I'd like to see some evidence."

Lila laughs that cheer-captain laugh. Then she gets to the stakes, because it's Senior Day and JMU has some explaining to do. The Dukes are 8-3, and they went into their bye week after a 46-22 loss at UConn that nobody in the Shenandoah Valley wants to replay. Before that, they'd lost two overtime games in a row, 34-31 at App State and 24-21 to Troy at home.

"Two overtime losses and one bad day in Connecticut," Lila says. "That's three losses. We had two weeks to think about it. You don't want to be the team that walks in after a JMU bye."

Goldie's team has had a different kind of year. Coastal Carolina is 4-7. The Chanticleers lost 21-8 to Old Dominion at home the week after a win at Georgia State, then beat Arkansas State and lost 38-24 at Louisiana last Saturday. Deuce Bailey has thrown for 2,517 yards and 21 touchdowns, and he can make throws that make you sit up. He also has 11 interceptions, and Goldie flinches every time he cocks his arm into traffic.

"Deuce is a gunslinger," Goldie says. "Sometimes the gun goes off in the wrong direction."

That's the worry against a JMU defense that has given up 21.7 points a game. JT Hooten has 6 sacks from linebacker, and Nakian Jackson has 82 tackles. Lila expects Saturday to look a lot more like the 54-23 night at Southern Miss than the one in Connecticut.

The history doesn't help Goldie, either. The Dukes have played the Chanticleers four times as Sun Belt teams and won all four, by scores like 47-7, 56-14 and last year's 59-10. Lila keeps those numbers in her phone. Goldie keeps hers in a drawer she doesn't open.

"That's history," Goldie says. "We're talking about this Saturday."

"So am I. This Saturday, there's free fries."

That much is true. Every time the Dukes kick a field goal at home, fans get a free small fry from McDonald's, and three field goals means a large. When the Dukes win, your ticket gets you free nuggets at the Chick-fil-A on East Market Street. Lila has never been happier to watch a kicker warm up.

JC Evans has taken the throws for the Dukes the last two games, and Camden Coleman handled most of the season with 1,814 yards and 17 touchdowns. George Pettaway leads the runners with 824 yards and 8 touchdowns, and Corey Scott is the top receiver at 562 yards. For Coastal, Clayton Coppock Jr. has 565 receiving yards and 7 touchdowns, and Marcellius Pulliam has made 115 tackles, more than anybody on either side.

"Pulliam's going to be in on every play," Goldie says. "You'll be sick of his name by halftime."

Here's how I see Saturday. The band alumni get the stadium rocking before the opening kickoff, and JMU scores on its first two drives even with Pettaway listed out. Bailey answers with a touchdown to Coppock that quiets things for a minute, but then he forces one into coverage and Hooten comes down with it. The Dukes kick a field goal before halftime and the whole student section cheers like it just won a bowl game, because it just won fries. Coastal scores again in the third quarter, Pulliam piles up tackles, and the Dukes pull away late.

Goldie stays for the alumni band's postgame show, and when the last note ends she admits, out loud and in front of witnesses, that the trumpets were on key. Lila gets it on video before Goldie can take it back.

My call: James Madison Dukes 36, Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 17.
