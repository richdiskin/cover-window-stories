Cincinnati vs BYU Pick: Bearcats +15.5 and the Under 56.5

Cincinnati Bearcats (6-5) at BYU Cougars (9-2)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 28 · LaVell Edwards Stadium · Fox

XFacebook

BYU's last home game falls on Thanksgiving weekend with a 1:30 kickoff that should wrap up around sunset in Provo, and Cincinnati comes in fresh off clinching a bowl trip. In this Cincinnati vs BYU prediction, the points are worth taking: Cincinnati Bearcats +15.5 on the spread, and the over/under goes low with the Under 56.5.

Bryce Halvorsen-Tuilagi is already bundled up. "Last home game, Skyline. Dress warm. We send our seniors out right."

Lionel "Skyline" Abernathy shrugs. "I'm from Cincinnati, Bryce. Cold doesn't scare me. Fifteen and a half points might scare you, though."

He has a point about the margin. BYU is 9-2, but its home wins against FBS teams came by 11 over Arizona, 14 over Iowa State, 4 over Notre Dame, 7 over Arizona State and 21 over Baylor. One of five cleared this number. The Cougars needed overtime to win 31-29 at Kansas last week, and Bear Bachmeier threw for 173 yards in that game. He has 14 touchdowns and 8 interceptions, four of them in the loss at Utah.

Cincinnati has a real offense. JC French IV went 18 for 19 against Colorado last week and has 18 touchdowns against just 3 interceptions. Cole Tabb has 1,097 rushing yards and 12 touchdowns, and he added 7 catches for 110 yards and two scores in that win. The road has been tough, with losses at Arizona, West Virginia, Houston and Iowa State, but the West Virginia and Houston games were decided by a score.

The market has climbed. DraftKings opened BYU −12.5 and 56.5, per ESPN. It's BYU −15.5 now. BYU Cougars 34, Cincinnati Bearcats 20 is a 14-point win, a point and a half short.

The Under is BYU's defense at home. The Cougars allow 19.5 a game, and only the opener against Utah Tech went over this total in Provo. LJ Martin ran 25 times for 127 yards at Kansas, and BYU will keep handing it to him to shorten the afternoon. Cincinnati allows 30.4, but scoring 34 is a long way from the Cougars blowing this open. I have it at 54.

Every total in the room is Under. The Formula has BYU 34-21, takes Cincinnati Bearcats +15.5 and goes Under 56.5. Next Man Up has 36-18, takes BYU Cougars −15.5 and likes the Under. Letdown Watch has 35-18 with BYU and the Under. In The Trenches has 31-24 and takes Cincinnati, with no over/under call. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Next Man Up: "Thirty-six to eighteen. Last home game, Senior Day feel. BYU doesn't let this get close."

The Formula: "They let Notre Dame get within four in that building. They let Arizona State get within seven. Fifteen and a half is a big ask."

My final is BYU by 14 with 54 points, two and a half under. The fans walk out into a cold Provo evening, and the Bearcats walk out inside the number.

BYU Cougars 34, Cincinnati Bearcats 20. Cincinnati Bearcats cover the 15.5. Under 56.5.
