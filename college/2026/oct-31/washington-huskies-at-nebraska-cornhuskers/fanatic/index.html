Halloween Night in Lincoln: Washington Visits Nebraska

Washington Huskies (4-3) at Nebraska Cornhuskers (5-2)

8:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Memorial Stadium (Lincoln, NE) · BTN

Washington (4-3, 1-3 Big Ten) at Nebraska (5-2, 2-2 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 8:00 p.m. ET (7:00 p.m. Central), Memorial Stadium, Lincoln, Neb., BTN.

Your hosts are Evangeline "Vangie" Tollefsrud, a Seattle native who rows crew and boats to every Husky home game, and Darla Rasmussen, a former Nebraska volleyball player and farm girl who loves to talk about the sellout streak.

Darla: Go Big Red! Welcome to Lincoln, Vangie. Every seat is going to be full. It always is.

Vangie: Bow Down to Washington! I looked for a lake to sail in on, Darla. I found a lot of corn.

Darla: Plenty of corn. Plenty of fans. That's Nebraska.

Players to watch

Washington's quarterback is a mystery for now. Demond Williams Jr. is 116 of 174 for 1,396 yards, eight touchdowns and three interceptions, but he has been out with a fractured rib since the Iowa game. Elijah Brown, a Stanford transfer, started at Purdue. Running back Brian Bonner Jr. has 296 yards, and wide receiver Dezmen Roebuck leads the team with 48 catches for 484 yards. On defense, safety Alex McLaughlin has 47 tackles and two interceptions, and linebacker Jacob Manu has 2.5 sacks.

Nebraska quarterback Anthony Colandrea is 137 of 219 for 1,570 yards, 14 touchdowns and four interceptions. Running back Jamal Rule has 391 yards and five touchdowns. Wide receiver Kwazi Gilmer has 38 catches for 492 yards and seven touchdowns. On defense, linebacker Owen Chambliss has 46 tackles, Jahsear Whittington has 3.5 sacks and Dwayne McDougle III has two interceptions.

Darla: Gilmer with seven touchdown catches. He sells out every route.

What happened last week

Both teams had an open date on Oct. 24.

Washington's last game was a 38-26 win at Purdue on Oct. 16, its first Big Ten win of the season. Brown went 21 of 27 for a career-high 302 yards and three touchdowns, and Trey Cooley ran eight times for 113 yards and a score.

Nebraska's last game was a 17-14 loss at Oregon on Oct. 17. Colandrea threw a touchdown and ran for another, and Gilmer caught six balls for 74 yards and a touchdown. A week before that, the Cornhuskers lost 20-17 to Indiana.

Vangie: Brown played like a captain on a calm morning.

Injuries and availability

No official Big Ten availability report had been posted as of Tuesday. Washington coach Jedd Fisch said he won't name his starting quarterback until game time. Williams is back at practice but still has to prove the rib is ready. Sports Illustrated reports that Washington center Landen Hatchett has a season-ending neck injury. Nebraska coach Matt Rhule said running back Mekhi Nelson has been playing through an ankle injury and that the bye week was good for a banged-up running back room.

What the coaches are up to

Fisch is in his third season at Washington. He said Williams and Brown have split the first-team reps and have been great teammates. "Rising tides lift all ships," he said.

Rhule is in his fourth season at Nebraska. He said his team has to prepare for both quarterbacks. "We won't know until the first snap of the game," he said.

Player storylines

Williams had started 21 straight games for Washington before missing the Purdue game. Brown came in during the Iowa game and has played well since. For Nebraska, Colandrea has been steady all year, and Rule leads a running game that has a lot of hands on deck.

What's happening on campus

Memorial Stadium opened in 1923 and seats 85,458. The Cornhusker Marching Band will be there, and the fight song is "Hail Varsity." Nebraska claims national titles from 1970, 1971, 1994, 1995 and 1997.

Washington's home is Husky Stadium in Seattle, built in 1920, with room for 70,138. That is where Vangie usually sails in by boat. The fight song is "Bow Down to Washington."

Anything else fun and true

These two have not met in the regular season in the last 10 years. Nebraska's two losses came by three points each, to Indiana and at Oregon. Washington's three losses came to Minnesota, USC and Iowa.

At home this year, Nebraska beat Ohio 49-21, Bowling Green 56-7, North Dakota 34-7 and Maryland 48-23.

Vangie: A Halloween night game in Lincoln. I'll trade my sailing jacket for a winter coat.

Darla: And I'll trade you a seat in a full house. Go Big Red.

My call: Nebraska Cornhuskers 33, Washington Huskies 19.
