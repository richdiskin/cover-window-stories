Washington Huskies at Nebraska Cornhuskers

Washington Huskies (4-3) at Nebraska Cornhuskers (5-2)

8:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Memorial Stadium (Lincoln, NE) · BTN

Vangie Tollefsrud rows crew on Lake Washington and boats to every home game, but there is no water route to Lincoln. Darla Rasmussen, a former Husker volleyball player and farm girl, has the porch light on and the sellout talk ready.

Washington (4-3) at Nebraska (5-2), Saturday at 8:00 p.m. ET on BTN at Memorial Stadium in Lincoln. It's a Big Ten game. DraftKings has Nebraska Cornhuskers −7.5 with a total of 49.5. My card: Nebraska Cornhuskers −7.5, Over 49.5, and a final of Nebraska Cornhuskers 33, Washington Huskies 19.

Vangie speaks for Washington. "Bow Down to Washington! Elijah Brown went 21 of 27 for 302 yards and three touchdowns at Purdue, and we won 38-26. Coach Fisch won't say who starts. Demond Williams Jr. is working back from a fractured rib. Either way, Dezmen Roebuck has 48 catches."

Darla speaks for Nebraska. "Go Big Red! We lost 17-20 to Indiana and 14-17 at Oregon. Two of the best teams in the country, and we lost both by three. Anthony Colandrea has 14 touchdown passes, and Kwazi Gilmer has seven touchdown catches. And yes, the place will be sold out again."

Here is the part that matters. Nebraska allows 15.4 points a game. At home the Cornhuskers beat Ohio 49-21, Bowling Green 56-7, North Dakota 34-7 and Maryland 48-23. Washington still isn't sure who its quarterback is. The Huskies also lost center Landen Hatchett for the season, according to Sports Illustrated.

Why Nebraska? The Formula, Next Man Up and In The Trenches all like the Cornhuskers. In The Trenches has Nebraska at 5.0 yards a carry against a Washington defense that allows 3.8. Washington runs for 3.5 a carry. The Huskies lost to Iowa 24-41 at home. Brown also threw three interceptions at Purdue.

The worry is the market. Action Network's opening number was Nebraska −9.5, and it has come down two points. Follow The Money likes Washington. Nebraska's two losses were by three each, and this offense went quiet at Oregon. If Williams is healthy, he gives Washington another gear.

Why the Over/Under? Nebraska's home games have scored plenty, 49-21, 56-7 and 48-23. Washington scored 38 at Purdue with Brown throwing it all over the field. Both teams had last week off, so both offenses are rested. The Formula, Next Man Up and In The Trenches all like the Over. My 33-19 adds up to 52, two and a half over the over/under 49.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Nebraska 32-19 and likes Nebraska and the Over. Next Man Up has Nebraska 33-18 and likes Nebraska and the Over. Follow The Money likes Washington after the move and has no total. In The Trenches likes Nebraska and the Over. Letdown Watch has no score this week.
