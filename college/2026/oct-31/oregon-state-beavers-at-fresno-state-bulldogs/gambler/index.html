Oregon State Beavers at Fresno State Bulldogs

Oregon State Beavers (5-2) at Fresno State Bulldogs (4-3)

11:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Valley Children's Stadium · USA Net

It's Halloween night in Fresno, and Juniper Ostrowski has her field guide out to identify every tree in the Valley. Roz Ayala has one question for her: which valley, babe? Both teams are coming off an open week.

Oregon State (5-2) at Fresno State (4-3), Saturday at 11:00 p.m. ET on USA Net at Valley Children's Stadium in Fresno. It's a Pac-12 game. DraftKings has Oregon State Beavers −5.5 with a total of 57.5. My card: Fresno State Bulldogs +5.5, Under 57.5, and a final of Oregon State Beavers 30, Fresno State Bulldogs 27.

Juniper speaks for Oregon State. "Measure twice, kick once. Braden Atkinson has 24 touchdown passes and two interceptions. Jesse Legree has 1,040 receiving yards and 14 touchdowns. And Coach Shephard says TJ Crandall is good to go at corner after his concussion. That's a healthy canopy."

Roz speaks for Fresno State. "Not that Valley, babe. Our defense allows 20.9 points a game, and The Oregonian says we're tied for the Pac-12 lead in turnover margin at plus-eight. Fresno State is unbeaten at home on Halloween. And we won in Corvallis last year, 36-27."

Here is the part that matters. Oregon State scores 42.7 points a game, and this defense is second in the Pac-12 in scoring, per The Oregonian. Fresno State's only blowout loss came in the opener at USC, 39-0. Since then, nobody has scored more than 38 on the Bulldogs.

Why Fresno State? Because 5.5 points is a nice cushion at home, at night, in a building where the Bulldogs know how to win. Action Network's opening number was Oregon State −6.5, and it has dropped a point toward Fresno State. Jayden Mandal has one interception all season. He just has to protect the ball. My final has Oregon State by three, inside the number.

The worry is Atkinson. He threw five touchdown passes in three of his last five games, and the Beavers have not trailed during their five-game winning run. Fresno State lost 26-23 at San Diego State, when Mandal threw for 141 yards. If the Bulldogs can't move the ball, Oregon State pulls away.

Why the Over/Under? Fresno State plays defense. Its seven games ended 39-0, 49-3, 26-10, 38-24, 26-6, 38-31 and 26-23. The total opened 56.5 and moved up to 57.5, but I want the defense. My 30-27 adds up to 57, half a point under the over/under 57.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Oregon State 30-27, takes Fresno State and the Under. Next Man Up has Oregon State 29-26, takes Fresno State and likes the Under. Follow The Money likes Fresno State after the move from +7 and likes the Over. In The Trenches sees the run gaps cancel and has no pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.
