Oregon State vs Fresno State: Halloween Night Under the Valley Lights

Oregon State Beavers (5-2) at Fresno State Bulldogs (4-3)

11:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Valley Children's Stadium · USA Net

Oregon State Beavers vs Fresno State Bulldogs is a Week 9 Halloween night at Valley Children's Stadium in Fresno, the first time these two meet as Pac-12 opponents, with kickoff at 11 p.m. ET on USA Sports. Juniper Ostrowski, an Oregon State forestry PhD who charts treetop weather and red-zone efficiency and corrects everyone's tree metaphors, says Braden Atkinson, Jesse Legree and Takari Hickle keep the Beavers on top of the league, and Rosalind "Roz" Ayala, a Fresno State fan and raisin-grower's daughter who calls herself the Valley Girl, says Jayden Mandal, Josiah Freeman and K'vion Thunderbird make it two in a row over Oregon State.

Roz: Not that Valley, babe! Welcome to Fresno on Halloween.

Juniper: Measure twice, kick once! I came as a Douglas fir.

Roz: Of course you did. The needles are on backward.

Juniper: Those are cones, Roz. That's a fir thing.

Fresno State is turning the Red and White Lot into a Trunk or Treat, with tailgaters signing up their picnic spaces for the kids. The Bulldogs have a good Halloween history, too: they're 7-4-1 on Oct. 31, including 5-0-1 at home, though they haven't played on Halloween since 2009. Oregon State is 4-9-1 on Halloween. Ryan Leaf has the call on TV, and former Bulldogs coach Pat Hill is on the radio with Paul Loeffler.

Juniper: Four and nine on Halloween. I'll hold my cones a little tighter.

These two go back to 1981. Fresno State leads the series 9-6 and is 6-1 at home against Oregon State, and the teams have traded wins in each of the last six meetings. In 2001, David Carr threw for 340 yards and four touchdowns as Fresno State beat No. 10 Oregon State 44-24. In 2003, Brett Visintainer kicked a career-long 47-yard field goal with 31 seconds left for a 16-14 win. In 2022, the Beavers scored as time expired to win 35-32. Last year, Fresno State won 36-27 in Corvallis, its first road win over the Beavers since 1991.

Roz: Trading wins every time. That means it's our turn to win again, right?

Juniper: That's not how forests work. Or calendars.

Oregon State is 5-2 and 3-0 in the league, first in the Pac-12, and has won five straight without trailing in any of them. Atkinson has completed 189 of 273 throws for 2,604 yards, 24 touchdowns and two interceptions, and he has gone 129 throws without an interception, with Matt Moore's school record at 182. Legree has 46 catches for 1,040 yards and 14 touchdowns, AJ Newberry has 431 rushing yards, Aiden Sullivan has 53 tackles and Hickle has six sacks. In the 48-30 win over Washington State on Oct. 17, Atkinson threw for 347 yards and three touchdowns. JaMarcus Shephard is 5-2 in his first season.

Juniper: Legree is a giant sequoia. Tall, strong and impossible to miss.

Fresno State is 4-3 and 1-2 in the league. The Bulldogs allow 20.9 points a game, second in the conference, and they have thrown one interception in 201 team attempts. Mandal, in his first season as the No. 1 quarterback, has 1,393 yards, eight touchdowns and that one interception, plus two rushing scores and a 21-yard catch. Freeman has 32 catches for 445 yards, Rayshon Luke has 333 rushing yards and Thunderbird has 61 tackles. The Bulldogs have outscored opponents 45-8 in the third quarter. Matt Entz is 13-7 at Fresno State and won two FCS national titles at North Dakota State.

Roz: Forty-five to eight in the third quarter. That's when the raisins dry, babe.

Juniper: That is not how raisins work.

Three Oregon State staffers have worked at Fresno State: offensive coordinator Mitch Dahlen, running backs coach Lee Marks and general manager Ron McKeefery. So some of the Beavers know their way around the building.

The Pac-12's advance availability report, updated at 10:04 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists two Oregon State players out, one for the season, and three Fresno State players out and five questionable.

Roz: I'm going as a raisin. Small, sweet and wrinkly.

Juniper: I'm staying a Douglas fir. Nobody wants a raisin next to a tree.

Roz: Whatever happens, Juniper, dinner's on me, and you say "Not that Valley, babe" on your podcast.

Juniper: Then you learn three tree species.

Roz: Deal. I already know raisin.

My call: Oregon State Beavers 30, Fresno State Bulldogs 27.
