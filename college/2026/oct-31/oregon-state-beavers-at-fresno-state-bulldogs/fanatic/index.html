Halloween Night in the Valley: Oregon State Visits Fresno State

Oregon State Beavers (5-2) at Fresno State Bulldogs (4-3)

11:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Valley Children's Stadium · USA Net

Oregon State (5-2, 3-0 Pac-12) at Fresno State (4-3, 1-2 Pac-12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 11:00 p.m. ET (8:00 p.m. Pacific), Valley Children's Stadium, Fresno, Calif., USA Net.

Your hosts are Juniper Ostrowski, an Oregon State forestry PhD who charts treetop weather and red-zone efficiency and corrects everyone's tree metaphors, and Rosalind "Roz" Ayala, a Fresno State fan and raisin-grower's daughter who calls herself the Valley Girl, from a different valley.

Roz: Not that Valley, babe! Welcome to Fresno on Halloween night.

Juniper: Measure twice, kick once! I packed a costume. I came as a Douglas fir.

Roz: Of course you did.

Players to watch

Oregon State quarterback Braden Atkinson is 189 of 273 for 2,604 yards, 24 touchdowns and just two interceptions. The Oregonian says he has thrown 129 straight throws without an interception, and the school record is 182 by Matt Moore. Wide receiver Jesse Legree has 46 catches for 1,040 yards and 14 touchdowns. Running back AJ Newberry has 431 yards. On defense, linebacker Aiden Sullivan has 53 tackles and linebacker Takari Hickle has six sacks.

Fresno State quarterback Jayden Mandal is 123 of 195 for 1,393 yards, eight touchdowns and one interception, and he has run for two scores. Rayshon Luke leads the rushers with 333 yards, and Josiah Freeman leads the receivers with 32 catches for 445 yards. Linebacker K'vion Thunderbird has 61 tackles, and defensive back Simeon Harris has three interceptions.

Juniper: Legree with 14 touchdowns is a giant sequoia. Tall, strong and impossible to miss.

What happened last week

Both teams had an open date on Oct. 24. Oregon State's last game was a 48-30 home win over Washington State on Oct. 17, when Atkinson threw for 347 yards and three touchdowns and Newberry ran for 105 yards and caught five balls for 120 more.

Fresno State lost 26-23 at San Diego State that same night. Bryson Donelson ran for a touchdown.

Injuries and availability

The official Pac-12 availability report usually posts on game day. The Oregonian reports that Oregon State cornerback TJ Crandall, who missed the Washington State game with a concussion, is good to go. Receiver Adonis McDaniel, who tore his Achilles in the winter, is expected to make his season debut. For Fresno State, Luke was listed as questionable with a concussion after the San Diego State game, so watch for his status on Saturday.

What the coaches are up to

JaMarcus Shephard is 5-2 in his first season at Oregon State. He was hired on Nov. 28, 2025. Matt Entz is 13-7 at Fresno State and won two FCS national championships at North Dakota State.

Roz: Coach Entz knows big games. Two national titles is a lot of raisins.

Juniper: That is not how raisins work.

Player storylines

McDaniel and Atkinson both came to Corvallis from Mercer. If McDaniel plays, two old teammates get to share a big Saturday night again.

Mandal is in his first season as a starter, and he has protected the ball all year with just one interception in seven games. He has also caught a 21-yard ball this year, so keep an eye on trick plays.

The Beavers have won five straight and have not trailed in any of them. Last year's meeting went the other way, a 36-27 Fresno State win in Corvallis.

What's happening on campus

It's Halloween in Fresno, and the Bulldogs haven't played on Oct. 31 since 2009. Fresno State is 7-4-1 on Halloween, including 5-0-1 at home. Oregon State is 4-9-1 on Halloween, so Juniper might want to keep that Douglas fir costume handy for luck.

Fresno State plays on Jim Sweeney Field, and Valley Children's Stadium seats 41,031. Oregon State's home, Reser Stadium, seats 43,363.

Anything else fun and true

Oregon State sits first in the Pac-12 at 3-0 in league games, and the Beavers have 17 sacks and nine takeaways. Fresno State's defense is second in the league in scoring, according to The Oregonian.

Juniper: A good defense is like an old-growth forest. You don't get through it quickly.

Roz: And a good offense is like a raisin. Sweet, a little wrinkly, and gone before you know it.

Juniper: I'm writing that down for the podcast.

My call: Oregon State Beavers 30, Fresno State Bulldogs 27.
