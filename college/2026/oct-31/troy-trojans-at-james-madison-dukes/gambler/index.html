Troy Trojans at James Madison Dukes

Troy Trojans (3-4) at James Madison Dukes (7-1)

7:30 PM ET Thu Oct 29 · Bridgeforth Stadium · ESPN2

Troy vs James Madison prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm taking Troy Trojans +21 and Over 49.5 at the DraftKings numbers on ESPN, with a final of James Madison Dukes 38, Troy Trojans 18. Harrisonburg spent Wednesday night walking the Hallway of Terror at Nightmare at UREC, and the scariest room for the Dukes on Thursday is the one where George Pettaway isn't standing.

Pettaway is out. He leads James Madison with 824 rushing yards and eight touchdowns, and Catie Harper of the Daily News-Record reported on Oct. 28 that he was hurt late against App State. The Sun Belt's game-day report, posted at 10:33 a.m. ET Thursday, Oct. 29, has him out and lists Troy quarterback Goose Crowder, who has been nursing a shoulder, as available.

Three touchdowns is a lot to lay without your lead back. The Dukes have been beatable by a little this season, winning 20-13 over Liberty and 27-24 at Georgia Southern before losing 34-31 in overtime at App State. Troy's defense allows 24.9 a game, and Donnie Smith has six sacks from the secondary. My final has James Madison by 20, one point short of 21.

Lila Shenandoah walked out of the hallway still cheering. "Give me a three-one! We're 3-1 all-time against Troy, we beat you 31-14 for the Sun Belt title last December, and Camden Coleman has 14 touchdown passes and two interceptions."

Marlo Pickens spent Tuesday at TROYAPALOOZA in the Janice Hawkins Cultural Arts Park and drove north with the fried Oreos wearing off. "Saturday was the hardest one I've watched. We had UL Monroe 14-3 and lost 21-14. But we gave you all you wanted in 2023, 16-14, and Smith has been in quarterbacks' laps all year."

Here is how Troy gets buried. The Trojans' quarterbacks went 9 of 18 against UL Monroe, and the week before, Troy lost 33-6 at Louisiana. If Crowder's shoulder flares and the offense stalls the way it did in those two games, the Dukes can win by 30 even without Pettaway.

The total runs the other direction from the side. DraftKings had 52.5 on Monday, and Action Network's opening listing was 51.5, so the slide to 49.5 hands the Over room it didn't have earlier in the week. James Madison scores 41.4 a game on its own, and a team trailing by three scores has to keep throwing late. My 38-18 adds up to 56.

The room splits on both. The Formula projects James Madison 35-19 and likes Troy and the Over. Next Man Up has the Dukes 41-16 and likes James Madison and the Over. Follow The Money likes James Madison and the Under, and it keeps no score of its own. In The Trenches has no pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "Forty-one to sixteen. Troy has not found a passer."

The Formula: "Mine had the Dukes by 16 before anyone mentioned Pettaway."

Harrisonburg will be in costume all weekend, from the fire department's Trunk or Treat on Friday to the Halloween show at Clementine. Lila expects a win to open it. Marlo expects Troy to still be in the hallway when the lights come on.

James Madison Dukes 38, Troy Trojans 18. Troy Trojans cover the 21. Over 49.5.
