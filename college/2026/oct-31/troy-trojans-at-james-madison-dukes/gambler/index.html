Troy Trojans at James Madison Dukes

Troy Trojans (3-4) at James Madison Dukes (7-1)

7:30 PM ET Thu Oct 29 · Bridgeforth Stadium · ESPN2

Marlo Pickens has a bag of boiled peanuts and a long drive north from the Wiregrass, and after Saturday nothing feels like peanuts. Lila Shenandoah has the pom-poms out in Harrisonburg and a cheer ready for a title game rematch.

Troy (3-4) at James Madison (7-1), Thursday at 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN2 at Bridgeforth Stadium in Harrisonburg. It's a Sun Belt game. DraftKings has James Madison Dukes −21 with a total of 49.5. My card: Troy Trojans +21, Over 49.5, and a final of James Madison Dukes 38, Troy Trojans 18.

Lila speaks for James Madison. "Give me a three-one! We are 3-1 all-time against Troy and we won the Sun Belt title game 31-14 last December. Camden Coleman has 14 touchdown passes and two interceptions. Purple reign, y'all."

Marlo speaks for Troy. "Saturday was the hardest one I've watched, buddy. We led UL Monroe 14-3 with under three minutes left and lost 21-14. Goose Crowder has been nursing a shoulder, but he's listed probable. But Donnie Smith has six sacks, and we gave you all you wanted in 2023, 16-14."

Here is the part that matters. James Madison will play this one without George Pettaway, its leading rusher with 824 yards and eight touchdowns. Catie Harper of the Daily News-Record confirmed it on Oct. 28, and RotoWire and the daily paper in Winchester have it too. Nick Herman and Seth Cromwell are expected to carry the load. On the other side, Troy quarterback Goose Crowder is listed probable on the Sun Belt availability report, per RotoWire.

Why Troy? Because 21 points is a lot to ask of a home team missing its lead back. The Dukes have also won 20-13 over Liberty and 27-24 at Georgia Southern. Troy's defense allows 24.9 a game, and Smith can make Coleman uncomfortable. My final has the Dukes by 20, a point inside the number.

The worry is obvious. Troy's quarterbacks combined to go 9 of 18 against UL Monroe, and the Trojans lost 33-6 at Louisiana the week before. If Crowder's shoulder acts up and the offense stalls again, this gets away.

Why the Over/Under? James Madison scores 41.4 a game by itself, and Troy has to chase. DraftKings had this total at 52.5 on Monday and Action Network's opening number was 51.5, so the drop to 49.5 buys us room. My 38-18 adds up to 56, six and a half over the over/under 49.5.

Now the room. The Formula has James Madison 35-19 and likes Troy and the Over. Next Man Up has the Dukes 41-16 and likes James Madison and the Over. Follow The Money likes James Madison and the Under after the moves. In The Trenches sees the run gaps cancel and has no pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "Forty-one to sixteen. Troy has not found a passer."

The Formula: "Mine had the Dukes by 16 before anyone mentioned Pettaway."

Follow The Money: "Spread up three and a half, total down three. The market thinks Troy cannot score."

Bottom of the card: a great home team without its best runner, laying three touchdowns to a wounded road team that has played the Dukes tough before. Lila gets the win. Marlo gets the points.

James Madison Dukes 38, Troy Trojans 18. Troy Trojans cover the 21. Over 49.5.
