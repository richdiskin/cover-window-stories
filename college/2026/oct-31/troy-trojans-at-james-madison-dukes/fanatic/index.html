Troy vs James Madison: TROYAPALOOZA Meets Nightmare at UREC

Troy Trojans (3-4) at James Madison Dukes (7-1)

7:30 PM ET Thu Oct 29 · Bridgeforth Stadium · ESPN2

Troy Trojans vs James Madison Dukes is the Week 9 Thursday night game at Bridgeforth Stadium, two nights after Troy fan Marlo Pickens ate fried Oreos at TROYAPALOOZA in the Janice Hawkins Cultural Arts Park and one night after James Madison fan Lila Shenandoah walked the Hallway of Terror at Nightmare at UREC. Lila says the scariest thing in Harrisonburg this week is still Camden Coleman, and Marlo, a former Troy snapper off a Wiregrass peanut farm, says Goose Crowder and a Troy defense with Donnie Smith in it are about to give the Dukes their own haunted hallway without George Pettaway.

Lila: The hallway ended in a room full of clowns, Marlo. I cheered at them. Give me a scream!

Marlo: We had the student band POPulus at the amphitheater, a costume contest, and a food tent frying Oreos. Troy throws a better party, and you know it.

Lila: Then save some energy, because it's Thursday night in the Shenandoah Valley. Troy (3-4, 1-2 Sun Belt) at James Madison (7-1, 4-1 Sun Belt), 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN2.

The biggest news came on the Sun Belt's game-day report, posted at 10:33 a.m. ET Thursday, Oct. 29. Pettaway, the James Madison running back who leads the team with 824 yards and eight touchdowns, is out. Catie Harper of the Daily News-Record reported on Oct. 28 that he was hurt late against App State. On the other sideline, Crowder has been dealing with a shoulder injury and is listed available.

Marlo: Available is the prettiest word I've heard since Saturday.

Saturday is the sore spot for Troy. The Trojans led UL Monroe 14-3 with less than three minutes to play and lost 21-14 at home as the Warhawks rallied. Jordan Lovett ran 17 times for 70 yards.

Marlo: I sat through that fourth quarter with a sack of boiled peanuts and didn't taste one.

Lila: Harrisonburg knows a hard Saturday too. James Madison lost 34-31 in overtime at App State on Oct. 22, its first loss of the season. Coleman went 22 of 39 for 232 yards and a touchdown, and Braeden Wisloski caught two balls for 84 yards and a score.

Coleman is why the Dukes stay calm. He's 131 of 212 for 1,567 yards, 14 touchdowns and only two interceptions. Corey Scott leads the receivers with 24 catches for 371 yards and four scores. On defense, Nakian Jackson has 59 tackles and JT Hooten has three sacks.

Lila: Two interceptions in 212 throws. That is a quarterback who reads the hallway before he walks it.

Marlo: Then let me introduce him to the fellow waiting at the end of it. Donnie Smith is a defensive back with six sacks, and he finds quarterbacks the way a crow finds a fresh-planted row.

Crowder is 102 of 183 for 1,162 yards, nine touchdowns and five interceptions. Gavin Griffin has 398 rushing yards, TK Keyes has 13 catches for 282 yards and three scores, and Keno Jones leads the Troy defense with 52 tackles.

The men in headsets have both had busy years. Billy Napier is in his first season at James Madison after going 40-12 at Louisiana from 2018 to 2021 and coaching Florida from 2022 to 2025. His Dukes score 41.4 points a game and allow 18.2. Gerad Parker, hired Dec. 18, 2023, is in his third season at Troy, and his team gets a short week and a long trip after a hard Saturday.

Marlo: A short week is just a short row. You hoe faster and complain later.

The Dukes have won 11 straight at Bridgeforth, which seats 24,877, and the town is already in costume. The Harrisonburg Fire Department runs a Trunk or Treat at the Public Safety Building on North Main Street on Friday, and on Halloween night Clementine hosts its annual Halloween show of local bands playing other bands' songs, one doing Devo and another doing Fugazi.

Lila: A Devo tribute act called Are We Not Crabs. Harrisonburg has a sense of humor, Marlo. Troy has a sense of fried food.

Marlo: Fried food never lost a title game.

That one stings in Alabama. This is the fifth meeting, and James Madison leads 3-1, including 2-0 at home. The teams met twice in the FCS playoffs, in 1994 and 1999. The Dukes won 16-14 in 2023, and last December they took the Sun Belt Championship Game 31-14 and went on to the College Football Playoff.

Marlo: Sixteen to fourteen. We were close enough to smell the popcorn.

Lila: Then come smell it again. Walk the hallway, Marlo. Bring Crowder. Just don't expect the clowns at the end to be the scariest thing you see in Harrisonburg.

My call: James Madison Dukes 38, Troy Trojans 18.
