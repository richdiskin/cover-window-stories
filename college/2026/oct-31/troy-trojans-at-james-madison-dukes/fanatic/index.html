Title Game Rematch on a Thursday: Troy Visits James Madison in Harrisonburg

Troy Trojans (3-4) at James Madison Dukes (7-1)

7:30 PM ET Thu Oct 29 · Bridgeforth Stadium · ESPN2

Troy (3-4, 1-2 Sun Belt) at James Madison (7-1, 4-1 Sun Belt). Thursday, Oct. 29, 7:30 p.m. ET, Bridgeforth Stadium, Harrisonburg, Va., ESPN2.

Your hosts are Marlo Pickens, a former Troy snapper from a Wiregrass peanut farm who never met a peanut pun he didn't like, and Lila Shenandoah, a former JMU cheer captain who turns every stat into a chant.

Marlo: Lila, I brought boiled peanuts for the whole Shenandoah Valley. Salted, Cajun or plain.

Lila: Purple reign, y'all! Give me a T, give me an R, give me a warm welcome for our guests!

Players to watch

James Madison quarterback Camden Coleman is 131 of 212 for 1,567 yards, 14 touchdowns and just two interceptions. Wide receiver Corey Scott leads the Dukes with 24 catches for 371 yards and four scores. On defense, linebacker Nakian Jackson has 59 tackles and linebacker JT Hooten has three sacks.

For Troy, quarterback Goose Crowder is 102 of 183 for 1,162 yards, nine touchdowns and five interceptions. Running back Gavin Griffin has 398 rushing yards, and wide receiver TK Keyes has 13 catches for 282 yards and three touchdowns. Defensive back Donnie Smith has six sacks, and linebacker Keno Jones leads the team with 52 tackles.

Marlo: Six sacks from a defensive back. That boy gets to the quarterback like a squirrel gets to a peanut.

What happened last week

James Madison lost 34-31 in overtime at App State on Oct. 22, its first loss of the season. Coleman went 22 of 39 for 232 yards and a touchdown, and Braeden Wisloski caught two passes for 84 yards and a score.

Troy lost 21-14 to UL Monroe at home on Oct. 24. The Trojans led 14-3 with less than three minutes to play before the Warhawks rallied in the final minutes. Jordan Lovett ran 17 times for 70 yards.

Lila: Give me a deep breath! Both teams want to turn the page.

Injuries and availability

The Sun Belt has not posted an availability report for this game. James Madison running back George Pettaway, the team's leading rusher with 824 yards and eight touchdowns, is out with a lower-body injury, according to the Daily News-Record. Nick Herman and Seth Cromwell are the next backs up. Troy quarterback Goose Crowder has been dealing with a shoulder injury, and coach Gerad Parker had not settled on a quarterback plan early in the week, according to AL.com. Tucker Kilcrease played after Crowder came out against UL Monroe. Watch the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Billy Napier is in his first season at James Madison. He went 40-12 at Louisiana from 2018 to 2021 and then coached Florida from 2022 to 2025. His Dukes score 41.4 points a game and allow 18.2.

Gerad Parker is in his third season at Troy. He was hired on Dec. 18, 2023. His team has a short week and a long trip after a tough Saturday.

Marlo: Coach Parker will have those boys ready. A short week is like a short peanut row. You just hoe faster.

Player storylines

The Troy quarterback room is the big question. Whoever plays will face a James Madison defense that allows just 18.2 points a game. On the other side, the Dukes have to replace Pettaway's carries, and Smith will be hunting Coleman all night.

Coleman has been careful with the football. Two interceptions in 212 attempts is a fine way to keep a team in every game.

Lila: Give me a T, give me an O! Turnover margin, y'all!

What's happening on campus

Bridgeforth Stadium seats 24,877, and the Dukes have won 11 straight games there. It is a Thursday night in the Shenandoah Valley two days before Halloween, so the night already feels like a party.

Marlo: If anybody shows up dressed as a peanut, Lila, it's me. Nobody's surprised.

Lila: Give me a P, give me an E, give me a costume contest!

Anything else fun and true

This is the fifth all-time meeting, and James Madison leads 3-1, including 2-0 at home. The teams met twice in the FCS playoffs, in 1994 and 1999. James Madison won the 2025 Sun Belt Championship Game 31-14 over Troy and went on to the College Football Playoff. In 2023, the Dukes won 16-14.

Marlo: Sixteen to fourteen in 2023. We were right there, Lila. That's not peanuts.

Lila: Give me a handshake, Marlo. See you Thursday.

My call: James Madison Dukes 38, Troy Trojans 18.
