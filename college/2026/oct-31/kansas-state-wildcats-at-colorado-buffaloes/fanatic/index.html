Harvest Time in the Flatirons: Kansas State Visits Colorado in Boulder

Kansas State Wildcats (4-3) at Colorado Buffaloes (2-5)

2:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Folsom Field · TNT

Kansas State (4-3, 1-3 Big 12) at Colorado (2-5, 0-4 Big 12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 2 p.m. ET, Folsom Field, Boulder, Colo., TNT.

Your hosts are Hattie Brandvold, a Kansas State fan with an agronomy degree who names every drive after a crop, and Moss Feldstein, a Colorado physicist who runs the numbers from a hammock under the Flatirons.

Moss: Welcome to Boulder, Hattie. Kombucha?

Hattie: Bless you, Moss, but I brought sweet tea and a field notebook with real Flint Hills dirt on it.

Moss: Dirt and data. My two best things after hacky sack.

Players to watch

Kansas State quarterback Avery Johnson does it all. He's 118 of 187 for 1,301 yards, 10 touchdowns and three interceptions, and he leads the team in rushing with 462 yards and three scores. Jacob Knuth threw the most passes at Arizona State last week. Jaron Tibbs leads the receivers with 34 catches for 325 yards and four touchdowns. On defense, end Wendell Gregory has 3.5 sacks and safety Logan Bartley has 45 tackles.

Colorado quarterback Isaac Wilson has thrown the most passes in each of the last four games. He's 77 of 128 for 979 yards, six touchdowns and seven interceptions. Julian Lewis threw the most in the first three games. JaQuail Smith leads the rushers with 298 yards, and Joseph Williams leads the receivers with 46 catches for 643 yards and five scores. Lamont Lester Jr. has five sacks, and linebacker Gideon Lampron leads the team with 65 tackles.

Hattie: Johnson running for 462 yards? That's winter wheat, Moss. Long, patient and golden.

What happened last week

Kansas State lost 28-17 at Arizona State on Oct. 24. Knuth was 26 of 41 for 194 yards and a touchdown, and Derrick Salley Jr. caught six balls for 63 yards.

Hattie: Dry sorghum patch. You can't rush a harvest. I planted a basil seed on Sunday.

Colorado lost 57-24 at Oklahoma State on Oct. 24. Wilson threw for 280 yards and two touchdowns, and Williams caught nine balls for 135 yards.

Moss: High variance. Physicists love that. Fans, less so. But Joseph Williams was a bright spot.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Johnson threw the most passes in K-State's first six games and Knuth did last week, while Wilson has done it in Colorado's last four, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Collin Klein was named Kansas State's 36th head coach on Dec. 4, 2025. He was a 2012 Heisman Trophy finalist and won the Johnny Unitas Award as the Wildcats' quarterback, and he's the first K-State alum to lead the program since Ellis Rainsberger in 1975-77.

Deion Sanders, "Coach Prime," has led Colorado since his hire on Dec. 3, 2022. He played 14 NFL seasons, won two Super Bowls, played nine Major League Baseball seasons, and is the only athlete to play in both a Super Bowl and a World Series.

Moss: Coach Prime keeps naming discipline as the thing to fix. I agree. My hammock agrees.

Hattie: Coach Klein grew up in our program. Homegrown crop.

Player storylines

The big matchup is the K-State ground game against a Colorado defense that allows 3.7 yards a carry. K-State's big guys up front have allowed only three sacks, so Johnson and company should have time. On the other side, Williams will be Wilson's top target, and Lester will try to chase down whoever plays quarterback for K-State.

What's happening on campus

Colorado has played at Folsom Field since 1924, and CFBD lists 50,183 seats. A Halloween afternoon under the Flatirons should bring plenty of black-and-gold costumes, and of course Ralphie will run.

Moss: I'm dressing as a hammock. Very relaxing for everyone.

Hattie: I'm dressing as a wheat sheaf. Very on brand.

Anything else fun and true

Kansas State has won the Sunflower Showdown against Kansas 18 straight times. Colorado won the 1990 national championship, shared with Georgia Tech, and has two Heisman winners, Rashaan Salaam in 1994 and Travis Hunter in 2024. When Bill Snyder took over in 1989, K-State was in the middle of a 27-game winless run, and he built it into a national contender. These teams have met each of the last two years: K-State won 31-28 in 2024 and 24-14 in 2025.

Hattie: Moss, whatever happens, supper's on me. We'll say grace first.

Moss: Grateful for that, Hattie, and for a mountain sunset afterward. Ralphie's running.

My call: Kansas State Wildcats 28, Colorado Buffaloes 23.
