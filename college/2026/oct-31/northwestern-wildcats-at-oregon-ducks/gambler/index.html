Northwestern Wildcats at Oregon Ducks

Northwestern Wildcats (6-1) at Oregon Ducks (4-3)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Autzen Stadium · Peacock

Northwestern vs Oregon prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on Oregon Ducks −3.5 and Over 47.5 at DraftKings, with a final of Oregon Ducks 29, Northwestern Wildcats 22. Autzen is a Halloween blackout, Oregon is wearing white feathers on black helmets for the first time, and a former Ducks head coach is running the other side's offense.

The case for Oregon is the home crowd and a Northwestern team that has been close on the road. The Wildcats lost 29-23 at Indiana and beat Michigan State 24-21 away from home. Oregon has won three of its four games at Autzen and beat Northwestern 34-14 last season. My final has Oregon by 7, past the 3.5.

Nix Wyatt-Aldana, already judging the tunnel lighting, has the Oregon case. "Matayo Uiagalelei has six sacks. Jordon Davison has seven touchdowns. And those helmets? Bold by design."

Ezra Lindqvist, clipboard and footnoted memo in hand, answers for Northwestern. "Aidan Chiles has 12 touchdown throws and one interception. We're 6-1. We scored 42 on Rutgers. Footnote: that's a lot."

What could go wrong is Oregon's offense. The Ducks scored 15 against UCLA, 17 against Nebraska and 10 at Illinois, and they have a new play-caller this week. Northwestern allows 16.6 points a game, and if the Ducks stall again, the Wildcats can win outright.

The Over is about Northwestern's offense. The Wildcats score 34.7 a game and put up 41 on Colorado, 45 on Ball State and 42 on Rutgers. Oregon scores 33.1 a game and has hit 34 or more three times. My 29-22 adds up to 51, above the 47.5.
