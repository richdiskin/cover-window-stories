Footnotes and Fashion: No. 20 Northwestern Visits Oregon on Halloween

Northwestern Wildcats (6-1) at Oregon Ducks (4-3)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Autzen Stadium · Peacock

No. 20 Northwestern (6-1, 3-1 Big Ten) at Oregon (4-3, 2-2 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, Autzen Stadium, Eugene, Ore., Peacock.

Your hosts are Ezra Lindqvist, a Northwestern data journalist who footnotes his own jokes, and Nix Wyatt-Aldana, a Eugene sneaker-and-uniform designer who reviews jerseys and route trees with equal ferocity.

Nix: Welcome to Autzen, Ezra! Before we begin, I have to review your outfit.

Ezra: I was afraid of that.

Nix: Purple quarter-zip, pumpkin pin for Halloween. Seven out of ten. The pin is carrying you.

Ezra: Footnote: the pin was my mother's idea, and she will be thrilled.

Players to watch

Northwestern quarterback Aidan Chiles has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 117 of 164 for 1,617 yards, 12 touchdowns and just one interception. Caleb Komolafe leads the rushers with 551 yards and five scores, and Griffin Wilde leads the receivers with 39 catches for 615 yards. Linebacker Braydon Brus leads the defense in both tackles, with 62, and sacks, with four.

For Oregon, Jordon Davison leads the rushers with 422 yards and seven touchdowns, and Dakorien Moore leads the receivers with 34 catches for 417 yards. Dylan Raiola threw the most passes last week, and Lanning has said Brock Thomas and Akili Smith Jr. are in the mix too. On defense, Matayo Uiagalelei has six sacks, and Koi Perich leads the team with 46 tackles.

Ezra: Chiles has one interception in seven games. I checked it three times.

Nix: Uiagalelei rushing the quarterback is pure runway, darling. Ten out of ten.

What happened last week

Northwestern beat Rutgers 42-14 at home on Oct. 24. Chiles threw for 219 yards and three touchdowns, Komolafe ran 13 times for 150 yards and a score, and Wilde caught nine balls for 133 yards and a touchdown.

Ezra: I've already filed my formal correction to everyone who doubted us. It's eleven pages.

Oregon lost 21-10 at Illinois on Oct. 24. Raiola completed 27 of 41 for 246 yards, Davison ran 14 times for 92 yards and the Ducks' touchdown, and Dakorien Moore caught nine balls.

Nix: Honest review? Somebody tripped on the runway. We get back up, we fix the hem.

Injuries and availability

The big news is at quarterback. On Monday, Oregon coach Dan Lanning said he doesn't expect Dante Moore back this season. Moore suffered a concussion at USC on Sept. 26 and has missed three games since. Before the injury he was 69 of 99 for 926 yards and nine touchdowns. Raiola and Thomas have started the last three games. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and Chiles has thrown the most passes for Northwestern every week, so check the teams' pregame notes.

Nix: We miss Dante. Get well, kid. You're always on the team.

What the coaches are up to

Lanning also made a change on his staff. Offensive coordinator Drew Mehringer was dismissed Sunday, and assistant coach Jay Johnson will coordinate the offense. Lanning was hired on Dec. 11, 2021, and his 2024 team went 13-1 and 9-0 in the Big Ten, winning the league title in its first season.

David Braun became Northwestern's interim coach in 2023, was named the permanent head coach that November and was the consensus Big Ten Coach of the Year. His team runs the ball and protects it, with only two turnovers lost all season.

Ezra: Two turnovers. Footnote: that's not a typo.

Player storylines

Raiola is the one to watch. He came off the bench against Nebraska, his former team, and threw the winning 4-yard touchdown to tight end Jamari Johnson with 28 seconds left. On the other side, Komolafe and Northwestern's big guys up front will test an Oregon defense that allows very little on the ground. These two met last season, and Oregon won 34-14.

What's happening on campus

It's Halloween in Eugene, and Autzen Stadium, opened in 1967 and listed at 54,000 seats, always sounds much bigger than that. Northwestern fans have had a wonderful fall at home. The new Ryan Field opened Oct. 2 against Penn State, exactly 100 years to the day after the original stadium hosted its first game, and the Wildcats have won all four home games.

Nix: I'm dressing as a jersey. A ten-out-of-ten jersey.

Ezra: I'm dressing as a footnote. Very small, very important.

Anything else fun and true

Oregon has played football since 1894, joined the Big Ten in 2024 and went 13-2 last season. Marcus Mariota became Oregon's first Heisman Trophy winner in 2014. Northwestern opened this season with two games at lakefront Martin Stadium, right on Lake Michigan, before moving into the new Ryan Field.

Nix: Whatever happens, Ezra, dinner's on me. We'll say grace first.

Ezra: Deal, Nix. Footnote: I'm holding you to it.

My call: Oregon Ducks 29, Northwestern Wildcats 22.
