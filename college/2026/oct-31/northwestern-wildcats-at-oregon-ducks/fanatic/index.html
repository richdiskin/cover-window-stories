Northwestern vs Oregon: White Wings, Black Helmets and an Old Coach

Northwestern Wildcats (6-1) at Oregon Ducks (4-3)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Autzen Stadium · Peacock

Northwestern Wildcats vs Oregon Ducks is a Week 9 Halloween blackout at Autzen Stadium, where the Ducks will wear black helmets with white feathers for the first time, against a No. 20 Northwestern offense run by a former Ducks head coach. Ezra Lindqvist, a Northwestern data journalist who footnotes his own jokes, says Aidan Chiles, Griffin Wilde and David Braun's Wildcats keep rolling, and Nix Wyatt-Aldana, a Eugene sneaker-and-uniform designer who reviews jerseys and route trees with equal ferocity, says Dylan Raiola, Matayo Uiagalelei and Dan Lanning's Ducks bounce back.

Nix: Welcome to Autzen, Ezra! Before we begin, I have to review your outfit.

Ezra: I was afraid of that.

Nix: Purple quarter-zip, pumpkin pin for Halloween. Seven out of ten. The pin is carrying you.

Ezra: Footnote: the pin was my mother's idea, and she will be thrilled.

On Wednesday, Oregon showed its look for the game: black helmets with white feathers, black jerseys with gray numbers and white pants. The Oregonian's Aaron Fentress noted that "Oregon has worn black helmets with silver feathers over the past two seasons, but not with white feathers." The team's caption was three words: "Bold by design." Fans are asked to wear black, and On SI says this is the second of three blackout games at Autzen this season.

Nix: White wings on a black helmet, Ezra. Ten out of ten. I may need to sit down.

Ezra: Footnote: you are already sitting down.

Nix: I'm sitting down harder.

The other story is on Northwestern's sideline. Northwestern's offensive coordinator used to coach Oregon. On SI wrote that Lanning has warned about how well that Northwestern offense works, and The Oregonian's headline called it a matchup "against a former Ducks coach." Oregon also has a new play-caller this week in Jay Johnson.

Ezra: A former Ducks coach, back in Eugene, on Halloween. Footnote: this is the scariest thing that's happened here all year.

Nix: Old coaches are always welcome, Ezra. Their offenses aren't.

Northwestern is 6-1 and 3-1 in the Big Ten. Its only loss was 29-23 at Indiana, and it beat Penn State 34-13 and Rutgers 42-14 last week. Against Rutgers, Chiles went 19 of 25 for 219 yards and three touchdowns, Caleb Komolafe ran 13 times for 150 yards, and Wilde caught nine balls for 133 yards.

Ezra: Forty-two points, Nix. I footnoted every one.

Chiles has led the Wildcats in throws in all seven games. He's 117 of 164 for 1,617 yards, 12 touchdowns and one interception. Komolafe has 551 rushing yards and five scores, Wilde has 39 catches for 615 yards, and Braydon Brus has 62 tackles and four sacks. Northwestern scores 34.7 points a game and allows 16.6.

Nix: One interception, Ezra? That's a clean silhouette. I hate it.

Oregon is 4-3 and 2-2 in the league, and it lost 21-10 at Illinois on Oct. 24. Raiola went 27 of 41 for 246 yards, Jordon Davison ran for 92 yards and a touchdown, and Dakorien Moore caught nine balls. The Ducks have lost three times, 39-31 at Oklahoma State, 17-15 to UCLA and 21-10 at Illinois. Raiola has led them in throws in three of the last four games.

Davison has 422 rushing yards and seven touchdowns, Dakorien Moore has 34 catches for 417 yards, Uiagalelei has six sacks and Koi Perich has 46 tackles. Oregon scores 33.1 a game and allows 20.7.

Nix: Six sacks for Matayo. He wears the uniform. The uniform doesn't wear him.

These two met last September in Evanston, and Oregon won 34-14 wearing a combination called "High voltage."

Ezra: Footnote: I have not recovered from High voltage.

Lanning was hired on Dec. 11, 2021. Braun became Northwestern's interim coach in 2023 and was named the permanent head coach that November.

Nix: Coach Lanning would tackle a bear for this team.

Ezra: Coach Braun would write the bear a thank-you note. Then tackle it.

The matchup is Komolafe and Northwestern's big guys up front against Uiagalelei and an Oregon front that needs a good day. On the other side, the Ducks want Davison running early so Raiola can play ahead of the chains.

No official availability report is out for this game, so check the teams' pregame notes.

Nix: I'm dressing as a white feather.

Ezra: I'm dressing as a footnote. Very small. Very important.

Nix: Whatever happens, Ezra, dinner's on me.

Ezra: Deal, Nix. Footnote: I'm holding you to it.

My call: Oregon Ducks 29, Northwestern Wildcats 22.
