Minnesota vs Indiana: Homecoming, Hoosierween and GameDay at the Fountain

Minnesota Golden Gophers (5-2) at Indiana Hoosiers (7-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Memorial Stadium (Bloomington, IN) · CBS

Minnesota Golden Gophers vs Indiana Hoosiers is Week 9 Homecoming on Halloween in Bloomington, with ESPN's College GameDay at Showalter Fountain from 9 a.m. to noon ET and a Hoosierween carnival in Dunn Meadow the night before. Tor Engebretsen, a Minnesota ski-hill Gopher who owns enough coats to lend one out, says Drake Lindsey, Maverick Baranowski and P.J. Fleck's Gophers crash the party, and Mallory Penhallow, a former Miss Monroe County runner-up, says Josh Hoover, Charlie Becker and Curt Cignetti's Hoosiers keep the crown.

Mallory: Welcome to Bloomington, Tor! Hello, Hoosier Nation!

Tor: Thank you, Mallory. I brought cocoa, and a spare coat in case you get chilly.

Mallory: That's very sweet. I'll wear it over the sash.

Tor: And I should mention we're carrying a bronze pig around this week. We kept Floyd of Rosedale on Saturday.

IU Bloomington Today calls the game a matchup that "pits the defending Big Ten and national champion Indiana Hoosiers against the Minnesota Golden Gophers." Homecoming week wraps with a parade Friday at 5:30 p.m. ET, beginning at Kirkwood and Washington Street and heading east to the Sample Gates. After the parade, the Union Board hosts a student carnival in Dunn Meadow with the theme "Hoosierween," and costumes are encouraged.

Mallory: Hoosierween, Tor. I'm going as last year's trophy.

Tor: I'm going as a Minnesota winter. I just have to stand here.

Then Saturday, GameDay is live from Showalter Fountain with Rece Davis, Desmond Howard, Pat McAfee, Nick Saban and Kirk Herbstreit. Tailgate fields open at 9 a.m. ET, and the stadium gates open 90 minutes before the 3:30 p.m. ET kickoff on Merchants Bank Field. The university's advice for the day is to bring extra layers in case it gets cold.

Tor: Extra layers. Mallory, they wrote that advisory for me.

Mallory: They wrote it because of you, Tor. You're wearing four coats.

Indiana is 7-1 and 4-1 in the Big Ten. Its only loss was 19-15 to Ohio State on Oct. 17, and last week it won 13-6 in overtime at Michigan. Turbo Richard ran 19 times for 68 yards in that one.

Mallory: Thirteen to six in overtime, Tor. Ugly is still a win. My pageant coach said that about my baton routine.

Hoover has led Indiana in throws in all eight games. He's 116 of 187 for 1,741 yards, 18 touchdowns and one interception. Richard has 711 rushing yards and six scores, Becker has 42 catches for 726 yards and eight touchdowns, Tobi Osunsanmi has nine sacks and Isaiah Jones has 61 tackles. The Hoosiers allow 12.0 points a game and have shut out Howard and Western Kentucky.

Tor: Eighteen touchdowns and one interception. Mallory, does Hoover ever get cold?

Mallory: He's never been to Minnesota, Tor.

Minnesota is 5-2 and 3-1 in the league, and it beat Iowa 13-10 on Oct. 24 to keep Floyd. Before that, it beat Michigan 20-14 and won 27-24 at Washington. The losses were 38-13 to Mississippi State and 41-13 at Purdue.

Lindsey has led the Gophers in throws in all seven games and is 141 of 222 for 1,472 yards, nine touchdowns and six interceptions. TJ Thomas has 325 rushing yards, Javon Tracy has 39 catches for 420 yards, Baranowski has 51 tackles and TJ Bush Jr. has four sacks. Minnesota allows 20.1 points a game.

Tor: Thirteen to ten over Iowa. That's a classic. Nobody scores, everybody's happy.

Cignetti was hired at Indiana on Nov. 30, 2023, and in 2025 the Hoosiers went 16-0 and beat Miami 27-21 for their first national championship. Fleck has led Minnesota since 2017.

Tor: Coach Fleck rows the boat. We all row together.

Mallory: Coach Cignetti said it best: "It can be done." I have it on a pillow.

Minnesota won the last two meetings, 38-31 in 2018 and 35-14 in 2021.

Tor: Two in a row, Mallory. I'm just saying it out loud so the pig can hear.

The matchup is Becker and Hoover against a Minnesota defense that's stingy against the throw. On the other side, the Gophers need their big guys up front to open running room against a front led by Osunsanmi.

The Big Ten's initial availability report, posted at 8 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists seven Minnesota players out, quarterback Michael Merdinger among them, and Drake Lindsey is not on it. Indiana lists two players out, with Josh Hoover probable.

Mallory: Whatever happens, Tor, I'll save you a spot at the fountain.

Tor: Deal, Mallory. The cocoa's on me, and the coat is yours to keep.

My call: Indiana Hoosiers 34, Minnesota Golden Gophers 11.
