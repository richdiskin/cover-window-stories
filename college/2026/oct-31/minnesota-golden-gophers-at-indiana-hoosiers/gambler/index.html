Minnesota Golden Gophers at Indiana Hoosiers

Minnesota Golden Gophers (5-2) at Indiana Hoosiers (7-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Memorial Stadium (Bloomington, IN) · CBS

Minnesota vs Indiana prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on Indiana Hoosiers −20.5 and Over 41.5 at DraftKings, with a final of Indiana Hoosiers 34, Minnesota Golden Gophers 11. It's Homecoming on Halloween in Bloomington, and College GameDay is at Showalter Fountain in the morning.

The case for Indiana is a defending champion at home against a visitor that has been blown out on the road. Indiana allows 12.0 points a game, beat North Texas 52-16 and shut out Howard and Western Kentucky at home. Minnesota lost 41-13 at Purdue and 38-13 to Mississippi State. My final has Indiana by 23, past the 20.5.

Mallory Penhallow, rhinestone clicker in hand, has the Indiana case. "Josh Hoover has 18 touchdown throws and one interception. Tobi Osunsanmi has nine sacks. We allow 12.0 a game."

Tor Engebretsen, in his fourth coat of the day, answers for Minnesota. "We beat Iowa 13-10 and Michigan 20-14. Maverick Baranowski has 51 tackles. Cold enough for ya?"

What could go wrong is Indiana's last two games. The Hoosiers lost 19-15 to Ohio State and won 13-6 in overtime at Michigan, and Hoover went 8 of 18 for 81 yards in Ann Arbor. Minnesota keeps games low, and its wins over Iowa and Michigan both stayed under 35 total points. The Big Ten's initial availability report, posted at 8 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists Minnesota quarterback Michael Merdinger out and Hoover probable, and Drake Lindsey is not on it.

The Over rests on Indiana's offense. The Hoosiers score 33.6 a game, and Hoover's 18 touchdowns came with Charlie Becker catching eight of them. Minnesota doesn't need to do much to help, because Indiana scored at least 38 in three of its five home games. My 34-11 adds up to 45, above the 41.5.

For the record, Action Network's consensus line opened at Indiana −20.5 with a total of 41.5 this week, and neither number has moved.

The room is with Indiana and the Over, and each has one backer. Next Man Up has Indiana 33-9, likes Indiana −20.5, and is on the Over without backing it. The Formula has Indiana 34-12, is on Indiana −20.5 without backing it, and likes the Over. In The Trenches has no pick. Follow The Money has no pick in this game, and it keeps no score of its own. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

A champion at home, a defense that allows 12 a game and a visitor that has struggled on the road. Give me the Hoosiers and the Over.

Indiana Hoosiers 34, Minnesota Golden Gophers 11. Indiana Hoosiers cover the 20.5. Over 41.5.
