Minnesota Golden Gophers at Indiana Hoosiers

Minnesota Golden Gophers (5-2) at Indiana Hoosiers (7-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Memorial Stadium (Bloomington, IN) · CBS

Mallory Penhallow is polishing a rhinestone clicker at a tailgate table outside Memorial Stadium when Tor Engebretsen arrives in his fourth winter coat of the day, carrying a thermos. "Tor," Mallory says, "that's a lot of coats." Tor unscrews the thermos. "Cold enough for ya? Good. I brought cocoa."

Minnesota (5-2) at No. 4 Indiana (7-1), Saturday at 3:30 p.m. ET on CBS at Memorial Stadium in Bloomington. It's a Big Ten game: Minnesota is 3-1 in league play and Indiana is 4-1. DraftKings has Indiana Hoosiers −20.5 with a total of 41.5. My card: Indiana Hoosiers −20.5, Over 41.5, and a final of Indiana Hoosiers 34, Minnesota Golden Gophers 11.

Mallory speaks for Indiana, clicker raised. "It's the explosives, darling! Josh Hoover has 18 touchdown throws and one interception. Charlie Becker has 726 receiving yards and eight scores. Tobi Osunsanmi has nine sacks. We allow 12.0 points a game, and we just won at Michigan in overtime. Full pageant wave."

Tor speaks for Minnesota, cocoa steaming. "Well, we beat Iowa 13-10 on Saturday, and we beat Michigan 20-14 and won at Washington. Maverick Baranowski has 51 tackles. TJ Bush Jr. has four sacks. And our defense doesn't give up much through the air. We're polite, but stubborn."

Why Indiana? Because the Hoosiers have been brutal against teams that can't move the ball. They beat North Texas 52-16, Howard 55-0 and Western Kentucky 38-0 in Bloomington, and won 47-15 at Rutgers. Minnesota's run game has struggled all year, and Indiana's defense allows 12.0 points a game. Purdue beat the Gophers 41-13, and Mississippi State beat them 38-13. My final has Indiana winning by more than three touchdowns.

The risk is the Indiana offense's last two games. Hoover went 20 of 40 in a 19-15 loss to Ohio State, then 8 of 18 for 81 yards at Michigan, where it took overtime to win 13-6. Minnesota's defense is sturdy, and if this turns into a slog, 20.5 is a lot of points to lay.

Why the Over? Indiana scores 33.6 points a game. Becker and Turbo Richard, who has 711 rushing yards, give the Hoosiers big plays. Minnesota scored 59 on Eastern Illinois and 41 on Akron,. The total has dropped since Monday, when DraftKings had it at 43.5. My 34-11 adds up to 45, over 41.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Indiana 34-12, and Next Man Up has Indiana 33-9. Both side with Indiana, and both land on the Over, with The Formula liking it. Follow The Money goes the other way: it likes Minnesota with the points and likes the Under after the total fell. In The Trenches sits out, since each run game has a matchup it likes and they cancel out.

Next Man Up started it: "Indiana at home is a different animal. Mallory, start the wave."

Follow The Money pushed back: "The money moved to Minnesota and to fewer points. I'm following."

The Formula answered: "Indiana's defense does the heavy lifting. The points still clear 41.5."

Bottom of the card: a fierce Indiana defense, a Minnesota offense that struggles to run, and a home crowd that expects a big afternoon. Tor offers Mallory cocoa, and she accepts graciously. Give me Indiana and the Over.

Indiana Hoosiers 34, Minnesota Golden Gophers 11. Indiana Hoosiers cover the 20.5. Over 41.5.
