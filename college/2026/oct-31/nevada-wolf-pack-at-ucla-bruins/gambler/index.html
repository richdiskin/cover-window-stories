Nevada Wolf Pack at UCLA Bruins

Nevada Wolf Pack (3-4) at UCLA Bruins (7-0)

4:15 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Rose Bowl · BTN

Kenzie Rhee is framing the Rose Bowl through her fingers like a camera when Ridge Corbell walks up in a snowboard jacket, squinting at the sunshine. "No powder in Pasadena?" he asks. Kenzie lowers her hands. "Darling, this is a movie set, not a ski lodge." Ridge grins. "Then let's send it, Kenzie. Roll camera."

Nevada (3-4, 2-2 Mountain West) visits No. 7 UCLA (7-0, 5-0 Big Ten) Saturday at 4:15 p.m. ET on BTN at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena. DraftKings has UCLA Bruins −22.5 with a total of 44.5. On Monday, DraftKings had UCLA −23.5 and 48.5. My card: Nevada Wolf Pack +22.5, Over 44.5, and a final of UCLA Bruins 34, Nevada Wolf Pack 14.

Ridge speaks for Nevada. "We beat San José State 28-13 last week. Luke Duncan threw two touchdowns with no turnovers, and he spent his first three college seasons at UCLA. Our leading rusher has 582 yards and five touchdowns, and EJ Smith has 76 tackles. Coach Jeff Choate wants to keep this close into the fourth quarter. Sick."

Kenzie speaks for UCLA. "We beat Michigan State 28-10, darling. Ty Dieffenbach threw a touchdown, and Anthony Woods ran for 112 yards. Nico Iamaleava is out for the season after a knee injury at Oregon, so Ty is our lead now. Wayne Knight has 12 rushing touchdowns, Sammy Omosigho has five and a half sacks, and Jalen Woods has 59 tackles."

Why Nevada? Because 22.5 is a lot of points for a UCLA offense that has slowed down. In their last three games, the Bruins won 17-15 at Oregon, 17-7 against Wisconsin and 28-10 against Michigan State. Nevada runs the ball and can shorten the game. My final has UCLA winning by 20, short of 22.5.

The risk is Nevada's quarterback room. Carter Jones and AJ Bianco both missed last week, and Choate hasn't said who will start. Nevada also lost 20-7 at home to Montana State and 31-17 at North Dakota State. If the Wolf Pack turn it over, UCLA's defense could put this away early.

Why the Over? UCLA has scored 45 at Cal, 52 against Purdue and 54 at Maryland, and Knight finds the end zone almost every week. Nevada scored 49 against Western Kentucky and 33 against Air Force. The total has dropped from where it opened, but I think UCLA still gets there. My 34-14 adds up to 48, over 44.5.

Now the room. The Formula has UCLA 34-15 and likes Nevada and the Over. Next Man Up has UCLA 34-13, sides with Nevada, and likes the Over. Follow The Money likes Nevada after the spread came down a point, and likes the Under after the total fell. In The Trenches sits out: the Nevada run game and the UCLA run game each have a matchup they like, so they cancel out.

Follow The Money started it: "The total dropped. I go Under."

The Formula fired back: "UCLA scores 34 by itself. Over."

Next Man Up said: "UCLA wins big, just not that big."

Bottom of the card: an unbeaten UCLA team with a new quarterback, a Nevada team with a quarterback question and a Halloween crowd at the Rose Bowl. Give me the Wolf Pack and the Over.

UCLA Bruins 34, Nevada Wolf Pack 14. Nevada Wolf Pack cover the 22.5. Over 44.5.
