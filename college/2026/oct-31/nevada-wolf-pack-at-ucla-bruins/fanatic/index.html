Nevada vs UCLA: Snowboards, Storyboards and a Rose Bowl Debut

Nevada Wolf Pack (3-4) at UCLA Bruins (7-0)

4:15 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Rose Bowl · BTN

Nevada Wolf Pack vs UCLA Bruins is a Week 9 Halloween afternoon at the Rose Bowl, where Nevada makes its first trip to Pasadena and UCLA students get in free and get Halloween candy on Bruin Family Weekend. Ridge Corbell, a Tahoe ski patroller and snowboarder whose motto is "Send It," says Dominic Kelley, EJ Smith and Ky Woods can make it a game, and Kenzie Rhee, a Westwood film-school producer who storyboards every drive, says Wayne Knight and Sammy Omosigho keep No. 7 UCLA unbeaten.

Kenzie: I've storyboarded your whole afternoon, Ridge. Panel one: you see the San Gabriel Mountains and forget to watch the kickoff.

Ridge: Panel two: I ask why there's no snow on them.

Kenzie: Panel three: I explain Southern California.

This is only the second meeting between these teams. The first was the 2013 season opener at the Rose Bowl, and UCLA won 58-20. Nevada trailed just 17-13 at halftime before UCLA scored 20 points in the third quarter, with a blocked punt returned for a touchdown. The Bruins' quarterback that day was Brett Hundley, and he's on the Big Ten Network call Saturday as the analyst.

Ridge: So the guy who beat us is calling the game.

Kenzie: He'll be fair. Mostly.

UCLA is 7-0 and 5-0 in the Big Ten, its first such marks since 2005. Bob Chesney, who came over from James Madison, is the first coach in UCLA history to open his tenure 7-0. Knight has 627 rushing yards and 12 rushing touchdowns, and he has run for a score in all seven games. Anthony Woods has added 459 yards and six touchdowns. Omosigho has 5.5 sacks and leads the country with five forced fumbles. Last week, Ty Dieffenbach threw a 63-yard touchdown to Brian Rowe Jr. to open the second half of a 28-10 win over Michigan State.

Kenzie: That's the best shot of the year. Wide angle, the ball in the air, Rowe running under it.

Ridge: You'd frame a punt if it had nice light.

Nevada is 3-4 and 2-2 in the Mountain West after a 28-13 win over San José State. Jeff Choate's roster has 17 Northern Nevada products, and Reno's Marshaun Brown caught a touchdown and threw one in that game. Kelley has 582 rushing yards and five touchdowns. Smith has 76 tackles, fifth in the country. Ky Woods returned a kickoff 90 yards against San José State and was tackled eight yards short of the end zone.

Ridge: Eight yards from the end zone. That's the difference between a tidy run and eating snow.

Kenzie: I'd have cut away before the tackle. Better ending.

The Wolf Pack defense is the reason Ridge drove south. Nevada allows 153.9 passing yards a game, sixth in the country, and only Western Kentucky has thrown for 200 yards against it. UCLA runs for 233.0 yards a game, though, and allows 15.3 points.

Ridge: We stop the throws, you run it. Fine. We'll sit in the snow and wait.

Kenzie: There's no snow, Ridge.

Ridge: I brought my own. It's in the cooler.

Kenzie: That's ice for the drinks.

Kenzie: Halftime is a dance performance and the UCLA Marching Band. Please don't try to join either one.

Ridge: I make no promises. Send it.

Kenzie: I'm storyboarding security walking you back to your seat.

Ridge: Make it a good shot. Slow motion.

Kenzie: Deal. Whatever happens, Ridge, a costume from the gift shop is on me.

Ridge: I'm already wearing one. Ski patrol.

Kenzie: That's your work jacket.

My call: UCLA Bruins 34, Nevada Wolf Pack 14.
