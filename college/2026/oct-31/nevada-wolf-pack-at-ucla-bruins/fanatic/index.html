Snowboards and Storyboards: Nevada Visits No. 7 UCLA on Halloween

Nevada Wolf Pack (3-4) at UCLA Bruins (7-0)

4:15 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Rose Bowl · BTN

Nevada (3-4, 2-2 Mountain West) at No. 7 UCLA (7-0, 5-0 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 4:15 p.m. ET, Rose Bowl, Pasadena, Calif., BTN.

Your hosts are Ridge Corbell, a Nevada fan, Tahoe ski patroller and snowboarder whose motto is "Send It," and Kenzie Rhee, a UCLA fan and Westwood film-school producer who storyboards every drive.

Kenzie: Welcome to Pasadena, Ridge! Sunshine, mountains in the distance and an unbeaten team. I'd greenlight it.

Ridge: Thanks, Kenzie. Those mountains have no snow on them, but I'll allow it.

Kenzie: This week's logline: "Mountain team visits the movie set."

Ridge: And the mountain team sends it. Strap in.

Players to watch

UCLA quarterback Ty Dieffenbach has thrown the most passes in the last two games. He went 22 of 33 for 264 yards in his first start against Wisconsin and 18 of 31 for 198 yards and a touchdown against Michigan State. Wayne Knight leads the rushers with 627 yards and 12 touchdowns, and Semaj Morgan leads the receivers with 31 catches for 383 yards. Linebacker Sammy Omosigho has five and a half sacks, and Jalen Woods leads the defense with 59 tackles.

Nevada's Luke Duncan threw the most passes last week, going 18 of 29 for 178 yards and two touchdowns with no turnovers. Carter Jones leads the team with 654 passing yards. Nevada's top running back has 582 yards and five touchdowns, and Gary Givens III leads the receivers with 19 catches for 305 yards. Linebacker EJ Smith leads the defense with 76 tackles, and Dylan LaBarbera has four sacks.

Kenzie: Wayne Knight has 12 rushing touchdowns. That's a franchise, darling.

Ridge: EJ Smith has 76 tackles. That dude never stops carving.

What happened last week

UCLA beat Michigan State 28-10 on Homecoming at the Rose Bowl, where the Bruins honored their 1966 Rose Bowl team. Anthony Woods ran 14 times for 112 yards and a touchdown, and Brian Rowe Jr. caught three balls for 74 yards and a score.

Kenzie: Homecoming, a callback to 1966 and a win. And. scene.

Nevada beat San José State 28-13 on Homecoming at Mackay Stadium. Duncan threw two touchdowns, and Marshaun Brown caught five balls for 52 yards and a score.

Ridge: Sick! I mimed a backflip. Nobody got hurt.

Injuries and availability

The official availability reports haven't been posted yet. UCLA quarterback Nico Iamaleava is out for the season after a knee injury in the Oregon game. For Nevada, starting quarterback Carter Jones (arm) and AJ Bianco (ankle) both missed last week, and coach Jeff Choate said it was too early to say who will start. Check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Bob Chesney is in his first season at UCLA, with a hire date of Dec. 6, 2025. He's a Dickinson College graduate who was head coach at Salve Regina, Assumption, Holy Cross and James Madison, and at Holy Cross he won five straight Patriot League titles. He's the first UCLA coach to begin his tenure 6-0, and the Bruins have kept on winning.

Jeff Choate was hired at Nevada on Dec. 4, 2023. This week he said his goal is to keep the game close: "Let's just drag this thing into deep water and find out who's gonna swim in the fourth quarter."

Kenzie: Coach Chesney walked onto the set and made a blockbuster. Breakout debut.

Ridge: Coach Choate wants deep water. I'm a snow guy, but I'm in.

Player storylines

The matchup to watch is Nevada's run game against a UCLA defense that allows 15.3 points a game. Duncan, if he plays, has a story all his own: he spent his first three college seasons at UCLA. On the other side, Knight and UCLA's big guys up front face a Nevada defense that has had trouble stopping the run. This is just the 10th time Nevada has played a top-10 team since moving up to the top level of college football.

What's happening on campus

It's Halloween at the Rose Bowl, listed at 88,565 seats, one week after UCLA's Homecoming. Back in Reno, Nevada fans just celebrated Homecoming Week and the football program's 130th birthday, since the team was founded on Oct. 24, 1896, as the Sagebrushers.

Kenzie: I'm dressing as a director's chair. Very supportive.

Ridge: I'm dressing as a snowboard. Pasadena needs a little winter.

Anything else fun and true

UCLA has been ranked in the AP top 10 at least once in every decade since the poll began in the 1930s, and the Bruins won the 1954 Coaches' Poll national title under Red Sanders. Nevada's Mackay Stadium field sits 4,610 feet above sea level, and Chris Ault, Frank Hawkins and Buck Shaw are in the College Football Hall of Fame.

Ridge: Whatever happens, Kenzie, supper's on me. We'll say grace first.

Kenzie: Deal, darling. I'll bring dessert.

My call: UCLA Bruins 34, Nevada Wolf Pack 14.
