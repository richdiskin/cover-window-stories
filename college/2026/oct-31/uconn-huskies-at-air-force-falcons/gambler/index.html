UConn Huskies at Air Force Falcons

UConn Huskies (4-3) at Air Force Falcons (5-2)

3:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Falcon Stadium · MW+

Lt. Col. Marissa Okonkwo-Hale, retired, is running a pre-flight checklist on a folding table outside Falcon Stadium when Jo Ferrante jogs up dribbling a basketball. "Ferrante," the colonel says, "your call sign is Fast Break. Approved." Jo spins the ball on one finger. "Colonel, I'll take it. Just don't make me run the option."

UConn (4-3) at Air Force (5-2), Saturday at 3 p.m. ET on MW+ at Falcon Stadium. It's a non-conference game: UConn is an FBS independent, and Air Force is 3-2 in the Mountain West. DraftKings has Air Force Falcons −2.5 with a total of 53.5. My card: UConn Huskies +2.5, Over 53.5, and a final of Air Force Falcons 29, UConn Huskies 27.

The colonel speaks for Air Force. "Evan Medders has run for 490 yards and six touchdowns. Blake Fletcher has 72 tackles. We beat Navy 14-9 and UNLV 41-31. We're 5-2, and we're climbing back to cruising altitude. Call sign: correct."

Jo speaks for UConn. "Kalieb Osborne has 12 touchdown throws and two interceptions. Cyncir Bowers has 564 rushing yards and seven touchdowns. We scored 35 on UMass last week. And we beat you 26-16 last year. Pack the Rent."

Why UConn? Because the Huskies stop the run, and that's all Air Force wants to do. UConn allows 3.3 yards a carry. Air Force gains 5.3 a carry, but it just managed 14 points at Wyoming. The Falcons have also cycled through quarterbacks: Liam Szarka, Kemper Hodges, Medders and Howard Fisher IV have each thrown the most passes in a game. UConn scores 35.6 points a game and has a quarterback with two interceptions all season. In a game this close to a coin flip, I want the points. My final has Air Force by less than a field goal.

The risk is the altitude and the clock. Air Force plays at elevation, and it holds the ball for long stretches. UConn lost close games at Miami (Ohio), 24-21, and to Syracuse, 42-41 in overtime.

Why the Over? UConn has scored 34 points or more in five of its seven games. Air Force scored 41 against UNLV at home, and its loss to North Dakota State finished 38-32. The total has come down this week, so the Over has a lower total to clear. My 29-27 adds up to 56, over 53.5.

Now the room, and everybody likes the Huskies. The Formula has Air Force 28-26 and sides with UConn, with its total landing Over. Next Man Up has Air Force 30-28, sides with UConn and likes the Over. Follow The Money likes UConn too, but takes the Under after the total dropped. In The Trenches has no pick, since the run games cancel out.

Next Man Up started it: "Air Force by a hair. UConn keeps the points. Colonel, log it."

Follow The Money agreed halfway: "Market moved to UConn. It also moved to the Under."

The Formula answered: "UConn scores 35.6 a game. Under is a tough flight plan."

Bottom of the card: a UConn defense that shuts down the run, an Air Force offense still sorting out its quarterbacks, and two teams that can put points up. Jo gets her fast break. Give me the Huskies and the Over.

Air Force Falcons 29, UConn Huskies 27. UConn Huskies cover the 2.5. Over 53.5.
