UConn vs Air Force: Space Force Day Black Out at Falcon Stadium

UConn Huskies (4-3) at Air Force Falcons (5-2)

3:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Falcon Stadium · MW+

UConn Huskies vs Air Force Falcons is Week 9's Space Force Day Black Out at Falcon Stadium, a Halloween afternoon at the Academy with fireworks waiting for every Falcon score. Jo Ferrante, a Hartford-area former point guard who counts possessions, says Kalieb Osborne, Cyncir Bowers and Jason Candle's Huskies run it back from last November, and Lt. Col. (Ret.) Marissa Okonkwo-Hale, a retired Air Force instructor pilot who gives everyone a call sign, says Evan Medders, Blake Fletcher and Troy Calhoun's Falcons get even.

Colonel: Welcome to the Academy, Ferrante. Your call sign is Fast Break. Wear black.

Jo: Colonel, it's Halloween. I was going to come as a point guard.

Colonel: You are a point guard.

Jo: Exactly. No costume needed.

Air Force has dressed the day up anyway. The home schedule lists it as Space Force Day and a Black Out, and the athletic department's game-day guide promises enhanced fireworks, pyrotechnics and stadium lighting when the Falcons take the field, when they score, before the fourth quarter and when they win. Falcon Alley opens three hours before kickoff, and somewhere on the grounds is the BobbleBird, a 6-foot-tall bobblehead of The Bird that moves to a new spot every home game.

Jo: A 6-foot bobblehead. That's taller than my whole backcourt.

Colonel: Find him, take a picture, and report back. That's an order, Fast Break.

The Halloween bit already has its punch. KRDO's Rob Namnoum, in a Halloween piece this week, wrote: "All we know is that this Saturday Air Force will dress up as football players."

Colonel: Football players. Ten-hut. Best costume on the field.

The Falcons are 5-2 and 3-2 in the Mountain West, and they run it the way they always have. Medders leads the team with 490 rushing yards and six touchdowns, Liam Szarka is 19 of 38 for 296 yards, and Simon Kibbee leads the receivers with eight catches for 135 yards. Fletcher has 72 tackles and Max Mustell has three sacks. Air Force runs for 343.0 yards a game, second in FBS, and holds the ball 36:22 a game.

The quarterback room has been busy. Szarka, Kemper Hodges, Medders and Howard Fisher IV have each led Air Force in throws in a game this season. Last week the Falcons lost 28-14 at Wyoming, with Medders running 21 times for 115 yards and Fisher throwing the most for Air Force.

Colonel: Rough landing in Laramie. We debriefed. Wheels up again Saturday.

Before that, the Falcons beat UNLV 41-31 at home, won 28-7 at Northern Illinois and beat Navy 14-9 in the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy chase.

UConn is 4-3 as an FBS independent and comes in off a 35-16 home win over UMass on Oct. 23. Osborne was 14 of 19 for 173 yards and two touchdowns, Bowers ran 15 times for 131 yards and a score, and JD Willoughby caught a touchdown.

Jo: Pack the Rent! That's how you close out a game, Colonel.

Osborne has led UConn in throws in every game. He's 112 of 182 for 1,594 yards, 12 touchdowns and two interceptions. Bowers has 564 yards and seven touchdowns, and Shamar Porter leads the receivers with 14 catches for 265 yards and three scores. Hayden Pegg has 47 tackles and K'Von Sherman has 3.5 sacks. The Huskies score 35.6 points a game.

Jo: Two interceptions all year. That's a point guard who never turns it over.

Colonel: Fast Break, two interceptions is what we call a clean preflight.

The Huskies have had a few close ones go the other way, 24-21 at Miami (Ohio) and 42-41 in overtime against Syracuse. They also beat Air Force 26-16 last November, the only meeting between these two in CollegeFootballData's records back to 2016.

Colonel: One meeting, Fast Break. That's a sample size of one.

Jo: One for one, Colonel. In basketball we call that a perfect night at the free-throw stripe.

The matchup is the Falcon option against a UConn defense that allows 3.3 yards a carry. Pegg and the Huskies up front have to stay home on every snap. On the other side, Bowers and Osborne meet an Air Force defense that allows 20.9 points a game. Possessions will be few, and Jo will be counting every one.

No official availability report is out for this game, so check the teams' pregame notes.

Candle is in his first season at UConn, hired on Dec. 6, 2025, after leading Toledo from 2016 to 2025. Calhoun has led Air Force since 2007, and CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 22, 2006.

Colonel: Twenty seasons in the cockpit. That's rare in this sport.

Jo: Respect, Colonel. Coach Candle is in year one and already has us scoring 35.6 a game.

Colonel: Fast Break, when the fireworks go off for us, you're allowed to clap.

Jo: Colonel, when Osborne finds Porter, you're allowed to say "nice dish."

Colonel: Deal. Dinner afterward, my treat, and you can bring the jersey.

My call: Air Force Falcons 29, UConn Huskies 27.
