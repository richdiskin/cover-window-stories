Fast Break Meets the Flight Deck: UConn Visits Air Force on Halloween

UConn Huskies (4-3) at Air Force Falcons (5-2)

3:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Falcon Stadium · MW+

UConn (4-3, FBS independent) at Air Force (5-2, 3-2 Mountain West). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3 p.m. ET, Falcon Stadium, USAF Academy, Colo., MW+.

Your hosts are Jo Ferrante, a Hartford-area former point guard who counts possessions, and Lt. Col. (Ret.) Marissa Okonkwo-Hale, a retired Air Force instructor pilot who gives everyone a call sign.

Colonel: Welcome to the Academy, Ferrante. Your call sign is Fast Break.

Jo: Colonel, I love it. Next play, next possession.

Colonel: Hydrate. We're at about 2,025 meters up here. Your lungs will notice before your legs do.

Players to watch

UConn quarterback Kalieb Osborne has thrown the most passes in every game. He's 112 of 182 for 1,594 yards, 12 touchdowns and only two interceptions. Running back Cyncir Bowers has 564 yards and seven touchdowns, and Shamar Porter leads the receivers with 265 yards and three scores on 14 catches. On defense, linebacker Hayden Pegg has 47 tackles and K'Von Sherman has 3.5 sacks.

Air Force runs the option, so the quarterback who carries it matters most. Evan Medders leads the team with 490 rushing yards and six touchdowns. Liam Szarka leads in throwing at 19 of 38 for 296 yards, and four different quarterbacks have thrown the most passes in a game this year. Tight end Simon Kibbee leads the receivers with eight catches for 135 yards. Linebacker Blake Fletcher has 72 tackles, and defensive back Max Mustell has three sacks.

Jo: Two interceptions all year for Osborne. In basketball terms, that's a point guard who never turns it over. I'd start him every night.

What happened last week

UConn beat UMass 35-16 at home on Oct. 23. Osborne was 14 of 19 for 173 yards and two touchdowns, Bowers ran 15 times for 131 yards and a score, and JD Willoughby caught a touchdown.

Jo: Pack the Rent! That's how you close out a game.

Air Force lost 28-14 at Wyoming on Oct. 23. Medders ran 21 times for 115 yards, and Howard Fisher IV threw the most passes.

Colonel: Rough landing. We debriefed. Wheels up again Saturday.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Osborne has thrown the most passes in every UConn game, while four different Air Force quarterbacks have done it, including Fisher last week, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Jason Candle is UConn's new head coach, hired on Dec. 6, 2025. He led Toledo from 2016 to 2025 after starting there as an assistant in 2009, and his UConn offense plays with pace, running for 195.3 yards a game.

Troy Calhoun has led Air Force since 2007, and CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 22, 2006. His Falcons run for 343.0 yards a game, second in FBS, and hold the ball 36:22 a game.

Colonel: Twenty seasons in the cockpit. That's a rare thing in this sport.

Jo: Respect, Colonel. Coach Candle is in year one, and he already has us scoring 35.6 points a game.

Player storylines

The big matchup is the Air Force option against a UConn defense that allows only 3.3 yards a carry. Pegg and the Husky big guys up front will need to stay disciplined on every snap. On the other side, Bowers and Osborne will try to keep up with an Air Force defense that allows 20.9 points a game. Possessions will be precious, and Jo will be counting every one.

What's happening on campus

Falcon Stadium sits north of Colorado Springs, and CFBD lists it as built in 1962 with 46,692 seats. A Halloween afternoon at the Academy with the mountains in the background is hard to beat. The three service academies compete for the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy each year, and the Falcons beat Navy 14-9 this season.

Colonel: I'll be in my flight suit. Not a costume. A uniform.

Jo: I'll be in my old jersey. Also not a costume. A lifestyle.

Anything else fun and true

UConn first fielded a team in 1896 and went independent in 2019. The Huskies went 9-4 in both 2024 and 2025, and they beat Air Force 26-16 last season. Air Force has been in the Mountain West since the league was founded in 1999, and the Falcons went 10-3, 10-3 and 9-4 earlier in this decade.

Jo: Colonel, whatever happens, dinner's on me afterward. We'll say grace first.

Colonel: Accepted, Fast Break. God bless these young men on both sidelines. Call sign: correct.

My call: Air Force Falcons 29, UConn Huskies 27.
