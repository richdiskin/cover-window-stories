Hotdish Meets Hatch Chile: UTEP Visits North Dakota State on Halloween

UTEP Miners (1-6) at North Dakota State Bison (6-2)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Fargodome · MW+

XFacebook

UTEP (1-6, 0-3 Mountain West) at North Dakota State (6-2, 3-2 Mountain West). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, Fargodome, Fargo, N.D., MW+.

Your hosts are Neto Galindo, a UTEP fan and Sun Bowl ironworker's kid who grades effort by chile heat, and Inga Halvorsen, a North Dakota State fan and agricultural economist who has tracked every Bison title since she was a girl.

Inga: Well, look who made it to Fargo! Welcome, Neto.

Neto: Thanks, Inga. I brought green chile from El Paso. It's Halloween, so I figured I'd bring the scariest thing I own.

Inga: And I brought tater tot hotdish. I bring hotdish either way, hon.

Neto: Then let's make it Hatch-hot hotdish. Neighbors share.

Players to watch

North Dakota State quarterback Nathan Hayes has thrown the most passes in all eight games. He's 123 of 196 for 1,470 yards, 12 touchdowns and seven interceptions. Myles Mitchell leads the rushers with 393 yards, and Jackson Williams leads the receivers with 32 catches for 503 yards. Linebacker Donovan Woolen leads the defense with 55 tackles, and Kelton McCaslin has four sacks.

For UTEP, Raymond Moore III has thrown the most passes the last two games and leads the team in rushing with 414 yards. EJ Colson Jr. threw the most in the first five games and leads in passing with 628 yards and seven touchdowns. Kam Thomas leads the receivers with 270 yards, linebacker Isaiah Bogerty leads the defense with 42 tackles, and Sterling Miles has 3.5 sacks.

Inga: Against Nevada, Jackson Williams caught a missed field goal in the end zone and ran it back 100 yards. That young man deserves extra hotdish.

Neto: Raymond Moore III runs like a five-alarm chile. Effort grade: Hatch-hot.

What happened last week

North Dakota State lost 21-16 at New Mexico on Oct. 24, on Homecoming weekend in Albuquerque. Hayes threw for 198 yards and two touchdowns, and Williams caught four balls for 71 yards. It was the first meeting on record between the schools.

Inga: Oh, yah, that's a tough one. I made a second hotdish on the bus.

UTEP had an open week. The Miners last played on Oct. 17, losing 24-11 to San José State at the Sun Bowl. Moore threw for 195 yards, and Thomas caught four balls for 96 yards.

Neto: A week off to rest the legs and sharpen the effort. We'll be fresh.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Hayes has thrown the most passes for North Dakota State every week, and Moore has done it for UTEP the last two games, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Tim Polasek leads North Dakota State, with a hire date of Dec. 17, 2023. His team plays Bison football: run it, defend it, don't give it away. The Bison allow 12.5 points a game, they've taken the ball away 14 times, and they shut out Fordham 38-0 and Wyoming 28-0.

Scotty Walden leads UTEP, with a hire date of Dec. 4, 2023. His offense runs the ball on most plays, and his team's best day came Sept. 12, a 51-10 win over Texas Southern. The open week gave his staff extra time to prepare.

Inga: Coach Polasek is Fargo through and through. Practical, like a good snow shovel.

Neto: Coach Walden is building it brick by brick, like my dad on a Sun Bowl job site.

Player storylines

The matchup to watch is North Dakota State's defense against Moore, who can hurt you with his legs. The Bison's big guys up front have been tough to run on all season. On the other side, Hayes wants a clean day after five interceptions in the two losses, and UTEP's defense will try to force him into more mistakes. This is the first meeting between these schools in our records from 2016 through 2025.

What's happening on campus

Halloween in Fargo means bundling up, and the Fargodome, opened in 1992 and listed at 18,700 seats, keeps everybody warm and loud inside. UTEP fans call the Sun Bowl home, built in 1963 and listed at 51,500, with the Sound of the Southwest band and mascot Paydirt Pete.

Neto: I'm dressing as Paydirt Pete. Pickaxe and all.

Inga: I'm dressing as a casserole dish. Very warm, very Fargo.

Anything else fun and true

North Dakota State has won 18 national championships, including 10 FCS titles between 2011 and 2024, and 39 conference championships. The Bison went 14-1, 12-3, 11-4, 14-2 and 12-1 in their last five Missouri Valley seasons. UTEP's fight song is "El Paso Fight."

Inga: Whatever happens, Neto, supper's on me. We'll say grace first.

Neto: Deal, Inga. Share the chile, and the hotdish.

My call: North Dakota State Bison 37, UTEP Miners 10.
