Halloween Night in Ames: No. 24 Oklahoma State Visits Iowa State

Oklahoma State Cowboys (5-2) at Iowa State Cyclones (3-4)

7:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Jack Trice Stadium · FS1

Oklahoma State (5-2, 3-1 Big 12) at Iowa State (3-4, 1-3 Big 12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 7:00 p.m. ET (6:00 p.m. CT), Jack Trice Stadium, Ames, Iowa, FS1.

Your hosts are Dusty Lockridge, an Oklahoma State fan and ag-econ grad who explains offense with rodeo timing, and Twyla Hemmingsen, an Iowa State fan who forecasts every Saturday from her kitchen weather center.

Twyla: Good evening, Cyclone Nation! Tonight's forecast: cold front from the south, wearing orange.

Dusty: Orange from my hat to my boots, Twyla. We're riding into Ames for the full eight seconds.

Players to watch

Oklahoma State quarterback Drew Mestemaker has completed 157 of 239 throws for 2,326 yards, with 20 touchdowns and six interceptions. He's thrown for 300 yards or more in six straight games. Wide receiver Wyatt Young has 36 catches for 639 yards and six scores. Running back Caleb Hawkins has 712 yards on 142 carries. Linebacker Ethan Wesloski leads the defense with 70 tackles and 3.5 sacks.

Iowa State running back Aiden Flora has 716 yards and six touchdowns on 102 carries. Quarterback Jaylen Raynor has thrown for 1,107 yards and seven touchdowns. Linebacker Sullivan Schlimgen has 71 tackles, and defensive end Isaac Terrell has three sacks.

Twyla: Flora is the jet stream. Watch the radar.

What happened last week

Oklahoma State beat Colorado 57-24 at home. Mestemaker went 21 of 36 for 386 yards and four touchdowns. Justin Bowick caught five balls for 138 yards and a score, and Hawkins ran for 120 yards.

Iowa State lost 39-3 at No. 22 Arizona. Raynor fumbled late in the first half, and Jimmy Rogers gave backup Connor Moberly the second half to look for a spark. Flora was held to 36 yards.

Dusty: Tough night in the desert. Those Cyclones will come home hungry.

Injuries and availability

No official Big 12 availability report had been posted as of Tuesday. Oklahoma State safety Mose Phillips hurt his shoulder against UCF and is expected to miss the rest of the season. Safety Quinton Hammonds was hurt recovering a fumble against Colorado and came back to the sideline in a sling. "Hopeful that we can get Q back," Eric Morris said.

Dusty: Prayers for Mose and Q. Good young men, both of them.

What the coaches are up to

Jimmy Rogers settled the quarterback question early. "We're gonna go with Jaylen," he said, calling Raynor "the best leader on the team." Raynor will make his 43rd straight start, counting his time at Arkansas State. Rogers also said of Moberly, "his future's bright."

Eric Morris is in his first season in Stillwater. Mestemaker and Hawkins followed him from North Texas, and the Cowboys are No. 24 in the country after going 1-11 last season.

Iowa State defensive coordinator Jesse Bobbit was honest about the challenge. "He can make the throws," he said of Mestemaker. "You can see it on tape."

Player storylines

Mestemaker ran 75 yards on one play in the 39-31 win over Oregon. That one run is half of his season rushing total.

Raynor is one of six team captains, elected by his teammates in the preseason. When he was benched, he spent the second half coaching up and cheering on Moberly.

Twyla: That's a captain. Sunshine in the forecast for that young man.

What's happening on campus

Jack Trice Stadium holds 61,500. It's named for Jack Trice, Iowa State's first Black athlete, and it got his name in 1997. It is still the only major college football stadium named for a Black man. Every visitor should know his story.

Oklahoma State has played football since 1901. The Cowboys claim the 1945 national championship, and Barry Sanders won the Heisman Trophy in 1988.

Anything else fun and true

This is Iowa State's fifth game against a ranked team in its first eight. The Cyclones are 3-1 at home this season, and their home wins include a 45-42 thriller over West Virginia.

Dusty: I'll bring the stopwatch, Twyla. You bring the forecast.

Twyla: Barometer's falling, Dusty. That's Cyclone weather.

My call: Oklahoma State Cowboys 30, Iowa State Cyclones 27.
