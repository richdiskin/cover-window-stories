Oklahoma State vs Iowa State: All Orange Meets All Black in Ames

Oklahoma State Cowboys (5-2) at Iowa State Cyclones (3-4)

7:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Jack Trice Stadium · FS1

Oklahoma State Cowboys vs Iowa State Cyclones is a Week 9 Halloween night at Jack Trice Stadium, where Iowa State wears all black and asks fans to "Pack The Jack, Wear Black" while No. 24 Oklahoma State comes in all orange. Dusty Lockridge, an Oklahoma State ag-econ grad who explains offense with rodeo timing, says Drew Mestemaker, Wyatt Young and Ethan Wesloski keep rolling, and Twyla Hemmingsen, an Iowa State fan who forecasts every Saturday from her kitchen weather center, says Aiden Flora, Sullivan Schlimgen and Kyle Konrardy send a front through.

Twyla: Forecast for Ames, Saturday night: 61,500 people dressed in black, with a chance of noise.

Dusty: And one section of orange. We'll look like a jack-o'-lantern.

Twyla: A jack-o'-lantern that's about to get carved.

Dusty: Easy, Twyla. It's a holiday.

This is the 60th meeting, and the 31st in Ames. Oklahoma State leads the series 34-22-3, but Iowa State has won the last two meetings at Jack Trice and won 20-13 in Stillwater in last season's finale, when the Cyclones ran for 233 yards to Oklahoma State's 27. Iowa State has beaten a ranked team at home in five of the last nine seasons, and Cyclone fans still talk about 2011, when Iowa State beat the No. 2 Cowboys 37-31 in double overtime in Ames.

Dusty: You had to bring up 2011.

Twyla: My kitchen weather center has a framed photo of it. Next to the barometer.

Oklahoma State is 5-2 and 3-1 in the Big 12 under first-year coach Eric Morris, who brought in 91 new players. The Cowboys have scored 30 or more in six games in a row and beat Colorado 57-24 last week, when Mestemaker threw for 386 yards and four touchdowns. He has 2,326 yards, 20 touchdowns and six interceptions this season, and he has thrown for 300 yards in six games in a row. Young has 36 catches for 639 yards, Justin Bowick has 34 for 607, Caleb Hawkins has 712 rushing yards, and Wesloski has 70 tackles.

Dusty: Rodeo timing, Twyla. Mestemaker gets the snap, counts two, and the ball's gone. Eight seconds is a long ride for a defense.

Twyla: Eight seconds is about how long your Cowboys lasted at Tulsa.

Dusty: That was the opener. We've won five of six since.

Iowa State is 3-4 and 1-3 in the league under Jimmy Rogers, and this is its third ranked opponent in a row. Last week was rough, a 39-3 loss at Arizona. Flora has 716 rushing yards and six touchdowns, and he's the only player in the country this season with a rushing touchdown, a throwing touchdown and a punt return touchdown. Schlimgen leads the Big 12 with 10.1 tackles a game and made 15 at Arizona. Konrardy has hit 20 field goals in a row from 45 yards and in, and his career best is 63.

Twyla: Sixty-three yards, Dusty. You could hang laundry on that kick.

Dusty: In Ames in October, it'd freeze in midair before it dried.

Twyla: That's why we use the dryer.

The Big 12's initial availability report, posted at 10 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists one Oklahoma State player out and three questionable, and three Iowa State players out, with quarterback Connor Moberly questionable.

Twyla: Costumes are welcome, but masks aren't allowed. So no hiding when the second half goes sideways.

Dusty: I'm coming as a rodeo clown. Face paint only.

Twyla: Fitting. You'll be the one running from the bull.

Dusty: Whatever happens, Twyla, your hot cocoa is on me, and I'll leave that barometer alone.

Twyla: Then you're wearing black to Stillwater next year.

Dusty: Let's not get crazy. Charcoal, maybe.

My call: Oklahoma State Cowboys 30, Iowa State Cyclones 27.
