Cougars and Aztecs Meet Under the San Diego Lights

Washington State Cougars (1-7) at San Diego State Aztecs (3-5)

7:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Snapdragon Stadium · CBSSN

Washington State (1-7, 0-4 Pac-12) at San Diego State (3-5, 2-2 Pac-12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 7:00 p.m. ET, Snapdragon Stadium, San Diego, CBSSN.

Your hosts are Hank Palouse, a Washington State fan who drives a combine and keeps a shoebox of game tapes in the cab, and Kai Delgado-Sorensen, a San Diego State fan who surfs Ocean Beach most mornings.

Kai: Welcome to sunshine, Hank. You can leave the combine at home.

Hank: Brought the shoebox, though. Cougs never quit.

Players to watch

Washington State quarterback Owen Eshelman has started the last two games. Caden Pinnick, who started the first six, has thrown for 952 yards. Wide receiver Branden Ganashamoorthy leads the team with 30 catches for 401 yards and three touchdowns, and Kirby Vorhees has 275 rushing yards. On defense, linebacker Keith Brown has 60 tackles, and defensive tackle Jirah Leaupepetele has 2.5 sacks.

San Diego State quarterback Stone Saunders, a redshirt freshman and Kentucky transfer, gets his second straight start. Running back Javion Kinnard leads the team with 526 rushing yards. Wide receiver Jaylon Hawkins has 322 receiving yards and three touchdowns. Linebacker Sione Hala has 83 tackles, and linebacker Djibril Rahman has 3.5 sacks.

Kai: Hala's in on every play. That's a surfer who never gets out of the water.

What happened last week

Washington State lost 31-10 at home to Boise State. Eshelman completed 21 throws for 191 yards and ran the ball 23 times. Ganashamoorthy and Daniel Blood played all 76 snaps on offense.

San Diego State lost 56-49 at Colorado State in a wild one. The 105 combined points were the most in a game in San Diego State's Division I history. Saunders went 23 of 33 for 369 yards and two touchdowns in his first college start. Hawkins caught six balls for a career-high 164 yards and scored a touchdown for the fifth straight game. Lucky Sutton ran for 98 yards and two scores, and Kinnard added two more on just 10 carries.

Hank: One hundred sixty-four yards for Hawkins. That one's going in the shoebox, even if he's not ours.

Injuries and availability

The Pac-12's advance availability report, updated at 10:03 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists one Washington State player out for the season and two questionable, and six San Diego State players out, one doubtful and seven questionable. Coach Kirby Moore said wide receiver Darrius Clemons (ankle) and safety Jeremiah Bernard (shoulder) are day-to-day and set to return to practice this week. For San Diego State, Jayden Denegal remains day-to-day.

What the coaches are up to

Moore is in his first season at Washington State. He wants sharper practices this week. "We talk about deliberate practice," he said. "That really starts with bringing great energy, having verbal combat, doing your assignment, making sure you're not thinking out there, that you're just reacting."

Sean Lewis is in his third season at San Diego State. He named Saunders the starter on Monday. "Stone will start this week," Lewis said. "So, he'll get the bulk of the one reps as we go forward."

Kai: Clear call from the coach. Gnarly.

Player storylines

Saunders came into his first start with just 15 career throws. His 369 yards were the most ever by an Aztec quarterback making his first start on the road, going back to 1969. He also became the first Aztec to throw for 300 yards since Jalen Mayden in the 2022 Hawai'i Bowl.

Ganashamoorthy has played 503 snaps this season, the most of any wide receiver in the Pac-12. Washington State's only win so far came 48-7 over Duquesne, and the Cougars lost 17-16 at Utah State. They're looking for their first win over an FBS team.

Hank: One-point loss. I've watched it twice. Still hurts.

What's happening on campus

Snapdragon Stadium opened in 2022 and seats 35,000. San Diego State began playing football in 1921 and has won 21 conference championships.

Washington State is in Pullman, where the first Cougar team took the field in 1894. Home is Martin Stadium, which opened in 1972 on ground that dates back to 1892, when it was called Soldier Field.

Anything else fun and true

The Aztecs went 9-4 last year and are trying to get to a bowl game for a second straight season. They have won two close home games this year, 31-29 over Texas State and 26-23 over Fresno State.

Hank: Ganashamoorthy, five hundred and three snaps. That young man has earned a nap.

Kai: Bring him to the beach after, Hank. Respect.

My call: San Diego State Aztecs 27, Washington State Cougars 25.
