Washington State Cougars (1-7) at San Diego State Aztecs (3-5)

Washington State (1-7) at San Diego State (3-5), Saturday at 7:00 p.m. ET on CBSSN at Snapdragon Stadium in San Diego. It's a Pac-12 game. DraftKings has San Diego State Aztecs −2.5 with a total of 48.5. Action Network opened San Diego State −2.5 with a total of 48.5. My card: Washington State Cougars +2.5, Over 48.5, and a final of San Diego State Aztecs 27, Washington State Cougars 25.

Saunders and Hawkins can make plays, and this Aztec defense gives up points. My 27-25 adds up to 52, three and a half over the over/under 48.5.
