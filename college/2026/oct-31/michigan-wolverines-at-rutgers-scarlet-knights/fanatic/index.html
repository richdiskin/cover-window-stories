Michigan vs Rutgers: Halloween On the Banks in Piscataway

Michigan Wolverines (4-3) at Rutgers Scarlet Knights (2-5)

12:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · SHI Stadium · BTN

Michigan Wolverines vs Rutgers Scarlet Knights is a Week 9 "Halloween On the Banks" game at SHI Stadium, with trick-or-treating on the Rutgers Boardwalk from 8:30 a.m. ET and a haunted halftime show by the Marching Scarlet Knights. Gus Hartwell, a Michigan engineering-school stats savant in a maize sweater, says Kyle Whittingham's defense will keep KJ Duff quiet, and Vinny Scolari, an Exit 9 Jersey guy who gives directions by Turnpike exit, says Greg Schiano's Knights and Antwan Raymond are due on Halloween.

Vinny: Gus! Welcome to Exit 9! Grab a trick-or-treat bag. The food trucks are giving out free candy.

Gus: Vincent, I brought a laminated map, three backup routes and a workbook titled "Champions of the West."

Vinny: Fuhgeddaboudit. Follow the mechanical pumpkin.

That pumpkin is real. Rutgers says the Boardwalk will have a mechanical pumpkin, face painting, Halloween games, inflatables and a costume contest. BTN Tailgate goes live at Athletes Glen at 10 a.m. ET, and the band's halftime show is billed as haunted.

Rutgers likes this date. Its game notes say this is the sixth Halloween home game since 1964, and the Knights won four of the previous five. The famous one came in 1992, a Halloween Homecoming against Virginia Tech. Rutgers trailed 42-23 in the third quarter and 49-44 with 1:32 left, then went 78 yards, and Bryan Fortay found Chris Brantley in the end zone as time expired. Final: 50-49. It was Brantley's fourth receiving touchdown that day, a school record.

Vinny: Fifty to forty-nine, Gus. I was eight. I still have the ticket stub in my glove box at Exit 9.

Gus: I've added it to my workbook under "Things Vincent Mentions Weekly."

There's another number in those notes. Rutgers has played 29 Big Ten games since it last faced Michigan, and Schiano is 0-4 against the Wolverines.

Gus: Victors valiant, Vincent.

Vinny: Not on Halloween, pal.

Michigan is 4-3 and lost 13-6 in overtime to Indiana on Oct. 24. Tommy Carr went 20 of 32 for 167 yards, and JJ Buchanan caught six balls. Bryce Underwood led the Wolverines in throws in their first six games and is 72 of 124 for 924 yards, five touchdowns and six interceptions. Jordan Marshall leads the rushers with 319 yards, and Buchanan has 33 catches for 539 yards and four scores. Troy Bowles has 55 tackles, and John Henry Daley has four sacks.

Rutgers is 2-5 and lost 42-14 at Northwestern on Oct. 24, though Raymond ran 18 times for 150 yards and a touchdown. Dylan Lonergan is 74 of 127 for 1,066 yards, 10 touchdowns and six interceptions, and AJ Surace has split the job with him. Raymond has 648 yards and six touchdowns. Duff has 45 catches for 774 yards and nine touchdowns. Chris Joines leads the tacklers with 34.

Vinny: Nine touchdowns for Duff. That's more than the exits between here and the shore. Almost.

The Big Ten's initial availability report, posted at 8 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists Rutgers quarterback Dylan Lonergan out and KJ Duff doubtful, and AJ Surace is not on it. Michigan lists six players out and six questionable, John Henry Daley among them, and Bryce Underwood is not on it.

The matchup is Raymond against a Michigan defense that allows 2.8 yards a carry and 15.7 points a game. On the other side, Michigan scores 20.3 points a game and gains 3.7 yards a carry against a Rutgers defense that allows 5.8. Rutgers ranks second nationally in fewest penalty yards a game, according to its notes.

Whittingham was named Michigan's 22nd head coach on Dec. 26, 2025, after 21 seasons at Utah. Schiano returned to Rutgers on Nov. 30, 2019, for his second stint.

Gus: Coach Whittingham would admire your discipline, Vincent.

Vinny: I'd yell "discipline" from the top of the Driscoll Bridge.

History is on everybody's side here. Rutgers beat Princeton 6-4 in New Brunswick on Nov. 6, 1869, in the first college football game, and the Knights have played 1,441 games, more than anyone. Michigan has more wins than any program, plus three Heisman winners: Tom Harmon, Desmond Howard and Charles Woodson.

Gus: I'm dressing as a workbook. Very scary for opponents.

Vinny: I'm dressing as the Turnpike. Very scary for everybody.

Gus: Win or lose, I'll split your six-foot sub. We'll say grace first.

Vinny: Six feet, Gus. Everybody eats.

My call: Michigan Wolverines 30, Rutgers Scarlet Knights 22.
