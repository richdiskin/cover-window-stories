Maize Meets the Turnpike: Michigan Visits Rutgers in Piscataway on Halloween

Michigan Wolverines (4-3) at Rutgers Scarlet Knights (2-5)

12:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · SHI Stadium · BTN

Michigan (4-3, 1-3 Big Ten) at Rutgers (2-5, 1-3 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12:30 p.m. ET, SHI Stadium, Piscataway, N.J., BTN.

Your hosts are Gus Hartwell, a Michigan engineering-school stats savant in a maize sweater who labels his data with fight-song lyrics, and Vinny Scolari, an Exit 9 Jersey guy who gives directions by Turnpike exit.

Vinny: Gus! Welcome to Exit 9! You found the place okay?

Gus: Vincent, I brought a laminated map, three backup routes and a spreadsheet titled "Champions of the West."

Vinny: Fuhgeddaboudit. Just follow the smell of the six-foot sub.

Players to watch

Michigan quarterback Bryce Underwood leads the team in passing at 72 of 124 for 924 yards, five touchdowns and six interceptions, though Tommy Carr threw the most passes against Indiana last week. Running back Jordan Marshall leads the rushers with 319 yards and four touchdowns. JJ Buchanan is the top receiver with 33 catches for 539 yards and four scores. On defense, linebacker Troy Bowles has 55 tackles and end John Henry Daley has four sacks.

Rutgers quarterback Dylan Lonergan leads the team at 74 of 127 for 1,066 yards, 10 touchdowns and six interceptions. AJ Surace threw the most passes against Northwestern last week. Running back Antwan Raymond has 648 yards and six touchdowns, and receiver KJ Duff has been a highlight machine with 45 catches for 774 yards and nine touchdowns. Defensive back Chris Joines leads the tacklers with 34, and Rondo Porter has 2.5 sacks.

Vinny: Nine touchdowns for KJ Duff, Gus. That's more touchdowns than there are exits between here and the shore. Almost.

What happened last week

Michigan lost 13-6 in overtime to Indiana at home on Oct. 24. Carr was 20 of 32 for 167 yards, and Buchanan caught six balls for 57 yards.

Gus: Victors. valiant. later. I spent Sunday in the library.

Rutgers lost 42-14 at Northwestern on Oct. 24. Raymond was the bright spot with 18 carries for 150 yards and a touchdown.

Vinny: I was around Exit 7A after that one. Maybe 7. But Raymond? Exit 9 all day.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Underwood threw the most passes in Michigan's first six games and Carr did against Indiana, while Lonergan and Surace have traded that role for Rutgers, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Kyle Whittingham was named Michigan's 22nd head coach on Dec. 26, 2025, after 21 seasons at Utah, where he went 177-88 with eight seasons of 10 or more wins. His Wolverines are built on defense, allowing 15.7 points a game.

Greg Schiano returned to Rutgers on Nov. 30, 2019, for his second stint. His team is the least penalized in FBS at 28.7 penalty yards a game.

Vinny: Discipline, baby. I'd yell that from the top of the Driscoll Bridge.

Gus: Coach Whittingham would approve. He'd also ask you to come down safely.

Player storylines

The big story is Raymond against a Michigan defense that allows only 2.8 yards a carry. Something has to give. On the other side, Buchanan and the Michigan offense want to get rolling after a long day against Indiana. Michigan's big guys up front will try to push around a Rutgers defense that gives up 5.8 yards a carry. And Duff will be looking for touchdown number 10.

What's happening on campus

SHI Stadium is listed at 52,454, and a Halloween kickoff in Piscataway should bring plenty of scarlet costumes. Gus notes that Michigan Stadium's record crowd is 115,109, set against Notre Dame on Sept. 7, 2013.

Gus: I'm dressing as a spreadsheet. Very scary for opponents.

Vinny: I'm dressing as the Turnpike. Very scary for everybody.

Anything else fun and true

The very first college football game was played in New Brunswick on Nov. 6, 1869, and Rutgers beat Princeton 6-4. Michigan played its first game on May 30, 1879, and has more wins than any program in college football. Three Wolverines have won the Heisman: Tom Harmon in 1940, Desmond Howard in 1991 and Charles Woodson in 1997.

Vinny: So Gus, the birthplace of football is hosting the winningest program. Saturday's got history all over it.

Gus: Agreed, Vincent. Win or lose, I'll split that sub with you. We'll say grace first.

Vinny: Six feet, Gus. Everybody eats.

My call: Michigan Wolverines 30, Rutgers Scarlet Knights 22.
