Michigan Wolverines at Rutgers Scarlet Knights

Michigan Wolverines (4-3) at Rutgers Scarlet Knights (2-5)

12:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · SHI Stadium · BTN

Vinny Scolari has a six-foot sub on a folding table in the SHI Stadium lot, just in case. Gus Hartwell, maize sweater, VICTORS mug, walks up with a laminated spreadsheet and says, "Vincent, I've labeled this column 'Champions of the West.'" Vinny says, "Gus, that's Exit 9. Out here, it's Jersey."

Michigan (4-3) at Rutgers (2-5), Saturday at 12:30 p.m. ET on BTN at SHI Stadium in Piscataway. It's a Big Ten game, and both teams are 1-3 in league play. DraftKings has Michigan Wolverines −14 with a total of 46.5. My card: Rutgers Scarlet Knights +14, Over 46.5, and a final of Michigan Wolverines 30, Rutgers Scarlet Knights 22.

Gus speaks for Michigan, spreadsheet open. "Hail to the spreadsheet. Our defense allows 15.7 points a game. JJ Buchanan has 33 catches for 539 yards. Troy Bowles has 55 tackles. We beat Penn State 21-18. And we've won every meeting in this series since 2016."

Vinny speaks for Rutgers, sub knife in hand. "Gus, buddy, KJ Duff has 45 catches for 774 yards and nine touchdowns. Antwan Raymond just ran 18 times for 150 yards at Northwestern. We won 37-30 at Maryland. Exit 9 never lies."

Why Rutgers? Because Michigan doesn't blow anybody out. The Wolverines score 20.3 points a game, and their four wins came 13-12, 17-10, 52-17 and 21-18. Only UTEP lost to them by more than a touchdown. Michigan scored six points in regulation and overtime combined against Indiana, and Tommy Carr threw the most passes in that one, not Bryce Underwood. Michigan's run game gains 3.7 yards a carry. My final has Michigan by a touchdown and change.

The worry is the Rutgers defense. It allows 33.3 points a game and 5.8 yards a carry, and Northwestern beat it 42-14 last week. Rutgers has also been flipping between Dylan Lonergan and AJ Surace at quarterback. And history isn't kind: Michigan beat Rutgers 78-0 in 2016 and 52-0 in 2019.

Why the Over? Every Rutgers game this season has gone past 46.5. Duff and Lonergan can throw it, and the Rutgers defense gives up points too. DraftKings had this total at 47.5 on Monday, and it ticked down to 46.5. My 30-22 adds up to 52, comfortably over 46.5.

Now the room, and it likes the Knights. The Formula has Michigan 30-19, takes Rutgers +14 and the Over. Next Man Up has Michigan 29-24, same side, same Over. In The Trenches likes Rutgers +14 too, because the Knights' run game matches up better. Follow The Money skips the side and takes the Under after the total ticked down.

Next Man Up started it: "Three of us say Rutgers. Gus, put that in a spreadsheet."

Follow The Money held out: "The total dropped a point. I'll take the Under and the quiet."

The Formula answered: "Every Rutgers game has gone Over. That's not quiet. That's Exit 9."

Bottom of the card: a Michigan offense that wins close or not at all, a Rutgers team that scores, and a total that every Rutgers game has cleared. Vinny gets his six-foot sub. Give me the Knights and the Over.

Michigan Wolverines 30, Rutgers Scarlet Knights 22. Rutgers Scarlet Knights cover the 14. Over 46.5.
