Court Is in Session in Durham: Boston College Brings Its Gavel to Wallace Wade, and Duke Brings a Syllabus

Boston College Eagles (4-3) at Duke Blue Devils (5-2)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Wallace Wade Stadium · CW

Boston College (4-3, 2-2 ACC) at Duke (5-2, 2-2 ACC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12 p.m. ET, Wallace Wade Stadium, Durham, N.C., CW.

Your hosts are Siobhan Dunleavy, a Boston College grad student and former debate champion who argues every play like a moot-court case, and Harrison Wexley-Moore IV, a Duke pre-everything overachiever with a degree for every occasion.

Siobhan: All rise! The Honorable Siobhan Dunleavy presiding. Also prosecuting. Also on the jury.

Harrison: Objection, your honor. That's a conflict of interest. I wrote a paper on it. Footnotes available upon request.

Players to watch

Boston College quarterback Mason McKenzie has thrown the most passes in all seven Eagle games, and he's also the team's leading rusher. He's 107 of 185 for 1,363 yards, eight touchdowns and seven interceptions, and he has 614 rushing yards and five rushing scores. Javarius Green leads the receivers with 26 catches for 286 yards. On defense, linebacker Anthony Palano has 61 tackles and end Demetrius Ballard has 4.5 sacks.

Duke quarterback Walker Eget has thrown the most passes in all seven Blue Devil games. He's 122 of 197 for 1,440 yards, eight touchdowns and three interceptions. Running back Nate Sheppard is the workhorse, with 875 yards and nine touchdowns on 153 carries. Jonah Burton leads the receivers with 21 catches for 298 yards. Linebacker Nick Morris Jr. has 44 tackles, and end Kevin O'Connor has four sacks.

Harrison: Eight hundred seventy-five yards. Sheppard's carries deserve their own peer-reviewed journal.

What happened last week

Boston College won 49-27 at Georgia Tech on Oct. 24. McKenzie ran 16 times for 170 yards and two touchdowns and threw for 101 yards and another score.

Siobhan: Objection overruled, we WON! I banged the gavel so hard it needs a cast.

Duke won 19-16 at Virginia on Oct. 23. Eget went 14 of 25 for 211 yards, Sheppard ran for a touchdown, and Burton caught four passes for 59 yards.

Harrison: A three-point win on the road. I'll accept that grade with gratitude.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. McKenzie and Eget have each thrown the most passes in every game for their teams this season, so check the teams' pregame notes before kickoff.

What the coaches are up to

Bill O'Brien was named Boston College's 37th head coach on Feb. 9, 2024. He went 15-9 at Penn State in 2012 and 2013, won four AFC South titles with the Houston Texans, and coached Heisman winner Bryce Young as Alabama's offensive coordinator.

Manny Diaz was hired at Duke on Dec. 7, 2023. He was head coach at Miami from 2019 to 2021 and defensive coordinator at Penn State in 2022 and 2023. His Duke defense allows 16 points a game this season, and it held Tulane to three points and Stanford to seven.

Siobhan: Two coaches with Penn State on the résumé. I move that they share a pregame handshake.

Harrison: Seconded. Motion carries.

Player storylines

The case to watch is McKenzie against a Duke defense that allows 3.3 yards a carry. If the Duke big guys up front keep him from breaking loose the way he did in Atlanta, the Eagles will have to throw. On the other side, Sheppard gains plenty of yards behind a Duke run game that averages 5.3 yards a carry, and BC allows 4.0. Whoever controls the ground game probably controls the afternoon.

What's happening on campus

Wallace Wade Stadium opened in 1929 and seats 40,004. Duke has won 18 conference championships, eight in the ACC and ten in the Southern Conference, and the program counts 53 All-Americans. A Halloween noon kickoff means expect a few Blue Devil costumes in the stands, which is the easiest costume in Durham.

Siobhan: I'm coming as a Supreme Court justice. Again.

Anything else fun and true

Duke's football rebirth under David Cutcliffe from 2008 to 2021 included the school's first Coastal Division title in 2013. Since then, the Blue Devils have stacked four straight winning seasons: 9-4, 8-5, 9-4 and 9-5. Boston College plays its home games at Alumni Stadium in Chestnut Hill. These two have split their last two meetings: BC won 26-6 in 2020, and Duke won 38-31 in 2022.

Harrison: Siobhan, whatever the verdict, lunch is on me after the game. Grace first, then barbecue.

Siobhan: The court accepts. Objection: sustained.

My call: Duke Blue Devils 29, Boston College Eagles 18.
