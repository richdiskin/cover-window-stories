Boston College vs Duke: BooFest Saturday and a Halloween Trial

Boston College Eagles (4-3) at Duke Blue Devils (5-2)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Wallace Wade Stadium · CW

Boston College Eagles vs Duke Blue Devils is a Week 9 Halloween noon ET kickoff at Wallace Wade Stadium, on the same Saturday Durham's Museum of Life and Science runs BooFest, with a costume catwalk and pumpkins popped in Gateway Park. Siobhan Dunleavy, a BC grad student and former debate champion, says Mason McKenzie and Bill O'Brien's Eagles are coming off a 399-yard rushing day, and Harrison Wexley-Moore IV, a Duke pre-everything overachiever, says Manny Diaz's defense and Nate Sheppard will rule the afternoon.

Siobhan: All rise! The Honorable Siobhan Dunleavy presiding. Also prosecuting. Also on the jury.

Harrison: Objection, your honor. That's a conflict of interest. I wrote a paper on it. Footnotes available upon request.

Durham has a full Halloween calendar. BooFest runs from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. ET, which means half the families in the stands will have candy in their pockets before kickoff. That afternoon, the Great British Bike Off fills Durham Central Park with vintage British motorcycles, horror movies and a bonfire. Then everybody gets an extra hour of sleep, because the clocks go back at 2 a.m. ET Sunday.

Harrison: An extra hour. I'll use it to grade the game film.

Siobhan: You'll need it, counselor. Exhibit A is Georgia Tech.

Exhibit A is hard to argue. Boston College won 49-27 in Atlanta on Oct. 24 and ran for 399 yards, its most since it ran for 496 at Syracuse in 2019. McKenzie carried 16 times for a career-high 170 yards and two touchdowns and threw for 101 yards and another score. Bo MacCormack III added a career-high 155 yards and three touchdowns. Charlie Comella intercepted two passes, taking the second one 47 yards for a touchdown, and the Eagles have 10 interceptions, the most in the ACC. Their 49 points were the most O'Brien's BC has scored against a Power Four opponent.

Siobhan: Exhibit B. McKenzie has 614 rushing yards, the most of any ACC quarterback.

McKenzie has thrown the most passes in all seven BC games. He's 107 of 185 for 1,363 yards, eight touchdowns and seven interceptions, with five rushing scores. Javarius Green leads the receivers with 26 catches for 286 yards. On defense, Anthony Palano has 61 tackles and Demetrius Ballard has 4.5 sacks.

Duke won 19-16 at Virginia on Oct. 23. Walker Eget went 14 of 25 for 211 yards, Sheppard ran for a touchdown, and Jonah Burton caught four passes for 59 yards.

Harrison: A three-point win on the road. I accept that grade with gratitude.

Eget has thrown the most passes in all seven Duke games and is 122 of 197 for 1,440 yards, eight touchdowns and three interceptions. Sheppard has 875 yards and nine touchdowns on 153 carries. Burton leads the receivers with 21 catches for 298 yards. On defense, Nick Morris Jr. has 44 tackles and Kevin O'Connor has four sacks.

Harrison: Eight hundred seventy-five yards. Sheppard's carries deserve their own peer-reviewed journal.

The defense is Duke's closing argument. Diaz's Blue Devils allow 16 points a game and 3.3 yards a carry. They held Tulane to three and Stanford to seven. Diaz was hired on Dec. 7, 2023, after coaching Miami and running Penn State's defense. O'Brien, named BC's 37th head coach on Feb. 9, 2024, also coached at Penn State, then won four AFC South titles with the Houston Texans.

Siobhan: Two Penn State men on one field. I move that they share a pregame handshake.

Harrison: Seconded. Motion carries.

The ACC's initial availability report, posted at 8 p.m. ET Wednesday, lists six Boston College players out and one questionable, and 11 Duke players out, four questionable and one probable. Mason McKenzie and Nate Sheppard are not on it.

The ground game probably settles it. Duke runs for 5.3 yards a carry and BC allows 4.0, while BC runs for 4.7 and Duke allows 3.3. If McKenzie breaks loose again, the Eagles can play their game. If Duke keeps him bottled up, BC has to throw.

Wallace Wade opened in 1929 and seats 40,004. Duke has won 18 conference titles, eight in the ACC and ten in the Southern Conference. The last two meetings were split: BC won 26-6 in 2020, and Duke won 38-31 in 2022.

Siobhan: I'm coming as a Supreme Court justice. Again.

Harrison: I'm coming as a Blue Devil. It's the easiest costume in Durham.

Siobhan: After the verdict, I'm taking the bonfire at Central Park.

Harrison: Lunch first, counselor. Grace, then barbecue, then motorcycles.

My call: Duke Blue Devils 29, Boston College Eagles 18.
