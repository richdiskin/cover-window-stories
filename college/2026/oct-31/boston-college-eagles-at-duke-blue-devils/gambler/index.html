Boston College Eagles (4-3) at Duke Blue Devils (5-2)

Boston College vs Duke prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on Duke Blue Devils −7 and Under 47.5 at DraftKings, with a final of Duke Blue Devils 29, Boston College Eagles 18. It's a Halloween noon ET kickoff at Wallace Wade Stadium, and the question is whether Duke's defense can slow a Boston College ground game that just ran over Georgia Tech.

Begin with that defense. Manny Diaz's Blue Devils allow 16 points a game and 3.3 yards a carry, and they haven't given up more than 28 points in any game this season. At home they beat Tulane 17-3, Stanford 35-7 and William & Mary 62-7. Boston College's passing game has eight touchdowns and seven interceptions, and Mason McKenzie is also its leading rusher. Keep him in the pocket and the Eagles get one-dimensional. My final has Duke by eleven.

Siobhan Dunleavy, the BC grad student, has her toy gavel out. "Exhibit A: McKenzie ran 16 times for 170 yards and two touchdowns at Georgia Tech, and we won 49-27. Anthony Palano has 61 tackles. Objection sustained."

Harrison Wexley-Moore IV, the Duke overachiever, brought a syllabus. "Counselor, Nate Sheppard has 875 rushing yards and nine touchdowns. We held Tulane to three and Stanford to seven, and we won 19-16 at Virginia. Class is in session."

Siobhan's exhibit is the real risk. BC gains 4.7 yards a carry, and Duke's last three games were all decided by four points or fewer: a 28-25 loss at Georgia Tech, a 24-20 loss to North Carolina and the 19-16 win at Virginia. If the Duke offense stalls the way it did in those, this is a one-score game late.

The Under comes from the same place. BC scored 15, 22, 14 and 16 in four of its games this season, and Duke runs the ball and keeps it. Duke gains 5.3 yards a carry and BC allows 4.0, so the clock should keep moving. My 29-18 adds up to 47, a half point under 47.5.
