Boston College Eagles at Duke Blue Devils

Boston College Eagles (4-3) at Duke Blue Devils (5-2)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Wallace Wade Stadium · CW

Siobhan Dunleavy bangs her toy gavel on a Wallace Wade bleacher and calls the court to order. Harrison Wexley-Moore IV objects on procedural grounds, cites three footnotes, and reminds everyone that he also passed that exam. Whichever one you're thinking of.

Boston College (4-3) at Duke (5-2), Saturday at 12:00 p.m. ET on CW at Wallace Wade Stadium in Durham. It's an ACC game, and both teams are 2-2 in league play. DraftKings has Duke Blue Devils −7 with a total of 47.5. My card: Duke Blue Devils −7, Under 47.5, and a final of Duke Blue Devils 29, Boston College Eagles 18.

Siobhan speaks for Boston College, iced coffee in hand. "Exhibit A: Mason McKenzie ran 16 times for 170 yards and two touchdowns at Georgia Tech, and we won 49-27. Exhibit B: we've won two straight. Anthony Palano has 61 tackles. Objection: sustained."

Harrison speaks for Duke, and he brought a syllabus. "Counselor, Nate Sheppard has 875 rushing yards and nine touchdowns. Our defense allows 16 points a game. We held Tulane to three and Stanford to seven. And we won 19-16 at Virginia last week. Class is in session."

Why Duke? Because this is a defense game, and Duke has the better defense. The Blue Devils allow 3.3 yards a carry and 16 points a game. Boston College's passing game has eight touchdowns and seven interceptions, and McKenzie is also its leading rusher, so if Duke keeps him in the pocket, the Eagles get one-dimensional. At home Duke beat Tulane 17-3, Stanford 35-7 and William & Mary 62-7. My final has Duke by eleven, and that clears the 7.

The worry is the run game on the other side. BC gains 4.7 yards a carry, and McKenzie just broke Georgia Tech for 170. Duke's last three games were all decided by four points or fewer, a 28-25 loss at Georgia Tech, a 24-20 loss to North Carolina and the 19-16 win at Virginia. If the Duke offense stalls the way it did in those, this is a one-score game.

Why the Under? Duke hasn't allowed more than 28 points in any game this season. BC scored 15, 22, 14 and 16 in four of its games this season. Duke runs the ball and holds it, and that shortens the game. My 29-18 adds up to 47, a half point under 47.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Duke 28-19, Duke −7 and the Under. Next Man Up has Duke 30-16, same side, same Under. Follow The Money sits out because the market hasn't moved, and In The Trenches says the run-game gaps cancel. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up started it: "Nobody's arguing with Duke this week. Siobhan's going to file an appeal anyway."

The Formula nodded: "Let her. Duke allows 16 points a game. That's the whole brief."

Follow The Money kept quiet: "Market didn't budge, so neither do I."

Bottom of the card: the better defense at home, a BC offense that relies on one runner, and two scores in the room under the total. Harrison grades it an A. Give me the Blue Devils and the Under.

Duke Blue Devils 29, Boston College Eagles 18. Duke Blue Devils cover the 7. Under 47.5.
