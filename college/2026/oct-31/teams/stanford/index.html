Cardinal Versus Cardinals: Stanford Brings Its Birds and Brains to Louisville on Halloween

Meet Your Insider
Hey, team! I'm Ignatius Yamamoto-Ross, Iggy for short, your Stanford insider. I've founded three apps that failed and built one Cardinal model that works. I travel and live with this team, and I describe football in startup jargon. A two-minute drill is a sprint. A punt is a pivot. A blowout loss is a learning opportunity, and we've had a few.

Here's my mission statement. I disrupt hype, not facts. Every number in this report has a source. If I don't know something, I'll mark it "pending data." I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I'm grateful to God for a night under the lights. Let's ship it.

The School in 60 Seconds
Stanford has fielded teams nearly every year since 1892. The school dropped football in favor of rugby from 1906 to 1917. Fun branding history: the teams were the "Cardinal," then mostly the "Cardinals," then the "Indians" from 1930 to 1972, then "Cardinals" again, and finally "Cardinal" since the 1982 season. A student vote in 1975 to become the "Robber Barons" wasn't approved. Best rejected rebrand ever.

Stanford now plays in the ACC after many years in the Pac-12. Home is Stanford Stadium, which CFBD lists as built in 2006 with 50,424 seats. The last five seasons were rough: four straight 3-9 years from 2021 to 2024, then 4-8 in 2025. This is a rebuild, and we're in the early funding rounds.

The Coach and the Scheme
Tavita Pritchard is the new head coach, hired for the 2026 season. CFBD records his hire date as Nov. 28, 2025. He's a Stanford alum and former quarterback. He played on the 2007 team that upset USC, widely considered one of the biggest upsets in college football history. He started coaching as a Stanford graduate assistant in 2010 and became offensive coordinator in 2018. From 2023 to 2025 he coached quarterbacks for the Washington Commanders. Founder returns to the company. Love that story.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 19.9 points per game and allowing 35.5.
