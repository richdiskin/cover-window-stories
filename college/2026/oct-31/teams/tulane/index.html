Green Wave in the Queen City: Tulane Rolls Into Charlotte for Friday Night Football

Meet Your Insider
Roll Wave! Hey now, I'm Antoine Ducros-Labat, but all Uptown calls me Twan. I've lived within earshot of a brass band my whole life, and I believe every football game is a brass-band parade: you dance your way through it, no matter what. I travel with the Green Wave and I live with the Green Wave, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines, with a little music and a lot of food. You'll hear me say a big play "deserved a beignet." I mean that literally. I keep a bag of them in the booth. Uptown on game day, there's a brass band on every corner and a Green Wave flag on every porch. When we win, the parade rolls all night. When we lose, we still dance home. Roll Wave, and save me a beignet.

The School in 60 Seconds
The Green Wave represent Tulane University and compete in the American Conference. Home is Yulman Stadium on campus in Uptown New Orleans, opened in 2014 and listed at 30,000 seats. And the music lately has been sweet: after 2-10 in 2021, Tulane went 12-2, 11-3, 9-5 and 11-3. That's four straight years of brass band, y'all.

The Coach and the Scheme
Will Hall is the head coach in his first season; CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 8, 2025.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 20.3 points per game and allowing 24.6.

Players to Know
QB Kadin Semonza: 64 of 119, 804 yards, 4 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB Johnnie Daniels: 394 yards on 66 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Destyn Hill: 26 catches, 339 yards, 2 touchdowns.
DL Ed Smith IV: 3 sacks, the team lead.
LB Dickson Agu: a team-high 64 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Tulane is 3-4 overall and 1-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 7th in American.
The season so far: L 3-17 at Duke Blue Devils (Sept. 5), W 28-24 vs South Alabama Jaguars (Sept. 12), L 20-31 at Kansas State Wildcats (Sept. 19), W 24-21 vs Southern Miss Golden Eagles (Sept. 26), L 17-20 at Army Black Knights (Oct. 10), W 31-17 vs Memphis Tigers (Oct. 16), L 19-42 vs UTSA Roadrunners (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Tulane (3-4, 1-2 American) at Charlotte (1-6, 1-2 American). Friday, Oct. 30, 7 p.m. ET (6 p.m. Central), Jerry Richardson Stadium, Charlotte, N.C., ESPN2.
Last time out. Charlotte had the week off after beating Temple 38-31 on Oct. 17 at Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia. It was the 49ers' first win of the season and the first American Conference win for coach Tim Albin. Rutledge ran for a career-high 123 yards, Derrick Eley caught six passes for 96 yards, and Aiden Bui, kicking for the first time in his career, hit a 47-yard field goal in the final two minutes.
Tulane lost 42-19 to UTSA at home on Oct. 24. Johnson went 24 of 44 for 265 yards, and Daniels ran eight times for 104 yards and a touchdown.
Players to watch. Charlotte quarterback Conner Harrell went 23 of 31 for 281 yards and two touchdowns at Temple and ran for two more scores. Running back Henry Rutledge leads the team with 331 rushing yards, and wide receiver Jaden Barnes leads the receivers with 40 catches for 396 yards. On defense, linebacker Gavin Willis has 53 tackles and DJ Burgess has two sacks.
For Tulane, freshman quarterback Trace Johnson has thrown the most passes in each of the last two games. Running back Johnnie Daniels has 394 yards and four touchdowns on 66 carries, and wide receiver Destyn Hill has 26 catches for 339 yards. Linebacker Dickson Agu leads the team with 64 tackles, and defensive lineman Ed Smith IV has three sacks.
Availability. The American has not posted an availability report for this game. Tulane tackle Ryan Mickow, who was hurt against UTSA, will be unavailable Friday, according to nola.com. Sophomore Reese Baker has moved between tackle and guard and has been the one constant among the big guys up front. No injury news has surfaced for either starting quarterback, so watch the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Will Hall is in his first season at Tulane. He was hired on Dec. 8, 2025. After the UTSA game he said, "I'm glad it's a short week. We know we've got to play better." He also called Charlotte "explosive on offense."
Tim Albin is in his second season at Charlotte. He went 30-12 in his last three years at Ohio before taking over the 49ers.
Storylines. Harrell made his second start of the season at Temple after dealing with injuries, and he looked sharp, with four different receivers going over 50 yards. Tulane's freshman quarterback, meanwhile, threw for nearly 200 yards in the first half against UTSA. "Trace was very poised," Hill said.
Tulane's Jah'rie Garner, who chases quarterbacks for a living, is staying humble. "I see them as a faceless opponent," he said. "In this league you can beat anybody."
Fun and true. Tulane won the last two meetings, 34-3 in 2024 and 27-0 in 2025. The Temple win was the 49th in Charlotte program history, so the next one will be No. 50. Tulane's three wins this season came over South Alabama, Southern Miss and Memphis.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Priya Halloran, Charlotte's insider. Here's what I said:
"Darlin', I brought beignets for the whole section. Roll Wave!"
"Sixty-four tackles from Agu. That man shows up to every parade."
"Coach Hall wants a clean game. No fumbles, no fuss, just a good parade."
"Fifteen thousand three hundred? That's a cozy block party. I love it."
"Then we'll both bring the brass band, darlin'. Roll Wave!"
And the last word from Priya: "Win No. 50 would be a milestone worth framing, Twan."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Jerry Richardson Stadium opened in 2013 and has a listed capacity of 15,300. The Pride of Niner Nation Marching Band will be there, and if you visit, ask somebody about the 49ers name, which dates to the school's founding in 1946.

My call: Tulane Green Wave 31, Charlotte 49ers 19.
