Take Me Home, Country Bye Week: The Mountaineers Rest Up in the Hills

Meet Your Insider
Mornin', Mountaineers. Welcome. I'm Jolene Buckhannon. I used to captain the rifle team, so I count possessions the way I used to count rounds: every one matters and you don't waste any. I travel with this team and I live with this team. I'm calm, flat, deadpan as a holler on a Sunday morning. until I'm not. I also keep a running pepperoni-roll tally of every fumble the other team coughs up. One roll per fumble, and I eat the tally afterward. When we win, I sing one verse of "Country Roads," drop the mic, then lift it right back up, because mics cost money. When we lose, I count to ten out loud, real slow, like I'm reloading. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, straight shooting. "Load up, I don't miss."

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Milan Puskar Stadium in Morgantown, listed at 60,000, and when that place sings "Country Roads," you feel it in your boots. The man in charge is a familiar one. Rich Rodriguez was named the 36th head coach in school history in December 2024, returning to the program he once led. His teams have always run the football, and this one sure does.

The Coach and the Scheme
Rodriguez was hired on Dec. 11, 2024.

The Conference Picture
West Virginia is 5-3 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 11th in Big 12.
The season so far: W 31-24 vs Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (Sept. 5), W 52-7 vs UT Martin Skyhawks (Sept. 12), W 38-27 at Virginia Cavaliers (Sept. 19), L 24-41 vs Oklahoma State Cowboys (Sept. 26), L 42-45 at Iowa State Cyclones (Oct. 3), L 31-52 vs Arizona Wildcats (Oct. 10), W 45-42 vs Cincinnati Bearcats (Oct. 17), W 24-20 at TCU Horned Frogs (Oct. 24).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for West Virginia this week. We're off from Tuesday, Oct. 27, through Saturday, Oct. 31, so the rest of this report is about where we stand and what's next.
Last time out. We beat the TCU Horned Frogs 24-20 on the road on Oct. 24. Michael Hawkins Jr. went 7 of 15 for 79 yards, with 1 touchdown and 1 interception. Cam Cook ran 18 times for 90 yards. Prince Strachan caught 2 balls for 24 yards.
Up next. We play the Texas Tech Red Raiders on the road on Saturday, Nov. 7, with the kickoff time still to be announced, at Galaxy Stadium in Lubbock.
The open week is for rest, healing and a good week of practice. I'll be back with a full preview before the next kickoff.
