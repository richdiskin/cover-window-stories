The Teal Tide Rolls Into Atlanta: Coastal Carolina Visits Georgia State

Meet Your Insider
Teal deal, y'all! I'm Marigold "Goldie" Seabrook, and I'm your Coastal Carolina insider. I grew up in Conway in a shrimp-boat family, and I'm an English major who writes like the tide. On the podcast, my segment is called Low Tide Notes. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, you won't hear it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Brooks Stadium in Conway, built in 2003 with a listed capacity of 21,000. The playing surface is James C. Benton Field. The last five seasons went 11-2, 9-4, 8-5, 6-7 and 6-7, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about the teal turf. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Ryan Beard leads the Chanticleers, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 11, 2025.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 26.1 points per game and allowing 25.9.

Players to Know
QB Deuce Bailey: 109 of 204, 1,638 yards, 16 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Dominic Lee-Knicely: 452 yards on 80 carries, 3 touchdowns.
WR Colton Hinton: 13 catches, 297 yards, 4 touchdowns.
DE Ibrahim Diawara: 5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Marcellius Pulliam: a team-high 67 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Coastal Carolina is 2-5 overall and 1-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 4th in Sun Belt East.
The season so far: L 24-31 at West Virginia Mountaineers (Sept. 5), W 45-7 vs Fordham Rams (Sept. 12), L 14-22 at Delaware Blue Hens (Sept. 19), L 17-34 vs Liberty Flames (Sept. 24), L 24-31 vs Georgia Southern Eagles (Oct. 3), W 27-21 at Marshall Thundering Herd (Oct. 10), L 32-35 vs App State Mountaineers (Oct. 16).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Coastal Carolina (2-5, 1-2 Sun Belt) at Georgia State (4-3, 1-2 Sun Belt). Saturday, Oct. 31, 2 p.m. ET, Center Parc Stadium, Atlanta, ESPN+.
Last time out. Coastal had an open date last week. Its last game was a 35-32 loss to App State on Oct. 16, when Bailey threw for 298 yards and three touchdowns, Lee-Knicely ran 18 times for 90 yards, and Robby Washington caught five balls for 118 yards and a score.
Georgia State lost 38-32 at Arkansas State on Oct. 24. Pereira threw two touchdowns, Qwantavius Wiggins ran eight times for 99 yards and a score, and Dorsey caught three balls for 85 yards and a touchdown.
Players to watch. Coastal quarterback Deuce Bailey has thrown the most passes in every game. He's 109 of 204 for 1,638 yards, 16 touchdowns and four interceptions. Dominic Lee-Knicely leads the rushers with 452 yards and three scores, and Colton Hinton leads the receivers with 297 yards and four touchdowns on 13 catches. On defense, linebacker Marcellius Pulliam has 67 tackles and end Ibrahim Diawara has five sacks.
Georgia State quarterback Cameran Brown threw the most passes in each of the first six games. He's 110 of 166 for 1,356 yards, 14 touchdowns and five interceptions, and he threw six touchdowns against Old Dominion. Ayden Pereira threw the most passes last week. Lanear McCrary Jr. leads the rushers with 399 yards and four touchdowns, and Rodshaun Dorsey leads the receivers with 24 catches for 418 yards and eight scores. Linebacker Xavier Esqillion has seven sacks, and Jaydon Hood has 45 tackles.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Bailey has thrown the most passes in every Coastal game, while Brown did for Georgia State in its first six games and Pereira did last week, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Ryan Beard leads Coastal Carolina, and CollegeFootballData dates his hire to Dec. 11, 2025. His defense has 15 sacks, 37 tackles for loss and 14 takeaways.
Dell McGee leads Georgia State, and CFBD dates his hire to Feb. 23, 2024. His offense holds the ball 32:34 a game and runs for 206 yards a game.
Storylines. The matchup to watch is the Georgia State ground game against a Coastal defense that allows 4.8 yards a carry. Coastal's big guys up front will need to slow McCrary and Wiggins. On the other side, Bailey and his receivers want to keep throwing deep, while Esqillion hunts for sack number eight. Georgia State is 3-0 at home this season, so Coastal will have to bring its best.
Fun and true. Georgia State football was founded in 2010. Coastal plays at Brooks Stadium in Conway, built in 2003, and its teal turf is the tradition Goldie would show you first. These two have met in every season since 2017, and Coastal has won five of those nine games, including a 51-0 win in 2020 and a 40-27 win in 2025. Georgia State won 42-40 in 2021 and 30-17 in 2023.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Center Parc Stadium sits about ten minutes from Georgia State's downtown campus and is listed at 25,000 seats. A Halloween afternoon in Atlanta should bring plenty of blue-and-white costumes, and Coastal fans will bring their teal.

My call: Georgia State Panthers 33, Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 25.
