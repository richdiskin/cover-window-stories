Boilers Head to Happy Valley: Purdue Brings Its Train to Penn State on Halloween

Meet Your Insider
Boiler up! I'm Barnaby Wolcott-Reese, and I'm your Purdue insider. I'm a West Lafayette native and a mechanical engineer, and I built a model Boilermaker Special in my garage. On the podcast, my segment is called Blueprint. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, you won't hear it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Ross-Ade Stadium in West Lafayette, built in 1924 with a listed capacity of 57,282. The band is the Purdue All-American Marching Band, and the fight song is "Hail Purdue!" The last five seasons went 9-4, 8-6, 4-8, 1-11 and 2-10, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about the Boilermaker Special. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Barry Odom leads the Boilermakers in his second season, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 10, 2024.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 31.3 points per game and allowing 32.3.

Players to Know
QB Ryan Browne: 145 of 221, 1,822 yards, 10 touchdowns, 3 interceptions.
RB Fame Ijeboi: 568 yards on 130 carries, 9 touchdowns.
WR Asaad Waseem: 38 catches, 588 yards, 1 touchdown.
DE Keyshawn Burgos: 1.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Jojo Hayden: a team-high 48 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Purdue is 3-4 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 8th in Big Ten.
The season so far: W 44-19 vs Indiana State Sycamores (Sept. 4), L 36-38 (OT) vs Wake Forest Demon Deacons (Sept. 12), L 38-52 at UCLA Bruins (Sept. 19), L 10-49 vs Notre Dame Fighting Irish (Sept. 26), W 24-17 at Illinois Fighting Illini (Oct. 3), W 41-13 vs Minnesota Golden Gophers (Oct. 10), L 26-38 vs Washington Huskies (Oct. 16).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Purdue (3-4, 2-2 Big Ten) at Penn State (3-4, 0-4 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 4:15 p.m. ET, Beaver Stadium, University Park, Pennsylvania, BTN.
Last time out. Both teams had a bye. Purdue last played on Oct. 16 and lost 38-26 to Washington at home. Browne threw for 273 yards, and Waseem caught nine balls for 116 yards.
Penn State last played on Oct. 17 and lost 21-18 at Michigan. Becht went 28 of 44 for 267 yards, and Sowell had a big day with 11 catches for 151 yards and a touchdown.
Players to watch. Purdue quarterback Ryan Browne has completed 145 of 221 throws for 1,822 yards, with 10 touchdowns and only three interceptions. Running back Fame Ijeboi has 568 yards and nine touchdowns on 130 carries. Wide receiver Asaad Waseem has 38 catches for 588 yards. Linebacker Jojo Hayden leads the defense with 48 tackles.
Penn State quarterback Rocco Becht has completed 132 of 210 throws for 1,664 yards and 12 touchdowns. Wide receiver Chase Sowell has 34 catches for 510 yards. Running back Carson Hansen has four rushing touchdowns. Linebacker Tony Rojas does a little of everything, with 55 tackles, five sacks and two interceptions.
Availability. No official Big Ten availability report had been posted as of Tuesday. Penn State wide receiver Koby Howard has missed the last four games with an undisclosed injury. He returned to practice and is considered questionable for Saturday.
The coaches. Matt Campbell is in his first season at Penn State, and he brought Becht with him from Iowa State. After the Michigan game, Campbell made it clear Becht is his quarterback. "We have complete faith in Rocco," he said. "Rocco has continued to obviously play good football." Penn State is still looking for its first Big Ten win under Campbell.
Barry Odom is in his second season at Purdue. The Boilermakers went 2-10 in 2025, so a 3-4 start with two Big Ten wins is real progress. They won 24-17 at Illinois and beat Minnesota 41-13.
Storylines. Both teams are 3-4, and both want to get back to even. Penn State's four losses came by four, 21, eight and three points, so the Nittany Lions have been close in most of them.
Purdue opened the year with Browne going 23 of 29 for 317 yards and three touchdowns against Indiana State. He threw for 378 yards at UCLA, and the Boilermakers took Wake Forest to overtime before losing 38-36.
Becht started his season in style, going 13 of 18 for 271 yards and four touchdowns in a 45-0 win over Marshall.
Fun and true. Penn State's defense allows only 18.9 points a game. Purdue scores 31.3 a game. That's a fun matchup on Halloween afternoon.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Beaver Stadium holds 106,572. The field is West Shore Home Field, and the Penn State Blue Band will be out in force. Penn State claims national championships from 1982 and 1986, and the White Out is the tradition every visitor should see once.
Purdue's Ross-Ade Stadium opened in 1924. The Boilermaker Special is the tradition Barnaby would show you first, and the Purdue All-American Marching Band makes the trip a treat.

My call: Penn State Nittany Lions 34, Purdue Boilermakers 23.
