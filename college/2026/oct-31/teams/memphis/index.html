Barbecue and Black Knights: Memphis Hosts Army on Halloween Night

Meet Your Insider
Well, hello there, friends. I'm Otis Delaney-Crum, your Memphis insider. I'm a Memphis native. I play blues guitar on weeknights and sing bass in the gospel choir on Sunday mornings, and to me football and music are the same thing. A good drive has a groove. A great one has a backbeat.

Here's how I work. I hum a riff whenever the Tigers score, and I rank opposing fight songs on my personal soul scale. I'm smooth and I laugh loud, but I don't play loose with facts. Every number I give you has a source. If I don't know something, I'll tell you I'm still tuning that string. I celebrate our players as young men first, and I respect every opponent who walks into our house. Go Tigers Go.

The School in 60 Seconds
Memphis started playing football in 1912. Since then the Tigers have won more than 500 games and appeared in 17 bowl games. For a long stretch, football lived in the shadow of the university's basketball program. Things changed starting with coach Justin Fuente's tenure.

The Tigers play in the American Conference at Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium. CFBD lists it at 58,325 seats, built in 1965, with an artificial surface. The last five seasons went 6-6, 7-6, 10-3, 11-2 and 8-5. That 11-2 in 2024 is a record I play on repeat.

The Coach and the Scheme
Charles Huff is new on the bandstand. CFBD records his hire date as Dec. 8, 2025. Wikipedia says he coached Southern Miss in 2025 and Marshall from 2021 to 2024. Before that he was associate head coach and running backs coach at Alabama, with stints at Mississippi State and Penn State. He's known as one of the top recruiters in college football. He was born in 1983, so he's a young bandleader.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 36.1 points per game and allowing 23.8.

Players to Know

RB Jaylin Carter: 833 yards on 128 carries, 8 touchdowns.

The Conference Picture
Memphis is 6-2 overall and 3-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 4th in American.
The season so far: W 27-21 at UNLV Rebels (Aug. 29), W 42-24 vs Arkansas State Red Wolves (Sept. 5), L 20-38 at Boise State Broncos (Sept. 12), W 45-21 vs UT Martin Skyhawks (Sept. 19), W 59-8 at Charlotte 49ers (Oct. 3), W 38-13 vs UAB Blazers (Oct. 10), L 17-31 at Tulane Green Wave (Oct. 16), W 41-34 (OT) vs East Carolina Pirates (Oct. 22).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Army (5-2, 4-1 American) at Memphis (6-2, 3-1 American). Saturday, Oct. 31, 8:00 p.m. ET (7:00 p.m. Central), Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium, Memphis, Tenn., ESPNU.
Last time out. Army won 38-35 at Tulsa on Friday night, Oct. 23, for its third win in a row. The Black Knights trailed 35-28 in the fourth quarter. Hellums tied it with a 1-yard run with 1:50 left, and Dawson Jones kicked a 50-yard field goal as time ran out. Jones also made one from 51, and the school says he's the first Army kicker to make two field goals of 50 or more in one game. Hellums ran for 121 yards and three touchdowns, and Godspower Nwawuihe ran 12 times for 135 yards, including a 66-yard touchdown.
Memphis beat East Carolina 41-34 in overtime on Thursday, Oct. 22. Stokes threw for 320 yards and accounted for all five touchdowns, three throwing and two running. Jemyri Davis caught seven balls for 91 yards and two scores. The win made Memphis bowl-eligible for the 13th straight season.
Players to watch. Army quarterback Cale Hellums is the engine of the option. He has 159 carries for 840 yards and 16 rushing touchdowns, and he has completed 39 of 69 throws for 422 yards and three touchdowns. Brady Anderson leads the team with 10 catches for 163 yards. On defense, linebacker Elijah Walton has 52 tackles and linebacker Victor Afolabi has three sacks.
Memphis quarterback Marcus Stokes is 150 of 224 for 2,161 yards, 21 touchdowns and six interceptions. Running back Jaylin Carter has 128 carries for 833 yards and eight touchdowns. Tight end Hunter Tipton leads the receivers with 30 catches for 409 yards and five touchdowns. On defense, Elijah Kinsler has 5.5 sacks and linebacker Mike Montgomery has 58 tackles.
Availability. No official availability report had been posted for this game as of Tuesday. Early injury pages show no reported injuries for either team. Watch for the official report closer to kickoff.
The coaches. Jeff Monken has led Army since 2014, and he coached Georgia Southern before that. After the Tulsa win, he said the Black Knights still need to improve, especially after a rough third quarter. Offensive coordinator Cody Worley even called a trick play at Tulsa, a 45-yard throw from slotback Zach Mundell to Anderson.
Charles Huff is in his first season at Memphis after coaching Southern Miss and Marshall. His offense moves the ball on the ground and through the air, and Stokes has thrown at least two touchdowns in all but one game.
Storylines. Hellums scored twice on fourth-and-1 at Tulsa, including a 41-yard run on the opening drive. Army went 3-for-4 on fourth down that night. For Memphis, Stokes threw for 384 yards at Boise State and 318 at Charlotte.
Fun and true. The two teams have not played each other in the regular season in the last 10 years. Army beat Tulane 20-17 on Oct. 10, and Memphis lost 31-17 at Tulane six days later, so Tulane is the one shared opponent so far.
Army's defense had two sacks, five tackles for loss, an interception and a blocked punt at Tulsa, and Walton made 10 tackles.
