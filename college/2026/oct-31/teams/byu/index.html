Mountain High on Halloween: No. 8 BYU Welcomes Arizona State to Provo

Meet Your Insider
Oh my heck, Cougar Nation! I'm Bryce Halvorsen-Tuilagi, your BYU insider. I'm a mountain-biking trail guide in Provo, and I chart every play like I'm reading a trail map from the top of Y Mountain. I tell dad jokes so clean they sparkle.

My promise to you is simple. Every number in this report has a source. If I don't know something, I'll tell you. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I thank God for mountains, family and fall Saturdays.

The School in 60 Seconds
BYU started playing college football in 1922 and has won 23 conference championships and the 1984 national championship. The Cougars were an FBS independent from 2011 to 2022 and joined the Big 12 on July 1, 2023. Home is LaVell Edwards Stadium, named for the coach who won 19 conference titles and that 1984 national title. Wikipedia lists 62,073 seats and CFBD lists 63,470.

The last five seasons: 10-3, 8-5, 5-7, 11-2 and 12-2. Oh my heck.

The Coach and the Scheme
Kalani Sitake played fullback for BYU under LaVell Edwards and graduated in 2000. He became head coach in 2016 (CFBD lists Dec. 19, 2015, as his hire date). Through 2025 he had four 10-win seasons. He's the first Tongan head coach in college football, and he entered the Polynesian Football Hall of Fame in 2025.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 33.1 points per game and allowing 18.6.

Players to Know
QB Bear Bachmeier: 116 of 184, 1,514 yards, 11 touchdowns, 3 interceptions.
RB LJ Martin: 344 yards on 44 carries, 3 touchdowns.
WR Legend Glasker: 28 catches, 498 yards, 4 touchdowns.
DE Tausili Akana: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Cade Uluave: a team-high 45 tackles.

The Conference Picture
BYU is 6-1 overall and 3-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 2nd in Big 12. We're ranked No. 8 in ESPN's rankings this week.
The season so far: W 63-7 vs Utah Tech Trailblazers (Sept. 5), W 28-17 vs Arizona Wildcats (Sept. 12), W 41-23 at Colorado State Rams (Sept. 19), W 17-10 at TCU Horned Frogs (Oct. 3), W 24-10 vs Iowa State Cyclones (Oct. 9), W 28-24 vs Notre Dame Fighting Irish (Oct. 17), L 31-39 (OT) at UCF Knights (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Arizona State (4-3, 2-2 Big 12) at No. 8 BYU (6-1, 3-1 Big 12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, LaVell Edwards Stadium, Provo, Utah, Fox.
