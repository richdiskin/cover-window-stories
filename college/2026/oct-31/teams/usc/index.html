Coming Soon to the Coliseum: USC Hosts Ohio State in a Halloween Classic

Meet Your Insider
In a world... where one team plays every game like it's opening night... Good evening, I'm Sterling Ashworth-Vance, film buff, football historian and your guide to the Trojans. I treat every game like a premiere, because at USC, every game is one. The Coliseum is the grandest movie set in sports, and every fall our Trojans play the lead. When we win, the music swells and I say, "Roll credits." When we lose, I call it a second-act setback, because the best films always have one. Fight On!

The School in 60 Seconds
The Trojans were formed in 1888. The program has more than 860 wins and claims 11 national championships, seven of them from the AP and/or Coaches polls. We now play in the Big Ten, at the 77,500-seat Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum in Exposition Park, right next to campus. CFBD dates the Coliseum to 1923. The last five seasons: 4-8, 11-3, 8-5, 7-6 in our first Big Ten year, and 9-4. A classic comeback arc.

The Coach and the Scheme
Lincoln Riley is in the director's chair, in his fifth season. CFBD dates his hire to Nov. 28, 2021.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 32.8 points per game and allowing 25.0.

Players to Know
QB Jayden Maiava: 183 of 274, 2,302 yards, 19 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB King Miller: 602 yards on 125 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Kayden Dixon-Wyatt: 32 catches, 443 yards, 4 touchdowns.
S Kennedy Urlacher: 3 sacks, the team lead.
LB Deven Bryant: a team-high 44 tackles.

The Conference Picture
USC is 6-2 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 7th in Big Ten.
The season so far: W 42-26 vs San José State Spartans (Aug. 29), W 39-0 vs Fresno State Bulldogs (Sept. 4), W 49-30 vs Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns (Sept. 12), W 42-35 at Rutgers Scarlet Knights (Sept. 19), L 27-41 vs Oregon Ducks (Sept. 26), W 25-21 vs Washington Huskies (Oct. 3), W 31-23 at Penn State Nittany Lions (Oct. 10), L 7-24 at Wisconsin Badgers (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
No. 2 Ohio State (6-1, 4-0 Big Ten) at USC (6-2, 3-2 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 7:30 p.m. ET (4:30 p.m. Pacific), Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum, Los Angeles, NBC.
Last time out. Ohio State had a bye week. Its last game was a 19-15 win at Indiana on Oct. 17, when Smith caught a touchdown and Jaylen McClain came up with a key strip sack.
USC lost 24-7 at Wisconsin. Maiava completed 31 throws for 258 yards and a touchdown to Taniela Tupou, but the Trojans could not get the run game going.
Players to watch. Ohio State quarterback Julian Sayin has thrown for 2,007 yards, 19 touchdowns and four interceptions, but he is out with an elbow injury. Luke Fahey or Tavien St. Clair will make his first career start. Wide receiver Jeremiah Smith has 52 catches for 984 yards and 12 touchdowns. Bo Jackson leads the ground game with 516 yards. On defense, safety Earl Little Jr. has 38 tackles, and Kenyatta Jackson Jr. has three sacks.
USC quarterback Jayden Maiava has completed 183 of 274 throws for 2,302 yards, 19 touchdowns and five interceptions. Running back King Miller has 602 yards and four touchdowns, and wide receiver Kayden Dixon-Wyatt has 32 catches for 443 yards. Linebacker Deven Bryant has 44 tackles, and safety Kennedy Urlacher has three sacks.
Availability. No official availability report had been posted as of Tuesday, and Ohio State's first report is set for Wednesday night. Ryan Day said Sayin is out indefinitely but is in good spirits and should travel with the team. Day said the bye week came at a good time and he expects most of his injured defenders back. Austin Siereveld is expected to return from an illness, and Day said the Buckeyes should have "a full arsenal of guys up front." Running back Ja'Kobi Jackson remains out after foot surgery.
The coaches. Day is in his eighth season leading Ohio State. He has not said who will start at quarterback, and he said it's "definitely possible" both young players see the field. "We all need to rally around the young quarterback," he said. He also called Lincoln Riley "a tremendous mind and a really good coach."
Riley is in his fifth season at USC. His offense likes to throw it around, and this week he needs his big guys up front to open running lanes again.
Storylines. Whoever starts for Ohio State gets a huge first assignment: a sold-out Coliseum on a Saturday night. Center Carson Hinzman said the blockers need "to take a lot off their plate." McClain said, "It's pressure that you want."
For USC, Jaylen McClain called Maiava a "really good veteran quarterback." Tupou caught the only USC touchdown at Wisconsin.
Fun and true. USC leads the all-time series 13-10-1 and has won eight of the last 10 meetings. Seven meetings came on New Year's Day in the Rose Bowl. In the Rose Bowl after the 1979 season, Charles White scored on a 1-yard run with 1:32 left, and USC won 17-16. The 1973 Rose Bowl between these two drew 106,869 fans, still an NCAA bowl game record. USC has eight Heisman Trophy winners and Ohio State has seven.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Brecken "Buck" Holloway, Ohio State's insider. Here's what I said:

"Get well soon, Julian. Everybody wants to see a healthy cast."
"And scene. Fight on."
And the last word from Buck: "Seven Heismans. I'll run a celebration route in the hallway."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
The Coliseum opened in 1923 and seats 77,500, right next to campus in Exposition Park. USC began playing football in 1888 and claims 11 national championships. Traveler and Tommy Trojan are campus icons.
Ohio State's home is Ohio Stadium, built in 1922 with room for 102,780. The Buckeyes claim national titles from 1942, 1954, 1957, 1961, 1968, 1970, 2002, 2014 and 2024. Brutus Buckeye leads the cheers.

My call: Ohio State Buckeyes 34, USC Trojans 23.
