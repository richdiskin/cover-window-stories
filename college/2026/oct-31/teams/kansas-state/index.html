Harvest Time in Boulder: Kansas State Brings the Combine to Colorado

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Well, howdy, Wildcat family. I'm Hattie Brandvold. I have an agronomy degree, a purple denim jacket, a wheat-sheaf brooch and a field notebook with real Flint Hills dirt on it. Every drive gets named after a crop. A three-and-out is a dry sorghum patch. A long, patient march is winter wheat. And a touchdown? That's a bumper harvest, and you will hear me holler it. When we win, I do the Wabash Cannonball in my chair, tidy and joyful. When we lose, I whisper my most treasured words, "You can't rush a harvest," and I plant a tiny basil seed right there on set. Sweet as prairie pie, until I hand you the one fact that ends the argument.

The School in 60 Seconds
Kansas State football is the story of the biggest turnaround in the sport. When Bill Snyder took over in 1989, the program was in the middle of a 27-game winless streak. He turned it into a national contender that went to 11 straight bowl games and had six 11-win seasons in a seven-year stretch. Snyder won two Big 12 championships, one in 2003 with Darren Sproles and one in 2012. He went into the College Football Hall of Fame while still coaching, in December 2015. The Wildcats play at Bill Snyder Family Stadium in Manhattan, and the rivalry with Kansas is the Sunflower Showdown, played for the Governor's Cup. Now that's a crop that keeps coming back every spring.

The Coach and the Scheme
Collin Klein was named the 36th head coach in school history on Dec. 4, 2025. You may remember him as the quarterback: he was a 2012 Heisman Trophy finalist and won the Johnny Unitas Award that year, when K-State won the Big 12. He is the first K-State alum to be head coach since Ellis Rainsberger in 1975-77. Before this job he coached nine years in Manhattan, including two as offensive coordinator (2022-23) that went 19-8 with a Big 12 title, then ran the offense at Texas A&M.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 32.9 points per game and allowing 21.1.

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Kansas State (4-3, 1-3 Big 12) at Colorado (2-5, 0-4 Big 12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 2 p.m. ET, Folsom Field, Boulder, Colo., TNT.
Last time out. Kansas State lost 28-17 at Arizona State on Oct. 24. Knuth was 26 of 41 for 194 yards and a touchdown, and Derrick Salley Jr. caught six balls for 63 yards.
Colorado lost 57-24 at Oklahoma State on Oct. 24. Wilson threw for 280 yards and two touchdowns, and Williams caught nine balls for 135 yards.
Players to watch. Kansas State quarterback Avery Johnson does it all. He's 118 of 187 for 1,301 yards, 10 touchdowns and three interceptions, and he leads the team in rushing with 462 yards and three scores. Jacob Knuth threw the most passes at Arizona State last week. Jaron Tibbs leads the receivers with 34 catches for 325 yards and four touchdowns. On defense, end Wendell Gregory has 3.5 sacks and safety Logan Bartley has 45 tackles.
Colorado quarterback Isaac Wilson has thrown the most passes in each of the last four games. He's 77 of 128 for 979 yards, six touchdowns and seven interceptions. Julian Lewis threw the most in the first three games. JaQuail Smith leads the rushers with 298 yards, and Joseph Williams leads the receivers with 46 catches for 643 yards and five scores. Lamont Lester Jr. has five sacks, and linebacker Gideon Lampron leads the team with 65 tackles.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Johnson threw the most passes in K-State's first six games and Knuth did last week, while Wilson has done it in Colorado's last four, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Collin Klein was named Kansas State's 36th head coach on Dec. 4, 2025. He was a 2012 Heisman Trophy finalist and won the Johnny Unitas Award as the Wildcats' quarterback, and he's the first K-State alum to lead the program since Ellis Rainsberger in 1975-77.
Deion Sanders, "Coach Prime," has led Colorado since his hire on Dec. 3, 2022. He played 14 NFL seasons, won two Super Bowls, played nine Major League Baseball seasons, and is the only athlete to play in both a Super Bowl and a World Series.
Storylines. The big matchup is the K-State ground game against a Colorado defense that allows 3.7 yards a carry. K-State's big guys up front have allowed only three sacks, so Johnson and company should have time. On the other side, Williams will be Wilson's top target, and Lester will try to chase down whoever plays quarterback for K-State.

Colorado won the 1990 national championship, shared with Georgia Tech, and has two Heisman winners, Rashaan Salaam in 1994 and Travis Hunter in 2024.
