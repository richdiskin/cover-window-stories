Parade Time in Mobile: South Alabama Hosts Louisiana Tech on Halloween

Meet Your Insider
Well, hey, y'all! I'm Azalea Fairhope-Grice, your South Alabama insider. I'm a Mobile native and a Mardi Gras float-builder, and I coached high school volleyball long enough to keep a whistle in my purse. I still have it. I will use it.

Here's my promise. I'll bring Southern charm and a competitive streak in equal measure. You'll get real numbers, honest takes, and gracious words for every opponent, because Mama raised me right. You won't get made-up stats or rumors dressed up as news. If I don't know, I'll say, "Darlin', I'm still checking." Mobile is where Mardi Gras started in America, and our Jaguars bring that same parade energy to every game. Pull up a lawn chair, honey.

The School in 60 Seconds
South Alabama started its football program in 2009 and joined the FBS in 2012 as a member of the Sun Belt Conference. That makes it one of the youngest programs in the country, and it has already had a 10-win season: 10-3 in 2022. Home is Hancock Whitney Stadium, which opened in 2020, and CFBD lists its capacity at 25,450.

The last five seasons: 5-7, 10-3, 7-6, 7-6 and 4-8.

The Coach and the Scheme
Major Applewhite has been head coach since 2024. CFBD lists his hire date as Jan. 18, 2024. He was head coach at Houston in 2017 and 2018, after serving as the Cougars' offensive coordinator and quarterbacks coach. He has also coached at Texas, Syracuse, Rice and Alabama. As a player, he quarterbacked Texas from 1998 to 2001, sharing the job with Chris Simms.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 35.1 points per game and allowing 32.4.

Players to Know
QB Jared Hollins: 68 of 125, 1,003 yards, 7 touchdowns, 3 interceptions.
RB Keenan Phillips: 514 yards on 96 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Brendan Jenkins: 25 catches, 430 yards, 3 touchdowns.
LB William Felton IV: 2 sacks, the team lead.
S Cole Blaylock: a team-high 46 tackles.

The Conference Picture
South Alabama is 4-3 overall and 2-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 2nd in Sun Belt West.
The season so far: W 39-14 vs SE Louisiana Lions (Sept. 5), L 24-28 at Tulane Green Wave (Sept. 12), W 41-36 vs Ohio Bobcats (Sept. 19), L 21-45 at Kentucky Wildcats (Sept. 26), W 52-35 vs UL Monroe Warhawks (Oct. 3), W 56-49 at Arkansas State Red Wolves (Oct. 8), L 13-20 at Marshall Thundering Herd (Oct. 20).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Louisiana Tech (5-2, 3-0 Sun Belt) at South Alabama (4-3, 2-1 Sun Belt). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3 p.m. ET, Hancock Whitney Stadium, Mobile, Ala., ESPN+.
Last time out. Louisiana Tech shut out Old Dominion 26-0 at home on Oct. 24. Baker went 28 of 34 for 303 yards and a touchdown, Burnette ran 20 times for 63 yards, and Eli Finley caught 10 balls for 128 yards and a score.
South Alabama lost 20-13 at Marshall on Tuesday, Oct. 20, so the Jaguars have had some extra days to get ready. Hollins threw for 148 yards, Phillips ran 16 times for 70 yards, and Jenkins caught four balls for 75 yards.
Players to watch. Louisiana Tech quarterback Blake Baker is 96 of 133 for 1,160 yards, 10 touchdowns and one interception. Trey Kukuk threw the most passes against Baylor and Army, but Baker has handled that role the last three games. Andrew Burnette leads the rushers with 349 yards, and Jalen Mickens leads the receivers with 40 catches for 586 yards and three scores. Linebacker Alonzo Jackson Jr. leads the defense with 48 tackles and 6.5 sacks.
South Alabama runs the ball first. Keenan Phillips has 514 rushing yards and five touchdowns on 96 carries. Jared Hollins plays quarterback and is 68 of 125 for 1,003 yards, seven touchdowns and three interceptions, after Bishop Davenport threw the most passes in the first two games. Brendan Jenkins leads the receivers with 25 catches for 430 yards and three scores. Safety Cole Blaylock leads the team with 46 tackles, and linebacker William Felton IV has two sacks.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Baker has thrown the most passes for Louisiana Tech in five of seven games, and Hollins has done it for South Alabama in each of the last five, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Sonny Cumbie is Louisiana Tech's head coach, hired Nov. 30, 2021. His defense takes the ball away, with 13 takeaways and a plus-5 turnover margin, and his team has won tight ones, 31-29 over Army and 32-31 over Louisiana.
Major Applewhite has led South Alabama since 2024. He was head coach at Houston in 2017 and 2018, and as a player he quarterbacked Texas from 1998 to 2001. His Jaguars run the ball much more than they throw it and score 35.1 points a game.
Storylines. The matchup to watch is Phillips and the South Alabama ground game against a Tech defense that has not allowed a point in two weeks. The Jaguars' big guys up front will need to open lanes. On the other side, Baker has been sharp, and Mickens and Finley give him places to throw. Tech is trying to stay unbeaten in league play in its first Sun Belt season.
Fun and true. Louisiana Tech first played football in 1901 and has won three Division II national championships and 25 conference titles. After 13 seasons in Conference USA, Tech joined the Sun Belt on July 1, 2026. South Alabama started its program in 2009, joined the FBS in 2012 and went 10-3 in 2022. These two have met before: Tech won 34-16 in 2017 and 30-26 in 2018, and South Alabama won 38-14 in 2022.
