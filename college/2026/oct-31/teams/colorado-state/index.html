Ram Tough on the Trail: Colorado State Laces Up for Logan on Halloween

Meet Your Insider
Mountain air, thick defense. Hello, friends, I'm Ansel Rimrock. Most mornings you'll find me on a trail in the foothills west of town, and on fall Saturdays you'll find me with the Rams. I travel with this team and I live with this team, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines. I'm a calm guy, but don't mistake calm for quiet: I'm competitive right down to my trail shoes. I think football is just a long trail run with blocking. You pace yourself, you climb, you don't panic on the switchbacks, and you finish strong. Fort Collins on a fall Saturday is green and gold from the foothills to the stadium. Visiting teams breathe a little harder up here, and our kids love it. Ram tough, trail ready.

The School in 60 Seconds
The Colorado State Rams compete in the Pac-12. Our big rivalries are with Colorado, Wyoming and Air Force. We've played at Canvas Stadium since 2017; before that, the Rams spent 49 seasons, 1968 to 2016, at Sonny Lubick Field at Hughes Stadium. CFBD lists Canvas at 36,500 seats. The last five seasons were a steep climb: 3-9, 3-9, 5-7, a nice 8-5 in 2024 and then 2-10 in 2025. Every trail has a hard stretch. We're climbing out of one.

The Coach and the Scheme
Jim Mora was hired on Nov. 26, 2025, and this is his first season.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 38.6 points per game and allowing 44.0.

Players to Know
QB Hauss Hejny: 71 of 104, 952 yards, 13 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB Mel Brown: 454 yards on 52 carries, 3 touchdowns.
WR RJ Vick: 24 catches, 367 yards, 4 touchdowns.
DL, the team sack leader: 1.5 sacks.
LB Oumar Diomande: a team-high 67 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Colorado State is 3-4 overall and 1-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 5th in Pac-12.
The season so far: W 35-13 vs Wyoming Cowboys (Sept. 5), W 58-24 vs Southern Utah Thunderbirds (Sept. 12), L 23-41 vs BYU Cougars (Sept. 19), L 45-59 at UTSA Roadrunners (Sept. 26), L 26-56 vs Oregon State Beavers (Oct. 3), L 27-66 at Texas State Bobcats (Oct. 15), W 56-49 vs San Diego State Aztecs (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Colorado State (3-4, 1-2 Pac-12) at Utah State (2-5, 1-2 Pac-12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, Maverik Stadium, Logan, Utah, USA Net.
Last time out. Colorado State beat San Diego State 56-49 at home on Oct. 24. Hejny threw for 333 yards and three touchdowns, Durell Robinson ran for two scores, and Tommy Maher caught six balls for 108 yards and two touchdowns.
Utah State lost 20-17 at Texas State on Oct. 24, on Homecoming night in San Marcos. Brosterhous threw for 206 yards, and Anthony Garcia caught eight balls for 87 yards.
Players to watch. Colorado State quarterback Hauss Hejny threw the most passes in the first two games and again last week. He's 71 of 104 for 952 yards, 13 touchdowns and two interceptions. K'saan Farrar threw the most in the four games in between. Mel Brown leads the rushers with 454 yards on just 52 carries, and RJ Vick leads the receivers with 24 catches for 367 yards. Linebacker Oumar Diomande leads the defense with 67 tackles.
Utah State quarterback Grady Brosterhous has thrown the most passes in six straight games. He's 99 of 178 for 1,120 yards, seven touchdowns and six interceptions. Javen Jacobs leads the rushers with 435 yards, and Eli Wood leads the receivers with 37 catches for 479 yards and five touchdowns. Safety Brevin Hamblin leads the defense with 58 tackles, and Jordan Pendleton has 2.5 sacks.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Hejny threw the most passes for Colorado State last week, and Brosterhous has done it for Utah State in six straight games, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Jim Mora was hired at Colorado State on Nov. 26, 2025, and this is his first season. His offense can move the ball and takes care of it, with only four turnovers all season. The defense is the work in progress, and Mora has kept his team swinging, as last week's shootout showed.
Bronco Mendenhall leads Utah State, with a hire date of Dec. 6, 2024. His Aggie defense has kept games close, including a 16-14 loss at Washington and a 17-16 win over Washington State. The offense is the piece he's still building.
Storylines. The matchup to watch is Colorado State's run game against a Utah State defense that has been steady all season. Brown has been breaking long runs, and the Aggies' big guys up front need to keep him in front of them. On the other side, Brosterhous and Wood need to find the end zone against a Rams defense that has had a hard time. Utah State won five of the eight meetings from 2016 through 2025, but Colorado State won the last one, 42-37 in 2024.
Fun and true. Utah State football started in 1892, one of the oldest programs in the western United States, and the Aggies have won 13 conference championships across four leagues, most recently in 2021, when they went 11-3. Colorado State spent 49 seasons, from 1968 to 2016, at Sonny Lubick Field at Hughes Stadium before moving to Canvas.
