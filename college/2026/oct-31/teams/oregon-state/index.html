Beavers Branch Out: Oregon State Visits Fresno State on Halloween Night

Meet Your Insider
Measure twice, kick once! I'm Juniper Ostrowski, and I'm your Oregon State insider. I'm a forestry PhD who charts treetop weather and red-zone efficiency. Fair warning: I correct everyone's tree species in metaphors. On the podcast, that segment is called Tree ID. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, you won't hear it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Reser Stadium in Corvallis, built in 1953 with a listed capacity of 43,363. The fight song is "Hail to Old OSU," and the band is the Oregon State University Marching Band. The last five seasons went 7-6, 10-3, 8-5, 5-7 and 2-10, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about Benny the Beaver. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
JaMarcus Shephard leads the Beavers, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Nov. 28, 2025.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 42.7 points per game and allowing 23.1.

Players to Know
QB Braden Atkinson: 189 of 273, 2,604 yards, 24 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB AJ Newberry: 431 yards on 65 carries, 3 touchdowns.
WR Jesse Legree: 46 catches, 1,040 yards, 14 touchdowns.
LB Takari Hickle: 6 sacks, the team lead.
LB Aiden Sullivan: a team-high 53 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Oregon State is 5-2 overall and 3-0 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 1st in Pac-12.
The season so far: L 20-33 at Houston Cougars (Sept. 5), L 24-35 vs Texas Tech Red Raiders (Sept. 12), W 52-17 vs Montana Grizzlies (Sept. 19), W 33-7 at UTEP Miners (Sept. 26), W 56-26 at Colorado State Rams (Oct. 3), W 66-14 vs San Diego State Aztecs (Oct. 10), W 48-30 vs Washington State Cougars (Oct. 17).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Oregon State (5-2, 3-0 Pac-12) at Fresno State (4-3, 1-2 Pac-12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 11:00 p.m. ET (8:00 p.m. Pacific), Valley Children's Stadium, Fresno, Calif., USA Net.
Last time out. Both teams had an open date on Oct. 24. Oregon State's last game was a 48-30 home win over Washington State on Oct. 17, when Atkinson threw for 347 yards and three touchdowns and Newberry ran for 105 yards and caught five balls for 120 more.
Fresno State lost 26-23 at San Diego State that same night. Bryson Donelson ran for a touchdown.
Players to watch. Oregon State quarterback Braden Atkinson is 189 of 273 for 2,604 yards, 24 touchdowns and just two interceptions. The Oregonian says he has thrown 129 straight throws without an interception, and the school record is 182 by Matt Moore. Wide receiver Jesse Legree has 46 catches for 1,040 yards and 14 touchdowns. Running back AJ Newberry has 431 yards. On defense, linebacker Aiden Sullivan has 53 tackles and linebacker Takari Hickle has six sacks.
Fresno State quarterback Jayden Mandal is 123 of 195 for 1,393 yards, eight touchdowns and one interception, and he has run for two scores. Rayshon Luke leads the rushers with 333 yards, and Josiah Freeman leads the receivers with 32 catches for 445 yards. Linebacker K'vion Thunderbird has 61 tackles, and defensive back Simeon Harris has three interceptions.
Availability. The official Pac-12 availability report usually posts on game day. The Oregonian reports that Oregon State cornerback TJ Crandall, who missed the Washington State game with a concussion, is good to go. Receiver Adonis McDaniel, who tore his Achilles in the winter, is expected to make his season debut. For Fresno State, Luke was listed as questionable with a concussion after the San Diego State game, so watch for his status on Saturday.
The coaches. JaMarcus Shephard is 5-2 in his first season at Oregon State. He was hired on Nov. 28, 2025. Matt Entz is 13-7 at Fresno State and won two FCS national championships at North Dakota State.
Storylines. McDaniel and Atkinson both came to Corvallis from Mercer. If McDaniel plays, two old teammates get to share a big Saturday night again.
Mandal is in his first season as a starter, and he has protected the ball all year with just one interception in seven games. He has also caught a 21-yard ball this year, so keep an eye on trick plays.
The Beavers have won five straight and have not trailed in any of them. Last year's meeting went the other way, a 36-27 Fresno State win in Corvallis.
Fun and true. Oregon State sits first in the Pac-12 at 3-0 in league games, and the Beavers have 17 sacks and nine takeaways. Fresno State's defense is second in the league in scoring, according to The Oregonian.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Rosalind "Roz" Ayala, Fresno State's insider. Here's what I said:
"Measure twice, kick once! I packed a costume. I came as a Douglas fir."
"Legree with 14 touchdowns is a giant sequoia. Tall, strong and impossible to miss."
"That is not how raisins work."
"A good defense is like an old-growth forest. You don't get through it quickly."
"I'm writing that down for the podcast."
And the last word from Roz: "And a good offense is like a raisin. Sweet, a little wrinkly, and gone before you know it."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Halloween in Fresno, and the Bulldogs haven't played on Oct. 31 since 2009. Fresno State is 7-4-1 on Halloween, including 5-0-1 at home. Oregon State is 4-9-1 on Halloween, so Juniper might want to keep that Douglas fir costume handy for luck.
Fresno State plays on Jim Sweeney Field, and Valley Children's Stadium seats 41,031. Oregon State's home, Reser Stadium, seats 43,363.

My call: Oregon State Beavers 30, Fresno State Bulldogs 27.
