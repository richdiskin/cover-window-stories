Philly Grit Sails South: Temple Visits East Carolina on Halloween

Meet Your Insider
Yo, that's Temple Made! I'm Teddy Fiorella, and I'm your Temple insider. I'm a North Philly gym rat who grades effort like a Rocky montage. Fair warning: I defend Philly against every insult, including ones nobody made. On the podcast, that segment is called Philly Defense. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, you won't hear it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia, built in 2003 with a listed capacity of 69,879. The fight song is "Fight! Temple Fight!" and the band is the Temple University Diamond Marching Band. The last five seasons went 3-9, 3-9, 3-9, 3-9 and 5-7, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about Hooter the Owl. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
K.C. Keeler leads the Owls, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 1, 2024.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 25.7 points per game and allowing 28.0.

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Temple (2-5, 1-2 American) at East Carolina (3-4, 1-2 American). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3 p.m. ET, Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium, Greenville, N.C., ESPN+.
Last time out. Temple had an open date last week. Its last game was a 38-31 loss to Charlotte on Oct. 17, when Sheppard threw for 236 yards and ran for two touchdowns, and Peter Clarke caught five balls for 127 yards and a score.
East Carolina lost 41-34 in overtime at Memphis on Thursday, Oct. 22. Griffis threw for 199 yards, Engleman ran 18 times for 92 yards and a touchdown, and Landon Sides caught seven balls for 130 yards and a score.
Players to watch. Temple quarterback Ajani Sheppard has thrown the most passes in six of seven games. He's 74 of 120 for 1,127 yards and eight touchdowns, and he can run, too. Keveun Mason leads the rushers with 513 yards and three scores, and Colin Chase leads the receivers with 18 catches for 308 yards. Linebacker Curly Ordonez leads the defense with 63 tackles and two sacks.
East Carolina has used two quarterbacks. Emory Williams leads the team at 73 of 117 for 716 yards, seven touchdowns and two interceptions, and Mitch Griffis threw the most passes at Memphis last week. TJ Engleman Jr. leads the rushers with 582 yards and four touchdowns, and Ray Ray Joseph Jr. leads the receivers with 31 catches. Linebacker DJ Johnson Jr. has 51 tackles, and Jasiyah Robinson has 2.5 sacks.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Sheppard has thrown the most passes for Temple in six of seven games, while Griffis and Williams have traded that role for East Carolina, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. K.C. Keeler leads Temple, and CollegeFootballData dates his hire to Dec. 1, 2024. His Owls run the ball on most plays, gaining 202.4 rushing yards a game, and they're disciplined, with only 37.3 penalty yards a game.
Blake Harrell is East Carolina's head coach, and CFBD records his hire date as Nov. 27, 2024. His defense allows 187.5 passing yards a game and has returned two interceptions for touchdowns this season.
Storylines. The matchup to watch is Mason and the Temple ground game against an ECU defense that allows 3.3 yards a carry. Temple's big guys up front will need to win in the trenches. On the other side, Engleman wants another big day, and Sides is coming off a big night at Memphis. ECU won the last three meetings, so Temple has some motivation.
Fun and true. East Carolina played its first season in 1932 and has won seven conference championships and eleven bowl games. The Pirates went 9-4 last year. Temple went 5-7 last season, and its fight song is "Fight! Temple Fight!" These two have met often: ECU won 49-46 in 2022, 56-34 in 2024 and 45-14 in 2025, while Temple won 49-6 in 2018.
