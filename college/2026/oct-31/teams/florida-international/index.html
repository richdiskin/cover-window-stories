Cafecito to Go: FIU Brings Miami Flavor to Lynchburg on Halloween

Meet Your Insider
¡Hola, Panther people! I'm Lourdes Ibarguen-Tate, and I grew up in Sweetwater, five minutes from campus, at a kitchen table where my abuela taught me football with cafecito and dominoes. The dominoes were the players. The sugar packets were the defense. To this day I can't watch a fourth-down stop without craving a colada.

Here's what I am: the loudest, warmest, most bilingual hype machine FIU has ever had, and I will never, ever let anybody sleep on this program. Here's what I'm not: a person who makes things up. Every week I'll bring you the real numbers, the real schedule, and an honest, kind look at whoever we're playing. If I don't know something, I'll say "no sé" and go find out. Miami has a lot of teams, but only one is right down the street from my abuela's house. ¡Vamos, Panthers!

The School in 60 Seconds
FIU plays in Conference USA at the FBS level, and home is Pitbull Stadium in Miami. The stadium opened in 1995, and CFBD lists its capacity at 20,000, which matches Wikipedia's figure. The program's trophy case includes a share of the 2010 Sun Belt championship and three bowl appearances.

Look at the last five seasons and you'll see why last year mattered so much to my family: 1-11 in 2021, then three straight 4-8 years, then 7-6 in 2025 and the first bowl trip since 2019. The Panthers also play Florida Atlantic every year in the Shula Bowl.

The Coach and the Scheme
Willie Simmons is in Year Two. CFBD lists his hire date as Dec. 7, 2024. Before FIU, he was head coach at Florida A&M from 2018 to 2023, winning a black college national championship and a SWAC title in 2023, and at Prairie View A&M from 2015 to 2017. He played quarterback at Clemson and The Citadel.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 20.1 points per game and allowing 16.3.

Players to Know

The Conference Picture
Florida International is 3-4 overall and 1-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 6th in CUSA.
The season so far: L 9-19 at South Florida Bulls (Sept. 5), W 33-20 vs Buffalo Bulls (Sept. 12), L 10-16 at Florida Atlantic Owls (Sept. 19), W 20-3 vs Long Island University Sharks (Sept. 26), W 22-3 vs New Mexico State Aggies (Oct. 7), L 24-27 at Jacksonville State Gamecocks (Oct. 13), L 23-26 vs Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders (Oct. 20).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
FIU (3-4, 1-2 Conference USA) at Liberty (6-1, 3-0 Conference USA). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, Williams Stadium, Lynchburg, Va., CBSSN.
Last time out. Liberty won 34-6 at Kennesaw State on Thursday, Oct. 22. Purdie threw for 176 yards and a touchdown, and Davis ran for a score. Kennesaw State had won the last three meetings, so this one meant plenty.
FIU lost 26-23 to Middle Tennessee at home on Tuesday, Oct. 20, a "Paws at the Pit" night. Kohl threw three touchdowns, Lyons ran 19 times for 115 yards, and Kyle McNeal caught a scoring ball. The week before, the Panthers lost 27-24 at Jacksonville State.
Players to watch. Liberty quarterback Deshawn Purdie has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 80 of 133 for 1,226 yards and nine touchdowns, and he hasn't thrown an interception all season. Kam Davis leads the rushers with 583 yards and five scores, and Makai Jackson leads the receivers with 23 catches for 540 yards and five touchdowns. Linebacker Derrell Farrar leads the defense with 39 tackles, and Brenton Williams has three sacks.
FIU quarterback JJ Kohl has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 131 of 227 for 1,637 yards, 10 touchdowns and eight interceptions. Devonte Lyons leads the rushers with 383 yards, and Maguire Anderson leads the receivers with 36 catches for 568 yards. DeeJay Holmes Jr. has five sacks, and DeAndre Boykins leads the team with 37 tackles.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Purdie has thrown the most passes for Liberty every week, and Kohl has done the same for FIU, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Jamey Chadwell has led Liberty since the 2023 season, after head coaching stops at North Greenville, Delta State, Charleston Southern and Coastal Carolina. His first Liberty team went 13-1. This year's Flames run the ball and play defense, allowing 14.0 points a game.
Willie Simmons is in his second year at FIU. In his first season, the Panthers went 7-6 and made their first bowl trip since 2019. Before that, he won a black college national championship and a SWAC title at Florida A&M in 2023. He played quarterback at Clemson and The Citadel.
Storylines. The matchup to watch is Davis and Liberty's big guys up front against an FIU defense that allows only 16.3 points a game. On the other side, Kohl needs to find Anderson against a Liberty defense that has been stingy all season. These teams have met four times since 2020. Liberty won three, but FIU won the most recent one, 34-27 last season.
Fun and true. Liberty moved from FCS to FBS and became a full member in 2019. In 2020 the Flames entered the AP poll for the first time, at No. 25, and they joined Conference USA in 2023. FIU shared the 2010 Sun Belt championship and plays Florida Atlantic every year in the Shula Bowl.
