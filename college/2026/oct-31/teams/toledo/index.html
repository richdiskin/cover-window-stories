Rockets on the Launch Pad: Toledo Takes the Bye Week and Refuels

Meet Your Insider
How ya doin', Rocket fans! I'm Rocco Szczepanski, your Toledo insider. That's Szczepanski. It's silent... nah, it's all pronounced, just fast. I come from a Jeep-plant family here in the Glass City, so I grade offenses the only way I know how: in horsepower. A sputtering three-and-out is a lawnmower engine. A long, physical drive is a big V8 rumbling down Front Street. When we win, I rev an imaginary engine and do a burnout gesture right there on set. When we lose, I say "engine trouble" and pop the hood on the depth chart. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, Northwest Ohio style. "That's 400 horsepower of run game."

The School in 60 Seconds
Toledo plays at the Glass Bowl, listed at 26,038, a great old stadium in a city built on hard work. The Rockets won the MAC championship in 2022.

The Coach and the Scheme
Mike Jacobs came from Mercer to replace Jason Candle and was named head coach on Dec. 10, 2025.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 37.4 points per game and allowing 25.4.

Players to Know
QB John Alan Richter: 173 of 259, 2,244 yards, 17 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB CJ Miller: 609 yards on 109 carries, 7 touchdowns.
WR Adjatay Dabbs: 40 catches, 592 yards, 7 touchdowns.
DE Andrew Zock: 8 sacks, the team lead.
LB Julian Fox: a team-high 65 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Toledo is 6-2 overall and 3-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 2nd in MAC.
The season so far: L 20-30 at Michigan State Spartans (Sept. 4), W 63-10 vs Central Connecticut Blue Devils (Sept. 12), W 49-48 vs Temple Owls (Sept. 19), W 41-16 vs San Diego State Aztecs (Sept. 26), W 39-24 at Ball State Cardinals (Oct. 3), L 8-23 vs Buffalo Bulls (Oct. 10), W 34-14 at Eastern Michigan Eagles (Oct. 17), W 45-38 vs Western Michigan Broncos (Oct. 24).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for Toledo this week. We're off from Tuesday, Oct. 27, through Saturday, Oct. 31, so the rest of this report is about where we stand and what's next.
Last time out. We beat the Western Michigan Broncos 45-38 at home on Oct. 24. John Alan Richter went 29 of 41 for 391 yards, with 3 touchdowns and 1 interception. CJ Miller ran 33 times for 183 yards, with 1 touchdown. Adjatay Dabbs caught 8 balls for 124 yards, with 2 touchdowns.
Up next. We play the Sacramento State Hornets on the road on Wednesday, Nov. 4, 7:00 p.m. ET, at Hornet Stadium in Sacramento.
The open week is for rest, healing and a good week of practice. I'll be back with a full preview before the next kickoff.
