Meet Your Insider
Hey hey, Chippewas, and welcome! I'm Brynn Haverkamp, your Central Michigan insider. I'm a snowmobile-racing marketing grad from up north, and I scout Mount Pleasant like it's opening day of hunting season. I travel with this team and I live with this team. I track sacks like hunting-season tags, and every time we get one, I slip into a full Michigan "ope." When we win, it's "Ope, that's a W!" and I rev an imaginary snowmobile. When we lose, I retreat to my deer camp, which is a tent we set up right on set. It's very glamping-chic. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, straight from the north woods.

The School in 60 Seconds
Our stadium in Mount Pleasant has been home since 1972. It got its current name in 1983, expanded to 30,255 seats in 1998, and is the largest on-campus stadium in the MAC. That's a lot of maroon and gold.

The Coach and the Scheme
Matt Drinkall was named Central Michigan's 30th head coach on Dec. 9, 2024. He spent six seasons on the Army staff, where he coached the offensive front and was co-offensive coordinator. His 2024 Army group won the Joe Moore Award, given to the best blocking group in the country, and in 2023 Army ranked No. 7 nationally in rushing yards per game.

The Conference Picture
Central Michigan is 3-5 overall and 1-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 10th in MAC.
The season so far: L 7-38 at New Mexico Lobos (Sept. 5), W 13-10 vs Colgate Raiders (Sept. 12), W 24-10 vs Wyoming Cowboys (Sept. 19), L 3-52 at Miami Hurricanes (Sept. 26), W 41-17 vs Akron Zips (Oct. 3), L 14-31 at Ohio Bobcats (Oct. 10), L 14-21 vs Western Michigan Broncos (Oct. 17), L 27-28 vs Miami (OH) RedHawks (Oct. 24).
