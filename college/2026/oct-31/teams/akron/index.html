Zips on Pause: Akron Takes the Bye and Keeps the Wagon Wheel Dream Rolling

Meet Your Insider
Greetings, Zips fans, and welcome! I'm Hollister Grubbs, but everybody calls me Zip. I'm a polymer-science grad from the Rubber City, and I measure football the only scientific way: in tire tread depth. Deep, grippy tread means a team that holds the road on third down. Bald tires mean a team sliding all over the place. I travel with this team and I live with this team, usually with a clipboard of formulas. When we win, I bounce like a superball: BOING! When we lose, I say, "Some tread wear today," and start scribbling equations to figure out why. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, vulcanized for durability. "That's some sticky tread."

The School in 60 Seconds
Akron plays at InfoCision Stadium, listed at 30,000, in a city that put the world on rubber tires. Our big rivalry is the Wagon Wheel game against Kent State. The trophy dates to 1946, and Kent State won the first nine games for it. Kent State won the 2025 meeting 42-35. Before that, we won the 2023 and 2024 meetings, 31-27 and 38-17.

The Coach and the Scheme
Joe Moorhead was hired on Dec. 4, 2021. Before Akron he was Mississippi State's head coach in 2018 and 2019, then Oregon's offensive coordinator and quarterbacks coach in 2020 and 2021.

Players to Know
QB Brayden Roggow: 112 of 190, 1,343 yards, 7 touchdowns, 9 interceptions.
RB Jordan Gant: 618 yards on 127 carries, 6 touchdowns.
WR Marcel Williams: 34 catches, 375 yards, 3 touchdowns.
DL Cyrus Durham: 2.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Donelius Johnson: a team-high 71 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Akron is 1-7 overall and 0-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 12th in MAC.
The season so far: L 16-38 at Wake Forest Demon Deacons (Sept. 3), W 45-10 vs Robert Morris Colonials (Sept. 12), L 7-41 at Minnesota Golden Gophers (Sept. 19), L 10-38 vs UNLV Rebels (Sept. 26), L 17-41 at Central Michigan Chippewas (Oct. 3), L 23-25 vs Eastern Michigan Eagles (Oct. 10), L 14-34 at Miami (OH) RedHawks (Oct. 17), L 20-35 at Kent State Golden Flashes (Oct. 24).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for Akron this week. We're off from Tuesday, Oct. 27, through Saturday, Oct. 31, so the rest of this report is about where we stand and what's next.
Last time out. We lost to the Kent State Golden Flashes 35-20 on the road on Oct. 24. Brayden Roggow went 20 of 34 for 275 yards, with 1 interception. Jordan Gant ran 28 times for 162 yards, with 2 touchdowns. Kyan Mason caught 7 balls for 129 yards.
Up next. We play the Ohio Bobcats at home on Tuesday, Nov. 3, 7:00 p.m. ET, at InfoCision Stadium in Akron.
The open week is for rest, healing and a good week of practice. I'll be back with a full preview before the next kickoff.
