Geaux Rest: LSU Takes the Bye Week and Simmers Like a Good Gumbo

Meet Your Insider
Well, bonjour, Tiger fans! I'm Celestine Thibodeaux, but darlin', everybody calls me Cece. I'm a Baton Rouge girl through and through. I'll out-scout any coordinator in the SEC between two trays of crawfish, and I'll do it in heels. I also rank every defense on a spice scale. When we win, I lead a brass-band parade around the room and hand out imaginary beignets to everybody in reach. When we lose, I declare a "gumbo of grief" and list every ingredient that went wrong. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, seasoned just right.

The School in 60 Seconds
LSU plays in Tiger Stadium in Baton Rouge, listed at 102,321. When that place gets rocking at night, you can feel it in your bones, cher. The 2019 Tigers won the national championship, and quarterback Joe Burrow won the Heisman Trophy that season.

The Coach and the Scheme
Lane Kiffin came over from Ole Miss to take the LSU job, hired on Nov. 30, 2025.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 40.1 points per game and allowing 16.0.

Players to Know
QB Sam Leavitt: 180 of 256, 2,173 yards, 17 touchdowns, 8 interceptions.
RB Dilin Jones: 657 yards on 149 carries, 6 touchdowns.
WR Jackson Harris: 36 catches, 634 yards, 7 touchdowns.
DE Princewill Umanmielen: 8.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB TJ Dottery: a team-high 58 tackles.

The Conference Picture
LSU is 6-2 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 4th in SEC.
The season so far: W 51-10 vs Clemson Tigers (Sept. 5), W 45-14 vs Louisiana Tech Bulldogs (Sept. 12), L 24-32 at Ole Miss Rebels (Sept. 19), W 35-6 vs Texas A&M Aggies (Sept. 26), W 63-14 vs McNeese Cowboys (Oct. 3), L 34-35 at Kentucky Wildcats (Oct. 10), W 31-0 vs Mississippi State Bulldogs (Oct. 17), W 38-17 at Auburn Tigers (Oct. 24).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for LSU this week. We're off from Tuesday, Oct. 27, through Saturday, Oct. 31, so the rest of this report is about where we stand and what's next.
Last time out. We beat the Auburn Tigers 38-17 on the road on Oct. 24. Sam Leavitt went 32 of 45 for 387 yards, with 3 touchdowns. Dilin Jones ran 21 times for 92 yards, with 1 touchdown. Jackson Harris caught 9 balls for 178 yards, with 2 touchdowns.
Up next. We play the Alabama Crimson Tide at home on Saturday, Nov. 7, 3:30 p.m. ET, at Tiger Stadium in Baton Rouge.
The open week is for rest, healing and a good week of practice. I'll be back with a full preview before the next kickoff.
