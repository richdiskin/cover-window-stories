Space Game at the Bounce House: UCF Welcomes Baylor to Orlando for Friday Night Liftoff

Meet Your Insider
Charge On, Knights! I'm Knox Brevard-Stiles, your UCF insider. I grew up in Orlando, I'm a theme-park ride engineer, and I swear the stands really move. I measure crowd noise with a ride-safety gauge, and I wear a knight's helmet after big wins. Please keep your hands and feet inside the stadium at all times.

My Ride Check: every cheer gets measured, every claim gets checked. Every number in this report has a source. If I don't know something, I'll say so. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I thank God for Florida sunshine and fall football.

The School in 60 Seconds
UCF first fielded a varsity team in 1979 as an NCAA Division III program and reached Division I-A, now the FBS, in 1996. That made UCF the first program in NCAA history to play in all four divisions of college football. As a Division I-AA program, the Knights made the 1990 and 1993 playoffs. Today they play in the Big 12.

Home is the Acrisure Bounce House on UCF's main campus east of Orlando. Wikipedia lists 45,906 seats and CFBD lists 44,206. CFBD says it opened in 2007. The first game there was a 35-32 loss to No. 6 Texas on Sept. 15, 2007, and UCF led 24-23 early in the fourth quarter. Ride Check: the stands held up just fine that day.

The last five seasons: 9-4, 9-5, 6-7, 4-8 and 5-7.

The Coach and the Scheme
Scott Frost is back for a second run in Orlando. He coached UCF in 2016 and 2017, and his 2017 team went 13-0. Then he coached Nebraska from 2018 to 2022. CFBD lists his return as Dec. 7, 2024. As a player, he led Nebraska to the 1997 national championship. He has coached Heisman winner Marcus Mariota and Heisman candidate McKenzie Milton.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 32.7 points per game and allowing 23.0.

The Conference Picture
UCF is 4-3 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 7th in Big 12.
The season so far: W 73-6 vs Bethune-Cookman Wildcats (Sept. 3), L 7-12 at Pittsburgh Panthers (Sept. 12), W 44-30 vs Georgia State Panthers (Sept. 19), W 21-13 vs TCU Horned Frogs (Sept. 26), L 17-27 at Houston Cougars (Oct. 3), L 28-42 at Oklahoma State Cowboys (Oct. 10), W 39-31 (OT) vs BYU Cougars (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Baylor (4-3, 2-2 Big 12) at UCF (4-3, 2-2 Big 12). Friday, Oct. 30, 7:30 p.m. ET (6:30 p.m. Central), Acrisure Bounce House, Orlando, ESPN.
Last time out. UCF beat No. 2 BYU 39-31 in double overtime at home. Jenkins went 25 of 41 for 293 yards and three touchdowns, ran for another score, and found Derry on the first play of overtime. Tyren Hornes caught six passes for 137 yards.
Baylor lost 25-24 at Kansas. A 45-yard field goal try with 22 seconds left was blocked, the third Baylor field goal try the Jayhawks blocked that day. Freeman caught nine passes for 123 yards.
Players to watch. UCF quarterback Keyone Jenkins is 108 of 159 for 1,099 yards, 11 touchdowns and three interceptions, and he has run for four more scores. Running back Duke Watson has 577 yards on 85 carries, and wide receiver Josh Derry leads the team with 34 catches for 358 yards. Linebacker Tackett Curtis has 42 tackles, and Isaiah Nixon has 2.5 sacks.
Baylor quarterback DJ Lagway is 113 of 217 for 1,352 yards, nine touchdowns and four interceptions. Wide receiver Gavin Freeman has 58 catches for 678 yards and ranks third in the nation in receptions per game. Linebacker Travion Barnes has 51 tackles, and defensive end Garrick Ponder has four sacks.
Availability. The Big 12 has not posted an availability report for this game. UCF coach Scott Frost said Jenkins will start again. Alonza Barnett III, who hurt his hamstring in Week 2, is making progress, and Frost said, "I think he probably could play," though he was not sure Barnett was fully healthy yet. No injury news has surfaced for Baylor's starting quarterback, so watch the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Baylor parted ways with seventh-year coach Dave Aranda after the Kansas game. Athletic director Doug McNamee called Aranda "a genuine and honorable person" who "deeply cares for his players." Joe Klanderman, the first-year defensive coordinator, is the interim head coach for the rest of the season. He won four national titles at North Dakota State and led the defense at Kansas State before coming to Waco.
Scott Frost is in his second year of his second stint at UCF. He led the Knights in 2016 and 2017, and his 2017 team went 13-0.
Storylines. Jenkins has been the steady hand while Barnett heals. After the BYU win, Jenkins said, "It's still his team. Still his starting job." That is a great teammate talking.
Baylor's defense ranks third in the nation in sacks and fifth in tackles for loss per game. Watson and the big guys up front for UCF will need a strong night.
Fun and true. Baylor leads the series 2-1. UCF won the first meeting, 52-42 in the 2013 Fiesta Bowl. In 2023 in Orlando, Baylor came back from 35-7 down in the third quarter to win 36-35, the biggest comeback in Bears history, and in 2025 Baylor won 30-3 in Waco. After this one, Baylor goes home for Homecoming against Iowa State on Nov. 7.
