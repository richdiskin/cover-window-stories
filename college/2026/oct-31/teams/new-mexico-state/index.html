Chile Heat in Las Cruces: New Mexico State Hosts Jax State on Wednesday Night

Meet Your Insider
¡Buenas noches, Aggies! I'm Esperanza Lujan-Tafoya, your New Mexico State insider. I'm a Las Cruces native, a chile farmer's daughter and an ag-science grad. That means I can tell you the soil pH of the practice field and how much Scoville heat a fourth-down stop packs. (A lot. A fourth-down stop is Christmas-style, red and green.)

Everything gets a chile grade from me. A routine first down? Mild. A 40-yard run? Hatch hot. But I don't heat up the facts. Every number I give you is real and sourced. Every opponent gets respect. If I don't know something, I'll say so, and then I'll go find out. In the Mesilla Valley, fall smells like roasting chile and fresh-cut grass. Our Aggies play with that same heat, and the Organ Mountains watch every snap.

The School in 60 Seconds
New Mexico State plays FBS football in Conference USA. The road here was winding. The Aggies were independent in 2013, a football-only Sun Belt member from 2014 to 2017, and independent again from 2018. They announced in November 2021 that they would join Conference USA in all sports starting in 2023. Home is Aggie Memorial Stadium in Las Cruces, which CFBD lists as built in 1978 with a capacity of 28,853.

The recent highs are real, though. The Aggies went 10-5 in 2023. The last five seasons: 2-10, 7-6, 10-5, 3-9 and 4-8.

The Coach and the Scheme
Tony Sanchez is the head coach. CFBD lists his hire date as Dec. 23, 2023. He's an NMSU alum and a former Aggie wide receiver. He was head coach at UNLV from 2014 to 2019, and before that at Bishop Gorman High School in Las Vegas.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 24.6 points per game and allowing 25.4.

The Conference Picture
New Mexico State is 3-4 overall and 2-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 3rd in CUSA.
The season so far: L 17-34 at Florida State Seminoles (Aug. 29), W 51-14 vs Mercyhurst Lakers (Sept. 5), L 19-29 at Hawai'i Rainbow Warriors (Sept. 12), L 18-42 vs New Mexico Lobos (Sept. 26), W 34-13 vs Western Kentucky Hilltoppers (Oct. 1), L 3-22 at Florida International Panthers (Oct. 7), W 30-24 at Sam Houston Bearkats (Oct. 21).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Jacksonville State (5-2, 3-0 CUSA) at New Mexico State (3-4, 2-1 CUSA). Wednesday, Oct. 28, 8 p.m. ET, Aggie Memorial Stadium, Las Cruces, N.M., ESPN2.
Last time out. New Mexico State won 30-24 at Sam Houston on Oct. 21. Jones ran 27 times for 178 yards and a touchdown, Adam Damante went 19 of 29 for 160 yards and two touchdowns, and AJ Williams III caught two of them.
Jacksonville State had an open week. Its last game was a 27-24 home win over FIU on Oct. 13. Creel went 16 of 22 for 214 yards and two touchdowns and ran 19 times for 95 yards, and Johnson caught four passes for 93 yards and a score.
Players to watch. Jacksonville State runs through quarterback Caden Creel. He is 129 of 192 for 1,637 yards, 12 touchdowns and six interceptions, and he is also the team's leading rusher with 500 yards and four scores. Wide receiver Deondre Johnson has 22 catches for 431 yards and three touchdowns. Safety Caleb Nix leads the defense with 43 tackles and two interceptions, and defensive lineman Tae Floyd has two sacks.
New Mexico State's headliner is running back James Jones, a Delaware State transfer who leads Conference USA with 777 rushing yards on 119 carries. Defensive end Gabe Peterson has 5.5 sacks, safety Nick Session leads the team with 52 tackles, and wide receiver TK King has 23 catches for 307 yards and three scores.
Availability. Conference USA has not posted an availability report for this game, and no injury news has surfaced for either starting quarterback, so watch the teams' pregame notes. Neither school's box scores mark a starter. Creel has thrown the most passes for Jacksonville State in every game, and Damante has done it for New Mexico State in each of the last four games.
The coaches. Jacksonville State's head coach is in his second season with the Gamecocks. His first team went 9-5, and this one is 3-0 in league play. During the bye he said the focus was fundamentals and doing your job "for six seconds at a time."
Tony Sanchez is an NMSU alum and former Aggie wide receiver who coached UNLV from 2014 to 2019. The Jax State coach says the two are friends, and he called the Aggies' ground game "the best running attack that we played against."
Storylines. The big matchup is Jones against the Jacksonville State front. The Gamecocks hold opponents to 4.2 yards a carry, and the Aggies run for 4.6. On the other side, the Aggie defense allows just 3.3 yards a carry, and Peterson has been chasing quarterbacks all season.
Creel has had a busy year. He threw five touchdown passes against Eastern Kentucky and 311 yards at Kennesaw State. The Gamecocks have won three straight close ones: 31-27, 27-26 and 27-24.
Fun and true. The series is tied 1-1. New Mexico State won 20-17 in 2023, and Jacksonville State won 54-13 at home in 2024 behind five rushing touchdowns. Both teams have played FIU this year: the Gamecocks won 27-24, and the Aggies lost 22-3.
