Hotdish Meets Hatch Chile: The Bison Welcome UTEP to Fargo on Halloween

Meet Your Insider
Oh for cute, they think they can run on us. Well, hi there, I'm Ingeborg Halvorsen, Inga to pretty much all of Fargo. I'm an agricultural economist by trade, and I've tracked every Bison championship since I was a girl with a notebook in the Fargodome stands. These days I keep a little model on my laptop about how a team moves from the FCS to the FBS, and so far, the Bison are making it look easy. I travel with this team and I live with this team, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines, nice as can be, until it's time not to be. When we win, I say, "Oh, for cute, we won!" When we lose, I say, "Oh, yah, that's a tough one," and I bring hotdish. Truth be told, I bring hotdish either way. Tater tot, of course.

The School in 60 Seconds
The Bison represent North Dakota State and now play in the FBS as members of the Mountain West. Home is the Fargodome, 18,700 seats, opened in 1992. And the trophy case, oh my. The Bison have won 18 national championships, none in their current division, and 39 conference championships. That includes 10 FCS national titles between 2011 and 2024. The last five seasons in the Missouri Valley went 14-1, 12-3, 11-4, 14-2 and 12-1. Yah, we're used to winning around here.

The Coach and the Scheme
Tim Polasek is the head coach; CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 17, 2023. This team plays Bison football: run it, defend it, don't give it away.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 27.8 points per game and allowing 12.5.

Players to Know
QB Nathan Hayes: 123 of 196, 1,470 yards, 12 touchdowns, 7 interceptions.
RB Myles Mitchell: 393 yards on 90 carries, 3 touchdowns.
WR Jackson Williams: 32 catches, 503 yards, 1 touchdown.
DE Kelton McCaslin: 4 sacks, the team lead.
LB Donovan Woolen: a team-high 55 tackles.

The Conference Picture
North Dakota State is 6-2 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 3rd in Mountain West.
The season so far: W 33-7 vs Jacksonville State Gamecocks (Aug. 29), W 38-0 vs Fordham Rams (Sept. 5), W 38-32 at Air Force Falcons (Sept. 12), W 31-10 at Sacramento State Hornets (Sept. 19), W 28-0 vs Wyoming Cowboys (Oct. 3), L 7-13 at UNLV Rebels (Oct. 10), W 31-17 vs Nevada Wolf Pack (Oct. 17), L 16-21 at New Mexico Lobos (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides

UTEP had an open week. The Miners last played on Oct. 17, losing 24-11 to San José State at the Sun Bowl. Moore threw for 195 yards, and Thomas caught four balls for 96 yards.
