Sea of Red on Halloween Night: Nebraska Welcomes Washington to Lincoln

Meet Your Insider
Go Big Red! I'm Darla Rasmussen, and I'm your Nebraska insider. I'm an ex-Husker volleyballer and farm girl who sells out every argument. Fair warning: I mention the sellout streak every segment. On the podcast, that segment is called Sellout Report. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, you won't hear it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Memorial Stadium in Lincoln, built in 1923 with a listed capacity of 85,458. The band is the Cornhusker Marching Band, and the fight song is "Hail Varsity." The program claims national titles from 1970, 1971, 1994, 1995 and 1997. The last five seasons went 3-9, 4-8, 5-7, 7-6 and 7-6, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about the sellout streak. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Matt Rhule leads the Cornhuskers, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Nov. 26, 2022. This is his fourth season in Lincoln.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 35.6 points per game and allowing 15.4.

Players to Know
QB Anthony Colandrea: 137 of 219, 1,570 yards, 14 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Jamal Rule: 391 yards on 60 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Kwazi Gilmer: 38 catches, 492 yards, 7 touchdowns.
DL Jahsear Whittington: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Owen Chambliss: a team-high 46 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Nebraska is 5-2 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 8th in Big Ten.
The season so far: W 49-21 vs Ohio Bobcats (Sept. 5), W 56-7 vs Bowling Green Falcons (Sept. 12), W 34-7 vs North Dakota Fighting Hawks (Sept. 19), W 31-13 at Michigan State Spartans (Sept. 26), W 48-23 vs Maryland Terrapins (Oct. 3), L 17-20 vs Indiana Hoosiers (Oct. 10), L 14-17 at Oregon Ducks (Oct. 17).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Washington (4-3, 1-3 Big Ten) at Nebraska (5-2, 2-2 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 8:00 p.m. ET (7:00 p.m. Central), Memorial Stadium, Lincoln, Neb., BTN.
Last time out. Both teams had an open date on Oct. 24.
Washington's last game was a 38-26 win at Purdue on Oct. 16, its first Big Ten win of the season. Brown went 21 of 27 for a career-high 302 yards and three touchdowns, and Trey Cooley ran eight times for 113 yards and a score.
Nebraska's last game was a 17-14 loss at Oregon on Oct. 17. Colandrea threw a touchdown and ran for another, and Gilmer caught six balls for 74 yards and a touchdown. A week before that, the Cornhuskers lost 20-17 to Indiana.
Players to watch. Washington's quarterback is a mystery for now. Demond Williams Jr. is 116 of 174 for 1,396 yards, eight touchdowns and three interceptions, but he has been out with a fractured rib since the Iowa game. Elijah Brown, a Stanford transfer, started at Purdue. Running back Brian Bonner Jr. has 296 yards, and wide receiver Dezmen Roebuck leads the team with 48 catches for 484 yards. On defense, safety Alex McLaughlin has 47 tackles and two interceptions, and linebacker Jacob Manu has 2.5 sacks.
Nebraska quarterback Anthony Colandrea is 137 of 219 for 1,570 yards, 14 touchdowns and four interceptions. Running back Jamal Rule has 391 yards and five touchdowns. Wide receiver Kwazi Gilmer has 38 catches for 492 yards and seven touchdowns. On defense, linebacker Owen Chambliss has 46 tackles, Jahsear Whittington has 3.5 sacks and Dwayne McDougle III has two interceptions.
Availability. No official Big Ten availability report had been posted as of Tuesday. Washington coach Jedd Fisch said he won't name his starting quarterback until game time. Williams is back at practice but still has to prove the rib is ready. Sports Illustrated reports that Washington center Landen Hatchett has a season-ending neck injury. Nebraska coach Matt Rhule said running back Mekhi Nelson has been playing through an ankle injury and that the bye week was good for a banged-up running back room.
The coaches. Fisch is in his third season at Washington. He said Williams and Brown have split the first-team reps and have been great teammates. "Rising tides lift all ships," he said.
Rhule is in his fourth season at Nebraska. He said his team has to prepare for both quarterbacks. "We won't know until the first snap of the game," he said.
Storylines. Williams had started 21 straight games for Washington before missing the Purdue game. Brown came in during the Iowa game and has played well since. For Nebraska, Colandrea has been steady all year, and Rule leads a running game that has a lot of hands on deck.
Fun and true. These two have not met in the regular season in the last 10 years. Nebraska's two losses came by three points each, to Indiana and at Oregon. Washington's three losses came to Minnesota, USC and Iowa.
At home this year, Nebraska beat Ohio 49-21, Bowling Green 56-7, North Dakota 34-7 and Maryland 48-23.
