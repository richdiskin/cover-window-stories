The Royal Court Is in Session: Old Dominion Welcomes the Herd to Norfolk

Meet Your Insider
Hear ye, hear ye! Let it be proclaimed from the harbor to the Hampton Roads Bridge-Tunnel that the Monarchs' royal court reporter has arrived. I am Winifred Pocklington, Winnie to my loyal subjects, and I treat every first down like a knighthood and every win like a coronation. I'm regal, I'm playful, and I'm salt-air tough, because Norfolk does that to a person. I travel with this team and I live with this team, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines with a royal-court flourish and an honest heart. When we win, there will be trumpets (metaphorical, mostly). When we lose, the crown gets polished and the court reconvenes on Monday. Long live the Monarchs.

The School in 60 Seconds
Hear ye: Old Dominion's football story has two chapters. The first team, in 1930, was the William & Mary Norfolk Division Braves. The modern Monarchs were founded in 2009, played two seasons as an FCS independent, joined the Colonial Athletic Association in 2011, moved up to Conference USA in the FBS in 2014 and joined the Sun Belt in 2022. Home is S.B. Ballard Stadium in Norfolk, listed at 20,118 seats. Our five most recent seasons went 6-7, 3-9, 6-7, 5-7 and then a royal 10-3 in 2025. That last one deserved a parade down Hampton Boulevard. The crown, as they say, is heavy. We carry it anyway.

The Coach and the Scheme
Ricky Rahne was hired Dec. 7, 2019.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 15.5 points per game and allowing 26.8.

The Conference Picture
Old Dominion is 2-6 overall and 1-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 7th in Sun Belt East.
The season so far: W 31-10 vs Norfolk State Spartans (Sept. 5), L 21-44 at Virginia Tech Hokies (Sept. 12), L 17-20 vs East Carolina Pirates (Sept. 19), L 20-46 vs James Madison Dukes (Sept. 26), L 10-42 at Georgia State Panthers (Oct. 3), W 10-3 at App State Mountaineers (Oct. 10), L 15-23 vs Georgia Southern Eagles (Oct. 15), L 0-26 at Louisiana Tech Bulldogs (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Marshall (4-3, 1-2 Sun Belt) at Old Dominion (2-6, 1-4 Sun Belt). Saturday, Oct. 31, 1 p.m. ET, S.B. Ballard Stadium, Norfolk, Va., ESPN+.
Last time out. Marshall beat South Alabama 20-13 at home on Tuesday, Oct. 20. Turner was 21 of 33 for 181 yards, Khalil Wilkins ran eight times for 69 yards, and Toby Payne caught seven balls for 69 yards.
Old Dominion lost 26-0 at Louisiana Tech on Oct. 24. Potter was 18 of 34 for 154 yards, and Leon Haughton Jr. caught four balls for 45 yards.
Players to watch. Marshall has used two quarterbacks. Carlos Del Rio-Wilson leads the team at 90 of 140 for 1,041 yards, six touchdowns and five interceptions, and he threw for 413 yards against Gardner-Webb. Zion Turner threw the most passes in each of the last two games. Judah Price leads the rushers with 237 yards, and Xayvion Turner-Bradshaw leads the receivers with 25 catches for 335 yards and four scores. Defensive back Caleb Clark-Glover has 51 tackles, and Kelvon McBride has two sacks.
Old Dominion's top passer is Quinn Henicle, who is 54 of 98 for 656 yards, four touchdowns and four interceptions. Ryan Huff and Jaxon Potter have also thrown the most passes in a game this year. Devin Roche leads the rushers with 382 yards, and TJ Johnson leads the receivers with 17 catches for 265 yards. On defense, Javen Gibson has five sacks and safety Lucas Struck has 61 tackles.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Turner threw the most passes in Marshall's last two games, and Potter did for Old Dominion last week, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Tony Gibson is in his second season at Marshall. He's a 1994 Glenville State graduate and was previously defensive coordinator at West Virginia and NC State.
Ricky Rahne was hired at Old Dominion on Dec. 7, 2019. Last year his Monarchs went 10-3, and this year the defense has forced 12 turnovers.
Storylines. Marshall's offense gains 4.4 yards a carry, and Old Dominion's defense allows only 3.5. That's the matchup to watch. Old Dominion's big guys up front have had a rough year, allowing 32 sacks in seven games, so protecting whoever plays quarterback is job one. Gibson and the Monarch defense will want to pressure Turner, and Clark-Glover will be busy in the Marshall secondary.
Fun and true. Old Dominion's first team, in 1930, was the William & Mary Norfolk Division Braves. The modern Monarchs were founded in 2009 and joined the Sun Belt in 2022. Marshall's first game against another college was a 15-0 win over Shelton College on Nov. 24, 1903, and the 2014 Herd went 13-1. These two have met often lately: Marshall won 42-35 in 2024 and 48-24 in 2025, and Old Dominion won 38-14 in 2016.
