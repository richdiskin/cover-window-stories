Tar Heels Ready for No. 1: North Carolina Hosts Miami in Chapel Hill

Meet Your Insider
Carolina blue, through and through. Hello, darlings, I'm Ainsley Pemberton. I grew up in Chapel Hill, I came out at a debutante ball, and then I went and earned a PhD in kinesiology, because a lady should understand exactly how a linebacker generates force. I travel with the Tar Heels and I live with the Tar Heels, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines, with Southern charm and, I'll confess, a gentle little roast of a certain school eight miles down the road. I don't even need Duke to be on the schedule. When we win, I graciously thank our guests, then whisper "Carolina blue, through and through." When we lose, I say "Carolina blue skies will return," and I absolutely refuse to cry on camera. Mostly.

The School in 60 Seconds
The Tar Heels, often just "Carolina," represent the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill in the ACC. North Carolina has played in 37 bowl games and won three Southern Conference titles and five ACC titles. Home is Kenan Stadium, opened in 1927 and listed at 50,500 seats, and darling, there is no prettier place in college football on an autumn evening. The last five seasons: 6-7, a lovely 9-5 in 2022, 8-5, 6-7 and 4-8 last year.

The Coach and the Scheme
Bill Belichick is our head coach; CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 11, 2024.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 24.4 points per game and allowing 20.9.

Players to Know
QB Billy Edwards Jr.: 121 of 201, 1,531 yards, 11 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Benjamin Hall: 283 yards on 57 carries.
WR Jordan Shipp: 41 catches, 543 yards, 2 touchdowns.
DL Xavier Lewis: 7 sacks, the team lead.
LB Derek McDonald: a team-high 71 tackles.

The Conference Picture
North Carolina is 4-3 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 6th in ACC.
The season so far: W 35-3 vs East Tennessee State Buccaneers (Sept. 12), L 20-28 at Clemson Tigers (Sept. 19), L 26-37 vs Notre Dame Fighting Irish (Oct. 3), W 21-17 at Pittsburgh Panthers (Oct. 10), W 24-20 at Duke Blue Devils (Oct. 17), L 30-31 (OT) vs Syracuse Orange (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
No. 1 Miami (7-0, 5-0 ACC) at North Carolina (4-3, 2-2 ACC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 7:30 p.m. ET, Kenan Stadium, Chapel Hill, North Carolina, ABC.
Last time out. Miami beat Pittsburgh 52-23. Mensah went 31 of 37 for 387 yards and four touchdowns, and Toney caught 16 balls for 194 yards and three scores. Mallory ran for two touchdowns.
North Carolina lost 31-30 to Syracuse in overtime at Kenan Stadium. Edwards opened the game with 11 straight completions and finished with season highs of 27 completions and 271 yards. Shipp had eight catches for 102 yards and a touchdown, and Demon June ran 25 yards for the team's longest rushing score of the year. McDonald, a Syracuse transfer facing his old team, made a career-high 16 tackles.
Players to watch. Miami quarterback Darian Mensah has completed 166 of 204 throws for 2,151 yards, 22 touchdowns and just one interception. Wide receiver Malachi Toney has 66 catches for 952 yards and 11 touchdowns. Running back Javian Mallory has 444 yards and seven touchdowns on only 59 carries. On defense, Marquise Lightfoot has five sacks, and linebacker Mohamed Toure has 35 tackles.
North Carolina quarterback Billy Edwards Jr. has thrown for 1,531 yards, 11 touchdowns and five interceptions. Wide receiver Jordan Shipp has 41 catches for 543 yards. Benjamin Hall leads the running backs with 283 yards. On defense, Xavier Lewis has seven sacks, and linebacker Derek McDonald has 71 tackles.
Availability. No official ACC availability report had been posted as of Tuesday. Mensah and Toney have no reported injury concerns.
The coaches. Mario Cristobal is in his fifth season at Miami. Last season the Canes reached the national championship game, losing 27-21 to Indiana on Jan. 19, 2026.
Bill Belichick is in his second season at North Carolina. After the Syracuse game he said, "They made a couple more plays than we did today, and that was the difference in the game." On Monday he talked about Toney. "He's good at everything," Belichick said. "He's even a good blocker." He added, "At his age, that's pretty impressive. I don't see any weak points in his game."
Storylines. Toney is going for his third straight game with more than 150 receiving yards. On3's Heisman poll has him third, right behind his own quarterback, Mensah.
For Carolina, Shipp said the Tar Heels moved the ball well but need to finish drives. "We need to turn those field goals into touchdowns," he said. Rece Verhoff made all three of his field goals against Syracuse, with a long of 39 yards. Tight end Jelani Thurman caught a career-best seven passes.
Fun and true. Miami has scored at least 33 points in every game this season, including 77 against Florida A&M. The Canes won 43-35 in overtime against Florida State.
Carolina won close road games at Pittsburgh, 21-17, and at Duke, 24-20. The Tar Heels and Syracuse have now played nine times, and Carolina still leads that series 5-4.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Javi "The Breeze" Castillo, Miami's insider. Here's what I said:
"Welcome to Chapel Hill, darling. Do you need a sweater?"
"Sixteen tackles against his old school, darling. That's good manners and good football."
"Halloween in Chapel Hill, darling. Kenan is lovely by moonlight."
And the last word from Javi: "I'll bring the sunglasses anyway. Canes get it done."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
This is North Carolina's 100th season playing in Kenan Stadium, which opened in 1927 and seats 50,500. The Tar Heels have won five ACC titles and played in 37 bowl games. Saturday continues a homestand for Carolina.
Miami claims five national championships: 1983, 1987, 1989, 1991 and 2001. The Canes are No. 1 in both the AP and Coaches polls.

My call: Miami Hurricanes 35, North Carolina Tar Heels 20.
