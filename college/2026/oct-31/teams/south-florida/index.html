Bulls by the Bay: South Florida Hosts UAB in Tampa on Halloween

Meet Your Insider
Pirate-approved! I'm Reina Valdés-Kowalski, and I'm your South Florida insider. I'm a boat-show model turned Bulls cap-and-roster maven, and I bring pirate bits from the Gasparilla festival, always. On the podcast, my segment is called Gasparilla Report, a pirate-flavored preview. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, you won't hear it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Raymond James Stadium in Tampa, built in 1998 with a listed capacity of 65,828. The last five seasons went 2-10, 1-11, 7-6, 7-6 and 9-4, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about Rocky the Bull. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Brian Hartline leads the Bulls, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 3, 2025.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 26.6 points per game and allowing 15.1.

Players to Know
QB Michael Van Buren Jr.: 115 of 179, 1,266 yards, 6 touchdowns.
RB D.J. Crowther: 518 yards on 105 carries, 7 touchdowns.
WR Bryson Rodgers: 30 catches, 334 yards, 2 touchdowns.
DE Michael Williams II: 4 sacks, the team lead.
DB Ayden Jackson: a team-high 38 tackles.

The Conference Picture
South Florida is 5-2 overall and 1-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 7th in American.
The season so far: W 19-9 vs Florida International Panthers (Sept. 5), W 28-24 at Army Black Knights (Sept. 12), W 59-17 vs Delaware State Hornets (Sept. 19), W 14-6 at Bowling Green Falcons (Sept. 26), L 13-17 vs Temple Owls (Oct. 3), L 24-31 at UTSA Roadrunners (Oct. 8), W 29-2 vs Kent State Golden Flashes (Oct. 17).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
UAB (4-3, 2-1 American) at South Florida (5-2, 1-2 American). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12 p.m. ET, Raymond James Stadium, Tampa, Fla., ESPNU.
Last time out. Both teams had the week off. UAB's last game was a 30-17 overtime win over East Carolina on Oct. 15. Burton threw for 262 yards and two touchdowns, and Smith caught eight balls for 119 yards.
South Florida's last game was a 29-2 win over Kent State on Oct. 17. Van Buren went 22 of 27 for 193 yards and a touchdown, and Cameron Seldon caught a scoring throw.
Players to watch. UAB quarterback Ryder Burton has thrown the most passes in all seven Blazer games. He's 91 of 167 for 1,184 yards, six touchdowns and five interceptions. Rod Robinson II leads the rushers with 436 yards, and CJ Smith leads the receivers with 350 yards on 19 catches. On defense, linebacker Muaaz Byard has a team-high 69 tackles and linebacker Marlin Dean has five sacks.
South Florida quarterback Michael Van Buren Jr. has thrown the most passes in all seven Bulls games. He's 115 of 179 for 1,266 yards and six touchdowns, and he hasn't thrown an interception in any of them. Running back D.J. Crowther has 518 yards and seven touchdowns, and Bryson Rodgers leads the receivers with 30 catches for 334 yards. Defensive back Ayden Jackson has 38 tackles, and end Michael Williams II has four sacks.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Burton and Van Buren have each thrown the most passes in every game for their teams, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Both head coaches are in their first season. Brian Hartline was hired at South Florida on Dec. 3, 2025, and his Bulls allow just 15.1 points a game. They've won at Army and at Bowling Green and beaten Kent State 29-2.
Alex Mortensen was hired at UAB on Dec. 5, 2025, two days after Hartline. His Blazers like to run the ball, and they've already won three home games over FBS teams: UL Monroe, Navy and East Carolina.
Storylines. The matchup to circle is Burton against a South Florida defense that held Florida International to nine points and Bowling Green to six. On the other side, Crowther and the Bulls big guys up front face a UAB defense that allows 4.4 yards a carry. Both teams run for about four yards a carry, so expect a ground battle.
Fun and true. South Florida has climbed fast. From 2021 through 2025, the Bulls went 2-10, 1-11, 7-6, 7-6 and 9-4. UAB went 9-4 in 2021, and this year's Blazers have already matched last season's four wins. These two have met three times since 2023. UAB won a wild one 56-35 in 2023, and South Florida won 35-25 in 2024 and 48-18 last season.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Raymond James Stadium opened in 1998 and seats 65,828. A Halloween noon kickoff in Tampa means sunshine, pirate hats and Rocky the Bull leading the cheers. UAB fans will bring the spirit of Blaze the dragon and the UAB Marching Blazers from Protective Stadium, which opened in Birmingham in 2021.
