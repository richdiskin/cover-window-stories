Chomp in Atlanta: No. 17 Florida Takes On No. 3 Georgia on Halloween

Meet Your Insider
That play had scales! I'm Dax Marchetti, and I'm your Florida insider. I'm a herpetology minor, and I compare every defense to a reptile species. Fair warning: I classify every opponent as a species, lovingly, and my reptile index is weirdly accurate. On the podcast, that segment is called Reptile Index. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, you won't hear it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Ben Hill Griffin Stadium in Gainesville, built in 1930 with a listed capacity of 88,548. The playing surface is Steve Spurrier–Florida Field, the band is the Pride of the Sunshine, and the fight song is "The Orange and Blue." The program claims national titles from 1996, 2006 and 2008. The last five seasons went 6-7, 6-7, 5-7, 8-5 and 4-8, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about the Swamp. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Jon Sumrall leads the Gators, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Nov. 30, 2025. It's his first season in Gainesville.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 39.6 points per game and allowing 28.3.

Players to Know
QB Aaron Philo: 105 of 149, 1,341 yards, 9 touchdowns, 3 interceptions.
RB Jadan Baugh: 763 yards on 135 carries, 13 touchdowns.
WR Dallas Wilson: 29 catches, 403 yards, 3 touchdowns.
LB Jayden Woods: 4.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Myles Graham: a team-high 48 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Florida is 5-2 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 4th in SEC. We're ranked No. 17 in ESPN's rankings this week.
The season so far: W 66-21 vs Florida Atlantic Owls (Sept. 5), W 52-3 vs Campbell Fighting Camels (Sept. 12), W 44-39 at Auburn Tigers (Sept. 19), W 52-28 vs Ole Miss Rebels (Sept. 26), L 17-45 at Missouri Tigers (Oct. 3), L 19-38 vs South Carolina Gamecocks (Oct. 10), W 27-24 at Texas Longhorns (Oct. 17).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
No. 17 Florida (5-2, 3-2 SEC) vs. No. 3 Georgia (6-1, 4-1 SEC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, Mercedes-Benz Stadium, Atlanta, Ga. (neutral site), ABC.
Last time out. Both teams had an open date last week, so everybody is rested.
Florida's last game was a 27-24 win at Texas on Oct. 17. Jones went 22 of 35 for 155 yards and two touchdowns, and Baugh ran 25 times for 91 yards and a score.
Georgia's last game was a 27-24 loss to Auburn on Oct. 17. Stockton threw for 272 yards and two touchdowns, Phillips ran for 68 yards and a score, and Dandridge caught four balls for 88 yards.
Players to watch. Georgia quarterback Gunner Stockton has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 117 of 158 for 1,570 yards, 16 touchdowns and four interceptions. Dwight Phillips Jr. leads the rushers with 348 yards, and Craig Dandridge leads the receivers with 310 yards. Linebacker Raylen Wilson leads the defense with 44 tackles and four sacks, and defensive back KJ Bolden has an interception.
Florida running back Jadan Baugh is the engine, with 763 yards and 13 touchdowns on 135 carries. Aaron Philo leads the team in passing at 105 of 149 for 1,341 yards, nine touchdowns and three interceptions, and Tramell Jones Jr. threw the most passes at Texas. Dallas Wilson leads the receivers with 29 catches for 403 yards. Linebacker Myles Graham leads the defense with 48 tackles, Jayden Woods has 4.5 sacks, and safety DJ Coleman has two interceptions.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Stockton has thrown the most passes for Georgia in every game. Philo did it for Florida in the first six games and Jones did it at Texas, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Kirby Smart leads Georgia, and CollegeFootballData dates his hire to Dec. 6, 2015. His teams went 14-1, 15-0, 13-1, 11-3 and 12-2 over the last five seasons, and Georgia claims national titles from 2021 and 2022. This year's Bulldogs score 43.0 points a game and allow 15.9.
Jon Sumrall is in his first season leading Florida, and CFBD dates his hire to Nov. 30, 2025. His Gators already have five wins after going 4-8 last season, and they score 39.6 points a game with a ground game that gains 194.3 yards a game.
Storylines. The matchup to watch is Baugh and the Florida big guys up front against a Georgia defense that allows 15.9 points a game and has 22 sacks. On the other side, Stockton faces a Florida defense that also has 22 sacks. Georgia has won eight of the last 10 meetings, including 24-20 last year, so Florida has some history to change, and Georgia wants to bounce back from the Auburn loss.
Fun and true. Florida claims national titles from 1996, 2006 and 2008, its fight song is "The Orange and Blue," and its band is the Pride of the Sunshine. Georgia claims titles from 1942, 1980, 2021 and 2022, its band is the Georgia Redcoat Marching Band, and its fight song is "Hail to Georgia." Florida's last win in this series came in 2020, 44-28.
