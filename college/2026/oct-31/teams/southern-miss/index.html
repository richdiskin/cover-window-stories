Pearls on the Road: Southern Miss Visits UL Monroe on Halloween, Sugar

Meet Your Insider
To the top, sugar! Hey, y'all, I'm Delphine Broussard, but please call me Dee. I was sorority president at Southern Miss, and these days I scout football with the same eye I used to plan a formal: I notice everything, I forget nothing, and I say it all with a smile. My one weakness is the gentle Southern burn. I can't help it. When we win, I'll point skyward and holler "To the top, y'all!" When we lose, I'll sigh "Bless it," fix my pearls and get right back to work. Pull up a rocker.

The School in 60 Seconds
Southern Miss plays in Hattiesburg and competes in the Sun Belt. Our home stadium has two names depending on who you ask: Wikipedia lists Renasant Stadium, and CFBD lists M. M. Roberts Stadium, opened in 1932 with 36,000 seats. Either way, sugar, it's ours, and it's loud. The last five seasons have been a roller coaster: 3-9 in 2021, 7-6 in 2022, 3-9, then a hard 1-11 in 2024 and a lovely 7-6 bounce back in 2025. That 2025 team proved this program can climb fast, and climbing is what we do. To the top.

The Coach and the Scheme
Blake Anderson is in his first season, and CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 11, 2025.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 25.7 points per game and allowing 32.4.

Players to Know
QB Landry Lyddy: 101 of 170, 1,196 yards, 7 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Steven Robinson: 577 yards on 121 carries, 8 touchdowns.
WR Mario Sanders: 40 catches, 569 yards, 5 touchdowns.
DL Jameer Lewis: 4 sacks, the team lead.
LB Mathis Haygood: a team-high 63 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Southern Miss is 2-5 overall and 1-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 5th in Sun Belt West.
The season so far: W 49-3 vs Alcorn State Braves (Sept. 5), L 8-43 at Auburn Tigers (Sept. 12), L 20-48 vs UConn Huskies (Sept. 19), L 21-24 at Tulane Green Wave (Sept. 26), L 34-55 at Troy Trojans (Oct. 6), L 13-23 vs Arkansas State Red Wolves (Oct. 17), W 35-31 vs Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Southern Miss (2-5, 1-2 Sun Belt) at UL Monroe (1-6, 1-2 Sun Belt). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, Malone Stadium, Monroe, La., ESPN+.
Last time out. UL Monroe won its first game of the season, 21-14 at Troy on Oct. 24, on Troy's Military Appreciation day. Armenta went 13 of 22 for 147 yards and two touchdowns with no interceptions.
Southern Miss beat Louisiana 35-31 on Homecoming at "The Rock" on Oct. 24. Jamarice Wilder ran 13 times for 154 yards and a touchdown, and Sanders caught six balls for 77 yards.
Players to watch. Southern Miss quarterback Landry Lyddy has thrown the most passes in the last five games. He's 101 of 170 for 1,196 yards, seven touchdowns and four interceptions. Steven Robinson leads the rushers with 577 yards and eight scores, and Mario Sanders leads the receivers with 40 catches for 569 yards and five touchdowns. Linebacker Mathis Haygood leads the defense with 63 tackles, and Jameer Lewis has four sacks.
UL Monroe quarterback Aidan Armenta has thrown the most passes in the last four games. He's 96 of 153 for 1,121 yards, eight touchdowns and three interceptions. Austin Carlisle, who threw the most passes in the second and third games, leads the team in rushing with 237 yards, and Marcellus Jackson leads the receivers with 32 catches. Linebacker Noah Flemmings leads the defense with 65 tackles, and Kevontay Wells has three sacks.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Lyddy has thrown the most passes for Southern Miss in five straight games, and Armenta has done it for UL Monroe in four straight, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Blake Anderson is in his first season at Southern Miss, with a hire date of Dec. 11, 2025. His team throws it well and just got a big day from the run game, too. The defense is the work in progress, but the Golden Eagles found a way against Louisiana.
Bryant Vincent leads UL Monroe, with a hire date of Dec. 5, 2023. Two of his team's early losses were one-score games, 26-20 at UAB and 38-35 to SE Louisiana, and last week his defense held Troy to 14 points.
Storylines. The matchup to watch is the Southern Miss run game against a UL Monroe defense that has had a hard time stopping the run. Robinson and Wilder will test the Warhawks' big guys up front. On the other side, Armenta wants another clean day after throwing for 362 yards at South Alabama earlier this season. Southern Miss won four of the six meetings since 2017, including 49-21 last season, but UL Monroe won 38-21 in 2024.
Fun and true. UL Monroe football dates to 1931. The school's teams were the Northeast Louisiana Indians for a long stretch and became the Warhawks in 2006, and the program moved up to what is now FBS in 1994. Southern Miss went 7-6 last season, a lovely bounce back after a 1-11 year in 2024.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Halloween in Monroe, and Malone Stadium, opened in 1978 and listed at 27,617 seats, gets the home crowd back after last week's trip to Troy. Southern Miss fans just celebrated Homecoming at The Rock, which wears a new name this fall, Renasant Stadium. The nickname isn't going anywhere.

My call: Southern Miss Golden Eagles 32, UL Monroe Warhawks 26.
