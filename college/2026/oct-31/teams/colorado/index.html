Harvest Time in the Flatirons: The Buffs Welcome Kansas State to Boulder

Meet Your Insider
Hey, friends. I'm Moss Feldstein, your Colorado insider. I'm a physicist, a hacky-sack enthusiast and the guy running a quant model of the Buffs from a hammock under the Flatirons. I travel and live with this team, and I bring kombucha on every road trip. It's an acquired taste.

Here's my approach: chill vibes, sharp numbers. Every stat in this report is sourced, and when I compute one myself, I'll tell you. If I don't know something, I'll say the data's not in yet. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I'm grateful to God for mountain sunsets and the chance to keep fighting. Ralphie's running.

The School in 60 Seconds
Colorado has played at Folsom Field in Boulder since 1924. CFBD lists 50,183 seats. The Buffs won the 1990 national championship, shared with Georgia Tech. They have two Heisman winners: Rashaan Salaam in 1994 and Travis Hunter in 2024. The all-time record is 735-556-36 through 2025.

Colorado was a charter Big 12 member, left for the Pac-12 after 2010 and returned to the Big 12 in 2024. The last five seasons swing wildly: 4-8, 1-11, 4-8, a radical 9-4 in 2024 and 3-9 in 2025. High variance. Physicists love that. Fans, less so.

The Coach and the Scheme
Deion Sanders, "Coach Prime," leads the program. CFBD records his hire date as Dec. 3, 2022. His résumé is unlike anyone's. He played 14 NFL seasons, won two Super Bowls and played nine Major League Baseball seasons. He's the only athlete to play in both a Super Bowl and a World Series. At Florida State he won the 1988 Jim Thorpe Award.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 20.1 points per game and allowing 30.1.
