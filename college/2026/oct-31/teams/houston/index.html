Coogs on Cruise Control: Houston Takes the Bye Week and Gets Ready for Liftoff

Meet Your Insider
Houston, we have liftoff! I'm Yolanda Pacheco, but everybody calls me Yo. I'm your Houston insider. By day I'm a space-center engineer, and by night I grind Coogs film. I make NASA puns at every opportunity. Sorry in advance. Not really.

Here's the flight plan: every number has a source, and if I don't know something, I'll tell you. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I thank God for the Space City and the people in it.

The School in 60 Seconds
Houston plays in the Big 12. Since 2014, the Cougars have played on campus at TDECU Stadium, built on the site of old Robertson Stadium. CFBD lists 40,000 seats. The program has won 11 conference championships and has several College Football Hall of Famers, including a Heisman Trophy winner.

The last five seasons: 12-2, 8-5, 4-8, 4-8 and 10-3. That 10-win season last year was the launch.

The Coach and the Scheme
Willie Fritz is in his third season, and CFBD lists his hire date as Dec. 3, 2023. He has been a head coach for decades: Blinn College (two straight NJCAA national titles, 1995 and 1996), Central Missouri, Sam Houston, Georgia Southern and Tulane.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 36.0 points per game and allowing 21.0.

Players to Know
QB Conner Weigman: 153 of 233, 2,053 yards, 20 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB Makhi Hughes: 516 yards on 117 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Amare Thomas: 41 catches, 620 yards, 6 touchdowns.
DL Ashton Porter: 3 sacks, the team lead.
DB Kentrell Webb: a team-high 46 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Houston is 6-2 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 6th in Big 12.
The season so far: W 33-20 vs Oregon State Beavers (Sept. 5), W 77-6 vs Southern Jaguars (Sept. 12), L 26-28 at Texas Tech Red Raiders (Sept. 18), W 42-28 at Georgia Southern Eagles (Sept. 26), W 27-17 vs UCF Knights (Oct. 3), W 35-16 at Kansas State Wildcats (Oct. 10), W 35-32 vs Oklahoma State Cowboys (Oct. 17), L 13-21 at Utah Utes (Oct. 24).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for Houston this week. We're off from Tuesday, Oct. 27, through Saturday, Oct. 31, so the rest of this report is about where we stand and what's next.
Last time out. We lost to the Utah Utes 21-13 on the road on Oct. 24. Conner Weigman went 19 of 35 for 282 yards. Makhi Hughes ran 19 times for 52 yards, with 1 touchdown. Amare Thomas caught 5 balls for 133 yards.
Up next. We play the Cincinnati Bearcats at home on Saturday, Nov. 7, with the kickoff time still to be announced, at TDECU Stadium in Houston.
The open week is for rest, healing and a good week of practice. I'll be back with a full preview before the next kickoff.
