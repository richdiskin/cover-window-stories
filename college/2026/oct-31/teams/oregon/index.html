Fashion Week in Eugene: The Ducks Host No. 20 Northwestern on Halloween

Meet Your Insider
Hey, Duck fans, welcome! I'm Phoenix Wyatt-Aldana, but please call me Nix. I'm a sneaker-and-uniform designer from Eugene, which means I review jerseys and route trees with exactly the same ferocity. A slant with sloppy footwork? That's a wrinkled hem, darling. I travel with this team and I live with this team, and before I analyze anybody, I rate their uniform. It's the law. My catchphrase is "Uniform of the week? Ten out of ten." When we win, the review reads "10 out of 10, uniform AND execution." When we lose, I review it like a runway show where somebody tripped in the wrong shoes: honest, kind and very detailed. Each week you get the players, the coaches and the matchup, plus a little sparkle.

The School in 60 Seconds
Oregon has played football since 1894. Autzen Stadium opened in 1967 and is listed at 54,000 seats, though anyone who's been inside knows it sounds much bigger. In 2014 quarterback Marcus Mariota became Oregon's first Heisman Trophy winner, after throwing for 4,454 yards and 42 touchdowns. He finished with the second-most points of any Heisman winner, behind only O.J. Simpson. The Ducks moved to the Big Ten in 2024 and won the league right away: 13-1 overall and 9-0 in conference play. Last year they went 13-2 and 8-1 in the league. Two straight seasons of runway-ready football.

The Coach and the Scheme
Dan Lanning was hired on Dec. 11, 2021. His 2024 team went 9-0 in the Big Ten and won the title.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 33.1 points per game and allowing 20.7.

Players to Know

WR Dakorien Moore: 34 catches, 417 yards, 4 touchdowns.

The Conference Picture
Oregon is 4-3 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 8th in Big Ten.
The season so far: W 34-27 vs Boise State Broncos (Sept. 5), L 31-39 at Oklahoma State Cowboys (Sept. 12), W 84-0 vs Portland State Vikings (Sept. 18), W 41-27 at USC Trojans (Sept. 26), L 15-17 vs UCLA Bruins (Oct. 10), W 17-14 vs Nebraska Cornhuskers (Oct. 17), L 10-21 at Illinois Fighting Illini (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
No. 20 Northwestern (6-1, 3-1 Big Ten) at Oregon (4-3, 2-2 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, Autzen Stadium, Eugene, Ore., Peacock.
Last time out. Northwestern beat Rutgers 42-14 at home on Oct. 24. Chiles threw for 219 yards and three touchdowns, Komolafe ran 13 times for 150 yards and a score, and Wilde caught nine balls for 133 yards and a touchdown.
Oregon lost 21-10 at Illinois on Oct. 24. Raiola completed 27 of 41 for 246 yards, Davison ran 14 times for 92 yards and the Ducks' touchdown, and Dakorien Moore caught nine balls.
Players to watch. Northwestern quarterback Aidan Chiles has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 117 of 164 for 1,617 yards, 12 touchdowns and just one interception. Caleb Komolafe leads the rushers with 551 yards and five scores, and Griffin Wilde leads the receivers with 39 catches for 615 yards. Linebacker Braydon Brus leads the defense in both tackles, with 62, and sacks, with four.
For Oregon, Jordon Davison leads the rushers with 422 yards and seven touchdowns, and Dakorien Moore leads the receivers with 34 catches for 417 yards. Dylan Raiola threw the most passes last week, and Lanning has said Brock Thomas and Akili Smith Jr. are in the mix too. On defense, Matayo Uiagalelei has six sacks, and Koi Perich leads the team with 46 tackles.
Availability. The big news is at quarterback. On Monday, Oregon coach Dan Lanning said he doesn't expect Dante Moore back this season. Moore suffered a concussion at USC on Sept. 26 and has missed three games since. Before the injury he was 69 of 99 for 926 yards and nine touchdowns. Raiola and Thomas have started the last three games. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and Chiles has thrown the most passes for Northwestern every week, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Lanning also made a change on his staff. Offensive coordinator Drew Mehringer was dismissed Sunday, and assistant coach Jay Johnson will coordinate the offense. Lanning was hired on Dec. 11, 2021, and his 2024 team went 13-1 and 9-0 in the Big Ten, winning the league title in its first season.
David Braun became Northwestern's interim coach in 2023, was named the permanent head coach that November and was the consensus Big Ten Coach of the Year. His team runs the ball and protects it, with only two turnovers lost all season.
Storylines. Raiola is the one to watch. He came off the bench against Nebraska, his former team, and threw the winning 4-yard touchdown to tight end Jamari Johnson with 28 seconds left. On the other side, Komolafe and Northwestern's big guys up front will test an Oregon defense that allows very little on the ground. These two met last season, and Oregon won 34-14.
