Cougars by the Bay: Washington State Visits San Diego State at Snapdragon Stadium

Meet Your Insider
Wave the flag. Howdy, neighbors, I'm Hank Palouse. I drive a combine for a living out in the wheat country, and I haven't missed a game in Pullman since cassette tapes. And yes, I still have the tapes. A whole shoebox of them, labeled by year, in the cab of my pickup. I travel with the Cougars and I live with the Cougars, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines, with a little dry Palouse humor and a lot of love for Ol' Crimson, the flag that shows up at every big game in the country. When we win, I wave Ol' Crimson right here in the studio. When we lose, I say "Cougs never quit," and then I tell you a 20-minute story about harvest. You've been warned.

The School in 60 Seconds
Washington State University is in Pullman, and the first Cougar football team took the field in 1894. We play in the Pac-12. Home is Gesa Field at Martin Stadium, with Gesa naming the playing surface. Martin Stadium opened in 1972, and the ground there dates back to 1892, when it was called Soldier Field. CFBD lists 32,952 seats. The last five seasons were 7-6, 7-6, 5-7, 8-5 and 7-6. Steady folks out here, like the wheat: some years are a bumper crop, some years you pray for rain, but you always plant again.

The Coach and the Scheme
Kirby Moore is in his first season, and CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 12, 2025.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 18.9 points per game and allowing 27.6.

Players to Know
QB Caden Pinnick: 110 of 178, 952 yards, 3 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Kirby Vorhees: 275 yards on 59 carries, 3 touchdowns.
WR Branden Ganashamoorthy: 30 catches, 401 yards, 3 touchdowns.
DT Jirah Leaupepetele: 2.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Keith Brown: a team-high 60 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Washington State is 1-7 overall and 0-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 8th in Pac-12.
The season so far: L 10-24 at Washington Huskies (Sept. 6), L 7-34 at Kansas State Wildcats (Sept. 12), W 48-7 vs Duquesne Dukes (Sept. 19), L 24-34 vs Arizona Wildcats (Sept. 26), L 6-26 vs Fresno State Bulldogs (Oct. 3), L 16-17 at Utah State Aggies (Oct. 9), L 30-48 at Oregon State Beavers (Oct. 17), L 10-31 vs Boise State Broncos (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Washington State (1-7, 0-4 Pac-12) at San Diego State (3-5, 2-2 Pac-12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 7:00 p.m. ET (4:00 p.m. Pacific), Snapdragon Stadium, San Diego, CBSSN.
Last time out. Washington State lost 31-10 at home to Boise State. Eshelman completed 21 throws for 191 yards and ran the ball 23 times. Ganashamoorthy and Daniel Blood played all 76 snaps on offense.
San Diego State lost 56-49 at Colorado State in a wild one. The 105 combined points were the most in a game in San Diego State's Division I history. Saunders went 23 of 33 for 369 yards and two touchdowns in his first college start. Hawkins caught six balls for a career-high 164 yards and scored a touchdown for the fifth straight game. Lucky Sutton ran for 98 yards and two scores, and Kinnard added two more on just 10 carries.
Players to watch. Washington State quarterback Owen Eshelman has started the last two games. Caden Pinnick, who started the first six, has thrown for 952 yards. Wide receiver Branden Ganashamoorthy leads the team with 30 catches for 401 yards and three touchdowns, and Kirby Vorhees has 275 rushing yards. On defense, linebacker Keith Brown has 60 tackles, and defensive tackle Jirah Leaupepetele has 2.5 sacks.
San Diego State quarterback Stone Saunders, a redshirt freshman and Kentucky transfer, gets his second straight start. Running back Javion Kinnard leads the team with 526 rushing yards. Wide receiver Jaylon Hawkins has 322 receiving yards and three touchdowns. Linebacker Sione Hala has 83 tackles, and linebacker Djibril Rahman has 3.5 sacks.
Availability. No official Pac-12 availability report had been posted as of Tuesday. Coach Kirby Moore said wide receiver Darrius Clemons (ankle) and safety Jeremiah Bernard (shoulder) are day-to-day and set to return to practice this week. For San Diego State, Jayden Denegal remains day-to-day.
The coaches. Moore is in his first season at Washington State. He wants sharper practices this week. "We talk about deliberate practice," he said. "That really starts with bringing great energy, having verbal combat, doing your assignment, making sure you're not thinking out there, that you're just reacting."
Sean Lewis is in his third season at San Diego State. He named Saunders the starter on Monday. "Stone will start this week," Lewis said. "So, he'll get the bulk of the one reps as we go forward."
Storylines. Saunders came into his first start with just 15 career throws. His 369 yards were the most ever by an Aztec quarterback making his first start on the road, going back to 1969. He also became the first Aztec to throw for 300 yards since Jalen Mayden in the 2022 Hawai'i Bowl.
Ganashamoorthy has played 503 snaps this season, the most of any wide receiver in the Pac-12. Washington State's only win so far came 48-7 over Duquesne, and the Cougars lost 17-16 at Utah State. They're looking for their first win over an FBS team.
Fun and true. The Aztecs went 9-4 last year and are trying to get to a bowl game for a second straight season. They have won two close home games this year, 31-29 over Texas State and 26-23 over Fresno State.
