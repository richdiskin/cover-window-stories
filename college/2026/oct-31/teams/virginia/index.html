Wahoowa on the Road: Virginia Visits No. 14 Wake Forest on Halloween

Meet Your Insider
Good evening, and welcome. I am Prescott Ashby-Lowell, your Virginia insider. I refer to our university's home only as "the Grounds." Anyone who uses the other word owes me a nickel, payable in bagels.

A word on method. As Mr. Jefferson would've charted it, every claim deserves evidence, so every number here carries a source. If I don't know a thing, I'll say so plainly. I celebrate our players as fine young people first, I respect every opponent, and I'm grateful to God for autumn in Charlottesville. Hoos fans, the architecture is coming along nicely.

The School in 60 Seconds
Virginia football was established in 1887. Home is Scott Stadium, capacity 61,500, which sits next to the World Heritage Site portion of the university, including the historic Academical Village. CFBD lists the stadium as built in 1931.

History, you say? The program put together 28 straight winning seasons after its first in 1887 and won 12 Southern championships in 20 seasons. In 1915 Virginia became the first Southern program to beat Yale, 10-0, at the Yale Bowl.

The recent record is a renovation in progress: 6-6, 3-7, 3-9, 5-7, then a splendid 11-3 in 2025. Eleven wins. I had it framed.

The Coach and the Scheme
Tony Elliott is head coach. CFBD records his hire date as Dec. 10, 2021. Before Virginia he coached at Clemson from 2011 to 2021, finishing as associate head coach, offensive coordinator and tight ends coach. He won the 2017 Broyles Award as the top assistant in college football. He also played wide receiver at Clemson from 2000 to 2003.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 28.2 points per game and allowing 19.6.

Players to Know

RB Jekail Middlebrook: 513 yards on 105 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Da'Shawn Martin: 17 catches, 247 yards, 5 touchdowns.

The Conference Picture
Virginia is 4-4 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 10th in ACC.
The season so far: W 34-8 vs NC State Wolfpack (Aug. 29), W 59-3 vs Norfolk State Spartans (Sept. 11), W 42-3 vs Delaware Blue Hens (Sept. 26), L 7-38 at Florida State Seminoles (Oct. 3), W 28-25 vs Syracuse Orange (Oct. 10), L 13-23 at SMU Mustangs (Oct. 17), L 16-19 vs Duke Blue Devils (Oct. 23).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Virginia (4-4, 2-3 ACC) at No. 14 Wake Forest (6-1, 4-1 ACC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:45 p.m. ET, Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium, Winston-Salem, N.C., ESPN2.
Last time out. Wake Forest had an open date. The Demon Deacons' last game was a 41-7 win at Cal on Oct. 17, when Lopez threw four touchdowns and Dominick Barry caught four balls for 102 yards and three scores. Their only loss this season came against Miami, 33-20.
Virginia lost 19-16 to Duke at Scott Stadium on Friday, Oct. 23. Middlebrook ran 24 times for 117 yards and a touchdown, and John Rogers caught five balls for 65 yards.
Players to watch. Wake Forest quarterback Gio Lopez has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 140 of 207 for 1,947 yards, 17 touchdowns and just one interception. Carlos Hernandez leads the receivers with 49 catches for 720 yards and four scores, and Ty Clark III leads the rushers with 328 yards and four touchdowns. Linebacker Vincent Firenze leads the defense with 49 tackles, and Langston Hardy has five and a half sacks.
Virginia quarterback Beau Pribula has thrown the most passes in all eight games. He's 119 of 183 for 1,369 yards, 13 touchdowns and two interceptions. Jekail Middlebrook leads the rushers with 513 yards and four scores, and Da'Shawn Martin has five touchdown catches. Matthew Fobbs-White has four sacks, and cornerback Jacobie Henderson leads the team with 40 tackles.
Availability. The official ACC availability report hasn't been posted yet. Virginia coach Tony Elliott said starting safety Brandyn Hillman and safety Ethan Minter are day-to-day, and he confirmed that Pribula remains the starting quarterback. Hernandez was banged up in the Cal game, and Wake Forest used the open week to heal, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Jake Dickert leads Wake Forest, with a hire date of Dec. 18, 2024. His team scores 37.4 points a game, and the defense has 18 sacks. The Demon Deacons beat Louisville on the road and have won their last three ACC games by at least 24 points.
Tony Elliott has led Virginia since being hired Dec. 10, 2021. Before that he coached at Clemson from 2011 to 2021, finishing as associate head coach and offensive coordinator, and he won the 2017 Broyles Award as the top assistant in college football. He also played wide receiver at Clemson. Last season his Cavaliers went 11-3.
Storylines. The matchup to watch is Lopez and Hernandez against a Virginia defense that allows 19.6 points a game. On the other side, Middlebrook and Virginia's big guys up front need to keep Hardy away from Pribula. Wake Forest won four of the five meetings since 2016, including 16-9 last season, but Virginia won a 31-30 thriller in 2024.
