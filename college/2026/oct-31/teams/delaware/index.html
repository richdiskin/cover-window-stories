Wing-T on the Hill: Delaware Visits Western Kentucky on a Tuesday Night

Meet Your Insider
Good evening, and please lower your voices; this is a library. Kidding. Shout all you like. I'm Henrietta Lofland, but my friends and the circulation desk call me Hen, and I'm your Delaware insider. I'm a Newark native, a librarian by day and a wing-T devotee by night. I have opinions about the buck sweep that I've been asked not to share at dinner parties.

Delaware is the First State, and our Blue Hens are writing a brand-new chapter at the FBS level. I'm here to annotate it carefully. You'll get footnotes, because every fact I give you will have a source. You'll get honesty, because if we stumble I'll say so, kindly. You'll also get a librarian's promise: if I don't know something, I'll go look it up before I say it. I brought a pen. A blue one, naturally.

The School in 60 Seconds
Delaware plays in Conference USA at the FBS level. Before that, the Blue Hens were an FCS power. They claim six national titles, in 1946, 1963, 1971, 1972, 1979 and 2003, and they reached the FCS title game again in 2007 and 2010. Delaware has produced quarterbacks Rich Gannon and Joe Flacco, among others.

Home is Tubby Raymond Field at Delaware Stadium in Newark. The stadium dates to 1952 per CFBD. Capacity is conflicting: CFBD lists 22,000, while Wikipedia says 18,500 seats. The last five seasons: 5-6, 8-5, 9-4, 9-2 and 7-6.

The Coach and the Scheme
Ryan Carty has led the program since the 2022 season. CFBD lists his hire date as Dec. 10, 2021.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 28.3 points per game and allowing 28.4.

Players to Know
QB Nick Minicucci: 79 of 132, 1,063 yards, 10 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Jo Silver: 331 yards on 71 carries, 2 touchdowns.
WR Sean Wilson: 31 catches, 408 yards, 3 touchdowns.
LB Blake Matthews: 4.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Gavin Moul: a team-high 46 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Delaware is 3-4 overall and 1-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 6th in CUSA.
The season so far: W 42-7 vs Merrimack Warriors (Sept. 3), L 26-35 at Vanderbilt Commodores (Sept. 12), W 22-14 vs Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (Sept. 19), L 3-42 at Virginia Cavaliers (Sept. 26), L 14-30 vs Liberty Flames (Oct. 2), L 30-33 at Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders (Oct. 13), W 61-38 vs Missouri State Bears (Oct. 20).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Delaware (3-4, 1-2 CUSA) at Western Kentucky (3-4, 2-1 CUSA). Tuesday, Oct. 27, 7 p.m. ET, Houchens Industries-L.T. Smith Stadium, Bowling Green, Ky., CBS Sports Network.
Last time out. Delaware beat Missouri State 61-38 on Oct. 20 for its first Conference USA win of the season. Minicucci went 20 of 29 for 225 yards and five touchdown passes. Silver ran 13 times for 101 yards and a score, and Wilson caught six passes for 69 yards.
Western Kentucky had an open week after a 16-3 win at Sam Houston on Oct. 14. Glenn went 14 of 30 for 127 yards and a touchdown and also carried 22 times for 54 yards. The week before that, the Hilltoppers beat Missouri State 34-13 at home.
Players to watch. Delaware quarterback Nick Minicucci is 79 of 132 for 1,063 yards, 10 touchdowns and four interceptions. Jo Silver leads the Blue Hens with 331 rushing yards and two touchdowns, and wide receiver Sean Wilson has 31 catches for 408 yards and three scores. On defense, linebacker Blake Matthews has 4.5 sacks and linebacker Gavin Moul leads the team with 46 tackles.
Western Kentucky's passing leader is Rodney Tisdale Jr., 80 of 119 for 857 yards, four touchdowns and two interceptions. Brock Glenn threw the most passes in each of the last two games, and both were wins. Ajay Allen leads the rushers with 346 yards and four touchdowns, and wide receiver Jonathan Stafford Jr. has 41 catches for 369 yards. Defensive back Jaylen Lewis leads the Hilltoppers with 56 tackles, and linebacker Westen Ard has 2.5 sacks.
Availability. Conference USA has not posted an availability report for this game, and no injury news has surfaced for either team, so watch the teams' pregame notes. Neither school's box scores mark a starting quarterback. Minicucci threw the most passes for Delaware last week, and Glenn did for Western Kentucky in each of its last two games.
The coaches. Tyson Helton leads Western Kentucky, and CollegeFootballData dates his hire to Nov. 27, 2018. His teams went 9-5, 9-5, 8-5, 8-6 and 9-4 from 2021 through 2025. This year's group took its lumps early at Nevada, Georgia and Indiana, then allowed just 13 and 3 points in its last two games.
Ryan Carty is in his fifth season at Delaware. He led the Blue Hens' move to FBS and Conference USA, and Delaware is the 2025 68 Ventures Bowl champion. His offense scores 28.3 points a game.
Storylines. The matchup to circle is Minicucci and Wilson against Lewis and the Hilltopper secondary. Lewis leads both rosters in tackles. On the other side, Matthews and his 4.5 sacks will test whichever Western Kentucky quarterback takes the snaps. The Blue Hens run for 3.6 yards a carry, so the big guys up front for WKU can make life hard if they win early.
Fun and true. These two met last season, and Western Kentucky won 27-24, so Delaware arrives with a three-point memory. The Blue Hens claim six national titles, from 1946 to 2003, and produced quarterbacks Rich Gannon and Joe Flacco.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Houchens Industries-L.T. Smith Stadium was built in 1968 and lists a capacity of 22,113. The fight song is "Stand Up and Cheer!" and the band is the Big Red Marching Band. And if you visit, ask somebody about Big Red, the mascot Hilltopper fans love to show off first.

My call: Western Kentucky Hilltoppers 30, Delaware Blue Hens 27.
