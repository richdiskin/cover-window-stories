Bears in Springfield: Missouri State Hosts Sam Houston on a Tuesday Night

Meet Your Insider
Howdy from the Ozarks! I'm Clementine Bilyeu, your Missouri State insider. I'm a Springfield native and a float-trip guide, and I've been maroon since the day I was born. Mama swears my first word was "Bears." It was probably "boat," but I've chosen my story and I'm sticking with it.

I take a practical view of football, the same way I take a river. Read the current, respect the rocks, and keep everybody in the canoe. Missouri State is on a brand-new ride as an FBS program, and I'm thrilled to be your guide. I'll give you real numbers, honest takes, and kind words for every opponent. If I don't know something, I'll tell you, and then I'll paddle off and find out. Springfield is the heart of the Ozarks, and our Bears are taking the whole region along. Pack a snack. It's going to be a fun float.

The School in 60 Seconds
Missouri State University is in Springfield, Missouri, and its first football team took the field in 1909. Until 2005, the school was known as Southwest Missouri State. The Bears were a founding member of the Gateway Football Conference in 1985, which later became the Missouri Valley Football Conference.

Home is Plaster Sports Complex, which CFBD lists with a capacity of 17,337 and a 1941 construction year. The last five seasons: 8-4, 5-6, 4-7, 8-4 and 7-6.

The Coach and the Scheme
Casey Woods is in his first season as head coach. CFBD lists his hire date as Dec. 19, 2025. He was previously offensive coordinator at SMU and played wide receiver at Tennessee.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 24.4 points per game and allowing 34.9.

Players to Know
QB Henry Belin IV: 73 of 108, 887 yards, 6 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Ramone Green Jr.: 246 yards on 57 carries, 2 touchdowns.
WR Isaiah Myers: 38 catches, 796 yards, 7 touchdowns.
DE Josh Lukusa: 1.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Jared Lloyd: a team-high 80 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Missouri State is 2-5 overall and 1-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 6th in CUSA.
The season so far: L 0-50 at Texas A&M Aggies (Sept. 5), W 48-14 vs Lindenwood Lions (Sept. 12), L 24-30 vs Marshall Thundering Herd (Sept. 19), L 24-34 at SMU Mustangs (Sept. 26), L 13-34 at Western Kentucky Hilltoppers (Oct. 8), W 24-21 vs Kennesaw State Owls (Oct. 14), L 38-61 at Delaware Blue Hens (Oct. 20).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Sam Houston (1-6, 0-3 CUSA) at Missouri State (2-5, 1-2 CUSA). Tuesday, Oct. 27, 7 p.m. CT (8 p.m. ET), Robert W. Plaster Stadium, Springfield, Mo., ESPN2.
Last time out. Missouri State lost 61-38 at Delaware on Oct. 20, but Myers had a night to remember: 11 catches for 242 yards and two touchdowns. Belin went 26 of 35 for 337 yards and two scores, and Connor Lair ran 15 times for 85 yards and two touchdowns.
Sam Houston lost 30-24 to New Mexico State on Oct. 21 in Huntsville. Locke went 18 of 31 for 235 yards and a touchdown, Burns caught four passes for 117 yards and a score, and MJ Dowdy ran one in.
Players to watch. Missouri State wide receiver Isaiah Myers is the headliner: 38 catches for 796 yards and seven touchdowns. Henry Belin IV has thrown the most passes for the Bears in each of the last two games and is 73 of 108 for 887 yards, six touchdowns and four interceptions. Ramone Green Jr. leads the rushers with 246 yards and two scores. Linebacker Jared Lloyd leads the team with 80 tackles, and defensive end Josh Lukusa has 1.5 sacks.
Sam Houston quarterback Landyn Locke is 147 of 236 for 1,653 yards, 12 touchdowns and five interceptions. Wide receiver Tim Burns Jr. has 15 catches for 329 yards and three scores, more than 20 yards a catch. Alton McCaskill leads the rushers with 220 yards. Linebacker Dean Ford leads the Bearkats with 44 tackles, and linebacker Elijah Groves has 2.5 sacks.
Availability. Conference USA has not posted an availability report for this game, and no injury news has surfaced for either team, so watch the teams' pregame notes. Box scores don't mark starters, but Belin threw the most passes for Missouri State in each of its last two games, and Locke has done it in every Sam Houston game this season.
The coaches. Casey Woods is in his first season at Missouri State after serving as SMU's offensive coordinator. His Bears beat Kennesaw State 24-21 on Oct. 14 for their first FBS win under him, and the offense has scored 38 or more twice this season.
Phil Longo is in his second season at Sam Houston. He was named the program's 16th head coach in December 2024, and he's an air raid coach who previously ran offenses at Wisconsin, North Carolina and Ole Miss. Locke has thrown for at least 300 yards three times this year.
Storylines. The matchup to circle is Myers against a Sam Houston defense that allows 25.3 points a game. On the other side, Locke and Burns face a Missouri State defense that allows 34.9 points a game. Both teams struggle to run it: Sam Houston gains 2.9 yards a carry and Missouri State 3.9, so the big guys up front on both sides have plenty to prove.
Fun and true. Missouri State first fielded a football team in 1909 and was known as Southwest Missouri State until 2005. Sam Houston's close calls this year include a 24-21 loss at Troy in the opener and a 23-17 loss to Tulsa, and its lone win was a 59-0 shutout of Nicholls. Both teams also share a common opponent: Western Kentucky beat Missouri State 34-13 and Sam Houston 16-3.
