Ragin' Cajuns at Rest: Louisiana Takes the Bye Week and Stirs the Pot

Meet Your Insider
Laissez les bons stats rouler! Bonjour, mes amis, I'm Amélie Fontenot, but everybody from Lafayette to Breaux Bridge calls me Mimi. By weekday I'm an accountant. A touchdown? Magnifique. A turnover? Ça c'est bon (if it's ours). Every week you'll get the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines with a little accordion in the background. When we win, I two-step around the desk. When we lose, I say "Laissez les bons temps... later," and eat my boudin solemnly. Allons!

The School in 60 Seconds
The Ragin' Cajuns represent the University of Louisiana at Lafayette, and you may remember the old names, Southwestern Louisiana and USL. We play in the Sun Belt. Home since 1971 has been Cajun Field in Lafayette, listed at 41,426 seats. Our recent history has some magnifique years: 13-1 in 2021 and 10-4 in 2024, with 6-7 seasons in between and 6-7 again in 2025. And the gameday? Cher, the food alone is worth the drive. I've never met a tailgate that didn't have a pot of something on it.

The Coach and the Scheme
Michael Desormeaux was hired Dec. 5, 2021.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 30.1 points per game and allowing 21.3.

Players to Know
QB Lunch Winfield: 130 of 195, 1,452 yards, 6 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
Lunch Winfield on the ground: 470 yards on 110 carries, 9 touchdowns.
WR KeDarius Wade: 13 catches, 279 yards, 2 touchdowns.
LB Lester Jones: 2 sacks, the team lead.
LB Jake St. Andre: a team-high 60 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Louisiana is 5-3 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 3rd in Sun Belt West.
The season so far: W 38-7 vs Lamar Cardinals (Sept. 5), L 30-49 at USC Trojans (Sept. 12), W 21-14 vs UAB Blazers (Sept. 19), W 34-7 at Charlotte 49ers (Sept. 26), W 23-20 vs Arkansas State Red Wolves (Oct. 3), L 31-32 at Louisiana Tech Bulldogs (Oct. 10), W 33-6 vs Troy Trojans (Oct. 17), L 31-35 at Southern Miss Golden Eagles (Oct. 24).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for Louisiana this week. We're off from Tuesday, Oct. 27, through Saturday, Oct. 31, so the rest of this report is about where we stand and what's next.
Last time out. We lost to the Southern Miss Golden Eagles 35-31 on the road on Oct. 24. Lunch Winfield went 22 of 41 for 299 yards, with 2 touchdowns and 1 interception. JJ Garner ran 7 times for 52 yards, with 1 touchdown. Shelton Sampson Jr. caught 5 balls for 58 yards.
Up next. We play the South Alabama Jaguars at home on Saturday, Nov. 7, 5:00 p.m. ET, at Our Lady of Lourdes Stadium in Lafayette.
The open week is for rest, healing and a good week of practice. I'll be back with a full preview before the next kickoff.
