Roll Tide, Rest Tide: Alabama Takes the Bye Week and Gets Ready for November

Meet Your Insider
Roll Tide, darling, and I mean that as a greeting. Mostly. I'm Mary-Kathryn Strickland, but everyone from the Quad to the press box calls me MK. I rode for the Tide equestrian team, I read blitz pickups the way some girls read Southern Living, and I can quote a legendary coaching maxim for every situation, perfectly, usually at dinner. My family has asked me to stop. I will not stop. I travel with this team and I live with this team, and every week you'll get the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines with a little belle-of-the-ball polish. When we win, you'll hear "Roll Tide" in seventeen different tones, each more satisfied than the last. When we lose, I'll say "We will not speak of it," and I will not speak of it.

The School in 60 Seconds
Alabama claims 18 national championships, 13 of them wire-service titles from the poll era. Paul "Bear" Bryant won six from 1958 to 1982, and Nick Saban won six more between 2007 and 2023. The Tide's Heisman winners are Mark Ingram II (2009), Derrick Henry (2015), DeVonta Smith (2020) and Bryce Young (2021). Alabama has 30 SEC championships, an NCAA-record 79 bowl appearances and 46 bowl wins, also a record, plus a record 16 straight 10-win seasons from 2008 through 2023. Bryant-Denny Stadium seats 101,821 and opened in 1929. Coach Bryant has a maxim for this, and I'll save it for dinner.

The Coach and the Scheme
Kalen DeBoer was hired Jan. 12, 2024, and his first two Tide teams went 9-4 and 11-4.

Players to Know

RB Daniel Hill: 332 yards on 71 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Lotzeir Brooks: 29 catches, 488 yards, 1 touchdown.
LB Yhonzae Pierre: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Caleb Woodson: a team-high 63 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Alabama is 6-2 overall and 4-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 3rd in SEC.
The season so far: W 48-10 vs East Carolina Pirates (Sept. 5), W 45-17 at Kentucky Wildcats (Sept. 12), W 50-36 vs Florida State Seminoles (Sept. 19), W 49-18 vs South Carolina Gamecocks (Sept. 26), W 56-23 at Mississippi State Bulldogs (Oct. 3), L 17-20 vs Georgia Bulldogs (Oct. 10), L 10-26 at Tennessee Volunteers (Oct. 17), W 31-20 vs Texas A&M Aggies (Oct. 24).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for Alabama this week. We're off from Tuesday, Oct. 27, through Saturday, Oct. 31, so the rest of this report is about where we stand and what's next.
Last time out. We beat the Texas A&M Aggies 31-20 at home on Oct. 24. Keelon Russell went 19 of 30 for 311 yards, with 3 touchdowns and 1 interception. Daniel Hill ran 9 times for 36 yards. Rico Scott caught 6 balls for 156 yards.
Up next. We play the LSU Tigers on the road on Saturday, Nov. 7, 3:30 p.m. ET, at Tiger Stadium (LA) in Baton Rouge.
The open week is for rest, healing and a good week of practice. I'll be back with a full preview before the next kickoff.
