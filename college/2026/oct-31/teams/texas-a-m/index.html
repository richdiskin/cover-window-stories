Gig 'Em at Ease: Texas A&M Uses the Bye Week to Recharge in College Station

Meet Your Insider
HOWDY! Howdy, and let me yell this: welcome, Aggies! I'm Colt Wendeborn, petroleum engineering graduate, and I report on football practice the way the Yell Leaders lead Midnight Yell, with my whole chest. I travel with this team and I live with this team. My one weakness is that I explain everything with an Aggie tradition, and then I explain the tradition, and then sometimes I explain the explanation. You'll learn a lot. Every week you'll get the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines, delivered with the straight-arrow earnestness of a man who irons his maroon. When we win, I yell. Just yell. Joyfully. When we lose, I go practice silent yell drills in a closet until I feel better. Gig 'em, and thank you for reading.

The School in 60 Seconds
Texas A&M competes in the SEC and claims three national titles and 18 conference championships. Home is Kyle Field, an outdoor stadium on campus that seats 102,733. CFBD dates it to 1927. (A tradition explained: the student section stands the entire game because the 12th Man is ready to go in. That's why I'm standing while I write this.) The last five seasons have run 8-4, 5-7, 7-6, 8-5 and then a big 11-2 in 2025, Mike Elko's second year.

The Coach and the Scheme
Elko was hired Nov. 27, 2023.

Players to Know
QB Marcel Reed: 126 of 222, 1,390 yards, 8 touchdowns, 8 interceptions.
RB KJ Edwards: 360 yards on 55 carries, 2 touchdowns.
WR Isaiah Horton: 23 catches, 314 yards, 3 touchdowns.
DE Marco Jones: 2.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Ray Coney: a team-high 40 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Texas A&M is 4-4 overall and 1-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 15th in SEC.
The season so far: W 50-0 vs Missouri State Bears (Sept. 5), W 48-20 vs Arizona State Sun Devils (Sept. 12), L 21-31 vs Kentucky Wildcats (Sept. 19), L 6-35 at LSU Tigers (Sept. 26), W 34-7 vs Arkansas Razorbacks (Oct. 3), L 6-27 at Missouri Tigers (Oct. 10), W 41-0 vs The Citadel Bulldogs (Oct. 17), L 20-31 at Alabama Crimson Tide (Oct. 24).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for Texas A&M this week. We're off from Tuesday, Oct. 27, through Saturday, Oct. 31, so the rest of this report is about where we stand and what's next.
Last time out. We lost to the Alabama Crimson Tide 31-20 on the road on Oct. 24. Marcel Reed went 14 of 21 for 163 yards, with 1 touchdown and 2 interceptions. KJ Edwards ran 8 times for 43 yards. Mario Craver caught 6 balls for 45 yards, with 1 touchdown.
Up next. We play the South Carolina Gamecocks on the road on Saturday, Nov. 7, 3:00 p.m. ET, at Williams-Brice Stadium in Columbia.
The open week is for rest, healing and a good week of practice. I'll be back with a full preview before the next kickoff.
