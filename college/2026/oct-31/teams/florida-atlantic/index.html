Owls Over Denton: Florida Atlantic Brings Its Air Show to North Texas on Thursday Night

Meet Your Insider
Hey, Owls! I'm Kaia Brightwater, your Florida Atlantic insider. I used to play beach volleyball here in Boca Raton, and now I analyze offenses like swell reports. Choppy means three-and-outs. Clean means long, smooth drives. And a closeout? That's when the whole wave crashes at once, which is how a defense feels when we go tempo. The beachy talk is real, but so is the analysis underneath it, I promise. When we win, it's "a perfect swell day" and a slow-motion hair flip. When we lose, I sigh "flat water" and post a sunset anyway, because it's still Boca. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, sun and stats. "Surf's up on tempo, babe."

The School in 60 Seconds
Florida Atlantic football is young, and it was built by a legend. Howard Schnellenberger founded the program from scratch and served as its first head coach. I love a program that's still writing its story.

The Coach and the Scheme
Zach Kittley was named the Hagerty Family Head Football Coach on Dec. 2, 2024.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 37.3 points per game and allowing 34.0.

Players to Know
QB Caden Veltkamp: 190 of 260, 2,084 yards, 18 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Ethan Ervin: 368 yards on 87 carries, 2 touchdowns.
WR Easton Messer: 58 catches, 709 yards, 6 touchdowns.
LB Joseph Sipp Jr.: 2 sacks, the team lead.
LB Joseph Sipp Jr.: a team-high 49 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Florida Atlantic is 5-2 overall and 2-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 5th in American.
The season so far: L 21-66 at Florida Gators (Sept. 5), W 38-30 vs Navy Midshipmen (Sept. 12), W 16-10 vs Florida International Panthers (Sept. 19), W 45-17 at UL Monroe Warhawks (Sept. 26), W 66-10 vs Texas Southern Tigers (Oct. 3), L 24-56 at Army Black Knights (Oct. 17), W 51-49 vs Rice Owls (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Florida Atlantic (5-2, 2-1 American) at North Texas (5-2, 3-0 American). Thursday, Oct. 29, 7:30 p.m. ET, DATCU Stadium, Denton, Texas, ESPN.
Last time out. North Texas won 49-39 at Navy on Oct. 24. Jackson went 17 of 23 for 305 yards and three touchdowns, White ran 20 times for 174 yards and two scores, and Stevenson caught eight passes for 209 yards and two touchdowns.
Florida Atlantic beat Rice 51-49 at home on Oct. 24. Veltkamp went 29 of 37 for 339 yards and three touchdowns and ran one in from a yard out. Messer caught 12 passes for 162 yards and two scores, and Leonard Farrow ran 17 times for 86 yards.
Players to watch. North Texas quarterback Tayven Jackson is 160 of 221 for 2,173 yards, 18 touchdowns and five interceptions. Running back Jahiem White has 861 yards and 11 touchdowns on 116 carries, and wide receiver Justin Stevenson has 41 catches for 662 yards and seven scores. Linebacker Zakye Barker leads the defense with 57 tackles, and defensive lineman Davion Rhodes has four sacks.
Florida Atlantic's Caden Veltkamp is 190 of 260 for 2,084 yards, 18 touchdowns and only four interceptions. Wide receiver Easton Messer has 58 catches for 709 yards and six scores, and Ethan Ervin leads the rushers with 368 yards. Linebacker Joseph Sipp Jr. leads the team with 49 tackles and two sacks.
Availability. The American has not posted an availability report for this game, and no injury news has surfaced for either team, so watch the teams' pregame notes. Neither school's box scores mark a starter. Jackson has thrown the most passes for North Texas in every game this season, and Veltkamp has done the same for Florida Atlantic.
The coaches. Neal Brown is in his first season at North Texas. He was hired on Dec. 2, 2025, as the program's 21st head coach, after Eric Morris left for Oklahoma State. Brown went 72-51 as a head coach at Troy and West Virginia and is 5-1 in bowl games. His team is 3-0 in league play.
Zach Kittley is in his second season at Florida Atlantic. He was named the Hagerty Family Head Football Coach on Dec. 2, 2024. His Owls throw it all over the yard and score 37.3 points a game.
Storylines. Veltkamp has bounced back in a big way. He was cleared from a shoulder issue before fall camp after a 2025 season with 3,641 passing yards and 24 touchdowns. He threw five touchdown passes against Navy and has four games with three or more.
White is the North Texas headliner on the ground. He averages more than seven yards a carry and ran for 174 at Navy. Florida Atlantic's defense will have to tackle well, because opponents run for 6.2 yards a carry against the Owls.
Fun and true. North Texas has won the last three meetings: 41-38 in 2018, 45-28 in 2022 and 41-37 in 2024. Florida Atlantic won both games in 2017, 69-31 and 41-17. Big scores are a tradition in this series. Both teams also came into this week at 5-2.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
DATCU Stadium seats 30,850, and Denton is a town with music on just about every block. Florida Atlantic will bring a little salt air from Boca Raton, where the field is named for Howard Schnellenberger, who founded the program and was its first head coach.

My call: North Texas Mean Green 48, Florida Atlantic Owls 31.
