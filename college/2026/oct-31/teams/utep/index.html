Hatch Chile Heads North: UTEP Visits North Dakota State on Halloween

Meet Your Insider
That's Hatch-hot! I'm Ernesto "Neto" Galindo, and I'm your UTEP insider. I'm a Sun Bowl ironworker's kid who grades effort like ore quality, and I measure effort in chile heat. On the podcast, that segment is called Chile Heat. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, you won't hear it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is the Sun Bowl in El Paso, built in 1963 with a listed capacity of 51,500. The fight song is "El Paso Fight," and the band is the Sound of the Southwest. The last five seasons went 7-6, 5-7, 3-9, 3-9 and 2-10, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about Paydirt Pete. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Scotty Walden leads the Miners, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 4, 2023.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 15.1 points per game and allowing 37.3.

Players to Know
QB EJ Colson Jr.: 55 of 83, 628 yards, 7 touchdowns, 3 interceptions.
QB Raymond Moore III: 414 yards on 67 carries, 2 touchdowns.
RB Kam Thomas: 16 catches, 270 yards, 1 touchdown.
DE Sterling Miles: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Isaiah Bogerty: a team-high 42 tackles.

The Conference Picture
UTEP is 1-6 overall and 0-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 8th in Mountain West.
The season so far: L 0-51 at Oklahoma Sooners (Sept. 4), W 51-10 vs Texas Southern Tigers (Sept. 12), L 17-52 at Michigan Wolverines (Sept. 19), L 7-33 vs Oregon State Beavers (Sept. 26), L 7-61 at New Mexico Lobos (Oct. 3), L 13-30 vs Nevada Wolf Pack (Oct. 10), L 11-24 vs San José State Spartans (Oct. 17).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
UTEP (1-6, 0-3 Mountain West) at North Dakota State (6-2, 3-2 Mountain West). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, Fargodome, Fargo, N.D., MW+.
Last time out. North Dakota State lost 21-16 at New Mexico on Oct. 24, on Homecoming weekend in Albuquerque. Hayes threw for 198 yards and two touchdowns, and Williams caught four balls for 71 yards. It was the first meeting on record between the schools.
UTEP had an open week. The Miners last played on Oct. 17, losing 24-11 to San José State at the Sun Bowl. Moore threw for 195 yards, and Thomas caught four balls for 96 yards.
Players to watch. North Dakota State quarterback Nathan Hayes has thrown the most passes in all eight games. He's 123 of 196 for 1,470 yards, 12 touchdowns and seven interceptions. Myles Mitchell leads the rushers with 393 yards, and Jackson Williams leads the receivers with 32 catches for 503 yards. Linebacker Donovan Woolen leads the defense with 55 tackles, and Kelton McCaslin has four sacks.
For UTEP, Raymond Moore III has thrown the most passes the last two games and leads the team in rushing with 414 yards. EJ Colson Jr. threw the most in the first five games and leads in passing with 628 yards and seven touchdowns. Kam Thomas leads the receivers with 270 yards, linebacker Isaiah Bogerty leads the defense with 42 tackles, and Sterling Miles has 3.5 sacks.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Hayes has thrown the most passes for North Dakota State every week, and Moore has done it for UTEP the last two games, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Tim Polasek leads North Dakota State, with a hire date of Dec. 17, 2023. His team plays Bison football: run it, defend it, don't give it away. The Bison allow 12.5 points a game, they've taken the ball away 14 times, and they shut out Fordham 38-0 and Wyoming 28-0.
Scotty Walden leads UTEP, with a hire date of Dec. 4, 2023. His offense runs the ball on most plays, and his team's best day came Sept. 12, a 51-10 win over Texas Southern. The open week gave his staff extra time to prepare.
Storylines. The matchup to watch is North Dakota State's defense against Moore, who can hurt you with his legs. The Bison's big guys up front have been tough to run on all season. On the other side, Hayes wants a clean day after five interceptions in the two losses, and UTEP's defense will try to force him into more mistakes. This is the first meeting between these schools in our records from 2016 through 2025.
Fun and true. North Dakota State has won 18 national championships, including 10 FCS titles between 2011 and 2024, and 39 conference championships. The Bison went 14-1, 12-3, 11-4, 14-2 and 12-1 in their last five Missouri Valley seasons. UTEP's fight song is "El Paso Fight."
