Polka at Kinnick: The Hawkeyes Host No. 25 Wisconsin for a Halloween Noon Slugfest

Meet Your Insider
Hello, neighbors. I'm Orrin Kalvelage, your Iowa insider. I grew up on a farm near Iowa City and haven't missed a home opener in my life. I own one black-and-gold sweater, and I wear it with pride every single week. My wife says it needs retiring. I say it's got plenty of Saturdays left in it.

I'm gentle and I'm stubborn, and I'm wildly proud of defense and special teams. I believe a good punt is a love letter to your defense. Here's my pledge: every number in this report has a source. If I don't know something, I'll tell you plainly. I celebrate our players as young men first, I respect every opponent, and I give thanks to God for honest work and honest football. Go Hawks.

The School in 60 Seconds
Iowa joined the conference that became the Big Ten in 1899 and played its first league season in 1900. The Hawkeyes play at Kinnick Stadium in Iowa City, capacity 69,250. They've won 13 conference championships, including 11 in the Big Ten. Iowa has been ranked No. 1 in the AP and Coaches polls 15 times.

The last five seasons are steady as a fence post: 10-4, 8-5, 10-4, 8-5 and 9-4. That's Iowa football. You know what you're getting, and what you're getting is tough.

The Coach and the Scheme
Kirk Ferentz is in his 28th season as head coach, the longest current tenure in FBS. He's led Iowa since 1999. He's the Big Ten's all-time leader in wins. Before Iowa he was head coach at Maine from 1990 to 1992 and an assistant with the Cleveland Browns and Baltimore Ravens. He played linebacker at UConn.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 28.0 points per game and allowing 14.3.

Players to Know
QB Hank Brown: 89 of 145, 968 yards, 6 touchdowns, 3 interceptions.
RB Kamari Moulton: 541 yards on 86 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Tony Diaz: 29 catches, 386 yards, 1 touchdown.
DE Joseph Anderson: 4 sacks, the team lead.
LB Cam Buffington: a team-high 62 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Iowa is 5-2 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 8th in Big Ten.
The season so far: W 40-0 vs Northern Illinois Huskies (Sept. 5), W 16-13 vs Iowa State Cyclones (Sept. 12), W 55-0 vs Northern Iowa Panthers (Sept. 19), W 20-19 at Michigan Wolverines (Sept. 26), L 14-31 vs Ohio State Buckeyes (Oct. 3), W 41-24 at Washington Huskies (Oct. 9), L 10-13 at Minnesota Golden Gophers (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
No. 25 Wisconsin (5-2, 3-1 Big Ten) at Iowa (5-2, 2-2 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12 p.m. ET, Kinnick Stadium, Iowa City, Iowa, Fox.
Last time out. Wisconsin beat USC 24-7 at Camp Randall on Oct. 24. Joseph went 12 of 14 with a touchdown throw, and Abu Sama III ran 17 times for 71 yards.
Iowa lost 13-10 at Minnesota on Oct. 24. Brown threw a touchdown throw to Reece Vander Zee, who caught four passes for 69 yards.
Players to watch. Wisconsin quarterback Colton Joseph has thrown the most passes in all seven Badger games, and he's also the team's leading rusher. He's 99 of 173 for 1,256 yards, 11 touchdowns and five interceptions, with 365 rushing yards and four rushing scores. Tyrell Henry leads the receivers with 28 catches for 343 yards. On defense, linebacker Cooper Catalano has 60 tackles and linebacker Sebastian Cheeks has five sacks.
Iowa quarterback Hank Brown has 968 passing yards, six touchdowns and three interceptions, and he has thrown the most passes in six of Iowa's seven games. Jeremy Hecklinski did it in the 41-24 win at Washington. Running back Kamari Moulton leads the team with 541 rushing yards and four scores. Tony Diaz leads the receivers with 29 catches for 386 yards. Linebacker Cam Buffington has 62 tackles, and end Joseph Anderson has four sacks.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Joseph has thrown the most passes in every Wisconsin game, and Brown did for Iowa last week, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Kirk Ferentz is in his 28th season at Iowa, the longest current tenure in FBS, and he's the Big Ten's all-time leader in wins. He's led the Hawkeyes since 1999, and before that he was head coach at Maine from 1990 to 1992. His defense allows 14.3 points a game this season.
Luke Fickell was hired at Wisconsin on Nov. 27, 2022. His Badgers allow 15.3 points a game, and they've already won 24-20 at Penn State and beaten Michigan State 31-3.
Storylines. The matchup to circle is the Iowa run game against the Wisconsin defense. The Hawkeyes gain 5.7 yards a carry, and the Badgers allow just 2.8. On the other side, Joseph and the Wisconsin big guys up front face an Iowa defense that allows only 2.4 yards a carry. Somebody's ground game is going to have a long afternoon.
