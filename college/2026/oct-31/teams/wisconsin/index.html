Polka Comes to Iowa City: No. 25 Wisconsin Visits Kinnick for a Halloween Noon Kickoff

Meet Your Insider
Hand me the curds. (Polka accordion: oom-pah, oom-pah.) Hiya, folks, I'm Lars Ellefson, but everybody calls me Brat. I was a fullback for the Badgers back in my day, and I still carry a cheese wedge to every game. Every single one. You'll notice I play a little polka before I share a stat. My doctor says it's good for my heart. When we win, I jump around, literally, and hand out cheese curds. When we lose, I eat a brat in thoughtful silence. Then I eat another one.

The School in 60 Seconds
(Oom-pah.) The Badgers have played in the Big Ten since it formed in 1896. Wisconsin is one of 26 programs to win 700 or more games. Home is Camp Randall Stadium, the fourth-oldest stadium in college football, opened in 1917 and listed at 80,321 seats. The last five seasons were 9-4, 7-6, 7-6, 5-7 and 4-8.

The Coach and the Scheme
Luke Fickell is the head coach, and CFBD dates his hire to Nov. 27, 2022. (Oom-pah.) His teams are built on defense, the way a good barn is built on a stone foundation. Spoken like a fullback.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 27.0 points per game and allowing 15.3.

Players to Know
QB Colton Joseph: 99 of 173, 1,256 yards, 11 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
Colton Joseph on the ground: 365 yards on 84 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Tyrell Henry: 28 catches, 343 yards, 1 touchdown.
LB Sebastian Cheeks: 5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Cooper Catalano: a team-high 60 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Wisconsin is 5-2 overall and 3-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 4th in Big Ten. We're ranked No. 25 in ESPN's rankings this week.
The season so far: W 36-9 vs Western Illinois Leathernecks (Sept. 12), W 54-10 vs Eastern Michigan Eagles (Sept. 19), W 24-20 at Penn State Nittany Lions (Sept. 26), W 31-3 vs Michigan State Spartans (Oct. 3), L 7-17 at UCLA Bruins (Oct. 17), W 24-7 vs USC Trojans (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
No. 25 Wisconsin (5-2, 3-1 Big Ten) at Iowa (5-2, 2-2 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12 p.m. ET, Kinnick Stadium, Iowa City, Iowa, Fox.
Last time out. Wisconsin beat USC 24-7 at Camp Randall on Oct. 24. Joseph went 12 of 14 with a touchdown throw, and Abu Sama III ran 17 times for 71 yards.
Iowa lost 13-10 at Minnesota on Oct. 24. Brown threw a touchdown throw to Reece Vander Zee, who caught four passes for 69 yards.
Players to watch. Wisconsin quarterback Colton Joseph has thrown the most passes in all seven Badger games, and he's also the team's leading rusher. He's 99 of 173 for 1,256 yards, 11 touchdowns and five interceptions, with 365 rushing yards and four rushing scores. Tyrell Henry leads the receivers with 28 catches for 343 yards. On defense, linebacker Cooper Catalano has 60 tackles and linebacker Sebastian Cheeks has five sacks.
Iowa quarterback Hank Brown has 968 passing yards, six touchdowns and three interceptions, and he has thrown the most passes in six of Iowa's seven games. Jeremy Hecklinski did it in the 41-24 win at Washington. Running back Kamari Moulton leads the team with 541 rushing yards and four scores. Tony Diaz leads the receivers with 29 catches for 386 yards. Linebacker Cam Buffington has 62 tackles, and end Joseph Anderson has four sacks.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Joseph has thrown the most passes in every Wisconsin game, and Brown did for Iowa last week, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Kirk Ferentz is in his 28th season at Iowa, the longest current tenure in FBS, and he's the Big Ten's all-time leader in wins. He's led the Hawkeyes since 1999, and before that he was head coach at Maine from 1990 to 1992. His defense allows 14.3 points a game this season.
Luke Fickell was hired at Wisconsin on Nov. 27, 2022. His Badgers allow 15.3 points a game, and they've already won 24-20 at Penn State and beaten Michigan State 31-3.
Storylines. The matchup to circle is the Iowa run game against the Wisconsin defense. The Hawkeyes gain 5.7 yards a carry, and the Badgers allow just 2.8. On the other side, Joseph and the Wisconsin big guys up front face an Iowa defense that allows only 2.4 yards a carry. Somebody's ground game is going to have a long afternoon.
Fun and true. Wisconsin has played in the Big Ten since the conference formed in 1896, and it's one of 26 programs with 700 or more wins. Iowa has been steady as a fence post, with records of 10-4, 8-5, 10-4, 8-5 and 9-4 over the last five seasons. This rivalry is as even as it gets lately. In the last ten regular-season meetings, each team has won five, and Iowa won 37-0 last season.

"(Oom-pah!) I jumped around so hard my cheese wedge fell off. Twice."

And the last word from Orrin: "I'll be there, Lars. Go Hawks."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Kinnick Stadium seats 69,250, and a Halloween noon kickoff in Iowa City should bring out plenty of black-and-gold costumes. Iowa has won 13 conference championships, 11 in the Big Ten, and has been ranked No. 1 in the AP and Coaches polls 15 times. Wisconsin's home, Camp Randall Stadium, opened in 1917 and is the fourth-oldest stadium in college football.

My call: Iowa Hawkeyes 22, Wisconsin Badgers 17.
