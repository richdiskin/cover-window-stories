Hokies at Rest: Virginia Tech Takes the Bye Week and Gets Ready to Enter Sandman Again

Meet Your Insider
Hey, Hokie Nation! I'm Ruthanne Dellinger, your Virginia Tech insider. I grew up on a farm outside Blacksburg, and I was a drum major in the Corps of Cadets. I analyze defense like it's a lunch-pail job, because it is.

Here's my promise. I've got mountain-girl spunk, but I don't stretch the truth. Every number in this report has a source. If I don't know something, I'll tell you straight. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I thank God for country roads, crisp fall air and Hokie football. Exit light, enter stats.

The School in 60 Seconds
Virginia Tech has played football since 1892 and has won more than 700 games. The Hokies have appeared in 33 bowl games, including the 2000 BCS National Championship Game. They play in the ACC after previously competing in the Big East. Home is Lane Stadium in Blacksburg, with seating for more than 65,000. ESPN voted it No. 2 on its 2007 list of the "Top 20 Scariest Places to Play." CFBD lists 65,632 seats.

The last five seasons were a climb: 6-7, 3-8, 7-6, 6-7 and 3-9.

The Coach and the Scheme
James Franklin is in his first season as our head coach. CFBD lists his hire date as Nov. 17, 2025. He coached Vanderbilt from 2011 to 2013 and Penn State from 2014 to 2025.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 35.0 points per game and allowing 20.5.

Players to Know
QB Ethan Grunkemeyer: 187 of 261, 2,140 yards, 17 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB Jeffrey Overton Jr.: 639 yards on 113 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Que'Sean Brown: 51 catches, 685 yards, 3 touchdowns.
DL Aycen Stevens: 8 sacks, the team lead.
LB Keon Wylie: a team-high 42 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Virginia Tech is 6-2 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 4th in ACC.
The season so far: W 73-3 vs VMI Keydets (Sept. 5), W 44-21 vs Old Dominion Monarchs (Sept. 12), W 35-26 at Maryland Terrapins (Sept. 19), W 21-14 at Boston College Eagles (Sept. 26), L 33-35 vs Pittsburgh Panthers (Oct. 2), L 20-25 at California Golden Bears (Oct. 10), W 34-24 vs Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets (Oct. 17), W 20-16 at Clemson Tigers (Oct. 24).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for Virginia Tech this week. We're off from Tuesday, Oct. 27, through Saturday, Oct. 31, so the rest of this report is about where we stand and what's next.
Last time out. We beat the Clemson Tigers 20-16 on the road on Oct. 24. Ethan Grunkemeyer went 20 of 32 for 156 yards, with 2 touchdowns. Jeffrey Overton Jr. ran 10 times for 26 yards. Que'Sean Brown caught 5 balls for 69 yards.
Up next. We play the SMU Mustangs on the road on Friday, Nov. 6, 7:00 p.m. ET, at Gerald J. Ford Stadium in Dallas.
The open week is for rest, healing and a good week of practice. I'll be back with a full preview before the next kickoff.
