Cradle of Coaches, Cradle of Calm: Miami Takes a Bye Week Breather in Oxford

Meet Your Insider
Good afternoon, RedHawks, and welcome. I'm Cordelia Van Doren, your Miami (OH) insider. I'm a brick-campus girl through and through, and I know every coach who has ever walked through Oxford. I travel with this team and I live with this team, usually in a cardigan. I do have one small habit. I ambush my co-hosts with coaching-tree trivia when they least expect it. When we win, I add a gold sticker to my Cradle of Coaches chart. When we lose, I remind everyone that even legends lost once, and then I tell you exactly which legends and when. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, with a little history pinned to the lapel. "Another coach from the Cradle, darling."

The School in 60 Seconds
Miami is the Cradle of Coaches, and that's not my phrase. The term was coined in 1959 by Bob Kurz. The honor roll includes Paul Brown, Weeb Ewbank, Ara Parseghian and Bo Schembechler. Pop quiz: name another campus that produced all four. I'll wait. In the modern era the RedHawks keep adding chapters. Under Chuck Martin, Miami won the 2019 MAC championship, beating Central Michigan 26-21 in the title game, and the 2023 title, beating No. 23 Toledo 23-14. Home is Yager Stadium in Oxford, listed at 24,286.

The Coach and the Scheme
Chuck Martin was hired on Dec. 3, 2013, which makes him one of the longest-serving coaches in the conference.

The Conference Picture
Miami (OH) is 5-3 overall and 3-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 2nd in MAC.
The season so far: L 14-59 at Pittsburgh Panthers (Sept. 5), W 45-7 vs Holy Cross Crusaders (Sept. 12), L 31-35 at Cincinnati Bearcats (Sept. 19), W 24-21 vs UConn Huskies (Sept. 26), L 20-24 vs Bowling Green Falcons (Oct. 3), W 20-19 at Massachusetts Minutemen (Oct. 10), W 34-14 vs Akron Zips (Oct. 17), W 28-27 at Central Michigan Chippewas (Oct. 24).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for Miami (OH) this week. We're off from Tuesday, Oct. 27, through Saturday, Oct. 31, so the rest of this report is about where we stand and what's next.
