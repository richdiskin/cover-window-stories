Cowboys in Cyclone Country: No. 24 Oklahoma State Rides Into Ames on Halloween Night

Meet Your Insider
Well, howdy, Cowboy fans. I'm Dusty Lockridge, your Oklahoma State insider. I'm an ag-econ grad, I wear orange from my hat to my boots, and I travel and live with this team. I explain offense with rodeo timing. A quick slant is a two-second ride. A long touchdown drive is the full eight seconds, with a good score from the judges.

Here's my word as a cowboy. Every number I give you has a source. When I do math myself, I'll tell you. If I don't know something, I'll say so. No bull, so to speak. I celebrate our players as young men first, I respect every opponent, and I thank the good Lord for fall Saturdays in Stillwater. Ride 'em, Pokes.

The School in 60 Seconds
Oklahoma State football started in 1901 and plays at Boone Pickens Stadium in Stillwater. CFBD lists 55,509 seats. The Cowboys claim the 1945 national championship, awarded by the AFCA, plus eleven conference titles. They've played in 34 bowl games and gone 22-12, including five New Year's Six wins. Running back Barry Sanders won the Heisman in 1988. The program counts 66 All-Americans.

The last five seasons have been a bucking bronco: 12-2, 7-6, 10-4, then 3-9 and 1-11.

The Coach and the Scheme
Eric Morris is our first-year head coach. Wikipedia says he was introduced on Dec. 8, 2025, while CFBD lists his hire date as Nov. 25, 2025. I'll flag that small difference. Before Stillwater he was head coach at Incarnate Word and North Texas, and offensive coordinator at Washington State.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 40.0 points per game and allowing 23.7.

Players to Know
QB Drew Mestemaker: 157 of 239, 2,326 yards, 20 touchdowns, 6 interceptions.
RB Caleb Hawkins: 712 yards on 142 carries, 2 touchdowns.
WR Wyatt Young: 36 catches, 639 yards, 6 touchdowns.
LB Ethan Wesloski: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Ethan Wesloski: a team-high 70 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Oklahoma State is 5-2 overall and 3-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 2nd in Big 12. We're ranked No. 24 in ESPN's rankings this week.
The season so far: L 10-24 at Tulsa Golden Hurricane (Sept. 5), W 39-31 vs Oregon Ducks (Sept. 12), W 59-0 vs Murray State Racers (Sept. 19), W 41-24 at West Virginia Mountaineers (Sept. 26), W 42-28 vs UCF Knights (Oct. 10), L 32-35 at Houston Cougars (Oct. 17), W 57-24 vs Colorado Buffaloes (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Oklahoma State (5-2, 3-1 Big 12) at Iowa State (3-4, 1-3 Big 12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 7:00 p.m. ET (6:00 p.m. CT), Jack Trice Stadium, Ames, Iowa, FS1.
Last time out. Oklahoma State beat Colorado 57-24 at home. Mestemaker went 21 of 36 for 386 yards and four touchdowns. Justin Bowick caught five balls for 138 yards and a score, and Hawkins ran for 120 yards.
Iowa State lost 39-3 at No. 22 Arizona. Raynor fumbled late in the first half, and Jimmy Rogers gave backup Connor Moberly the second half to look for a spark. Flora was held to 36 yards.
Players to watch. Oklahoma State quarterback Drew Mestemaker has completed 157 of 239 throws for 2,326 yards, with 20 touchdowns and six interceptions. He's thrown for 300 yards or more in six straight games. Wide receiver Wyatt Young has 36 catches for 639 yards and six scores. Running back Caleb Hawkins has 712 yards on 142 carries. Linebacker Ethan Wesloski leads the defense with 70 tackles and 3.5 sacks.
Iowa State running back Aiden Flora has 716 yards and six touchdowns on 102 carries. Quarterback Jaylen Raynor has thrown for 1,107 yards and seven touchdowns. Linebacker Sullivan Schlimgen has 71 tackles, and defensive end Isaac Terrell has three sacks.
Availability. No official Big 12 availability report had been posted as of Tuesday. Oklahoma State safety Mose Phillips hurt his shoulder against UCF and is expected to miss the rest of the season. Safety Quinton Hammonds was hurt recovering a fumble against Colorado and came back to the sideline in a sling. "Hopeful that we can get Q back," Eric Morris said.
The coaches. Jimmy Rogers settled the quarterback question early. "We're gonna go with Jaylen," he said, calling Raynor "the best leader on the team." Raynor will make his 43rd straight start, counting his time at Arkansas State. Rogers also said of Moberly, "his future's bright."
Eric Morris is in his first season in Stillwater. Mestemaker and Hawkins followed him from North Texas, and the Cowboys are No. 24 in the country after going 1-11 last season.
Iowa State defensive coordinator Jesse Bobbit was honest about the challenge. "He can make the throws," he said of Mestemaker. "You can see it on tape."
Storylines. Mestemaker ran 75 yards on one play in the 39-31 win over Oregon. That one run is half of his season rushing total.
Raynor is one of six team captains, elected by his teammates in the preseason. When he was benched, he spent the second half coaching up and cheering on Moberly.
Fun and true. This is Iowa State's fifth game against a ranked team in its first eight. The Cyclones are 3-1 at home this season, and their home wins include a 45-42 thriller over West Virginia.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Jack Trice Stadium holds 61,500. It's named for Jack Trice, Iowa State's first Black athlete, and it got his name in 1997. It is still the only major college football stadium named for a Black man. Every visitor should know his story.
Oklahoma State has played football since 1901. The Cowboys claim the 1945 national championship, and Barry Sanders won the Heisman Trophy in 1988.

My call: Oklahoma State Cowboys 30, Iowa State Cyclones 27.
