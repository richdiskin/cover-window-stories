Yinz Ready for Halloween Night: Pitt Welcomes Georgia Tech to the North Shore

Meet Your Insider
Hey, Pitt fans! How's it goin', n'at? Welcome! I'm Dominic Bertolucci, but around here they call me Yinz. I played linebacker back in a little steel-mill town, and these days I grade every tackle this team makes on my personal scale. It goes from "yinz," meaning a beautiful, textbook form tackle, all the way down to "yikes," meaning somebody whiffed. I blow a homemade whistle for every grade. The producers love it. Okay, they tolerate it. I travel with this team and I live with this team. When we win, I holler "Yinz SEE that?!" so loud the board op flinches. When we lose, I eat a sandwich with the fries right inside it, in total silence. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, loud and lovable.

The School in 60 Seconds
Pitt has a national championship in its trophy case: the 1976 Panthers went 12-0, and running back Tony Dorsett won the Heisman Trophy that season. Then in 2021 under Pat Narduzzi, Pitt won its first ACC championship, beating Wake Forest 45-21 in the title game. Home is Acrisure Stadium, listed at 68,400, right there on the North Shore. Dat's history, n'at.

The Coach and the Scheme
Pat Narduzzi was hired on Dec. 16, 2014. Defense is his thing.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 31.6 points per game and allowing 20.9.

Players to Know
QB Mason Heintschel: 98 of 148, 1,304 yards, 14 touchdowns, 1 interception.
RB Damon Ferguson Jr.: 477 yards on 92 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Cataurus Hicks: 31 catches, 649 yards, 5 touchdowns.
DL Jaeden Moore: 4.5 sacks, the team lead.
DB Cruce Brookins: a team-high 55 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Pittsburgh is 5-3 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 10th in ACC.
The season so far: W 59-14 vs Miami (OH) RedHawks (Sept. 5), W 12-7 vs UCF Knights (Sept. 12), W 27-13 vs Syracuse Orange (Sept. 17), W 59-0 vs Bucknell Bison (Sept. 26), W 35-33 at Virginia Tech Hokies (Oct. 2), L 17-21 vs North Carolina Tar Heels (Oct. 10), L 21-27 at Boston College Eagles (Oct. 17), L 23-52 at Miami Hurricanes (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Georgia Tech (2-5, 1-3 ACC) at Pittsburgh (5-3, 2-3 ACC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 7:30 p.m. ET, Acrisure Stadium, Pittsburgh, ESPN2.
Last time out. Georgia Tech lost 49-27 at home to Boston College. Mendoza threw for 186 yards and a touchdown, Haynes ran for a score, and Dalen Penson had a career day with five catches for 88 yards and a touchdown. Boston College ran for 399 yards.
Pitt lost 52-23 at No. 1 Miami. Geriner went 18 of 32 for 268 yards and a touchdown, and Hicks caught five passes for 149 yards and a score. Ferguson ran for a touchdown too.
Players to watch. Georgia Tech quarterback Alberto Mendoza is 153 of 215 for 1,714 yards, 10 touchdowns and six interceptions. Running back Justice Haynes has 515 yards and four touchdowns, and wide receiver Jordan Allen leads the team with 42 catches for 517 yards. On defense, safety Tae Harris has 45 tackles, and Noah Carter has three sacks.
Pitt's season leader at quarterback is Mason Heintschel, with 1,304 yards, 14 touchdowns and one interception, but he is out for the year. Holden Geriner is the starter now. Running back Damon Ferguson Jr., a freshman, has 477 yards and four touchdowns. Wide receiver Cataurus Hicks has 31 catches for 649 yards and five touchdowns. On defense, Cruce Brookins has 55 tackles, and Jaeden Moore has 4.5 sacks.
Availability. The official ACC availability report had not been posted as of Tuesday. Pitt coach Pat Narduzzi announced in early October that Heintschel was injured at Virginia Tech and will miss the rest of the season. "We're heartbroken for Mason, but he's a resilient young man," Narduzzi said. Pitt running back Ja'Kyrian Turner was listed out for the Miami game. Georgia Tech has been hit hard by injuries; Carter, defensive tackle Tawfiq Thomas, cornerback Daiquan White and safety Savion Riley were all listed out for the Boston College game.
The coaches. Brent Key has led Georgia Tech since 2022. After the Boston College game he took the blame. "It's on coaching, so that's on me. I'll take full responsibility," he said. He also told his team, "I want to play. I want to compete."
Narduzzi has led Pitt since December 2014. He named Geriner his starter after the Miami game. Earlier this season he said, "We have a team that can win an ACC championship with Mason or without Mason."
Storylines. Geriner is a fifth-year player who has waited a long time for this chance, and he looked steadier at Miami. Ferguson is a freshman who has become Pitt's top runner, and veteran La'Vell Wright adds four rushing touchdowns of his own. For Georgia Tech, Mendoza threw for 335 yards at Virginia Tech two weeks ago, and Penson is coming off his best game.
Fun and true. Last year Pitt won 42-28 in Atlanta in a night game with a lot riding on it. Georgia Tech won the 2022 meeting 26-21.
Georgia Tech's best win this season was 28-25 over Duke, which was unbeaten at the time.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Wendell Quist, Georgia Tech's insider. Here's what I said:
"How's it goin', n'at? Welcome to Pittsburgh, Wendell."
"Brookins with 55 tackles. That's a whole lotta yinz."
"Prayers for everybody on that list, from both sides."
"My whistle says 'yinz' tonight, Wendell. Happy Halloween."
And the last word from Wendell: "I ran the numbers on a Halloween comeback. The curve points up."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)

Georgia Tech plays at Bobby Dodd Stadium at Hyundai Field in Midtown Atlanta and claims national titles from 1917, 1928 and 1990.

My call: Pittsburgh Panthers 36, Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets 21.
