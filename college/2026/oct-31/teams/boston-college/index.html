Court Is in Session at Wallace Wade: Boston College Brings Its Gavel to Durham

Meet Your Insider
All rise, Eagles fans. I'm Siobhan Dunleavy, your Boston College insider, grad student and former debate champion. I argue every play like it's a moot-court case, and I always appoint myself the judge, which my co-hosts find wicked unfair. When we win, I bang my toy gavel and announce, "Objection overruled, we WON." When we lose, I file an appeal with the Court of Iced Coffee. The court has never ruled in my favor, and I keep filing anyway. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, argued on the merits. "Objection: sustained."

The School in 60 Seconds
Boston College plays at Alumni Stadium in Chestnut Hill, listed at 44,500.

The Coach and the Scheme
Bill O'Brien was named Boston College's 37th head coach on Feb. 9, 2024. His résumé is a long exhibit list. At Penn State in 2012 and 2013 he went 15-9 and won the Bear Bryant and Maxwell Club coach of the year awards in 2012. With the Houston Texans he went 52-48 and won four AFC South titles.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 24.4 points per game and allowing 23.6.

Players to Know
QB Mason McKenzie: 107 of 185, 1,363 yards, 8 touchdowns, 7 interceptions.
Mason McKenzie on the ground: 614 yards on 119 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Javarius Green: 26 catches, 286 yards, 1 touchdown.
DE Demetrius Ballard: 4.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Anthony Palano: a team-high 61 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Boston College is 4-3 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 6th in ACC.
The season so far: L 15-34 at Cincinnati Bearcats (Sept. 5), W 28-21 vs Rutgers Scarlet Knights (Sept. 11), W 22-16 vs Maine Black Bears (Sept. 19), L 14-21 vs Virginia Tech Hokies (Sept. 26), L 16-25 at SMU Mustangs (Oct. 3), W 27-21 vs Pittsburgh Panthers (Oct. 17), W 49-27 at Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Boston College (4-3, 2-2 ACC) at Duke (5-2, 2-2 ACC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12 p.m. ET, Wallace Wade Stadium, Durham, N.C., CW.
Last time out. Boston College won 49-27 at Georgia Tech on Oct. 24. McKenzie ran 16 times for 170 yards and two touchdowns and threw for 101 yards and another score.
Duke won 19-16 at Virginia on Oct. 23. Eget went 14 of 25 for 211 yards, Sheppard ran for a touchdown, and Burton caught four passes for 59 yards.
Players to watch. Boston College quarterback Mason McKenzie has thrown the most passes in all seven Eagle games, and he's also the team's leading rusher. He's 107 of 185 for 1,363 yards, eight touchdowns and seven interceptions, and he has 614 rushing yards and five rushing scores. Javarius Green leads the receivers with 26 catches for 286 yards. On defense, linebacker Anthony Palano has 61 tackles and end Demetrius Ballard has 4.5 sacks.
Duke quarterback Walker Eget has thrown the most passes in all seven Blue Devil games. He's 122 of 197 for 1,440 yards, eight touchdowns and three interceptions. Running back Nate Sheppard is the workhorse, with 875 yards and nine touchdowns on 153 carries. Jonah Burton leads the receivers with 21 catches for 298 yards. Linebacker Nick Morris Jr. has 44 tackles, and end Kevin O'Connor has four sacks.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. McKenzie and Eget have each thrown the most passes in every game for their teams this season, so check the teams' pregame notes before kickoff.
The coaches. Bill O'Brien was named Boston College's 37th head coach on Feb. 9, 2024. He went 15-9 at Penn State in 2012 and 2013, won four AFC South titles with the Houston Texans, and coached Heisman winner Bryce Young as Alabama's offensive coordinator.
Manny Diaz was hired at Duke on Dec. 7, 2023. He was head coach at Miami from 2019 to 2021 and defensive coordinator at Penn State in 2022 and 2023. His Duke defense allows 16 points a game this season, and it held Tulane to three points and Stanford to seven.
Storylines. The case to watch is McKenzie against a Duke defense that allows 3.3 yards a carry. If the Duke big guys up front keep him from breaking loose the way he did in Atlanta, the Eagles will have to throw. On the other side, Sheppard gains plenty of yards behind a Duke run game that averages 5.3 yards a carry, and BC allows 4.0. Whoever controls the ground game probably controls the afternoon.
Fun and true. Duke's football rebirth under David Cutcliffe from 2008 to 2021 included the school's first Coastal Division title in 2013. Since then, the Blue Devils have stacked four straight winning seasons: 9-4, 8-5, 9-4 and 9-5. Boston College plays its home games at Alumni Stadium in Chestnut Hill. These two have split their last two meetings: BC won 26-6 in 2020, and Duke won 38-31 in 2022.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Wallace Wade Stadium opened in 1929 and seats 40,004. Duke has won 18 conference championships, eight in the ACC and ten in the Southern Conference, and the program counts 53 All-Americans. A Halloween noon kickoff means expect a few Blue Devil costumes in the stands, which is the easiest costume in Durham.

My call: Duke Blue Devils 29, Boston College Eagles 18.
