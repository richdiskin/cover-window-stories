Pony Up in the Loud House: No. 19 SMU Heads North to Syracuse

Meet Your Insider
Well, hello, Mustangs! I'm Tatum Delacroix-Price, your SMU insider. I run a little boutique in Highland Park. I know every handshake in this town, and I know every nickel blitz in our playbook. Now, fair warning: I grade the other team's sideline outfits on air, and darling, I am never wrong. Polished, sweet as can be, and then savage. That's the Dallas way. When we win, I pop imaginary confetti and declare the Boulevard officially iconic. When we lose, I put on bigger sunglasses. Much bigger. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, with just a little sparkle. My motto: "Pony up or sit down, sugar."

The School in 60 Seconds
SMU has a Heisman Trophy winner: Doak Walker in 1948. Then came the Pony Express era, with Eric Dickerson and Craig James in the backfield. More recently, the Mustangs played in the 2024 ACC championship game and lost to Clemson 34-31 on a field goal at the end. Home is Gerald J. Ford Stadium in University Park, listed at 32,000, an intimate little jewel box of a stadium. And it's spotless.

The Coach and the Scheme
Rhett Lashlee was hired on Nov. 30, 2021.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 36.4 points per game and allowing 21.1.

Players to Know
QB Kevin Jennings: 156 of 215, 2,062 yards, 19 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Kendrick Raphael: 641 yards on 121 carries, 8 touchdowns.
WR Yamir Knight: 38 catches, 443 yards, 2 touchdowns.
DE Marques White: 5.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Alexander Kilgore: a team-high 39 tackles.

The Conference Picture
SMU is 6-1 overall and 4-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 2nd in ACC. We're ranked No. 19 in ESPN's rankings this week.
The season so far: W 27-24 at Florida State Seminoles (Sept. 7), W 56-10 vs UC Davis Aggies (Sept. 12), L 31-41 at Louisville Cardinals (Sept. 19), W 34-24 vs Missouri State Bears (Sept. 26), W 25-16 vs Boston College Eagles (Oct. 3), W 23-13 vs Virginia Cavaliers (Oct. 17), W 59-20 vs California Golden Bears (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
No. 19 SMU (6-1, 4-1 ACC) at Syracuse (3-4, 1-4 ACC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12 p.m. ET, JMA Wireless Dome, Syracuse, N.Y., ESPN2.
Last time out. SMU beat California 59-20 on Oct. 24. Jennings went 16 of 23 for 223 yards and three touchdowns, and Raphael ran 22 times for 104 yards and two scores.
Syracuse won 31-30 in overtime at North Carolina on Oct. 24. Angeli went 20 of 34 for 256 yards and two touchdowns. Ahmad Miller carried 30 times for 125 yards and a score, and Hatcher caught four passes for 103 yards and a touchdown.
Players to watch. SMU quarterback Kevin Jennings has thrown the most passes in all seven Mustang games. He's 156 of 215 for 2,062 yards, 19 touchdowns and five interceptions, and he threw for 430 yards in the opener at Florida State. Running back Kendrick Raphael has 641 rushing yards and eight touchdowns. Yamir Knight leads the receivers with 38 catches for 443 yards. On defense, end Marques White has 5.5 sacks, and linebacker Alexander Kilgore leads the team with 39 tackles.
Syracuse wide receiver Umari Hatcher is the name to know. He has 54 catches for 887 yards and eight touchdowns. Steve Angeli leads the Orange with 987 passing yards, six touchdowns and five interceptions. Tylik Hill has 461 rushing yards. Linebacker Chris D'Appolonia leads the team with 51 tackles, and defensive lineman Tunmise Adeleye has four sacks.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Syracuse has had three different quarterbacks throw the most passes in a game this season: Angeli, Amari Odom and Malachi Nelson. Angeli did it last week, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Rhett Lashlee was hired at SMU on Nov. 30, 2021. His Mustangs played in the 2024 ACC championship game and lost 34-31 to Clemson on a field goal at the end. This year they're second in the ACC, and they've won all five games at home in University Park.
Fran Brown was hired at Syracuse on Nov. 28, 2023, and his first team went 10-3 in 2024. This year's Orange score 31.6 points a game, and they've already won two overtime thrillers, 42-41 at UConn and 31-30 at North Carolina.
Storylines. The big matchup is Hatcher against an SMU defense that held Boston College to 16 points and Virginia to 13. On the other side, the Syracuse big guys up front will try to slow Raphael. SMU gains 4.4 yards a carry and Syracuse allows 4.2, while the Orange gain 3.8 and SMU allows 3.8, so the ground game could be a grind. And watch Jennings early. He threw 33 of 39 for 439 yards against Missouri State.
Fun and true. SMU has a Heisman Trophy winner, Doak Walker in 1948, and the Pony Express backfield of Eric Dickerson and Craig James is still legendary in Dallas. Syracuse has had its ups and downs, from 10-3 in 2024 to 3-9 last season. These two met last season, and SMU won 31-18.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
