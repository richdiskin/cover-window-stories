Rock Chalk on the Range: Kansas Brings Its Hoops Math to Fort Worth on Halloween

Meet Your Insider
Hi, everybody. I'm Fitz Calloway, your Kansas insider. I'm a lifelong Lawrence native and a hoops-first guy who converted to football analytics out of spite. I travel and live with this team, and I convert every football stat into basketball points. A touchdown is two and a half three-pointers. I refuse to stop.

Here's my promise. I'm self-deprecating, but I'm not sloppy. Every number in this report has a source, and when I do the math myself, I'll say so. If I don't know something, I'll admit it. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I thank God for this town, this team and this stubborn hope. I'm only here because of spite and the stats. Okay, also love. Rock Chalk.

The School in 60 Seconds
Kansas football began in 1890, which makes it one of the oldest programs in the country. Home is David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium, which opened in 1921 and is one of the oldest college football stadiums anywhere. Until 2014 it had a running track around the field. CFBD lists 47,000 seats.

The last five seasons: 2-10 in 2021, then 6-7, a joyful 9-4 in 2023, and 5-7 twice. That 9-4 season is a memory I cherish.

The Coach and the Scheme
Lance Leipold has been head coach since 2021. CFBD lists his hire date as April 30, 2021. At Wisconsin-Whitewater from 2007 to 2014, he won six Division III national championships. He reached 100 wins faster than any coach in NCAA history, in his 106th game. Then he coached Buffalo from 2015 to 2020.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 31.0 points per game and allowing 24.0.

Players to Know
QB Isaiah Marshall: 105 of 196, 1,506 yards, 11 touchdowns, 7 interceptions.
RB Dylan Edwards: 531 yards on 105 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Nik McMillan: 25 catches, 466 yards, 5 touchdowns.
DE Leroy Harris III: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Trey Lathan: a team-high 48 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Kansas is 3-4 overall and 1-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 12th in Big 12.
The season so far: W 51-6 vs Long Island University Sharks (Sept. 4), L 21-38 vs Missouri Tigers (Sept. 11), W 55-0 vs Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders (Oct. 3), L 24-41 at Utah Utes (Oct. 10), L 24-35 at Kansas State Wildcats (Oct. 17), W 25-24 vs Baylor Bears (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Kansas (3-4, 1-3 Big 12) at TCU (3-4, 1-3 Big 12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, Amon G. Carter Stadium, Fort Worth, Texas, FS1.
Last time out. Kansas beat Baylor 25-24 on Homecoming in Lawrence on Oct. 24. Edwards carried 30 times for 156 yards, Marshall threw two touchdowns, and McMillan caught a scoring ball. It was KU's 114th Homecoming, and the parade on Jayhawk Boulevard was back for the first time in seven years.
TCU lost 24-20 to West Virginia at home on Oct. 24. Craig completed 33 of 47 for 372 yards and a touchdown, and Kari Ashley caught six balls for 118 yards. The week before, the Frogs won 17-6 at Baylor.
Players to watch. TCU quarterback Jaden Craig has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 162 of 240 for 2,066 yards, 11 touchdowns and six interceptions. Jeremy Payne leads the rushers with 429 yards, and Ed Small leads the receivers with 38 catches for 514 yards. On defense, Koron Hayward has 4.5 sacks, and linebacker Hudson Hooper leads the team with 41 tackles.
Kansas quarterback Isaiah Marshall has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 105 of 196 for 1,506 yards, 11 touchdowns and seven interceptions. Dylan Edwards leads the rushers with 531 yards and four scores, and Nik McMillan leads the receivers with 25 catches for 466 yards and five touchdowns. Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Craig has thrown the most passes for TCU in every game, and Marshall has done the same for Kansas, so check the teams' pregame notes.

Lance Leipold has led Kansas since 2021. Before that he won six Division III national championships at Wisconsin-Whitewater and reached 100 wins faster than any coach in NCAA history, in his 106th game. His 2023 team went 9-4. This year his offense runs the ball well, behind Edwards and the big guys up front.
