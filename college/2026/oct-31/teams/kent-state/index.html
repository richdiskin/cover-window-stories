Flashes Out West: Kent State Makes Its First Trip to Hornet Stadium for Fireworks Friday

Meet Your Insider
Hello, Golden Flashes fans. Welcome. I'm Ozzie Brannigan, your Kent State insider. I graduated from a campus with a famous fashion school, so I rate two things every week: uniforms and fourth-down decisions. I travel with this team and I live with this team. I'm a pessimist, and the annoying part is how often I'm right. When we lose, I say, "I expected this," and I mean it. When we win, there's a stunned silence, and then I say, "I expected this," which is not true at all. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, with realistic expectations. Very realistic. "I expected this. I always expect this."

The School in 60 Seconds
Kent State plays at Dix Stadium, listed at 25,319. Our rivalry with Akron is the Wagon Wheel game, a trophy that dates to 1946. Here is a rare happy fact: Kent State won the first nine Wagon Wheel games. And another one: we beat Akron 42-35 last season. We also won in 2020, 2021 and 2022, including a 69-35 game in 2020. Akron won in 2023 and 2024.

The Coach and the Scheme
Mark Carney is Kent State's 24th head coach. He was interim coach starting in April 2025, and the school removed the interim tag on Oct. 30, 2025.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 14.9 points per game and allowing 30.5.

Players to Know
QB Dru DeShields: 124 of 218, 1,439 yards, 8 touchdowns, 6 interceptions.
RB Brandon White: 235 yards on 68 carries.
WR Wayne Harris: 40 catches, 552 yards, 4 touchdowns.
LB Hayden Dawahare: 4 sacks, the team lead.
LB Hayden Dawahare: a team-high 43 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Kent State is 3-5 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 7th in MAC.
The season so far: L 0-57 at South Carolina Gamecocks (Sept. 5), W 36-15 vs Wofford Terriers (Sept. 12), L 3-59 at Ohio State Buckeyes (Sept. 19), W 26-13 vs Ball State Cardinals (Sept. 26), L 10-13 vs Ohio Bobcats (Oct. 3), L 7-38 at Western Michigan Broncos (Oct. 10), L 2-29 at South Florida Bulls (Oct. 17), W 35-20 vs Akron Zips (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Kent State (3-5, 2-2 MAC) at Sacramento State (1-7, 0-5 MAC). Friday, Oct. 30, 10:30 p.m. ET (7:30 p.m. Pacific), Hornet Stadium, Sacramento, Calif., ESPN2.
Last time out. Kent State beat Akron 35-20 at home to keep the Wagon Wheel. After a 7-7 halftime tie, DeShields threw four touchdown passes and ran for another, and Harris caught nine passes for 156 yards and three scores.
Sacramento State lost 34-17 at Ball State, but the Hornets led twice in the first half. Sharman threw two touchdown passes, Jace Wilson caught a 55-yard score, and Silvera picked off two passes and made 11 tackles.
Players to watch. Kent State quarterback Dru DeShields is 124 of 218 for 1,439 yards, eight touchdowns and six interceptions, and he has run for a team-high four scores. Wide receiver Wayne Harris has 40 catches for 552 yards and four touchdowns, and running back Brandon White leads the rushers with 235 yards. Linebacker Hayden Dawahare leads the defense with 43 tackles and four sacks.
Sacramento State quarterback Jackson Sharman is 123 of 218 for 1,257 yards, eight touchdowns and six interceptions. Wide receiver Matt Coleman has 39 catches for 492 yards and five scores, fourth in the MAC in receiving yards. Running back Isaiah Ene has 303 yards. Safety Boogsie Silvera leads the team with 61 tackles.
Availability. The MAC has not posted an availability report for this game, and no injury news has surfaced for either starting quarterback, so watch the teams' pregame notes. Sacramento State has used seven different starting groups on offense this year, and Silvera is the only Hornets defender to start all eight games.
The coaches. Mark Carney is in his second season as Kent State's head coach and is 8-12. Kent State removed his interim tag on Oct. 30, 2025, so Friday is one year to the day. His assistant head coach and defensive coordinator, Cherokee Valeria, coached Sacramento State's defensive backs from 2019 to 2024 and was part of three Big Sky championships.
Alonzo Carter is a first-time head coach. Sacramento State hired him on Dec. 15, 2025, and he's leading the Hornets through their first FBS season.
Storylines. Silvera is a fifth-year player whose path ran through Charleston Southern, McNeese and USF. He has three games this season with double-digit tackles.
The Hornets have football families, too. Coleman's father, Herman, played quarterback at Southern, and Onterrio Smith Jr., who has 33 catches for 350 yards, is the son of former Tennessee and Oregon running back Onterrio Smith, who played for the Minnesota Vikings.
The Sacramento State special teams group calls itself the Wefense. Kicker Grant Meadors hit a career-long 47-yarder at Ball State, and punter Hamish Adams of Melbourne, Australia, averaged 45.3 yards on six punts.
Fun and true. This is the first meeting between the schools. Sacramento State went 7-5 in 2025, its last FCS season, and has played four Friday night games since 2000. Adams is the program's third straight punter from Australia, and all three came through ProKick Australia.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Mateo Villaseñor, Sacramento State's insider. Here's what I said:
"I expected fireworks. I always expect fireworks. I just don't expect them to be for us."
"Four touchdown passes. I'm happy. I'm also nervous that I'm happy."
"Healthcare Appreciation Night is perfect. I'll need a nurse by the fourth quarter."
"Fine. Great punting. I expected that too."
And the last word from Mateo: "Three Aussie punters in a row. That's a family recipe."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Friday is Healthcare Appreciation and AANHPI Heritage Night at Hornet Stadium, and the postgame fireworks are back. Ted Emrich and former Texas running back Fozzy Whitaker will call the game on ESPN2.

My call: Kent State Golden Flashes 27, Sacramento State Hornets 22.
