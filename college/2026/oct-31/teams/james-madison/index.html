Dukes of Harrisonburg: The Title Game Rematch Comes Home on a Thursday Night

Meet Your Insider
Purple reign, y'all! I'm Lila Shenandoah, your James Madison insider. I'm a former JMU cheer captain who somehow became the program's living encyclopedia. I travel with this team, I live with this team, and I turn every stat into a cheer. Yes, even penalties. "Give me a FLAG! Give me a FIVE! Give me. a moment to collect myself!"

Here's what you get from me. Glam, volume, and receipts. I'm excited, I'm all-in, and every number I chant has a source behind it. Every opponent gets a respectful pom-pom wave, and I don't invent anything, ever. If I don't know, I'll say, "Hold that chant, I'm checking." The Shenandoah Valley shows up loud and proud for the Dukes. Give me a J, give me an M, give me a third-down stop.

The School in 60 Seconds
JMU first fielded a football team in 1972, and the Dukes play on campus at Bridgeforth Stadium in Harrisonburg, Virginia. CFBD lists it as built in 1975 with a capacity of 24,877. JMU won FCS national championships in 2004 and 2016 and finished as national runner-up in 2017 and 2019. The Dukes accepted an invitation to the FBS in 2021 and joined the Sun Belt. Famous Dukes include Charles Haley, who won five Super Bowl rings and is in both the College and Pro Football Halls of Fame.

The last five seasons are pure cheer material: 12-2, 8-3, 11-2, 9-4 and 12-2. Give me a W! Give me lots of them!

The Coach and the Scheme
Billy Napier is in his first season. CFBD lists his hire date as Dec. 6, 2025. He was head coach at Louisiana from 2018 to 2021, going 40-12 with three straight seasons of 10 or more wins, and then at Florida from 2022 to 2025. Before becoming a head coach, he was offensive coordinator and quarterbacks coach at Arizona State in 2017.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 41.4 points per game and allowing 18.2.

The Conference Picture
James Madison is 7-1 overall and 4-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 1st in Sun Belt East.
The season so far: W 20-13 vs Liberty Flames (Sept. 5), W 87-3 vs Wagner Seahawks (Sept. 12), W 26-13 at San Diego State Aztecs (Sept. 19), W 46-20 at Old Dominion Monarchs (Sept. 26), W 45-17 vs Marshall Thundering Herd (Oct. 3), W 27-24 at Georgia Southern Eagles (Oct. 10), W 49-22 vs Georgia State Panthers (Oct. 17), L 31-34 (OT) at App State Mountaineers (Oct. 22).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Troy (3-4, 1-2 Sun Belt) at James Madison (7-1, 4-1 Sun Belt). Thursday, Oct. 29, 7:30 p.m. ET, Bridgeforth Stadium, Harrisonburg, Va., ESPN2.
Last time out. James Madison lost 34-31 in overtime at App State on Oct. 22, its first loss of the season. Coleman went 22 of 39 for 232 yards and a touchdown, and Braeden Wisloski caught two passes for 84 yards and a score.
Troy lost 21-14 to UL Monroe at home on Oct. 24. The Trojans led 14-3 with less than three minutes to play before the Warhawks rallied in the final minutes. Jordan Lovett ran 17 times for 70 yards.
Players to watch. James Madison quarterback Camden Coleman is 131 of 212 for 1,567 yards, 14 touchdowns and just two interceptions. Wide receiver Corey Scott leads the Dukes with 24 catches for 371 yards and four scores. On defense, linebacker Nakian Jackson has 59 tackles and linebacker JT Hooten has three sacks.
For Troy, quarterback Goose Crowder is 102 of 183 for 1,162 yards, nine touchdowns and five interceptions. Running back Gavin Griffin has 398 rushing yards, and wide receiver TK Keyes has 13 catches for 282 yards and three touchdowns. Defensive back Donnie Smith has six sacks, and linebacker Keno Jones leads the team with 52 tackles.
Availability. The Sun Belt has not posted an availability report for this game. James Madison running back George Pettaway, the team's leading rusher with 824 yards and eight touchdowns, is out with a lower-body injury, according to the Daily News-Record. Nick Herman and Seth Cromwell are the next backs up. Troy quarterback Goose Crowder has been dealing with a shoulder injury, and coach Gerad Parker had not settled on a quarterback plan early in the week, according to AL.com. Tucker Kilcrease played after Crowder came out against UL Monroe. Watch the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Billy Napier is in his first season at James Madison. He went 40-12 at Louisiana from 2018 to 2021 and then coached Florida from 2022 to 2025. His Dukes score 41.4 points a game and allow 18.2.
Gerad Parker is in his third season at Troy. He was hired on Dec. 18, 2023. His team has a short week and a long trip after a tough Saturday.
Storylines. The Troy quarterback room is the big question. Whoever plays will face a James Madison defense that allows just 18.2 points a game. On the other side, the Dukes have to replace Pettaway's carries, and Smith will be hunting Coleman all night.
Coleman has been careful with the football. Two interceptions in 212 attempts is a fine way to keep a team in every game.
Fun and true. This is the fifth all-time meeting, and James Madison leads 3-1, including 2-0 at home. The teams met twice in the FCS playoffs, in 1994 and 1999. James Madison won the 2025 Sun Belt Championship Game 31-14 over Troy and went on to the College Football Playoff. In 2023, the Dukes won 16-14.
