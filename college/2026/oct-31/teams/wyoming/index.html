Cowboys at the Campfire: Wyoming Rests Up on the Bye and Saddles Up for the Stretch Run

Meet Your Insider
Yep. I'm Colter Sprague, Wyoming insider. Grew up on a cattle ranch outside Laramie. I scout in a Carhartt and chart wind gusts. I travel and live with this team.

On the show, I count my words. Record's 31. My editor says a report needs more than 31. So this one's going to hurt a little. I'll make every word count.

Rules I ride by. Every number has a source. If I don't know, I say so. No tall tales. I respect every opponent, I celebrate our players as good young men, and I thank the Lord for big sky and Friday football. Good team.

The School in 60 Seconds
Wyoming plays in the Mountain West and has won 14 conference titles. Home is War Memorial Stadium in Laramie. CFBD lists it as built in 1950 with 29,181 seats, at an elevation of about 2,200 meters. The air up there is lighter. Visitors notice.

Wyoming's known as a stepping-stone job. Bowden Wyatt, Bob Devaney, Fred Akers, Pat Dye, Dennis Erickson and Joe Tiller all coached here right before they made their names at bigger programs. Good coaches come through Laramie. Some of us stay.

Last five seasons: 7-6, 7-6, 9-4, 3-9, 4-8. Fight song's "Ragtime Cowboy Joe," per Wikipedia's infobox. I hum it while I fix fence.

The Coach and the Scheme
Jay Sawvel runs the outfit. Wikipedia says he's been head coach since 2024, and CFBD records his hire date as Dec. 6, 2023. Before that he was Wyoming's defensive coordinator and safeties coach from 2020 to 2023. He played at Mount Union from 1989 to 1993. He's also coached at Wake Forest, Minnesota, Northern Illinois, Southern Illinois, Ferris State, Notre Dame and Eastern Kentucky. Lot of miles on that truck.

Players to Know

The Conference Picture
Wyoming is 5-3 overall and 4-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 1st in Mountain West.
The season so far: L 13-35 at Colorado State Rams (Sept. 5), W 21-13 vs Northern Colorado Bears (Sept. 12), L 10-24 at Central Michigan Chippewas (Sept. 19), W 27-10 vs Hawai'i Rainbow Warriors (Sept. 26), L 0-28 at North Dakota State Bison (Oct. 3), W 16-13 at San José State Spartans (Oct. 9), W 41-23 vs Northern Illinois Huskies (Oct. 17), W 28-14 vs Air Force Falcons (Oct. 23).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for Wyoming this week. We're off from Tuesday, Oct. 27, through Saturday, Oct. 31, so the rest of this report is about where we stand and what's next.
Last time out. We beat the Air Force Falcons 28-14 at home on Oct. 23. Tyler Hughes went 10 of 17 for 113 yards, with 2 touchdowns. He also ran 12 times for 146 yards, with 1 touchdown. Jake Wilson caught 2 balls for 36 yards, with 1 touchdown.
Up next. We play the UNLV Rebels on the road on Saturday, Nov. 7, 10:30 p.m. ET, at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas.
