Hoosier Hysteria, Football Edition: Indiana Hosts Minnesota and Floyd's Keepers in Bloomington

Meet Your Insider
Well hello, Hoosier Nation, and welcome! I'm Mallory Penhallow, former Miss Monroe County runner-up, and these days I run a 40-tab efficiency model on everything this football team does. I travel with the team, I live with the numbers, and yes, the clicker is rhinestone. My pageant answer to every question is the same: "It's the explosives, darling." Each week I give one opponent my honorary Miss Congeniality sash. It goes to whoever I roast the hardest, and I always do it with love. When we win, you get the full pageant wave. When we lose, it's "grace in defeat". and then I quietly open tab 41.

The School in 60 Seconds
Before Curt Cignetti showed up, Indiana had the most losses of any program in college football, 713 of them over 130-plus years. It had won three bowl games in its whole history, and the last one came in 1991. Then 2025 happened. The Hoosiers went 16-0 and beat Miami 27-21 at Hard Rock Stadium on Jan. 19, 2026, to win their first national championship. The last team to win 16 games without a loss was Yale in 1894. Quarterback Fernando Mendoza won the Heisman, and he scored the deciding touchdown himself on a 12-yard run on fourth-and-4. Cignetti's quote still sounds like an acceptance speech to me: "We won the national championship at Indiana University. It can be done."

The Coach and the Scheme
Cignetti was hired on Nov. 30, 2023. Part of his rebuild came from bringing 13 transfers with him from his old school, James Madison.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 33.6 points per game and allowing 12.0.

Players to Know

Indiana is 7-1 overall and 4-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 3rd in Big Ten. We're ranked No. 4 in ESPN's rankings this week.
The season so far: W 52-16 vs North Texas Mean Green (Sept. 5), W 55-0 vs Howard Bison (Sept. 12), W 38-0 vs Western Kentucky Hilltoppers (Sept. 19), W 29-23 vs Northwestern Wildcats (Sept. 25), W 47-15 at Rutgers Scarlet Knights (Oct. 3), W 20-17 at Nebraska Cornhuskers (Oct. 10), L 15-19 vs Ohio State Buckeyes (Oct. 17), W 13-6 (OT) at Michigan Wolverines (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Minnesota (5-2, 3-1 Big Ten) at No. 4 Indiana (7-1, 4-1 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, Memorial Stadium, Bloomington, Ind., CBS.
Last time out. Minnesota beat Iowa 13-10 at home on Oct. 24, on Homecoming, and took back Floyd of Rosedale, the bronze pig trophy introduced in 1935. Lindsey threw for 137 yards, and Tracy caught seven balls for 45 yards. The defense did the heavy lifting, holding Iowa to 10 points.
Indiana won 13-6 in overtime at Michigan on Oct. 24. Hoover went 8 of 18 for 81 yards, Richard ran 19 times for 68 yards, and the defense held Michigan to six points. It came one week after a 19-15 loss to Ohio State.
Players to watch. Indiana quarterback Josh Hoover has thrown the most passes in all eight games. He's 116 of 187 for 1,741 yards, 18 touchdowns and one interception. Turbo Richard leads the rushers with 711 yards and six scores, and Charlie Becker leads the receivers with 42 catches for 726 yards and eight touchdowns. On defense, Tobi Osunsanmi has nine sacks, and linebacker Isaiah Jones leads the team with 61 tackles.
Minnesota quarterback Drake Lindsey has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 141 of 222 for 1,472 yards, nine touchdowns and six interceptions. TJ Thomas leads the rushers with 325 yards, and Javon Tracy leads the receivers with 39 catches for 420 yards. Linebacker Maverick Baranowski leads the defense with 51 tackles, and TJ Bush Jr. has four sacks.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Hoover has thrown the most passes for Indiana in every game, and Lindsey has done the same for Minnesota, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Curt Cignetti was hired at Indiana on Nov. 30, 2023. In 2025, the Hoosiers went 16-0 and beat Miami 27-21 at Hard Rock Stadium on Jan. 19, 2026, to win their first national championship. This year's team allows only 12.0 points a game.
P.J. Fleck has led Minnesota since 2017, after coaching Western Michigan from 2013 to 2016. He played receiver at Northern Illinois and coached receivers for the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. His defense is the strength this year, stingy against the throw and good on third down.
Storylines. The matchup to watch is Becker and Hoover against a Minnesota defense that allows only 159.7 passing yards a game. On the other side, the Gophers need their big guys up front to open running room against an Indiana defense that allows very little on the ground. Minnesota won the two recent meetings in this series, 38-31 in 2018 and 35-14 in 2021, so the Gophers have some happy memories. Indiana wants to stay near the top of the Big Ten race.
Fun and true. Minnesota started playing football in 1882 and has been in the Big Ten since the league began in 1896 as the Western Conference. The Gophers claim seven national championships.
