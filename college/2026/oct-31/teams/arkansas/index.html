Hogs Call on Halloween Night: Arkansas Hosts Missouri in a Border Battle

Meet Your Insider
Woooo, Pig, here comes the data! I'm Bubba Fayette-Dorsey, and I'm your Arkansas insider. I'm a grade-school science teacher from up in the Ozarks, and I call the Hogs before every stat, even the bad ones. On the podcast, my segment is called Hog Call: I call the Hogs, then I read the stat. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, you won't hear it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Razorback Stadium in Fayetteville, built in 1938 with a listed capacity of 80,000. The playing surface is Frank Broyles Field, the band is the Best in Sight and Sound, and the fight song is "Arkansas Fight." The program claims a national title from 1964. The last five seasons went 9-4, 7-6, 4-8, 7-6 and 2-10, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about Calling the Hogs. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Ryan Silverfield became the 35th head coach in Arkansas history, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Nov. 30, 2025. He coached Memphis against Missouri in 2023, so he knows the folks coming to town this week.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 21.0 points per game and allowing 26.7.

Players to Know
QB KJ Jackson: 131 of 215, 1,444 yards, 7 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Braylen Russell: 205 yards on 58 carries, 2 touchdowns.
WR Chris Marshall: 43 catches, 633 yards, 2 touchdowns.
DL Quincy Rhodes Jr.: 3 sacks, the team lead.
LB Ja'Quavion Smith: a team-high 48 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Arkansas is 3-4 overall and 1-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 14th in SEC.
The season so far: W 31-14 vs North Alabama Lions (Sept. 5), L 10-43 at Utah Utes (Sept. 12), L 17-45 vs Georgia Bulldogs (Sept. 19), W 34-6 vs Tulsa Golden Hurricane (Sept. 26), L 7-34 at Texas A&M Aggies (Oct. 3), L 17-24 vs Tennessee Volunteers (Oct. 10), W 31-21 at Vanderbilt Commodores (Oct. 17).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
No. 23 Missouri (5-2, 2-2 SEC) at Arkansas (3-4, 1-3 SEC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 7:45 p.m. ET (6:45 p.m. Central), Donald W. Reynolds Razorback Stadium, Fayetteville, Ark., SEC Network.
Last time out. Both teams had the week off on Oct. 24.
Missouri's last game was a 31-3 loss at Ole Miss on Oct. 17. Simmons threw for 339 yards, and Donovan Olugbode caught nine passes for 127 yards. The Tigers got into the red zone three times and came away with three points.
Arkansas' last game was a 31-21 win at Vanderbilt on Oct. 17, its first SEC win in two years. Jackson went 21 of 31 for 251 yards and ran for a touchdown. Antonio Jordan, a Warren, Ark., native, had career highs with five catches for 74 yards.
Players to watch. Missouri quarterback Austin Simmons, an Ole Miss transfer, is 132 of 209 for 1,773 yards, 14 touchdowns and two interceptions. Running back Jamal Roberts leads the SEC in rushing with 867 yards and eight touchdowns on 167 carries. Wide receiver Cayden Lee has 47 catches for 678 yards and five touchdowns. On defense, Daeden Hopkins has seven sacks, and linebacker Nicholas Rodriguez has 76 tackles.
Arkansas quarterback KJ Jackson is 131 of 215 for 1,444 yards, seven touchdowns and four interceptions. Wide receiver Chris Marshall has 43 catches for 633 yards, second in the SEC in both. Braylen Russell leads the running backs with 205 yards. On defense, linebacker Ja'Quavion Smith has 48 tackles, and Quincy Rhodes Jr. has three sacks.
Availability. No official SEC availability report had been posted as of Tuesday. Silverfield said Marshall, who hurt his leg at Vanderbilt, is expected to play. Arkansas center Caden Kitler, a team captain, is listed as the starter, though Kash Courtney started at Vanderbilt. Missouri coach Eli Drinkwitz said wide receiver Blair is back from a hamstring injury and will be fully available. Missouri running back Ahmad Hardy has been sidelined since an offseason accident.
The coaches. Drinkwitz grew up in Alma, Ark., and has led Missouri since 2020. "I think the SEC is better when Arkansas is good," he said last fall.
Ryan Silverfield became the 35th head coach in Arkansas history the morning after last year's game. He coached Memphis against Missouri in 2023. "We know that they're going to be sound and well-coached," he said. He praised the Missouri wide zone running game and said it gets stronger in the fourth quarter.
Storylines. Simmons faced his old school, Ole Miss, two weeks ago, and he keeps it simple. "It's just another game for me," he said. Roberts has carried the load with Hardy out. For Arkansas, Jordan showed he can make plays when Marshall is out, and now the Hogs may have both.
Fun and true. The winner takes home the rivalry trophy. Missouri won the last four meetings, including 31-17 last year in Fayetteville. Arkansas last won in 2021, 34-17. Eight of the 16 games in the series were decided by one score. Only one first-year Arkansas coach has beaten Missouri: Glen Rose, 7-6 in St. Louis in 1944.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
