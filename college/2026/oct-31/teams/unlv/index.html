Lights On in Las Vegas: UNLV Hosts Northern Illinois on Halloween Night

Meet Your Insider
And that's the show, folks! I'm Vegas Monroe-Kalani, and I'm your UNLV insider. I'm an ex-headliner dancer who choreographs blitz packages and can count every snap like a showrunner counts cues. Fair warning: I call every game a show, and I review the opening act. On the podcast, that segment is called Opening Act. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, you won't hear it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas, built in 2020 with a listed capacity of 65,000. The fight song is "Win with the Rebels." The last five seasons went 2-10, 5-7, 9-5, 11-3 and 10-4, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about the Fremont Cannon. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Dan Mullen leads the Rebels, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 12, 2024.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 24.1 points per game and allowing 23.7.

Players to Know
QB Jackson Arnold: 108 of 182, 1,386 yards, 10 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB Jai'Den Thomas: 543 yards on 112 carries, 3 touchdowns.
WR Kayden McGee: 16 catches, 330 yards, 4 touchdowns.
DB Tre Fulton: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
S Jake Pope: a team-high 63 tackles.

The Conference Picture
UNLV is 4-3 overall and 2-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 3rd in Mountain West.
The season so far: L 21-27 vs Memphis Tigers (Aug. 29), W 21-6 at Hawai'i Rainbow Warriors (Sept. 5), L 6-44 at North Texas Mean Green (Sept. 12), W 38-10 at Akron Zips (Sept. 26), W 39-31 vs California Golden Bears (Oct. 3), W 13-7 vs North Dakota State Bison (Oct. 10), L 31-41 at Air Force Falcons (Oct. 17).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Northern Illinois (0-7, 0-3 Mountain West) at UNLV (4-3, 2-1 Mountain West). Saturday, Oct. 31, 10:30 p.m. ET (7:30 p.m. Pacific), Allegiant Stadium, Las Vegas, CBSSN.
Last time out. Northern Illinois lost 56-21 at home to Hawai'i on Oct. 24. The game was tied at 14 in the second quarter before Hawai'i scored 35 points in a row. Rogers caught eight balls for 115 yards and two touchdowns, of 49 and 25 yards. Dickens threw for 171 yards, and Johnson ran for 63.
UNLV had an open date on Oct. 24, its second bye of the season. The Rebels' last game was a 41-31 loss at Air Force on Oct. 17. McGee caught five balls for 99 yards and two touchdowns that day, and Arnold ran for a score.
Players to watch. Northern Illinois quarterback Taron Dickens is 138 of 210 for 1,364 yards, six touchdowns and five interceptions. Running back Telly Johnson Jr. has 95 carries for 502 yards and four touchdowns. Wide receiver DeAree Rogers leads the team with 57 catches for 537 yards and four touchdowns. On defense, Jack Murray has 49 tackles and Avery Jones has two sacks.
UNLV quarterback Jackson Arnold is 108 of 182 for 1,386 yards, 10 touchdowns and two interceptions. The school says he leads the league with 16 total touchdowns, 10 throwing and six running. Running back Jai'Den Thomas has 112 carries for 543 yards and three touchdowns. Wide receiver Kayden McGee has 16 catches for 330 yards and four touchdowns. Safety Jake Pope leads the defense with 63 tackles, Tre Fulton has 3.5 sacks and Mumu Bin-Wahad has three interceptions.
Availability. The official Mountain West availability report hadn't been posted as of Tuesday and usually comes out on game day. Unofficial notes from mid-October list UNLV receiver DeAngelo Irvin Jr. as out. McGee was listed as questionable that week but played at Air Force. Dickens was listed as questionable before the Hawai'i game and played.
The coaches. Rob Harley was the Huskies' defensive coordinator until he was promoted to interim head coach when Thomas Hammock resigned on Feb. 18 to return to the NFL. After the Hawai'i game, Harley said, "We have to find a way, and it starts with me first, to find a way to increase the consistency with the base stuff that we do."
Dan Mullen is in his second season at UNLV after being hired on Dec. 12, 2024. The Rebels are 7-2 at home under Mullen. This week he talked about tackling and getting healthy after the break.
Storylines. Rogers is on a roll for the Huskies. Arnold, an Auburn transfer, didn't throw his first interception of the season until the North Dakota State game on Oct. 10. That came after 142 straight throws without one.
Fun and true. The Huskies have won all five meetings with UNLV, the latest 38-30 in 2015. UNLV is still looking for its first win in the series.
UNLV says it has already played in five different time zones this season and is the only school in the top level of college football scheduled to do that in 2026. The Rebels are 2-7 all-time on Halloween, and their last Halloween win was 35-31 over San Jose State in 1992.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Nola Brandvik, Northern Illinois's insider. Here's what I said:
"The show starts Saturday night. Welcome to Las Vegas, Nola."
"Saturday night, under the lights, on Halloween. That's my kind of opening act."
And the last word from Nola: "Knee-high by the Fourth of July. Or by Halloween. I'll take either."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
UNLV is throwing a Halloween party at Allegiant Stadium. The Rebel Village Fan Zone in Lot C is free, and a band called The Lucky Devils plays before the game.
Halloween is also Nevada Day, the day Nevada became the 36th state in 1864. UNLV last played on Halloween in 2020, when it hosted Nevada in the first game ever played at Allegiant Stadium.
Northern Illinois is in its first season in the Mountain West as a football-only member, so this trip is all new.

My call: UNLV Rebels 35, Northern Illinois Huskies 15.
