Fire Up the Grill: The Wolfpack Hosts Cal for a Halloween Noon Cookout in Raleigh

Meet Your Insider
Hey there, Pack! I'm Rutherford Pegram, but everybody calls me Ruff, and I'm your NC State insider. I'm a Wake County native and an engineer, and I grill at the same Carter-Finley lot every home game. I explain schemes with grill thermometers. A defense running at 225 degrees? That's low and slow. An offense at 450? That's a sear.

Here's my promise. I'm loud, I'm warm and I'm gloriously dramatic, but I don't cook the numbers. Every stat here has a source. If I don't know something, I'll tell you the thermometer's still reading. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I thank God for good friends, good food and good football. I howl once per segment, no more, no less. Wolfpack, howl!

The School in 60 Seconds
NC State plays in the ACC and was a founding member in 1953, after years in the Southern Conference. The Wolfpack has won seven conference championships and played in 34 bowl games, winning 17. Since 1966, home has been Carter-Finley Stadium, the largest college football stadium in North Carolina. CFBD lists it at 58,000 seats. In 2010 the school brought back a live mascot, a Tamaskan dog named Tuffy. Good boy.

The last five seasons have been steady heat: 9-3, 8-5, 9-4, 6-7 and 8-5. Four winning seasons in five years. The grill stays lit in Raleigh.

The Coach and the Scheme
Dave Doeren led NC State starting in 2013. CFBD records his hire date as Dec. 1, 2012. Before Raleigh he was head coach at Northern Illinois in 2011 and 2012. The school relieved Doeren of his duties after the loss at Stanford, and running backs coach and special teams coordinator Todd Goebbel will serve as interim head coach for the rest of the season. He has promised a team that will be "hard, tough and together." We thank Coach Doeren for every Saturday he gave this program.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 32.3 points per game and allowing 28.1.

The Conference Picture
NC State is 3-4 overall and 1-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 14th in ACC.
The season so far: L 8-34 at Virginia Cavaliers (Aug. 29), W 73-0 vs Richmond Spiders (Sept. 11), L 31-35 at Vanderbilt Commodores (Sept. 19), W 41-31 vs App State Mountaineers (Sept. 26), W 31-28 vs Louisville Cardinals (Oct. 3), L 14-38 vs Wake Forest Demon Deacons (Oct. 10), L 28-31 at Stanford Cardinal (Oct. 23).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
California (3-5, 2-3 ACC) at NC State (3-4, 1-3 ACC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12 p.m. ET, Carter-Finley Stadium, Raleigh, N.C., ACC Network.
Last time out. Cal lost 59-20 at SMU on Oct. 24. Sagapolutele went 27 of 37 for 265 yards and a touchdown, and Hendricks caught seven passes for 83 yards and a score.
NC State lost 31-28 at Stanford on Oct. 23. Bailey went 17 of 30 for 256 yards and two touchdowns and was also the team's leading rusher, with 59 yards on nine carries. Deandre Desinor caught three passes for 79 yards and a touchdown.
Players to watch. Cal quarterback Jaron-Keawe Sagapolutele is 149 of 219 for 1,555 yards, 10 touchdowns and five interceptions. Jackson Brousseau threw the most passes in three straight games in October, and Sagapolutele was back on top at SMU. Wide receiver Chase Hendricks leads the Bears with 54 catches for 498 yards and three scores. Adam Mohammed leads the rushers with 336 yards. On defense, defensive back Isaiah Crosby has 46 tackles and linebacker JD McKinley has three sacks.
NC State quarterback CJ Bailey is 143 of 219 for 1,763 yards, 11 touchdowns and four interceptions, and he has thrown the most passes in every Wolfpack game this season. Wide receiver Keenan Jackson has 30 catches for 456 yards and six touchdowns. Jayden Scott leads the rushers with 358 yards and four scores. Linebacker Popo Aguirre leads the team with 60 tackles, and linebacker Elijah Satchell has four sacks.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and neither school's box scores mark starters. Bailey has thrown the most passes for NC State in all seven games, and Sagapolutele did for Cal last week, so watch the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. NC State has new leadership this week. The school relieved Dave Doeren of his duties after the loss at Stanford, ending a run that began in 2013, and running backs coach and special teams coordinator Todd Goebbel will serve as interim head coach for the rest of the season. Saturday is his first game in charge, and he has promised a team that will be "hard, tough and together." This year's offense scores 32.3 points a game, including a 73-0 win over Richmond and a 31-28 win over Louisville.
Tosh Lupoi was named the 35th head coach in Cal history on Dec. 4, 2025, replacing Justin Wilcox. His first Cal team picked up ACC wins over Syracuse and Virginia Tech, both by one score.
Storylines. The matchup to circle is Bailey and Jackson against a Cal defense that allows 31.6 points a game. Jackson has six touchdown catches, the most of anyone on either roster. On the other side, Hendricks has caught 54 passes, and the Bears like to throw it to him early and often. Cal gains 3.1 yards a carry, so the Wolfpack big guys up front will try to make the Bears one-dimensional.
