Skyline Chili and No. 6 Utah: Cincinnati Hosts the Utes on Halloween

Meet Your Insider
Hello, Bearcats, and welcome! I'm Lionel Abernathy, but everybody calls me Skyline, for reasons that will become obvious very quickly. I'm an engineering co-op kid, I chart everything, and I rank it all in Cincinnati chili ways. A three-way is respectable. A four-way is impressive. A five-way, with onions AND beans, is reserved for greatness. That goes for quarterbacks, defensive fronts and, yes, coaches' haircuts. When we win, I upgrade the team on air, three-way to five-way, no hesitation. When we lose, I downgrade to "plain spaghetti" and stare quietly into the distance. Every week I'll bring you the players, the coaches and the matchup, plus a food tangent that somehow loops back into a very sharp stat. I travel with this team and I live with this team. That's a five-way take.

The School in 60 Seconds
Cincinnati has played football on the Nippert Stadium site since 1901, which makes it the second-oldest playing site in college football. That's history with extra cheese. In 2021 the Bearcats became the first team from outside the Power Five to make the College Football Playoff, where they played Alabama in the Cotton Bowl. Now we're in the Big 12. Nippert is listed at 40,000 seats, and every one of them should be full.

The Coach and the Scheme
Scott Satterfield became the 43rd head coach in program history on Dec. 5, 2022.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 35.4 points per game and allowing 26.4.

Players to Know
QB JC French IV: 127 of 183, 1,510 yards, 13 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB Cole Tabb: 621 yards on 65 carries, 9 touchdowns.
WR JV Gibson: 29 catches, 385 yards, 4 touchdowns.
Defender Patrick Bauer: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Jonathan Thompson: a team-high 60 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Cincinnati is 5-2 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 7th in Big 12.
The season so far: W 34-15 vs Boston College Eagles (Sept. 5), W 62-10 vs Western Carolina Catamounts (Sept. 12), W 35-31 vs Miami (OH) RedHawks (Sept. 19), W 31-26 vs Kansas State Wildcats (Sept. 26), L 7-34 at Arizona Wildcats (Oct. 3), L 42-45 at West Virginia Mountaineers (Oct. 17), W 37-24 vs Texas Tech Red Raiders (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
No. 6 Utah (7-0, 4-0 Big 12) at Cincinnati (5-2, 2-2 Big 12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:45 p.m. ET, Nippert Stadium, Cincinnati, ESPN.
Last time out. Utah beat Houston 21-13 at home on Oct. 24, the Utes' first win ever over the Cougars. Dampier threw for 204 yards and ran for 150 more with two touchdowns.
Cincinnati beat No. 8 Texas Tech 37-24 on Homecoming in front of a sold-out Nippert crowd. French threw four touchdowns, and Tabb ran 16 times for 173 yards and a score. It was only the third top-10 win in Nippert Stadium history.
Players to watch. Utah quarterback Devon Dampier has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 136 of 198 for 1,616 yards, 15 touchdowns and six interceptions, and he ran for 150 yards and two scores last week. Wayshawn Parker leads the rushers with 644 yards and eight touchdowns, and Braden Pegan leads the receivers with 34 catches for 459 yards and six scores. Linebacker Trey Reynolds leads the defense with 44 tackles, and Karson Kaufusi has two sacks.
Cincinnati quarterback JC French IV has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 127 of 183 for 1,510 yards, 13 touchdowns and only two interceptions. Cole Tabb leads the rushers with 621 yards and nine scores on just 65 carries, and JV Gibson leads the receivers with 29 catches for 385 yards. Linebacker Jonathan Thompson leads the defense with 60 tackles, and Patrick Bauer has three and a half sacks.
Availability. The official Big 12 availability report hasn't been posted yet. Utah coach Morgan Scalley said starting offensive lineman Kelvin Obot, who was hurt at Colorado and missed the Houston game, has no timetable and is getting a second opinion. Cincinnati coach Scott Satterfield said the team is close to getting starting nose tackle Josh Hough back after he missed last week, and starting center Taran Tyo, who left last week's game, likely won't have an answer until late in the week. Check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Morgan Scalley is in his first season as Utah's head coach after taking over in December 2025. He played defensive back for the Utes from 2001 to 2004, joined the staff in 2007 and spent 10 seasons as defensive coordinator. After the Colorado win he said, "It's not always pretty, but we did enough."
Scott Satterfield became Cincinnati's 43rd head coach on Dec. 5, 2022. His offense runs the ball well and almost never gives it away.
Storylines. The matchup to watch is Tabb and the Cincinnati big guys up front against a Utah defense that allows only 14.6 points a game. On the other side, Dampier and Parker face a Bearcats defense that just slowed down Texas Tech. These teams met for the first time last season, on Nov. 1 in Salt Lake City, where College GameDay was in town, and Utah won 45-14.
Fun and true. Utah football began in 1892 and has played on the current site of Rice-Eccles Stadium since 1927. The Utes have won two BCS bowls, the 2005 Fiesta Bowl and the 2009 Sugar Bowl. Cincinnati became the first team from outside the Power Five to make the College Football Playoff in the 2021 season, when the Bearcats played Alabama in the Cotton Bowl.
