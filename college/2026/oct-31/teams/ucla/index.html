Storyboards in the Rose Bowl: No. 7 UCLA Welcomes Nevada for a Halloween Feature

Meet Your Insider
Hello, darlings! I'm Kensington Rhee, Kenzie to everyone who matters, and I'm your UCLA insider. I'm a Westwood film-school producer, I storyboard every drive, and I travel and live with this team. Every game is a movie pitch to me.

Here's my promise. Glam, yes. Fiction, never. Every number in this report has a source, like a properly cleared script. If I don't know something, I'll say it's still in development. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I'm grateful to God for every sunny Saturday in Pasadena. And. scene.

The School in 60 Seconds
UCLA plays its home games at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena. CFBD lists 88,565 seats. The Bruins now play in the Big Ten. UCLA has been ranked in the AP top 10 at least once in every decade since the poll began in the 1930s. Under Red Sanders in the 1950s, UCLA won the 1954 Coaches' Poll national championship and three conference titles. Under Terry Donahue in the 1980s and 1990s, the Bruins went 151-74-8 with an NCAA-record eight straight bowl wins.

The recent box office: 8-4, 9-4, 8-5, then 5-7 and 3-9.

The Coach and the Scheme
Bob Chesney is the first-year head coach. CFBD lists his hire date as Dec. 6, 2025. He's a Dickinson College graduate who has been head coach at Salve Regina, Assumption, Holy Cross and James Madison. At Holy Cross he won five straight Patriot League titles.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 34.4 points per game and allowing 15.3.

Players to Know
QB Nico Iamaleava: 72 of 116, 982 yards, 4 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Wayne Knight: 627 yards on 99 carries, 12 touchdowns.
WR Semaj Morgan: 31 catches, 383 yards, 2 touchdowns.
LB Sammy Omosigho: 5.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Jalen Woods: a team-high 59 tackles.
Nico Iamaleava is out for the season after a knee injury in the Oregon game, and we're praying for a full recovery.

The Conference Picture
UCLA is 7-0 overall and 5-0 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 1st in Big Ten. We're ranked No. 7 in ESPN's rankings this week.
The season so far: W 45-24 at California Golden Bears (Sept. 5), W 28-10 vs San Diego State Aztecs (Sept. 12), W 52-38 vs Purdue Boilermakers (Sept. 19), W 54-3 at Maryland Terrapins (Sept. 26), W 17-15 at Oregon Ducks (Oct. 10), W 17-7 vs Wisconsin Badgers (Oct. 17), W 28-10 vs Michigan State Spartans (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Nevada (3-4, 2-2 Mountain West) at No. 7 UCLA (7-0, 5-0 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 4:15 p.m. ET, Rose Bowl, Pasadena, Calif., BTN.
Last time out. UCLA beat Michigan State 28-10 on Homecoming at the Rose Bowl, where the Bruins honored their 1966 Rose Bowl team. Anthony Woods ran 14 times for 112 yards and a touchdown, and Brian Rowe Jr. caught three balls for 74 yards and a score.
Nevada beat San José State 28-13 on Homecoming at Mackay Stadium. Duncan threw two touchdowns, and Marshaun Brown caught five balls for 52 yards and a score.
Players to watch. UCLA quarterback Ty Dieffenbach has thrown the most passes in the last two games. He went 22 of 33 for 264 yards in his first start against Wisconsin and 18 of 31 for 198 yards and a touchdown against Michigan State. Wayne Knight leads the rushers with 627 yards and 12 touchdowns, and Semaj Morgan leads the receivers with 31 catches for 383 yards. Linebacker Sammy Omosigho has five and a half sacks, and Jalen Woods leads the defense with 59 tackles.
Nevada's Luke Duncan threw the most passes last week, going 18 of 29 for 178 yards and two touchdowns with no turnovers. Carter Jones leads the team with 654 passing yards. Nevada's top running back has 582 yards and five touchdowns, and Gary Givens III leads the receivers with 19 catches for 305 yards. Linebacker EJ Smith leads the defense with 76 tackles, and Dylan LaBarbera has four sacks.
Availability. The official availability reports haven't been posted yet. UCLA quarterback Nico Iamaleava is out for the season after a knee injury in the Oregon game. For Nevada, starting quarterback Carter Jones (arm) and AJ Bianco (ankle) both missed last week, and coach Jeff Choate said it was too early to say who will start. Check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Bob Chesney is in his first season at UCLA, with a hire date of Dec. 6, 2025. He's a Dickinson College graduate who was head coach at Salve Regina, Assumption, Holy Cross and James Madison, and at Holy Cross he won five straight Patriot League titles. He's the first UCLA coach to begin his tenure 6-0, and the Bruins have kept on winning.
Jeff Choate was hired at Nevada on Dec. 4, 2023. This week he said his goal is to keep the game close: "Let's just drag this thing into deep water and find out who's gonna swim in the fourth quarter."
Storylines. The matchup to watch is Nevada's run game against a UCLA defense that allows 15.3 points a game. Duncan, if he plays, has a story all his own: he spent his first three college seasons at UCLA. On the other side, Knight and UCLA's big guys up front face a Nevada defense that has had trouble stopping the run. This is just the 10th time Nevada has played a top-10 team since moving up to the top level of college football.
