Jersey Proud on Halloween: The Scarlet Knights Welcome Michigan to Piscataway

Meet Your Insider
Yo, Rutgers! How you doin'? Welcome! Vinny Scolari here, your Rutgers insider, born and raised off Exit 9 of the New Jersey Turnpike. I give directions by Turnpike exit for everything, and I mean everything. Where's the stadium? Exit 9. How am I feeling after a loss? Somewhere around Exit 7A. I grade every Big Ten trench battle like a boardwalk arm-wrestle: who pushed in, who flinched, who walked away with the stuffed animal. I travel with this team and I live with this team. When we win, I order a sub big enough for the whole studio. Six feet. Everybody eats. When we lose, I say "Fuhgeddaboudit". and then I can't. I really can't. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, Jersey-style. "Exit 9 never lies."

The School in 60 Seconds
Here's something every college football fan should know. The very first college football game was played right in New Brunswick on Nov. 6, 1869, and Rutgers beat Princeton 6-4. That's right: the birthplace of the whole sport is off Exit 9. Home now is SHI Stadium in Piscataway, listed at 52,454. The man running the show is Greg Schiano, who came back to coach Rutgers again in December 2019. The Scarlet Knights play in the Big Ten, and every week is a heavyweight fight.

The Coach and the Scheme
Schiano's second stint began with his return on Nov. 30, 2019.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 28.7 points per game and allowing 33.3.

Players to Know

RB Antwan Raymond: 648 yards on 123 carries, 6 touchdowns.
WR KJ Duff: 45 catches, 774 yards, 9 touchdowns.

The Conference Picture
Rutgers is 2-5 overall and 1-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 13th in Big Ten.
The season so far: L 21-37 vs Massachusetts Minutemen (Sept. 3), L 21-28 at Boston College Eagles (Sept. 11), L 35-42 vs USC Trojans (Sept. 19), W 58-7 vs Howard Bison (Sept. 25), L 15-47 vs Indiana Hoosiers (Oct. 3), W 37-30 at Maryland Terrapins (Oct. 17), L 14-42 at Northwestern Wildcats (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Michigan (4-3, 1-3 Big Ten) at Rutgers (2-5, 1-3 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12:30 p.m. ET, SHI Stadium, Piscataway, N.J., BTN.
Last time out. Michigan lost 13-6 in overtime to Indiana at home on Oct. 24. Carr was 20 of 32 for 167 yards, and Buchanan caught six balls for 57 yards.
Rutgers lost 42-14 at Northwestern on Oct. 24. Raymond was the bright spot with 18 carries for 150 yards and a touchdown.
Players to watch. Michigan quarterback Bryce Underwood leads the team in passing at 72 of 124 for 924 yards, five touchdowns and six interceptions, though Tommy Carr threw the most passes against Indiana last week. Running back Jordan Marshall leads the rushers with 319 yards and four touchdowns. JJ Buchanan is the top receiver with 33 catches for 539 yards and four scores. On defense, linebacker Troy Bowles has 55 tackles and end John Henry Daley has four sacks.
Rutgers quarterback Dylan Lonergan leads the team at 74 of 127 for 1,066 yards, 10 touchdowns and six interceptions. AJ Surace threw the most passes against Northwestern last week. Running back Antwan Raymond has 648 yards and six touchdowns, and receiver KJ Duff has been a highlight machine with 45 catches for 774 yards and nine touchdowns. Defensive back Chris Joines leads the tacklers with 34, and Rondo Porter has 2.5 sacks.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Underwood threw the most passes in Michigan's first six games and Carr did against Indiana, while Lonergan and Surace have traded that role for Rutgers, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Kyle Whittingham was named Michigan's 22nd head coach on Dec. 26, 2025, after 21 seasons at Utah, where he went 177-88 with eight seasons of 10 or more wins. His Wolverines are built on defense, allowing 15.7 points a game.
Greg Schiano returned to Rutgers on Nov. 30, 2019, for his second stint. His team is the least penalized in FBS at 28.7 penalty yards a game.
Storylines. The big story is Raymond against a Michigan defense that allows only 2.8 yards a carry. Something has to give. On the other side, Buchanan and the Michigan offense want to get rolling after a long day against Indiana. Michigan's big guys up front will try to push around a Rutgers defense that gives up 5.8 yards a carry. And Duff will be looking for touchdown number 10.
Fun and true. The very first college football game was played in New Brunswick on Nov. 6, 1869, and Rutgers beat Princeton 6-4. Michigan played its first game on May 30, 1879, and has more wins than any program in college football. Three Wolverines have won the Heisman: Tom Harmon in 1940, Desmond Howard in 1991 and Charles Woodson in 1997.
