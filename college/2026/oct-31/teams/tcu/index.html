Frogs Ready for the Cattle Drive: TCU Hosts Kansas and Its Hoops Math on Halloween

Meet Your Insider
Well, howdy, Frog fans, and welcome! I'm Ryder Galloway, your TCU insider. I'm a Stockyards cowboy who became a finance guy, so I wear boots with a quarter-zip and I explain defenses as cattle drives. You've got your point riders, your flank riders and your drag riders, and when everyone holds their spot, nobody gets loose. I travel with this team and I live with this team. Fair warning: if something goes wrong, anything at all, I will probably blame Dallas. Flat tire? Dallas. Missed field goal? Dallas. When we win, it's "Yeehaw!" and my hat goes flying. When we lose, I mutter about Dallas some more. And the best sound in football is me hollering "Yeehaw, that's a turnover!" Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, Fort Worth style.

The School in 60 Seconds
TCU has a national title on the wall. The 1938 Horned Frogs went 11-0, beat Carnegie Tech 15-7 in the Sugar Bowl and won the national championship. Quarterback Davey O'Brien won the Heisman Trophy that year. The 2010 team went 13-0 and won the Rose Bowl, and in 2022 Sonny Dykes led TCU all the way to the College Football Playoff national championship game. Home is Amon G. Carter Stadium in Fort Worth, listed at 46,000. Not one bit of that history happened in Dallas. And the rivalry with Baylor has a name now, the Revivalry, and we just won it in Waco. Ten more minutes on that and I'd be throwing my hat again.

The Coach and the Scheme
Sonny Dykes was hired on Nov. 29, 2021, and in his first season he took the Frogs to the national title game.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 23.4 points per game and allowing 13.9.

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Kansas (3-4, 1-3 Big 12) at TCU (3-4, 1-3 Big 12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, Amon G. Carter Stadium, Fort Worth, Texas, FS1.
Last time out. Kansas beat Baylor 25-24 on Homecoming in Lawrence on Oct. 24. Edwards carried 30 times for 156 yards, Marshall threw two touchdowns, and McMillan caught a scoring ball. It was KU's 114th Homecoming, and the parade on Jayhawk Boulevard was back for the first time in seven years.
TCU lost 24-20 to West Virginia at home on Oct. 24. Craig completed 33 of 47 for 372 yards and a touchdown, and Kari Ashley caught six balls for 118 yards. The week before, the Frogs won 17-6 at Baylor.
Players to watch. TCU quarterback Jaden Craig has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 162 of 240 for 2,066 yards, 11 touchdowns and six interceptions. Jeremy Payne leads the rushers with 429 yards, and Ed Small leads the receivers with 38 catches for 514 yards. On defense, Koron Hayward has 4.5 sacks, and linebacker Hudson Hooper leads the team with 41 tackles.
Kansas quarterback Isaiah Marshall has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 105 of 196 for 1,506 yards, 11 touchdowns and seven interceptions. Dylan Edwards leads the rushers with 531 yards and four scores, and Nik McMillan leads the receivers with 25 catches for 466 yards and five touchdowns. Linebacker Trey Lathan leads the defense with 48 tackles, and Leroy Harris III has 3.5 sacks.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Craig has thrown the most passes for TCU in every game, and Marshall has done the same for Kansas, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Sonny Dykes was hired at TCU on Nov. 29, 2021, and in his first season he took the Frogs to the national championship game. This year his defense is the story. TCU allows only 13.9 points a game, and every one of its losses has been close.
Lance Leipold has led Kansas since 2021. Before that he won six Division III national championships at Wisconsin-Whitewater and reached 100 wins faster than any coach in NCAA history, in his 106th game. His 2023 team went 9-4. This year his offense runs the ball well, behind Edwards and the big guys up front.
Storylines. The matchup to watch is Edwards against TCU's defense, which has been very hard to run on all season. On the other side, Craig has thrown the ball a lot, including 47 times last week, and Kansas will try to make him hurry. TCU won seven of the eight meetings from 2016 through 2025, including a 38-27 win in 2024, but Kansas won a 27-26 thriller in 2018.
Fun and true. TCU's 1938 team went 11-0, beat Carnegie Tech 15-7 in the Sugar Bowl and won the national title, and quarterback Davey O'Brien won the Heisman Trophy. The 2010 Frogs went 13-0 and won the Rose Bowl, and this year's team opened the season against North Carolina in Dublin, Ireland. Kansas football began in 1890, one of the oldest programs in the country.
