Cardinals Versus Cardinal: Louisville Hosts Stanford on Halloween

Meet Your Insider
Hold your hats! I'm Bianca Thornberry, and I'm your Louisville insider. I'm a Derby-party hostess who knows tempo stats as well as millinery. Fair warning: I rate every game's hat-worthiness. On the podcast, that segment is called Hat-Worthiness. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, you won't hear it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is L&N Federal Credit Union Stadium in Louisville, built in 1998 with a listed capacity of 60,000. The fight song is "Fight! UofL," and the band is the U of L Cardinal Marching Band, the Marching Cards. The last five seasons went 6-7, 8-5, 10-4, 9-4 and 9-4, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about the Cardinal Bird. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Jeff Brohm leads the Cardinals, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 7, 2022.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 38.7 points per game and allowing 27.4.

Players to Know
QB Lincoln Kienholz: 121 of 180, 1,844 yards, 10 touchdowns, 3 interceptions.
RB Isaac Brown: 568 yards on 83 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Lawayne McCoy: 25 catches, 513 yards, 4 touchdowns.
DL Clev Lubin: 5 sacks, the team lead.
LB T.J. Capers: a team-high 53 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Louisville is 4-3 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 4th in ACC.
The season so far: W 59-13 vs Villanova Wildcats (Sept. 11), W 41-31 vs SMU Mustangs (Sept. 19), L 27-30 vs Wake Forest Demon Deacons (Sept. 26), L 28-31 at NC State Wolfpack (Oct. 3), W 44-20 vs Florida State Seminoles (Oct. 9), W 34-26 at Syracuse Orange (Oct. 17).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Stanford (4-4, 2-3 ACC) at Louisville (4-3, 3-2 ACC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:15 p.m. ET, L&N Federal Credit Union Stadium, Louisville, Ky., ACC Network.
Last time out. Stanford beat NC State 31-28 at home on Friday, Oct. 23. Warren went 22 of 37 for 303 yards and four touchdowns, Ford ran 22 times for 150 yards, and High caught nine balls for 103 yards and a score. It was Stanford's second straight win, after a 34-7 victory over Elon.
Louisville had an open date. Its last game was a 34-26 win at Syracuse on Oct. 17, when Kienholz threw for 310 yards and ran for two touchdowns, and McCoy caught five balls for 93 yards.
Players to watch. Louisville quarterback Lincoln Kienholz has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 121 of 180 for 1,844 yards, 10 touchdowns and three interceptions. Isaac Brown leads the rushers with 568 yards and four scores, and Lawayne McCoy leads the receivers with 513 yards and four touchdowns. Linebacker T.J. Capers leads the defense with 53 tackles, Clev Lubin has five sacks, and defensive back Kaleb Beasley has five interceptions.
Stanford quarterback Davis Warren has thrown the most passes in all eight games. He's 163 of 271 for 1,933 yards, 11 touchdowns and two interceptions. Micah Ford leads the rushers with 679 yards and eight touchdowns, and Caden High leads the receivers with 30 catches for 440 yards. Safety Jay Green leads the team with 49 tackles.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Kienholz has thrown the most passes for Louisville in every game, and Warren has done the same for Stanford, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Jeff Brohm leads Louisville, and CollegeFootballData dates his hire to Dec. 7, 2022. His teams went 10-4, 9-4 and 9-4 the last three seasons. This year's Cardinals score 38.7 points a game, run the ball well, and have 12 takeaways against six giveaways.
Tavita Pritchard is in his first season as Stanford's head coach, and CFBD records his hire date as Nov. 28, 2025. He's a Stanford alum who played quarterback on the 2007 team that upset USC, and he coached quarterbacks for the Washington Commanders from 2023 to 2025.
Storylines. The matchup to watch is the Louisville ground game against a Stanford defense that has struggled to stop the run. Brown and Kienholz both carry the ball, and Louisville's big guys up front like to push people around. On the other side, Ford is coming off a 150-yard game, and Warren has thrown for more than 300 yards in three games. Stanford won the only recent meeting, 38-35 in 2024, so Louisville has a memory to fix.
Fun and true. Stanford has fielded teams nearly every year since 1892, and it played rugby instead of football from 1906 to 1917. The teams were known as the "Indians" from 1930 to 1972, and they've been the "Cardinal" since the 1982 season. A 1975 student vote to become the "Robber Barons" wasn't approved. Stanford Stadium was built in 2006 and seats 50,424.

My call: Louisville Cardinals 42, Stanford Cardinal 19.
