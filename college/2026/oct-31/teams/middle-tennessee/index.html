Blue Raiders at Floyd: Middle Tennessee Hosts Kennesaw State on Wednesday Night

Meet Your Insider
Well, hey there, sugar. Come in, sit down, and have a biscuit. I'm Opal Stonebriar, your Middle Tennessee insider. Folks call me the front-row grandma of Murfreesboro. I'm 34. It's a spiritual title.

Here's how this works. I will feed you, I will love on these boys like they're my own, and if you say something about my team that isn't true, I will correct you sweetly and with a citation. I don't make up numbers, I don't make up injuries, and when I don't know something I'll say, "Honey, I don't know yet." Murfreesboro is a city that grew up fast, and so did our program. Come sit with us, and you'll leave a Blue Raider. I promise.

The School in 60 Seconds
Middle Tennessee plays FBS football in Conference USA, out of Murfreesboro, Tennessee. The Blue Raiders have played in 14 bowl games and seven I-AA playoffs. Home is Johnny "Red" Floyd Stadium, which opened in 1933. The sources disagree on its size. CFBD lists a capacity of 30,788, and Wikipedia lists 27,303. I'm noting both until I check the media guide.

The last five seasons have been a climb and then a dip: 7-6 in 2021, 8-5 in 2022, 4-8 in 2023, and 3-9 in both 2024 and 2025.

The Coach and the Scheme
Derek Mason was hired on Dec. 6, 2023, as the 15th head coach in program history. Before Murfreesboro, he was head coach at Vanderbilt from 2014 to 2020.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 23.3 points per game and allowing 27.6.

The Conference Picture
Middle Tennessee is 4-3 overall and 2-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 3rd in CUSA.
The season so far: W 38-14 vs Murray State Racers (Sept. 5), L 26-28 at Marshall Thundering Herd (Sept. 12), W 27-20 vs Nevada Wolf Pack (Sept. 19), L 13-23 at Jacksonville State Gamecocks (Sept. 26), L 0-55 at Kansas Jayhawks (Oct. 3), W 33-30 vs Delaware Blue Hens (Oct. 13), W 26-23 at Florida International Panthers (Oct. 20).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Kennesaw State (1-6, 0-3 CUSA) at Middle Tennessee (4-3, 2-1 CUSA). Wednesday, Oct. 28, 7 p.m. ET, Johnny "Red" Floyd Stadium, Murfreesboro, Tenn., CBS Sports Network.
Last time out. Middle Tennessee won 26-23 at FIU on Oct. 20. Gagliano went 16 of 27 for 200 yards and a touchdown, and Terrence Brown ran 18 times for 158 yards and a score. Lacy caught six passes for 68 yards. That came a week after a 33-30 home win over Delaware.
Kennesaw State lost 34-6 to Liberty on Oct. 22. Collins went 15 of 26 for 124 yards, and Munroe caught three passes for 53 yards. Linebacker Baron Hopson made a season-high 12 tackles. Late in the fourth quarter, coach Jerry Mack gave Landon Varnes some snaps at quarterback.
Players to watch. Middle Tennessee quarterback Roman Gagliano is 126 of 215 for 1,404 yards, 12 touchdowns and just two interceptions. Antonio Martin leads the rushers with 480 yards, and wide receiver Cam'ron Lacy has 41 catches for 437 yards and four scores. Linebacker Korey Smith leads the defense with 66 tackles.
Kennesaw State's Rickie Collins does a little of everything. He is 144 of 210 for 1,426 yards with four touchdowns, and he is also the Owls' leading rusher with 295 yards and five touchdowns. Wide receiver Brayden Munroe has 22 catches for 352 yards. Linebacker Tywon Christopher leads the team with 54 tackles, and defensive lineman Ugonna Nwoha has three sacks.
Availability. Conference USA has not posted an availability report for this game, and no injury news has surfaced for either starting quarterback, so watch the teams' pregame notes. Neither school's box scores mark a starter. Gagliano has thrown the most passes for Middle Tennessee in every game this season, and Collins has done the same for Kennesaw State.
The coaches. Derek Mason is in his third season at Middle Tennessee, after coaching Vanderbilt from 2014 to 2020. His Blue Raiders went 3-9 in both 2024 and 2025, so four wins already tops last season. Three of those wins came by seven points or fewer.
Jerry Mack is in his second season at Kennesaw State. He went 31-15 as head coach at North Carolina Central and later worked at Rice. His Owls are the defending Conference USA champions, and three of their six losses this year came by three points or fewer.
Storylines. Gagliano has been steady all season. He threw two touchdown passes in each of the first four games and three against Delaware.
In the Owls' 27-26 loss to Jacksonville State on Oct. 7, he threw for 293 yards. Hopson was a big part of last year's title team, with 138 tackles.
Fun and true. These two have split their last two meetings, both low scoring. Middle Tennessee won 14-5 in 2024, and Kennesaw State won 24-16 last September in Kennesaw. Both teams have also played Jacksonville State close this year: the Gamecocks beat Middle Tennessee 23-13 and Kennesaw State 27-26.
