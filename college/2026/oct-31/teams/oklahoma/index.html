Boomer Sooner on Halloween: Oklahoma Welcomes South Carolina to Norman

Meet Your Insider
Boomer! (You say Sooner.) Louder, Norman, I can't hear you over the Schooner wheels. I'm Savannah Redbird-Lowe, former Oklahoma rodeo princess, and I read a defense the way I used to read a barrel pattern: clean turn, tight pocket, no tipped barrels. "Sooner!" call-and-response at least once in every single report. That is my one rule, and I'm sweet about it. Every week you'll get the players, the matchups, the storylines and the history, told straight with a little Plains-state sass. When we win, you'll hear me holler "Boomer!" and wait for you. When we lose, I'll lift my chin and tell you we'll roll the Schooner again. Saddle up, y'all.

The School in 60 Seconds
Oklahoma football started in 1895. The program claims seven national championships (1950, 1955, 1956, 1974, 1975, 1985 and 2000), 50 conference titles and seven Heisman Trophy winners. The Sooners also hold the Division I record for consecutive wins, 47 straight, and Oklahoma is the only program where four different coaches have won more than 100 games. These days we're an SEC program. Home is Gaylord Family Oklahoma Memorial Stadium in Norman, listed at 86,112 seats in the CFBD venue file. Last season the Sooners went 10-3. That is a lot of trophies to dust, and I do it in boots.

The Coach and the Scheme
Brent Venables is the head coach. The CFBD coaches file dates his hire to Dec. 5, 2021, and he has led the program since 2022.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 21.6 points per game and allowing 17.7.

Players to Know
QB John Mateer: 136 of 204, 1,591 yards, 11 touchdowns, 7 interceptions.
RB Lloyd Avant: 322 yards on 73 carries, 2 touchdowns.
WR Isaiah Sategna III: 41 catches, 578 yards, 3 touchdowns.
DL Adepoju Adebawore: 4 sacks, the team lead.
LB Owen Heinecke: a team-high 53 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Oklahoma is 4-3 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 8th in SEC.
The season so far: W 51-0 vs UTEP Miners (Sept. 4), L 10-17 at Michigan Wolverines (Sept. 12), W 14-6 vs New Mexico Lobos (Sept. 19), L 13-41 at Georgia Bulldogs (Sept. 26), L 13-21 vs Kentucky Wildcats (Oct. 17), W 23-15 at Mississippi State Bulldogs (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
South Carolina (4-3, 2-3 SEC) at Oklahoma (4-3, 2-2 SEC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 4:15 p.m. ET (3:15 p.m. CT), Memorial Stadium, Norman, Oklahoma, SEC Network.
Last time out. South Carolina beat No. 11 Tennessee 27-24 at Williams-Brice Stadium, and it was a thriller. Trailing 24-17 late, the defense got a fourth-down stop. Sellers led a 61-yard drive and tied it with a run where he broke a number of tackles and reached the ball across the pylon. Then Dylan Stewart forced a fumble on a sack, and Tomiwa Durojaiye recovered it with 33 seconds left. Malik Orsan kicked a 48-yard field goal as time expired, and the fans stormed the field. Orsan was named SEC Special Teams Player of the Week, and center Nolan Hay was SEC Offensive Lineman of the Week.
Oklahoma won 23-15 at Mississippi State. Mateer went 22 of 29 for 283 yards and two touchdowns, and Sategna caught eight balls for 164 yards and a touchdown.
Players to watch. South Carolina quarterback LaNorris Sellers has completed 133 of 219 throws for 1,548 yards, with 13 touchdowns and five interceptions, and he runs it too. Running back Matt Fuller has 513 yards and five touchdowns on 90 carries. Wide receiver Mazeo Bennett Jr. leads the team with 31 catches for 324 yards. Linebacker Justin Okoronkwo leads the defense with 53 tackles.
Oklahoma quarterback John Mateer has completed 136 of 204 throws for 1,591 yards and 11 touchdowns. Wide receiver Isaiah Sategna III has 41 catches for 578 yards. Linebacker Owen Heinecke has 53 tackles, and defensive lineman Adepoju Adebawore has four sacks.
Availability. The first official SEC availability report for this game was due Wednesday. Oklahoma center Jake Maikkula got hurt at Mississippi State, and Caleb Nitta replaced him. Coach Brent Venables said Nitta could start at center if Maikkula isn't available. "He's a tough guy," Venables said.
There was happy news in Norman, too. Sooners player Sean Hutton had a health scare at practice and went to the hospital, and the team wore a helmet decal for him at Mississippi State. He was released and was waiting to greet the team when it got home. "It's a miracle, nothing short of that," Venables said. "I'm thankful for his health and for the doctors and the team that helped him through that episode."
The coaches. Shane Beamer is in his sixth season at South Carolina, and he knows Oklahoma well. He coached on Lincoln Riley's staff in Norman from 2018 to 2020. His team has beaten two straight ranked opponents, at Florida 38-19 and Tennessee.
Venables wants a cleaner offense. "Again, just get out of our own way and execute with great precision," he said. He also praised Sellers, Stewart and wide receiver Nyck Harbor. "Because of the dynamic ability of their playmakers, you have to know where everybody is."
Storylines. These teams have met twice, and each has won once. In 2024, South Carolina won 35-9 in Norman, with a school-record nine sacks. Last year, Oklahoma won 26-7 in Columbia, and the Sooners had six sacks.
Stewart's forced fumble against Tennessee was the ninth of his career, which tied the South Carolina school record held by Jadeveon Clowney.
Fun and true. South Carolina is one of 13 FBS teams with more than one set of brothers on the roster. LaNorris Sellers shares the roster with his brother, wide receiver Jayden Sellers.
