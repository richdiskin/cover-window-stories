Bobcats Rest Up in Athens: Ohio Takes the Bye With a Winning Record in Hand

Meet Your Insider
Brick by brick, friends. I'm Penny Trautman, and I live in Athens, Ohio, where the streets are brick, the hills are green, and the Bobcats run the football like they're laying Court Street, one sturdy block at a time. I'm a brick-street archivist by hobby (it's a real hobby; I have a binder), and I chart this run game the same way. I travel with this team and I live with this team, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines with some hill-country wit and an unreasonable number of brick puns. When we win, I tap a brick for luck and grin. When we lose, I say "brick by brick" and start rebuilding by Monday. Welcome to the porch. One more thing: I'll always be honest with you, even when the mortar's cracked.

The School in 60 Seconds
Ohio is the senior member of the Mid-American Conference. The Bobcats have played home games at Peden Stadium since 1929, and CFBD lists it at 24,000 seats. The recent run has been one of the steadiest in the league: 10-4 in 2022, 10-3 in 2023, 11-3 in 2024 and 9-4 in 2025. That's four straight winning seasons stacked like good masonry.

The Coach and the Scheme
John Hauser is in his first season as head coach; CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 26, 2025.

Players to Know
QB Nick Poulos: 57 of 97, 769 yards, 6 touchdowns.
RB Victor Rosa: 364 yards on 77 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Riley Neer: 37 catches, 497 yards, 5 touchdowns.
LB Michael Molnar: 5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Michael Molnar: a team-high 70 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Ohio is 6-2 overall and 4-0 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 1st in MAC.
The season so far: L 21-49 at Nebraska Cornhuskers (Sept. 5), W 29-27 vs Jacksonville State Gamecocks (Sept. 12), L 36-41 at South Alabama Jaguars (Sept. 19), W 35-7 vs Stonehill Skyhawks (Sept. 26), W 13-10 at Kent State Golden Flashes (Oct. 3), W 31-14 vs Central Michigan Chippewas (Oct. 10), W 48-7 at Sacramento State Hornets (Oct. 17), W 24-20 vs Eastern Michigan Eagles (Oct. 24).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for Ohio this week. We're off from Tuesday, Oct. 27, through Saturday, Oct. 31, so the rest of this report is about where we stand and what's next.
Last time out. We beat the Eastern Michigan Eagles 24-20 at home on Oct. 24. Nick Poulos went 19 of 29 for 266 yards, with 1 touchdown. Victor Rosa ran 4 times for 40 yards. Dom Dorwart caught 4 balls for 125 yards, with 1 touchdown.
Up next. We play the Akron Zips on the road on Tuesday, Nov. 3, 7:00 p.m. ET, at InfoCision Stadium in Akron.
The open week is for rest, healing and a good week of practice. I'll be back with a full preview before the next kickoff.
