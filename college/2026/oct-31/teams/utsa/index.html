Roadrunners at Rest: UTSA Takes the Bye Week Sitting Pretty

Meet Your Insider
Birds up! ¡Hola, y'all! I'm Marisol Ybarra, born and raised in San Antonio, and I have a confession: I collect Fiesta medals. Hundreds of them. They fill a whole wall of my living room, and every great Roadrunner play earns a brand-new medal. I travel with this team and I live with this team, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines, with Tex-Mex warmth and Fiesta energy. I read an offense the way I read a parade route: every float has to be on time, every band in step. When we win, I yell "Birds up!" and crack a cascarón full of confetti over the nearest head. When we lose, I tell you, "The parade will come back around." It always does in San Antonio.

The School in 60 Seconds
The Roadrunners represent the University of Texas at San Antonio and compete in the American Conference. Home is the Alamodome, which seats 65,000 but is normally set up for about 35,000 for UTSA games; CFBD lists 36,582. Jeff Traylor has coached the team since 2020. And the medal wall from these years is full: 12-2 in 2021, 11-3 in 2022, 9-4, 7-6 and 7-6. This program has built something special in a short time.

The Coach and the Scheme
Coach Traylor was hired Dec. 9, 2019, per CFBD.

Players to Know
QB Owen McCown: 169 of 243, 1,751 yards, 10 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Will Henderson III: 664 yards on 109 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR DJ Allen Jr.: 25 catches, 347 yards, 2 touchdowns.
LB Owen Pewee: 4 sacks, the team lead.
LB Brandon Tucker: a team-high 56 tackles.

The Conference Picture
UTSA is 7-1 overall and 4-0 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 1st in American.
The season so far: W 45-16 vs UT Rio Grande Valley Vaqueros (Sept. 5), W 31-26 at Texas State Bobcats (Sept. 12), L 6-30 at Texas Longhorns (Sept. 19), W 59-45 vs Colorado State Rams (Sept. 26), W 16-14 at Rice Owls (Oct. 3), W 31-24 vs South Florida Bulls (Oct. 8), W 38-34 vs Navy Midshipmen (Oct. 17), W 42-19 at Tulane Green Wave (Oct. 24).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for UTSA this week. We're off from Tuesday, Oct. 27, through Saturday, Oct. 31, so the rest of this report is about where we stand and what's next.
Last time out. We beat the Tulane Green Wave 42-19 on the road on Oct. 24. Owen McCown went 16 of 23 for 173 yards, with 2 interceptions. Will Henderson III ran 15 times for 102 yards, with 1 touchdown. David Amador II caught 5 balls for 47 yards.
Up next. We play the Florida Atlantic Owls on the road on Thursday, Nov. 5, 7:30 p.m. ET, at Flagler Credit Union Stadium in Boca Raton.
The open week is for rest, healing and a good week of practice. I'll be back with a full preview before the next kickoff.
