Cardinals Fly Low for a Week: Ball State Takes the Bye and Tells a Few Jokes

Meet Your Insider
Howdy, Cardinals fans! I'm Gus Ponder, your Ball State insider. By day I produce community radio here in Muncie. By night I chart Ball State special teams on a whiteboard in my garage. I have one dad joke per punt, and yes, the punt count is tracked. Why did the football coach go to the bank? To get his quarterback. You're welcome. When we win, I wear my celebratory cat tie and tell a joke so bad it's good. When we lose, I sigh, "Sounds like a Monday," and pull up the punt chart. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, with a groan or two. "Here's a joke and a punt chart."

The School in 60 Seconds
Ball State plays at Scheumann Stadium in Muncie, listed at 22,500. Our MAC neighborhood has a new face, too: Sacramento State is in its first season in the MAC, a football-only member taking the spot of Northern Illinois.

The Coach and the Scheme
Mike Uremovich was named Ball State's 19th head coach in December 2024. He came from Butler, where he led the Bulldogs to three straight winning seasons, their first such run in nearly four decades. Before that, he spent six seasons as an assistant at Northern Illinois and helped win MAC titles in 2012 and 2018. His stated plan: run the ball on offense, be physical on defense, be good on special teams, and don't beat yourself.

Through 8 games this season, we're scoring 22.0 points per game and allowing 35.3.

Players to Know

LB Kaleb Stewart: a team-high 37 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Ball State is 3-5 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 7th in MAC.
The season so far: L 3-56 at Ohio State Buckeyes (Sept. 5), W 41-17 vs Stony Brook Seawolves (Sept. 12), L 15-51 at Liberty Flames (Sept. 19), L 13-26 at Kent State Golden Flashes (Sept. 26), L 24-39 vs Toledo Rockets (Oct. 3), L 14-45 at Northwestern Wildcats (Oct. 10), W 32-31 at Bowling Green Falcons (Oct. 17), W 34-17 vs Sacramento State Hornets (Oct. 24).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for Ball State this week. We're off from Tuesday, Oct. 27, through Saturday, Oct. 31, so the rest of this report is about where we stand and what's next.
