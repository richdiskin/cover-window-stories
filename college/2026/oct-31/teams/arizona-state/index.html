Meet Your Insider
Hi, Sun Devil Nation! I'm Skylar Vanterpool. Yes, I'm the girl by the pool in the giant maroon sunhat, and yes, I also have a master's degree in sports science, so I will talk your ear off about recovery windows and sweat rates. Fair warning: about every 90 seconds I'm going to remind you to drink some water. It's not a bit. Okay, it's a little bit a bit. My motto is simple: "Hydrate and dominate." When we win, I cannonball into a kiddie pool on set. When we lose, it's "Hydrate, reflect, recover," and then a well-earned nap. Every week I'll bring you the players, the coaches and the matchup, with plenty of sunshine and zero sunburn.

The School in 60 Seconds
Sun Devil football has a Rose Bowl ring to it. On Jan. 1, 1987, quarterback Jeff Van Raaphorst led a comeback as Arizona State beat Michigan 22-15 in Pasadena. More recently, the Sun Devils won the 2024 Big 12 championship, beating Iowa State 45-19 in the title game. Home is Mountain America Stadium in Tempe, listed at a capacity of 65,870. Sip of water. Okay, moving on.

The Coach and the Scheme
Kenny Dillingham became Arizona State's 26th head coach on Nov. 27, 2022, and took the Sun Devils to the Big 12 title in his second season. His offense loves to throw it.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 30.9 points per game and allowing 29.9.

Players to Know
QB Cutter Boley: 105 of 173, 1,360 yards, 13 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Cardae Mack: 400 yards on 55 carries, 6 touchdowns.
WR Reed Harris: 47 catches, 800 yards, 8 touchdowns.
DL Julian Hugo: 4 sacks, the team lead.
LB Owen Long: a team-high 63 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Arizona State is 4-3 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 7th in Big 12.
The season so far: W 70-7 vs Morgan State Bears (Sept. 5), L 20-48 at Texas A&M Aggies (Sept. 12), L 19-55 vs Baylor Bears (Oct. 3), W 30-12 vs Hawai'i Rainbow Warriors (Oct. 10), L 25-53 at Texas Tech Red Raiders (Oct. 17), W 28-17 vs Kansas State Wildcats (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Arizona State (4-3, 2-2 Big 12) at No. 8 BYU (6-1, 3-1 Big 12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, LaVell Edwards Stadium, Provo, Utah, Fox.
Last time out. BYU lost 39-31 in overtime at UCF on Oct. 24. Bachmeier threw for 257 yards and two touchdowns, and Kyler Kasper caught six balls for 129 yards and a score. It was the Cougars' first loss of the season.
Arizona State beat Kansas State 28-17 at home on Oct. 24. Mack ran 26 times for 159 yards and four touchdowns, Keene threw for 160 yards, and Harris caught six balls for 147 yards.
Players to watch. BYU quarterback Bear Bachmeier has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 116 of 184 for 1,514 yards, 11 touchdowns and three interceptions, and he ran for two scores and caught another against Notre Dame. LJ Martin leads the rushers with 344 yards, and freshman Legend Glasker leads the receivers with 28 catches for 498 yards. Linebacker Cade Uluave leads the defense with 45 tackles, and Tausili Akana has 3.5 sacks.
Arizona State receiver Reed Harris is the headliner, with 47 catches for 800 yards and eight touchdowns. Cutter Boley leads the team in passing at 1,360 yards and 13 touchdowns, but Mikey Keene has thrown the most passes the last two games. Cardae Mack leads the rushers with 400 yards and six scores. Linebacker Owen Long leads the defense with 63 tackles, and Julian Hugo has four sacks.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Bachmeier has thrown the most passes for BYU in every game. For Arizona State, Boley did it in the first five games and Keene in the last two, so check the teams' pregame notes.
The coaches. Kalani Sitake played fullback for BYU under LaVell Edwards and became head coach in 2016. He's the first Tongan head coach in college football, and he entered the Polynesian Football Hall of Fame in 2025. His teams went 11-2 and 12-2 the last two seasons, and this year's defense allows 18.6 points a game.
Kenny Dillingham became Arizona State's 26th head coach on Nov. 27, 2022, and took the Sun Devils to the 2024 Big 12 championship, beating Iowa State 45-19 in the title game. His offense loves to throw it, and Harris is the proof.
Storylines. The matchup to watch is Harris against a BYU defense that doesn't allow much. On the other side, the Cougars want their big guys up front to get Martin and the run game going against an Arizona State defense that has given up yards on the ground. These two split the last two meetings: BYU won 27-17 in 2021, and Arizona State won 28-23 in 2024.
Fun and true. BYU started playing college football in 1922, has won 23 conference championships and the 1984 national championship, and joined the Big 12 on July 1, 2023. Arizona State beat Michigan 22-15 in the Rose Bowl on Jan. 1, 1987, and earlier this season it beat Kansas 24-17 at Wembley Stadium in London.
