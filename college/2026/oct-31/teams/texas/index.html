The School in 60 Seconds
The Texas Longhorns began playing in 1893 and compete in the SEC. We claim four consensus national championships: 1963, 1969, 1970 and 2005, and the program has been led by three Hall of Fame coaches. Home is DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium in Austin, opened in 1924 and listed at 100,119 seats. The last five seasons: 5-7, 8-5, then 12-2, 13-3 and 10-3. That, friends, is a blue-chip portfolio.

The Coach and the Scheme
Steve Sarkisian has been our head coach since CFBD's hire date of Jan. 2, 2021.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 30.7 points per game and allowing 19.1.

The Conference Picture
Texas is 5-2 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 8th in SEC. We're ranked No. 11 in ESPN's rankings this week.
The season so far: W 59-7 vs Texas State Bobcats (Sept. 5), W 24-23 vs Ohio State Buckeyes (Sept. 12), W 30-6 vs UTSA Roadrunners (Sept. 19), W 20-17 at Tennessee Volunteers (Sept. 26), L 24-27 vs Florida Gators (Oct. 17), W 34-27 vs Ole Miss Rebels (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Mississippi State (4-3, 2-3 SEC) at No. 11 Texas (5-2, 2-2 SEC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 7:30 p.m. ET (6:30 p.m. Central), DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium, Austin, Texas, ESPN.
Last time out. Mississippi State lost 23-15 to Oklahoma at home. Taylor started but left in the first half. Puckett went 17 of 24 for 158 yards and a touchdown, Bothwell ran for 111 yards, and Sanfrisco Magee had three catches for 65 yards and a touchdown.
Texas beat Ole Miss 34-27. Brown ran 23 times for 157 yards, and Wingo caught seven passes for 111 yards and a touchdown. Manning threw for 212 yards.
Players to watch. Mississippi State quarterback Kamario Taylor was 104 of 159 for 1,460 yards, 17 touchdowns and four interceptions, but he is out for the season. Parker Puckett takes over. Running back Fluff Bothwell has 514 yards and three touchdowns, and wide receiver Anthony Evans III leads the team with 53 catches for 647 yards. On defense, linebacker Tyler Lockhart has 48 tackles, and Jaray Bledsoe has 2.5 sacks.
Texas quarterback Arch Manning is 124 of 196 for 1,507 yards, 12 touchdowns and seven interceptions. Running back Raleek Brown has 422 yards and five touchdowns. Wide receiver Ryan Wingo has 33 catches for 456 yards and four touchdowns. On defense, Colin Simmons has 10.5 sacks, and Jelani McDonald has 54 tackles.
Availability. No official SEC availability report had been posted as of Tuesday. Mississippi State announced that Taylor will miss the rest of the season with a foot injury that needs surgery, and he is expected to make a full recovery. "Missing the rest of this season is hard to accept, but I know God has a plan," Taylor said. For Texas, Simmons hurt his ankle against Florida and missed the Ole Miss game.
The coaches. Jeff Lebby is in his third season at Mississippi State. "Nobody wants to be on that field more than Kamario," he said. "He's the ultimate teammate and competitor." Lebby is also weighing Puckett and AJ Swann at quarterback.
Steve Sarkisian is in his sixth season at Texas. He loved how his defense played without Simmons. "Nobody was just waiting for Colin to make a play," he said. "It was all 11 guys playing really well collectively together." He also said, "Believe me, we want Colin back as bad as anybody else."
Storylines. Taylor had huge games in wins over Minnesota, South Carolina and Missouri, and he says he is all in with his teammates. "Starkville is home," he said. Puckett now gets his chance. For Texas, McDonald played his best game of the year against Ole Miss, according to Sarkisian Brown carried the load with 23 carries and will try to do it again.
Fun and true. These teams have met the last two years. Texas won 35-13 in 2024 and 45-38 in 2025.
Mississippi State started this season 4-0, with wins over UL Monroe, at Minnesota, at South Carolina and over Missouri. After Saturday, the Bulldogs play three straight home games.
