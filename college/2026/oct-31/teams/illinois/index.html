Charts, Crabs and the Illini: Illinois Brings Its Big Oregon Win to College Park

Meet Your Insider
Good afternoon, class! Oh, sorry. Old habits. Welcome, Illini Nation. I'm Margery Vandermolen, your Illinois insider, and I explain football the way your best-loved teacher explained long division: patiently, cheerfully and with a lot of charts. I'm a prairie-state numbers whiz. I've crunched the numbers on just about everything, and the numbers always say the same thing: orange. You'll get corn jokes from me. You'll get chart jokes. Sometimes you'll get a corn chart, which is both. And every Saturday morning I'll ask you the same question: "Orange you glad it's Saturday?" When something wonderful happens on the field, there's only one thing to say: "Oskee-wow-wow!" Each week I'll give you the players, the coaches and the matchup, explained clearly enough that Grandma and the grandkids can both follow along.

The School in 60 Seconds
Here's your history lesson, and yes, it will be on the test. Illinois is a founding member of the Big Ten and claims five national championships: 1914, 1919, 1923, 1927 and 1951. The 1923 title team opened Memorial Stadium and introduced the world to Harold "Red" Grange, the Galloping Ghost. On Oct. 18, 1924, at the stadium's dedication game, Grange scored four touchdowns against Michigan in the first 12 minutes. He ran for a fifth and threw for a sixth in a 39-14 win. ESPN once chose the legendary No. 77 as the greatest player in college football history. The stadium honors University of Illinois students who died in World War I. Illinois also claims 15 Big Ten titles. High fives all around.

The Coach and the Scheme
Bret Bielema was named the 26th head coach in Illinois history on Dec. 19, 2020. He's an Illinois native, and he said at the time, "Illinois and the Big Ten is home for me". One of his early wins was a nine-overtime victory at Penn State in 2021, the longest game in college football history.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 30.3 points per game and allowing 25.3.

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
Illinois (4-3, 2-2 Big Ten) at Maryland (2-5, 0-4 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12 p.m. ET, SECU Stadium, College Park, Md., FS1.
Last time out. Illinois beat Oregon 21-10 at home on Oct. 24. Laughery ran 13 times for 123 yards and a touchdown, and Dixon caught six passes for 65 yards and a score.
Maryland didn't play last week. Its last game was a 37-30 loss to Rutgers on Oct. 17, when Washington threw for 263 yards and two touchdowns and Durr caught six passes for 104 yards.
Players to watch. Illinois quarterback Katin Houser threw the most passes in the first six Illini games. He's 122 of 184 for 1,440 yards, 12 touchdowns and four interceptions. Michael Clayton II threw the most in the win over Oregon, going 11 of 18 with two touchdown throws. Wide receiver Collin Dixon is the go-to target, with 54 catches for 678 yards and 10 touchdowns. Aidan Laughery leads the rushers with 514 yards. On defense, defensive back Matthew Bailey has 49 tackles.
Maryland quarterback Malik Washington has thrown the most passes in all seven Terrapin games. He's 155 of 261 for 1,615 yards, nine touchdowns and six interceptions. Chris Durr Jr. leads the receivers with 52 catches for 580 yards. DeJuan Williams leads the rushers with 281 yards and three scores. Linebacker Daniel Wingate has 44 tackles, and linebacker Zion Elee has 3.5 sacks.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Houser threw the most passes for Illinois in six games, but Clayton did against Oregon, so watch the teams' pregame notes for who takes the first snap. Washington has thrown the most passes in every Maryland game.
The coaches. Bret Bielema was named the 26th head coach in Illinois history on Dec. 19, 2020. He's an Illinois native, and one of his early wins was a nine-overtime thriller at Penn State in 2021, the longest game in college football history. His team has already won 38-37 in overtime at Michigan State this year.
Maryland has new leadership this week. Mike Locksley stepped down as head coach last week, and co-defensive coordinator Aazaar Abdul-Rahim was named interim head coach. Abdul-Rahim is a Washington, D.C., native in his second stint with the Terps, and Saturday is his first game in charge. This year's Terps score 28.3 points a game, including a 62-0 win over Hampton and a 38-14 win at UConn.
Storylines. The matchup to circle is Dixon against a Maryland defense that allows 33.3 points a game. On the other side, Washington loves to throw it, and he went 30 of 44 for 359 yards and three touchdowns against Virginia Tech. Both teams run the ball well, too. Illinois gains 5.2 yards a carry and Maryland gains 4.5, so the big guys up front on both sides will be busy.
