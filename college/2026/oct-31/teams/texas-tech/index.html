Guns Up at Noon: No. 13 Texas Tech Welcomes No. 22 Arizona to Lubbock

Meet Your Insider
Howdy. Bodie Ratliff. Texas Tech insider. I ride bareback at the rodeo, and in tumbleweed season I chart Air Raid concepts on the back of feed-store receipts. I travel with the team. I live with the team. People say I don't talk much. They're right. One word usually does it, and the wind fills in the rest. Somebody asks how the defense looked? "Mean." How was the trip? "Windy." When we win, I say "Guns up," real quiet, and then I grin longer than anybody's comfortable with. When we lose, I stare into the wind with my hat in my hand. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup from inside the program, West Texas plain. My motto: "Guns up, wind's up."

The School in 60 Seconds
Last season was the best in school history. Texas Tech went 12-2, crushed BYU 34-7 to win the Big 12 championship and made its first College Football Playoff. That was the program's first outright conference title since 1955. The run ended with a 23-0 loss to Oregon in the Orange Bowl. Home is Jones AT&T Stadium in Lubbock, listed at 60,454. Long story short. "Good."

The Coach and the Scheme
Joey McGuire was hired on Nov. 8, 2021.

Through 7 games this season, we're scoring 35.9 points per game and allowing 20.4.

Players to Know
QB Will Hammond: 139 of 205, 1,561 yards, 6 touchdowns, 6 interceptions.
RB J'Koby Williams: 619 yards on 86 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Kenny Johnson: 31 catches, 499 yards, 1 touchdown.
DL A.J. Holmes Jr.: 6.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Austin Romaine: a team-high 53 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Texas Tech is 6-1 overall and 3-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have us 2nd in Big 12. We're ranked No. 13 in ESPN's rankings this week.
The season so far: W 33-10 vs Abilene Christian Wildcats (Sept. 5), W 35-24 at Oregon State Beavers (Sept. 12), W 28-26 vs Houston Cougars (Sept. 18), W 49-14 vs Sam Houston Bearkats (Sept. 26), W 29-7 at Colorado Buffaloes (Oct. 3), W 53-25 vs Arizona State Sun Devils (Oct. 17), L 24-37 at Cincinnati Bearcats (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
No. 22 Arizona (6-1, 3-1 Big 12) at No. 13 Texas Tech (6-1, 3-1 Big 12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12 p.m. ET, Galaxy Stadium, Lubbock, Texas, ESPN.
Last time out. Arizona rolled past Iowa State 39-3 at home on Oct. 24. Fifita went 27 of 34 for 308 yards and two touchdowns, Mizell caught eight passes for 134 yards and a score, and Quincy Craig ran nine times for 51 yards and a touchdown. The Wildcat defense allowed just one field goal.
Texas Tech had its first loss of the season, 37-24 at Cincinnati on Oct. 24. Hammond went 24 of 37 for 227 yards, Williams ran 12 times for 81 yards, and Johnson caught seven passes for 81 yards.
Players to watch. Arizona quarterback Noah Fifita is 167 of 225 for 2,064 yards, 18 touchdowns and four interceptions. Wide receiver Isaiah Mizell leads the Wildcats with 27 catches for 493 yards and five touchdowns. Kedrick Reescano leads the rushers with 303 yards and five scores. On defense, linebacker Chase Kennedy has four sacks and linebacker Taye Brown leads the team with 42 tackles.
Texas Tech quarterback Will Hammond is 139 of 205 for 1,561 yards, six touchdowns and six interceptions. Running back J'Koby Williams is the workhorse, with 619 yards and five touchdowns on 86 carries. Wide receiver Kenny Johnson has 31 catches for 499 yards. Defensive lineman A.J. Holmes Jr. leads the team with 6.5 sacks, and linebacker Austin Romaine leads with 53 tackles.
Availability. No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and the teams' box scores don't mark starters. Fifita has thrown the most passes for Arizona in all seven games, and Hammond has done the same for Texas Tech, so check the teams' pregame notes on Saturday morning.
The coaches. Joey McGuire was hired at Texas Tech on Nov. 8, 2021. Last season was the best in school history: the Red Raiders went 12-2, beat BYU 34-7 to win the Big 12 championship and made their first College Football Playoff. It was the program's first outright conference title since 1955. This year's team is built on the ground and on defense, and it allows 20.4 points a game.
Brent Brennan was hired at Arizona on Jan. 16, 2024. He went 4-8 in his first season, then turned it around with a 9-4 year in 2025 that ended in the Holiday Bowl. His 2026 Wildcats score 36.1 points a game and allow just 16.7.
Storylines. The matchup to circle is Fifita against a Texas Tech defense that has 6.5 sacks from Holmes alone. If the Red Raider big guys up front can get to Fifita, they can slow the Arizona passing game. On the other side, Williams and the Tech ground game face an Arizona defense that held Cincinnati to 7 points and Iowa State to 3. Whichever running game finds room first could decide the afternoon.
Fun and true. Arizona's rallying cry, "Bear Down," goes back to October 1926 and John "Button" Salmon, the student-body president and the Wildcats' quarterback. Before he died after a car accident, he asked coach J.F. "Pop" McKale to tell the team to "bear down," and it has been the school's motto ever since. These two have split their last two meetings: Arizona won 28-14 in 2019, and Texas Tech won 28-22 in 2024.
