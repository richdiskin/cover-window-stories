Pearls and an Optimism Counter: Southern Miss Visits UL Monroe on Halloween

Southern Miss Golden Eagles (2-5) at UL Monroe Warhawks (1-6)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Malone Stadium · ESPN+

Southern Miss (2-5, 1-2 Sun Belt) at UL Monroe (1-6, 1-2 Sun Belt). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, Malone Stadium, Monroe, La., ESPN+.

Your hosts are Dee Broussard, a Southern Miss fan and former sorority president who calls everybody "sugar" and means it, and Darnell Vidrine, a UL Monroe fan and overnight Delta radio host who keeps an optimism counter running all season.

Darnell: Good evening, good morning, good whatever-time-it-is! Welcome to Monroe, Dee.

Dee: Thank you, sugar. Is that the famous optimism counter?

Darnell: Set to 100, like every week. But this week, Dee, I didn't have to reset it. We WON.

Dee: Well, bless it. Congratulations, Darnell. Truly.

Players to watch

Southern Miss quarterback Landry Lyddy has thrown the most passes in the last five games. He's 101 of 170 for 1,196 yards, seven touchdowns and four interceptions. Steven Robinson leads the rushers with 577 yards and eight scores, and Mario Sanders leads the receivers with 40 catches for 569 yards and five touchdowns. Linebacker Mathis Haygood leads the defense with 63 tackles, and Jameer Lewis has four sacks.

UL Monroe quarterback Aidan Armenta has thrown the most passes in the last four games. He's 96 of 153 for 1,121 yards, eight touchdowns and three interceptions. Austin Carlisle, who threw the most passes in the second and third games, leads the team in rushing with 237 yards, and Marcellus Jackson leads the receivers with 32 catches. Linebacker Noah Flemmings leads the defense with 65 tackles, and Kevontay Wells has three sacks.

Dee: Steven Robinson runs like he's late for a formal. Eight touchdowns, sugar.

Darnell: Noah Flemmings has 65 tackles. I can FEEL it every time he hits somebody.

What happened last week

UL Monroe won its first game of the season, 21-14 at Troy on Oct. 24, on Troy's Military Appreciation day. Armenta went 13 of 22 for 147 yards and two touchdowns with no interceptions.

Darnell: I KNEW IT! I may have sniffled on the air. Okay, I definitely sniffled.

Southern Miss beat Louisiana 35-31 on Homecoming at "The Rock" on Oct. 24. Jamarice Wilder ran 13 times for 154 yards and a touchdown, and Sanders caught six balls for 77 yards.

Dee: Homecoming win at home. To the top, y'all! I pointed skyward so hard I lost an earring.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Lyddy has thrown the most passes for Southern Miss in five straight games, and Armenta has done it for UL Monroe in four straight, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Blake Anderson is in his first season at Southern Miss, with a hire date of Dec. 11, 2025. His team throws it well and just got a big day from the run game, too. The defense is the work in progress, but the Golden Eagles found a way against Louisiana.

Bryant Vincent leads UL Monroe, with a hire date of Dec. 5, 2023. Two of his team's early losses were one-score games, 26-20 at UAB and 38-35 to SE Louisiana, and last week his defense held Troy to 14 points.

Dee: Coach Anderson walked in and got to work. I respect a man with a plan.

Darnell: Coach Vincent keeps the faith. He's a counter-reset kind of coach.

Player storylines

The matchup to watch is the Southern Miss run game against a UL Monroe defense that has had a hard time stopping the run. Robinson and Wilder will test the Warhawks' big guys up front. On the other side, Armenta wants another clean day after throwing for 362 yards at South Alabama earlier this season. Southern Miss won four of the six meetings since 2017, including 49-21 last season, but UL Monroe won 38-21 in 2024.

What's happening on campus

It's Halloween in Monroe, and Malone Stadium, opened in 1978 and listed at 27,617 seats, gets the home crowd back after last week's trip to Troy. Southern Miss fans just celebrated Homecoming at The Rock, which wears a new name this fall, Renasant Stadium. The nickname isn't going anywhere.

Dee: I'm dressing as a string of pearls. Elegant, timeless, a little bit sparkly.

Darnell: I'm dressing as a 2 a.m. radio show. Everybody's welcome to call in.

Anything else fun and true

UL Monroe football dates to 1931. The school's teams were the Northeast Louisiana Indians for a long stretch and became the Warhawks in 2006, and the program moved up to what is now FBS in 1994. Southern Miss went 7-6 last season, a lovely bounce back after a 1-11 year in 2024.

Darnell: Whatever happens, Dee, supper's on me. We'll say grace first.

Dee: Deal, sugar. I'll bring the sweet tea.

My call: Southern Miss Golden Eagles 32, UL Monroe Warhawks 26.
