Southern Miss (2-5, 1-2 Sun Belt) at UL Monroe (1-6, 1-2 Sun Belt), Saturday at 3:30 p.m. ET on ESPN+ at Malone Stadium in Monroe. DraftKings has Southern Miss Golden Eagles −3.5 with a total of 55.5. My card: Southern Miss Golden Eagles −3.5, Over 55.5, and a final of Southern Miss Golden Eagles 32, UL Monroe Warhawks 26.

Dee speaks for Southern Miss, smoothing her dress. "We beat Louisiana 35-31 last week, sugar. Jamarice Wilder ran for 154 yards in that one, Steven Robinson has eight rushing touchdowns, Mario Sanders has 569 receiving yards, and Mathis Haygood has 63 tackles. To the top."

Darnell speaks for UL Monroe, counter in hand. "I KNEW IT! We won 21-14 at Troy, and Aidan Armenta threw two touchdowns with no interceptions. He threw for 362 yards at South Alabama. Noah Flemmings has 65 tackles. And we beat Southern Miss 38-21 in 2024. Keep the faith, y'all."

Why Southern Miss? Because the Golden Eagles can run on this defense. UL Monroe allows 37.3 points a game and gave up 62 to Mississippi State, 45 to Florida Atlantic and 52 at South Alabama. Robinson and Wilder should find room. Southern Miss also won the last meeting 49-21. My final has the Golden Eagles winning by six.

The risk is the Southern Miss defense, which allows 32.4 points a game and gave up 48 to UConn and 55 at Troy. Landry Lyddy didn't throw a touchdown last week either. UL Monroe is at home, coming off its first win, and Armenta has had big days.

Why the Over? Neither defense has slowed many people down. Southern Miss games have finished 55-34 at Troy, 48-20 against UConn and 35-31 last week. UL Monroe games have finished 62-13, 38-35, 45-17 and 52-35. The market pushed the total up from where it opened. My 32-26 adds up to 58, over 55.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Southern Miss 33-25, likes the Southern Miss side, and lands on the Over. Next Man Up has Southern Miss 30-26, sides with Southern Miss, and lands on the Over. Follow The Money has no side pick and likes the Over 55.5. In The Trenches has no pick.

Follow The Money started it: "The total went up. I go with it."

The Formula answered: "Southern Miss runs the ball and wins by a score."

Next Man Up added: "Both defenses give up yards. Points are coming."

Bottom of the card: two teams coming off wins, two defenses that have had a hard time all season and a Southern Miss run game that should keep moving. Give me the Golden Eagles and the Over.

Southern Miss Golden Eagles 32, UL Monroe Warhawks 26. Southern Miss Golden Eagles cover the 3.5. Over 55.5.
