Virginia vs Wake Forest prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on Virginia Cavaliers +12.5 and Under 49.5 at DraftKings, with a final of Wake Forest Demon Deacons 30, Virginia Cavaliers 19. It's Halloween in Winston-Salem, with trick-or-treating inside Gate 5 and "Thriller" at halftime.

Wake Forest is the better team, but 12.5 is a lot against a Virginia team that took Duke to the final minutes. The Demon Deacons are 6-1, score 37.4 points a game and beat Stanford, NC State and Cal by a combined 136-24. Gio Lopez has 17 touchdowns and one interception. Virginia allows 19.6 points a game, and it lost to Duke 19-16 on a late touchdown. Wake won last year's meeting 16-9.

Jake Dickert knows what's coming. "I have a lot of respect for who we're playing, because the style is very different and they're more than happy to grind it out and kind of do it the old-fashioned way," he said Monday.

Eloise Brightman-Rowe holds up a debate timer. "Resolved, Prescott: Lopez throws for three touchdowns and Carlos Hernandez is back."

My concern is the Wake passing game after the off week. Lopez threw for 267 yards and four touchdowns at Cal, and Hernandez has 720 receiving yards. Virginia lost 38-7 at Florida State, so a bad afternoon away from home is possible.

The total comes from the Virginia offense and the recent Wake defense. Virginia scored 7 against Florida State and 16 against Duke, and Beau Pribula threw no touchdown passes against Duke. Wake has allowed 14 or fewer in each of its last three games. My 30-19 adds up to 49. DraftKings has Wake Forest −12.5 and 49.5. The consensus spread opened at Wake Forest −12.5 and hasn't moved. The consensus total opened at 49.5 and hasn't moved either.
