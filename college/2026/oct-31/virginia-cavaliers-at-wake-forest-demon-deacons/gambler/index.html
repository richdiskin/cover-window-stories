Virginia Cavaliers at Wake Forest Demon Deacons

Virginia Cavaliers (4-4) at Wake Forest Demon Deacons (6-1)

3:45 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium · ESPN2

Prescott Ashby-Lowell straightens his bow tie as he steps off the bus in Winston-Salem, and Eloise Brightman-Rowe is waiting with a roll of toilet paper in each hand. "Roll the Quad!" she says. Prescott raises an eyebrow. "Splendid tradition. But on the Grounds we keep our trees tidy." Eloise grins. "Debate Club rules, Prescott. You get two minutes for rebuttal."

Virginia (4-4, 2-3 ACC) visits No. 14 Wake Forest (6-1, 4-1 ACC) Saturday at 3:45 p.m. ET on ESPN2 at Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium in Winston-Salem. DraftKings has Wake Forest Demon Deacons −12.5 with a total of 49.5. On Monday, DraftKings had Wake Forest −13.5 and 49.5. My card: Virginia Cavaliers +12.5, Under 49.5, and a final of Wake Forest Demon Deacons 30, Virginia Cavaliers 19.

Prescott speaks for Virginia. "We lost 19-16 to Duke last week, a close one. Jekail Middlebrook ran for 117 yards. Beau Pribula has 13 touchdown throws and only two interceptions, and Matthew Fobbs-White has four sacks. Our defense allows 19.6 points a game. Coach Tony Elliott is sticking with Pribula."

Eloise speaks for Wake Forest. "We had an open date last week after a 41-7 win at Cal, where Gio Lopez threw four touchdowns and Dominick Barry caught three. Lopez has 17 touchdowns and one interception. Carlos Hernandez has 49 catches for 720 yards. Langston Hardy has five and a half sacks. We score 37.4 points a game."

Why Virginia? Because the Cavaliers don't give up much. Virginia allows 19.6 points a game, and its last two losses were 23-13 at SMU and 19-16 to Duke. Pribula protects the ball, and Middlebrook can shorten a game. My final has Wake winning by 11, short of 12.5.

The risk is Lopez. He threw for 350 yards against Akron and 303 at NC State, and Wake has beaten Stanford 57-3, NC State 38-14 and Cal 41-7 in three of its last four games. Virginia has scored 16 points or fewer in three of its last four games. If the Cavaliers fall behind early, this gets away from them.

Why the Under? Virginia's offense has stalled, scoring 13 against SMU and 16 against Duke, and those two games finished 23-13 and 19-16. Last season, Wake beat Virginia 16-9. Both defenses allow under 20 points a game. My 30-19 adds up to 49, under 49.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Wake Forest 31-21 and likes Virginia and the Over. Next Man Up has Wake Forest 28-16, sides with Virginia, and likes the Under. Follow The Money likes Virginia after the spread came down a point, and sits out the total since it hasn't budged. In The Trenches sits out: the Virginia run game and the Wake Forest run game each have a matchup they like, so they cancel out.

The Formula started it: "Virginia keeps it close, but Wake scores enough for the Over."

Next Man Up disagreed: "Virginia can't score. Under."

Follow The Money said: "The market came toward Virginia. That's all I need."

Bottom of the card: a rested Wake Forest team at home, a Virginia defense that keeps games close and an offense that has struggled. Give me the Cavaliers and the Under.

Wake Forest Demon Deacons 30, Virginia Cavaliers 19. Virginia Cavaliers cover the 12.5. Under 49.5.
