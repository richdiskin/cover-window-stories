Virginia vs Wake Forest: Pumpkins, Parachutes and "Thriller"

Virginia Cavaliers (4-4) at Wake Forest Demon Deacons (6-1)

3:45 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium · ESPN2

Virginia Cavaliers vs Wake Forest Demon Deacons is a Week 9 Halloween game in Winston-Salem, where trick-or-treating runs inside Gate 5 before kickoff and the band plays "Thriller" at halftime. Prescott Ashby-Lowell, a fourth-generation Wahoo in a game-day bow tie who refuses to say "campus," says Tony Elliott, Beau Pribula and Jekail Middlebrook can slow it down, and Eloise Brightman-Rowe, a Wake Forest debate grad, says Jake Dickert, Gio Lopez and Carlos Hernandez will roll the Quad by dinner.

Eloise: Resolved, Prescott: Wake Forest is the best place in America to spend Halloween.

Prescott: I accept the premise with one amendment. The Grounds are a close second.

Eloise: You can't say "Grounds" here. It's a Deacon Halloween. There's a pumpkin patch at Gate 5.

Prescott: Then I shall select a pumpkin with good posture.

Wake Forest is 6-1, 4-1 in the ACC and second in the conference. The Demon Deacons have won four in a row and beat Stanford 57-3, NC State 38-14 and Cal 41-7 in their last three games before an off week. Lopez is 140 of 207 for 1,947 yards, 17 touchdowns and one interception. He threw for 267 yards and four touchdowns in the win at Cal. Hernandez missed that game but still has 49 catches for 720 yards, Ty Clark III leads the rushers with 328 yards, Langston Hardy has 5.5 sacks, and Vincent Firenze leads the defense with 49 tackles.

Dickert spent the off week worried about rust, and he said Monday that a team can't just show up after two weeks off and expect game speed.

Eloise: Rebuttal time, Prescott. One interception all season.

Prescott: I concede the interception. I do not concede the afternoon.

Eloise: Noted for the record. The judges are unmoved.

Prescott: The judges are wearing Wake Forest sweaters, Eloise.

Virginia is 4-4 and 2-3 in the league. Pribula is 119 of 183 for 1,369 yards, 13 touchdowns and two interceptions. Middlebrook has 513 rushing yards and ran for 117 and a touchdown against Duke last week, and Jacobie Henderson leads the Cavaliers with 40 tackles. Matthew Fobbs-White has four sacks.

The Duke game hurt. Will Bettridge's 30-yard field goal put Virginia up 16-13 with three minutes left, and Duke answered with a seven-play, 75-yard drive to win 19-16.

Prescott: Three minutes, Eloise. We were three minutes from a proper Saturday.

Eloise: I'll give you that one. Bow tie to bow tie.

Elliott didn't hide from it. "Guys have got to strain a little bit harder," he said.

Dickert sounded like he'd studied the Cavaliers' style. "I have a lot of respect for who we're playing, because the style is very different and they're more than happy to grind it out and kind of do it the old-fashioned way," he said.

Prescott: The old-fashioned way. He's quoting my grandfather.

The teams know each other well. Wake won 16-9 in Charlottesville last season, when Virginia was ranked No. 14.

The ACC's initial availability report, posted at 8 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists 17 Virginia players out and Jekail Middlebrook doubtful. Wake Forest lists three players out, and Carlos Hernandez is probable.

The Halloween schedule is a full day. Lots open at 10:45 a.m. ET, the Deacon leads Coach Dickert down the Deacon Walk on his bike at 1:30 p.m. ET, and the Golden Knights parachute team is scheduled to drop in during the national anthem. Jack Skellington and Sally are expected, too, and there's a costume contest on the field.

Eloise: I'm going as a debate trophy.

Prescott: I'm going as myself. The bow tie is orange. That's a costume.

Eloise: Prescott, orange is Virginia's color.

Prescott: It's also a pumpkin's. Diplomacy.

Eloise: Whatever happens, supper's on me afterward.

Prescott: Splendid. I'll bring my manners.

My call: Wake Forest Demon Deacons 30, Virginia Cavaliers 19.
