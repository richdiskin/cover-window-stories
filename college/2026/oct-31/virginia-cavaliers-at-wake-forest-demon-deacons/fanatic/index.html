Bow Ties and Rolled Trees: Virginia Visits No. 14 Wake Forest on Halloween

Virginia Cavaliers (4-4) at Wake Forest Demon Deacons (6-1)

3:45 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium · ESPN2

Virginia (4-4, 2-3 ACC) at No. 14 Wake Forest (6-1, 4-1 ACC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:45 p.m. ET, Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium, Winston-Salem, N.C., ESPN2.

Your hosts are Prescott Ashby-Lowell, a fourth-generation Wahoo in a game-day bow tie who refuses to say "campus," and Eloise Brightman-Rowe, a Wake Forest grad and debate-team darling who rolls the Quad and politely roasts bad tackling.

Eloise: Welcome to Winston-Salem, Prescott! Resolved: Wake Forest is the best place to spend Halloween.

Prescott: I'll accept the premise, Eloise, with one amendment. The Grounds are a close second.

Eloise: Two minutes for rebuttal.

Prescott: I only need one. Go 'Hoos.

Players to watch

Wake Forest quarterback Gio Lopez has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 140 of 207 for 1,947 yards, 17 touchdowns and just one interception. Carlos Hernandez leads the receivers with 49 catches for 720 yards and four scores, and Ty Clark III leads the rushers with 328 yards and four touchdowns. Linebacker Vincent Firenze leads the defense with 49 tackles, and Langston Hardy has five and a half sacks.

Virginia quarterback Beau Pribula has thrown the most passes in all eight games. He's 119 of 183 for 1,369 yards, 13 touchdowns and two interceptions. Jekail Middlebrook leads the rushers with 513 yards and four scores, and Da'Shawn Martin has five touchdown catches. Matthew Fobbs-White has four sacks, and cornerback Jacobie Henderson leads the team with 40 tackles.

Eloise: One interception all season. Gio Lopez would win any debate about ball security.

Prescott: Mr. Fobbs-White has four sacks. Mr. Jefferson would've charted every one.

What happened last week

Wake Forest had an open date. The Demon Deacons' last game was a 41-7 win at Cal on Oct. 17, when Lopez threw four touchdowns and Dominick Barry caught four balls for 102 yards and three scores. Their only loss this season came against Miami, 33-20.

Eloise: The open week was for rest, film and a little extra Quad rolling.

Virginia lost 19-16 to Duke at Scott Stadium on Friday, Oct. 23. Middlebrook ran 24 times for 117 yards and a touchdown, and John Rogers caught five balls for 65 yards.

Prescott: A close one. I straightened my bow tie and went home to think.

Injuries and availability

The official ACC availability report hasn't been posted yet. Virginia coach Tony Elliott said starting safety Brandyn Hillman and safety Ethan Minter are day-to-day, and he confirmed that Pribula remains the starting quarterback. Hernandez was banged up in the Cal game, and Wake Forest used the open week to heal, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Jake Dickert leads Wake Forest, with a hire date of Dec. 18, 2024. His team scores 37.4 points a game, and the defense has 18 sacks. The Demon Deacons beat Louisville on the road and have won their last three ACC games by at least 24 points.

Tony Elliott has led Virginia since being hired Dec. 10, 2021. Before that he coached at Clemson from 2011 to 2021, finishing as associate head coach and offensive coordinator, and he won the 2017 Broyles Award as the top assistant in college football. He also played wide receiver at Clemson. Last season his Cavaliers went 11-3.

Eloise: Coach Dickert's team has won four straight since the Miami game. That's a winning argument.

Prescott: Coach Elliott built an 11-win season. I had it framed.

Player storylines

The matchup to watch is Lopez and Hernandez against a Virginia defense that allows 19.6 points a game. On the other side, Middlebrook and Virginia's big guys up front need to keep Hardy away from Pribula. Wake Forest won four of the five meetings since 2016, including 16-9 last season, but Virginia won a 31-30 thriller in 2024.

What's happening on campus

It's Halloween in Winston-Salem, and Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium, built in 1968 and listed at 31,500 seats, gets the Demon Deacons back after the open week. The Spirit of the Old Gold & Black will play "O' Here's to Wake Forest," and rolling the Quad is the tradition every Deacon fan shows visitors first.

Prescott: I'm dressing as Mr. Jefferson. Bow tie, of course.

Eloise: I'm dressing as a rolled tree. Very festive, very white.

Anything else fun and true

Virginia football started in 1887, and Scott Stadium sits next to the university's Academical Village, part of a World Heritage Site. In 1915 Virginia became the first Southern program to beat Yale, winning 10-0 at the Yale Bowl. Before Duke, all three of Virginia's losses came away from Scott Stadium.

Eloise: Whatever happens, Prescott, supper's on me. We'll say grace first.

Prescott: Splendid, Eloise. I'll bring the bagels.

My call: Wake Forest Demon Deacons 30, Virginia Cavaliers 19.
