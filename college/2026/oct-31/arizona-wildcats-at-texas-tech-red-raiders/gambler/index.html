Arizona Wildcats at Texas Tech Red Raiders

Arizona Wildcats (6-1) at Texas Tech Red Raiders (6-1)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Galaxy Stadium · ESPN

Arizona vs Texas Tech prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on Texas Tech Red Raiders −3.5 and Under 54.5 at DraftKings, with my final at Texas Tech Red Raiders 29, Arizona Wildcats 25. Lubbock holds its first-ever community Halloween parade on Broadway an hour before the eleven a.m. Central kickoff, and the home crowd shows up having just watched its team lose 37-24 at Cincinnati.

That loss is the reason the price is this short. Action Network's opening listing had Tech −7.5, DraftKings had −4.5 on Monday, and it sits at 3.5 now. A one-loss home team that runs for 5.0 yards a carry and allows 3.5 is getting less respect than it did a week ago, and my final has Tech by four, so I'll take the cheaper number.

Inez Paloverde came up from Tucson with a note for the All Souls Procession urn that reads "Bear Down." She has a case. "Noah Fifita went 27 of 34 for 308 yards against Iowa State, and we won 39-3. He has 18 touchdown passes and four interceptions. We allow 16.7 points a game."

Bodie Ratliff, the Tech fan who skips the parade for his seat, keeps his answer short. "Williams. 619 yards. Holmes. 6.5 sacks. Romaine. 53 tackles."

The Arizona offense is Fifita's arm, and that arm now has to work against a defense that holds opponents to 20.4 points and held Colorado to 7. Arizona's ground game has to find room against a Tech front that gives up 3.5 a carry, which makes Fifita carry more of the load than usual.

The risk sits with Will Hammond. He is 139 of 205 for 1,561 yards, but he has six touchdown passes and six interceptions, and he went 24 of 37 without a touchdown at Cincinnati. If he hands Fifita short fields, Arizona can win outright.

The Under comes from two defenses that stop the run. Arizona allows 3.1 yards a carry and held Cincinnati to 7 and Iowa State to 3. Tech won the 2024 meeting 28-22. The total opened at 52.5, was 55.5 on Monday and is 54.5 now. My 29-25 adds up to 54, a half point below 54.5.

The room is split. Next Man Up has Tech 29-24, on Tech and the Under without backing either. The Formula has Tech 30-25, on Tech and the Over without backing either. Follow The Money likes Arizona +3.5 and likes the Over, and it keeps no score of its own. In The Trenches has no pick, and Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Follow The Money: "Arizona comes in hot, and the price keeps shrinking."

Next Man Up: "Tech is home, and Tech runs the ball. That's the whole story."

The Formula: "Everybody here has Tech winning. We're only arguing about the margin."

Bodie gets the last word, and as usual it's one: "Guns."

Texas Tech Red Raiders 29, Arizona Wildcats 25. Texas Tech Red Raiders cover the 3.5. Under 54.5.
