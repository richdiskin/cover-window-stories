Arizona Wildcats at Texas Tech Red Raiders

Arizona Wildcats (6-1) at Texas Tech Red Raiders (6-1)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Galaxy Stadium · ESPN

Bodie Ratliff answers most questions with one word, and after a loss at Cincinnati the word is "Hungry." Inez Paloverde flew in from Tucson with a potted saguaro and enough sunshine for both sidelines.

No. 22 Arizona (6-1) at No. 13 Texas Tech (6-1), Saturday at 12:00 p.m. ET on ESPN at Galaxy Stadium in Lubbock. It's a Big 12 game between two teams that are both 3-1 in the league. DraftKings has Texas Tech Red Raiders −3.5 with a total of 54.5. My card: Texas Tech Red Raiders −3.5, Under 54.5, and a final of Texas Tech Red Raiders 29, Arizona Wildcats 25.

Inez speaks for Arizona, and she talks faster than a dust devil. "Noah Fifita went 27 of 34 for 308 yards against Iowa State, and we won 39-3. He has 18 touchdown passes and four interceptions. Our defense is a cactus, honey. Pretty from a distance, and you do not want to touch it."

Bodie speaks for Texas Tech. He tips his hat, studies the sky and says, "Wind." Inez keeps staring, so he adds a little more. "J'Koby Williams. 619 yards. A.J. Holmes Jr. 6.5 sacks. Austin Romaine. 53 tackles. Cincinnati was one bad night."

Why Texas Tech? Home field and a run game. Tech gains 5.0 yards a carry and gives up just 3.5. Arizona's offense is Fifita's arm, and that arm now has to work in front of a Lubbock crowd that watched its team lose 37-24 last week. The market has drifted toward Arizona: DraftKings had Tech −4.5 on Monday, and now it's 3.5. My final has Tech by four, so I'll take the cheaper price.

The worry is Will Hammond. He's 139 of 205 for 1,561 yards, but he has six touchdown passes and six interceptions, and he went 24 of 37 with no touchdowns at Cincinnati. If he hands Fifita short fields, Inez will be watering that cactus with joy by halftime.

Why the Under? These are two defenses that stop the run. Arizona allows 16.7 points a game and 3.1 yards a carry, and it held Cincinnati to 7 and Iowa State to 3. Tech allows 20.4 and held Colorado to 7. The last time these two met, in 2024, Tech won 28-22. My 29-25 adds up to 54, a half point under 54.5.

Now the room. Next Man Up is with me all the way, Tech 29-24 and the Under. The Formula likes Tech at 30-25 but goes Over. Follow The Money reads the slide toward Arizona, so it takes Arizona +3.5 and the Over. In The Trenches sits this one out, and Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Follow The Money started it: "Four points of movement, Next Man Up. Somebody knows something."

Next Man Up shrugged: "Somebody knows Tech lost on the road. I know Tech is home."

The Formula settled it: "We all have Tech winning. The only argument is by how much."

Bottom of the card: a home team with one loss, a ground game that eats the clock, and two defenses that make this total a little rich. Bodie says one word. "Guns." I'll take Tech and the Under.

Texas Tech Red Raiders 29, Arizona Wildcats 25. Texas Tech Red Raiders cover the 3.5. Under 54.5.
