Saturday Noon in Lubbock: Fifita's Wildcats Bring the Desert to a Red Raider Team Looking to Bounce Back

Arizona Wildcats (6-1) at Texas Tech Red Raiders (6-1)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Galaxy Stadium · ESPN

No. 22 Arizona (6-1, 3-1 Big 12) at No. 13 Texas Tech (6-1, 3-1 Big 12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12 p.m. ET, Galaxy Stadium, Lubbock, Texas, ESPN.

Your hosts are Inez Paloverde, an Arizona fan and desert-sunrise optimist who will defend this team to the last saguaro, and Bodie Ratliff, a Texas Tech bareback rider who answers most questions in one word.

Inez: Hi, hi, hi, Bodie! Happy Halloween! I brought you a cactus. It's a dry hype, get it?

Bodie: Thanks.

Inez: That's it? I fly all the way to Lubbock and I get one word?

Bodie: Windy.

Players to watch

Arizona quarterback Noah Fifita is 167 of 225 for 2,064 yards, 18 touchdowns and four interceptions. Wide receiver Isaiah Mizell leads the Wildcats with 27 catches for 493 yards and five touchdowns. Kedrick Reescano leads the rushers with 303 yards and five scores. On defense, linebacker Chase Kennedy has four sacks and linebacker Taye Brown leads the team with 42 tackles.

Texas Tech quarterback Will Hammond is 139 of 205 for 1,561 yards, six touchdowns and six interceptions. Running back J'Koby Williams is the workhorse, with 619 yards and five touchdowns on 86 carries. Wide receiver Kenny Johnson has 31 catches for 499 yards. Defensive lineman A.J. Holmes Jr. leads the team with 6.5 sacks, and linebacker Austin Romaine leads with 53 tackles.

Inez: Eighteen touchdown passes! Noah throws the ball the way the sun comes up over the Catalinas. Every single day, and beautifully.

Bodie: Sunburn.

What happened last week

Arizona rolled past Iowa State 39-3 at home on Oct. 24. Fifita went 27 of 34 for 308 yards and two touchdowns, Mizell caught eight passes for 134 yards and a score, and Quincy Craig ran nine times for 51 yards and a touchdown. The Wildcat defense allowed just one field goal.

Texas Tech had its first loss of the season, 37-24 at Cincinnati on Oct. 24. Hammond went 24 of 37 for 227 yards, Williams ran 12 times for 81 yards, and Johnson caught seven passes for 81 yards.

Bodie: Lesson.

Inez: That was almost a sentence, Bodie. I'm so proud of you.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and the teams' box scores don't mark starters. Fifita has thrown the most passes for Arizona in all seven games, and Hammond has done the same for Texas Tech, so check the teams' pregame notes on Saturday morning.

What the coaches are up to

Joey McGuire was hired at Texas Tech on Nov. 8, 2021. Last season was the best in school history: the Red Raiders went 12-2, beat BYU 34-7 to win the Big 12 championship and made their first College Football Playoff. It was the program's first outright conference title since 1955. This year's team is built on the ground and on defense, and it allows 20.4 points a game.

Brent Brennan was hired at Arizona on Jan. 16, 2024. He went 4-8 in his first season, then turned it around with a 9-4 year in 2025 that ended in the Holiday Bowl. His 2026 Wildcats score 36.1 points a game and allow just 16.7.

Inez: Coach Brennan took a seedling and grew a whole desert garden. Bear Down!

Bodie: Respect.

Player storylines

The matchup to circle is Fifita against a Texas Tech defense that has 6.5 sacks from Holmes alone. If the Red Raider big guys up front can get to Fifita, they can slow the Arizona passing game. On the other side, Williams and the Tech ground game face an Arizona defense that held Cincinnati to 7 points and Iowa State to 3. Whichever running game finds room first could decide the afternoon.

Bodie: Trenches.

What's happening on campus

It's Halloween in Lubbock, and the noon kickoff means plenty of time for costumes after the final whistle. Expect plenty of "Guns Up" signs from the Red Raider faithful.

Anything else fun and true

Arizona's rallying cry, "Bear Down," goes back to October 1926 and John "Button" Salmon, the student-body president and the Wildcats' quarterback. Before he died after a car accident, he asked coach J.F. "Pop" McKale to tell the team to "bear down," and it has been the school's motto ever since. These two have split their last two meetings: Arizona won 28-14 in 2019, and Texas Tech won 28-22 in 2024.

Inez: Bear Down, friends! And Happy Halloween to everybody in Lubbock.

Bodie: Guns up.

Inez: Two words! It's a Halloween miracle.

My call: Texas Tech Red Raiders 29, Arizona Wildcats 25.
