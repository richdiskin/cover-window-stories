Arizona vs Texas Tech: Haunted Hollow Parade Meets Bear Down

Arizona Wildcats (6-1) at Texas Tech Red Raiders (6-1)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Galaxy Stadium · ESPN

Arizona Wildcats vs Texas Tech Red Raiders kicks off Week 9's Halloween noon ET window at Galaxy Stadium, one hour after Lubbock's first-ever community Halloween parade, Haunted Hollow on Broadway, rolls through downtown. Tech fan Bodie Ratliff, a bareback rider who talks in single words, has to choose between the floats and his seat, while Arizona fan Inez Paloverde says Noah Fifita and No. 22 Arizona came to No. 13 Texas Tech to spoil the costume party.

Inez: Happy Halloween, Bodie! Your town finally got a parade, and you're going to miss it for football. I respect that.

Bodie: Priorities.

The parade is a genuine first for Lubbock. It steps off at 11 a.m. ET near Avenue A and East Broadway with floats, music and candy, and it ends at Mae Simmons Park. Kickoff is noon ET, so anybody who wants both had better wear running shoes under the costume.

Inez: I'll take a parade float home in my carry-on. Tucson's own big night is the All Souls Procession on Nov. 8, when the whole Westside walks from Barrio Hollywood to the Mercado District and burns the urn at the finale. I wrote a note for your Red Raiders to go in it. It says "Bear Down."

Bodie: Rude.

Both teams come in at 6-1 and 3-1 in the Big 12, and they took opposite roads last Saturday. Arizona crushed Iowa State 39-3 at home. Fifita went 27 of 34 for 308 yards and two touchdowns, Isaiah Mizell caught eight balls for 134 yards and a score, and Quincy Craig ran for a touchdown. The Wildcat defense allowed one field goal.

Texas Tech suffered its first loss, 37-24 at Cincinnati. Will Hammond went 24 of 37 for 227 yards, J'Koby Williams ran 12 times for 81 yards, and Kenny Johnson caught seven balls for 81 yards.

Bodie: Lesson.

Inez: That's almost a whole sentence. I'm so proud of you.

Fifita's season has been steady: 167 of 225 for 2,064 yards, 18 touchdowns and four interceptions. Mizell has 27 catches for 493 yards and five scores, and Kedrick Reescano leads the rushers with 303 yards and five touchdowns. Chase Kennedy has four sacks, and Taye Brown leads the team with 42 tackles.

Tech wins on the ground and on defense. Williams has 619 yards and five touchdowns on 86 carries, and Johnson has 31 catches for 499 yards. Hammond is 139 of 205 for 1,561 yards with six touchdowns and six interceptions. A.J. Holmes Jr. leads the defense with 6.5 sacks, and Austin Romaine leads it with 53 tackles. The Red Raiders allow 20.4 points a game, and they held Colorado to 7.

Inez: Our defense is a cactus, Bodie. Pretty from a distance, and you don't want to touch it. We allow 16.7 points a game.

Bodie: Spines.

The Big 12's initial availability report, posted at 10 p.m. ET Wednesday, lists four Arizona players out, one doubtful and seven questionable, and one Texas Tech player out, six questionable and four probable. Neither Fifita nor Hammond is on it. Fifita has thrown the most passes for Arizona in all seven games, and Hammond has done the same for Tech.

The coaching stories are big ones. Joey McGuire, hired on Nov. 8, 2021, is coming off the best season in Texas Tech history: 12-2, a 34-7 win over BYU for the Big 12 championship, the first outright conference title since 1955 and the first College Football Playoff trip. Brent Brennan arrived at Arizona on Jan. 16, 2024, went 4-8, then 9-4 with a Holiday Bowl trip in 2025. His 2026 Wildcats score 36.1 points a game.

Inez: Coach Brennan planted a seedling and grew a whole desert garden.

Bodie: Watered.

Arizona's rallying cry has a history worth telling on a day for remembering. In October 1926, student-body president and quarterback John "Button" Salmon was dying after a car accident and asked coach J.F. "Pop" McKale to tell the team to "bear down." It has been the school's motto ever since. Lubbock's Día de los Muertos gathering at Resthaven runs the same Saturday, with mariachis, pan dulce and a community ofrenda.

These two have split their last two meetings. Arizona won 28-14 in 2019, and Texas Tech won 28-22 in 2024.

Inez: Bear Down, Lubbock. Enjoy the parade, and save me some candy.

Bodie: Guns up.

Inez: Two words! It's a Halloween miracle.

My call: Texas Tech Red Raiders 29, Arizona Wildcats 25.
