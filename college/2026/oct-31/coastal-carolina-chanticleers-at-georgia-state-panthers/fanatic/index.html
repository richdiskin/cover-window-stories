Teal Tide Meets the Big City: Coastal Carolina Visits Georgia State in Atlanta

Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (2-5) at Georgia State Panthers (4-3)

2:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Center Parc Stadium · ESPN+

Coastal Carolina (2-5, 1-2 Sun Belt) at Georgia State (4-3, 1-2 Sun Belt). Saturday, Oct. 31, 2 p.m. ET, Center Parc Stadium, Atlanta, ESPN+.

Your hosts are Goldie Seabrook, a Conway native from a shrimp-boat family who writes like the tide, and Malik Overstreet, a Georgia State fan who knows every MARTA stop and every Panther third-down tendency.

Malik: Goldie! Welcome to the big city. How was the drive?

Goldie: Long, Malik. I hit traffic on the Connector and thought about turning the car into a boat.

Malik: That's the Connector at rush hour. Welcome to my world.

Players to watch

Coastal quarterback Deuce Bailey has thrown the most passes in every game. He's 109 of 204 for 1,638 yards, 16 touchdowns and four interceptions. Dominic Lee-Knicely leads the rushers with 452 yards and three scores, and Colton Hinton leads the receivers with 297 yards and four touchdowns on 13 catches. On defense, linebacker Marcellius Pulliam has 67 tackles and end Ibrahim Diawara has five sacks.

Georgia State quarterback Cameran Brown threw the most passes in each of the first six games. He's 110 of 166 for 1,356 yards, 14 touchdowns and five interceptions, and he threw six touchdowns against Old Dominion. Ayden Pereira threw the most passes last week. Lanear McCrary Jr. leads the rushers with 399 yards and four touchdowns, and Rodshaun Dorsey leads the receivers with 24 catches for 418 yards and eight scores. Linebacker Xavier Esqillion has seven sacks, and Jaydon Hood has 45 tackles.

Goldie: Sixteen touchdown throws for Deuce Bailey, Malik. That's a full net of shrimp.

What happened last week

Coastal had an open date last week. Its last game was a 35-32 loss to App State on Oct. 16, when Bailey threw for 298 yards and three touchdowns, Lee-Knicely ran 18 times for 90 yards, and Robby Washington caught five balls for 118 yards and a score.

Goldie: Low tide. But we had a whole week to rest and get ready.

Georgia State lost 38-32 at Arkansas State on Oct. 24. Pereira threw two touchdowns, Qwantavius Wiggins ran eight times for 99 yards and a score, and Dorsey caught three balls for 85 yards and a touchdown.

Malik: I blamed the traffic on 75/85. Then I moved on.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Bailey has thrown the most passes in every Coastal game, while Brown did for Georgia State in its first six games and Pereira did last week, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Ryan Beard leads Coastal Carolina, and CollegeFootballData dates his hire to Dec. 11, 2025. His defense has 15 sacks, 37 tackles for loss and 14 takeaways.

Dell McGee leads Georgia State, and CFBD dates his hire to Feb. 23, 2024. His offense holds the ball 32:34 a game and runs for 206 yards a game.

Malik: Coach McGee's offense moves like the HOV lane. Smooth and fast.

Goldie: Coach Beard's defense comes in like the tide. Steady and strong.

Player storylines

The matchup to watch is the Georgia State ground game against a Coastal defense that allows 4.8 yards a carry. Coastal's big guys up front will need to slow McCrary and Wiggins. On the other side, Bailey and his receivers want to keep throwing deep, while Esqillion hunts for sack number eight. Georgia State is 3-0 at home this season, so Coastal will have to bring its best.

What's happening on campus

Center Parc Stadium sits about ten minutes from Georgia State's downtown campus and is listed at 25,000 seats. A Halloween afternoon in Atlanta should bring plenty of blue-and-white costumes, and Coastal fans will bring their teal.

Malik: I'm dressing as a MARTA train. I'll arrive on time.

Goldie: I'm dressing as a shrimp boat. I'll arrive whenever the tide says.

Anything else fun and true

Georgia State football was founded in 2010. Coastal plays at Brooks Stadium in Conway, built in 2003, and its teal turf is the tradition Goldie would show you first. These two have met in every season since 2017, and Coastal has won five of those nine games, including a 51-0 win in 2020 and a 40-27 win in 2025. Georgia State won 42-40 in 2021 and 30-17 in 2023.

Goldie: Malik, win or lose, the shrimp are on me after the game. We'll say grace first.

Malik: Deal, Goldie. Let's ride.

My call: Georgia State Panthers 33, Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 25.
