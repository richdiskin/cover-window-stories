Coastal Carolina vs Georgia State: Boo & Brew Blackout in Atlanta

Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (2-5) at Georgia State Panthers (4-3)

2:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Center Parc Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Coastal Carolina Chanticleers vs Georgia State Panthers is Week 9's Boo & Brew Halloween game at Center Parc Credit Union Stadium, where Georgia State wants a blackout crowd dressed in black and lets young Panthers in costume in for free. Goldie Seabrook, a Conway native from a shrimp-boat family who measures everything in tides, says Deuce Bailey and Ryan Beard's Chanticleers own this series, and Malik Overstreet, who knows every MARTA stop and every Panther third-down tendency, says Cameran Brown, Rodshaun Dorsey and Dell McGee's Panthers finally win it at home.

Malik: Goldie, wear black. Georgia State asked the whole stadium for a blackout.

Goldie: I brought teal, Malik. Teal is what the ocean wears at night.

Malik: The ocean is not on the guest list.

Georgia State has built a full afternoon around it. Panther Pregame comes with live music and a pumpkin-carving station, and once fans get inside, kids can trick-or-treat throughout the stadium. There are Halloween giveaways to take home, too.

Downtown will be crowded before the Panthers even kick off. Georgia and Florida bring their rivalry to Mercedes-Benz Stadium this year for the 2026 Invesco QQQ Atlanta Gridiron Classic, with a 3:30 p.m. ET kickoff, and Centennial Yards runs a Rise ATL party for both fan bases from 1 to 4 p.m. ET.

Malik: Two big games downtown on Halloween, Goldie. Take MARTA. Nobody is parking today.

Goldie: I've docked a shrimp boat in a crowded harbor, Malik. I'll find a spot.

The series is the part Goldie wants to talk about. These two have met every season since 2017, and Coastal has won five of the nine, including a 51-0 game in 2020 and a 40-27 win last season. Georgia State's game notes say Saturday is the 10th meeting and the fifth on the Panthers' home field, and the Panthers are still looking for their first home win against Coastal.

Goldie: Five of nine, Malik. The tide comes in on schedule.

Malik: The tide hasn't seen this Parc yet.

He has a point. Georgia State is 4-3 and 1-2 in the Sun Belt, and 3-0 at home after beating North Carolina A&T 59-10, Northern Illinois 35-14 and Old Dominion 42-10 on Homecoming. The Panthers have outscored those three visitors 136-34 and held every one of them to 14 or fewer, per the team's notes. Brown threw six touchdowns against Old Dominion, a program record, and Xavier Esqillion tied the school's single-game record with three sacks that day.

Malik: Six touchdowns, Goldie. That's a whole train car of them.

Brown is 110 of 166 for 1,356 yards, 14 touchdowns and five interceptions. Dorsey has 24 catches for 418 yards and eight scores, Lanear McCrary Jr. has run for 399 yards and four touchdowns, Esqillion has seven sacks and Jaydon Hood has 45 tackles. The Panthers have lost two straight on the road, though, 49-22 at James Madison and 38-32 at Arkansas State, where Ayden Pereira threw the most for Georgia State and had two touchdowns, and Qwantavius Wiggins ran eight times for 99 yards and a score.

Goldie: Low tide for you two weeks running, Malik. It happens to everybody.

Coastal is 2-5 and 1-2 in the league, and it had last week off. Its last game was a 35-32 loss to App State on Oct. 16, when Bailey threw for 298 yards and three touchdowns and Robby Washington caught five balls for 118 yards. Bailey is 109 of 204 for 1,638 yards, 16 touchdowns and four interceptions. Dominic Lee-Knicely has run for 452 yards, and Colton Hinton has 13 catches for 297 yards and four scores. Marcellius Pulliam leads the defense with 67 tackles, and Ibrahim Diawara has five sacks.

The Chanticleers also don't get blown out. Five of their seven games were decided by eight points or fewer, including a 31-24 loss at West Virginia and a 27-21 win at Marshall.

Goldie: We keep it close, then we take it home. Ask anybody in Conway.

The Sun Belt's initial availability report, updated at 8:53 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists Georgia State quarterback Cameran Brown doubtful, with seven Panthers out. Coastal Carolina lists five players out and two questionable.

The matchup is the Georgia State ground game, which gains 5.4 yards a carry per the team's notes, against a Coastal defense that allows 4.8. The Chanticleers will need Pulliam and Diawara to slow McCrary and Wiggins.

Beard is in his first season at Coastal. CollegeFootballData dates his hire to Dec. 11, 2025. McGee was hired on Feb. 23, 2024, and with four wins already, he has matched what his Panthers won across 2024 and 2025 combined.

Malik: I'm dressing as a MARTA train. I'll arrive on time.

Goldie: I'm dressing as a shrimp boat. I'll arrive when the tide says so.

Malik: Goldie, win or lose, I'll ride you back to your car.

Goldie: Deal, Malik. The boiled shrimp are on me, and I brought enough for the blackout section.

My call: Georgia State Panthers 33, Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 25.
