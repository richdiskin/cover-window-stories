Coastal Carolina Chanticleers at Georgia State Panthers

Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (2-5) at Georgia State Panthers (4-3)

2:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Center Parc Stadium · ESPN+

Malik Overstreet steps off the MARTA train at Georgia State station, swipes a Breeze card and finds Goldie Seabrook waiting on the platform with a cooler of boiled shrimp. "Goldie," he says, "you took I-20 in on a Saturday? Brave." Goldie hands him a shrimp. "Malik, I grew up on a shrimp boat. Traffic doesn't scare me. Tides do."

Coastal Carolina (2-5) at Georgia State (4-3), Saturday at 2 p.m. ET on ESPN+ at Center Parc Stadium in Atlanta. It's a Sun Belt game, and both teams are 1-2 in league play. DraftKings has Georgia State Panthers −6.5 with a total of 58.5. My card: Georgia State Panthers −6.5, Under 58.5, and a final of Georgia State Panthers 33, Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 25.

Malik speaks for Georgia State. "Next stop, the end zone. Cameran Brown has 14 touchdown throws. Rodshaun Dorsey has eight touchdown catches. Xavier Esqillion has seven sacks. And at Center Parc we beat North Carolina A&T 59-10, Northern Illinois 35-14 and Old Dominion 42-10. Let's ride."

Goldie speaks for Coastal. "Teal deal, Malik. Deuce Bailey has 16 touchdown throws and four interceptions. Marcellius Pulliam has 67 tackles. Ibrahim Diawara has five sacks. We beat you 40-27 last year, and we've had an extra week to get ready."

Why Georgia State? Because the Panthers are a different team at home. They're 3-0 at Center Parc, and nobody has come close there. Their losses all came on the road. Georgia State scores 35.9 points a game, and In The Trenches likes its run game, 5.4 yards a carry, against a Coastal defense that allows 4.8. My final has the Panthers by more than a touchdown.

The risk is that Coastal plays everybody close. Five of its seven games were decided by eight points or fewer, including a 31-24 loss at West Virginia and a 35-32 loss to App State. Coastal has won five of the nine meetings since 2017. Georgia State's turnover margin is minus-4, and Ayden Pereira threw the most passes in last week's 38-32 loss at Arkansas State, not Brown.

Why the Under? Six of Coastal's seven games stayed under 58.5. The Chanticleers score 26.1 points a game and allow 25.9, and their biggest scoring day was a 35-32 loss. My 33-25 adds up to 58, just under 58.5.

Now the room, and it's split down the tracks. The Formula has Georgia State 31-25, a little short of the 6.5, and sides with Coastal and the Under. Next Man Up has Georgia State 35-24 and likes the Panthers, with its total on the Over. In The Trenches likes Georgia State. Follow The Money skips the side and likes the Over after the total ticked up.

Next Man Up started it: "Panthers by double digits. Malik, find that parking spot."

The Formula disagreed: "Coastal plays close games. I'll take the points, but I'm with you on the Under."

Follow The Money held out: "The total ticked up. Over for me."

Bottom of the card: a Georgia State team that has rolled at home, a Coastal team that keeps games close and rarely turns them into shootouts, and a total that's a hair too high. Malik dances like he found a spot downtown. Give me the Panthers and the Under.

Georgia State Panthers 33, Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 25. Georgia State Panthers cover the 6.5. Under 58.5.
