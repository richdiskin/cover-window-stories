Missouri Tigers at Arkansas Razorbacks

Missouri Tigers (5-2) at Arkansas Razorbacks (3-4)

7:45 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Donald W. Reynolds Razorback Stadium · SEC Network

Delphine Arceneaux-Moss has her fake news ticker warmed up for Fayetteville. Bubba Fayette-Dorsey will call the Hogs before every number, even the ones he doesn't like. Halloween night, a trophy on the sideline, and a border rival in town.

Missouri (5-2) at Arkansas (3-4), Saturday at 7:45 p.m. ET on SEC Network at Donald W. Reynolds Razorback Stadium in Fayetteville. It's an SEC game. DraftKings has Missouri Tigers −10 with a total of 49.5. My card: Arkansas Razorbacks +10, Under 49.5, and a final of Missouri Tigers 27, Arkansas Razorbacks 21.

Delphine speaks for Missouri. "Show me the tape! Breaking news: Jamal Roberts has 867 rushing yards and eight touchdowns. Austin Simmons has 14 touchdown passes and two interceptions. We beat Florida 45-17 and Texas A&M 27-6. Ole Miss, 3-31, we don't talk about."

Bubba speaks for Arkansas. "Woooo, Pig! KJ Jackson has 1,444 passing yards. Chris Marshall has 43 catches for 633 yards, and coach Ryan Silverfield expects him back from a leg injury. We won 31-21 at Vanderbilt. We almost beat Tennessee, 17-24."

Here is the part that matters. Missouri is at its best at home. On the road it won 38-21 at Kansas, but it lost 24-31 at Mississippi State and 3-31 at Ole Miss. Sports Illustrated says Missouri came away with three points from three red zone trips at Ole Miss. Now it goes to a rivalry road game at night.

Why Arkansas? This rivalry stays close. Look at the scores since 2016: 28-24, 48-45, 50-48 and 29-27 were all one-score games, and 2024 was 28-21. Arkansas has a new coach and a team that just won an SEC game on the road. Its last home game was a 17-24 loss to Tennessee, and the Hogs led that game going into the fourth quarter. The Formula and Next Man Up both like Arkansas.

The worry is the run defense. Arkansas gave up 43 at Utah, 45 to Georgia and 34 at Texas A&M. Roberts leads the SEC in rushing, and Silverfield said the Missouri run game gets stronger in the fourth quarter. If Missouri runs it forty times, ten points is not a lot.

Why the Over/Under? Missouri likes to run the ball and run the clock. Arkansas scores 21.0 a game and Missouri allows 19.6. The Formula has 49 and Next Man Up has 46. Follow The Money likes the Over because the total moved from 48.5 to 49.5, and Action Network's opening total was 48.5. My 27-21 adds up to 48, a point and a half under the over/under 49.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Missouri 28-21 and likes Arkansas and the Under. Next Man Up has Missouri 26-20 and likes Arkansas and the Under. Follow The Money has no side and likes the Over. In The Trenches has no pick this week. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "No official report yet. Marshall back helps the Hogs."

The Formula: "Twenty-eight to twenty-one. The points are enough."

Follow The Money: "The total moved one point. I follow it."

Bottom of the card: Missouri should keep the trophy, but Arkansas keeps it inside ten. Delphine gets her headline. Bubba gets a fourth quarter worth calling the Hogs for.

Missouri Tigers 27, Arkansas Razorbacks 21. Arkansas Razorbacks cover the 10. Under 49.5.
