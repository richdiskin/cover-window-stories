Missouri vs Arkansas: Homecoming Hogs Host a Halloween Night Visit

Missouri Tigers (5-2) at Arkansas Razorbacks (3-4)

7:45 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Donald W. Reynolds Razorback Stadium · SEC Network

Missouri Tigers vs Arkansas Razorbacks is a Week 9 Halloween night at Donald W. Reynolds Razorback Stadium in Fayetteville, the last stop of the University of Arkansas' 105th annual Homecoming Week, with kickoff at 7:45 p.m. ET on SEC Network. Delphine Arceneaux-Moss, a Missouri journalism-school style reporter who breaks obvious news with a fake ticker sound, says Jamal Roberts, Austin Simmons and Daeden Hopkins keep the trophy in Columbia, and Bubba Fayette-Dorsey, an Ozark grade-school science teacher who calls the Hogs before every stat, says KJ Jackson, Chris Marshall and Quincy Rhodes Jr. bring it home.

Delphine: Ding-ding-ding! Breaking news, Bubba: it is Halloween.

Bubba: Woooo, Pig! Breaking news back at you. It's our Homecoming.

Delphine: Ding-ding! Also breaking: water is wet.

Arkansas is throwing a party. The school calls Razorback Stadium "the biggest trick-or-treat location in the State of Arkansas" for this one. Fans of all ages can trick-or-treat from the tailgate lots to inside the stadium, the first 300 kids in costume get a free hot dog and drink, and a Halloween laser show takes over halftime. The Hog Walk is at 5:15 p.m. ET, gates open at 6:15 p.m. ET, and the Trick-or-Treat Trailgating Parade rolls from the Upper Ramble to Maple Street at 6:45 p.m. ET. Friday night, the stadium gets a red glow. It's the first time since 2015 that the Hogs have played in Fayetteville on Halloween.

Bubba: Free hot dogs for the first 300 kids. I'm going as a kid.

Delphine: Ding! Breaking news: you are a grown man, Bubba.

Bubba: Science says I'm young at heart. Woooo, Pig!

The history belongs mostly to Missouri. The Tigers lead the series 13-4, this is the 18th meeting and the sixth in Fayetteville, and it's the first night game between these teams in Arkansas. Missouri has won the last four, including 31-17 last year. Arkansas last won 34-17 on Nov. 26, 2021. The games keep getting tight, though: 28-24 in 2016, 48-45 in 2017, 50-48 in 2020, 29-27 in 2022 and 28-21 in 2024.

Missouri is 5-2 and 2-2 in the SEC. Roberts leads the SEC in rushing with 867 yards and eight touchdowns on 167 carries. Simmons, an Ole Miss transfer, has thrown for 1,773 yards, 14 touchdowns and two interceptions. Cayden Lee has 47 catches for 678 yards, Hopkins has seven sacks and Nicholas Rodriguez has 76 tackles. The Tigers beat Florida 45-17 and Texas A&M 27-6 at home, then lost 31-3 at Ole Miss on Oct. 17, when Simmons threw for 339 yards and Donovan Olugbode caught nine balls for 127. Simmons keeps it calm: "It's just another game for me."

Delphine: Ding-ding! Eight hundred sixty-seven yards. That's a headline, Bubba.

Arkansas is 3-4 and 1-3 in the league. Jackson, from Montgomery, Ala., has completed 131 of 215 throws for 1,444 yards and seven touchdowns, and he leads the team with three rushing touchdowns. Marshall has 43 catches for 633 yards, second in the SEC in both. Rhodes, a North Little Rock native, leads the defense with 5.5 tackles for loss and three sacks, and Ja'Quavion Smith has 48 tackles. The Hogs won 31-21 at Vanderbilt on Oct. 17, their first SEC win in two years, when Jackson ran for a touchdown and Warren native Antonio Jordan had career highs with five catches for 74 yards.

Bubba: Five catches for a Warren kid! My whole fourth-grade class made posters.

The coaches have Arkansas roots on both sides. Eli Drinkwitz grew up in Alma, Ark., and said last fall, "I think the SEC is better when Arkansas is good." Ryan Silverfield was hired the morning after last year's game and is looking for his first win over Missouri. "Having faced Missouri in the past, we know that they're going to be sound and well-coached," he said.

Delphine: Ding! Breaking news: Drinkwitz is being nice to you.

Bubba: He grew up in Alma. He knows how to call the Hogs.

The SEC's initial availability report, posted at 8:10 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists three Missouri players out and four Arkansas players out, with Chris Marshall questionable.

Delphine: I'm dressing as a news anchor. Same as every day, but with a cape.

Bubba: I'm going as a Razorback, snout and all, for the laser show.

Delphine: If Missouri wins, you read my ticker on air for a week.

Bubba: If Arkansas wins, you call the Hogs from the top row. Loud.

Delphine: Ding-ding! Deal.

My call: Missouri Tigers 27, Arkansas Razorbacks 21.
