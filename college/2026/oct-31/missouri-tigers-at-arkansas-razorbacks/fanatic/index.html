A Border Rivalry on Halloween Night: Missouri Visits Arkansas

Missouri Tigers (5-2) at Arkansas Razorbacks (3-4)

7:45 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Donald W. Reynolds Razorback Stadium · SEC Network

No. 23 Missouri (5-2, 2-2 SEC) at Arkansas (3-4, 1-3 SEC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 7:45 p.m. ET (6:45 p.m. Central), Donald W. Reynolds Razorback Stadium, Fayetteville, Ark., SEC Network.

Your hosts are Delphine Arceneaux-Moss, a Missouri journalism-school style reporter who breaks obvious news with a fake ticker sound, and Bubba Fayette-Dorsey, an Ozark grade-school science teacher who calls the Hogs before every stat.

Delphine: Show me the tape! Breaking news, Bubba: it is Halloween, and there is a trophy on the field.

Bubba: Woooo, Pig! Breaking news back at you, Delphine. It's our house tonight.

Players to watch

Missouri quarterback Austin Simmons, an Ole Miss transfer, is 132 of 209 for 1,773 yards, 14 touchdowns and two interceptions. Running back Jamal Roberts leads the SEC in rushing with 867 yards and eight touchdowns on 167 carries. Wide receiver Cayden Lee has 47 catches for 678 yards and five touchdowns. On defense, Daeden Hopkins has seven sacks, and linebacker Nicholas Rodriguez has 76 tackles.

Arkansas quarterback KJ Jackson is 131 of 215 for 1,444 yards, seven touchdowns and four interceptions. Wide receiver Chris Marshall has 43 catches for 633 yards, second in the SEC in both. Braylen Russell leads the running backs with 205 yards. On defense, linebacker Ja'Quavion Smith has 48 tackles, and Quincy Rhodes Jr. has three sacks.

Bubba: Woooo, Pig! Seven sacks for Hopkins. Our quarterback should keep his eyes up.

What happened last week

Both teams had the week off on Oct. 24.

Missouri's last game was a 31-3 loss at Ole Miss on Oct. 17. Simmons threw for 339 yards, and Donovan Olugbode caught nine passes for 127 yards. The Tigers got into the red zone three times and came away with three points.

Arkansas' last game was a 31-21 win at Vanderbilt on Oct. 17, its first SEC win in two years. Jackson went 21 of 31 for 251 yards and ran for a touchdown. Antonio Jordan, a Warren, Ark., native, had career highs with five catches for 74 yards.

Delphine: Breaking news: the Hogs are feeling good.

Injuries and availability

No official SEC availability report had been posted as of Tuesday. Silverfield said Marshall, who hurt his leg at Vanderbilt, is expected to play. Arkansas center Caden Kitler, a team captain, is listed as the starter, though Kash Courtney started at Vanderbilt. Missouri coach Eli Drinkwitz said wide receiver Blair is back from a hamstring injury and will be fully available. Missouri running back Ahmad Hardy has been sidelined since an offseason accident.

What the coaches are up to

Drinkwitz grew up in Alma, Ark., and has led Missouri since 2020. "I think the SEC is better when Arkansas is good," he said last fall.

Ryan Silverfield became the 35th head coach in Arkansas history the morning after last year's game. He coached Memphis against Missouri in 2023. "We know that they're going to be sound and well-coached," he said. He praised the Missouri wide zone running game and said it gets stronger in the fourth quarter.

Player storylines

Simmons faced his old school, Ole Miss, two weeks ago, and he keeps it simple. "It's just another game for me," he said. Roberts has carried the load with Hardy out. For Arkansas, Jordan showed he can make plays when Marshall is out, and now the Hogs may have both.

What's happening on campus

Razorback Stadium opened in 1938 and seats 80,000. Fans will call the Hogs, the band is known as the Best in Sight and Sound, and the fight song is "Arkansas Fight." Arkansas claims a 1964 national title.

Missouri plays at Memorial Stadium in Columbia, with Faurot Field and Marching Mizzou. The fight song is "Every True Son, Fight Tiger," and the Tigers claim a 1960 national title. This is the first SEC night game Arkansas has hosted since Oct. 19, 2024.

Anything else fun and true

The winner takes home the rivalry trophy. Missouri won the last four meetings, including 31-17 last year in Fayetteville. Arkansas last won in 2021, 34-17. Eight of the 16 games in the series were decided by one score. Only one first-year Arkansas coach has beaten Missouri: Glen Rose, 7-6 in St. Louis in 1944.
