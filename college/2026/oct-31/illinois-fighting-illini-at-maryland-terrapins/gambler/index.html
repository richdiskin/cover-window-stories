Illinois Fighting Illini at Maryland Terrapins

Illinois Fighting Illini (4-3) at Maryland Terrapins (2-5)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · SECU Stadium · FS1

Margery Vandermolen sets up an easel outside SECU Stadium and unveils a hand-drawn chart titled "Why Orange Wins, in Four Colors." Abby Chesapeake walks right up, shakes Old Bay on the corner of it, and says, "There. Now it's seasoned."

Illinois (4-3) at Maryland (2-5), Saturday at 12:00 p.m. ET on FS1 at SECU Stadium in College Park. Maryland is 0-4 in Big Ten play. DraftKings has Illinois Fighting Illini −2.5 with a total of 54.5. My card: Illinois Fighting Illini −2.5, Over 54.5, and a final of Illinois Fighting Illini 33, Maryland Terrapins 27.

Margery speaks for Illinois, pointer in hand. "Class, we just beat Oregon 21-10. Aidan Laughery ran for 123 yards. Collin Dixon has 54 catches and 10 touchdowns. And we won 38-37 in overtime at Michigan State. Oskee-wow-wow!"

Abby speaks for Maryland, cracking a crab for emphasis. "Margery, honey, we had a week off to rest. Malik Washington threw for 359 yards against Virginia Tech and 347 at UConn. Chris Durr Jr. has 52 catches. Zion Elee has 3.5 sacks. Old Bay on that!"

Why Illinois? Because Maryland's defense has been giving up points in bunches. The Terps allow 33.3 a game, and they gave up 54 to UCLA, 48 at Nebraska, 45 at Ohio State and 37 to Rutgers. Illinois runs for 5.2 yards a carry and scores 30.3 points a game, and it has only lost to Duke, Ohio State and Purdue. Last year Illinois won this matchup 24-6. My final has Illinois by six, and that clears the 2.5.

The worry is the Illinois quarterback spot. Katin Houser threw the most passes in the first six games, with 12 touchdowns and four interceptions, but Michael Clayton II threw the most against Oregon, 11 of 18 for 84 yards. If Illinois can't throw, Maryland's run game gets 4.5 yards a carry against an Illini defense that allows 4.0, and the extra rest could tip a close game.

Why the Over? Six of Maryland's seven games have finished above 54.5 total points, and five of Illinois' seven have too. Maryland throws it around, scores 28.3 points a game, and allows 33.3. Illinois allows 25.3. My 33-27 adds up to 60, five and a half points over 54.5.

Now the room, and it's a cozy one. The Formula has Illinois 33-26, takes Illinois −2.5 and the Over. Next Man Up has Illinois 32-28, also Illinois and the Over. Follow The Money saw the total climb, so it takes the Over and skips the side. In The Trenches says the run-game gaps cancel, and Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up started it: "Three Overs in one room. Abby's going to run out of Old Bay."

Follow The Money nodded: "The market went up two points on the total. I'm riding it."

The Formula pointed at Margery's easel: "Even her chart says Over. In four colors."

Bottom of the card: a Maryland defense that has allowed 37 or more four times, an Illinois offense that runs the ball and scores, and three Overs in the room. Margery gets her oskee-wow-wow. Give me the Illini and the Over.

Illinois Fighting Illini 33, Maryland Terrapins 27. Illinois Fighting Illini cover the 2.5. Over 54.5.
