Charts Meet Crabs in College Park: Illinois Brings a Big Win Over Oregon to Maryland on Halloween

Illinois Fighting Illini (4-3) at Maryland Terrapins (2-5)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · SECU Stadium · FS1

Illinois (4-3, 2-2 Big Ten) at Maryland (2-5, 0-4 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12 p.m. ET, SECU Stadium, College Park, Md., FS1.

Your hosts are Margery Vandermolen, a prairie-state numbers whiz who explains football like a beloved teacher, with charts, and Abigail "Abby" Chesapeake, a D.C.-suburb networker who cracks crabs on air and puts Old Bay on everything.

Margery: Good afternoon, class! Orange you glad it's Saturday?

Abby: Margery, sweetie, I'm glad it's Saturday because I brought a whole bushel. Old Bay on that!

Players to watch

Illinois quarterback Katin Houser threw the most passes in the first six Illini games. He's 122 of 184 for 1,440 yards, 12 touchdowns and four interceptions. Michael Clayton II threw the most in the win over Oregon, going 11 of 18 with two touchdown throws. Wide receiver Collin Dixon is the go-to target, with 54 catches for 678 yards and 10 touchdowns. Aidan Laughery leads the rushers with 514 yards. On defense, defensive back Matthew Bailey has 49 tackles.

Maryland quarterback Malik Washington has thrown the most passes in all seven Terrapin games. He's 155 of 261 for 1,615 yards, nine touchdowns and six interceptions. Chris Durr Jr. leads the receivers with 52 catches for 580 yards. DeJuan Williams leads the rushers with 281 yards and three scores. Linebacker Daniel Wingate has 44 tackles, and linebacker Zion Elee has 3.5 sacks.

Margery: Ten touchdown catches for Dixon. I made a chart, and the column went right off the top of the poster board.

What happened last week

Illinois beat Oregon 21-10 at home on Oct. 24. Laughery ran 13 times for 123 yards and a touchdown, and Dixon caught six passes for 65 yards and a score.

Margery: Oskee-wow-wow! I hugged three strangers and a mascot.

Maryland didn't play last week. Its last game was a 37-30 loss to Rutgers on Oct. 17, when Washington threw for 263 yards and two touchdowns and Durr caught six passes for 104 yards.

Abby: A week off means a week of rest, a week of practice and a week of crab cakes. All three matter.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Houser threw the most passes for Illinois in six games, but Clayton did against Oregon, so watch the teams' pregame notes for who takes the first snap. Washington has thrown the most passes in every Maryland game.

What the coaches are up to

Bret Bielema was named the 26th head coach in Illinois history on Dec. 19, 2020. He's an Illinois native, and one of his early wins was a nine-overtime thriller at Penn State in 2021, the longest game in college football history. His team has already won 38-37 in overtime at Michigan State this year.

Maryland has new leadership this week. Mike Locksley stepped down as head coach last week, and co-defensive coordinator Aazaar Abdul-Rahim was named interim head coach. Abdul-Rahim is a Washington, D.C., native in his second stint with the Terps, and Saturday is his first game in charge. This year's Terps score 28.3 points a game, including a 62-0 win over Hampton and a 38-14 win at UConn.

Abby: Coach Abdul-Rahim is a D.C. guy, and I know everybody in D.C. We should start a networking breakfast.

Player storylines

The matchup to circle is Dixon against a Maryland defense that allows 33.3 points a game. On the other side, Washington loves to throw it, and he went 30 of 44 for 359 yards and three touchdowns against Virginia Tech. Both teams run the ball well, too. Illinois gains 5.2 yards a carry and Maryland gains 4.5, so the big guys up front on both sides will be busy.

What's happening on campus

SECU Stadium opened in 1950 and seats 51,802. The Mighty Sound of Maryland band will be on hand, and if you visit, ask somebody about Testudo, the diamondback terrapin who's at the heart of campus tradition. A Halloween noon kickoff should mean plenty of turtle shells in the stands.

Abby: I'm going as a crab. Obviously. Seasoned.

Anything else fun and true

Illinois is a founding member of the Big Ten and claims five national championships, from 1914 to 1951. On Oct. 18, 1924, Harold "Red" Grange scored four touchdowns against Michigan in the first 12 minutes of the Memorial Stadium dedication game. These two teams have split their last four meetings, and Illinois won 24-6 last year. Back in 2018, Maryland won a wild one, 63-33.
