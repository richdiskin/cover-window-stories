Army Black Knights at Memphis Tigers

Army Black Knights (5-2) at Memphis Tigers (6-2)

8:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium · ESPNU

Army vs Memphis prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on Army Black Knights +6 and Under 59.5 at DraftKings, with a final of Memphis Tigers 31, Army Black Knights 28. It's Homecoming and Hero Day in Memphis on Halloween night, the first meeting between these two since two thousand two.

The case for Army is the clock and the kicker. The Black Knights run the option, keep the ball and score 35.3 a game. They just won 38-35 at Tulsa on Dawson Jones's fifty-yard field goal as time expired, their third straight win. Their two losses were 28-24 to South Florida and 31-29 at Louisiana Tech. My final has Memphis by 3, inside the 6.

Otis Delaney-Crum, a Memphis blues guitarist who sings bass in his gospel choir, makes the Memphis case. "Marcus Stokes has 21 touchdown throws. We came back from two scores down to beat East Carolina in overtime. And we've got fireworks now, Captain."

Capt. (Ret.) Thaddeus Rourke, retired Army logistics, answers with a mission brief. "Cale Hellums has 840 rushing yards and 16 rushing touchdowns. Godspower Nwawuihe ran for 135 at Tulsa. We keep your offense on the bench."

What could go wrong is Memphis's speed. The Tigers score 36.1 a game, Stokes has 2,161 yards and Jaylin Carter has 833 on the ground. Hunter Tipton has 30 catches for 409 yards. Army allows 22.1 a game, and if Stokes hits early, Army has to throw, which it doesn't want to do.

The Under is about Army's pace. Hellums ran 30 times at Tulsa, and long runs keep the clock moving. Memphis defensive coordinator Lance Guidry also saw the option in two thousand twenty-one, when his Marshall team beat Navy forty-nine to seven. Army's wins over Temple and Tulane finished 21-17 and 20-17. My 31-28 adds up to 59, just under the 59.5.
