Army Black Knights at Memphis Tigers

Army Black Knights (5-2) at Memphis Tigers (6-2)

8:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium · ESPNU

Capt. Thaddeus Rourke has his boots shined and his mission brief typed, and Otis Delaney-Crum has a new riff ready to hum. A retired logistics officer and a Memphis blues man meet on Halloween night, and both expect a long, slow song.

Army (5-2) at Memphis (6-2), Saturday at 8:00 p.m. ET on ESPNU at Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium in Memphis. It's an American game. DraftKings has Memphis Tigers −6 with a total of 59.5. My card: Army Black Knights +6, Under 59.5, and a final of Memphis Tigers 31, Army Black Knights 28.

Thaddeus speaks for Army. "Mission brief: run the ball, protect the ball. We beat Tulsa 38-35 on a field goal at the gun. Godspower Nwawuihe ran 12 times for 135 yards. Cale Hellums has 840 rushing yards and 16 rushing touchdowns. Outstanding execution, at least in the fourth quarter."

Otis speaks for Memphis. "We beat East Carolina 41-34 in overtime, and Marcus Stokes threw for 320 yards. He has 21 touchdown passes. That drive had a backbeat, friends. But Army doesn't let you play many songs."

Here is the part that matters. Army's two losses were 24-28 to South Florida and 29-31 at Louisiana Tech. The Black Knights have not lost by more than four all season. They beat Tulane 20-17, the same Tulane team that beat Memphis 31-17. Army keeps games close because it keeps the ball.

Why Army? Because six points is a lot against a team that runs this well. In The Trenches has Army at 6.4 yards a carry against a Memphis defense that allows 4.7. That is the bigger run gap in this game, and In The Trenches likes Army. Next Man Up likes Army too and has it 31-29. The Formula has Memphis by four. My final has Memphis by three.

The worry is Stokes. He threw for 384 yards at Boise State and 318 at Charlotte. Memphis scores 36.1 points a game, and if the Tigers get ahead early, Army's offense isn't built to chase.

Why the Over/Under? Army's games against Tulane, Temple and South Florida ended 20-17, 21-17 and 24-28. The option eats the clock and keeps the other offense on the bench. Memphis wants to play fast, but it can't play fast without the ball. The Formula and In The Trenches like the Under. My 31-28 adds up to 59, just under the over/under 59.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Memphis 31-27 and has Army and the Under. Next Man Up has Memphis 31-29 and likes Army and the Over. Follow The Money sees the total climbing and likes the Over, with no side. In The Trenches likes Army and the Under. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

In The Trenches: "Army runs it, Army covers it."

Next Man Up: "Two points either way. Take the six."

Follow The Money: "The total moved three points. I'm with the Over."

Bottom of the card: Army plays close games, and six points is plenty of room. Thaddeus will call it a mission accomplished. Otis will still hum a riff.

Memphis Tigers 31, Army Black Knights 28. Army Black Knights cover the 6. Under 59.5.
