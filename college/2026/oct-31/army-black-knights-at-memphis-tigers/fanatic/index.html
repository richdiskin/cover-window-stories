Halloween Night in Memphis: Army Marches Into the Bluff City

Army Black Knights (5-2) at Memphis Tigers (6-2)

8:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium · ESPNU

Army (5-2, 4-1 American) at Memphis (6-2, 3-1 American). Saturday, Oct. 31, 8:00 p.m. ET (7:00 p.m. Central), Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium, Memphis, Tenn., ESPNU.

Your hosts are Capt. (Ret.) Thaddeus Rourke, a retired Army logistics officer who reads every option play like a supply chain, and Otis Delaney-Crum, a Memphis blues guitarist who sings bass in his gospel choir.

Otis: Well, hello there, friends. Welcome to Memphis, Captain. I'll hum you a riff when we score.

Thaddeus: Attention on deck. I've got my boots shined, Otis. Mission brief: run the ball, protect the ball.

Otis: Brother, that's not a song. That's a march.

Players to watch

Army quarterback Cale Hellums is the engine of the option. He has 159 carries for 840 yards and 16 rushing touchdowns, and he has completed 39 of 69 throws for 422 yards and three touchdowns. Brady Anderson leads the team with 10 catches for 163 yards. On defense, linebacker Elijah Walton has 52 tackles and linebacker Victor Afolabi has three sacks.

Memphis quarterback Marcus Stokes is 150 of 224 for 2,161 yards, 21 touchdowns and six interceptions. Running back Jaylin Carter has 128 carries for 833 yards and eight touchdowns. Tight end Hunter Tipton leads the receivers with 30 catches for 409 yards and five touchdowns. On defense, Elijah Kinsler has 5.5 sacks and linebacker Mike Montgomery has 58 tackles.

Thaddeus: Sixteen rushing touchdowns from the quarterback. Outstanding.

What happened last week

Army won 38-35 at Tulsa on Friday night, Oct. 23, for its third win in a row. The Black Knights trailed 35-28 in the fourth quarter. Hellums tied it with a 1-yard run with 1:50 left, and Dawson Jones kicked a 50-yard field goal as time ran out. Jones also made one from 51, and the school says he's the first Army kicker to make two field goals of 50 or more in one game. Hellums ran for 121 yards and three touchdowns, and Godspower Nwawuihe ran 12 times for 135 yards, including a 66-yard touchdown.

Memphis beat East Carolina 41-34 in overtime on Thursday, Oct. 22. Stokes threw for 320 yards and accounted for all five touchdowns, three throwing and two running. Jemyri Davis caught seven balls for 91 yards and two scores. The win made Memphis bowl-eligible for the 13th straight season.

Otis: Five touchdowns from one young man. That drive had a backbeat.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report had been posted for this game as of Tuesday. Early injury pages show no reported injuries for either team. Watch for the official report closer to kickoff.

What the coaches are up to

Jeff Monken has led Army since 2014, and he coached Georgia Southern before that. After the Tulsa win, he said the Black Knights still need to improve, especially after a rough third quarter. Offensive coordinator Cody Worley even called a trick play at Tulsa, a 45-yard throw from slotback Zach Mundell to Anderson.

Charles Huff is in his first season at Memphis after coaching Southern Miss and Marshall. His offense moves the ball on the ground and through the air, and Stokes has thrown at least two touchdowns in all but one game.

Player storylines

Hellums scored twice on fourth-and-1 at Tulsa, including a 41-yard run on the opening drive. Army went 3-for-4 on fourth down that night. For Memphis, Stokes threw for 384 yards at Boise State and 318 at Charlotte.

What's happening on campus

Memphis has a long football story that began in 1912, and the Tigers have been bowl-eligible every year since 2014. Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium opened in 1965 and seats 58,325.

At West Point, the Black Knights keep one big date circled every year, the Navy game.

Anything else fun and true

The two teams have not played each other in the regular season in the last 10 years. Army beat Tulane 20-17 on Oct. 10, and Memphis lost 31-17 at Tulane six days later, so Tulane is the one shared opponent so far.

Army's defense had two sacks, five tackles for loss, an interception and a blocked punt at Tulsa, and Walton made 10 tackles.

Otis: Captain, you bring the march, and I'll bring the blues.

Thaddeus: Copy that, Otis. And beat Navy.

Otis: Go Tigers Go.

My call: Memphis Tigers 31, Army Black Knights 28.
