Louisiana Tech Bulldogs at South Alabama Jaguars

Louisiana Tech Bulldogs (5-2) at South Alabama Jaguars (4-3)

3:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Hancock Whitney Stadium · ESPN+

Azalea Fairhope-Grice is gluing the last sequins onto a red-and-blue parade float outside Hancock Whitney Stadium when Booker "Champ" Tullos rolls up with a bushel of peaches under each arm. "Champ," Azalea says, "this is a float, not a fruit stand." Champ sets a peach on the front bumper. "Darlin', now it's both."

Louisiana Tech (5-2) at South Alabama (4-3), Saturday at 3 p.m. ET on ESPN+ at Hancock Whitney Stadium in Mobile. It's a Sun Belt game: Tech is 3-0 in league play in its first season in the conference, and South Alabama is 2-1. DraftKings has Louisiana Tech Bulldogs −5.5 with a total of 57.5. My card: South Alabama Jaguars +5.5, Over 57.5, and a final of Louisiana Tech Bulldogs 33, South Alabama Jaguars 30.

Champ speaks for Louisiana Tech, peach in hand. "Peachy! Blake Baker has 10 touchdown throws and one interception all year. Jalen Mickens has 586 receiving yards. Alonzo Jackson Jr. has 6.5 sacks and 48 tackles. And we've posted back-to-back shutouts, 24-0 at UL Monroe and 26-0 over Old Dominion. That's cobbler, served warm."

Azalea speaks for South Alabama, whistle around her neck. "Honey, we run the ball. Keenan Phillips has 514 yards and five touchdowns. Jared Hollins threw for 321 yards against UL Monroe. We scored 56 at Arkansas State. Somebody throw that man some beads."

Why South Alabama? Because the Jaguars can score with anybody, and their run game fits this matchup. In The Trenches likes South Alabama's ground attack against a Tech run defense that has given up yards. And they're home. Tech has won close ones, 31-29 over Army and 32-31 over Louisiana, so a game decided by a field goal is right in its wheelhouse. My final has Tech winning by a field goal, inside the number.

The risk is the South Alabama passing game. Hollins went 12 of 33 for 148 yards and an interception in the 20-13 loss at Marshall. And the Tech defense just held two straight opponents scoreless. If that keeps up, the Jaguars won't stay close.

Why the Over? South Alabama games fly. The Jaguars score 35.1 points a game and allow 32.4. Tech scores 32.3. South Alabama's games this year finished 41-36, 52-35 and 56-49. Baker threw for 303 yards last week. My 33-30 adds up to 63, over 57.5.

Now the room. Next Man Up has Louisiana Tech 33-30 and likes South Alabama and the Over. The Formula has Tech 32-29, sides with South Alabama, and likes the Over. In The Trenches likes the Jaguars and lands on the Over too. Follow The Money likes Louisiana Tech after the market moved toward the Bulldogs, and it likes the Over.

Next Man Up started it: "Tech wins, Jaguars stay close. Peaches and parades."

Bottom of the card: a Tech team that wins close games and a South Alabama offense that scores in bunches at home. Champ leaves a peach on the float for luck. Give me the Jaguars and the Over.

Louisiana Tech Bulldogs 33, South Alabama Jaguars 30. South Alabama Jaguars cover the 5.5. Over 57.5.
