Peaches Meet the Parade in Mobile: Louisiana Tech Visits South Alabama on Halloween

Louisiana Tech Bulldogs (5-2) at South Alabama Jaguars (4-3)

3:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Hancock Whitney Stadium · ESPN+

Louisiana Tech (5-2, 3-0 Sun Belt) at South Alabama (4-3, 2-1 Sun Belt). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3 p.m. ET, Hancock Whitney Stadium, Mobile, Ala., ESPN+.

Your hosts are Booker "Champ" Tullos, a Louisiana Tech fan who grew up on a Ruston-area peach farm and thinks everything is peachy, and Azalea Fairhope-Grice, a Mobile Mardi Gras float-builder with a whistle in her purse.

Azalea: Well, hey, Champ! Welcome to Mobile. Pull up a lawn chair, honey.

Champ: Thank you kindly, Azalea. I brought peaches. A whole bushel.

Azalea: Darlin', this is a parade town. We throw beads, not fruit.

Players to watch

Louisiana Tech quarterback Blake Baker is 96 of 133 for 1,160 yards, 10 touchdowns and one interception. Trey Kukuk threw the most passes against Baylor and Army, but Baker has handled that role the last three games. Andrew Burnette leads the rushers with 349 yards, and Jalen Mickens leads the receivers with 40 catches for 586 yards and three scores. Linebacker Alonzo Jackson Jr. leads the defense with 48 tackles and 6.5 sacks.

South Alabama runs the ball first. Keenan Phillips has 514 rushing yards and five touchdowns on 96 carries. Jared Hollins plays quarterback and is 68 of 125 for 1,003 yards, seven touchdowns and three interceptions, after Bishop Davenport threw the most passes in the first two games. Brendan Jenkins leads the receivers with 25 catches for 430 yards and three scores. Safety Cole Blaylock leads the team with 46 tackles, and linebacker William Felton IV has two sacks.

Champ: Alonzo Jackson Jr. is the juiciest peach in the orchard, Azalea. Six and a half sacks!

Azalea: And Keenan Phillips is a float with a turbo engine.

What happened last week

Louisiana Tech shut out Old Dominion 26-0 at home on Oct. 24. Baker went 28 of 34 for 303 yards and a touchdown, Burnette ran 20 times for 63 yards, and Eli Finley caught 10 balls for 128 yards and a score.

Champ: Two shutouts in a row. Peach cobbler, served warm.

South Alabama lost 20-13 at Marshall on Tuesday, Oct. 20, so the Jaguars have had some extra days to get ready. Hollins threw for 148 yards, Phillips ran 16 times for 70 yards, and Jenkins caught four balls for 75 yards.

Azalea: We'll take the extra rest, darlin'. The float needed new sequins anyway.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Baker has thrown the most passes for Louisiana Tech in five of seven games, and Hollins has done it for South Alabama in each of the last five, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Sonny Cumbie is Louisiana Tech's head coach, hired Nov. 30, 2021. His defense takes the ball away, with 13 takeaways and a plus-5 turnover margin, and his team has won tight ones, 31-29 over Army and 32-31 over Louisiana.

Major Applewhite has led South Alabama since 2024. He was head coach at Houston in 2017 and 2018, and as a player he quarterbacked Texas from 1998 to 2001. His Jaguars run the ball much more than they throw it and score 35.1 points a game.

Champ: Coach Cumbie, give me the ball at the 2. I'm available.

Azalea: Champ, honey, they have a running back for that.

Player storylines

The matchup to watch is Phillips and the South Alabama ground game against a Tech defense that has not allowed a point in two weeks. The Jaguars' big guys up front will need to open lanes. On the other side, Baker has been sharp, and Mickens and Finley give him places to throw. Tech is trying to stay unbeaten in league play in its first Sun Belt season.

What's happening on campus

Hancock Whitney Stadium opened in 2020, and CFBD lists its capacity at 25,450. Mobile is where Mardi Gras started in America, and a Halloween afternoon by the bay should bring plenty of costumes alongside the red and blue. Louisiana Tech fans will make the trip from Ruston.

Azalea: I'm dressing as a parade float. A walking one.

Champ: I'm dressing as a peach. I've been practicing my roll.

Anything else fun and true

Louisiana Tech first played football in 1901 and has won three Division II national championships and 25 conference titles. After 13 seasons in Conference USA, Tech joined the Sun Belt on July 1, 2026. South Alabama started its program in 2009, joined the FBS in 2012 and went 10-3 in 2022. These two have met before: Tech won 34-16 in 2017 and 30-26 in 2018, and South Alabama won 38-14 in 2022.

Champ: Win or lose, Azalea, the peaches are yours. We'll say grace before the cobbler.

Azalea: Deal, Champ. And you're riding on my float after the game.

My call: Louisiana Tech Bulldogs 33, South Alabama Jaguars 30.
