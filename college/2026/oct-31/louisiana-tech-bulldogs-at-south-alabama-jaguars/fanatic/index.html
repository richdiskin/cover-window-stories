Louisiana Tech vs South Alabama: Military Day, Halloween in Mobile

Louisiana Tech Bulldogs (5-2) at South Alabama Jaguars (4-3)

3:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Hancock Whitney Stadium · ESPN+

Louisiana Tech Bulldogs vs South Alabama Jaguars is Week 9's Military Appreciation Day, Honor Band and USA Day at Hancock Whitney Stadium, and on Halloween it's the first meeting between these two as Sun Belt rivals. Booker "Champ" Tullos, who grew up on a Ruston-area peach farm and thinks everything is peachy, says Blake Baker, Alonzo Jackson Jr. and Sonny Cumbie's Bulldogs stay perfect in league play, and Azalea Fairhope-Grice, a Mobile Mardi Gras float-builder with a whistle in her purse, says Keenan Phillips, Cole Blaylock and Major Applewhite's Jaguars go 4-0 at home.

Azalea: Well, hey, Champ! Welcome to Mobile. Pull up a lawn chair, honey.

Champ: Thank you kindly, Azalea. I brought peaches. A whole bushel.

Azalea: Darlin', this is a parade town. We throw beads, not fruit.

Saturday is crowded on the calendar, too. South Alabama has it down as Military Appreciation Day, Honor Band day and USA Day, all on Halloween, and it's the first time the Jaguars and Bulldogs meet as conference foes since Tech officially joined the league on July 1.

Champ: First conference game in Mobile. I wore my good overalls.

Azalea: Honey, those are your only overalls.

There's a home record at stake. South Alabama's game notes say the Jaguars are 3-0 at home for the first time since 2022 and the first time under Applewhite, and they're trying to open 4-0 at home for the first time since 2011. They beat Southeastern Louisiana 39-14, Ohio 41-36 and UL Monroe 52-35 at Hancock Whitney. Then they scored 56 at Arkansas State, which the notes call the program's most in a Sun Belt game. The blockers up front also made the Joe Moore Midseason Honor Roll on Oct. 14, the only Sun Belt group on the list.

Azalea: Fifty-six points, Champ. We had to build a second float to carry them all.

South Alabama is 4-3 and 2-1 in the league. Its last game was a 20-13 loss at Marshall on Tuesday, Oct. 20, so the Jaguars have had extra days to get ready. Jared Hollins threw for 148 yards, Phillips ran 16 times for 70 yards, and Brendan Jenkins caught four balls for 75 yards. Phillips has 514 rushing yards and five touchdowns, Hollins is 68 of 125 for 1,003 yards and seven touchdowns, Jenkins has 25 catches for 430 yards, Blaylock leads the team with 46 tackles, and William Felton IV has two sacks.

Azalea: We'll take the extra rest, darlin'. The float needed new sequins anyway.

Louisiana Tech is 5-2 and 3-0 in its first Sun Belt season, and the defense hasn't allowed a point in two games. The Bulldogs won 24-0 at UL Monroe and then shut out Old Dominion 26-0 at home on Oct. 24, when Baker went 28 of 34 for 303 yards and a touchdown and Eli Finley caught 10 balls for 128 yards and a score.

Champ: Two shutouts in a row, Azalea. Peach cobbler, served warm.

Baker is 96 of 133 for 1,160 yards, 10 touchdowns and one interception. Trey Kukuk led the Bulldogs in throws against Baylor and Army, but Baker has done it the last three games. Andrew Burnette has 349 rushing yards, Jalen Mickens has 40 catches for 586 yards and three scores, and Jackson has 48 tackles and 6.5 sacks. Tech has also won the tight ones, 31-29 over Army and 32-31 over Louisiana.

Champ: Alonzo Jackson Jr. is the juiciest peach in the orchard. Six and a half sacks!

Azalea: And Keenan Phillips is a float with a turbo engine.

The series is short. Tech won 34-16 in 2017 in Ruston and 30-26 in 2018, and South Alabama won the last one, 38-14 in Mobile in 2022. The Jaguars' notes say Caullin Lacy returned a punt 57 yards for a score that day and Yam Banks added a 24-yard interception return for another.

Champ: That was four years ago, Azalea. The peaches have grown since then.

Azalea: So has the float, honey.

Cumbie is in his fifth season at Louisiana Tech, and his defense has 13 takeaways and a plus-5 turnover margin. Applewhite has led South Alabama since 2024. He was head coach at Houston in 2017 and 2018, and as a player he quarterbacked Texas from 1998 to 2001. His Jaguars score 35.1 points a game.

The matchup is Phillips and the South Alabama ground game against a Tech defense that has gone two weeks without allowing a point. On the other side, Baker has been efficient, and Mickens and Finley give him places to throw.

No official availability report is out for this game, so check the teams' pregame notes.

Azalea: I'm dressing as a parade float. A walking one.

Champ: I'm dressing as a peach. I've been practicing my roll.

Azalea: Champ, honey, win or lose, you're riding on my float after the game.

Champ: Deal, Azalea. And the peaches are yours. Every last one.

My call: Louisiana Tech Bulldogs 33, South Alabama Jaguars 30.
