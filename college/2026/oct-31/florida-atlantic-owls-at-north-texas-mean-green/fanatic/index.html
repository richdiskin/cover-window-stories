Coffins on Hickory Street, Berlioz in Boca: Two 18-Touchdown Arms Meet in Denton

Florida Atlantic Owls (5-2) at North Texas Mean Green (5-2)

7:30 PM ET Thu Oct 29 · DATCU Stadium · ESPN

Kaia Brightwater got to Denton five days too late. Last Saturday, the Day of the Dead Festival sent homemade coffins racing down Hickory Street, every one with a helmeted driver, a pusher and brakes checked by inspectors. Grady Mayhew-Ochoa, the North Texas fan who hears every offense like a record, was out there with his headphones around his neck. Kaia, the former Boca Raton beach volleyball player who files surf reports on offenses, has only the photos and a grudge.

Grady: Coffins with working brakes, Kaia. Denton has been racing them for more than a decade, and there's a lantern parade at twilight. You missed the most Denton afternoon of the year.

Kaia: Babe, Boca has its own spooky weekend. Friday night the library shows Hocus Pocus outside on the Spanish River patio. Saturday the Lynn Philharmonia plays Danse Macabre and the Symphonie Fantastique.

Grady: Berlioz? That symphony ends with a witches' sabbath. That's our offense after halftime.

Thursday's gig is Florida Atlantic (5-2, 2-1 American) at North Texas (5-2, 3-0 American), 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN, at DATCU Stadium, which seats 30,850. North Texas plays in 7/8 time, and the Owls brought their own rhythm section.

Tayven Jackson leads it for the Mean Green. He's 160 of 221 for 2,173 yards, 18 touchdowns and five interceptions. Jahiem White has 861 yards and 11 touchdowns on 116 carries. Justin Stevenson has 41 catches for 662 yards and seven scores. On defense, linebacker Zakye Barker has 57 tackles and lineman Davion Rhodes has four sacks.

Kaia: Now read mine slowly. Caden Veltkamp, 190 of 260, 2,084 yards, 18 touchdowns, four interceptions. Easton Messer, 58 catches, 709 yards, six scores. Ethan Ervin leads us on the ground with 368. Joseph Sipp Jr. has 49 tackles and two sacks.

Grady: Eighteen touchdown passes each. That's not a duel, that's a split single.

Both bands played loud last week. North Texas won 49-39 at Navy, and Annapolis is not an easy room. Jackson went 17 of 23 for 305 yards and three touchdowns, White carried 20 times for 174 yards and two scores, and Stevenson caught eight balls for 209 yards and two more.

Kaia: We beat Rice 51-49 at home. Veltkamp went 29 of 37 for 339 yards and three touchdowns and dove in from a yard out. Messer caught 12 for 162 and two scores. Leonard Farrow ran 17 times for 86. My heart still hasn't paddled back in.

That Veltkamp afternoon means more than one Saturday. He was cleared from a shoulder issue before fall camp, after a 2025 season with 3,641 passing yards and 24 touchdowns. He threw five touchdown passes against Navy and has four games with three or more.

Grady: White is the hook on our record, though. More than seven yards a carry, and your defense lets people run for 6.2.

Kaia: We tackle like we dig. Low, early and with our whole body.

Neither side has a name on an injury list that we could find, and neither school's box scores mark a starter. Jackson has thrown the most passes for North Texas in every game, and Veltkamp has done the same for the Owls.

The coaches are newer to these jobs than the rivalry is. Neal Brown was hired on Dec. 2, 2025, as the 21st head coach in North Texas history, after Eric Morris left for Oklahoma State. He went 72-51 at Troy and West Virginia and is 5-1 in bowl games. Zach Kittley is in his second season in Boca, named the Hagerty Family Head Football Coach on Dec. 2, 2024. His Owls score 37.3 points a game.

Kaia: Same hire date, a year apart. Somebody in this league has a sense of humor.

The field Kaia misses most is named for Howard Schnellenberger, who founded the Florida Atlantic program and was its first coach. The history between these two reads like a set list nobody wanted to end: Florida Atlantic won twice in 2017, 69-31 and 41-17, and North Texas took the last three, 41-38 in 2018, 45-28 in 2022 and 41-37 in 2024.

Grady: Sixty-nine points. That's a seven-minute guitar solo with no chorus.

Kaia: Then let's play the encore, babe. Sunset's at the beach either way.

Grady: Not in my house. Mean Green, baby. Turn it up.

My call: North Texas Mean Green 48, Florida Atlantic Owls 31.
