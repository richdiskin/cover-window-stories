FAU vs North Texas: Denton Coffin Races Meet Boca's Berlioz

Florida Atlantic Owls (5-2) at North Texas Mean Green (5-2)

7:30 PM ET Thu Oct 29 · DATCU Stadium · ESPN

Florida Atlantic Owls vs North Texas Mean Green kicks off Week 9 on Thursday night at DATCU Stadium, five days after Denton's Day of the Dead Festival sent homemade coffins racing down Hickory Street, and North Texas fan Grady Mayhew-Ochoa has not stopped reminding Florida Atlantic fan Kaia Brightwater that she missed it. Kaia answers that Caden Veltkamp and her Owls came to Denton to race Tayven Jackson's offense, not a box on wheels, and that a 3-0 mark in the American means nothing until somebody from Boca Raton tests it.

Grady: Every coffin had a helmeted driver and a pusher, Kaia, and inspectors checked the brakes before it rolled. That's how we play offense. Jackson drives. Jahiem White pushes.

Kaia: And nobody checks the brakes on our side, which is why we score.

Denton has been racing coffins since October 2012, and the twilight lantern and costume parade closes out the festival downtown. Kaia's town spent the same stretch getting ready for its own spooky weekend. The Spanish River Library shows Hocus Pocus outside on its patio Friday, and on Saturday the Lynn University Philharmonia plays Night on a Bald Mountain, Danse Macabre and the Symphonie Fantastique at the Wold Performing Arts Center.

Grady: Berlioz ends that symphony with a witches' sabbath. That is what our offense sounds like after halftime.

Kaia: Then I'll bring earplugs and a better defense.

Florida Atlantic is 5-2 and 2-1 in the American. North Texas is 5-2 and 3-0. The Mean Green have scored 43.1 a game, and their quarterback is the reason. Jackson is 160 of 221 for 2,173 yards, 18 touchdowns and five interceptions. White has 861 yards and 11 touchdowns on 116 carries, and Justin Stevenson has 41 catches for 662 yards and seven scores. On defense, Zakye Barker has 57 tackles and Davion Rhodes has four sacks.

Kaia: Now the Boca side. Veltkamp is 190 of 260 for 2,084 yards, 18 touchdowns and four interceptions. Easton Messer has 58 catches for 709 yards and six scores. Ethan Ervin leads us on the ground with 368. Joseph Sipp Jr. has 49 tackles and two sacks.

Grady: Eighteen touchdown throws each. Somebody has to blink first, and it won't be ours.

Both quarterbacks come in hot. North Texas won 49-39 at Navy last Saturday. Jackson went 17 of 23 for 305 yards and three touchdowns, White carried 20 times for 174 yards and two scores, and Stevenson caught eight balls for 209 yards and two more.

Florida Atlantic needed every second at home to beat Rice 51-49. Veltkamp went 29 of 37 for 339 yards and three touchdowns and dove in from a yard out, Messer caught 12 for 162 and two scores, and Leonard Farrow ran 17 times for 86.

Kaia: My heart is still somewhere off the Boca inlet after that one.

Veltkamp's season came after an offseason worry. He was cleared from a shoulder issue before fall camp, after a 2025 season with 3,641 passing yards and 24 touchdowns.

Grady: Your problem isn't the quarterback, it's the tackling. White runs for more than seven a carry, and your defense lets people run for 6.2.

Kaia: We tackle like we dig in the sand. Low and early.

There is no official conference availability report for this game, and neither school's box scores flag a first-teamer as missing, so the only paperwork filed in Denton this week belonged to the coffin inspectors. Jackson has thrown the most passes for North Texas in every game, and Veltkamp has done the same for the Owls.

The men on the sidelines share a date. Neal Brown was named the 21st head coach in North Texas history on Dec. 2, 2025, after Eric Morris left for Oklahoma State. He went 72-51 at Troy and West Virginia. Zach Kittley was named Florida Atlantic's Hagerty Family Head Football Coach on Dec. 2, 2024, and his Owls score 37.3 points a game.

Kaia: Same day, one year apart. The American schedulers have a sense of humor.

Kaia's home field carries the name of Howard Schnellenberger, who founded the Florida Atlantic program and was its first coach, and she points out that his program has beaten North Texas before. The Owls won twice in 2017, 69-31 and 41-17. North Texas has taken the last three, 41-38 in 2018, 45-28 in 2022 and 41-37 in 2024.

Grady: Three in a row, two of them by four points or fewer. Denton knows how to finish a race.

Kaia: Your coffins needed a pusher. Mine just need Messer open in the fourth quarter.

Grady: Bring him. On Hickory Street, the brakes get checked before the race. On Thursday night, we find out whether Florida Atlantic's defense has any.

My call: North Texas Mean Green 48, Florida Atlantic Owls 31.
