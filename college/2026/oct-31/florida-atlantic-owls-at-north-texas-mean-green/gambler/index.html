Florida Atlantic Owls at North Texas Mean Green

Florida Atlantic Owls (5-2) at North Texas Mean Green (5-2)

7:30 PM ET Thu Oct 29 · DATCU Stadium · ESPN

Grady Mayhew-Ochoa has his headphones on in Denton, and the North Texas offense is still playing in 7/8 time. Kaia Brightwater drove in from Boca Raton with a swell report, a tempo offense and one honest admission: the Florida Atlantic defense has been flat water on the road.

Florida Atlantic (5-2) at North Texas (5-2), Thursday at 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN at DATCU Stadium in Denton. It's an American game. DraftKings has North Texas Mean Green −13.5 with a total of 77.5. My card: North Texas Mean Green −13.5, Over 77.5, and a final of North Texas Mean Green 48, Florida Atlantic Owls 31.

Grady speaks for North Texas. "We score 43.1 a game. Tayven Jackson has 2,173 yards and 18 touchdown passes, Jahiem White has 861 rushing yards and 11 scores, and Justin Stevenson just caught eight balls for 209 yards at Navy. That is not a song, Kaia. That is a whole album."

Kaia speaks for Florida Atlantic. "Cute album, babe. Caden Veltkamp has 18 touchdown passes and four interceptions, and Easton Messer caught 12 for 162 against Rice. We beat Rice 51-49. We can surf with anybody."

Here is the part that matters. Florida Atlantic has left Boca three times this season. It lost 66-21 at Florida, beat UL Monroe 45-17 and lost 56-24 at Army. North Texas at home has won 44-6, 63-14 and 50-10. Those are not close games.

Why North Texas? Because the Owls cannot stop the run and cannot run the ball. Florida Atlantic allows 6.2 yards a carry and gains 3.2. White against that front is the mismatch of the night, and the Owls' passing game has to carry everything. My final has North Texas by 17, clear of the 13.5.

The worry is history. North Texas won the last three meetings, but by 3, 17 and 4. And Veltkamp can stack touchdowns fast enough to make a late score feel like a backdoor. In The Trenches sees the run gaps cancel and stays out.

Why the Over/Under? Florida Atlantic's last two games finished 56-24 and 51-49. North Texas has already played in shootouts of 49-35 at Texas State, 45-44 at Tulsa and 49-39 at Navy. Action Network's opening number on the total was 65.5, DraftKings had 70.5 on Monday, and now it sits at 77.5. The market has been chasing the scoreboard all week, and it is right to. My 48-31 adds up to 79, a point and a half over the over/under 77.5.

Now the room. Next Man Up has North Texas 52-33 and likes the Mean Green and the Over. Follow The Money likes North Texas and the Over after the moves. The Formula has 43-29, same side, but takes the Under. In The Trenches has no pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

The Formula: "Seventy-two is plenty of points. Seventy-eight is greedy."

Next Man Up: "Eighty-five says hello."

Follow The Money: "Twelve points of movement says hello too. Welcome to the party."

Bottom of the card: a home offense that hits every note against a road defense that has been washed out away from the beach. Grady turns it up. Kaia paddles hard.

North Texas Mean Green 48, Florida Atlantic Owls 31. North Texas Mean Green cover the 13.5. Over 77.5.
