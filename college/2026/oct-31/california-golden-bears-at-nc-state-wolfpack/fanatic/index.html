Halloween Noon in Raleigh: The Wolfpack Fires Up the Grill as Cal Comes East With a Sign for Every Occasion

California Golden Bears (3-5) at NC State Wolfpack (3-4)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Carter-Finley Stadium · ACC Network

California (3-5, 2-3 ACC) at NC State (3-4, 1-3 ACC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12 p.m. ET, Carter-Finley Stadium, Raleigh, N.C., ACC Network.

Your hosts are Rutherford "Ruff" Pegram, a Wake County engineer who grills in the same Carter-Finley lot every home game, and Quentin Oyelaran-Fitch, a Berkeley philosophy Ph.D. student with a picket sign for every bad play call.

Ruff: Welcome to Raleigh, Quentin! Grill's at 450. That's a sear. Wolfpack, howl!

Quentin: Greetings, comrade. I brought a new sign for the occasion. It says "TRICK PLAYS ARE A HUMAN RIGHT."

Players to watch

Cal quarterback Jaron-Keawe Sagapolutele is 149 of 219 for 1,555 yards, 10 touchdowns and five interceptions. Jackson Brousseau threw the most passes in three straight games in October, and Sagapolutele was back on top at SMU. Wide receiver Chase Hendricks leads the Bears with 54 catches for 498 yards and three scores. Adam Mohammed leads the rushers with 336 yards. On defense, defensive back Isaiah Crosby has 46 tackles and linebacker JD McKinley has three sacks.

NC State quarterback CJ Bailey is 143 of 219 for 1,763 yards, 11 touchdowns and four interceptions, and he has thrown the most passes in every Wolfpack game this season. Wide receiver Keenan Jackson has 30 catches for 456 yards and six touchdowns. Jayden Scott leads the rushers with 358 yards and four scores. Linebacker Popo Aguirre leads the team with 60 tackles, and linebacker Elijah Satchell has four sacks.

Ruff: Sixty tackles. Popo Aguirre is the kind of linebacker who flips burgers, cleans the grill and still helps you pack the cooler.

What happened last week

Cal lost 59-20 at SMU on Oct. 24. Sagapolutele went 27 of 37 for 265 yards and a touchdown, and Hendricks caught seven passes for 83 yards and a score.

Quentin: I have filed a formal grievance with the scoreboard.

NC State lost 31-28 at Stanford on Oct. 23. Bailey went 17 of 30 for 256 yards and two touchdowns and was also the team's leading rusher, with 59 yards on nine carries. Deandre Desinor caught three passes for 79 yards and a touchdown.

Ruff: Three points. That one stayed on the grill a little too long.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and neither school's box scores mark starters. Bailey has thrown the most passes for NC State in all seven games, and Sagapolutele did for Cal last week, so watch the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

NC State has new leadership this week. The school relieved Dave Doeren of his duties after the loss at Stanford, ending a run that began in 2013, and running backs coach and special teams coordinator Todd Goebbel will serve as interim head coach for the rest of the season. Saturday is his first game in charge, and he has promised a team that will be "hard, tough and together." This year's offense scores 32.3 points a game, including a 73-0 win over Richmond and a 31-28 win over Louisville.

Tosh Lupoi was named the 35th head coach in Cal history on Dec. 4, 2025, replacing Justin Wilcox. His first Cal team picked up ACC wins over Syracuse and Virginia Tech, both by one score.

Quentin: Coach Lupoi is building something, comrade. Respectfully, but firmly.

Player storylines

The matchup to circle is Bailey and Jackson against a Cal defense that allows 31.6 points a game. Jackson has six touchdown catches, the most of anyone on either roster. On the other side, Hendricks has caught 54 passes, and the Bears like to throw it to him early and often. Cal gains 3.1 yards a carry, so the Wolfpack big guys up front will try to make the Bears one-dimensional.

What's happening on campus

Carter-Finley Stadium has been NC State's home since 1966, and it's the largest college football stadium in North Carolina. In 2010 the school brought back a live mascot, a Tamaskan dog named Tuffy, and a Halloween noon kickoff means Tuffy may see a few costumes in the stands.

Anything else fun and true

NC State was a founding member of the ACC in 1953 and has won seven conference championships. Cal fans have their own claim to fame: The Play, from the 1982 Big Game against Stanford, when the Bears returned the final kickoff through five laterals and Kevin Moen scored as the Stanford band wandered onto the field. Cal won 25-20. These two met in 2024, and NC State won 24-23, so there's some history here, too.

Quentin: Five laterals, Ruff. That's democracy in action.

Ruff: Then let's have a fair election. Grab a plate, Quentin. The game's about to start, and grace comes before brisket.

My call: NC State Wolfpack 36, California Golden Bears 24.
