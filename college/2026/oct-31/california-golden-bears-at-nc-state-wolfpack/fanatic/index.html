Cal vs NC State: Amato's Red Shoes and a Homecoming Reset

California Golden Bears (3-5) at NC State Wolfpack (3-4)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Carter-Finley Stadium · ACC Network

California Golden Bears vs NC State Wolfpack is a Week 9 Halloween noon ET Homecoming at Carter-Finley Stadium, and the Wolfpack will wear a red shoe sticker with Chuck Amato's initials on the back of their helmets, one week after the school relieved Dave Doeren of his duties. Ruff Pegram, an NC State grill man from Wake County, says interim coach Todd Goebbel and CJ Bailey owe the old coach a win, and Cal fan Quentin Oyelaran-Fitch, a Berkeley philosophy Ph.D. student with a sign for every occasion, says Tosh Lupoi's Bears came east to crash the reunion.

Ruff: Grill's at 450, Quentin. Homecoming, Halloween and a new man on the headset. Big day for the Pack.

Quentin: Respectfully, comrade, my sign today says "ALL COACHES ARE INTERIM."

It has been a heavy two weeks in Raleigh. Amato died on Oct. 19 at 80 at his Raleigh home. He played at N.C. State in the 1960s, then went 49-37 as its head coach from 2000 to 2006, with a school-record 11 wins in 2002, and he recruited Philip Rivers. Five days later, after a 31-28 loss at Stanford, athletic director Boo Corrigan announced Doeren's dismissal, and Goebbel, the running backs coach and special teams coordinator, took over for the rest of the season. Goebbel, a former quarterback at Kent State and Northern Iowa, has never been a head coach.

On Monday, Goebbel announced the helmet tribute. "This Saturday, we will honor the memory of former head coach Chuck Amato by wearing a red shoe sticker with the initials on the back of our helmet. As we all know, the fashion statement was real," he said. He also set his standard: "A foundation and a culture have been set, and the one thing we will stick to is hard, tough and together."

Ruff: Duke's Manny Diaz said it best. "I don't know anybody who loved N.C. State more than Chuck Amato did." I'm grilling in red today, top to bottom.

Quentin: I'll lower my sign for that one, Ruff. Respect for the man.

Then the sign goes back up. Cal lost 59-20 at SMU on Oct. 24, the week after a 41-7 home loss to Wake Forest. Jaron-Keawe Sagapolutele went 27 of 37 for 265 yards and a touchdown at SMU, and Chase Hendricks caught seven balls for 83 yards and a score. Sagapolutele is 149 of 219 for 1,555 yards, 10 touchdowns and five interceptions, after Jackson Brousseau threw the most passes in three straight October games. Hendricks leads the Bears with 54 catches for 498 yards, and Adam Mohammed has 336 rushing yards.

Quentin: I have filed a formal grievance with the scoreboard. It has not responded.

At Stanford, Bailey went 17 of 30 for 256 yards and two touchdowns and also led the team in rushing with 59 yards. Deandre Desinor caught three balls for 79 yards and a touchdown. Bailey is 143 of 219 for 1,763 yards with 11 touchdowns and four interceptions, Keenan Jackson has six touchdown catches, the most on either roster, and Jayden Scott has 358 rushing yards. On defense, Popo Aguirre leads the team with 60 tackles and Elijah Satchell has four sacks.

Ruff: Sixty tackles. Popo flips the burgers, scrubs the grill and still helps you load the cooler.

The ACC's initial availability report, posted at 8 p.m. ET Wednesday, lists Jayden Scott among 12 NC State players out. Cal lists four players out and 10 questionable, Adam Mohammed among them. Neither Bailey nor Sagapolutele is on it, and Bailey has thrown the most passes for NC State in all seven games.

The numbers point at NC State's offense. The Wolfpack score 32.3 points a game, including a 73-0 win over Richmond and a 31-28 win over Louisville, and Cal allows 31.6. The Bears gain just 3.1 yards a carry.

Lupoi, named Cal's 35th head coach on Dec. 4, 2025, after Justin Wilcox, has ACC wins over Syracuse and Virginia Tech, both by one score.

Quentin: Coach Lupoi is building something, comrade. Respectfully, but firmly.

Ruff: Build it in Berkeley.

Cal still has the most famous ending in the sport. In the 1982 Big Game, the Bears ran the final kickoff back through five laterals, and Kevin Moen scored as the Stanford band wandered onto the field. Cal won 25-20. The last time these two met, in 2024, NC State won 24-23.

Quentin: Five laterals, Ruff. That's democracy in action.

Ruff: Then here's a fair election. Downtown, Boxcar is running a costume party from noon to 1 a.m. ET, with a puppy contest at 4 p.m. ET. Win or lose, I'm bringing you, and you're wearing red.

Quentin: Only if my sign gets a costume too.

My call: NC State Wolfpack 36, California Golden Bears 24.
