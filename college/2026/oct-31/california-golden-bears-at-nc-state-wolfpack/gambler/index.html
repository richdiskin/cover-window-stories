California Golden Bears at NC State Wolfpack

California Golden Bears (3-5) at NC State Wolfpack (3-4)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Carter-Finley Stadium · ACC Network

Ruff Pegram has his grill lit in the same Carter-Finley lot, and he's reading the thermometer like a scouting report. Quentin Oyelaran-Fitch arrives from Berkeley with a picket sign that says "RUN IT ON SECOND DOWN, PLEASE (RESPECTFULLY)."

California (3-5) at NC State (3-4), Saturday at 12:00 p.m. ET on ACC Network at Carter-Finley Stadium in Raleigh. It's an ACC game, and the Wolfpack has lost two straight. DraftKings has NC State Wolfpack −7 with a total of 56.5. My card: NC State Wolfpack −7, Over 56.5, and a final of NC State Wolfpack 36, California Golden Bears 24.

Quentin speaks for Cal, and he's already organizing. "Comrades, Jaron-Keawe Sagapolutele went 27 of 37 for 265 yards at SMU. Chase Hendricks has 54 catches. The revolution will be thrown, not run."

Ruff speaks for NC State, and he points a spatula at the sign. "Quentin, buddy, the revolution gave up 59 points last week. CJ Bailey has 1,763 passing yards and 11 touchdowns, and Keenan Jackson has six touchdown catches. We lost 31-28 at Stanford by a field goal. Saturday's a sear."

Why NC State? Because Cal can't stop anybody right now. The Bears allow 31.6 points a game, and they gave up 41 to Wake Forest and 59 at SMU in the last two weeks. Their run defense gives up 5.2 yards a carry, and NC State gains 4.4. On the other side, Cal runs for 3.1 yards a carry, so the Bears have to throw into a home crowd that's hungry after two losses. My final has NC State by a dozen, well clear of the 7.

The worry is the NC State defense. It allows 28.1 points a game, and Wake Forest put 38 on it in Raleigh. Last time these two met, in 2024, NC State won 24-23. If Sagapolutele gets hot the way he did against Wagner, this turns into a one-score game late.

Why the Over? NC State scores 32.3 points a game, and Cal's defense has been giving up more than that. The Wolfpack offense scored 31 against Louisville and 28 at Stanford, and Cal has scored 20 or more in six of its eight games. My 36-24 adds up to 60, three and a half points over 56.5.

Now the room. Next Man Up is the loudest howl in the parking lot, NC State 38-23 and the Over. The Formula agrees on both, NC State 33-25. Follow The Money saw the market come down toward Cal, so it takes California +7 and skips the total. In The Trenches sits this one out, and Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up started it: "Two of us have the Wolfpack by more than a touchdown, Follow The Money. You're picketing with Quentin?"

Follow The Money held its sign high: "A move is a move. I follow it."

The Formula had the last word: "Then follow it to the scoreboard. Cal gave up 59 last week."

Bottom of the card: a home team that wants a sear after two losses, a Cal defense that has bled points for two weeks, and a total two scores in the room clear. Ruff gets his howl. Give me the Wolfpack and the Over.

NC State Wolfpack 36, California Golden Bears 24. NC State Wolfpack cover the 7. Over 56.5.
