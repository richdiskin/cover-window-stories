California Golden Bears at NC State Wolfpack

California Golden Bears (3-5) at NC State Wolfpack (3-4)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Carter-Finley Stadium · ACC Network

Cal vs NC State prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on NC State Wolfpack −7 and Over 56.5 at DraftKings, with my final at NC State Wolfpack 36, California Golden Bears 24. It is Homecoming in Raleigh, the Wolfpack will wear Chuck Amato's initials on the back of their helmets, and Todd Goebbel coaches his first game in charge after Dave Doeren's dismissal last Saturday.

A program in that kind of week either folds or plays with a chip on every shoulder, and the matchup decides which one this is. Cal allows 31.6 points a game, and it gave up 41 to Wake Forest and 59 at SMU over the last two Saturdays. The Bears' run defense gives up 5.2 yards a carry, and NC State gains 4.4. Cal runs for only 3.1 a carry, so Jaron-Keawe Sagapolutele has to throw into a Homecoming crowd. My final has NC State by a dozen.

Ruff Pegram has the grill at 450 and his whole outfit in red. "CJ Bailey has 1,763 passing yards and 11 touchdowns, and Keenan Jackson has six touchdown catches. Goebbel says hard, tough and together. I say we owe Coach Amato a sear."

Quentin Oyelaran-Fitch, the Berkeley Ph.D. student, holds up a sign that reads "ALL COACHES ARE INTERIM." Then he makes his case. "Sagapolutele went 27 of 37 for 265 yards at SMU, and Chase Hendricks has 54 catches. Coach Lupoi already has two ACC wins."

The danger is NC State's own defense, which allows 28.1 points a game. Wake Forest put 38 on it in Raleigh, and the 2024 meeting ended 24-23. Sagapolutele threw for 413 yards against Wagner, and if that version shows up, this is a one-score game late.

The Over fits both sides. NC State scores 32.3 points a game and put up 31 against Louisville and 28 at Stanford, while Cal has scored 20 or more in six of eight games. Action Network's opening listing had 57.5, and the total is 56.5 now. My 36-24 adds up to 60.

The room mostly sides with the Wolfpack. Next Man Up has NC State 38-23 and likes NC State and the Over. The Formula has NC State 33-25 and is on NC State and the Over without backing either. Follow The Money likes California +7 and has no total pick, and it keeps no score of its own. In The Trenches has no pick, and Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "Two of us have the Wolfpack by more than a touchdown. Follow The Money, are you holding one of Quentin's signs?"

Follow The Money: "The price came toward Cal. I go where it goes."

The Formula: "Then go to the scoreboard. Cal gave up 59 last week."

The opening listing had NC State at 7.5, and the price is 7 now. A Homecoming crowd in red, an interim coach and a Cal defense that has bled points for two weeks push me to the Wolfpack.

NC State Wolfpack 36, California Golden Bears 24. NC State Wolfpack cover the 7. Over 56.5.
