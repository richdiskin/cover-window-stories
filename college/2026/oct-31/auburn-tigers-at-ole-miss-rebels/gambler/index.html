Auburn Tigers at Ole Miss Rebels

Auburn Tigers (4-3) at Ole Miss Rebels (5-2)

12:45 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Vaught-Hemingway Stadium · SEC Network

Lacey Bellamy has staked out a table in the Grove, lemonade in hand, rehearsing her reenactment of the field goal that beat Georgia. Langston Fairbanks drifts over in seersucker with a Faulkner paperback and says, "Miss Bellamy, the past is never dead." Lacey smiles. "Neither is Auburn, honey. Bless your heart."

Auburn (4-3) at No. 12 Ole Miss (5-2), Saturday at 12:45 p.m. ET on SEC Network at Vaught-Hemingway Stadium in Oxford. It's an SEC game: Auburn is 2-3 in league play, Ole Miss 3-2. DraftKings has Ole Miss Rebels −13.5 with a total of 57.5. My card: Auburn Tigers +13.5, Under 57.5, and a final of Ole Miss Rebels 33, Auburn Tigers 22.

Lacey speaks for Auburn, drawl turned up. "Sugar, we went into Athens and beat Georgia 27-24. Elijah Melendez has 57 tackles and four sacks. Byrum Brown has run for 471 yards and six touchdowns. We don't get blown out much. War Eagle."

Langston speaks for Ole Miss. "Madam, Trinidad Chambliss has thrown for 1,926 yards and 16 touchdowns. Deuce Alexander has 43 catches for 610 yards. We score 36 points a game. We held Missouri to three points. I could go on for three paragraphs."

Lacey cuts in: "Please don't."

Why Auburn? Because the Tigers keep it close. Their games this season ended 17-16, 43-8, 39-44, 21-15, 14-24, 27-24 and 17-38. Only LSU beat them by more than 13.5. They lost to Florida by less than a touchdown and won at Georgia. Ole Miss, meanwhile, just had a rough day at Texas, where Chambliss went 25 of 52 with three interceptions in a 34-27 loss. DraftKings had Ole Miss −11.5 on Monday, and the move to 13.5 hands Auburn a couple more points. My final has Ole Miss by less than two touchdowns.

The risk is the Auburn offense. Brown ran 19 times for 21 yards against LSU, and he's thrown seven interceptions. Auburn has allowed 23 sacks. Ole Miss beat Vanderbilt 52-17 and Missouri 31-3.

Why the Under? Auburn's defense is the steady piece, allowing 24.1 points a game and only 3.7 yards a carry. Six of Auburn's seven games stayed under 57.5. DraftKings had this total at 55.5 on Monday, and it climbed to 57.5, so the Under gets the better price. My 33-22 adds up to 55, under 57.5.

Now the room, and it splits. The Formula has Ole Miss 32-24 and sides with Auburn and the Under. Next Man Up has Ole Miss 34-20, likes the Under, and lands on Ole Miss for the side. Follow The Money likes Ole Miss and the Over, because the market moved both ways toward the Rebels. In The Trenches has no pick, since the run games cancel out.

Next Man Up started it: "Ole Miss by two touchdowns. Langston, keep it to one paragraph."

Follow The Money answered: "The market moved toward Oxford. I'm going with it."

The Formula held firm: "Auburn won in Athens. A few points in Oxford is not a stretch."

Bottom of the card: an Auburn team that rarely gets run over, an Ole Miss team coming off three picks at Texas, and a total that rose on the week. Lacey keeps her lemonade and her dignity. Give me Auburn and the Under.

Ole Miss Rebels 33, Auburn Tigers 22. Auburn Tigers cover the 13.5. Under 57.5.
