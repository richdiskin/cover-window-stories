Sweet Tea and Seersucker: Auburn Visits No. 12 Ole Miss in the Grove

Auburn Tigers (4-3) at Ole Miss Rebels (5-2)

12:45 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Vaught-Hemingway Stadium · SEC Network

Auburn (4-3, 2-3 SEC) at No. 12 Ole Miss (5-2, 3-2 SEC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12:45 p.m. ET, Vaught-Hemingway Stadium, Oxford, Miss., SEC Network.

Your hosts are Lacey Bellamy, a former Auburn sorority president who can reenact every Iron Bowl and always plays the hero, and Langston Fairbanks, an Ole Miss fan in seersucker who reads Faulkner and Rebel game film in the same sitting.

Langston: Welcome to the Grove, Miss Bellamy. I've prepared a brief introduction. It runs three paragraphs.

Lacey: Bless your heart, Langston. I brought lemonade. You get one paragraph per glass.

Players to watch

Auburn quarterback Byrum Brown does a little bit of everything. He's 134 of 209 for 1,365 yards, eight touchdowns and seven interceptions, and he's also the leading rusher with 471 yards and six scores. Keshaun Singleton leads the receivers with 36 catches for 431 yards. Linebacker Elijah Melendez leads the defense in tackles with 57 and in sacks with four.

Ole Miss quarterback Trinidad Chambliss has thrown the most passes in every Rebel game. He's 167 of 262 for 1,926 yards, 16 touchdowns and seven interceptions. Running back Kewan Lacy has 388 yards and five touchdowns. Deuce Alexander leads the receivers with 43 catches for 610 yards and five scores. On defense, end Blake Purchase has 4.5 sacks and linebacker Keaton Thomas has 45 tackles.

Lacey: Byrum Brown leads us in throwing and running. In my reenactments, I play him. Obviously.

What happened last week

Auburn lost 38-17 to LSU at home on Oct. 24. Brown was 15 of 24 for 160 yards and ran for a touchdown, and Singleton caught four balls for 64 yards. The week before, Auburn beat No. 1 Georgia 27-24 on a field goal as time expired.

Lacey: I sipped my lemonade with great dignity after LSU. But that Georgia kick? I'm still shrieking "War Eagle."

Ole Miss lost 34-27 at Texas on Oct. 24. Chambliss threw two touchdowns, and Alexander caught eight balls for 87 yards.

Langston: The past is never dead. It isn't even past. But it is, mercifully, over.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Brown and Chambliss have each thrown the most passes in every game for their teams, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Alex Golesh came to Auburn from South Florida and was hired on Nov. 30, 2025. His Tigers run a balanced offense, and the defense has been the strength, with 16 sacks.

Pete Golding is in his first full season as Ole Miss head coach. His offense has scored on every red zone trip, first in FBS. Defensive tackle Will Echoles said, "Stopping the run has been an issue for us at times this year, but it's gotten better." He had reason to smile after the Rebels held Missouri to 39 rushing yards on 26 carries.

Langston: First in FBS in the red zone. I've written a sonnet about it.

Lacey: Sugar, read it at halftime.

Player storylines

Brown against the Ole Miss defense is the headline. If he can run and throw the way he did at Georgia, Auburn can hang around. Chambliss wants to bounce back after a 25 of 52 day at Texas, and Alexander is his go-to target. Melendez will try to crash the pocket, and both teams' big guys up front will decide who controls the clock.

What's happening on campus

Vaught-Hemingway Stadium opened in 1915 and is listed at 64,038 seats. Since 2023, the Rebels are 25-1 at home. Halloween in the Grove means tents, chandeliers and plenty of red-and-blue costumes. Auburn fans will bring their orange and blue, and Lacey will bring a pitcher.

Lacey: I'm dressed as Aubie. Langston is dressed as a library.

Anything else fun and true

Auburn won the 2010 national championship, the season Cam Newton led the Tigers, and Jordan-Hare Stadium is listed at 87,451. Ole Miss was founded in 1893 and won six SEC titles: 1947, 1954, 1955, 1960, 1962 and 1963. Last year the Rebels went 13-2. In this series, Auburn won 31-20 in 2021, and Ole Miss won 48-34 in 2022 and 28-21 in 2023.

Langston: Miss Bellamy, whatever happens, dinner is on me afterward. We'll say grace first.

Lacey: That's sweet, Langston. War Eagle, bless your heart.

My call: Ole Miss Rebels 33, Auburn Tigers 22.
