Auburn vs Ole Miss: Powder Blue for Oxford's Halloween Return

Auburn Tigers (4-3) at Ole Miss Rebels (5-2)

12:45 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Vaught-Hemingway Stadium · SEC Network

Auburn Tigers vs Ole Miss Rebels is a Week 9 Halloween game at Vaught-Hemingway Stadium, the first one played there since 1998, and Ole Miss has asked its crowd to wear powder blue. Langston Fairbanks, an Ole Miss fan in seersucker who reads Faulkner and Rebel film in the same sitting, says Trinidad Chambliss and Pete Golding's No. 12 Rebels will keep Oxford undefeated, and Lacey Bellamy, a former Auburn sorority president who reenacts every Iron Bowl, says Byrum Brown and Alex Golesh have one more upset in them.

Langston: Welcome to the Grove, Miss Bellamy. I've prepared a brief introduction. It runs three paragraphs.

Lacey: Bless your heart, Langston. I brought lemonade. You get one paragraph per glass.

Langston: Then I'll use my glass on 1998. Ole Miss notes say the last Halloween game in this stadium was against LSU, and the Rebels won 37-31 in overtime.

Lacey: Sugar, you're dressed as powder blue. I'm dressed as Aubie.

The Rebels have played on Oct. 31 sixteen times before this one and are 8-7-1, by the school's count. Two of those were against Auburn, both on the Plains. Auburn won 33-20 in 2009, and Ole Miss won 27-19 in 2015.

Lacey: We'll call that a tie, and Auburn leads the whole series 35-13 on the field.

Langston: Ole Miss won the last meeting, 28-21 in Auburn in 2023. The past is never dead, Miss Bellamy.

Lacey: Neither is Auburn, honey.

Auburn is 4-3 and 2-3 in the SEC, coming off a 38-17 home loss to LSU on Oct. 24. The week before, the Tigers won 27-24 at Georgia, their first win in Athens since 2005, according to Ole Miss's preview. Brown does a little bit of everything. He's 134 of 209 for 1,365 yards, eight touchdowns and seven interceptions, and he leads the team with 471 rushing yards and six scores. Keshaun Singleton has 36 catches for 431 yards. Elijah Melendez has 57 tackles and four sacks.

Lacey: In my reenactment of Athens, I play Brown and the kicker. Obviously.

Ole Miss is 5-2 and 3-2 in the league after a 34-27 loss at Texas. Chambliss is 167 of 262 for 1,926 yards, 16 touchdowns and seven interceptions, though he went 25 of 52 with three interceptions in Austin. Kewan Lacy has 388 yards and five touchdowns, and Deuce Alexander has 43 catches for 610 yards. Blake Purchase has 4.5 sacks, and Keaton Thomas has 45 tackles.

Langston: Our offense scores 36 points a game, Miss Bellamy. I've written a sonnet about it.

Lacey: Sugar, read it at halftime. I'll be getting more lemonade.

No official availability report is out for this game yet, so check the teams' pregame notes.

The real fight is at home in Oxford. Ole Miss has won 15 home games in a row, dating to a 26-14 win over Oklahoma on Oct. 26, 2024, and is 25-1 at Vaught-Hemingway since September 2023. Auburn allows 24.1 points a game and 3.7 yards a carry, and that defense will need every bit of it.

Golesh came to Auburn from South Florida, where he went 23-15 and won a bowl game in each of his three seasons. Golding is in his first full season running the Rebels. The Rebels held Missouri to 39 rushing yards in a 31-3 win.

Langston: Missouri left Oxford with three points and a chicken tender. We doubled the tender stands this year, you know.

Lacey: That's the kindest thing Ole Miss has ever done for a visitor.

Saturday is the 49th meeting between these two, by Ole Miss's count, and the 17th Halloween game in Rebel history.

Langston: Whatever happens, Miss Bellamy, dinner is on me. We'll say grace first.

Lacey: That's sweet, Langston. War Eagle, bless your heart.

My call: Ole Miss Rebels 33, Auburn Tigers 22.
