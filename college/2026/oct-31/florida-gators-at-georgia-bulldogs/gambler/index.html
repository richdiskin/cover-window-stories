Florida Gators at Georgia Bulldogs

Florida Gators (5-2) at Georgia Bulldogs (6-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Mercedes-Benz Stadium · ABC

Thad Okafor-Brandt is spinning a vinyl record on a portable turntable outside Mercedes-Benz Stadium when Dax Marchetti strolls up wearing an orange-and-blue scarf and carrying a laminated chart of reptiles. "Dax," Thad says, "this is track one, side A. The Dawgs walk out to it." Dax taps his chart. "Thad, I've classified your defense. Snapping turtle. I respect it."

No. 17 Florida (5-2) vs. No. 3 Georgia (6-1), Saturday at 3:30 p.m. ET on ABC at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta, a neutral site. It's an SEC game: Florida is 3-2 in league play and Georgia is 4-1. DraftKings has Georgia Bulldogs −11.5 with a total of 55.5. My card: Florida Gators +11.5, Over 55.5, and a final of Georgia Bulldogs 37, Florida Gators 28.

Thad speaks for Georgia, needle on the record. "Track nine: blitz! Gunner Stockton has 16 touchdown throws and four interceptions. Raylen Wilson has 44 tackles and four sacks. We score 43.0 a game and allow 15.9. We won at Alabama 20-17. And we've won eight of the last 10 in this series."

Dax speaks for Florida, chart held high. "That play had scales! Jadan Baugh has 763 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns. We score 39.6 a game. We beat Ole Miss 52-28 and won 27-24 at Texas. Jayden Woods has 4.5 sacks. Gators bite."

Why Florida? Because this Gator offense scores, and it just won a tough road game. Florida put 44 on Auburn, 52 on Ole Miss and 66 on Florida Atlantic, and Baugh's 13 rushing touchdowns give it a finisher near the end zone. Georgia isn't untouchable either: it lost 27-24 at home to Auburn last time out and squeaked by Alabama 20-17. The last two meetings were 34-20 and 24-20, and my final has Georgia winning by more than a touchdown but short of 11.5.

The risk is the Florida quarterback spot. Aaron Philo threw the most passes in the first six games, but Tramell Jones Jr. took that role at Texas. And the Gators have been blown out twice, 45-17 at Missouri and 38-19 to South Carolina. If that team shows up, Georgia runs away.

Why the Over? Both offenses can fly. Georgia scores 43.0 a game and Florida scores 39.6. Florida allows 28.3. Georgia hung 70 on Western Kentucky and 45 at Arkansas. Florida's win at Auburn finished 44-39. My 37-28 adds up to 65, over 55.5.

Now the room, and it's all on one page. Next Man Up has Georgia 37-28, and The Formula has Georgia 37-27. Both like Florida with the points and both like the Over. Follow The Money likes Florida and the Over too. In The Trenches stays out of it: Georgia's run game and Florida's run game each have a matchup they like, so they cancel out.

Next Man Up started it: "Georgia wins, Gators keep it inside. Thad, flip the record."

Follow The Money agreed: "The market is drifting Florida's way. I'm with it."

The Formula added: "Two offenses this good? The points come."

Bottom of the card: two rested teams, two offenses that score in bunches, and a Florida team that just won at Texas. Dax holds up a picture of an alligator. Give me the Gators and the Over.

Georgia Bulldogs 37, Florida Gators 28. Florida Gators cover the 11.5. Over 55.5.
