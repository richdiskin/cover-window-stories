Florida Gators at Georgia Bulldogs

Florida Gators (5-2) at Georgia Bulldogs (6-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Mercedes-Benz Stadium · ABC

Florida vs Georgia prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on Florida Gators +11.5 and Over 55.5 at DraftKings, with a final of Georgia Bulldogs 37, Florida Gators 28. This one left Jacksonville this year, so on Halloween the rivalry is in Atlanta at Mercedes-Benz Stadium while EverBank Stadium is rebuilt.

The case for the Gators is an offense that scores and a rested team that just won a tough road game. Florida put 44 on Auburn, 52 on Ole Miss and 66 on Florida Atlantic, and Jadan Baugh's 13 rushing touchdowns give it a finisher near the end zone. Georgia isn't untouchable: it lost 27-24 at home to Auburn last time out and won 20-17 at Alabama. The last two meetings were 34-20 and 24-20. My final has Georgia by nine, inside the 11.5.

Thad Okafor-Brandt, needle on a vinyl record, makes the Georgia case. "Gunner Stockton has 16 touchdown throws and four interceptions. Raylen Wilson has 44 tackles and four sacks. We've won eight of the last 10 in this series."

Dax Marchetti, laminated reptile chart in hand, answers for Florida. "Jadan Baugh has 763 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns. We beat Ole Miss 52-28 and won 27-24 at Texas. Gators bite."

What could go wrong is the Florida quarterback spot. Aaron Philo led the Gators in throws in the first six games, but Tramell Jones Jr. did it at Texas. And the Gators have been blown out twice, 45-17 at Missouri and 38-19 to South Carolina. If that team shows up, Georgia runs away.

The Over is about two offenses that score. Georgia scores 43.0 a game and Florida scores 39.6, and Florida allows 28.3. Georgia hung 70 on Western Kentucky and 45 at Arkansas. Florida's win at Auburn finished 44-39. My 37-28 adds up to 65, above the 55.5.
