Gators and Dawgs in Atlanta: No. 17 Florida Meets No. 3 Georgia on Halloween

Florida Gators (5-2) at Georgia Bulldogs (6-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Mercedes-Benz Stadium · ABC

No. 17 Florida (5-2, 3-2 SEC) vs. No. 3 Georgia (6-1, 4-1 SEC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, Mercedes-Benz Stadium, Atlanta, Ga. (neutral site), ABC.

Your hosts are Dax Marchetti, a Florida herpetology minor who classifies every opponent as a reptile species, and Thaddeus "Thad" Okafor-Brandt, an indie-rock DJ turned defensive film savant who calls every blitz like a set list.

Thad: Welcome to Atlanta, Dax! Track one, side A. This rivalry is a classic record.

Dax: Thanks, Thad. I've been studying your defense all week. I'm classifying it as a snapping turtle.

Thad: Is that a compliment?

Dax: Very much so. Snapping turtles are patient, and they don't let go.

Players to watch

Georgia quarterback Gunner Stockton has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 117 of 158 for 1,570 yards, 16 touchdowns and four interceptions. Dwight Phillips Jr. leads the rushers with 348 yards, and Craig Dandridge leads the receivers with 310 yards. Linebacker Raylen Wilson leads the defense with 44 tackles and four sacks, and defensive back KJ Bolden has an interception.

Florida running back Jadan Baugh is the engine, with 763 yards and 13 touchdowns on 135 carries. Aaron Philo leads the team in passing at 105 of 149 for 1,341 yards, nine touchdowns and three interceptions, and Tramell Jones Jr. threw the most passes at Texas. Dallas Wilson leads the receivers with 29 catches for 403 yards. Linebacker Myles Graham leads the defense with 48 tackles, Jayden Woods has 4.5 sacks, and safety DJ Coleman has two interceptions.

Dax: Thirteen rushing touchdowns, Thad. Jadan Baugh is a crocodile in the red zone.

Thad: Stockton completing that many throws is a perfect guitar solo, Dax.

What happened last week

Both teams had an open date last week, so everybody is rested.

Florida's last game was a 27-24 win at Texas on Oct. 17. Jones went 22 of 35 for 155 yards and two touchdowns, and Baugh ran 25 times for 91 yards and a score.

Dax: Winning in Austin? That play had scales.

Georgia's last game was a 27-24 loss to Auburn on Oct. 17. Stockton threw for 272 yards and two touchdowns, Phillips ran for 68 yards and a score, and Dandridge caught four balls for 88 yards.

Thad: Every great album has one track you skip. We're moving on.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Stockton has thrown the most passes for Georgia in every game. Philo did it for Florida in the first six games and Jones did it at Texas, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Kirby Smart leads Georgia, and CollegeFootballData dates his hire to Dec. 6, 2015. His teams went 14-1, 15-0, 13-1, 11-3 and 12-2 over the last five seasons, and Georgia claims national titles from 2021 and 2022. This year's Bulldogs score 43.0 points a game and allow 15.9.

Jon Sumrall is in his first season leading Florida, and CFBD dates his hire to Nov. 30, 2025. His Gators already have five wins after going 4-8 last season, and they score 39.6 points a game with a ground game that gains 194.3 yards a game.

Thad: Coach Smart is the producer who never misses.

Dax: Coach Sumrall is building a whole new swamp ecosystem. It's thriving.

Player storylines

The matchup to watch is Baugh and the Florida big guys up front against a Georgia defense that allows 15.9 points a game and has 22 sacks. On the other side, Stockton faces a Florida defense that also has 22 sacks. Georgia has won eight of the last 10 meetings, including 24-20 last year, so Florida has some history to change, and Georgia wants to bounce back from the Auburn loss.

What's happening on campus

This one is in Atlanta, at Mercedes-Benz Stadium, so both fan bases will split the building. Expect orange and blue on one side and red and black on the other, plus plenty of Halloween costumes. Back home, Florida plays at Ben Hill Griffin Stadium, the Swamp, listed at 88,548, and Georgia plays at Sanford Stadium, between the hedges, listed at 92,746.

Dax: I'm dressing as an alligator. Obviously.

Thad: I'm dressing as Uga. With a turntable.

Anything else fun and true

Florida claims national titles from 1996, 2006 and 2008, its fight song is "The Orange and Blue," and its band is the Pride of the Sunshine. Georgia claims titles from 1942, 1980, 2021 and 2022, its band is the Georgia Redcoat Marching Band, and its fight song is "Hail to Georgia." Florida's last win in this series came in 2020, 44-28.

Dax: Whatever happens, Thad, dinner's on me afterward. We'll say grace first.

Thad: Deal, Dax. I'll bring the music.

My call: Georgia Bulldogs 37, Florida Gators 28.
