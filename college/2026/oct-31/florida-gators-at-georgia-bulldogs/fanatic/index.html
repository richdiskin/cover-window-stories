Florida vs Georgia: The Rivalry Leaves Jacksonville for Atlanta

Florida Gators (5-2) at Georgia Bulldogs (6-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Mercedes-Benz Stadium · ABC

Florida Gators vs Georgia Bulldogs left Jacksonville for Week 9, and on Halloween the old neutral-site rivalry lands at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta while EverBank Stadium is rebuilt. Dax Marchetti, a Florida herpetology minor who classifies every opponent as a reptile species, says Jadan Baugh, Aaron Philo and Jon Sumrall's Gators win in the Bulldogs' home state, and Thaddeus "Thad" Okafor-Brandt, an indie-rock DJ turned defensive film savant, says Gunner Stockton, Raylen Wilson and Kirby Smart's Bulldogs keep their grip on the series.

Thad: Welcome to Atlanta, Dax! Track one, side A. Same classic record, new turntable.

Dax: Thanks, Thad. I've been studying your defense all week. I'm classifying it as a snapping turtle.

Thad: Is that a compliment?

Dax: Very much so. Snapping turtles are patient, and they don't let go.

Georgia's athletics site says this game has been played in Jacksonville since 1933 with only two exceptions. This year it's billed as the Invesco QQQ Atlanta Gridiron Classic, gates open at 2 p.m. ET and kickoff is at 3:30 p.m. ET. It goes to Tampa next fall and returns to Jacksonville in 2028, once the renovation is done. Scott Stricklin, Florida's athletic director when the deal was announced in 2024, said of Atlanta and Tampa, "Both cities are special to Gator fans and offer venues that will provide wonderful experiences for both fans and players."

Dax: Atlanta is in your home state, Thad. Our lizards are brave.

Thad: Brave lizards are still lizards, Dax.

The party still found a home. Sports Radio 680 The Fan is throwing a free Cocktail Party Tailgate at 123 Marietta Street from noon to 3 p.m. ET, with food, games and music, and Centennial Yards has a free Rise ATL game-day setup from 1 to 4 p.m. ET. The stadium points everybody to the SEC District MARTA stop.

Thad: A cocktail party with a train stop. That's a remix, and I like it.

Dax: I'm riding MARTA in a full alligator suit. Nobody sit near the tail.

Then there's the football. Georgia is 6-1 and 4-1 in the SEC. The Bulldogs had last week off after a 27-24 home loss to Auburn on Oct. 17, when Stockton threw for 272 yards and two touchdowns, Dwight Phillips Jr. ran for 68 yards and a score, and Craig Dandridge caught four balls for 88 yards. They won 20-17 at Alabama the week before.

Thad: Every great album has one track you skip. We're moving on.

Stockton has led Georgia in throws in all seven games and is 117 of 158 for 1,570 yards, 16 touchdowns and four interceptions. Phillips has 348 rushing yards, Dandridge has 310 receiving yards, and Wilson has 44 tackles and four sacks. The Bulldogs score 43.0 points a game and allow 15.9.

Florida is 5-2 and 3-2 in the league and also had last week off. Its last game was a 27-24 win at Texas on Oct. 17, when Tramell Jones Jr. went 22 of 35 for 155 yards and two touchdowns and Baugh ran 25 times for 91 yards and a score. Philo had led the Gators in throws in the first six games.

Dax: Winning in Austin? That play had scales.

Baugh has 763 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns on 135 carries. Philo is 105 of 149 for 1,341 yards and nine touchdowns, Dallas Wilson has 29 catches for 403 yards, Myles Graham has 48 tackles and Jayden Woods has 4.5 sacks. Florida scores 39.6 points a game and put up 66 on Florida Atlantic, 52 on Ole Miss and 44 at Auburn.

Dax: Thirteen rushing touchdowns, Thad. Jadan Baugh is a crocodile near the end zone.

Thad: Stockton completing that many throws is a perfect guitar solo, Dax.

Smart has led Georgia since 2016, and his teams went 14-1, 15-0, 13-1, 11-3 and 12-2 over the last five seasons, with national titles in 2021 and 2022. Sumrall is in his first season at Florida, and the Gators already have five wins after going 4-8 last year.

Thad: Coach Smart is the producer who never misses.

Dax: Coach Sumrall is building a whole new swamp ecosystem. It's thriving.

The series has been Georgia's lately. The Bulldogs have won eight of the last 10, including 34-20 in 2024 and 24-20 last year. Florida's last win was 44-28 in 2020.

Thad: Eight of 10, Dax. That's a greatest-hits album.

Dax: 2020 is on it too, Thad. Track seven. Crowd pleaser.

The matchup is Baugh and the Florida blockers against a Georgia defense with 22 sacks. On the other side, Stockton faces a Florida defense that also has 22 sacks.

The SEC's initial availability report, posted at 8:10 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists Florida quarterbacks Tramell Jones Jr. and Aaron Philo questionable. Georgia lists three players out and three questionable, and Gunner Stockton is not on it.

Thad: I'm dressing as Uga. With a turntable.

Dax: Then I'll dress as Albert, and we'll ride MARTA home together.

Thad: Deal, Dax. Dinner's on me afterward, and I'll bring the music.

My call: Georgia Bulldogs 37, Florida Gators 28.
