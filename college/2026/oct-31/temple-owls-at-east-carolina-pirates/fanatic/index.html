Temple vs East Carolina: Marching PeeDee Helmets on Halloween

Temple Owls (2-5) at East Carolina Pirates (3-4)

3:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium · ESPN+

Temple Owls vs East Carolina Pirates is Week 9's Fan Appreciation Day at Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium, and the Pirates are debuting new helmets with "marching PeeDee" on a Halloween afternoon in Greenville. Teddy Fiorella, a north Philly gym rat who grades effort like a Rocky montage, says Ajani Sheppard, Keveun Mason and K.C. Keeler's Owls end a three-game skid in this series, and Bart Quidley, a Pitt County tailgater in a purple tricorn hat, says TJ Engleman Jr., Landon Sides and Blake Harrell's Pirates make it four.

Bart: Ahoy, Teddy! New helmets, PeeDee marching right on the side. Hoist the colors.

Teddy: Nice lids, Bart. Does PeeDee march to Philly? We've got stairs for him.

Bart: PeeDee doesn't do stairs. PeeDee does parades.

He really does on Saturday. ECU's Halloween schedule has the Pirate Walk at 12:45 p.m. ET, with the team and the cheerleaders handing out candy along the route, and a costume parade following the team in. Kids can meet PeeDee at the Howard & Lyn Swain Promenade to join it. After the walk, ECU athletes hand out candy in front of TowneBank Tower, there's a kids costume contest at 2 in Charley's Kids Zone near Gate 4, and The Embers featuring Craig Woolard play the Tailgate Plaza before kickoff. Season-ticket holders can buy extra seats for $10.31, and student guest tickets are $10.31, too.

Teddy: Ten thirty-one for Ten thirty-one. Okay, Bart, that's clever. I'll give you that one.

Bart: First compliment from Philly all year. I'm framing it.

Then there's the football, and Bart has recent history on his side. ECU won the last three meetings, 49-46 in 2022, 56-34 in 2024 and 45-14 last season. In CollegeFootballData's meetings back to 2016, East Carolina leads 5-4, and Temple's biggest one was a 49-6 win in 2018.

Teddy: 2018, Bart. Write it on the tricorn.

Bart: There's no room. It's full of 2024.

East Carolina is 3-4 and 1-2 in the American. The Pirates have lost two straight in overtime, 30-17 at UAB and 41-34 at Memphis on Thursday, Oct. 22. Mitch Griffis threw for 199 yards at Memphis, Engleman ran 18 times for 92 yards and a touchdown, and Sides caught seven balls for 130 yards and a score. At home, ECU beat Rice 28-12 and North Carolina Central 42-9 and lost to App State 27-24.

Bart: Two overtimes on the road. We battled. The hull held, the sails didn't.

Emory Williams is 73 of 117 for 716 yards, seven touchdowns and two interceptions, and Griffis has led the Pirates in throws in four games. Engleman has 582 rushing yards and four touchdowns, Ray Ray Joseph Jr. has 31 catches, DJ Johnson Jr. has 51 tackles and Jasiyah Robinson has 2.5 sacks.

Temple is 2-5 and 1-2 in the league and had last week off. Its last game was a 38-31 loss to Charlotte on Oct. 17, when Sheppard threw for 236 yards and ran for two touchdowns and Peter Clarke caught five balls for 127 yards and a score.

Teddy: We rested. We trained. We drank raw eggs. Okay, not the eggs.

Sheppard has led Temple in throws in six of seven games and is 74 of 120 for 1,127 yards and eight touchdowns. Mason has 513 rushing yards and three scores, Colin Chase has 18 catches for 308 yards, and Curly Ordonez leads the defense with 63 tackles and two sacks. The Owls nearly won at Toledo, losing 49-48 in overtime, lost 21-17 to Army and beat South Florida 17-13 in Tampa.

Teddy: Curly Ordonez leads us in tackles and sacks, Bart. Running up the museum steps every snap.

Keeler leads Temple, and CollegeFootballData dates his hire to Dec. 1, 2024. His Owls run for 202.4 yards a game and give up only 37.3 penalty yards a game. Harrell is East Carolina's head coach, and CFBD records his hire date as Nov. 27, 2024. His defense allows 187.5 passing yards a game and has returned two interceptions for touchdowns.

The matchup is Mason and the Temple run game against an ECU defense that allows 3.3 yards a carry. On the other side, Engleman wants another big day and Sides is coming off his best night of the year.

Bart: I'm dressing as PeeDee, Teddy. Marching version.

Teddy: I'm dressing as Hooter the Owl. Nobody insult Hooter.

Bart: Teddy, nobody has insulted Hooter. Hooter is a fine owl.

Teddy: See, that's Pirate kind. After the game, the cheesesteaks are on me.

Bart: And the barbecue's on me. Eastern style, vinegar, no arguments.

My call: East Carolina Pirates 30, Temple Owls 23.
