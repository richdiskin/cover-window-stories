Philly Grit Sails Into Greenville: Temple Visits East Carolina on Halloween

Temple Owls (2-5) at East Carolina Pirates (3-4)

3:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium · ESPN+

Temple (2-5, 1-2 American) at East Carolina (3-4, 1-2 American). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3 p.m. ET, Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium, Greenville, N.C., ESPN+.

Your hosts are Teddy Fiorella, a north Philly gym rat who grades effort like a Rocky montage, and Bart Quidley, a Pitt County tailgater in a purple tricorn hat.

Bart: Ahoy, Teddy! Welcome aboard. My family has tailgated this exact spot since before I could walk.

Teddy: Nice spot, Bart. Not as nice as South Philly, but nice. And before you say anything about cheesesteaks, don't.

Bart: Teddy, I didn't say a word.

Teddy: You were thinking it.

Players to watch

Temple quarterback Ajani Sheppard has thrown the most passes in six of seven games. He's 74 of 120 for 1,127 yards and eight touchdowns, and he can run, too. Keveun Mason leads the rushers with 513 yards and three scores, and Colin Chase leads the receivers with 18 catches for 308 yards. Linebacker Curly Ordonez leads the defense with 63 tackles and two sacks.

East Carolina has used two quarterbacks. Emory Williams leads the team at 73 of 117 for 716 yards, seven touchdowns and two interceptions, and Mitch Griffis threw the most passes at Memphis last week. TJ Engleman Jr. leads the rushers with 582 yards and four touchdowns, and Ray Ray Joseph Jr. leads the receivers with 31 catches. Linebacker DJ Johnson Jr. has 51 tackles, and Jasiyah Robinson has 2.5 sacks.

Teddy: Curly Ordonez leads us in tackles and sacks. That's a Rocky montage, Bart. Running up the steps every single play.

What happened last week

Temple had an open date last week. Its last game was a 38-31 loss to Charlotte on Oct. 17, when Sheppard threw for 236 yards and ran for two touchdowns, and Peter Clarke caught five balls for 127 yards and a score.

Teddy: We rested. We trained. We drank raw eggs. Okay, not the eggs.

East Carolina lost 41-34 in overtime at Memphis on Thursday, Oct. 22. Griffis threw for 199 yards, Engleman ran 18 times for 92 yards and a touchdown, and Landon Sides caught seven balls for 130 yards and a score.

Bart: Overtime on the road. We battled. I graded the Memphis tiger's costume, and it was brushed.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Sheppard has thrown the most passes for Temple in six of seven games, while Griffis and Williams have traded that role for East Carolina, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

K.C. Keeler leads Temple, and CollegeFootballData dates his hire to Dec. 1, 2024. His Owls run the ball on most plays, gaining 202.4 rushing yards a game, and they're disciplined, with only 37.3 penalty yards a game.

Blake Harrell is East Carolina's head coach, and CFBD records his hire date as Nov. 27, 2024. His defense allows 187.5 passing yards a game and has returned two interceptions for touchdowns this season.

Bart: The defense is our sturdy hull, Teddy.

Teddy: Our ground game is our Rocky montage. We'll see whose works best.

Player storylines

The matchup to watch is Mason and the Temple ground game against an ECU defense that allows 3.3 yards a carry. Temple's big guys up front will need to win in the trenches. On the other side, Engleman wants another big day, and Sides is coming off a big night at Memphis. ECU won the last three meetings, so Temple has some motivation.

What's happening on campus

Except for the 1999 Miami game, the Pirates have played every home game at Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium since 1963, and CFBD lists it at 50,000 on natural grass. A Halloween afternoon in Greenville should bring plenty of purple-and-gold costumes, and Bart's tricorn hat is coming out for this one. Temple plays home games at Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia, listed at 69,879.

Teddy: I'm dressing as Hooter the Owl. Nobody insult Hooter.

Bart: Teddy, nobody has insulted Hooter.

Anything else fun and true

East Carolina played its first season in 1932 and has won seven conference championships and eleven bowl games. The Pirates went 9-4 last year. Temple went 5-7 last season, and its fight song is "Fight! Temple Fight!" These two have met often: ECU won 49-46 in 2022, 56-34 in 2024 and 45-14 in 2025, while Temple won 49-6 in 2018.

Bart: Teddy, whatever happens, the barbecue's on me after the game. We'll say grace first.

Teddy: Deal, Bart. That's Temple Made, and that's Pirate kind.

My call: East Carolina Pirates 30, Temple Owls 23.
