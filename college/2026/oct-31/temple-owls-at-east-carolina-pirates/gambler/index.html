Temple Owls at East Carolina Pirates

Temple Owls (2-5) at East Carolina Pirates (3-4)

3:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium · ESPN+

Temple vs East Carolina prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on East Carolina Pirates −6 and Over 49.5 at DraftKings, with a final of East Carolina Pirates 30, Temple Owls 23. It's Fan Appreciation Day at Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium, on Halloween, and the Pirates debut new helmets with marching PeeDee on the side.

The case for the Pirates is this series and their home field. ECU won the last three meetings, 49-46, 56-34 and 45-14. At Dowdy-Ficklen this year, the Pirates beat Rice 28-12 and North Carolina Central 42-9 and lost to App State by a field goal. Temple allows 28.0 points a game and has lost three of its last four, including a 34-20 home loss to UConn. ECU just took Memphis to overtime on the road, and Landon Sides caught seven balls for 130 yards. My final has the Pirates by seven.

Bart Quidley, purple tricorn hat on, does the ECU talking. "TJ Engleman Jr. has run for 582 yards. DJ Johnson Jr. has 51 tackles. And we beat Temple 56-34 two years ago and 45-14 last year."

Teddy Fiorella, shadowboxing in a gray hoodie, isn't having it. "Bart, Ajani Sheppard has eight touchdown throws. Keveun Mason has 513 rushing yards. Curly Ordonez has 63 tackles. We beat South Florida 17-13 in Tampa, and we had a week off."

What could go wrong is the ECU offense. It has gone back and forth between Mitch Griffis and Emory Williams at quarterback, and Griffis threw two interceptions at Memphis. Temple keeps games close, too: it lost 49-48 in overtime at Toledo and 21-17 to Army.

The Over is about the scoreboard in this series. Each of the last three meetings finished well past 49.5. Temple's last two games ended 34-20 and 38-31, and ECU's last one ended 41-34. Temple allows 28.0 points a game and ECU allows 26.3. My 30-23 adds up to 53, above the 49.5.
