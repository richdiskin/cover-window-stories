Wednesday Night at Floyd: Gagliano's Blue Raiders Try to Make It Three Straight Against a Kennesaw State Team That Plays Everyone Close

Kennesaw State Owls (1-6) at Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders (4-3)

7:00 PM ET Wed Oct 28 · Johnny "Red" Floyd Stadium · CBSSN

Kennesaw State (1-6, 0-3 CUSA) at Middle Tennessee (4-3, 2-1 CUSA). Wednesday, Oct. 28, 7 p.m. ET, Johnny "Red" Floyd Stadium, Murfreesboro, Tenn., CBS Sports Network.

Your hosts are Opal Stonebriar, a Murfreesboro biscuit baker who keeps Grandmama's ledger of every Blue Raiders game, and Ximena Holcombe-Reyes, a Marietta coder who has cheered Kennesaw State since version 1.0.

Opal: Come on in, sugar. The biscuits are warm.

Ximena: I brought my laptop. I'm logging every snap.

Players to watch

Middle Tennessee quarterback Roman Gagliano is 126 of 215 for 1,404 yards, 12 touchdowns and just two interceptions. Antonio Martin leads the rushers with 480 yards, and wide receiver Cam'ron Lacy has 41 catches for 437 yards and four scores. Linebacker Korey Smith leads the defense with 66 tackles.

Kennesaw State's Rickie Collins does a little of everything. He is 144 of 210 for 1,426 yards with four touchdowns, and he is also the Owls' leading rusher with 295 yards and five touchdowns. Wide receiver Brayden Munroe has 22 catches for 352 yards. Linebacker Tywon Christopher leads the team with 54 tackles, and defensive lineman Ugonna Nwoha has three sacks.

Ximena: Collins is basically the whole app. Passing module, running module, all of it.

What happened last week

Middle Tennessee won 26-23 at FIU on Oct. 20. Gagliano went 16 of 27 for 200 yards and a touchdown, and Terrence Brown ran 18 times for 158 yards and a score. Lacy caught six passes for 68 yards. That came a week after a 33-30 home win over Delaware.

Opal: One hundred fifty-eight yards for Terrence Brown. Grandmama underlined that one twice.

Kennesaw State lost 34-6 to Liberty on Oct. 22. Collins went 15 of 26 for 124 yards, and Munroe caught three passes for 53 yards. Linebacker Baron Hopson made a season-high 12 tackles. Late in the fourth quarter, coach Jerry Mack gave Landon Varnes some snaps at quarterback.

Ximena: Not our best build. Patch notes are coming.

Injuries and availability

Neither school's box scores mark a starter. Gagliano has thrown the most passes for Middle Tennessee in every game this season, and Collins has done the same for Kennesaw State.

What the coaches are up to

Derek Mason is in his third season at Middle Tennessee, after coaching Vanderbilt from 2014 to 2020. His Blue Raiders went 3-9 in both 2024 and 2025, so four wins already tops last season. Three of those wins came by seven points or fewer.

Jerry Mack is in his second season at Kennesaw State. He went 31-15 as head coach at North Carolina Central and later worked at Rice. His Owls are the defending Conference USA champions, and three of their six losses this year came by three points or fewer.

Opal: Coach Mason has us winning the close ones. Grandmama always said a win by three still goes in the ledger.

Player storylines

Gagliano has been steady all season. He threw two touchdown passes in each of the first four games and three against Delaware.

In the Owls' 27-26 loss to Jacksonville State on Oct. 7, he threw for 293 yards. Hopson was a big part of last year's title team, with 138 tackles.

Ximena: Hopson tackles like he's closing tickets. Twelve last week, and he wants more.

What's happening on campus

Middle Tennessee is calling this one Halloween at Floyd, a few days before the real thing. For Kennesaw State, it's a short week and only the second trip to Murfreesboro in program history.

Opal: Halloween at Floyd! I'm dressing as a biscuit, and I'm not sorry.

Ximena: I'm coming as a semicolon. Nobody will get it.

Anything else fun and true

These two have split their last two meetings, both low scoring. Middle Tennessee won 14-5 in 2024, and Kennesaw State won 24-16 last September in Kennesaw. Both teams have also played Jacksonville State close this year: the Gamecocks beat Middle Tennessee 23-13 and Kennesaw State 27-26.

Opal: Fourteen to five. Grandmama wrote that one in pencil because she couldn't believe it.

Ximena: One to one in the version history. Tonight is the tiebreaker commit.

Opal: Biscuits for both sidelines, sugar. But I know which side gets the honey.

Ximena: Fair. Let's go, Owls.

My call: Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders 24, Kennesaw State Owls 23.
