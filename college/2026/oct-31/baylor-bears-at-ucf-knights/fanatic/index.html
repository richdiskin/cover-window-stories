Baylor vs UCF: Silobration Waco Meets Mission X Space Game

Baylor Bears (4-3) at UCF Knights (4-3)

7:30 PM ET Fri Oct 30 · Acrisure Bounce House · ESPN

Baylor Bears vs UCF Knights is the Week 9 Friday night Space Game at the Acrisure Bounce House, where the Knights will wear all-white Mission X uniforms inspired by Mercury, Gemini and Apollo spacesuits while Baylor fan Maggie Whitlock is still talking about Magnolia's Silobration in downtown Waco. Maggie, an interior designer who spent last weekend at the Silos vendor fair, says interim coach Joe Klanderman's defense is the one remodel that survived Waco's hard week, and UCF fan Knox Brevard-Stiles says Keyone Jenkins is about to send Baylor's week into orbit.

Knox: Midfield in our stadium sits on the exact latitude of Launch Complex 39A, Maggie. Thirty-one miles east. We aim everything that way.

Maggie: Sugar, I spent Saturday listening to Dylan Gossett at the Silos and checking my phone for the Kansas score. You don't want to hear about the rest of my weekend.

Here is the rest of her weekend. Baylor lost 25-24 at Kansas when Rhett Armstrong's 45-yard field goal try was blocked with 22 seconds left, the third Baylor kick the Jayhawks got a hand on that day. Gavin Freeman caught nine passes for 123 yards. After the game, athletic director Doug McNamee relieved seventh-year head coach Dave Aranda of his duties and elevated Klanderman, the first-year defensive coordinator and safeties coach, to interim head coach for the rest of the season.

Maggie: Klanderman won four national titles at North Dakota State and ran the defense at Kansas State for seven years. You keep the man who built the frame.

Knox spent the same Saturday screaming in Orlando. UCF beat No. 2 BYU 39-31 in double overtime at home. Jenkins went 25 of 41 for 293 yards and three touchdowns, ran for another, and found Josh Derry on the first play of overtime. Tyren Hornes caught six balls for 137 yards.

Knox: The highest-ranked team ever to play at the Bounce House, and we sent it home. My throat still hurts.

The Space Game is a real piece of UCF history. The school was founded in 1963 as Florida Technological University to supply people for the space program at Kennedy Space Center and Cape Canaveral, and Friday is the 10th annual edition. UCF is 8-1 all-time in Space Games after Houston won 30-27 in 2025, and the Knights go back to their original Citronauts name for the night. It is the first Space Game against Baylor.

Maggie: Eight and one. So your rocket has come down before.

Knox: Once. Gravity got lucky.

Frost made the quarterback call early and kept Jenkins in charge. "He's the hot hand right now," he said, and he added that Alonza Barnett III, out since a hamstring injury in Week 2, "probably could play" but might not be fully healthy. Jenkins is at 1,099 passing yards, 11 touchdowns and three interceptions, with four rushing scores. Duke Watson has 577 yards on the ground.

The Big 12's updated availability report, posted at 10 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists Baylor with five players out. It lists UCF quarterback Alonza Barnett III questionable, with five other players out and one questionable.

Baylor's offense goes through DJ Lagway and Freeman. Lagway has 1,352 passing yards with nine touchdowns and four interceptions, and Freeman ranks third in the nation in receptions per game with 58 catches for 678 yards. The defense is the real calling card, though. Baylor ranks third in the nation in sacks and fifth in tackles for loss per game and allows 16.1 points a game. Garrick Ponder has four sacks.

Maggie: That defense is the load-bearing wall in this house. Don't touch it.

The coaches carry very different stories into Orlando. Scott Frost is in his second year of his second stint at UCF after leading the Knights in 2016 and 2017, when his 2017 team went 13-0. Klanderman is coaching his first game as the man in charge.

The series is short and loud. UCF won the first meeting, 52-42 in the 2013 Fiesta Bowl. In 2023 in Orlando, Baylor rallied from 35-7 down in the third quarter to win 36-35, the largest comeback in Baylor history. In 2025 the Bears won 30-3 in Waco.

Knox: That comeback still makes my stomach drop like the first hill on a coaster. I've ridden a lot of them in this town.

Maggie: Then sit tight, Knox. Waco is going to keep its party going. Saturday the Día de los Muertos parade rolls through Indian Spring Park, and I'd like to show up with a win.

Knox: You can have the parade. Friday night, the rocket is ours.

My call: UCF Knights 27, Baylor Bears 25.
