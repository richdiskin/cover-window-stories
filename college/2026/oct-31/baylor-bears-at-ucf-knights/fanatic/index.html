Space Game Friday at the Bounce House: Baylor Brings an Interim Coach to Orlando

Baylor Bears (4-3) at UCF Knights (4-3)

7:30 PM ET Fri Oct 30 · Acrisure Bounce House · ESPN

Baylor (4-3, 2-2 Big 12) at UCF (4-3, 2-2 Big 12). Friday, Oct. 30, 7:30 p.m. ET (6:30 p.m. Central), Acrisure Bounce House, Orlando, ESPN.

Your hosts are Maggie Whitlock, a Waco interior designer who renovates roster weaknesses live on air, and Knox Brevard-Stiles, a UCF roller-coaster devotee who runs a Ride Check before every big game.

Knox: Ride check, Maggie! Lap bar down, arms inside, and welcome to the annual Space Game.

Maggie: Sugar, I brought my tape measure and a hard hat. It's been a big week of remodeling in Waco.

Players to watch

UCF quarterback Keyone Jenkins is 108 of 159 for 1,099 yards, 11 touchdowns and three interceptions, and he has run for four more scores. Running back Duke Watson has 577 yards on 85 carries, and wide receiver Josh Derry leads the team with 34 catches for 358 yards. Linebacker Tackett Curtis has 42 tackles, and Isaiah Nixon has 2.5 sacks.

Baylor quarterback DJ Lagway is 113 of 217 for 1,352 yards, nine touchdowns and four interceptions. Wide receiver Gavin Freeman has 58 catches for 678 yards and ranks third in the nation in receptions per game. Linebacker Travion Barnes has 51 tackles, and defensive end Garrick Ponder has four sacks.

Maggie: Freeman is the load-bearing wall in this house. Don't touch him.

What happened last week

UCF beat No. 2 BYU 39-31 in double overtime at home. Jenkins went 25 of 41 for 293 yards and three touchdowns, ran for another score, and found Derry on the first play of overtime. Tyren Hornes caught six passes for 137 yards.

Baylor lost 25-24 at Kansas. A 45-yard field goal try with 22 seconds left was blocked, the third Baylor field goal try the Jayhawks blocked that day. Freeman caught nine passes for 123 yards.

Knox: Thirty-nine to thirty-one over the No. 2 team in the country. My hands are still up in the air.

Injuries and availability

The Big 12 has not posted an availability report for this game. UCF coach Scott Frost said Jenkins will start again. Alonza Barnett III, who hurt his hamstring in Week 2, is making progress, and Frost said, "I think he probably could play," though he was not sure Barnett was fully healthy yet. No injury news has surfaced for Baylor's starting quarterback, so watch the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Baylor parted ways with seventh-year coach Dave Aranda after the Kansas game. Athletic director Doug McNamee called Aranda "a genuine and honorable person" who "deeply cares for his players." Joe Klanderman, the first-year defensive coordinator, is the interim head coach for the rest of the season. He won four national titles at North Dakota State and led the defense at Kansas State before coming to Waco.

Scott Frost is in his second year of his second stint at UCF. He led the Knights in 2016 and 2017, and his 2017 team went 13-0.

Maggie: New management, same foundation. Coach Klanderman built this defense.

Player storylines

Jenkins has been the steady hand while Barnett heals. After the BYU win, Jenkins said, "It's still his team. Still his starting job." That is a great teammate talking.

Baylor's defense ranks third in the nation in sacks and fifth in tackles for loss per game. Watson and the big guys up front for UCF will need a strong night.

Knox: Jenkins giving his teammate the credit? That's a first-class ride, Maggie. Pure class.

What's happening on campus

Friday is UCF's annual Space Game, one of the fun themed nights on the schedule. Mike Monaco, Jordan Rodgers and Dana Boyle have the call on ESPN.

Maggie: A space theme? I'm repainting the den in galaxy blue the minute I get home.

Anything else fun and true

Baylor leads the series 2-1. UCF won the first meeting, 52-42 in the 2013 Fiesta Bowl. In 2023 in Orlando, Baylor came back from 35-7 down in the third quarter to win 36-35, the biggest comeback in Bears history, and in 2025 Baylor won 30-3 in Waco. After this one, Baylor goes home for Homecoming against Iowa State on Nov. 7.

Knox: A 28-point comeback in our house? That one still makes my stomach drop like the big hill.

Maggie: Then buckle up, Knox. We like a late build.

My call: UCF Knights 27, Baylor Bears 25.
