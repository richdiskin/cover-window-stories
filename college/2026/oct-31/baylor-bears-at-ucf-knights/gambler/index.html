Baylor Bears at UCF Knights

Baylor Bears (4-3) at UCF Knights (4-3)

7:30 PM ET Fri Oct 30 · Acrisure Bounce House · ESPN

Baylor vs UCF prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: UCF Knights −1.5 and Over 47.5 at DraftKings, with my final at UCF Knights 27, Baylor Bears 25. It is the 10th annual Space Game at the Acrisure Bounce House, and the case for the Knights is simple: one sideline just launched its season against No. 2 BYU, and the other spent the week changing head coaches.

Begin with Baylor's week, because it shapes everything. Maggie Whitlock was at Magnolia's Silobration in downtown Waco on Saturday when the Bears lost 25-24 at Kansas, with three field goal tries blocked, the last one with 22 seconds left. Dave Aranda was relieved of his duties afterward, and defensive coordinator Joe Klanderman is the interim head coach. It is Baylor's second straight road trip, and the Bears have lost two in a row, 17-6 to TCU and then Kansas.

"Sugar, the frame is still good," Maggie says. "Klanderman built this defense, we allow 16.1 points a game, and Gavin Freeman has 58 catches for 678 yards. We beat you 30-3 last year."

Knox Brevard-Stiles, who measures every UCF season like a coaster ride, is still on the high from Saturday. "Keyone Jenkins went 25 of 41 for 293 yards and three touchdowns against BYU. Scott Frost says he's the hot hand. Duke Watson has 577 rushing yards. Arms inside the car, Maggie."

The matchup that decides the side is on the ground. Baylor gets only 3.8 yards a carry, and UCF's defense allows 3.2, so DJ Lagway has to throw for everything. Jenkins, meanwhile, has 11 touchdown passes against three interceptions and threw four at Oklahoma State before the BYU game. A home team with the steadier quarterback needs only two points here, and my final gives it exactly that.

The danger is the defense Klanderman knows best. Baylor held six straight opponents to 19 or fewer before Kansas, and if Lagway plays the way he did in a 55-19 win at Arizona State, with 306 yards and four touchdowns, the Bears win outright. A two-point margin leaves no room for a blocked kick going the other way.

On the total, both offenses have shown they can run up a scoreboard. UCF lost 42-28 at Oklahoma State and beat BYU 39-31. Action Network's opening listing had 48.5, DraftKings still showed 48.5 on Monday, and it has slipped to 47.5. My 27-25 adds up to 52.

The room disagrees on the side. Next Man Up projects UCF 29-23 and likes the Knights and the Over. The Formula has Baylor 26-25 and lands on Baylor without backing it, while liking the Over. Follow The Money has no side pick and likes the Under, and it keeps no score of its own. In The Trenches has no pick, and Letdown Watch has no score this week.

The Formula: "Twenty-six to twenty-five. A coin flip with a Baylor sticker on it."

Next Man Up: "Coin flip in Orlando lands on the Knights."

Midfield at the Bounce House sits on the same latitude as Launch Complex 39A, thirty-one miles east. Knox expects liftoff. Maggie expects her defense to keep the rocket on the pad.

UCF Knights 27, Baylor Bears 25. UCF Knights cover the 1.5. Over 47.5.
