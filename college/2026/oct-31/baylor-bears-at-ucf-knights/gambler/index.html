Baylor Bears at UCF Knights

Baylor Bears (4-3) at UCF Knights (4-3)

7:30 PM ET Fri Oct 30 · Acrisure Bounce House · ESPN

Baylor fired Dave Aranda after a 25-24 loss at Kansas, and now interim coach Joe Klanderman takes the Bears to Orlando on a Friday night. Maggie Whitlock packed her toolbox for a full remodel. Knox Brevard-Stiles is still riding the high from a double-overtime win over No. 2 BYU.

Baylor (4-3) at UCF (4-3), Friday at 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN at the Acrisure Bounce House in Orlando. It's a Big 12 game. DraftKings has UCF Knights −1.5 with a total of 47.5. My card: UCF Knights −1.5, Over 47.5, and a final of UCF Knights 27, Baylor Bears 25.

Knox speaks for UCF. "Ride check: Keyone Jenkins went 25 of 41 for 293 yards and three touchdowns in the overtime win over BYU, and Coach Frost says he's the hot hand. Duke Watson has 577 rushing yards. Keep your arms inside the car, Maggie."

Maggie speaks for Baylor. "Sugar, we're under new management, but the foundation is still good. Coach Klanderman built this defense, and we allow 16.1 points a game. Gavin Freeman has 58 catches for 678 yards. And we beat you 30-3 last year."

Here is the part that matters. Baylor has lost two straight, 17-6 to TCU and 25-24 at Kansas, where three of its field-goal tries were blocked. A midseason coaching change can light a team up or knock it flat, and this is a second straight road trip.

Why UCF? Because the Knights have found a quarterback. Jenkins has 11 touchdown passes and only three interceptions, and he threw four touchdown passes at Oklahoma State before beating BYU. Baylor's run game gets 3.8 yards a carry against a UCF defense that allows 3.2, and DJ Lagway has to carry the offense. My final has UCF by two, just enough.

The worry is that Baylor defense. Klanderman knows it best, and the Bears held six straight opponents to 19 or fewer before Kansas. If Lagway plays like he did at Arizona State, 306 yards and four touchdowns, Baylor wins this outright. The Formula has Baylor by one.

Why the Over/Under? UCF lost 42-28 at Oklahoma State and beat BYU 39-31. Baylor scored 55 at Arizona State. DraftKings had this total at 48.5 on Monday and it has dipped to 47.5, the wrong direction with a hot home quarterback. My 27-25 adds up to 52, four and a half over the over/under 47.5.

Now the room. Next Man Up has UCF 29-23 and likes the Knights and the Over. The Formula has Baylor 26-25, takes Baylor and likes the Over. Follow The Money stays off the side and likes the Under after the total fell. In The Trenches sees the run gaps cancel and has no pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

The Formula: "Twenty-six to twenty-five. A coin flip with a Baylor sticker on it."

Next Man Up: "Coin flip in Orlando lands on the Knights."

Follow The Money: "The total slipped two points. Somebody respects the defenses."

Bottom of the card: a home team that just found its quarterback against a road team that just lost its coach. Knox says the ride is open. Maggie says her renovation is still in progress.

UCF Knights 27, Baylor Bears 25. UCF Knights cover the 1.5. Over 47.5.
