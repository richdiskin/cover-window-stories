Jacksonville State Gamecocks at New Mexico State Aggies

Jacksonville State Gamecocks (5-2) at New Mexico State Aggies (3-4)

8:00 PM ET Wed Oct 28 · Aggie Memorial Stadium · ESPN2

Burl Ashcraft-Neeley has had his smoker going since the open week ended, because low and slow wins the fourth quarter. Esperanza Lujan-Tafoya has the roaster set to Hatch hot in Las Cruces, and she has one name ready for every question: James Jones.

Jacksonville State (5-2) at New Mexico State (3-4), Wednesday at 8:00 p.m. ET on ESPN2 at Aggie Memorial Stadium in Las Cruces. It's a Conference USA game. DraftKings has Jacksonville State Gamecocks −3 with a total of 49.5. My card: New Mexico State Aggies +3, Over 49.5, and a final of Jacksonville State Gamecocks 27, New Mexico State Aggies 26.

Burl speaks for Jacksonville State. "We are 3-0 in the league and rested. Caden Creel leads us in passing with 1,637 yards and in rushing with 500 more. And we know how to close: 31-27 over Georgia Southern, 27-26 at Kennesaw State, 27-24 over FIU. Fourth quarter is dinner time."

Esperanza speaks for New Mexico State. "Dinner? Jones has 777 rushing yards and just ran for 178 at Sam Houston. The Jacksonville State coach himself said it will be the best running attack his team has played against. And remember 2023, Burl. Aggies 20, Gamecocks 17."

Here is the part that matters. Jacksonville State's last three wins came by four points, one point and three points. They win, but they do not pull away, and they are 1-2 on the road.

Why New Mexico State? Because three points is the exact margin of the way Jacksonville State has been winning. The Aggies are home, they rush behind a back who leads the league, and Adam Damante has thrown the most passes in each of the last four games. My final has the Gamecocks by one, and the Aggies stay inside the 3.

The worry is the other side of the ball. In The Trenches notes Jacksonville State runs for 4.5 yards a carry against a New Mexico State defense that allows 3.3, and that gap favours the Gamecocks. And the last meeting was ugly: 54-13 Gamecocks in 2024.

Why the Over/Under? Jacksonville State scores 27.3 a game and allows 22.7. New Mexico State scores 24.6 and allows 25.4. Both offenses live in the twenties. DraftKings had this total at 50.5 on Monday, and the slide to 49.5 is a gift. My 27-26 adds up to 53, three and a half clear of the over/under 49.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Jacksonville State 26-24 with New Mexico State and the Over. Next Man Up actually has the Aggies winning, 27-26, and likes the Aggies and the Over. In The Trenches likes Jacksonville State. Follow The Money skips the side and likes the Under after the drop. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "I have the home team winning outright. Getting three on top of that is a nice dessert."

In The Trenches: "Enjoy dessert. The Gamecocks run on that front all night."

Follow The Money: "The total dropped a point. I am the only one who noticed."

Bottom of the card: a road team that wins by a field goal or less against a home team with the best back in the league. Burl keeps the brisket. Esperanza keeps the points.

Jacksonville State Gamecocks 27, New Mexico State Aggies 26. New Mexico State Aggies cover the 3. Over 49.5.
