Arizona State Sun Devils at BYU Cougars

Arizona State Sun Devils (4-3) at BYU Cougars (6-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · LaVell Edwards Stadium · Fox

Bryce Halvorsen-Tuilagi is unfolding a trail map of the LaVell Edwards Stadium parking lots when Skylar Vanterpool strolls up in a maroon sunhat the size of a patio umbrella, handing out water bottles. "Bryce," Skylar says, "you're at elevation. Drink this." Bryce takes the bottle. "Oh my heck. Why did the Sun Devil bring sunscreen to Provo? Because it heard our offense was blinding." Skylar sighs. "Hydrate."

Arizona State (4-3) at No. 8 BYU (6-1), Saturday at 3:30 p.m. ET on Fox at LaVell Edwards Stadium in Provo. It's a Big 12 game: Arizona State is 2-2 in league play and BYU is 3-1. DraftKings has BYU Cougars −16.5 with a total of 48.5. My card: BYU Cougars −16.5, Over 48.5, and a final of BYU Cougars 37, Arizona State Sun Devils 19.

Bryce speaks for BYU, map in hand. "Bear Bachmeier has 11 touchdown throws. Legend Glasker has 498 receiving yards. Cade Uluave has 45 tackles. We beat Notre Dame 28-24 and won at TCU. And we allow 18.6 points a game."

Skylar speaks for Arizona State, sunhat tilted. "Hydrate and dominate! Reed Harris has 800 receiving yards and eight touchdowns. Cardae Mack ran for 159 yards and four scores against Kansas State. Owen Long has 63 tackles. And we beat BYU 28-23 the last time we played. Sip of water."

Why BYU? Because BYU is unbeaten at home, and this Arizona State defense has been run over by good teams. The Sun Devils lost 48-20 at Texas A&M and 53-25 at Texas Tech, and Baylor beat them 55-19 in Tempe. BYU beat Utah Tech 63-7, Iowa State 24-10 and Notre Dame at home. My final has BYU winning by more than 16.5.

The risk is Arizona State's passing game. Harris can take over a game, and he caught six balls for 147 yards against Kansas State. BYU's offense also stalled at TCU, where Bachmeier threw for 62 yards in a 17-10 win.

Why the Over? Arizona State games have piled up points. The Sun Devils scored 70 on Morgan State, and their three losses finished 48-20, 55-19 and 53-25. ASU allows 29.9 points a game. BYU scored 63 on Utah Tech and 41 at Colorado State. My 37-19 adds up to 56, over 48.5.

Now the room. Next Man Up has BYU 38-18 and likes the BYU side and the Over. The Formula has BYU 36-20, sides with Arizona State since its score lands inside the 16.5, and likes the Over. Follow The Money likes Arizona State and the Under after the total dropped. In The Trenches sits out: the BYU run game and the Arizona State run game each have a matchup they like, so they cancel out.

Next Man Up started it: "BYU comes home mad. Bryce, save the dad jokes for halftime."

Follow The Money pushed back: "The market moved to Arizona State and to fewer points. I'm following."

The Formula answered: "BYU wins, ASU keeps it close. But the points come."

Bottom of the card: a BYU team coming home after a tough loss, an Arizona State defense that has given up big numbers, and plenty of offense on both sides. Skylar hands Bryce another water bottle. Give me BYU and the Over.

BYU Cougars 37, Arizona State Sun Devils 19. BYU Cougars cover the 16.5. Over 48.5.
