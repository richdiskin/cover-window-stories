Sunhats in the Mountains: Arizona State Visits No. 8 BYU on Halloween

Arizona State Sun Devils (4-3) at BYU Cougars (6-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · LaVell Edwards Stadium · Fox

Arizona State (4-3, 2-2 Big 12) at No. 8 BYU (6-1, 3-1 Big 12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, LaVell Edwards Stadium, Provo, Utah, Fox.

Your hosts are Skylar Vanterpool, an Arizona State fan whose motto is "Hydrate and Dominate," and Bryce Halvorsen-Tuilagi, a BYU superfan and Provo trail guide whose dad jokes are so clean they sparkle.

Bryce: Oh my heck, Skylar! Welcome to Provo. That's Y Mountain right there.

Skylar: It's beautiful, Bryce. Also, you're at altitude. Drink some water.

Bryce: Why did the Sun Devil drink so much water in Provo? Because it heard the Cougars were a thirsty bunch.

Skylar: Hydrate, Bryce. And maybe workshop that one.

Players to watch

BYU quarterback Bear Bachmeier has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 116 of 184 for 1,514 yards, 11 touchdowns and three interceptions, and he ran for two scores and caught another against Notre Dame. LJ Martin leads the rushers with 344 yards, and freshman Legend Glasker leads the receivers with 28 catches for 498 yards. Linebacker Cade Uluave leads the defense with 45 tackles, and Tausili Akana has 3.5 sacks.

Arizona State receiver Reed Harris is the headliner, with 47 catches for 800 yards and eight touchdowns. Cutter Boley leads the team in passing at 1,360 yards and 13 touchdowns, but Mikey Keene has thrown the most passes the last two games. Cardae Mack leads the rushers with 400 yards and six scores. Linebacker Owen Long leads the defense with 63 tackles, and Julian Hugo has four sacks.

Skylar: Reed Harris catches deep balls like I catch rays. Effortlessly.

Bryce: Glasker's numbers are like my jokes, Skylar. They just keep landing.

What happened last week

BYU lost 39-31 in overtime at UCF on Oct. 24. Bachmeier threw for 257 yards and two touchdowns, and Kyler Kasper caught six balls for 129 yards and a score. It was the Cougars' first loss of the season.

Bryce: That one stung. But you climb back up the trail one step at a time.

Arizona State beat Kansas State 28-17 at home on Oct. 24. Mack ran 26 times for 159 yards and four touchdowns, Keene threw for 160 yards, and Harris caught six balls for 147 yards.

Skylar: Four touchdowns for Cardae Mack! I cannonballed into the kiddie pool. Twice.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Bachmeier has thrown the most passes for BYU in every game. For Arizona State, Boley did it in the first five games and Keene in the last two, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Kalani Sitake played fullback for BYU under LaVell Edwards and became head coach in 2016. He's the first Tongan head coach in college football, and he entered the Polynesian Football Hall of Fame in 2025. His teams went 11-2 and 12-2 the last two seasons, and this year's defense allows 18.6 points a game.

Kenny Dillingham became Arizona State's 26th head coach on Nov. 27, 2022, and took the Sun Devils to the 2024 Big 12 championship, beating Iowa State 45-19 in the title game. His offense loves to throw it, and Harris is the proof.

Bryce: Coach Sitake was a fullback. He still runs downhill.

Skylar: Coach Dillingham coaches with so much energy, he must run on electrolytes.

Player storylines

The matchup to watch is Harris against a BYU defense that doesn't allow much. On the other side, the Cougars want their big guys up front to get Martin and the run game going against an Arizona State defense that has given up yards on the ground. These two split the last two meetings: BYU won 27-17 in 2021, and Arizona State won 28-23 in 2024.

What's happening on campus

LaVell Edwards Stadium is named for the coach who won 19 conference titles and the 1984 national title, and CFBD lists it at 63,470 seats. A Halloween afternoon under the mountains should bring plenty of royal blue in the stands, along with some very creative costumes. Arizona State's home, Mountain America Stadium in Tempe, is listed at 65,870.

Skylar: I'm dressing as a cactus. A hydrated one.

Bryce: I'm dressing as a trail map. You can find yourself on me.

Anything else fun and true

BYU started playing college football in 1922, has won 23 conference championships and the 1984 national championship, and joined the Big 12 on July 1, 2023. Arizona State beat Michigan 22-15 in the Rose Bowl on Jan. 1, 1987, and earlier this season it beat Kansas 24-17 at Wembley Stadium in London.

Bryce: Whatever happens, Skylar, dinner's on me. We'll say grace first.

Skylar: Deal, Bryce. I'll bring the water.

My call: BYU Cougars 37, Arizona State Sun Devils 19.
