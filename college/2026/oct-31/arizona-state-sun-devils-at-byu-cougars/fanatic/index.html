Arizona State vs BYU: All-Navy Cougars and an Endless Summer Soda

Arizona State Sun Devils (4-3) at BYU Cougars (6-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · LaVell Edwards Stadium · Fox

Arizona State Sun Devils vs BYU Cougars is a Week 9 Halloween afternoon at LaVell Edwards Stadium, and No. 8 BYU is wearing all navy, head to toe, against the team its own schedule video paired with a soda called "Endless Summer." Skylar Vanterpool, an Arizona State fan whose motto is "Hydrate and Dominate," says Reed Harris, Cardae Mack and Kenny Dillingham's Sun Devils spoil the costume, and Bryce Halvorsen-Tuilagi, a Provo trail guide whose dad jokes are so clean they sparkle, says Bear Bachmeier, Cade Uluave and Kalani Sitake's Cougars bounce back.

Bryce: Oh my heck, Skylar! Welcome to Provo. That's Y Mountain right there.

Skylar: It's beautiful, Bryce. Also, you're at altitude. Drink some water.

Bryce: The Sun Devil drank so much water in Provo. It heard the Cougars were a thirsty bunch.

Skylar: Hydrate, Bryce. And maybe workshop that one.

Back in January, BYU released its schedule as a set of Swig dirty sodas, calling it "a very on-brand schedule release," and KSL Sports called it a natural fit, since two Swig shops sit just outside the stadium's west side. Utah got "Drama Queen." The open week got "Water." Arizona State got "Endless Summer": Mountain Dew, pomegranate, grapefruit and fresh lime.

Skylar: Endless Summer. Bryce, that's not a soda. That's a lifestyle.

Bryce: It's a lot of Mountain Dew for a team that came to drink water.

Then on Monday, BYU showed its uniform for the game: navy helmet, navy jersey, navy pants, with white as the only other color. ColorWay Sports noted it's different from the navy jersey BYU wore against Iowa State on Oct. 9 with a white helmet and white pants, and that the last time BYU went navy from top to bottom was a 41-16 win over Southern Utah in 2023.

Bryce: All navy on Halloween. We're dressed as the night sky.

Skylar: You're dressed as a bruise, Bryce. A very handsome bruise.

BYU is 6-1 and 3-1 in the Big 12, and it's coming off its first loss, 39-31 in overtime at UCF on Oct. 24. Bachmeier threw for 257 yards and two touchdowns, and Kyler Kasper caught six balls for 129 yards and a score. Before that, the Cougars beat Notre Dame 28-24, Iowa State 24-10 and TCU 17-10.

Bryce: Why did the Cougar lose in Orlando? Too many rides. We're home now.

Bachmeier has led BYU in throws in all seven games and is 116 of 184 for 1,514 yards, 11 touchdowns and three interceptions. LJ Martin has 344 rushing yards, Legend Glasker has 28 catches for 498 yards, Uluave has 45 tackles and Tausili Akana has 3.5 sacks. The Cougars allow 18.6 points a game.

Arizona State is 4-3 and 2-2 in the league, and it beat Kansas State 28-17 at home on Oct. 24. Mack ran 26 times for 159 yards and four touchdowns, Mikey Keene threw for 160 yards, and Harris caught six balls for 147 yards.

Skylar: Four touchdowns, Bryce. Cardae Mack was the most hydrated man in Tempe.

Keene has led the Sun Devils in throws the last two games, after Cutter Boley did it in the first five. Boley is 105 of 173 for 1,360 yards, 13 touchdowns and five interceptions. Harris has 47 catches for 800 yards and eight scores, Mack has 400 rushing yards and six touchdowns, Owen Long has 63 tackles and Julian Hugo has four sacks. The road has been rough: Arizona State lost 48-20 at Texas A&M and 53-25 at Texas Tech.

Skylar: Eight hundred yards, Bryce. Reed Harris is the whole summer.

Sitake played fullback for BYU under LaVell Edwards, and his teams went 11-2 and 12-2 the last two seasons. Dillingham took Arizona State to the 2024 Big 12 title, beating Iowa State 45-19 in the championship game.

Bryce: Coach Sitake was a fullback. He still runs downhill.

Skylar: Coach Dillingham coaches with so much energy, he probably runs on electrolytes.

These two have split their recent meetings. BYU won 27-17 in 2021, and Arizona State won 28-23 in 2024.

The matchup is Harris against a BYU defense that doesn't allow much. On the other side, the Cougars want Martin and the run game to keep Bachmeier ahead of the chains.

No official availability report is out for this game, so check the teams' pregame notes.

Bryce: I'm dressing as Cosmo the Cougar. Navy edition.

Skylar: I'm dressing as an Endless Summer. Sunhat, sunglasses, one large cup.

Bryce: Whatever happens, Skylar, the first round of dirty sodas is on me.

Skylar: Deal, Bryce. I'll bring the water.

My call: BYU Cougars 37, Arizona State Sun Devils 19.
