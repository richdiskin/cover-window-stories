Ohio State Buckeyes at USC Trojans

Ohio State Buckeyes (6-1) at USC Trojans (6-2)

7:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum · NBC

Halloween night at the Coliseum, and Ohio State shows up with a quarterback who has never started a college game. Buck Holloway runs routes in the press box hallway. Sterling Ashworth-Vance narrates everything like a movie trailer.

Ohio State (6-1) at USC (6-2), Saturday at 7:30 p.m. ET on NBC at Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum in Los Angeles. It's a Big Ten game. DraftKings has Ohio State Buckeyes −6.5 with a total of 51.5. Action Network's opening number was Ohio State −7.5. My card: Ohio State Buckeyes −6.5, Over 51.5, and a final of Ohio State Buckeyes 34, USC Trojans 23.

Buck speaks for Ohio State. "That's a Columbus problem! Julian Sayin is out with the elbow he hurt at Indiana. He had 19 touchdown passes and four interceptions. Ryan Day hasn't picked between Luke Fahey and Tavien St. Clair. But Jeremiah Smith has 52 catches for 984 yards and 12 scores, and the bye week got most of our hurt defenders back."

Sterling speaks for USC. "In a world where a team ran for minus seventeen yards in Madison... that was us, Buck. We lost 7-24 at Wisconsin. But Jayden Maiava has 2,302 yards and 19 touchdowns. We beat Washington here, and Oregon beat us here, so I won't oversell it. Eight of the last ten meetings belong to us. Roll credits."

Here is the part that matters. Ohio State allows 13.4 points a game. Its worst offensive night was 19 points at Indiana, and the defense still won that game. USC's run game just disappeared. If the Trojans can't run, Maiava has to throw into a defense that gave Indiana 15.

Why Ohio State? The Formula and Next Man Up both back the Buckeyes. Day says the front is a "full arsenal" again, with Austin Siereveld back from illness. A young quarterback with a healthy line and Smith outside can manage this. USC's defense allows 25.0 a game.

The worry is real. Next Man Up made no quarterback change because there's no official report yet. That's a hole in its number, and Sayin's absence is the biggest news in this game. Follow The Money backs USC after the spread came down from 7.5 to 6.5, and In The Trenches also likes the Trojans.

Why the Over/Under? Ohio State scores 39.3 a game. USC scores 32.8. The Formula has 55 and Next Man Up has 58. Follow The Money likes the Under after the total fell from 55.5 to 51.5, and I won't pretend the quarterback news doesn't matter. My 34-23 adds up to 57, five and a half over the over/under 51.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Ohio State 33-22 and likes the Buckeyes and the Over. Next Man Up has Ohio State 34-24 and likes the Buckeyes and the Over. Follow The Money likes USC and the Under. In The Trenches likes USC. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

The Formula: "The defense travels. It always does."

Follow The Money: "Spread down a point, total down four. I go with the move."

In The Trenches: "USC has to run. Last week says it can't."

Bottom of the card: the Buckeyes ride Smith and that defense. Sterling cuts to black.

Ohio State Buckeyes 34, USC Trojans 23. Ohio State Buckeyes cover the 6.5. Over 51.5.
