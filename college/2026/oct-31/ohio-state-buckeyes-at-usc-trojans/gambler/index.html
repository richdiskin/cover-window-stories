Ohio State Buckeyes (6-1) at USC Trojans (6-2)

Ohio State vs USC prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on Ohio State Buckeyes −6.5 and Over 51.5 at DraftKings, with a final of Ohio State Buckeyes 34, USC Trojans 23. It's Halloween night and Trojan Family Weekend at a sold-out Coliseum, the Buckeyes and Trojans meet for the first time as Big Ten opponents, and USC is seven and six all-time on Halloween.

Ohio State's defense is the reason. The Buckeyes allow 13.4 points a game and score 39.3, and their only loss was 24-23 at Texas. Their worst offensive night was 19 points at Indiana, and they still won that game 19-15. Jeremiah Smith has 52 catches for 984 yards and 12 touchdowns, and Bo Jackson has 516 rushing yards. USC just scored 7 at Wisconsin, where Jayden Maiava threw 50 times for 258 yards.

Brecken "Buck" Holloway, the former Ohio State scout-team receiver, runs a slant down the tailgate row in Exposition Park. "Nine hundred eighty-four yards for Smith, Sterling. That's a Columbus problem for everybody else."

Sterling Ashworth-Vance, the USC film buff, answers in his movie-trailer voice. "In a world where the Trojans have won eight of the last ten meetings... one team remembers."

My concern is the USC passing game. Maiava has 2,302 yards and 19 touchdowns, King Miller has 602 rushing yards, and USC won 31-23 at Penn State and 25-21 over Washington. The Big Ten's initial availability report, posted at 8 p.m. ET Wednesday, lists Julian Sayin out, one of seven Buckeyes out. If Maiava gets going at home, six and a half is a lot.

The total comes down to two offenses that put up points. Ohio State scores 39.3 a game and USC scores 32.8, and USC allows 25.0. My 34-23 adds up to 57. DraftKings has Ohio State −6.5 and 51.5. The consensus spread opened at Ohio State −6.5 and hasn't moved. The consensus total opened at 51.5 and hasn't moved.

The room is mostly with me. The Formula has Ohio State 33-22 and likes the Buckeyes and the Over. Next Man Up has Ohio State 34-24 and likes the Buckeyes and the Over. In The Trenches likes USC. Follow The Money has no pick in this game, and it keeps no score of its own. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "Julian Sayin is out on the Big Ten report. That's 2,007 passing yards on the sideline."

The Formula: "The defense travels. It always does."

In The Trenches: "USC runs better than this defense lets people run."

A defense that gives up 13.4 a game, Smith on the outside and a sold-out Halloween crowd in Los Angeles. Give me the Buckeyes and the Over.

Ohio State Buckeyes 34, USC Trojans 23. Ohio State Buckeyes cover the 6.5. Over 51.5.
