Ohio State vs USC: Halloween Night at a Sold-Out Coliseum

Ohio State Buckeyes (6-1) at USC Trojans (6-2)

7:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum · NBC

Ohio State Buckeyes vs USC Trojans is a Week 9 Halloween night at a sold-out Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum, on Trojan Family Weekend, with kickoff at 7:30 p.m. ET on NBC. Brecken "Buck" Holloway, a former Ohio State scout-team receiver who still runs routes in the press box hallway, says Jeremiah Smith, Bo Jackson and Earl Little Jr. carry the Buckeyes home, and Sterling Ashworth-Vance, a USC film buff who narrates every game like a movie trailer, says Jayden Maiava, King Miller and Kayden Dixon-Wyatt write a happier ending for the Trojans.

Sterling: In a world where two old powers meet on Halloween night...

Buck: That's a Columbus problem! I mean, it's a great problem. Hi, Sterling.

Sterling: You interrupted my trailer, Buck. That's a ten-yard penalty in Hollywood.

USC is going all in on the holiday. The Trojans' game notes open with "FOOLISH MORTALS…" and promise that "the ghosts of the Coliseum are rising from their eternal slumber and preparing for Halloween night." USC has played on Halloween just 13 times and is 7-6 in those games. This is the second sellout of the season at the Coliseum, after Oregon drew 75,500 on Sept. 26. Parking opens at 1:30 p.m. ET and gates open at 6 p.m. ET, and Metro has two E train stops next to the stadium.

Buck: The ghosts of the Coliseum? I've been running routes with ghosts in the hallway for years.

Sterling: That's not a ghost, Buck. That's a custodian.

These two go way back. USC leads the series 13-10-1 and has won eight of the last 10 meetings, and seven of the games came on New Year's Day in the Rose Bowl. After the 1979 season, Charles White scored on a 1-yard run with 1:32 left, and USC won 17-16. The 1973 Rose Bowl between them drew 106,869 fans. This is the first meeting since 2017, and the first as Big Ten opponents. USC has eight Heisman Trophy winners and Ohio State has seven.

Buck: Seven Heismans. I'll run a celebration route in the hallway.

Sterling: Eight, Buck. Roll the credits slowly.

Ohio State is 6-1 and 4-0 in the Big Ten, with the only loss a 24-23 game at Texas. The Buckeyes score 39.3 points a game and allow 13.4. Smith has 52 catches for 984 yards and 12 touchdowns, Bo Jackson has 516 rushing yards, Little has 38 tackles and Kenyatta Jackson Jr. has three sacks. Julian Sayin has thrown for 2,007 yards, 19 touchdowns and four interceptions this season. The Buckeyes are coming off a bye after a 19-15 win at Indiana on Oct. 17, when Smith caught a touchdown.

Ryan Day had kind words for the other sideline. He called Lincoln Riley a "tremendous mind and a really good coach."

Sterling: That's a quote for the poster, Buck.

USC is 6-2 and 3-2 in the league. Maiava has completed 183 of 274 throws for 2,302 yards, 19 touchdowns and five interceptions. Miller has 602 rushing yards and four touchdowns, Dixon-Wyatt has 32 catches for 443 yards, Deven Bryant has 44 tackles and Kennedy Urlacher has three sacks. The Trojans won 31-23 at Penn State and 25-21 over Washington, and they lost 41-27 to Oregon. Last week they lost 24-7 at Wisconsin, where Maiava completed 31 throws for 258 yards and found Taniela Tupou for the only touchdown.

Buck: Nine hundred eighty-four yards for Smith. That's a box office number, Sterling. Even you have to say it.

Sterling: Box office, yes. But the sequel is in our building.

The Big Ten's initial availability report, posted at 8 p.m. ET Wednesday, lists Sayin out, one of seven Ohio State players out, with five more questionable. USC lists seven players out and four questionable, and Maiava is not on it.

Buck: I'm coming as Brutus Buckeye. Big head, big smile.

Sterling: I'm coming as a director. Beret, megaphone, folding chair with my name on it.

Buck: Whatever happens, Sterling, dinner's on me, and you narrate my hallway routes in the trailer voice.

Sterling: Then you run one lap around Tommy Trojan and say "Fight On."

Buck: Deal. I'll run it as a post route.

My call: Ohio State Buckeyes 34, USC Trojans 23.
