Fireworks Friday in Sacramento: Kent State Makes Its First Trip to Hornet Stadium

Kent State Golden Flashes (3-5) at Sacramento State Hornets (1-7)

10:30 PM ET Fri Oct 30 · Hornet Stadium · ESPN2

Kent State (3-5, 2-2 MAC) at Sacramento State (1-7, 0-5 MAC). Friday, Oct. 30, 10:30 p.m. ET (7:30 p.m. Pacific), Hornet Stadium, Sacramento, Calif., ESPN2.

Your hosts are Ozzie Brannigan, a Kent State pessimist who grades uniforms and fourth-down decisions, and Mateo Villaseñor, a Sacramento State fan and farm-to-fork chef who explains defense like a recipe.

Mateo: Welcome to Sacramento, Ozzie! Farm-to-fork capital, Friday night lights and postgame fireworks.

Ozzie: I expected fireworks. I always expect fireworks. I just don't expect them to be for us.

Players to watch

Kent State quarterback Dru DeShields is 124 of 218 for 1,439 yards, eight touchdowns and six interceptions, and he has run for a team-high four scores. Wide receiver Wayne Harris has 40 catches for 552 yards and four touchdowns, and running back Brandon White leads the rushers with 235 yards. Linebacker Hayden Dawahare leads the defense with 43 tackles and four sacks.

Sacramento State quarterback Jackson Sharman is 123 of 218 for 1,257 yards, eight touchdowns and six interceptions. Wide receiver Matt Coleman has 39 catches for 492 yards and five scores, fourth in the MAC in receiving yards. Running back Isaiah Ene has 303 yards. Safety Boogsie Silvera leads the team with 61 tackles.

Mateo: Look at that, Ozzie. Our quarterbacks have the same attempts, the same touchdowns and the same interceptions. That's a matched pair of plates.

What happened last week

Kent State beat Akron 35-20 at home to keep the Wagon Wheel. After a 7-7 halftime tie, DeShields threw four touchdown passes and ran for another, and Harris caught nine passes for 156 yards and three scores.

Sacramento State lost 34-17 at Ball State, but the Hornets led twice in the first half. Sharman threw two touchdown passes, Jace Wilson caught a 55-yard score, and Silvera picked off two passes and made 11 tackles.

Ozzie: Four touchdown passes. I'm happy. I'm also nervous that I'm happy.

Injuries and availability

The MAC has not posted an availability report for this game, and no injury news has surfaced for either starting quarterback, so watch the teams' pregame notes. Sacramento State has used seven different starting groups on offense this year, and Silvera is the only Hornets defender to start all eight games.

What the coaches are up to

Mark Carney is in his second season as Kent State's head coach and is 8-12. Kent State removed his interim tag on Oct. 30, 2025, so Friday is one year to the day. His assistant head coach and defensive coordinator, Cherokee Valeria, coached Sacramento State's defensive backs from 2019 to 2024 and was part of three Big Sky championships.

Alonzo Carter is a first-time head coach. Sacramento State hired him on Dec. 15, 2025, and he's leading the Hornets through their first FBS season.

Mateo: Coach Valeria coming home? We'll save him a seat at the table. A small one.

Player storylines

Silvera is a fifth-year player whose path ran through Charleston Southern, McNeese and USF. He has three games this season with double-digit tackles.

The Hornets have football families, too. Coleman's father, Herman, played quarterback at Southern, and Onterrio Smith Jr., who has 33 catches for 350 yards, is the son of former Tennessee and Oregon running back Onterrio Smith, who played for the Minnesota Vikings.

The Sacramento State special teams group calls itself the Wefense. Kicker Grant Meadors hit a career-long 47-yarder at Ball State, and punter Hamish Adams of Melbourne, Australia, punted six times for 45.3 yards a kick.

What's happening on campus

Friday is Healthcare Appreciation and AANHPI Heritage Night at Hornet Stadium, and the postgame fireworks are back. Ted Emrich and former Texas running back Fozzy Whitaker will call the game on ESPN2.

Ozzie: Healthcare Appreciation Night is perfect. I'll need a nurse by the fourth quarter.

Anything else fun and true

This is the first meeting between the schools. Sacramento State went 7-5 in 2025, its last FCS season, and has played four Friday night games since 2000. Adams is the program's third straight punter from Australia, and all three came through ProKick Australia.

Mateo: Three Aussie punters in a row. That's a family recipe.

Ozzie: Fine. Great punting. I expected that too.

My call: Kent State Golden Flashes 27, Sacramento State Hornets 22.
