Kent State Golden Flashes at Sacramento State Hornets

Kent State Golden Flashes (3-5) at Sacramento State Hornets (1-7)

10:30 PM ET Fri Oct 30 · Hornet Stadium · ESPN2

XFacebook

Kent State flies across the country for a 7:30 p.m. Pacific kickoff, and Ozzie Brannigan has already decided the jet lag will be a problem. Mateo Villaseñor is in Sacramento with a fresh recipe and a defense that finally showed some flavor at Ball State.

Kent State (3-5) at Sacramento State (1-7), Friday at 10:30 p.m. ET on ESPN2 at Hornet Stadium in Sacramento. It's a MAC game and the first meeting between these schools. DraftKings has Kent State Golden Flashes −10 with a total of 46.5. My card: Sacramento State Hornets +10, Over 46.5, and a final of Kent State Golden Flashes 27, Sacramento State Hornets 22.

Ozzie speaks for Kent State. "Dru DeShields threw four touchdown passes and ran for another against Akron. We won 35-20. I expected to lose. Now I expect the plane to be late. Wayne Harris caught nine balls for 156 yards and three scores, and I'm suspicious of that too."

Mateo speaks for Sacramento State. "Plate it up, Ozzie. We led Ball State twice in the first half. Boogsie Silvera picked off two passes, and our special teams are a five-course meal. Matt Coleman has 39 catches for 492 yards."

Here is the part that matters. Before Akron, Kent State scored 0, 3, 10, 7 and 2 points in its five losses. The Flashes run for 3.0 yards a carry. A team that scores 14.9 points a game has no business laying 10 on the road.

Why Sacramento State? Because 10 points is a lot of cushion for a home team with a punter who flips the field and a kicker who just hit from 47. Kent State's defensive coordinator, Cherokee Valeria, coached Sacramento State's defensive backs for six seasons, so this is no mystery to either side. My final has Kent State by five, well inside the 10.

The worry is the Hornets' offense. Sacramento State has not run for a touchdown in six games and is minus-9 in turnover margin. It lost 48-7 to Ohio and 49-3 at Fresno State. If DeShields carries the Akron game into this one, the Flashes pull away.

Why the Over/Under? These offenses are poor, but these defenses are worse. Kent State allows 30.5 points a game and Sacramento State allows 32.9. Kent State's last game finished 35-20 and Sacramento State's finished 34-17. The total has ticked up a point, and Follow The Money likes that move. My 27-22 adds up to 49, two and a half over the over/under 46.5.

Now the room. Next Man Up has Kent State 25-24, takes Sacramento State and likes it, and goes Over. The Formula has Kent State 28-19, also on the Hornets, and the Over. Follow The Money stays off the side and likes the Over. In The Trenches has no pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "Twenty-five to twenty-four. One point. Bring the fireworks."

The Formula: "Nine points. Still inside the 10."

Follow The Money: "The total moved up. Somebody stayed up late for this one."

Bottom of the card: two struggling teams on a Friday night, and the home team gets 10 points. Ozzie expects the worst. Mateo just wants the plate to look good.

Kent State Golden Flashes 27, Sacramento State Hornets 22. Sacramento State Hornets cover the 10. Over 46.5.
