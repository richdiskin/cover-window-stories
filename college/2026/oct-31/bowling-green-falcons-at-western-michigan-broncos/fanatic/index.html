Bowling Green vs Western Michigan: Family Weekend Halloween

Bowling Green Falcons (2-6) at Western Michigan Broncos (5-3)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Waldo Stadium · CBSSN

Bowling Green Falcons vs Western Michigan Broncos is a Week 9 Halloween noon ET game at Waldo Stadium on WMU Family Weekend, with a costume-friendly Family Fun Zone in Lot 3 before kickoff and Bowling Green chasing a fourth straight win in the series. Corwin Dahlquist, a Kalamazoo paper engineer who grades opponents like diner breakfasts, says Lance Taylor's Broncos and Jalen Buckley will serve a full stack, and Darcy Fenwick, a former Bowling Green club hockey goalie, says Eddie George's Falcons and Myles Bradley will stop everything.

Corwin: Welcome to Kalamazoo, Darcy! It's Family Weekend. I saved you a booth and a plush to stuff.

Darcy: Corwin, I'm a goalie. I stuff things for a living. Mostly pucks.

That plush is real. Western Michigan's Family Weekend runs Oct. 30 through Nov. 1, and Friday night includes Stuff-a-Plush, hat decorating with the Alumni Association and Halloween Escape Rooms and Glow Night on Sangren Mall. On Saturday, the free Family Fun Zone runs from 9 to 11:30 a.m. ET in Lot 3 at Oliver Road and Oliver Lane, and kids are encouraged to wear costumes to the game. There's a historical walking tour at The Oaklands at 9:30 a.m. ET, and Sunday ends with family brunch at Valley Dining Center.

Corwin: Brunch on Sunday. Pancakes. I'm emotionally prepared.

Darcy: You're always emotionally prepared for pancakes.

Bowling Green's own release calls it "a Halloween matchup inside Waldo Stadium," and it comes with history. This is the 59th meeting. The Falcons lead the series 35-20-3, they're 17-10-1 in Kalamazoo, and they've won the last three, including 34-10 on their last trip here in 2023.

Darcy: Seventeen wins in your building, Corwin. We know where the syrup is.

Corwin: Bold talk for a team that's 2-6.

Bowling Green lost 31-24 at Buffalo on Oct. 24. Jay Kastantin threw for 217 yards and three touchdowns. He's led the Falcons in throws in all eight games and is 130 of 194 for 1,418 yards, 10 touchdowns and six interceptions. Austyn Dendy leads the rushers with 371 yards, and Winn Sharp leads the receivers with 25 catches. Dorian Pringle has 74 tackles. Bradley has eight sacks, and 4.5 of them came against South Florida, which Bowling Green says is a school record for one game.

Darcy: Four and a half sacks in one night. In goalie terms, that's a shutout with an assist.

Western Michigan is 5-3 and lost 45-38 at Toledo on Oct. 24. Broc Lowry threw two touchdowns, Buckley ran for 92 yards and a score, and Aveion Chenault caught six balls for 105 yards and a touchdown. Lowry is 112 of 176 for 1,251 yards, 10 touchdowns and three interceptions. Buckley leads the team with 634 yards and eight touchdowns, and Chenault has 28 catches for 431 yards. Justyce Betts has 58 tackles, and Scoop Gardner Jr. has four sacks.

Corwin: Forty-five allowed at Toledo. Dry toast. I said it on air, and I meant it.

The matchup is Buckley against a Bowling Green defense that allows 5.6 yards a carry, and Bradley against a Western Michigan front that has given up only nine sacks. The Broncos score 26.6 points a game and allow 20.4. The Falcons score 18.8 and allow 29.8.

Taylor was hired at Western Michigan on Dec. 8, 2022, was named MAC Coach of the Year last season and won the 2025 MAC championship, only the fourth conference title in school history. George was named Bowling Green's 21st head coach on March 9, 2025. He won the 1995 Heisman Trophy at Ohio State, was NFL Rookie of the Year in 1996 and once played on Broadway in "Chicago."

Corwin: A Heisman winner on Broadway. That's a full stack with whipped cream.

Darcy: And he's ours. Goalie-approved.

This is also Bowling Green's last road Saturday of the year. Its only other road game left is the Battle of I-75 at Toledo on a Friday night, Nov. 20.

Corwin: I'm dressing up as a pancake for the Fun Zone. A full stack, naturally.

Darcy: I'm coming as a goalie. It's not a costume. It's a lifestyle.

Corwin: Win or lose, brunch is on me Sunday. We'll say grace first.

Darcy: Deal. Extra syrup, and a prayer for my glove hand.

My call: Western Michigan Broncos 31, Bowling Green Falcons 14.
