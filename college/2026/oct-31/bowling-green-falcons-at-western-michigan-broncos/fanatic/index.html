Hockey Meets Pancakes in Kalamazoo: Bowling Green Visits the Defending MAC Champions on Halloween

Bowling Green Falcons (2-6) at Western Michigan Broncos (5-3)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Waldo Stadium · CBSSN

Bowling Green (2-6, 2-2 MAC) at Western Michigan (5-3, 3-1 MAC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12 p.m. ET, Waldo Stadium, Kalamazoo, Mich., CBSSN.

Your hosts are Darcy Fenwick, a former Bowling Green club hockey goalie who watches football from the crease, and Corwin Dahlquist, a Kalamazoo paper engineer who rates opponents like diner breakfasts.

Corwin: Welcome to Kalamazoo, Darcy! I saved you a booth. Short stack or full stack?

Darcy: Corwin, I'm a goalie. I'll take whatever stops pucks. And syrup. Lots of syrup.

Players to watch

Bowling Green quarterback Jay Kastantin has thrown the most passes in all eight Falcon games. He's 130 of 194 for 1,418 yards, 10 touchdowns and six interceptions. Austyn Dendy leads the rushers with 371 yards and four scores, and Winn Sharp leads the receivers with 25 catches for 305 yards. The defense has two players to know: end Myles Bradley, with eight sacks, and linebacker Dorian Pringle, with 74 tackles.

Western Michigan quarterback Broc Lowry has thrown the most passes in all eight Bronco games. He's 112 of 176 for 1,251 yards, 10 touchdowns and three interceptions. Running back Jalen Buckley leads the team with 634 yards and eight touchdowns. Aveion Chenault leads the receivers with 28 catches for 431 yards. Linebacker Justyce Betts has 58 tackles, and end Scoop Gardner Jr. has four sacks.

Darcy: Eight sacks for Myles Bradley. That's a hat trick, plus a hat trick, plus two more. In goalie terms, that's a brick wall.

What happened last week

Bowling Green lost 31-24 at Buffalo on Oct. 24. Kastantin threw for 217 yards and three touchdowns, and Sharp caught five balls for 50 yards.

Darcy: Two minutes in the penalty box for me. I've served my time.

Western Michigan lost 45-38 at Toledo on Oct. 24. Lowry threw two touchdowns, Buckley ran 18 times for 92 yards and a score, and Chenault caught six balls for 105 yards and a touchdown.

Corwin: Short stack today. Dry toast on air, as promised. Accountability matters.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Kastantin and Lowry have each thrown the most passes in every game for their teams, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Lance Taylor was hired at Western Michigan on Dec. 8, 2022, and in December 2025 he signed an extension through the 2030 season. Last year he was MAC Coach of the Year, and the Broncos won the 2025 MAC championship, only the fourth conference title in school history.

Eddie George was named Bowling Green's 21st head coach on March 9, 2025. He won the 1995 Heisman Trophy at Ohio State, when he ran for 1,927 yards and 24 touchdowns. He was NFL Rookie of the Year in 1996, made four Pro Bowls, and is in the College Football Hall of Fame. He even performed on Broadway in "Chicago."

Corwin: A Heisman winner on Broadway. That's a full stack with whipped cream.

Darcy: And he's ours. Goalie-approved.

Player storylines

The matchup to circle is Buckley against a Bowling Green run defense that allows 5.6 yards a carry. On the other side, Bradley will try to crash the pocket against a Western Michigan team that has given up only nine sacks. Lowry was named MAC Offensive Player of the Year last season, and he'll want to bounce back after a 12 of 29 day at Toledo. The Bronco big guys up front will be key in both directions.

What's happening on campus

Waldo Stadium seats 30,200, and last season the Broncos averaged 20,000 fans a game at home for the first time since 2016. A Halloween noon kickoff in Kalamazoo means crisp fall air and plenty of brown-and-gold costumes. Bowling Green's home is Doyt L. Perry Stadium, which seats 24,000.

Corwin: I'm dressing up as a pancake. A full stack, naturally.

Anything else fun and true

Western Michigan won the Cannon Trophy in 2023 and 2025, and the 2025 team's seven conference wins tied for the second-most in school history. Bowling Green has won the last two meetings with the Broncos, 34-10 in 2023 and 31-13 in 2024. Before that, Western Michigan won 49-10 in 2019.

Darcy: Corwin, win or lose, breakfast is on me after the game. We'll say grace first.

Corwin: Deal, Darcy. Full stack, extra syrup, and a prayer of thanks.

My call: Western Michigan Broncos 31, Bowling Green Falcons 14.
