Florida International Panthers at Liberty Flames

Florida International Panthers (3-4) at Liberty Flames (6-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Williams Stadium (VA) · CBSSN

Gideon Fairweather is shaking hands with every tailgater outside Williams Stadium when Lourdes Ibarguen-Tate arrives with a thermos of cafecito and a box of dominoes. "Praise the Lord and grab a program," Gideon says. "What's in the box?" Lourdes opens it. "My abuela's playbook. The dominoes are the players." Gideon studies it. "And the sugar packets?" Lourdes smiles. "Defense. Always defense."

FIU (3-4, 1-2 Conference USA) at Liberty (6-1, 3-0 Conference USA), Saturday at 3:30 p.m. ET on CBSSN at Williams Stadium in Lynchburg. DraftKings has Liberty Flames −14 with a total of 44.5. My card: Liberty Flames −14, Under 44.5, and a final of Liberty Flames 27, Florida International Panthers 12.

Gideon speaks for Liberty, hand on his heart. "Deshawn Purdie hasn't thrown an interception all season, son. Kam Davis has 583 rushing yards. Makai Jackson has 540 receiving yards on 23 catches. We've won six straight, and we beat Kennesaw State 34-6 last week. We allow 14.0 points a game."

Lourdes speaks for FIU, cafecito in hand. "¡Vamos! DeeJay Holmes Jr. has five sacks. Maguire Anderson has 568 receiving yards. Devonte Lyons ran for 115 against Middle Tennessee. We allow 16.3 points a game. And we beat Liberty 34-27 last season. No sé what else you need."

Why Liberty? Because the Flames have been rolling. Since a one-point win over Gardner-Webb, Liberty has beaten Ball State 51-15, Coastal Carolina 34-17, Delaware 30-14, Sam Houston 35-3 and Kennesaw State 34-6. The run game is the engine, and Purdie doesn't give the ball away. FIU's offense scores 20.1 points a game, and JJ Kohl has eight interceptions. My final has Liberty winning by more than two touchdowns.

The risk is the FIU defense. The Panthers allow 16.3 points a game and lost to Jacksonville State 27-24 and Middle Tennessee 26-23. They play close games, and a field goal at the end can decide whether 14 holds. FIU also won last season's meeting.

Why the Under? Both defenses. Liberty allows 14.0 points a game, and FIU allows 16.3. FIU games have finished 19-9, 16-10, 20-3 and 22-3, and the Panthers haven't scored more than 33 all season. Liberty's last two games ended 35-3 and 34-6, with the defense doing most of the work. My 27-12 adds up to 39, under 44.5.

Now the room. Next Man Up has Liberty 27-10, sides with Liberty, and likes the Under. The Formula has Liberty 27-14, sides with FIU since its score lands inside the 14, and likes the Under. Follow The Money likes Liberty after the spread moved its way, and sits out the total since it hasn't budged. In The Trenches likes Liberty because the Flames' run game should find room.

In The Trenches started it: "Kam Davis runs downhill all afternoon. Liberty wins big."

The Formula pushed back: "FIU's defense keeps it closer than 14."

Next Man Up settled it: "Two good defenses. I like the Under."

Bottom of the card: a Liberty team at home on a six-game winning run, a quarterback who doesn't throw interceptions, and two defenses that keep scores low. Lourdes pours Gideon a cafecito. Give me the Flames and the Under.

Liberty Flames 27, Florida International Panthers 12. Liberty Flames cover the 14. Under 44.5.
