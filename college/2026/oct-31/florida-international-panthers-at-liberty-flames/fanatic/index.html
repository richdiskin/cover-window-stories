FIU vs Liberty: Candy, a Parade and Homecoming on Halloween

Florida International Panthers (3-4) at Liberty Flames (6-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Williams Stadium (VA) · CBSSN

Florida International Panthers vs Liberty Flames is a Week 9 Homecoming and Hall of Fame Weekend game in Lynchburg, and the Halloween morning parade rolls down University Boulevard tossing candy before the 3:30 p.m. ET kickoff at Williams Stadium. Lourdes Ibarguen-Tate, an FIU fan from Sweetwater who learned football from her abuela over cafecito and dominoes, says JJ Kohl and Willie Simmons have Liberty's number, and Gideon Fairweather, a Lynchburg youth pastor who coaches middle-school football, says Jamey Chadwell, Kam Davis and Deshawn Purdie will keep the Flames perfect in Conference USA.

Gideon: Lourdes! You made the parade. Did you catch any candy?

Lourdes: Two Tootsie Rolls and a pencil. I'm saving the pencil for the scorebook.

Gideon: That's the spirit. Liberty's alumni tailgate has Family Candy Games at 1 p.m. ET. You can enter.

Lourdes: Gideon, I'm a grown woman. Of course I'm entering.

This series is short and loud. Liberty leads it 3-1, and the last two meetings both went to overtime, with the home team winning both. The Flames won 31-24 in Lynchburg in 2024. Last November in Miami, FIU won 34-27 for its first win over Liberty. The Flames tied that one with a field goal at the final horn, and the Panthers won it in overtime with an interception on fourth down after Maguire Anderson caught a touchdown.

Lourdes: My abuela watched that one with me. She hasn't stopped talking about it.

Gideon: I prayed for her. Then I went home and watched film with my seventh graders.

Liberty is 6-1, 3-0 in Conference USA and first in the conference. The Flames have won six in a row since a 20-13 loss at James Madison to open the season. They score 31.6 points a game, the most in the league, and allow 14.0, the fewest. Liberty's notes say the Flames haven't thrown an interception this year. Purdie is 80 of 133 for 1,226 yards and nine touchdowns, Davis has 583 rushing yards and five touchdowns on 83 carries, and Makai Jackson has 23 catches for 540 yards and five touchdowns. Derrell Farrar leads the defense with 39 tackles.

Gideon: Makai Jackson gets more than 23 yards a catch. My middle schoolers get about three, and they're thrilled.

FIU is 3-4 and 1-2 in the league, and it has lost two straight conference games by three points, 27-24 at Jacksonville State and 26-23 to Middle Tennessee at home. Kohl is 131 of 227 for 1,637 yards, 10 touchdowns and eight interceptions. Devonte Lyons had a career-high 115 rushing yards against Middle Tennessee and has 383 for the year. Anderson has 36 catches for 568 yards and four touchdowns, DeeJay Holmes Jr. has five sacks, and DeAndre Boykins leads with 37 tackles.

Lourdes: Three points, three points. My abuela says the dominoes owe us.

The defenses are why both fan bases are nervous. FIU allows 16.3 points a game, second in the league behind Liberty, and its athletic department says opponents have scored touchdowns on only five of 19 trips inside the Panthers' 20. Liberty has held five straight opponents under 20 points, and its notes say the Flames are 14-0 under Chadwell when they do that.

Gideon: Fourteen and oh. I put that on the whiteboard in the youth room.

Lourdes: Put this under it. FIU won the last one.

Chadwell is in his fourth season at Liberty, and his first Flames team went 13-1. Simmons is in his second season at FIU, after a first year in which the Panthers won their last four to reach a bowl. The Panthers are 0-3 on the road this season, and the FIU notes say this is the program's first Halloween game since 2015.

Lourdes: Coach Simmons turns seasons around. Ask anyone in the 305.

Gideon: Coach Chadwell turns Homecoming into a family reunion. His Flames have gone 3-0 on Homecoming.

Friday night, the Presbyterian Cemetery Ghost Walk leads lantern tours on Bailey Street, with popcorn and cider at the end. After the game, Homecoming's Blacklight Bowling at Montview runs until midnight ET.

Gideon: Ghost walk Friday, bowling Saturday. Lourdes, I'll even let you keep score with your pencil.

Lourdes: Deal. And I'll teach your seventh graders dominoes.

My call: Liberty Flames 27, Florida International Panthers 12.
