Cafecito in Lynchburg: FIU Visits Liberty on Halloween

Florida International Panthers (3-4) at Liberty Flames (6-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Williams Stadium (VA) · CBSSN

FIU (3-4, 1-2 Conference USA) at Liberty (6-1, 3-0 Conference USA). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, Williams Stadium, Lynchburg, Va., CBSSN.

Your hosts are Lourdes Ibarguen-Tate, an FIU fan from Sweetwater who learned football from her abuela with cafecito and dominoes, and Gideon Fairweather, a Lynchburg youth pastor who coaches middle-school football on the side.

Gideon: Welcome to Lynchburg, Lourdes! Game day here feels like a family reunion with a marching band.

Lourdes: Gracias, Gideon. I brought cafecito and my abuela's dominoes.

Gideon: Dominoes? On Halloween?

Lourdes: The dominoes are the players, the sugar packets are the defense. It's the scariest playbook in Miami.

Players to watch

Liberty quarterback Deshawn Purdie has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 80 of 133 for 1,226 yards and nine touchdowns, and he hasn't thrown an interception all season. Kam Davis leads the rushers with 583 yards and five scores, and Makai Jackson leads the receivers with 23 catches for 540 yards and five touchdowns. Linebacker Derrell Farrar leads the defense with 39 tackles, and Brenton Williams has three sacks.

FIU quarterback JJ Kohl has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 131 of 227 for 1,637 yards, 10 touchdowns and eight interceptions. Devonte Lyons leads the rushers with 383 yards, and Maguire Anderson leads the receivers with 36 catches for 568 yards. DeeJay Holmes Jr. has five sacks, and DeAndre Boykins leads the team with 37 tackles.

Gideon: Zero interceptions for Purdie. I preach a whole sermon on patience, and that young man is living it.

Lourdes: DeeJay Holmes Jr. gets to the quarterback like my abuela gets to the last pastelito. Fast.

What happened last week

Liberty won 34-6 at Kennesaw State on Thursday, Oct. 22. Purdie threw for 176 yards and a touchdown, and Davis ran for a score. Kennesaw State had won the last three meetings, so this one meant plenty.

Gideon: Grace and grit, Lourdes. Grace and grit.

FIU lost 26-23 to Middle Tennessee at home on Tuesday, Oct. 20, a "Paws at the Pit" night. Kohl threw three touchdowns, Lyons ran 19 times for 115 yards, and Kyle McNeal caught a scoring ball. The week before, the Panthers lost 27-24 at Jacksonville State.

Lourdes: Two three-point losses in a row. My abuela needed two coladas. Then she said, "Mija, next week."

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Purdie has thrown the most passes for Liberty every week, and Kohl has done the same for FIU, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Jamey Chadwell has led Liberty since the 2023 season, after head coaching stops at North Greenville, Delta State, Charleston Southern and Coastal Carolina. His first Liberty team went 13-1. This year's Flames run the ball and play defense, allowing 14.0 points a game.

Willie Simmons is in his second year at FIU. In his first season, the Panthers went 7-6 and made their first bowl trip since 2019. Before that, he won a black college national championship and a SWAC title at Florida A&M in 2023. He played quarterback at Clemson and The Citadel.

Gideon: Coach Chadwell builds a team the way I build a youth group. Steady, with a lot of snacks.

Lourdes: Coach Simmons wins everywhere he goes. ¡Vamos!

Player storylines

The matchup to watch is Davis and Liberty's big guys up front against an FIU defense that allows only 16.3 points a game. On the other side, Kohl needs to find Anderson against a Liberty defense that has been stingy all season. These teams have met four times since 2020. Liberty won three, but FIU won the most recent one, 34-27 last season.

What's happening on campus

It's Halloween afternoon in Lynchburg, and Williams Stadium, built in 1989 and listed at 25,000, should be full of Flames fans, plus a costume or two. FIU's Pitbull Stadium, opened in 1995, is listed at 20,000, and its Homecoming is still to come later this fall.

Lourdes: I'm dressing as a domino. Double six. The best one.

Gideon: I'm dressing as a middle-school water bottle. That's what those kids care about most.

Anything else fun and true

Liberty moved from FCS to FBS and became a full member in 2019. In 2020 the Flames entered the AP poll for the first time, at No. 25, and they joined Conference USA in 2023. FIU shared the 2010 Sun Belt championship and plays Florida Atlantic every year in the Shula Bowl.

Gideon: Whatever happens, Lourdes, supper's on me. We'll say grace first.

Lourdes: Deal, Gideon. I'll bring the cafecito.

My call: Liberty Flames 27, Florida International Panthers 12.
