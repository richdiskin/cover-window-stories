Purdue Boilermakers at Penn State Nittany Lions

Purdue Boilermakers (3-4) at Penn State Nittany Lions (3-4)

4:15 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Beaver Stadium · BTN

Luann Kessler ranks every defense by Creamery ice cream flavor. Barnaby Wolcott-Reese built a miniature Boilermaker Special in his garage. Both of their teams are 3-4, both are coming off a bye, and only one gets back to even on Halloween afternoon in Happy Valley.

Purdue (3-4) at Penn State (3-4), Saturday at 4:15 p.m. ET on BTN at Beaver Stadium in University Park. It's a Big Ten game. DraftKings has Penn State Nittany Lions −7.5 with a total of 54.5. My card: Penn State Nittany Lions −7.5, Over 54.5, and a final of Penn State Nittany Lions 34, Purdue Boilermakers 23.

Luann speaks for Penn State. "We Are, and we are 0-4 in the Big Ten, which hurts. But we lost 18-21 at Michigan, 23-31 to USC and 20-24 to Wisconsin. Close games. Rocco Becht threw for 267 yards at Michigan, and Chase Sowell caught 11 balls for 151. Matt Campbell says we have complete faith in Rocco. Koby Howard is questionable after missing four games. Tony Rojas has 55 tackles and 5 sacks. That defense is rocky road."

Barnaby speaks for Purdue. "Boiler up! Ryan Browne has 1,822 yards and only 3 interceptions. Fame Ijeboi has 9 rushing touchdowns, and Asaad Waseem caught 9 balls for 116 against Washington. We won at Illinois 24-17 and beat Minnesota 41-13."

Here is the part that matters. Penn State allows 18.9 points a game. Purdue allows 32.3. The Boilermakers gave up 38 to Washington, 49 to Notre Dame and 52 at UCLA. Penn State scored 45, 27 and 55 in its three wins, and even its losses were mostly one-score games.

Why Penn State? The Formula likes the Nittany Lions at 33-23, and Next Man Up likes them at 34-23. Penn State is home, rested and hungry for its first Big Ten win under Campbell. Purdue's defense has eight sacks all season, so Becht should have time.

The worry is the Penn State offense. Becht went 12 of 29 against Wisconsin and threw two interceptions at Michigan. Follow The Money likes Purdue after the number dropped a point, to −7.5.

Why the Over/Under? The Formula has 56, and Next Man Up has 57. Next Man Up likes the Over, and Follow The Money likes the Over after the total climbed two points. In The Trenches has no call: Purdue runs for 3.7 yards a carry against a Penn State defense that allows 4.9, and Penn State gets 3.8 against a Purdue defense that allows 5.2. Both offenses throw it well, and Purdue scores 31.3 a game. My 34-23 adds up to 57, two and a half over the over/under 54.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Penn State 33-23 and likes the Nittany Lions. Next Man Up has Penn State 34-23 and likes Penn State and the Over. Follow The Money likes Purdue and the Over. In The Trenches has no score this week. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "No official report, so no quarterback adjustment."

Bottom of the card: Becht against a defense that bends.

Penn State Nittany Lions 34, Purdue Boilermakers 23. Penn State Nittany Lions cover the 7.5. Over 54.5.
