Purdue vs Penn State: Halloween, All-U Day and a Velveeta Show

Purdue Boilermakers (3-4) at Penn State Nittany Lions (3-4)

4:15 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Beaver Stadium · BTN

Purdue Boilermakers vs Penn State Nittany Lions is a Week 9 Halloween game at Beaver Stadium on All-U Day, with a band called Velveeta playing the Block Party at 10 a.m. ET and a volleyball White Out against Purdue in Rec Hall at 12:30 p.m. ET. Barnaby Wolcott-Reese, a West Lafayette mechanical engineer who built a scale Boilermaker Special in his garage, says Ryan Browne and Fame Ijeboi will keep rolling, and Luann Kessler, a former Penn State volleyball setter who loves Creamery ice cream, says Rocco Becht, Chase Sowell and Tony Rojas end the skid.

Luann: Barnaby, you're early. The Block Party doesn't open until 10 a.m. ET.

Barnaby: I drove my scale Special. It has a top speed of four miles an hour.

Luann: So you left Thursday.

Barnaby: Wednesday. Engineers allow for margin.

There's a lot happening in University Park on Saturday. Penn State women's volleyball hosts Purdue at Rec Hall for its White Out and Senior Day, and the first 1,000 fans who show a mobile football ticket at the doors get in free. The Blue Band marches to the stadium at 3 p.m. ET, and Penn State has played 20 previous games on Halloween. The Nittany Lions are 11-8-1 in them, dating to 1888, and last played on Halloween in 2020 against Ohio State.

Luann: Volleyball first, Barnaby. I'll teach you how to call a ball out.

Barnaby: I'll calculate whether it was actually out.

Luann: That's why nobody sits with you.

Penn State is 3-4 and 0-4 in the Big Ten after losses to Wisconsin, Northwestern, USC and Michigan, the last one 21-18 in Ann Arbor. Becht is 132 of 210 for 1,664 yards, 12 touchdowns and five interceptions. Sowell had a career-high 11 catches for 151 yards at Michigan and has 34 catches for 510 yards on the year. Rojas has five sacks and two interceptions, and he has posted double-digit tackles in three games in a row.

Matt Campbell talked about adversity on Tuesday. "How do you not waver and flinch when adversity hits?" he asked.

He also stood by his locker room. "As I continue to say, we have the right people in the locker room," he said.

Luann: That's a coach, Barnaby. Steady hands. Like a good set.

Barnaby: Steady hands are my whole profession.

Purdue is 3-4 and 2-2 in the league, coming off a bye after a 38-26 loss to Washington on Oct. 16. Even in that game, the Boilermakers' defense forced five takeaways. Browne is 145 of 221 for 1,822 yards, 10 touchdowns and three interceptions. Ijeboi has 568 rushing yards and nine touchdowns, Asaad Waseem has 38 catches for 588 yards, and Jojo Hayden leads the defense with 48 tackles. Purdue won 24-17 at Illinois and beat Minnesota 41-13 in October.

Barnaby: Five takeaways, Luann. Our defense was a vending machine.

Luann: And you still lost by 12.

Barnaby: The vending machine was out of change.

The series has been one-sided. Penn State leads it 17-3-1 and has won 11 straight against Purdue, dating to 2005. The last meeting was a 49-10 Penn State win in West Lafayette in 2024.

Barnaby: Eleven straight. I've run the numbers. Twelve is unlikely.

Luann: You said that about the cheese band, too.

The Big Ten's initial availability report, posted at 8 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists two Purdue players out and two questionable, and 11 Penn State players out, with Chase Sowell questionable. Rocco Becht is not on it.

Barnaby: I am going to see Velveeta, by the way. Ten a.m. ET. Front row.

Luann: You'll love them. They're smooth.

Barnaby: Everything at Penn State is smooth. The ice cream, the cheese band, the setter.

Luann: Flattery won't help you on Saturday. But I'll buy the Creamery cones after.

Barnaby: Deal. I'll drive us there in the Special.

Luann: We'll walk. We'd like to get there tonight.

My call: Penn State Nittany Lions 34, Purdue Boilermakers 23.
