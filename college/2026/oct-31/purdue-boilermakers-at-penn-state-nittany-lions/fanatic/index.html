Halloween in Happy Valley: Purdue Visits Penn State

Purdue Boilermakers (3-4) at Penn State Nittany Lions (3-4)

4:15 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Beaver Stadium · BTN

Purdue (3-4, 2-2 Big Ten) at Penn State (3-4, 0-4 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 4:15 p.m. ET, Beaver Stadium, University Park, Pennsylvania, BTN.

Your hosts are Barnaby Wolcott-Reese, a West Lafayette native and mechanical engineer who built a model Boilermaker Special in his garage, and Luann Kessler, a former Penn State volleyball setter who loves Creamery ice cream and linebackers equally.

Barnaby: Boiler up, Luann! I measured the drive from West Lafayette. Every mile was worth it.

Luann: We Are glad you came, Barnaby. Bring an appetite. The Creamery is open.

Players to watch

Purdue quarterback Ryan Browne has completed 145 of 221 throws for 1,822 yards, with 10 touchdowns and only three interceptions. Running back Fame Ijeboi has 568 yards and nine touchdowns on 130 carries. Wide receiver Asaad Waseem has 38 catches for 588 yards. Linebacker Jojo Hayden leads the defense with 48 tackles.

Penn State quarterback Rocco Becht has completed 132 of 210 throws for 1,664 yards and 12 touchdowns. Wide receiver Chase Sowell has 34 catches for 510 yards. Running back Carson Hansen has four rushing touchdowns. Linebacker Tony Rojas does a little of everything, with 55 tackles, five sacks and two interceptions.

Luann: Tony Rojas is mint chocolate chip. Cool, smooth and everywhere.

What happened last week

Both teams had a bye. Purdue last played on Oct. 16 and lost 38-26 to Washington at home. Browne threw for 273 yards, and Waseem caught nine balls for 116 yards.

Penn State last played on Oct. 17 and lost 21-18 at Michigan. Becht went 28 of 44 for 267 yards, and Sowell had a big day with 11 catches for 151 yards and a touchdown.

Barnaby: Eleven catches at the Big House. That young man earned his ice cream.

Injuries and availability

No official Big Ten availability report had been posted as of Tuesday. Penn State wide receiver Koby Howard has missed the last four games with an undisclosed injury. He returned to practice and is considered questionable for Saturday.

Luann: Praying Koby gets back to full speed, whenever that is.

What the coaches are up to

Matt Campbell is in his first season at Penn State, and he brought Becht with him from Iowa State. After the Michigan game, Campbell made it clear Becht is his quarterback. "We have complete faith in Rocco," he said. "Rocco has continued to obviously play good football." Penn State is still looking for its first Big Ten win under Campbell.

Barry Odom is in his second season at Purdue. The Boilermakers went 2-10 in 2025, so a 3-4 start with two Big Ten wins is real progress. They won 24-17 at Illinois and beat Minnesota 41-13.

Player storylines

Both teams are 3-4, and both want to get back to even. Penn State's four losses came by four, 21, eight and three points, so the Nittany Lions have been close in most of them.

Purdue opened the year with Browne going 23 of 29 for 317 yards and three touchdowns against Indiana State. He threw for 378 yards at UCLA, and the Boilermakers took Wake Forest to overtime before losing 38-36.

Becht started his season in style, going 13 of 18 for 271 yards and four touchdowns in a 45-0 win over Marshall.

Barnaby: That's the blueprint, Luann. Draw it up, then build it.

What's happening on campus

Beaver Stadium holds 106,572. The field is West Shore Home Field, and the Penn State Blue Band will be out in force. Penn State claims national championships from 1982 and 1986, and the White Out is the tradition every visitor should see once.

Purdue's Ross-Ade Stadium opened in 1924. The Boilermaker Special is the tradition Barnaby would show you first, and the Purdue All-American Marching Band makes the trip a treat.

Anything else fun and true

Penn State's defense allows only 18.9 points a game. Purdue scores 31.3 a game. That's a fun matchup on Halloween afternoon.

Luann: Final Creamery Rankings this week, Barnaby. Your offense is rocky road.

Barnaby: I'll take that as a compliment, Luann. Boiler up.

My call: Penn State Nittany Lions 34, Purdue Boilermakers 23.
