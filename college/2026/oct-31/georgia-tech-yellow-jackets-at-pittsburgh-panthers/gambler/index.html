Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets at Pittsburgh Panthers

Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets (2-5) at Pittsburgh Panthers (5-3)

7:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Acrisure Stadium · ESPN2

Georgia Tech vs Pittsburgh prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on Pittsburgh Panthers −7.5 and Over 54.5 at DraftKings, with a final of Pittsburgh Panthers 36, Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets 21. It's Halloween night on the North Shore and Hail to Heroes at Acrisure Stadium, where Pitt honors military members, veterans and first responders, and fans with the theme ticket go home in a knit camo beanie.

Pitt needs this one. The Panthers opened 5-0, beating Miami (Ohio) 59-14, UCF 12-7, Syracuse 27-13 and Bucknell 59-0 at home and winning 35-33 at Virginia Tech, then lost to North Carolina, at Boston College and at Miami. Georgia Tech arrives off a 27-49 home loss to Boston College and allows 30.3 points a game. Holden Geriner went 18 of 32 for 268 yards at Miami, and Cataurus Hicks caught five balls for 149 yards there. Damon Ferguson Jr. has 477 rushing yards. The ACC's initial availability report, posted at 8 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists Pitt quarterback Mason Heintschel out and Ferguson questionable.

Wendell Quist, the Georgia Tech aerospace engineer, has a punt-trajectory chart taped to his cooler. "Alberto Mendoza is 153 of 215 for 1,714 yards, Yinz. That's a pretty arc."

Dominic "Yinz" Bertolucci blows his homemade whistle twice. "Pretty arc, sure. Boston College still ran right through yinz, n'at."

My concern is the Pitt offense lately. The Panthers have lost three straight, and Georgia Tech beat Duke 28-25, so the Jackets can still play with a good team. Mendoza threw for 335 yards at Virginia Tech, and Dalen Penson had five catches for 88 yards against Boston College. If Georgia Tech moves it through the air, seven and a half is a lot.

The total comes down to two offenses that score. Georgia Tech scores 26.7 a game and allows 30.3. Pitt scores 31.6. My 36-21 adds up to 57. DraftKings has Pitt −7.5 and 54.5. The consensus spread opened at Pittsburgh −7.5 and hasn't moved. The consensus total opened at 54.5 and is 53.5 as of Oct 27, 2026, 4:44 PM ET.
