Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets at Pittsburgh Panthers

Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets (2-5) at Pittsburgh Panthers (5-3)

7:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Acrisure Stadium · ESPN2

Wendell Quist charts the flight of every punt, and lately the Georgia Tech numbers have only gone one direction. Dominic "Yinz" Bertolucci has his homemade whistle and a Pitt team that needs a win just as badly. Halloween night on the North Shore.

Georgia Tech (2-5) at Pittsburgh (5-3), Saturday at 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN2 at Acrisure Stadium in Pittsburgh. It's an ACC game. DraftKings has Pittsburgh Panthers −7.5 with a total of 54.5. My card: Pittsburgh Panthers −7.5, Over 54.5, and a final of Pittsburgh Panthers 36, Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets 21.

Wendell speaks for Georgia Tech. "Alberto Mendoza is 153 of 215 for 1,714 yards. That's a good arc. The trouble is the other side. Boston College ran for three hundred ninety-nine yards on us in a 27-49 loss. Coach Brent Key called it embarrassing and said, 'It's on coaching, so that's on me.'"

Yinz speaks for Pitt. "How's it goin', n'at? We lost Mason Heintschel for the season at Virginia Tech, and we've lost three since. But Holden Geriner is our guy now, Coach Narduzzi said so. Cataurus Hicks caught five for 149 at Miami. And Georgia Tech can't stop the run. That's a whole lotta yinz."

Here is the part that matters. With Heintschel, Pitt started 5-0, beating Miami (Ohio) 59-14, UCF 12-7, Syracuse 27-13 and Bucknell 59-0 at home and winning 35-33 at Virginia Tech. The losses since came against North Carolina, at Boston College and at Miami. Georgia Tech allows 30.3 points a game. Its last two were losses of 24-34 at Virginia Tech and 27-49 at home. The Yellow Jackets have a long list of injured players, and their best run defender, Tawfiq Thomas, was out against Boston College.

Why Pitt? The Formula and Next Man Up both like the Panthers. Pitt's freshman back Damon Ferguson Jr. has 477 yards, and the Panthers have run it well in ACC play. Geriner was steadier at Miami, 18 of 32 for 268 yards. That defense is hurting.

The worry is In The Trenches, which likes Georgia Tech. Pitt has given up some points lately, and without Heintschel the offense has been uneven. Georgia Tech beat Duke 28-25, so this group can still play.

Why the Over/Under? Georgia Tech allows 30.3 a game and scores 26.7. Pitt scores 31.6. The Formula has 55, Next Man Up has 58, and Follow The Money likes the Over because the total moved from 52.5 to 54.5. My 36-21 adds up to 57, two and a half over the over/under 54.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Pittsburgh 33-22 and likes Pitt and the Over. Next Man Up has Pittsburgh 38-20 and likes Pitt and the Over. Follow The Money has no side and likes the Over. In The Trenches likes Georgia Tech and has no total. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "No official report yet. Georgia Tech's injury list is long."

The Formula: "Thirty-three to twenty-two. Pitt clears it."

Follow The Money: "The total climbed two points. I go with it."

Bottom of the card: Pitt runs the ball, Geriner keeps it clean, and the slide ends at home. Yinz blows the whistle. Wendell recalculates.

Pittsburgh Panthers 36, Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets 21. Pittsburgh Panthers cover the 7.5. Over 54.5.
