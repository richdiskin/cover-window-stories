Georgia Tech vs Pitt: Hail to Heroes on Halloween Night

Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets (2-5) at Pittsburgh Panthers (5-3)

7:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Acrisure Stadium · ESPN2

Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets vs Pittsburgh Panthers is a Week 9 Halloween night on the North Shore, where Acrisure Stadium hosts Hail to Heroes for military members, veterans and first responders, and theme-ticket fans get a knit camo beanie. Wendell Quist, a Georgia Tech aerospace engineer who charts the flight of every punt, says Alberto Mendoza, Justice Haynes and Jordan Allen can quiet the place, and Dominic "Yinz" Bertolucci, a steel-town fan who grades every tackle from "yinz" to "yikes" with a homemade whistle, says Holden Geriner, Cataurus Hicks and Damon Ferguson Jr. will keep the Panthers home happy.

Yinz: How's it goin', n'at? Welcome to Pittsburgh, Wendell.

Wendell: Thank you, Yinz. I brought my calculator, a warm coat and a pumpkin with a slide rule carved in it.

Yinz: A slide rule. On Halloween. You're a scary man, Wendell.

Saturday is the Aldrich Foundation Hail to Heroes game, sponsored by GBU Life and the Aldrich Foundation, with on-field and in-game recognitions for those who serve. Pitt calls it "one of the most meaningful days on the home schedule each season." Kickoff is 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN2, and 93.7 The Fan has the radio call.

Wendell: I'll take the beanie. It's going to be cold on the river.

Yinz: The beanie's camo, Wendell. You'll finally blend in with a crowd.

This is an old series. Pitt and Georgia Tech meet for the 19th time in a series that dates to 1918, and the Panthers lead it 12-6. Pitt is 5-2 against Georgia Tech in Pittsburgh and has won five of the last six meetings. Last season Pitt won 42-28 in Atlanta over a Georgia Tech team ranked No. 15 at the time. The Jackets won the 2022 meeting 26-21.

Wendell: I have the 2022 game laminated.

Yinz: I have the 2025 game tattooed. Kidding. It's a sticker.

Georgia Tech is 2-5 and 1-3 in the ACC. Mendoza is 153 of 215 for 1,714 yards, 10 touchdowns and six interceptions, and he threw for 335 yards at Virginia Tech two weeks ago. Haynes has 515 rushing yards and four touchdowns, and Allen leads the team with 42 catches for 517 yards. On defense, safety Tae Harris has 45 tackles, and Noah Carter has three sacks. The Jackets' best win this season was 28-25 over Duke. Last week they lost 49-27 at home to Boston College, which ran for 399 yards, though Dalen Penson had a career day with five catches for 88 yards and a touchdown.

Coach Brent Key took it on himself. "It's on coaching, so that's on me. I'll take full responsibility," he said.

Wendell: That's a coach who checks his own math.

Yinz: Respect, Wendell. That's a yinz answer.

Pitt is 5-3 and 2-3 in the league. The Panthers opened 5-0, with home wins over Miami (Ohio), UCF, Syracuse and Bucknell and a 35-33 win at Virginia Tech, and then lost to North Carolina, at Boston College and at Miami. Geriner went 18 of 32 for 268 yards and a touchdown in the 52-23 loss at Miami, and Hicks caught five balls for 149 yards and a score there. Hicks has 31 catches for 649 yards and five touchdowns on the season. Ferguson, a freshman, has 477 rushing yards and four touchdowns. Cruce Brookins leads the defense with 55 tackles, and Jaeden Moore has 4.5 sacks.

Pat Narduzzi has been sure about this group all fall. "We have a team that can win an ACC championship with Mason or without Mason," he said.

Yinz: Brookins with 55 tackles. That's a whole lotta yinz.

Wendell: My chart says Hicks has a very long flight path. I don't love that chart.

The ACC's initial availability report, posted at 8 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists 16 Georgia Tech players out, Noah Carter among them. Pitt lists nine players out, including quarterback Mason Heintschel, and Damon Ferguson Jr. questionable.

Wendell: I'm coming as a punt. A high one. With hang time.

Yinz: I'm coming as a Terrible Towel. Same as every year.

Wendell: Whatever happens, Yinz, dinner's on me, and you say "y'all" on the jumbotron.

Yinz: Then you say "n'at" at your next engineering meeting.

Wendell: Deal. I'll put it in the minutes.

My call: Pittsburgh Panthers 36, Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets 21.
