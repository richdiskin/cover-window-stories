Kansas Jayhawks at TCU Horned Frogs

Kansas Jayhawks (3-4) at TCU Horned Frogs (3-4)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Amon G. Carter Stadium · FS1

Kansas vs TCU prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on Kansas Jayhawks +6 and Under 46.5 at DraftKings, with a final of TCU Horned Frogs 25, Kansas Jayhawks 20. TCU calls it the Haunting at the Carter, a Halloween blackout and the Frogs' first home game on Halloween since two thousand nine.

The case for Kansas is that TCU doesn't run away from anybody. The Frogs score 23.4 a game, and all four of their losses were by eight or fewer: 15-10, 21-13, 17-10 and 24-20. Kansas just beat Baylor 25-24, and Dylan Edwards ran for 156 yards in that one. My final has TCU by 5, inside the 6.

Ryder Galloway, boots on and quarter-zip zipped, has the TCU case. "We allow 13.9 points a game. Jaden Craig threw for 372 yards last week. Koron Hayward has 4.5 sacks."

Fitz Calloway, spinning a basketball on one finger, answers for Kansas. "Edwards has 531 rushing yards. Isaiah Marshall has 11 touchdown throws. We score 31.0 a game, and that's a lot of free throws."

What could go wrong is the history. TCU has won the last five meetings, including 59-23 in Fort Worth in 2020. Craig can also throw it. He has 2,066 yards, and Kansas allows 24.0 a game. If he hits the big ones, six isn't enough.

The Under is about TCU's defense. The Frogs' games finished 15-10, 21-13, 17-10, 17-6 and 24-20 against everyone but Grambling and Arkansas State. Kansas likes to run with Edwards, which shortens the game. My 25-20 adds up to 45, below the 46.5.

For the record, DraftKings has TCU −6.

The room is with the Jayhawks and the Under, and each has one backer. In The Trenches likes Kansas +6 and makes no over/under call. The Formula has TCU 25-21 and is on Kansas +6 and the Under without backing either. Next Man Up has TCU 25-18, is on TCU −6 without backing it, and likes the Under. Follow The Money has no pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.
