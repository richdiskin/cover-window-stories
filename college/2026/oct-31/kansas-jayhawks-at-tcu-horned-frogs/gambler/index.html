Kansas Jayhawks at TCU Horned Frogs

Kansas Jayhawks (3-4) at TCU Horned Frogs (3-4)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Amon G. Carter Stadium · FS1

Ryder Galloway is standing at the rail outside Amon G. Carter Stadium in boots and a quarter-zip when Fitz Calloway walks up spinning a basketball on one finger. Kansas (3-4, 1-3 Big 12) at TCU (3-4, 1-3 Big 12), Saturday at 3:30 p.m. ET on FS1 at Amon G. Carter Stadium in Fort Worth. DraftKings has TCU Horned Frogs −6 with a total of 46.5. My card: Kansas Jayhawks +6, Under 46.5, and a final of TCU Horned Frogs 25, Kansas Jayhawks 20.

Ryder speaks for TCU, thumbs in his belt loops. "Every rider holds his spot on this defense. We allow 13.9 points a game. Jaden Craig threw for 372 yards against West Virginia, Koron Hayward has 4.5 sacks, and we just beat Baylor 17-6 in Waco. Yeehaw."

Fitz speaks for Kansas, ball tucked under his arm. "Dylan Edwards ran 30 times for 156 yards against Baylor, and we won 25-24. Isaiah Marshall has 11 touchdown throws, Nik McMillan has 466 receiving yards, and Trey Lathan has 48 tackles. We run the ball. That's a layup line, Ryder."

Why Kansas? Because TCU's big games stay close. The Frogs lost 15-10 to North Carolina, 21-13 at UCF, 17-10 to BYU and 24-20 to West Virginia. Their one Big 12 win came 17-6 at Baylor. Kansas brings a run game that can shorten the game and keep it close, and Edwards just carried the ball 30 times. My final has TCU winning, but by five.

The risk is the Kansas turnovers. Marshall has seven interceptions, including two against Baylor. If the Jayhawks give the Frogs a short field twice, six points goes fast.

Why the Under? TCU's defense. Six of TCU's seven opponents have scored 21 points or fewer, and the Frogs' offense scored 10, 13, 10 and 20 in its four losses. TCU scores 23.4 points a game. Kansas runs the ball and keeps the clock moving. My 25-20 adds up to 45, under 46.5.

Now the room. The Formula has TCU 25-21, sides with Kansas since its score lands inside the 6, and lands on the Under. Next Man Up has TCU 25-18, sides with TCU, and likes the Under. Follow The Money likes TCU and the Under after both numbers moved that way. In The Trenches likes Kansas because the Jayhawks' run game should find room.

In The Trenches started it: "Edwards gets his carries. Kansas hangs around."

Follow The Money answered: "The market went to TCU. I don't argue with the market."

Bottom of the card: a TCU defense that keeps everything close, a Kansas run game that keeps the clock moving and two teams fighting to get back to an even record. Fitz spins the ball one more time. Give me Kansas with the points, and the Under.

TCU Horned Frogs 25, Kansas Jayhawks 20. Kansas Jayhawks cover the 6. Under 46.5.
