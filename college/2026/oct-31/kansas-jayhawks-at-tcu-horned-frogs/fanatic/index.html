Kansas vs TCU: A Halloween Blackout for the Haunting at the Carter

Kansas Jayhawks (3-4) at TCU Horned Frogs (3-4)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Amon G. Carter Stadium · FS1

Kansas Jayhawks vs TCU Horned Frogs is the Week 9 Haunting at the Carter, a Halloween blackout in Fort Worth and the first time the Frogs have hosted a home game on Halloween since 2009. Fitz Calloway, a hoops-first Lawrence native who turns every football stat into basketball points, says Dylan Edwards, Isaiah Marshall and Lance Leipold's Jayhawks steal the night, and Ryder Galloway, a Stockyards cowboy turned finance guy who explains defenses as cattle drives, says Jaden Craig, Koron Hayward and Sonny Dykes's Frogs keep the herd together.

Ryder: Howdy, Fitz, and welcome to Fort Worth! Not Dallas. Never Dallas.

Fitz: Thanks, Ryder. I brought a basketball. Old habits.

Ryder: Leave it in the truck, partner. Tonight everybody wears black.

Fitz: Black? Ryder, I came in crimson and blue. I'm going to look like a highlighter at a funeral.

TCU's game notes put it plainly: the game "is a blackout and marks the first time the Frogs have hosted a home game on Halloween since 2009." It's also the first TCU home game this season with a 3:30 p.m. ET kickoff, and the first meeting in Fort Worth between these two since 2021.

Ryder: Seventeen years without a home Halloween, Fitz. We've been saving up.

Fitz: For seventeen years you've been saving costumes? That's a lot of cowboy hats.

The history runs purple. TCU leads the all-time series 27-9-4, including 12-2-2 in Fort Worth, and the notes say the Frogs "have won five straight and 11 of the last 12 against KU." The one Kansas win in recent memory came in 2018, 27-26. The last two in Fort Worth went 59-23 in 2020 and 31-28 in 2021.

Fitz: One point in 2018, Ryder. A free throw. I remember every free throw.

Both teams are 3-4 and 1-3 in the Big 12. Kansas is coming off a 25-24 win over Baylor, with Edwards running 30 times for 156 yards and Marshall throwing two touchdown throws. Nik McMillan caught a touchdown among his 66 yards.

Fitz: Thirty carries, Ryder. Dylan Edwards played the whole fourth quarter like it was overtime.

TCU lost 24-20 to West Virginia on Oct. 24, even though Craig went 33 of 47 for 372 yards. Kari Ashley caught six balls for 118 yards. Every TCU loss has been close: 15-10 to North Carolina, 21-13 at UCF, 17-10 to BYU and 24-20 to West Virginia.

Ryder: Four losses by a combined twenty-four points. That's not a stampede, Fitz. That's a few strays.

Craig, a Harvard transfer, is TCU's first new quarterback in charge in three seasons, and he has led the Frogs in throws in all seven games. He's 162 of 240 for 2,066 yards, 11 touchdowns and six interceptions. Jeremy Payne leads the rushers with 429 yards, Ed Small has 38 catches for 514 yards, Hayward has 4.5 sacks and Hudson Hooper has 41 tackles. The defense allows 13.9 points a game, and the notes say it has given up only 17 plays of 20 yards or more, the fewest in the Big 12.

Ryder: Seventeen big plays all year. That's a fence nobody gets through.

Marshall has led Kansas in throws in all seven games. He's 105 of 196 for 1,506 yards, 11 touchdowns and seven interceptions. Edwards has 531 rushing yards and four touchdowns, McMillan has 25 catches for 466 yards and five scores, Trey Lathan has 48 tackles and Leroy Harris III has 3.5 sacks. Kansas scores 31.0 points a game.

Fitz: Thirty-one a game, Ryder. In my world that's a bad shooting night, but we'll take it.

Dykes is in his fifth season at TCU, and the notes say the 2022 National Coach of the Year won 36 games in his first four, the most by a Frogs coach since Francis Schmidt won 37 from 1929-32. Leipold has led Kansas since 2021.

Fitz: Coach Leipold wins everywhere he goes. That's a Hall of Fame jump shot.

Ryder: Coach Dykes has more wins here than anybody since the Hoover administration. Not one of them in Dallas.

The matchup is Edwards against a TCU front that has been very hard to run on. On the other side, Craig wants to push the ball down the field against a Kansas defense that allows 24.0 a game.

The Big 12's initial availability report, posted at 10 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists six Kansas players out, with Isaiah Marshall probable, and seven TCU players out, with Jeremy Payne doubtful and Kari Ashley questionable.

Fitz: I'm dressing as a jayhawk. A real one. Beak and all.

Ryder: I'm dressing as a cowboy, Fitz.

Fitz: You're always dressed as a cowboy.

Ryder: Exactly. Saves a costume. Whatever happens, the brisket's on me.

Fitz: Deal, Ryder. Rock Chalk, and save me a burnt end.

My call: TCU Horned Frogs 25, Kansas Jayhawks 20.
