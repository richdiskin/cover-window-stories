Hoops Math Meets the Cattle Drive: Kansas Visits TCU on Halloween

Kansas Jayhawks (3-4) at TCU Horned Frogs (3-4)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Amon G. Carter Stadium · FS1

Kansas (3-4, 1-3 Big 12) at TCU (3-4, 1-3 Big 12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:30 p.m. ET, Amon G. Carter Stadium, Fort Worth, Texas, FS1.

Your hosts are Fitz Calloway, a hoops-first Lawrence native who converts every football stat into basketball points, and Ryder Galloway, a Stockyards cowboy turned finance guy who explains defenses as cattle drives.

Ryder: Howdy, Fitz, and welcome to Fort Worth! Not Dallas. Never Dallas.

Fitz: Thanks, Ryder. I brought a basketball. Old habits.

Ryder: This is a football game, partner.

Fitz: Sure. But a touchdown is two and a half three-pointers, and nobody can tell me otherwise.

Players to watch

TCU quarterback Jaden Craig has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 162 of 240 for 2,066 yards, 11 touchdowns and six interceptions. Jeremy Payne leads the rushers with 429 yards, and Ed Small leads the receivers with 38 catches for 514 yards. On defense, Koron Hayward has 4.5 sacks, and linebacker Hudson Hooper leads the team with 41 tackles.

Kansas quarterback Isaiah Marshall has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 105 of 196 for 1,506 yards, 11 touchdowns and seven interceptions. Dylan Edwards leads the rushers with 531 yards and four scores, and Nik McMillan leads the receivers with 25 catches for 466 yards and five touchdowns. Linebacker Trey Lathan leads the defense with 48 tackles, and Leroy Harris III has 3.5 sacks.

Ryder: Hayward gets after a quarterback like a flank rider after a stray. Yeehaw.

Fitz: McMillan has five touchdown catches on just 25 grabs. That's a fast break, Ryder.

What happened last week

Kansas beat Baylor 25-24 on Homecoming in Lawrence on Oct. 24. Edwards carried 30 times for 156 yards, Marshall threw two touchdowns, and McMillan caught a scoring ball. It was KU's 114th Homecoming, and the parade on Jayhawk Boulevard was back for the first time in seven years.

Fitz: One-point win, Ryder. That's a buzzer-beater.

TCU lost 24-20 to West Virginia at home on Oct. 24. Craig completed 33 of 47 for 372 yards and a touchdown, and Kari Ashley caught six balls for 118 yards. The week before, the Frogs won 17-6 at Baylor.

Ryder: Four points. I'm blaming Dallas. I don't know how yet, but I'll figure it out.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Craig has thrown the most passes for TCU in every game, and Marshall has done the same for Kansas, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Sonny Dykes was hired at TCU on Nov. 29, 2021, and in his first season he took the Frogs to the national championship game. This year his defense is the story. TCU allows only 13.9 points a game, and every one of its losses has been close.

Lance Leipold has led Kansas since 2021. Before that he won six Division III national championships at Wisconsin-Whitewater and reached 100 wins faster than any coach in NCAA history, in his 106th game. His 2023 team went 9-4. This year his offense runs the ball well, behind Edwards and the big guys up front.

Fitz: Coach Leipold wins everywhere he goes. That's a Hall of Fame jump shot.

Ryder: Coach Dykes took us to the title game in year one. Not one bit of that happened in Dallas.

Player storylines

The matchup to watch is Edwards against TCU's defense, which has been very hard to run on all season. On the other side, Craig has thrown the ball a lot, including 47 times last week, and Kansas will try to make him hurry. TCU won seven of the eight meetings from 2016 through 2025, including a 38-27 win in 2024, but Kansas won a 27-26 thriller in 2018.

What's happening on campus

Both teams just finished Homecoming weekends, and now comes a Halloween kickoff in Fort Worth. Amon G. Carter Stadium is listed at 46,000. Kansas plays at David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium, which opened in 1921 and had a running track around the field until 2014.

Ryder: I'm dressing as a cattle drive. All of it. The point riders, the flank riders, the drag riders.

Fitz: I'm dressing as a basketball. I've been training for this my whole life.

Anything else fun and true

TCU's 1938 team went 11-0, beat Carnegie Tech 15-7 in the Sugar Bowl and won the national title, and quarterback Davey O'Brien won the Heisman Trophy. The 2010 Frogs went 13-0 and won the Rose Bowl, and this year's team opened the season against North Carolina in Dublin, Ireland. Kansas football began in 1890, one of the oldest programs in the country.

Ryder: Whatever happens, Fitz, the barbecue's on me. We'll say grace first.

Fitz: Deal, Ryder. Rock Chalk, and save me some brisket.

My call: TCU Horned Frogs 25, Kansas Jayhawks 20.
