Mississippi State Bulldogs at Texas Longhorns

Mississippi State Bulldogs (4-3) at Texas Longhorns (5-2)

7:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium · ESPN

Wade Pettus draws Air Raid routes on feed-store receipts, and this week he has to draw them without his quarterback. Caro Whitfield, former rodeo queen and ex-consultant, prices every Longhorn like a stock. Halloween night in Austin.

Mississippi State (4-3) at Texas (5-2), Saturday at 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN at DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium in Austin. It's an SEC game. DraftKings has Texas Longhorns −20.5 with a total of 50.5. My card: Mississippi State Bulldogs +20.5, Over 50.5, and a final of Texas Longhorns 35, Mississippi State Bulldogs 19.

Wade speaks for Mississippi State. "Kamario Taylor is done for the season with a foot injury. That hurts. He threw 17 touchdown passes. But Fluff Bothwell ran 17 times for 111 yards against Oklahoma, and Anthony Evans III has 53 catches. Parker Puckett has the job now. One ring of the cowbell for him."

Caro speaks for Texas. "Darling, we beat Ole Miss 34-27, and Raleek Brown ran 23 times for 157 yards. Colin Simmons has 10.5 sacks, but he is an asset under repair with a high ankle sprain. Our defense played well without him. Still, we lost 24-27 to Oklahoma and 24-27 to Florida. We don't win big very often."

Here is the part that matters. Texas has not been a blowout team. It beat Ohio State 24-23, Tennessee 20-17 and Ole Miss 34-27. Only Texas State and UTSA were blowouts, 59-7 and 30-6. The offense can stall, and Arch Manning has seven interceptions. Twenty and a half is a lot to ask of this team.

Why Mississippi State? The Formula, Next Man Up and In The Trenches all like the Bulldogs. In The Trenches has Mississippi State at 4.7 yards a carry against a Texas defense that allows 3.0. Bothwell is the plan. Lebby's offense still scored 15 against Oklahoma, all after halftime, and last year the Bulldogs lost only 38-45 to Texas.

The worry is real. Without Taylor, the Bulldogs were shut out 0-31 at LSU. Puckett threw for 121 yards there. Follow The Money likes Texas because the spread moved two points, from 18.5 to 20.5. If Texas gets the ball early and often, this can get away.

Why the Over/Under? Mississippi State allows 27.7 a game, and Texas scores 30.7. The Formula and Next Man Up both have 54. Follow The Money likes the Under after the total fell from 52.5 to 50.5. My 35-19 adds up to 54, three and a half over the over/under 50.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Texas 35-19 and likes Mississippi State and the Over. Next Man Up has Texas 35-19 and likes Mississippi State and the Over. Follow The Money likes Texas and the Under. In The Trenches likes Mississippi State and has no total. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "No official report yet. No official report yet, so no quarterback change in my numbers."

The Formula: "Thirty-five to nineteen. The points are too many."

In The Trenches: "Bothwell can run on anybody. Even here."

Bottom of the card: Texas wins, but the Longhorns don't run away from people. Caro books a profit. Wade rings once, politely, when the Bulldogs score late.

Texas Longhorns 35, Mississippi State Bulldogs 19. Mississippi State Bulldogs cover the 20.5. Over 50.5.
