Mississippi State Bulldogs (4-3) at Texas Longhorns (5-2)

Mississippi State vs Texas prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on Mississippi State Bulldogs +20.5 and Over 50.5 at DraftKings, with a final of Texas Longhorns 35, Mississippi State Bulldogs 19. It's Halloween night in Austin, with Uncle Kracker playing the LBJ Lawn before kickoff and the Bulldogs closing a brutal October stretch.

Texas wins, but Texas doesn't win big against good teams. The Longhorns beat Ohio State 24-23, Tennessee 20-17 and Ole Miss 34-27, and they lost 27-24 to Oklahoma and 27-24 to Florida. Only Texas State and UTSA lost by more than 20.5, at 59-7 and 30-6. Arch Manning has seven interceptions, and he threw two against Ole Miss. Twenty and a half points is a lot to ask.
