Mississippi State vs Texas: Cowbells, Kracker and a Halloween Night

Mississippi State Bulldogs (4-3) at Texas Longhorns (5-2)

7:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium · ESPN

Mississippi State Bulldogs vs Texas Longhorns is a Week 9 Halloween night at DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium, where Uncle Kracker plays the LBJ Lawn before kickoff and a sold-out crowd fills the stadium for a 7:30 p.m. ET kickoff. Wade Pettus, a poultry-science grad who sketches Mississippi State plays on feed-store receipts, says Fluff Bothwell, Anthony Evans III and Parker Puckett can ring the cowbell in Austin, and Caro Whitfield, a former rodeo queen who prices every Longhorn like a blue-chip stock, says Arch Manning, Raleek Brown and Ryan Wingo will send the Bulldogs home quietly.

Caro: Welcome to Austin, darling. Hook 'em.

Wade: Thank you, ma'am. I'll ring once for you. Politely. (ding.)

Caro: Once is plenty. We have a concert going.

Longhorn City Limits runs a Halloween edition on the LBJ Lawn. The Tito's Tailgate and Bulleit Frontier Lounge open at 3 p.m. ET, Julianna Rankin takes the stage at 4 p.m. ET, and Uncle Kracker is scheduled for about 5:30 p.m. ET. Admission is free, and Pinkerton's BBQ is on site.

"Halloween night at DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium is going to be something special," athletics director Chris Del Conte said.

Wade: I'll be singing "Drift Away" while the Bulldogs do the opposite.

Caro: Darling, that song spent 28 weeks at No. 1 on the adult contemporary chart. Your Bulldogs have not.

There's some old history here. Texas leads the all-time series 4-2, and the first meeting was in Austin in 1921, a 54-7 Texas win. Mississippi State's last win in the series came in Austin, too, 28-10 in 1992. Texas has won the last three meetings, 38-11 in Dallas on Jan. 1, 1999, 35-13 in Austin in 2024 and 45-38 in overtime in Mississippi last season. That one had 83 combined points, the most in the series.

Wade: Thirty-four years since we won here. I've been saving receipts that long.

Caro: You've been saving receipts since the first Bush administration, darling.

One fun note from the Mississippi State notes: the Bulldogs are one of four SEC programs that have used the same five men up front on offense in every game this season, and Texas is one of the other three. Nobody on either front has needed a name tag all year.

Wade: Same five boys, every week. Like a church choir with shoulder pads.

Caro: Our five sing better.

Mississippi State is 4-3 and 2-3 in the SEC, and it lost 23-15 to Oklahoma at home last week. Puckett went 17 of 24 for 158 yards and a touchdown, Bothwell ran 17 times for 111 yards, and Sanfrisco Magee caught three balls for 65 yards and a touchdown. Evans leads the team with 53 catches for 647 yards and five touchdowns, and Bothwell has 514 rushing yards. On defense, Tyler Lockhart has 48 tackles and Jaray Bledsoe has 2.5 sacks. The Bulldogs opened 4-0, with wins over UL Monroe, at Minnesota, at South Carolina and over Missouri. The notes also credit tight end Sam West with a touchdown throw to Camden Capehart on a trick play against Oklahoma.

Wade: A tight end throwing touchdowns. That's my kind of receipt.

Caro: That's a one-time dividend, sugar.

Texas is 5-2 and 2-2 in the league, and it beat Ole Miss 34-27 last week. Brown ran 23 times for 157 yards, Ryan Wingo caught seven balls for 111 yards and a touchdown, and Manning threw for 212 yards. Manning has 1,507 passing yards, 12 touchdowns and seven interceptions on the season. Wingo has 33 catches for 456 yards and four touchdowns, Simmons has 10.5 sacks, and Jelani McDonald has 54 tackles. The Longhorns beat Ohio State 24-23 and won 20-17 at Tennessee, and they lost 27-24 to Oklahoma and Florida.

Caro: Ten and a half sacks. Darling, that is a premium asset.

Wade: My cowbell has a premium too. It's just louder.

Mississippi State said Tuesday that quarterback Kamario Taylor will miss the rest of the season with a foot injury that requires surgery, and the SEC's initial availability report, posted at 8:10 p.m. ET Wednesday, lists him out. It lists Colin Simmons questionable for Texas, and Arch Manning is not on it.

Wade: I'm coming as a cowbell. Big one. Painted maroon.

Caro: I'm coming as a rodeo queen, which is to say I'm coming as myself.

Wade: Whatever happens, Caro, dinner's on me, and you ring my cowbell on Speedway.

Caro: Then you learn the words to "The Eyes of Texas" and sing them nicely.

Wade: Deal. I'll write them on a receipt.

Caro: Keep the receipt, darling. Receipts are good business.

My call: Texas Longhorns 35, Mississippi State Bulldogs 19.
