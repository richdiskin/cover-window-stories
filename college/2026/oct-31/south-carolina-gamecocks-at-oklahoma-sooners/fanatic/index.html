South Carolina vs Oklahoma: Norman Moves Halloween to Friday

South Carolina Gamecocks (4-3) at Oklahoma Sooners (4-3)

4:15 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Memorial Stadium (Norman, OK) · SEC Network

XFacebook

South Carolina Gamecocks vs Oklahoma Sooners is a Week 9 SEC game in Norman, where the city asked families to go trick-or-treating on Friday, Oct. 30, because the Sooners are home on Halloween for the first time since 2009. Beaumont "Beau" Cheraw, a Gamecock superfan who times everything to dramatic music, says LaNorris Sellers, Matt Fuller and Dylan Stewart will make it three in a row, and Savannah Redbird-Lowe, a former Oklahoma rodeo princess who reads defenses like barrel patterns, says John Mateer, Isaiah Sategna III and Brent Venables' defense will close the gate.

Beau: (hums a space-movie fanfare) Savannah. I've arrived.

Savannah: Boomer, sugar. You hummed that in the parking lot, too.

Beau: Every entrance deserves a score.

Savannah: Then you'll love this one. Norman moved Halloween for us.

The City of Norman recommended trick-or-treating from 7 to 9 p.m. ET Friday, citing game-day traffic and road closures. The last time the Sooners played at home on Halloween, against Kansas State in 2009, the city made the same call. Not everyone is going along. Norman resident Michael Gamboa told the OU Daily his neighborhood will go out on Saturday anyway. "They're always ready for the 31st, not the 30th, not the 29th," he said.

Beau: I respect Mr. Gamboa. That's a man with his own soundtrack.

South Carolina is 4-3 and 2-3 in the SEC after back-to-back wins over ranked teams, 38-19 at Florida and 27-24 over Tennessee. Against the Vols, Sellers ran for 96 yards and two touchdowns, Stewart forced a fumble in the final minute, and Malik Orsan hit a 48-yard field goal as time expired. Sellers is 133 of 219 for 1,548 yards, 13 touchdowns and five interceptions. Fuller has 513 rushing yards and five touchdowns, Mazeo Bennett Jr. has 31 catches for 324 yards, and Justin Okoronkwo leads the defense with 53 tackles.

Beau: Walk-off field goal, Savannah. I had the music cued. Fans on the field.

Savannah: Oh, I saw. Very cinematic.

Shane Beamer isn't letting his team live in that moment. "We can't let that be the highlight of the season," he said.

Oklahoma is 4-3 and 2-2 in the league after a 23-15 win at Mississippi State. Mateer went 22 of 29 for 283 yards and two touchdowns, and Sategna had eight catches for 164 yards. Mateer is 136 of 204 for 1,591 yards, 11 touchdowns and seven interceptions for the season, and Sategna has 41 catches for 578 yards. Owen Heinecke leads the defense with 53 tackles, and Adepoju Adebawore has four sacks. The Sooners allow 17.7 points a game, and they beat Texas 27-24 earlier this month.

Savannah: That's a tidy barrel pattern, Beau. Seventeen a game.

Venables has seen what Stewart did to Tennessee. "He can wreck a game if you let him," he said.

Beamer, for his part, sounded impressed by the Sooners' defense. "It's a blur when you watch them on tape," he said.

Beau: A blur. That's what my music does to people.

Oklahoma won last year's meeting 26-7.

Savannah: Twenty-six to seven, sugar. I'll hum something for you.

Beau: Please don't. You hum like a gate hinge.

The SEC's initial availability report, posted at 8:10 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists five South Carolina players out and two doubtful, and eight Oklahoma players out, one doubtful and four questionable. LaNorris Sellers is not on it.

Beau: What's the plan after the game, Savannah? Candy?

Savannah: Saturday trick-or-treating, Gamboa style. You can carry the music.

Beau: I brought a speaker. It plays "Sandstorm" on loop.

Savannah: In Norman, we have the Pride of Oklahoma. They'll drown you out.

Beau: Let them try.

Savannah: Whatever happens, Beau, the first plate of fried okra is on me.

Beau: Deal. I'll provide the entrance music.

My call: Oklahoma Sooners 26, South Carolina Gamecocks 21.
