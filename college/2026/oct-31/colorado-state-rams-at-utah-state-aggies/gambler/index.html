Colorado State Rams (3-4) at Utah State Aggies (2-5)

Colorado State vs Utah State prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on Utah State Aggies −6.5 and Under 63.5 at DraftKings, with a final of Utah State Aggies 34, Colorado State Rams 27. It's Halloween afternoon at Maverik Stadium in Logan, where Utah State has asked the whole crowd to wear black.

The Aggies have the defense, and the Rams haven't found a road game they can win. Utah State allows 23.0 points a game and ranks first in the Pac-12 in total defense. Colorado State allows 44.0 points a game and gave up 41 or more in five of its seven games. Away from Fort Collins, it lost 59-45 at UTSA and 66-27 at Texas State. Utah State won five straight in this series from 2018 through 2023 and has won five of the past six in Logan.

Hyrum Tolleson hands out cups of Aggie Ice Cream in the Maverik Stadium lot. "Asher Cunningham made 17 tackles and forced two fumbles against Washington State, and we came back from 16-3. Eli Wood has 37 catches and five touchdowns. Have a double scoop, neighbor."

Ansel Rimrock, still in trail shoes, takes a single. "Hauss Hejny just threw for 333 yards and ran for two scores against San Diego State, and Tre'Shawn Moore took an interception back for a touchdown. We put up 56, Hyrum."

My concern is the Utah State offense. The Aggies score 14.9 points a game, Grady Brosterhous has six interceptions, and they scored 17 or fewer in five games, including a 20-17 loss at Texas State last week. If Hejny's offense plays like it did against San Diego State, a touchdown margin won't hold.

The total is about Utah State's games. None of the Aggies' seven games reached 56 combined points, and five finished at 37 or fewer. Colorado State's games run high, including those 59-45 and 66-27 road losses, but Utah State hasn't scored more than 21 in any game this season. Action Network's consensus line opened at Utah State −6.5 and 63.5 this week, and neither number has moved. My 34-27 adds up to 61.
