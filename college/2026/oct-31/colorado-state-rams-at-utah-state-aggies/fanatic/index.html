Colorado State vs Utah State: Wear Black on Halloween in Logan

Colorado State Rams (3-4) at Utah State Aggies (2-5)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Maverik Stadium · USA Net

Colorado State Rams vs Utah State Aggies is a Week 9 Halloween afternoon game at Maverik Stadium, where Utah State has asked everyone to wear black and Logan's downtown shops spend Friday handing out treats to kids in costume. Ansel Rimrock, a Fort Collins trail runner who measures every trip in foothill miles, says Jim Mora's Rams have Hauss Hejny back, and Hyrum Tolleson, a Cache Valley dairyman who has been up before the sun since he was 8, says Bronco Mendenhall's Aggies, Asher Cunningham and Eli Wood will make the Rams earn every yard.

Hyrum: Welcome to Logan, neighbor. Double scoop of Aggie Ice Cream?

Ansel: It's Halloween, Hyrum. And I ran here. Technically, I ran the last two miles.

Hyrum: Then you've earned the double. Black spoon, to match the dress code.

This is the 81st meeting of an old mountain rivalry. The series began in 1902 and has run through six conferences, from the Rocky Mountain Athletic Conference to the rebuilt Pac-12. Colorado State's game notes have the Rams ahead 40-38-2. Utah State won five straight from 2018 through 2023, and Colorado State won the last one, 42-37 in Fort Collins in 2024, after a 29-point fourth-quarter comeback.

Ansel: Twenty-nine points in one quarter, Hyrum. That's a sunrise summit.

Hyrum: I remember. I milked the cows in silence the next morning. The cows understood.

Colorado State is 3-4 and coming off a 56-49 win over San Diego State. Hejny, back at quarterback for the first time since Sept. 19 after a hamstring injury, threw for 333 yards and three touchdowns and ran for two more. The Pac-12 named him its offensive player of the week, and safety Tre'Shawn Moore was the defensive player of the week after returning an interception 38 yards for a touchdown.

Hejny was honest about the nerves. "Nerves are good," he said, in quotes reported by Nathan Wright of the Loveland Reporter-Herald. "I like when I have nerves because it means it matters to me and it's a big game and I'm excited to play."

Ansel: Nerves are just your legs telling you the climb is real.

For the season, Hejny is 71 of 104 for 952 yards, 13 touchdowns and two interceptions. Mel Brown has 454 rushing yards on just 52 carries, RJ Vick has 24 catches for 367 yards, and Oumar Diomande has 67 tackles. Durell Robinson ran for two scores against the Aztecs.

Utah State is 2-5 and lost 20-17 at Texas State last week. Grady Brosterhous is 99 of 178 for 1,120 yards, seven touchdowns and six interceptions. Javen Jacobs has 435 rushing yards, and Wood has 37 catches for 479 yards and five touchdowns. He's on the Biletnikoff Award watch list. Brevin Hamblin has 58 tackles, and Jordan Pendleton has 2.5 sacks.

Hyrum: Eli Wood catches everything and says thank you after. That's how his mother raised him.

The Aggies' defense is the reason this game can be close. Utah State allows 344.0 yards a game, first in the Pac-12. Cunningham was the Walter Camp national defensive player of the week after the 17-16 win over Washington State on Oct. 9, with 17 tackles and two forced fumbles. Both fumbles came in the second half and led to touchdowns as Utah State came back from 16-3 down at halftime.

Hyrum: Seventeen tackles. That's a full barn before breakfast.

Ansel: Our offense runs like the Horsetooth trail. Uphill, downhill, no stopping.

Colorado State has scored 38.6 points a game and allowed 44.0. Utah State has scored 14.9 and allowed 23.0. The Rams lost 59-45 at UTSA and 66-27 at Texas State, and they're still looking for a road win.

Hyrum: You're 0-2 away from home, Ansel. Bring a map.

Ansel: I've got a map. I've also got Hejny.

The Pac-12's advance availability report, updated at 10:02 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists three Colorado State players out and Mel Brown questionable, and Hejny is not on it. Utah State lists eight players out, four for the season, with Eli Wood questionable.

Mora was hired at Colorado State on Nov. 26, 2025. Mendenhall has led Utah State since his hire on Dec. 6, 2024. KSL's Brian Preece put it plainly in Logan this week: the Aggies have five games left and need four wins to reach a bowl.

Hyrum: Four of five. On a dairy, that's called a Tuesday.

Colorado State has played on Halloween before, and its notes say it beat Utah State both times the two met on Oct. 31.

Ansel: History is on the trail with me.

Hyrum: History didn't have to eat lunch in Cache Valley.

After the game, Logan throws a free Halloween Silent Disco at Laub Plaza from 9 p.m. to midnight ET.

Ansel: Headphones on, Hyrum. You can dance to whatever you want, and I won't have to hear it.

Hyrum: Polka. It's always polka. Come by the dairy in the morning, and I'll put you to work.

Ansel: Deal. Bring the spoons.

My call: Utah State Aggies 34, Colorado State Rams 27.
