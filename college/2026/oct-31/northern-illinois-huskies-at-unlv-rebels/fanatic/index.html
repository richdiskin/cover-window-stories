Halloween Night on the Strip: Northern Illinois Visits UNLV

Northern Illinois Huskies (0-7) at UNLV Rebels (4-3)

10:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Allegiant Stadium · CBSSN

Northern Illinois (0-7, 0-3 Mountain West) at UNLV (4-3, 2-1 Mountain West). Saturday, Oct. 31, 10:30 p.m. ET (7:30 p.m. Pacific), Allegiant Stadium, Las Vegas, CBSSN.

Your hosts are Nola Brandvik, a Northern Illinois agricultural scientist from DeKalb who thinks about the Huskies like a corn crop, and Vegas Monroe-Kalani, a former headliner dancer who calls every UNLV game a show and rates the opening act.

Vegas: The show starts Saturday night. Welcome to Las Vegas, Nola.

Nola: Thank you. I'm calm. Very calm. I brought a sweater. It's October.

Players to watch

Northern Illinois quarterback Taron Dickens is 138 of 210 for 1,364 yards, six touchdowns and five interceptions. Running back Telly Johnson Jr. has 95 carries for 502 yards and four touchdowns. Wide receiver DeAree Rogers leads the team with 57 catches for 537 yards and four touchdowns. On defense, Jack Murray has 49 tackles and Avery Jones has two sacks.

UNLV quarterback Jackson Arnold is 108 of 182 for 1,386 yards, 10 touchdowns and two interceptions. The school says he leads the league with 16 total touchdowns, 10 throwing and six running. Running back Jai'Den Thomas has 112 carries for 543 yards and three touchdowns. Wide receiver Kayden McGee has 16 catches for 330 yards and four touchdowns. Safety Jake Pope leads the defense with 63 tackles, Tre Fulton has 3.5 sacks and Mumu Bin-Wahad has three interceptions.

What happened last week

Northern Illinois lost 56-21 at home to Hawai'i on Oct. 24. The game was tied at 14 in the second quarter before Hawai'i scored 35 points in a row. Rogers caught eight balls for 115 yards and two touchdowns, of 49 and 25 yards. Dickens threw for 171 yards, and Johnson ran for 63.

UNLV had an open date on Oct. 24, its second bye of the season. The Rebels' last game was a 41-31 loss at Air Force on Oct. 17. McGee caught five balls for 99 yards and two touchdowns that day, and Arnold ran for a score.

Nola: After the Hawai'i game I stared at my cornfield photo for 30 seconds. It helped.

Injuries and availability

The official Mountain West availability report hadn't been posted as of Tuesday and usually comes out on game day. Unofficial notes from mid-October list UNLV receiver DeAngelo Irvin Jr. as out. McGee was listed as questionable that week but played at Air Force. Dickens was listed as questionable before the Hawai'i game and played.

What the coaches are up to

Rob Harley was the Huskies' defensive coordinator until he was promoted to interim head coach when Thomas Hammock resigned on Feb. 18 to return to the NFL. After the Hawai'i game, Harley said, "We have to find a way, and it starts with me first, to find a way to increase the consistency with the base stuff that we do."

Dan Mullen is in his second season at UNLV after being hired on Dec. 12, 2024. The Rebels are 7-2 at home under Mullen. This week he talked about tackling and getting healthy after the break.

Player storylines

Rogers is on a roll for the Huskies. Arnold, an Auburn transfer, didn't throw his first interception of the season until the North Dakota State game on Oct. 10. That came after 142 straight throws without one.

What's happening on campus

UNLV is throwing a Halloween party at Allegiant Stadium. The Rebel Village Fan Zone in Lot C is free, and a band called The Lucky Devils plays before the game.

Halloween is also Nevada Day, the day Nevada became the 36th state in 1864. UNLV last played on Halloween in 2020, when it hosted Nevada in the first game ever played at Allegiant Stadium.

Northern Illinois is in its first season in the Mountain West as a football-only member, so this trip is all new.

Anything else fun and true

The Huskies have won all five meetings with UNLV, the latest 38-30 in 2015. UNLV is still looking for its first win in the series.

UNLV says it has already played in five different time zones this season and is the only school in the top level of college football scheduled to do that in 2026. The Rebels are 2-7 all-time on Halloween, and their last Halloween win was 35-31 over San Jose State in 1992.

Vegas: Saturday night, under the lights, on Halloween. That's my kind of opening act.

Nola: Knee-high by the Fourth of July. Or by Halloween. I'll take either.

My call: UNLV Rebels 35, Northern Illinois Huskies 15.
