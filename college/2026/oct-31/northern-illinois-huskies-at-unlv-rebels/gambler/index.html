Northern Illinois Huskies at UNLV Rebels

Northern Illinois Huskies (0-7) at UNLV Rebels (4-3)

10:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Allegiant Stadium · CBSSN

Nola Brandvik spent 30 seconds staring at a photo of a cornfield after the Hawai'i game, and she says it helped. Now she's flying to Las Vegas on Halloween, where Vegas Monroe-Kalani has already rated the opening act and promised a show.

Northern Illinois (0-7) at UNLV (4-3), Saturday at 10:30 p.m. ET on CBSSN at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas. It's a Mountain West game. DraftKings has UNLV Rebels −23.5 with a total of 53.5. My card: Northern Illinois Huskies +23.5, Under 53.5, and a final of UNLV Rebels 35, Northern Illinois Huskies 15.

Nola speaks for Northern Illinois. "I'm calm. Very calm. We were tied with Hawai'i in the second quarter, and then the crop got hailed on. DeAree Rogers caught eight balls for 115 yards and two touchdowns. Telly Johnson Jr. has 502 rushing yards. The roots are still in the ground."

Vegas speaks for UNLV. "And that's the show, folks. Jackson Arnold has 10 touchdown passes and two interceptions, and he runs it too. We held Hawai'i to six and North Dakota State to seven. But I'll be honest, our last act was Air Force, and that one got a bad review, 41-31."

Here is the part that matters. UNLV scores 24.1 points a game. That is not a team built to win by four touchdowns. The Rebels' four wins were 21-6, 38-10, 39-31 and 13-7. To cover 23.5, UNLV has to do something it has done once all season.

Why Northern Illinois? Because the Huskies have stayed within this number in five of seven games. They lost 24-28 to Illinois State, 7-28 to Air Force and 23-41 at Wyoming. Action Network's opening number was UNLV −24.5, and it has come down a point. Both The Formula and Next Man Up like the Huskies. My final has UNLV by twenty.

The worry is the Northern Illinois defense. It allows 38.6 points a game, and Hawai'i scored 56 last week. Taron Dickens threw two interceptions. If UNLV gets the running game going with Jai'Den Thomas and Arnold, this could be the one blowout.

Why the Over/Under? UNLV plays low-scoring games. Its seven games ended 21-27, 21-6, 6-44, 38-10, 39-31, 13-7 and 31-41. Northern Illinois scores 15.1 points a game. UNLV runs the ball and uses the clock. Action Network's opening total was 52.5 and it has moved up to 53.5, but I want the Under. My 35-15 adds up to 50, three and a half under the over/under 53.5.

Now the room. The Formula has UNLV 35-16 and likes Northern Illinois and the Under. Next Man Up has UNLV 35-14 and likes Northern Illinois and the Under. Follow The Money likes Northern Illinois after the move and likes the Over. In The Trenches sees the run gaps cancel and has no pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "Thirty-five to fourteen. The Huskies hang around."

The Formula: "Same result. Take the points."

Bottom of the card: a winless road team gets more than three touchdowns against a home team that rarely blows anybody out. Vegas wants a show. Nola just wants a harvest.

UNLV Rebels 35, Northern Illinois Huskies 15. Northern Illinois Huskies cover the 23.5. Under 53.5.
