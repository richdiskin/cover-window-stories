Notre Dame Fighting Irish at Navy Midshipmen

Notre Dame Fighting Irish (6-1) at Navy Midshipmen (2-5)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Gillette Stadium · ABC

Notre Dame vs Navy prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on Navy Midshipmen +30.5 and Over 56.5 at DraftKings, with a final of Notre Dame Fighting Irish 43, Navy Midshipmen 15. The series reaches New England for the first time at noon Saturday, with Navy as the home team at Gillette Stadium and the Brigade marching onto the field before kickoff.

Thirty points is a lot to lay against an option team. Nobody has blown out Navy this season. Its losses came by eight at Florida Atlantic, four at UAB, five at Air Force, four at UTSA and ten to North Texas. Navy runs for 5.7 yards a carry, and long drives mean fewer possessions for CJ Carr. My final has Notre Dame by 28.

Declan Moriarty-Chen rides the early train down from South Station in his sweater vest. "Proposition one, Commander: Carr has 16 touchdown throws and one interception. Proposition two: we score 41.1 points a game and allow 13.4. Proposition three: we beat Stanford 58-7."

Cmdr. Annabel Merriweather, retired, stands by the lighthouse in the north end zone. "Young man, Jackson Gutierrez ran 26 times for 228 yards and three touchdowns against North Texas. He has 869 rushing yards and 12 rushing scores. Coleman Cauley has 58 tackles. Anchors aweigh."

My concern is history. Notre Dame beat Navy 51-14 in 2024 and 49-10 last season, and both margins would clear 30.5. The Irish allow 2.4 yards a carry and have 16 takeaways. If Carr gets short fields off Navy turnovers, this gets out of hand.

The total is about Navy's defense. Five of Navy's seven games have finished above 56.5 combined points, including 49-39 last week and 38-34 at UTSA. Navy allows 29.1 points a game. Notre Dame scores 41.1 and has scored 49 or more three times. Action Network's opening listing had 53.5, DraftKings had 52.5 on Monday morning, and it is 56.5 now. My 43-15 adds up to 58.

On the side, Action Network's opening listing had Notre Dame −28.5. DraftKings had −30.5 on Monday, and it is still −30.5.

The room splits on the side. The Formula has Notre Dame 43-14 and is on Navy +30.5 and the Over without backing either. Next Man Up has Notre Dame 42-15, likes Navy +30.5, and is on the Over without backing it. Follow The Money likes Notre Dame −30.5 and the Over, and it keeps no score of its own. In The Trenches has no pick, and Letdown Watch has no score this week.

An option team that hasn't lost by more than ten, a Notre Dame offense that scores in bunches and a total the whole room is on. Give me the Midshipmen and the Over.

Notre Dame Fighting Irish 43, Navy Midshipmen 15. Navy Midshipmen cover the 30.5. Over 56.5.
