Notre Dame vs Navy: The Brigade Marches Into Foxborough

Notre Dame Fighting Irish (6-1) at Navy Midshipmen (2-5)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Gillette Stadium · ABC

Notre Dame Fighting Irish vs Navy Midshipmen is a Week 9 Halloween noon ET game at Gillette Stadium, where the Brigade of Midshipmen marches onto the field at about 11:40 a.m. ET and the 99-game series is played in New England for the first time. Declan Moriarty-Chen, a sweater-vested Notre Dame grad student, says CJ Carr and No. 5 Notre Dame will make Foxborough feel like South Bend, and Cmdr. (Ret.) Annabel Merriweather, a former surface warfare officer, says Jackson Gutierrez and the option will take the clock and the crowd.

Declan: I would posit, Commander, that this is the most respectful rivalry in the sport.

Commander: You would posit correctly. Now mind the lighthouse. It's in our end zone today.

That's a real detail. Gillette Stadium has a lighthouse in its north end zone, and for one Saturday it belongs to the Navy home team. The stadium's event page says Navy is the designated home team and that this is Notre Dame's first game at Gillette. Navy has been here before, for the 2023 Army-Navy Game.

Commander: We know the harbor, young man. You're the tourist.

Declan: A tourist with 11 national championships, Commander.

The day begins early. Parking opens at 8 a.m. ET, and so does the official Fan Fest outside the stadium. The gates open at 10 a.m. ET. The MBTA is running a special event train from South Station at 9:50 a.m. ET, with stops at Back Bay and Dedham Corporate Center, that reaches Foxboro Station at 10:50 a.m. ET. Then, at about 11:40 a.m. ET, the Brigade marches on in formation.

Declan: I'll be on the 9:50 with a thermos and a rosary.

Commander: I'll be at the 50 for the march. Nobody talks during the march. Not even you.

Notre Dame is 6-1 and had the week off. Its last game was a 28-24 loss at BYU on Oct. 17, when Carr threw for 261 yards and Jaden Greathouse caught eight balls for 125 yards. Carr has led the Irish in throws in all seven games and is 129 of 193 for 1,639 yards, 16 touchdowns and one interception. Jordan Faison leads the receivers with 34 catches, and Jonaz Walton leads the rushers with 426 yards. On defense, Drayk Bowen has 38 tackles and Boubacar Traore has 3.5 sacks.

Declan: One interception in seven games. I've lost more library books.

Navy is 2-5 and lost 49-39 to North Texas on Oct. 24. Gutierrez ran 26 times for 228 yards and three touchdowns. He has 869 rushing yards and 12 rushing touchdowns, and he's thrown for 692 yards and seven scores on just 64 attempts. Jordan Collins has turned six catches into 212 yards and three touchdowns. On defense, Coleman Cauley has 58 tackles and MarcAnthony Parker has four sacks.

Commander: Six catches, 212 yards. Collins is a submarine. You never see him until he surfaces.

The matchup is simple to state. Navy gains 5.7 yards a carry. Notre Dame allows 2.4. The Irish score 41.1 points a game and allow 13.4, and they've forced 16 turnovers while losing two. Navy allows 29.1 points a game.

Marcus Freeman was hired at Notre Dame on Dec. 3, 2021, and his teams went 9-4, 10-3, 14-2 and 10-2 over the last four seasons. Brian Newberry was hired at Navy on Dec. 19, 2022. Last season his Midshipmen went 11-2, won the Liberty Bowl 35-13 and won the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy for the second straight year.

Declan: Eleven wins and a trophy named for your boss. I concede the point.

The history runs deep. The series began in 1927, and the teams met 93 years in a row through 2019, the longest continuous intersectional rivalry in college football. Notre Dame has won eight of the last nine regular-season meetings, including 49-10 last year. Navy's lone win in that stretch was 28-27 in 2016.

Commander: Twenty-eight to twenty-seven. I still have the program framed in my den.

Declan: I've seen it. It's next to a framed photo of the Naval Academy chapel.

It's Halloween, too, so expect leprechauns in the parking lots and at least one admiral in a pumpkin costume by the Tom Brady statue at Patriot Place.

Commander: I'm going as a lighthouse. Practical and festive.

Declan: Commander, whatever happens, I'd be honored to say grace with you after the game.

Commander: The honor is mine, young man. Anchors aweigh.

My call: Notre Dame Fighting Irish 43, Navy Midshipmen 15.
