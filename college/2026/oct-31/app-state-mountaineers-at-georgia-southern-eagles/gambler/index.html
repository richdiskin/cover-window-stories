App State Mountaineers at Georgia Southern Eagles

App State Mountaineers (5-2) at Georgia Southern Eagles (3-4)

6:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Allen E. Paulson Stadium · ESPN+

Zeb Coldiron whittles on a Watauga County porch and calls every drive a hike. Royce Ogeechee grew up in Bulloch County hearing his granddad tell stories about the old Eagles dynasty. On Halloween night in Statesboro, these old rivals meet for the forty-second time, and the East Division is on the table.

App State (5-2) at Georgia Southern (3-4), Saturday at 6:30 p.m. ET on ESPN+ at Allen E. Paulson Stadium in Statesboro. It's a Sun Belt game. DraftKings has Georgia Southern Eagles −5.5 with a total of 56.5. Action Network opened Georgia Southern −3.5 with a total of 54.5. My card: Georgia Southern Eagles −5.5, Under 56.5, and a final of Georgia Southern Eagles 29, App State Mountaineers 23.

Royce speaks for Georgia Southern. "Hail Southern! Max Johnson hurt his throwing shoulder at Old Dominion, and he's questionable. Clay Helton says the rehab progressed pretty fast, and Johnson walked out there and spun it. Turner Helton went 23 of 30 in relief, and we won 23-15. Anthony Bynum was Sun Belt Defensive Player of the Week. And we've had sixteen days to rest up."

Zeb speaks for App State. "Come on up the mountain. Malachi Singleton went 26 of 40 for 271 yards and two touchdowns in our overtime win over James Madison. Jaquari Lewis has 8 rushing touchdowns, and Caleb Sandstrom has 5 sacks. But steady footing matters. We scored three points against Old Dominion."

Here is the part that matters. Both teams are 2-1 in the league, tied for second in the East. Georgia Southern gives up 23.0 points a game, and App State gives up 23.1. Neither run game has found much room.

Why Georgia Southern? Follow The Money likes the Eagles after a two-point move, from −3.5 to −5.5. Next Man Up has Georgia Southern 30-22. Clay Helton is four and one against App State, and the Eagles lead the series in Statesboro thirteen to six. They're also unbeaten on Halloween.

The worry is the quarterback. If Johnson's shoulder isn't right, Georgia Southern turns to a backup. The Formula has only a four-point game, 27-23. App State won two of its three road games.

Why the Over/Under? The Formula has 50 for the total, and Next Man Up has 52. Both like the Under. Follow The Money likes the Over after the total climbed from 54.5. In The Trenches has no call: App State runs for 4.0 yards a carry against a defense that allows 3.5, and Georgia Southern gets 4.3 against an App State defense that allows 3.6. My 29-23 adds up to 52, four and a half under the over/under 56.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Georgia Southern 27-23 and likes the Under. Next Man Up has Georgia Southern 30-22 and likes the Under. Follow The Money likes Georgia Southern and the Over. In The Trenches has no score this week. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

The Formula: "Fifty points of football. The total is too high."

Next Man Up: "No official report, so Johnson gets no adjustment."

Follow The Money: "Two points on the side, two on the total. The market is loud."

Bottom of the card: Johnson's shoulder and Bynum's motor.

Georgia Southern Eagles 29, App State Mountaineers 23. Georgia Southern Eagles cover the 5.5. Under 56.5.
