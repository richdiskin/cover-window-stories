Halloween Rivalry Night: App State Visits Georgia Southern

App State Mountaineers (5-2) at Georgia Southern Eagles (3-4)

6:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Allen E. Paulson Stadium · ESPN+

App State (5-2, 2-1 Sun Belt) at Georgia Southern (3-4, 2-1 Sun Belt). Saturday, Oct. 31, 6:30 p.m. ET, Allen E. Paulson Stadium, Statesboro, Georgia, ESPN+.

Your hosts are Zeb Coldiron, a fourth-generation Watauga County whittler who treats every week like a hike, and Royce Ogeechee, a Bulloch County farm kid who grew up hearing about the Eagles' dynasty years from his granddad.

Zeb: Come on down off the mountain, Royce. I brought a jacket for your grandma.

Royce: Hail Southern, Zeb! She says thank you, and she says the Eagles are 4-0 on Halloween.

Players to watch

App State quarterback Malachi Singleton has completed 139 of 209 throws for 1,700 yards, with nine touchdowns and just two interceptions. Running back Jaquari Lewis has 420 yards and eight touchdowns, and wide receiver Sam Pickett III has 32 catches for 474 yards. Caleb Sandstrom has five sacks, and linebacker Colton Phares leads with 51 tackles.

Georgia Southern quarterback Max Johnson has thrown for 1,601 yards, with 11 touchdowns and only two interceptions. Wide receiver Alex Taylor has 24 catches for 342 yards. Linebacker Anthony Bynum has four sacks, and linebacker Brandon Tyson has 38 tackles.

Zeb: Two interceptions each. Those are careful young men.

What happened last week

App State beat James Madison 34-31 in overtime. Singleton went 26 of 40 for 271 yards and two touchdowns, and Nate Branch caught six balls for 73 yards.

Georgia Southern had a bye. In its last game, on Oct. 15, the Eagles won 23-15 at Old Dominion. Turner Helton came in for Johnson and went 23 of 30 for 206 yards and a touchdown. Bynum had three tackles for loss, two sacks and a forced fumble, and he was named Sun Belt Defensive Player of the Week.

Royce: That's a farm-strong day's work from Anthony.

Injuries and availability

No official Sun Belt availability report had been posted as of Tuesday. Johnson hurt his throwing shoulder at Old Dominion and is listed as questionable. "It's really progressed pretty fast, to be honest with you," Clay Helton said. "He walked out there and spun it." For App State, Singleton played through an ankle issue against James Madison.

Zeb: Praying both quarterbacks are healthy. A rivalry deserves its best.

What the coaches are up to

Clay Helton, who led USC to a Rose Bowl title, is 4-1 against App State. His son, Turner, is the backup quarterback who won the Old Dominion game. Georgia Southern has had 16 days to rest and get healthy.

Dowell Loggains is in his second season in Boone. He came from South Carolina, where he was offensive coordinator, and before that he called plays in the NFL for the Titans, Bears, Dolphins and Jets.

Player storylines

Here's a fun one. Johnson's father, Brad, and App State backup Henry Hasselbeck's father, Matt, both played in the NFL, and they played in two games against each other, in 2004 and 2006.

Georgia Southern lineman Colin Alexander said a win over App State would make the team "really, really confident" about reaching the conference championship.

Royce: That's the goal, Zeb. A conference title game.

What's happening on campus

Allen E. Paulson Stadium opened in 1984 and holds 25,000. Ask anybody about Beautiful Eagle Creek, and the Southern Pride Marching Band will be in full voice.

App State won three straight FCS national championships from 2005 to 2007. Home in Boone is Kidd Brewer Stadium, named for the coach whose 1937 team outscored its opponents 206-0 in the regular season. Armanti Edwards won the Walter Payton Award back to back, in 2008 and 2009, the first player to do it.

Zeb: Three straight titles, Royce. That's a mountain worth the climb.

Anything else fun and true

This is the 42nd meeting. App State leads the series 21-19-1, but Georgia Southern leads 13-6 in Statesboro. The teams have played every year since 1993, and these two programs are tied for second in the East Division.

Zeb: Steady climbing, honest footing, Royce. Then a view at the top.

Royce: Hail Southern, Zeb. The creek is calling.

My call: Georgia Southern Eagles 29, App State Mountaineers 23.
