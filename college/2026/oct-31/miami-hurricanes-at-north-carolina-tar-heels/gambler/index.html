Miami Hurricanes at North Carolina Tar Heels

Miami Hurricanes (7-0) at North Carolina Tar Heels (4-3)

7:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Kenan Stadium · ABC

Javi "The Breeze" Castillo is scouting Chapel Hill in linen and sunglasses. Ainsley Pemberton, former debutante and kinesiology PhD, has already offered him a sweater. The No. 1 team in the country comes to Kenan Stadium on Halloween night.

Miami (7-0) at North Carolina (4-3), Saturday at 7:30 p.m. ET on ABC at Kenan Stadium in Chapel Hill. It's an ACC game. DraftKings has Miami Hurricanes −14.5 with a total of 52.5. Action Network opened Miami −14.5 with a total of 50.5. My card: Miami Hurricanes −14.5, Over 52.5, and a final of Miami Hurricanes 35, North Carolina Tar Heels 20.

Javi speaks for Miami. "It's all about the U, baby! Darian Mensah has 22 touchdown passes and one interception. Malachi Toney caught 16 balls for 194 yards and three scores against Pitt. Even Bill Belichick says he can't find a weak point in Toney's game. Number one in the sun, Canes get it done."

Ainsley speaks for North Carolina. "Darling, we lost 30-31 to Syracuse in overtime, and it stung. Billy Edwards Jr. opened that game with eleven straight completions. Xavier Lewis has 7 sacks and Derek McDonald has 71 tackles. We won at Pitt and at Duke. We know how to keep a game close."

Here is the part that matters. Miami scores 49.0 points a game and has not been held under 33. North Carolina scores 24.4. The Tar Heels kicked three field goals against Syracuse, and Jordan Shipp said they have to turn those into touchdowns. Against this defense, field goals won't keep pace.

Why Miami? The Formula and Next Man Up both have Miami 35-20, a 15-point final. That's only a half point past the spread, so neither card gets excited, and Follow The Money sees no move on the side. I'll take the Canes anyway. Miami won at Clemson 41-13, where Carolina lost 20-28, and Mensah has thrown one interception all year.

The worry is the Belichick factor. Carolina allows only 20.9 a game, and Belichick will build a plan for Toney. In The Trenches shows a Carolina run game at 3.9 yards a carry against a Miami defense that allows 2.4, but Miami runs for 5.6 against a defense that allows 3.9. Those cancel out. A slow, careful game could land the Heels inside two scores.

Why the Over/Under? The Formula has 55 and Next Man Up has 55. Follow The Money likes the Over after the total climbed from 50.5 to 52.5. Miami scored 52 against Pitt and 43 against Florida State. My 35-20 adds up to 55, two and a half over the over/under 52.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Miami 35-20 and no strong feeling on either side or total. Next Man Up has Miami 35-20 and likes the Over. Follow The Money has no side and likes the Over. In The Trenches has nothing this week. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "No official report. Mensah and Toney are the whole show."

Follow The Money: "Two points up on the total. I go with the move."

In The Trenches: "Both run games point in different directions."

Bottom of the card: Miami pulls away late. Javi offers Ainsley a cafecito. She offers him the sweater again.

Miami Hurricanes 35, North Carolina Tar Heels 20. Miami Hurricanes cover the 14.5. Over 52.5.
