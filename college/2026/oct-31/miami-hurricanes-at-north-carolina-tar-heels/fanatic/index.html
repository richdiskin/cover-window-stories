Miami vs North Carolina: Neon Trees and No. 1 in Chapel Hill

Miami Hurricanes (7-0) at North Carolina Tar Heels (4-3)

7:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Kenan Stadium · ABC

Miami Hurricanes vs North Carolina Tar Heels is a Week 9 Halloween night at Kenan Stadium, where Neon Trees plays Chapel Thrill Game Day on Polk Place and Carolina hosts the nation's No. 1 team for the sixth time ever. Javi "The Breeze" Castillo, a former Miami defensive back who scouts in a white suit and sunglasses, says Darian Mensah, Malachi Toney and Javian Mallory keep the Canes rolling, and Ainsley Pemberton, a North Carolina fan, former debutante and kinesiology PhD, says Billy Edwards Jr., Jordan Shipp and Xavier Lewis can make it a long night for the visitors.

Javi: It's all about the U, baby! Number one in the sun, Canes get it done!

Ainsley: Welcome to Chapel Hill, darling. Do you need a sweater?

Javi: It's Halloween, Ainsley. I'm dressed as a guy who doesn't need a sweater.

Chapel Thrill opens three hours before kickoff in front of Wilson Library, and about two and a half hours before kickoff the Tar Heels take the Old Well Walk through Polk Place and around the Bell Tower to Kenan. Neon Trees broke out in 2010 with "Animal," which reached No. 1 on the Billboard Alternative charts. It's also Rams Club Day, and this is Carolina's 100th season in Kenan Stadium, which opened in 1927 and seats 50,500.

Ainsley: I'll be singing "Animal" on Polk Place, darling. With manners.

Javi: I'll be singing it too. About Toney.

The last No. 1 team to visit Kenan was Clemson in 2019. The recent history here belongs to Carolina, which has won the last five meetings with Miami, including three at Kenan. The 2023 game was a 41-31 Tar Heels win, and the 2021 game was 45-42. Carolina won 62-26 in 2020.

Javi: Five straight. Ainsley, I'm not here to talk about history. I'm here to make some.

Ainsley: Bless your heart, darling. History is very patient.

Miami is 7-0 and 5-0 in the ACC and has scored at least 33 points in every game, including 77 against Florida A&M. Mensah has completed 166 of 204 throws for 2,151 yards, 22 touchdowns and one interception. Toney has 66 catches for 952 yards and 11 touchdowns, Mallory has 444 yards and seven touchdowns on 59 carries, Marquise Lightfoot has five sacks and Mohamed Toure has 35 tackles. Last week the Canes beat Pittsburgh 52-23, with Mensah going 31 of 37 for 387 yards and four touchdowns and Toney catching 16 balls for 194 yards and three scores. They also won 41-13 at Clemson and 43-35 in overtime against Florida State.

Bill Belichick spent Monday praising the other team's receiver. "At his age, that's pretty impressive. I don't see any weak points in his game," he said of Toney.

Javi: When Coach Belichick says that, you frame it, hermano.

North Carolina is 4-3 and 2-2 in the league. Edwards has thrown for 1,531 yards, 11 touchdowns and five interceptions, Shipp has 41 catches for 543 yards and Benjamin Hall has 283 rushing yards. Lewis has seven sacks and Derek McDonald has 71 tackles. Carolina lost 31-30 in overtime to Syracuse last week, when Edwards opened with 11 straight completions and McDonald, facing his old school, made a career-high 16 tackles. The Tar Heels won at Pittsburgh, 21-17, and at Duke, 24-20.

Shipp knows what has to change. "We need to turn those field goals into touchdowns," he said.

Ainsley: Sixteen tackles against his old school, darling. That's good manners and good football.

Javi: Good manners. I'll put that on a T-shirt and sell it in Coral Gables.

The ACC's initial availability report, posted at 8 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists six Miami players out, and five North Carolina players out and five questionable.

Javi: I'm coming as a sunbeam. Orange and green.

Ainsley: I'm coming as Carolina blue, darling. It's not a costume. It's a lifestyle.

Javi: Whatever happens, Ainsley, the cafecito is on me, and you say "It's all about the U."

Ainsley: Then you wear the sweater on Franklin Street.

Javi: Deal. But the sunglasses stay on.

My call: Miami Hurricanes 35, North Carolina Tar Heels 20.
