No. 1 Comes to Chapel Hill: Miami Visits North Carolina

Miami Hurricanes (7-0) at North Carolina Tar Heels (4-3)

7:30 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Kenan Stadium · ABC

No. 1 Miami (7-0, 5-0 ACC) at North Carolina (4-3, 2-2 ACC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 7:30 p.m. ET, Kenan Stadium, Chapel Hill, North Carolina, ABC.

Your hosts are Javi "The Breeze" Castillo, a former Miami defensive back who scouts in linen and sunglasses, and Ainsley Pemberton, a North Carolina fan, former debutante and kinesiology PhD.

Javi: It's all about the U, baby! Number one in the sun, Canes get it done!

Ainsley: Welcome to Chapel Hill, darling. Do you need a sweater?

Players to watch

Miami quarterback Darian Mensah has completed 166 of 204 throws for 2,151 yards, 22 touchdowns and just one interception. Wide receiver Malachi Toney has 66 catches for 952 yards and 11 touchdowns. Running back Javian Mallory has 444 yards and seven touchdowns on only 59 carries. On defense, Marquise Lightfoot has five sacks, and linebacker Mohamed Toure has 35 tackles.

North Carolina quarterback Billy Edwards Jr. has thrown for 1,531 yards, 11 touchdowns and five interceptions. Wide receiver Jordan Shipp has 41 catches for 543 yards. Benjamin Hall leads the running backs with 283 yards. On defense, Xavier Lewis has seven sacks, and linebacker Derek McDonald has 71 tackles.

Javi: Toney in the end zone, mi amigo, that's a done zone!

What happened last week

Miami beat Pittsburgh 52-23. Mensah went 31 of 37 for 387 yards and four touchdowns, and Toney caught 16 balls for 194 yards and three scores. Mallory ran for two touchdowns.

North Carolina lost 31-30 to Syracuse in overtime at Kenan Stadium. Edwards opened the game with 11 straight completions and finished with season highs of 27 completions and 271 yards. Shipp had eight catches for 102 yards and a touchdown, and Demon June ran 25 yards for the team's longest rushing score of the year. McDonald, a Syracuse transfer facing his old team, made a career-high 16 tackles.

Ainsley: Sixteen tackles against his old school, darling. That's good manners and good football.

Injuries and availability

No official ACC availability report had been posted as of Tuesday. Mensah and Toney have no reported injury concerns.

What the coaches are up to

Mario Cristobal is in his fifth season at Miami. Last season the Canes reached the national championship game, losing 27-21 to Indiana on Jan. 19, 2026.

Bill Belichick is in his second season at North Carolina. After the Syracuse game he said, "They made a couple more plays than we did today, and that was the difference in the game." On Monday he talked about Toney. "He's good at everything," Belichick said. "He's even a good blocker." He added, "At his age, that's pretty impressive. I don't see any weak points in his game."

Javi: When Coach Belichick says that, you frame it, hermano.

Player storylines

Toney is going for his third straight game with more than 150 receiving yards. On3's Heisman poll has him third, right behind his own quarterback, Mensah.

For Carolina, Shipp said the Tar Heels moved the ball well but need to finish drives. "We need to turn those field goals into touchdowns," he said. Rece Verhoff made all three of his field goals against Syracuse, with a long of 39 yards. Tight end Jelani Thurman caught a career-best seven passes.

What's happening on campus

This is North Carolina's 100th season playing in Kenan Stadium, which opened in 1927 and seats 50,500. The Tar Heels have won five ACC titles and played in 37 bowl games. Saturday continues a homestand for Carolina.

Miami claims five national championships: 1983, 1987, 1989, 1991 and 2001. The Canes are No. 1 in both the AP and Coaches polls.

Anything else fun and true

Miami has scored at least 33 points in every game this season, including 77 against Florida A&M. The Canes won 43-35 in overtime against Florida State.

Carolina won close road games at Pittsburgh, 21-17, and at Duke, 24-20. The Tar Heels and Syracuse have now played nine times, and Carolina still leads that series 5-4.

Ainsley: Halloween in Chapel Hill, darling. Kenan is lovely by moonlight.

Javi: I'll bring the sunglasses anyway. Canes get it done.

My call: Miami Hurricanes 35, North Carolina Tar Heels 20.
