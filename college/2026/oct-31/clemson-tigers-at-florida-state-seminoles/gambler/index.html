Clemson Tigers at Florida State Seminoles

Clemson Tigers (4-3) at Florida State Seminoles (3-4)

7:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Doak Campbell Stadium · ACC Network

Gianna Ravello reads football coverage like legislation, and this week's bill is a big one. Dixie-Lynn Pruitt owns 43 orange items and is packing most of them for Tallahassee.

Clemson (4-3) at Florida State (3-4), Saturday at 7:00 p.m. ET on ACC Network at Doak Campbell Stadium in Tallahassee. It's an ACC game. DraftKings has Florida State Seminoles −6 with a total of 49.5. Action Network opened Clemson −1.5 with a total of 51.5. My card: Florida State Seminoles −6, Over 49.5, and a final of Florida State Seminoles 29, Clemson Tigers 21.

Gianna speaks for Florida State. "Motion to reconsider: denied! We lost 35-43 in overtime at Miami, the No. 1 team, and Ashton Daniels threw four touchdown passes. Duce Robinson caught two. The bad news is real. Ousmane Kromah's season ended with an injury at Miami, and Samuel Singleton Jr. won't play this week. The bill on the floor says Quintrevion Wisner carries the load."

Dixie-Lynn speaks for Clemson. "Orange you glad I said it? Bless us, sugar, we lost 16-20 to Virginia Tech at home. Tait Reynolds has thrown one touchdown all season. But Will Heldt has 6 sacks, and Christopher Vizzina is back from his elbow injury. Corey Myrick has 53 tackles. Our defense keeps us in games."

Here is the part that matters. Clemson scores 21.4 points a game. Reynolds threw for 153 yards against Virginia Tech and ran for a touchdown. Florida State scores 31.6 and put up 35 on Miami's defense. One offense can stretch the field with Daniels and Robinson. The other has trouble finishing drives.

Why Florida State? Next Man Up, Follow The Money and In The Trenches all back the Seminoles. Follow The Money sees a move of seven and a half points, from Clemson −1.5 to Florida State −6. Florida State has won three of four at home. Next Man Up has Florida State 30-19.

The worry is the run game. In The Trenches backs Florida State at 4.7 yards a carry against a Clemson defense that allows 3.7, but Kromah was the lead back. He had six straight 80-yard games to start the year. The Formula has only a five-point game, 28-23, and Florida State allows 27.9 a game. Clemson has also won eight of the last nine meetings.

Why the Over/Under? The Formula has 51 for the total, and Next Man Up has 49. Follow The Money likes the Under after the total fell from 51.5 to 49.5. I'll go the other way. Daniels has thrown 12 touchdown passes, and Florida State's defense gives up points. My 29-21 adds up to 50, a half point over the over/under 49.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Florida State 28-23 and no strong feeling either way. Next Man Up has Florida State 30-19 and likes the Seminoles. Follow The Money likes Florida State and the Under. In The Trenches likes Florida State. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Follow The Money: "Seven and a half points of movement. That's a loud vote."

Next Man Up: "No official report. Vizzina's status matters."

In The Trenches: "Kromah's absence makes me nervous."

Bottom of the card: Daniels to Robinson decides it.

Florida State Seminoles 29, Clemson Tigers 21. Florida State Seminoles cover the 6. Over 49.5.
