Halloween in Tallahassee: Clemson Visits Florida State

Clemson Tigers (4-3) at Florida State Seminoles (3-4)

7:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Doak Campbell Stadium · ACC Network

Clemson (4-3, 2-2 ACC) at Florida State (3-4, 1-3 ACC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 7:00 p.m. ET, Doak Campbell Stadium, Tallahassee, Florida, ACC Network.

Your hosts are Dixie-Lynn Pruitt, a Clemson fan and former Tiger Band flag captain who owns 43 orange items, and Gianna Ravello, a Florida State fan and capitol-lobbyist type who reads football coverage like legislation.

Dixie-Lynn: Orange you glad I came, Gianna? I brought my orange rain boots. Their name is Darlene.

Gianna: Motion to welcome Darlene: approved. Everybody else in orange, we'll debate it.

Players to watch

Clemson quarterback Tait Reynolds has completed 120 of 180 throws for 1,096 yards. Christopher Vizzina is back from an elbow injury. Running back Gideon Davidson has 492 yards and five touchdowns, and wide receiver Bryant Wesco Jr. has 34 catches for 378 yards. On defense, Will Heldt has six sacks, and Corey Myrick has 53 tackles.

Florida State quarterback Ashton Daniels has thrown for 1,618 yards and 12 touchdowns. Wide receiver Duce Robinson has 22 catches for 407 yards and four touchdowns. On defense, Rylan Kennedy has six sacks, and linebacker Chris Jones has 46 tackles.

Gianna: Four hundred seven yards on 22 catches. That's a bill that passes itself.

What happened last week

Clemson lost 20-16 to Virginia Tech at home. Reynolds went 20 of 26 for 153 yards and ran for 63 yards and a touchdown. Tyler Brown caught eight balls.

Florida State had a bye. In its last game, on Oct. 17, the Seminoles lost 43-35 in overtime at No. 1 Miami. Daniels threw four touchdowns, and Robinson caught five balls for 113 yards and two scores.

Dixie-Lynn: Taking Miami to overtime on the road? My heart, y'all. That's a good team.

Injuries and availability

No official ACC availability report had been posted as of Tuesday. Florida State running back Ousmane Kromah, the team's leading rusher with 609 yards, suffered a season-ending injury at Miami. Mike Norvell said running back Samuel Singleton Jr. is making progress but won't play this week. Linebacker Blake Nichelson is getting close to a return. For Clemson, Vizzina has come off the injury report after missing four games.

Gianna: Prayers for Ousmane. We're praying for a full recovery.

What the coaches are up to

Dabo Swinney has led Clemson since the middle of the 2008 season, and he won national championships in 2016 and 2018. He's the winningest head coach in Clemson and ACC history.

Mike Norvell is in his seventh season at Florida State. This week he has to rebuild the backfield. Quintrevion Wisner ran nine times for 63 yards at Miami.

Player storylines

Kromah began the season with six straight games of more than 80 rushing yards, matching a Florida State school record set by Warrick Dunn in 1995. Before his injury, he ranked third in the ACC in rushing yards per game.

For Clemson, kicker Nolan Hauser made a career-long 59-yard field goal against Charleston Southern. He also made the 56-yard walk-off kick that won the 2024 ACC title over SMU. If this one comes down to a kick, Dixie-Lynn will be twirling.

Dixie-Lynn: Fifty-nine yards! I named a scarf after that kick.

What's happening on campus

Doak Campbell Stadium was built in 1950 and seats 67,277. The field is named Bobby Bowden Field, and the Marching Chiefs will be in full voice. Florida State claims national titles from 1993, 1999 and 2013.

Clemson started playing football in 1896 and has won 27 conference championships. Its Memorial Stadium seats 81,500. The Tigers made six straight College Football Playoff appearances from 2015 to 2021.

Anything else fun and true

Florida State is 3-1 at home this season. Its best night came against Virginia, when the Seminoles scored the last 38 points to win 38-7 and held the Cavaliers to just 37 rushing yards.

Clemson has won eight of the nine meetings between these teams since 2016, including 29-13 in 2024 and 24-10 in 2025.

Gianna: The floor recognizes the history, and the floor would like a new chapter.

Dixie-Lynn: Bless you, sugar. Orange you glad it's a fun one?

My call: Florida State Seminoles 29, Clemson Tigers 21.
