Clemson vs Florida State: Drones, Dabo and a 130th Birthday

Clemson Tigers (4-3) at Florida State Seminoles (3-4)

7:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Doak Campbell Stadium · ACC Network

Clemson Tigers vs Florida State Seminoles is a Week 9 Halloween night at Doak Campbell Stadium, where Florida State debuts its first-ever drone show, and Clemson plays on the 130th anniversary of its first game, a 14-6 win over Furman on Oct. 31, 1896. Dixie-Lynn Pruitt, a former Tiger Band flag captain who owns 43 orange items, says Tait Reynolds, Gideon Davidson and Will Heldt bring the Tigers home happy, and Gianna Ravello, a Tallahassee lobbyist type who reads football news like legislation, says Ashton Daniels, Duce Robinson and Rylan Kennedy carry it on a voice vote.

Gianna: Dixie-Lynn, I've read the whole game day release. Three times. With a highlighter.

Dixie-Lynn: I've read none of it. I'm wearing all 43 orange items.

Gianna: All of them?

Dixie-Lynn: The socks count as two.

Florida State calls the day Operation Garnet Storm. Trunk or Treat opens on Langford Green at 3 p.m. ET, the game ball arrives by parachute with the USSOCOM Para-Commandos, and concession stands will have candy for fans in costume. Peter Warrick gets the National Football Foundation On-Campus Salute in the first quarter before his College Football Hall of Fame induction. At halftime, the Marching Chiefs lead into Garnet Hour, with the drone show and a synchronized light show.

"Operation Garnet Storm is a comprehensive campaign to provide our fans a one-of-a-kind experience in Doak Campbell Stadium," interim director of athletics Bruce Warwick said.

Gianna: Section one, subsection drone show. I've already filed my approval.

Dixie-Lynn: I'll be watching the drones for orange ones.

There's history on the Clemson side, too. Dabo Swinney is 116-30 in ACC regular-season play, and a win would give him 117 and tie Bobby Bowden's conference mark, on the field named for Bowden. Clemson has won its last four games in Tallahassee, including 29-13 in 2024, when Swinney passed Bowden for the most overall wins as an ACC head coach.

Gianna: Tie Bowden. At Bobby Bowden Field. That's bad manners, Dixie-Lynn.

Dixie-Lynn: That's a history lesson, sugar.

Clemson is 4-3 and 2-2 in the ACC, coming off a 20-16 home loss to Virginia Tech. Reynolds has completed 120 of 180 throws for 1,096 yards, and he ran for a touchdown against the Hokies. Davidson has 492 rushing yards and five touchdowns, Bryant Wesco Jr. has 34 catches for 378 yards, and Heldt has six sacks. Corey Myrick leads the Tigers with 53 tackles.

Dixie-Lynn: Heldt's going to visit your quarterback so often, he'll need a guest room.

Gianna: Noted for the record. Objection also noted.

Florida State is 3-4 and 1-3 in the league and had last week off after a 43-35 overtime loss at Miami. Daniels threw for 298 yards and four touchdowns in that game and has 1,618 yards, 12 touchdowns and eight interceptions on the year. Robinson caught five balls for 113 yards and two scores at Miami and has 407 receiving yards. Kennedy has six sacks, and Chris Jones has 46 tackles. Earlier this month, Florida State scored the final 38 points in a 38-7 win over Virginia.

Gianna: Thirty-eight unanswered. I'd like that entered into the minutes.

Dixie-Lynn: Enter this: you lost to SMU at home.

Gianna: Tabled.

The ACC's initial availability report, posted at 8 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists seven Clemson players out and one questionable, and nine Florida State players out, with quarterback Malachi Marshall probable.

Dixie-Lynn: I'm coming as a Tiger. I'm always coming as a Tiger.

Gianna: I'm coming as the drone show. I've got a light-up headband.

Dixie-Lynn: If the Seminoles win, I'll wear one garnet item. Small. Maybe a hair tie.

Gianna: If Clemson wins, I'll draft you a resolution honoring Furman, 1896.

Dixie-Lynn: Make it a joint resolution. Furman deserves it.

Gianna: Motion carried.

My call: Florida State Seminoles 29, Clemson Tigers 21.
