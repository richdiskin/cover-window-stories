Texas State Bobcats at Boise State Broncos

Texas State Bobcats (4-3) at Boise State Broncos (6-1)

10:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Albertsons Stadium · CW

Yesenia Calderwood floated into homecoming last week on a sun hat and a prayer, and her Bobcats escaped with a field goal. Now she is headed for the blue turf, where Thatcher Bluestem has painted his car, his pancakes and his opinions to match.

Texas State (4-3) at No. 20 Boise State (6-1), Saturday at 10:00 p.m. ET on CW at Albertsons Stadium in Boise. It's a Pac-12 game. DraftKings has Boise State Broncos −11.5 with a total of 64.5. My card: Boise State Broncos −11.5, Under 64.5, and a final of Boise State Broncos 38, Texas State Bobcats 26.

Yesenia speaks for Texas State. "Two weeks ago we beat Colorado State 66-27, and it felt like the whole river was rising. Then we beat Utah State 20-17, and our coach called the offense a bad dream. Brad Jackson has 14 touchdown passes, and Torrance Burgess Jr. has 889 rushing yards. Go with the flow, y'all."

Thatcher speaks for Boise State. "Paint it blue! Maddux Madsen still has zero interceptions. Seven games, friends. Dylan Riley has 963 rushing yards, and he ran for 128 and two touchdowns at Washington State. That's six straight wins,."

Here is the part that matters. Boise State does not give the ball away and does not give up the run. Its wins were 38-20 over Memphis, 32-7 at Western Michigan, 37-18 over Utah State and 31-10 at Washington State. The Broncos lost only at Oregon, 27-34.

Why Boise State? Texas State is a different team on the road. The Bobcats lost 7-59 at Texas and 29-31 at San Diego State. Their offense sputtered against Utah State. Madsen is 124 of 189 for 1,445 yards and 14 touchdowns, and Riley carries the load. The Formula and Next Man Up both have Boise State by a dozen or more.

The worry is the Texas State run game. In The Trenches has the Bobcats at 5.8 yards a carry, and Jackson can run too. If Burgess gets loose, a backdoor touchdown is always possible against a team that likes to sit on a lead. Follow The Money sees the market move toward Texas State.

Why the Over/Under? Boise State's games ended 27-34, 38-20, 38-24, 32-7, 37-18, 38-31 and 31-10. Only one of those went past this total. The Broncos control the ball and shorten the game. Action Network's opening total was 65.5, and it has come down to 64.5. My 38-26 adds up to 64, just under the over/under 64.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Boise State 37-25 and likes Boise State and the Under. Next Man Up has Boise State 39-26 and likes Boise State and the Over. Follow The Money likes Texas State and the Under. In The Trenches sees the run gaps cancel and has no pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

The Formula: "Thirty-seven to twenty-five. Broncos by twelve."

Next Man Up: "Riley and Madsen are healthy. That's enough."

Follow The Money: "The total came down a point. I'm with the Under."

Bottom of the card: Boise State keeps the ball, keeps it clean and wins at home. Thatcher will paint something else blue.

Boise State Broncos 38, Texas State Bobcats 26. Boise State Broncos cover the 11.5. Under 64.5.
