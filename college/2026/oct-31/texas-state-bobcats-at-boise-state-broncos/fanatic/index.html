Halloween on the Blue Turf: Texas State Visits Boise State

Texas State Bobcats (4-3) at Boise State Broncos (6-1)

10:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Albertsons Stadium · CW

Texas State (4-3, 2-1 Pac-12) at No. 20 Boise State (6-1, 3-0 Pac-12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 10:00 p.m. ET (8:00 p.m. Mountain), Albertsons Stadium, Boise, Idaho, CW.

Your hosts are Yesenia Calderwood, a Texas State fan who floats the San Marcos River with a sun hat and a radio, and Thatcher Bluestem, a Boise State fan whose car, pancakes and opinions are all blue.

Thatcher: Paint it blue! Welcome to Boise, Yesenia. Bring sunglasses. The field looks even bluer in person.

Yesenia: Eat 'em up, Cats! I brought a sun hat, Thatcher. It's going to be cold, isn't it?

Players to watch

Texas State quarterback Brad Jackson is 133 of 194 for 1,606 yards, 14 touchdowns and five interceptions, and he's a runner too. The school says he has 26 career rushing touchdowns. Running back Torrance Burgess Jr. has 118 carries for 889 yards and five touchdowns. Wide receiver Chris Dawn Jr. leads the team with 36 catches for 396 yards and four touchdowns. On defense, Jayden Bradley has 47 tackles and Tymere Jackson has 2.5 sacks.

Boise State quarterback Maddux Madsen is 124 of 189 for 1,445 yards and 14 touchdowns. The school says he has gone seven straight games without an interception, the first Bronco starter to do that since the team joined the top level of college football in 1996. Running back Dylan Riley has 140 carries for 963 yards and eight touchdowns. Freshman receiver Rasean Jones has 29 catches for 437 yards. Linebacker Boen Phelps leads the defense with 41 tackles, and linebacker Jayden Virgin-Morgan has three sacks.

Thatcher: Seven games, no interceptions. That's bluer than a cloudless October sky.

What happened last week

Texas State beat Utah State 20-17 at homecoming on Oct. 24. Jacob Bonilla kicked a 37-yard field goal with less than two minutes left, after making one from 51 earlier. Jaylen Jenkins returned a kickoff 100 yards for a touchdown, and Jackson ran for a score. A week earlier, the Bobcats set a school record with 716 yards in a 66-27 win over Colorado State.

Boise State won 31-10 at Washington State. Riley ran 13 times for 128 yards and two touchdowns, and Madsen threw a touchdown to Cam Bates. It was Boise State's first win ever in Pullman and its sixth win in a row.

Yesenia: A 100-yard kickoff return at homecoming. Now that's going with the flow.

Injuries and availability

The official Pac-12 availability report hadn't been posted as of Tuesday and usually comes out on game day. Unofficial notes don't list Madsen, Riley or Jackson. Texas State's Cole Snodgrass is listed as out.

What the coaches are up to

G.J. Kinne has 27 wins at Texas State, which ties him for second among Bobcat coaches at the Division I level. His teams are 17-7 at home. After the Utah State game he called the offense a bad dream, so expect him to want a better night.

Spencer Danielson won his 30th game at Boise State last week, the eighth Bronco head coach to get there. Boise State has started 3-0 in league play in each of his three full seasons. His teams love to run the ball and keep it away from the other side.

Player storylines

Madsen moved into fourth in Boise State history with 631 career completions and has 64 career touchdown throws. He's 17-1 as a starter in league games. For Texas State, Jackson and Burgess give the Bobcats two runners who can break a long one.

What's happening on campus

The blue turf at Albertsons Stadium is one of the most famous fields in the country, and the stadium seats 36,387. Boise State is now bowl-eligible for the 29th season in a row. The school says only Georgia has a longer active run.

Last week a Bronco player carried the Dan Paul Hammer onto the field in Pullman, a Boise State tradition.

Anything else fun and true

This is the first meeting between Texas State and Boise State. Both schools are new to the Pac-12 this season. The Bobcats come from the warm Hill Country, and the Broncos come from the foothills, so a Halloween night in Boise will be a change of scenery for Yesenia.

Texas State drew 18,587 fans for homecoming last week. Boise State has scored 30 or more points in six straight games for the first time since 2023.

Yesenia: First meeting, new conference, blue grass. I'm floating into the unknown, Thatcher.

Thatcher: And we'll paint it blue, Yesenia. Every inch.

My call: Boise State Broncos 38, Texas State Bobcats 26.
