Texas State vs Boise State: Halloween Black-Out on the Blue Turf

Texas State Bobcats (4-3) at Boise State Broncos (6-1)

10:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Albertsons Stadium · CW

Texas State Bobcats vs Boise State Broncos is a Week 9 Halloween night at Albertsons Stadium in Boise, the first The Blue After Dark game, with kickoff at 10 p.m. ET on CW and a Kellen Moore bronze statue unveiled on the blue turf at halftime. Yesenia Calderwood, a Texas State fan who floats the San Marcos River with a sun hat and a radio, says Brad Jackson, Torrance Burgess Jr. and Jacob Bonilla can steal one in the first meeting, and Thatcher Bluestem, a Boise State fan whose car, pancakes and opinions are all blue, says Maddux Madsen, Dylan Riley and Boen Phelps keep the Broncos rolling.

Thatcher: Paint it blue! Welcome to Boise, Yesenia. Tonight we wear black.

Yesenia: Eat 'em up, Cats! You told me everything here is blue.

Thatcher: Black on the outside. Blue on the inside. Like a good pancake.

Yesenia: That is not how pancakes work, Thatcher.

Boise State is going big for the holiday. Fans are asked to wear black, the first 3,000 students get a custom game day shirt, and the tailgate lots turn into a trunk-or-treat with candy for the kids. Parking opens at 2 p.m. ET and gates open at 8 p.m. ET. Costumes are welcome inside, but masks come off at security, and candy has to come in clear containers. At halftime, the Keith Stein Blue Thunder Marching Band and Spirit Squad perform with new lighting, and then the Kellen Moore statue is unveiled on the field. Athletic director Jeramiah Dickey put it this way: "It's about 6 games, 6 sellouts. Everything counts and everything matters."

Yesenia: Candy in clear bags. Back home we float the river with snacks in a cooler.

Thatcher: Bring the cooler. Just make it clear.

This is the first meeting between these schools, and both are new to the Pac-12 this season. Boise State is 6-1 and 3-0 in the league and has won six in a row, and the Broncos are bowl-eligible for the 29th straight season, second only to Georgia among active runs. Spencer Danielson won his 30th game at Boise State last week, the eighth Bronco head coach to get there. Texas State is 4-3 and 2-1 in the league, and G.J. Kinne's 27th win with the Bobcats tied him for second among their Division I head coaches. His teams are 17-7 at home.

Madsen has completed 124 of 189 throws for 1,445 yards and 14 touchdowns, with no interceptions through seven games. The school says he's the first Bronco to do that since the program joined the top level in 1996. He's 17-1 in league games he has led and fourth in school history with 631 career completions. Riley has 963 rushing yards and eight touchdowns on 140 carries, freshman Rasean Jones has 29 catches for 437 yards, Phelps has 41 tackles and Jayden Virgin-Morgan has three sacks. At Washington State last week, Riley ran 13 times for 128 yards and two touchdowns in a 31-10 win, Boise State's first ever in Pullman.

Thatcher: Seven games, no interceptions. That's bluer than an October sky.

Jackson has completed 133 of 194 throws for 1,606 yards, 14 touchdowns and five interceptions, and he has 26 career rushing touchdowns. Burgess has 889 yards and five scores on 118 carries, Chris Dawn Jr. has 36 catches for 396 yards and Jayden Bradley has 47 tackles. Two weeks ago, the Bobcats set a school record with 716 yards in a 66-27 win over Colorado State. Last week at homecoming they beat Utah State 20-17 in front of 18,587, when Bonilla hit from 51 and then from 37 with less than two minutes left, and Jaylen Jenkins returned a kickoff 100 yards. Kinne called the offense a "bad dream" afterward.

Yesenia: A 100-yard kickoff return at homecoming. That's going with the flow.

Thatcher: And a 51-yard field goal. Okay, I'm a little nervous.

The Pac-12's advance availability report, updated at 10:18 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists six Texas State players out and three questionable, with Torrance Burgess Jr. probable, and one Boise State player out and three questionable.

Yesenia: I'm coming as a river tube. Big, round and floating.

Thatcher: I'm coming as the blue turf. Very flat, very blue.

Yesenia: Whatever happens, Thatcher, dinner's on me, and you float the San Marcos in a maroon sun hat.

Thatcher: Then you eat a stack of blue pancakes in your tube costume.

Yesenia: Deal. Eat 'em up, Cats.

My call: Boise State Broncos 38, Texas State Bobcats 26.
