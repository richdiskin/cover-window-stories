Sam Houston Bearkats at Missouri State Bears

Sam Houston Bearkats (1-6) at Missouri State Bears (2-5)

8:00 PM ET Tue Oct 27 · Robert W. Plaster Stadium · ESPN2

Lem Coxwell teaches history in Walker County, and he says every Texas tale has a turn. Clementine Bilyeu guides float trips in the Ozarks, and she says the river decides where you land.

Sam Houston (1-6) at Missouri State (2-5), Tuesday at 8:00 p.m. ET on ESPN2 at Robert W. Plaster Stadium in Springfield. It's a Conference USA game, and these two haven't met in the regular season since at least 2016. DraftKings has Missouri State Bears −6 with a total of 52.5. My card: Sam Houston Bearkats +6, Over 52.5, and a final of Missouri State Bears 29, Sam Houston Bearkats 25.

Clementine speaks for Missouri State, and she leads with her paddle. "Lem, honey, Isaiah Myers caught 11 passes for 242 yards and two touchdowns last week. He has 796 yards and seven scores on the year. When the current's this strong, you hold on."

Lem speaks for Sam Houston, and he opens his grade book. "Ma'am, Landyn Locke has 1,653 passing yards and 12 touchdowns. Tim Burns Jr. caught four for 117 last week. We lost 24-21 at Troy, 23-17 to Tulsa and 30-24 to New Mexico State. Every chapter came down to the last page."

That's the Bearkat case in one breath: 1-6, but three of those losses came by six points or fewer.

Why Sam Houston? Missouri State hasn't earned this many. The Bears allow 34.9 points a game. They just gave up 61 at Delaware, and Western Kentucky beat them 34-13 a week before WKU beat Sam Houston 16-3. My final has Missouri State by four, which keeps the Bearkats inside the 6.

The risk is the Sam Houston offense. The Bearkats score 20.1 points a game, managed 3 against Liberty and 3 against Western Kentucky, and run for 2.9 yards a carry. If Locke can't find Burns, a four-point game turns into ten in a hurry.

Why the Over? This is the Missouri State defense, which has allowed 30 or more in five of seven games, against a Sam Houston quarterback who just threw for 235. Henry Belin IV has thrown the most passes for the Bears in each of the last two games, and he threw for 337 at Delaware. My 29-25 adds up to 54, a point and a half over 52.5.

Now the room, and everybody who takes a side takes Sam Houston. The Formula has Missouri State 27-25 and likes the Bearkats with the Under. Next Man Up has Missouri State 30-25, also takes Sam Houston +6, and goes Over with me. Follow The Money skips the side and likes the Over. In The Trenches sits this one out, and Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up started it: "The Formula, two points? You think this is a library?"

The Formula shrugged: "Two points or five, it's still Sam Houston plus six. We agree on the part that matters."

Follow The Money smiled: "Then I'll take the part you're arguing about. Over."

Bottom of the card: a road team that loses close, a home defense that gives up a lot, and six points in a game my final calls by four. Clementine gets her current. Lem gets his turn. I'll take the points.

Missouri State Bears 29, Sam Houston Bearkats 25. Sam Houston Bearkats cover the 6. Over 52.5.
