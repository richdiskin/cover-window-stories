Marshall Thundering Herd at Old Dominion Monarchs

Marshall Thundering Herd (4-3) at Old Dominion Monarchs (2-6)

1:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · S.B. Ballard Stadium · ESPN+

Winnie Pocklington stands at the gate of S.B. Ballard Stadium in a paper crown, proclaiming each arriving fan a knight of the realm. Mercy Holderby walks up in green scrubs under her Marshall jacket and says, "Winnie, I'm off shift for exactly one game." Winnie bows. "Then the court grants you safe conduct, nurse. Mostly."

Marshall (4-3) at Old Dominion (2-6), Saturday at 1 p.m. ET on ESPN+ at S.B. Ballard Stadium in Norfolk. It's a Sun Belt game: Marshall is 1-2 in league play, Old Dominion 1-4. DraftKings has Marshall Thundering Herd −2.5 with a total of 45.5. My card: Old Dominion Monarchs +2.5, Under 45.5, and a final of Old Dominion Monarchs 25, Marshall Thundering Herd 20.

Mercy speaks for Marshall, honest as always. "We Are. We beat South Alabama 20-13 last time out. Caleb Clark-Glover has 51 tackles. Xayvion Turner-Bradshaw has 25 catches for 335 yards. And we beat Old Dominion 48-24 last year."

Winnie speaks for Old Dominion, crown tilted. "Hear ye! Javen Gibson has five sacks. Lucas Struck has 61 tackles. We went to Boone and beat App State 10-3. Our defense allows 26.8 points a game, and it keeps this kingdom in games. Long live the Monarchs."

Why Old Dominion? Because Marshall's defense is the weakest piece on the field. The Herd allows 30.7 points a game and 5.3 yards a carry, and its turnover margin sits at minus-9. Even Marshall's wins have been tight: 28-26, 30-24, 36-35 and 20-13. Old Dominion's defense can steal a game, as App State found out. With the home crowd and 2.5 points, the Monarchs are my side, and my final has them winning outright.

The risk is the Old Dominion offense. It scores 15.5 points a game and was shut out 26-0 at Louisiana Tech last week. Three different quarterbacks have thrown the most passes in a game this season. The Monarchs have also struggled to protect the quarterback. And Marshall has won eight of the nine meetings since 2016.

Why the Under? Old Dominion scored 17 points or fewer in five of its eight games, and five of its games stayed under 45.5. Marshall's last game finished 20-13. The total ticked down from 46.5 to 45.5 during the week, and I'm with the move. My 25-20 adds up to 45, under 45.5.

Now the room, and it likes the Monarchs. The Formula has Old Dominion 25-23 and takes the Monarchs, though its total lands Over. Next Man Up has Old Dominion 25-18, same side, with the Under. Follow The Money skips the side and takes the Under. In The Trenches has no pick, since the run games cancel out.

Next Man Up started it: "Monarchs by a touchdown. Winnie, cue the metaphorical trumpets."

The Formula agreed on the side: "Home team, short price, better defense. I'll take it."

Follow The Money shrugged: "The side didn't move. The total did. Under."

Bottom of the card: a Marshall defense that gives up yards in bunches, an Old Dominion defense that can win ugly, and a total built for a low-scoring afternoon. Winnie gets her coronation. Give me the Monarchs and the Under.

Old Dominion Monarchs 25, Marshall Thundering Herd 20. Old Dominion Monarchs cover the 2.5. Under 45.5.
