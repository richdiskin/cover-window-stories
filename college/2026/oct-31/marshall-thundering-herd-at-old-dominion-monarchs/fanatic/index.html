Marshall vs Old Dominion: Monarch Masquerade, No Masks Allowed

Marshall Thundering Herd (4-3) at Old Dominion Monarchs (2-6)

1:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · S.B. Ballard Stadium · ESPN+

Marshall Thundering Herd vs Old Dominion Monarchs is Week 9's Homecoming and "Monarch Masquerade" at S.B. Ballard Stadium, where ODU wants costumes and face paint but has banned masks. Winnie Pocklington, an Old Dominion fan who treats every first down like a knighthood, says Javen Gibson and the Monarch defense will crown a Homecoming winner, and Mercy Holderby, a Huntington nurse and the daughter of two Marshall grads, says Tony Gibson's Herd is going home 5-3.

Winnie: Hear ye, hear ye! The court welcomes the lady from Huntington to the Masquerade!

Mercy: Thank you, Winnie. Where's your mask?

Winnie: Confiscated, nurse. ODU says masks and toy weapons stay in the car. My scepter is in the trunk.

That part is true. Old Dominion's game preview invites fans to wear costumes and says face paint is welcome, but masks and replica weapons aren't allowed anywhere on campus, tailgates included. So it's a masquerade with painted faces.

Mercy: I'll be in green scrubs. Not a costume. I'm on call after this.

It's also Homecoming week in Norfolk, which ODU says runs Oct. 25 through Nov. 1. There's DaBaby and Friends at Chartway Arena, a Friday night boardwalk with fireworks at Whitehurst Beach, and a Spirit Walk before the game Saturday. At halftime, the 2026 Homecoming Royalty will be crowned.

Winnie: A coronation, Mercy. At halftime. I've been rehearsing my curtsy since August.

Marshall is 4-3 and 1-2 in the Sun Belt, and it's had extra rest after a 20-13 win over South Alabama on Tuesday, Oct. 20. Zion Turner went 21 of 33 for 181 yards, Khalil Wilkins ran eight times for 69 yards and Toby Payne caught seven balls. Carlos Del Rio-Wilson is 90 of 140 for 1,041 yards, six touchdowns and five interceptions, and Turner has led the Herd in throws the last two games. Judah Price has 237 rushing yards. Xayvion Turner-Bradshaw has 25 catches for 335 yards and four scores, Caleb Clark-Glover has 51 tackles and Kelvon McBride has two sacks.

Mercy: Twenty to thirteen. I'll take a win with my whole heart, every single time.

Old Dominion is 2-6 and 1-4 after a 26-0 loss at Louisiana Tech. Jaxon Potter, a Washington State transfer, got his first big chance there and went 18 of 34 for 154 yards. Ja'Mez Drummer made a career-high 15 tackles, and Lucas Struck leads the team with 61. Quinn Henicle is 54 of 98 for 656 yards, four touchdowns and four interceptions, Devin Roche has 382 rushing yards and TJ Johnson has 17 catches for 265 yards. Javen Gibson has five sacks, and he made four tackles for loss in the 10-3 win at App State.

Winnie: Five sacks for Sir Javen. I've knighted him five times. He's running out of shoulders.

Mercy: Two Gibsons in one game, Winnie. Ours is the coach.

No official availability report is out for this game, so check both teams' notes for quarterbacks.

There are Norfolk fingerprints on the Marshall sideline, too. ODU's notes say Herd cornerbacks coach Chip West was on Old Dominion's first coaching staff, from 2007 to 2009, and Marshall strength coach Dwight Galt IV held the same job at ODU from 2019 to 2023.

Winnie: Traitors to the crown! Lovely men, I'm sure.

Then there's the history. ODU lists the series at 10-1 Marshall. The Monarchs' one win came 38-14 in 2016. Marshall won 42-35 in 2024 and 48-24 last season.

Mercy: Ten and one. I'm not bragging, Winnie. I'm charting.

Winnie: Chart this: Old Dominion's defense allows 26.8 points a game, and Marshall's allows 30.7. The court sees an opening.

Mercy: Our defense needs to show up for four quarters. I'll bring it coffee.

Winnie: Whatever happens, Mercy, dinner's on the court. We'll say grace first.

Mercy: The court is very kind. We Are, and God bless the Monarchs.

My call: Old Dominion Monarchs 25, Marshall Thundering Herd 20.
