Cardinal Meets Cardinals: Stanford Visits Louisville on Halloween

Stanford Cardinal (4-4) at Louisville Cardinals (4-3)

3:15 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · L&N Federal Credit Union Stadium · ACC Network

Stanford (4-4, 2-3 ACC) at Louisville (4-3, 3-2 ACC). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:15 p.m. ET, L&N Federal Credit Union Stadium, Louisville, Ky., ACC Network.

Your hosts are Ignatius "Iggy" Yamamoto-Ross, the founder of three failed apps, and Bianca Thornberry, a Louisville derby-party hostess who rates every game's hat-worthiness.

Bianca: Welcome to Louisville, Iggy! Hold your hats.

Iggy: Thanks, Bianca. Quick question. Is it confusing that we're both Cardinals?

Bianca: Not at all, darling. We have an S on the end. And a much bigger hat budget.

Iggy: Noted. I'll put it in the deck.

Players to watch

Louisville quarterback Lincoln Kienholz has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 121 of 180 for 1,844 yards, 10 touchdowns and three interceptions. Isaac Brown leads the rushers with 568 yards and four scores, and Lawayne McCoy leads the receivers with 513 yards and four touchdowns. Linebacker T.J. Capers leads the defense with 53 tackles, Clev Lubin has five sacks, and defensive back Kaleb Beasley has five interceptions.

Stanford quarterback Davis Warren has thrown the most passes in all eight games. He's 163 of 271 for 1,933 yards, 11 touchdowns and two interceptions. Micah Ford leads the rushers with 679 yards and eight touchdowns, and Caden High leads the receivers with 30 catches for 440 yards. Safety Jay Green leads the team with 49 tackles.

Iggy: Micah Ford is pure product-market fit. Four touchdowns against Elon tied a Stanford single-game record.

Bianca: Kaleb Beasley has five interceptions, darling. That's a five-hat secondary.

What happened last week

Stanford beat NC State 31-28 at home on Friday, Oct. 23. Warren went 22 of 37 for 303 yards and four touchdowns, Ford ran 22 times for 150 yards, and High caught nine balls for 103 yards and a score. It was Stanford's second straight win, after a 34-7 victory over Elon.

Iggy: Two wins in a row. We're officially trending.

Louisville had an open date. Its last game was a 34-26 win at Syracuse on Oct. 17, when Kienholz threw for 310 yards and ran for two touchdowns, and McCoy caught five balls for 93 yards.

Bianca: Rest, film and a little extra time in the ice tub. I spent mine shopping for feathers.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Kienholz has thrown the most passes for Louisville in every game, and Warren has done the same for Stanford, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Jeff Brohm leads Louisville, and CollegeFootballData dates his hire to Dec. 7, 2022. His teams went 10-4, 9-4 and 9-4 the last three seasons. This year's Cardinals score 38.7 points a game, run the ball well, and have 12 takeaways against six giveaways.

Tavita Pritchard is in his first season as Stanford's head coach, and CFBD records his hire date as Nov. 28, 2025. He's a Stanford alum who played quarterback on the 2007 team that upset USC, and he coached quarterbacks for the Washington Commanders from 2023 to 2025.

Iggy: Founder returns to the company. Best origin story in the deck.

Bianca: That's a lovely story, Iggy. I'd wear a hat for it.

Player storylines

The matchup to watch is the Louisville ground game against a Stanford defense that has struggled to stop the run. Brown and Kienholz both carry the ball, and Louisville's big guys up front like to push people around. On the other side, Ford is coming off a 150-yard game, and Warren has thrown for more than 300 yards in three games. Stanford won the only recent meeting, 38-35 in 2024, so Louisville has a memory to fix.

What's happening on campus

L&N Federal Credit Union Stadium was built in 1998 and has a listed capacity of 60,000. The Marching Cards will be there, and so will "Fight! UofL." Halloween in Louisville means red costumes, and at least one very large red hat. If you visit, ask somebody about the Cardinal Bird.

Iggy: I'm dressing as a cardinal. Singular. On brand.

Bianca: Then I'll dress as a whole flock.

Anything else fun and true

Stanford has fielded teams nearly every year since 1892, and it played rugby instead of football from 1906 to 1917. The teams were known as the "Indians" from 1930 to 1972, and they've been the "Cardinal" since the 1982 season. A 1975 student vote to become the "Robber Barons" wasn't approved. Stanford Stadium was built in 2006 and seats 50,424.

Bianca: Whatever happens, Iggy, there's a derby pie waiting after the game. We'll say grace first.

Iggy: Deal, Bianca. Ship it. Go Card.

My call: Louisville Cardinals 42, Stanford Cardinal 19.
