Chili and Slalom Gates: No. 6 Utah Visits Cincinnati on Halloween

Utah Utes (7-0) at Cincinnati Bearcats (5-2)

3:45 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Nippert Stadium · ESPN

No. 6 Utah (7-0, 4-0 Big 12) at Cincinnati (5-2, 2-2 Big 12). Saturday, Oct. 31, 3:45 p.m. ET, Nippert Stadium, Cincinnati, ESPN.

Your hosts are Aspen Mortensen, a Utah fan and former ski racer who reads defenses like slalom gates, and Lionel "Skyline" Abernathy, a Cincinnati engineering co-op kid who ranks everything in chili ways.

Skyline: Welcome to Nippert, Aspen! Two Saturdays, two top-10 visitors. That's a five-way schedule.

Aspen: Thanks, Skyline. I'll warn you now, there will be avalanche puns.

Skyline: I can handle puns. Can your defense handle Cole Tabb?

Aspen: Our defense eats gates for breakfast. Send it, no fear.

Players to watch

Utah quarterback Devon Dampier has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 136 of 198 for 1,616 yards, 15 touchdowns and six interceptions, and he ran for 150 yards and two scores last week. Wayshawn Parker leads the rushers with 644 yards and eight touchdowns, and Braden Pegan leads the receivers with 34 catches for 459 yards and six scores. Linebacker Trey Reynolds leads the defense with 44 tackles, and Karson Kaufusi has two sacks.

Cincinnati quarterback JC French IV has thrown the most passes in all seven games. He's 127 of 183 for 1,510 yards, 13 touchdowns and only two interceptions. Cole Tabb leads the rushers with 621 yards and nine scores on just 65 carries, and JV Gibson leads the receivers with 29 catches for 385 yards. Linebacker Jonathan Thompson leads the defense with 60 tackles, and Patrick Bauer has three and a half sacks.

Aspen: Dampier runs like he's on a fresh powder day. Nobody catches him.

Skyline: Nine touchdowns for Tabb on 65 carries. That's onions AND beans.

What happened last week

Utah beat Houston 21-13 at home on Oct. 24, the Utes' first win ever over the Cougars. Dampier threw for 204 yards and ran for 150 more with two touchdowns.

Aspen: Five losses to Houston, then finally a win. Every ski run has a bottom, and we made it to the lodge.

Cincinnati beat No. 8 Texas Tech 37-24 on Homecoming in front of a sold-out Nippert crowd. French threw four touchdowns, and Tabb ran 16 times for 173 yards and a score. It was only the third top-10 win in Nippert Stadium history.

Skyline: Five-way. Extra onions. I'm still smiling.

Injuries and availability

The official Big 12 availability report hasn't been posted yet. Utah coach Morgan Scalley said starting offensive lineman Kelvin Obot, who was hurt at Colorado and missed the Houston game, has no timetable and is getting a second opinion. Cincinnati coach Scott Satterfield said the team is close to getting starting nose tackle Josh Hough back after he missed last week, and starting center Taran Tyo, who left last week's game, likely won't have an answer until late in the week. Check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Morgan Scalley is in his first season as Utah's head coach after taking over in December 2025. He played defensive back for the Utes from 2001 to 2004, joined the staff in 2007 and spent 10 seasons as defensive coordinator. After the Colorado win he said, "It's not always pretty, but we did enough."

Scott Satterfield became Cincinnati's 43rd head coach on Dec. 5, 2022. His offense runs the ball well and almost never gives it away.

Aspen: Coach Scalley has been a Ute for a long time. He knows every gate on this course.

Skyline: Coach Satterfield has us at a five-way. I'm not downgrading anything this week.

Player storylines

The matchup to watch is Tabb and the Cincinnati big guys up front against a Utah defense that allows only 14.6 points a game. On the other side, Dampier and Parker face a Bearcats defense that just slowed down Texas Tech. These teams met for the first time last season, on Nov. 1 in Salt Lake City, where College GameDay was in town, and Utah won 45-14.

What's happening on campus

For the first time in program history, Cincinnati is hosting ranked opponents in back-to-back weeks, and it all happens on Halloween. Football has been played on the Nippert site since 1901, which Cincinnati calls the second-oldest playing site in college football.

Aspen: I'm dressing as an avalanche. Big, fast and loud.

Skyline: I'm dressing as a five-way. Onions and beans included.

Anything else fun and true

Utah football began in 1892 and has played on the current site of Rice-Eccles Stadium since 1927. The Utes have won two BCS bowls, the 2005 Fiesta Bowl and the 2009 Sugar Bowl. Cincinnati became the first team from outside the Power Five to make the College Football Playoff in the 2021 season, when the Bearcats played Alabama in the Cotton Bowl.

Skyline: Whatever happens, Aspen, chili's on me. We'll say grace first.

Aspen: Deal, Skyline. I'll bring the fry sauce.

My call: Utah Utes 33, Cincinnati Bearcats 25.
