Utah vs Cincinnati: Wear Black, Bring a Costume, No Masks

Utah Utes (7-0) at Cincinnati Bearcats (5-2)

3:45 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Nippert Stadium · ESPN

Utah Utes vs Cincinnati Bearcats is a Week 9 Halloween game at Nippert Stadium, where the Bearcats have asked fans to wear black for Breast Cancer Awareness and invited kids to come in costume, as long as nobody wears a mask. Aspen Mortensen, a Utah fan and former ski racer who reads defenses like slalom gates, says Devon Dampier and Wayshawn Parker will keep the Utes perfect, and Lionel "Skyline" Abernathy, a UC engineering co-op student who ranks everything in chili ways, says Scott Satterfield, JC French IV and Cole Tabb are about to do it again.

Skyline: Aspen! You came in costume.

Aspen: I'm a ski racer, Skyline. This is my regular jacket.

Skyline: It's black. You're following the dress code. I'm proud of you.

Aspen: I'm following the snow. Black shows up better in an avalanche.

Last Saturday, Cincinnati beat No. 8 Texas Tech 37-24 on the university's 100th Homecoming. The athletic department called it the Bearcats' first home win over an AP top-10 team since a 30-11 win over Rutgers in 2006. Tabb ran for a career-high 173 yards on 16 carries, including a 58-yard touchdown in the fourth quarter. French threw a career-best four touchdowns, and MJ Cannon forced a fumble and made a one-handed interception just before halftime.

Skyline: That's a five-way win, Aspen. Spaghetti, chili, cheese, onions and beans. The full plate.

Aspen: Enjoy it. We beat you 45-14 last year.

Skyline: We remember. Coach Satterfield remembers. That's why he asked for help.

Satterfield wanted the whole building in it this week. "I know it's Halloween, but dress up and come out yelling in this game," he said Monday.

Aspen: Yelling doesn't scare me. I've heard a glacier crack.

Utah is 7-0, 4-0 in the Big 12, and first in the conference. The Utes score 37.4 points a game and allow 14.6. Dampier is 136 of 198 for 1,616 yards, 15 touchdowns and six interceptions, and he ran for 150 yards and two touchdowns in last week's 21-13 win over Houston. Parker has 644 rushing yards and eight touchdowns, Braden Pegan has 34 catches for 459 yards and six touchdowns, and Trey Reynolds leads the defense with 44 tackles.

Satterfield saw that Houston game. "Their quarterback, he had a great game last week against Houston," he said.

Aspen: Dampier runs like a downhill racer. Straight down, no brakes.

Cincinnati is 5-2 and 2-2 in the league. French is 127 of 183 for 1,510 yards, 13 touchdowns and two interceptions. Tabb has 621 rushing yards and nine touchdowns on just 65 carries. JV Gibson has 29 catches for 385 yards, Patrick Bauer has 3.5 sacks, and Jonathan Thompson leads the defense with 60 tackles. The Bearcats score 35.4 points a game and allow 26.4.

Skyline: Tabb is a three-way. Fast, smart and somehow still underrated.

Aspen: Three-way is spaghetti, chili and cheese. Even I know that.

Skyline: You've been studying.

The Bearcats lost 34-7 at Arizona and 45-42 at West Virginia before beating Texas Tech. Utah has won two road games, 31-17 at Iowa State and 27-24 at Colorado.

Aspen: Close one in Boulder. I'll admit it. Tight gates.

The Big 12's initial availability report, posted at 10 p.m. ET Wednesday, Oct. 28, lists two Utah players out and one questionable, and two Cincinnati players out, one doubtful and two questionable.

Before kickoff, The Den student tailgate runs from noon ET on Sigma Sigma Commons with LaRosa's pizza, a costume contest for a pair of Bearcats basketball tickets and a field-goal kick contest for $50.

Skyline: I'm entering the kick contest. Engineering. It's all angles.

Aspen: I'm entering the costume contest. As a ski racer.

Skyline: That's not a costume.

Aspen: It is in Ohio.

Skyline: Whatever happens, Aspen, the chili's on me afterward. Four-way, onions.

Aspen: Deal. Hold the onions on the ride home.

My call: Utah Utes 33, Cincinnati Bearcats 25.
