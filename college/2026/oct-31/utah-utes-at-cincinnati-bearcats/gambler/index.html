Utah Utes at Cincinnati Bearcats

Utah Utes (7-0) at Cincinnati Bearcats (5-2)

3:45 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Nippert Stadium · ESPN

Skyline Abernathy is spooning chili onto a plate of spaghetti outside Nippert Stadium when Aspen Mortensen walks up in a ski jacket. "Five-way?" he asks. "Onions and beans. Reserved for greatness." Aspen eyes the plate. "Send it, no fear," she says, grabbing a fork. "But I'm warning you, Skyline. There will be avalanche puns."

No. 6 Utah (7-0, 4-0 Big 12) visits Cincinnati (5-2, 2-2 Big 12) Saturday at 3:45 p.m. ET on ESPN at Nippert Stadium. DraftKings has Utah Utes −6.5 with a total of 56.5. On Monday, DraftKings had Utah −6.5 and 55.5. My card: Utah Utes −6.5, Over 56.5, and a final of Utah Utes 33, Cincinnati Bearcats 25.

Aspen speaks for Utah. "We beat Houston 21-13 last week for our first win ever over the Cougars. Devon Dampier ran for 150 yards and two touchdowns. He has 15 touchdown throws, Wayshawn Parker has eight rushing touchdowns, and Braden Pegan has six receiving touchdowns. We allow 14.6 points a game. That's a black-diamond defense."

Skyline speaks for Cincinnati. "We beat No. 8 Texas Tech 37-24 last week, only the third top-10 win in Nippert Stadium history. JC French threw four touchdowns, and Cole Tabb ran for 173 yards. French has only two interceptions all season. Tabb has nine rushing touchdowns. Five-way, no hesitation."

Why Utah? Because the Utes do everything well. They've beaten Arkansas 43-10, Utah State 33-0 and Kansas 41-24, and they allowed 13 points to Houston. Utah allows 14.6 points a game while scoring 37.4, and Dampier has thrown and run for big plays all season. My final has the Utes winning by eight.

The risk is that Cincinnati is rolling at home. The Bearcats score 35.4 points a game, and Tabb has 621 rushing yards on just 65 carries. Utah won its last two games 27-24 at Colorado and 21-13 against Houston, and Dampier threw two interceptions in each. Utah starting offensive lineman Kelvin Obot was hurt at Colorado and missed Houston, and coach Morgan Scalley says there's no timetable.

Why the Over? Cincinnati doesn't play many quiet games. The Bearcats lost 45-42 at West Virginia, beat Miami (OH) 35-31 and scored 37 against Texas Tech. Utah scored 66 against Idaho, 43 against Arkansas and 41 against Kansas. Cincinnati allows 26.4 points a game, and Utah should get its share. My 33-25 adds up to 58, over 56.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Utah 32-25 and sides with Utah and the Over. Next Man Up has Utah 34-25 and lands on Utah and the Over. Follow The Money likes Cincinnati after the spread came down from where it opened, and sits out the total since it hasn't budged. In The Trenches sits out: the Utah run game and the Cincinnati run game each have a matchup they like, so they cancel out.

Follow The Money said it plainly: "The market moved toward Cincinnati. I go with the market."

Next Man Up shook his head: "Utah is unbeaten for a reason."

The Formula shrugged: "Utah by a touchdown and change."

Bottom of the card: an unbeaten Utah team with a top defense, a Cincinnati offense that scores in bunches and a Nippert crowd still buzzing from last week. Give me the Utes and the Over.

Utah Utes 33, Cincinnati Bearcats 25. Utah Utes cover the 6.5. Over 56.5.
