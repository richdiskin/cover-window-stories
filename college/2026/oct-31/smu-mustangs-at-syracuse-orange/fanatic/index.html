SMU vs Syracuse: Otto Hits the Helmets for a Halloween Noon

SMU Mustangs (6-1) at Syracuse Orange (3-4)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · JMA Wireless Dome · ESPN2

SMU Mustangs vs Syracuse Orange is a Week 9 Halloween noon game at the JMA Wireless Dome, and for the first time ever, Syracuse will wear helmets featuring Otto, its Hall of Fame mascot, while kids in costumes parade the same Quad Walk route the team takes into the building. Nico Calabrese, a Salt City lifer, says Umari Hatcher and Fran Brown's Orange will treat the No. 19 Mustangs to a trick, and Tatum Delacroix-Price, a Highland Park boutique owner who grades sideline outfits, says Kevin Jennings and Rhett Lashlee's SMU are the only treat that matters.

Nico: Tatum! Otto on the helmets! Fifty years of me in an orange wig, and the team finally dressed up too.

Tatum: Nico, darling, a citrus fruit on a football helmet. I need a minute. I give it a B-plus for courage.

Syracuse Athletics announced the plan this week. The team walks into the Dome at 9:40 a.m., and right behind it, young fans in costumes get the same walk and the same cheers. There's trick-or-treating on the Quad, and from 10:30 to 11:30 a.m., participating suites inside the Dome hand out candy in a promotion called "Suite-4-Treats." Families can get four hot dogs, four sodas and two popcorns for $30.

Tatum: Suites handing out candy? Sugar, in Dallas the suites hand out valet tickets.

Nico: And nobody in Dallas has ever walked anywhere.

Then the costumes come off and the Mustangs show up with the best offense on the field. SMU is 6-1 and second in the ACC, and it beat California 59-20 on Oct. 24. Jennings went 16 of 23 for 223 yards and three touchdowns that day, and Kendrick Raphael ran 22 times for 104 yards and two scores. Jennings has thrown the most passes in all seven SMU games and is 156 of 215 for 2,062 yards, 19 touchdowns and five interceptions. He threw for 430 yards in the opener at Florida State and went 33 of 39 for 439 against Missouri State. Raphael has 641 rushing yards and eight touchdowns, and Yamir Knight leads the receivers with 38 catches for 443 yards.

Tatum: Two thousand yards in seven games. That's not a quarterback, Nico. That's a whole season of television.

Syracuse brings the drama. The Orange won 31-30 in overtime at North Carolina on Oct. 24. Steve Angeli went 20 of 34 for 256 yards and two touchdowns, Ahmad Miller carried 30 times for 125 yards and a score, and Hatcher caught four passes for 103 yards and a touchdown. Hatcher has 54 catches for 887 yards and eight touchdowns this season, and Angeli leads the team with 987 passing yards.

Nico: Overtime at UConn, 42-41. Overtime at Carolina, 31-30. This team doesn't do anything until it's dark out. Perfect for Halloween.

There's a costume question at quarterback too. Angeli, Amari Odom and Malachi Nelson have each thrown the most passes for Syracuse in at least one game, and no official availability report is out yet. Angeli did it last week.

On defense, SMU's Marques White has 5.5 sacks and Alexander Kilgore leads the Mustangs with 39 tackles. They held Boston College to 16 points and Virginia to 13. For Syracuse, Chris D'Appolonia has a team-high 51 tackles and Tunmise Adeleye has four sacks.

The ground game may be a grind. SMU gains 4.4 yards a carry and Syracuse allows 4.2, while the Orange gain 3.8 and SMU allows 3.8. Syracuse scores 31.6 points a game, so this Dome won't be quiet.

Lashlee, hired at SMU on Nov. 30, 2021, took the Mustangs to the 2024 ACC championship game, where they lost 34-31 to Clemson on a field goal at the end. This year SMU has won all five home games in University Park. Brown, hired on Nov. 28, 2023, went 10-3 in his first year and 3-9 last season. SMU won last year's meeting 31-18.

Tatum: We've got a Heisman, Nico. Doak Walker, 1948. And the Pony Express with Eric Dickerson and Craig James.

Nico: We've got Otto. On a helmet. Beat that.

The Dome opened in 1980, seats 49,057 and goes by "The Loud House." Nobody has to worry about lake-effect snow on the costumes.

Nico: After the game, you're coming to my mom's for Sunday dinner. We say grace, then we share the salt potatoes.

Tatum: I'll bring the sweet tea and grade your mother's apron. Pony up or sit down.

My call: SMU Mustangs 32, Syracuse Orange 26.
