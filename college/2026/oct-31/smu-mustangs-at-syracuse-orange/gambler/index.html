SMU Mustangs at Syracuse Orange

SMU Mustangs (6-1) at Syracuse Orange (3-4)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · JMA Wireless Dome · ESPN2

SMU vs Syracuse prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on Syracuse Orange +10 and Under 58.5 at DraftKings, with a final of SMU Mustangs 32, Syracuse Orange 26. Syracuse debuts helmets with Otto on them for the first time on Halloween at noon in the JMA Wireless Dome, and the Orange have a habit of turning every game into a late one.

Look at the last four Syracuse scores. The Orange fell 21-18 to Cal and 28-25 at Virginia, then won 42-41 at UConn and 31-30 in overtime at North Carolina. Every one of them was decided by eight points or fewer. A team that lives in one-score games is the kind you take when the number is 10.

SMU has been a home team almost all year. Its two road games were a 27-24 win at Florida State and a 41-31 loss at Louisville, and the other five came at Gerald J. Ford Stadium. Now the Mustangs fly to central New York for an early kick in a building called the Loud House. My final has SMU by six.

Nico Calabrese, the Salt City lifer, thinks the Otto helmets are worth a touchdown on their own. "Umari Hatcher has 54 catches for 887 yards and eight touchdowns. We've played two overtime games and won both. You're not leaving this Dome by 10."

Tatum Delacroix-Price, the Highland Park boutique owner, has her sunglasses on indoors. "Sugar, Kevin Jennings has 19 touchdown passes and five interceptions, and we beat Cal 59-20. Kendrick Raphael ran for 104 yards and two scores in that one. Pony up or sit down."

Here's the risk with Syracuse. Steve Angeli, Amari Odom and Malachi Nelson have each thrown the most passes in at least one game, and Angeli threw three interceptions against Cal. If Jennings keeps getting short fields, the margin gets ugly.

For the total, SMU's defense held Boston College to 16 and Virginia to 13. Syracuse runs for 3.8 yards a carry and SMU allows 3.8, so the Orange have to grind it out. SMU won last year's meeting 31-18. My 32-26 adds up to 58, a half point under 58.5. Action Network's opening listing had SMU −12.5 and a total of 55.5, DraftKings had SMU −9.5 and 56.5 on Monday, and the number is 10 and 58.5 now.

Every side in the room is on the Orange. The Formula has SMU 32-25, likes Syracuse +10, and is on the Under without backing it. Next Man Up has SMU 31-27, likes Syracuse +10, and is on the Under without backing it. Follow The Money likes Syracuse +10 and the Over, and it keeps no score of its own. In The Trenches has no pick, and Letdown Watch has no score this week.

SMU Mustangs 32, Syracuse Orange 26. Syracuse Orange cover the 10. Under 58.5.
