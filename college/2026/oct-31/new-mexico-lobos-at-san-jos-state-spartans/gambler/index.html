New Mexico Lobos at San José State Spartans

New Mexico Lobos (6-1) at San José State Spartans (4-4)

4:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · CEFCU Stadium · MW+

Tessa Nakagawa-Ruiz is sketching a game-week roadmap on a whiteboard outside CEFCU Stadium when Paloma Begaye-Ortiz walks up holding two foil-wrapped burritos. "Red or green?" Paloma asks. Tessa studies them like a product spec. "Can I A/B test both?" Paloma laughs. "Both, Tessa. Always both."

New Mexico (6-1, 3-0 Mountain West) visits San José State (4-4, 2-2 Mountain West) Saturday at 4 p.m. ET on MW+ at CEFCU Stadium in San Jose. DraftKings has New Mexico Lobos −14.5 with a total of 42.5. My card: New Mexico Lobos −14.5, Over 42.5, and a final of New Mexico Lobos 31, San José State Spartans 15.

Paloma speaks for New Mexico. "We beat North Dakota State 21-16 last week, and Kiefer Sibley ran for 91 yards and a touchdown. Scottre Humphrey has nine rushing touchdowns, Brett Karhu has five sacks, and Mercury Swaim has 43 tackles. Our defense allows 11.3 points a game. Red or green? BOTH!"

Tessa speaks for San José State. "Rollback. We lost 28-13 at Nevada, and our quarterback spot is a work in progress. Daniel Rolovich got hurt early, and Luke Weaver came back late. Weaver has 11 touchdown throws. Carson Clark has 47 catches, Jayland McGlothen has six sacks and Dylan Lee has 71 tackles. And we've won the last four meetings."

Why New Mexico? Because the Lobos defend and run the ball. New Mexico allows 11.3 points a game, and its only loss was 14-6 at Oklahoma. San José State scored 13 points at Nevada and 13 against Wyoming, and it's sorting out its quarterbacks. My final has the Lobos winning by 16.

The risk is the New Mexico passing game. Toa Fa'avae threw for 92 yards last week and 59 at Hawai'i, with interceptions in both, and the Lobos won those games 21-10 and 21-16. McGlothen can get after the quarterback, and the Spartans have won the last four meetings, including 35-28 last season. If New Mexico can't throw, this stays close.

Why the Over? New Mexico can score in a hurry: 38 against Central Michigan, 70 against Mercyhurst, 42 at New Mexico State and 61 against UTEP. The last five meetings between these teams finished 48-41, 32-21, 38-21, 52-24 and 35-28. My 31-15 adds up to 46, over 42.5.

Now the room. The Formula has New Mexico 31-15, sides with New Mexico, and likes the Over. Next Man Up has New Mexico 30-15, sides with New Mexico, and likes the Over. Follow The Money sits out both, since the spread and the total haven't budged. In The Trenches sits out: the New Mexico run game and the San José State run game each have a matchup they like, so they cancel out.

The Formula opened: "New Mexico's defense wins this by more than two scores."

Next Man Up agreed: "And the Lobos score enough for the Over."

Follow The Money shrugged: "Nothing moved. I'm watching."

Bottom of the card: a New Mexico defense that takes the ball away, a San José State team sorting out its quarterbacks and a Halloween crowd in San Jose. Give me the Lobos and the Over.

New Mexico Lobos 31, San José State Spartans 15. New Mexico Lobos cover the 14.5. Over 42.5.
