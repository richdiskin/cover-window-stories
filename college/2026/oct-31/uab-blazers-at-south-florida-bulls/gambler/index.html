UAB Blazers at South Florida Bulls

UAB Blazers (4-3) at South Florida Bulls (5-2)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Raymond James Stadium · ESPNU

Reina Valdés-Kowalski is wearing a full Gasparilla pirate hat in the Raymond James lot, waving a foam cutlass. Ozella Birchfield, Birmingham born and steel-family proud, walks up in a welder's apron and says, "Honey, that sword's plastic. Ours are forged."

UAB (4-3) at South Florida (5-2), Saturday at 12:00 p.m. ET on ESPNU at Raymond James Stadium in Tampa. It's an American game, and both teams are coming off an open week. DraftKings has South Florida Bulls −8.5 with a total of 42.5. My card: South Florida Bulls −8.5, Over 42.5, and a final of South Florida Bulls 30, UAB Blazers 16.

Ozella speaks for UAB, and she plays it like opening night. "Forged, not given! We beat East Carolina 30-17 in overtime. Ryder Burton threw for 262 yards and CJ Smith caught eight balls for 119. Muaaz Byard has 69 tackles, and Marlin Dean has five sacks."

Reina speaks for South Florida, cutlass raised. "Arrr, Ozella, we beat Kent State 29-2. Michael Van Buren Jr. hasn't thrown an interception all season. D.J. Crowther has 518 rushing yards and seven touchdowns. And our defense allows 15.1 points a game. Pirate-approved!"

Why South Florida? The Bulls have the best defense on the field. It held Florida International to nine, Bowling Green to six and Kent State to two. UAB's offense scores 23.3 points a game, and on the road it has struggled: a 42-23 loss at Illinois, a 21-14 loss at Louisiana and a 38-13 loss at Memphis. Burton has five interceptions. Last season South Florida won this matchup 48-18. My final has the Bulls by two touchdowns, and that clears the 8.5.

The worry is the South Florida offense when it stalls. The Bulls scored 13 in a loss to Temple and 14 at Bowling Green. The run games are about even, with both teams near four yards a carry. If USF settles for field goals, UAB can hang around the way it did in a 24-20 win over Navy.

Why the Over? The last three meetings in this series went 56-35, 35-25 and 48-18, and every one of them sailed past 42.5. South Florida has scored 24 or more in four games, and UAB has scored 23 or more in five. Both offenses are rested. My 30-16 adds up to 46, three and a half points over 42.5.

Now the room. The Formula has South Florida 28-16, takes the Bulls −8.5 and the Over. Next Man Up has South Florida 31-15, same side, same Over. Follow The Money saw the market slide toward UAB and the total drop, so it takes UAB +8.5 and the Under. In The Trenches finds no gap worth a call, and Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up started it: "Two of us have the Bulls by two touchdowns. Follow The Money, you're with the dragon?"

Follow The Money shrugged: "The total fell. I don't argue with a falling number."

The Formula answered: "Then argue with the last three meetings. All of them went Over."

Bottom of the card: the better defense at home, a UAB team that hasn't won on the road, and a total too low for this series. Reina gets her pirate parade. Give me the Bulls and the Over.

South Florida Bulls 30, UAB Blazers 16. South Florida Bulls cover the 8.5. Over 42.5.
