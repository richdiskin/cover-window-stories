UAB vs South Florida: Black Out Halloween at Raymond James

UAB Blazers (4-3) at South Florida Bulls (5-2)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Raymond James Stadium · ESPNU

XFacebook

UAB Blazers vs South Florida Bulls is a Week 9 Black Out Halloween noon ET game at Raymond James Stadium, where the Bulls wear black-on-black and the first 10,000 fans get Black Out rally towels. Reina Valdés-Kowalski, a Tampa Bulls roster maven, says Brian Hartline's Bulls and Michael Van Buren Jr. are ready for November, and Ozella Birchfield, a Birmingham steel-family granddaughter and community-theater actor, says Alex Mortensen's Blazers and Ryder Burton just pulled off the comeback of the year.

Reina: Ahoy, Ozella! Black towels, black jerseys, black sails. Welcome to Tampa.

Ozella: Listen, in Birmingham we invented black. It's called soot. Forged, not given!

USF's promotional calendar has listed Oct. 31 as Black Out/Halloween since August. The Bulls say it's the first game of a five-game stretch run, and Hartline isn't hiding the stakes. "We own our own destiny in our opinion," he said this week.

Reina: Destiny, Ozella. Also towels. Mostly towels.

UAB arrives with a story. On Oct. 15 against East Carolina, the Blazers trailed 17-9 with 1:02 left. They drove 93 yards for a touchdown, made the two-point conversion and then won in overtime on an 80-yard interception return for a touchdown. Final: 30-17.

Ozella: I gave a standing ovation so long the ushers asked me to sit. Then I stood again. Curtain call.

UAB is 4-3 and has been off since that night. Burton has led the Blazers in throws in all seven games. He's 91 of 167 for 1,184 yards, six touchdowns and five interceptions, and he threw for 262 yards and two scores against the Pirates. Rod Robinson II leads the rushers with 436 yards, and CJ Smith leads the receivers with 19 catches for 350 yards. Muaaz Byard has a team-high 69 tackles, and Marlin Dean has five sacks.

South Florida is 5-2 and beat Kent State 29-2 on Homecoming, Oct. 17. Van Buren went 22 of 27 for 193 yards and a touchdown. He's 115 of 179 for 1,266 yards and six touchdowns and hasn't thrown an interception all season. D.J. Crowther has 518 rushing yards and seven touchdowns, and Bryson Rodgers leads the receivers with 30 catches for 334 yards. Ayden Jackson has 38 tackles, and Michael Williams II has four sacks.

Reina: No interceptions in seven games. Van Buren guards that ball like buried treasure.

Hartline spent the week off cutting things down. "Minimizing the call sheet will help with that," he said. "We want to make it simpler, not easier, but simpler will hopefully allow higher execution."

Ozella: Simpler, not easier. That's Shakespeare's note to every actor who ever overdid a death scene.

Both coaches are in their first year. Hartline was hired at South Florida on Dec. 3, 2025, and his Bulls allow 15.1 points a game. They held Florida International to nine and Bowling Green to six, and they won at Army 28-24. Mortensen was hired at UAB two days later, on Dec. 5, and his Blazers have beaten UL Monroe, Navy and East Carolina at home. UAB scores 23.3 points a game. South Florida scores 26.6.

Reina: Two rookies. One of them gets a towel thrown at him by 10,000 people.

The series is young and loud. UAB won 56-35 in 2023, and South Florida won 35-25 in 2024 and 48-18 last season.

Ozella: Fifty-six, Reina. Write that on your towel.

Raymond James Stadium opened in 1998 and seats 65,857. UAB fans will bring Blaze the dragon and the Marching Blazers from Protective Stadium, which opened in Birmingham in 2021.

Reina: I'm dressing up as a pirate. Which is what I wear every week.

Ozella: I'm coming as Lady Macbeth in a black towel. Out, damned spot!

Van Buren summed up the Bulls' week. "I feel like it's all adding up to us having a great November," he said.

Ozella: November begins Sunday, Reina. Saturday is still October, and October is Birmingham's.

Reina: After the game, I'm taking you to dinner on the water. We'll say grace, and you can tell me theater stories.

Ozella: Deal, matey.

My call: South Florida Bulls 30, UAB Blazers 16.
