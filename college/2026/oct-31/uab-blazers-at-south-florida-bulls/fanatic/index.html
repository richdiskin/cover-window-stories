Pirates and Dragons on Halloween: UAB Visits South Florida as Two Rested Teams Meet in Tampa

UAB Blazers (4-3) at South Florida Bulls (5-2)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Raymond James Stadium · ESPNU

UAB (4-3, 2-1 American) at South Florida (5-2, 1-2 American). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12 p.m. ET, Raymond James Stadium, Tampa, Fla., ESPNU.

Your hosts are Ozella Birchfield, a Birmingham native, steel-family granddaughter and community-theater actor, and Reina Valdés-Kowalski, a boat-show model turned Bulls roster maven who can't go five minutes without a Gasparilla pirate bit.

Reina: Ahoy, Ozella! Welcome to Tampa. Mind the cannon fire. It's ceremonial.

Ozella: Darling, I'm from Birmingham. We were making steel when your pirates were still drawing maps. Forged, not given!

Players to watch

UAB quarterback Ryder Burton has thrown the most passes in all seven Blazer games. He's 91 of 167 for 1,184 yards, six touchdowns and five interceptions. Rod Robinson II leads the rushers with 436 yards, and CJ Smith leads the receivers with 350 yards on 19 catches. On defense, linebacker Muaaz Byard has a team-high 69 tackles and linebacker Marlin Dean has five sacks.

South Florida quarterback Michael Van Buren Jr. has thrown the most passes in all seven Bulls games. He's 115 of 179 for 1,266 yards and six touchdowns, and he hasn't thrown an interception in any of them. Running back D.J. Crowther has 518 yards and seven touchdowns, and Bryson Rodgers leads the receivers with 30 catches for 334 yards. Defensive back Ayden Jackson has 38 tackles, and end Michael Williams II has four sacks.

Reina: Not one interception in seven games. Van Buren guards that ball like buried treasure.

What happened last week

Both teams had the week off. UAB's last game was a 30-17 overtime win over East Carolina on Oct. 15. Burton threw for 262 yards and two touchdowns, and Smith caught eight balls for 119 yards.

Ozella: Overtime! I gave a standing ovation so long the ushers asked me to sit.

South Florida's last game was a 29-2 win over Kent State on Oct. 17. Van Buren went 22 of 27 for 193 yards and a touchdown, and Cameron Seldon caught a scoring throw.

Reina: Two points allowed. The defense walked the plank, and Kent State fell off it.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Burton and Van Buren have each thrown the most passes in every game for their teams, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Both head coaches are in their first season. Brian Hartline was hired at South Florida on Dec. 3, 2025, and his Bulls allow just 15.1 points a game. They've won at Army and at Bowling Green and beaten Kent State 29-2.

Alex Mortensen was hired at UAB on Dec. 5, 2025, two days after Hartline. His Blazers like to run the ball, and they've already won three home games over FBS teams: UL Monroe, Navy and East Carolina.

Ozella: Two first-year coaches. It's like opening night at the community theater. Everybody's nervous and everybody's brilliant.

Player storylines

The matchup to circle is Burton against a South Florida defense that held Florida International to nine points and Bowling Green to six. On the other side, Crowther and the Bulls big guys up front face a UAB defense that allows 4.4 yards a carry. Both teams run for about four yards a carry, so expect a ground battle.

What's happening on campus

Raymond James Stadium opened in 1998 and seats 65,828. A Halloween noon kickoff in Tampa means sunshine, pirate hats and Rocky the Bull leading the cheers. UAB fans will bring the spirit of Blaze the dragon and the UAB Marching Blazers from Protective Stadium, which opened in Birmingham in 2021.

Reina: I'm dressing up as a pirate. Which is what I wear every week.

Ozella: I'm coming as Lady Macbeth. I've played her twice.

Anything else fun and true

South Florida has climbed fast. From 2021 through 2025, the Bulls went 2-10, 1-11, 7-6, 7-6 and 9-4. UAB went 9-4 in 2021, and this year's Blazers have already matched last season's four wins. These two have met three times since 2023. UAB won a wild one 56-35 in 2023, and South Florida won 35-25 in 2024 and 48-18 last season.

Ozella: Reina, after the game, I'm taking you to dinner. We'll say grace and you can tell me pirate stories.

Reina: Deal, matey. Pirate-approved!

My call: South Florida Bulls 30, UAB Blazers 16.
