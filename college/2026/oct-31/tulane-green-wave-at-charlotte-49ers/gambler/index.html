Tulane Green Wave (3-4) at Charlotte 49ers (1-6)

Twan Ducros-Labat has the trombone back in its case after a rough Saturday Uptown, and he is ready to play it again in North Carolina. Priya Halloran is in the stands in Charlotte with a spreadsheet, and she has finally priced a drive that paid off.

Tulane (3-4) at Charlotte (1-6), Friday at 7:00 p.m. ET on ESPN2 at Jerry Richardson Stadium in Charlotte. It's an American game. DraftKings has Tulane Green Wave −10 with a total of 46.5. My card: Tulane Green Wave −10, Over 46.5, and a final of Tulane Green Wave 31, Charlotte 49ers 19.

My final has Tulane by 12, clear of the 10.
