Friday Night in the Queen City: Tulane Visits a Charlotte Team Fresh Off Its First Win

Tulane Green Wave (3-4) at Charlotte 49ers (1-6)

7:00 PM ET Fri Oct 30 · Jerry Richardson Stadium · ESPN2

Tulane (3-4, 1-2 American) at Charlotte (1-6, 1-2 American). Friday, Oct. 30, 7 p.m. ET (6 p.m. Central), Jerry Richardson Stadium, Charlotte, N.C., ESPN2.

Your hosts are Twan Ducros-Labat, an Uptown Tulane fan who believes every game is a brass-band parade, and Priya Halloran, an uptown Charlotte bank risk analyst who prices every drive like a loan.

Twan: Darlin', I brought beignets for the whole section. Roll Wave!

Priya: Price that drive, Twan. Today the beignets are on the house, and the interest rate is friendly.

Players to watch

Charlotte quarterback Conner Harrell went 23 of 31 for 281 yards and two touchdowns at Temple and ran for two more scores. Running back Henry Rutledge leads the team with 331 rushing yards, and wide receiver Jaden Barnes leads the receivers with 40 catches for 396 yards. On defense, linebacker Gavin Willis has 53 tackles and DJ Burgess has two sacks.

For Tulane, freshman quarterback Trace Johnson has thrown the most passes in each of the last two games. Running back Johnnie Daniels has 394 yards and four touchdowns on 66 carries, and wide receiver Destyn Hill has 26 catches for 339 yards. Linebacker Dickson Agu leads the team with 64 tackles, and defensive lineman Ed Smith IV has three sacks.

Twan: Sixty-four tackles from Agu. That man shows up to every parade.

What happened last week

Charlotte had the week off after beating Temple 38-31 on Oct. 17 at Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia. It was the 49ers' first win of the season and the first American Conference win for coach Tim Albin. Rutledge ran for a career-high 123 yards, Derrick Eley caught six passes for 96 yards, and Aiden Bui, kicking for the first time in his career, hit a 47-yard field goal in the final two minutes.

Tulane lost 42-19 to UTSA at home on Oct. 24. Johnson went 24 of 44 for 265 yards, and Daniels ran eight times for 104 yards and a touchdown.

Priya: A stand at the two-yard mark, four stops in a row, before halftime at Temple. I'd frame that drive and hang it in the lobby.

Injuries and availability

The American has not posted an availability report for this game. Tulane tackle Ryan Mickow, who was hurt against UTSA, will be unavailable Friday, according to nola.com. Sophomore Reese Baker has moved between tackle and guard and has been the one constant among the big guys up front. No injury news has surfaced for either starting quarterback, so watch the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Will Hall is in his first season at Tulane. He was hired on Dec. 8, 2025. After the UTSA game he said, "I'm glad it's a short week. We know we've got to play better." He also called Charlotte "explosive on offense."

Tim Albin is in his second season at Charlotte. He went 30-12 in his last three years at Ohio before taking over the 49ers.

Twan: Coach Hall wants a clean game. No fumbles, no fuss, just a good parade.

Player storylines

Harrell made his second start of the season at Temple after dealing with injuries, and he looked sharp, with four different receivers going over 50 yards. Tulane's freshman quarterback, meanwhile, threw for nearly 200 yards in the first half against UTSA. "Trace was very poised," Hill said.

Tulane's Jah'rie Garner, who chases quarterbacks for a living, is staying humble. "I see them as a faceless opponent," he said. "In this league you can beat anybody."

Priya: Two quarterbacks, both trending up. That's a healthy portfolio.

What's happening on campus

Jerry Richardson Stadium opened in 2013 and has a listed capacity of 15,300. The Pride of Niner Nation Marching Band will be there, and if you visit, ask somebody about the 49ers name, which dates to the school's founding in 1946.

Twan: Fifteen thousand three hundred? That's a cozy block party. I love it.

Anything else fun and true

Tulane won the last two meetings, 34-3 in 2024 and 27-0 in 2025. The Temple win was the 49th in Charlotte program history, so the next one will be No. 50. Tulane's three wins this season came over South Alabama, Southern Miss and Memphis.

Priya: Win No. 50 would be a milestone worth framing, Twan.

Twan: Then we'll both bring the brass band, darlin'. Roll Wave!

My call: Tulane Green Wave 31, Charlotte 49ers 19.
