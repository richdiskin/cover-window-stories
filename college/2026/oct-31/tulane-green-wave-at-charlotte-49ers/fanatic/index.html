Tulane vs Charlotte: Krewe of BOO Meets a Haunted Friday

Tulane Green Wave (3-4) at Charlotte 49ers (1-6)

7:00 PM ET Fri Oct 30 · Jerry Richardson Stadium · ESPN2

Tulane Green Wave vs Charlotte 49ers lands on the Friday night of Week 9, six days after the Krewe of BOO! rolled up Canal Street in New Orleans while Tulane fan Twan Ducros-Labat sat Uptown at Yulman Stadium watching his team lose to UTSA. Charlotte fan Priya Halloran, a bank risk analyst who works in Uptown Charlotte, has spent the week telling Twan that he chose the wrong parade, and that Conner Harrell and a rested 49ers team are about to give Trace Johnson the scariest night of Halloween weekend.

Twan: Darlin', the Krewe had an Interview with the Vampire float for the book's fiftieth birthday, and they were throwing Zapp's and CheeWees, and I was in the student section watching six turnovers.

Priya: Six turnovers is the kind of number that gets a loan officer fired, Twan. Meanwhile, Charlotte had two weeks off to admire a win.

That win is the reason Priya is smiling. On Oct. 17 at Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia, Charlotte beat Temple 38-31 for its first win of the season and the first American win for coach Tim Albin. Harrell went 23 of 31 for 281 yards and two touchdowns and ran for two more on quarterback draws. Henry Rutledge ran for a career-high 123 yards, Derrick Eley caught six balls for 96, and Aiden Bui, kicking in his first career game, hit a 47-yard field goal in the final two minutes. DJ Burgess added a fourth-down sack.

Priya: Four straight stops at the two before halftime. I priced that sequence at zero risk and I'd do it again.

Tulane's Saturday went the other way. UTSA won 42-19 at Yulman, running for 223 yards and six touchdowns. Johnson, a freshman running the offense for only the second time, went 24 of 44 for 265 yards with two interceptions, and Johnnie Daniels ran eight times for 104 yards and a score. Four fumbles hurt before the interceptions did.

Twan: Destyn Hill said, "Trace was very poised," and he was, for a half. A good brass band doesn't stop because one song went sour.

Both coaches are honest about where things stand. Will Hall, hired Dec. 8, 2025, is in his first season at Tulane. "I'm glad it's a short week. We know we've got to play better," he told nola.com. He also called Charlotte "explosive on offense." Albin won 30 games in his last three years at Ohio and is in his second season in Charlotte.

Nola.com reported that Tulane tackle Ryan Mickow, carted off against UTSA, will be unavailable Friday. Sophomore Reese Baker has bounced between tackle and guard and is the only Tulane blocker who has been in the first group up front in every game. Hall's words: "We're really banged up there."

Priya: A banged-up front facing a rested defense. My department calls that exposure.

Charlotte's numbers still look like a ledger in the red. The 49ers are last in the American in scoring and in scoring defense, giving up 40.6 points a game. Rutledge leads them with 331 rushing yards, Jaden Barnes has 40 catches for 396 yards, and on defense Gavin Willis has 53 tackles. Tulane brings Daniels with 394 yards and four touchdowns, Hill with 26 catches for 339 yards, Dickson Agu with 64 tackles and Ed Smith IV with three sacks. Jah'rie Garner, who chases quarterbacks for Tulane, said it plainly: "I see them as a faceless opponent."

Twan: Faceless is good. It's Halloween weekend. Everybody's in a mask.

Priya's city has its own haunted Friday. Mecklenburg County runs Ray's Haunted Tour and Halloween Carnival at Ray's Splash Planet from 6 to 9 p.m. ET, Uptown's official Halloween crawl checks people in at Encore Nightclub, and the Rural Hill Corn Maze in Huntersville is in its last two days.

Priya: I'll be at Jerry Richardson Stadium. It has 15,300 seats, the Pride of Niner Nation Marching Band and, for once, a team that won its last game.

Twan: Tulane won the last two of these, 34-3 in 2024 and 27-0 in 2025. Your band played a lot of halftime shows in those games without a touchdown to celebrate.

Priya: And the Temple win was the 49th in program history. The next one is No. 50, and I'd like it on a Friday night against a team from New Orleans.

Twan: Then we'll do it like a krewe, darlin'. You bring the band, I'll bring the throws, and the Green Wave will be the ones rolling home on Saturday. Roll Wave!

My call: Tulane Green Wave 31, Charlotte 49ers 19.
