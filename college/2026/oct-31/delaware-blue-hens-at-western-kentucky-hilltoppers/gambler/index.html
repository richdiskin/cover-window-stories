Delaware Blue Hens at Western Kentucky Hilltoppers

Delaware Blue Hens (3-4) at Western Kentucky Hilltoppers (3-4)

7:00 PM ET Tue Oct 27 · Houchens Industries-L.T. Smith Stadium · CBSSN

Thurman "Tops" Whitsitt has cut hair in Warren County for three decades, and he saved a chair for Hen Lofland. The Newark librarian is not sitting down. She just watched her quarterback throw five touchdown passes.

Delaware (3-4) at Western Kentucky (3-4), Tuesday at 7:00 p.m. ET on CBSSN at Houchens Industries-L.T. Smith Stadium in Bowling Green. It's a Conference USA game. DraftKings has Western Kentucky Hilltoppers −1.5 with a total of 55.5. My card: Western Kentucky Hilltoppers −1.5, Over 55.5, and a final of Western Kentucky Hilltoppers 30, Delaware Blue Hens 27.

Hen speaks for Delaware, and she has a new entry for the card catalog. "Nick Minicucci is back on the shelf where he belongs. He went 20 of 29 for 225 yards and five touchdown passes in a 61-38 win over Missouri State, and Jo Silver ran for 101."

Tops speaks for Western Kentucky, and he keeps sweeping while he talks. "Hen, darlin', we've won two straight in this league. We beat Missouri State 34-13 and went to Huntsville and won 16-3. And Jaylen Lewis has 56 tackles. That man never misses a customer."

The honest part: Delaware scores 28.3 a game, WKU allows 30.6. But WKU gave up 49, 70 and 38 at Nevada, Georgia and Indiana, then 13 and 3 in its last two.

Why Western Kentucky? Home field, a defense that has found itself, and a Delaware ground game that runs for 3.6 yards a carry. In The Trenches is on the Hilltoppers for exactly that reason. The market agrees: DraftKings opened WKU at +1.5, and WKU lays the 1.5 as of Oct 27, 2026, 4:44 PM ET. My final has WKU by three, clear of the 1.5.

The worry is the Hilltopper quarterback room. Rodney Tisdale Jr. leads in passing with 857 yards, but Brock Glenn threw the most passes in each of the last two games, and he went 14 of 30 at Sam Houston. If WKU stalls and Minicucci gets hot again, a field goal decides it.

Why the Over? Delaware has scored 61 and 30 in its last two games, and the Blue Hens' defense gives up 28.4 a game. Missouri State put 38 on it last week. My 30-27 adds up to 57, a point and a half over 55.5.

Now the room. Next Man Up is the loudest Hilltopper fan here, with WKU 31-26 and the Over. The Formula has WKU 28-27, a one-point win, so it takes Delaware +1.5 and the Under. Follow The Money and In The Trenches both like WKU, and both say Under. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up started it: "The Formula, you've got the Hilltoppers winning and you're taking the points? That's paying for the haircut and skipping the trim."

The Formula didn't flinch: "One point is one point. Ask Delaware about 33-30 at Middle Tennessee."

Follow The Money chimed in: "I'm with you on the side, Next Man Up. On the total, you're on your own."

Bottom of the card: a home team that has won two straight in the league, last season's 27-24 win in this series, and a road offense that has to throw. Tops gets his chair back. Give me the Hilltoppers and the Over.

Western Kentucky Hilltoppers 30, Delaware Blue Hens 27. Western Kentucky Hilltoppers cover the 1.5. Over 55.5.
