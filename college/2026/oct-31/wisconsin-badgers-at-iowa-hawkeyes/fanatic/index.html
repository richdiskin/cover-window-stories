Halloween Noon at Kinnick: The Polka Comes to Iowa City as No. 25 Wisconsin Meets the Hawkeyes

Wisconsin Badgers (5-2) at Iowa Hawkeyes (5-2)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Kinnick Stadium · Fox

No. 25 Wisconsin (5-2, 3-1 Big Ten) at Iowa (5-2, 2-2 Big Ten). Saturday, Oct. 31, 12 p.m. ET, Kinnick Stadium, Iowa City, Iowa, Fox.

Your hosts are Lars "Brat" Ellefson, a former Badgers fullback who carries a cheese wedge to every game and talks to a polka beat, and Orrin Kalvelage, an Iowa farm-country Hawkeye who wears the same sweater every week.

Brat: (Oom-pah, oom-pah.) Hiya, Orrin! I brought curds for the whole tailgate. Squeaky fresh.

Orrin: Thank you kindly, Lars. I brought my sweater. It's the same one. It's always the same one.

Players to watch

Wisconsin quarterback Colton Joseph has thrown the most passes in all seven Badger games, and he's also the team's leading rusher. He's 99 of 173 for 1,256 yards, 11 touchdowns and five interceptions, with 365 rushing yards and four rushing scores. Tyrell Henry leads the receivers with 28 catches for 343 yards. On defense, linebacker Cooper Catalano has 60 tackles and linebacker Sebastian Cheeks has five sacks.

Iowa quarterback Hank Brown has 968 passing yards, six touchdowns and three interceptions, and he has thrown the most passes in six of Iowa's seven games. Jeremy Hecklinski did it in the 41-24 win at Washington. Running back Kamari Moulton leads the team with 541 rushing yards and four scores. Tony Diaz leads the receivers with 29 catches for 386 yards. Linebacker Cam Buffington has 62 tackles, and end Joseph Anderson has four sacks.

Orrin: Sixty-two tackles. Cam Buffington is the kind of linebacker who'd help you fix a fence and then stay for supper.

What happened last week

Wisconsin beat USC 24-7 at Camp Randall on Oct. 24. Joseph went 12 of 14 with a touchdown throw, and Abu Sama III ran 17 times for 71 yards.

Brat: (Oom-pah!) I jumped around so hard my cheese wedge fell off. Twice.

Iowa lost 13-10 at Minnesota on Oct. 24. Brown threw a touchdown throw to Reece Vander Zee, who caught four passes for 69 yards.

Orrin: A three-point loss on the road. We'll be fine. The sweater has been through worse.

Injuries and availability

No official availability report has been posted for this game yet, and box scores don't mark starters. Joseph has thrown the most passes in every Wisconsin game, and Brown did for Iowa last week, so check the teams' pregame notes.

What the coaches are up to

Kirk Ferentz is in his 28th season at Iowa, the longest current tenure in FBS, and he's the Big Ten's all-time leader in wins. He's led the Hawkeyes since 1999, and before that he was head coach at Maine from 1990 to 1992. His defense allows 14.3 points a game this season.

Luke Fickell was hired at Wisconsin on Nov. 27, 2022. His Badgers allow 15.3 points a game, and they've already won 24-20 at Penn State and beaten Michigan State 31-3.

Brat: Two coaches who love defense. This game might be decided by a punt. (Oom-pah.)

Orrin: Lars, a good punt is a love letter to your defense.

Player storylines

The matchup to circle is the Iowa run game against the Wisconsin defense. The Hawkeyes gain 5.7 yards a carry, and the Badgers allow just 2.8. On the other side, Joseph and the Wisconsin big guys up front face an Iowa defense that allows only 2.4 yards a carry. Somebody's ground game is going to have a long afternoon.

What's happening on campus

Kinnick Stadium seats 69,250, and a Halloween noon kickoff in Iowa City should bring out plenty of black-and-gold costumes. Iowa has won 13 conference championships, 11 in the Big Ten, and has been ranked No. 1 in the AP and Coaches polls 15 times. Wisconsin's home, Camp Randall Stadium, opened in 1917 and is the fourth-oldest stadium in college football.

Orrin: I'm dressing up as myself. In the sweater.

Anything else fun and true

Wisconsin has played in the Big Ten since the conference formed in 1896, and it's one of 26 programs with 700 or more wins. Iowa has been steady as a fence post, with records of 10-4, 8-5, 10-4, 8-5 and 9-4 over the last five seasons. This rivalry is as even as it gets lately. In the last ten regular-season meetings, each team has won five, and Iowa won 37-0 last season.

Brat: Orrin, whatever happens, come by the tailgate after. We'll say grace and have a brat.

Orrin: I'll be there, Lars. Go Hawks.

My call: Iowa Hawkeyes 22, Wisconsin Badgers 17.
