Wisconsin vs Iowa: Halloween Homecoming and the 100th Meeting

Wisconsin Badgers (5-2) at Iowa Hawkeyes (5-2)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Kinnick Stadium · Fox

Wisconsin Badgers vs Iowa Hawkeyes is a Week 9 Halloween Homecoming Black Out at Kinnick Stadium, the 100th meeting between the two schools, with the Heartland Trophy at stake and Iowa hosting the Badgers for Homecoming for the first time since 1988. Lars "Brat" Ellefson, a former Badgers fullback who talks to a polka beat, says Colton Joseph and Luke Fickell's No. 25 Badgers will take the trophy back to Madison, and Orrin Kalvelage, a farm-country Hawkeye in the same sweater every week, says Kirk Ferentz's Hawkeyes aren't giving it up.

Brat: (Oom-pah, oom-pah.) Hiya, Orrin! Hundredth meeting, and we lead it 49-48-2. I brought curds for the whole tailgate.

Orrin: Thank you kindly, Lars. We've won four straight and outscored you 118-26. I brought my sweater. It's black today, same as everybody's.

Iowa's game notes lay out the history. The series is 49-48-2 Wisconsin. The Heartland Trophy began in 2004, was designed and crafted by former Iowa player Frank Strub, and has stayed in Iowa City since 2022. The last time Iowa hosted Wisconsin for Homecoming, in 1988, the Hawkeyes won 31-6. Iowa has also sold out 32 straight games at Kinnick.

Halloween adds a few house rules. Iowa says costumes are welcome, but no prop weapons, and no costumes that could be mistaken for police or game officials.

Brat: No referee costumes? There goes my whole plan. I was gonna throw a flag on Orrin's sweater.

Orrin: That's why they made the rule, Lars.

Wisconsin arrives after a 24-7 win over USC at Camp Randall on Oct. 24. Joseph went 12 of 14 with a touchdown throw, and Abu Sama III ran 17 times for 71 yards. Joseph has thrown the most passes in all seven Badger games and leads the team in rushing. He's 99 of 173 for 1,256 yards, 11 touchdowns and five interceptions, with 365 rushing yards and four rushing scores. Tyrell Henry leads the receivers with 28 catches for 343 yards. On defense, Cooper Catalano has 60 tackles and Sebastian Cheeks has five sacks.

Brat: (Oom-pah!) USC came to Madison and left with seven. I jumped so hard my cheese wedge fell off. Twice.

Iowa lost 13-10 at Minnesota on Oct. 24. Hank Brown threw a touchdown to Reece Vander Zee, who caught four balls for 69 yards. Iowa's notes say Brown and Jeremy Hecklinski split reps in that game, and Hecklinski led the 41-24 win at Washington. Brown has 968 passing yards, six touchdowns and three interceptions. Kamari Moulton leads the team with 541 rushing yards and four scores, and Tony Diaz has 29 catches for 386 yards. On defense, Cam Buffington has 62 tackles and Joseph Anderson has four sacks.

Orrin: Sixty-two tackles. Cam Buffington would help you fix a fence and then stay for supper.

No official availability report is out for this game.

These are two of the stingiest defenses in the Big Ten. Iowa allows 14.3 points a game and Wisconsin allows 15.3. Iowa gains 5.7 yards a carry against a Badger defense that allows 2.8, and Wisconsin's ground game runs into an Iowa defense that allows 2.4. Somebody's running backs are going to have a long afternoon.

Ferentz, in his 28th season, is the longest-tenured active head coach in college football and the winningest coach in Big Ten history. Fickell, hired on Nov. 27, 2022, has already won 24-20 at Penn State this year and beaten Michigan State 31-3.

Brat: Two coaches who love defense. This one might come down to a punt. (Oom-pah.)

Orrin: Lars, a good punt is a love letter to your defense. Our punter's from Hope Island, Australia. He writes long letters.

Wisconsin has played in the Big Ten since 1896, and Camp Randall, opened in 1917, is the fourth-oldest stadium in college football. Iowa won last year's meeting 37-0 and has won 12 of its last 16 Homecoming games.

Brat: Ninety-nine games and we still lead. That's a Wisconsin winter of patience, Orrin.

Orrin: Come by after the game, Lars. We'll say grace, have a brat, and you can visit the trophy. It likes it here.

My call: Iowa Hawkeyes 22, Wisconsin Badgers 17.
