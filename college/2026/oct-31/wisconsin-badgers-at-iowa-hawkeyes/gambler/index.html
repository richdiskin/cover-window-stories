Wisconsin Badgers at Iowa Hawkeyes

Wisconsin Badgers (5-2) at Iowa Hawkeyes (5-2)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 31 · Kinnick Stadium · Fox

Brat Ellefson squeezes a polka out of his accordion in the Kinnick parking lot, oom-pah, oom-pah. Orrin Kalvelage, in the same black-and-gold sweater he's worn every week, waits politely for the song to end and then says, "Lars, we beat you 37-0 last year."

No. 25 Wisconsin (5-2) at Iowa (5-2), Saturday at 12:00 p.m. ET on Fox at Kinnick Stadium in Iowa City. It's a Big Ten game. DraftKings has Iowa Hawkeyes −3.5 with a total of 36.5. My card: Iowa Hawkeyes −3.5, Over 36.5, and a final of Iowa Hawkeyes 22, Wisconsin Badgers 17.

Brat speaks for Wisconsin, accordion still wheezing. "Oom-pah! We just beat USC 24-7. Colton Joseph has 11 touchdown passes and he's our leading rusher. Sebastian Cheeks has five sacks, and Cooper Catalano has 60 tackles. We allow 15.3 points a game. Have some curds."

Orrin speaks for Iowa, and he's calm as a fence post. "Neighbor, we allow 14.3. Kamari Moulton has 541 rushing yards, and Cam Buffington has 62 tackles. We won 41-24 at Washington. A good punt is a love letter, and we've got plenty of stamps."

Why Iowa? Wisconsin's passing game has gone quiet. Joseph went 6 of 16 for 66 yards in the 17-7 loss at UCLA and 12 of 14 for 58 yards against USC. Iowa's run defense allows 2.4 yards a carry. On the road this season the Badgers lost 41-13 to Notre Dame and 17-7 at UCLA, with a 24-20 win at Penn State in between. Iowa gains 5.7 yards a carry. My final has Iowa by five, past the 3.5.

The worry is the Iowa passing game. Hank Brown threw two interceptions in the 13-10 loss at Minnesota last week, and the Hawkeyes scored just 10. Wisconsin's run defense allows 2.8 yards a carry. If Iowa can't run, a field goal decides it, and three and a half is a lot in a field-goal game.

Why the Over? DraftKings had this total at 39.5 on Monday, and it has dropped to 36.5. That's an overcorrection for two teams that each score at least 27 points a game. Iowa put up 41 at Washington, and Wisconsin scored 31 against Michigan State. The last two meetings, 42-10 and 37-0, both cleared 36.5. My 22-17 adds up to 39, two and a half points over 36.5.

Now the room, and it's split every which way. The Formula has Iowa 22-20, takes Wisconsin +3.5 and likes the Over. Next Man Up has Iowa 21-14, sides with me on Iowa but goes Under. Follow The Money takes Wisconsin +3.5 and the Under, because both numbers moved. In The Trenches says the run-game gaps cancel, and Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up started it: "Iowa by a touchdown. I'll say it to Brat's face."

The Formula raised a hand: "Iowa by two. That's why I take the points."

Follow The Money shrugged: "The market dropped the total three points. Somebody knows something."

Bottom of the card: a home defense that allows 2.4 yards a carry, a Badger passing game stuck in neutral, and a total that fell too far. Orrin gets his love letter. Give me the Hawkeyes and the Over.

Iowa Hawkeyes 22, Wisconsin Badgers 17. Iowa Hawkeyes cover the 3.5. Over 36.5.
