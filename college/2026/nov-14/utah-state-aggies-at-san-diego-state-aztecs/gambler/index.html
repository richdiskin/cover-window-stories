Utah State vs San Diego State Pick: Aztecs +1.5, Over 41.5

Utah State Aggies (4-5) at San Diego State Aztecs (4-5)

Utah State vs San Diego State prediction: two 4-5 Mountain West teams meet at Snapdragon Stadium in San Diego for San Diego State's Senior Night, and it's a late one, 9:30 p.m. ET Saturday. I'm on San Diego State Aztecs +1.5 and Over 41.5, with San Diego State Aztecs 24, Utah State Aggies 21 as my final.

Kai Delgado-Sorensen, the Ocean Beach surfer, likes the setup. "We're 4-1 at home. We had last week off. Javion Kinnard has 613 rushing yards, and Sione Hala has 90 tackles." Hyrum Tolleson, who milks cows outside Logan before every road trip, has the series on his side. "We've won four of the last six meetings, including 41-20 the last time. We've won two straight, and our defense allows 20.6."

The side comes from Utah State on the road. The Aggies are 0-4 away from Logan this year: 16-14 at Washington, 33-0 at Utah, 37-18 at Boise State and 20-17 at Texas State. They score 16.9 points a game, and they haven't topped 18 on the road all season. San Diego State has won four of five at home, including 26-23 over Fresno State and 26-20 over Washington State in its last home game. The Aztecs also got the extra rest, last playing Oct. 31. The market opened Utah State −2.5 and slid to −1.5. I have the Aztecs winning outright by three.

The Over comes from the San Diego State side of the scoreboard. Aztec games have finished above 41.5 combined points in seven of nine, and the defense allows 34.3 a game. Utah State's offense is the problem, but Javen Jacobs has 527 rushing yards and McCae Hillstead has taken over at quarterback in the last two games, both wins. The posted number dropped from 44.5 to 41.5. My 45 combined points clear it.

The risk is the Utah State defense. The Aggies held Fresno State to 10 last week and Colorado State to 14 before that. If they turn this into a 17-14 game, the Over dies and Utah State could finally get its first road win. San Diego State's quarterback spot has also moved between Jayden Denegal and Stone Saunders, and Saunders threw for only 102 yards in the last game.

The cards are split down the middle. The Formula has Utah State Aggies −1.5 and likes the Over at Utah State 26-24. Next Man Up likes San Diego State Aztecs +1.5 and likes the Under at San Diego State 20-18. Letdown Watch has Utah State Aggies −1.5 and likes the Over. In The Trenches likes San Diego State Aztecs +1.5 and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Next Man Up, to The Formula: "Twenty to eighteen, home team, Senior Night. Easy." The Formula: "Easy for a team that scored 14 at Oregon State." San Diego State is at 26.4 points per game this season.

San Diego State Aztecs 24, Utah State Aggies 21. San Diego State Aztecs cover the 1.5. Over 41.5.
