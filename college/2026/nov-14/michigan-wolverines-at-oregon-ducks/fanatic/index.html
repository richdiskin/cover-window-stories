Michigan vs Oregon: Eugene Wears Green for the Wolverines

Michigan Wolverines (5-4) at Oregon Ducks (6-3)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Autzen Stadium · FS1

XFacebook

Eugene has its Saturday dress code posted: Oregon's game page for Michigan Wolverines vs Oregon Ducks in Week 11 says "Wear Green," and the Lane County Farmers Market will be selling greens downtown until 2 p.m., two hours after the 12:30 p.m. Pacific kickoff at Autzen Stadium. Oregon insider Phoenix "Nix" Wyatt-Aldana has a custom green jacket ready, and Michigan insider Augustin "Gus" Hartwell has packed a maize sweater and a spreadsheet that says Dylan Raiola is about to face one of the stingiest defenses in the country.

Nix: Gus, welcome to the greenest stadium in America. Look at it. The whole crowd is one color story. Ten out of ten before anybody even kicks off.

Gus: Nix, I respect the fashion. But I'm an engineer. I don't care what color the crowd wears. I care that our defense has allowed 16.2 points a game.

This is only the seventh meeting between these two programs, and the all-time series is tied at three wins apiece, according to Michigan's weekly notes. Michigan won the first three, in 1948, 1960 and 1973. Oregon won the last three, in 2003, 2007 and 2024, when the Ducks went into Michigan Stadium and won 38-17. Michigan is 0-1 all time at Autzen.

Gus: Three and three. That's a perfectly balanced equation. I would like to solve for x on Saturday.

Nix: Solve for green, Gus. The answer is always green.

Oregon comes in feeling like it just walked a runway in Paris. The Ducks won 41-28 at Ohio State last Saturday. Raiola went 25 of 40 for 257 yards and two touchdowns, Dierre Hill Jr. ran for 75 yards and two scores, and Dakorien Moore caught a touchdown among his seven grabs.

Nix: Columbus was a flawless look. Clean throws, sharp cuts, two Hill touchdowns. I'd frame that game film and hang it in the studio.

Gus: And I'd frame our defense. Nobody has scored more than 21 on us all season. Michigan's notes say we're fourth in the nation in yards allowed, and the Associated Press says we're the 15th team in the last 15 seasons to hold every one of its first nine opponents to 21 or fewer.

That defense is the real thing. Linebacker Troy Bowles leads it with 72 tackles, and John Henry Daley has six sacks and 9.0 tackles for loss. The offense is where Gus begins staring at the ceiling. Michigan scores 19.6 points a game, and it lost to Michigan State 15-6 last week. Tommy Carr, whose first game as the No. 1 quarterback came against Indiana, went 14 of 26 for 138 yards with two interceptions against the Spartans.

Gus: Tommy Carr went 22 of 27 at Rutgers two weeks ago. Michigan's notes call it the best completion rate ever for a freshman in that role at Michigan. I have the decimal points.

Nix: I have the scoreboard. Six points last week. That's a sample-size problem and a style problem.

Michigan's best weapon in Nix's nightmares is JJ Buchanan, who has 40 catches for 646 yards and five touchdowns. Andrew Marsh has 44 catches. Oregon's defense lives on Matayo Uiagalelei, who has 7.5 sacks, and Koi Perich, who leads the Ducks with 58 tackles.

Nix: Matayo is couture. He shows up in the backfield like he was invited.

Gus: Buchanan will make him change outfits twice.

The Ducks have scored 34.1 points a game this season. They also have a home loss to UCLA, 17-15, in a game where the offense never found its stride, and Gus has written that score on an index card.

Gus: UCLA got you at home. Every engineer knows a structure can crack.

Nix: And every designer knows you don't wear last month's outfit. We beat Nebraska and Northwestern at Autzen after that.

Here is the matchup that decides it. If Michigan's defense keeps Raiola from hitting Moore downfield, the Wolverines can drag this into the fourth quarter. If Oregon gets Hill going early, Gus's equation runs out of variables.

Gus: Hail to the Victors, Nix. Off-key, but proud.

Nix: Sing it at the farmers market. They're open till two. We'll be busy after that.

My call: Oregon Ducks 29, Michigan Wolverines 15.
