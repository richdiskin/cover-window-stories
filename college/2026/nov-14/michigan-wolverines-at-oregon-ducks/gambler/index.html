Michigan vs Oregon Prediction: Ducks −10.5, Over 39.5

Michigan Wolverines (5-4) at Oregon Ducks (6-3)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Autzen Stadium · FS1

XFacebook

Oregon's game page carries two words for Saturday, "Wear Green," and Eugene will show up that way at Autzen Stadium. The Michigan vs Oregon spread is Oregon −10.5 with a 39.5 over/under, and my Week 11 pick is Oregon Ducks −10.5 with the Over 39.5.

Phoenix "Nix" Wyatt-Aldana, the Eugene sneaker designer who grades route trees like runway looks, is still glowing from Columbus. "We won 41-28 at Ohio State. Dylan Raiola threw for 257 yards, and Dierre Hill Jr. scored twice. Ten out of ten, uniform and execution."

Augustin "Gus" Hartwell, Michigan's maize-sweater stats savant, has a different exhibit. "Our defense has allowed 16.2 points a game and has not given up more than 21 all season. Michigan Monday says we're fourth in the country in yards allowed."

Gus is right, and that's why the over/under crashed. DraftKings opened this game at Oregon −13.5 with 53.5 on the over/under, and it now sits at −10.5 and 39.5, a 14-point drop on the over/under. I think the market went too far. Michigan's offense is the problem in this matchup, not its defense. Tommy Carr went 14 of 26 with two interceptions in a 6-15 loss to Michigan State, and the Wolverines have scored 19.6 points a game.

That's also my side. Oregon scores 34.1 points a game, and its offense has scored 34 or more in five of nine games. Raiola has 11 touchdown throws against two interceptions, and Dakorien Moore has seven receiving scores. Michigan has dropped two of its last three, and in losses to Indiana and Michigan State it scored six points each time. Asking that offense to keep pace on the road at Autzen is a tall order.

My final is Oregon Ducks 29, Michigan Wolverines 15. That's a 14-point margin, past the 10.5, and 44 combined, over the 39.5.

Here's the room. The Formula has Oregon 29-18, sits on Oregon −10.5 and likes the Over. Next Man Up likes Oregon −10.5 at 30-11 and has the Over. Letdown Watch has Oregon −10.5 at 29-17 and likes the Over. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call here. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Next Man Up: "Thirty to eleven. The Formula gave Michigan seven extra points for good manners."

Letdown Watch: "Next Man Up thinks Michigan's offense took a red-eye and never landed. It still has JJ Buchanan."

Buchanan, with 646 receiving yards, is the one Wolverine who can change my math, and John Henry Daley's six sacks can make Raiola uncomfortable. I still think Oregon gets to 29, and Michigan's defense can't win this alone.

Green is the dress code in Eugene. The Ducks look ready to match it with a double-digit win.

Oregon Ducks 29, Michigan Wolverines 15. Oregon Ducks cover the 10.5. Over 39.5.
