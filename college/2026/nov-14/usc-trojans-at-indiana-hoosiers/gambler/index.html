USC vs Indiana Prediction: Hoosiers −7.5, Over 49.5

USC Trojans (7-2) at Indiana Hoosiers (8-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Memorial Stadium (Bloomington, IN) · CBS

XFacebook

Bloomington has a full Saturday planned, with the musical SIX running matinee and evening shows at IU Auditorium on either side of a 3:30 kickoff at Memorial Stadium. In the USC vs Indiana spread battle, I'm on Indiana Hoosiers −7.5 and the Over 49.5, with a Week 11 crowd chasing something that has never happened.

The Daily Hoosier puts it plainly: Indiana has never beaten USC. Mallory Penhallow, the former pageant runner-up who keeps a 40-tab spreadsheet on the Hoosiers, has highlighted that sentence in crimson. "Tobi Osunsanmi has 10 sacks. Josh Hoover has 19 touchdowns and two interceptions. Grace in victory, Sterling. We'll practice."

Sterling Ashworth-Vance, the USC film-school grad, answers in trailer voice. "In a world where Indiana hasn't scored 24 since early October, one quarterback has thrown for 2,524 yards and 20 touchdowns. Jayden Maiava. Coming to Bloomington."

He has a point about the recent scripts. Indiana beat Michigan 13-6 in overtime and Minnesota 23-16 in overtime, and Hoover went 8 of 18 and 8 of 19 in those games. The market noticed. DraftKings opened Indiana −10.5 with 58.5 on the over/under, and it's down to −7.5 and 49.5.

That move is why I like both sides of my ticket at these prices. Indiana's defense allows 12.4 points a game, the best number on this field, and Osunsanmi is the most disruptive player in the game. USC lost 7-24 at Wisconsin, its only road loss, against the kind of defense it sees again here. Both teams had last week off, so nobody gets a rest bump.

The Over is about talent over recent box scores. Indiana still scores 32.4 points a game, and Charlie Becker has 875 receiving yards and nine scores, with eight catches for 149 against Minnesota. Turbo Richard has 745 rushing yards. USC scores 32.4 too, gives up 24.2 and has King Miller's 680 rushing yards plus Trent Mosley, who had 101 receiving yards against Ohio State. A total that fell nine points from its open is a gift if either offense wakes up, and I think Indiana's does.

My final is Indiana Hoosiers 31, USC Trojans 20. That's 11 points, past the 7.5, and 51 combined, over the 49.5.

The room lines up with me for once. The Formula has Indiana 29-22 and sits on Indiana −7.5 and the Over. Next Man Up has the same side and Over at 30-21. Letdown Watch likes Indiana −7.5 at 33-17 and has the Over. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call this week. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Letdown Watch: "Thirty-three to seventeen. The Formula left seven points on the IU Auditorium stage."

The Formula: "Letdown Watch thinks Paddy McAteer bails Indiana out every week. He did it once."

The queens of SIX get two curtain calls Saturday. Indiana gets one chance at a first, and I think the Hoosiers take it with the defense doing the singing.

Indiana Hoosiers 31, USC Trojans 20. Indiana Hoosiers cover the 7.5. Over 49.5.
