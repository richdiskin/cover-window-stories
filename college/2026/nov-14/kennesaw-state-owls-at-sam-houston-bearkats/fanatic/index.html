Kennesaw State at Sam Houston: Two Interim Coaches, One First Home Game in Huntsville

Kennesaw State Owls (3-6) at Sam Houston Bearkats (1-8)

2:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Elliott T. Bowers Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Bowers Stadium hosts a first Saturday: Sam Houston's first home game under co-interim head coaches Eric Raisbeck and Freddie Aughtry-Lindsay, who took over Oct. 28. The school notes that the Kats will become one of only a handful of FBS programs since 2013 with three head coaches of record in one season, and per NCAA guidance both interims get credit for the same final four games.

Huntsville has a reason to hope that has nothing to do with the record. Since moving to FBS in 2023, Sam Houston is 7-5 in November and 8-25 from August through October, per the school, and November accounted for every one of the program's wins in 2023 and 2025. The Kats are 1-8 and still looking for their first Conference USA win.

This series has a habit of going late. Sam Houston has won all three meetings, every one by seven points or fewer. There was a fourth-down stop at its own 15 with 1:24 left in the 2017 FCS quarterfinals, then Colby Sessums' 35-yard field goal at the horn in 2023 for the program's first FBS win, then a 23-17 overtime win in 2024. The defense has made the biggest jump in FBS against the throw, from 275.9 passing yards a game allowed last year to 161.4 now.

Kennesaw State comes in fresh off a 42-40 win over Delaware. Rickie Collins has 1,687 passing yards and Boogie Woods III has eight rushing touchdowns. Landyn Locke is 37 yards from moving up to 19th on Sam Houston's career passing list, and eight of his 13 touchdown passes this season have gone for 30 yards or more. Two interim coaches, one home crowd and a November habit: Huntsville gets to find out if that habit is real.

My call: Kennesaw State Owls 27, Sam Houston Bearkats 21.
