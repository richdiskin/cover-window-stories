Kennesaw State vs Sam Houston: Family Weekend Fills Bowers Stadium

Kennesaw State Owls (3-6) at Sam Houston Bearkats (1-8)

2:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Elliott T. Bowers Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Kennesaw State Owls vs Sam Houston Bearkats is Bearkat Family Weekend in Huntsville, with registered families packing Bowers Stadium for a 1 p.m. local kickoff and a Conference USA game that feels bigger on campus than the 1-8 record suggests. Lemuel "Lem" Coxwell beat the families to Bowers Stadium and is talking about Landyn like the Lowman Student Center check-in crowd is still moving. Ximena Holcombe-Reyes rides with Kennesaw State and has Boogie Woods III's name already circled.

Lem: Family Weekend means the stands are full of people who drove in for this. Landyn still has 13 touchdowns. We can make this loud.

Ximena: Woods has eight rushing scores. We just won 42-40 over Delaware. Your loud still has to stop that.

Kennesaw State is hunting a bowl résumé after that Delaware escape. Sam Houston is hunting a home win after a 14-47 loss at Jacksonville State. The Owls score 25.1 points a game and allow 27.0. The Bearkats score 17.6 and allow 27.6. That is not a pretty pair of defenses, and it is not a track meet waiting to happen either. It is a Saturday that will be decided by who finishes drives in front of relatives wearing orange.

Rickie Collins has 1,687 passing yards with seven touchdowns and seven interceptions. Brayden Munroe has 440 receiving yards. Tywon Christopher has 69 tackles. When Woods gets going, Kennesaw starts looking like the team that outlasted Delaware. When he doesn't, Collins has to live in long down-and-distance, and that is where Bowers Stadium can get chippy.

Sam Houston's counter is Landyn and home noise. Landyn has 1,779 yards. Alton McCaskill leads the rushers with 235 yards. Chris Reed has 437 receiving yards and four scores. Josh Alexander-Felton has 4 sacks. Dean Ford has 52 tackles. The Bearkats do not need to look like a first-place team. They need to look like a Family Weekend host that refuses to get run out of its own building.

Lem: Free Rec Center passes are nice. A stop near the goal is nicer.

Ximena: Family Weekend photos are nice. Woods in the end zone is nicer.

Huntsville will treat the afternoon like a reunion. Kennesaw will treat it like a résumé builder. One of those moods usually wins in November, and Bowers will decide which one before the families head back to the hotels.

Lem will talk Landyn. Ximena will talk Woods. Family Weekend will talk about whether the home team remembered how to spoil a visitor's plans.

Bearkat Family Weekend stretches from Thursday check-in at the Lowman Student Center through Saturday football, with free Campus Rec passes for registered families and a stadium that suddenly feels fuller than a 1-8 record would suggest. That is the point of Family Weekend. The people who show up are not checking KenPom. They are checking whether the home team will play hard in front of relatives. Kennesaw State is happy to test that idea. Woods runs with unfinished business from the Delaware game, and Collins will throw it if Sam Houston loads the box.

Lem keeps talking about Landyn's touchdowns. Ximena keeps talking about Woods' scores. Bowers Stadium will decide which family goes home happier.

Conference USA games in November are rarely pretty, and this one will not be either. The winning formula is short fields, finished drives, and one defensive stand when the visitor smells blood. Sam Houston has spent most of the year searching for that formula. Kennesaw State found a version of it against Delaware. Family Weekend is about to find out which version travels.

Huntsville calls itself Huntsvegas when the weekend gets busy, and Family Weekend is the busiest kind of busy for this campus. The registered families already have their bags and schedules. The football team still has to earn the ending those schedules promised. Kennesaw State did not drive to Texas to be anybody's backdrop. Woods will keep getting the ball until somebody wraps him up cleanly, and that is the whole defensive assignment for a Bearkat team that has spent too many Saturdays searching for that wrap.

The photos will look the same either way. The final score will not.

Family Weekend photos all look the same. The bus ride home only feels good for Kennesaw State. My call: Kennesaw State Owls 27, Sam Houston Bearkats 20.
