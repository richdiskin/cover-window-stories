Bowers Stadium in a Conference USA matchup between a bowl-hunting visitor and a 1-8 home team. My Kennesaw State vs Sam Houston prediction is Kennesaw State Owls 27, Sam Houston Bearkats 20, with Sam Houston Bearkats +8.5 and Under 47.5.

Lemuel "Lem" Coxwell, the Bearkats insider, talks like the family weekend bags are already packed. "Landyn has 13 touchdowns. Family Weekend does not do blowouts if we can help it." Ximena Holcombe-Reyes, Kennesaw State's insider, answers with Boogie Woods III. "Eight rushing scores. We just survived Delaware 42-40. Your number is still too soft."

The market moved toward Kennesaw State. DraftKings opened Kennesaw State −7.5 with 44.5 on the over/under, and it went to −8.5 and 47.5. I want the points on the home side. Kennesaw State scores 25.1 a game. Sam Houston allows 27.6. That is not a runaway profile. Sam Houston scores 17.6 and just lost 14-47 at Jacksonville State, which is ugly, but 8.5 points at home on Family Weekend is still a number I will take when the visitor needed overtime energy to beat Delaware.

Rickie Collins has 1,687 passing yards for Kennesaw State. Woods has 448 rushing yards and eight scores. Ugonna Nwoha has 5.5 sacks. Sam Houston's quarterback has 1,779 yards and 13 touchdowns. Chris Reed has four receiving scores. Dean Ford has 52 tackles. One offense is trying to close a season with a bowl résumé. The other is trying to spoil Family Weekend with a home dig-in.

The Under is about Sam Houston's ceiling. The Bearkats have been held under 21 points in six of nine games. Kennesaw State's Delaware win finished at 42-40, which is the outlier, not the rule. My 47 points sit under 47.5, and the market already climbed three points off the open toward a number I still like from underneath.
