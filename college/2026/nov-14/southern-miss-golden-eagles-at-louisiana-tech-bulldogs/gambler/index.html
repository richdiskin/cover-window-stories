Southern Miss vs Louisiana Tech Pick: USM +17.5, Under 55.5

Southern Miss Golden Eagles (3-6) at Louisiana Tech Bulldogs (7-2)

5:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Joe Aillet Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Ruston is turning red on Saturday, with Louisiana Tech calling it a Red Out and a chance to clinch the Sun Belt West against an old regional rival. My Southern Miss vs Louisiana Tech pick at Joe Aillet Stadium in Week 11 is Southern Miss Golden Eagles +17.5, and I'm on the Under 55.5 as well.

Booker "Champ" Tullos grew up on a peach farm and played fullback for Tech, so he likes football that moves one hard yard at a time. "Blake Baker has 14 touchdown throws and one interception. One. He went 16 of 19 at Troy in a 41-17 win. We're 5-0 in the league, Dee."

Dee Broussard scouts the Golden Eagles from the Hattiesburg stands and smiles while she pushes back. "Brag all you want, Champ. We beat Louisiana 35-31. Y'all beat them 32-31. And Steven Robinson has 782 rushing yards and ten touchdowns."

Dee's point matters. Louisiana Tech is 7-2 and the better team, but its league wins over Army and Louisiana came by two points and one point. The Bulldogs score 33.6 points a game and allow 20.4, and Alonzo Jackson Jr. has nine sacks and 68 tackles. That defense also has two shutouts, 24-0 at UL Monroe and 26-0 over Old Dominion.

Southern Miss is 3-6, and the 23-54 loss to James Madison was ugly. The Golden Eagles allow 33.6 points a game. But they have a real runner in Robinson, a real target in Mario Sanders, who has 698 receiving yards, and a tackling machine in Mathis Haygood, with 83 stops. They lost 21-24 at Tulane, and they won 35-21 at UL Monroe.

The series has been tight for a decade. Southern Miss won four of the seven meetings in that stretch, including 21-20 in 2018, and Louisiana Tech won 31-30 in 2020 before last year's 30-20. Dee will tell you three of those games were decided by seven points or fewer.

My final is Louisiana Tech Bulldogs 35, Southern Miss Golden Eagles 20. Tech wins comfortably, and fifteen points stays short of 17.5.

On the over/under, I'm on the Under 55.5. DraftKings opened it at 54.5 and it has moved up a point. Louisiana Tech has held four of its last five opponents under 21, and Baker doesn't give the ball away. My 35-20 lands at 55, half a point under.

The room is split down the middle. The Formula likes Southern Miss +17.5 and has the Under at 35-20. In The Trenches likes Southern Miss +17.5 at 29-22 and makes no over/under call. Letdown Watch has Southern Miss +17.5 and the Under at 36-19. Next Man Up likes Louisiana Tech −17.5 and likes the Over at 39-19. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Next Man Up: "Thirty-nine points. Baker throws one interception a season. Y'all are scared of a shadow."

In The Trenches: "Twenty-nine to twenty-two. I'm scared of nothing. I'm just realistic."

Champ wants a clinched division and a peach cobbler on Sunday. Dee wants her Golden Eagles to make Ruston sweat. I think Tech wins and Southern Miss keeps it inside the number.

Louisiana Tech Bulldogs 35, Southern Miss Golden Eagles 20. Southern Miss Golden Eagles cover the 17.5. Under 55.5.
