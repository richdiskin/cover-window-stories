Utah vs Arizona: Stripe the Sol for the Unbeaten Utes

Utah Utes (9-0) at Arizona Wildcats (7-2)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Casino Del Sol Stadium · Fox

XFacebook

Utah Utes vs Arizona Wildcats is the biggest night Casino Del Sol Stadium has had in a long time. No. 3 Utah, unbeaten at 9-0, comes to Tucson to face No. 22 Arizona at 5:30 p.m. local time, and the school says it's the highest-ranked opponent to visit since 2007. The ask for the crowd is "Stripe the Sol," with sections assigned colors so the whole bowl comes out striped. Tucson native Inez Paloverde, who played piccolo in the marching band for four years and still hums the fight song in the grocery store, has her stripe assignment and she's taking it seriously. Utah's Aspen Mortensen, a former ski racer who treats every road trip like a downhill course, flew down in a parka and took it off at the airport.

Aspen: It's seventy degrees in November. How does anybody here get anything done?

Inez: We get plenty done. Ask your team about the last two times we played.

Aspen: I knew you'd say that.

She was always going to say that. Arizona won the last two meetings, 42-18 and 23-10, and for a Utah program that went 6-2 against the Wildcats across the last decade, those two still sting. The difference now is that Utah hasn't lost to anybody this year.

The Utes have been about as steady as a team can be. They've beaten Arkansas 43-10, shut out Utah State 33-0, won 27-24 at Colorado and 49-25 at Cincinnati, and handled BYU 28-20 last week. Devon Dampier has 20 touchdown throws and ran for two more against BYU. Wayshawn Parker has 762 rushing yards and nine scores. Braden Pegan has eight touchdown catches, two of them last Saturday.

Aspen: Dampier's like a good slalom skier. He doesn't do anything flashy. He just never misses a gate.

Inez: He threw two interceptions against Houston.

Aspen: And we still won. That's the point.

Arizona's season has been louder. The Wildcats put up 52 at West Virginia, beat Iowa State 39-3, and beat Cincinnati 34-7, the same Cincinnati team Utah beat by 24 on the road. They lost at BYU and lost 34-26 at Texas Tech, but they've won all five home games, and last week they survived TCU 29-28 on a Friday night here. Noah Fifita has 2,794 throwing yards and 21 touchdowns. He went for 417 yards at Texas Tech. Isaiah Mizell has 753 receiving yards, and Giovanni Richardson caught 11 balls for 171 yards against TCU.

Inez: Fifita is the best quarterback in this league. I'll argue that with anyone, including you.

Aspen: I'm not arguing. I'm just saying our defense gives up 16.3 points a game.

Inez: Ours gives up 19.9, and we've played better offenses.

Aspen: Have you?

Inez: We played Texas Tech.

That's the matchup in a sentence. Utah wants to run Parker and Dampier, keep the clock moving and keep Fifita on the sideline. Arizona wants to throw early and often and turn the game into a shootout. Utah's front has been good against the run all season, and that's where Arizona's offense is weakest. Kedrick Reescano leads the Wildcats with 373 rushing yards, which tells you the offense lives in Fifita's arm. Linebacker Chase Kennedy has six sacks for Arizona, and Utah's blockers will have to account for him on every snap.

Aspen: When you race downhill, the one thing you never do is panic in the middle of the course. You stay in your tuck, so to speak.

Inez: So to speak.

Aspen: Fine. You stay calm. Utah doesn't panic.

Inez: Arizona doesn't panic either. We just make noise. You'll hear it.

She's right about that. A striped stadium, a top-five opponent and the Wildcats' best home crowd of the year will make Tucson loud. Utah's going to have to handle the noise on third downs, especially in the second half when Arizona's defense feeds off the crowd.

Inez: When we win, I'm bringing my piccolo to the student section and playing "Bear Down" until security asks me to stop.

Aspen: And when Utah wins?

Inez: I'll still play it. Just slower.

Aspen: I'll take slower.

This feels like a game decided by one drive late, maybe by a kick. Utah has the steadier résumé and the better defense. Arizona has the crowd, the quarterback and a two-game run against the Utes that nobody in Salt Lake has forgotten. I'll take the unbeaten team, by just enough.

Stripe the Sol all you want, Tucson. The Utes leave unbeaten. My call: Utah Utes 28, Arizona Wildcats 25.
