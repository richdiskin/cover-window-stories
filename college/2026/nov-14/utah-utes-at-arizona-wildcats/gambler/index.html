Utah vs Arizona Pick: Utes −2.5, Under 53.5

Utah Utes (9-0) at Arizona Wildcats (7-2)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Casino Del Sol Stadium · Fox

XFacebook

Utah vs Arizona prediction: No. 3 Utah, unbeaten at 9-0, plays No. 22 Arizona at Casino Del Sol Stadium in Tucson on Saturday at 7:30 p.m. ET. I'm on Utah Utes −2.5 and Under 53.5, with Utah Utes 28, Arizona Wildcats 25 as my final.

Aspen Mortensen, the former ski racer, is all-in on Utah's steadiness. "Nine games, nine wins. Our defense allows 16.3 points a game. Dampier has 20 touchdown throws and never misses a gate." Tucson native Inez Paloverde, the old piccolo player, has the counter ready. "We won the last two meetings, 42-18 and 23-10. Fifita has 2,794 yards and we're perfect at home."

The side comes down to who Utah has beaten. The Utes won 27-24 at Colorado, 31-17 at Iowa State and 49-25 at Cincinnati, so they've already handled a road trip into a loud building three times. They're scoring 37.7 points a game and allowing 16.3. Arizona is good too, at 34.2 scored and 19.9 allowed, and the Wildcats beat that same Cincinnati team 34-7. But Arizona has lost twice on the road, 28-17 at BYU and 34-26 at Texas Tech, and Utah just beat BYU 28-20. The market opened Utah −1.5 and moved to −2.5. I have Utah by three, which clears it by a hair.

The Under comes from Utah's tempo. Utah wants Wayshawn Parker and Devon Dampier running the ball and keeping Noah Fifita on the sideline. Kedrick Reescano leads Arizona with 373 rushing yards, so the Wildcats' offense is mostly Fifita's arm against a defense that has allowed 24 or fewer in eight of nine games. Utah's last three games finished 21-13, 49-25 and 28-20. The posted number climbed from 51.5 to 53.5, and my 53 sits just under it.

The risk is Fifita. He threw for 417 yards at Texas Tech and 341 at West Virginia. If Isaiah Mizell gets loose early and this turns into a shootout, the Under goes away fast and the home team can win outright.

The cards are split. The Formula has Utah Utes −2.5 and the Under at Utah 28-25, the same score I have. Next Man Up has Utah Utes −2.5 and the Over at 29-26. Letdown Watch takes Arizona Wildcats +2.5 and the Under. In The Trenches likes Utah Utes −2.5 and stays out of the over/under, pointing to a Utah run game that should find room. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Letdown Watch, to the room: "Unbeaten team, a week of hype, road crowd in stripes. I've seen this movie." Next Man Up: "Then you've seen Utah win at Colorado."

Inez says Tucson is about to be the loudest it's been in years. Aspen says that's fine, Utah has heard loud before. I think the Utes do just enough to stay perfect.

Utah Utes 28, Arizona Wildcats 25. Utah Utes cover the 2.5. Under 53.5.
