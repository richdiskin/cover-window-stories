Texas Tech at Oklahoma State: Stillwater Blacks Out for Eric Morris' Old School

Texas Tech Red Raiders (8-1) at Oklahoma State Cowboys (6-3)

9:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Boone Pickens Stadium · ESPN

XFacebook

Boone Pickens Stadium is a blackout tonight, per Oklahoma State Athletics, and The O'Colly says a blackout there “is always a danger to the road team.” The opponent is Texas Tech, the alma mater of first-year Cowboys coach Eric Morris, at 8:30 p.m. Central.

The number as of Nov. 14, 2026, 10:08 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at Oklahoma State −3.5, total 73.5. Every pick in this story, mine and each handicapper's, is checked against that number, not Monday's. Monday's card had Texas Tech +3.5 with a 72.5 total. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

Morris was a Tech receiver in the 2007 game in Stillwater that people remember for Mike Gundy's “I’m a man, I’m 40” rant; Morris caught a 12-yard touchdown in Tech's 49-45 loss, AP notes. He is trying to keep this week about football. “I think anytime you try to make anything about yourself or the past, bad things happen to you,” he told News 9.

His quarterback has nothing to lose and says so. “We’ve all been doubted, told we’re not good enough, not big enough, all that stuff,” Drew Mestemaker told AP. “We aren’t supposed to be here technically. Like, why not just let it rip now?” He leads the Big 12 at 329 passing yards a game with 29 touchdowns, and he has eight straight 300-yard games, a school record.

Thomas Castellanos has won both starts for Texas Tech since replacing Will Hammond, and last week he became the first power-conference quarterback since 2020 with 350 passing yards, five passing touchdowns and 100 rushing yards in one game. “He makes you defend everything,” Morris said. The Red Raiders arrived before the ground stop, per USA Today as tracked by Undrafted. These two have combined for 70 points in 10 of their last 13 meetings.
