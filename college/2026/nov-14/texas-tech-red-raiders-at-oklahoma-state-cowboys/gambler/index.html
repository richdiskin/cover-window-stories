Texas Tech vs Oklahoma State Pick: Red Raiders +3.5, Under 72.5

Texas Tech Red Raiders (8-1) at Oklahoma State Cowboys (6-3)

9:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Boone Pickens Stadium · ESPN

Texas Tech vs Oklahoma State prediction: No. 15 Texas Tech, 8-1, goes to Boone Pickens Stadium in Stillwater to face Oklahoma State, 6-3, on Saturday at 9:30 p.m. ET. I'm on Texas Tech Red Raiders +3.5 and Under 72.5, with Texas Tech Red Raiders 33, Oklahoma State Cowboys 32 as my final.

Dusty, the Oklahoma State ag-economist who times rodeo events for fun, is all-in on his offense. "We just put up 66 at Kansas State. Drew Mestemaker has 29 touchdown throws. Caleb Hawkins went for 175 last week." Bodie Ratliff, the Texas Tech bareback rider, says hang on. "We're 8-1. Thomas Castellanos threw five touchdowns against West Virginia, and we beat you 42-0 last year."

The side comes down to Texas Tech getting points while being the better team on the season. The Red Raiders are 8-1 and have won at Oregon State and 29-7 at Colorado. Their only loss came at Cincinnati. Oklahoma State has been great at home, beating Oregon 39-31, UCF 42-28 and Colorado 57-24, but it also lost at Tulsa, at Houston and 51-49 at Iowa State. The number has swung all the way from Texas Tech −15.5 at the open to Oklahoma State −3.5. That's a huge move, and it gives me a full field goal plus a hook with the team that has the better results. I have Texas Tech by one.

The Under goes against the last two weeks. Oklahoma State's last two games reached a hundred combined points and then ninety-four, and Texas Tech's last game reached a hundred and one. But those look like outliers. Seven of Texas Tech's other eight games finished under 72.5, and the Red Raiders allow 24.1 points a game. J'Koby Williams has 954 rushing yards and can keep the ball away from Mestemaker. The posted number has gone from 61.5 to 72.5, and my 65 combined points land under it.

The risk is obvious. Mestemaker has thrown for 2,962 yards, and Justin Bowick has 10 touchdown catches. Oklahoma State scores 43.9 points a game. If the Cowboys get going early and Castellanos has to chase, this becomes another track meet and the home team covers easily.

The cards agree on the side. The Formula likes Texas Tech Red Raiders +3.5 and likes the Under at Oklahoma State 31-30. Next Man Up has Texas Tech Red Raiders +3.5 but has the Over at Oklahoma State 38-36. Letdown Watch likes Texas Tech Red Raiders +3.5 and likes the Under, with Texas Tech winning 34-27. In The Trenches stays off both sides and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Next Man Up, to The Formula: "Thirty-one to thirty in Stillwater? Have you seen these two teams?" The Formula: "I've seen the last two weeks. I've also seen the rest of the season."

Dusty says the clock in Stillwater runs fast on visiting teams. Bodie says eight seconds is all a rider needs. I think Texas Tech holds on.

Texas Tech Red Raiders 33, Oklahoma State Cowboys 32. Texas Tech Red Raiders cover the 3.5. Under 72.5.
