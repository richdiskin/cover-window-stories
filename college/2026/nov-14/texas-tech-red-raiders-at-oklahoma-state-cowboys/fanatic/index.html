Texas Tech vs Oklahoma State: Eight Seconds in Stillwater

Texas Tech Red Raiders (8-1) at Oklahoma State Cowboys (6-3)

9:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Boone Pickens Stadium · ESPN

XFacebook

Texas Tech Red Raiders vs Oklahoma State Cowboys is a late Saturday night in Stillwater, 8:30 p.m. local at Boone Pickens Stadium, with No. 15 Texas Tech coming up from Lubbock at 8-1 and Oklahoma State coming off one of the wildest nights in program history. Dusty, an Oklahoma State ag-economist who runs the timing clock at rodeos across the state on summer weekends, says he's never seen the Cowboys play like this. Bodie Ratliff, who rode bareback for the Texas Tech rodeo team and still walks like he just got off a horse, has heard all about it.

Bodie: Sixty-six points at Kansas State. Everybody in Lubbock heard about it.

Dusty: It was a program record in Manhattan. Sixty-six to twenty-eight.

Bodie: We scored 53 the same night.

Dusty: Against West Virginia. They scored 48.

Bodie: We still won.

That's this matchup. Two offenses that can score on anybody, and two fan bases that keep a running count of each other's points.

Oklahoma State is 6-3, and Drew Mestemaker has become one of the most dangerous quarterbacks in the country. He has 2,962 throwing yards and 29 touchdowns, and he threw four more at Kansas State. Caleb Hawkins went past 1,000 rushing yards in that game, with 175 yards and three scores. Justin Bowick has 10 touchdown catches. The Cowboys beat Oregon 39-31 at home and Colorado 57-24, but they also lost 51-49 at Iowa State and 35-32 at Houston.

Dusty: I've timed a lot of events. Mestemaker gets the ball out faster than any calf roper I've ever clocked.

Bodie: That's a weird compliment.

Dusty: It's the best one I've got.

Texas Tech is 8-1, with the only loss at Cincinnati. The Red Raiders beat Arizona State 53-25, won 29-7 at Colorado and held off Arizona 34-26. Thomas Castellanos has taken over at quarterback the last two weeks, and he threw for 362 yards and five touchdowns against West Virginia. J'Koby Williams has 954 rushing yards, and he ran for 160 against the Mountaineers. Linebacker Austin Romaine has 81 tackles, and A.J. Holmes Jr. has 7.5 sacks.

Bodie: Castellanos plays like a bareback rider. He holds on with one hand and lets the rest of him fly.

Dusty: That's how you get thrown.

Bodie: Not if you hold on for eight seconds.

Dusty: Football games are longer than eight seconds.

Bodie: Then he'll hold on longer.

The last two meetings went Texas Tech's way, 56-48 in 2024 and 42-0 last year, and Bodie brings up the shutout constantly. Dusty would like everyone to know that Oklahoma State is a different team now.

Dusty: Last year was last year. We had a bad year. This year we can score on anybody.

Bodie: You gave up 51 at Iowa State.

Dusty: And we scored 49.

Bodie: You still lost.

Dusty: By two. That's a rounding error.

The key is the Texas Tech defense against Mestemaker. If Holmes and the Red Raider front can get pressure, Mestemaker has thrown eight interceptions and can be forced into mistakes. If not, the Cowboys will score at home, the way they have all year. On the other side, Williams needs to control the clock and keep Mestemaker on the sideline.

Bodie: When we win, you're timing my next ride, and you're not allowed to round down.

Dusty: And if Oklahoma State wins?

Bodie: Then you time it anyway. And I'll know you rounded down.

Dusty: I never round down.

Bodie: You just told me two points was a rounding error.

This feels like a game decided in the last two minutes. Texas Tech has the better season and the better defense. Oklahoma State has the hotter offense and the home crowd. I'll take the Red Raiders, barely.

My call: Texas Tech Red Raiders 33, Oklahoma State Cowboys 32.
