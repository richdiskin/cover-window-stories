James Madison vs UConn Pick: Dukes −3.5, Over 57.5

James Madison Dukes (8-2) at UConn Huskies (4-5)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Pratt & Whitney Stadium · CBSSN

XFacebook

James Madison at UConn kicks off at noon ET in East Hartford in the middle of a full Husky weekend, with UConn basketball hosting Ohio State downtown on Friday and the hockey team back at PeoplesBank Arena after the football game. My James Madison vs UConn prediction says the Dukes leave town happy: James Madison Dukes 34, UConn Huskies 26, with James Madison Dukes −3.5 and Over 57.5.

Lila Shenandoah, the JMU insider, came north with purple pom-poms and the 2023 box score. "Forty-four to six. We're 8-2, we score 40.6 a game and George Pettaway has 824 rushing yards." Jo Ferrante runs the UConn side like a point guard calling a set. "Kalieb Osborne has 16 touchdowns and four interceptions, and we score 32.9 a game. Bring your defense."

The market agrees with me and has been moving that way. DraftKings opened James Madison −1.5 with 56.5 on the over/under, and it got to −3.5 and 57.5. I'm fine laying the extra two. JMU allows 19.3 points a game and UConn allows 26.7, and the Dukes have been better on both sides of the ball. Their two losses came in overtime, 34-31 at App State and 24-21 to Troy, and they answered with a 54-23 win at Southern Miss. UConn has dropped two straight, including a 38-21 home loss to North Carolina.

There is a quarterback question, and I'm pricing it in. Camden Coleman leads JMU with 1,814 throwing yards and 17 touchdowns, but JC Evans had the most attempts at Southern Miss and went 19-of-26 for 251 yards. The run game doesn't change with the passer, and Pettaway is the engine. My Dukes win by eight, clear of 3.5.

The Over comes from UConn's offense. Osborne threw for 314 yards in the 42-41 overtime loss to Syracuse, and the Huskies scored 26 at Air Force and 21 against North Carolina even in losses. JMU has scored at least 20 in every game. My 60 points sit above 57.5.

The room is with James Madison on the side and split on the over/under. The Formula likes James Madison Dukes −3.5 but goes Under 57.5, with JMU 32-25. Next Man Up likes James Madison Dukes −3.5 and likes Over 57.5, 36-29. Letdown Watch likes James Madison Dukes −3.5 and has Over 57.5, 35-23. In The Trenches has no side this week and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Next Man Up to The Formula: "Under? Did you see the Syracuse score? Forty-two to forty-one." The Formula: "I saw it. I also saw who lost."

Jo gets Osborne and a full house for the Huskies & Heroes salute. Lila gets Pettaway late and a purple bus ride home before the hockey puck drops.

James Madison Dukes 34, UConn Huskies 26. James Madison Dukes cover the 3.5. Over 57.5.
