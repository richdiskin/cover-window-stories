Fresno State vs Texas State Pick: Fresno State +9.5, Under 60.5

Fresno State Bulldogs (5-4) at Texas State Bobcats (5-4)

4:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · UFCU Stadium · CW

XFacebook

Four common Pac-12 opponents tell you almost everything about Saturday in San Marcos, and they say these teams are closer than 9.5 points. My Fresno State vs Texas State pick is Fresno State Bulldogs +9.5, and I'm taking the Under 60.5 too in this Week 11 game at UFCU Stadium, where Texas State is marking Heroes Day and Community Day.

Here's the comparison. Both teams lost to Boise State, Texas State 45-46 on the road and Fresno State 31-38 at home. Both lost at San Diego State, Texas State 29-31 and Fresno State 23-26. Both beat Oregon State, Texas State 42-21 and Fresno State 38-22. The only real split is Utah State, which Texas State beat 20-17 and Fresno State lost to 10-17 last week. That's not a ten-point gap.

Yesenia Calderwood floats the San Marcos River most weekends and isn't worried. "Go with the flow, Roz. Torrance Burgess Jr. ran 28 times for 196 yards at Oregon State. He's got 1,118 on the year. Brad Jackson has 19 touchdown throws. We score 38.6 a game."

Roz Ayala sells the Fresno Valley like a fruit stand owner and pushes back. "And you give up 30.8, Yesenia. We give up 20.6. Jayden Mandal has thrown two interceptions all year. Josiah Freeman caught eight balls for 114 yards at Utah State. Dylan Hampsten has six sacks."

Roz has the stronger defensive argument. Texas State allows 30.8 points a game, Fresno State allows 20.6, and K'vion Thunderbird has 69 tackles in the middle of that Bulldogs group. Fresno State's offense has gone quiet at times, with 10 points at Utah State, but Mandal's 1,857 yards and 11 touchdowns against two interceptions mean the Bulldogs rarely hand games away.

My final is Texas State Bobcats 31, Fresno State Bulldogs 26. The Bobcats win at home, but a five-point game sits well inside 9.5.

On the over/under, I'm on the Under 60.5. DraftKings opened it at 58.5 and it has moved up two points. Texas State's offense is a real threat, but Fresno State hasn't allowed more than 39 in any game. My 31-26 lands at 57, clear of the 60.5.

The room mostly agrees. The Formula likes Fresno State +9.5 and likes the Under at 29-27. Letdown Watch likes Fresno State +9.5 and likes the Under at 30-26. Next Man Up has Fresno State +9.5 but sits on the Over, 35-26. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call here. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Letdown Watch: "The Formula and I agree again. Somebody frame it."

Next Man Up: "Thirty-five points from Texas State. Y'all forgot that offense dropped 66 on Colorado State."

The Formula: "Nobody forgot. It also allowed 46 to Boise State."

Yesenia wants the river rolling into a big November. Roz wants a long trip from the Valley to end with something sweet. I think Texas State wins a shootout that never quite becomes one.

Texas State Bobcats 31, Fresno State Bulldogs 26. Fresno State Bulldogs cover the 9.5. Under 60.5.
