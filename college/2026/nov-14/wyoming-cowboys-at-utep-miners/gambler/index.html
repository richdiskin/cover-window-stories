Wyoming vs UTEP Pick: Miners +9.5, Over 40.5

Wyoming Cowboys (6-3) at UTEP Miners (1-8)

10:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Sun Bowl · FS1

XFacebook

Wyoming vs UTEP prediction: Wyoming, 6-3, takes a four-game winning run to the Sun Bowl in El Paso to face 1-8 UTEP on Senior Night. The ball goes up at 10 p.m. ET Saturday. I'm on UTEP Miners +9.5 and Over 40.5, with Wyoming Cowboys 27, UTEP Miners 18 as my final.

Colter Sprague, the Laramie rancher in the same Carhartt jacket he's worn for a decade, likes the momentum. "Four wins in a row. We just put up 41 at UNLV. Tyler Hughes has 676 rushing yards." Neto Galindo, the ironworker's kid who grades everything like ore, admits the year has been rough but likes the trend. "We lost 17-14 to Hawai'i last week. We're getting closer every game, and this is Senior Night."

The side is about how much Wyoming does away from Laramie. The Cowboys lost 35-13 at Colorado State, 24-10 at Central Michigan and 28-0 at North Dakota State, and their road win at San José State took overtime to finish 16-13. Wyoming scores 21.9 points a game and allows 21.7. That's not a profile that runs away from anybody. UTEP's home losses have been shrinking, too: 30-13 to Nevada, 24-11 to San José State and then 17-14 to Hawai'i. The market opened Wyoming −10 and is down to −9.5. I have the Cowboys by nine, so the Miners stay inside it.

The Over comes from Wyoming's recent form. The Cowboys' last three games finished 41-23, 28-14 and 41-35, and all three cleared 40.5. Samuel Harris ran for 128 yards and two scores at UNLV. UTEP allows 34.3 points a game, so Wyoming should get to the mid-20s or better, and the Miners need only their 14.1 a game, plus a little more, to get it over. The posted number dropped from 43.5 to 40.5, and my 45 combined points clear it.

The risk is UTEP's offense. The Miners have scored 14 or fewer in seven of nine games, and the quarterback spot has moved between EJ Colson Jr. and Raymond Moore III. If Wyoming's defense, led by Esaia Bogar and his 5.5 sacks, holds the Miners to single digits, both of my picks are in trouble.

The cards mostly agree. The Formula has UTEP Miners +9.5 and likes the Over at Wyoming 27-20. Next Man Up has UTEP Miners +9.5 and likes the Over at Wyoming 26-18. Letdown Watch has Wyoming Cowboys −9.5 and likes the Over. In The Trenches stays off both sides and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Letdown Watch, to Next Man Up: "Four straight wins, long road trip, a 1-8 team. Sounds like a blowout to me." Next Man Up: "That's the opposite of what your name means."

Colter says the wind in Laramie made the Cowboys tough. Neto says the Sun Bowl in November is no picnic either. I think Wyoming wins and UTEP keeps it respectable.

Wyoming Cowboys 27, UTEP Miners 18. UTEP Miners cover the 9.5. Over 40.5.
