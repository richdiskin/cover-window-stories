East Carolina vs Charlotte: Gold Rush Day in University City

East Carolina Pirates (5-4) at Charlotte 49ers (1-8)

1:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Jerry Richardson Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

East Carolina Pirates vs Charlotte 49ers is the Gold Rush rivalry game, and Charlotte's Priya Halloran plans to wear so much gold to Jerry Richardson Stadium for the 1 p.m. kickoff that ECU's Bart Quidley will need sunglasses to find his seat. Bart has been tailgating in the same Greenville spot since before he could walk, and he has driven across the state to tell her that a Pirate ship doesn't care what color the harbor is.

Priya: It's the Gold Rush, Bart. Everybody in gold. Even the risk committee.

Bart: Arrr-guably the most expensive color you could have picked for a 1-8 season, Priya. But I respect the commitment.

The history is shorter than either of them would like and spicier than most rivalries twice its age. Charlotte leads the series 2-1. The 49ers won 10-7 in Greenville in 2023, then drilled the Pirates 55-24 here in 2024, the first and only time this rivalry had been played at Jerry Richardson Stadium until now. Last November, East Carolina returned the favor in Greenville, 48-22.

Priya: Fifty-five to twenty-four. I keep that one in a frame on my desk, right next to my Uptown bank's quarterly report. Both show a positive return on investment.

Bart: Forty-eight to twenty-two keeps me warm at night, matey. We'll call it a running tab and settle it today.

Okay, he said "matey." Pirate talk is allowed, in moderation, and Bart has now used his allotment for the afternoon.

East Carolina comes in 5-4 and feeling better about itself. The Pirates have won two of their last three, 17-9 over Temple and 20-17 over South Florida, and they have been winning ugly with a defense that allows 23.3 points a game. The quarterback job has bounced between Emory Williams and Mitch Griffis, and Williams had the most attempts against USF, going 19-of-27 for 175 yards and two touchdowns. Landon Sides caught 14 balls for 126 yards and both of those scores. TJ Engleman Jr. leads the rushers with 695 yards, Ashton Gray ran for 101 against the Bulls, and DJ Johnson Jr. has 60 tackles on defense.

Bart: Fourteen catches for Landon Sides. That's not a box score, Priya, that's a treasure map.

Priya: It's a lot of catches. I'd underwrite it.

Charlotte's season has been rougher, and Priya won't spin it. The 49ers are 1-8, they allow 38.3 points a game, and their only win came 38-31 at Temple in October. But there were close calls, too. They lost 43-41 in overtime to The Citadel in the opener, 26-21 at App State and 38-31 in overtime to Tulane. Conner Harrell has taken over the offense, with 1,006 throwing yards and six touchdowns against only two interceptions, and Jaden Barnes has 54 catches for 542 yards. Gavin Willis has 74 tackles from the middle of the defense, and he will be busy.

Priya: Harrell protects the football. In my business, that's called a sound fundamental.

Bart: In mine, that's called a sailor who doesn't fall overboard. Good for him.

The key for Charlotte is simple. The 49ers have to slow down the ECU run game, because if Engleman and Gray get the Pirates into short third downs, Williams can sit back and find Sides all afternoon. If Charlotte can make East Carolina throw on third and long, Harrell gets a few extra possessions, and the gold in the stands gets louder by the minute.

The weekend matters on campus, too. This is one of just two home games left in University City, with the finale against Navy still to come, and Priya wants the seniors to hear a real crowd while they can. She also admits she opened a spreadsheet titled 'Write-Downs' back in October and hasn't closed it.

Priya: I'm going full NASCAR on the pregame. Gold firesuit, gold sunglasses, gold everything.

Bart: You'll look like a trophy, Priya. Problem is, trophies sit on a shelf while somebody else holds them.

Priya: Then I'll hold the shelf. Respectfully, Bart, a 2-1 series lead still beats a pirate's map.

Priya brings the gold. Bart sails off with the Gold Rush win. My call: East Carolina Pirates 32, Charlotte 49ers 19.
