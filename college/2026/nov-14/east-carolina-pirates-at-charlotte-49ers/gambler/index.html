East Carolina at Charlotte is the Gold Rush rivalry game at Jerry Richardson Stadium, where kickoff is 1:00 PM ET, the 49ers crowd will be dressed in gold, and the Pirates are looking to make it two in a row in this series. My East Carolina vs Charlotte prediction says the gap between these two teams this season is wider than the price: East Carolina Pirates 32, Charlotte 49ers 19, with East Carolina Pirates −11.5 and Over 44.5.

Bart Quidley made the drive from Pitt County in purple and gold and is in a generous mood. "Arrr-guably our defense is the reason we're 5-4. Twenty-three points allowed a game. Landon Sides had 14 catches last week. Today we take the harbor." Priya Halloran is pricing every drive from the home side. "Conner Harrell has six touchdowns and two picks. We beat you 55-24 here. Don't get comfortable, Bart."

The history sits on Charlotte's side of the ledger, two wins in three meetings, but the 2026 teams are not close. DraftKings opened East Carolina −13.5 with 47.5 on the over/under, and it came down to −11.5 and 44.5. Charlotte is 1-8 and gives up 38.3 points a game, and the only win came at Temple. The 49ers lost by 51 to Memphis, by 40 at North Texas and by 27 to Louisiana. East Carolina has won two of its last three, beating Temple 17-9 and South Florida 20-17. My Pirates by 13 clear the 11.5, and the two points of movement since the open give me a better number than the early market had.

The run game is the reason. TJ Engleman Jr. has 695 rushing yards, and Ashton Gray ran for 101 against USF. Charlotte's best runner, Henry Rutledge, has 454 for the season. If East Carolina controls the clock, Charlotte's offense gets fewer chances to keep up.

The Over is about the Charlotte defense. Seven of Charlotte's nine games went above 44.5 combined points, including the 43-41 overtime loss to The Citadel and back-to-back 38-31 games against Temple and Tulane. East Carolina put up 42 on North Carolina Central and 34 at Memphis. My 51 points sit above 44.5, and the drop from 47.5 means I'm getting three points of room.
