Louisiana vs UL Monroe: A New Stadium Name and an Old Rivalry

Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns (6-3) at UL Monroe Warhawks (2-7)

9:00 PM ET Thu Nov 12 · Malone Stadium · ESPN2

XFacebook

Darnell Vidrine walks in through the home entrance on Thursday night and stops to say hello to Coach James Malone. The football stadium in Monroe has a new name this season, FMOL Health | St. Francis Stadium, but ULM made a promise when it announced the deal in August: an installation honoring Malone, the man the school calls the father of ULM Athletics, goes right at the home entrance. Darnell figures a man who coached the Indians from 1934 to 1953 has seen a few hard years. He figures Malone would tell him to keep showing up.

So he shows up. Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns vs UL Monroe Warhawks is the in-state rivalry game on a Thursday night, and Amélie "Mimi" Fontenot has driven up from Lafayette with her calculator, her dancing shoes and a bag of boudin she says is for emergencies.

"Darnell, mon ami," she says. "Two and seven. I'm an accountant, cher. I can't make that add up for you."

"Mimi," Darnell says, "we've won two in a row. Nobody else in this conference is talking about that, so I will."

He's right about the two wins. UL Monroe beat Troy 21-14 on the road on Oct. 24, then won 19-17 at Arkansas State last Saturday. Darnell hosts an overnight radio show in the Delta, and he spent most of Sunday morning telling his callers about it.

But there's a catch, and Mimi finds it fast. ULM hasn't won a game in the newly named stadium yet. The Warhawks are 0-4 at home, losing 38-35 to Southeastern Louisiana, 45-17 to Florida Atlantic on Homecoming, 24-0 to Louisiana Tech and 35-21 to Southern Miss.

"Thursday is the night," Darnell says. "First home win in the new place. I can feel it in my radio."

Mimi likes her own team's chances a lot more. Louisiana is 6-3 and second in the Sun Belt West, scoring 29.9 points a game. Lunch Winfield does a little bit of everything. He's thrown for 1,599 yards and he leads the team on the ground with 567 yards and 10 rushing touchdowns. Last Saturday against South Alabama, JJ Garner carried 10 times for 136 yards in a 28-26 win. KeDarius Wade leads the receivers with 300 yards, and Jake St. Andre leads the defense with 69 tackles.

"Lunch," Mimi says, "is the best meal of the day and the best quarterback in Louisiana. Laissez les bons temps rouler."

Darnell answers with his own list. Aidan Armenta has thrown for 1,413 yards with 10 touchdowns. Austin Carlisle, the other quarterback, leads the Warhawks on the ground with 363 yards, and he ran 19 times for 111 at Arkansas State. Marcellus Jackson has 35 catches for 370 yards. Noah Flemmings has 83 tackles, and Kevontay Wells has 4 sacks.

"Two quarterbacks," Darnell says. "Twice the hope."

Then Mimi brings out the family history. Louisiana's game page lists 35 wins and 26 losses against ULM all-time, and they've won eight of the last ten meetings, including the last three: 52-21 in 2023, 37-23 in 2024 and 30-27 last year.

Darnell has his own memories ready, because of course he does. ULM won 56-50 in 2017, and the Warhawks won 21-17 in 2022. "I was on the air the whole night in 2017," he says. "I lost my voice in the fourth quarter. Best night of my life."

Down Warhawk Way, the Monroe Chamber of Commerce is throwing its pregame tailgate at Lou St. Amant Field, the baseball park, from 5 p.m. on Mimi wants to go. Darnell has already been twice that afternoon to make sure the grills work.

Play it out. Darnell's crowd is loud early, and ULM hangs around in the first half, with Armenta finding Jackson and Carlisle gaining yards on the ground. But Winfield is too much. He runs, he throws, and Garner breaks a long one in the third quarter. Louisiana pulls away after halftime, and the Cajuns make it four in a row in the rivalry.

Mimi two-steps around the press box with an accordion riff on her phone. Darnell sighs, says "Keep the faith, y'all," and resets his optimism counter right back to the top. Then he stops at the home entrance on the way out and tells Coach Malone they'll get the next one.

Mimi two-steps all night while Darnell resets the counter for next week. My call: Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns 33, UL Monroe Warhawks 18.
