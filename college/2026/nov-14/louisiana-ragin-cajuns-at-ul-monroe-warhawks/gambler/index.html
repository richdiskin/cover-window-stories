Louisiana vs UL Monroe Pick: Cajuns −11.5 in Thursday Rivalry

Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns (6-3) at UL Monroe Warhawks (2-7)

9:00 PM ET Thu Nov 12 · Malone Stadium · ESPN2

XFacebook

Monroe's business crowd has its Thursday night planned. The Monroe Chamber of Commerce is hosting a pregame tailgate at Lou St. Amant Field, the ULM baseball park on Warhawk Way, and the whole thing leads into the in-state rivalry game under the lights. In this Louisiana vs UL Monroe spread, my side is Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns −11.5 with the Over 48.5, and my score is Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns 33, UL Monroe Warhawks 18, kicking off at 9:00 PM ET at Malone Stadium.

Mimi Fontenot has her calculator out before the coin toss. "Darnell, cher, you give up 34.8 points a game. My Cajuns score 29.9. I'm an accountant. That math balances itself."

Darnell Vidrine has heard worse on his overnight radio show. "Two straight wins, Mimi. We beat Troy 21-14 and won at Arkansas State 19-17. The Warhawks are rolling."

The 11.5 doesn't scare me. DraftKings opened Louisiana −10.5 and it has climbed a full point since, so the market agrees. UL Monroe is 2-7 and hasn't won at home this year. Three of those four home losses came by 14 or more, including 45-17 to Florida Atlantic and 24-0 to Louisiana Tech. Both wins came on the road, and the one at Arkansas State came with Aidan Armenta throwing 2 interceptions. That's a team living on turnover luck. Louisiana won three of its six games by more than 11.5, and the Cajuns have won the last three meetings, 52-21, 37-23 and 30-27.

The Louisiana engine is Lunch Winfield. He has 1,599 passing yards and leads the team on the ground with 567 yards and 10 rushing touchdowns. JJ Garner ran for 136 on just 10 carries against South Alabama. On the other side, Armenta has thrown for 1,413 yards with 10 touchdowns and 5 interceptions, and Noah Flemmings has 83 tackles. Quarterback Austin Carlisle leads ULM on the ground with 363 yards, and he ran for 111 at Arkansas State. Jake St. Andre has 69 tackles for Louisiana.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 48.5 and it hasn't moved, and I'm on the Over 48.5. UL Monroe games have gone past that number in five of nine. In the last ten meetings, seven went past 48.5, including a 56-50 shootout in 2017 and 70-20 in 2020. My 33-18 lands on 51.

The room is almost unanimous on Louisiana. The Formula has Louisiana 34-19 and likes Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns −11.5 and the Over 48.5. Letdown Watch has 34-18 and likes the Cajuns −11.5 and the Over. Next Man Up has 31-17, takes the Cajuns −11.5 and goes Over 48.5. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Next Man Up: "Darnell, I want to like the Warhawks. My card just won't let me."

Darnell: "Your card has never been to Monroe on a Thursday night, Next Man Up."

Mimi taps her calculator and saves Darnell a seat for the drive home. Darnell turns his optimism dial up one more notch, because that's what he does.

Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns 33, UL Monroe Warhawks 18. Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns cover the 11.5. Over 48.5.
