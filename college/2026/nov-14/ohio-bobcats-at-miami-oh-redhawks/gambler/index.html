Ohio vs Miami (OH) Pick: RedHawks +1.5 in the Battle of the Bricks

Ohio Bobcats (7-2) at Miami (OH) RedHawks (5-4)

8:00 PM ET Tue Nov 10 · Yager Stadium · ESPN2

XFacebook

Ohio players get a mug every time they beat Miami, and the Bobcats have won the last two meetings. My Ohio vs Miami (OH) prediction for Tuesday's Battle of the Bricks at Yager Stadium takes Miami (OH) RedHawks +1.5 and the Over 46.5, with a final of Miami (OH) RedHawks 27, Ohio Bobcats 24, at 8:00 PM ET in Oxford.

Penny Trautman wants a third mug for the cupboard. "Ohio is 7-2 and 5-0 in the MAC. Nick Poulos has taken the most throws in each of our last four games, Duncan Brune ran for 111 at Akron, and Michael Molnar has 6 sacks and 86 tackles. Cordelia, clear a shelf."

Cordelia Van Doren smooths her scarf and doesn't blink. "Miami leads the all-time series, Penny, and I'll spare you the count because you'd only argue with it. Maleek Huggins has 473 receiving yards. Roosevelt Andrews III has 5.5 sacks. We scored 31 on Buffalo last Tuesday and lost in overtime, and nobody in this building has forgotten it."

Here's why I take the points. The number flipped. DraftKings opened Miami (OH) RedHawks −1.5 with the over/under at 47.5, and now Ohio is the road team laying 1.5 and the over/under is still 47.5. Miami lives in one-score games: 24-21 over UConn, 20-19 at Massachusetts, 28-27 at Central Michigan and that 34-31 overtime loss to Buffalo. Ohio has been tight on the road too, 13-10 at Kent State and 28-20 at Akron. Miami (OH) is at 27.4 points per game this season, and Ohio allows 21.7. My final has Miami by 3, which clears the 1.5.

Both quarterback rooms carry two names. Matt Vezza took the most Ohio throws in four September and early October games before Poulos took over, and Poulos has 978 yards with 7 touchdowns. Miami's David McComb leads the team with 1,121 yards, but Caleb Heavner has taken the most throws in each of the last five games. I'm pricing Heavner, and Miami still comes out ahead at home.

On the over/under, I'm on the Over 46.5. Miami has scored at least 28 in each of its last three games: 34 against Akron, 28 at Central Michigan and 31 against Buffalo. Ohio scores 29.4 a game. My 27-24 gets to 51.

The room splits right down the middle. The Formula has Miami 26-25, takes Miami (OH) RedHawks +1.5 and likes the Over 46.5. Next Man Up has 27-27, also takes the RedHawks +1.5 and likes the Over. Letdown Watch has Ohio 26-24, lays the −1.5 and likes the Over. In The Trenches likes Ohio Bobcats −1.5 and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money has no side pick and likes the Over 47.5.

The Formula: "Twenty-six to twenty-five, Next Man Up. One point, still to the home team. You gave both teams 27 like a split dinner check."

Next Man Up: "Twenty-seven apiece means both bands get to play the fight song. You'd split a brick in half and keep the bigger piece."

Penny wraps both hands around a travel mug and grins at Cordelia. Cordelia raises an eyebrow, checks the clock on the scoreboard and asks whether that mug is about to be the last one.

Miami (OH) RedHawks 27, Ohio Bobcats 24. Miami (OH) RedHawks cover the 1.5. Over 46.5.
