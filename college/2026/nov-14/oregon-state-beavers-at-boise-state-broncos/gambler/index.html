Oregon State at Boise State: College GameDay Comes to the Blue, and the Beavers Barely Made It

Oregon State Beavers (5-4) at Boise State Broncos (8-1)

6:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Albertsons Stadium · USA Net

XFacebook

The Treasure Valley has company this weekend. Boise State on SI reports ESPN's College GameDay is in town for this Pac-12 game, and Boise State is asking everybody to wear blue for a 4 p.m. Mountain kickoff on the field that made blue famous. The Broncos are 8-1 and 5-0 in the league, sitting on top of the Pac-12.

The number as of Nov. 14, 2026, 10:08 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at Boise State −16.5, total 63.5. Every pick in this story, mine and each handicapper's, is checked against that number, not Monday's. Monday's card had Boise State −10.5 with a 66.5 total. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

Oregon State spent Friday finding out whether it would get there at all. The Daily Barometer says the team was due to land in Boise at 3:20 p.m. Pacific, and the flight never left. ESPN's Pete Thamel reported the Beavers were lining up buses and drivers for a nine-hour Saturday ride as a backup. Then the ground stop lifted, and Boise State on SI's update says Oregon State got out of Eugene around 8:30 p.m. Mountain. Kickoff did not move.

The matchup is a fastball against a lawn mower. AP lists Oregon State first in FBS at 370.4 passing yards a game, with Braden Atkinson at 3,169 yards and 25 touchdowns and Jesse Legree at 1,209 receiving yards and 14 scores. Boise State answers with Dylan Riley, who has 1,241 rushing yards and ran for 192 more in a 64-38 win over Colorado State last week. The Broncos hold the ball 35 minutes and 29 seconds a game, second in the country, which is a fine way to keep a passing offense on the bench.

Injury check: the Pac-12 report (Second Report, Thursday 10:01 PM ET) lists Oregon State quarterback Braden Atkinson doubtful. No other player status is assumed; check the teams' game-day reports.

Next Man Up: Boise State 40, Oregon State 28. That makes it Oregon State +16.5 and Over 63.5. Letdown Watch: Boise State 36, Oregon State 24. That makes it Oregon State +16.5 and Under 63.5. In The Trenches: Boise State 34, Oregon State 22, and it stays with Boise State −16.5 off the run-game matchup, no total pick.

A full house in blue, a national TV crew on campus and a visitor that spent Friday at an airport. If Oregon State is going to spoil the party, it has to do it with its arm and do it early.

The Cover Writer's card at today's number: Oregon State Beavers +16.5 and Under 63.5. My final is Boise State Broncos 36, Oregon State Beavers 25. Monday's card laid 10.5 with Boise State. The number has run all the way to 16.5, and our 36-25 is an 11-point Boise win, so the honest pick flips to Oregon State. The Pac-12 report lists Atkinson doubtful, so check his status before kickoff.

Boise State Broncos 36, Oregon State Beavers 25. Oregon State Beavers cover the 16.5. Under 63.5.
