Stanford vs Virginia Tech Pick: Hokies −15.5, Under 59.5

Stanford Cardinal (4-5) at Virginia Tech Hokies (6-3)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Lane Stadium · ACC Network

XFacebook

Stanford vs Virginia Tech prediction: Stanford, 4-5, plays Virginia Tech, 6-3, at Lane Stadium in Blacksburg, a 7:30 p.m. ET kickoff Saturday night. I'm on Virginia Tech Hokies −15.5 and Under 59.5, with Virginia Tech Hokies 37, Stanford Cardinal 20 as my final.

Ruthanne Dellinger, the old Corps drum major with the real lunch pail, is sure about this one. "Stanford's road games went 35-7, 57-3, 58-7 and 52-24. We've got Overton and Grunkemeyer." Iggy Yamamoto-Ross, the founder of four failed apps, takes the optimist's view. "Warren has 14 touchdown throws and only three picks. We had an extra week. We're due."

The side is about Stanford on the road. The Cardinal have lost all four road games, 35-7 at Duke, 57-3 at Wake Forest, 58-7 at Notre Dame and 52-24 at Louisville, and every one of those losses was by more than this number. Stanford allows 37.3 points a game. Virginia Tech scores 34.2 and has Jeffrey Overton Jr., who ran for 123 yards at SMU, and Ethan Grunkemeyer, who has 18 touchdown throws against three interceptions. The market opened Virginia Tech −16.5 and dropped a point to −15.5. I have the Hokies by 17, and that clears it.

The Under comes from what Stanford does away from Palo Alto. The Cardinal scored 7, 3, 7 and 24 in those four road games. Virginia Tech's home results include 44-21 over Old Dominion and 34-24 over Georgia Tech. If the Hokies get to 37, Stanford would need a big night it hasn't had on the road to push this past 59.5. My 57 combined points land under it.

The risk on the side is the Virginia Tech defense, which just allowed 51 at SMU and gives up 23.9 a game. Davis Warren threw for 396 yards against Georgia Tech and four touchdowns against NC State. If he gets hot early, the Cardinal can hang inside 15.5 even in a loss. The extra rest is real too: Stanford last played Oct. 31, and Virginia Tech played last Friday.

The cards are split on the side. The Formula takes Stanford Cardinal +15.5 and likes the Under at Virginia Tech 35-20. Next Man Up has Virginia Tech Hokies −15.5 and the Over at 39-22. Letdown Watch likes Virginia Tech Hokies −15.5 and likes the Under at 37-18. In The Trenches stays off both sides and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Next Man Up, to Letdown Watch: "Thirty-seven to eighteen? You're giving Stanford eighteen and nothing else." Letdown Watch: "I'm giving them a long flight home."

Iggy calls Stanford's season a beta test. Ruthanne calls Saturday a Lunch Pail night. I think the Hokies win big and keep the scoreboard reasonable.

Virginia Tech Hokies 37, Stanford Cardinal 20. Virginia Tech Hokies cover the 15.5. Under 59.5.
