Stanford at Virginia Tech: Blacksburg Is a Two-Plane Trip, and Stanford Took Both

Stanford Cardinal (4-5) at Virginia Tech Hokies (6-3)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Lane Stadium · ACC Network

XFacebook

Getting to Blacksburg is part of the test. The Stanford Daily says the Cardinal's last long road trip of the year turned into a two-plane, two-hotel trip, because there is no major airport anywhere near the place. Hokies coach James Franklin has been talking about that lack of a nearby airport, too.

The number as of Nov. 14, 2026, 10:08 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at Virginia Tech −16.5, total 57.5. Every pick in this story, mine and each handicapper's, is checked against that number, not Monday's. Monday's card had Virginia Tech −15.5 with a 59.5 total. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

Waiting at Lane Stadium is a theme night for Ag Day and Veterans Day, per Virginia Tech on SI, in a mountain town of about 45,000 that empties into the stadium. Stanford offensive coordinator Terry Heffernan, who spent three years at Virginia, told the Daily the whole town shows up and the place rocks.

Stanford is 4-5 and needs two of its last three to reach a bowl. Senior receiver Caden High called the locker room “very optimistic and confident.” Virginia Tech is 6-3 but coming off a 51-28 loss to SMU that was close until SMU scored 21 straight points to start the second half, per the Daily.

Ethan Grunkemeyer has thrown for 2,431 yards and 18 touchdowns with three interceptions, and Jeffrey Overton Jr. has 762 rushing yards, per AP. Heffernan thinks there is room to throw: “SMU was able to find some cracks, and we feel like we’ve got a pretty good game plan for that.”
