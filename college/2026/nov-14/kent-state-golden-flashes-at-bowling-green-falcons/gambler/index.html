Kent State vs Bowling Green Pick: Falcons −5.5 on Founders Day

Kent State Golden Flashes (3-6) at Bowling Green Falcons (2-7)

7:00 PM ET Tue Nov 10 · Doyt L. Perry Stadium · ESPNU

XFacebook

Bowling Green throws itself a birthday party on Tuesday night, and Kent State keeps showing up with the one present the Falcons want back. My Kent State vs Bowling Green pick is Bowling Green Falcons −5.5 with the Under 44.5, and my final is Bowling Green Falcons 27, Kent State Golden Flashes 17, as BGSU celebrates Founders Day at Doyt L. Perry Stadium with the Anniversary Award up for grabs at 7:00 PM ET.

That trophy honors two schools that were founded the same year, and Darcy Fenwick wants it home for the birthday. "Myles Bradley has 9 sacks. Dorian Pringle has 86 tackles. Kent State scores 14.3 a game, Ozzie. I've stopped scarier breakaways in a Saturday morning youth clinic."

Ozzie Brannigan sighs like a man who has seen this movie. "I expected that. But Dru DeShields threw for 294 and four touchdowns against Akron. Wayne Harris had 122 yards last week. And we took the trophy last year, 24-21. I expected to lose that one too."

Here's why I lay the 5.5. Kent State's offense has been stuck on the road all year: 0 at South Carolina, 3 at Ohio State, 7 at Western Michigan, 2 at South Florida and 10 at Sacramento State. The Flashes score 14.3 a game and allow 30.6. Bowling Green is 2-7, but the Falcons put 24 on Miami and 38 on Sacramento State, lost to Ball State 32-31 in overtime and fell to Buffalo 31-24. Jay Kastantin has completed 150 of 225 with 11 touchdowns. My final has Bowling Green by 10, four and a half clear of the number.

Neither quarterback spot is a question. DeShields has taken the most throws in all nine Kent State games, and Kastantin has done the same for Bowling Green. Bradley against a Kent State front that has to protect DeShields on the road is the matchup I keep circling.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 44.5 and it's down to 43.5, while the spread is back at 5.5. I'm staying Under 44.5. Kent State has scored 10 or fewer in six of nine games, and Bowling Green managed 10 at Western Michigan last time out. My 27-17 lands on 44.

Most of the room rides with the Falcons, and the total splits. Next Man Up has Bowling Green 26-17, likes the −5.5 and goes Under 44.5. Letdown Watch has it 29-16, likes the Falcons −5.5 and goes Over 44.5. The Formula has 27-19, lays the 5.5 and goes Over. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call on this one. Follow The Money has no side pick and likes the Under 43.5.

Darcy taps her stick twice on the rail. Ozzie folds his hands and waits for the worst, which is exactly how he waited last October.

Bowling Green Falcons 27, Kent State Golden Flashes 17. Bowling Green Falcons cover the 5.5. Under 44.5.
