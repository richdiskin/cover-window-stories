Kent State vs Bowling Green: Founders Day Fight for the Anniversary Award

Kent State Golden Flashes (3-6) at Bowling Green Falcons (2-7)

7:00 PM ET Tue Nov 10 · Doyt L. Perry Stadium · ESPNU

XFacebook

Bowling Green turns Tuesday night into a birthday party. Kent State Golden Flashes vs Bowling Green Falcons is the Founders Day game at Doyt L. Perry Stadium, and the trophy waiting on the sideline was built for exactly this kind of night: the Anniversary Award, made to honor two schools that were born in the same year, 1910.

Darcy Fenwick has been staring at that trophy since last October, when Kent State kept it with a 24-21 win. Ozzie Brannigan has been staring at the schedule, the weather and the ceiling, because Ozzie expects the worst every week and gets grumpy when he's wrong.

"Founders Day," Darcy says. "Fan and Student Appreciation Night. Students claim free tickets. The whole campus shows up to celebrate the school's birthday, and you're the guy who brought the present we want back."

"I expected a warm welcome," Ozzie says. "I did not get one. This is fine. This is what I expected."

The history of the thing is wonderful, and both of them know it cold. The Lowry Bill created both schools in 1910. The alumni offices dreamed up the Anniversary Award in 1985 to mark their 75th birthdays, and Bowling Green has won it 27 times to Kent State's 13. Darcy says those numbers like a goalie reading off a save chart. Ozzie counters with the recent stuff, because the recent stuff is the only part he enjoys.

"We won it five years in a row," he says. "Twenty-eighteen through twenty-twenty-two. Sixty-two points, twice. Then you won it back two straight, then we took it last year. So it's our turn to lose. I've made peace with that."

"You have not made peace with anything," Darcy says. "You're wearing two jackets."

He is wearing two jackets.

Here's the football. Kent State is 3-6 and 2-3 in the MAC, and the Golden Flashes run their offense through Dru DeShields, who has thrown for 1,629 yards with 9 touchdowns and 7 interceptions. Wayne Harris is the guy he looks for first, with 43 catches for 674 yards and 5 scores, and Harris went for 122 yards at Sacramento State on Halloween eve. When DeShields gets time, he can sling it: 294 yards and four touchdowns against Akron in October.

The trouble has been everywhere else. Kent State scores 14.3 points a game and gives up 30.6. Away from home this year, the Flashes have put up 0 at South Carolina, 3 at Ohio State, 7 at Western Michigan, 2 at South Florida and 10 at Sacramento State.

"Those were tough rooms," Ozzie says. "I expected them to be tough rooms."

"This is a tough room," Darcy says. "It's my room."

Bowling Green walks in at 2-7, also 2-3 in the league, and the Falcons have lost three straight by a combined 21 points. That hurts, but it also tells you how close they've been. Ball State beat them 32-31 in overtime. Buffalo won 31-24. Before that, Bowling Green went to Oxford and beat Miami 24-20, then put 38 on Sacramento State.

Jay Kastantin has completed 150 of his 225 throws for 1,567 yards and 11 touchdowns. Jeremiah Scoby, the tight end, leads the team with 31 catches, and Austyn Dendy has 371 rushing yards and 4 scores. But Darcy's heart lives on the other side of the ball, where Myles Bradley has 9 sacks and Dorian Pringle has 86 tackles.

"Nine sacks," Darcy says. "That's a guy who lives in the crease. Ozzie, your quarterback is going to see him in his sleep."

"I already do," Ozzie says. "I expected to."

Kent State has its own disrupter in Jaihien Roy, who has 5 sacks, and Hayden Dawahare has 50 tackles at linebacker. Ozzie lists them quietly, the way some people list their blessings, then immediately adds that Bowling Green has thrown 8 interceptions and that he will be watching every one of Kastantin's throws like it owes him money.

Here's how the birthday goes. The crowd is louder than a 2-7 team usually earns, because Founders Day crowds don't care about records. Bradley gets home early and DeShields spends the first half running for his life. Harris breaks one deep in the second quarter to keep Kent State close, and Ozzie stands up, then sits back down because he doesn't trust it. Kastantin finds Scoby on the long third downs, Dendy grinds out the clock, and Pringle is in on every tackle that matters. Kent State scores late, but it's too late.

Darcy bangs her stick on the rail like the horn just sounded. Ozzie takes off one of his two jackets, folds it over his arm and stares at the trophy as it heads back across the field.

"I expected this," he says, and for once, he really did.

My call: Bowling Green Falcons 27, Kent State Golden Flashes 17.
