Jacksonville State vs Western Kentucky: Homecoming Hill Fight

Jacksonville State Gamecocks (7-2) at Western Kentucky Hilltoppers (3-6)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Houchens Industries-L.T. Smith Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Big Red's World Tour is the Homecoming theme on the Hill this year, and the tour's first big stop is Jacksonville State Gamecocks vs Western Kentucky Hilltoppers in Week 11 at Houchens-Smith Stadium in Bowling Green. Jacksonville State insider Burl Ashcraft-Neeley brought a smoker and a Conference USA lead, and Western Kentucky insider Thurman "Tops" Whitsitt brought his barber clippers and a Homecoming crowd dressed in red.

Burl: Thurman, I've had brisket on since four in the morning. Low and slow. That's how we do football in Calhoun County too.

Thurman: Burl, I've had a chair open since six. Low and slow is how you'll leave Bowling Green. Sit down. You need a trim before the parade.

Jacksonville State is 7-2 and 5-0 in Conference USA play, alone in first place. The Gamecocks opened with a 33-7 loss at North Dakota State and a 29-27 overtime loss at Ohio, and they haven't lost since. Last Saturday they ran past Sam Houston 47-14. Caden Creel completed 18 of 28 throws for 246 yards and two touchdowns, then ran 13 times for 87 yards and another score.

Burl: Creel is the whole pit, Thurman. He's the fire, the wood and the guy holding the tongs.

Thurman: He's also thrown eight interceptions, Burl. That fire sparks a little.

Burl: Watch it, now.

Thurman isn't wrong about the sparks. Creel has 1,987 throwing yards with 15 touchdowns and eight interceptions, plus 800 rushing yards and six touchdowns on the ground. Deondre Johnson is his top deep target, with 520 yards on just 26 catches. And the Gamecocks have lived dangerously in tight games, winning 27-26 at Kennesaw State, 27-24 over FIU and 31-27 over Georgia Southern.

Put plainly, Creel throws the ball well, runs it better than most backs, and gives a few away. Johnson stretches the field. And Jacksonville State has won those close games by one, three and four points.

Burl: Close is close. We win them.

Western Kentucky is 3-6 and 2-3 in Conference USA, and Thurman will tell you the first month was brutal. The Hilltoppers opened at Nevada, at Georgia and at Indiana, and lost all three. But they've shown life. They beat Missouri State 34-13 and won 16-3 at Sam Houston. Last week they lost a wild one at Middle Tennessee, 51-42, and the offense finally hummed.

Thurman: Forty-two points at Murfreesboro, Burl. Ajay Allen ran for 116 yards. Jonathan Stafford Jr. caught eight balls for 90. Brock Glenn didn't throw an interception. I had that game on the shop radio, and three customers walked out with half a haircut.

Burl: That's on you, not on football.

Allen leads the Hilltoppers with 535 rushing yards and six touchdowns. Stafford has 54 catches. Jaylen Lewis has 66 tackles on a defense that has been asked to make a lot of them. Western Kentucky gives up 33.3 points a game, while Jacksonville State allows 19.2.

Burl: That's the difference, Thurman. Our defense shows up. Caleb Nix has 56 tackles from safety.

Thurman: And our offense just put up 42. Somebody has to give.

These two have made a habit of drama. Jacksonville State won 20-17 in 2023, Western Kentucky won 19-17 in late 2024, and the Gamecocks took the league title game a week later, 52-12. Last year Jacksonville State won again, 37-34. Burl says the Gamecocks own the series 3-1 in the recent meetings. Thurman says the 19-17 game is the only one he remembers.

Thurman: Selective memory, Burl. It's a barber's gift. I forget the bad haircuts too.

Burl: I've seen your haircuts. You forget a lot of them.

So here's the Homecoming question in Bowling Green. Can the Hilltoppers run Allen enough to keep Creel on the sideline, or does Creel keep doing Creel things until the Gamecocks pull away late?

Thurman: If we win, I'm giving free haircuts Monday. Gamecocks fans get the bowl cut.

Burl: If we win, I'm bringing brisket to your shop. You'll have to sweep up the burnt ends.

Thurman: Deal. Bring sauce.

Burl brings the brisket. Thurman brings the sauce. The Gamecocks bring home the win. My call: Jacksonville State Gamecocks 30, Western Kentucky Hilltoppers 23.
