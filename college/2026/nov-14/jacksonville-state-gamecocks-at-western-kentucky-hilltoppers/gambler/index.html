Jacksonville State vs WKU Pick: WKU +7.5, Under 53.5

Jacksonville State Gamecocks (7-2) at Western Kentucky Hilltoppers (3-6)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Houchens Industries-L.T. Smith Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Western Kentucky's Homecoming theme is Big Red's World Tour, and the first stop on Saturday is a Conference USA leader that keeps winning close games. In Week 11 at Houchens-Smith Stadium in Bowling Green, my Jacksonville State vs Western Kentucky pick is Western Kentucky Hilltoppers +7.5, plus the Under 53.5.

Burl Ashcraft-Neeley cooks brisket in Calhoun County and says Jacksonville State football is the same job. "Low and slow, Thurman. We're 5-0 in conference. Caden Creel threw for 246 yards and ran for 87 against Sam Houston. We won 47-14."

Thurman "Tops" Whitsitt cuts hair in Warren County and has heard every brag in the chair. "Your boys beat Kennesaw State 27-26 and FIU 27-24. You beat Georgia Southern 31-27. That's not low and slow. That's sweating it out."

Thurman is onto something. Jacksonville State is 7-2 and 1st in Conference USA, but three of its seven wins came by four points or fewer. The Gamecocks score 29.1 points a game and allow 19.2. Creel is the whole operation, with 1,987 throwing yards, 15 touchdowns and 800 rushing yards. He also has eight interceptions, and he threw two in each of the wins over Middle Tennessee and New Mexico State.

Western Kentucky is 3-6 and gives up 33.3 points a game, so I'm not pretending the Hilltoppers are good. But they score 23.6, and they put up 42 at Middle Tennessee last week in a 42-51 loss. Ajay Allen ran 15 times for 116 yards in that game, and Jonathan Stafford Jr. caught eight balls for 90 yards. Allen has 535 rushing yards and six touchdowns on the season.

This series has teeth. Jacksonville State won 37-34 last year, and the 2023 meeting ended 20-17. The one rout, 52-12, came in a title game. Homecoming in Bowling Green is not the spot for a blowout.

My final is Jacksonville State Gamecocks 30, Western Kentucky Hilltoppers 23. That's a seven-point game, short of 7.5, so I want the Hilltoppers with the hook.

On the over/under, I'm on the Under 53.5. DraftKings opened it at 52.5 and it has moved up a point. The Gamecocks hold opponents under 20 a game, and Creel's offense chews clock with his legs. My 30-23 lands at 53, half a point under.

The room is split. The Formula has Western Kentucky +7.5 and the Under at 29-23. Next Man Up has Western Kentucky +7.5 but sits on the Over, 31-24. Letdown Watch goes the other way, with Jacksonville State −7.5 and the Under at 30-21. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call here. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Letdown Watch: "Seven-two team, three-six team. I don't need a haircut to see this one."

Next Man Up: "You need a haircut, period. Thurman's open Saturday morning."

Burl wants the slow-cooked conference title chase to stay on schedule. Thurman wants one more Homecoming memory on the Hill. I think the Gamecocks win and the Hilltoppers keep it inside the hook.

Jacksonville State Gamecocks 30, Western Kentucky Hilltoppers 23. Western Kentucky Hilltoppers cover the 7.5. Under 53.5.
