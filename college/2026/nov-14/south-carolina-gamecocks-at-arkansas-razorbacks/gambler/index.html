South Carolina vs Arkansas Pick: Gamecocks −7.5, Over 51.5

South Carolina Gamecocks (6-3) at Arkansas Razorbacks (3-6)

7:45 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Donald W. Reynolds Razorback Stadium · SEC Network

XFacebook

South Carolina vs Arkansas prediction: No. 21 South Carolina, 6-3, plays Arkansas, 3-6, at Razorback Stadium in Fayetteville on Saturday at 7:45 p.m. ET. I'm on South Carolina Gamecocks −7.5 and Over 51.5, with South Carolina Gamecocks 31, Arkansas Razorbacks 22 as my final.

Beau Cheraw, the old Gamecock drumline snare who still air-drums the Gamecocks' entrance music in traffic, likes the run his team is on. "Four straight wins. We won at Florida and at Oklahoma, then held Texas A&M to nine. LaNorris Sellers has 17 touchdown throws and 637 rushing yards." Bubba Fayette-Dorsey, the Ozark science teacher, has the home crowd and a memory. "We beat you 44-30 in 2022. Cam Settles ran for 100 at Auburn last week, and we lost that one by three."

The side comes from South Carolina's form. The Gamecocks beat Florida 38-19, Tennessee 27-24, Oklahoma 42-25 on the road and Texas A&M 17-9. They score 34.7 points a game. Arkansas scores 20.0 and has lost big to the best teams on its schedule: 43-10 at Utah, 45-17 to Georgia and 34-10 to Missouri at home two weeks ago. The Razorbacks did lose only 26-23 at Auburn, so they're not done fighting. The market opened South Carolina −8.5 and slipped to −7.5. My nine-point Gamecocks win clears it.

The Over is mostly a South Carolina call. Gamecock games have finished above 51.5 combined points in seven of nine, and Sellers creates points with his legs as well as his arm. Arkansas allows 27.4 a game, so the Gamecocks should get to 30. The Razorbacks need to chip in, and KJ Jackson has thrown for 1,853 yards with Chris Marshall catching 698 of them. Arkansas put up 31 at Vanderbilt and 34 against Tulsa. The posted number is 51.5, and my 53 clears it.

The risk is the South Carolina defense in a road night game. The Gamecocks gave up 41 to Mississippi State and 35 to Kentucky at home, and Arkansas has a crowd that can make it loud. If South Carolina's offense stalls the way it did against Texas A&M, 17 points won't cover anything.

This room agrees on the side. The Formula, Next Man Up and Letdown Watch all have South Carolina Gamecocks −7.5, and In The Trenches likes South Carolina Gamecocks −7.5 too. The Formula has it 30-22, Next Man Up likes the Over at 31-23, and Letdown Watch has the Over as well. In The Trenches makes no over/under call. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Letdown Watch, to Next Man Up: "Ranked team, fourth straight win, road trip to a 3-6 team. Classic trap." Next Man Up: "Then why do you have South Carolina by eight?" Letdown Watch: "Because I can count."

Bubba says Fayetteville is where good seasons go to get complicated. Beau says the Gamecocks are past complicated. I think Sellers makes the difference.

South Carolina Gamecocks 31, Arkansas Razorbacks 22. South Carolina Gamecocks cover the 7.5. Over 51.5.
