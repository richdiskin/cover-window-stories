Nebraska vs Rutgers: Flag Helmets and a Flyover in Piscataway

Nebraska Cornhuskers (5-4) at Rutgers Scarlet Knights (2-7)

12:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · SHI Stadium · BTN

XFacebook

Nebraska Cornhuskers vs Rutgers Scarlet Knights is Rutgers' Military Appreciation game at SHI Stadium, and the Scarlet Knights will take the field in their American flag Block R helmets with military vehicles parked along The Rutgers Boardwalk and a flyover planned right after the national anthem. Vinny Scolari has been on the Boardwalk since it opened at 8:30, working through the food trucks, and Nebraska's Darla Rasmussen arrives in red with the patient look of a fan who has watched four straight close Saturdays slip away.

Vinny: Darla, you came all the way from Lincoln for a flyover and a Jersey food truck. That's the smartest thing anybody in red has done all month.

Darla: I came for a win, Vinny. The food truck is a bonus. The flyover is a blessing.

Both of them mean the second part. The day honors current service members, veterans and ROTC cadets throughout the game, and some lucky fans will help hold the U.S. flag on the field during the anthem. Vinny swears he'll keep quiet for the whole thing, which is a first for a man from Exit 9.

The football part starts with Nebraska's slide. The Huskers began 5-0, beating Maryland 48-23 and Michigan State 31-13 along the way, then lost four in a row by a combined 24 points, 20-17 to Indiana, 17-14 at Oregon, 17-10 to Washington and 24-13 at Illinois. Darla calls it the most frustrating month since she gave up volleyball.

Darla: Four losses, and we were in every one of them. Our defense allows 16.6 points a game. That's a real defense, Vinny.

Vinny: It's a real defense. Your offense just keeps parking in the wrong lot.

Anthony Colandrea is the guy who can fix that. He has 2,109 throwing yards with 16 touchdowns, and he threw for 284 yards on 32-of-43 at Illinois even in a loss. Kwazi Gilmer has 46 catches for 603 yards and seven touchdowns, and Jamal Rule leads the run game with 532 yards. On defense, Jahsear Whittington has 4.5 sacks and linebacker Owen Chambliss has 55 tackles.

Rutgers is 2-7 and has had a long autumn, but Vinny isn't hiding from it. The Scarlet Knights have been close more than once, 28-21 at Boston College, 42-35 to USC and 28-21 to Michigan on Halloween. They also won 37-30 at Maryland, which Vinny considers a holiday. Antwan Raymond is the workhorse, 789 rushing yards and six touchdowns on 149 carries, and KJ Duff has 774 receiving yards and nine touchdowns. The quarterback spot has rotated. Dylan Lonergan leads the team in throwing yards, while AJ Surace had the most throws in each of the last three games.

Vinny: Whoever's back there, Raymond's getting the ball. That kid runs like he's late for the train to Penn Station.

Darla: And our front seven is the reason trains run on time in Nebraska.

That's the heart of the game. Raymond against the Nebraska front, and Colandrea against a Rutgers defense that has given up 32.4 points a game. The Huskers have won the last four meetings, but three of them were close, 14-13 here in 2022, 14-7 in Lincoln in 2024 and 28-21 in 2020. Nobody in Piscataway is conceding anything before kickoff.

Vinny: Four in a row, sure. One by a point and one by a touchdown. Point is, we hang.

Darla: You hang. We win. That's the Big Ten in one sentence.

The Boardwalk has free rides and games for the kids before the pregame parade and the Scarlet Walk, and by the time the team arrives, the whole lot will be red, white and blue. Darla admits the American flag helmets look sharp. Vinny admits Nebraska fans travel like nobody else in the conference.

Vinny: I'll even let you have a sub from the Boardwalk. On the house. Don't tell anybody I'm nice.

Darla: Your secret's safe. Ours is that we've been waiting a month to throw a party, and today's the day we bring the balloons.

Vinny: Bring 'em. Just don't let 'em go before the flyover.

Darla: Vinny, they go up when we score. You'll see plenty.

The flyover gets the loudest cheer of the day, right up until the Huskers pull away. My call: Nebraska Cornhuskers 33, Rutgers Scarlet Knights 21.
