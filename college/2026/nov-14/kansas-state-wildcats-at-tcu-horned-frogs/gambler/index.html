Kansas State at TCU: Fort Worth Waits to See Which Wildcat Takes the Snap

Kansas State Wildcats (4-5) at TCU Horned Frogs (4-5)

2:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Amon G. Carter Stadium · ESPNU

XFacebook

Market check, Kansas State at TCU: DraftKings TCU -13.5, total 44.5.

Fort Worth will see a Kansas State quarterback Saturday. The question is which one. The Wildcats have started four different quarterbacks in four straight games, a first in program history, per the Kansas State Collegian. Collin Klein says Blake Barnett and Dillon Duff are both competing, and both could play at Amon G. Carter Stadium.

The number as of Nov. 14, 2026, 4:18 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at TCU -13.5, total 44.5. The Cover Window's published pick is Kansas State +9.5 and Over 46.5. The market has gone to TCU -13.5 with a total of 44.5, so the Wildcats now get four more points than the card did and the total is two points below the published Over. The card's projected final is TCU Horned Frogs 27, Kansas State Wildcats 21 (Total 48). That pick was published against the Nov. 9, 2026, 9:18 AM ET line, it has not been changed, and this story issues no new one. Prices move, so check yours before kickoff. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

Injuries and lineup: the Collegian (Nov. 13) reports the Barnett-Duff quarterback competition is unresolved. We found no Big 12 availability list in our check.

K-State is coming off a 66-28 loss to Oklahoma State that Klein called one of the most frustrating losses of his coaching career. Now he gets a defense he respects. “It's their front seven,” Klein said. “They're extremely disruptive. They can rush the passer. They really get off blocks and destroy blocks in the run game.”

TCU is hurting in its own way. The Frogs led Arizona by eight with under seven minutes left last week, then lost 29-28 after a late score, a recovered pooch kick and a field goal with 38 seconds to go. Jaden Craig accounted for all three touchdowns, tight end Kari Ashley caught a career-high 14 passes, and linebacker Hudson Hooper returned an interception 93 yards for a score.

Both teams are 4-5. K-State has won the last three meetings, but its last trip to Fort Worth in 2022 went sideways when TCU erased a 28-10 deficit to win 38-28. ESPN's data capsule has TCU allowing 15.8 points a game, 12th in FBS, and K-State running for 196.8 a game. The Frogs' fans want a finish that doesn't sting. The Wildcats just want to know who's under center.

The published card stays Kansas State Wildcats +9.5 and Over 46.5 at the numbers it was written against. My final is TCU Horned Frogs 27, Kansas State Wildcats 21. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

TCU Horned Frogs 27, Kansas State Wildcats 21. Kansas State Wildcats cover the 9.5. Over 46.5.
