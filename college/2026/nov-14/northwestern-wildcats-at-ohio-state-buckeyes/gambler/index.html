Northwestern vs Ohio State Prediction: Wildcats +11.5, Over 47.5

Northwestern Wildcats (6-3) at Ohio State Buckeyes (6-3)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Ohio Stadium · Fox

XFacebook

Northwestern at Ohio State is the Military Appreciation Game at Ohio Stadium, presented through the Our Honor Defend program, with a noon kickoff on FOX a week after both teams lost. My Northwestern vs Ohio State prediction is Ohio State Buckeyes 30, Northwestern Wildcats 19, with Northwestern Wildcats +11.5 and Over 47.5.

Brecken "Buck" Holloway, the Buckeyes insider, talks like the band already started. "Julian Sayin has 19 touchdowns. Jeremiah Smith has 1,200 yards. Military Appreciation does not do soft scripts." Ezra Lindqvist, Northwestern's insider, answers with Aidan Chiles. "1,898 yards and one interception. We just played Iowa tough. Your number is still too wide."

The market moved toward Ohio State. DraftKings opened Ohio State −9.5 with 48.5 on the over/under, and it's now −11.5 and 47.5. I still want the points on Northwestern. Ohio State is 6-3 after a 28-41 loss to Oregon. Northwestern is coming off a 10-24 loss to Iowa. The Buckeyes score 35.7 a game. The Wildcats allow 19.3. That gap is real, but 11.5 points after an Ohio State loss is a number I will take on a road dog that keeps games ugly.

Sayin has 2,007 passing yards. Smith has 69 catches for 14 scores. Bo Jackson has 685 rushing yards. Ohio State still looks like a top-shelf offense on the weeks it finishes drives. Northwestern's Aidan Chiles has 1,898 yards with 13 touchdowns and one interception. Caleb Komolafe has 643 rushing yards. Griffin Wilde has 813 receiving yards. The Wildcats can move it. They just have to survive Smith's deep shots long enough for the points to matter.

The Over is about Ohio State's floor against a Northwestern defense that just gave up 24 to Iowa. Six of Ohio State's nine games cleared 47.5 combined points. Northwestern's last two finished in a one-score road fight and a 10-24 loss to Iowa. My 49 points sit above 47.5, and the market already shaved a point off the open.

The room mostly lays the points. The Formula has Ohio State Buckeyes −11.5 and likes Over 47.5 at 32-20. Next Man Up likes Ohio State Buckeyes −11.5 and Over 47.5 at 32-18. Letdown Watch likes Ohio State Buckeyes −11.5 and Over 47.5 at 31-18. In The Trenches likes Northwestern Wildcats +11.5 and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

In The Trenches: "You three laid 11 and a half after Oregon hung 41 on you." Next Man Up: "Smith still has 1,200 yards. The points can wait."

Military Appreciation gets the ceremony. Northwestern get the points in a Horseshoe that still favors explosive plays upstairs.

Ohio State Buckeyes 30, Northwestern Wildcats 19. Northwestern Wildcats cover the 11.5. Over 47.5.
