Texas at LSU: Death Valley Retires Bert Jones' Jersey and Welcomes Arch Home

Texas Longhorns (7-2) at LSU Tigers (6-3)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Tiger Stadium (LA) · ABC

XFacebook

Tiger Stadium closes its home schedule with a big one. LSU says this is the first meeting in Tiger Stadium between LSU and Texas since 1953, on Senior Night, with LED bracelets for the crowd and Bert Jones, the Tigers' first All-American quarterback, getting his jersey retired at halftime. LSU lists kickoff at 6:45 p.m. Central on ABC.

The number as of Nov. 14, 2026, 10:08 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at LSU −4.5, total 46.5. Every pick in this story, mine and each handicapper's, is checked against that number, not Monday's. Monday's card had Texas +3.5 with a 45.5 total. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

The Tigers walk down Victory Hill at 4:46 p.m. Central, by LSU's own timeline, and the Golden Band from Tigerland follows a few minutes later. Texas almost missed the parade. The Longhorns took off from Austin just after 10 p.m. Central Friday and were expected in Baton Rouge before midnight, according to Horns247 reports cited by Burnt Orange Nation and tracked by Undrafted.

Arch Manning grew up in New Orleans and went to big LSU home games as a kid, AP notes. Now he gets a Saturday night in Death Valley against a defense that has 36 sacks, third in the country, led by Princewill Umanmielen with 8.5. Texas has given up 27. Steve Sarkisian called LSU “arguably the most talented team” Texas will face: “It’s hard to see how they lost three games.”

Lane Kiffin has heard the noise after a 31-13 home loss to Alabama. “The basketball hoop’s stupid, and they shouldn’t have a little kid on the smoke machine,” he said, summing up the critics. “I just know all that’s what comes when you lose. When you win, it’s all different.” He also said this: “The games that we’ve needed to finish we’ve gone 0-for-3.” Kiffin and Sarkisian are old friends from USC and Alabama, and Sarkisian recalled Pete Carroll's lesson that “there’s nothing more gratifying than beating your friends.”

Injury check, SEC availability report Friday, Nov. 13 (Inside Texas/On3): Texas out: LT Trevor Goosby, RB Hollywood Smothers, TE Nick Townsend, DE Richard Wesley; DT Hero Kanu probable; OL Cole Hutson questionable. WR Cam Coleman and DE Colin Simmons were not on the report all week. LSU: LB Whit Weeks questionable, OT Weston Davis probable, four players out.

The desks at today's number. Follow The Money has no side pick and no total pick. The Formula: LSU 26, Texas 25. That makes it Texas +4.5 and Over 46.5. Next Man Up: LSU 25, Texas 23. That makes it Texas +4.5 and Over 46.5. Letdown Watch: LSU 25, Texas 24. That makes it Texas +4.5 and Over 46.5. In The Trenches has no pick: its run-game reads favour different teams.

A jersey goes up in the rafters, a Louisiana kid comes home in the other team's colors and two old friends try to beat each other. Death Valley at night does not need more than that.

The Cover Writer's card at today's number: Texas Longhorns +4.5 and Over 46.5. My final is LSU Tigers 25, Texas Longhorns 24. Texas gets a point more than Monday. Texas losing by one covers 4.5, and our 49 is still over 46.5. Arch Manning comes home, and I'm taking the points with him.

LSU Tigers 25, Texas Longhorns 24. Texas Longhorns cover the 4.5. Over 46.5.
