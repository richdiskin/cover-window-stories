Colorado State vs Washington State Pick: Rams +9.5, Under 67.5

Colorado State Rams (3-6) at Washington State Cougars (1-8)

10:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Martin Stadium · CBSSN

XFacebook

Colorado State vs Washington State prediction: Colorado State, 3-6, makes the trip to Martin Stadium in Pullman to play 1-8 Washington State, and the kickoff is 10:30 p.m. ET Saturday. I'm on Colorado State Rams +9.5 and Under 67.5, with Washington State Cougars 35, Colorado State Rams 27 as my final.

Hank Palouse, who grows wheat outside Pullman and has sat through every one of these losses, thinks this is the week. "We had last week off. Owen Eshelman threw for 299 at San Diego State. Colorado State gives up 44.8 a game." Ansel Rimrock, the Fort Collins trail runner, isn't buying a 1-8 favorite. "Hauss Hejny has 16 touchdown throws and two picks. We scored 38 on Boise State last week. Your team scores 19 a game."

The side is about what Washington State can actually produce. The Cougars score 19.0 points a game and have scored 24 or fewer in seven of nine. They lost 26-6 to Fresno State and 31-10 to Boise State at home. Covering 9.5 would take a big offensive night from a team that hasn't had many. Colorado State has scored 23 or more in eight of nine games, including 45 at UTSA and 56 against San Diego State. The market opened Washington State −6.5 and climbed to −9.5, which feels like a reaction to how bad the Rams' defense is. I have Washington State by eight.

The Under is a Washington State call. Cougar games have stayed under 67.5 in eight of nine, and the only exception was a 48-30 loss at Oregon State. Colorado State's games go high, but this one is in Pullman in November against an offense that doesn't push the pace. The posted number climbed from 62.5 to 67.5. My 62 combined points land under it.

The risk is the Colorado State defense. The Rams allow 44.8 points a game, gave up 64 to Boise State last week and 66 at Texas State. If Washington State's offense finally wakes up against the worst defense it's seen, the Cougars could win by three scores and push this over by themselves.

The cards are split. The Formula likes Colorado State Rams +9.5 and likes the Under at Washington State 32-27. Next Man Up has Washington State Cougars −9.5 and the Over at 39-29. Letdown Watch likes Washington State Cougars −9.5 and likes the Under at 37-22. In The Trenches likes Colorado State Rams +9.5 and stays out of the over/under. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Next Man Up, to Letdown Watch: "A 1-8 team laying nine and a half, and you like it?" Letdown Watch: "I like the other team's defense even less."

Hank says the Cougars are due for one good night. Ansel says the Rams can outrun anybody's bad season. I think Washington State wins and Colorado State keeps it inside the number.

Washington State Cougars 35, Colorado State Rams 27. Colorado State Rams cover the 9.5. Under 67.5.
