San José State vs Air Force Pick: Spartans +11.5, Over 47.5

San José State Spartans (5-5) at Air Force Falcons (7-2)

7:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Falcon Stadium · CBSSN

XFacebook

San José State vs Air Force prediction: the Falcons come home to Colorado Springs with the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy after beating Navy and then Army 24-21 at West Point, and Falcon Stadium gets the Wings of Blue, a flyover and live falcons at halftime for a 7 p.m. ET kickoff. I'm on San José State Spartans +11.5 and Over 47.5, with Air Force Falcons 29, San José State Spartans 19 as my final.

Marissa Okonkwo-Hale, the retired Air Force instructor pilot, is still riding the high. "Evan Medders has 549 rushing yards and seven touchdowns, and Kemper Hodges ran for 119 at Army. Three quarterbacks, one offense." Tessa Nakagawa-Ruiz, the San José State product lead, came with a plan. "We allow 22.0 points a game. Dylan Lee has 88 tackles. We beat you 17-7 in 2024."

The side is about how Air Force wins. The Falcons are 7-2, but against top-division teams their wins came by 3, 5, 21, 10, 9 and 3. Only one of those cleared 11.5, at Northern Illinois. The option grinds clock, which shortens the game and keeps the other team close. San José State lost by three in overtime to Wyoming and by five to New Mexico, and it just beat Northern Illinois 30-19. My ten-point Falcons win lands inside 11.5.

There's a letdown angle, too. Air Force just won the trophy that defines its season, on a rainy night at West Point. DraftKings opened Air Force −10.5 with 49.5 points on the over/under, and the side has gone up a point to −11.5 since. I'm taking the extra point with the Spartans.

The over/under dropped two points to 47.5, and that's where I go the other way. Air Force's last two home games ended 41-31 against UNLV and 35-26 against UConn. The Falcons scored 36 at Nevada and 32 against North Dakota State. San José State put up 30 last week against Northern Illinois. My 48 combined points sit above 47.5.

The room is split everywhere. The Formula matches my score at 29-19, takes San José State Spartans +11.5 and takes the Over. Next Man Up goes the other way on both, Air Force Falcons −11.5 and the Under at 29-17. Letdown Watch likes San José State Spartans +11.5 and takes the Under. In The Trenches likes Air Force Falcons −11.5 and offers no over/under call. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Next Man Up, to The Formula: "Same score as the Cover Writer? You copying homework now?" The Formula: "Great minds. You're the one who thinks the Falcons blow out anybody."

Tessa says the altitude won't bother her. Marissa says the Falcons do the bothering in the fourth quarter, and I agree with her on the winner while taking the points that Air Force's own close finishes keep handing out.

Air Force Falcons 29, San José State Spartans 19. San José State Spartans cover the 11.5. Over 47.5.
