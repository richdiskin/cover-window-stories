Tennessee vs Texas A&M: Operation Blackout at Kyle Field

Tennessee Volunteers (7-2) at Texas A&M Aggies (4-5)

3:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Kyle Field · ESPN

XFacebook

Tennessee Volunteers vs Texas A&M Aggies is Operation Blackout at Kyle Field, and the Aggies want every one of those seats filled with black when the gates open at 12:30 and the ball goes up at 2 p.m. Colt Wendeborn has been practicing his yells all week in a closet so the neighbors don't complain, and he has a black shirt, black hat and black socks ready to go.

Colt: This is the third Blackout since Coach Elko got here, Earl. The first one, we beat No. 8 LSU, 38-23. Last year, we held Mississippi State to nine. Darkness is good to us.

Earl "Rocky Top" Haskew, a former Vol long snapper, plays a banjo intro before he answers, because Earl plays a banjo intro before everything.

Earl: Colt, I'm bringing orange into the dark. A little checkerboard never hurt anybody.

Colt: It hurts the Blackout, Earl. That's the whole point of the Blackout.

Colt then explains the Blackout. Then he explains why the 12th Man stands the entire game. Then he explains the explanation. Earl strums through all of it.

The meetings between these two have been memorable. In 2016, the Aggies won a wild one here, 45-38. In 2020 they went to Knoxville and won 34-13. Tennessee got one back at home in 2023, 20-13. That puts A&M up 2-1 in the recent meetings, and Colt will mention it several times.

Earl: Two-one isn't a dynasty, Colt. It's a bluegrass tune with three verses, and we're about to write the fourth.

Tennessee comes in 7-2 and playing the kind of football Earl's banjo was made for. The Vols score 32.4 points a game and allow just 15.1. They beat Alabama 26-10, won at Arkansas 24-17 and handled Kentucky 34-6 last week. Their two losses came by three points apiece, 20-17 to Texas and 27-24 at South Carolina. George MacIntyre has taken over the passing in October, and DeSean Bishop is the engine of the whole thing, with 1,066 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns. He ran for 109 and two scores against Kentucky. Mike Matthews has 566 receiving yards, Tyree Weathersby has seven sacks and Arion Carter has 59 tackles.

Earl: DeSean Bishop runs like Rocky Top on fast tempo. You can't stop him, you can only hum along.

Texas A&M is 4-5, and Colt is processing it in his closet. The Aggies are 1-5 in the SEC and lost their last two, 31-20 at Alabama and 17-9 at South Carolina. But the defense has been better than the record. A&M allows 18.7 points a game, and it shut out Missouri State and The Citadel. Marcel Reed has 1,641 throwing yards, Rueben Owens II ran for 137 yards at South Carolina, Isaiah Horton leads the receivers and Ray Coney leads the defense in tackles.

Colt: Rueben Owens ran like a Yell Leader on a dead sprint. That's a tradition, too. I just made it up, but it's a tradition now.

Earl: That's how all traditions get made, Colt. Somebody yells it first.

The key for the Aggies is the run defense. If Bishop gets 25 carries again, the Vols can play the way they want, keep the ball and wear down a defense in the fourth quarter. A&M has to win early downs and get Tennessee into passing situations, then let Marco Jones and the front go after MacIntyre. For Tennessee, the job is staying patient. Kyle Field gets loud, and the Blackout will make it look like midnight in the middle of the afternoon.

Colt: When that crowd sways, Earl, you'll feel it in your banjo strings.

Earl: My banjo has been to Razorback Stadium and Williams-Brice this year. It's not scared of a little darkness.

These two programs share a lot. Big stadiums, loud crowds and fans who will tell you every tradition whether you ask or not. Colt says the Aggies need this one for the season. Earl says the Vols need it for something bigger.

Earl: If we win, I'm playing Rocky Top on the drive back to Knoxville. The whole way home.

Colt: If we win, I'm yelling. Just yelling. Joyfully. For about an hour.

Colt yells for an hour. Earl plays Rocky Top all the way to Knoxville. My call: Tennessee Volunteers 25, Texas A&M Aggies 23.
