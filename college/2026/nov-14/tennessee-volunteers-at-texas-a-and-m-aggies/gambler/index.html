Tennessee vs Texas A&M Pick: Aggies +2.5, Over 42.5

Tennessee Volunteers (7-2) at Texas A&M Aggies (4-5)

3:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Kyle Field · ESPN

XFacebook

Tennessee at Texas A&M is Operation Blackout at Kyle Field, with the 12th Man in black and kickoff set for 3:00 PM ET. My Tennessee vs Texas A&M prediction has the Vols winning a close, physical game that finds more points than this price expects: Tennessee Volunteers 25, Texas A&M Aggies 23, with Texas A&M Aggies +2.5 and Over 42.5.

Earl Haskew tuned his banjo before he said a word. "DeSean Bishop has 1,066 yards and 13 touchdowns. We allow 15.1 points a game. Our two losses were by three each." Colt Wendeborn is in full Blackout. "We allow 18.7, Earl. Rueben Owens ran for 137 at South Carolina. And we're 2-1 against you in the last three meetings."

This is the biggest market move of my week. DraftKings opened Texas A&M −9.5 with 55.5 on the over/under. By Monday morning it had Tennessee −2.5 and 42.5. That's a twelve-point swing on the side and thirteen points off the total, and there is no injury report here to explain it, so I won't guess. What I can see is the season. Tennessee is 7-2 and beat Alabama 26-10. A&M is 4-5 and 1-5 in the SEC and has lost its last two, 31-20 at Alabama and 17-9 at South Carolina. That's why the price flipped. But it flipped all the way to a road favorite in a stadium where the Aggies beat the Vols 45-38 the last time they came here. My Vols by 2 means the 2.5 on A&M is the right side of a close game.

The Aggie defense keeps it close. A&M shut out Missouri State and The Citadel and held Arkansas to seven. Marcel Reed threw 43 times at South Carolina without an interception. Tennessee's quarterback position moved from Faizon Brandon to George MacIntyre in October, and MacIntyre has two touchdown passes for the season.

The Over is about how far the total fell. At 42.5, Tennessee's offense has to do little more than its usual work. The Vols have scored at least 24 points in eight of their nine games, with 34 against Kentucky last week. A&M has scored 20 or more in six of nine. My 48 points sit comfortably above 42.5, and the drop from 55.5 gives me plenty of room.

The room is split on the side and with me on the total. The Formula has Texas A&M Aggies +2.5 and likes Over 42.5, with Tennessee 25-23. Next Man Up likes Texas A&M Aggies +2.5 and has Over 42.5, with A&M 22-21. Letdown Watch has Tennessee Volunteers −2.5 and likes Over 42.5, 26-22. In The Trenches likes Tennessee Volunteers −2.5 and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Letdown Watch to Next Man Up: "You've got A&M winning outright? They've lost two straight." Next Man Up: "At home, in the dark, against a quarterback with two touchdown passes. I'll take it."

Earl gets a win and a long banjo ride home. Colt gets a Blackout crowd that never sits down and a game decided late.

Tennessee Volunteers 25, Texas A&M Aggies 23. Texas A&M Aggies cover the 2.5. Over 42.5.
