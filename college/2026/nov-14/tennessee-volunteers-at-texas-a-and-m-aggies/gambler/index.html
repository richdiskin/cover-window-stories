Tennessee at Texas A&M: Operation Blackout Gets a Visitor Who Landed After Midnight

Tennessee Volunteers (7-2) at Texas A&M Aggies (4-5)

3:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Kyle Field · ESPN

XFacebook

College Station is dressing in black today. Texas A&M picked Tennessee for Operation Blackout, the third one since Mike Elko took over, and the 12th Man has been asked to fill Kyle Field in black for a 2 p.m. Central kickoff. The first two Blackouts under Elko ended with wins over LSU and Mississippi State. The house has a dress code and a track record.

The number as of Nov. 14, 2026, 10:00 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at Tennessee −1.5, total 43.5. Every pick in this story, mine and each handicapper's, is checked against that number, not Monday's. Monday's card had Texas A&M +2.5 with a 42.5 total. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

Tennessee nearly missed the invitation. The Vols were on the tarmac in Knoxville when Friday's nationwide FAA ground stop hit, per On3 as tracked by Undrafted. The Daily Beacon logged the rest of the night: wheels up at 10:11 p.m. Eastern, touchdown in College Station just after midnight, and buses rolling at 12:27 a.m., more than eight hours after the team was supposed to leave. Texas A&M said after talking with the SEC and Tennessee that the game would go on at 2 p.m. Central as planned.

The man A&M has to tackle is DeSean Bishop. AP says his 109 yards against Kentucky pushed him past 1,000 for a second straight season, something only Johnnie Jones (1983-84) had done before him at Tennessee. His position coach, De’Rail Sims, is already campaigning: “There’s no doubt about it. He deserves it,” Sims said of the Heisman talk. On the other side, Rueben Owens II ran for 137 at South Carolina, and AP notes the Aggies gained 467 yards there with no turnovers and still did not score a touchdown, a first in college football history. Elko is keeping Holmon Wiggins as his play caller.

The injury news is heavy for the visitors. Tyree Weathersby, who had seven sacks, is done for the season with a knee injury, per AP. Rocky Top Insider and Vols on SI report backup running back Daune Morris broke his leg in a car accident Thursday night and is out for the year; we are praying for a full recovery. Guard Nic Moore was ruled out Thursday. For A&M, lineman Trovon Baugh and receiver Ashton Bethel-Roman are out, and receiver Mario Craver is questionable on Friday's report.

The desks at today's number. Follow The Money likes Texas A&M +1.5 and the Over 44.5. The Formula: Tennessee 25, Texas A&M 23. That makes it Tennessee −1.5 and Over 43.5. Next Man Up: Texas A&M 22, Tennessee 21. That makes it Texas A&M +1.5 and Under 43.5. Letdown Watch: Tennessee 26, Texas A&M 22. That makes it Tennessee −1.5 and Over 43.5. In The Trenches: Tennessee 26, Texas A&M 25, and it stays with Tennessee −1.5 off the run-game matchup, no total pick.

The Cover Writer's card at today's number: Tennessee Volunteers −1.5 and Over 43.5. My final is Tennessee Volunteers 25, Texas A&M Aggies 23. Monday's card took Texas A&M plus 2.5. The number has shrunk to 1.5, and our 25-23 has Tennessee by two, so the honest pick flips to Tennessee. Half a point decided it. I've lost arguments over less.

Tennessee Volunteers 25, Texas A&M Aggies 23. Tennessee Volunteers cover the 1.5. Over 43.5.
