Wake Forest vs SMU Pick: Deacons −1.5, Under 61.5

Wake Forest Demon Deacons (8-1) at SMU Mustangs (7-2)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Gerald J. Ford Stadium · ESPN

XFacebook

Wake Forest at SMU kicks off at noon ET on the Hilltop, a few hours after the Homecoming parade rolls down Bishop Boulevard and on the same morning SMU asks its whole crowd to wear blue for Senior Day. My Wake Forest vs SMU prediction is the visitors in a close one: Wake Forest Demon Deacons 30, SMU Mustangs 28, with Wake Forest Demon Deacons −1.5 and Under 61.5.

Eloise Brightman-Rowe, the Wake insider, packed the black and gold and a framed photo of last October. "Thirteen to twelve, on a walk-off kick. Gio Lopez has 18 touchdowns and one interception, and our defense gives up 15.9 points a game." Tatum Delacroix-Price answers from the Boulevard. "Kevin Jennings has 25 touchdowns. We're 7-2, it's Homecoming, and you're in our house."

The market has already run toward SMU. DraftKings opened Wake Forest −3.5 with 55.5 on the over/under, and the number had come down to −1.5 and 61.5 by Monday morning. I'll take the cheaper price on the better team. Wake is 8-1, scores 43.3 a game and allows 15.9. Its only loss was 33-20 to Miami. SMU is a good team, 7-2 and 6-0 at home, so this is no walkover, and that's exactly why I want the short number instead of the 3.5 that opened. Lopez has protected the ball all season, and that matters on a parade morning when the home crowd wants a turnover to explode on.

The Under is the six-point jump. The over/under climbed from 55.5 to 61.5 without a reason I can find in the box scores. Wake has held seven of nine opponents to 27 or fewer, and SMU's defense allows 22.9. My 58 sits clear of 61.5, and a two-point Wake win clears the 1.5.

The room mostly agrees with me on the over/under and splits on the side. The Formula has Wake Forest Demon Deacons −1.5 and likes Under 61.5, with Wake 30-27. Next Man Up has Wake Forest Demon Deacons −1.5 and Under 61.5 too, at 32-29. Letdown Watch takes SMU Mustangs +1.5 and likes Under 61.5. In The Trenches has no side this week and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Next Man Up to Letdown Watch: "You picked the parade over the 8-1 team? Enjoy the float." Letdown Watch: "I picked Homecoming. Ask the last Deacons team that played in Dallas how that went."

Tatum gets the parade, the choir and a stadium full of blue. Eloise gets Lopez on third down and one more trip home with a story.

Wake Forest Demon Deacons 30, SMU Mustangs 28. Wake Forest Demon Deacons cover the 1.5. Under 61.5.
