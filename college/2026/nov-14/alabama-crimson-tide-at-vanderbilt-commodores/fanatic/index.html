Alabama vs Vanderbilt: Fan Appreciation Day Meets The Spazmatics

Alabama Crimson Tide (7-2) at Vanderbilt Commodores (4-5)

12:45 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · FirstBank Stadium · SEC Network

XFacebook

Alabama Crimson Tide vs Vanderbilt Commodores is Fan Appreciation Day at FirstBank Stadium, and Vandyville opens at 8:45 a.m. with the player walk at 9:05 and The Spazmatics taking the Commodore Court stage at 9:45 in skinny ties and horn-rimmed glasses. Mary-Kathryn "MK" Strickland shows up early talking about Keelon Russell like the free embroidered bandanas are already spoken for. Penelope "Pippa" Ashcroft arrives with a glitter-bar plan and a reminder that Nashville has beaten this visitor before.

MK: Russell has 18 touchdowns. We just won at LSU. Fan Appreciation is still our kind of Saturday.

Pippa: Sherrill has 747 yards. We won 40-35 over No. 1 Alabama in this building in 2024. Your Saturday still has to climb that memory.

Alabama is rolling after a 31-13 win at LSU. Vanderbilt just won 25-23 at Mississippi State. The Crimson Tide score 37.4 points a game and allow 20.3. The Commodores score 23.0 and allow 29.4. That is a mismatch on paper and a reunion on the calendar, because Nashville has already written one Alabama story that did not follow the paper.

Keelon Russell has 2,263 passing yards with 18 touchdowns and five interceptions. Rico Scott has 501 receiving yards. Trae'shawn Brown has 359 rushing yards and five scores. Devan Thompkins has 5 sacks. Caleb Woodson has 70 tackles. When Alabama's protection holds, FirstBank starts looking like every other SEC venue that tried to host this offense and ended up chasing it.

Vanderbilt's counter is home noise and third-down stubbornness. Jared Curtis has 1,657 yards. Sedrick Alexander has six rushing touchdowns. Junior Sherrill has 56 catches. Issa Ouattara has 4 sacks. CJ Heard has 63 tackles. The Commodores do not need to outscore Alabama for a full game. They need to keep the game close enough for the fourth quarter to remember 2024.

Pippa: Free samples from Dunkin' and Pop-Up Bagels are nice. A stop on third down is nicer.

MK: Bandanas are nice. Russell throwing it over the top is nicer.

Vandyville will have the Max Nance Kids' Zone, a glitter bar, and local food from Hattie B's and Edley's. The football will have Russell, Sherrill, and a Fan Appreciation crowd that paid for one more memory before the season turns toward Tennessee. One of those plans will feel like a party. The other will feel like a statement.

MK will talk touchdowns. Pippa will talk 2024. Nashville will talk about whether Fan Appreciation came with a sequel.

Fan Appreciation Day at Vanderbilt is built for early arrivals. Free samples from Dunkin', Pop-Up Bagels, Celsius, and Tiff's Treats fill Commodore Court while kids bounce through the Max Nance Kids' Zone and the glitter bar keeps a queue moving. The Spazmatics will play throwback hits from that decade. The football will not. Alabama wants to leave Nashville with a statement after LSU. Vanderbilt wants to leave the alumni with one more memory that feels like 2024. Those two wishes cannot both survive the fourth quarter.

MK keeps pointing at Russell's touchdown total. Pippa keeps pointing at the building's last Alabama story. FirstBank will decide which argument was louder before the glitter gets wiped off.

The SEC Network cameras will catch The Spazmatics, the bandanas, and the glitter. They will also catch whether Vanderbilt's front can bother Russell long enough for Curtis to keep pace. Alabama has spent all fall turning those possessions into 35-point afternoons. Vanderbilt has spent the last two weeks proving it can win ugly on the road and at home. Fan Appreciation Day is about to ask which habit is stronger when the visitor wears crimson.

Nashville knows how to throw a party. Alabama knows how to end one. FirstBank gets to host both moods until the clock says otherwise.

My call: Alabama Crimson Tide 35, Vanderbilt Commodores 18.
