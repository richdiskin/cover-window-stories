Cal vs Virginia Prediction: Cavaliers −12.5, Over 47.5

California Golden Bears (3-6) at Virginia Cavaliers (4-5)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Scott Stadium · ACC Network

XFacebook

Scott Stadium turns Saturday into Virginia's Heroes Appreciation Game, with the Seats for Service program sending donated tickets to veterans and active-duty service members. The Cal vs Virginia spread is Virginia −12.5 and the over/under is 47.5, and I'm taking Virginia Cavaliers −12.5 with the Over 47.5 in a Week 11 game where both teams are trying to end three-game skids.

Prescott Ashby-Lowell, a fourth-generation Wahoo who refuses to call it anything but the Grounds, clears his throat. "As Mr. Jefferson would have charted it, we are 4-1 at Scott Stadium. Beau Pribula has 14 touchdown throws and three interceptions."

Quentin Oyelaran-Fitch, the Berkeley philosophy doctor with a protest sign for every bad play call, raises one here. "Our only road win this year was 21-18 at Syracuse. I am marching for a second."

That's the core of my side. California is 3-6, allows 31.6 points a game and has been blown out on the road at SMU 20-59 and outscored 31-10 at NC State. Away from Berkeley, the Bears also lost 31-39 at UNLV. Virginia has been a different team at home, beating NC State 34-8, Delaware 42-3 and Syracuse 28-25, with a 16-19 loss to Duke the lone blemish. Pribula is efficient, and Da'Shawn Martin has six touchdown catches on 26 grabs.

I won't pretend Virginia's last outing was pretty. Wake Forest beat the Cavaliers 51-7. Cal's last game was rough too, and both teams have had an extra week to stew. Coming off the same week of rest, I'd rather back the team with the better home form and the quarterback who protects the ball.

My final is Virginia Cavaliers 31, California Golden Bears 17. That's a 14-point game, clear of the 12.5.

On the over/under, I want the Over 47.5. DraftKings opened it right at 47.5, and it hasn't moved. Virginia scores 25.9 a game and Cal gives up 31.6, so the Hoos can get to 30 here. Jaron-Keawe Sagapolutele has thrown for 1,751 yards and can find Chase Hendricks, who has 61 catches, enough to get the Bears to 17. That's 48.

The room is split, and it shows. The Formula sits on California +12.5 with Virginia 31-20 and likes the Over. Next Man Up has Virginia −12.5 at 30-15 and likes the Under. Letdown Watch likes Virginia −12.5 at 33-17 and has the Over. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call here. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Letdown Watch: "Thirty-three. The Formula took Cal because it got a nice postcard from Berkeley."

The Formula: "Next Man Up gave the Bears 15 points and called it hospitality."

Quentin will protest every whistle. Prescott will quote the founders. Somewhere between the two, Virginia's home crowd gets a reason to stand, and the veterans in those donated seats get the loudest ovation of the day.

Virginia Cavaliers 31, California Golden Bears 17. Virginia Cavaliers cover the 12.5. Over 47.5.
