Nevada vs Northern Illinois Prediction: NIU +7, Over 46.5

Nevada Wolf Pack (3-6) at Northern Illinois Huskies (0-9)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Huskie Stadium · MW+

XFacebook

Northern Illinois has zero wins and a stadium full of reasons to keep showing up. Saturday is high school senior night for the Huskie Marching Band, and in that Nevada vs Northern Illinois matchup my Week 11 pick against the spread is Northern Illinois Huskies +7 with the Over 46.5.

Nola Brandvik, the DeKalb ag-science grad who talks about wins like corn yields, keeps it dry. "We lost 24-28 to Illinois State and 24-31 at UNLV. Telly Johnson Jr. has 629 rushing yards, DeAree Rogers has 70 catches. The crop is close."

Ridge Corbell, Nevada's ski patroller, shrugs like it's a slow day on the lift. "We beat San José State 28-13 three weeks ago, Nola. EJ Smith has 94 tackles and Dylan LaBarbera has 5.5 sacks. Our defense is fine."

His defense is fine. His offense is the problem, and that's why I'll take the seven. Nevada scored 0 at UCLA and 3 against New Mexico in its last two games, and it has used three quarterbacks this season, Carter Jones, AJ Bianco and Luke Duncan. Jones leads the team with 654 passing yards. The Wolf Pack score 20.8 points a game and are 1-3 on the road. Laying a full touchdown with that offense is asking for trouble.

Northern Illinois is 0-9 for real reasons. It allows 36.8 points a game. But Taron Dickens has thrown for 1,876 yards, and the Huskies just scored 19 at San José State. The market has already moved toward them. DraftKings opened Nevada −10.5, and it is down to −7.

My final is Nevada Wolf Pack 27, Northern Illinois Huskies 21. Six points, inside the seven.

The over/under is the other place I trust the matchup more than the reputations. The number has sat at 46.5 since it opened. Northern Illinois gives up 36.8 a game, so Nevada's sputtering offense should find something. The Huskies have scored at least 17 in six of their nine games. My 27-21 lands at 48, over the 46.5.

The room mostly disagrees with me on the side. The Formula sits on Nevada −7 at 29-21 but likes the Over. Next Man Up and Letdown Watch both have Nevada −7 and the Under at 27-19. In The Trenches likes Northern Illinois +7 at 28-25 and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

In The Trenches: "Twenty-eight to twenty-five. Next Man Up and Letdown Watch turned in the same homework."

Next Man Up: "In The Trenches took an 0-9 team. I'll save it a seat at the band's senior night."

A first win would make senior night unforgettable for a lot of kids in red and black. Even without it, I think the Huskies keep this inside a touchdown.

Nevada Wolf Pack 27, Northern Illinois Huskies 21. Northern Illinois Huskies cover the 7. Over 46.5.
