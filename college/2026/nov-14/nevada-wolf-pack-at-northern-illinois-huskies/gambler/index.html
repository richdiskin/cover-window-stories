Nevada at Northern Illinois: DeKalb Keeps Showing Up, and Nevada Was Told to Bring Its Own Energy

Nevada Wolf Pack (3-6) at Northern Illinois Huskies (0-9)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Huskie Stadium · MW+

XFacebook

DeKalb has a full house planned in the band section, at least. The Huskie Marching Band's schedule lists Saturday against Nevada as high school senior night, so a stadium of future college students gets a look at Huskie Stadium. Northern Illinois is 0-9, the only winless team in FBS, per Nevada Sports Net, and the people still coming out deserve a lot of credit.

The number as of Nov. 14, 2026, 10:00 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at Nevada −5.5, total 47.5. Every pick in this story, mine and each handicapper's, is checked against that number, not Monday's. Monday's card had Northern Illinois +7 with a 46.5 total. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

Nevada coach Jeff Choate is preparing his team for quiet. “I think it's gonna be a BYOE game, bring your own energy,” he said, noting NIU's last home game drew around 7,000 fans by his read of the game sheet. “This is about us.” He also said his team looked like it was “running in cement” in a 31-3 loss to New Mexico, with an illness going around Reno during a short week.

Nevada is 3-6 and starting what the Nevada Appeal calls a three-game quest. The Pack is minus-14 in turnovers, 138th in FBS per ESPN's AP capsule. NIU has a real weapon in DeAree Rogers, with 70 catches for 719 yards, and Dickens threw for 259 in a 30-19 loss to San Jose State.

Injury check: we found no public availability report for either team as of 4:30 AM ET Saturday, Nov. 14.

The Huskies have kept showing up every Saturday without a win to show for it, and the band is bringing high school seniors to see the place. One afternoon of energy from the home side would give everybody in DeKalb a story to take home.
