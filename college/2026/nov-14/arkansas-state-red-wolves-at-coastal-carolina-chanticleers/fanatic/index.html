Arkansas State at Coastal Carolina: Conway Says Goodbye to 23 Seniors and Hello to a Must-Win

Arkansas State Red Wolves (4-5) at Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (3-6)

1:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Brooks Stadium (SC) · ESPN+

XFacebook

Conway closes the home schedule with a full afternoon. Brooks Stadium hosts Senior Day and Salute to Service, and 23 Chanticleers are expected to be recognized before kickoff, per the school. Then the work starts: Coastal is 3-6 and has to win out to reach six wins and a seventh straight bowl.

Arkansas State coach Butch Jones knows where this gets decided. He told the Arkansas Democrat-Gazette that the biggest thing that sticks out about Coastal is the play up front: “They're all fourth- and fifth-year players.” He was blunt about his own front: “We're undersized up front right now in the defensive front. It is what it is.” Louisiana-Monroe ran for 268 yards on the Red Wolves in last week's 19-17 upset.

Quarterback Trey Owens isn't sulking. “There is no reason for us to waver off of one loss,” he said. Cornerback Noah Flores has his eye on Deuce Bailey, who followed coach Ryan Beard from Missouri State and leads the Sun Belt with 19 touchdown passes. Flores said the front has to “create some pressure so we can get him off balance.”

There's a fun side story. Beard guided Missouri State to its first bowl appearance, then left for Coastal before that game, which Arkansas State won 34-28 in the Xbox Bowl. Linebacker Marcellius Pulliam set Coastal's single-game record with 22 tackles against Old Dominion and needs three more for 100 on the season. The Chants have won three straight in this series. Teal will be all over the place for the seniors, and a must-win always gets a crowd's attention.

My call: Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 27, Arkansas State Red Wolves 24.
