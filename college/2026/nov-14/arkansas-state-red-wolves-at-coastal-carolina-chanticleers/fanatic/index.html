Arkansas State vs Coastal Carolina: Syrup Day and a Tiebreaker in Conway

Arkansas State Red Wolves (4-5) at Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (3-6)

1:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Brooks Stadium (SC) · ESPN+

XFacebook

Arkansas State Red Wolves vs Coastal Carolina Chanticleers is a 1 p.m. kickoff at Brooks Stadium, and Coastal's Goldie Seabrook plans to spend her morning a few miles away at the L.W. Paul Living History Farm, where Syrup Day runs from 9 until noon. Volunteers will crush sugar cane, cook the juice down over a fire into sweet cane syrup, and teach the kids old farm games. Goldie says it's the most Conway way to get ready for a football game, and she will be wearing sandals to it, because November or not, closed-toe shoes are for other people.

Goldie: You cook it slow and you don't rush it, Ty. Same as a good drive.

Ty Doucet-Harmon has come from the Delta with a duck call on a cord around his neck, and he blows it once, just to say hello.

Ty: That's my good-morning call, Goldie. You'll hear a happier one late this afternoon.

Ty: Syrup, huh? Down home we'd pour that on biscuits and call it a holiday.

This is the seventh meeting between these two, and it couldn't be more even. The series is tied 3-3. Arkansas State won the first three, including a 51-17 blowout in Jonesboro in 2017 and a 28-27 thriller in 2019. Then Coastal flipped it, winning 52-23 here at Brooks in 2020, 52-20 the next year, and 27-17 in Jonesboro in 2023. Somebody breaks the tie on Saturday.

Goldie: Three in a row for us. The tide comes in, Ty. It always comes in.

Ty: And then it goes back out. That's the whole point of a tide.

Coastal comes in 3-6 and frustrated. The Chanticleers have scored exactly as many points as they've allowed, 222 each, and last week's 21-8 loss to Old Dominion hurt. Deuce Bailey was intercepted three times in that game, but he has been a playmaker all year, with 2,178 throwing yards and 19 touchdowns. Clayton Coppock Jr. has 408 receiving yards and five scores, Dominic Lee-Knicely has 498 rushing yards, Marcellius Pulliam has 96 tackles and Ibrahim Diawara has five sacks.

Goldie: Marcellius Pulliam hits like a nor'easter. You hear him before you see him.

Ty: Trey Owens hears everybody. He's got 2,133 yards and 12 touchdowns, Goldie. He threw for 418 against South Alabama.

Arkansas State is 4-5 and lost a 19-17 heartbreaker to UL Monroe last week. The Red Wolves have played a lot of tight games, beating Kennesaw State 17-14 and losing 23-20 at Louisiana and 56-49 to South Alabama. Owens is the engine. Jaylen Bonelli has 518 receiving yards, Solomon Baggett leads the rushers with 344 yards, and safety Joe Drew has 83 tackles.

Ty: Joe Drew is a retriever. Ball goes in the water, Joe Drew brings it back.

Goldie, who is unexpectedly fierce about blocking, has her own scouting note.

Goldie: Here's the thing nobody says out loud. Our offensive front has to give Deuce time. When he has time, he finds Coppock deep. When he doesn't, he forces it, and that's how you get three interceptions against Old Dominion.

Ty: And our front has to get after him. If Owens gets rhythm, we score with anybody.

That's the whole game, really. Two quarterbacks who can throw it all over the field, two defenses that have been up and down, and a tie in the series that has to be broken. Both teams have something to fix. Coastal has to clean up the mistakes from last week, and Arkansas State has to finish the close ones it has been letting slip away.

Goldie: The syrup will be done by noon, the sun will be out, and the Teal Nation will be loud.

Ty: Save me a jar, Goldie. I'll need something sweet on the drive home if this goes wrong.

Ty blows the duck call again. It is not clear whether that's a threat or a thank-you.

My call: Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 27, Arkansas State Red Wolves 24.
