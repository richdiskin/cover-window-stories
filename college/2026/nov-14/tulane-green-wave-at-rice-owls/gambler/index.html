Tulane vs Rice Pick: Green Wave +3, Over 48.5

Tulane Green Wave (4-5) at Rice Owls (1-8)

2:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · First Community Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Tulane at Rice is Homecoming and Alumni Weekend at First Community Stadium, a 2:00 PM ET kickoff in a long series that has swung back toward Tulane lately. My Tulane vs Rice prediction has the home side winning a coin flip that the market has turned into a field goal: Rice Owls 26, Tulane Green Wave 25, with Tulane Green Wave +3 and Over 48.5.

Odalys Chen-Moreau has a small sample and loves it. "Rice won both previous Homecomings against Tulane. Jacurri Brown has 686 rushing yards. Statistically, Twan, we're due." Twan Ducros-Labat brought his trumpet from Uptown. "We won the last two meetings, 30-28 and 24-10. Johnnie Daniels has six rushing touchdowns. Play it again."

This is the strangest move on the board. DraftKings opened Tulane −1.5 with 46.5 on the over/under and now has Rice −3 and 48.5. That is a flip of four and a half points toward a 1-8 team. I understand part of it. Tulane lost 60-10 to Tulsa last week, and the Green Wave allow 29.2 points a game. But Rice allows 34.0, has lost eight straight since the opener and dropped the last two meetings with Tulane. Tulane beat Memphis 31-17 and won 38-31 in overtime at Charlotte in the last month. Rice has close losses, 51-49 at Florida Atlantic and 16-14 to UTSA, but close losses are still losses. My Owls by 1 means I'm taking the 3 with Tulane, and a lot of the result fits inside that window.

The quarterback picture is unsettled on both sides. Trace Johnson has handled Tulane's throwing the last four games. Gael Ochoa had the most attempts for Rice the last two weeks, while Brown leads the team in rushing. That puts the game on the ground, with Daniels and Brown carrying it.

The Over is about two defenses that give up points. Six of Rice's nine games finished above 48.5 combined points, including the 51-49 loss at Florida Atlantic and the 52-23 loss at North Texas. Five of Tulane's nine went over it. Rice put 49 on Florida Atlantic, and Tulane has scored 31 or more in two of its last four. My 51 points sit above 48.5.

The room sides with Tulane. The Formula likes Tulane Green Wave +3 and likes Over 48.5, with Tulane 27-25. Next Man Up has Tulane Green Wave +3 and likes Under 48.5, with Rice 24-22. Letdown Watch likes Tulane Green Wave +3 and likes Over 48.5, 28-23 Tulane. In The Trenches likes Rice Owls −3 and makes no over/under call, with Rice 34-23. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Letdown Watch to In The Trenches: "Thirty-four points for Rice? They scored 23 at North Texas." In The Trenches: "Brown runs on anybody. Tulane allowed 60 last week."

Odalys gets a Homecoming win and her perfect sample. Twan gets a Tulane team that stays within a field goal and a brass-band parade on the way out.

Rice Owls 26, Tulane Green Wave 25. Tulane Green Wave cover the 3. Over 48.5.
