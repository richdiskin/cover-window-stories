Tulane vs Rice: Homecoming Weekend and a 39th Meeting in Houston

Tulane Green Wave (4-5) at Rice Owls (1-8)

2:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · First Community Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Tulane Green Wave vs Rice Owls is Homecoming and Alumni Weekend at First Community Stadium, a 1 p.m. kickoff in Houston, and Rice's Odalys Chen-Moreau has spent the week reviewing the evidence like a stats cop with a badge. Her finding: Tulane has been Rice's Homecoming opponent twice before, and the Owls won both, 42-34 in 2005 and 28-20 in 2009.

Odalys: Two for two, Twan. Small sample, I admit it. But it's a perfect small sample.

Antoine "Twan" Ducros-Labat has driven in from Uptown with his trumpet case, and he insists every road game deserves a brass-band parade.

Twan: Odalys, two games from before some of these players were born is not evidence. That's a bedtime story.

Odalys: N is too small to panic. I'm not panicking. I'm noting.

This is the 39th meeting between two old conference rivals, and the series is about as even as it gets. Rice leads 20-17-1. But Tulane owns the recent part of it. The Green Wave won 30-28 here in 2023 and 24-10 at home in 2024, and Rice hasn't beaten them since a 17-13 win in 2013.

Twan: Thirteen years, Odalys. That win deserves a beignet and a museum.

Odalys: Thirteen years is just a longer sample. Patience.

Now the hard part, because neither fan base is enjoying this season. Rice is 1-8, and the Owls have lost eight straight since beating Houston Christian in the opener. A few of those were painful. They lost 51-49 at Florida Atlantic, 16-14 to UTSA and 28-21 to Western Michigan. Jacurri Brown is the heartbeat of the offense. He's the leading rusher with 686 yards and six touchdowns and has also thrown for 478, and Gael Ochoa has handled most of the throwing the last two weeks. AJ Brown has 65 tackles in the secondary.

Odalys: Jacurri Brown is statistically significant. That's my highest compliment.

Twan: He's a whole brass section by himself. I'll give you that one.

Tulane is 4-5 and coming off a rough one, a 60-10 loss to Tulsa that Twan will not be discussing at length. Before that, the Green Wave had won two of three, beating Memphis 31-17 and Charlotte 38-31 in overtime. Trace Johnson has taken over at quarterback, Johnnie Daniels has 542 rushing yards and six touchdowns, Destyn Hill has 445 receiving yards, and Dickson Agu has 86 tackles.

Twan: Dickson Agu is the bass drum. Everything else marches to him.

Odalys: The MOB has a bass drum too. Ours occasionally wears a costume.

The Marching Owl Band is famous for halftime shows nobody can predict, which is the whole point. Odalys hopes for something nerdy. Twan hopes for a song he can play along with.

The key for Tulane is getting back to its identity. The Green Wave need Daniels running downhill so Johnson doesn't have to throw from behind all day. For Rice, it's turning Brown loose. If Brown can get outside and gain yards with his legs, the Owls can control the clock and keep the Tulane offense on the sideline.

Twan: If we win, I'm leading a brass-band parade around the stadium. Trumpet up, umbrella in the air, the whole thing.

Odalys: If we win, I'm doing a nerdy dance and publishing the results. Peer review optional.

Homecoming crowds bring out the alumni, the old jerseys and a lot of hope, and Rice has had plenty of reasons to need all three.

Odalys: It's a perfect Homecoming sample, Twan. Two for two. We make it three.

Twan: Ask me again at the final whistle. I'll be the one with the trumpet.

My call: Rice Owls 26, Tulane Green Wave 25.
