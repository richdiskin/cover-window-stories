Purdue vs Iowa Pick: Hawkeyes −17.5, Over 47.5

Purdue Boilermakers (3-6) at Iowa Hawkeyes (7-2)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Kinnick Stadium · NBC

XFacebook

Purdue vs Iowa prediction: Purdue, 3-6, visits No. 19 Iowa, 7-2, at Kinnick Stadium in Iowa City on Saturday at 7:30 p.m. ET. I'm on Iowa Hawkeyes −17.5 and Over 47.5, with Iowa Hawkeyes 35, Purdue Boilermakers 15 as my final.

Orrin Kalvelage, who farms a few miles outside Iowa City, sees a mismatch up front. "We allow 12.2 points a game. We've shut out three teams in this building. Purdue has lost three in a row." Barnaby Wolcott-Reese, the Purdue mechanical engineer with a scale replica of the Boilermaker Special in his garage, has a counter. "Ryan Browne has 2,260 throwing yards. Fame Ijeboi has 10 rushing scores. We beat Iowa 24-7 in 2021."

The side is all about the Iowa defense. The Hawkeyes beat Northern Illinois 40-0, Northern Iowa 55-0 and Wisconsin 30-0 at Kinnick, and they just held Northwestern to 10 on the road. Purdue allows 32.3 points a game and has lost three straight: 38-26 to Washington, 31-17 at Penn State and 34-29 to Maryland. Browne went 10 of 29 at Penn State, and that's the kind of night Iowa's defense produces. The market opened Iowa −16.5 and climbed to −17.5. I have the Hawkeyes by 20, which clears it.

The Over is the less obvious call. Iowa games tend to be low, but Purdue games aren't. The Boilermakers have finished above 47.5 combined points in eight of their nine games, and they score 29.4 a game even with their record. Iowa put up 41 at Washington and 30 on Wisconsin, and if the Hawkeyes reach 35, Purdue needs only 13 to get it over. The posted number rose from 45.5 to 47.5, and my 50 combined points clear it.

The risk on the side is the Iowa offense. Hank Brown has only 1,316 throwing yards, and the Hawkeyes managed 10 points in a loss at Minnesota and 14 against Ohio State. If Iowa sits on a lead and runs the clock, Purdue's backdoor is open. Asaad Waseem has 709 receiving yards and can pile up late yards against a soft shell.

The cards split on the side. The Formula likes Purdue Boilermakers +17.5 and likes the Over at Iowa 32-19. Next Man Up has Iowa Hawkeyes −17.5 and the Over at 34-16. Letdown Watch likes Iowa Hawkeyes −17.5 and has the Over. In The Trenches likes Purdue Boilermakers +17.5 and stays out of the over/under. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Letdown Watch, to The Formula: "You're taking Purdue at Kinnick in November? On purpose?" The Formula: "I'm taking seventeen and a half. Purdue is just along for the ride."

Barnaby says the Boilermakers have spoiled this building before. Orrin says that was a long time ago and he's still mad about it. I think Iowa's defense makes Saturday short.

Iowa Hawkeyes 35, Purdue Boilermakers 15. Iowa Hawkeyes cover the 17.5. Over 47.5.
