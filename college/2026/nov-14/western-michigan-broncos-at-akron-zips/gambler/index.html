Western Michigan vs Akron Pick: Broncos Lay 14 on Troops Night

Western Michigan Broncos (6-3) at Akron Zips (1-8)

7:00 PM ET Tue Nov 10 · InfoCision Stadium · CBSSN

XFacebook

Western Michigan vs Akron prediction, pick against the spread and over/under for Tuesday night MACtion: I'm laying Western Michigan Broncos −14 at DraftKings and taking the Over 48.5, with my final at Western Michigan Broncos 32, Akron Zips 17. Akron calls this one Salute the Troops night at InfoCision Stadium on the eve of Veterans Day, and the 7:00 PM ET kickoff puts a 6-3 team from Kalamazoo against a 1-8 team that badly wants to give the folks in uniform something to stand up for.

Corwin Dahlquist opens with the ground game, the way Kalamazoo raised him. "Jalen Buckley has 711 yards and 8 touchdowns. Cole Cabana went for 110 on seven carries against Bowling Green. We push people backward, Zip, and we do it politely."

Hollister "Zip" Grubbs has a flag pin on his collar and a comeback ready. "Jordan Gant ran for 112 against Ohio last Tuesday, and Ohio leads this conference. We lost 28-20. That's one score, Corwin. Donelius Johnson has 80 tackles. Rubber City bounces."

Here's why I lay the 14. Western Michigan is 4-1 in the MAC and sits second in the conference, scoring 26.2 a game and allowing 19.2. Akron has dropped seven in a row since beating Robert Morris and allows 32.2 a game. Broc Lowry doesn't give the ball away, with 11 touchdown throws and 3 interceptions on 194 attempts, while Brayden Roggow has thrown 9 interceptions for Akron, 4 of them at Miami. Austin Alexander has 5 sacks for the Broncos and Justyce Betts has 65 tackles. My final has Western Michigan by 15, one past the number.

Akron's quarterback room is a two-name story. Roggow took the most throws in six straight games, then Cibastian Broughton threw 34 times against Ohio for 232 yards. I'm pricing Broughton, and the Zips still come up short of two scores.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 47.5 and has moved up to 48.5, while the spread drifted from 13.5 to 14. Western Michigan hung 38 on Toledo and 49 on Monmouth, and Akron has allowed 34 or more six times this year. My 32-17 lands on 49, past the 48.5.

The room splits on the side, and nobody in it joins me on the total. Next Man Up has Western Michigan 30-14, lays the −14 and likes the Under 48.5. Letdown Watch has it 35-12, likes Western Michigan −14 and sits on the Under. The Formula has Western Michigan 31-17, takes Western Michigan Broncos −14 and goes Under 48.5. In The Trenches likes Akron Zips +14 at 31-23 and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Western Michigan Broncos 32, Akron Zips 17. Western Michigan Broncos cover the 14. Over 48.5.
