Illinois vs UCLA: Golden Knights Drop In on the Rose Bowl

Illinois Fighting Illini (6-3) at UCLA Bruins (9-0)

9:00 PM ET Fri Nov 13 · Rose Bowl · Fox

XFacebook

Kensington "Kenzie" Rhee has the whole night storyboarded, and she's proudest of the halftime shot: the U.S. Army Golden Knights dropping out of the sky over the Rose Bowl, parachutes open, landing on the field in the middle of UCLA's annual Salute to Service game.

"Act two opens in the air," she tells Margery Vandermolen. "Nobody's ever seen a cold open like that."

"Kenzie, they do it at a lot of games," Margery says. "But I'll admit it's a good one."

Illinois Fighting Illini vs UCLA Bruins is a Friday night game in Pasadena, kicking off at 6 p.m. local time, and UCLA has packed the day with extras. The Bruin Walk begins outside Gate A at 3:20 p.m., with the UCLA Marching Band and Spirit Squad welcoming Coach Chesney and the team. The Sierra Winds from the U.S. Air Force Band of the Golden West perform the national anthem. UCLA will present its 2026 Veteran of the Year award during the game, and Operation Mend will be recognized too.

Then there's the giveaway Margery can't stop thinking about. Two lucky UCLA students will each win one quarter of in-state tuition, but only if they check in and stay for the whole game.

"That's the most effective attendance policy I've ever seen," says Margery, who grew up in Champaign-Urbana with an ag-economics professor for a dad. "I'm taking notes for my own classroom."

Kenzie has a bigger story to tell. UCLA is 9-0, 6-0 in the Big Ten and first in the conference. The Bruins score 33.6 points a game and give up only 14.1. They won 17-15 at Oregon, shut out Nevada 27-0 and beat Minnesota 34-20 on the road last Saturday.

"Nine episodes," Kenzie says. "Nine happy endings. The network's already ordered another season."

Her lead actor might be a running back. Wayne Knight has 789 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns, and he ran 18 times for 109 yards at Minnesota. Ty Dieffenbach has taken the most throws in each of the last four games, and against the Gophers he completed just 8 throws for 303 yards and 3 touchdowns. Aidan Mizell caught 2 of them for 150 yards and 2 scores. Semaj Morgan leads the receivers with 469 yards. On defense, Jalen Woods has 73 tackles and Sammy Omosigho has 5.5 sacks.

"Eight completions, 303 yards," Kenzie says. "That's not a quarterback. That's a highlight reel with a helmet."

Margery pulls out a chart, because of course she does. It's orange, with corn stalks drawn in the margins.

"Illinois is 6-3 and third in the Big Ten," she says, pointing. "We've won four in a row. We beat Michigan State 38-37 in overtime. We beat Oregon 21-10 at home. You beat Oregon by two. I've crunched the numbers, and the numbers say orange."

Margery's headliner is Collin Dixon. He has 68 catches for 910 yards and 12 touchdowns, and he caught 8 balls for 129 yards and 2 scores against Nebraska last Friday. Katin Houser has thrown for 1,955 yards with 16 touchdowns and only 5 interceptions, and he threw for 292 and 3 touchdowns in that 24-13 win. Aidan Laughery leads the runners with 615 yards, and Matthew Bailey leads the defense with 65 tackles.

"Dixon," Margery says, "is what happens when you plant good seed and wait."

Kenzie laughs. "That's a corn joke."

"Every joke in Illinois is a corn joke. It's state law."

The two teams haven't played each other in the regular season in the last ten years, so there's no rerun to watch. Kenzie says that's fine. She likes originals.

Kenzie's storyboard reads like this. The Rose Bowl is loud from the Bruin Walk on, and UCLA scores first behind Knight. Houser and Dixon answer, and Illinois keeps it close into halftime. The Golden Knights land, the crowd goes wild, and the Bruins come out of the break with more energy. Dieffenbach hits one deep shot in the third quarter, Knight grinds out the clock, and Illinois scores late to make it respectable. UCLA stays unbeaten.

Kenzie snaps her clapboard and yells, "And... scene! That's a wrap!" Margery adds one more row to her chart, writes "next time" in orange, and asks if the Golden Knights give lessons.

Kenzie gets her Golden Knights shot, and the Bruins give her an ending worth keeping. My call: UCLA Bruins 29, Illinois Fighting Illini 20.
