Illinois vs UCLA Pick: Illini +10 at the Rose Bowl Friday

Illinois Fighting Illini (6-3) at UCLA Bruins (9-0)

9:00 PM ET Fri Nov 13 · Rose Bowl · Fox

XFacebook

Two UCLA students are going to win a quarter of in-state tuition on Friday night, but only if they stay for the whole game at the Rose Bowl. That's the deal UCLA is offering on its annual Salute to Service night, when the U.S. Army Golden Knights parachute in at halftime. In this Illinois vs UCLA spread, give me Illinois Fighting Illini +10 and the Over 46.5, with a final of UCLA Bruins 29, Illinois Fighting Illini 20, for the 9:00 PM ET kickoff in Pasadena.

Kenzie Rhee pitches it like a studio meeting. "Nine and oh, Margery. We give up 14.1 a game. Wayne Knight has 789 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns. Ty Dieffenbach threw for 303 at Minnesota on eight completions. That's a blockbuster."

Margery Vandermolen has a chart, naturally. "Kenzie, we beat Oregon 21-10. You beat Oregon 17-15. Collin Dixon has 910 receiving yards and 12 touchdowns. My chart says orange."

Here's why I'm taking the 10. DraftKings opened UCLA −12.5 and it has fallen all the way to 10, so the market agrees the Illini belong in this game. Illinois has won four in a row, and its three losses came by 4, 7 and 23. UCLA's last five wins came by 2, 10, 18, 27 and 14, and two of those five came by 10 or fewer. My score has UCLA by nine, so the Illini stay inside the number.

The UCLA quarterback picture has shifted. Nico Iamaleava took the most throws in the first five games, and Dieffenbach has led the throws in each of the last four. Dieffenbach has 986 yards with 6 touchdowns and 2 interceptions, and I'm pricing him. On the other side, Katin Houser has 1,955 yards, 16 touchdowns and 5 interceptions, and he threw for 292 and 3 scores against Nebraska last Friday.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 47.5 and it's down to 46.5, and I'm on the Over 46.5. UCLA's home games have mostly stayed low, so this one isn't free. But Illinois games have gone past 46.5 in five of nine, and both of these offenses can hit the big play. Aidan Mizell caught 2 balls for 150 yards and 2 touchdowns in Minneapolis. My 29-20 lands on 49.

The room tilts to UCLA on the side but agrees on the Over. The Formula has UCLA 28-22 and likes Illinois Fighting Illini +10 and the Over 46.5. Next Man Up has UCLA 30-18, takes UCLA Bruins −10 and goes Over 46.5. Letdown Watch has 29-19, takes the Bruins −10 and goes Over. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Letdown Watch: "Ten points against an unbeaten team at home? The Formula's being generous."

The Formula: "Unbeaten teams win by two at Oregon too. I wrote it down."

Kenzie snaps an imaginary clapboard and storyboards the halftime jump. Margery adds a new row to her chart and labels it Pasadena.

UCLA Bruins 29, Illinois Fighting Illini 20. Illinois Fighting Illini cover the 10. Over 46.5.
