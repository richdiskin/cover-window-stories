Minnesota at Penn State: Happy Valley Salutes the Military While Minnesota Races the Clock

Minnesota Golden Gophers (5-4) at Penn State Nittany Lions (5-4)

7:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Beaver Stadium · FS1

XFacebook

Penn State's Military Appreciation game is tonight at Beaver Stadium, per the school's game themes, and the Governor's Victory Bell is on the line. The visitors had the tighter Friday. A University of Minnesota spokesperson told Gophers Nation the team “was on its charter flight about to depart for Penn State when the FAA nationwide ground stoppage went into affect.”

The number as of Nov. 14, 2026, 10:08 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at Penn State −11.5, total 42.5. Every pick in this story, mine and each handicapper's, is checked against that number, not Monday's. Monday's card had Penn State −9.5 with a 43.5 total. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

The Gophers never got out Friday, and Gophers Nation's update says they were expected to leave early Saturday morning for a 7 p.m. Eastern kickoff on FS1. The bus alternative would have been roughly 15 hours. A same-day flight sounds a lot better.

Both teams are 5-4. Penn State beat Washington 28-25 last week behind Rocco Becht's two touchdown passes and Chase Sowell's 125 receiving yards, per AP. Minnesota lost 34-20 to UCLA. The Nittany Lions' passing defense allows 144.8 yards a game, third in the country, and Minnesota's offense ranks 129th in yards. On the other hand, Penn State is 132nd in red-zone defense and Minnesota is 13th in red-zone offense, so the Gophers know what to do once they get close.

Both sides protect the ball, too: Penn State is plus-7 in turnovers and Minnesota is plus-6, per AP.
