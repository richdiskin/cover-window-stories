Louisville vs North Carolina Pick: Cardinals −3, Under 60.5

Louisville Cardinals (6-3) at North Carolina Tar Heels (5-4)

7:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Kenan Stadium · ESPN2

XFacebook

Louisville vs North Carolina prediction: Kenan Stadium hosts its third straight night game, a 7 p.m. ET kickoff for a program that played its first five games at noon, and Louisville arrives in Chapel Hill for the first time since 2017. I'm on Louisville Cardinals −3 and Under 60.5, with Louisville Cardinals 30, North Carolina Tar Heels 25 as my final.

Bianca Thornberry, the Louisville Derby-party hostess, has a four-week parade to show off. "Florida State 44-20. Stanford 52-24. Georgia Tech 44-17. Lincoln Kienholz has 14 touchdowns and three interceptions." Ainsley Pemberton, the Tar Heels kinesiology PhD, has two painful nights at Kenan to answer for. "Syracuse beat us by one in overtime and Miami beat us by three. Billy Edwards Jr. threw four touchdowns against Miami. We're close."

Close is the problem. North Carolina lost both of those night games at home, and the one-point and three-point losses tell me the Heels compete but don't finish. Louisville has won four in a row by 24, 8, 28 and 27. DraftKings opened Louisville −2.5 with 59.5 points on the over/under, and it ticked up to −3 and 60.5. My five-point Louisville win clears the field goal.

The Cardinals have the run game to close it. Isaac Brown has 745 rushing yards and eight touchdowns, and Kienholz ran for 81 at Georgia Tech. North Carolina's Derek McDonald, with 89 tackles, is the best defender on the field, but he can't be in two places on a read option.

The Under is about the Tar Heels. North Carolina allows 22.8 points a game, and four of its nine games ended with neither team reaching 30. Louisville scores 40.8 a game, but the Heels drag opponents into their kind of game, the kind that ends 21-17 at Pittsburgh and 24-20 at Duke. My 55 combined points stay under 60.5, and the number has crept up a point from the open.

Every card in the room with an over/under call is on the Under, and the side splits two and two. The Formula has North Carolina Tar Heels +3 and likes the Under, with Louisville 29-27. Next Man Up also has North Carolina Tar Heels +3 and the Under, and its 30-29 Louisville score is about as close as it gets. Letdown Watch likes Louisville Cardinals −3 and likes the Under. In The Trenches likes Louisville Cardinals −3 and keeps quiet on the over/under. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Letdown Watch, to Next Man Up: "Thirty to twenty-nine and you're taking the home team? That's a coin flip in a bow tie." Next Man Up: "Better than your Derby hat theory."

Ainsley swears the first two night games taught the Heels something. Bianca thinks a hot team on the road in prime time is the best lesson in the sport, and I'm with the hat.

Louisville Cardinals 30, North Carolina Tar Heels 25. Louisville Cardinals cover the 3. Under 60.5.
