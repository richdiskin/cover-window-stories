Memphis vs South Florida Pick: Bulls +5.5 at Salute to Service

Memphis Tigers (7-2) at South Florida Bulls (6-3)

8:00 PM ET Thu Nov 12 · Raymond James Stadium · ESPN

XFacebook

There will be barbecue smoke over the parking lots at Raymond James Stadium on Thursday, because USF's Office of Veteran Success is throwing a Sonny's BBQ tailgate for its Salute to Service game, and the visitors come from Memphis, a town that takes its barbecue personally. For this Memphis vs South Florida spread, I'm taking South Florida Bulls +5.5 and the Over 48.5, and my final for the 8:00 PM ET kickoff in Tampa is South Florida Bulls 27, Memphis Tigers 26.

Otis Delaney-Crum checks the menu and shakes his head. "Florida barbecue, Reina. That's brave. Marcus Stokes has 25 touchdown passes and 6 interceptions. He threw for 371 against Army last time out. We score 37.6 a game. Bring more napkins."

Reina Valdés-Kowalski doesn't flinch. "We give up 15.1 a game, Otis. Kent State got 2 points in this stadium. UAB got 10. We're 6-3, and nobody has hung more than 31 on us all year."

The 5.5 is worth grabbing. DraftKings opened Memphis −6 and it has slipped to 5.5, so the early market is on the home side. Both Memphis losses came on the road, 38-20 at Boise State and 31-17 at Tulane, and the Tigers needed overtime to get past East Carolina at home. South Florida lost 20-17 at East Carolina last Friday, the same Pirates team that took Memphis to overtime. My score has the Bulls winning outright, and even a Memphis win by a field goal stays inside the number.

The quarterback question is in Tampa. Michael Van Buren Jr. has 1,273 passing yards and led the throws in seven games, but Luke Kromenhoek took the most throws in each of the last two, and he went 17 of 21 for 179 at East Carolina. I'm pricing Kromenhoek. D.J. Crowther ran for 111 in that game and has 689 on the season, and a ground game that moves the chains keeps Stokes on the sideline.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 49.5 and it's down to 48.5, and I'm on the Over 48.5. South Florida games have mostly stayed low, but Memphis has scored 38 or more in six of nine. These two also tend to light it up against each other. Five of the last six regular-season meetings went past 48.5 combined, including 59-50 in 2023 and 34-31 last season. My 27-26 lands on 53.

Three of the four cards with a side take the Bulls. The Formula has Memphis 27-24, likes South Florida Bulls +5.5 and goes Over 48.5. Next Man Up has South Florida 27-26 and likes both the Bulls +5.5 and the Over 48.5. Letdown Watch has it tied 26-26, likes the Bulls +5.5 and likes the Over. In The Trenches likes Memphis Tigers −5.5 and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Letdown Watch: "In The Trenches, three of us like the Bulls. And you're laying it with Memphis?"

In The Trenches: "I grade the fronts. The scoreboard can sort itself out."

Reina waves a pirate flag she hasn't earned yet. Otis hums a slow blues line and saves room for the brisket.

South Florida Bulls 27, Memphis Tigers 26. South Florida Bulls cover the 5.5. Over 48.5.
