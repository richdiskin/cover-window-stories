Baylor vs BYU: Cougar Canyon Opens Before the Walk to Edwards Stadium

Baylor Bears (6-3) at BYU Cougars (7-2)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · LaVell Edwards Stadium · Fox

XFacebook

Baylor Bears vs BYU Cougars begins long before kickoff on Canyon Road, where Cougar Canyon opens three and a half hours before the game and the Cougar Walk routes players and coaches straight through a corridor of fans outside LaVell Edwards Stadium. Bryce Halvorsen-Tuilagi has been on Canyon Road since Cougar Canyon opened, talking about Bear Bachmeier like the Walk is already a highlight reel. Magnolia "Maggie" Whitlock carries the Baylor flag into Provo with DJ Lagway's touchdown total written on a napkin from the drive.

Bryce: Bachmeier throws it and runs it. Glasker wins the route. Cougar Canyon will be loud for that.

Maggie: Lagway has 15 touchdowns. Freeman has 852 yards. Your canyon still has to hear us.

BYU is 7-2 after a 20-28 loss at Utah that snapped some of the November shine. Baylor just beat Iowa State 42-31 and scored 31.9 points a game overall while allowing 17.0. The Cougars score 31.0 and allow 19.8. That is a Big 12 game between two offenses that can hurt you and two defenses that can keep a Saturday from turning into a track meet.

Bear Bachmeier has 2,022 passing yards with 13 touchdowns and eight interceptions, plus 395 rushing yards and seven scores with his legs. Legend Glasker has 703 receiving yards. Tausili Akana has 3.5 sacks. Cade Uluave has 59 tackles. When Bachmeier gets downhill, Edwards Stadium starts sounding like a place that forgot last week's final in Salt Lake City.

Baylor's answer is Lagway and a defense that has been stingy all fall. Lagway has 1,900 yards. Caden Knighten has 361 rushing yards. Gavin Freeman has 69 catches. Garrick Ponder has 4 sacks. Travion Barnes has 63 tackles. The Bears do not need to out-Provo Provo. They need to keep Bachmeier from ending drives early and then let Lagway answer.

Maggie: The Walk is nice. A third-down stop is nicer.

Bryce: The Canyon is nice. Bachmeier in the end zone is nicer.

Provo will treat the afternoon like a festival that happens to include football. Baylor will treat it like a road test that can still reshape a résumé. One of those moods usually wins in November, and Edwards Stadium will decide which one before Cougar Canyon packs up for the night.

Bryce will talk Bachmeier. Maggie will talk Lagway. The Canyon will talk about whether the Walk led to a finish.

Cougar Canyon stays open until 30 minutes before kickoff, which is enough time for a full festival and a football argument. BYU wants Bachmeier to look like the dual-threat problem Utah just slowed down. Baylor wants Lagway to look like the quarterback who lit up Iowa State. Edwards Stadium will get both wishes for a while. Only one will still be standing when the Canyon lights shut off.

Bryce keeps talking about Bachmeier's legs. Maggie keeps talking about Lagway's arm. Provo will decide which one traveled better.

Big 12 road trips to Provo are a full sensory test. The Canyon noise, the Walk, the altitude talk, the early jump balls. Baylor has already proved it can score on anybody in this league. BYU has already proved it can defend enough to stay in every November conversation. The team that wins the first two third downs will probably win the afternoon, and both coaching staffs know it.

Lagway will keep throwing it. Bachmeier will keep running it. Edwards Stadium will keep asking which one breaks first.

Provo will sell the Walk. Baylor will sell the answers. Somebody has to sell a stop.

The Walk will be packed. The stands will be louder. Somebody still has to win third down.

Provo gets its Walk first and its win second. My call: BYU Cougars 27, Baylor Bears 21.
