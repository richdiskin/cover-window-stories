UNLV at New Mexico: Albuquerque Chases a Record from 1928

UNLV Rebels (5-4) at New Mexico Lobos (8-1)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · University Stadium (NM) · CW

XFacebook

University Stadium is chasing a number that has stood since 1928. New Mexico has won 11 straight home games, one short of the school mark of 12, and the Lobos are 5-0 in the Mountain West for the first time in school history, per New Mexico Athletics. This is also their first home game as an AP Top 25 team, and the school lists a 5:40 p.m. Mountain kickoff on The CW.

The number as of Nov. 14, 2026, 10:08 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at New Mexico −17.5, total 46.5. Every pick in this story, mine and each handicapper's, is checked against that number, not Monday's. Monday's card had New Mexico −14.5 with a 46.5 total. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

The Lobos also want the crowd. The school says a crowd of 32,142 would give UNM the Mountain West attendance lead, and its attendance has gone up every game this year. That is a town getting hooked on a winner.

The defense is why. New Mexico leads the nation in scoring defense at 10.9 points a game and in interceptions with 17, per the Lobos. UNLV needs one win for bowl eligibility after a 41-35 loss to Wyoming, a game in which the Rebels rallied from 41-21 down late in the third quarter and threw an interception in the end zone in the final two minutes. Jai'Den Thomas has 753 rushing yards, per AP.

One warning for the home side: the visiting team has won seven of the last eight in this series, the Lobos' own notes say. InForum reported Friday that UNLV was grounded in Las Vegas and had not left yet; no kickoff change has been announced.

A record from 1928, an attendance crown and the best defense in the country. Albuquerque has waited a long time for a Saturday like this, and it plans to be loud about it.
