UNLV vs New Mexico: The Lobos Wait for the Right Wind

UNLV Rebels (5-4) at New Mexico Lobos (8-1)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · University Stadium (NM) · CW

XFacebook

UNLV Rebels vs New Mexico Lobos is a Saturday night in Albuquerque with first place in the Mountain West at stake for the home team, a 5:30 p.m. kickoff at University Stadium and an Indigenous Heritage theme for the night. Paloma Begaye-Ortiz, a hot-air balloon pilot who flies the October fiesta every year and has a Lobo painted on her basket, says this is the best New Mexico team she's ever seen. Monroe-Kalani, the UNLV loyalist who flew in wearing a red visor she swears has never lost a road game, came to remind her about last fall.

Monroe-Kalani: You know we've won four of the last six against you.

Paloma: We won last year.

Monroe-Kalani: By five.

Paloma: Still counts. We go up in balloons at dawn. We know how to wait for the right wind.

That's the vibe in Albuquerque this week. The Lobos are 8-1 and 5-0 in the league, and it's been a long time since anyone around here felt this calm about a November game.

New Mexico's defense has been the reason. The Lobos give up 10.9 points a game. They beat Central Michigan 38-7, UTEP 61-7 and Mercyhurst 70-7 at home, and they just went to Nevada and won 31-3. Even their lone loss was respectable, 14-6 at Oklahoma. Defensive end Brett Karhu has 7.5 sacks, and linebacker Mercury Swaim has 55 tackles.

Paloma: When the wind is right, a balloon goes exactly where you want it. When the defense is right, so does the other team. Backwards.

Monroe-Kalani: Is that a real balloon saying?

Paloma: It is now.

The offense is built around the run. Scottre Humphrey has 647 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns, and he ran for 107 yards and two scores at Nevada. Toa Fa'avae doesn't throw it much, but tight end Cade Keith has 358 receiving yards and caught a touchdown last week.

UNLV is 5-4 and has been a coin flip all year. The Rebels beat Cal 39-31 and Northern Illinois 31-24, but they lost 41-31 at Air Force and 41-35 at home to Wyoming last Saturday. Jai'Den Thomas has 753 rushing yards and ran for two scores against Wyoming. Jackson Arnold has 15 touchdown throws but went 7 of 18 last week. Kayden McGee has six touchdown catches, and safety Jake Pope has 76 tackles.

Monroe-Kalani: Arnold had a bad night. Everybody has a bad night.

Paloma: Fa'avae threw three interceptions at San José State and we still won.

Monroe-Kalani: That's my point. You're beatable.

Paloma: Beatable isn't beaten.

That's the question for Saturday. New Mexico has won close games when the offense sputters, 21-16 twice in a row before Nevada, and UNLV will try to make it another one of those. The Rebels need Thomas to run and Arnold to avoid the big mistake. The Lobos need Humphrey to wear the defense down and the crowd to show up loud.

The crowd should be there. The Kids Free Initiative is in place for the game, and tickets run from $16 to $88, so it'll be a family night as much as a football night. Paloma has already promised her nieces and nephews a balloon ride at the fiesta next year if the Lobos win the league.

Monroe-Kalani: That's a lot of pressure on a football team.

Paloma: They can handle it. They're 8-1.

The New Mexico defense should be the difference. UNLV can score some, and Thomas will get his yards, but the Lobos are a different team this year, and they've been better than this opponent all season.

Monroe-Kalani: If UNLV wins, you take me up in the balloon.

Paloma: And if New Mexico wins?

Monroe-Kalani: I still want the balloon ride.

Paloma: Then wear the visor. Let's see if it works at a thousand feet.

My call: New Mexico Lobos 33, UNLV Rebels 16.
