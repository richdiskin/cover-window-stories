Auburn at Mississippi State: Starkville Gets Its Military Day and an Auburn Team Fresh Off a Long Bus Ride

Auburn Tigers (5-4) at Mississippi State Bulldogs (4-5)

4:15 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Davis Wade Stadium · ESPN2

XFacebook

Starkville has Military Appreciation Day at Davis Wade Stadium, and Mississippi State wants everybody in maroon. The kickoff is new: the SEC moved the game to 3:15 p.m. Central (4:15 PM ET), now on SEC Network, because Auburn could not fly Friday. The Tigers rode a bus about five hours from Columbus, Georgia, to Tupelo and got to the team hotel late Friday night, per Auburn on SI and Auburn Undercover.

The number as of Nov. 14, 2026, 10:03 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at Auburn −2.5, total 51.5. Every pick in this story, mine and each handicapper's, is checked against that number, not Monday's. Monday's card had Mississippi State +2.5 with a 50.5 total. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

Alex Golesh had worried all week about the original start. “This is as early of a start with a legit travel and a bus ride in the morning,” he said Wednesday, asking for “the right focus, the right detail, the right energy level to go start fast.” He got a bus ride the night before and a later kickoff instead. He also gave Mississippi State's run defense a big compliment: “They've not allowed people to run it and that's literally the entire schedule.”

Auburn is the team that went into Athens four weeks ago and beat No. 1 Georgia 27-24, AP noted this week. Byrum Brown ran for more than 120 yards and threw two touchdowns in last week's 26-23 win over Arkansas. Auburn defensive coordinator DJ Durkin on the State quarterback: “He’s got really incredible arm talent.” A.J. Swann threw two touchdowns in a 25-23 loss to Vanderbilt, both to Sanfrisco Magee, who turned two catches into 95 yards. Anthony Evans III has 711 receiving yards.

Injury check: we did not find an SEC availability report for this game in public sources as of 4:30 AM ET Saturday, Nov. 14. Golesh said Wednesday the Tigers “went full go” in practice. No player status is assumed.

The desks at today's number. Follow The Money has no side pick and likes the Over 51.5. The Formula: Mississippi State 26, Auburn 26. That makes it Mississippi State +2.5 and Over 51.5. Next Man Up: Mississippi State 28, Auburn 24. That makes it Mississippi State +2.5 and Over 51.5. Letdown Watch: Auburn 26, Mississippi State 25. That makes it Mississippi State +2.5 and Under 51.5. In The Trenches: Mississippi State 27, Auburn 21, and it stays with Auburn −2.5 off the run-game matchup, no total pick.

The Famous Maroon Band and a stadium full of maroon get to see whether a team that spent Friday night on a bus can start fast in the late afternoon. Starkville would like the answer to be no.

The Cover Writer's card at today's number: Mississippi State Bulldogs +2.5 and Over 51.5. My final is Mississippi State Bulldogs 28, Auburn Tigers 24. Same spread. The total went up a point, and our 52 still clears it by a half. We have State winning outright, so the 2.5 is a bonus. I'll accept a bonus from anybody.

Mississippi State Bulldogs 28, Auburn Tigers 24. Mississippi State Bulldogs cover the 2.5. Over 51.5.
