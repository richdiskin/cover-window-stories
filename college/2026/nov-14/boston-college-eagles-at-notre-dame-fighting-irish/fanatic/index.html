Boston College vs Notre Dame: Irish History Week Under the Dome

Boston College Eagles (4-5) at Notre Dame Fighting Irish (8-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Notre Dame Stadium · NBC

XFacebook

Boston College Eagles vs Notre Dame Fighting Irish in Week 11 arrives the same weekend Hesburgh Library is showing "The Delirium of the Brave: Sacrifice for the Soul of the Irish Nation" in its rare books room, so two very Irish fan bases are walking past the same history on the way to Notre Dame Stadium. Notre Dame insider Declan Moriarty-Chen has already read every placard twice, and Boston College insider Siobhan Dunleavy says Mason McKenzie and the Eagles are here to file a formal objection to the whole idea of an easy afternoon.

Declan: Siobhan, I would posit that you chose the most dangerous possible weekend to visit. We have an Irish history exhibit in the library, the Marching Band parading from the Main Building on Friday afternoon and an organ concert in the Basilica called Thunder from the Sky.

Siobhan: Objection. Boston has been Irish for a long, long time, Declan. We brought our own thunder, and it's wicked loud.

The trouble for Siobhan is that the recent history in this series belongs to the Golden Dome. Notre Dame has won all five meetings since 2017: 49-20, 40-7, 45-31, 44-0 and 25-10. The Irish are 8-1, ranked No. 11, and their only loss came at BYU, 28-24.

Declan: Five straight. I keep them in a binder with tabs. The 44-0 tab is laminated.

Siobhan: The 25-10 tab is the newest one, Declan. That's a trend of a different sort. Every year we get closer to the courtroom door.

Here's what Siobhan is selling. Mason McKenzie is the whole Boston College offense in one jersey. He leads the Eagles with 1,616 throwing yards and 10 touchdowns, and he also leads them in rushing with 676 yards and five scores on 145 carries. Against Florida State last week, Bo MacCormack III ran 16 times for 105 yards and a touchdown in a 31-24 loss that stayed close all afternoon.

Siobhan: McKenzie runs like he's late for a final exam. You can't defend a quarterback who refuses to stay in the pocket. That's not a scheme, that's a closing argument.

Declan: And Notre Dame's defense is the judge. We give up 14.6 points a game. The jury has been seated since September.

The Irish have their own exhibit hall of weapons. CJ Carr has thrown for 2,168 yards with 21 touchdowns and one interception all season. Against Miami last Saturday he went 29 of 40 for 319 yards and three scores, and Micah Gilbert caught nine balls for 98 yards and two touchdowns. Nolan James Jr. has run for 571 yards and nine scores, and Jordan Faison leads the receivers with 574 yards.

Declan: One interception. One. I've seen more turnovers at a bake sale outside the Basilica.

Siobhan: Give it time. The rare books room has plenty of stories about the mighty getting humbled.

Boston College's defense has some fight in it. Linebacker Anthony Palano has 76 tackles, and Demetrius Ballard has 4.5 sacks. The problem is the scoreboard on the road. The Eagles lost 47-2 at Duke two weeks ago, and they lost at Cincinnati and SMU too, with their lone road win a 49-27 shootout at Georgia Tech.

Declan: I don't want to bring up Duke.

Siobhan: Then don't. That game has been sealed by court order.

The Irish defense up front is led by Boubacar Traore with four sacks, and Drayk Bowen leads the team with 45 tackles. At home this fall, Notre Dame beat Rice 52-0, Stanford 58-7, Michigan State 27-10 and Miami 31-24. The Stadium has been a hard place to make a point.

Siobhan: Here's my plan. McKenzie keeps it, Palano hits somebody, and we keep the ball long enough to make Carr sit on the bench and read a book.

Declan: That's a good plan. It is also the same plan the last five Boston College teams brought. The exhibit upstairs is about the brave. It isn't about the ones who won.

There is real affection under the arguing. These are two Catholic schools with deep Irish roots, and both fan bases will walk past the same Dome and the same Basilica before kickoff. One of them walks back out a lot happier.

Siobhan: If we pull this off, I'm taking a photo with the exhibit and captioning it "Sustained."

Declan: If we win big, I'm adding a sixth tab to the binder and calling it Exhibit F.

Siobhan: Objection.

Declan: Overruled. The exhibit room closes at five on Friday. Bring a pencil.

Declan gets his history lesson, plus a blowout for extra credit. My call: Notre Dame Fighting Irish 41, Boston College Eagles 9.
