Boston College vs Notre Dame Prediction: Irish −29.5, Under 52.5

Boston College Eagles (4-5) at Notre Dame Fighting Irish (8-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Notre Dame Stadium · NBC

XFacebook

Hesburgh Library has an exhibit on the Irish nation's sacrifices hanging in its rare books room this weekend, and Boston College walks into Notre Dame Stadium needing a little of that courage. My Boston College vs Notre Dame pick against the spread is Notre Dame Fighting Irish −29.5, with Under 52.5 on the over/under, and I know that's a lot of points to ask of anybody in Week 11.

Declan Moriarty-Chen, who can recite Notre Dame schedules back to the leather helmets, opens with a thesis. "I would posit that CJ Carr has thrown 21 touchdowns and one interception. One. He just went 29 of 40 for 319 yards and three scores against Miami."

Siobhan Dunleavy, Boston College's resident debate champ, objects before he finishes. "Mason McKenzie leads us in throwing and running, Declan. He has 676 rushing yards. You can't tackle an argument you can't catch."

Here's why I'll lay the big number with the Irish. Notre Dame allows 14.6 points a game. Boston College scores 21.9 and just went to Duke and scored two points in a 2-47 loss. The Irish have beaten the Eagles five straight times in this series since 2017, by scores of 49-20, 40-7, 45-31, 44-0 and 25-10, and two of those margins topped this spread. At home this year Notre Dame beat Rice 52-0 and Stanford 58-7.

The Eagles do get one honest argument from me. McKenzie is a load on the ground, and Bo MacCormack III ran for 105 yards against Florida State. If Boston College can keep the ball, the clock becomes its friend. That's exactly why I trust the Under as much as the side.

DraftKings opened Notre Dame −31.5, and the market has come down two points. I'm fine there. Nolan James Jr. has nine rushing touchdowns, Jordan Faison has 574 receiving yards, and Micah Gilbert caught two scores against Miami. My final is Notre Dame Fighting Irish 41, Boston College Eagles 9, a 32-point margin and 50 combined points, which keeps me under 52.5.

The room is crowded on my side of the field. Next Man Up likes Notre Dame −29.5 and likes the Under at 42-8. Letdown Watch likes the Irish too, 43-9, and has the Under without loving it. The Formula has Notre Dame 40-11 but sits on Boston College +29.5 and the Under. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call on this one. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Next Man Up: "Forty-two to eight. The Formula took the Eagles because it felt sorry for the visitors' bus."

The Formula: "Next Man Up thinks every Saturday ends in a shutout. Ask Rice how that felt."

Siobhan will argue every snap, and she should. But Notre Dame has the quarterback, the defense and the series history, and Saturday at the Stadium feels like the Irish keep writing the chapter.

Notre Dame Fighting Irish 41, Boston College Eagles 9. Notre Dame Fighting Irish cover the 29.5. Under 52.5.
