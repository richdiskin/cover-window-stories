Arizona State at UCF: Orlando Has Warped Tour Downtown and a Sun Devil Team Flying In This Morning

Arizona State Sun Devils (5-4) at UCF Knights (5-4)

5:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Acrisure Bounce House · ESPN2

XFacebook

Orlando is loud this weekend even before the Bounce House starts shaking. Downtown, the Warped Tour has two days of punk rock booked on the grounds around the old Citrus Bowl. Out east, UCF hosts Arizona State for the first time ever, in only the third meeting between the programs, per Sun Devils on SI.

The number as of Nov. 14, 2026, 10:03 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at UCF −3.5, total 47.5. Every pick in this story, mine and each handicapper's, is checked against that number, not Monday's. Monday's card had UCF −3 with a 48.5 total. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

The Sun Devils had a rough Friday. On3 reports they were about to leave Phoenix on an American Airlines charter at 4 p.m. Eastern when the FAA stopped every flight in the country. Kenny Dillingham eventually sent the team home rather than keep waiting at the airport, and ASU planned to fly Saturday morning. Kickoff moved to 5:30 p.m. Eastern and from ESPN2 to ESPNU.

Scott Frost has history with the visitors. “Coach Dillingham, and I've known him for a long time,” Frost said, calling Dillingham's brother-in-law one of his better friends. He likes ASU's young quarterback, Jake Fette, and its run defense, and he named what he thinks decides games like this: “The biggest differentiator to me is quarterback play when you have two really even teams.”

Both teams are 5-4. UCF beat Kansas 33-32 last week as Josh Derry caught eight passes for 143 yards, per ESPN's AP capsule, and Alonza Barnett III threw two touchdowns. ASU won 20-10 over Colorado behind Fette and Reed Harris, who has 907 receiving yards and nine touchdowns. Both teams rank in the top 25 in FBS for fewest penalty yards.

Injury check: we found no public injury or availability report for either team as of 4:30 AM ET Saturday, Nov. 14. No player status is assumed.

In The Trenches: UCF 28, Arizona State 21, and it stays with UCF −3.5 off the run-game matchup, no total pick.

The Bounce House gets two extra hours to warm up and a visitor that will have spent its morning on a plane. Frost says the quarterback with the special day wins this one, and Orlando would like it to be the one wearing black and gold.

The Cover Writer's card at today's number: UCF Knights −3.5 and Over 47.5. My final is UCF Knights 29, Arizona State Sun Devils 24. UCF got half a point more expensive, and the total dropped a point. UCF by five clears 3.5, and our 53 clears 47.5 by plenty. Arizona State spent its morning on a plane. I spent mine on this.

UCF Knights 29, Arizona State Sun Devils 24. UCF Knights cover the 3.5. Over 47.5.
