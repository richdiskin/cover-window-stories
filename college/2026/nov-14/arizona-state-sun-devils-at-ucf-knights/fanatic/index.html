Arizona State vs UCF: Warped Tour Noise Meets the Bounce House

Arizona State Sun Devils (5-4) at UCF Knights (5-4)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Acrisure Bounce House · ESPN2

XFacebook

Arizona State Sun Devils vs UCF Knights in Week 11 lands on the same Saturday that Vans Warped Tour takes over the Camping World Stadium campus in Orlando, so this town gets two loud stages and only one of them keeps score. UCF insider Knox Brevard-Stiles, a theme-park ride engineer, swears the Acrisure Bounce House will out-shake every mosh pit downtown, and Arizona State insider Skylar Vanterpool says Jake Fette, Reed Harris and the Sun Devils packed sunscreen for a November road win.

Knox: Welcome to Orlando, Skylar. Downtown has Third Eye Blind, Simple Plan and Jimmy Eat World this weekend. We have a stadium that moves when the students jump. Keep your hands inside the stadium at all times.

Skylar: Knox, I came from Tempe, where November is still pool weather. A bouncing bleacher does not scare me. Hydrate and dominate.

Here's why both of them showed up a little jittery. UCF just survived the wildest ride of its season, a 33-32 win at Kansas. Alonza Barnett III threw for 251 yards and two touchdowns, and Josh Derry caught eight balls for 143 yards and a score. One week earlier, Baylor beat the Knights 41-9 in Orlando, so this fan base did the full loop-the-loop in seven days.

Knox: That isn't a slump and a rebound. That's a launch track. You never judge a coaster by the first hill.

Skylar: You judge it by how many people need a bench afterward.

Arizona State's road has been just as twisty. The Sun Devils beat Colorado 20-10 last Saturday. Fette went 13 of 19 for 187 yards and a touchdown, and Harris caught four balls for 103 yards and a score. Harris has 52 catches for 907 yards and nine touchdowns this season, the kind of production that makes a secondary check where the exits are.

Skylar: Reed Harris is my entire personality right now. Long strides, soft hands, zero fear. Your safeties are going to need a cooldown tent.

Knox: Our safeties have Tackett Curtis behind them. Fifty-nine tackles. He meets people at the bottom of the drop.

The quarterback story is a two-park ticket of its own, minus the gift shop. Arizona State has had three different quarterbacks lead it in throws this fall: Cutter Boley early, then Mikey Keene, then Fette the last two weeks. Boley still owns the team lead with 1,360 yards and 13 touchdowns. UCF has two. Keyone Jenkins leads the Knights with 1,181 yards and 11 touchdowns, and he went 19 of 20 against TCU, but Barnett handled the throwing at Kansas.

Knox: Two quarterbacks, one ride. Jenkins was nearly perfect against TCU. Barnett went into Lawrence and came home with a win. Either one can sit in the front car.

Skylar: We've had three. That's not a quarterback room, Knox. That's a carpool. And the carpool keeps arriving.

On the ground, Cardae Mack is the Sun Devils' engine with 603 rushing yards and six touchdowns, and he carried 24 times against Colorado. UCF counters with Duke Watson and his 596 yards. Arizona State linebacker Owen Long has 89 tackles, and Jalen Thompson has five sacks, which means Knox's front five had better be strapped in.

Skylar: Owen Long is a tackling machine with a sunburn. He will find your running back in the dark.

Knox: Duke Watson runs like he's late for the parade. Good luck finding him at all.

There's history, too. The last time these two met, in 2024, Arizona State won 35-31, and Skylar has not let anybody forget it since she landed.

Skylar: I'm not saying 35-31 is destiny. I'm saying it's my phone background.

Knox: Then let me give you a new one. Your team has played three true road games this year, at Texas A&M, Texas Tech and BYU, and it lost all three. The Bounce House is not a place you visit. It's a place you survive.

Skylar: We've survived worse. We've survived a Tempe parking lot in August.

The difference, as Knox sees it, is home cooking. The Knights score 30.1 points a game, and the Sun Devils give up 27.3. Arizona State answers with 28.4 points of its own against a UCF defense allowing 26.0. Nobody in Orlando expects a quiet afternoon, not with Harris and Derry on the field and Warped Tour guitars rolling across town.

Knox: Here's the deal, Skylar. The bands get the downtown crowd. We get the bounce.

Skylar: If we win, I'm crowd-surfing all the way to Camping World Stadium.

Knox: If we win, you're riding home in the back car with the lap restraint down.

My call: UCF Knights 29, Arizona State Sun Devils 24.
