Florida at Kentucky: Lexington Wears Black, and Florida Brings Back Its Old Quarterback

Florida Gators (5-4) at Kentucky Wildcats (7-2)

6:15 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Kroger Field · ESPN

XFacebook

Kroger Field is sold out and dressed for night work. Kentucky's game with Florida carries Night Ops and Salute to Veterans activities with a stadium Black Out, and UK Athletics is asking fans to wear black. It is the fourth home sellout of the season, and the school is warning that Cooper Drive closes four hours before kickoff, so leave early.

Florida touched down Friday with a quarterback change. “Wheels down in Lexington,” the Gators posted, and Gators senior writer Scott Carter reports Aaron Philo is back as the starter after Tramell Jones Jr. went 10 of 22 with two interceptions in last week's 23-7 loss to Oklahoma. Philo has completed 70.5 percent of his passes this year. Florida started 4-0 and has lost four of five since, and it needs one more win to reach a bowl.

Kentucky has its own bruise to shake off, a 34-6 loss at Tennessee that snapped a six-game winning streak, per AP. Kenny Minchey set a school record with 242 passes to start a season with only one interception, Carter notes. The Wildcats are the least-penalized team in the SEC; Florida gives up 71.1 penalty yards a game, last in the league. Kentucky has also won three straight at home in this series.

The Gator to watch is Jadan Baugh, third in the SEC with 836 rushing yards, most of it early. After 600 yards in the first four games, he has run for 13, 59, 91, 38 and 35. “Baugh didn't forget how to run,” Carter writes. Somebody has to make defenses stop crowding the box.

Black shirts, a sold-out house and a Florida team trying to find the offense it had in September. If Baugh gets loose early, Lexington gets nervous; if he doesn't, the Gators could spend a long night in the dark.

My call: Kentucky Wildcats 28, Florida Gators 27.
