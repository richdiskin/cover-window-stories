Florida vs Kentucky Pick: Gators +3, Over 51.5

Florida Gators (5-4) at Kentucky Wildcats (7-2)

6:15 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Kroger Field · ESPN

XFacebook

Florida vs Kentucky prediction: Kroger Field is sold out for the Black Out, and Lexington will be dressed in black when the No. 24 Gators visit the No. 6 Wildcats at 6:15 p.m. ET. I'm on Florida Gators +3 and Over 51.5, with Kentucky Wildcats 28, Florida Gators 27 as my final.

Rhett Abernathy-Coyle, the Kentucky horse-country man, likes the crowd. "Kenny Minchey has 14 touchdowns and two interceptions. We beat LSU and South Carolina in overtime. A full barn in black is our kind of night." Dax Marchetti, Florida's herpetology minor, likes his back. "Jadan Baugh has 14 rushing touchdowns. A gator waits in the water. We went to Austin and beat Texas 27-24."

DraftKings opened Kentucky −1.5 with 55.5 points on the over/under, and the market has moved to Kentucky −3 and 51.5. I'm taking both moves back. The spread went up after Kentucky lost 34-6 at Tennessee, which is a strange time to pay more for the Wildcats, and the over/under dropped four points after Florida scored 8 and 7 against Georgia and Oklahoma.

On the side, my number is one point. Kentucky's two biggest wins this year, at South Carolina and against LSU, were both 35-34 in overtime. The Wildcats win close, and close is what I see here. Florida has a real road win in Austin, and Baugh's 836 rushing yards give the Gators a way to shorten the game. Kentucky by one keeps Florida Gators +3 alive with room to spare.

On the over/under, the low-scoring Florida stretch is the reason the number dropped, and I think it dropped too far. Before the slump, Florida games finished 66-21, 52-28 and 44-39. Kentucky allows 25.8 points a game and Florida allows 27.6. The September Gators scored 52 twice. Minchey has led 34 or more three times in league play. My 55 combined points sit above 51.5.

The room agrees with me more than it argues. The Formula has Florida Gators +3 and the Over with Kentucky 28-27. Next Man Up also takes Florida Gators +3, likes the Over, and has Kentucky winning 30-28. Letdown Watch is on Florida Gators +3 and the Over as well. In The Trenches goes the other way and likes Kentucky Wildcats −3. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

In The Trenches, to The Formula: "Three of you on the Gators? Somebody has to defend the barn." The Formula: "You can have the barn. We'll take the field goal."

Black jackets, a sold-out stadium, a one-score SEC game under the lights. Rhett gets his win. Dax gets his back-door chance at the wire, and Florida is at 32.4 points per game this season.

Kentucky Wildcats 28, Florida Gators 27. Florida Gators cover the 3. Over 51.5.
