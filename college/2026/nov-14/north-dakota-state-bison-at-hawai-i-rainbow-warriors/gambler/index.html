North Dakota State at Hawai'i: The Bison Toured Pearl Harbor While the Country Stopped Flying

North Dakota State Bison (7-2) at Hawai'i Rainbow Warriors (3-6)

11:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Clarence T.C. Ching Athletics Complex · CW

XFacebook

North Dakota State's traveling party was inside the theater at the USS Arizona Memorial, watching the 23-minute film about the Pearl Harbor attack, when phones lit up with the news that the FAA had stopped every flight in the country. “We’re watching a movie about a World War, and to get out of the theatre and see all the texts was pretty remarkable,” coach Tim Polasek told InForum. “Obviously we’re thankful to be here so we get a chance to play a football game.”

The number as of Nov. 14, 2026, 10:17 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at North Dakota State −7, total 39.5. Every pick in this story, mine and each handicapper's, is checked against that number, not Monday's. Monday's card had North Dakota State −6.5 with a 38.5 total. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

They were lucky to be there at all. InForum says mechanical trouble with the charter pushed the Bison's Thursday 11:30 a.m. departure from Fargo to just before 1 a.m. Friday, and the team landed in Honolulu at 4:45 a.m. local time. Athletic director Matt Larsen: “nothing surprises you with this group of guys and I think they’ve handled it really well.”

Hawai'i calls it Military Night, per its game themes, a fitting one for a Veterans Day weekend and a visitor fresh off a Pearl Harbor visit. It is the first meeting between these programs. The Rainbow Warriors are 3-6 and looking for a third straight win after a 17-14 victory over UTEP in which Micah Alejado threw for 303 yards, per AP.

NDSU's defense allows 11.9 points a game, second in FBS, and its red-zone defense ranks first, per AP. Hawai'i ranks 133rd in red-zone offense. Points will be hard to find at the Ching Complex.
