Houston vs Colorado Pick: Buffaloes +10 on Buffs Wear Black Night

Houston Cougars (7-2) at Colorado Buffaloes (3-6)

10:15 PM ET Fri Nov 13 · Folsom Field · ESPN

Folsom Field is going dark on Friday night, on purpose. Colorado's Friday night home game is a Buffs Wear Black night, and at halftime a drone show from the Colorado Tourism Office lights up the Boulder sky. For this Houston vs Colorado spread, I'll take Colorado Buffaloes +10 and the Under 50.5, and my score for the 10:15 PM ET kickoff is Houston Cougars 28, Colorado Buffaloes 20.

Yo Pacheco runs it like a mission briefing. "Conner Weigman has 22 touchdown passes and 2 interceptions, Moss. Two. We score 35.4 a game. We beat Cincinnati 31-24 last week and Re'Shaun Sanford II ran for 126. Houston has liftoff."

Moss Feldstein swings gently in his hammock. "Boom or bust, Yo. We lost 57-24 at Oklahoma State and then beat Kansas State 27-24 in overtime the next week. You can't chart us. Gideon Lampron has 99 tackles and 5.5 sacks. That guy's a whole weather system."

Here's why I'm taking the 10. DraftKings opened Houston −10.5 and it's down to 10, so the market is drifting my way. Houston's road games have been tight: a 28-26 loss at Texas Tech, a 21-13 loss at Utah, and wins at Georgia Southern and Kansas State. Colorado lost to Utah 27-24 here, a team that beat Houston by eight. The Cougars also won three of their seven games by 10 or fewer, including 35-32 over Oklahoma State and 31-24 over Cincinnati. My final has Houston by eight, so Colorado stays inside the number.

The Colorado offense is the worry. Isaac Wilson has taken the most throws in each of the last six games, with 1,412 yards, 8 touchdowns and 8 interceptions. Colorado is at 19.8 points per game this season. Joseph Williams is the go-to target with 853 receiving yards, and he caught 7 for 121 at Arizona State. I'm pricing Wilson, and that's exactly why I also like the Under.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 47.5 and it's been pushed up to 50.5, and I'm going the other way with the Under 50.5. Colorado has been held to 14 or fewer in five of its nine games. The Buffs score 19.8 and give up 28.3, and Houston games have gone higher, so this isn't a free one. My 28-20 lands on 48.

The whole room is on Houston for the side. Next Man Up has Houston 29-15, likes Houston Cougars −10 and likes the Under 50.5. In The Trenches likes Houston −10 with a 26-24 score and makes no over/under call. The Formula has 32-20, takes Houston −10 and goes Over 50.5. Letdown Watch has 31-20, takes the Cougars −10 and goes Over. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Moss sips his kombucha and watches the drones. Yo counts down from ten and calls it a launch window.

Houston Cougars 28, Colorado Buffaloes 20. Colorado Buffaloes cover the 10. Under 50.5.
