Houston vs Colorado: Drones, Black Hoodies and a Friday in Boulder

Houston Cougars (7-2) at Colorado Buffaloes (3-6)

10:15 PM ET Fri Nov 13 · Folsom Field · ESPN

XFacebook

Moss Feldstein has his black hoodie on, his black beanie on and, for the first time anyone can remember, black socks. Friday night at Folsom Field is a Buffs Wear Black game, and Moss takes a dress code seriously when he agrees with it. He also has a thermos of kombucha and a good seat for the halftime drone show. Moss doesn't like to call it a plan. Plans are stressful.

Houston Cougars vs Colorado Buffaloes kicks off at 8:15 p.m. in Boulder, which makes it a school night for a lot of people. CU says classes won't be canceled, you'll need a Buff OneCard to get into the academic buildings, and the Earth Sciences and Map Library closes at 2 p.m. Moss thinks that's the most Boulder sentence ever written.

At halftime, Colorado and the Colorado Tourism Office are putting on a drone show over the stadium. Yolanda "Yo" Pacheco can't wait.

"Drones," she says. "Moss, I'm from Space City. We put people on the moon. You're putting lights in the sky and calling it a show."

"It is a show," Moss says. "It's very chill. You'll like it."

Yo is not chill. Yo has been counting down to kickoff since Monday. Houston is 7-2 and third in the Big 12, scoring 35.4 points a game. The Cougars beat Cincinnati 31-24 last Saturday, and Re'Shaun Sanford II carried 15 times for 126 yards and a touchdown. Amare Thomas caught 6 balls for 78 yards and 2 scores.

Then there's Conner Weigman. He's completed 170 of 257 throws for 2,280 yards with 22 touchdowns and only 2 interceptions. He threw 5 touchdowns against Oklahoma State and 4 each against Georgia Southern and Kansas State.

"Two interceptions in nine games," Yo says. "That's not a quarterback. That's mission control."

Thomas leads the receivers with 698 yards and 8 touchdowns, and Makhi Hughes leads the runners with 606 yards. Kentrell Webb has 50 tackles, and Ashton Porter has 3 sacks.

Moss nods slowly, like he's listening to a song he already knows. Colorado is 3-6 and 14th in the Big 12, and he's not going to pretend otherwise. The Buffs score 19.8 points a game and give up 28.3. They lost 41-7 at Northwestern and 57-24 at Oklahoma State. But then, a week after Stillwater, they beat Kansas State 27-24 in overtime right here on Halloween.

"That's the thing about us," Moss says. "You never know which Buffs show up. Honestly, neither do we. It keeps things interesting."

Isaac Wilson has taken the most throws in each of the last six games and has 1,412 yards with 8 touchdowns. Joseph Williams is his go-to target, though Moss doesn't like ranking his friends. Williams has 58 catches for 853 yards and 6 touchdowns, and he caught 7 balls for 121 yards at Arizona State last week. JaQuail Smith leads the runners with 384 yards.

And then there's Gideon Lampron. The linebacker has 99 tackles and 5.5 sacks.

"Ninety-nine," Moss says, and for a second he sounds almost excited. "Gideon is a whole mountain range."

The two teams met last season, and Houston won 36-20. Yo remembers. Moss says he was at a yoga retreat and only heard about it later, which was probably for the best.

Here's how it goes. Folsom Field is black from top to bottom, and the Buffs come out loud. Lampron makes a big stop early, and Colorado hangs in through halftime. The drones light up the sky, and even Yo admits it's pretty cool. But Weigman settles in after the break. He finds Thomas for one touchdown, Sanford grinds out the clock, and Houston pulls away late. Colorado scores in the fourth quarter to keep it respectable.

Yo jumps up and yells, "Houston, we have a WIN!" loud enough to scare a drone. Moss shrugs, says, "It's all good, man," and quietly opens his laptop in the hammock to re-run the model.

My call: Houston Cougars 28, Colorado Buffaloes 20.
