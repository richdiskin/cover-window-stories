Duke vs Miami Prediction: Hurricanes −13.5, Over 54.5

Duke Blue Devils (7-2) at Miami Hurricanes (8-1)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Hard Rock Stadium · ABC

XFacebook

Duke at Miami is Homecoming at Hard Rock Stadium, presented by The Islands of the Bahamas, with a noon kickoff that pulls alumni back into the building a week after the Canes lost at Notre Dame. My Duke vs Miami prediction is Miami Hurricanes 35, Duke Blue Devils 21, with Miami Hurricanes −13.5 and Over 54.5.

Javi "The Breeze" Castillo, the Hurricanes insider, talks like the palm trees are already swaying. "Darian Mensah has 28 touchdowns and two interceptions. Malachi Toney has 1,027 yards. Homecoming doesn't do quiet." Harrison Wexley-Moore IV, Duke's insider, answers with Nate Sheppard. "He has 1,189 yards and 17 scores. We just put 41 on NC State. Bring the brass band."

The market moved hard toward Duke. DraftKings opened Miami −20.5 with 55.5 on the over/under, and it's down to −13.5 and 54.5. Seven points off the open is the market telling you Duke's defense is real. Duke allows 13.0 points a game. That is the best number on this field. I still lay the points because Miami scores 45.0 a game and just dropped 52 on Pittsburgh and 43 on Florida State. Mensah has 2,694 passing yards. Mark Fletcher Jr. has nine rushing scores. Even after a 24-31 loss at Notre Dame, this offense is still built to clear 30.

Duke's offense is no orphan. Walker Eget has 1,849 yards, and Sheppard just ran for 217 and three scores at Carter-Finley. The problem is the other side of the ball. Miami allows 19.2, which is fine until Mensah starts stacking completions on third down, and Duke has to chase.

The Over is about Miami's ceiling. Six of the Canes' nine games finished with at least 54.5 combined points. Duke's last two were 47-2 and 41-3, so the Blue Devils can put points up when the run game hits. My 56 points sit above 54.5, and the drop from 55.5 still leaves me room if either offense is late to the party.

The room mostly rides Miami on the side and splits the total. The Formula has Miami Hurricanes −13.5 and likes Under 54.5 at 34-20. Next Man Up likes Miami Hurricanes −13.5 and Over 54.5 at 38-20. Letdown Watch takes Duke Blue Devils +13.5 and likes Under 54.5 at 32-21. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call this week. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Next Man Up: "Letdown Watch took the points after a Homecoming open fell seven." Letdown Watch: "Duke just scored 41 on the road. The open was a fantasy."

Homecoming gets the noise. Miami gets the touchdowns. I get the points and the total.

Miami Hurricanes 35, Duke Blue Devils 21. Miami Hurricanes cover the 13.5. Over 54.5.
