Ole Miss vs Oklahoma Prediction: Sooners −2.5, Under 48.5

Ole Miss at Oklahoma got bumped a day. The SEC moved it to Sunday, Nov. 15, at 1 p.m. ET on SEC Network, after the FAA's nationwide ground stop Friday left Ole Miss stuck in Oxford. My Ole Miss vs Oklahoma prediction is Oklahoma Sooners 26, Ole Miss Rebels 19, with Oklahoma Sooners −2.5 and Under 48.5.

The number as of Nov. 15, 2026, 1:39 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at Oklahoma −2.5, total 48.5. Every pick in this story, mine and each card's, is checked against that number, not Monday's. Monday's card had Ole Miss −1.5 with a 49.5 total. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

Langston Fairbanks, the Rebels insider, spent Saturday refreshing a flight tracker like it owed him money. "Chambliss has 18 touchdowns. He can throw them on a Sunday." Savannah Redbird-Lowe, Oklahoma's insider: "Our athletic director said this isn't fair. Fine. We'll make it unfair on the field too."

Per the SEC statement reported by ESPN, Ole Miss could not leave Oxford Friday night, its charter could not take a late-night departure once the ground stop lifted, and the trip slid to Saturday afternoon. It was the only game moved to Sunday.

Injury check, SEC availability report Friday, Nov. 13 (On SI): Ole Miss has 12 out, including RB Kewan Lacy, LB Keaton Thomas, OL Patrick Kutas and four defensive backs; RB Makhi Frazier is questionable. Oklahoma lists WR Trell Harris, TE Trynae Washington, OL Fred Hinton, LB Taylor Heim and DB Dakoda Fields out; RB Tory Blaylock is questionable. That report was filed for Saturday, so check game-day updates.

I had Ole Miss on Monday. That report moved me. Lacy is the Rebels' leading rusher with 388 yards and five scores. Thomas is their leading tackler with 55. Ole Miss scored seven against Georgia, and now it gets an Oklahoma defense that allows 19.2 and just held Florida to seven. Mateer has 15 touchdowns and Isaiah Sategna III has 625 receiving yards. Oklahoma does not need a shootout. It needs Ole Miss to play like a team whose Friday flight never left.

The Under is the weather and the injuries. ESPN's forecast for kickoff is 42 degrees, gusts to 18 mph and a 49 percent chance of precipitation. My 45 points sit 3.5 under 48.5.
