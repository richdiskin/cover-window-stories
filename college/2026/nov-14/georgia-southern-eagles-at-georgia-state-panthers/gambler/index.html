Georgia Southern vs Georgia State Pick: GSU +5.5, Under 56.5

Georgia Southern Eagles (5-4) at Georgia State Panthers (5-4)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Center Parc Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

The third-annual Georgia Grown Bowl puts a farm-state name on a rivalry tied at six wins apiece, per Georgia State Athletics, and the 13th meeting kicks off Saturday at Center Parc Stadium in downtown Atlanta. My Georgia Southern vs Georgia State pick is Georgia State Panthers +5.5 with the Under 56.5, and in Week 11 I want the points with the home team.

Royce Ogeechee grew up on a Bulloch County farm and talks about Georgia Southern like a family heirloom. "Max Johnson went 19 of 26 for 271 yards and four touchdowns against Marshall. Fifteen touchdown throws, two interceptions. That's a harvest, Malik."

Malik Overstreet knows every MARTA stop and every Panthers third-down habit, and he's not impressed. "We just won 39-23 at App State. Ayden Pereira threw three touchdowns, and Lanear McCrary Jr. ran 21 times for 123 yards. Xavier Esqillion has nine sacks. Get off at Georgia State station and wait for us."

Both men have a case, and that's why five and a half points matter. Georgia Southern has won three straight and sits first in the Sun Belt East at 4-1. But the Eagles lost on the road at Jacksonville State 27-31 and won only 31-24 at Coastal Carolina. Georgia State is 3-1 at home, scores 34.4 points a game and has Esqillion to make Johnson move. Rodshaun Dorsey has nine touchdown catches on 29 grabs, the scoring threat Georgia Southern has to find first.

The last ten meetings split 5-5, and Georgia Southern won the most recent two, 38-21 and 41-24. That's real, but nobody has owned this series for long.

My final is Georgia Southern Eagles 28, Georgia State Panthers 25. A three-point game sits well inside the 5.5.

On the over/under, I'm on the Under 56.5. DraftKings opened it at 55.5 and it has ticked up a point. Georgia State's offense is the reason the number is this high, but Georgia Southern's defense has been the steadier group. Anthony Bynum has five sacks, and the Eagles held App State to three points two weeks ago. My 28-25 lands at 53, clear of the 56.5.

The room loves the Panthers. The Formula likes Georgia State +5.5 and likes the Under at 28-25. Next Man Up likes Georgia State +5.5 too, with the Panthers winning 29-28, but has the Over. Letdown Watch has Georgia State +5.5 and likes the Under at 28-25. In The Trenches likes Georgia State +5.5 at 30-22 and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Next Man Up: "Twenty-nine to twenty-eight, Panthers. The Formula and Letdown Watch copied each other's homework again."

In The Trenches: "Next Man Up went Over in a rivalry game. Somebody check its fuel gauge."

Royce wants a third straight win in this series. Malik wants the Georgia Grown trophy staying downtown. I think the Eagles get the win and the Panthers keep it close. Georgia Southern allows 19.7 points per game.

Georgia Southern Eagles 28, Georgia State Panthers 25. Georgia State Panthers cover the 5.5. Under 56.5.
