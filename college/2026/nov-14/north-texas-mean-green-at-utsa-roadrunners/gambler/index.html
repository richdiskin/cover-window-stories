North Texas vs UTSA Pick: Mean Green −1.5, Under 77.5

North Texas Mean Green (7-2) at UTSA Roadrunners (7-2)

9:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Alamodome · ESPNU

North Texas vs UTSA prediction: two 7-2 teams meet at the Alamodome in San Antonio on Saturday at 9 p.m. ET, with North Texas bringing the American's loudest offense into UTSA's Homecoming. I'm on North Texas Mean Green −1.5 and Under 77.5, with North Texas Mean Green 35, UTSA Roadrunners 32 as my final.

Grady Mayhew-Ochoa, the Denton jazz drummer, has the tempo on his side. "We score 44.7 a game. Jahiem White has 1,186 rushing yards and 16 touchdowns. Tayven Jackson has 23 touchdown throws. We beat you 55-17 last year." Marisol Ybarra, who has a sash full of Fiesta medals, has the home case. "We've won six of the last eleven meetings. Owen McCown threw for 359 against Navy. We just had nine days to get over Florida Atlantic."

The side is about North Texas' offense against a UTSA defense that just gave up 60 at Florida Atlantic. The Roadrunners allow 29.8 points a game, and North Texas has scored 44 or more in seven of nine. White ran for 139 yards and three scores against Rice last week. UTSA can answer, at 33.0 points a game, but the Mean Green beat Navy 49-39 on the road and Florida Atlantic 48-41, two teams UTSA struggled with. DraftKings opened North Texas −1.5, and that's still the number. I have the Mean Green by three.

The Under is the more interesting call. This is a huge number, and the series says it's too high. None of the last eleven meetings went higher than seventy-five combined points, and most of them were well short of that. North Texas allows 30.9 and UTSA scores 33.0, so the Mean Green will give up points, but UTSA has scored 31 or fewer in five of its nine games. The posted number climbed from 75.5 to 77.5. My 67 combined points sit well under it.

The risk is that both defenses are bad. North Texas has given up 39, 41 and 44 in three of its last five, and UTSA's 59-45 win over Colorado State shows how wild a Roadrunners game can get. If this turns into a track meet, the Under is gone by halftime.

The cards are split everywhere. The Formula has North Texas Mean Green −1.5 and likes the Under at 34-30. Next Man Up has UTSA Roadrunners +1.5 and likes the Over at North Texas 41-40. Letdown Watch likes North Texas Mean Green −1.5 and likes the Under at 37-31. In The Trenches likes UTSA Roadrunners +1.5 and stays out of the over/under. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Next Man Up, to Letdown Watch: "Forty-one to forty. That's a real football game." Letdown Watch: "That's a basketball game with helmets."

Marisol says the Alamodome on Homecoming is a different building. Grady says the Mean Green have the better rhythm section. I think North Texas wins a game that stays a little more sensible than the number.

North Texas Mean Green 35, UTSA Roadrunners 32. North Texas Mean Green cover the 1.5. Under 77.5.
