North Texas at UTSA: The Alamodome Gets the American's Game of the Year at Homecoming

North Texas Mean Green (7-2) at UTSA Roadrunners (7-2)

9:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Alamodome · ESPNU

XFacebook

San Antonio is calling it the game of the year in the American, and UTSA Today says plenty of broadcasters and pundits agree. It is Homecoming and Family Weekend, it is on ESPNU at 8 p.m. Central, and first place is on the table at the Alamodome. North Texas is 5-0 in the league; UTSA is 4-1.

The number as of Nov. 14, 2026, 10:08 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at North Texas −2.5, total 76.5. Every pick in this story, mine and each handicapper's, is checked against that number, not Monday's. Monday's card had North Texas −1.5 with a 77.5 total. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

North Texas took the long way. The Mean Green took buses to San Antonio on Friday, with players, coaches and medical staff leaving first and everybody else following Saturday morning, per ESPN sources as tracked by Undrafted.

History likes the hosts. UTSA leads the series 8-6, is 6-1 against the Mean Green in San Antonio and is 6-0 in Homecoming games under Jeff Traylor, per UTSA's notes. The Roadrunners are 27-1 at home against league opponents since 2020. UTSA Today still remembers the 31-27 Homecoming win over North Texas in 2022.

North Texas has scored at least 44 points in all seven of its wins, per UTSA Today. Jahiem White has 1,193 rushing yards and 16 touchdowns, and Tayven Jackson has 23 touchdown passes, per AP. The Mean Green won the last meeting 55-17 in Denton.
