North Texas vs UTSA: Homecoming Bebop at the Alamodome

North Texas Mean Green (7-2) at UTSA Roadrunners (7-2)

9:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Alamodome · ESPNU

XFacebook

North Texas Mean Green vs UTSA Roadrunners is Homecoming at the Alamodome, an 8 p.m. kickoff in San Antonio and the 15th meeting in what has become the most-played series in UTSA history. The first 10,000 fans get homecoming jerseys, so the building should be a sea of the same shirt. Marisol Ybarra, who wears her Fiesta medals on a sash she's been adding to since high school, plans to wear the jersey over the sash. Grady Mayhew-Ochoa, a Denton jazz drummer who has played every dive on Fry Street, came down I-35 with a pair of brushes in his back pocket.

Marisol: You brought brushes to a football game.

Grady: Brushes are for ballads. I brought them as a joke. This game's not going to be a ballad.

Marisol: What is it, then?

Grady: Bebop. Fast, loud, and nobody knows the chord changes.

That might be the best description of this matchup anyone's going to give. North Texas is 7-2 and scores 44.7 points a game. UTSA is 7-2 and scores 33.0. Both defenses have been leaky, and the last two meetings ended 48-27 and 55-17.

Grady: Fifty-five to seventeen last year. I still have the ticket stub framed.

Marisol: That one hurt. Here's the thing, though. We lead the series 8-6, and we're 5-1 at home.

Grady: In San Antonio?

Marisol: In San Antonio.

Grady: I didn't know that.

Marisol: Nobody in Denton does.

The North Texas offense is the headliner. Jahiem White has 1,186 rushing yards and 16 touchdowns, and he ran for 139 yards and three scores against Rice last week. Tayven Jackson has 2,880 throwing yards and 23 touchdowns, including a 492-yard night at Texas State. Justin Stevenson has 898 receiving yards. The Mean Green beat Navy 49-39 on the road, beat Florida Atlantic 48-41 and won 45-44 in overtime at Tulsa.

Grady: White runs like a drummer playing double-time. You can't keep up with him.

Marisol: We have Darion White.

Grady: Different White.

Marisol: Same name. Different attitude. He has 59 tackles.

UTSA has been steadier than its scores suggest. The Roadrunners won 31-26 at Texas State, beat Navy 38-34, won 42-19 at Tulane and beat Colorado State 59-45. Owen McCown has 2,016 throwing yards and threw for 359 against Navy. Will Henderson III has 682 rushing yards. The low point was last week, a 60-29 loss at Florida Atlantic, but UTSA played that one on a Thursday and has had nine days to fix it.

Marisol: Nine days is a long time. You can rebuild a whole defense in nine days.

Grady: You can rebuild a whole band in nine days. Doesn't mean it'll swing.

Marisol: It'll swing.

The key is whether UTSA can slow White at all. North Texas allows 30.9 points a game, so the Roadrunners will score. But if White gets to the second level all night, the Mean Green can match whatever UTSA puts up and then some. McCown needs a big night, and Mekhi Anderson, who caught two touchdowns at Florida Atlantic, needs to be part of it.

Marisol: The Alamodome on Homecoming is loud. Our alumni come back for this one.

Grady: North Texas fans travel too. We'll be in the upper deck, making noise.

Marisol: The upper deck is very far away.

Grady: We'll be loud and far away.

The series has swung back and forth for a decade, with neither side owning it. This year both teams are good enough to make it close. North Texas has the better offense and the better back, and UTSA has the crowd.

Marisol: When we win, you're playing a drum solo at my cousin's quinceañera next month.

Grady: And if North Texas wins?

Marisol: Then you still play. But you play a cumbia.

Grady: I know two cumbias. That's enough.

Grady plays both of his cumbias on the way out of the Alamodome. My call: North Texas Mean Green 35, UTSA Roadrunners 32.
