Troy vs South Alabama: Battle for the Belt Night in Mobile

Troy Trojans (4-5) at South Alabama Jaguars (4-5)

7:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Hancock Whitney Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Troy Trojans vs South Alabama Jaguars is the Battle for the Belt, a Saturday night in Mobile at Hancock Whitney Stadium with a 7 p.m. ET kickoff and a belt waiting for whichever side survives. Mobile Mardi Gras float-builder Azalea Fairhope-Grice has been polishing the case where she plans to keep that belt, and Troy's Marlo Pickens, the old long snapper and peanut farmer's son drove down to Mobile reminding her that his school has held it far more often.

Marlo: Ten wins to four, Azalea. That's the series. You can look it up.

Azalea: I did. Then I looked at 2024. We beat you 25-9 right here, in this stadium.

Marlo: And then last year we beat you 31-24 in overtime at our place.

Azalea: I don't remember that.

Marlo: You remember it fine.

That's the Battle for the Belt in a nutshell. It's the in-state Sun Belt fight that both towns treat like a family argument at Thanksgiving, and the belt itself is the prop that keeps it personal. Troy has had it more often over the last decade, winning eight of the last ten meetings, but Azalea brings up the 2024 win in Mobile every chance she gets.

The records tell you how even this is. Both teams are 4-5. Both are 2-3 in the conference and third in the West. Neither has much margin left to fix the season.

Troy's year swung hard. The Trojans hung 55 on Southern Miss, then lost 33-6 at Louisiana and 21-14 to UL Monroe, then stunned James Madison 24-21 in overtime on the road, then lost 41-17 to Louisiana Tech last Saturday. The quarterback job has moved between Goose Crowder, who has nine touchdown throws, and Tucker Kilcrease, who has had the ball the last two games.

Marlo: We beat James Madison on their field. That's a real team. People forget that.

Azalea: People remember the 41 last week.

Marlo: Fair.

South Alabama's year has been a roller coaster on the other end of the scoreboard. The Jaguars put up 52 on UL Monroe and won a 56-49 track meet at Arkansas State, but they've dropped three straight since, by seven at Marshall, 35-20 to Louisiana Tech and 28-26 at Louisiana last weekend. Bishop Davenport threw for 210 yards and ran for two scores in that last one, and Jared Hollins leads the team with 1,195 throwing yards and nine touchdowns.

Azalea: We score. We score 32.4 points a game. Keenan Phillips has 595 rushing yards. Brendan Jenkins went for 101 yards last week. We just have to stop people.

Marlo: You give up 32.2.

Azalea: I said we just have to stop people.

That's the matchup. Troy's best player might be defensive back Donnie Smith, who has eight sacks, a ridiculous number for a defensive back, and linebacker Keno Jones has 70 tackles. If Smith keeps getting home on blitzes, South Alabama's quarterbacks will be throwing off their back foot all night. South Alabama safety Cole Blaylock leads the Jaguars with 46 tackles, and that tells you how often the ball has gotten to the third level against this defense.

Marlo: Smith is the best blitzer in this league. I snapped for twelve years and I never saw a DB time it like that.

Azalea: Then we'll throw it before he gets there. Jenkins runs, Davenport throws, and Phillips mops up the rest.

Marlo: Gavin Griffin has 465 yards for us. We can run too.

The belt has a way of making these games weird. Six of the last ten meetings were decided by eight points or more, and then last year's went to overtime. Mobile on a November night, with a belt at stake and two teams that know each other too well, points to another tight one.

Azalea: When we win, I'm putting that belt on the lead float in February. Front and center.

Marlo: The belt doesn't ride floats.

Azalea: It will this year. I've already built the stand.

Marlo: Then you'd better hope Davenport throws it before Donnie Smith gets there.

Azalea gets her belt for the stand, and Marlo drives back to Troy grumbling. My call: South Alabama Jaguars 30, Troy Trojans 27.
