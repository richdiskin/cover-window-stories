Troy vs South Alabama Pick: Jaguars −2.5, Over 55.5

Troy Trojans (4-5) at South Alabama Jaguars (4-5)

7:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Hancock Whitney Stadium · ESPN+

Troy vs South Alabama prediction: the Battle for the Belt comes to Hancock Whitney Stadium in Mobile on Saturday night, 7 p.m. ET, with two 4-5 Sun Belt teams fighting over a belt and the last chance to steady a season. I'm on South Alabama Jaguars −2.5 and Over 55.5, with South Alabama Jaguars 30, Troy Trojans 27 as my final.

Azalea Fairhope-Grice, the Mobile Mardi Gras float-builder, has a spot on her lead float already picked out for the belt. "We beat you 25-9 in this stadium in 2024. We score 32.4 points a game." Marlo Pickens, the Troy long snapper turned peanut man, has the bigger history. "Eight of the last ten meetings are ours, and we won 31-24 in overtime last year. Donnie Smith has eight sacks."

The side is close because the teams are close, but home matters in this series lately. South Alabama's 2024 win came in Mobile, and two of the Jaguars' three straight losses were one-score games: 20-13 at Marshall and 28-26 at Louisiana last week. Troy just lost 41-17 at home to that same Louisiana Tech team and has scored 6, 14 and 17 in three of its last four. The Trojans' quarterback spot has bounced between Goose Crowder and Tucker Kilcrease. DraftKings opened South Alabama −2.5 and hasn't moved. My three-point Jaguars win clears it.

The Over comes from the South Alabama side of the field. The Jaguars score 32.4 points a game and allow 32.2, and their games have finished 52-35, 56-49 and 41-36 this year. Troy's defense just gave up 41. Troy's offense can wake up too, as it did with 55 against Southern Miss. The posted number slipped a point from the DraftKings open of 56.5 to 55.5, and my 57 combined points clear it.

The honest counter is the series itself. None of the last ten meetings reached 56 points, and South Alabama's 13 points at Marshall shows this offense can stall. If Smith keeps getting home from the secondary, the Jaguars could look like that again.

The room is unanimous on one thing. Every card that takes a side has South Alabama Jaguars −2.5: The Formula, Next Man Up, Letdown Watch and In The Trenches. The over/under splits. The Formula has the Under at 29-26 Jaguars. Next Man Up likes the Over and has South Alabama 34-28. Letdown Watch takes the Under. In The Trenches makes no over/under call. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Next Man Up, to The Formula: "Twenty-nine to twenty-six and you can't find the Over? It's Mobile, not a library." The Formula: "Thirty-four points from a team that scored 13 at Marshall. Bold."

Marlo says the belt goes home to Troy the way it usually does. Azalea says it rides a float in February, and on a night when the Jaguars get the home crowd, I'll side with the float.

South Alabama Jaguars 30, Troy Trojans 27. South Alabama Jaguars cover the 2.5. Over 55.5.
