Florida State vs Pitt: Friday the 13th Senior Night Party

Florida State Seminoles (4-5) at Pittsburgh Panthers (6-3)

7:00 PM ET Fri Nov 13 · Acrisure Stadium · ESPN

XFacebook

Some people stay home on Friday the 13th. Dominic "Yinz" Bertolucci is going to a party on the North Shore. Pitt's last home game of the year is Senior Night at Acrisure Stadium, a Friday night tribute to the seniors, and the school promised fans a season-ending party. Yinz has his homemade whistle, a gold Pitt scarf and a seat close enough to hear the seniors' names called.

Gianna Ravello flew up from Tallahassee anyway. She doesn't trust bad luck. She trusts procedure.

"I'd like to enter something into the minutes," she says as she sits down. "Florida State won at Boston College last Saturday, 31-24. Amari Thomas ran for 98 yards and 2 touchdowns. The motion to feel good about ourselves is on the floor."

"Seconded by nobody," Yinz says. "How's it goin', n'at? Welcome to Pittsburgh."

Florida State is 4-5 and 10th in the ACC, and Gianna knows it has been a bumpy year. The Seminoles lost 27-24 to SMU, 50-36 at Alabama, 44-20 at Louisville, 43-35 in overtime at Miami and 36-24 to Clemson. But the wins have been loud. They beat Virginia 38-7, and they scored 30.7 points a game across nine games.

Ashton Daniels does a lot of the talking. He's thrown for 2,146 yards and 14 touchdowns, with 12 interceptions. He threw for 377 at Alabama and 350 against Clemson, and he had 4 touchdown throws at Miami. Ousmane Kromah has 609 rushing yards, and Micahi Danzy leads the receivers with 487 yards and caught 7 balls for 86 at Boston College. On defense, Rylan Kennedy has 6 sacks and Chris Jones has 64 tackles.

"The Daniels administration," Gianna says, "has a strong economy and a few scandals. Every administration does."

Yinz grades it all with his whistle. Pitt is 6-3, 5-1 at home and allows 19.8 points a game. The only team to win on this field this season was North Carolina, 21-17. Pitt opened the year by beating Miami (OH) 59-14 and Bucknell 59-0, and Mason Heintschel threw 7 touchdowns in that first game. He has 14 touchdown throws and only 1 interception this year. Holden Geriner has taken the most throws in three of the last four games, and he threw for 180 and a touchdown against Georgia Tech.

But Yinz's best part of that night was on the ground. Damon Ferguson Jr. carried 31 times for 185 yards and 2 touchdowns in the 34-11 win.

"Thirty-one carries," Yinz says, and blows the whistle twice. "That's not a running back, Gianna. That's a steel mill."

Ferguson has 662 rushing yards this year. Cataurus Hicks has 698 receiving yards and 6 touchdowns. Cruce Brookins leads the defense with 61 tackles, and Jaeden Moore has 4.5 sacks.

Then Yinz brings up last year. Pitt beat Florida State 34-31. Before that, the Seminoles won 24-7 in 2023, and Pitt won 41-17 in 2020.

"Two out of three," Yinz says. "I'd call that a mandate."

"That's my word," Gianna says. "You can't use my word."

"I just did. Filibuster me."

She tries. She talks for almost a full minute about Danzy's route tree, the Seminoles' defensive front and the history of the Sunshine State. Yinz waits her out, then hands her a pierogi from the tailgate on the North Shore.

Run the tape forward. The seniors get their moment before kickoff, and the crowd is loud from the first snap. Daniels hits a couple of big throws early, and Florida State hangs around into the third quarter. But Pitt keeps handing it to Ferguson, and the Seminoles spend the second half chasing him. Brookins and the Pitt defense take one ball away from Daniels late, and Pitt's seniors walk off the field winners on their last night at home.

Yinz jumps up and yells "Yinz SEE that?!" so loud the guy running the scoreboard flinches. Gianna quietly drafts emergency legislation to fix the secondary and promises to bring it to a vote in Tallahassee.

Friday the 13th turns lucky on the North Shore. My call: Pittsburgh Panthers 31, Florida State Seminoles 23.
