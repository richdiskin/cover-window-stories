Florida State vs Pitt Pick: Panthers −3.5 on Senior Night

Florida State Seminoles (4-5) at Pittsburgh Panthers (6-3)

7:00 PM ET Fri Nov 13 · Acrisure Stadium · ESPN

XFacebook

Friday the 13th is Senior Night on the North Shore. Pitt closes its home schedule at Acrisure Stadium with a tribute to its seniors, and the school is calling it a season-ending party. For this Florida State vs Pittsburgh spread, I'm laying Pittsburgh Panthers −3.5, going Under 54.5, and my final is Pittsburgh Panthers 31, Florida State Seminoles 23, for the 7:00 PM ET kickoff.

Gianna Ravello opens like she's in front of a committee in Tallahassee. "Let the minutes show we won at Boston College 31-24 last week. Amari Thomas ran for 98 yards and 2 touchdowns. We score 30.7 a game. I yield nothing."

Dominic "Yinz" Bertolucci blows his homemade whistle. "Yinz score 30.7 and give up 28.3, Gianna. We give up 19.8. Damon Ferguson Jr. ran 31 times for 185 against Georgia Tech. That's how you close a game, n'at."

Laying the 3.5 comes down to a few things. DraftKings opened Pitt −3 and it has moved to 3.5, so the market came my way. Pitt is 5-1 at home, and the only loss on this field was 21-17 to North Carolina. Florida State is 1-3 on the road, with losses at Alabama, Louisville and Miami. Ashton Daniels has 2,146 passing yards and 14 touchdowns, but he's also thrown 12 interceptions, and Cruce Brookins has 61 tackles for a Pitt defense that allows 19.8. Pitt beat the Seminoles 34-31 last season.

The Pitt quarterback picture has changed. Mason Heintschel had 14 touchdowns and 1 interception and took the most throws in the first five games. Holden Geriner has led the throws in three of the last four. I'm pricing Geriner, which is why I'm counting on the run. Ferguson has 662 rushing yards, and Cataurus Hicks has 698 receiving yards for the deep shots.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 53.5 and it's up to 54.5, and I'm on the Under 54.5. Four of Pitt's six home games stayed under that number, and a Pitt offense that hands it to Ferguson 31 times shortens the night. Florida State games have gone higher, so this one's tight. My 31-23 lands on 54, half a point under.

The room is all over the map. Letdown Watch has Pitt 30-24, likes Pittsburgh Panthers −3.5 and goes Under 54.5. Next Man Up has Pitt 32-26, likes Pitt −3.5 and likes the Over 54.5. The Formula has Pitt 28-26, takes Florida State Seminoles +3.5 and goes Under. In The Trenches likes Florida State +3.5 and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Next Man Up: "In The Trenches, three of us have Pitt winning, and you're taking the points?"

In The Trenches: "My fronts don't care about your scoreboard."

Gianna calls for a unanimous vote and gets one, from herself. Yinz orders a sandwich with fries in it, just in case.

Pittsburgh Panthers 31, Florida State Seminoles 23. Pittsburgh Panthers cover the 3.5. Under 54.5.
