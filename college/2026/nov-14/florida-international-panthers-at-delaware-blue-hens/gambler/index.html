Florida International at Delaware: Newark Stacks Homecoming, Champions and a Military Salute Into One Afternoon

Florida International Panthers (4-5) at Delaware Blue Hens (4-5)

3:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Delaware Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Newark is trying to fit five parties into one afternoon. Delaware's promotional schedule lists Saturday against FIU as Homecoming, Celebration of Champions, Military Appreciation, MVP day and Faculty and Staff Appreciation, all at Delaware Stadium at 3 p.m. If you know somebody who served, this is a good day to bring them.

The number as of Nov. 14, 2026, 10:00 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at Delaware −3.5, total 54.5. Every pick in this story, mine and each handicapper's, is checked against that number, not Monday's. Monday's card had Delaware −5.5 with a 54.5 total. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

The guy everybody in Newark is watching is Nick Minicucci. Delaware says his 35 completions at Kennesaw State moved him past Super Bowl MVP Joe Flacco for the most completions in a Delaware career, and his 408 yards made him the first Blue Hen with back-to-back 400-yard games. He also tied Flacco's school record with his 11th career 300-yard game. The Hens lost that one 42-40, so the records came with a sour taste.

FIU coach Willie Simmons is not pretending this is easy. “Nick has thrown for a ton of yards and he’s highly efficient,” he told Caplin News. He also gave the best road-trip speech of the week, telling his players to block out the hotel, the bus and the strange locker room: “The one common denominator is 100 by 53 and a third.” Simmons calls his group a “no-name defense,” and lineman Zaire Flournoy said of Delaware's front: “They bring a lot of stuff.”

FIU arrives fresh off a 23-20 win over Missouri State in which Lyons ran 24 times for 216 yards and two scores, per ESPN's AP capsule. Delaware brings Blake Matthews and his five sacks, part of a defense with 23 sacks on the season. These teams met last year in Miami, and Delaware won 38-16.

Injury check: we found no public injury or availability report for either team as of 4:30 AM ET Saturday, Nov. 14. No player status is assumed.

That makes it Delaware −3.5 and Under 54.5. Letdown Watch: Delaware 28, FIU 22. That makes it Delaware −3.5 and Under 54.5. In The Trenches has no pick: the trenches look even.

Newark gets champions honored, service members saluted and a quarterback chasing the biggest name in Blue Hens history. FIU's nameless defense would love to leave Delaware with everybody learning its name.

The Cover Writer's card at today's number: Delaware Blue Hens −3.5 and Under 54.5. My final is Delaware Blue Hens 28, Florida International Panthers 22. The spread shrank two points, which makes laying it easier. Delaware by six still clears 3.5 with room to spare.

Delaware Blue Hens 28, Florida International Panthers 22. Delaware Blue Hens cover the 3.5. Under 54.5.
