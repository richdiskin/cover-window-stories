FIU vs Delaware Pick: Blue Hens −5.5, Under 54.5

Florida International Panthers (4-5) at Delaware Blue Hens (4-5)

3:00 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Delaware Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

FIU at Delaware is Homecoming at Delaware Stadium, a Military Appreciation afternoon in Newark with kickoff at 3:00 PM ET. My FIU vs Delaware prediction has the Blue Hens winning at home in a game that stays more controlled than their recent shootouts: Delaware Blue Hens 28, Florida International Panthers 22, with Delaware Blue Hens −5.5 and Under 54.5.

Hen Lofland has her Homecoming reading list ready. "Nick Minicucci has 16 touchdown passes and four interceptions. We beat you 38-16 last year. Small state, big Saturdays." Lourdes Ibarguen-Tate brought cafecito and her abuela's forecast. "Devonte Lyons ran for 216 yards last week. Our defense allows 20.3 a game, Hen. Abuela says we win."

Lyons is the reason FIU can stay in it, and I'm not dismissing him. DraftKings opened Delaware −6.5 with 50.5 on the over/under, and by Monday morning it was −5.5 and 54.5. Both teams are 4-5, but Delaware has the quarterback who can take over. Minicucci threw for 414 yards at Western Kentucky and 408 at Kennesaw State, and Sean Wilson has 639 receiving yards. FIU's JJ Kohl has 10 interceptions to go with 11 touchdown passes, and he threw three at Florida Atlantic. The Panthers have lost by three twice, at Jacksonville State and to Middle Tennessee, but they also lost 49-10 at Liberty. My Blue Hens by 6 clear the 5.5.

The Under is about FIU. Eight of the Panthers' nine games finished below 54.5 combined points. FIU scores 19.3 points a game and allows 20.3, and Lyons gives them a way to shorten the game. Delaware's games have been wilder, with a 61-38 win over Missouri State and a 42-40 loss at Kennesaw State. But the Blue Hens also played 22-14 against Coastal Carolina and 30-14 against Liberty. The total moved up four points from 50.5, and that gives me room. My 50 points sit below 54.5.

The room is with me on the total. The Formula has Florida International Panthers +5.5 and likes Under 54.5, with Delaware 27-22. Next Man Up has Delaware Blue Hens −5.5 and likes Under 54.5, 28-22. Letdown Watch has Delaware Blue Hens −5.5 and likes Under 54.5, 28-22. In The Trenches has no side this week and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

The Formula to Next Man Up: "Twenty-seven to twenty-two is a five-point game. You're laying the five and a half?" Next Man Up: "Homecoming crowd, quarterback with four interceptions all year. I'll lay it."

Hen gets a Homecoming win and a bird pun nobody asked for. Lourdes gets Lyons running hard and a phone call to Sweetwater either way.

Delaware Blue Hens 28, Florida International Panthers 22. Delaware Blue Hens cover the 5.5. Under 54.5.
