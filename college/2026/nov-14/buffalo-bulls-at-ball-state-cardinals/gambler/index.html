Buffalo vs Ball State Pick: Cardinals +2.5 and the Over in Muncie

Buffalo Bulls (6-3) at Ball State Cardinals (3-6)

7:30 PM ET Wed Nov 11 · Scheumann Stadium · ESPN2

XFacebook

Muncie spends Wednesday afternoon at Minnetrista hearing the story of Cyrus Dallin's two downtown statues, The Passing of the Buffalo among them, and Wednesday night a Buffalo team shows up with no plans to go quietly. My Buffalo vs Ball State spread pick is Ball State Cardinals +2.5 with the Over 51.5, and the final reads Buffalo Bulls 28, Ball State Cardinals 26 at Scheumann Stadium, 7:30 PM ET.

Kendra Woolcott has heard that statue title all day. "We've won four in a row, Gus. Chance Morrow caught 12 balls for 199 yards and two scores at Miami. Messiah Burch has 901 yards. Nobody's passing anything but the ball tonight."

Gus Ponder answers with a dad joke and then the facts. "Why did the Bull cross the Indiana state line? To lose by a field goal. TJ Horton has 9 rushing touchdowns on 72 carries, Keldric Luster has 10 touchdown throws, and we lost at UMass by one in overtime. Jalen Bonds ran for 132 that night. We are closer than the record."

My case for the 2.5 is simple. Buffalo has been living on the wire for a month: 25-18 in overtime over Massachusetts, 31-24 over Bowling Green and 34-31 in overtime at Miami. Ball State plays the same kind of game, 32-31 in overtime at Bowling Green and that 31-30 overtime loss at UMass. DraftKings opened Buffalo −3 and it has slipped to 2.5, so I'm giving up the key number of 3, and my 2-point final still lands on the right side of 2.5. The Bulls are 6-3 and second in the MAC, but they've allowed 26.0 a game, more than they score.

Both quarterback spots are settled. Jason Wright has taken the most throws in eight straight Buffalo games, and he threw 46 times for 358 yards at Miami. Luster has done it in eight of Ball State's nine. Wright's 9 touchdowns and 6 interceptions on 231 attempts say Buffalo wins on volume, not precision.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 51.5 and hasn't moved, and I'm on the Over 51.5. Ball State allows 34.8 a game. The last meeting, in 2024, finished Buffalo 51, Ball State 48. Morrow has 8 touchdown catches. My 28-26 lands on 54.

Opinions in the room run every direction. Next Man Up has it 29-29, likes Ball State Cardinals +2.5 and likes the Over 51.5. In The Trenches likes the Cardinals +2.5 with Ball State winning 28-25 and makes no over/under call. The Formula has Buffalo 28-24, lays the −2.5 and goes Over. Letdown Watch has Buffalo 29-21, likes the Bulls −2.5 and takes the Under. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Letdown Watch: "Four straight wins for Buffalo, Next Man Up, and you drew a tie like it was a dare."

Next Man Up: "Two of those four went to overtime, Letdown Watch. That's not finding a way. That's borrowing one."

Kendra changes into shorts, November in Indiana or not, because she plans to celebrate. Gus tells her the Campus Orchestra is playing Sursa Hall at kickoff and offers to have them play her something slow on the way out.

Buffalo Bulls 28, Ball State Cardinals 26. Ball State Cardinals cover the 2.5. Over 51.5.
