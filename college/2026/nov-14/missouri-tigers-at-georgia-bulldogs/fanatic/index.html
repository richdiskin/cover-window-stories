Missouri vs Georgia: A Sold-Out Saturday at Sanford Stadium

Missouri Tigers (6-3) at Georgia Bulldogs (8-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Sanford Stadium · ABC

XFacebook

A sold-out sign is already up in Athens for Missouri, because Georgia's own schedule marked the Dooley Field at Sanford Stadium date as gone, and the new No. 1 team in the country kicks off at 3:30 p.m. ET. Thaddeus "Thad" Okafor-Brandt has been talking about the afternoon like a release date. Delphine Arceneaux-Moss arrived from Columbia with a press badge, a notebook, and no interest in being treated like a visiting formality.

Thad: Sold out. No. 1. Last week's 42-7 at Ole Miss is still ringing in the hedges. This is the A-side.

Delphine: Breaking: a sold-out crowd does not snap the ball. Show me the tape.

Thad: Track one is Gunner Stockton, 9-of-15, 186 yards, four scores. Track two is Nate Frazier for 107 yards. Track three is Talyn Taylor, 4 catches, 140 yards, three scores. That's a double album.

Delphine: And track four is Missouri putting 34 on Arkansas the week before Texas. We are 6-3. We did not come to clap along.

Georgia is 8-1 and 6-1 in the league, sitting first in the SEC and first in the College Football Playoff field after moving up from No. 2. The Bulldogs are scoring 41.1 points a game and allowing 14.0. Gunner Stockton has 1,857 passing yards and 20 touchdown throws this season. Dwight Phillips Jr. has 481 rushing yards. Talyn Taylor has 25 catches for 426 yards and 6 scores. The only loss was 24-27 to Auburn in this same stadium. Since then they have beaten Florida 27-8 and Ole Miss 42-7, and the home side has not forgotten the Auburn night.

Thad: That Auburn game was a B-side we don't play again. This building is going to sound like a packed house because it is one. Between the hedges, you can hear a packed house from the parking deck.

Delphine: Packed houses have seen us before. We beat Florida 45-17 and Texas A&M 27-6 in Columbia. Austin Simmons has 2,204 passing yards, 18 touchdown throws and 2 interceptions. Jamal Roberts has 1,071 rushing yards and 9 scores. Cayden Lee has 62 catches for 835 yards. Nicholas Rodriguez has 98 tackles. We can move the ball, and we can find people.

The other recent tape is harder on Missouri. Texas just left Columbia with a 34-20 win, and Ole Miss beat the Tigers 31-3 on the road. Those are the nights Delphine files under questions remain, then flips the notebook to the next series anyway. A 3-point afternoon in Oxford is not the story she wants to read back to a sold-out crowd in Athens, so she is not reading it twice.

Delphine: I'm not writing the concession stand menu. I'm writing the first drive.

Thad: Write whatever you want. Between the hedges, the blitz is track nine, and the crowd already knows the hook. Chris Cole has 5 sacks and Raylen Wilson has 58 tackles, and that front seven plays like an encore you don't talk through.

Delphine: I'll talk through whatever I want. That's the job.

In the regular-season meetings from 2016 through 2025, Georgia won all eight. Missouri has not taken one of those from the Bulldogs in that stretch. That history will be on the walk into the stadium, along with a sold-out Saturday that Georgia's own schedule already marked as gone.

Thad: Album review, early copy: loud, heavy, and finished before the encore if the front seven plays its set. I'll keep the private score in my head if Delphine is taking notes.

Delphine: I'm always taking notes. I'll take the last possession, thank you. Breaking: we came to win.

Does a sold-out Saturday in Athens sound different when the home team is No. 1? Ask Thad. He already has the needle ready. My call: Georgia Bulldogs 34, Missouri Tigers 17.
