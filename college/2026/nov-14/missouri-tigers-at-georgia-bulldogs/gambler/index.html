Missouri at Georgia: Athens Is Sold Out and Still Remembers the Last No. 1 Week

Missouri Tigers (6-3) at Georgia Bulldogs (8-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 14 · Sanford Stadium · ABC

XFacebook

Athens is sold out, according to Georgia's schedule page, and the place has a memory. AP points out that the last time the Bulldogs were No. 1, Auburn came between the hedges four weeks ago and won 27-24. Georgia answered with 27-8 over Florida and 42-7 at Ole Miss, and now the Dawgs are back on top. Kirby Smart is not impressed with that last part. “The ranking means nothing,” he said.

Newest number: ESPN lists DraftKings at Georgia -16.5, total 47.5, checked Nov. 14, 2026, 4:15 AM ET. Prices move, so check again before kickoff. That is the market's price, not a Cover Window recommendation.

Missouri is coming in hot for a different reason. After a 34-20 loss to Texas with a disputed fumble call in the middle of it, athletic director Laird Veatch asked the SEC for a formal review of the officiating, per AP. Eli Drinkwitz drew a hard line on excuses: “We are not victims, and I refuse to give in to being-a-victim mentality.” He also called Georgia “the benchmark for the league right now.”

The best human story is Ahmad Hardy. The Red & Black recounts that he was shot in the upper leg in May in Laurel, Mississippi, and his return was uncertain. He made his season debut at Arkansas on Oct. 31 to a roar from the Missouri crowd, and AP says he is back in a limited role. Jamal Roberts leads the SEC at 119 rushing yards a game. Georgia safety Khalil Barnes: “We know what he is, and we know everything that he can bring to the table.” Missouri has not beaten Georgia since 2013, The Red & Black notes.

Georgia's Gunner Stockton is back in Heisman conversations after four touchdown passes and a rushing score at Ole Miss; he has 20 touchdowns and four interceptions this year. “Explosive player,” lineman Dontrell Glover said. And with officials scrambling for rides Friday, Smart told Paul Finebaum, “We’re wondering how the officials are going to get to where they got to get to,” per Undrafted. Missouri flew in as scheduled.

Injury check: SEC availability report, latest we verified. Georgia (Wednesday report, AJC, posted Nov. 11): DL Joshua Horton questionable, OLB Amaris Williams out; CB Ellis Robinson not listed. Missouri (Thursday night report, On3 Mizzou Today): RB Malae Fonoti (season-ending ACL), OL Josh Atkins and TE Gavin Hoffman among those out.

The published card was set at Georgia −15.5 and 47.5, with a projected 34-17 score. The newest DraftKings number is a point higher on Georgia with the same total. The question for anybody laying that many points is whether Missouri's two-back run game can keep this short, and Georgia's run defense has allowed 83.8 yards a game per AP, best in the SEC.

Sanford Stadium gets a sellout, a top-ranked home team and a running back who fought his way back from a gunshot wound to play football again. Georgia's job is to prove its coach right about the ranking meaning nothing, by playing like it means everything.

The published card stays Georgia Bulldogs −15.5 and Over 47.5 at the numbers it was written against. My final is Georgia Bulldogs 34, Missouri Tigers 17. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Georgia Bulldogs 34, Missouri Tigers 17. Georgia Bulldogs cover the 15.5. Over 47.5.
