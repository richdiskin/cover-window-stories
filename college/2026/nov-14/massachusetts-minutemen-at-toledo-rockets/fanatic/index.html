UMass vs Toledo: Veterans Day Fife and Drums in the Glass City

Massachusetts Minutemen (5-4) at Toledo Rockets (7-2)

7:30 PM ET Wed Nov 11 · Glass Bowl · CBSSN

XFacebook

Toledo gets up early for Veterans Day. Before most of the Glass City has finished its coffee, Savage Arena fills up for UToledo's 22nd annual Veterans Appreciation Breakfast, and by Wednesday night the Massachusetts Minutemen vs Toledo Rockets game at the Glass Bowl turns into Salute to Service night, with Rocco Szczepanski and Abner Kettleworth both saving a seat for somebody who served.

The breakfast runs from 8 to 11 in the morning, free to every current and former service member and their families, with more than 35 military-focused community groups on hand. The Rossford High School S.O.S. club, short for Serving Our Soldiers, greets people at the door and helps them find a seat and a plate. Fourth-graders from Waterville Primary School handle the music. The Toledo Opera sings the national anthem. And for the country's 250th anniversary, the Fort Meigs Historical Fife & Drum Corps presents the colors in replica uniforms.

Abner, a Minuteman to his boots, nearly falls out of his chair when he hears about the fife and drums. "Replica uniforms," he says. "Rocco, they're dressing up as my mascot. This is the warmest welcome I've ever gotten in Ohio."

"They're not dressing up as your mascot," Rocco says. "They're honoring veterans. And your mascot is about to get run over by a Jeep."

That's how Rocco talks about football, like a family that has built engines in Toledo for generations. He grades his team in horsepower, and this year the engine is a big one. Toledo is 7-2, 4-1 in the MAC and second in the conference. The Rockets score 35.4 points a game. John Alan Richter has thrown for 2,304 yards with 18 touchdowns and only 3 interceptions. He threw for 429 yards and 4 scores in a 49-48 overtime thriller against Temple, and 391 more against Western Michigan in a 45-38 win.

"Four hundred and twenty-nine," Rocco says. "In one night. That's not a quarterback. That's a V8."

CJ Miller is the other half of the engine, with 744 rushing yards and 7 touchdowns, and he carried 27 times for 135 yards in the 20-17 escape at Sacramento State last week. Adjatay Dabbs is the deep threat with 40 catches for 592 yards and 7 scores. On defense, Andrew Zock has 9.5 sacks off the end and Julian Fox has 73 tackles.

Abner listens politely, the way a farmer listens to a weather report he doesn't plan to follow. UMass is 5-4 and 2-3 in the MAC, and the Minutemen opened the year by beating Rutgers 37-21 on the road. That one still makes him smile. Then came a tough October: a 38-14 loss to Eastern Michigan, a one-point loss to Miami, an overtime loss at Buffalo, and a 35-16 loss at UConn. Then last Wednesday, UMass beat Ball State 31-30 in overtime, and Abner's voice still cracks a little when he talks about it.

"William Watson III," he says. "Seventeen of twenty-five for 302 yards. Two interceptions all season. You know what we call a quarterback like that in the Pioneer Valley? Patient."

Watson has completed 113 of his 163 throws this year. T.Y. Harding leads the receivers with 397 yards, and Kezion Dia-Johnson had 97 yards on just three catches against Ball State. Elijah Faulkner has 400 rushing yards. The defense runs through Timmy Hinspeter, who has 81 tackles, and Joshua Iseah, who has 3.5 sacks.

"Our defense gives up 22.1 a game," Abner says. "Yours gives up 24.4. Write that down, Rocco."

"I wrote it down," Rocco says. "Then I wrote down the last four times we played you."

That's the part Abner would rather skip. Toledo has won all four meetings since 2021: 45-7, 55-10, 41-24 and 38-23. Abner has no explanation for any of them. He has a theory that it's the lake air. Rocco says it's the horsepower.

This is the night I see. The Glass Bowl is packed with veterans and families, and the crowd is ready early. Watson comes out sharp and keeps UMass in it for a quarter, finding Harding on the sideline. Then Toledo's engine turns over. Miller grinds, Richter hits Dabbs, and Zock gets to Watson at least once in a big spot. The Rockets build a cushion by halftime and keep it. UMass scores in the second half to make it a game for a while, but Toledo pulls away.

Abner tips his cap to the veterans on the way out and quietly plans to plant a tree back home in honor of a good fight. Rocco closes the hood on his imaginary engine and says the Rockets are running just fine.

Salute to Service night at the Glass Bowl ends with the Rockets finishing the job.

My call: Toledo Rockets 34, Massachusetts Minutemen 19.
