Ball State vs Ohio Pick: Bobcats −11.5 and the Over 49.5

Ball State Cardinals (3-7) at Ohio Bobcats (7-3)

7:00 PM ET Tue Nov 17 · Peden Stadium · CBSSN

XFacebook

Ohio has asked everybody at Peden Stadium to wear black on Tuesday night, which fits a Bobcats team still sore from blowing a 17-0 lead at Miami. My Ball State vs Ohio pick is Ohio Bobcats −11.5 plus the Over 49.5, because the MAC leader comes home to Athens looking to take that bruise out on somebody.

Penny Trautman is dressed for the theme. "Black shirt, black boots, and I tapped a brick on Court Street on the way in. We have won seventeen straight in this building. Ball State hasn't won at Peden in fifteen years."

Gus Ponder doesn't flinch. "We beat you 20-14 last year, Penny. You remember. You wore black that day too, emotionally."

That win in Muncie is real, and so is the gap between these teams now. Ohio is 7-3 and 5-1 in the conference, first in the MAC. Ball State is 3-7, giving up 34.1 points a game, and has allowed 45 or more three times. The Cardinals are also playing on a six-day turnaround after a 28-7 loss to Buffalo in the rain, where they drove inside the Buffalo 30 six times and came away empty every time.

Nick Poulos has taken the most throws in each of Ohio's last five games, with 8 touchdowns and 1 interception on the year. Riley Neer has 625 receiving yards, and Michael Molnar has 7 sacks and 94 tackles for a defense that should live in Keldric Luster's backfield. Luster has 1,618 yards, 10 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. My margin of 14 comes from that matchup: an Ohio front that gets home against a team that can't finish drives.

The Over is about Ball State's defense more than Ohio's offense. Eight of the Cardinals' ten games have gone past 49.5 combined points. TJ Horton has 9 rushing touchdowns and Donovan Hamilton caught 11 balls against Buffalo, so Ball State can chip in its share. DraftKings opened Ohio Bobcats −9.5 with a 50.5 total, and the side has climbed two points while the total slipped one. As of 6:03 AM Tuesday, DraftKings has Ohio −11.5, a point above the −10.5 this preview carried Monday night, and the total is still 49.5.

Three of the cards are on Ohio and the Over. The Formula has Ohio 32-20 with Ohio Bobcats −11.5 and goes Over 49.5. Next Man Up has 35-20 and likes both Ohio and the Over. Letdown Watch has 33-18, likes Ohio, and goes Over. In The Trenches has no score this week, sees the two fronts as even, and takes neither side nor total. Follow The Money has no side pick and its total is pending.

Letdown Watch: "Everybody wants a letdown after Oxford. I looked. There isn't one. Ohio comes home angry."

Next Man Up: "First time you've agreed with me all month, Letdown Watch. Bring a black shirt."

My final is Ohio Bobcats 33, Ball State Cardinals 19. That's a 14-point win, two and a half points clear of the number, and 52 points, two and a half past the total. Penny gets to tap her brick. Gus gets a long bus ride back to Indiana with a Garfield comic and his thoughts.

Ohio Bobcats 33, Ball State Cardinals 19. Ohio Bobcats cover the 11.5. Over 49.5.
