Ball State Cardinals vs Ohio Bobcats: Black Out on the Bricks

Ball State Cardinals (3-7) at Ohio Bobcats (7-3)

7:00 PM ET Tue Nov 17 · Peden Stadium · CBSSN

XFacebook

Black is the dress code in Athens on Tuesday night, because Ohio has asked every fan at Frank Solich Field at Peden Stadium to wear it. Ball State Cardinals vs Ohio Bobcats kicks off at 7:00 PM ET, and Penny Trautman has her black boots laced while Gus Ponder shows up from Muncie in a red sweater and a grin, insisting black is just red that stayed up too late.

"Seventeen straight home wins," Penny says. "Longest run in the country. Nobody has walked out of this building happy since before some of these freshmen could ride a bike."

"Somebody has to be eighteen," Gus says. "I volunteer us. Ball State, always helping."

Penny laughs, but she's got a bruise she isn't hiding. Last Tuesday in Oxford, Ohio led Miami 17-0 midway through the second quarter and lost 34-17 in the Battle of the Bricks. The Bobcats didn't score again after building that lead.

"I've been walking the bricks on Court Street all week," she says. "Thinking. Brick by brick. You rebuild a street one stone at a time and you rebuild a season one half at a time."

"I walked to my mailbox," Gus says. "Also thinking."

Here's what Penny is thinking about. Ohio is 7-3, 5-1 in the conference, first in the MAC. Nick Poulos has thrown 8 touchdowns against 1 interception, and he ran 17 times at Miami because he refuses to go down quietly. Riley Neer has 47 catches for 625 yards and 6 touchdowns. On defense, Michael Molnar is everywhere: 94 tackles and 7 sacks, the kind of linebacker who makes the visiting quarterback check over his shoulder in the parking lot.

"Molnar is going to find Keldric Luster," Penny says. "He finds everybody. He found my car keys once."

"Keldric Luster has 1,618 yards and 10 touchdown throws," Gus says, puffing up. "And Donovan Hamilton caught 11 balls against Buffalo last week. Career high. You know who else peaked on a Wednesday?"

"Who?"

"Nobody. Garfield hates Mondays and loves lasagna, and he never once peaked on a Wednesday. Donovan did. That's growth."

The Buffalo game is where Gus has to get honest. Ball State hung with the Bulls for three quarters in the rain, tied 7-7 for most of the night, and the Cardinals drove inside the Buffalo 30 six times. Six. They came away with three missed field goals and three stopped fourth downs, and their only touchdown came on a blocked punt. Then Buffalo broke a 67-yard run late and pulled away 28-7.

"Six trips," Gus says quietly. "If we score on half of them, I'm doing this interview from a parade float."

And now the Cardinals turn around on six days' rest for their third straight midweek game. TJ Horton has 9 rushing touchdowns and ran for 92 yards on just 11 carries against Buffalo, and Sterling Smith leads the defense with 56 tackles. Gus has real football here. He also has a defense that has allowed 34.1 points a game.

"We beat you last year, though," he says. "Twenty to fourteen in Muncie. First MAC game for our coach. You were the champs."

"I remember," Penny says. "I wore black that day too."

History in this building is all Penny's. Ohio has won the last three meetings in Athens, and Ball State's last win at Peden came in 2011. The series is closer overall than she likes to admit, with Ball State ahead 16-13 coming into the 30th meeting, but the last decade has mostly been green.

Here's how Tuesday plays out. Ball State starts fast, the way it did against Buffalo, and Horton gets the Cardinals on the board early enough for Gus to stand on his chair. Then Molnar and the Ohio front settle in. Poulos hits Neer twice down the sideline, Duncan Brune grinds the middle, and the Bobcats spend the second half making Luster throw from his own end. Ball State scores late, Gus celebrates like it's a holiday, and the black-shirted crowd walks home up the hill with an eighteenth straight home win.

Penny taps a brick on the way out and grins. Gus pulls a Garfield comic out of his pocket, reads the Monday strip twice, and decides it's about him.

My call: Ohio Bobcats 33, Ball State Cardinals 19.
