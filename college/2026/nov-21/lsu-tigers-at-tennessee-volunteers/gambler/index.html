LSU vs Tennessee Pick: Vols +1.5 and the Over as Kiffin Returns

LSU Tigers (6-4) at Tennessee Volunteers (8-2)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Neyland Stadium · ABC

XFacebook

Lane Kiffin walks back into Neyland Stadium on Saturday wearing purple and gold, and Knoxville has never needed help remembering his name. The LSU vs Tennessee pick is the home team with the points, Tennessee Volunteers +1.5, plus the Over 45.5, and the projected final is Tennessee Volunteers 26, LSU Tigers 24 when the Vols send off their seniors at 3:30 PM ET.

Earl "Rocky Top" Haskew plucks two notes before he says a word. "Five wins in six home games. DeSean Bishop has 1,163 yards and 13 touchdowns. We went to College Station and won 22-7. We beat Alabama 26-10 in this building. The man can come home all he wants, Cece. The stadium remembers."

Cece Thibodeaux has heard all of it before and sets her drink down. "Sam Leavitt has 2,797 yards and 19 touchdowns. Jackson Harris has 864 receiving yards. Princewill Umanmielen has nine and a half sacks. We lost to Texas by one point, Earl. One. We're angry, and angry Tigers travel well."

Here is the side. Tennessee's defense gives up 14.3 points a game, and the Vols have not needed George MacIntyre to win shootouts; he threw only 17 times at Texas A&M. They win by running Bishop and making the other quarterback beat them. Leavitt threw three interceptions against Texas and has 12 on the year. LSU is 1-2 on the road, with losses at Ole Miss and Kentucky. Getting points with the home team that wins this way feels like a gift.

The market has the Vols right where I want them. DraftKings opened LSU −1.5 and is still LSU −1.5. My 26-24 says Tennessee wins outright, so the hook is a bonus, not a requirement.

The over/under is the bigger story. DraftKings opened at 57.5 and the number has crashed to 45.5. That is twelve points of air let out of the ball, and I think it went too far. LSU scores 35.5 a game and Dilin Jones ran for 125 against Texas. Tennessee scores 31.4. Even two good defenses give up 50 when both offenses can run. My 50 lands over the 45.5.

This is the most united room on the card. The Formula has a 25-25 tie, takes Tennessee +1.5 and likes the Over 45.5. Next Man Up has Tennessee 28, LSU 24, likes the Vols +1.5 and likes the Over. Letdown Watch has Tennessee 26, LSU 23, likes Tennessee +1.5 and likes the Over. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call on this one. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Next Man Up: "The Formula called a tie in a game this loud. That's a whole afternoon of sitting on your hands."

The Formula: "A tie is honest. You needed four points of Tennessee to feel brave, Next Man Up."

The Vol Navy will be tied up along the river by noon. Kiffin's return is the headline. The seniors are the reason the Vols win it.

Tennessee Volunteers 26, LSU Tigers 24. Tennessee Volunteers cover the 1.5. Over 45.5.
