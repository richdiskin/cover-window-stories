UCLA vs Michigan Pick: Wolverines +3.5 and the Over 36.5

UCLA Bruins (10-0) at Michigan Wolverines (5-5)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Michigan Stadium · CBS

XFacebook

Seventeen bells hang above the Kerrytown market in Ann Arbor, and on Saturday morning anyone can climb up and play them for half an hour before heading to Michigan Stadium. The UCLA vs Michigan spread sends me toward the home team in its final home game: Michigan Wolverines +3.5, Over 36.5, with UCLA Bruins 21, Michigan Wolverines 19 at 3:30 PM ET.

Kensington "Kenzie" Rhee has the season storyboarded. "Ten wins, no losses, first in the Big Ten. We gave up 14.1 points a game. Wayne Knight has 845 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns. Ty Dieffenbach threw for 232 against Illinois. Act three, Gus. We're in act three."

Augustin "Gus" Hartwell taps the margin of his notes. "Kenzie, your offense scored 17 at Oregon, 17 against Wisconsin and 22 against Illinois. Our defense allows 17.7 a game. John Henry Daley has six sacks and Troy Bowles has 79 tackles. Close games are our whole personality."

That last part is the case for the points. Michigan has played seven home games, and five were decided by seven points or fewer: 13-12 over Western Michigan, 17-10 over Oklahoma, a 20-19 loss to Iowa, 21-18 over Penn State and a 13-6 overtime loss to Indiana. UCLA's 17-15 win at Oregon shows this team can win ugly on the road too, and ugly games tend to stay inside a field goal and a hook. My 21-19 puts the Bruins up two, inside the 3.5.

There's no hiding Michigan's offense. The Wolverines score 18.3 a game, Tommy Carr went 4 for 12 for 45 yards at Oregon, and Bryce Underwood has six interceptions. That's why my number sits so low. UCLA's defense has allowed 20 points or fewer in seven straight games.

The Over is the tighter call. DraftKings opened the total at 42.5, as shown in ESPN pickcenter, and as of 7:31 AM ET Saturday it has dropped six points to 36.5. At 36.5, six of UCLA's ten games and five of Michigan's ten have gone over. My projection lands on 40, two and a half points above the 37.5, built on Knight's ground game and Savion Hiter's 107 rushing yards at Oregon.

The cards split three ways on the side. The Formula has UCLA 23, Michigan 21, takes Michigan +3.5 and likes the Over 36.5. Letdown Watch has UCLA 21, Michigan 20, likes Michigan +3.5 and likes the Over. Next Man Up has UCLA 20, Michigan 15, lays the 3.5 with UCLA and takes the Under 36.5. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call on this one. Follow The Money has no pick in this game.

Letdown Watch: "Unbeaten team, road trip, rivalry game for Michigan next week. Everybody's looking past somebody."

Next Man Up: "UCLA hasn't allowed more than 20 in seven straight games. I don't see 19 Michigan points in there."

Kerrytown's chime keeps more than two hundred songs written out by number. Michigan only needs one Saturday, the fight song after a close one.

UCLA Bruins 21, Michigan Wolverines 19. Michigan Wolverines cover the 3.5. Over 36.5.
