Miami (OH) vs Kent State Pick: Flashes +9.5, Over 45.5

Miami (OH) RedHawks (6-4) at Kent State Golden Flashes (3-7)

7:00 PM ET Tue Nov 17 · Zoeller Field at Dix Stadium · ESPNU

XFacebook

Three of Miami's six wins this season came by three points or fewer, and that's the whole reason I'm taking the points in Kent on Tuesday night. Kent State Golden Flashes +9.5 is my Miami (OH) vs Kent State pick, paired with Over 45.5, on a Dix Stadium Senior Night when every fan gets a Gold Jacket koozie at the gate.

Ozzie Brannigan already has his koozie. He is holding it like a lost puppy. "Senior Night, a free koozie, and the RedHawks in town. Somebody's going to be crying into a gold jacket. Historically, it's us."

Cordelia Van Doren taps her history sheet. "Historically, Ozzie, Miami owns this series by more than thirty games. I printed it. Laminated it, too."

I care more about the quarterback. Caleb Heavner took the most throws in five straight games, then left the Ohio game hurt in the second quarter, according to Miami's own notes, and freshman Drew Bellisari finished it with 99 throwing yards and a big scramble in a 34-17 comeback. By The Belt reports Chuck Martin called Heavner doubtful, but Miami's preview names no starter and the MAC report is not out, so I'm pricing Bellisari. A freshman on the road in what would be his first start is not the guy I want laying nine and a half.

Miami's margins tell the same story. The RedHawks beat UConn 24-21, UMass 20-19 and Central Michigan 28-27. They also lost to Bowling Green and Buffalo by four and three. Close is all I need.

Kent State is 3-7, so this isn't about the Flashes being good. It's about Dru DeShields throwing for 332 yards at Bowling Green in a 33-28 loss, with Wayne Harris catching 8 balls for 143 yards and 2 touchdowns. Harris has 817 receiving yards on the season. That connection plays at home.

The Over follows from both defenses. Kent State allows 30.8 a game and Miami allows 25.7. Seven of Miami's ten games went past 45.5, and DeShields has thrown 289 times because the Flashes run for so little. DraftKings opened Miami −8.5 with a 45.5 total, and the side has grown by a point since.

The room splits on the side and stacks up on the total. The Formula has Miami 30-20, takes Miami (OH) RedHawks −9.5 and likes the Over 45.5. Next Man Up is with me on Kent State Golden Flashes +9.5 at 30-21 and likes the Over. Letdown Watch has 30-19 on Miami and likes the Over. In The Trenches likes Miami at 27-21 and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Next Man Up: "Thirty to twenty-one, Kent with the points. Letdown Watch has Miami bowl-eligible and still wants them to beat a Senior Night crowd by double digits. Sure."

Letdown Watch: "And you've got a freshman quarterback losing by nine, Next Man Up. You and the Cover Writer can share a koozie."

My final is Miami (OH) RedHawks 29, Kent State Golden Flashes 20. Miami wins by nine, half a point short of the number, and the two teams combine for 49, three and a half past the total. Cordelia gets her winning season, and Ozzie gets a koozie and a close one.

Miami (OH) RedHawks 29, Kent State Golden Flashes 20. Kent State Golden Flashes cover the 9.5. Over 45.5.
