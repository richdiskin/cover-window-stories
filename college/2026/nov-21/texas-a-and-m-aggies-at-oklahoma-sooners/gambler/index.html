Texas A&M at Oklahoma: One Win From a Bowl, and a Rivalry They Want to Rekindle

Market check, Texas A&M at Oklahoma: DraftKings OU -7, total 37.5.

Mike Elko did not sound like a man who had run out of ideas, even after a 7-22 home loss to Tennessee. "We're going to continue every week to do whatever we think is possible to tweak, twist, change and to come up with what we believe is the best opportunity to go out Saturday and have success," the Texas A&M coach said in the school's Oklahoma-week press conference. "You don't ever stop trying to look for solutions." He also said a win Saturday would put the Aggies within a game of bowl eligibility, and that he still sees value in getting there. "I don't see that as a reason to not want to be bowl eligible."

The number as of Nov. 21, 2026, 9:08 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at OU -7, total 37.5. The Cover Window's published pick still reads Texas A&M Aggies +7 and Over 37.5. The market still matches the line the card used. The card's projected final is Oklahoma Sooners 22, Texas A&M Aggies 19 (Total 41). That pick was published against the Nov. 20, 2026, 9:20 PM ET line (Oklahoma Sooners −7, total 37.5), it has not been changed, and this story issues no new one. Prices move, so check yours before kickoff. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

Asked whether TK Norman, Aaron Gregory and Mario Craver would be available this weekend, Elko said in Texas A&M's official Oklahoma-week press conference: "TK will be touch and go all week, we'll have to wait and see kind of where he's at. Obviously, he went down and wasn't able to get back into the game. No reason to believe Mario and Aaron won't be available." That is a dated coach comment, not a Saturday depth chart. Oklahoma's game primer did not publish a matching injury list, and ESPN's game page returned no injury report. Recheck both schools before the 3:45 p.m. ET kickoff.

Saturday is 2:45 p.m. CT at Gaylord Family-Oklahoma Memorial Stadium, on ESPN2, the first meeting of these programs as SEC opponents. Texas A&M's how-to-follow note called it the 32nd meeting all-time and the first since Jan. 4, 2013, when the Aggies beat the Sooners 41-13 in the Cotton Bowl. Elko called it a chance "to kind of rekindle a rivalry with Oklahoma, a team that we haven't played in a long time."

Oklahoma is 6-4 after a 24-17 win over Ole Miss. The Sooners' official game primer said Texas A&M is OU's first opponent since the UTEP opener that was not ranked or receiving votes in at least one of the two major polls, ending a streak of eight such games. The live card on this site also lists Oklahoma as unranked. ESPN's game header showed the Sooners at No. 22. This story stays with the card and flags the network number. Texas A&M is 4-6.

Elko on Oklahoma's defense: "They're very aggressive and how they attack, they're very pressure oriented, they've got really talented players and they give you a lot of multiple looks." The Aggies have to find an answer for that, and for a Sooners team that just beat a ranked Ole Miss club at home.

Oklahoma was supposed to honor its seniors before this game. Back in September, Brent Venables said the team captains asked to move Senior Day to the New Mexico game so it wouldn't distract from Texas A&M on Nov. 21, a game that looked huge on the schedule. Instead the Aggies arrive in Norman at 4-6, and the Texas A&M vs Oklahoma spread has moved four and a half points since it opened. I'm still taking the points: Texas A&M Aggies +7, Over 37.5, with Oklahoma Sooners 22, Texas A&M Aggies 19 at 3:45 PM ET.

Savannah Redbird-Lowe reads it like a barrel pattern. "Three wins in our last four, Colt. We beat Florida 23-7 on the road and Ole Miss 24-17. Owen Heinecke has 82 tackles. John Mateer has 2,153 passing yards and ran for 70 against Ole Miss. Boomer."

Colt Wendeborn answers like a Yell Leader with nothing left to yell. "Our defense gives up 19.0 a game, Savannah, same as yours. Marco Jones has three and a half sacks. Ray Coney has 54 tackles. KJ Edwards scored against Tennessee. We're not dead yet."

Here's the market part first. DraftKings opened Oklahoma −2.5 with a total of 51.5, as shown in ESPN pickcenter, and as of 9:20 PM ET Friday it sits at −7 and 37.5. That's a four-and-a-half-point move on the side and a 14-point drop on the total, and A&M's last month explains it. The Aggies have lost three straight, scoring 20, nine and seven. Marcel Reed went 9 for 22 for 71 yards against Tennessee, and Mike Elko called his own offense "inept" afterward.

So why the points? Because Oklahoma rarely runs away from anybody. The Sooners score 22.3 a game, and Mateer threw for 111 yards against Ole Miss. Three of Oklahoma's last four wins came by eight points or fewer: 27-24 over Texas, 23-15 at Mississippi State and 24-17 over Ole Miss. My 22-19 has Oklahoma by three.

The honest risk: every one of A&M's six losses came by eight points or more. If the offense stays stuck, this ticket dies early.

The Over is the closer call. Seven of A&M's ten games went over 37.5, but the last two stayed under. Six of Oklahoma's ten went over. My 41 is three and a half points above the 37.5.

All four cards with a side are on A&M. The Formula has Oklahoma 24, Texas A&M 20, likes Texas A&M +7 and likes the Over 37.5. In The Trenches has Texas A&M 25, Oklahoma 24, likes Texas A&M +7, and has no total pick. Follow The Money has no side pick and likes the Under 37.5.

Next Man Up: "Eighteen to fourteen. Neither offense has earned more than that."

The seniors already got their flowers in September. Saturday is just football, and in a game this low, a full touchdown is a lot of football.
