Texas A&M vs Oklahoma Pick: Aggies +7.5 and the Over 39.5

Texas A&M Aggies (4-6) at Oklahoma Sooners (6-4)

3:45 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Memorial Stadium (Norman, OK) · ESPN2

XFacebook

Oklahoma was supposed to honor its seniors before this game. Back in September, Brent Venables said the team captains asked to move Senior Day to the New Mexico game so it wouldn't distract from Texas A&M on Nov. 21, a game that looked huge on the schedule. Instead the Aggies arrive in Norman at 4-6, and the Texas A&M vs Oklahoma spread has moved five points since it opened. I'm still taking the points: Texas A&M Aggies +7.5, Over 39.5, with Oklahoma Sooners 22, Texas A&M Aggies 19 at 3:45 PM ET.

Savannah Redbird-Lowe reads it like a barrel pattern. "Three wins in our last four, Colt. We beat Florida 23-7 on the road and Ole Miss 24-17. Owen Heinecke has 82 tackles. John Mateer has 2,153 passing yards and ran for 70 against Ole Miss. Boomer."

Colt Wendeborn answers like a Yell Leader with nothing left to yell. "Our defense gives up 19.0 a game, Savannah, same as yours. Marco Jones has three and a half sacks. Ray Coney has 54 tackles. KJ Edwards scored against Tennessee. We're not dead yet."

Here's the market part first. DraftKings opened Oklahoma −2.5 with a total of 51.5, as shown in ESPN pickcenter, and it now sits at −7.5 and 39.5. That's a five-point move on the side and a 12-point drop on the total, and A&M's last month explains it. The Aggies have lost three straight, scoring 20, nine and seven. Marcel Reed went 9 for 22 for 71 yards against Tennessee, and Mike Elko called his own offense "inept" afterward.

So why the points? Because Oklahoma rarely runs away from anybody. The Sooners score 22.3 a game, and Mateer threw for 111 yards against Ole Miss. Three of Oklahoma's last four wins came by eight points or fewer: 27-24 over Texas, 23-15 at Mississippi State and 24-17 over Ole Miss. My 22-19 has Oklahoma by three.

The honest risk: every one of A&M's six losses came by eight points or more. If the offense stays stuck, this ticket dies early.

The Over is the closer call. Seven of A&M's ten games went over 39.5, but the last two stayed under. Five of Oklahoma's ten went over. My 41 is a point and a half above the 39.5.

All four cards with a side are on A&M. The Formula has Oklahoma 24, Texas A&M 20, likes Texas A&M +7.5 and likes the Over 39.5. Letdown Watch has Oklahoma 23, Texas A&M 19, likes the Aggies +7.5 and goes Over. Next Man Up has Oklahoma 18, Texas A&M 14, likes the Aggies +7.5 and likes the Under. In The Trenches has Texas A&M 25, Oklahoma 24, likes Texas A&M +7.5, and has no total pick. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Next Man Up: "Eighteen to fourteen. Neither offense has earned more than that."

The seniors already got their flowers in September. Saturday is just football, and in a game this low, a touchdown and a hook is a lot of football.

Oklahoma Sooners 22, Texas A&M Aggies 19. Texas A&M Aggies cover the 7.5. Over 39.5.
