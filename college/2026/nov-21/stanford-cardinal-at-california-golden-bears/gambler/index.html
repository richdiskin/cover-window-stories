Stanford vs California Pick: Golden Bears +2.5, Under 55.5

Stanford Cardinal (5-5) at California Golden Bears (3-7)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · California Memorial Stadium · ACC Network

XFacebook

The Campanile, Sather Gate, Doe Library and Wheeler Hall are lit blue and gold every night this week until two in the morning, and the one hundred twenty-ninth Big Game kicks off at 4:30 p.m. Pacific in Berkeley. The Stanford vs California spread opened Stanford −1.5 at DraftKings and has moved a point. I'm taking the home team anyway: California Golden Bears +2.5 and Under 55.5, with California Golden Bears 28, Stanford Cardinal 26 as the final.

Iggy Yamamoto-Ross, the Stanford founder of three failed apps who now pitches Cardinal football like a funding round, has his deck open. "Micah Ford: 27 carries, 149 yards, three touchdowns at Virginia Tech. Five wins. We're scaling." Quentin Oyelaran-Fitch, the Berkeley philosophy PhD who protests bad play calls with hand-lettered signs, holds one up that says "LOOK AT THE ROAD GAMES."

He has a point. Away from home, Stanford lost 35-7 at Duke, 57-3 at Wake Forest, 58-7 at Notre Dame and 52-24 at Louisville before winning 40-22 at Virginia Tech last week. Stanford gives up 35.8 points a game. Cal has lost four straight, and the offense scored 7, 20, 10 and 7 in those games, which is why this number moved. But the Bears played Stanford dead even over the last ten meetings, five wins apiece, and they won the last one in Berkeley 24-21 in 2024. Jaron-Keawe Sagapolutele threw for 413 yards against Wagner and 271 against UCLA, and Chase Hendricks has 65 catches. In a game where neither defense has stopped much, getting points at home in a rivalry game is the side I want. I have Cal by two.

The Under 55.5 is built on Cal's offense staying grounded. Six of Cal's ten games stayed under 55.5, and the Bears have topped 25 points just twice. Stanford's games run hotter, going over in seven of ten, but three of those overs came from Stanford getting blown out. Davis Warren has 14 touchdown throws and three interceptions, and he's been careful. The total opened at 55.5, went up a point and is back at 55.5 as of 9:20 PM ET Friday. My 54 combined points sit under it.

The risk is the Stanford ground game. Ford ran over Virginia Tech, and if he does it again, Stanford wins by ten and the Over comes in too.

The three cards with scores are all close. The Formula has Cal 27-26, likes California +2.5 and likes the Under 55.5. Next Man Up has Cal 29-27, likes California +2.5 and has flipped to the Over. Letdown Watch has Stanford 27-26 but still likes California +2.5, and it likes the Under. In The Trenches has no side this week, because the two running games pull it in opposite directions, and it doesn't take a total. Follow The Money has no pick in this game.

Letdown Watch, to the room: "I've got Stanford winning and I'm still on Cal. Explain that to my mother." The Formula: "Your mother understands two and a half points."

Iggy pivots to the run game, Quentin waves his sign, and the Bears get just enough to send the campus lights out on a happy note.

California Golden Bears 28, Stanford Cardinal 26. California Golden Bears cover the 2.5. Under 55.5.
