NC State vs Florida State: Turquoise Debut on Homecoming at Doak

NC State Wolfpack (4-6) at Florida State Seminoles (5-5)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Doak Campbell Stadium · ACC Network

GameDay Report

NC State at Florida State: A New Head Coach Named, Then a Trip to Tallahassee

NC State will walk into Doak Campbell Stadium a week after the school remade the letterhead. On Nov. 13, athletics director Boo Corrigan named former Wolfpack quarterback Philip Rivers the head coach, a five-year agreement, with a formal introduction on Nov. 16. On Nov. 16, Rivers and Corrigan named Jatavis Sanders general manager. On Nov. 18, Rivers named Jason Michael offensive coordinator. The official releases do not say who is calling Saturday's game in Tallahassee, and this story will not invent a sideline. What they do say is that the program has a new head coach, a new GM and a new coordinator, and that the team is still 4-6 after a 31-34 home loss to Syracuse in which the Wolfpack gave up a fourth-quarter lead.

Florida State is 5-5 after a 16-10 win at Pittsburgh, a second straight road win in the school's own recap headline. The Seminoles' Nov. 20 story on homecoming and Seminole Heritage Game activations redirected to a general gameday page, so this story will not invent a heritage script the useful text did not give us. The date is still 3:30 p.m. on ACC Network, in Tallahassee.

CJ Bailey has 2,289 passing yards, 14 touchdowns and five interceptions for the Wolfpack. Jayden Scott has 504 rushing yards. Ashton Daniels has 2,309 passing yards, 14 scores and 12 interceptions for Florida State; Ousmane Kromah leads the Seminoles' rush in the facts we can source. The AccuWeather note on ESPN's game page had a 20 percent rain chance and a 14-mph gust, not a storm, just a November afternoon in Tallahassee.

A 5-5 home team coming off a tight road win, and a 4-6 visitor whose week was about press conferences: that is the ACC Network window. Rivers, in his introductory remarks, said, "In many ways, I feel like I'm coming home." Saturday is still a road game in a stadium that does not care about last week's announcements.

Turquoise is coming to Doak Campbell Stadium. For the first time in program history, Florida State will wear turquoise uniforms in a real game, and the NC State Wolfpack vs Florida State Seminoles matchup is the Seminole Heritage game and Homecoming all at once, which has Gianna Ravello talking like she's whipping votes and Ruff Pegram talking like he brought his own tailgate tent into the capitol.

"Zae Thomas said it best," Gianna says. "He's a member of the Seminole Tribe, and he called turquoise a color with deep meaning. Our heritage games started in 2023, and we have never lost one. Three for three. Add Homecoming and FSU175. Ruff, this is the most important Saturday in Tallahassee since, well, since last week."

Ruff, who has never met a parking lot he could not turn into a barbecue, waves a pair of tongs. "We've beaten y'all six times in the last eight meetings, Gianna. Last year, 21-11. I don't care what color the jerseys are. I care what color the scoreboard is."

The Seminoles have had a fall that felt like three seasons stacked on top of each other. Florida State started 1-2, beat Central Arkansas and Virginia, then lost three straight, including an overtime heartbreak at Miami. On Nov. 1, the school made a leadership change and named Rick Stockstill interim head coach. Stockstill was one of Bobby Bowden's first quarterbacks, and the team has won both games since, 31-24 at Boston College and 16-10 at Pitt. The Seminoles are 5-5. One more win makes them bowl eligible.

"Coach Stockstill has these guys playing like they remember who they are," Gianna says. "Tough, simple and loud."

The engine is Ashton Daniels, who has thrown for 2,309 yards and 14 touchdowns. He threw for 377 at Alabama and 350 against Clemson. Ousmane Kromah leads the runners with 609 yards, and Micahi Danzy is the deep threat, with 511 receiving yards. On defense, Rylan Kennedy has six sacks and Chris Jones has 71 tackles.

Ruff answers with his own list, loud enough for three tailgates over. CJ Bailey has thrown for 2,289 yards with 14 touchdowns and just five interceptions. Jayden Scott ran for 127 yards against Syracuse last week. Keenan Jackson has six touchdown catches. Popo Aguirre has 84 tackles at linebacker, and Elijah Satchell has five sacks.

"Bailey doesn't throw it to the other team," Ruff says. "That's the whole game plan. Daniels has thrown twelve. We just have to be standing in the right place."

It's a fair shot, and Gianna knows it. Daniels has thrown 12 interceptions, including three against Alabama and three against Clemson. A Florida State team that hands the ball away is a team that has lost games it could have won.

But NC State has its own weight to carry. The Wolfpack are 4-6, and they have not won a road game this season: Virginia, Vanderbilt and Stanford all sent them home with losses. Two weeks ago Duke beat them 41-3 in Raleigh. Last week Syracuse won 34-31.

"The road has been rough," Ruff admits. "But you know what's rougher? Tallahassee in November thinking it already won."

The all-time series belongs to Florida State, 27-17, and the Seminoles are 16-6 at home against the Wolfpack. Ruff, of course, prefers the recent stuff, including the 47-28 win in 2018 that he describes in loving detail at every tailgate.

Here's how it goes. The turquoise comes out of the tunnel and Doak goes wild. Florida State scores early when Daniels hits Danzy deep. NC State answers with Scott running hard and Bailey moving the chains, and the Wolfpack section starts to believe. Kennedy gets a big sack before halftime that kills a Pack drive. In the second half, Kromah takes over, and the Seminoles pull away with two long scoring drives. Bailey gets one more late, but the Homecoming crowd gets what it came for: a bowl-eligible team in brand-new colors.

Ruff packs up his tongs. "Nice jerseys," he says. "Grill's still warm next year."

"Bring the grill back next year," Gianna says. "We'll still be in turquoise."

My call: Florida State Seminoles 35, NC State Wolfpack 23.
