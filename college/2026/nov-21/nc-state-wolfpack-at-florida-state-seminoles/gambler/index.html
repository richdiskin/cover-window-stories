NC State vs Florida State Pick: Seminoles −10.5, Over 56.5

NC State Wolfpack (4-6) at Florida State Seminoles (5-5)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Doak Campbell Stadium · ACC Network

XFacebook

Florida State has never worn turquoise in a real football game until Saturday. The Seminole Heritage uniforms come out for Homecoming against NC State at Doak Campbell Stadium, and the NC State vs Florida State spread gets my vote for the home team: Florida State Seminoles −10.5, Over 56.5, with Florida State Seminoles 35, NC State Wolfpack 23 at 3:30 PM ET.

Gianna Ravello works the room like it's the last week of session. "Rick Stockstill has been interim head coach for two games, and we've won both, 31-24 at Boston College and 16-10 at Pitt. One more and we're bowl eligible. Ousmane Kromah has 609 rushing yards. Rylan Kennedy has six sacks. The votes are counted, Ruff."

Rutherford "Ruff" Pegram lights the grill out of habit. "We've won six of the eight meetings since 2016, Gianna, including 21-11 last year. CJ Bailey has 2,289 throwing yards and only five interceptions. Jayden Scott ran for 127 against Syracuse. The Pack loves a road upset."

The road is where my side starts. NC State is 0-3 away from home this year, losing by 26 at Virginia, by four at Vanderbilt and by three at Stanford. The Wolfpack also got crushed 41-3 by Duke at home two weeks ago and then lost to Syracuse 34-31. The defense allows 28.2 points a game. Florida State's offense scores 29.2, and Ashton Daniels has thrown for 2,309 yards. My 35-23 is a 12-point win, a point and a half past the 10.5.

DraftKings opened Florida State −10, and as of 9:20 PM ET Friday the line is −10.5. The over/under opened at 54.5 and has climbed two points to 56.5.

The Over has the strongest numbers in this story. Six of NC State's ten games have gone over 56.5, including 73-0 over Richmond, 41-31 over App State and 31-28 over Louisville. Four of Florida State's ten went over too. Daniels has 12 interceptions, and short fields add points for both sides. Micahi Danzy has 511 receiving yards and Keenan Jackson has six touchdown catches for NC State. My 58 clears the 56.5.

The room splits on the side and mostly backs the Over. The Formula has Florida State 32, NC State 23, flips to NC State +10.5 and goes Under 56.5. Letdown Watch has Florida State 33, NC State 22, lays the 9.5 it took Monday and keeps the Over it took at 54.5. Next Man Up has Florida State 35, NC State 26, takes NC State +10.5 and likes the Over. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call on this one. Follow The Money likes Florida State Seminoles −10.5 and the Over 56.5.

Next Man Up: "Nine points of margin is Florida State winning and the Pack still getting there late. That's college football in November."

The Formula: "And Under is me remembering a team that scored three against Duke. Somebody here has to keep receipts."

Stockstill was one of Bobby Bowden's first quarterbacks. Saturday he gets a turquoise Homecoming and a chance to make this a bowl team again.

Florida State Seminoles 35, NC State Wolfpack 23. Florida State Seminoles cover the 10.5. Over 56.5.
