Clemson Tigers vs Duke Blue Devils: Friday Night Gridlock in Durham

Clemson Tigers (6-4) at Duke Blue Devils (7-3)

7:30 PM ET Fri Nov 20 · Wallace Wade Stadium · ESPN

XFacebook

If you work on Duke's campus, leave early on Friday, because Duke University Road shuts down to the public as kickoff approaches and the university has warned of heavy traffic starting four hours before the game. Clemson Tigers vs Duke Blue Devils kicks off at 7:30 PM ET at Brooks Field at Wallace Wade Stadium, and Harrison Wexley-Moore IV plans to stroll past the gridlock with a pocket square while Dixie-Lynn Pruitt rolls up from the Upstate in an orange convertible that has opinions about Durham parking.

"Students in the Blue Zone have to move their cars by 4:30," Harrison says. "I read the memo. Twice. I annotated it."

"Of course you did," Dixie-Lynn says. "I brought a cooler and a folding chair and the patience of a woman who has sat through a lot of Death Valley traffic. Durham doesn't scare me."

Harrison straightens his tie. He has reason to feel good. Duke is 7-3, and the Blue Devils have been punishing people at home and away. They beat Boston College 47-2 and then went to Raleigh and beat NC State 41-3 the next week. The defense gives up 15.3 points a game, the kind of figure Harrison likes to say slowly so everybody can enjoy it.

"And last year," he says. "Forty-six to forty-five. In Clemson. On your grass."

Dixie-Lynn takes a long breath. "You were going to bring that up eventually."

"Eventually was ninety seconds ago."

There's no getting around it: that one stung in the Upstate, and Dixie-Lynn has been stewing on it for a full year. But her bigger worry is right now. Clemson is 6-4, and last week the Tigers lost 20-6 at home to Georgia Tech. Christopher Vizzina took most of the throws in that game after Tait Reynolds handled the job from mid-September through the win at Syracuse. Six points at home, in November, with the whole orange crowd watching.

"I put on more orange," Dixie-Lynn says. "For strength. It didn't help. So I'm wearing more tonight."

"How much more orange can a person wear?"

"Watch me."

Clemson still has a defense worth respecting. Will Heldt has 7 sacks off the end, and Corey Myrick leads the team with 77 tackles from safety. Gideon Davidson runs hard, with 528 yards and 5 touchdowns, and Bryant Wesco Jr. has 47 catches. The Tigers won at Florida State 36-24 on Halloween, and that version of Clemson could show up anywhere.

"That's my team," Dixie-Lynn says. "The one from Tallahassee. If she shows up in Durham, you're in trouble."

"She'll be stuck on Duke University Road," Harrison says. "It's closed."

The trouble for Clemson is Nate Sheppard. Duke's running back has 1,320 rushing yards and 19 touchdowns, and he's the face on the university's own traffic alert, celebrating a score against Boston College. Last week at Miami he ran for 131 yards and caught 12 balls, which is the kind of night that makes defensive coordinators stare at the ceiling. Walker Eget has thrown for 2,044 yards with 11 touchdowns and just 4 interceptions, and Kevin O'Connor leads the defense with 4.5 sacks.

"Sheppard isn't just a running back," Harrison says. "He's a logistics problem. Like the traffic. You can't route around him."

"Will Heldt is going to route right through your blockers, Harrison."

"Our blockers have perfect grades in footwork, Dixie-Lynn. Figuratively. Also possibly literally. I haven't checked."

Here's how Friday goes. The traffic eases once the game starts, just like the memo promised, and Clemson comes out swinging with Davidson running hard and Heldt making an early sack to send the orange section into a frenzy. Then Duke settles in. Sheppard breaks one in the second quarter, then another, and Eget keeps the chains moving without taking risks. The Blue Devils' defense does what it has done all year, swarming every Clemson throw, and the Tigers have to scratch out a field goal. Clemson gets a late touchdown to give Dixie-Lynn something to wave her pompoms about, but Duke wins comfortably and walks out into a quiet, traffic-free night.

Harrison lists every Duke accomplishment of the evening in alphabetical order, starting with "awareness, situational." Dixie-Lynn adds one more orange scarf for the drive home and says the Upstate will remember this.

My call: Duke Blue Devils 28, Clemson Tigers 10.
