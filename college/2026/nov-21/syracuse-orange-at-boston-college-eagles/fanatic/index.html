Syracuse vs Boston College: Jay McGillis Senior Day

Syracuse Orange (5-5) at Boston College Eagles (4-6)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Alumni Stadium (Chestnut Hill, MA) · ACC Network

XFacebook

Jay McGillis was Boston College's strong safety in 1991, and on Saturday his name sits on the Syracuse vs Boston College game at Alumni Stadium, the second Jay McGillis Memorial Senior Day. Boston College insider Siobhan Dunleavy says that makes the home finale bigger than a record, while Syracuse insider Nico Calabrese says Steve Angeli and an Orange team one win from bowl eligibility are coming to Chestnut Hill with every reason in the world to spoil it.

The story behind the name is why this one lands differently on the Heights. McGillis played for Tom Coughlin, was diagnosed with leukemia during that 1991 season and died in the summer of 1992 after chemotherapy and a bone marrow transplant from his older brother. Coughlin started the Jay Fund in 1996 to help families facing childhood cancer, and a memorial scholarship in McGillis' name still goes to a senior defensive back every year.

Siobhan: Nico, I'll keep the courtroom closed for one minute. Saturday our seniors get honored under the name of a teammate who never got his own senior day. Our guys know that story. They'll play like it.

Nico: I respect that completely, Siobhan. Nobody from Syracuse is going to say a word against it. We'll just try to ruin your afternoon the old-fashioned way, with a receiver named Umari and a few of his friends.

Then the courtroom reopens, because these two have been arguing about this rivalry since long before this week.

Siobhan: Exhibit A. The Eagles have won the last two meetings, 37-31 here in 2024 and 34-12 at your place last November.

Nico: Objection. Different year, different Orange. Exhibit B. Steve Angeli threw for 272 yards at NC State last Saturday, we won 34-31, and Calvin Russell III caught 17 balls. Seventeen, Siobhan. That's not a box score. That's a blizzard. Two feet of snow, minimum.

Seventeen catches for 137 yards in one game is the kind of thing an opposing defensive coordinator stares at on a Sunday night. And Russell isn't even Syracuse's main target. That's Umari Hatcher, who has 66 catches for 1,045 yards and eight touchdowns. When a defense chases one of them, the other wanders open.

Siobhan: Anthony Palano has 87 tackles. He has been in on more plays than some of our fans. I'll put him on whoever's catching it and let the jury decide.

Nico: Keyshawn Johnson has eight sacks for us. Your quarterback had a rough day at South Bend.

That's the hard part for Boston College. Mason McKenzie is the Eagles' leading thrower and leading rusher, with 686 yards on the ground, but last week at Notre Dame he went 8 of 21 for 51 yards in a 41-7 loss. The week before, Florida State won 31-24 at Alumni Stadium. The week before that, Duke won 47-2. Boston College has dropped three straight and has been searching for its offense since the leaves turned.

Siobhan: Overruled. Mason ran our offense at Georgia Tech and we scored 49. When he gets outside the pocket, he becomes a running back with a better haircut.

Nico: That's fair. I've seen it, and I didn't enjoy it.

Syracuse comes in with the momentum. The Orange have won three of their last four, including a 31-30 overtime win at North Carolina and last week's 34-31 win at NC State. Angeli has been the steady hand since he got the job back in late October, and Tylik Hill gives the Orange 591 rushing yards to keep defenses honest.

Nico: We're 5-5, Siobhan. One more win and we're going bowling. I would drive to Chestnut Hill in a whiteout for that.

Siobhan: Bring a shovel. And bring a coat for your secondary, because Demetrius Ballard has four and a half sacks and he plays like the wind off the reservoir.

Even the closing arguments sound a little different this week. Both of them come back, without being asked, to the man whose name is on the game.

Siobhan: Whatever happens, I want our seniors to walk off that field knowing they honored Jay McGillis with how hard they played.

Nico: That I can promise you. They'll have to play hard, because we will.

The verdict is a two-point game on the Heights, with the home side honoring its seniors and the man in whose name they play by holding on at the end.

My call: Boston College Eagles 28, Syracuse Orange 26.
