UTSA at UAB: Senior Day and a Shot at a Perfect Home Year

UTSA Roadrunners (8-2) at UAB Blazers (6-4)

8:00 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Protective Stadium · ESPNU

GameDay Report

UTSA at UAB: A Perfect Home Season Still Intact, and an 8-2 Visitor

UAB's official preview said the Blazers are hosting UTSA at Protective Stadium for Senior Night, with bowl eligibility already secured and an undefeated home record at stake. UAB is 6-4 and 5-0 at Protective Stadium this season after a 36-10 win at Temple that, the school said, was the program's first bowl-eligible season since 2022. The Blazers also said they remain in contention for a berth in the American Conference Championship Game with two regular-season games left. Kickoff is 8 p.m. ET on ESPNU.

UTSA is 8-2 after a 35-16 win against North Texas. Owen McCown has 2,101 passing yards, 13 touchdowns and five interceptions. Will Henderson III has 752 rushing yards. UAB's Ryder Burton has 1,607 passing yards, six touchdowns and nine interceptions. Rod Robinson II has 753 rushing yards. The Roadrunners' official note framed Saturday as a night road bout for an 8-2 team. The Blazers framed it as a home finale with a perfect home season still intact and, they said, two regular-season games left in a season that still includes championship-game talk.

A 5-0 home record, a Senior Night, and a visitor coming off a 35-16 win: that is Saturday night in Birmingham. If Henderson runs like 752 yards of work, the Blazers will have to tackle to keep the home slate clean. If Robinson keeps moving the ball the way 753 yards suggest, Protective Stadium will have to finish a Senior Night against an 8-2 visitor, not a footnote.

Ozella Birchfield has written a farewell verse for UAB's seniors, and on Saturday at Protective Stadium she plans to deliver it before kickoff to anyone who will stand still. Marisol Ybarra, who came up from San Antonio with a sash full of fiesta medals clinking at her hip, has agreed to be the first audience, on the condition that it rhymes.

Ozella: It's blank verse.

Marisol: So no rhymes.

Ozella: The seniors are the rhyme. The verse is just the frame.

Marisol: I'm going to need a medal for sitting through this.

It's a big Saturday in Birmingham. This is UAB's home finale, Senior Day, and the Blazers come in 5-0 at Protective Stadium, with a chance to finish unbeaten at home for the first time since 2019 and for the first time since moving into this building in 2021. The program will also recognize its new Hall of Fame class during the game, including former Blazer football standout Rodregis Brooks, after the class is inducted Friday night at The Florentine downtown.

Ozella: Friday the old guard gets its plaques. Saturday the seniors take their bow. Two curtain calls in two nights.

Marisol: Two parades. I approve.

UAB is 6-4 and bowl eligible for the first time since 2022 after a 36-10 win at Temple. Rod Robinson II carried 22 times for 202 yards and three touchdowns in that one, and he leads the team with 753 rushing yards. Ryder Burton has thrown for 1,607 yards, and CJ Smith leads the receivers with 450. The defense is the strength. Marlin Dean has 11.5 sacks, and Muaaz Byard has 87 tackles.

Ozella: Dean doth hunt the quarterback as hounds the hare.

Marisol: That one almost rhymed.

Ozella: Almost is the hardest word in poetry.

UTSA arrives 8-2 and first in the American, so the Roadrunners have plenty on the table too. They've won some close ones, beating Rice 16-14 on the road and Navy 38-34 at home. The only real blemish lately was a 60-29 loss at Florida Atlantic, and they bounced back last week by beating North Texas 35-16. A'Marion Peterson ran for 179 yards and four touchdowns in that game. Will Henderson III leads the team with 752 rushing yards, and Owen McCown has thrown for 2,101 yards and 13 touchdowns. Darion White leads the defense with 67 tackles, and Owen Pewee has four sacks.

Marisol: Peterson ran like the last float in the parade. Everybody waits for it and it's the best one.

Ozella: And McCown?

Marisol: McCown is the marching band. Steady, keeps everyone in step.

These two have split the last seven meetings, four for UAB and three for UTSA, but the Roadrunners won the last three, including 44-38 in 2022 and 41-20 in 2023.

Ozella: The last three were a tragedy in three acts.

Marisol: For you. For me it was a fiesta in three nights.

Here's what the game could turn on: UAB has to protect Burton, who has thrown nine interceptions, and let Robinson grind out yards. UTSA has to deal with Dean, who can take over a game by himself. If the Blazers keep the ball out of trouble and win on the ground, they have a real chance to finish 6-0 at home. If McCown gets time, UTSA can pull away.

The way it plays out: Robinson scores on UAB's opening drive and the crowd gets loud. UTSA answers with Henderson and Peterson, and the two teams trade scores into the fourth quarter. A late UTSA field goal makes the difference, and UAB's last drive ends near midfield.

Marisol: The parade comes back around, but this time it's ours.

Ozella: Then I'll save the final stanza for the seniors. They earned it whether the clock is kind or not.

Ozella clears her throat and reads the closing verse to the empty stands after everyone has gone home, and for once Marisol stays to listen.

My call: UTSA Roadrunners 29, UAB Blazers 25.
