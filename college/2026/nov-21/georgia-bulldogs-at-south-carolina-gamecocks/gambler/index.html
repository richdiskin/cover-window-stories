Georgia vs South Carolina Pick: Gamecocks +16.5, Over 51.5

Georgia Bulldogs (9-1) at South Carolina Gamecocks (6-4)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Williams-Brice Stadium · ABC

XFacebook

South Carolina's last home game of the season kicks off at 7:30 p.m. under the lights at Williams-Brice, and the price on the Georgia vs South Carolina spread has moved six points since DraftKings opened Georgia −10.5. One ugly night at Arkansas did that. I'm taking the points: South Carolina Gamecocks +16.5 and Over 51.5, with Georgia Bulldogs 35, South Carolina Gamecocks 20 as the final.

Beau Cheraw, the Gamecock snare drummer who hums the entrance music in the press box, isn't hiding from the Arkansas tape. "Cutter Woods threw it 43 times, completed 24, and three went to the other team. We scored 6. But this team won at Florida 38-19 and at Oklahoma 42-25." Thad Okafor-Brandt, the indie-rock DJ who calls Georgia's blitzes like a set list, reads his own liner notes. "Gunner Stockton: 21 touchdown throws, four interceptions. We allow 14.7 points a game. Chris Cole has 64 tackles and five sacks."

Georgia is the better team, and nobody in Columbia is arguing about that. The question is whether 16.5 is too much on the road at night. Two of South Carolina's home losses came by seven to Mississippi State and by one in overtime to Kentucky, and at home it beat Tennessee 27-24 and Texas A&M 17-9. Georgia's toughest SEC road trip was a 20-17 win at Alabama, and Auburn beat the Bulldogs 27-24 in Athens. The move from 10.5 to 16.5 says the market has written off the Gamecocks after one week. LaNorris Sellers has 637 rushing yards and seven rushing touchdowns, and if he's under center the backdoor stays open late. I have Georgia by 15, inside the number.

The Over 51.5 has history on both sides. Seven of South Carolina's ten games went over it, and six of Georgia's did. Georgia scored 48 on Missouri and 42 at Ole Miss in its last two, and the Bulldogs score 41.8 points a game. South Carolina scores 31.8. The total only moved a point from the open of 50.5, and my 55 combined points sit over it.

The risk is that the Arkansas offense shows up again. If South Carolina's passing game throws three more interceptions, Georgia wins 38-6 and both of those numbers look silly.

Four cards have scores, and all four have the Bulldogs winning big. The Formula has Georgia 33-21, likes South Carolina +16.5 and likes the Over 51.5. Next Man Up, at 36-23, also likes South Carolina +16.5 and the Over. Letdown Watch has Georgia 36-17 and likes Georgia −16.5, while taking the Over. In The Trenches has no side this week, because its run-game reads point at different teams, and it doesn't take a total. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Letdown Watch, to the room: "Georgia beat Missouri by 27. You two think they'll take the night off?" The Formula: "I think the home team in a final home game shows up. That's all."

Thad drops the needle, Beau starts humming, and the Gamecocks hang around long enough to make a lot of people nervous.

Georgia Bulldogs 35, South Carolina Gamecocks 20. South Carolina Gamecocks cover the 16.5. Over 51.5.
