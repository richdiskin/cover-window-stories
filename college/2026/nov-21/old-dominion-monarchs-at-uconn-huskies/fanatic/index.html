Old Dominion vs UConn: Bowl Ticket at Stake on Senior Day

Old Dominion Monarchs (3-7) at UConn Huskies (5-5)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Pratt & Whitney Stadium · CBSSN

XFacebook

Grandma's Attic opens at 9 a.m. Saturday on Main Street in East Hartford, and Jo Ferrante plans to be the first one through the door. The First Congregational Church of East Hartford is holding its Holiday Village Fair, with a Christmas Boutique, homemade cookies and candy, a free card-making booth for kids and lunch at Chuck's Diner, and Jo has the whole morning mapped out like a fast break.

"Attic first, cookies second, Chuck's Diner third," she says. "Then I drive over to the stadium for Senior Day and watch us go bowling. That's a perfect Saturday in Connecticut."

Winnie Pocklington, Norfolk native and summer harbor-tour guide, has come north in a navy peacoat and a paper crown. "A church fair," she says, delighted. "How civilized. In Norfolk, we'd have a ship in the background. Hear ye, hear ye, the Monarchs have arrived."

So the Old Dominion Monarchs vs UConn Huskies game becomes a holiday morning in East Hartford followed by the biggest home game of UConn's season at Pratt & Whitney Stadium. The Huskies are 5-5. One more win, and they're bowl eligible.

Jo has a reason to feel good. Last Saturday, UConn beat James Madison 46-22 in maybe its most complete game of the year. Kalieb Osborne completed 16 of 20 passes for 274 yards and three touchdowns, Cyncir Bowers ran for 123 yards and scored three times, and Ky "Flash" Wilson returned a kickoff 100 yards for a touchdown. UConn scored on seven of its ten possessions.

"Seven of ten," Jo says. "In my point guard days we called that a good night. On a football field that's a clinic."

On the year, Osborne has thrown for 2,376 yards with 19 touchdowns and only four interceptions. Bowers has 762 rushing yards and ten touchdowns. Shamar Porter caught four balls for 135 yards against James Madison, including a 67-yard touchdown. On defense, linebacker Hayden Pegg leads the team with 79 tackles, and Joshua Mickens has four sacks.

"Know them by number," Jo says. "Seven is Osborne. Twenty-three is Bowers. Five is Porter. Write it down, Winnie."

Winnie bows slightly. "Noted for the royal record. Now allow me."

Old Dominion is 3-7, but the Monarchs have played some tough road games well. They won 10-3 at App State in October and 21-8 at Coastal Carolina on Nov. 7. They've also had an extra week to get ready, because they didn't play last weekend. Javen Gibson has five sacks. Safety Lucas Struck has 74 tackles. Devin Roche leads the runners with 441 yards, and Josh Rodriguez leads the receivers with 436.

"The defense travels," Winnie says. "Ask App State. Ask Coastal. A rested Monarch is a dangerous Monarch."

The offense is the harder part of her speech. Old Dominion scores 16.8 points a game, and three different quarterbacks have led the team in throws this season: Quinn Henicle, Ryan Huff and Jaxon Potter. Potter has thrown the most in each of the last three games, and he threw for 329 yards against Marshall.

"We are in a rebuilding season," Winnie admits, adjusting the crown. "Even castles need renovating."

"Our defense sacked James Madison three times last week," Jo says. "Bring the renovation crew."

Here's how it goes. Jo makes it from Chuck's Diner to the stadium in time to see the seniors honored, cookies still in her coat pocket. UConn takes the opening kickoff and scores, with Bowers running it in from close. Old Dominion's defense holds on the next two drives, and Gibson gets home for a sack that gets the Monarch section loud. Potter finds Rodriguez for a touchdown to make it close in the second quarter. But Osborne hits Porter deep before halftime, Bowers adds another score in the third, and the Huskies pull away. The Monarchs add a late field goal and a touchdown, and the senior class walks off as a bowl team.

Jo holds up a cookie like a trophy. "Bowl eligible. Somebody tell Grandma's Attic."

Winnie removes her crown with great ceremony. "The court will reconvene next season," she says. "Bring more cookies."

My call: UConn Huskies 33, Old Dominion Monarchs 18.
