GameDay Report

Pittsburgh at Louisville: A Home Finale the Card Leaves Unranked

Pittsburgh's official game-week note billed Saturday as a prime-time trip to No. 23 Louisville, a 7:30 p.m. ET game at L&N Stadium on ESPN2. Louisville's own preview billed the home finale and listed the Cardinals at No. 21 in the AP poll, No. 22 in the coaches poll, and No. 23 in the College Football Playoff rankings. The live card on this site does not list Louisville as ranked. ESPN's game header showed No. 23. This story stays with the card and flags the school's numbers.

Louisville is 7-3 after a 16-14 win at North Carolina, a night Lincoln Kienholz went 23-of-31 for 154 yards and the official recap credited the defense with six sacks and two takeaways. Kienholz has 2,430 passing yards. Isaac Brown has 804 rushing yards and eight scores. Pittsburgh is 6-4 after a 10-16 loss to Florida State on Nov. 13. Corey Dailey went 16-of-34 for 157 yards in that box; Mason Heintschel still leads the season passing numbers (1,304 yards, 14 touchdowns, one interception). Damon Ferguson Jr. has 694 rushing yards.

Louisville's preview also pointed back to last year's meeting, a 34-27 Cardinals comeback. That is last year's score, not Saturday's. This is a home finale in Louisville, a prime-time visitor at 6-4, and a ranking the card and the school do not share.

If Louisville's pass rush looks like six sacks again, Pittsburgh will have a long night at L&N Stadium. If Heintschel or Dailey can move the ball better than 157 yards, the Cardinals' farewell will have to be earned, not assumed.

Bianca Thornberry has a hat for every occasion, and for Louisville's Senior Day she has chosen a wide red brim with a cardinal feather that sticks out far enough to block the view of anyone sitting behind her. Dominic "Yinz" Bertolucci found that out at the Thursday team walkthrough, when he spent twenty minutes leaning sideways to see the field.

Dominic: Is that a hat or an awning?

Bianca: It's a statement. Saturday is the last home game, the seniors get their send-off, and the season-ticket members get thanked. That calls for a brim.

Dominic: Back home a hat is a hat. It keeps the snow off your head. Done.

Bianca: That's why you'll never be invited to a Derby party.

It is a big night at L&N Stadium. Pitt is the sixth and last home opponent of the year, and the Cardinals have folded Season Ticket Member Appreciation into Senior Day. The kickoff is 7:30 Eastern, the seniors walk out first, and then the Cardinals try to finish the home schedule with a win.

Louisville comes in at 7-3 and third in the ACC. It's been a strange season. Every one of its three losses came by three points: 41-38 to Ole Miss in Nashville, 30-27 to Wake Forest, and 31-28 at NC State. Since then it's been mostly smooth, with blowouts of Florida State, Stanford and Georgia Tech, before a nervy 16-14 win at North Carolina last week.

Bianca: Last week was a little hat-brim-down. But a win is a win.

Dominic: Sixteen points. That's a Pitt score. Welcome to our neighborhood.

Lincoln Kienholz has had a fine season at quarterback, with 2,430 yards, 15 touchdowns and only three interceptions. Isaac Brown has 804 rushing yards and eight scores, and Tre Richardson leads the receivers with 708 yards. Clev Lubin has eight sacks, and Stanquan Clark leads the team with 74 tackles.

Bianca: Kienholz is a garden-party hat. Classy, never a mess, the kind of thing you'd wear to meet a governor.

Dominic: And what's Brown?

Bianca: Brown is the kind of hat you wear when you plan to dance on a table.

Pitt arrives 6-4 with a defense Dominic will defend until his voice goes. The Panthers allow 19.4 points a game, and they held UCF to seven and Georgia Tech to 11. Cruce Brookins leads the team with 71 tackles, and Jaeden Moore has 4.5 sacks. The offense has been harder to figure. Mason Heintschel threw for 1,304 yards and 14 touchdowns, mostly over the first five games, before Holden Geriner and Corey Dailey took over the passing work. Damon Ferguson Jr. has 694 rushing yards, and Cataurus Hicks has 730 receiving yards with six touchdowns.

Dominic: On my scale, Brookins is a full yinz. Every tackle. Never a yikes.

Bianca: What's the scale between?

Dominic: There's yinz, there's yikes, and there's "n'at," which is when it's complicated.

Bianca: And Pitt's quarterback situation?

Dominic: Deep n'at.

The two have played five times since 2020, with Louisville winning three, including 37-9 in 2024 and 34-27 last season. Pitt's wins in that stretch were 23-20 in 2020 and 38-21 in 2023.

Dominic: 38-21. I wore my lucky Terrible Towel to that one.

Bianca: Isn't that the Steelers?

Dominic: In Pittsburgh, a towel is a towel. Also a hat, in emergencies.

The game could hinge on whether Pitt can run against a defense that's been tough to push around and whether its quarterbacks can avoid the mistakes that cost it against Boston College and Florida State. Louisville wants Kienholz to keep things tidy, and it wants Brown out in space. If the Cardinals score early, the crowd will be on its feet the rest of the night. If Pitt keeps it a field-goal game into the fourth quarter, Dominic will be standing on his seat.

Here's how it goes: Brown breaks a long one early, Kienholz finds Richardson for a score before halftime, and Pitt answers with Ferguson and a couple of short fields set up by its defense. The Panthers make it close enough in the fourth to keep everyone honest, but Louisville closes it out and the seniors get a proper goodbye.

Bianca: Final assessment: hat-worthy. Brim up.

Dominic: I'll give the Pitt defense a yinz and the Pitt quarterbacks a n'at.

Bianca tips her red brim to the senior class as they jog off, and for once Dominic doesn't complain that he can't see.

My call: Louisville Cardinals 32, Pittsburgh Panthers 23.
