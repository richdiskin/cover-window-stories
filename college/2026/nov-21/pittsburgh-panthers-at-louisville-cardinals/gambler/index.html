Pittsburgh vs Louisville Pick: Panthers +13.5, Over 50.5

Pittsburgh Panthers (6-4) at Louisville Cardinals (7-3)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · L&N Federal Credit Union Stadium · ESPN2

XFacebook

Louisville sends off its seniors Saturday night in the last of six home games at L&N Stadium, and it's also the night the Cardinals thank their season-ticket members. The Pittsburgh vs Louisville spread has moved a point toward the home team since DraftKings opened Louisville −12.5, and I'm going the other way: Pittsburgh Panthers +13.5 and Over 50.5, with Louisville Cardinals 32, Pittsburgh Panthers 23 as the final.

Dominic "Yinz" Bertolucci, the old steel-town linebacker who grades every Pitt tackle from yinz to yikes, starts with the defense. "We allow 19.4 points a game. Cruce Brookins has 71 tackles, and Jaeden Moore has 4.5 sacks. Our losses to North Carolina, Boston College and Florida State came by four, six and six." Bianca Thornberry, the Derby-party hostess who judges every Louisville drive by its hat-worthiness, answers with offense. "Lincoln Kienholz has 15 touchdown throws and three interceptions. Isaac Brown has 804 yards. We score 38.3 a game."

Here's the side. Louisville's three losses all came by three points, and it's hard to trust a team that wins close as a two-touchdown home favorite. Its last game was a 16-14 escape at North Carolina. Pitt has used three quarterbacks: Mason Heintschel threw the most passes in each of the first five games, then Holden Geriner and Corey Dailey took turns. Even with that mess, three of Pitt's four losses were by six or fewer, and only Miami pulled away. Damon Ferguson Jr. has 694 rushing yards, and Cataurus Hicks has 730 receiving yards. I have Louisville by nine, well short of the number.

The total is Louisville's story. Nine of its ten games went over 50.5, and the only one that didn't was the 16-14 game at North Carolina. Its games ended 38-41, 59-13, 41-31, 44-20 and 52-24. Pitt is the other way, going over in four of ten, but it scored 59 twice and 35 at Virginia Tech. DraftKings opened the total at 51.5. My 55 combined points sit over it.

The risk is that the Pitt quarterback carousel produces another 10-point night like the Florida State loss. Then Louisville wins by 20 and the Under comes in too.

The three cards with scores all agree with me, and all three like both calls. The Formula has Louisville 32-23 with Pittsburgh +13.5 and the Over 50.5. Next Man Up has 33-23 and Letdown Watch has 31-23, with the same side and total. In The Trenches has no side this week, because the two running games pull it in opposite directions, and it doesn't take a total. Follow The Money has no side pick and likes the Over 50.5.

Next Man Up, to the room: "Three cards, same side, same total. Somebody check whether we share a brain." Letdown Watch: "We share a Louisville schedule. That's enough."

Bianca waves the hat, Dominic grumbles about a missed tackle, and Pitt is still inside two touchdowns when the seniors get their last look at the place.

Louisville Cardinals 32, Pittsburgh Panthers 23. Pittsburgh Panthers cover the 13.5. Over 50.5.
