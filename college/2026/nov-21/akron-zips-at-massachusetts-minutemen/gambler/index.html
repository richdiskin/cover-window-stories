Akron vs UMass Pick: Minutemen −3 and Over 46.5 in Amherst

Akron Zips (1-9) at Massachusetts Minutemen (5-5)

7:00 PM ET Wed Nov 18 · Warren McGuirk Alumni Stadium · CBSSN

XFacebook

Every one of Akron's ten games this season has finished with more than 46.5 points, and that one fact is half my card for Wednesday night in Amherst. So the Akron vs UMass pick is Massachusetts Minutemen −3 to go with Over 46.5, on Senior Night and Fan Appreciation night at McGuirk Alumni Stadium.

Abner Kettleworth wants to talk about the first meeting. "Our first win as an FBS program came against Akron. Four interceptions. I still have the ticket stub in a jar with my sourdough starter."

Hollister "Zip" Grubbs wants to talk about last year. "Forty-four to ten, Abner. We sent you home flatter than a blimp with a hole in it."

Both are true, and neither tells me much about this Wednesday. The present does. Akron is 1-9, 0-6 in the MAC and 12th in the conference, allowing 33.2 points a game. The Zips' only win came against Robert Morris in September. UMass is 5-5 and allows 22.3. On a neutral reading this is a mismatch, and three points is a small ask.

The catch is the UMass quarterback. William Watson III has completed 115 of 167 throws with 9 touchdowns and 2 interceptions, but the UMass game notes say he missed the Miami (OH) and Buffalo games and most of the Toledo game with lower body injuries. Logan Inagawa took the most throws at Toledo and went 10 for 27 in a 24-0 loss. I don't know who starts Wednesday, so I'm pricing Inagawa. Even at that price, UMass is the better team against a defense this soft, and Curtis Kimmons IV and T.Y. Harding give the Minutemen plays that don't need a quarterback to be perfect.

Akron changed quarterbacks too. Cibastian Broughton took over for the last two games and threw 2 touchdowns against Western Michigan, both to Marcel Williams. Jordan Gant has 791 rushing yards. That's enough to put points on the board, and that is the Over case. Akron games have landed at 48 or more combined points all year, every single time. DraftKings opened Massachusetts Minutemen −3.5 with a 47.5 total, and both have come down a little.

The room is together on Massachusetts. The Formula has UMass 29-21 and likes both Massachusetts Minutemen −3 and the Over 46.5. Next Man Up has 31-17, likes UMass, and goes Over. Letdown Watch has 30-18, likes UMass, and goes Over. In The Trenches has UMass 34-20, takes Massachusetts Minutemen −3, and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money has no side pick and no total pick.

The Formula: "In The Trenches has UMass by fourteen. I have them by eight. At least we agree on the Minutemen."

In The Trenches: "Better than the Cover Writer, who still thinks a 1-9 team can't hang around for three quarters."

My final is Massachusetts Minutemen 31, Akron Zips 19. That's a 12-point win, nine clear of the number, and 50 points, three and a half past the total. Abner rings his colonial bell for the seniors. Hollister drives back down the turnpike muttering about air pressure.

Massachusetts Minutemen 31, Akron Zips 19. Massachusetts Minutemen cover the 3. Over 46.5.
