Oregon Ducks vs Michigan State Spartans: Silver Bells Senior Night

Oregon Ducks (7-3) at Michigan State Spartans (3-7)

8:00 PM ET Fri Nov 20 · Spartan Stadium · Fox

XFacebook

Somewhere around 7:50 on Friday night, fireworks will go up behind the Capitol dome in downtown Lansing, the state Christmas tree will already be glowing, and about ten minutes later Oregon Ducks vs Michigan State Spartans kicks off at Spartan Stadium in East Lansing. Silver Bells in the City and Senior Night on the same Friday. Shay Kowalczyk calls it the best doubleheader in mid-Michigan, and she plans to catch the Electric Light Parade at 6 and then sprint to her seat.

"You can't do both," Nix Wyatt-Aldana says. Nix flew in from Eugene with three jackets, all of them green and yellow in slightly different shades, and is already planning the outfit change between the parade and the game.

"Watch me, eh," Shay says. "I've got a cousin with a car and a parking spot near Grand River. Strike one on your doubt."

Nix is arriving with a lot of swagger about the Ducks, and that's fair. Oregon is 7-3, No. 16 in the country, and coming off its two best performances of the year. The Ducks went to Columbus and beat Ohio State 41-28, then came home and beat Michigan 31-7. Dylan Raiola threw for 341 yards and 4 touchdowns against the Wolverines, and Jeremiah McClellan caught 4 balls for 105 yards and a score.

"That game was a full collection," Nix says. "The uniforms were perfect, the route trees were perfect, the defense was perfect. Ten out of ten."

"Uh-huh. You want to talk about the UCLA game?"

Nix does not want to talk about the UCLA game. Oregon lost that one 17-15 at home, and the Ducks also scraped past Nebraska 17-14 and lost 21-10 at Illinois. When Oregon is off, it looks like a runway show where somebody tripped on the stairs, and Nix has a whole speech about it that nobody asked for.

Still, the Ducks bring plenty. Raiola has 1,695 throwing yards, 15 touchdowns and just 2 interceptions. Dakorien Moore has 702 receiving yards and 8 touchdowns, Dierre Hill Jr. leads the ground game with 572 yards, and Matayo Uiagalelei has 9.5 sacks. Devon Jackson leads Oregon with 63 tackles.

Michigan State is 3-7, and Shay will tell you every one of those losses has a story. The Spartans lost to Illinois 38-37 in overtime, to Northwestern 24-21, and to Washington 21-14 last week at home. She calls those three foul tips. You keep swinging, eh. Eventually one goes fair.

The Spartans also beat Michigan 15-6 in Ann Arbor earlier this month, which Shay has rewatched so many times her TV remote is shaped like her thumb.

"You beat Michigan by twenty-four," she tells Nix. "We beat 'em in their house. Different flavor. Ours tasted like a double scoop from the Dairy Store."

The quarterback picture is the big question for the Spartans. Alessio Milivojevic has handled most of the season with 1,559 throwing yards, 7 touchdowns and 3 interceptions. Cam Fancher took the most snaps throwing against Washington and went 14 of 22 for 134 yards. Whoever is back there will need Chrishon McCray, who has 414 receiving yards, and Jaziun Patterson, who leads the team with 327 rushing yards. Dion Crawford has 58 tackles at linebacker, and Anelu Lafaele leads the team with 3.5 sacks.

"Fancher or Milivojevic, I don't care," Shay says. "Call the right pitch, keep the ball low, let the seniors go out fighting."

Here's how Friday plays out. Oregon scores on its opening drive, because of course it does, and Nix stands up to applaud the Ducks' cleats. But Michigan State's defense settles in and the senior class plays like it knows it's the last night in Spartan Stadium. Patterson punches in a short touchdown in the second quarter, the student section is still buzzing from the fireworks, and the Spartans are within a score at halftime. Raiola and Moore pull away in the third quarter, and Uiagalelei gets to the quarterback twice. Michigan State answers with a late score to keep the crowd standing, and the seniors walk off to a real ovation.

Nix rates the win a nine out of ten, docking a point for a muddy sleeve. Shay heads to the Dairy Store for a triple scoop and eats every bite in silence, which is how she grieves and also how she eats ice cream.

My call: Oregon Ducks 29, Michigan State Spartans 18.
