Indiana vs Washington: Unbeaten-on-the-Road Hoosiers Visit Husky Stadium

Indiana Hoosiers (9-1) at Washington Huskies (6-4)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Husky Stadium · Peacock

XFacebook

Vangie Tollefsrud does her Saturday shopping before kickoff, and this Saturday her route runs up University Way NE, where the University District Farmers Market sets up between NE 50th and 52nd Streets from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. Rain or shine.

"Apples, mushrooms, a coffee, another coffee," she says, ticking it off while zipping her rain shell to her chin. "Then I walk down to the lake and get in the boat. That's a Husky Saturday."

Mallory Penhallow, who flew in from Bloomington with a garment bag, a laptop and a spreadsheet with forty tabs, looks at the sky like it personally insulted her hair. "How quaint that you have a farmers market, Vangie. In Indiana we have farms."

So the Indiana Hoosiers vs Washington Huskies game kicks off at 12:30 in Seattle with one insider holding a bag of apples and the other holding a win probability chart. Washington is making it Salute to Service and Senior Day at Husky Stadium. Indiana is making it another stop on a trip it would like to keep going for a while.

"Nine and one," Mallory says, sunny as a pageant stage. "We allow 12.5 points a game. Tobi Osunsanmi has ten sacks. Ten! Charlie Becker has 950 receiving yards and ten touchdown catches. Josh Hoover has 19 touchdown passes and two interceptions. And we've won all three of our road games."

Vangie sips her second coffee. "And the one you lost?"

Mallory's smile holds, but only just. "Ohio State. 19-15. We don't talk about tab 41."

Indiana is the second-best team in the Big Ten, and the defense is the reason. The Hoosiers have shut out Howard and Western Kentucky and held eight of their ten opponents to 17 points or fewer. Isaiah Jones leads them with 71 tackles. Last week, against USC, Grant Wilson threw the most passes for Indiana and put up 305 yards and two touchdowns in a 27-13 win. Khobie Martin ran for 82 yards and a score.

"So you have two quarterbacks who can throw it," Vangie says. "Great. Fantastic. Love that for you."

Washington's year has been a rowing race with a few crabbed oars. The Huskies started 3-0, then lost to Minnesota by three, at USC by four and to Iowa at home. They won at Purdue and at Nebraska, lost to Penn State by three, and won at Michigan State last week, 21-14. They are 6-4. Demond Williams Jr. has thrown for 1,955 yards with 13 touchdowns and just four interceptions. Chris Lawson leads the receivers with 851 yards, and he caught five balls for 124 yards and two touchdowns in East Lansing. Safety Alex McLaughlin has 73 tackles.

"We lose close ones," Vangie says. "Three by four points or less this year. You know what that means? It means we're right there. Every week. In the rain."

"It means you lose close ones," Mallory says brightly.

The two teams met in 2024, and Indiana won 31-17 in Bloomington. Mallory has that game on its own tab. Vangie has it filed under "it was sunny there, it doesn't count."

"Our weather is a twelfth man," Vangie says. "Bring your Indiana sunshine. We'll see how long it lasts."

Here's how it goes. The seniors and the service members get their moment before kickoff, and Husky Stadium is loud and damp. Washington takes the opening drive down the field and kicks a field goal. Indiana answers with Becker getting free on a deep ball, and Osunsanmi gets to Williams on third down to end the next Husky drive. Lawson catches a touchdown before halftime to keep it close. In the second half the Hoosier defense takes over, Indiana grinds out two scoring drives on the ground, and a late Washington touchdown makes the final look closer than the fourth quarter felt.

Mallory gives the full pageant wave from the visitors' section and reads the win probability chart out loud like an acceptance speech.

Vangie zips her shell back up and walks to the lake. "Good row," she says. "Wrong finish. See you at the market next Saturday."

My call: Indiana Hoosiers 27, Washington Huskies 18.
