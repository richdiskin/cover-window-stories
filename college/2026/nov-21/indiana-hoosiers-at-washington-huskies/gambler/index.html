Indiana at Washington: A Home Closer in Seattle, With No. 9 Coming to Town

Market check, Indiana at Washington: DraftKings IU -7.5, total 44.5.

Washington closes its home schedule Saturday at Husky Stadium, a 12:30 p.m. PT kickoff on Peacock against No. 9 Indiana. The Huskies' official preview called it the home finale, with the regular-season closer still to come after this one. Indiana just beat No. 18 USC 27-13 in Bloomington, a game the Hoosiers' gameday notes said ended with Jamari Sharpe intercepting Jayden Maiava and returning it 34 yards. The Hoosiers are 9-1. Josh Hoover has 1,860 passing yards, 19 touchdowns and two interceptions. Charlie Becker has 950 receiving yards and 10 scores, and Indiana's notes tied his 42-yard touchdown catch against USC as his 10th of the year. Washington is 6-4 after a 21-14 win at Michigan State on Nov. 14, a touchdown with 2:24 left in the school's recap. Demond Williams Jr. has 1,955 passing yards, 13 touchdowns and four interceptions. The Huskies' preview also said the program is already bowl eligible.

There is a human thread in Seattle this week that is not about the visitor. On Nov. 18 the school published a feature on punter Hunter Green and the No. 93 he wears for his father. It is a program story, not a scouting report, and it is part of what the home closer is supposed to feel like. Indiana is the 9-1 problem walking in the door.

A home finale in Seattle, a 9-1 visitor coming off a win over USC, and a 6-4 Husky team that already has its bowl math: that is the Peacock window. If Indiana's passing game looks like 19 touchdowns and two interceptions, the home finale will be a test. If Washington finishes the way it did at Michigan State, with 2:24 left on the clock, the students will still be standing at the whistle.

Indiana has won all three of its road games this season, and the fourth comes Saturday at Husky Stadium, where Washington has lost three straight home games. The Indiana vs Washington spread gets the Hoosiers from me: Indiana Hoosiers −7.5, Over 43.5, with Indiana Hoosiers 27, Washington Huskies 18 at 3:30 PM ET, half past noon in Seattle.

Mallory Penhallow reads it like a pageant scorecard, smiling the whole time. "Nine and one. We allow 12.5 points a game. Tobi Osunsanmi has ten sacks. Charlie Becker has 950 receiving yards and ten touchdowns. Josh Hoover has thrown 19 touchdown passes and two interceptions. Our only loss was 19-15 to Ohio State."

Evangeline "Vangie" Tollefsrud answers like a coxswain calling a sprint. "Mal, we lost to Penn State by three, to Minnesota by three and to USC by four. Demond Williams Jr. threw for 288 at Michigan State last week, and Chris Lawson caught two touchdowns. We've won two of three. Nobody walks into Seattle in November and relaxes."

The Indiana side starts with the defense. The Hoosiers have held eight of ten opponents to 17 points or fewer, and Washington scores 25.8 a game. The 2024 meeting went Indiana 31, Washington 17, and this Indiana team is better. Grant Wilson threw the most passes against USC last week, 305 yards and two touchdowns in a 27-13 win, and the offense looked fine with him throwing it. My 27-18 is a nine-point win, a point and a half past the 7.5.

The honest worry is Indiana's recent margins. The Hoosiers beat Nebraska by three, Michigan by seven in overtime and Minnesota by seven in overtime before beating USC by 14. Washington's four losses came by three, four, 17 and three. A late Washington field goal changes everything here.
