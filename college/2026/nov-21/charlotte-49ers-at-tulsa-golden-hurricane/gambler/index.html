Charlotte vs Tulsa Prediction: Tulsa −22.5, Over 51.5

Charlotte 49ers (1-9) at Tulsa Golden Hurricane (6-4)

2:00 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · H. A. Chapman Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Tulsa just scored 60 at Tulane and 38 against Florida Atlantic, and now it gets a 1-9 Charlotte team on Senior Day. That's why I'll lay the big number in Tulsa vs Charlotte. My pick is Tulsa Golden Hurricane −22.5 and Over 51.5.

DraftKings opened Tulsa −20.5, and the side has moved two points toward the Golden Hurricane. The total has slipped a point to 51.5 as of 9:20 PM ET Friday. I agree with the side move, and I think the total is a little low.

Start with Tulsa's offense. Baylor Hayes has 1,990 passing yards, 16 touchdowns and four interceptions, and he's thrown for 338 and 309 yards in the last two games. Oran Singleton Jr. has 775 receiving yards, and Damari Alston has eight rushing touchdowns. Tulsa scores 33.6 a game. Wiley Pettibone calls it a gusher: "Sixty in New Orleans. Thirty-eight on Homecoming. The well's flowing."

Charlotte has to stop that, and it hasn't stopped much. The 49ers allow 36.2 a game. They gave up 59 to Memphis, 50 to North Texas, 41 to Ole Miss and 34 to Louisiana. Priya Halloran knows the risk: "We've been close lately. One point to East Carolina. Overtime with Tulane. But the defense is a bad loan." Charlotte scores 19.3 a game, and Conner Harrell has 1,167 passing yards with seven touchdowns.

The close losses are real, but Tulsa is a big step up. The Golden Hurricane allow 22.6 a game, Kyran Duhon has seven and a half sacks, and Chris Thompson Jr. has 93 tackles. Their only losses came to Arkansas, Navy, Army and North Texas, and two of those were by a field goal or less.

My final is Tulsa Golden Hurricane 39, Charlotte 49ers 15. Tulsa wins by 24 and covers the 22.5. The 54 combined points go Over 51.5. Hayes and Singleton do most of the work on the scoreboard, and Charlotte should find a couple of scores against a team that's playing for its seniors.

The room is split on the side. The Formula likes Charlotte +22.5 at 37-17 and likes the Over 51.5. Next Man Up likes Tulsa −22.5 at 40-14 and likes the Over 51.5. Letdown Watch takes Tulsa −22.5 at 38-15 and is Over 51.5. In The Trenches calls the trenches even, so it has no side and no over/under call. Follow The Money has no side pick and likes the Under 51.5.

The Formula: "Twenty points is my margin. Charlotte keeps it under the number."

Next Man Up: "Twenty-six points. Tulsa's offense has scored that many all by itself lately."

Letdown Watch: "Senior Day after two big wins. Tulsa won't relax."

Band Day at Chapman brings the noise, and Tulsa brings the points.

Tulsa Golden Hurricane 39, Charlotte 49ers 15. Tulsa Golden Hurricane cover the 22.5. Over 51.5.
