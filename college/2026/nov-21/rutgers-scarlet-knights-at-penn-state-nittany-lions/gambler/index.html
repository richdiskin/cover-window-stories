Rutgers vs Penn State Pick: Scarlet Knights +26.5 and the Over 49.5

Rutgers Scarlet Knights (2-8) at Penn State Nittany Lions (6-4)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Beaver Stadium · FS1

XFacebook

Santa throws the switch on the evergreen at the Allen Street Gates on Thursday night, two days before Penn State sends off its seniors against Rutgers at Beaver Stadium. The Rutgers vs Penn State spread is a big one, and I'm taking the points with the Over: Rutgers Scarlet Knights +26.5, Over 49.5, with Penn State Nittany Lions 37, Rutgers Scarlet Knights 16 at 3:30 PM ET.

Luann Kessler is already in a holiday mood. "Three straight wins, bowl eligible at 6-4, and we beat Minnesota 42-14 last week. Carson Hansen ran for 126. Rocco Becht has 18 touchdown passes and five interceptions. Tony Rojas leads us in sacks and tackles. Linebacker U is open for business, Vinny."

Vinny Scolari grades it like a boardwalk arm-wrestle. "Lu, we're 2-8, I know. But we lost to youse 40-36 last year. KJ Duff has 774 receiving yards and nine touchdowns. Antwan Raymond has 810 on the ground. We show up."

Penn State has won all nine meetings since 2016, but 26.5 is a lot of football. Three of Penn State's six wins came by more than that, against Marshall, Buffalo and Minnesota. The other three came by 18 at Temple, 14 against Purdue and three at Washington. Penn State's offense scores 30.2 points a game, and my 37 is a good day, not a historic one. Rutgers has lost by more than 26.5 three times, to Indiana, Northwestern and Nebraska. It also lost to USC by seven and Michigan by seven. My 21-point margin sits five and a half points inside the number.

The risk is right there in the box scores. Rutgers was shut out 31-0 by Nebraska last week, and AJ Surace went 4 for 12 for 24 yards with two interceptions. Surace has thrown the most passes in each of the last four games. The Scarlet Knights allow 32.3 points a game.

DraftKings opened Penn State −20.5 and 49.5, as shown in ESPN pickcenter. As of 9:20 PM ET Friday, the side has moved six points toward Penn State and the total is back at 49.5.

The Over is where Rutgers' defense helps me. Six of Rutgers' ten games went over 49.5, including 42-35 against USC and 37-30 at Maryland. Four of Penn State's did. My 53 needs Penn State's offense to keep scoring and Rutgers to find two or three drives with Duff and Raymond.

Most of the room is with Rutgers. The Formula has Penn State 35, Rutgers 16, likes Rutgers +26.5 and goes Over 49.5. Next Man Up has Penn State 38, Rutgers 20, likes Rutgers +26.5 and likes the Over. Letdown Watch has Penn State 38, Rutgers 13, lays the 24.5 it took Monday and goes Over. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call on this one. Follow The Money likes Penn State Nittany Lions −26 and the Under 49.5.

Letdown Watch: "Senior Day, a bowl ticket punched, a rival that's lost four straight. This could get ugly."

Next Man Up: "Big Senior Day leads mean backups in the fourth. Backups don't stop KJ Duff."

Santa gets one switch to flip in State College this week. Penn State needs a lot more than one big play to win by four touchdowns.

Penn State Nittany Lions 37, Rutgers Scarlet Knights 16. Rutgers Scarlet Knights cover the 26.5. Over 49.5.
