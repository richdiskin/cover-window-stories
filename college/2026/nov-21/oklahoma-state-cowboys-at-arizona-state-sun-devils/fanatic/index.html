Oklahoma State at Arizona State: Homecoming Parade, Then a Late Kickoff

Oklahoma State Cowboys (6-4) at Arizona State Sun Devils (6-4)

10:15 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Mountain America Stadium · ESPN

XFacebook

At five o'clock Saturday, the ASU Homecoming Block Party opens on Old Main Lawn, and fifteen minutes later the parade starts down University Drive. Skylar Vanterpool has a spot picked out on the curb, an inflatable flamingo under one arm and a cooler of electrolyte drinks under the other. Dusty, who drove in from Stillwater in a pressed shirt and a hat he refuses to take off indoors, has been asked to carry the flamingo.

Dusty: I have roped a calf in under ten seconds. I have never carried a flamingo.

Skylar: There's a first time for everything. Hydrate.

Dusty: I'm hydrated.

Skylar: Hydrate more. It's a parade.

It's a full day in Tempe. Homecoming is the theme, the Sun Devils honor their seniors before kickoff, and the game starts at 8:15 p.m. local time at Mountain America Stadium. It's the last home game of the year, and it's a good matchup on paper: both teams are 6-4 and sit fourth in the Big 12.

Skylar: Senior night, homecoming, parade, block party. I'm going to need a nap at halftime.

Dusty: You'd miss the second half.

Skylar: I'd dream about it.

Oklahoma State's offense is a rodeo. Drew Mestemaker has thrown for 3,239 yards and 30 touchdowns, Caleb Hawkins has run for 1,172 yards, and Justin Bowick leads the receivers with 868 yards and ten scores. The Cowboys score 42.2 points a game and put up 66 at Kansas State and 57 against Colorado. Ethan Wesloski leads the defense in both tackles, with 85, and sacks, with four. But it's been bumpy lately: a 51-49 loss at Iowa State and, last week, a 52-27 home loss to Texas Tech.

Dusty: Rodeo timing. Some rides last eight seconds. Some rides you get thrown.

Skylar: And last week?

Dusty: Last week, the bull won. We tip our hats and get back on.

Arizona State has had a strange season at quarterback. Cutter Boley threw for 387 yards and six touchdowns in the opener, then Mikey Keene took over for a stretch, and Jake Fette has handled the passing work in each of the last three games. Through all of it, Reed Harris has been the constant, with 59 catches for 987 yards and nine touchdowns. Cardae Mack has 714 rushing yards. Owen Long leads the team with 98 tackles, and Jalen Thompson has seven sacks. The Sun Devils have won two straight, 20-10 over Colorado and 26-13 at UCF.

Skylar: Harris is the pool. Everything revolves around him.

Dusty: And the quarterbacks?

Skylar: The quarterbacks are the floaties. You rotate through them till you find one that holds air.

These two have met three times since 2022. Oklahoma State won the first two, 34-17 and 27-15, and Arizona State won 42-21 in 2024.

Dusty: Two out of three. I'll take that ride.

Skylar: The last one was ours. Remember that one.

What decides it? Arizona State's defense has to slow down Mestemaker, and the best way is pressure from Thompson. The Sun Devils need Mack to grind out yards and keep the Cowboys' offense on the sideline. Oklahoma State wants to get Hawkins going early and take shots to Bowick down the field.

Here's how it goes: Mestemaker and Bowick connect early for a long score, and Arizona State answers with Harris. The game swings back and forth all night, with neither team leading by more than a touchdown. Oklahoma State kicks a field goal in the final two minutes to go up by one, and Arizona State's last drive comes up short near midfield.

Dusty: Ride 'em, Pokes.

Skylar: Hydrate, reflect, recover.

Dusty hands back the flamingo, a little deflated after four quarters of being squeezed, and Skylar tucks it under her arm like a trophy anyway.

My call: Oklahoma State Cowboys 29, Arizona State Sun Devils 28.
