UL Monroe vs App State: Boone Fiddles, Then a Senior Day Send-Off

UL Monroe Warhawks (2-8) at App State Mountaineers (6-4)

2:30 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Kidd Brewer Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Thursday night at the Jones House in downtown Boone, somebody will tune a fiddle, somebody will pull a banjo out of its case, and the old-time jam will run until the last call for tunes. Two days later the UL Monroe Warhawks vs App State Mountaineers game turns Kidd Brewer Stadium into a Senior Day farewell, and Zeb Coldiron and Darnell Vidrine have very different ideas about who gets to play the closing song.

Zeb is in no hurry. Zeb is never in a hurry. He talks about this App State season the way he talks about a long hike: steady going, a couple of hard climbs, then a view.

"We started 3-0," he says. "Maine, a road win at East Carolina, Charlotte. Then the climb got steep. Lost at NC State. Lost to Old Dominion at home, 10-3, on Homecoming. That one still stings. Then we won at Coastal, beat James Madison in overtime, and I had to sit down on a rock for a while."

"You lost to Georgia State at home two weeks ago," Darnell says. "Thirty-nine points. On Heroes Day."

"I'm getting there," Zeb says. "That's the part of the trail with the mud. But we went to Huntington last week and beat Marshall 20-16, and Jaquari Lewis ran for 137 yards. The view is coming back."

Darnell hosts the overnight sports show back home, and he is the most hopeful man who has ever answered the phone at three in the morning. His Warhawks are 2-8. He knows it. He also knows exactly where the two wins came from, and he will tell you before you ask.

"Troy," he says. "On the road. Twenty-one to fourteen. Then Arkansas State, on the road, nineteen to seventeen. Zeb, we like other people's stadiums. We are the best guests in the Sun Belt."

There is something to it. UL Monroe has been rough at home, including that 42-21 loss to Louisiana last week in the in-state game Darnell will not discuss before sunrise. But on the road the Warhawks have been scrappy, and the reason is a defense led by Noah Flemmings, who has 89 tackles, and Kevontay Wells, who has four sacks. Aidan Armenta has thrown for 1,483 yards and 10 touchdowns, and Austin Carlisle runs the ball as a second quarterback with 465 yards. Bryson Roland gave the Louisiana defense 111 rushing yards to think about.

Zeb answers with the people Boone will be cheering for when the seniors walk out. Malachi Singleton has thrown for 2,287 yards, with 11 touchdowns. Sam Pickett III has 679 receiving yards and caught five balls for 89 at Marshall. Lewis has eight rushing touchdowns. And on defense the Mountaineers have Caleb Sandstrom, with seven sacks, and Colton Phares, with 81 tackles.

"Sandstrom against your quarterback is the whole afternoon," Zeb says. "He's going to be in that backfield more than your center."

"Armenta has been hit before," Darnell says. "He gets up. Monroe people get up. That's what we do."

The history is mostly Zeb's. App State has won five of the six meetings since 2016, including a 59-28 game in 2021 and a 52-7 game in 2019. But Darnell has his one, and he wears it like a church pin: the 2017 game, when UL Monroe went into Boone and won 52-45. The last meeting, in 2023, went to App State by a single point, 41-40.

"One point," Darnell says. "One more snap and that's a whole different conversation."

"It was one point," Zeb says, "and I say this as a man who hikes: the top of the hill counts the same no matter how hard you breathed getting there."

Senior Day changes the air in Boone. The stadium sits up in the mountains, the crowd knows every name being read, and the players who stayed through all of it get one more walk on that field before App State heads to South Alabama for the regular-season finale. Zeb gets a little quiet when he thinks about it. Darnell, to his credit, takes his hat off when the seniors are announced, because he is a gentleman even at 2-8.

Here is how it plays. App State runs Lewis early and often, and the home crowd gets loud when Singleton finds Pickett deep in the first half. Sandstrom gets a sack that makes the student section bounce. But UL Monroe does not fold the way people expect. Roland keeps finding cutback lanes, Carlisle steals a few first downs with his legs, and Flemmings makes tackle after tackle to keep the Warhawks within shouting distance. The Mountaineers pull away in the fourth quarter and the seniors get their win, while Darnell drives home talking into a recorder about how close it was.

Thursday's jam ends with a last tune. Saturday's ends with a happy mountain.

My call: App State Mountaineers 32, UL Monroe Warhawks 19.
