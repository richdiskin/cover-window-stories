Iowa State Cyclones vs UCF Knights: First Trip to the Bounce House

Iowa State Cyclones (5-5) at UCF Knights (5-5)

6:00 PM ET Fri Nov 20 · Acrisure Bounce House · FS1

XFacebook

Iowa State has been to Orlando one time in its football life, for the 2024 Pop-Tarts Bowl, and Twyla Hemmingsen still talks about that 42-41 win over Miami like it was a weather event she personally predicted. Iowa State Cyclones vs UCF Knights on Friday is the Cyclones' first visit to the Acrisure Bounce House, a 6:00 PM ET kickoff on UCF's Senior Knight, and Knox Brevard-Stiles is waiting at the gate to tell her this ride is a lot bumpier than a bowl game.

"Clear skies, light breeze, chance of Cyclones," Twyla says. "I checked the radar on the plane."

"Keep your hands and feet inside the stadium at all times," Knox says. "This is the last home game for our seniors. The building is going to shake. That's not a figure of speech. It's literally called the Bounce House."

Senior Knight is a big one for Knox, and for good reason. UCF is 5-5, and a win at home sends the seniors out on a high and gets the Knights to six. The Bounce House has been kind this year, with four wins in six home games, including a double-overtime thriller over BYU at homecoming.

"Thirty-nine to thirty-one in two overtimes," Knox says. "My heart did a loop-the-loop. I had to sit down on a bench by the lake for an hour."

"We had a thriller too," Twyla says. "Fifty-one to forty-nine over Oklahoma State on Halloween. I forecast every point. That's not true. I forecast about half the points. The rest were a surprise front."

Here's the thing about Iowa State right now: it's running people over. Aiden Flora has 1,335 rushing yards and 12 touchdowns, and last week against Cincinnati he carried 28 times for 216 yards and three scores in a 41-13 win. Jaylen Raynor has thrown 13 touchdowns and keeps the defense honest.

"Flora is a high-pressure system," Twyla says. "He builds slowly over the first quarter and then he's a storm warning."

"Josh Derry is a whole roller coaster by himself," Knox says. "Seven catches, 137 yards against Arizona State. 708 on the year. He's the first big drop. You think you're ready. You're not."

UCF's quarterback situation has had more than one loop of its own. Keyone Jenkins led the throwing from mid-September through Halloween week, including a 293-yard night in the BYU win, and Alonza Barnett III has taken over for the last two games. Barnett threw for 257 yards against Arizona State last week, but the Knights still lost 26-13. Duke Watson leads the ground game with 596 yards, and Tackett Curtis leads the defense with 67 tackles.

"We need that Kansas magic again," Knox says. "Thirty-three to thirty-two in Lawrence. One point. I screamed so loud on the drive home the GPS rerouted me."

"One-point games," Twyla says. "I don't love them. They're like hail. You hear them before you see them."

The last time these two met, in 2024, Iowa State won 38-35. Twyla mentions this every few minutes like a weather alert scrolling across the bottom of a screen.

"That was in Ames," Knox says. "Ames doesn't have a Bounce House."

"Ames has wind."

"Orlando has thrill rides. We win the vibes."

Both teams score plenty, and both give up plenty. Iowa State's last three games all turned into points festivals: 51-49, 42-31 and 41-13. UCF allows 26 points a game. Nobody in this one is going to win with a field goal and a prayer.

Here's how Friday goes. Senior Knight starts with the ceremony, and the Bounce House shakes on the opening kickoff, just as advertised. Iowa State answers right away with Flora, first a long run, then another, until the Knights start crowding the box. UCF punches back with Barnett to Derry over the top. It goes back and forth all night, a real rollercoaster for Knox and a shifting forecast for Twyla, and the fourth quarter starts with a one-score game. Flora grinds out a late touchdown, the Bounce House holds its breath, and UCF's last drive comes up a field goal, short of tying it. The seniors walk off, and the Cyclones take a three-point win on the road.

Knox sits back down, shakes his head, and says the ride still had a drop. Twyla checks the radar on her phone, sees the system moving back toward Ames, and calls it a successful flight with light turbulence.

My call: Iowa State Cyclones 27, UCF Knights 24.
