Iowa State vs UCF Pick: Cyclones −1.5 and the Over 48.5

Iowa State Cyclones (5-5) at UCF Knights (5-5)

6:00 PM ET Fri Nov 20 · Acrisure Bounce House · FS1

XFacebook

Iowa State has been to Orlando exactly once, for a 42-41 bowl win over Miami, and Friday is the Cyclones' first trip to the Acrisure Bounce House. UCF calls the 6:00 PM ET kickoff Senior Knight, and my Iowa State vs UCF pick takes Iowa State Cyclones −1.5 and the Over 48.5.

Twyla Hemmingsen is giving the forecast before anyone asks. "Clear skies over Aiden Flora with a strong chance of two hundred rushing yards. He ran for 216 against Cincinnati last week. Bring a jacket, Knox. The wind is coming from Ames."

Knox Brevard-Stiles has his arms up like he's at the top of the big drop. "Keep your hands inside the stadium, Twyla. This place bounces. It's the last home game for our seniors, and the Bounce House has a height requirement."

Here's the case for the Cyclones. Flora has 1,335 rushing yards and 12 touchdowns, and Iowa State has won two of its last three, scoring 51, 31 and 41. Jaylen Raynor has 13 touchdown throws against 6 interceptions. The Cyclones also beat UCF the last time these two met, 38-35 in 2024.

UCF is 5-5 and has had two different quarterback stretches. Keyone Jenkins took most of the throws from mid-September through the Baylor game, and Alonza Barnett III has led the last two, throwing for 257 yards against Arizona State. Josh Derry caught 7 balls for 137 in that game and has 708 yards on the year. That's a good receiver on a team that lost by 13 at home last week. The Knights do play well here, at 4-2 in this building, but last week's home loss and a wet, windy night are why I have the Cyclones winning it.

So the score is Iowa State Cyclones 27, UCF Knights 24. Three points. That covers the point and a half the Cyclones are laying.

The Over comes from Iowa State's recent games. The last three finished 51-49, 42-31 and 41-13, all past this total, and the 2024 meeting landed on 73. UCF allows 26.0 points a game and Iowa State allows 27.3. As of 9:21 AM ET Friday, DraftKings has flipped to Iowa State Cyclones −1.5 with a 48.5 total, five under Monday, and storms with a 15 mph wind are forecast for kickoff. That weather is why I have 51, and I'll still take the Over.

The room splits on the side. The Formula has UCF 28-27, likes UCF Knights +1.5 and likes the Over 48.5. Next Man Up has UCF 29-27, likes UCF Knights +1.5 and likes the Over. Letdown Watch has Iowa State winning 29-24, likes the Cyclones and likes the Over. In The Trenches has UCF 30-23, likes UCF Knights +1.5, and makes no over/under call. Follow The Money likes Iowa State Cyclones −1 and the Under 48.5.

Letdown Watch: "Three of you have UCF winning at home now. Some of us have the guts to stay with the Cyclones."

The Formula: "I moved to UCF by a point this morning, Letdown Watch. Guts are nice. Numbers pay better."

My final has the Cyclones winning on Senior Knight, with 51 combined points, two and a half over the total. Twyla gets the win and the cover. Knox gets the loud house.

Iowa State Cyclones 27, UCF Knights 24. Iowa State Cyclones cover the 1.5. Over 48.5.
