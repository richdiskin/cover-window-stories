Rice Owls vs Temple Owls: Fan Appreciation Night at the Linc

Rice Owls (1-9) at Temple Owls (2-8)

7:30 PM ET Thu Nov 19 · Lincoln Financial Field · ESPN

XFacebook

It's Owls against Owls on Thursday night at Lincoln Financial Field, and Temple has made it Fan Appreciation Night, which Teddy Fiorella has decided means the whole stadium should appreciate him personally. Rice Owls vs Temple Owls kicks off at 7:30 PM ET in South Philadelphia, and Odalys Chen-Moreau has flown up from Houston with a 1-9 team, a stack of note cards and a firm belief that the smarter Owl wins the night.

"Same mascot," Teddy says. "Different birds. Ours has a South Philly accent. Yours does homework."

"Ours finished its homework," Odalys says. "Yours is still looking for a pencil."

Two teams with rough seasons, sure. Temple is 2-8 and Rice is 1-9. But Teddy has never once in his life let a record keep him quiet, and Odalys has never met a bad season she couldn't break down into smaller, more manageable bad pieces.

"Fan Appreciation Night," Teddy says, throwing his arms wide like he's conducting an orchestra. "This is for the people who came to every game. In the cold. In September. In the rain on a Tuesday if they asked. We appreciate you. I appreciate you. I would run up the Art Museum steps for every single one of you."

"Would you, though?"

"In my heart I'm running them right now."

The Temple case is about having more ways to move the ball. Ajani Sheppard has thrown for 1,330 yards with 9 touchdowns and only 3 interceptions. Keveun Mason has 653 rushing yards, and Colin Chase leads the receivers at 395. And then there's Curly Ordonez, who has 100 tackles and 3 sacks and plays linebacker like he's late for a train on Broad Street.

"Curly is the best thing in this city that isn't on a roll," Teddy says.

Odalys counters with the close calls, and there are a lot of them. Rice lost to UTSA by two, to Florida Atlantic by two in a 51-49 track meet, to Western Michigan by seven, and to Tulane by three last week. These Owls have been right there.

"It's a small sample," she says. "But it's a consistent small sample. Statistically, we're due. Spiritually, we're overdue. Emotionally, I'd like a hug."

Rice's quarterback story changed halfway through the season. Jacurri Brown took the most throws for the first seven games and ran for 691 yards, more than anyone on the team. Then Gael Ochoa took over, and against Tulane he ran 17 times for 82 yards. Ochoa is a runner who throws when he has to. AJ Brown leads the Rice defense with 66 tackles from safety, and Chibby Nwajuaku gets after the quarterback.

"If the Marching Owl Band were here, they'd have a whole halftime bit about two kinds of owls," Odalys adds. "With instruments. Mostly instruments. Possibly a couch."

"There's no couch in a marching band."

"There is in ours. Don't ask questions you can't handle."

Here's what neither one wants to admit: the defenses in this game give up a lot of points. Temple allows 29.1 a game. Rice allows 32.6. Temple scored 48 at Toledo and lost in overtime, and put up 35 at Navy. Rice put up 49 at Florida Atlantic. When these offenses get rolling, the scoreboard operator earns his paycheck.

"So it's a shootout," Teddy says.

"It's a high-variance outcome," Odalys says. "Which is a shootout for people with library cards."

Here's how Thursday goes. Rice comes out with Ochoa running the ball outside, and the visiting Owls jump ahead early while Odalys flips through her note cards at top speed. Temple answers with Sheppard finding Chase down the sideline, and Mason starts grinding the middle. The teams trade scores into the third quarter, both defenses bending, neither breaking all the way. Then Ordonez makes the play of the night, a fourth-quarter stop in the backfield, and Sheppard leads one more drive to put Temple up by more than a score. Rice gets one back late, Odalys stands on her seat, and Temple runs out the clock in front of the fans it came to thank.

Teddy jogs up the Linc's steps two at a time, pretends they're the Art Museum's, and throws both fists in the air at the top. Odalys packs her note cards, mutters that the sample size is still too small to panic, and panics quietly all the way to the airport.

My call: Temple Owls 29, Rice Owls 24.
