South Florida at Florida Atlantic: A Red Out in Boca, Two Teams at 6-4

Market check, South Florida at Florida Atlantic: DraftKings USF -1.5, total 63.5.

Florida Atlantic asked the crowd to wear red, and the school's "Know Before You Go" framed Saturday as a Coca-Cola Senior Day at Flagler Credit Union Stadium, 2:30 p.m. on ESPN+, honoring senior players and the support staff who keep the place running. Both teams are 6-4. That is a rivalry game dressed as a farewell, and it is the last time this senior class will hear its own building at full volume.

South Florida has the better recent defensive reputation in this league. Florida Atlantic has the home finale, the red shirts, and a Senior Day list that includes the people who work the building, not just the ones who play in it. Somebody is going to leave Boca with a seventh win and a cleaner path to December. Somebody is going to leave with a 6-5 record and a long ride home. The Owls asked the crowd to show up in red. The Bulls asked themselves to get a stop when it counts.

Boca Raton lights the city Christmas tree at Mizner Park on Saturday night, and the South Florida vs Florida Atlantic pick has to be settled a few hours before the switch gets flipped. My USF vs FAU pick is South Florida Bulls −1.5 with the Under 63.5, and my final is South Florida Bulls 33, Florida Atlantic Owls 29, kickoff 2:30 PM ET at Flagler Credit Union Stadium, where the Owls close their home schedule in red.

Kaia Brightwater has been waiting all week to say it. "We are unbeaten in Paradise. Five home games, five wins. Caden Veltkamp has 3,086 yards and 29 touchdowns, Easton Messer has 1,033 receiving yards, and you want me to worry about a team that just gave up 52 to Memphis?"

Reina Valdés-Kowalski does not flinch. "We gave up 52 and still put up 41, and Luke Kromenhoek ran 30 times for 146 yards. Your defense allows 35.3 a game, Kaia. We beat you 48-13 last October. Bring your tree."

The market moved toward Tampa. DraftKings opened Florida Atlantic −1.5 with the total at 69.5, as shown in ESPN pickcenter, and as of 9:20 PM ET Friday it sits at South Florida −1.5 and 63.5. That is a three-point swing across the zero, and I agree with it. USF allows 18.8 points a game, the best defense on this field by a lot. FAU allows 35.3. When Veltkamp has to throw 58 times, as he did in the 21-38 loss at Tulsa, the Owls stop being a home-run team and start being a long-afternoon team.

Kromenhoek is the quarterback to price. He has taken the most throws in each of the last three USF games, and he threw for 271 and three scores against Memphis without an interception. D.J. Crowther gives the Bulls 689 rushing yards and seven touchdowns, which matters against a defense that bleeds points.

My Under is the harder sell, because FAU games have gone 51-49 with Rice and 60-29 with UTSA. But the total already fell six points from the DraftKings open, and six of USF's ten games have finished with forty or fewer combined points. My 62 stays under the 63.5.
