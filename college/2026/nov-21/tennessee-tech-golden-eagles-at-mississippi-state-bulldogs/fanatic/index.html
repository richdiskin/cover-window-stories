GameDay Report

Tennessee Tech at Mississippi State: Fan Fest at 8, Senior Day at 11:38, and a Slide to End

Starkville starts early, and the athletic department published the whole morning so nobody could miss it. Coca-Cola Fan Fest and the ticket office open at 8 a.m., the Dawg Walk begins at 9:55, the gates open at 10, and the Senior Day ceremony is set for 11:38 a.m. before a noon Central kickoff against Tennessee Tech. That is a lot of ritual for a 4-6 Mississippi State team that has been looking for its first win since Week 4, and it is a loud welcome for a 9-2 FCS visitor that just finished a conference championship run of its own.

The Bulldogs' Friday notes hung the pride on the people who have kept showing up. Anthony Evans III is tied for the SEC lead with 65 catches and has 800 yards. Sanfrisco Magee has three catches and three touchdowns over the last two games, tying for the league lead in receiving scores. Kyle Ferrie has made 100 consecutive extra points and is seven points shy of 300 for his career. Will Whitson has scored in back-to-back weeks, a fumble return against Vanderbilt and a safety against Auburn. Center Canon Boone, Evans and cornerback Kelley Jones are each one start from 24 in a row dating to last season.

Tennessee Tech coach Bobby Wilder, in midweek comments relayed nationally, did not pretend the size gap is small, and he also did not come to Davis Wade Stadium to collect a souvenir. Mississippi State coach Jeff Lebby has been evaluating quarterbacks Parker Puckett and Brodie McWhorter on who can move the chains, score and take care of the football. Senior Day in Starkville is supposed to feel like a thank-you. The Golden Eagles are 9-2 and do not have to donate one. If the Bulldogs want the send-off to land, they have to end the slide first.

Tennessee Tech vs Mississippi State is the final 2026 home date at Davis Wade Stadium this Saturday, and the published gameday schedule puts a Senior Day ceremony at 11:38 a.m. before a noon Central kickoff. Mississippi State insider Wade Pettus calls it a cowbell goodbye, while Tennessee Tech visitor Harlan Pike says Jax Leatherwood and a 9-2 Golden Eagle team did not drive down from Cookeville to sit quietly in the visitor section.

Wade: Harlan, they put the Senior Day ceremony on the clock at 11:38. I have been ringing this cowbell since breakfast. I brought a feed-store receipt and a bandana, and the bandana is for the ceremony only.

Harlan: Save a corner of that bandana for when Leatherwood throws one past your safety. Two thousand nine hundred twenty-three passing yards. Twenty-eight touchdown throws. Your own week was a 9-41 loss to Auburn at home. We didn't come here to sit quietly in the visitor section.

Starkville has a split-screen Saturday on its hands. The home folks are planning a farewell, and the visitors showed up to finish a regular season that already includes a 6-2 Southern Conference record and a 31-38 loss to Mercer. Jeff Lebby's club is 4-6 and 2-6 in the SEC after opening 4-0. Bobby Wilder's Golden Eagles are 9-2 and 6-2 in the Southern Conference, and on Nov 16 they were still No. 12 in the AFCA poll and No. 13 in the Stats Perform poll.

Wade: Let me tell you something about this Mississippi State team, Harlan. It can still throw a party when the night is easy. The opener was 62-13 over UL Monroe. Fluff Bothwell has 649 rushing yards. Anthony Evans III has 65 catches for 800 yards. When the cowbell is loud and the seniors are on the field, this place still knows how to score.

Harlan: And Kamario Taylor has 17 touchdown throws this season. The last three games, AJ Swann has had the most throwing attempts, and the last five scores have been 0, 15, 3, 23 and 9. Leatherwood just set the single-season records for touchdowns and passing yards, even in a loss. M.J. Flowers has 688 rushing yards and seven scores. Tre' Holloway has 55 catches for 735 yards and eight scores. That's not a visitor looking for a souvenir.

Pike keeps a scrapbook, and the scrapbook says Tech's season has been more fireworks than heartache, with a 66-14 win over VMI and a 42-38 shootout at Wofford in the ledger, plus a 24-9 win at Chattanooga. The Mercer defeat stung, 31-38 at Tucker Stadium, after Tech had tied it three times. Leatherwood still went 31 of 49 for 294 yards in that one, and Qua Ashley ran for 100 yards and a score.

Harlan: The Mercer game still stings. My whole review of it fits on a napkin: we lost, and we still rewrote the record book. We came to Starkville anyway.

Wade: Your defense allows 21.9 points per game, and every cowbell in this building heard that. Jaray Bledsoe has 4.5 sacks. Jardin Gilbert has 60 tackles.

Harlan: Ring it all you want. Greg Turner III has 73 tackles. Hudson Tucker has six sacks. Those two have never once asked anybody for a photo op.

On one thing they nod along together: this is a first meeting in the recent record, and that means nobody in the building is playing last year's script. Wade wants the seniors to leave Davis Wade with a win they can carry into the Nov 27 trip to Oxford. Harlan wants a road finish that looks like a 9-2 team, not like a walking visit.

Here is how Senior Day can bite. The ceremony comes first, the cowbells come right behind it, and emotional legs can feel heavy by the second quarter. If Mississippi State starts fast, with Bothwell going downhill and Evans catching it in space, Tech's second half becomes a scramble to keep Leatherwood on schedule. If the Bulldogs start daydreaming about the next bus ride too early, a 2,923-yard quarterback has plenty of time to get comfortable.

Wade: Ceremony first, cowbells right behind it, and that receipt gets filled in before the band takes the field. Ring it if you mean it.

Harlan: Ring away. Every clang is just a countdown to the next Holloway catch.

Underneath the noise, both of them want the folks in the seats to get a real football game and a goodbye with some teeth in it. Wade wants it in maroon. Harlan wants a road Saturday Cookeville can take home.

Wade: Ring the cowbell for the people who already put in four years here.

Harlan: Cookeville did not send a 9-2 team to sit still.

My call: Mississippi State Bulldogs 38, Tennessee Tech Golden Eagles 17.
