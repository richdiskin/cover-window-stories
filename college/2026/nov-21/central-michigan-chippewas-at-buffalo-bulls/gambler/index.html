Central Michigan vs Buffalo Pick: Bulls −1.5, Under 45.5

Central Michigan Chippewas (5-5) at Buffalo Bulls (7-3)

7:30 PM ET Wed Nov 18 · Broadview Stadium · ESPNU

XFacebook

Somebody at Broadview Stadium is going home Wednesday with a freezer full of ribeyes, because Buffalo's MACtion Meat Raffle is back for the home finale. The Central Michigan vs Buffalo spread is short, and I'll take the Bulls at Buffalo Bulls −1.5 with the Under 45.5, kickoff 7:30 PM ET.

Kendra Woolcott is wearing shorts. It is November in Western New York. "Hundreds of pounds of meat, Brynn. Roasts. Steaks. And Messiah Burch running through your defense like he's late for the drawing."

Brynn Haverkamp zips her parka all the way up. "Ope, you're going to freeze. We beat you 38-19 last year, and I brought a thermos."

That's the honest starting point. Buffalo is 7-3, 5-1 in the MAC and first in the conference, but its points for and against are almost even at 24.3 and 24.1 a game, and a lot of its wins were tight. Two of the last four came in overtime. Central Michigan is 5-5, has won two in a row, 35-7 and 28-7, and allows 22.1 a game. A point and a half is a fair fight.

I side with Buffalo because of what it does when the offense gets going. The Bulls haven't lost a game this season when they score 21 or more, and they're 7-0 in those games. Burch is the reason they get there. He has 1,110 rushing yards, and he ran 31 times for 209 yards and 2 touchdowns at Ball State last week. Chance Morrow has 9 touchdown catches, and Jason Wright has 10 touchdown throws. Central Michigan has split quarterback duties between Jadyn Glasser and Angel Flores, with Flores also the team's leading rusher at 618 yards. That's a run-first offense, and Buffalo just ran for more than three hundred yards of its own at Ball State.

The last five meetings all went past 43.5 combined points, the closest by six and a half, and over the last four meetings each team won both of its home games. DraftKings opened Buffalo Bulls −1.5 with a 43.5 total. As of 10:46 PM ET Tuesday, the side is Buffalo Bulls −1.5 and the total is 45.5.

The room agrees on Buffalo and argues about the total. The Formula has Buffalo 25-22, takes Buffalo Bulls −1.5 and goes Over 45.5. Next Man Up has 23-20 with Buffalo and likes the Under 45.5. Letdown Watch has 25-19, likes Buffalo and goes Under 45.5. In The Trenches has no score this week and calls the fronts even, with no side and no over/under. Follow The Money has no side pick and no total pick.

Next Man Up: "Forty-three points, two and a half under, and I'll be warm inside while you stand in the raffle line."

The Formula: "You have Buffalo by three, Next Man Up. That's a field goal and a pot roast. Enjoy the heated seat."

My final is Buffalo Bulls 24, Central Michigan Chippewas 20. A four-point win clears the number by two and a half points, and 44 points stays a point and a half under the total. Kendra spends the third quarter checking raffle numbers in shorts. Brynn retreats to a heated tent somewhere near the parking lot.

Buffalo Bulls 24, Central Michigan Chippewas 20. Buffalo Bulls cover the 1.5. Under 45.5.
