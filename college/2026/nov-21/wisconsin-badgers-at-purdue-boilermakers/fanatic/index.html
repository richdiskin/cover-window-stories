Wisconsin vs Purdue: Mackey Friday, Ross-Ade Saturday

Wisconsin Badgers (6-4) at Purdue Boilermakers (3-7)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Ross-Ade Stadium · NBC

XFacebook

Purdue fans get a doubleheader this weekend: Purdue basketball hosts Oakland at Mackey Arena on Friday night, and 17 hours after tipoff Wisconsin vs Purdue kicks off next door at Ross-Ade Stadium in the home finale. Purdue insider Barnaby Wolcott-Reese plans to do both on about five hours of sleep, and Wisconsin insider Lars "Brat" Ellefson says the Badgers came to West Lafayette to make sure Saturday is the quieter of the two nights.

Barnaby: Lars, this is a basketball school that happens to have a football stadium across the parking lot. Friday we fill Mackey. Saturday we walk over and finish the football season at home. I've drawn the walk on graph paper, shortcut through the grass included.

Lars: Barnaby, I've got a polka for every step. Wisconsin travels well. We'll bring the band in our heads and the cheese in our coats.

Here's what makes this matchup tricky to talk about. Neither team has been at its best lately. Purdue has lost four straight, to Washington, Penn State, Maryland and Iowa, and it's 3-7. Wisconsin is 6-4 and already bowl eligible, but it lost 24-21 at Maryland last Saturday and has spent the season with an offense that comes and goes.

Barnaby: Our losing run has a lot of near misses in it. We lost to Wake Forest in overtime by two. We lost to Maryland by five. Ryan Browne has thrown for 2,418 yards. Fame Ijeboi has 684 rushing yards and ten scores. We move the ball. We just need the last ten yards to cooperate.

Lars: You move it, sure. Then you give up 31.9 a game. Our defense allows 17.1. You can't engineer your way around that, Barnaby, no matter how many pencils you've got in that pocket protector.

Wisconsin's defense is the reason Lars sleeps at night. Cooper Catalano has 99 tackles, and Sebastian Cheeks has seven sacks. Iowa shut out the Badgers 30-0 and UCLA held them to seven, but Wisconsin's defense has also carried it through wins at Penn State and over USC.

Lars: Catalano doesn't tackle you. He files a claim on you. Ninety-nine tackles, Barnaby. That's a dairy herd's worth of stops.

Barnaby: And Asaad Waseem has 802 receiving yards for us. He catches it, and then your secondary goes to find him. Jojo Hayden has 77 tackles on our side, and he hits like a cooling tower falling over.

The quarterback picture on Wisconsin's side has shifted. Colton Joseph led the Badgers in throws most of the season, with 1,368 yards and 11 touchdowns. The last two weeks, Ryan Hopkins has thrown the most passes, throwing three touchdowns in a 31-10 win over Rutgers and then struggling at Maryland, where he went 11 of 22 for 77 yards.

Lars: Hopkins was great against Rutgers. Maryland was a hiccup. Darrion Dupree ran for two scores in that game. We'll run it more Saturday. It's November. In November, Wisconsin hands it off and dares you to stop us.

Barnaby: You'll hand it off into Ross-Ade with a crowd that just came off a basketball high. I'll ring my train bell every time Ijeboi gets loose.

Lars: Your train bell is going to be very lonely.

There's history here, and it isn't kind to Barnaby. Wisconsin has won all eight meetings since 2016, including a 52-6 game in 2024 and a 47-44 thriller in 2018. Purdue fans remember that last one. They'd rather not.

Barnaby: Eight is a lot. I know. But we beat Minnesota 41-13 this year. We beat Illinois on the road. This team can play. It just needs somebody to tell it the season isn't over until the last whistle at home.

Lars: I'll tell them myself. Then I'll beat them.

The football weekend ends at Ross-Ade, and Barnaby wants the band, the train and the seniors to get one more happy afternoon in West Lafayette. Lars wants a bowl team to look like one on the road.

Barnaby: Boiler up. I'm engineering a comeback.

Lars: Engineer whatever you want. We'll be running the same play twelve times.

Two nights in a row of noise on the Purdue side of campus, and the Badgers grind out the second one.

My call: Wisconsin Badgers 28, Purdue Boilermakers 21.
