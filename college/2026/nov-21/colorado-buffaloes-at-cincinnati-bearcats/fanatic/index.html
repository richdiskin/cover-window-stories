Colorado vs Cincinnati: Zoo Lights, Nippert Senior Day

Colorado Buffaloes (4-6) at Cincinnati Bearcats (5-5)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Nippert Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Two days before Colorado vs Cincinnati, the Cincinnati Zoo flips the switch on its 44th annual PNC Festival of Lights, five million bulbs glowing across town from Historic Nippert Stadium. By Saturday at noon, the Bearcats want a different kind of glow, because Cincinnati is honoring 20 seniors before kickoff, and Colorado insider Moss Feldstein says the Buffaloes came east to turn the lights out on Senior Day.

Lionel "Skyline" Abernathy got to the zoo on opening night and headed straight for the Outpost Café, because the zoo serves Skyline Chili and he has never once ignored a sign.

Lionel: Moss, you have to understand what Thursday means around here. Fiona's Fairyland lights up, the hippo becomes a holiday icon again, and the whole city puts on a sweater. Then Saturday we send off 20 seniors at Nippert. This is our week. That's a five-way take, with extra onions.

Moss: Statistically, Lionel, five million lights is a lot of electricity for a team that's lost four of its last five. I'm not here to be rude. I'm here to be accurate.

He has a point, and Lionel knows it. Cincinnati started 4-0 and has spent the second half of the season limping. The Bearcats lost 34-7 at Arizona, 45-42 at West Virginia, 49-25 to Utah, 31-24 at Houston and 41-13 at Iowa State last Saturday. The one bright spot in that stretch was a 37-24 win over Texas Tech at home.

Lionel: At home, Moss. That's the whole story. We're 4-1 at Nippert this year. On the road we turn into four-way without the beans. At home we're the full bowl.

Moss: I'll grant you the bowl. But you gave up 41 to Iowa State, and we just put 42 on Houston.

That Friday night in Boulder was the best evening Colorado has had in a while. The Buffaloes beat Houston 42-32. Richard Young ran 21 times for 99 yards and two scores, Isaac Wilson threw for 217 yards, and Joseph Williams caught five balls for 80 yards and a touchdown. Williams has 63 catches for 933 yards on the season, which makes him the receiver Cincinnati's secondary will be talking about all week.

Moss: Joseph Williams is my whole hammock philosophy. Relaxed, then gone. Your safeties will look up and he'll already be in the end zone eating a granola bar.

Lionel: Jasper Beeler has 77 tackles. He doesn't let anybody eat in the end zone. House rules.

Cincinnati's answer on offense is two names. JC French IV has thrown 16 touchdowns and only three interceptions all season, which means he takes care of the ball like it's the last cheese coney in the pot. Cole Tabb has 977 rushing yards and ten scores, and he ran for 165 even in the loss at Ames.

Moss: Tabb is legit. I'll say it out loud. But French threw for 97 yards last week.

Lionel: And Tabb ran for 165 and caught eight more balls. When a running back is your whole offense in a 41-13 loss, you give him the ball 30 times at home with the seniors watching.

Colorado has a road problem, and Moss doesn't hide from it. The Buffaloes are 1-4 away from Boulder this year, with the only road win a 14-13 squeaker at Georgia Tech in the opener. They lost 41-7 at Northwestern, 23-13 at Baylor, 57-24 at Oklahoma State and 20-10 at Arizona State.

Moss: That's the thing about altitude, Lionel. When we come down to sea level, we get light-headed in the other direction.

Lionel: Cincinnati isn't sea level. We're river level. It's worse.

The last time these two met was 2024, in Boulder, and Colorado won 34-23. Moss brings it up the way people bring up a road trip where everything went right.

Lionel: Different building. Different month. Different chili.

The Senior Day ceremony is where Lionel gets soft for a minute, which he'd deny under oath. Twenty players, all of them walking out to a home crowd that has watched them through good and bad Novembers. The zoo will be lit up again that night, and Lionel wants the families to drive past it on the way home after a win.

Lionel: I want those seniors under the lights at the zoo Saturday night, holding a hot cocoa, not answering a single question about what went wrong.

Moss: And I want Joseph Williams past a thousand yards by the fourth quarter. Then I'll buy you a hot cocoa myself.

Lionel: Deal. Three-way. No beans.

The lights at the zoo run into January. The seniors only get one walk at Nippert, and Tabb gives them a winner to remember.

My call: Cincinnati Bearcats 30, Colorado Buffaloes 24.
