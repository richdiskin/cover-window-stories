Georgia State vs Marshall Pick: Panthers −1.5 and the Under 57.5

Georgia State Panthers (5-5) at Marshall Thundering Herd (5-5)

4:00 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Joan C. Edwards Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Every one of Marshall's five home games has been decided by seven points or fewer, and the sixth is Senior Day against Georgia State, with both teams sitting at 5-5 and one win from bowl eligibility. The Georgia State vs Marshall spread is just 1.5, so this is close to a pick-the-winner game, and I'm with the visitors: Georgia State Panthers −1.5, Under 57.5, with Georgia State Panthers 30, Marshall Thundering Herd 27 at 4:00 PM ET.

Malik Overstreet has the third-down chart out. "We score 32.7 a game, Mercy. Xavier Esqillion has ten sacks. Rodshaun Dorsey has nine touchdown catches. We won 39-23 at App State two weeks ago, and Lanear McCrary Jr. ran for 86 against Georgia Southern."

Mercy Holderby answers with the loudest "We Are" in the room. "Malik, we're 3-1 against y'all and 2-0 in Huntington. We beat you 30-18 last year. Jo'Shon Barbie ran for 108 against App State. Caleb Clark-Glover has 71 tackles. This is our house."

Here's why I lay the small number. Georgia State's offense has a clear advantage over Marshall's. The Panthers score 32.7 a game, Marshall 21.7. Marshall's defense allows 30.7, and it gave up 49 at Georgia Southern and 45 at James Madison. Marshall's three home wins came by two over Middle Tennessee, one over Gardner-Webb and seven over South Alabama. Its home losses were by six to Coastal Carolina and four to App State. My 30-27 is a three-point Georgia State win, a point and a half past the 1.5.

The risk: Georgia State scored 17 against Georgia Southern last week, and Ayden Pereira threw for 75 yards. Pereira has led the team in throwing attempts for four straight games after Cameran Brown led the first six.

DraftKings opened Georgia State −1.5 and 57.5, as shown in ESPN pickcenter, and nothing has moved.

The Under is the closest call on my card, a half point. Five of Georgia State's ten games stayed under 57.5, and six of Marshall's did. Marshall's offense scored 13 and 16 in its last two games. My 57 needs one stalled drive somewhere.

The cards split. The Formula has Georgia State 29, Marshall 25, lays the 1.5 and likes the Under 57.5. Next Man Up has Georgia State 33, Marshall 29, lays the 1.5 and likes the Over. Letdown Watch has Marshall 28, Georgia State 27, likes Marshall +1.5 and likes the Under. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call on this one. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Letdown Watch: "One point on my card. When it's that close, I take the home team on Senior Day."

Next Man Up: "Both defenses give up 27 or more a game. Sixty points wouldn't shock me."

On Friday night, the Gallaher Village Public Library in Huntington sells books for a dollar apiece or five dollars a bag, any bag. Saturday, both teams want the same thing: a bowl trip and a few more weeks of football.

Georgia State Panthers 30, Marshall Thundering Herd 27. Georgia State Panthers cover the 1.5. Under 57.5.
