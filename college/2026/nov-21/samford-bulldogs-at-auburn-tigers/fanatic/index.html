Samford vs Auburn: Senior Day at Jordan-Hare

Samford Bulldogs (4-7) at Auburn Tigers (6-4)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Jordan-Hare Stadium · SEC Network

XFacebook

Samford vs Auburn closes Jordan-Hare Stadium for the 2026 home year this Saturday at 3:30, a date Auburn will open by honoring 27 seniors. Auburn insider Lacey Bellamy says that makes Saturday a War Eagle afternoon with the Alabama trip already dated Nov 28, while Samford visitor Avery Shades says a 3-3-5 defense and a month of close October wins did not ride over from Homewood just to watch the seniors walk.

Lacey: Avery, this is our house, and it is Senior Day. War Eagle is a greeting. It is also a starting gun.

Avery: You can save the starting gun for when we show you the pictures. Your own lineman, Kenneth McManus IV, called our 3-3-5 unique. He said it was something you have not really seen. Gabarri Johnson has 1,752 passing yards and nine scores. Marquise Henderson has 598 receiving yards. We took three of our last four from Furman, Wofford and East Tennessee State before a 12-17 home finale against Western Carolina. That is a real October, even if the year is 4-7.

That's the tension in Auburn this week. One side is 6-4 and 4-4 in the SEC, with Byrum Brown throwing for 1,972 yards and 14 scores, running for 644 yards and nine more, and coming off a 41-9 win at Mississippi State. The other side is 4-7 and 4-4 in the Southern Conference, finishing the regular season on the road in a stadium that has not lost to this visitor in 15 home dates.

Lacey: Here is the thing you don't understand about this Auburn team. It can still go a long way in a hurry. Forty-three to 8 over Southern Miss. Forty-one to 9 at Mississippi State, with Brown 19 of 26 for 232 yards and three scores, and Keshaun Singleton catching seven balls for 103 yards and two scores. Singleton already has 52 catches. Alex Golesh called the seniors an invested group and said this week is about finishing the right way for the fans and for them. A win makes us 7-4 before we go to Alabama.

Avery: And your largest winning margin this season is 35 points. You have not hung a 49-point night on anybody. The series is 29-0-1, I know the ledger, and I also know 24 of those games were played in 1945 or earlier. The last six since 1992 have gone 218-43 for you, including 45-13 here in 2023 and 52-0 here in 2019. We came to keep this in the clock, not to rewrite a book that starts before the war.

Shades did his homework too, and he will tell you Samford's season has been uneven and then suddenly stubborn. The Bulldogs lost at UAB and at Chattanooga, dropped Mercer at home, then won 13-6 at Furman, 10-9 over Wofford and 37-35 at East Tennessee State. John Grass has them in that 3-3-5 look, and Ken Cherry has 387 rushing yards. Will Shields was the passing leader in the Western Carolina game, 20 of 33 for 280 yards. I am not naming a starter from a box score. I am naming the form.

Avery: I can live with a last regular-season chapter that has Henderson in it and a defense that makes people think twice about the pictures. Grass is sending us into Jordan-Hare to play, not to visit.

Lacey: Your October is proud, and it should be. Our front is still coming. Elijah Melendez has 73 tackles and four sacks. The last two games have 25 tackles for loss, including 13 and seven sacks at Mississippi State. We remember 45-13. We remember 52-0. And we remember that 27 seniors walk this field one more time before kickoff.

Avery: We remember too. That is why we came.

They agree on one point: 29-0-1 does not tell you how Saturday will feel. Lacey wants the home sideline ready for the trip to Tuscaloosa. Avery wants a road finish that looks like a club that just spent October winning close ones.

The calendar is the danger here. If Auburn starts fast, with Brown throwing and running and Singleton getting loose, the Bulldogs will spend the second half trying to keep the clock honest. If the Tigers start thinking about Nov 28 before the first quarter is over, a 3-3-5 visitor can hang around the way a team that just won 10-9 and 13-6 is built to hang around.

Lacey: The band gets loud, the Tigers get going, and then the afternoon gets decided. That's the order of operations. War Eagle, and I mean it.

Avery: And while you're looking at Nov 28, we'll be showing you the pictures. That's my order of operations.

Lacey: Twenty-seven seniors walk this field once more, and then we go to Tuscaloosa.

Avery: We came to show you the 3-3-5 and to take a road Saturday back to campus.

My call: Auburn Tigers 45, Samford Bulldogs 7.
