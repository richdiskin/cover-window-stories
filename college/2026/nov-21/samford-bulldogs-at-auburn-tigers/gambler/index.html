Samford at Auburn: Jordan-Hare Closes the Year, and Samford Walks In

Market check, Samford at Auburn: DraftKings AUB -48.5, total 59.5.

Auburn's Saturday gameday advisory framed this as the final home game of 2026 at Jordan-Hare Stadium, a 2:30 p.m. CT kickoff against Samford on SEC Network, with Tiger Walk set 2.5 hours before kickoff and a pregame Senior Day ceremony. How many seniors walk? The school's own pages do not agree. The Opening Drive on Nov. 18 said 29. The Saturday advisory said 28. Both agree the ceremony is happening, and that disagreement is worth a footnote, not a guess.

The number as of Nov. 21, 2026, 9:08 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at AUB -48.5, total 59.5. The Cover Window's published pick still reads Samford Bulldogs +48.5 and Under 59.5. The market still matches the line the card used. The card's projected final is Auburn Tigers 45, Samford Bulldogs 7 (Total 52). That pick was published against the Nov. 20, 2026, 9:20 PM ET line (Auburn Tigers −48.5, total 59.5), it has not been changed, and this story issues no new one. Prices move, so check yours before kickoff. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

Auburn's gameday advisory and The Opening Drive were about Senior Day, Tiger Walk, stadium construction and the Samford look. Neither published a dated injury list for Saturday, and ESPN's game page returned no injury report. Samford's official wrap-up similarly did not designate anyone out or questionable. This story does not invent availability. Recheck both athletic departments before the 3:30 p.m. ET kickoff.

Coach Alex Golesh, in that Opening Drive, called the group invested. "That group is such a mix of guys who have been here, guys who came in," he said. "I tell those guys every Friday night I'm grateful for them." He also told his team not to take a breath: if Auburn is going to build a program that plays meaningful November games, "we have to work that way, practice that way, prepare that way." The Tigers are 6-4 and 4-4 in the SEC after a 41-9 win at Mississippi State. The Opening Drive said Auburn is 30-0-1 all-time against Samford and 16-0 at home in the series.

Samford is 4-7 after a 12-17 loss to Western Carolina, a game the Bulldogs listed as their home finale. The official Samford preview said the season wraps up Saturday on the Plains. Gabarri Johnson has 1,752 passing yards; Ken Cherry has 387 on the ground. Auburn's Byrum Brown has 1,972 passing yards and is also the Tigers' leading rusher in the facts we can source. Offensive lineman Kenneth McManus IV, talking about Samford's 3-3-5 look, said, "They present a lot of unique pictures. We'll definitely have to be prepared for that."

A home finale, a Senior Day whose head count even the school has written two ways, and an FCS visitor that just dropped its own closer: that is the afternoon on the Plains. Golesh asked his team to finish the week the right way for the fans and the seniors. The record against Samford says Auburn usually does. The 3-3-5 is the new picture in front of them.

Auburn's home finale at Jordan-Hare Stadium is 3:30 p.m. Saturday against Samford, and 27 seniors will be honored before kickoff, with Alabama already dated for Nov 28. DraftKings opened and still shows Auburn Tigers −48.5 and 59.5, so I am on Samford Bulldogs +48.5 and Under 59.5.

The Tigers will honor 27 seniors before kickoff. Alex Golesh, in Auburn's Opening Drive, called it an invested group and said the week is about finishing the right way for the fans and the seniors. A win would make Auburn 7-4 and clinch a winning season before the Tigers go to Alabama on Nov 28. That is a lot of calendar for one Saturday in November.

Lacey Bellamy, who treats "War Eagle" as a starting gun, has the ledger ready. Auburn leads the series 29-0-1, including 15-0 at home. Twenty-four of those games were played in 1945 or earlier. The last six meetings since 1992 have gone 218-43 for the Tigers, including 45-13 here in 2023 and 52-0 here in 2019. Byrum Brown has 1,972 passing yards, 14 touchdown throws, 644 rushing yards and nine rushing scores. Last Saturday at Mississippi State he was 19 of 26 for 232 yards and three scores in a 41-9 win. Keshaun Singleton caught seven balls for 103 yards and two scores in that game, and he already has 52 catches this season.

Avery Shades came over from Homewood with a 3-3-5 defense and a short, stubborn October. "You have not won a game this season by 49 points. The largest margin was 35, against Southern Miss. You just beat Mississippi State 41-9, and that is 32. Forty-eight and a half is a lot of orange for a Senior Day with Alabama already on the wall."

He has a case. Samford is 4-7 and 4-4 in the Southern Conference after a 12-17 home finale against Western Carolina. Gabarri Johnson has 1,752 passing yards and nine touchdown throws on the season. Marquise Henderson has 598 receiving yards. The Bulldogs did take three of four from Furman, Wofford and East Tennessee State before that Western Carolina loss, and John Grass will try to keep this in the clock with a look Auburn's own linemen called unique. Kenneth McManus IV said it was something the Tigers have not really seen.

DraftKings opened this at Auburn −48.5 with a 59.5 total, and that card has not moved. The Action Network consensus sits on the same number. Follow The Money has no side pick and no total pick.

My final is Auburn Tigers 45, Samford Bulldogs 7. Auburn wins by 38, so Samford stays inside the 48.5. The 52 combined points stay under the 59.5. Brown and Singleton should get the Tigers a lead on Senior Day. Samford should still find the end zone once, and then the clock, and a long list of seniors, will do the rest.

The Formula has no pick. In The Trenches has Auburn Tigers 41, Samford Bulldogs 10 and takes Samford Bulldogs +48.5, with no over/under call.

A 6-4 home team should finish Jordan-Hare with a win. Asking for a 49-point night against a visitor that just spent a month winning close ones is a taller order, and the trip to Alabama is already dated Nov 28.

Auburn Tigers 45, Samford Bulldogs 7. Samford Bulldogs cover the 48.5. Under 59.5.
