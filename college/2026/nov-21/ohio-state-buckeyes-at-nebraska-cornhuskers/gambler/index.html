Ohio State vs Nebraska Prediction: OSU −3, Over 41.5

Ohio State Buckeyes (7-3) at Nebraska Cornhuskers (6-4)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Memorial Stadium (Lincoln, NE) · Fox

XFacebook

Lincoln sold out its final home game early, and the Ohio State vs Nebraska spread has drifted toward the Huskers ever since DraftKings opened it. The number now is Ohio State −3, total 41.5. I'm taking Ohio State Buckeyes −3 and Over 41.5, my pick for Senior Day at Memorial Stadium.

That line move is the story of this handicap. DraftKings opened Ohio State −5.5 with a 47.5 total. The spread has dropped two and a half points and the total six. The market has marked down Ohio State's offense since Luke Fahey took over the throwing three weeks ago, and it has marked up a Nebraska defense allowing 14.9 points a game. I think the market went too far.

Darla Rasmussen will tell you the sellout and the seniors are worth a field goal. "Bowl eligible. Shut out Rutgers 31-0. Three of our four losses in that skid were one-score games." All true. Nebraska is better than its 6-4 suggests.

But here is Buck Holloway's case, and I agree with it. Fahey went 21 of 32 for 201 yards and three touchdowns against Northwestern, and he's throwing to Jeremiah Smith, who has 80 catches for 1,317 yards and 15 touchdowns. Bo Jackson ran for 114 last week. Ohio State scores 34.9 a game and allows 18.4, and it has won all seven meetings in this stretch since 2016.

Nebraska's offense is the concern. The Huskers scored 17, 14, 10 and 13 in their four straight losses before Rutgers. Anthony Colandrea has 18 touchdown throws, but against good defenses this team has struggled to find the end zone.

My final is Ohio State Buckeyes 27, Nebraska Cornhuskers 21. Ohio State wins by six and clears the 3. The 48 combined points clear 41.5 comfortably. A six-point drop in the total is an overreaction for a game with Smith on the field and a Nebraska offense that just scored 31 on the road.

The room agrees on the side. The Formula likes Ohio State −3 at 28-22. Letdown Watch likes Ohio State −3 at 26-21. Next Man Up takes Ohio State −3 at 25-21. All three like the Over 41.5. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call this week. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Next Man Up: "Twenty-five points. I'm the cautious one, and I still have Ohio State covering."

Letdown Watch: "Cautious is how The Formula got a 28-22. Sold-out crowd, Senior Day, and you three still trust a new quarterback on the road."

The Formula: "I trust Jeremiah Smith. The quarterback just has to find him."

Memorial Stadium will be red and loud for the seniors. Smith is the difference, and Ohio State pulls away late.

Ohio State Buckeyes 27, Nebraska Cornhuskers 21. Ohio State Buckeyes cover the 3. Over 41.5.
