Wofford at Ole Miss: A 62-Week Ranking Streak Meets a Farewell Class

Market check, Wofford at Ole Miss: DraftKings MISS -49.5, total 59.5.

Oxford will honor a senior class that coach Pete Golding says is 40-11 and can become the winningest group in Ole Miss history with three more victories, and it will do it under a ranking streak the school is proud of. Ole Miss is No. 25 this week, which the program's Friday "What To Watch For" notes said extends a run of 62 consecutive weeks in the AP Poll, the second-longest streak in school history behind only 1957-63.

The number as of Nov. 21, 2026, 8:53 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at MISS -49.5, total 59.5. The Cover Window's published pick still reads Wofford Terriers +49.5 and Under 59.5. The market still matches the line the card used. The card's projected final is Ole Miss Rebels 49, Wofford Terriers 7 (Total 56). That pick was published against the Nov. 20, 2026, 9:20 PM ET line (Ole Miss Rebels −49.5, total 59.5), it has not been changed, and this story issues no new one. Injuries: Ole Miss and Wofford had not posted a dated conference availability list in the official notes we used. ESPN's game file also returned no injury report. This story does not declare any player's status. Refresh official designations before kickoff.

The Rebels are 6-4 and host 5-6 Wofford at 11 a.m. Central at Vaught-Hemingway Stadium, just the second meeting in the series after a 2016 game in Oxford. Golding spent practice week talking about the people, not the mismatch. He called center Brycen Sanders "a warrior" who "loves this place" and "loves the game" and will give everything he has "regardless if he's 100 percent or not." Tight end Caleb Odom, who has 34 catches this season and ranks ninth in program history among tight ends with 53 career receptions, said the goal now is finishing with nine wins. "We built a brotherhood over this year and that's something that can't be broken," Odom said. He also said the Egg Bowl is out there, "though the focus right now is on Wofford."

That visitor is closing its own season. Wofford announced this month that head coach Shawn Watson will conclude his coaching career after 2026, and the school named Mike Houston as his successor. The Terriers' own preview called Saturday the final game of the year, coming after three straight losses. Ole Miss quarterback Trinidad Chambliss is among the national top 20 in passing touchdowns (19) and passing yards (2,486), and pass rusher Suntarine Perkins has 7.0 tackles for loss and 3.5 sacks this season, adding to career marks the school lists among the program's best.

Golding has been telling his team to treat the day like an SEC game anyway. Odom's version was simpler: "We just need to go out there and have fun. Just play free and just play fast." Oxford has a farewell to give. The Rebels still have to earn it.

Vaught-Hemingway Stadium will honor 17 Ole Miss seniors at noon Saturday, with Wofford visiting and Mississippi State already dated for Nov 27. DraftKings lists the Ole Miss Rebels −49.5 and a 59.5 total, and my play is Wofford Terriers +49.5 and Under 59.5.

The athletic department will honor those 17 student-athletes before kickoff at Vaught-Hemingway Stadium. Pete Golding has already done the math on the class. "They're 40 and 11 right now, that group, and they have the opportunity to go win three more," he said. "They'll be the winningest class in the history of the program." Tight end Caleb Odom put the target in plain English: finishing with nine wins.

Langston Fairbanks, who can turn a first down into a paragraph, has the Terriers filed under "last chapter." Wofford has lost its last three, the most recent a 17-20 home defeat to East Tennessee State, and this is the final game of Shawn Watson's coaching career. Watson announced he will retire at the conclusion of the 2026 season, and Wofford named Mike Houston as the next head coach on Nov 16. Saturday is still Watson's goodbye.

Calhoun Vines came up from Spartanburg with a running back and a last-game stubbornness. "Gerald Modest Jr. has 1,054 yards and nine scores on 220 carries. He is 11th in the FCS. Your largest winning margin this season is 35 points, against Vanderbilt. Forty-nine and a half is a lot of Grove, even on Senior Day."

He has a case. Trinidad Chambliss is 11-1 as a starter at Vaught-Hemingway Stadium, he has 2,486 passing yards and 19 touchdown throws, and he is a Davey O'Brien Award semifinalist. Tah'j Butler had 15 tackles at Oklahoma, a career high. Joshua Dye ran 11 times for 58 yards and a score in that same 17-24 loss. The Rebels will get theirs. They also have not won a game this season by 49 points, and they have the Egg Bowl next on Nov 27. A team that just lost to Georgia 7-42 and to Oklahoma 17-24 has every reason to start fast and every reason to get people off the field once the lead is safe.

DraftKings opened this at Ole Miss −49.5 with a 60.5 total. The side hasn't budged, and the total has dipped to 59.5 as of 9:20 PM ET Friday. The Action Network open on the total was 59.5, and the consensus now sits at 60.5. Follow The Money has no side pick and likes the Over 60.5.

My final is Ole Miss Rebels 49, Wofford Terriers 7. Ole Miss wins by 42, so Wofford stays inside the 49.5. The 56 combined points stay under the 59.5. Modest can still find the end zone late, after the seniors have had their moment and the backups are on the field. Chambliss and the honor ceremony should produce a first half that looks like a farewell party. The second half looks like a clock.

The Formula has no pick. In The Trenches has Ole Miss Rebels 48, Wofford Terriers 10 and takes Wofford Terriers +49.5, with no over/under call.

A Grove farewell can still be a win without turning into a 50-point night, especially with the Egg Bowl already dated Nov 27.

Ole Miss Rebels 49, Wofford Terriers 7. Wofford Terriers cover the 49.5. Under 59.5.
