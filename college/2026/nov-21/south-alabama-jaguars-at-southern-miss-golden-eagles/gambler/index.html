South Alabama vs Southern Miss Pick: Golden Eagles +4.5 and the Under 63.5

South Alabama Jaguars (5-5) at Southern Miss Golden Eagles (3-7)

4:00 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · M. M. Roberts Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

South Alabama has one road win all season, 56-49 at Arkansas State back in October, and that's the number I keep coming back to on South Alabama vs Southern Miss. Southern Miss closes its home schedule with Senior Day and Military Appreciation, plus the second annual Turkey Toss in Spirit Park, and I'll take the home team and the points with the Under: Southern Miss Golden Eagles +4.5, Under 63.5, with South Alabama Jaguars 32, Southern Miss Golden Eagles 30 at 4:00 PM ET.

Azalea Fairhope-Grice blows her whistle like it's a float parade. "We beat Troy 27-14 last week, Dee. Trae'von Dunbar ran for 153 yards and two touchdowns. PJ Martin has 639 on the year. And we've won all six meetings since 2020."

Delphine "Dee" Broussard smiles sweetly and gets spicy. "Azalea, y'all are 1-4 on the road. You lost by four at Tulane, by seven at Marshall and by two at Louisiana. Steven Robinson has 876 rushing yards and ten touchdowns, Mario Sanders has 817 receiving yards, and Mathis Haygood has 90 tackles."

That road record is my case. South Alabama's road games ended 24-28, 21-45, 56-49, 13-20 and 26-28. Four of those five finished within seven points. The Jaguars score 31.9 a game, but outside the Arkansas State shootout their road scores were 24, 21, 13 and 26. Southern Miss won 35-31 over Louisiana at home in October. My 32-30 is a two-point South Alabama win, inside the 4.5.

The risk is loud. Southern Miss lost 54-23 to James Madison and 63-31 at Louisiana Tech, and John White threw four interceptions last week. The Golden Eagles allow 36.5 points a game. South Alabama also beat Southern Miss by seven or more in all six of those meetings, including 42-35 last year.

DraftKings opened South Alabama −5.5 and 62.5, as shown in ESPN pickcenter. The side has come down a point to −4.5, and the total is up a point to 63.5.

The Under needs South Alabama's road offense to show up again. Six of South Alabama's ten games stayed under 63.5, and five of Southern Miss's did. Southern Miss's last two went way over. My 62 is a point and a half under the 63.5.

The cards split again. The Formula has South Alabama 31, Southern Miss 28, takes Southern Miss +4.5 and likes the Under 63.5. Next Man Up has South Alabama 37, Southern Miss 35, takes Southern Miss +4.5 and likes the Over. Letdown Watch has South Alabama 33, Southern Miss 27, lays the 4.5 and likes the Under. In The Trenches has Southern Miss 31, South Alabama 26, likes South Alabama −4.5, and has no total pick. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick.

Letdown Watch: "Southern Miss has given up more than a hundred points in its last two games. I can't take the points with that defense."

The Formula: "South Alabama on the road is a different team. Three points or so feels right."

Blake Anderson's first season at Southern Miss has one more home game in it, and Senior Day can make a struggling team play its best four quarters of the year.

South Alabama Jaguars 32, Southern Miss Golden Eagles 30. Southern Miss Golden Eagles cover the 4.5. Under 63.5.
