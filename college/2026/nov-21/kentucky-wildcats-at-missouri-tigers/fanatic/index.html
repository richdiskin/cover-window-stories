Kentucky at Missouri: Military Night, One Day After a Centennial

Kentucky Wildcats (7-3) at Missouri Tigers (6-4)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Memorial Stadium · SEC Network

XFacebook

Memorial Stadium was dedicated on November 20, 1926, so Saturday night's Kentucky Wildcats vs Missouri Tigers game comes one hundred years and one day later, on Mizzou's Military Appreciation night, with the crowd asked to wear black for a building named for the 117 Missouri students and alumni who died in World War I. Delphine Arceneaux-Moss, who reports every Mizzou development like it just crossed the wire, has been counting down to this date for months, and Rhett Abernathy-Coyle drove in from horse country outside Lexington with a garment bag full of black and a Kentucky lapel pin he refuses to take off.

Delphine: Breaking news. The stadium is a hundred years old and one day.

Rhett: That's not breaking. That's a calendar.

Delphine: It's breaking for anybody who hasn't checked the calendar.

Rhett: Fair enough. I'll wear black. I won't wear gold.

The tribute is the heart of the night, and both of them know it. Missouri students and alumni raised the money and did a lot of the labor to build the place, and it was dedicated to the ones who never came home. Mizzou has spent this whole centennial year honoring that, and Saturday is the most fitting night of the bunch.

Then there's the football, and it's tense. Both teams have lost two straight. Kentucky is 7-3 but fell 34-6 at Tennessee and 21-14 at home to Florida. Missouri is 6-4 after losing 34-20 to Texas and 48-21 at Georgia.

Rhett: We needed a breather. We're rested now.

Delphine: You lost to Florida at home. That's not rest. That's a nap.

Rhett has his reasons for hope, and they start with the quarterback. Kenny Minchey has thrown for 2,160 yards with 15 touchdowns and only two interceptions, and before the last two weeks he was winning tight games all over the league: 35-34 in overtime at South Carolina, 35-34 in overtime against LSU, 21-13 at Oklahoma. CJ Baxter has eight rushing touchdowns, and Kenny Darby leads the receivers with 601 yards. On defense, Grant Godfrey has 91 tackles, and Mi'Quise Humphrey-Grace has six sacks.

Rhett: Minchey has the bloodlines. Calm, strong finish, never panics in the stretch. You can't teach that, you breed it.

Delphine: He's a quarterback, not a colt.

Rhett: Everybody's a colt to me.

Missouri's case starts with Jamal Roberts, who has carried it 228 times for 1,133 yards and nine touchdowns. Austin Simmons has 21 touchdown throws and just three interceptions, and Cayden Lee has 65 catches for 879 yards. The defense has teeth too: Nicholas Rodriguez has 105 tackles, and Daeden Hopkins has seven sacks.

Delphine: Developing story. Roberts is going to run the ball forty times.

Rhett: Is that confirmed?

Delphine: Sources close to me say yes.

Rhett: The source is you.

Delphine: I'm very reliable.

The history favors the visitors lately. Kentucky won six of the eight meetings from 2016 through 2023, including 15-14 in 2018 and 29-7 in 2019. Missouri won 38-21 in 2023, the most recent meeting. But the Tigers are 4-1 at home this season, with wins over Florida 45-17 and Texas A&M 27-6, and the lone loss came against Texas.

Rhett: Six of eight. That's a strong sire.

Delphine: And the last one was ours. New bloodline.

What decides it? Kentucky has to find the offense it had in October, and Minchey has to make throws against a defense that will sell out to stop Baxter. Missouri has to keep feeding Roberts and let Simmons choose his moments. The Tigers have the better ground game and are at home in a building that means a lot this week.

Delphine: Final bulletin. Black jerseys in the stands, Roberts in the end zone, and a hundred-year-old stadium that still sounds young.

Rhett: My bulletin says it's close until the fourth quarter.

Delphine: I'll print both. Mine on the front page.

Roberts wears down the Kentucky front, Simmons hits Lee for the big one, and the crowd in black stays loud right to the end.

My call: Missouri Tigers 29, Kentucky Wildcats 22.
