Kentucky vs Missouri Pick: Tigers −4.5, Over 48.5

Kentucky Wildcats (7-3) at Missouri Tigers (6-4)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Memorial Stadium · SEC Network

XFacebook

One hundred years and one day after Memorial Stadium was dedicated to the one hundred seventeen Missouri students and alumni who died in World War I, Mizzou hosts Kentucky on its Military Appreciation night, with the crowd asked to wear black for the 6:30 p.m. Central kickoff. The Kentucky vs Missouri spread is short for two teams that have both lost two straight, and I'm laying it: Missouri Tigers −4.5 and Over 48.5, with Missouri Tigers 29, Kentucky Wildcats 22 as my final.

Delphine Arceneaux-Moss, the Mizzou loyalist who announces everything like a breaking bulletin, has her lead written. "Jamal Roberts: 1,133 rushing yards. Nicholas Rodriguez: 105 tackles. We're 4-1 in this building." Rhett Abernathy-Coyle, the Kentucky horse-farm kid who grades football players like yearlings at a sale, likes his own pedigree. "Kenny Minchey has 15 touchdown throws and two interceptions. We won at Texas A&M and at Oklahoma. We've won six of the last eight against you."

The side is about where each team is trending. Kentucky scored 6 at Tennessee and 14 against Florida in its last two, and that offense was carried for weeks by close escapes: 35-34 in overtime at South Carolina and 35-34 in overtime against LSU. Missouri's losses came against Mississippi State, Ole Miss, Texas and Georgia, and at home the Tigers beat Florida 45-17 and Texas A&M 27-6. Roberts gives Missouri a way to grind out a margin when the throwing game stalls. DraftKings opened Missouri −6.5, and it has come down two points to −4.5 as of 9:20 PM ET Friday. I have the Tigers by seven.

The total is the bigger call. Seven of Kentucky's ten games have gone over 48.5, and six of Missouri's have. Austin Simmons has 21 touchdown throws against three interceptions, and Cayden Lee has 879 receiving yards. Kentucky's defense allows 25.3 points a game, and Grant Godfrey, with 91 tackles, has been busy all year. The total opened at 49.5 and dipped. My 51 combined points sit over it.

The risk is Kentucky's last two weeks being the real Kentucky offense. If Minchey can't move it, Missouri wins 24-10 and the Over dies.

The room is split all over. The Formula has Missouri 28-24, takes Kentucky Wildcats +4.5 and likes the Over 48.5. Next Man Up has the same 29-22 I do, likes Missouri −4.5 and likes the Over. Letdown Watch, at 30-20, likes Missouri −4.5 and takes the Over. In The Trenches has Missouri 29-21, takes Missouri Tigers −5.5, and stays away from totals. Follow The Money likes Kentucky Wildcats +4.5 and has no total pick.

The Formula, to Next Man Up: "You copied the Cover Writer's homework." Next Man Up: "I turned mine in first. Check the timestamp."

Delphine gets her headline, Rhett gets a close one into the fourth, and the black-clad crowd gets to sing in a building that just turned a hundred.

Missouri Tigers 29, Kentucky Wildcats 22. Missouri Tigers cover the 4.5. Over 48.5.
