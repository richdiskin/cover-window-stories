Coastal Carolina vs Louisiana Pick: Cajuns −10.5, Under 49.5

Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (4-6) at Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns (7-3)

3:00 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Our Lady of Lourdes Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Downtown Lafayette spends the weekend at the movies, with the Southern Screen Festival running through Sunday, and Our Lady of Lourdes Stadium gets the closing reel on Saturday. For Coastal Carolina vs Louisiana I'm laying it with the home team, Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns −10.5, and riding the Under 49.5 to a projected Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns 30, Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 18 when the seniors take the field at 3:00 PM ET.

Mimi Fontenot reads the script like she wrote it. "Five home games, five wins. Lunch Winfield has run for 604 yards and 10 touchdowns. We beat South Alabama 28-26 and Troy 33-6 in this building. Goldie, this is the part of the movie where the hero walks off into the sunset."

Goldie Seabrook is unbothered. "Deuce Bailey went 21 of 24 last week. Marcellius Pulliam has 104 tackles. We won at Marshall and we won at Georgia State. And we beat you twice in the four meetings since 2016, Mimi. Sequels are a thing."

Here is why I lay the 10.5. Louisiana's offense is built around Winfield's legs, and Coastal's issue is that it gives up yards in bunches when it falls behind. The Chanticleers are 4-6 and lost three games by a touchdown or less, but they also scored 8 against Old Dominion and threw three interceptions doing it. Bailey has 9 interceptions on the year. Louisiana's defense allows 21.7 a game and has Jake St. Andre with 83 tackles to clean up anything Bailey leaves on the field.

The market agrees and then some. DraftKings opened Louisiana −7.5 and deals −10.5 as of 9:20 PM ET Friday, a full field goal of movement in the Cajuns' direction. I don't love chasing a number, but my 30-18 has Louisiana winning by 12, and that's clear of the 10.5.

The Under is the cleaner call. Four of Louisiana's five home games stayed under 49.5 points, and only the 28-26 South Alabama game went past it. The Cajuns like to run, and a team that runs holds the ball. Winfield threw only 13 times in the 42-21 win at UL Monroe, and Steven Blanco carried 15 times for 113 yards. Coastal's best defensive player, Ibrahim Diawara, has six sacks and will make Winfield earn every throw. My 48 lands under the 49.5.

The room is split on the total and mostly with Louisiana. Next Man Up has Louisiana 30, Coastal Carolina 18, lays the 10.5 and takes the Under. Letdown Watch has 32-19 Louisiana with the Cajuns −10.5 and the Under it took at 51.5. In The Trenches has Louisiana 32, Coastal Carolina 20, likes Louisiana −10.5, and has no total pick. The Formula breaks from the pack with Louisiana 30, Coastal Carolina 21, taking Coastal Carolina +10.5 and flipping to the Over. Follow The Money has no side pick and likes the Under 49.5.

The Formula: "Nine points of margin and everybody acts like I fell off the roof. Coastal plays close. Look it up."

In The Trenches: "Coastal plays close until it has to block Louisiana's front for four quarters. I looked it up."

Lafayette gets a festival full of films that end on time. The seniors want a final home game that ends the same way.

Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns 30, Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 18. Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns cover the 10.5. Under 49.5.
