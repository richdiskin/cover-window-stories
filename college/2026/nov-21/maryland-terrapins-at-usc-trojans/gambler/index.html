Maryland at USC: A Year Later, Maryland Comes Back to the Coliseum

Market check, Maryland at USC: DraftKings USC -17.5, total 56.5.

Maryland has already beaten USC once in this series, 29-28, on Oct. 19, 2024, and Saturday the Terrapins go back to the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum to face No. 18 USC for, Maryland's official preview said, the first time in program history on the road in this matchup. Kickoff is 6:15 p.m. ET on Big Ten Network. The live card on this site and ESPN's game header both list USC at No. 18. The Trojans are 7-3 after a 13-27 loss at Indiana, a game in which Jayden Maiava threw for 311 yards, one score and three interceptions. Maryland is 4-6 after a 24-21 win over Wisconsin.

The number as of Nov. 21, 2026, 9:08 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at USC -17.5, total 56.5. The Cover Window's published pick still reads Maryland Terrapins +17.5 and Under 56.5. The market still matches the line the card used. The card's projected final is USC Trojans 35, Maryland Terrapins 20 (Total 55). That pick was published against the Nov. 21, 2026, 7:31 AM ET line (USC Trojans −17.5, total 56.5), it has not been changed, and this story issues no new one. Prices move, so check yours before kickoff. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

Maryland's Game 11 preview of the trip to USC, and USC's how-to-watch note, did not publish a dated Saturday injury report. ESPN's game page returned none. This story does not invent designations. Recheck both schools before the 6:15 p.m. ET kickoff.

That Wisconsin win is the new starting point. Maryland's preview said Cardell Williams, in his first start as a Terp, went 21-of-23 for 183 yards and accounted for all three touchdowns (one passing, two rushing). Sean O'Haire kicked a 30-yard field goal with 29 seconds left, his first career game-winner, and was named Big Ten special teams player of the week. Preston Howard caught a 39-yard score that tied the game. USC still has Maiava, and Trent Mosley had nine catches for 130 yards in Bloomington, but the Trojans also had those three interceptions, including the late interception by Jamari Sharpe that closed Indiana's win.

A 4-6 visitor that just won on a kick with 29 seconds left, a No. 18 home team coming off a three-interception afternoon, and a series that already has one Maryland win in it: that is Saturday night at the Coliseum. If Williams is as sharp as 21-of-23, the Trojans will have to answer late again. If USC cleans up the football, the home night will look more like a ranked team at home and less like last week in Bloomington.

Jayden Maiava walks out of the Coliseum tunnel for the last time as a home quarterback Saturday, on a Senior Day that USC is also billing as Fan Appreciation and Los Troyanos day, and the Maryland vs USC spread asks him to win his farewell by more than 17.5. My pick is Maryland Terrapins +17.5 and Under 56.5, and my final is USC Trojans 35, Maryland Terrapins 20.

Sterling Ashworth-Vance, who grew up a few blocks from Exposition Park and narrates every replay like a movie trailer, is ready for the send-off. "In a world where Maiava has 2,835 yards and 21 touchdowns... King Miller has 727 on the ground. We beat Ohio State 30-18 right here." Abby Chesapeake, who flew in from the Maryland suburbs for this one, isn't flinching. "We've won two straight. Cardell Williams went 21 of 23 against Wisconsin. And we beat you 29-28 the last time we played."

Start with how USC wins. The Trojans are 7-3, and only two of their seven wins came by 18 or more: 39-0 over Fresno State and 49-30 over Louisiana. Against Big Ten opponents they won by 7 at Rutgers, 4 over Washington, 8 at Penn State and 12 over Ohio State. They are also coming off a 27-13 loss at Indiana in which Maiava threw three interceptions. Maryland has been blown out, no hiding it, 54-3 by UCLA and 45-16 at Ohio State, but the Terrapins have played tighter lately: 37-30 against Rutgers, 20-17 against Illinois, then wins at Purdue and over Wisconsin. Chris Durr Jr. has 804 receiving yards and gives the Terps a way to move the ball late. DraftKings opened USC −16.5. I have the Trojans by 15.

The total rests on USC's recent games. Since the Oregon loss, the Trojans have played 25-21, 31-23, 7-24, 30-18 and 13-27, and every one of those finished under 56.5. Luke Wafle and the USC front have kept games from turning into track meets, and Maryland's offense has produced 24 or fewer in five of its last seven. The posted total opened at 56.5, nudged up to 57.5 on Friday, and is back at 56.5 as of 7:31 AM ET Saturday. My 55 points stay under.

The risk is Maiava having one of his big days. He threw for 349 against Fresno State and five touchdowns against Louisiana. If Maryland's secondary looks like it did against UCLA, this gets out of hand in a hurry.

The room splits. The Formula, at 35-19, takes Maryland Terrapins +17.5 and likes the Under 56.5. Next Man Up has 40-22 and takes USC Trojans −17.5 and the Over. Letdown Watch, at 39-15, likes USC −17.5 and likes the Under. In The Trenches has USC by only 26-23, likes Maryland +17.5, and doesn't take a total. Follow The Money has no pick in this game.

Letdown Watch, to In The Trenches: "Twenty-six to twenty-three? At the Coliseum?" In The Trenches: "You gave the Terps 15. I gave them a pulse."

Sterling gets his trailer moment, the seniors get the win and the crowd gets its blankets. Abby just wants the final scene to stay inside the margin.

USC Trojans 35, Maryland Terrapins 20. Maryland Terrapins cover the 17.5. Under 56.5.
