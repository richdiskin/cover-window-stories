BYU at Kansas: The Best 4-6 Team Poppinga Has Ever Seen

Market check, BYU at Kansas: DraftKings BYU -5.5, total 46.5.

Lawrence is supposed to be breezy on Saturday, with AccuWeather on ESPN's game page showing 25 mph gusts and only a 4 percent chance of rain, which is a fitting welcome for a No. 10 BYU team trying to keep a College Football Playoff hope alive against a 4-6 Kansas team that just ran over West Virginia. BYU defensive coordinator Poppinga did not dress it up. "So, yeah, they are the best 4-6 team I've ever seen," he said, "and I am not kidding when I say that. They caught my attention immediately when I turned on the film on Sunday."

The number as of Nov. 21, 2026, 8:53 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at BYU -5.5, total 46.5. The Cover Window's published pick still reads Kansas Jayhawks +5.5 and Over 46.5. The market still matches the line the card used. The card's projected final is BYU Cougars 27, Kansas Jayhawks 24 (Total 51). That pick was published against the Nov. 21, 2026, 7:31 AM ET line (Kansas Jayhawks +5.5, total 46.5), it has not been changed, and this story issues no new one. Prices move, so check yours before kickoff. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

Injuries: KSL Sports, updating Friday morning, said BYU's availability report lists LB Isaiah Glasker, RB Sione Moa and TE Will Zundel out, with CB Evan Johnson and TE Roger Saleapaga II doubtful. Star RB LJ Martin was not on the report; KSL noted he is still working back from a high-ankle sprain and was limited after his ankle was stepped on against Baylor. A final report is due 90 minutes before kickoff. We did not find a complete Kansas game-day list in the same check. This story does not declare any player active. The Jayhawks are 4-6 and 2-5 in the Big 12, one of two remaining wins from bowl eligibility, and they arrive with momentum from a 37-14 victory at West Virginia. Quarterback Isaiah Marshall rushed for a career-high 164 yards in that game, his fourth 100-yard rushing game of the season, and completed 15 of 18 passes for 152 yards. Poppinga said Marshall "is as dynamic as we've seen as a runner down the field." He even compared the way Marshall runs and throws on the move to Lamar Jackson, then laughed at the shared No. 8 jersey. Linebacker Jake Clifton, likely making his fourth start with rover Isaiah Glasker ruled out, called Marshall "very comparable to Utah's quarterback" and warned that he can sit in the pocket "and then take off for 40 yards."

BYU is 8-2 and 5-2 in the league, and Deseret News noted the Cougars are 1-2 in Big 12 road games this season. The other quarterback in this one, Bear Bachmeier, has 449 rushing yards and 10 rushing touchdowns, and KSL's game-day preview pointed out that both sophomores lead their own teams in rushing. Kansas has a rushing offense the Deseret story put at 210.7 yards a game; BYU's rushing defense was listed at 100.3. Strength on strength, in the weather, at David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium.

The Jayhawks need this one to keep a disappointing season from ending early. The Cougars need it so the bigger goals don't leak away on a breezy Saturday in Lawrence. Poppinga said Kansas has his team's "full attention." The Booth will find out if that was just polite talk.

Lawrence closes two seasons on Saturday. The Lawrence Farmers Market packs up its 50th year on New Hampshire Street at 11:30 a.m. Central, half an hour after Kansas kicks off its home finale against No. 10 BYU, and the BYU vs Kansas spread sits at BYU −5.5 with a 46.5 over/under on DraftKings. My pick is Kansas Jayhawks +5.5 and Over 46.5.

Saturday is also Senior Day at David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium, and this Kansas team has given its seniors plenty of near misses. The Jayhawks are 4-6, but the losses include 33-32 to UCF, 22-16 at TCU and 24-17 to Arizona State. That's a team that keeps finishing within a score, which is exactly what a 4.5-point underdog wants to be.

The line has already come our way. DraftKings opened BYU −7 with a 50.5 total, and both numbers have dropped, to BYU −5.5 and 46.5 as of 7:31 AM ET Saturday. I'm still taking the points at 5.5.

Fitz Calloway, who converts every football stat into basketball points, brought last week's tape. "Isaiah Marshall went 15 of 18 and ran for 164 yards at West Virginia. Thirty points in the first half, 37-14 final." Kansas has now beaten BYU twice in a row, 38-27 in Lawrence and 17-13 in Provo.

Bryce Halvorsen-Tuilagi has the better team and knows it. "We're 8-2. We beat Notre Dame. Bear Bachmeier ran for three scores against Baylor." All true. BYU allows 18.5 points a game, and Bachmeier has 10 rushing touchdowns.

The trouble for the Cougars is the road. Their two losses came away from Provo, 39-31 in overtime at UCF and 28-20 at Utah, where Bachmeier threw four interceptions. Marshall gives Kansas a quarterback who can run as well as Bachmeier, with 692 rushing yards to Bachmeier's 449.

My final is BYU Cougars 27, Kansas Jayhawks 24. BYU wins by a field goal, and Kansas stays inside the 4.5. A team that has beaten BYU two years running and keeps finishing within a score doesn't get blown out on Senior Day. The 51 combined points clear 46.5, and Kansas scores 30.2 a game with Marshall on his feet. Two running quarterbacks extend drives, and that keeps the scoreboard moving.

BYU Cougars 27, Kansas Jayhawks 24. Kansas Jayhawks cover the 5.5. Over 46.5.
