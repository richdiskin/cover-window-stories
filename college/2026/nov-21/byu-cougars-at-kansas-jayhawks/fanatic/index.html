BYU vs Kansas: Lawrence Market Closes, Jayhawks Say Goodbye

BYU Cougars (8-2) at Kansas Jayhawks (4-6)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium · ABC

XFacebook

At 7:30 Saturday morning, three and a half hours before BYU vs Kansas kicks off, the Lawrence Farmers Market opens its tents on New Hampshire Street for the last time this year, closing out its 50th season on the same day the Jayhawks play their home finale. Kansas insider Fitz Calloway plans to buy apples downtown and walk them up the hill to David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium, and BYU insider Bryce Halvorsen-Tuilagi says the No. 10 Cougars came to Lawrence to take something home that isn't produce.

Fitz: Fifty seasons of that market, Bryce. Fifty. That's longer than some basketball programs have been any good. You're standing in a town that knows how to finish a season.

Bryce: Oh my heck, Fitz, I love a farmers market. I'm buying honey for my wife and a pie for the flight home. Then we're beating you.

Kansas is calling Saturday Senior Day too, which means the Jayhawks get two goodbyes before lunch, one to the market and one to the seniors. The crowd that shows up at 11 a.m. Central will have muddy shoes and full grocery bags, and it'll be loud.

Fitz: You know what I saw last Saturday in Morgantown? Isaiah Marshall going 15 of 18 and running for 164 yards. Thirty points in the first half. We held West Virginia to 14. I converted that to basketball. It's a 37-14 win, which is roughly a forty-point blowout in hoops terms, and I refuse to show my math.

Bryce: Show your math. I'll show mine. Bear Bachmeier ran for three touchdowns against Baylor in a 28-7 win. He has ten rushing touchdowns this year to go with 14 through the air. The kid runs like he's late for a ward potluck.

That is the matchup everybody in Lawrence will be watching: two quarterbacks who would rather run through you than around you. Marshall leads Kansas in throwing and running with 692 rushing yards. Bachmeier leads BYU in both with 449 on the ground. Whoever gets the last word on a third-down scramble probably gets the last word on the afternoon.

Bryce: I'll be honest with you, Fitz, because that's how we do it in Provo. Bear had a rough night at Utah. Four interceptions. We lost 28-20. But he came back the next week and didn't throw one against Baylor. That's a kid who listens to his coaches.

Fitz: And Kansas has a guy who listens to nobody. Leroy Harris III has seven and a half sacks. He hears a snap count and just goes. I want him in Bachmeier's ear all day long.

Bryce: Cade Uluave is waiting on your Marshall. Sixty-one tackles. Bodie Schoonover has four sacks. We bring our own trouble.

The history is where Fitz gets a little smug. Kansas beat BYU 38-27 in Lawrence in 2023, then went to Provo the next November and won 17-13. Kansas athletics notes this is only BYU's second trip to Lawrence.

Fitz: Two meetings this decade, two wins. That's a perfect free-throw night.

Bryce: Respectfully, those were different Cougars. This group is 8-2 and has beaten Notre Dame. We don't scare easy, and we don't eat our pie before the game.

Fitz isn't hiding from the season, either. Kansas is 4-6, and the losses have stung: 33-32 to UCF, 22-16 at TCU, 24-17 to Arizona State. Three one-score losses, three what-ifs. The home crowd remembers every one of them, and Senior Day is the last chance for these seniors to send everybody to the parking lot happy.

Fitz: We play close with everybody. I'm tired of close. I want one more Saturday where the band plays the alma mater and nobody is checking the clock with a stomachache.

Bryce: You'll get the band. I can't promise you the stomach.

Then there's Legend Glasker, BYU's leading receiver with 706 yards. Nik McMillan is Kansas' with 541. Neither one has carried an offense by himself this year, which keeps sending the story back to the quarterbacks.

Bryce: Here's my dad joke for the market, Fitz. What do you call a Jayhawk at a farmers market? A little late in the season.

Fitz: That's terrible. I'm laughing because I'm polite, and because I'm going to win.

The tents come down at 11:30 on New Hampshire Street, half an hour after kickoff, and Lawrence will walk into winter after that. I think BYU leaves town with a win in its grocery bag, and I think Kansas makes the Cougars sweat right up to the final whistle.

My call: BYU Cougars 27, Kansas Jayhawks 24.
