Florida International at Kennesaw State: Two 4-6 Teams, One Last Home Saturday in Kennesaw

Market check, Florida International at Kennesaw State: DraftKings KENN -2.5, total 44.5.

Kennesaw and FIU arrive at Walens Family Field at Fifth Third Stadium in the same boat: 4-6, with a 3 p.m. ET ESPN+ kickoff, and a league date that has to be decided on the field, not on the record. The Owls are 3-3 in league play. The Panthers are 2-4. That is the whole argument for why a late-November home Saturday in Kennesaw still matters.

The number as of Nov. 21, 2026, 9:08 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at KENN -2.5, total 44.5. The Cover Window's published pick still reads Kennesaw State Owls −2.5 and Under 44.5. ESPN's game page returned no injury report for FIU at Kennesaw State, and neither official athletics site published a dated Saturday designation list that we could source this week. This story does not fill that gap. The only weather detail attached to the ESPN game page was AccuWeather's 49 percent rain chance and an 18-mph gust at Fifth Third Stadium. Recheck both schools before kickoff.

FIU is coming off a 16-24 loss at Delaware, where JJ Kohl threw for 313 yards and a score but also had two interceptions, and Maguire Anderson caught nine balls for 122 yards. Kohl has 2,348 passing yards, 12 touchdowns and 12 interceptions on the year. Devonte Lyons has 706 rushing yards. Kennesaw, by contrast, just won 26-7 at Sam Houston. Rickie Collins has 1,687 passing yards, seven scores and seven interceptions; Boogie Woods III leads the Owls' ground game in the facts we can source.

The AccuWeather forecast on ESPN's game page had a 49 percent chance of rain and an 18-mph gust, which is enough to make a messy afternoon possible in Cobb County. Official sites for both programs were thin this week on a fresh game preview, so this story stays with what is dated and sourced: the records, the last boxes, and the stadium. No player is being declared out or available without a report.

Two 4-6 teams, one of them at home for a conference date, and a passing attack that just threw for 313 on the road: that is the 3 o'clock game in Kennesaw. If the Owls defend the way they did in a 26-7 win last week, the home stands will have a night. If Kohl keeps finding Anderson, the walk to the cars will be a long one.

At one moment on Saturday, Kennesaw State will ask every veteran and active-duty service member in the stands to rise and be recognized by Owl Nation. That's the Salute to Service game against FIU, the Owls' last home date, and it is the backdrop for a Florida International vs Kennesaw State pick I like more than the number suggests: Kennesaw State Owls −2.5, Under 44.5, Kennesaw State Owls 24, Florida International Panthers 19 at 3:00 PM ET.

FIU's road problem is my side. The Panthers are 0-5 away from home, and the scores tell you why: 9 at South Florida, 10 at Florida Atlantic, 24 at Jacksonville State, 10 at Liberty and 16 at Delaware. Kohl has 12 touchdowns and 12 interceptions, and two of those picks came last week. Kennesaw State has won both meetings, 27-26 in 2024 and 45-26 last year.

The market has drifted toward FIU. DraftKings opened Kennesaw State −4 and the spread sits at −2.5 as of 9:20 PM ET Friday. My 24-19 has the Owls by five, enough to clear the number without needing a blowout.

I'll take the extra two points. Kennesaw's defense has Ugonna Nwoha with five and a half sacks and Tywon Christopher with 74 tackles, and FIU has not scored more than 24 on the road. My 43 stays under the 44.5.
