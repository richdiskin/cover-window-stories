Northwestern vs Minnesota: Gophers ROW on Senior Day

Northwestern Wildcats (6-4) at Minnesota Golden Gophers (5-5)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Huntington Bank Stadium · FS1

XFacebook

At 10:58 a.m. Central on Saturday, two minutes before Northwestern vs Minnesota kicks off, everybody in Huntington Bank Stadium is supposed to grab an imaginary oar and row. Minnesota insider Tor Engebretsen has been practicing his stroke in the garage all week, and Northwestern insider Ezra Lindqvist says Nicco Marchiol and a bowl-eligible Wildcats team plan to leave the Gophers rowing upstream on Senior Day.

The schedule for the day reads like a Gopher fan's prayer book. Tailgate lots open at 7. The team arrives for the Ski-U-March near Gate C at 8:30. The Spirit Rally at Tribal Nations Plaza starts at 9:40. Then comes the Senior Day ceremony on the field at 10:30, the Pride of Minnesota Marching Band at 10:40 and the ROW chant right before the kick.

Tor: Ezra, I want you to understand the ROW chant. It isn't a cheer. It's a philosophy. You keep pulling no matter what the river does. We've had a rough river lately.

Ezra: Footnote one: rough river is generous. Footnote two: I'll row with you, Tor, but only to be polite.

The river has been rough for both sides, and that's what makes this one interesting. Minnesota has lost three straight: 23-16 in overtime at Indiana, 34-20 to UCLA and 42-14 at Penn State. At 5-5, the Gophers need one more win to get to a bowl game. Northwestern already has its six wins, but it's lost three in a row too, to Oregon, Iowa and Ohio State.

Tor: Here's how I rate teams. I ask how they'd handle January. A January team doesn't panic. We're a January team. Cold enough for ya? Good.

Ezra: Northwestern is a lakefront team. We handle wind off Lake Michigan that would knock a Gopher back into its hole. Footnote: affectionately.

The quarterbacks are where the conversation gets real. Drake Lindsey has thrown 326 times for Minnesota and has 1,982 yards. He threw two touchdowns at Penn State, and Darius Taylor caught four balls for 85 yards. Lindsey has been the steady name in a season that hasn't always been steady around him.

Northwestern's quarterback room is a two-chapter story. Aidan Chiles threw for 1,898 yards, 13 touchdowns and only one interception in the first eight games. The last two weeks, Marchiol has thrown the most passes for the Wildcats, and he went 18 of 31 for 225 yards and a touchdown at Ohio State in a 28-19 loss.

Ezra: Marchiol threw at the Horseshoe without blinking. Luke Dehnicke caught five for 88 and a score. Footnote: I was right about Dehnicke in August, and I have the notebook to prove it.

Tor: We'll see how Dehnicke likes Maverick Baranowski. Eighty tackles. That kid hits like an ice-out on Lake Minnetonka. Then there's TJ Bush Jr. up front with six sacks.

Ezra: Braydon Brus has 81 tackles and four sacks for us, Tor. He leads our team in both. I'd call that a full-service linebacker.

Then there's Griffin Wilde, Northwestern's top receiver with 843 yards. Caleb Komolafe has 697 rushing yards for the Wildcats. Minnesota counters with Javon Tracy, who has 55 catches, and TJ Thomas on the ground.

The series is as even as a canoe with two people who know what they're doing. In the last eight meetings, going back to 2016, Northwestern and Minnesota have each won four. Last year's game was 38-35 to the Wildcats. In 2023, Northwestern won 37-34. Ezra brings those up with a straight face.

Tor: Two close losses. That's what a river does. It pushes you sideways. Then you row.

Ezra: Or you get out and walk. Footnote: we walked to a bowl already.

That's the difference, and Tor knows it. Northwestern can relax a little. Minnesota can't. The Senior Day ceremony at 10:30 will be emotional, and then the seniors have a ballgame to win to keep their season going into December.

Tor: I'm going to cry at 10:30, I'm going to row at 10:58, and I'm going to yell for three hours after that. That's a full Saturday on the East Bank.

Ezra: I'll bring you tissues. Purple ones.

The oars come up at 10:58, the Gophers will pull hard, and the Wildcats will pull a little harder at the end.

My call: Northwestern Wildcats 27, Minnesota Golden Gophers 21.
