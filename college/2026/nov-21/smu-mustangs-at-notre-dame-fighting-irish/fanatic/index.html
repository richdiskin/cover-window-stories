SMU at Notre Dame: Senior Night, and a First ACC-Era Meeting

Notre Dame's midweek note put Senior Night opposite SMU at Notre Dame Stadium, on NBC beginning at 7:30 Eastern, and called it the first meeting since the Mustangs joined the ACC in 2024. The Irish, the school said, lead the series 10-3-0 and are 6-1 at home against SMU. The last meeting was a 59-6 Irish win in 1989. The first meeting inside Notre Dame Stadium came in 1930, a 20-14 Irish win. The live card on this site lists Notre Dame at No. 5. ESPN's game header showed No. 6. This story stays with the card and flags ESPN's number.

Notre Dame is 9-1 after a 41-7 win against Boston College. CJ Carr has 2,385 passing yards, 23 touchdowns and two interceptions. Jonaz Walton has 655 rushing yards. SMU is 7-3 after a 7-49 loss to Wake Forest. Kevin Jennings still has 2,973 passing yards, 26 touchdowns and seven interceptions, even on a night the Mustangs scored only seven. Kendrick Raphael has 809 rushing yards and 10 scores.

A Senior Night on the official calendar, a first ACC-era meeting, and a 7-3 visitor coming off a 49-7 loss: that is Saturday night at Notre Dame Stadium in South Bend. If Carr is as sharp as 23 touchdowns and two interceptions, the Mustangs will have to score early. If Jennings looks like 2,973 yards of work instead of last week's seven-point box, the farewell night will last longer than the 1989 score suggests. Notre Dame already put Senior Night on the schedule. SMU has to answer a 49-7 loss first.

At one minute past midnight on Saturday, the Notre Dame drummers will gather outside the Main Building and start the day with the Midnight Drummers' Circle, and Declan Moriarty-Chen will be standing at the front in a sweater vest, holding a thermos and quietly giggling. Tatum Delacroix-Price will be next to him in a camel coat she bought specifically for Indiana in November, wondering how anyone plays drums with gloves on.

Tatum: It's midnight. In Dallas we'd be at dinner.

Declan: In South Bend, we're at the dome. The drums go first, then everything else follows.

Tatum: Is it always this cold?

Declan: This is a mild one.

Saturday is a full day on campus. Notre Dame hosts SMU on Senior Day, at 7:30 p.m. Eastern, for the 14th meeting between them, the first since 1989, and the first time the Mustangs have played a night game in South Bend. Declan has the schedule memorized, of course.

5:15 p.m., the Player Walk at Stonehenge.

Declan: The team walks past the fountain on the way to the stadium, and the crowd gathers on both sides to cheer them in.

Tatum: I'll be counting how many of your guys look nervous. I've been waiting all season.

Notre Dame is 9-1 and ranked No. 5. The only loss came at BYU, 28-24, back in October. Since then the Irish beat Navy, edged Miami 31-24 and ran past Boston College 41-7 last week. CJ Carr has thrown for 2,385 yards, 23 touchdowns and only two interceptions. Jonaz Walton ran for 141 yards against Boston College and leads the team with 655. Jordan Faison leads the receivers with 615 yards and six scores. Boubacar Traore has five sacks, Luke Talich leads in tackles with 47, and the defense gives up 13.8 points a game.

5:30 p.m., Trumpets at the Dome.

Tatum: Trumpets. On the steps of a gold dome. I'll admit that's iconic.

Declan: You said "iconic" about a doughnut yesterday.

Tatum: It was an iconic doughnut.

SMU arrives 7-3 and stung. The Mustangs lost 49-7 at home to Wake Forest last week, and Tatum has been wearing larger sunglasses ever since. Before that, though, SMU looked like a real ACC contender. It won at Florida State to open the season, beat Virginia and Virginia Tech, and put 59 on Cal. Kevin Jennings has thrown for 2,973 yards and 26 touchdowns, and he went past four hundred passing yards twice. Kendrick Raphael has 809 rushing yards and ten touchdowns, Yamir Knight leads the receivers with 670 yards, and Marques White has 8.5 sacks.

Tatum: Last week didn't happen.

Declan: It's in the archive.

Tatum: Archives can be edited.

Declan: Not at Notre Dame. We laminate them.

6 p.m., the Concert on the Steps at Bond Hall.

Declan: The band plays on the steps, and people come for the music and stay until they march.

Tatum: SMU has a band too. Ours has more sparkle.

Declan: Ours wears more history.

6:40 p.m., the March Out from the Main Building.

The game could hinge on what Jennings can do against the best defense he's seen. Notre Dame's front got to quarterbacks all season, and SMU will need its offense to stay ahead of the chains against a crowd that will be loud from the opening kickoff. If Raphael can find room, SMU has a chance to keep it interesting. If not, Carr will have plenty of short fields.

Declan: The seniors get introduced, the band finishes, and then they play the game.

Tatum: And I get to wear my good coat on the national stage.

Here's how it goes: Notre Dame takes the opening drive in, Walton breaks one in the second quarter, and Carr finds Faison twice before halftime. SMU hits a big play to Knight in the third and gets a late score, but the Irish defense makes the difference, and the seniors walk off a winner in their last home game.

Declan, after the final whistle: That's one more for the archive.

Tatum: Put me in the footnotes. In a camel coat.

The drummers were the first thing either of them heard on Saturday, and the band's last note is the last thing Tatum hears before she finally goes looking for somewhere warm.

My call: Notre Dame Fighting Irish 37, SMU Mustangs 18.
