Northern Illinois vs North Dakota State Pick: Huskies +25.5 and the Over 44.5

Northern Illinois Huskies (0-10) at North Dakota State Bison (8-2)

4:00 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Fargodome · CW

XFacebook

Twenty-five and a half points is a big ask for a team that has won by more than that only three times all year, and that's my starting point on Northern Illinois vs North Dakota State. The Bison close their first FBS regular season with Senior Day and the Harvest Bowl in the Fargodome. My card is the road team plus the Over: Northern Illinois Huskies +25.5, Over 44.5, with North Dakota State Bison 35, Northern Illinois Huskies 12 at 4:00 PM ET.

Ingeborg "Inga" Halvorsen keeps a ledger for everything. "Eight and two, Nola. Five and oh in the Fargodome. We allow 12.0 points a game. Kelton McCaslin has five and a half sacks. Nathan Hayes has 14 touchdown passes. We won at Hawai'i 21-13 last week."

Nola Brandvik answers with a farmer's shrug. "We're 0-10, Inga. I know. But we lost to Illinois State by four, UNLV by seven and San José State by 11. Telly Johnson Jr. has 660 rushing yards, and DeAree Rogers has 80 catches. We don't fold."

That's the case for the points. North Dakota State's home wins came by 26 over Jacksonville State, 38 over Fordham, 28 over Wyoming, 14 over Nevada and 24 over UTEP. The Bison offense scores 27.4 a game, and in their last four games it scored 31, 16, 31 and 21. Northern Illinois has lost by more than 25.5 only twice, 40-0 at Iowa and 56-21 to Hawai'i. My 35-12 is a 23-point win, two and a half points short of the number.

The risk is obvious. Northern Illinois scored seven points against Nevada last week, and the Huskies allow 35.9 a game. Taron Dickens has eight interceptions, and North Dakota State's defense has two shutouts.

DraftKings opened North Dakota State −25.5 and 48.5, as shown in ESPN pickcenter. As of 9:20 PM ET Friday, the side hasn't moved, and the total is down four to 44.5.

The Over is the shakier half of my card. Only two of North Dakota State's ten games have gone over 44.5. Seven of Northern Illinois' ten have, because the Huskies' defense gives up so much. My 47 needs the Bison offense at its best and one or two Northern Illinois scores.

The room splits on the side. The Formula has North Dakota State 34, Northern Illinois 12, likes Northern Illinois +25.5 and goes Over 44.5. In The Trenches has North Dakota State 32, Northern Illinois 18, likes Northern Illinois +25.5, and has no total pick. Next Man Up has North Dakota State 37, Northern Illinois 7, likes the Bison −25.5 and goes Under. Letdown Watch has North Dakota State 36, Northern Illinois 9, lays the 25.5 and keeps the Under it took at 45.5. Follow The Money has no side pick and likes the Under 44.5.

Next Man Up: "Senior Day, last home game, an 0-10 opponent. The backups play the fourth quarter and still score."

The Formula: "Big home margins come early. By the fourth quarter, the Bison will be protecting a lead, not chasing a number."

Fans are asked to wear yellow for the Harvest Bowl. I'll be watching for the moment the Bison empty the bench.

North Dakota State Bison 35, Northern Illinois Huskies 12. Northern Illinois Huskies cover the 25.5. Over 44.5.
