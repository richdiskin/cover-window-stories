Arkansas at Texas: Austin After a One-Point Escape in Baton Rouge

Market check, Arkansas at Texas: DraftKings TEX -20.5, total 47.5.

Texas just survived LSU by a point, 22-21, on the road, and now Arkansas comes to DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium for a 3:30 p.m. ET game on ESPN. The live card on this site lists Texas at No. 6, and that is the ranking this story will use. The Longhorns' own site has been billing the team as No. 8/8/5 this week (including a Butkus Award finalist note on linebacker Biles). ESPN's game header showed Texas at No. 5. When the school, the network and the card disagree, we stay with the card and tell you the rest.

The number as of Nov. 21, 2026, 9:08 AM ET: ESPN lists DraftKings at TEX -20.5, total 47.5. The Cover Window's published pick still reads Arkansas Razorbacks +20.5 and Under 47.5. The market still matches the line the card used. The card's projected final is Texas Longhorns 32, Arkansas Razorbacks 13 (Total 45). That pick was published against the Nov. 20, 2026, 9:20 PM ET line (Texas Longhorns −20.5, total 47.5), it has not been changed, and this story issues no new one. Prices move, so check yours before kickoff. Full preview at thecoverwindow.com.

Texas posted a Nov. 19 Steve Sarkisian media availability and a run of awards notes (Biles as a Butkus finalist, Manning as a Unitas Golden Arm finalist). Those pages did not include a dated Saturday injury report, and ESPN's game page returned none for Arkansas at Texas. Arkansas' official football index was not publishing a usable game preview at the time of this report. No player is being declared out or available here. Recheck both schools before kickoff.

Arkansas is 4-6 after a 34-6 win over South Carolina. Texas is 8-2. Arch Manning has 2,198 passing yards, 19 touchdowns and 10 interceptions; Raleek Brown leads the Longhorns' rush. KJ Jackson has 2,041 passing yards for the Razorbacks, and Chris Marshall has 761 receiving yards. Steve Sarkisian held a Nov. 19 media availability, posted as video by Texas, but the school did not publish a full Saturday designation list in the pages we could source.

This is not last week's one-score escape in Baton Rouge, and it is not a ranked-on-ranked date on the card. It is a November afternoon in Austin, a 4-6 visitor that just dropped 34 on South Carolina, and a Texas team whose official site cannot even agree with the card on its own ranking. If the Longhorns play like a group that just won in Tiger Stadium on the last snap, the afternoon should tilt orange. If Arkansas' passing game finds the same rhythm it had against South Carolina, DKR will have to earn the rest of the day.

Creek Show has its last night on Saturday, ten light installations glowing along Waller Creek from 4th Street down to Lady Bird Lake, and Texas would love to have this one wrapped up before the first of them switches on. Arkansas vs Texas, my side and my over/under: Arkansas Razorbacks +20.5, Under 47.5, and a projected Texas Longhorns 32, Arkansas Razorbacks 13 at 3:30 PM ET in Darrell K Royal-Texas Memorial Stadium.

Caro Whitfield runs the Longhorns like a portfolio, and she opens the ledger. "Five home wins in six. Colin Simmons has 10.5 sacks. Jelani McDonald has 78 tackles. Our defense gives up 17.8 a game, and Arch Manning just walked into Baton Rouge and won 22-21. That's a blue-chip asset, Bubba."

Bubba Fayette-Dorsey calls the Hogs before he answers, then gets serious. "We just beat South Carolina 34-6. KJ Jackson threw two touchdowns. Chris Marshall has 761 receiving yards. We lost to Tennessee by seven and at Auburn by three. And in 2021 we put 40 on y'all in Fayetteville. Respect the hog."

I'll be honest about the risk on the points. Arkansas has lost by more than three touchdowns four times this year: Utah, Georgia, Texas A&M and Missouri. Twenty and a half is not a gift. But Texas has not been a blowout machine against good teams. The Longhorns beat Ohio State by one, Tennessee by three, Ole Miss by seven and LSU by one, and lost to Oklahoma and Florida by three each. Their three wins by more than 20 came against Texas State, UTSA and Mississippi State. Manning threw three interceptions last week. My 32-13 is a 19-point win, a point and a half shy of the number.

The market agrees with a little less. DraftKings opened Texas −21 with a 48.5 total, as shown in ESPN pickcenter, and the total dropped to 46.5 during the week before ticking back up to 47.5 as of 9:20 PM ET Friday, with the side at Texas −20.5.

The Under is where I feel best. Texas allows 17.8 points a game, and Simmons against the Arkansas front protecting Jackson is the matchup of the afternoon. Arkansas scored 7 at Texas A&M and 10 at Utah. Raleek Brown has 572 rushing yards and Texas will run the clock with a lead. My 45 lands under the 47.5.

The room splits both ways. The Formula has Texas 34, Arkansas 14, takes the Razorbacks +20.5 and goes Over 47.5. Letdown Watch has 33-15 Texas, takes Arkansas +20.5 and also goes Over. Next Man Up is the outlier at Texas 32, Arkansas 11, laying the 20.5 and liking the Under. In The Trenches has no side and no over/under call on this one. Follow The Money has no pick in this game.

Next Man Up: "Two cards taking the Hogs and the Over. That's a lot of faith in a team that scored seven in College Station."

Letdown Watch: "Texas just survived LSU by a point. If you think that team is fresh for a 21-point Saturday, I've got some lights on Waller Creek to sell you."

By the time the last installation goes dark, we'll know whether Texas can turn its close-game habit into a rout.

Texas Longhorns 32, Arkansas Razorbacks 13. Arkansas Razorbacks cover the 20.5. Under 47.5.
