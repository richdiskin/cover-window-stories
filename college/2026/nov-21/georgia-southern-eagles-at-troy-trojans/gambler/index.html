Georgia Southern vs Troy Pick: Trojans +8.5 and the Under 48.5

Georgia Southern Eagles (6-4) at Troy Trojans (4-6)

4:00 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Veterans Memorial Stadium (AL) · ESPN+

XFacebook

The Vet gets two bands on Saturday: Troy's Sound of the South and every high school band that signed up for Band Day, all of them sharing the halftime field in the Trojans' last home game. The Georgia Southern vs Troy spread asks Troy to stay within 8.5 of the Sun Belt East leader, and every card with a side thinks it can. So do I: Troy Trojans +8.5, Under 48.5, with Georgia Southern Eagles 26, Troy Trojans 21 at 4:00 PM ET.

Royce Ogeechee talks like a man flipping through his granddad's scrapbook. "Four straight wins, Marlo. We beat Marshall 49-13 and Georgia State 34-17. Max Johnson has 17 touchdown passes and three interceptions. We're 5-1 in the league and first in the East."

Marlo Pickens, an old long snapper, cracks a boiled peanut. "Royce, Donnie Smith has eight sacks. Keno Jones has 77 tackles. Troy has won five of the seven meetings since 2016, including 28-20 in 2024. And it's Senior Day."

The points start with Georgia Southern's road results. The Eagles won 31-24 at Coastal Carolina and 23-15 at Old Dominion, both by eight or less, and they lost 31-27 at Jacksonville State. Their big wins came at home against Marshall and App State, plus 34-17 at Georgia State. Troy has had a rough month, losing four of its last five, but it beat Sam Houston 24-21 at home and won 24-21 in overtime at James Madison. My 26-21 is a five-point game, three and a half points inside the 8.5.

The risk is the Troy offense. The Trojans score 21.5 a game, and Goose Crowder and Tucker Kilcrease have both led the team in throwing attempts. Troy scored six at Louisiana and 14 against UL Monroe.

DraftKings opened Georgia Southern −8.5 and 47.5, as shown in ESPN pickcenter. The side hasn't moved, and the total is up a point to 48.5.

The Under fits Troy's season. Seven of Troy's ten games have stayed under 48.5, and the Trojans' defense has Smith rushing the passer. Georgia Southern's games have gone over more often, but App State managed three points against the Eagles and Old Dominion managed 15. My 47 is a point and a half under the 48.5.

All four cards with a side like Troy. The Formula has Georgia Southern 27, Troy 20, takes Troy +8.5 and goes Under 48.5. Letdown Watch has Georgia Southern 27, Troy 20, takes Troy +8.5 and goes Under. Next Man Up has Georgia Southern 25, Troy 19, takes Troy +8.5 and likes the Under. In The Trenches has Troy 26, Georgia Southern 24, likes Troy +8.5, and has no total pick. Follow The Money's side and total are both pending.

Letdown Watch: "Georgia Southern has the East lead to protect and a road trip to make. That's a tight game, not a blowout."

In The Trenches: "I've got Troy winning outright. The Vet on Senior Day is a hard place to play."

The Sound of the South will have a lot of extra horns at halftime. Troy needs its defense to keep the Eagles quiet the rest of the afternoon.

Georgia Southern Eagles 26, Troy Trojans 21. Troy Trojans cover the 8.5. Under 48.5.
