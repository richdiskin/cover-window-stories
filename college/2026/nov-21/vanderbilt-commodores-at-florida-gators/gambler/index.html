Vanderbilt vs Florida Prediction: Vandy +13.5, Over 46.5

Vanderbilt Commodores (4-6) at Florida Gators (6-4)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 21 · Ben Hill Griffin Stadium · SEC Network

XFacebook

Florida says goodbye to its seniors at noon Saturday, and the Vanderbilt vs Florida spread asks a hard question about a Senior Day crowd: how many points does a farewell party add? DraftKings has Florida −13.5 with a 46.5 over/under for the last home game of Jon Sumrall's first season, and my pick is Vanderbilt Commodores +13.5 and Over 46.5.

The athletic department is telling people to arrive early for the pregame ceremony at Ben Hill Griffin Stadium. That's the part of Senior Day nobody prices. Emotional starts cut both ways, and a home team playing for the families in the stands tends to score early, then exhale.

Dax Marchetti, who classifies every opponent by species, has the Commodores filed under "wounded." He's not wrong about the form. Vanderbilt has lost five of its last six, and last week Jared Curtis threw 40 times against Alabama for 223 yards and six points. The Commodores score 21.3 a game and allow 28.6.

Pippa Ashcroft has heard all of that and still wants the points. "Junior Sherrill has 63 catches for 847 yards. Curtis didn't throw a pick against Alabama. And your quarterback had to run for 102 yards to beat Kentucky."

She has a case. Aaron Philo is back as Florida's starter, but the Kentucky win was a 21-14 grind where he threw for 119 yards. Florida has scored 7 and 8 points in two of its last three games. A team that needed its quarterback as a runner to win by seven on the road is a big ask to win by two touchdowns, even at home.

DraftKings opened this at Florida −13.5 with a 48.5 total, and the side hasn't budged. The total has come down two points since, to 46.5 as of 7:31 AM ET Saturday.

My final is Florida Gators 31, Vanderbilt Commodores 20. Florida wins by 11, so Vanderbilt stays inside the 13.5. The 51 combined points clear the 46.5. Jadan Baugh has 905 rushing yards and 15 scores, so the Gators will get theirs. Vanderbilt's defense gives up 28.6 a game, and Florida allows 26.2, so Curtis and Sherrill should find two or three trips to the end zone.

The room splits on the side. The Formula has Florida 32-20 and takes Vanderbilt +13.5. Letdown Watch likes Vanderbilt +13.5 at 29-23. Next Man Up goes the other way with Florida −13.5 at 33-18. In The Trenches has it 30-18 and takes Vanderbilt Commodores +13.5, with no over/under call. The Formula, Next Man Up and Letdown Watch all like the Over 46.5. Follow The Money has no side pick and likes the Under 45.5.

Next Man Up: "Thirteen and a half is nothing for a team with Baugh. Letdown Watch thinks every Senior Day is a hangover."

Letdown Watch: "Florida scored 7 against Oklahoma two weeks ago. That's not a hangover. That's a pattern."

The Formula: "You two argue. I'll take the 32-20 and the points."

Senior Day gives Florida plenty of reasons to win. It doesn't promise a blowout, and Vanderbilt has enough Sherrill to keep this inside two scores.

Florida Gators 31, Vanderbilt Commodores 20. Vanderbilt Commodores cover the 13.5. Over 46.5.
