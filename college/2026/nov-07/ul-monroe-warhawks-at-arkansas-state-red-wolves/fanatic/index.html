USA 250 Day in Jonesboro: Arkansas State Hosts UL Monroe

UL Monroe Warhawks (1-7) at Arkansas State Red Wolves (4-4)

3:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Centennial Bank Stadium · ESPN+

ShareXFacebook

UL Monroe Warhawks vs Arkansas State Red Wolves prediction for Saturday, November 7: Arkansas State is calling this one USA 250 Day, and it is also Heroes Day, Ag Day and Paragould Day, with the ROTC Hall of Heroes, an in-game commissioning ceremony and the 30th anniversary of the Ring of Honor. Darla Pickens says it is the busiest Saturday in Jonesboro since she can remember.

Remy Thibodeaux drove up from Monroe with a cooler of boudin and a grudge he has carried for a decade.

"One," he said, holding up a finger as he walked into Darla's tailgate. "One time we beat y'all in ten years. And it was last year. Twenty-eight to sixteen."

Darla runs a family farm outside town, and she explains football the way she explains soybeans: you plant, you wait, you harvest. "And nine times," she said, "we beat you."

That part is true. Arkansas State won 9 of the 10 meetings from 2016 through 2025. Some of them were wild. The Red Wolves won 67-50 in 2017 and 48-41 in 2019. Remy remembers both. He wishes he did not.

This fall has been hard for the Warhawks. UL Monroe is 1-7. It lost 13-62 at Mississippi State, 35-52 at South Alabama and 0-24 to Louisiana Tech. It scores 20.2 points a game and gives up 37.0. Remy does not hide from it. He just points to the one bright spot.

"We won at Troy," he said. "Twenty-one to fourteen. On the road. Aidan Armenta threw 2 touchdowns."

Darla gave him that one. Armenta has 1,323 yards and 10 touchdowns, with only 3 interceptions. Marcellus Jackson caught an 83-yard score last week against Southern Miss. Noah Flemmings has 75 tackles, and Kevontay Wells has 3 sacks. Remy calls Flemmings the bayou bouncer, because nobody gets past him without paying.

Arkansas State is 4-4, and Darla says the record does not tell the whole story. The Red Wolves lost 20-23 at Louisiana and 49-56 to South Alabama, and either of those could have gone the other way. Since then, they won 23-13 at Southern Miss and 38-32 over Georgia State. They have also had a week off to rest.

Trey Owens is the reason Darla sleeps well. He has 1,940 yards and 11 touchdowns. He threw for 418 against South Alabama and 309 against Georgia State. Jaylen Bonelli caught 7 balls for 150 yards in that last one. Joe Drew has 74 tackles from the safety spot. Solomon Baggett leads the team on the ground with 306 yards.

"Our defense gives up some," Darla admitted. "But your defense gives up more."

Remy had boudin in his mouth and could not argue.

Here is how it shakes out. Arkansas State comes out rested and loud, Owens finds Bonelli early, and the Red Wolves go up two scores before the first quarter ends. Armenta keeps UL Monroe in it for a while with some long throws, and Flemmings makes a dozen tackles, because he always does. But the Warhawks cannot slow Owens for four quarters. Arkansas State pulls away in the second half, and Darla's grill stays busy until dark.

The best part of the day happens before kickoff. The ROTC Hall of Heroes welcomes new names, and young officers take their oath right on the field in the commissioning ceremony. Darla has watched that ceremony for years, and she still tears up every time. Remy takes his hat off and stands for every word.

"That part," he said, "I can cheer for."

Then kickoff comes, and he goes right back to hollering for the Warhawks.

If the game gets out of hand, Darla says there is always the NEAVAL Art Show and Sale at the Harlan Henry Center that morning. Remy says he will go if they have a painting of the 2025 game.

My call: Arkansas State Red Wolves 37, UL Monroe Warhawks 19.
