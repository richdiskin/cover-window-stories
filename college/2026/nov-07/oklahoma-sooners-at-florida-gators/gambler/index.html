Oklahoma Sooners at Florida Gators

Oklahoma Sooners (4-4) at Florida Gators (5-3)

7:45 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Ben Hill Griffin Stadium · SEC Network

ShareXFacebook

Oklahoma vs Florida prediction: we're taking Oklahoma Sooners +7, we're on the Over 45.5, and the final we're calling is Florida Gators 28, Oklahoma Sooners 23. Kickoff is 7:45 PM ET Saturday at Ben Hill Griffin Stadium in Gainesville, where Florida is saluting those who serve.

Savannah Redbird-Lowe has had Oklahoma season tickets since college and says the Sooners are better than 4-4. Dax Marchetti grew up a short drive from the Swamp and says nobody comes into Gainesville at night and leaves happy.

"The Swamp at night," Savannah says. "I've heard about it."

"You're going to hear it," Dax says. "Up close."

Why the Sooners with the points: DraftKings opened Florida −4 and it has climbed to 7. That is a big jump, and it gives the Sooners room. Oklahoma is 4-4, but it beat Texas 27-24 and lost only 10-17 at Michigan. Florida is ranked, but it has a quarterback question. Aaron Philo has 1,367 throwing yards, but Tramell Jones Jr. took the snaps the last two games, including an 8-27 loss to Georgia. John Mateer has 1,835 yards and 13 touchdowns for Oklahoma, and he threw for 244 yards and 2 touchdowns last week. A touchdown is a lot of room in a game this close on paper.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 48.5 and it dropped to 45.5. We're on the Over 45.5. Florida scores 35.6 a game. Jadan Baugh has 801 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns. Oklahoma allowed 42 to South Carolina last week. Both teams get into the 20s.

The room is split on the side but together on the Over. The Formula lands on Oklahoma +7 and has it 28-25. Next Man Up takes Florida −7 and has it 28-20. In The Trenches is split and takes no side. The Formula lands on the Over. Next Man Up lands on the Over. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Florida lost 17-45 at Missouri and 19-38 to South Carolina. This Florida defense allows 28.1 a game. That is not a defense that shuts the door on a team with a quarterback like Mateer. Florida wins at home, but the Sooners keep it within a touchdown.

"Twenty-seven to twenty-four over Texas," Savannah says. "Remember that?"

"Florida beat Texas by the same score," Dax says. "In Austin."

"Fine," Savannah says. "Then we're even."

Florida Gators 28, Oklahoma Sooners 23. Oklahoma Sooners cover the 7. Over 45.5.
