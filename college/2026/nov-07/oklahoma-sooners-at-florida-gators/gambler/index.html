Oklahoma at Florida: Gainesville Has Two Teams Ready to Stop Discussing Halloween

Oklahoma Sooners (4-4) at Florida Gators (5-3)

7:45 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Ben Hill Griffin Stadium · SEC Network

XFacebook

Oklahoma at Florida: FLA -5.5; total 45.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

Gainesville gets Oklahoma-Florida with neither side eager to frame last week's score. Florida lost 27-8 to Georgia. Oklahoma lost 42-25 to South Carolina. ESPN's AP preview has the Gators 5-3 and the Sooners 4-4.

Oklahoma and Florida have never played on either campus, and that ends tonight with the Sooners' first visit to the Swamp, per Sports Illustrated. Oklahoma tight end Hayden Hansen adds a personal thread: he spent three seasons in Gainesville before transferring to Norman, per The Independent Florida Alligator.

Oklahoma's Ivan Carreon caught five for 88 yards and two touchdowns in that defeat. Florida's Jadan Baugh has 801 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns in the season figures. Follow the player who can make the home crowd get up, then the visiting receiver who can make it sit down.

John Mateer threw two touchdowns without an interception against South Carolina, per ESPN. Watch whether the Sooners turn that passing work into enough complete drives. Florida needs Baugh doing more than his 38 yards against Georgia. The Swamp gets to ask both questions at full volume, and neither team gets to answer with last week's excuse.

My published card stays Oklahoma Sooners +7 and Over 45.5. My final is Florida Gators 28, Oklahoma Sooners 23. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

That is a big jump, and it gives the Sooners room. Oklahoma is 4-4, but it beat Texas 27-24 and lost only 10-17 at Michigan. Florida is ranked, but it has a quarterback question. Aaron Philo has 1,367 throwing yards, but Tramell Jones Jr. took the snaps the last two games, including an 8-27 loss to Georgia. John Mateer has 1,835 yards and 13 touchdowns for Oklahoma, and he threw for 244 yards and 2 touchdowns last week. A touchdown is a lot of room in a game this close on paper.

We're on the Over 45.5. Florida scores 35.6 a game. Jadan Baugh has 801 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns. Oklahoma allowed 42 to South Carolina last week. Both teams get into the 20s.

Baugh is an obvious place for Florida to begin, but Oklahoma knows that too. The Gators need a possession that keeps moving after the first stopped run. Carreon's recent work gives the Sooners a different way to disturb the home crowd, provided the passing yards do not arrive after the game has escaped them. Mateer protecting the football against South Carolina is worth carrying forward even from an unhappy result. Oklahoma needs the rest of the possession to catch up with that part of the performance. Florida would rather make the passing game rush its answers.

Florida Gators 28, Oklahoma Sooners 23. Oklahoma Sooners cover the 7. Over 45.5.
