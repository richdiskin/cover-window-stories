Florida Rocks Gainesville, and Oklahoma Wants Its Pride

Oklahoma Sooners (4-4) at Florida Gators (5-3)

7:45 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Ben Hill Griffin Stadium · SEC Network

ShareXFacebook

Oklahoma vs Florida prediction for Saturday night in Gainesville: the Sooners come in at 4-4, the Gators are 5-3 and ranked No. 25, and Ben Hill Griffin Stadium will be rocking under the lights.

Savannah Redbird-Lowe has had Oklahoma season tickets since she was a student in Norman. She drives a crimson pickup with a Sooner Schooner sticker on the back window. Dax Marchetti grew up a short drive from the Swamp, owns three different orange-and-blue jerseys, and says the heat and noise in Gainesville can break anybody.

This one is the Saluting Those Who Serve game. Florida will honor members of the armed forces, veterans and local first responders, and a ticket gets donated to a local military hero. Outside the O'Connell Center at Gate 2, there's a free Fan Fest that opens three hours before kickoff. Kickoff is 7:45 p.m. ET.

"Fan Fest," Savannah says. "Do they have barbecue?"

"They have everything," Dax says. "Come early."

These two have met before, but not often. Florida won 24-14 in January 2009. Oklahoma won 55-20 in December 2020. Savannah brings up the second one. Dax brings up the first one.

"That was the big one," Dax says.

"That was seventeen years ago," Savannah says. "Ours was more recent."

Savannah goes first. John Mateer is the leader in Norman. He has 1,835 yards, 13 touchdowns and 7 interceptions. Last week he went 26 of 43 for 244 yards and 2 touchdowns, but Oklahoma lost 25-42 to South Carolina. Isaiah Sategna III is the top receiver with 591 yards. Lloyd Avant leads the backs with 347 yards. On defense, Adepoju Adebawore has 4 sacks and Owen Heinecke has 63 tackles.

"We beat Texas," Savannah says. "27-24."

"We beat Texas too," Dax says. "27-24. In Austin."

"Same score?" Savannah says.

"Same score," Dax says. "Except ours was on their field."

Dax takes his turn. Florida has a first-year head coach in Jon Sumrall, and the Gators have been fun to watch. Aaron Philo threw for 1,367 yards before Tramell Jones Jr. took over the last two games. Against Texas, Jones went 22 of 35 for 155 yards and 2 touchdowns. Against Georgia, it was tougher. Florida lost 8-27. Jadan Baugh is the workhorse with 801 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns. Dallas Wilson leads the receivers with 417 yards. On defense, Jayden Woods has 4.5 sacks and Myles Graham has 55 tackles.

"Baugh is going to run all night," Dax says.

"We'll be ready," Savannah says.

"You gave up 42 to South Carolina," Dax says.

"So did you," Savannah says. "Almost. You gave up 38."

Florida has put up big numbers at home. The Gators scored 66 on Florida Atlantic, 52 on Campbell and 52 on Ole Miss. They also lost 17-45 at Missouri and 19-38 to South Carolina at home. Oklahoma lost 13-41 at Georgia and 13-21 to Kentucky.

Take it from the opening kick. The Swamp is loud from the opening kick. Florida gets the ball first and Baugh runs right at the Sooners. He breaks a long run, and the Gators score early. Mateer answers. He hits Sategna for a big gain, then runs it in himself. It is tied after the first quarter.

The game goes back and forth. Florida takes the lead at halftime on another Baugh touchdown. In the third quarter, Mateer leads a long drive, and Oklahoma kicks a field goal. Jones finds a receiver deep in the fourth quarter, and Florida pulls ahead by two scores. Oklahoma answers with a late touchdown, but the Gators recover the onside kick and run out the clock.

Dax says the Swamp is undefeated at night. Savannah says that is not true, and that she will be back.

My call: Florida Gators 28, Oklahoma Sooners 23.
