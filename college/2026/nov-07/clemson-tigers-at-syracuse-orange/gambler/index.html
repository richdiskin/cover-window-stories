Clemson at Syracuse: Syracuse Has Already Spoiled One Party. Clemson Is Next Through the Door

Clemson Tigers (5-3) at Syracuse Orange (4-4)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · JMA Wireless Dome · ESPN2

ShareXFacebook

Clemson at Syracuse: CLEM -1.5; total 49.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

Syracuse does not need a visitor's ranking to make this interesting. The Orange just beat SMU 30-27, and Clemson comes in after winning 36-24 at Florida State. Two teams carrying fresh evidence that the next Saturday can look better than the last month.

Dabo Swinney has not forgotten last September. “They kicked our teeth in last year in the first half,” he said Tuesday, per TigerNet, recalling Syracuse's 34-21 win at Clemson. He also had kind words for the place he is headed: he said he has a lot of respect for this Syracuse team and called the Dome a tough place to play.

ESPN's AP preview puts Umari Hatcher at 952 receiving yards and eight touchdowns. Clemson quarterback Tait Reynolds ran for 85 yards and a score in the Florida State win. Watch Hatcher when Syracuse needs a chain-moving completion, then watch whether Reynolds can escape the pocket without turning every drive into a scramble drill.

Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and its total is pending too.

The Orange have just given their home fans a win over SMU, as ESPN reports. That gives the home crowd a real football reason to get loud; we do not need a made-up player speech to supply one. Clemson's 5-3 and Syracuse's 4-4 are the records in ESPN's preview. For somebody, that final month can still become a recovery story. Give this one a screen before the national games swallow the room.

The published Cover Writer card stays Syracuse Orange +1.5 and Over 49.5. My final is Syracuse Orange 26, Clemson Tigers 25. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

The Clemson case for most of the season has been defense, and that defense has holes. The Tigers allow 21.6 and score only 23.2, and they lost 10-51 at LSU, 13-41 to Miami and 16-20 to Virginia Tech. Tait Reynolds has 1,285 throwing yards with 3 touchdowns and 3 interceptions. Last week at Florida State he threw for 189 and 2 scores and ran 14 times for 85 and another touchdown in a 36-24 win. Gideon Davidson has 528 rushing yards, and Will Heldt has 7 sacks.

Syracuse has the better offense. The Orange score 31.4 a game and allow 26.4. Umari Hatcher has 60 catches for 952 yards and 8 touchdowns, which is a monster season for any receiver in the conference. Steve Angeli has 1,187 yards and was the quarterback with the most attempts in the last two wins, 31-30 in overtime at North Carolina and 30-27 over SMU. Keyshawn Johnson has 6 sacks.

The series is lopsided. Clemson won 7 of the 9 meetings since 2016. But the last one went Syracuse's way, 34-21 in 2025.

Syracuse Orange 26, Clemson Tigers 25. Syracuse Orange cover the 1.5. Over 49.5.
