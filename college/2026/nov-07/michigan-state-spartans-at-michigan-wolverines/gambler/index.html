Michigan State Gets 12.5 at the Big House and Keeps It Ugly

Michigan State Spartans (2-6) at Michigan Wolverines (5-3)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Michigan Stadium · Peacock

ShareXFacebook

Michigan State vs Michigan prediction: we're taking Michigan State Spartans +12.5, we're on the Over 39.5, and the final we're calling is Michigan Wolverines 26, Michigan State Spartans 15. Kickoff is 7:30 PM ET Saturday at Michigan Stadium, and Ann Arbor is calling it a Blue Out and Military Appreciation Game, with a flag for every branch on the field during the anthem.

Shay Kowalczyk has lived in East Lansing her whole life and says a 2-6 team is the most dangerous animal in the state. Gus Hartwell says the Big House is sold out and the Spartans should enjoy the view.

"Blue Out," Shay says. "Great. We'll be the only thing in green that moves."

"You'll be the only thing in green that loses," Gus says.

Why the Spartans with the points: DraftKings opened this at Michigan −18.5 and it has come all the way down to 12.5. Michigan is 5-3 and scores 21.2 a game. That is not an offense that runs teams off the field. Seven of Michigan's eight games were one-score games, including 13-12 over Western Michigan, 19-20 to Iowa and 6-13 in overtime to Indiana. Michigan State lost 37-38 in overtime to Illinois and 21-24 to Northwestern. Alessio Milivojevic has 1,263 yards, 7 touchdowns and only 3 interceptions. Twelve and a half is a lot of room for a Michigan team that grinds for everything.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 50.5 and the market dragged it to 39.5. That drop went too far. We're on the Over 39.5. Michigan State gives up 25.8 a game. Tommy Carr went 22 of 27 for 266 yards at Rutgers last week, and JJ Buchanan has 611 receiving yards. Michigan gets into the 20s here, and the Spartans find two scores.

The room agrees on the Spartans, which almost never happens. The Formula lands on Michigan State +12.5 and has it 26-16. Next Man Up lands on Michigan State +12.5 and has it 25-15. In The Trenches likes Michigan State +12.5 too, and it has no score this week. The Formula lands on the Over. Next Man Up lands on the Over. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Gus is already planning the walk to the tunnel. Shay is already planning the drive home with the window down, screaming about a backdoor touchdown.

"Two scores," Gus says. "We win by two scores."

"Two scores is not twelve and a half," Shay says. "Count it."

Michigan Wolverines 26, Michigan State Spartans 15. Michigan State Spartans cover the 12.5. Over 39.5.
