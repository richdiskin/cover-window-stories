Missouri State Paddles South to Face FIU at Homecoming

Missouri State Bears (3-5) at Florida International Panthers (3-5)

4:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Pitbull Stadium · ESPN+

ShareXFacebook

Missouri State Bears vs Florida International Panthers prediction for Saturday, November 7: Clementine Bilyeu has paddled her last float trip of the season out of Springfield and flown south in a fleece vest, and Lourdes Ibarguen-Tate is waiting for her at Pitbull Stadium with a thermos of her abuela's cafecito and a gold shirt in Clementine's size.

"Gold?" Clementine asks.

"It's Homecoming, mija," Lourdes says. "And Military Appreciation. Everybody wears gold. Even the visitors, if they have manners."

Saturday is Homecoming at FIU, and it's also Military Appreciation day, with the whole crowd asked to wear gold. Kickoff is 4:00 p.m. ET in Miami. Lourdes grew up in Sweetwater, a short drive from campus, and she says this is the one game a year her whole family shows up for, cousins included.

"How many cousins?" Clementine asks.

"Enough to fill a section," Lourdes says. "Abuela keeps the list."

Clementine pulls on the gold shirt over her fleece vest, which Lourdes says is a crime against the Miami weather. Clementine says she's dressed for a river in November, not a stadium in Florida.

Now the football.

Clementine goes first, the way she reads a river: calm, patient, looking for where it runs fast. Henry Belin IV took over at quarterback for Missouri State in October and has 1,071 passing yards and 8 touchdowns. His big target is Isaiah Myers, who has 41 catches for 873 yards and 8 touchdowns. Jared Lloyd leads the defense with 86 tackles, and Josh Lukusa has 2.5 sacks. Last week the Bears beat Sam Houston 24-3.

"Myers is the rapids," Clementine says. "You see him coming and you still can't stop him."

"We'll see," Lourdes says, pouring two tiny cups of cafecito.

Lourdes takes over. JJ Kohl has thrown for 1,801 yards and 11 touchdowns. Maguire Anderson leads the receivers with 41 catches for 596 yards. Devonte Lyons has 435 rushing yards. On defense, DeeJay Holmes Jr. has 5 sacks and DeAndre Boykins leads with 45 tackles. FIU gives up only 20.4 a game.

"Our defense is like Abuela's kitchen," Lourdes says. "You don't get through without permission."

Then Lourdes admits the hard part. FIU has lost three in a row, including 10-49 at Liberty last week. The Panthers score only 18.9 a game. Kohl has 9 interceptions.

"That's a muddy stretch of river," Clementine says, gently.

"It's a bad week," Lourdes says. "Every family has one. But we're 3-1 at home."

Clementine has one more thing. Missouri State won this matchup 28-21 last year. Lourdes says she does not remember that game, and her cousins don't either, and if anybody brings it up at the tailgate, they'll be cut off from the cafecito.

Both teams are 3-5. Missouri State scores 24.4 a game and gives up 30.9. These are two teams looking for a good afternoon, and only one of them will get it.

Then the ball gets kicked in Miami. The gold crowd is loud, the cousins fill their section, and the veterans in the stands get the biggest cheer of the day on Military Appreciation day. Myers catches a long one early, and Clementine stands up in her fleece. But Holmes Jr. gets after Belin IV, and the FIU defense settles in. Kohl finds Anderson for a touchdown before halftime, Lyons runs hard in the second half, and Boykins makes the stop that ends Missouri State's last drive.

Lourdes pours one more cafecito for Clementine and says she can come back next year, as long as she wears gold again. Clementine says she'll bring a lighter vest.

My call: Florida International Panthers 26, Missouri State Bears 21.
