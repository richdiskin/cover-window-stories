Missouri State at Florida International: Miami Has Another Football Game, and These Fans Count Too

Missouri State Bears (3-5) at Florida International Panthers (3-5)

4:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Pitbull Stadium · ESPN+

Missouri State at Florida International: FIU -4.5; total 49.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

Miami is not only the name on the biggest television matchup tonight. FIU has a home game against Missouri State this afternoon, with both teams 3-5. These players have their own families watching and their own Saturday to repair or build on.

FIU makes it Homecoming and Military Appreciation at Pitbull Stadium, and it is the first time this series has come to Miami, per FIU Athletics. Missouri State brings Isaiah Myers, who leads Conference USA with 873 receiving yards and eight touchdowns.

ESPN's AP preview says FIU lost 49-10 at Liberty while Missouri State beat Sam Houston 24-3. The Bears' Henry Belin IV threw two touchdowns without an interception in that win, and Jmariyae Robinson caught seven for 102 yards and a score.

Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and its total is pending too.

FIU's Maguire Anderson has 597 receiving yards in the report; Missouri State's Isaiah Myers has 873 and eight touchdowns. Follow the receivers rather than waiting for someone to tell you which game is fashionable. FIU can make its latest result feel less current, and Missouri State can make its last good performance feel less lonely. That is enough reason to pay attention to this corner of Miami.

The published Cover Writer card stays Florida International Panthers −4 and Under 50.5. My final is Florida International Panthers 26, Missouri State Bears 21. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

Why FIU: The Panthers have lost three straight, including 10-49 at Liberty, but they are 3-1 at home and their defense gives up only 20.4 a game. DeeJay Holmes Jr. has 5 sacks, and DeAndre Boykins has 45 tackles. Missouri State gives up 30.9 a game. JJ Kohl has 1,801 passing yards and 11 touchdowns, and Maguire Anderson has 41 catches for 596 yards. Back home, with the Homecoming crowd, FIU should play its best game in weeks.

We're on the Under 50.5. FIU scores only 18.9 a game. Missouri State scores 24.4, but the Bears just beat Sam Houston 24-3, and that was their defense as much as their offense. Kohl has thrown 9 interceptions. Neither side should pile it up.

Anderson and Myers give this game two receivers worth keeping in view even when another television is drawing the room's attention. Belin's clean performance against Sam Houston is the part Missouri State would like to take on the road. FIU has a different responsibility: make the visitors prove it again rather than accept the last result as today's script. For two teams trying to climb back toward a better season, that is plenty of football to work with.

Florida International Panthers 26, Missouri State Bears 21. Florida International Panthers cover the 4. Under 50.5.
