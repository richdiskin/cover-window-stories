FIU Lays 4 to Missouri State on Homecoming in Miami

Missouri State Bears (3-5) at Florida International Panthers (3-5)

4:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Pitbull Stadium · ESPN+

ShareXFacebook

Missouri State vs FIU prediction: our pick is Florida International Panthers −4, we're on the Under 50.5, and the final we're calling is Florida International Panthers 26, Missouri State Bears 21. Kickoff is 4:00 PM ET Saturday at Pitbull Stadium in Miami, and it's Homecoming for FIU.

Clementine Bilyeu guides float trips out of Springfield and reads a football game like a river, looking for where it gets shallow. Lourdes Ibarguen-Tate grew up in Sweetwater, a short drive from campus, and she brought her abuela's cafecito in a thermos.

"Four points is a gentle current," Clementine says.

"Mija, four points is a cafecito," Lourdes says. "Small, strong, and it wakes you up."

Why FIU: The Panthers have lost three straight, including 10-49 at Liberty, but they are 3-1 at home and their defense gives up only 20.4 a game. DeeJay Holmes Jr. has 5 sacks, and DeAndre Boykins has 45 tackles. Missouri State gives up 30.9 a game. JJ Kohl has 1,801 passing yards and 11 touchdowns, and Maguire Anderson has 41 catches for 596 yards. Back home, with the Homecoming crowd, FIU should play its best game in weeks.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 49.5 with FIU −4.5. Both have moved a little, to 50.5 and −4. We're on the Under 50.5. FIU scores only 18.9 a game. Missouri State scores 24.4, but the Bears just beat Sam Houston 24-3, and that was their defense as much as their offense. Kohl has thrown 9 interceptions. Neither side should pile it up.

The room is split on the side but not the over/under. The Formula lands on Missouri State +4 without backing it hard and has it FIU 25-22. Next Man Up likes FIU −4 and has it FIU 27-20. In The Trenches likes Missouri State +4. The Formula likes the Under. Next Man Up likes the Under too. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

The worry is Isaiah Myers. He has 41 catches for 873 yards and 8 touchdowns for Missouri State, and Henry Belin IV has 1,071 passing yards and 8 touchdowns in a few starts. Jared Lloyd has 86 tackles. Missouri State won 28-21 last year. But FIU at home is a different team, and we expect the Panthers to win by more than a field goal.

"The river's slow this week," Clementine says. "But it always gets somewhere."

"Have another cafecito," Lourdes says. "It's going to be a long afternoon."

Florida International Panthers 26, Missouri State Bears 21. Florida International Panthers cover the 4. Under 50.5.
