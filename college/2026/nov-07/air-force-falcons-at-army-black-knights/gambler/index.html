Air Force at Army: West Point Has a Trophy Mission and Room for Everybody to Care

Air Force Falcons (6-2) at Army Black Knights (5-3)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Michie Stadium · CBS

XFacebook

Air Force at Army: ARMY -4; total 43.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

West Point gets Air Force-Army under the lights. Army's official game notes say this opens its pursuit of the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy and that the Black Knights are seeking a fourth straight win over Air Force. That is a purpose bigger than needing a game to leave on in the background.

Air Force can take the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy home from West Point, and cornerback Korey Johnson did not hide how badly the Falcons want it. “We need this one. We want it. We want it so bad,” he said, per The Gazette of Colorado Springs.

The notes also say Michie's east stands have been rebuilt through a two-year preservation project. There is actual place-and-people news for a home Saturday: an old football setting with a major new piece, hosting a game its people recognize immediately.

ESPN's AP capsule has Air Force at 6-2 after beating UConn 35-26, and Army at 5-3 after a 49-22 loss at Memphis. Army's Cale Hellums has 17 rushing touchdowns in the season figures. The two offenses produce 342.5 and 341 rushing yards respectively. If you enjoy a defense having to honor every assignment, congratulations: your evening has been scheduled.

The official notes recall Dawson Jones' game-winning 27-yard field goal against Air Force last year after a 42-yard completion from Hellums to Brady Anderson. That is a reminder that a game built on running can still turn on one throw and one kick. Respect both uniforms, pull hard for your side, and give West Point the attention this meeting deserves.

My published card stays Army Black Knights −3.5 and Over 47.5. My final is Army Black Knights 31, Air Force Falcons 25. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

Cale Hellums has 874 rushing yards and 17 rushing touchdowns, and he has also thrown for 462 yards. Army scores 33.6 a game. Air Force is good on the ground too, but its quarterback room has shifted all year, from Liam Szarka to Evan Medders to Howard Fisher IV, and last week the ball went to Kemper Hodges, who carried it 35 times for 200 yards and 4 touchdowns against UConn. Army has won seven of the last ten meetings in this series, including 20-17 last year. We'll take the home team at less than a touchdown.

We're on the Over 47.5. This is not the old academy game. Air Force scores 29.2 a game and gives up 21.5. Army gives up 25.5, and it just allowed 49 at Memphis. Both offenses can finish drives.

Army Black Knights 31, Air Force Falcons 25. Army Black Knights cover the 3.5. Over 47.5.
