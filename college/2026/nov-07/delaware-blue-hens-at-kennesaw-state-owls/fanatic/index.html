Taste of Kennesaw and Homecoming: Delaware Visits Kennesaw State

Delaware Blue Hens (4-4) at Kennesaw State Owls (2-6)

3:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Walens Family Field at Fifth Third Stadium · ESPN+

ShareXFacebook

Delaware Blue Hens vs Kennesaw State Owls prediction, Homecoming Saturday at Kennesaw State: it doubles as the Mental Health Awareness game, Taste of Kennesaw fills downtown from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m., and Tasha Greer has the whole day mapped out.

Pete Marchetti flew down from Wilmington and did not bring a plan. He brought a towel.

"Blue Hens!" he hollered at the tailgate, swinging the towel over his head. "Two in a row! Four hundred fourteen yards at Western Kentucky!"

Tasha is a nurse, and she has seen louder men calmed down by quieter words. "Pete," she said, "you're 4-4."

Pete stopped swinging. She was right. Delaware opened by beating Merrimack 42-7, then lost 26-35 at Vanderbilt and beat Coastal Carolina 22-14. After that it fell apart for a stretch: 3-42 at Virginia, 14-30 to Liberty, 30-33 at Middle Tennessee. Then the Blue Hens woke up. They beat Missouri State 61-38 and won 35-21 at Western Kentucky.

Nick Minicucci is the reason. He has 1,477 yards and 14 touchdowns with only 4 interceptions. He threw 5 touchdowns against Missouri State and 4 more at Western Kentucky, where he went 32 for 38 for 414 yards. Sean Wilson caught 9 balls for 183 yards in that one, and he has 591 on the year. Blake Matthews has 4.5 sacks. Gavin Moul has 53 tackles.

"He is a different quarterback now," Pete said. "You'll see."

Tasha has watched her own team fight through a long fall. Kennesaw State is 2-6, and almost all of it has been heartbreak. The Owls lost 14-17 at Arkansas State, 26-27 to Jacksonville State and 21-24 at Missouri State. Every one of those came down to the last few minutes. Tasha says she aged a year in October.

But last week was different. Kennesaw State went to Middle Tennessee and won 44-28. Rickie Collins went 16 for 19 with 3 touchdowns. Boogie Woods III ran for 2 more scores. Dylan Gary caught 2 balls, and both went for touchdowns. Tasha watched it twice.

"Middle Tennessee beat you," she reminded Pete. "Thirty-three to thirty. We beat them by 16."

Pete thought about that for a while. Football does not work that way, he said. Tasha said she knows, but it felt good to say.

The Owls have pieces. Collins has 1,632 yards through the air. Woods has 358 rushing yards and 6 touchdowns. Javon Rogers has 397 receiving yards. Ugonna Nwoha has 5 sacks up front, and Tywon Christopher has 62 tackles. Tasha thinks Nwoha is the key. If he gets to Minicucci early, the Blue Hens cannot get comfortable.

Homecoming also matters to her for another reason. This is the Mental Health Awareness game, and Tasha has spent her career in hospitals. She talks about checking on friends the way some people talk about stretching before a run. Pete got quiet when she said it. Then he said that was the best thing anybody had said at a tailgate all year, and he meant it. Tasha told him the best part of Homecoming is seeing who shows up for each other, win or lose, and that a towel-waving Blue Hens fan counts too.

Here is how it shakes out. Delaware moves the ball, because Minicucci is playing as well as anybody in this conference right now. Wilson gets loose once. But the home crowd is huge, Nwoha gets home a couple of times, and Woods grinds out the tough yards in the fourth quarter. It comes down to the last drive, like everything does with this Owls team. This time it goes their way.

After the game, if there is anything left at Taste of Kennesaw, Tasha wants a funnel cake and some live music. Pete says the towel comes too.

My call: Kennesaw State Owls 27, Delaware Blue Hens 26.
