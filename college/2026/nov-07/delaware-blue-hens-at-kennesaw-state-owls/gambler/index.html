Delaware at Kennesaw State: Kennesaw Gets a Quarterback Duel Without the National Fuss

Delaware Blue Hens (4-4) at Kennesaw State Owls (2-6)

3:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Walens Family Field at Fifth Third Stadium · ESPN+

XFacebook

Delaware at Kennesaw State: DEL -1.5; total 57.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

Kennesaw, somebody brought a passing game. Delaware's Nick Minicucci threw for 414 yards and four touchdowns in a 35-21 win over Western Kentucky. Sean Wilson caught nine for 183 and two scores. ESPN's AP preview supplies the evidence; the Owls supply the next test.

Delaware and Kennesaw State have never played before, so somebody gets to write the first chapter, and the Owls get to do it at Homecoming. Delaware brings a quarterback on a heater: Nick Minicucci threw for 414 yards and four touchdowns, completing 32 of 38, in the win over Western Kentucky, per AP.

The home side has a response: Rickie Collins went 16-for-19 with three touchdown passes in Kennesaw State's 44-28 win over Middle Tennessee. Delaware is 4-4, Kennesaw 2-6, and both arrive off something they would willingly put on the stadium screen.

Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and its total is pending too.

Follow Wilson when Delaware needs to keep a drive alive. Follow Alan Woods III near the end zone after his two rushing touchdowns in the Owls' latest win. This is a game for the fan who enjoys finding the good matchup before the television studio notices. Kennesaw gets to decide whether those visiting passing numbers travel as well as the bus.

The published Cover Writer card stays Kennesaw State Owls +2.5 and Under 55.5. My final is Kennesaw State Owls 27, Delaware Blue Hens 26. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

Here is why I take the points. Kennesaw State is 2-6, but three of those losses were by a field goal or less: 14-17 at Arkansas State, 26-27 to Jacksonville State and 21-24 at Missouri State. The Owls finally broke through last week. Delaware is 4-4, and it gives up 27.5 a game. The Blue Hens lost 30-33 at Middle Tennessee, the same team the Owls just beat by 16. Delaware has also rotated quarterbacks: Minicucci, Braden Streeter, EJ Archield Jr. and Tyler Bell have each led in throws.

The risk is Minicucci. He has 14 touchdown throws and only 4 interceptions, and he threw 9 touchdowns in the last two games. Kennesaw State scores just 23.0 a game.

I think that went too far. Kennesaw State scored 9 at Tennessee, 14 at Arkansas State and 6 against Liberty. The Owls give up 25.4 a game.

The Owls cannot let Wilson's first useful catch become an afternoon of chasing him. Collins gives Kennesaw a way to answer, but trading possessions carelessly would let Delaware dictate the terms. I want the home offense making the visitors defend the entire drive, not waiting for its next turn while another Minicucci completion disappears up the sideline.

Kennesaw State Owls 27, Delaware Blue Hens 26. Kennesaw State Owls cover the 2.5. Under 55.5.
