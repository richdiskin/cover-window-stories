Buffalo vs Miami (OH) prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm laying Miami (OH) RedHawks −6.5 and taking Over 45.5 at DraftKings, with my final at Miami (OH) RedHawks 29, Buffalo Bulls 22. Oxford spends Election Day with Party at the Polls under the Bell Tower on High Street, and by 8:00 PM ET the crowd walks past the Cradle of Coaches statues at the south gate of Yager Stadium for a game between two 5-3 teams.

Here is why I lay it. Miami has won six of the nine meetings since 2016 in the CFBD file, and the RedHawks score 27.0 a game to Buffalo's 22.6. Buffalo has been living on the wire, winning 25-18 in overtime against Massachusetts and 31-28 over Robert Morris. For Buffalo, Jason Wright has 987 yards with 7 touchdowns and 4 interceptions, and Chance Morrow has 41 catches for 480 yards. If Burch controls the game, the Bulls hang around.
