Buffalo Brings Wings to Oxford, and the RedHawks Want Them All

Buffalo Bulls (5-3) at Miami (OH) RedHawks (5-3)

8:00 PM ET Tue Nov 3 · Yager Stadium · ESPN

ShareXFacebook

Buffalo Bulls vs Miami (OH) RedHawks prediction for Tuesday, November 3: Oxford votes all day at the Party at the Polls under the Bell Tower, then votes again at Yager Stadium. Kendra Woolcott drove in from Western New York with a cooler of wings, and Cordelia Van Doren is waiting at the south gate with a trivia card and a smile that means trouble.

The Bell Tower party at 501 E. High Street runs from 11 in the morning until 7:30 at night, and across campus a pianist named Sean Chen takes the stage at 7:30 too. Cordelia has made her choice. She'll take the band, the bricks and the bronze.

"Every visitor walks past my coaches first," she says, gesturing at the 11 bronze statues outside the stadium. "Paul Brown. Weeb Ewbank. Ara Parseghian. Bo Schembechler. John Harbaugh. Sean McVay. Kendra, name one Buffalo statue that tall."

"Name one Oxford wing that's worth the drive," Kendra says, cracking open the cooler. "I'll wait. Weather report, by the way: whatever the sky does tonight, it's not lake effect, so it's not a problem."

Both teams walk in at 5-3, and both sit 3-1 in the MAC. Neither one has lost since early October. Buffalo beat Toledo 23-8, Massachusetts 25-18 in overtime and Bowling Green 31-24. Miami beat Massachusetts 20-19, Akron 34-14 and Central Michigan 28-27.

"We win ugly and we like it," Kendra says. "Ugly travels."

"We win close and we like it," Cordelia says. "Close is a family tradition. Ask the statues."

The Bulls run through Messiah Burch, and there is no polite way to say how much. He carried 33 times for 184 yards and three touchdowns against Bowling Green and has 845 yards on 151 carries. Jason Wright has thrown for 987 yards with 7 touchdowns, and Chance Morrow is the one he looks for, with 41 catches for 480 yards and 6 scores.

"Burch is a snowplow," Kendra says. "You hear him before you see him, and then the road's clear."

Cordelia does not flinch. "Our defense has met snowplows. Roosevelt Andrews III has 4.5 sacks, and Malcolm McCain has 59 tackles. We've been clearing roads in Oxford since before your stadium had a parking lot."

That is a trivia ambush, and Kendra knows it. "You don't know when our parking lot opened."

"No," Cordelia admits. "But you hesitated."

For Miami, the question under center is a two-name story. David McComb leads the team with 1,121 yards and 7 touchdowns, and he threw for 334 at Cincinnati. Caleb Heavner has taken the most throws in each of the last four games, and he went 18 of 33 for 221 yards at Central Michigan, adding 58 on the ground and a touchdown run. Maleek Huggins caught seven balls for 122 yards that day and leads the RedHawks with 431 receiving yards.

"Whoever throws it, Huggins catches it," Cordelia says. "That's not a scheme. That's manners."

Buffalo's defense has its own anchor in SaVeon Brown, with 67 tackles, and Sedrick Vessah has 2 sacks off the corner. The Bulls have given up 25.4 points a game, and Miami has given up 25.8. Nobody in this matchup has been shy about letting the other side score.

"So this is a shootout," Kendra says.

"This is a conversation," Cordelia says. "With scoreboards."

The history tilts her way. In nine meetings from 2016 to 2025, Miami won 6 and Buffalo won 3. The last one, in 2025, ended Miami 37, Buffalo 20. Buffalo's best night in the stretch was a 51-42 win in 2018, and Kendra brings it up before anyone can stop her.

"Fifty-one," she says. "Write that on a statue."

"Even legends lost once," Cordelia says, "and I can tell you which ones."

Here's the shape of it. Burch gets his carries and keeps Buffalo in it through three quarters. Wright finds Morrow for a big score. Miami answers with Huggins over the top, and the RedHawks defense makes one stop late when Buffalo needs a drive. The south gate crowd heads home past the Bell Tower, and Cordelia adds one more gold mark to her chart.

Kendra packs up the cooler. "Just another lake-effect Tuesday," she says, and grins anyway.

My call: Miami (OH) RedHawks 29, Buffalo Bulls 22.
