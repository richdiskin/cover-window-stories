Marshall at Georgia Southern: Statesboro Has Fresh Rivalry Joy and a New Visitor

Marshall Thundering Herd (5-3) at Georgia Southern Eagles (4-4)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Allen E. Paulson Stadium · ESPN+

ShareXFacebook

Statesboro just got a 19-3 win over App State. Marshall comes in after beating Old Dominion 36-23. ESPN's AP preview gives us two teams with a good recent memory and only one opportunity to add another today.

Georgia Southern hosts Homecoming with first place in the East on its mind, and Clay Helton has a simple math problem for his team: when the Eagles win the rushing battle and the turnover battle, they are 4-0, and when they lose either, they are 0-4, per the Statesboro Herald. Marshall is turning to Zion Turner at quarterback.

Georgia Southern's Max Johnson threw for 318 yards against App State, with Alex Taylor catching 10 passes for 93. Marshall's Judah Price ran for 82 yards in the Old Dominion win. Follow Taylor when the Eagles need a reliable completion, and Price when the Herd wants to quiet the passing-game conversation.

The records are Marshall 5-3, Georgia Southern 4-4. The Eagles allow 111.3 rushing yards a game in ESPN's report. Marshall needs useful early-down gains before those passing situations become obvious. A home crowd celebrating last week's result still wants something new to celebrate by sundown.

My call: Georgia Southern Eagles 31, Marshall Thundering Herd 19.
