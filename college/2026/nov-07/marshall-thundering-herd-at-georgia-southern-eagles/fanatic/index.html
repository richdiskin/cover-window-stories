Homecoming in Statesboro: Georgia Southern Welcomes the Herd

Marshall Thundering Herd (5-3) at Georgia Southern Eagles (4-4)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Allen E. Paulson Stadium · ESPN+

ShareXFacebook

Marshall Thundering Herd vs Georgia Southern Eagles prediction from Statesboro: Royce Ogeechee has been posted up at the Bishop Alumni Center since before the coffee was ready, and Saturday hasn't even started yet. Mercy Holderby drove down from Huntington in a green sweater, green socks and, she swears, green earrings she was born wearing.

Georgia Southern is rolling Homecoming and Military Appreciation Day into one, and the alumni tailgate at the Bishop Alumni Center runs from noon until 3 p.m. as a Salute to Service tailgate for active-duty service members, veterans and military families. Kickoff is 3:30 p.m. ET.

And this one has history. Saturday is the 11th all-time meeting between these two schools. They played six times from 1989 to 1996, back when both were two of the most famous programs in I-AA, and then picked it back up when Marshall joined the Sun Belt in 2022.

"I-AA," Royce says, and his eyes get wet. "The option. The buses. Beautiful Eagle Creek. Mercy, those were the days."

"Those were our days too, Royce," Mercy says. "Huntington remembers every one of those games."

"So does Statesboro," Royce says. "Every single snap."

Mercy lets that one sit. She's a nurse. She has patience, and she has a long memory.

The last three meetings were decided by a combined 11 points. Marshall won 38-33 in 2023. Georgia Southern won 24-23 in 2024 and 24-19 last year in Huntington.

"Five points, one point, five points," Royce says. "Y'all keep making me nervous."

"Good," Mercy says.

Now the football.

Royce goes first, because it's his house. Max Johnson has thrown for 1,924 yards with 11 touchdowns and only 2 interceptions. Against App State last week he went 36 of 47 for 327 yards in a 19-3 win. Alex Taylor is his go-to guy, with 34 catches for 435 yards, and he caught 10 against App. On defense, Anthony Bynum has 5 sacks, and Brandon Tyson leads the team with 39 tackles. Georgia Southern gives up 20.5 a game.

"Our defense held App to 3," Royce says. "Three. That's a peanut-sized number, and I know peanuts."

Mercy takes the floor. Marshall is 5-3, and the Herd won 36-23 at Old Dominion last week. Carlos Del Rio-Wilson has 1,107 passing yards, and Zion Turner has taken his share of snaps too. Judah Price leads the ground game with 319 yards and ran for 82 at Old Dominion. Xayvion Turner-Bradshaw has 372 receiving yards and 4 touchdowns. Caleb Clark-Glover leads the defense with 59 tackles, and Kelvon McBride has 3 sacks.

"We are 5-3," Mercy says. "Say it with me. Five and three."

"Y'all also lost 0-45 at Penn State," Royce says.

"September," Mercy says. "That was September. We are a November team."

Marshall scores 23.5 a game and gives up 29.8. Georgia Southern scores 23.8 and gives up 20.5. Mercy reads the Marshall defense figure, then closes her eyes like she's checking a pulse.

"It's going to be fine," she says. "It's always fine. We rebuild. That's what we do."

Royce takes his hat off for that one. Everybody in Statesboro knows what Marshall means when it says that.

Fast-forward to kickoff in Statesboro. The Homecoming crowd is loud, and the veterans honored before kickoff get the biggest cheer of the day. Johnson gets the Eagles moving early with Taylor on short throws, and Georgia Southern punches in a couple of scores before halftime. Marshall stays in it with Price running hard and Turner-Bradshaw making a big catch down the sideline. But Bynum gets home on a third down in the third quarter, and the Eagles turn it into points. The Herd fights to the end, the way the Herd always does, but Georgia Southern pulls away late.

Royce cracks a bag of boiled peanuts and hugs a veteran at the tailgate. Mercy hums "We Are" all the way to the car, sniffles once, and promises Royce that Huntington will remember this.

My call: Georgia Southern Eagles 31, Marshall Thundering Herd 19.
