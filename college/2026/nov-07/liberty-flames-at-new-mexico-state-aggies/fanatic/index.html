7-1 Liberty Rolls Into Las Cruces for Aggie Homecoming

Liberty Flames (7-1) at New Mexico State Aggies (3-5)

8:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Aggie Memorial Stadium · ESPNU

ShareXFacebook

Liberty vs New Mexico State prediction for Saturday night in Las Cruces: the Flames come in at 7-1 and rolling, the Aggies are 3-5 and limping, and it is Homecoming and Ag Day at Aggie Memorial Stadium. Gideon Fairweather says that is nice for the alumni. Esperanza Lujan-Tafoya says that is the whole point.

Gideon has followed Liberty since the program was a small school on a hill in Lynchburg. He knows the fight song by heart and sings it in the car. Esperanza grew up in the Mesilla Valley, eats green chile with every meal, and has a red-and-white Aggies scarf she has worn to every Homecoming since high school. Kickoff is 8 p.m. ET, which is 6 p.m. out in the desert.

"Homecoming," Gideon says. "Who scheduled the Flames for Homecoming?"

"Somebody who likes a challenge," Esperanza says.

This series has history. The two teams have met nine times in the previous eight seasons, which is a lot for two schools that are not close to each other. Liberty has won four straight, including the 49-35 win in the 2023 Conference USA Championship. But Esperanza remembers 2022, when the Aggies beat Liberty 49-14. And 2018, when they won 49-41. Last year it was close, a 30-27 Liberty win.

"Two of nine," Gideon says. "You got two."

"We got two big ones," Esperanza says. "We'll take a third."

Gideon goes first. Deshawn Purdie is the best player on the field, and it is not close. He has 1,453 yards, 10 touchdowns and only 1 interception. Against FIU last week he went 16 of 19 for 227 yards in a 49-10 win. Kam Davis leads the backs with 603 yards and 7 touchdowns. Makai Jackson is the deep threat with 626 receiving yards and 5 touchdowns. On defense, Liberty allows 13.5 points a game. Derrell Farrar leads the team with 43 tackles, and Brenton Williams has 4 sacks.

"Our only loss was 13-20 at James Madison," Gideon says. "In the first week."

"You beat Gardner-Webb by one," Esperanza says. "Gardner-Webb."

"A win is a win," Gideon says.

Esperanza takes her turn, and she knows the Aggies have problems. The offense has struggled. Trey Hedden leads the team with 906 throwing yards, but Adam Damante has taken most of the snaps lately. Against Jacksonville State last week, Damante went 6 of 19 for 29 yards, and New Mexico State lost 0-24.

"Zero," Gideon says. "Your team scored zero."

"James Jones ran for 112," Esperanza says. "Look at that instead."

Jones is the real deal. He has 889 rushing yards on 139 carries this season. TK King leads the receivers with 307 yards. The defense has playmakers too. Gabe Peterson has 5.5 sacks, and Nick Session has 58 tackles.

The Aggies have shown what they can do. They beat Western Kentucky 34-13 and won 30-24 at Sam Houston. They also lost 18-42 to New Mexico, which still makes Esperanza angry.

"Don't bring up the Lobos," she says.

"I didn't," Gideon says.

"You were about to," Esperanza says.

Out in Las Cruces, the radio call is on 99.5 FM KXPZ, and Esperanza's grandfather will have it on in the garage, like he does every Homecoming.

Here's how it plays out. Liberty comes out fast. Purdie hits Jackson for a big gain on the first drive, and Davis punches it in. The Homecoming crowd goes quiet for a minute. Then the Aggies do what they do best. Jones runs it, and runs it again, and the Aggies grind out a long drive that ends with a field goal. Peterson gets to Purdie on a third down, and the crowd comes back to life.

In the second half, Liberty's defense makes it tough on the Aggie quarterback. The Flames add two more scores, but Jones keeps running hard into the fourth quarter, and the Aggies find the end zone late. Liberty wins it, but nobody in the stands goes home early.

Esperanza says she will remember every word Gideon said tonight. Gideon sings his fight song the whole way to the parking lot.

My call: Liberty Flames 31, New Mexico State Aggies 16.
