Miami Hurricanes at Notre Dame Fighting Irish

Miami Hurricanes (8-0) at Notre Dame Fighting Irish (7-1)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Notre Dame Stadium · NBC

ShareXFacebook

Miami vs Notre Dame prediction for Saturday night in South Bend: the Hurricanes, 8-0 and rolling, walk into Notre Dame Stadium at 7:30 PM ET against a 7-1 Irish team that has been waiting for this one since the summer of 2025.

Game day on campus has its rituals, and they start early. At 5:15 PM ET the team leaves the Basilica of the Sacred Heart for the Victory March, with the Band of the Fighting Irish playing along the route and the Notre Dame Bagpipe Band leading the players into the stadium. At 5:30 the Trumpets at the Dome play outside the Main Building, and the Glee Club sings on Library Quad. At 6:00 the band plays its Concert on the Steps of Bond Hall, and the gates open, 90 minutes before kickoff. Later the band marches out to the stadium from the Main Building.

I talked it over with two very different people.

Javi Castillo, who everybody calls The Breeze, played defensive back at The U and still dresses like it is always July in Coral Gables, all linen and sunglasses. He moves between English and Spanish depending on how excited he is. Declan Moriarty-Chen is a grad student at Notre Dame who wears a sweater vest every single day and can tell you the schedule from any season you name.

"Oye, I love this place," Javi said. "The bagpipes, the band. Very classy. And then we beat you."

"You beat us last year," Declan said. "27-24. In Miami. This is our house."

That 2025 game is still sitting with both of them. Miami won it 27-24, and the series before that had a 30-27 Notre Dame win in 2016 and a 41-8 Miami win in 2017. Miami has two of the last three.

Miami is unbeaten for a reason. The Canes score 47.6 points a game and allow 17.8. They won 45-6 at Stanford, 77-7 over Florida A&M, 41-13 at Clemson and 52-23 over Pitt. The last few have been closer, a 43-35 overtime win over Florida State and a 38-35 win at North Carolina last week.

"Darian Mensah," Javi said, taking off his sunglasses for emphasis. "2,425 yards. 25 touchdowns. One interception. Uno."

Malachi Toney has 1,027 receiving yards and 13 touchdowns, including 2 scores at North Carolina. Javian Mallory leads the rushers with 495. On defense, Marquise Lightfoot has 7 sacks and Mohamed Toure has 44 tackles.

Declan was ready. "Notre Dame is 7-1. We score 40.4 a game and allow 13.4. The only loss was 24-28 at BYU."

The Irish have been close to perfect otherwise. They beat Wisconsin 41-13, Rice 52-0, Purdue 49-10, Stanford 58-7 and Navy 35-13 last week. CJ Carr has 1,849 yards, 18 touchdowns and 1 interception.

"Both quarterbacks have one interception," Declan said. "Somebody has to make the second one Saturday."

Nolan James Jr. leads the Irish ground game with 519 yards and 8 touchdowns. Jordan Faison is the top receiver at 497. Boubacar Traore has 3.5 sacks and Drayk Bowen has 45 tackles. Jerome Bettis Jr. had 78 receiving yards against Navy, which made Declan very happy.

"Florida State put 35 on you," Declan said. "North Carolina put 35 on you."

"And we still won," Javi said. "Así es."

I asked them about the night. Declan said the Victory March is the best part of any game day, and that walking behind the bagpipes with the crowd along the route makes the hair on his arms stand up. Javi said he would be there too, just to see it, even in the other team's colors. Then he said he was going to need a much warmer jacket than he brought.

Here is how I see it. Miami's offense is the best Notre Dame has seen, and Mensah is going to make his plays. But the Hurricanes defense has bent in the last three weeks, and the Irish at home at night, with James running behind that front and Carr protecting the ball, are a tough out. Notre Dame grinds out a couple of long drives in the second half, Miami answers with big plays, and the Irish hang on in the fourth quarter.

Javi says the breeze always gets through. Declan says South Bend in November has its own weather.

My call: Notre Dame Fighting Irish 33, Miami Hurricanes 29.
