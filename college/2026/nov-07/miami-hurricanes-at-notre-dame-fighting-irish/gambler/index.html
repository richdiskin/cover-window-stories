Miami at Notre Dame: South Bend Gets the Night the Schedule Promised

Miami Hurricanes (8-0) at Notre Dame Fighting Irish (7-1)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Notre Dame Stadium · NBC

XFacebook

Miami at Notre Dame: ND -7; total 54.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

South Bend, this is the night the schedule promised. Unbeaten Miami at Notre Dame, with a rivalry large enough to bring its own memories. Notre Dame offensive lineman Anthonie Knapp says in the AP preview: “We kind of see it as a great opportunity. It's a great challenge to reach our full potential.” Miami quarterback Darian Mensah puts his own standard plainly: “For me, I think every game is my Super Bowl.” There is no shortage of motivation on either sideline. Miami is 8-0, Notre Dame 7-1. AP identifies Cooper Barkate and the running backs led by Mark Fletcher Jr. Watch where Mensah goes on the first third down, and how the Irish tackle after the catch. Mario Cristobal says the excitement will be “through the roof” but keeps bringing the conversation back to preparation. Good advice for a night with this much noise. Bring the history, bring your people, and leave the old demeaning rivalry labels in the past. South Bend has enough real football to fill the evening without them.

Notre Dame is turning the night into a tribute to Lou Holtz, per Notre Dame Athletics. Former Holtz-era captains including Tony Rice, Byron Spruell and Ricky Watters are the flag presenters, the band honors him at halftime and during the 1812 Overture, and fans will find light-up wristbands at their seats, technology the school says has not been used at a college football game before. Holtz's 1988 team beat No. 1 Miami 31-30 on its way to the national title.

The published Cover Writer card stays Miami Hurricanes +5.5 and Over 57.5. My final is Notre Dame Fighting Irish 33, Miami Hurricanes 29. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and its total is pending too.

Both quarterbacks have been nearly spotless, which is why I want the points. Miami scores 47.6 a game, but its last few have been tighter: 43-35 in overtime over Florida State and 38-35 at North Carolina. Notre Dame has handled lesser teams with ease, 52-0 over Rice and 58-7 over Stanford, but when it met real resistance at BYU, it lost by four. Laying more than five at home against an unbeaten team with this kind of passing game is a lot.

The risk for Miami is its defense in the last three weeks. Florida State put up 35, and North Carolina put up 35. Notre Dame's Nolan James Jr. has 519 rushing yards and 8 touchdowns and can grind the clock. Marquise Lightfoot has 7 sacks, and Miami will need him to hurry Carr.

Last season's result is part of why both fan bases have kept this date in view. It does not provide either sideline a shortcut through the next possession. Mensah still has to find the right throw, and Notre Dame still has to finish the tackle. For a game that brings this much history, those are wonderfully ordinary demands.

Notre Dame Fighting Irish 33, Miami Hurricanes 29. Miami Hurricanes cover the 5.5. Over 57.5.
