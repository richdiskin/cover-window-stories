Georgia Tech Takes 14.5 From a Louisville Team That Hung 52

Louisville Cardinals (5-3) at Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets (2-6)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Bobby Dodd Stadium · ACC Network

ShareXFacebook

Louisville vs Georgia Tech prediction, pick against the spread and over/under for Dodd After Dark: Georgia Tech is selling this one as a Saturday night show with halftime fireworks and Military Appreciation Day at Bobby Dodd Stadium, and at 7:30 PM ET the Yellow Jackets take 14.5 points from a Louisville team that just hung 52 on Stanford. My side is Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets +14.5, my call on points is Over 60.5, and my score is Louisville Cardinals 38, Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets 25.

Bianca Thornberry throws the biggest Derby party on her side of Louisville and handles a football preview like a seating chart. "Isaac Brown ran 9 times for 139 yards and 3 touchdowns last week. Lincoln Kienholz has 2,094 yards and 3 interceptions. We score 40.4 a game. Everybody gets a seat, and we fill the table."

Wendell Quist has an aerospace degree from Tech and tracks punt trajectories for fun. "Fine trajectory, wrong landing spot. Your three losses were 38-41, 27-30 and 28-31. You don't put people away, you finish close. And we hung 66 on you in 2018 and 46 in 2020."

Wendell's point is the whole case for the points. Louisville has scored with everybody, but it has also allowed 27.0 a game, and three of its eight games came down to a field goal. Its wins over SMU and Syracuse came 41-31 and 34-26. Two touchdowns and a hook is a lot to ask of a team that gives up yards like that.

Georgia Tech has given me reasons to worry. The Jackets are 2-6, lost 27-49 to Boston College and 11-34 at Pitt, and Alberto Mendoza threw for just 81 yards last week. But he has 1,795 yards on the year, Justice Haynes has 548 rushing yards and Jordan Allen has 543 receiving yards. Tech lost 13-14 to Colorado and 27-34 at Stanford, and it beat Duke 28-25. This team has played close plenty.

On points, the pieces are plain. Louisville scores 40.4 and allows 27.0, and Georgia Tech allows 30.8. My 38 and 25 make 63, past 60.5.

The room splits on the side. The Formula has Louisville 37-24, with Georgia Tech +14.5 and the Over. Next Man Up has Louisville 38-26, takes Georgia Tech +14.5 and likes the Over. In The Trenches takes Louisville −14.5 and likes it, and it reads sides only with no score this week. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Bianca says every good party runs long. Wendell says the fireworks at halftime will be the only thing in the building that goes straight up and comes down on target.

Louisville Cardinals 38, Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets 25. Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets cover the 14.5. Over 60.5.
