Louisville at Georgia Tech: Atlanta Has to Account for Isaac Brown Before Anything Else

Louisville Cardinals (5-3) at Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets (2-6)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Bobby Dodd Stadium · ACC Network

XFacebook

Louisville at Georgia Tech: LOU -14.5; total 62.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

Atlanta, the Louisville back coming to town just scored four touchdowns. Isaac Brown ran for three and caught another in a 52-24 win over Stanford, according to ESPN's AP preview. Georgia Tech's first planning meeting could reasonably begin and end with finding him.

Jeff Brohm comes to Atlanta one win shy of 100 for his career, per Kentucky Today, and Georgia Tech would rather not host the party. Brent Key did not dress up his own running game this week, either, saying plainly that it has not been good or consistent, per Sports Illustrated.

Louisville is 5-3; Tech is 2-6 after losing 34-11 to Pittsburgh. Lincoln Kienholz threw two touchdown passes without an interception against Stanford, and Tre Richardson caught 10 for 140 yards. The Cardinals are not bringing only one way to move the ball.

Tech's Justice Haynes has 548 rushing yards in ESPN's season figures. The home offense needs the kind of possession that lets its defense stop dealing with Brown for a while. Watch the tackling before you watch the scoreboard: allowing a useful gain to become a long run would make this job considerably harder. Atlanta gets its own night game, not an obligation to watch somebody else's.

My published card stays Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets +14.5 and Over 60.5. My final is Louisville Cardinals 38, Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets 25. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

Georgia Tech has given me reasons to worry. The Jackets are 2-6, lost 27-49 to Boston College and 11-34 at Pitt, and Alberto Mendoza threw for just 81 yards last week. But he has 1,795 yards on the year, Justice Haynes has 548 rushing yards and Jordan Allen has 543 receiving yards. Tech lost 13-14 to Colorado and 27-34 at Stanford, and it beat Duke 28-25. This team has played close plenty.

On points, the pieces are plain. Louisville scores 40.4 and allows 27.0, and Georgia Tech allows 30.8.

Brown's receiving touchdown belongs in Tech's preparation just as much as his rushing work. Finding him before the snap is not the same as finishing the tackle afterward. Louisville can make the home defense account for him in several places, which is why Haynes keeping a Tech possession alive matters. Tech cannot let the first Brown touchdown convince it the rest of the game has already been written. Getting Haynes into productive possessions is one way to keep the Cardinals from choosing every question. The home defense still has to tackle Brown, of course. There is no clever sentence that gets Atlanta out of that assignment.

Louisville Cardinals 38, Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets 25. Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets cover the 14.5. Over 60.5.
