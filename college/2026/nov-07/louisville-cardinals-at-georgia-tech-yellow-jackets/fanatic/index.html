Louisville at Georgia Tech: Atlanta Has to Account for Isaac Brown Before Anything Else

Louisville Cardinals (5-3) at Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets (2-6)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Bobby Dodd Stadium · ACC Network

XFacebook

Atlanta, the Louisville back coming to town just scored four touchdowns. Isaac Brown ran for three and caught another in a 52-24 win over Stanford, according to ESPN's AP preview. Georgia Tech's first planning meeting could reasonably begin and end with finding him.

Jeff Brohm comes to Atlanta one win shy of 100 for his career, per Kentucky Today, and Georgia Tech would rather not host the party. Brent Key did not dress up his own running game this week, either, saying plainly that it has not been good or consistent, per Sports Illustrated.

Louisville is 5-3; Tech is 2-6 after losing 34-11 to Pittsburgh. Lincoln Kienholz threw two touchdown passes without an interception against Stanford, and Tre Richardson caught 10 for 140 yards. The Cardinals are not bringing only one way to move the ball.

Tech's Justice Haynes has 548 rushing yards in ESPN's season figures. The home offense needs the kind of possession that lets its defense stop dealing with Brown for a while. Watch the tackling before you watch the scoreboard: allowing a useful gain to become a long run would make this job considerably harder. Atlanta gets its own night game, not an obligation to watch somebody else's.

My call: Louisville Cardinals 38, Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets 25.
