Southern Miss Takes 13.5 From James Madison on a Thursday Night

James Madison Dukes (7-2) at Southern Miss Golden Eagles (3-5)

8:00 PM ET Thu Nov 5 · M. M. Roberts Stadium · ESPN2

James Madison vs Southern Miss prediction, spread and over/under for Thursday: I have Southern Miss Golden Eagles +13.5 and Over 58.5, with James Madison Dukes 36, Southern Miss Golden Eagles 23 as my final. Kickoff is 8:00 PM ET at M. M. Roberts Stadium, and Hattiesburg spends the day with a photography exhibit on the Southern Miss campus before kickoff.

Lila Shenandoah still talks like the cheer captain she was. "James Madison is 7-2, first in the Sun Belt East, and we score 39.1 points a game. Camden Coleman has 16 touchdown throws. Two overtime losses don't change who we are."

Dee Broussard keeps her clipboard close. "Two overtime losses in a row, Lila. App State 34-31 and Troy 24-21. Meanwhile we won our last two, 35-31 over Louisiana and 35-21 at UL Monroe, and Steven Robinson ran for 151 yards in Monroe. We're rolling into a home night."

Here is why I take the points. James Madison has not won since October 17. The Dukes allow just 18.9 a game, which is why they win, but the offense has gone cold late, with Coleman throwing two interceptions against Troy. Southern Miss runs the ball with Robinson, who has 728 yards and 9 touchdowns, and that shortens the night enough to keep the margin inside two scores. My 36-23 is a 13-point Dukes win, under the 13.5.

The risk is real. James Madison beat Southern Miss 32-15 in 2024, and the Golden Eagles lost 8-43 at Auburn and 20-48 to UConn this fall. A JMU defense led by Nakian Jackson, with 66 tackles, can turn this into a runaway if Landry Lyddy turns it over.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 56.5 and it is up to 58.5. Southern Miss gives up 31.0 a game, and George Pettaway has 824 rushing yards for the Dukes. My 36 and 23 make 59, clear of 58.5 by a half.
