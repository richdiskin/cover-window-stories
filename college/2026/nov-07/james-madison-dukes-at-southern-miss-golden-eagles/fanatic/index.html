James Madison Invades Hattiesburg on Thursday Night to Face Southern Miss

James Madison Dukes (7-2) at Southern Miss Golden Eagles (3-5)

8:00 PM ET Thu Nov 5 · M. M. Roberts Stadium · ESPN2

XFacebook

James Madison Dukes vs Southern Miss Golden Eagles prediction for Thursday, November 5: Hattiesburg has a photography show hanging on Pearl Street, and Lila Shenandoah walked through it before dinner. Dee Broussard met her at the door, because if a Duke is going to spend a day on the Southern Miss campus, a Golden Eagle is going to give the tour.

The exhibit is called Two Photographers: Don Norris and Betty Press, at the Gallery of Art and Design in the George Hurst Building. Frozen moments, every one of them. Dee thinks that is exactly how James Madison should be remembered after Thursday night: a nice picture of a team that used to be rolling.

"Look at this one," Dee says, pointing at a print. "That's the Dukes in October. Pretty. In the past."

Lila laughs, the cheer-captain laugh that carries to the top row. "Dee, we're 7-2. We're first in the Sun Belt East. We beat Wagner 87-3. Our defense gives up 18.9 points a game. You want a photograph? Frame the scoreboard."

Dee has an answer ready. "Two straight overtime losses, Lila. App State got you 34-31. Troy got you 24-21 in your own house. You've been staring at overtime coin flips for two weeks."

That one lands, and Lila knows it. Camden Coleman has been the steady hand all fall, with 1,742 yards, 16 touchdown throws and only four interceptions. But he threw two against Troy, and the Dukes needed Seth Cromwell's 135 rushing yards just to get to extra time.

"Camden's fine," Lila says. "And George Pettaway is better than fine. Eight hundred twenty-four yards. Eight touchdowns. He runs like the Blue Ridge, all climb and no quit."

Southern Miss has its own snapshot to show off. The Golden Eagles won their last two, 35-31 over Louisiana and 35-21 at UL Monroe, and Steven Robinson carried 21 times for 151 yards in Monroe. He has 728 yards and nine touchdowns on the year. Mario Sanders leads the receivers with 44 catches for 600 yards, and Landry Lyddy has thrown for 1,326.

"Robinson is the whole show," Dee says. "You can hang him in this gallery. Title it 'Gone.'"

The defense in Hattiesburg is the question. Southern Miss gives up 31.0 points a game, and Auburn and UConn both ran away from the Golden Eagles in September. Mathis Haygood leads the team with 70 tackles and has been everywhere, but the Eagles need Jameer Lewis and his four sacks to get after Coleman early.

"If Jameer gets home twice," Dee says, "Lila does her cheers in a whisper."

"If Jameer gets home twice," Lila says, "Nakian Jackson gets home three times. Sixty-six tackles, Dee. He's going to be in Lyddy's backfield so often your kids will think he's family."

The history is short and it belongs to the Dukes. James Madison beat Southern Miss 32-15 in 2024, the only regular-season meeting in recent years.

"One photograph," Dee says. "One. I don't hang a whole wall on one picture."

"You should," Lila says. "It's a good one."

Friday night, the Mannoni Performing Arts Center opens Natasha, Pierre and the Great Comet of 1812. Dee says Thursday will be its own comet, the Golden Eagles flying across the sky. Lila says comets burn up when they get too close.

Here is how it shakes out. Robinson makes the home crowd loud early, and Southern Miss stays in it longer than anyone in Harrisonburg wants. But Coleman cleans it up, Pettaway wears down the Golden Eagles in the second half, and the James Madison defense gets the stops it needs. The Dukes break their two-game skid without needing another overtime.

Dee says she will be back at the gallery next week to choose a new print. Lila says she already has one: the final scoreboard.

My call: James Madison Dukes 36, Southern Miss Golden Eagles 23.
