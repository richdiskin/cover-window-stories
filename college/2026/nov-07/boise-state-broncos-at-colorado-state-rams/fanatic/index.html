Boise State Broncos at Colorado State Rams

Boise State Broncos (7-1) at Colorado State Rams (3-5)

6:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Canvas Stadium · USA Net

ShareXFacebook

Boise State vs Colorado State prediction for Saturday at Canvas Stadium: the Broncos bring a sea of blue to Fort Collins for a 6:00 PM ET kickoff, and the Rams get one more chance to prove their best night this season was not a fluke.

Boise State's schedule card for this road game says it in two words: Wear Blue. The Boise State alumni association is running a free Bronco Bash on South College Avenue before kickoff, from 3 to 5:30 PM ET, with music, game day swag and a drawing for a football signed by Coach Danielson for those who registered by Nov. 3. Colorado State, for its part, lists tailgating on the game page, and the parking office has event hours running all day out of the lots on West Pitkin Street.

I found two people to argue it out.

Thatcher Bluestem is from the Treasure Valley and has blue in every shade. Blue jacket, blue hat, blue socks. He says the blue turf is a lifestyle. Ansel Rimrock lives in Fort Collins and runs trails before work, which might explain why nothing seems to bother him.

"You know what I like about this trip?" Thatcher said. "We've been coming here forever and we've won almost every time. Eight of nine since 2016."

"The one we won was 31-30 in 2023," Ansel said. "I was there. I ran home."

"You ran home?"

"It was a good night. I had energy."

The series tilts hard to Boise, and last year's game was a 49-21 Broncos win. But this Colorado State team is a strange, fun mess. The Rams are 3-5 and score 35.5 points a game while allowing 42.4. They opened with a 35-13 win over Wyoming and a 58-24 win over Southern Utah. Then came four straight losses, including 45-59 at UTSA, 26-56 to Oregon State and 27-66 at Texas State. Then they turned around and beat San Diego State 56-49.

"That San Diego State game," Ansel said. "Hauss Hejny went 24 of 31 for 333 and 3 touchdowns. When he plays, we are a different team."

Hejny has 1,138 yards with 14 touchdowns and only 2 interceptions, and when he has taken the most throws the Rams have scored 35, 58, 56 and 14. Mel Brown leads the rushers with 454 yards, RJ Vick leads the receivers with 425, and Oumar Diomande is all over the field with 76 tackles.

"And the 14?" Thatcher asked.

"Utah State. Cold night. We don't talk about it."

Thatcher's turn. Boise State is 7-1 and ranked 20th. The Broncos score 35.9 a game and allow 23.6. The only loss was the opener, 27-34 at Oregon, and since then it has been seven wins in a row.

"Maddux Madsen has thrown 1 interception all year," Thatcher said. "One. In eight games. He has 1,681 yards and 15 touchdowns. And Dylan Riley has 1,049 rushing yards."

Riley has 9 rushing touchdowns, Rasean Jones leads the receivers with 554 yards, and Boen Phelps has 49 tackles. Jones had a monster night against Texas State last week with 9 catches for 117 yards and 3 touchdowns.

"About that Texas State game," Ansel said gently. "46-45."

Thatcher shrugged. "A win is a win."

"Texas State put 66 on us. They put 45 on you. That's not nothing."

He has a point. Boise won that one by one at home. The defense that gave up 23.6 a game over the season gave up 45 in a single afternoon. That is the opening Colorado State needs.

I asked Ansel what Fort Collins does on a game Saturday. He said the town walks to the stadium, the tailgates go all day, and the mountains sit right there to the west watching. Thatcher said he would be the guy in blue in the middle of it, being friendly about it.

So here is how I see it. Boise State is the better team, and Riley is going to run for a lot of yards against a defense that has given up 41 or more five times. Madsen will not give the ball away. The Broncos should be in control by halftime. But Colorado State scores too much at home to get run out of its own building, and Hejny will keep hitting Vick and Brown in the second half. The Rams keep it to a little over two scores.

Ansel says he will run home either way. Thatcher says he will wave from the bus.

My call: Boise State Broncos 44, Colorado State Rams 28.
