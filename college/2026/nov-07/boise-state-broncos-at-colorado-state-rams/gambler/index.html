Colorado State Takes 17.5 as Boise State Brings the Points

Boise State Broncos (7-1) at Colorado State Rams (3-5)

6:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Canvas Stadium · USA Net

ShareXFacebook

Boise State vs Colorado State prediction, pick against the spread and over/under for Saturday at Canvas Stadium: Boise State fans have been told to wear blue for the trip to Fort Collins, and the Broncos are laying more than two touchdowns at 6:00 PM ET to a Colorado State team that scores plenty and stops almost nobody. My side is Colorado State Rams +17.5, my call on points is Over 68.5, and my score is Boise State Broncos 44, Colorado State Rams 28.

Thatcher Bluestem grew up in the Treasure Valley and owns blue in every shade, including the socks. "We're 7-1 and No. 20. Maddux Madsen has 1,681 yards, 15 touchdowns and 1 interception. Dylan Riley has 1,049 rushing yards. That's a balanced offense with a workhorse back."

Ansel Rimrock runs the trails above Horsetooth in the mornings and does not rattle. "You gave up 45 to Texas State at home last week. We scored 56 on San Diego State. Hauss Hejny has 14 touchdown throws and 2 interceptions. Seventeen and a half is a long climb, but we'll be climbing all night."

The side comes down to how Boise wins. The Broncos have beaten Western Michigan 32-7 and Washington State 31-10, but they also needed everything to get by Texas State 46-45 and beat Fresno State 38-31. They give up 23.6 a game. Colorado State gives up 42.4, which is the obvious problem, but it also scores 35.5, and with Hejny taking the most throws the Rams put up 35, 58, 56 and 14.

The danger is that the 14 at Utah State was the real team. If the Rams cannot keep up, this turns into the 49-21 Boise win from last year. But Rasean Jones caught 3 touchdowns against Texas State, and that was a home game Boise only won by one.

On points, this is the easy part. Boise scores 35.9 a game, Colorado State scores 35.5 and allows 42.4 and gave up 59 at UTSA and 66 at Texas State. My 44 and 28 make 72, over 68.5.

The room splits on the side. The Formula has Boise State 44-25, with the Broncos −17.5 and the Over. Next Man Up has Boise State 46-30, sides with Colorado State +17.5 and likes the Over. In The Trenches says the trench reads split and has no side, and it reads sides only with no score this week. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Thatcher says blue looks good on a scoreboard. Ansel says he has run uphill longer than this and finished.

Boise State Broncos 44, Colorado State Rams 28. Colorado State Rams cover the 17.5. Over 68.5.
