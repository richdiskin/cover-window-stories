Mean Green Homecoming in Denton: Rice Comes Up With Footnotes

Rice Owls (1-7) at North Texas Mean Green (6-2)

4:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · DATCU Stadium · ESPNU

ShareXFacebook

Rice Owls vs North Texas Mean Green prediction for Saturday, November 7: Odalys Chen-Moreau, a Rice PhD student who carries a notebook full of footnotes, has driven up to Denton for Homecoming, and Grady Mayhew-Ochoa, a Denton jazz drummer, is tapping out a beat on the hood of her car before she has even parked.

"Is that a samba?" Odalys asks.

"It's the Mean Green fight tempo," Grady says. "Very fast. You'll see."

Here's the deal. This is the 80th annual Homecoming at North Texas. The Mean Green have held a Homecoming every season since 1946. This is just the third time Rice has been the Homecoming opponent. North Texas is 53-25-1 all-time on Homecoming and has won 7 of its last 9. Kickoff is 4:00 p.m. ET at DATCU Stadium.

"Eighty years," Odalys says. "Now that's a sample size I respect."

"You hear that?" Grady says. "She respects something."

Odalys reminds him that Rice has its own Homecoming crowd back in Houston, and that the Marching Owl Band would turn this whole parade into a skit. Grady says he would pay good money to see that, and he means it, and he would bring his own snare drum to join in.

Now the football.

Grady goes first, drumming on a cooler lid. Tayven Jackson has thrown for 2,577 yards with 22 touchdowns and only 5 interceptions. Last week he threw for 404 yards in a 48-41 win over Florida Atlantic. Jahiem White has run for 1,054 yards and 13 touchdowns, and he went for 193 against the Owls from Boca. Justin Stevenson has 50 catches for 781 yards and 8 touchdowns. North Texas scores 43.8 a game.

"That's bebop," Grady says. "Four hundred yards. A drum solo every drive."

"Correlation is not causation," Odalys says. "But I admit that's a lot of yards."

Odalys flips open her notebook. Jacurri Brown leads Rice with 669 rushing yards and has thrown for 478. But the headline is Gael Ochoa. At Florida Atlantic, Ochoa ran 12 times for 259 yards and 4 touchdowns. Rice lost that one 49-51, which Odalys describes as "statistically devastating." Jordan Clark leads the receivers with 257 yards. AJ Brown has 59 tackles, and Chibby Nwajuaku has 2 sacks.

"Twelve carries, 259 yards," Odalys says. "N is too small. But it's a very exciting N."

"That's a great solo," Grady says. "But can he play a full set?"

Here's the honest part. Rice is 1-7. North Texas is 6-2. The Mean Green give up 31.9 a game, so Rice can score. But the Owls give up 31.8, and North Texas has been scoring in bunches. Last year North Texas won 56-24, and the Mean Green are 7-1 against Rice since 2016.

"Seven and one," Grady says. "That's a pretty steady groove."

"Eight games," Odalys says. "Still small. I'm writing it down anyway."

Zakye Barker has 62 tackles for North Texas, and Davion Rhodes has 4 sacks. Grady says Barker plays like a bass player, never flashy, always there.

Here's how it plays out in Denton. The Homecoming crowd is loud early, and Jackson hits Stevenson for a long score in the first quarter. White adds two short touchdowns. Rice answers with Ochoa and Brown running hard, and the Owls hang around for a while. But North Texas just keeps pushing the tempo, and by the fourth quarter the Mean Green are playing in double time. Barker makes a stop on fourth down to end Rice's last real drive.

Odalys closes her notebook and admits the sample is getting bigger. Grady hands her a pair of drumsticks and tells her to count it off.

My call: North Texas Mean Green 42, Rice Owls 24.
