Rice at North Texas: Denton Gets a Track Meet Invitation From Rice

Rice Owls (1-7) at North Texas Mean Green (6-2)

4:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · DATCU Stadium · ESPNU

XFacebook

Rice at North Texas: UNT -15.5; total 64.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

Denton, these teams have been doing a poor job of protecting the scoreboard operator's afternoon. North Texas beat Florida Atlantic 48-41; Rice lost to Florida Atlantic 51-49 in its latest game. ESPN's AP preview has the recent evidence, and none of those numbers is a prediction for today.

Rice comes off its bye week with an offense that runs the ball like it means it, fourth nationally on the ground, per At The Roost. Safety AJ Brown said the time off reset the team and brought back that itch to get back out there and play.

Tayven Jackson threw for 404 yards and four touchdowns in the North Texas win. Jahiem White ran for 193 and two scores. Rice answered its own last opponent with 259 rushing yards and four touchdowns from Gael Ochoa. That is a real set of performances, not permission to assume every possession will look like a highlight.

Watch the difference in tempo and approach. North Texas produce 348.9 passing yards a game in ESPN's report, Rice 271.4 rushing yards. One wants to stretch the field through the air; the other has demonstrated how punishing the ground route can be. Denton gets two very different methods of making a defense wish the next break would arrive sooner.

My published card stays North Texas Mean Green −16.5 and Over 64.5. My final is North Texas Mean Green 42, Rice Owls 24. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

Why North Texas: The Mean Green score 43.8 a game. Tayven Jackson has 2,577 passing yards with 22 touchdowns and only 5 interceptions, and he threw for 404 in a 48-41 win over Florida Atlantic. Jahiem White has 1,054 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns. Justin Stevenson has 50 catches for 781 yards and 8 scores. Rice scores 21.0 and gives up 31.8. North Texas won 56-24 last year and is 7-1 against Rice since 2016. That is a lot of separation.

We're on the Over 64.5. North Texas does its share by itself. Rice just lost 49-51 at Florida Atlantic, and Gael Ochoa ran 12 times for 259 yards and 4 touchdowns in that game. Jacurri Brown has 669 rushing yards. Rice can score some too, and North Texas gives up 31.9 a game.

Jackson and Ochoa give the two offenses very different starting points. The defenses would probably appreciate either one taking an occasional break. The defense that finishes the ordinary tackle gets a chance to make the game less frantic. The defense that misses it may spend the next possession explaining why everyone is running again.

North Texas Mean Green 42, Rice Owls 24. North Texas Mean Green cover the 16.5. Over 64.5.
