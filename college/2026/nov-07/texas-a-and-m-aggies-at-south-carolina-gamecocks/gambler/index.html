Texas A&M at South Carolina: Columbia, South Carolina: Let LaNorris Sellers Make the Introduction

Texas A&M Aggies (4-4) at South Carolina Gamecocks (5-3)

3:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Williams-Brice Stadium · ESPN

ShareXFacebook

Texas A&M at South Carolina: SC -6.5; total 52.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

This Columbia belongs to South Carolina, and LaNorris Sellers has supplied enough introductions for one week. He threw three touchdown passes and ran for two more in a 42-25 win over Oklahoma. Texas A&M gets the next appointment.

Mike Elko still sells the old-fashioned stuff. “Playing for the name on the front of your jersey still matters,” he said this week, per Aggies Wire. He also handed LaNorris Sellers his flowers, saying the South Carolina quarterback is “playing right now at an elite level,” per The State. Sellers threw for 310 yards and three touchdowns and ran for two more in the 42-25 win over Oklahoma. Williams-Brice gets to welcome a team that knows exactly how hard he is to bring down.

ESPN's AP preview has the Gamecocks at 5-3 and the Aggies at 4-4. Sellers also threw two interceptions last week. The whole performance matters, not just the part that makes the highlight package. A&M comes off a 31-20 loss to Alabama in which Marcel Reed threw two interceptions of his own.

Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and its total is pending too.

Mazeo Bennett Jr. had 107 receiving yards against Oklahoma, per that report. Watch whether A&M can make Sellers stand still long enough to keep him from choosing the answer himself. Then watch whether Reed gives the Aggies a drive that lets their defense sit down. For the home fans, the appeal is simple: a quarterback who can give you something to shout about in more than one way.

The published Cover Writer card stays Texas A&M Aggies +7.5 and Over 51.5. My final is South Carolina Gamecocks 28, Texas A&M Aggies 26. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

Here is why I take the points. Texas A&M's defense is the best piece of this matchup. It held Arkansas to 7, Missouri State and The Citadel to 0, and Arizona State to 20. South Carolina gives up 25.2 a game, and it lost 18-49 at Alabama. The Aggies have also had a week off since losing 20-31 at Alabama on Oct 24.

The risk is Marcel Reed. He has 8 touchdown throws and 8 interceptions. The Aggies scored 6 points at LSU and 6 at Missouri. If Reed turns it over twice again, Sellers can turn this into a rout.

South Carolina scores 36.9 a game. Sellers has 16 touchdown throws and 6 rushing touchdowns. Last year's meeting ended 31-30.

Sellers can rescue a broken play, which is terrific until the whole afternoon starts depending on rescues. I want to see South Carolina make the ordinary completion before the extraordinary escape. Reed has a similar responsibility on the other sideline: give the Aggies a possession their defense can actually enjoy watching. The quarterbacks supplied enough turnovers last week to make both coaching staffs nervous.

South Carolina Gamecocks 28, Texas A&M Aggies 26. Texas A&M Aggies cover the 7.5. Over 51.5.
