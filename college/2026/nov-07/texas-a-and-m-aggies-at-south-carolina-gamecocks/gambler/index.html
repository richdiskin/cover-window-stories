Texas A&M vs South Carolina prediction, spread and over/under for Saturday: I have Texas A&M Aggies +7.5 and Over 51.5, with South Carolina Gamecocks 28, Texas A&M Aggies 26 as my final. Kickoff is 3:00 PM ET in Columbia, and the morning belongs to Palmetto Luna Arts' Day of the Dead Celebration on Boyd Plaza at the Columbia Museum of Art.

Colt Brazos speaks for the Aggies in a voice that fills a parking lot. "We give up 18.9 points a game. Best defense in this game. And we've won 8 of the last 10 meetings."

Beau Ravenel grins under a garnet cap. "LaNorris Sellers threw for 301 and ran for 96 at Oklahoma, Colt. We won 42-25. We've won three in a row."

Here is why I take the points. Texas A&M's defense is the best piece of this matchup. It held Arkansas to 7, Missouri State and The Citadel to 0, and Arizona State to 20. South Carolina gives up 25.2 a game, and it lost 18-49 at Alabama. The Aggies have also had a week off since losing 20-31 at Alabama on Oct 24. My 28-26 is a 2-point Gamecocks win, well inside the 7.5. DraftKings opened South Carolina −6.5 and it has moved to 7.5, so I am getting an extra point.

The risk is Marcel Reed. He has 8 touchdown throws and 8 interceptions. The Aggies scored 6 points at LSU and 6 at Missouri. If Reed turns it over twice again, Sellers can turn this into a rout.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 51.5 and it has held. South Carolina scores 36.9 a game. Sellers has 16 touchdown throws and 6 rushing touchdowns. Last year's meeting ended 31-30. My 28 and 26 make 54, two and a half over 51.5.

The room agrees. The Formula has South Carolina 28-26 and takes Texas A&M +7.5 with the Over. Next Man Up has South Carolina 29-25, takes the Aggies +7.5, and likes the Over. In The Trenches sees a split up front, so no side, and it reads sides only with no score this week. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

The Formula: "The column copied my homework, word for word."

Next Man Up: "Four points. Close enough to share a tailgate."

Beau wants to walk over to Boyd Plaza for the Day of the Dead Celebration before kickoff, for the music and the altars. Colt says he will go if he can bring his maroon flag. Beau says the flag can wait in the truck.

South Carolina Gamecocks 28, Texas A&M Aggies 26. Texas A&M Aggies cover the 7.5. Over 51.5.
