Peaches Versus Peanuts: Louisiana Tech Heads to Troy for Homecoming

Louisiana Tech Bulldogs (6-2) at Troy Trojans (4-4)

4:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Veterans Memorial Stadium (AL) · ESPN+

ShareXFacebook

Louisiana Tech Bulldogs vs Troy Trojans prediction for Saturday, November 7: Booker "Champ" Tullos, a former Louisiana Tech fullback who grows peaches in Ruston, has driven across three states with a crate of the last fruit of the season, and Marlo Pickens, who long-snapped at Troy, meets him downtown with a sack of boiled peanuts and a seat on the parade route.

"Peaches in November?" Marlo asks.

"Preserves," Champ says. "Same spirit."

It's Homecoming in Troy, and the theme this year is "Flight 1887," a nod to the university's founding in 1887. The parade rolls through downtown Troy at 10 a.m., presented by the Pike County Chamber of Commerce. The alumni tailgate starts at 11:30 at the Alumni House, with Hooks Bar-B-Q doing the cooking. From 11 to 1, there's "Pipers in the Park" at Janice Hawkins Cultural Arts Park. The Homecoming queen is crowned at halftime, and Troy Mayor Jason Reeves is among the Alumni of the Year. Kickoff is 4:00 p.m. ET.

"Bagpipes, barbecue and a parade," Champ says. "Y'all don't do anything halfway."

"Wait until you hear the Sound of the South," Marlo says. "That band will rattle your peaches."

Champ says he'll take the barbecue first and the bagpipes second. Marlo says that's the right order.

Now the football.

Champ goes first, standing up like he's still in the backfield. Louisiana Tech is 6-2. The Bulldogs score 32.6 a game and give up only 20.9. Blake Baker has thrown for 1,327 yards with 13 touchdowns and just 1 interception. Jalen Mickens leads the receivers with 43 catches for 618 yards. And then there's Alonzo Jackson Jr., who has 7 sacks and 57 tackles.

"Jackson is a wrecking ball," Champ says. "I'd have loved to block for a guy like that. Or better, not block against him."

"One interception all year?" Marlo asks.

"One," Champ says. "Baker takes care of the ball like I take care of my peach trees."

Last week the Bulldogs won 35-20 at South Alabama. Champ has the box score folded in his shirt pocket.

Marlo takes over, cracking a peanut. Troy is 4-4, but the Trojans just won 24-21 in overtime at James Madison with Tucker Kilcrease at quarterback. Goose Crowder has 1,162 passing yards on the season. Gavin Griffin leads the backs with 436 yards. TK Keyes has 282 receiving yards. On defense, Donnie Smith has 7 sacks, and Keno Jones leads with 65 tackles. Troy scores 23.0 a game and gives up 24.4.

"Donnie Smith has as many sacks as your guy," Marlo says.

"He does," Champ admits. "That's a fair fight."

"And Troy is 3-1 at home," Marlo adds, and Champ nods along.

These two teams haven't met since 2016, so there is no fresh grudge. Champ says that's good, because he'd rather make friends over barbecue.

Troy's Saturday ends with football, and the football goes like this. The parade crowd fills Veterans Memorial Stadium, the Sound of the South plays the Trojans onto the field, and Troy comes out with energy. Smith gets to Baker once early, and the Homecoming crowd goes wild. But Baker is patient, and he finds Mickens for two big gains before halftime. The queen is crowned at the break with Louisiana Tech in front. Troy fights back in the second half, Griffin running hard and Jones making stops, but Jackson Jr. comes up with a sack on Troy's final drive.

Marlo hands Champ the last of the boiled peanuts. Champ hands Marlo a jar of peach preserves. Both of them agree it was a good day in Troy.

My call: Louisiana Tech Bulldogs 27, Troy Trojans 22.
