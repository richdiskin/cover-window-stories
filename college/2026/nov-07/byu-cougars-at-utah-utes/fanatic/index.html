BYU Climbs the Hill to Rice-Eccles, and Utah Is Waiting

BYU Cougars (7-1) at Utah Utes (8-0)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Rice-Eccles Stadium · ABC

ShareXFacebook

BYU vs Utah prediction for Saturday at Rice-Eccles, from two people who cannot share a car ride without it turning into a debate. Bryce Halvorsen-Tuilagi guides hikers up the trails above Provo and points out the Y on the mountain to anybody who will listen. Aspen Mortensen raced downhill for years and still takes every corner like there is a clock on it.

Aspen: Friday night, downtown, the Eccles Theater on Main Street has Alex Boyé and The King of New York. Faith, history, pioneer stories, all set to hip-hop. You should come up early, Bryce. It will be the last time all weekend somebody in Salt Lake tells a story where BYU comes out on top.

Bryce: The story where BYU comes out on top is the last two years. Twenty-two to twenty-one in 2024. Twenty-four to twenty-one in 2025. I do not need a musical for that. I have the scores memorized and set to my own soundtrack.

Aspen: A one-point game and a three-point game. You are framing a pair of photo finishes like they were blowouts. We are 8-0. We are No. 3 in the country. We score 38.9 points a game. You could drive down from Provo, get lost on the way, and still miss fewer points than your defense will.

Bryce: You scored 21 both of the last two years. Same number twice, both losses. Your team has a ceiling and it is painted crimson.

Aspen: Devon Dampier has 1,876 yards and 18 touchdowns. Last week at Cincinnati he went 22 of 29 for 260 and 3 scores. Hunter Andrews caught 5 balls for 112 yards and 2 touchdowns, and we won 49-25. That is not a ceiling. That is a ski lift.

Bryce: Bear Bachmeier has 1,750 yards, 12 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. He has thrown three fewer interceptions than your guy. When I take people hiking, the first rule is do not take risks you do not need to take. Bear follows the trail. Dampier jumps off ledges and hopes there is snow.

Aspen: There is always snow up here, Bryce. That is the point. And Wayshawn Parker has 726 rushing yards and 9 touchdowns. He runs like he is late for the Disney On Ice show at the Delta Center this weekend. Find Your Hero, it is called. Your secondary is going to be looking for one all afternoon.

Bryce: Our secondary will be fine. We beat Notre Dame 28-24. We beat Arizona State 27-20 last week. Our only loss came in overtime at UCF, 39-31, and I still say that one was a road trip that went a little long.

Aspen: A little long. That is how you describe losing to UCF. We went to Colorado and won 27-24. We beat Arkansas 43-10. We held Utah State to zero. Zero. Your mountain has a letter on it, ours has a scoreboard.

Bryce: Legend Glasker has 585 receiving yards. You know what a legend is? Somebody people talk about for generations. Nobody is writing songs about Braden Pegan.

Aspen: Braden Pegan has 505 receiving yards and 6 touchdowns. Glasker has 4. If you want to talk about legends, talk about touchdowns, because that is what people remember on the drive home.

Bryce: The drive home is going to be long for you if this is close in the fourth quarter. The last two years it was. BYU knows how to win these. Utah knows how to explain them.

Aspen: Here is what I know. We score 38.9 and allow 15.9. You score 32.4 and allow 18.8. Both numbers point the same direction, and it is uphill toward Rice-Eccles. The crowd is going to be loud, the air is going to be cold, and every one of those red sweaters in the stands has been waiting two years for this.

Bryce: You have been waiting since 2024. We have been enjoying it since 2024. That is a different kind of waiting.

Aspen: Enjoy it on the hike back down. Dampier hits a big one early, Parker grinds the clock in the second half, and Bachmeier spends the fourth quarter throwing into a crowd of red jerseys.

Bryce: Fine. I will come up Friday, watch the show, and stay for the game. If Utah wins, I will buy dinner. If BYU wins, you have to hike the Y with me in November.

Aspen: Deal. Bring a jacket. You will want something to hide the blue.

My call: Utah Utes 30, BYU Cougars 22.
