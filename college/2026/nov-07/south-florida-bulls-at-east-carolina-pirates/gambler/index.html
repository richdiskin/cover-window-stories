South Florida Plus 1.5 Steals One From East Carolina by a Point

South Florida Bulls (6-2) at East Carolina Pirates (4-4)

7:30 PM ET Fri Nov 6 · Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium · ESPN2

ShareXFacebook

South Florida vs East Carolina prediction, spread and over/under for Friday night: I have South Florida Bulls +1.5 and Over 40.5, with South Florida Bulls 23, East Carolina Pirates 22 as my final. Kickoff is 7:30 PM ET at Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium, on the same night downtown Greenville throws its First Friday ArtWalk and runs a free trolley through the galleries.

Bart Quidley has tailgated the same patch of Pitt County grass since he was in diapers. "We beat Temple 17-9. TJ Engleman Jr. ran for 107. And we have won three of the last three against South Florida, 44-24, 29-14 and 48-28. We eat the Bulls."

Reina Valdés-Kowalski came up from Tampa and doesn't flinch. "We are 6-2 and we give up 14.5 points a game. We beat UAB 30-10 and we held Kent State to 2. Your quarterback has 5 interceptions in 126 throws, Bart. Our defense eats quarterbacks."

Here is why I take the points. This game flipped. DraftKings opened South Florida −1.5, and now East Carolina is −1.5. Nothing in the numbers says the Bulls got worse. South Florida allows 14.5 a game and East Carolina allows 24.1. The Pirates' last win came against Temple, a team that beat South Florida, sure, but Mitch Griffis threw for 130 yards in it. A one-point Bulls win is my final, and with the hook I get the half-point cushion either way.

The risk is the quarterback room in Tampa. Michael Van Buren Jr. leads the Bulls with 1,273 passing yards, but Luke Kromenhoek led the Bulls in throws against UAB, going 7 of 10 for 39 yards and running 15 times for 92. If South Florida cannot throw, East Carolina can sell out to stop D.J. Crowther, who has 578 yards and 7 scores.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 45.5 and it has dropped to 40.5. That is a big drop for a game where both teams have scored at least 24.0 a game this year. My 23 and 22 make 45, Over 40.5.

The room lines up with me. The Formula has East Carolina 23-22 but takes South Florida +1.5 and likes the Over. Next Man Up has South Florida 23-21, likes the Bulls +1.5 and likes the Over. In The Trenches sees run-game reads that favor different teams, so no side, and it reads sides only with no score this week. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "Same 23 for the Bulls, partner. You just handed the Pirates the extra point."

The Formula: "The extra point still loses by the hook. Read the whole score."

Reina says she will ride the trolley downtown after the game to celebrate. Bart says she will ride it to the bus station.

South Florida Bulls 23, East Carolina Pirates 22. South Florida Bulls cover the 1.5. Over 40.5.
