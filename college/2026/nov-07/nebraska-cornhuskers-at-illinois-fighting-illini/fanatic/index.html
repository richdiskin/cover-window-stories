Dads Weekend in Champaign: Illinois Hosts Nebraska Friday Night

Nebraska Cornhuskers (5-3) at Illinois Fighting Illini (5-3)

8:00 PM ET Fri Nov 6 · Gies Memorial Stadium · Fox

ShareXFacebook

Nebraska Cornhuskers vs Illinois Fighting Illini prediction for Friday, November 6: it is Dads Weekend in Champaign, and the university has turned Friday night into the official Illinois Dads Day Football Game. The Dads Weekend tailgate at Grange Grove sold out. There is a Casino Night with Dad at the Illini Union, and the flyer swears no dad is required. Saturday morning brings a 5K at the Arboretum for anyone who can still walk.

Margery Vandermolen grew up in Champaign-Urbana, the daughter of an ag-economics professor. She brought her dad and a cooler of corn jokes. Darla Rasmussen flew in from Nebraska with her own dad and the same farm-girl handshake she used back when she played volleyball in Lincoln.

"Welcome to Illinois," Margery says. "Our corn is taller and our dads are louder."

"Your corn is fine," Darla says. "Your defense is the problem. Twenty-four points a game, Margery. Ohio State hung 42 on you."

Margery has the receipts ready. Illinois beat Oregon 21-10 in October. The Illini won at Maryland 20-17 last week, and Collin Dixon caught six balls for 103 yards in that game. He has 60 catches, 781 yards and 10 touchdowns on the year.

"Collin Dixon is the best thing to come out of a cornfield since popcorn," Margery says. "And our dads have seen your team lose three in a row."

That is the sore spot, and Darla doesn't hide from it. Nebraska lost 17-20 to Indiana, 14-17 at Oregon and 10-17 to Washington. Three games, three losses by a field goal or a touchdown, and in the last one Anthony Colandrea threw three interceptions on 44 throws.

"Three close ones," Darla says. "We'll take that over getting run over. Our defense gives up 15.6 points a game. Owen Chambliss has 53 tackles. Jahsear Whittington has four and a half sacks. We just need Anthony to stop throwing to your guys."

Colandrea has thrown for 1,825 yards and 15 touchdowns, but seven interceptions have come along with them. Kwazi Gilmer has been his go-to target, with 42 catches, 545 yards and seven touchdowns.

"Kwazi catches everything," Darla says. "Even the ones Anthony throws to the wrong team."

Illinois has its own quarterback steadying the ship. Katin Houser has 1,663 yards and 13 touchdown throws, and Aidan Laughery has 560 rushing yards. Matthew Bailey leads the Illini defense with 55 tackles.

The series is a seesaw. Since 2016, Nebraska has won five meetings and Illinois has won four. The Illini won the last one 31-24 in 2024, and four of the last five.

"Four of five," Margery says. "Ask your dad."

"You sold out the tailgate," Darla says. "Doesn't mean you sold out the win."

The dads end up in the same spot, near the grill, comparing grandkids and arguing about play-calling. Margery's dad thinks Illinois wins if the Illini can protect Houser. Darla's dad thinks Nebraska wins if Colandrea can protect the football.

Darla brings up the common opponents, because a farm girl counts everything twice. Nebraska beat Maryland 48-23 and won at Michigan State 31-13. Illinois needed overtime to get past Michigan State 38-37 and squeezed out its 20-17 win at Maryland.

"Same teams, Margery," she says. "We beat them by a mile. You beat them by a whisker."

"A whisker counts," Margery says. "Ask Duke. They beat us 31-27 in September and nobody in Durham remembers the score. They just remember the win."

Darla laughs and hands her dad a bratwurst. "Then remember this one."

Here is how it shakes out. Dixon gets loose for a big play, the Illini crowd gets loud, and Illinois keeps it close well into the fourth quarter. But the Nebraska defense makes the stops that matter, Colandrea throws more to his own guys than to Margery's, and the Huskers snap their three-game slide in a tight one.

Darla promises to run the 5K on Saturday to celebrate. Margery says she will cheer from the car with her dad, and the heater on.

My call: Nebraska Cornhuskers 27, Illinois Fighting Illini 23.
