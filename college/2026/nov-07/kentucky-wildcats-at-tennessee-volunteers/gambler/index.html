Tennessee Lays 6.5 to No. 8 Kentucky at a Rowdy Neyland

Kentucky Wildcats (7-1) at Tennessee Volunteers (6-2)

6:15 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Neyland Stadium · ESPN

ShareXFacebook

Kentucky vs Tennessee prediction, pick against the spread and over/under for Homecoming at Neyland Stadium: Knoxville has spent the week telling everybody All Roads Lead to Tennessee, and at 6:15 PM ET the road brings in a 7-1 Kentucky team ranked No. 8 against a 6-2 Tennessee team ranked No. 16. My side is Tennessee Volunteers −6.5, my call on points is Over 50.5, and my score is Tennessee Volunteers 30, Kentucky Wildcats 23.

Rhett Abernathy-Coyle grew up on a horse farm a short ride from Keeneland and reads a football game like a past-performance page. "Look at the form. Kentucky won two overtime races in a row, 35-34 at South Carolina and 35-34 over LSU, then went to Norman and won 21-13. Kenny Minchey has 1,755 yards, 14 touchdowns and 1 interception. That horse does not throw a shoe."

Earl 'Rocky Top' Haskew snapped for the Vols and tunes his banjo during commercials. "Form is fine. Class is different. We give up 16.2 a game. We held Alabama to 10. Tyree Weathersby has 7 sacks, and DeSean Bishop has 957 yards and 11 touchdowns. And we beat y'all 56-34 last year."

Rhett's past-performance read is honest, and so is the trouble in it. Kentucky's two overtime wins were by one point each, and the one time the Wildcats ran against a top-class field at home they lost 17-45 to Alabama. Tennessee's two losses came 17-20 to Texas and 24-27 at South Carolina. Nobody has handled the Vols by more than a field goal.

The Tennessee case is the defense and the ground game. Bishop ran 32 times for 120 at South Carolina, and the Vols have held Auburn to 14, Arkansas to 17 and Alabama to 10 in the last month and a half. George MacIntyre does not need to win this game with his arm, and he has 728 throwing yards. He needs to hand it off and avoid mistakes.

Kentucky can make that hard. Minchey's 1 interception all year is the reason the Wildcats have survived close finishes. Grant Godfrey has 72 tackles and Mi'Quise Humphrey-Grace has 5 sacks. If the Vols fall behind early, this is a closer race than I think.

On points, the series has gone big lately, with 56-34 last year and 45-42 in 2021. Kentucky scores 32.9 and Tennessee scores 32.2. My 30 and 23 make 53, past 50.5.

The room splits on the side but not on points. The Formula has Tennessee 29-24, sides with Kentucky +6.5 and likes the Over. Next Man Up has Tennessee 31-23, sides with the Vols −6.5 and likes the Over. In The Trenches says the trench reads split and has no side, and it reads sides only with no score this week. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Rhett says his horse has the cleaner form. Earl says Neyland is a longer track than it looks.

Tennessee Volunteers 30, Kentucky Wildcats 23. Tennessee Volunteers cover the 6.5. Over 50.5.
