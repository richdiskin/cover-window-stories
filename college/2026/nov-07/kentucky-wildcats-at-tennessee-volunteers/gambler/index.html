Kentucky at Tennessee: Knoxville Meets the Team That Keeps Escaping by One

Kentucky Wildcats (7-1) at Tennessee Volunteers (6-2)

6:15 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Neyland Stadium · ESPN

XFacebook

Kentucky at Tennessee: TENN -6; total 49.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

Knoxville has Homecoming, new Dark Mode jerseys and Kentucky arriving with consecutive 35-34 overtime wins. If you are looking for a fan base that has recently practiced staring at a screen without blinking, there it is. AP's preview puts the Wildcats at 7-1 and Tennessee at 6-2. Kentucky coach Will Stein offers a less dramatic explanation for the results: “We're learning each week how to not beat ourselves.” Tennessee coach Josh Heupel describes the job as “doing the ordinary things at a really high level.” Apparently the secret is not a secret; the hard part is getting eleven people to do it together. The AP report says Kentucky has won only once at Neyland Stadium since 1984. That does not decide today's result, but it explains why this trip means something. A season leader is not a Saturday starter announcement. Watch the giveaways, the flags and the third-down decisions before hunting for a miracle finish. Kentucky's recent wins have been loud enough; its coach is talking about avoiding the self-inflicted part. Neyland gets to see whether ordinary execution can produce an extraordinary response. Tennessee's official campus guide puts the Vol Walk at 3:55 PM ET and the Pride of the Southland Band march at 4:35 PM ET. A campus tradition, a band and a border rival: that is a fairly good answer to anyone asking what is happening in town today.

Will Stein grew up a Kentucky fan, and he did not hide what that meant. “This is not a coach talking. This is young Will Stein at home cheering on the Cats,” he said this week, per On3, naming Tennessee as one of the three teams he always wanted to beat. Now he brings a 7-1 team into Neyland for the 122nd meeting, with the Vols debuting their Dark Mode jerseys for Homecoming, per the University of Tennessee.

The published Cover Writer card stays Tennessee Volunteers −6.5 and Over 50.5. My final is Tennessee Volunteers 30, Kentucky Wildcats 23. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and its total is pending too.

The Tennessee case is the defense and the ground game. Bishop ran 32 times for 120 at South Carolina, and the Vols have held Auburn to 14, Arkansas to 17 and Alabama to 10 in the last month and a half. George MacIntyre does not need to win this game with his arm, and he has 728 throwing yards. He needs to hand it off and avoid mistakes.

Kentucky can make that hard. Minchey's 1 interception all year is the reason the Wildcats have survived close finishes. Grant Godfrey has 72 tackles and Mi'Quise Humphrey-Grace has 5 sacks. If the Vols fall behind early, this is a closer race than I think.

Tennessee Volunteers 30, Kentucky Wildcats 23. Tennessee Volunteers cover the 6.5. Over 50.5.
