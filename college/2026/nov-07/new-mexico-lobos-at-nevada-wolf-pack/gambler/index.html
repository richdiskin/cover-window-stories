New Mexico vs Nevada prediction, spread and over/under for Friday night: I have Nevada Wolf Pack +10.5 and Over 38.5, with New Mexico Lobos 28, Nevada Wolf Pack 18 as my final. Kickoff is 9:00 PM ET at Mackay Stadium, and across town the Reno Public Market has a free funk band playing for anyone who wants to warm up before the walk to the stadium.

Paloma Begaye-Ortiz flies balloons over Albuquerque and talks like the burner is always lit. "New Mexico is 7-1 and allows 11.9 points a game. Scottre Humphrey has 11 rushing touchdowns. Darren Agu has 6 sacks. The Lobos won at San Jose State 21-16 last week."

Here is why I take the points with Nevada. New Mexico has scored 21 points in each of its last three wins, and Toa Fa'avae has 7 interceptions against 6 touchdown throws. Nevada beat San Jose State 28-13 two weeks ago, the same team the Lobos only got past 21-16. Nevada has won three of the last five meetings. My 28-18 is a 10-point Lobos win, inside the 10.5.

If Scottre Humphrey and Kiefer Sibley, who ran for 102 yards at San Jose State, take over on the ground, the Lobos clear the number.
