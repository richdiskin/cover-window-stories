Duke Blue Bloods Meet NC State's Parking Lot in Raleigh

Duke Blue Devils (6-2) at NC State Wolfpack (4-4)

12:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Carter-Finley Stadium · CW

ShareXFacebook

Duke vs NC State prediction for Saturday in Raleigh, from two men who park next to each other at Carter-Finley and have not agreed on anything in the history of that parking lot. Harrison Wexley-Moore IV went to Duke, as did his father, as did his father, and he will remind you of that before you can say hello. Ruff Pegram went to NC State, runs the grill in the next spot over and has been feeding Harrison for years out of what he calls Christian charity.

Ruff: Saturday is Military Appreciation at Carter-Finley. Kickoff at 12:30. Downtown, the Food Truck Rodeo is on Fayetteville Street, free, with every truck in the Triangle. But nobody in their right mind leaves my grill for a food truck. Harrison, you have eaten my brisket for six years.

Harrison: I have tolerated your brisket for six years. As a courtesy. A gentleman never refuses food from a neighbor, even one wearing that much red.

Ruff: And a gentleman says thank you. Here is your thank you. NC State has a running back named Duke. Jayden Scott. Wears number 1. Everybody calls him Duke. So on Saturday, there will be exactly one Duke on that field worth watching, and he is ours.

Harrison: That is the single most NC State thing I have ever heard. You could not beat us, so you named a running back after us.

Ruff: We did not name him. We just took the name. Like we take your lunch most years.

Harrison: Most years? We won 45-33 last year. We won 29-19 the year before that. We won 24-3 in 2023. Three. You scored three points, Ruff. I have seen more points on the scoreboard at a tennis match.

Ruff: You know what tennis has? Love. Which is what you will have for our offense when CJ Bailey throws for a few hundred yards. He has 1,955 yards and 13 touchdowns. Keenan Jackson has 456 receiving yards and 6 touchdowns. We score 32.1 a game. Duke scores 32.0. We score more than you do, Harrison. Write that down in your family Bible.

Harrison: By one-tenth of a point. And we allow 14.2. You allow 25.9. Our defense is a private club. Yours is a public park.

Ruff: Fair. But you went to Georgia Tech and lost 28-25. You lost to North Carolina 24-20. You beat Virginia 19-16 and needed every bit of it. On the road, your private club turns into a tent.

Harrison: Nate Sheppard has 972 rushing yards and 14 touchdowns. He scored 5 of them last week against Boston College. Five. We won 47-2. Boston College scored a safety. That is the only way anybody gets two points against us.

Ruff: Boston College is not NC State. We beat Louisville 31-28. We beat California last week 31-10. Davion Gause ran 19 times for 104 yards. He runs like a man late for the food trucks.

Harrison: Walker Eget has 1,641 yards and only 3 interceptions. He does not make mistakes. He has the composure of a man who has been to a lot of formal dinners.

Ruff: And Popo Aguirre has 76 tackles. He has the composure of a man who has never been invited to one and does not care. He is going to put Eget in the grass.

Harrison: Kevin O'Connor has 4.5 sacks for us. Your quarterback is going to see a lot of blue.

Ruff: It is a Saturday in November in Raleigh. Everybody sees a lot of blue. It is called the sky. We also see red, and that is what it is going to look like when the crowd stands up in the fourth quarter.

Harrison: I will grant you the crowd. Carter-Finley is loud. It is also a stadium where Duke has won three of the last four.

Ruff: And we won the one before that, 31-20. I remember it. I remember everything. That is what grilling teaches you. Patience and memory.

Harrison: Here is my prediction. Sheppard scores twice, Eget does not turn it over, and Duke wins a close one.

Ruff: Close is all I need. Bailey throws, Duke Scott runs, the real Duke, and the Wolfpack are right there with the ball at the end.

Harrison: And afterward?

Ruff: Afterward, you eat my brisket and say thank you, win or lose. It is Military Appreciation day. We can all be decent for one afternoon.

Harrison: For one afternoon. Then I go back to being right.

My call: Duke Blue Devils 28, NC State Wolfpack 25.
