Sacramento State Takes 25.5 From Toledo in a First-Ever Meeting

Toledo Rockets (6-2) at Sacramento State Hornets (2-7)

Toledo vs Sacramento State prediction, pick against the spread and over/under for Wednesday's first-ever meeting: I'm on Sacramento State Hornets +25.5 and Over 52.5, with a final of Toledo Rockets 38, Sacramento State Hornets 18. The Hornets close Hornet Stadium for the year with Senior Day and Native American Heritage Day, debuting uniforms made with the Wilton Rancheria tribe, and fireworks are scheduled for after the final whistle.

Rocco Szczepanski grades every offense in horsepower, and he flew west with a big number. "John Alan Richter has 2,244 yards, 17 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. CJ Miller ran 33 times for 183 yards against Western Michigan. We score 37.4 a game. That's a V8 with the windows down, Mateo."

Mateo Villaseñor, the farm-to-fork chef who used to play linebacker here, answers like he's plating a dish. "Our kitchen just served Kent State a 31-10 loss. Isaiah Ene ran for 107 and two scores. The defense took the ball away again and again. Senior Day is not a night to send out a cold plate."

Twenty-five and a half is a mountain, and Toledo has climbed it once, the 63-10 romp over Central Connecticut at home. But the Rockets on the road have looked different. They won 39-24 at Ball State, 34-14 at Eastern Michigan, and lost 30-20 at Michigan State. Not one road win cleared 25.5. Toledo also lost 8-23 at home to Buffalo, so this engine has misfired before. My final has Toledo by 20, short of the number.

The over/under has its own case. Toledo is scoring 37.4 and allowing 25.4, and the last game was a 45-38 shootout. Sacramento State gives up 30.3 a night. DraftKings opened 51.5 and went to 52.5, and my 38-18 gets to 56 without needing the Hornets to do anything wild. The worry is Jackson Sharman, who has 1,363 yards with 8 touchdowns and 6 interceptions, and Toledo's Andrew Zock, who has 8 sacks. If Sharman gets bogged down, the Hornets stall in the teens.

The Formula and Next Man Up are with me on Sac State +25.5 and the Over, and In The Trenches has no side. The Formula has Toledo 38-17 and likes Sacramento State Hornets +25.5 and the Over. Next Man Up has Toledo 38-19 and likes the same two. In The Trenches reads the trenches as even, has no side and no score this week. Follow The Money has no side pick and takes the Under 52.5. The consensus spread opened Sacramento State +25.5 and is +25.5 now, and the total opened 53.5 and is 52.5 now. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

The Formula: "Thirty-eight to seventeen, Next Man Up. Leave the Hornets a little dessert."

Next Man Up: "I gave them two more points. Call it the Senior Day course."

Rocco revs an imaginary engine in the visitors' section. Mateo wipes his hands on a towel and waits for the fireworks.

Toledo Rockets 38, Sacramento State Hornets 18. Sacramento State Hornets cover the 25.5. Over 52.5.
