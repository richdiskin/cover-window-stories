Senior Night at Hornet Stadium: Toledo Flies In to Face Sacramento State

Toledo Rockets (6-2) at Sacramento State Hornets (2-7)

7:00 PM ET Wed Nov 4 · Hornet Stadium · CBSSN

XFacebook

Toledo Rockets vs Sacramento State Hornets prediction for Wednesday, November 4: it's Senior Day, Native American Heritage Day and the final game of the year at Hornet Stadium, and Mateo Villaseñor has the whole menu planned. Rocco Szczepanski flew in from the Glass City to tell him the Rockets brought their own engine and a full tank of gas.

This is the first time these two programs have ever met, and it's the first time Sacramento State has ever played a Wednesday MAC night at home. The Hornets will recognize 27 seniors before kickoff. They will wear brand-new uniforms created in consultation with the Wilton Rancheria tribe. And when it's over, there are fireworks scheduled over the stadium for 916 Community night.

"You hear that, Rocco?" Mateo says, tying on an apron over his green and gold. "Fireworks. My mother is coming. My uncle is coming. The guy who sells me tomatoes is coming. Nobody is leaving this kitchen hungry."

Rocco cracks his knuckles. "Mateo, I grew up three streets from the Jeep plant. I grade offenses in horsepower. And the Toledo offense right now? That's four hundred horses and a racing stripe. You're cooking on a camp stove."

"A camp stove that just roasted Kent State 31-10," Mateo fires back. "Five turnovers, Rocco. Five. Fata Puloka forced two fumbles. Miles Bailey jumped on two more. We held the ball for nearly 35 minutes. That is slow food. That is a braise."

He is not wrong, and Rocco knows it. That was the first MAC win in Hornets history and their first win over an FBS team since the move up. Isaiah Ene ran for 107 yards and two touchdowns that night, and he leads the team with 410 yards on the season. Matt Coleman is the top receiver with 41 catches for 492 yards and 5 touchdowns. Boogsie Silvera roams the back of the defense with 71 tackles, and he hits like he's tenderizing a steak.

"Silvera is my secret ingredient," Mateo says. "You don't see him, you just taste him."

Rocco just smiles and lists parts. John Alan Richter has thrown for 2,244 yards with 17 touchdowns and only 2 interceptions. He threw for 429 yards in the overtime win over Temple and 391 against Western Michigan. CJ Miller has 609 yards and 7 touchdowns, and he carried it 33 times for 183 yards in that 45-38 win over the Broncos. Adjatay Dabbs is the deep threat, with 592 yards and 7 touchdowns.

"Then there's Andrew Zock," Rocco says. "Eight sacks. That's not a defensive end, that's a tow truck. He shows up and your quarterback goes for a ride."

"Jackson Sharman has been sacked before," Mateo says. "He gets up. He has thrown for 1,363 yards and 8 touchdowns this season. He's a short-order cook. He keeps the tickets moving."

The scoreboards are not kind to the Hornets this fall, and Mateo will tell you so himself. Fresno State beat them 49-3. Ohio won 48-7 here. Massachusetts came in and won 35-6. Ball State took a 34-17 win in Muncie. The record sits at 2-7, and the kitchen has had some long nights.

"Back to the kitchen," Mateo says, the way he does after every loss. "But Kent State was the night the sauce came together. You don't forget that recipe."

Toledo has its own scars. Buffalo beat the Rockets 23-8 in the Glass Bowl. Michigan State won 30-20 in the opener. Rocco calls those engine trouble and moves on, because 6-2 is 6-2, and the Rockets have scored at least 34 in six of their eight games.

"And here's the thing about the schedule," Rocco says, lowering his voice. "We last played October 24. Rested. Tuned. You played Friday night and turn around five days later. I'm not saying that's unfair. I'm saying our engine has been sitting in the garage cooling."

Mateo shrugs. "Seniors don't get tired on Senior Day."

So here is how it shakes out under the California sky. Richter finds Dabbs early, Miller grinds out the middle, and Zock makes Sharman's night a little too exciting. The Rockets build a real cushion by the second half. But the Hornets do not fold on their seniors. Ene keeps churning, Coleman makes a couple of grabs that bring the home crowd to its feet, and Silvera makes one of those hits the seniors will talk about at reunions. Toledo wins comfortably, and the fireworks still go off for Sacramento State.

Rocco does his burnout gesture in the aisle. Mateo gives a chef's kiss to his seniors anyway.

My call: Toledo Rockets 38, Sacramento State Hornets 18.
