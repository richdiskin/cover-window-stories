Kansas State Plus 4 Hangs With Oklahoma State in Manhattan

Oklahoma State Cowboys (5-3) at Kansas State Wildcats (4-4)

1:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Bill Snyder Family Stadium · TNT, HBO Max

ShareXFacebook

Oklahoma State vs Kansas State prediction, spread and over/under for Saturday: I have Kansas State Wildcats +3.5 and Over 62.5, with Oklahoma State Cowboys 32, Kansas State Wildcats 31 as my final. Kickoff is 1:00 PM ET at Bill Snyder Family Stadium, and downtown Manhattan spends the weekend on its Holiday Open House, the biggest shopping days of the year along Poyntz Avenue.

Dusty Lockridge explains football like a rodeo clock. "Eight seconds, Hattie. Drew Mestemaker has 2,649 yards and 25 touchdown throws. We score 41.1 a game. Caleb Hawkins ran for 194 yards at Iowa State."

Hattie Brandvold does not look up from her notes. "And you lost that game 49-51, Dusty. You give up 27.1 a game. We give up 21.9, and we won the last two meetings, 14-6 and 42-20."

Here is why I take the points. Oklahoma State scores, but it has not closed on the road: 32-35 at Houston and 49-51 at Iowa State, both one-score losses. Kansas State's defense is the best in this game by a wide margin, and Jayden Bryant has 4 sacks. The worry for the Wildcats is the quarterback picture. Avery Johnson leads the team with 1,301 yards, but Jacob Knuth threw the most at Arizona State and Blake Barnett threw the most at Colorado, with 3 touchdowns in a 24-27 overtime loss. My 32-31 is a one-point Cowboys win, well inside the 3.5. DraftKings opened Oklahoma State −6.5, and it has come down to 3.5.

The risk is that Oklahoma State is simply better at throwing it. The Cowboys beat Oregon 39-31 and Colorado 57-24, and Justin Bowick has 731 receiving yards and 9 touchdowns. Kansas State has lost three of its last four.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 60.5 and it is up to 62.5. Mestemaker threw 5 touchdowns at Iowa State, and Kansas State scores 31.8 a game, with Rodney Fields Jr. at 487 rushing yards. My 32 and 31 make 63, half a point over 62.5. That is the slimmest call on the board, and I know it.

The room agrees on the side and splits on the over/under. The Formula has Oklahoma State 32-29 and takes Kansas State +3.5 with the Under. Next Man Up has Kansas State 33-31, takes the Wildcats +3.5, and likes the Over. In The Trenches sees a split in the run game, so no side, and it reads sides only with no score this week. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "I have the home team winning it, partner. You're both being polite."

The Formula: "Three points of disagreement. That is a field goal, not a philosophy."

Hattie wants to walk Poyntz Avenue after the game and do her holiday shopping early. Dusty says he will come along if Sunday's card show at Manhattan Town Center has an old Cowboys card worth framing. Hattie says it will be cheaper than the four quarters he is about to sit through.

Oklahoma State Cowboys 32, Kansas State Wildcats 31. Kansas State Wildcats cover the 3.5. Over 62.5.
