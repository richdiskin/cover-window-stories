Family Weekend in Tempe: Sun Devils and Buffaloes Throw Down

Colorado Buffaloes (3-5) at Arizona State Sun Devils (4-4)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Mountain America Stadium · ESPN2

ShareXFacebook

Colorado vs Arizona State prediction for Family Weekend in Tempe: Saturday is a double celebration at Mountain America Stadium, with Arizona State honoring its Salute to Service week and welcoming families for Family Weekend, and the Sun Devils and Buffaloes kick off at 7:30 PM ET.

The day begins with Sparky's Touchdown Tailgate at College Avenue and 6th Street, the free pregame party ASU throws before every home game. It opens 2.5 hours before kickoff, with games and live entertainment for the whole family. This week it doubles as a thank-you to service members, and it should be packed with parents in maroon and gold visiting their kids for the weekend.

I sat down with two people who could not be more different.

Moss Feldstein studies physics in Boulder, plays hacky sack between classes and does his serious thinking in a hammock below the Flatirons. Skylar Vanterpool is from Tempe, is finishing a master's in sports science and carries a stopwatch everywhere.

"I'm coming in hot," Moss said. "We just beat Kansas State in overtime. Momentum is a physical quantity. Look it up."

"I measure things for a living," Skylar said. "Your momentum is one game."

Colorado is 3-5. The Buffaloes score 21.0 points a game and allow 29.4. They won 14-13 at Georgia Tech to open the year and beat Weber State 52-21. Then came five losses in a row: 7-41 at Northwestern, 13-23 at Baylor, 7-29 to Texas Tech, 24-27 to Utah and 24-57 at Oklahoma State. Then the 27-24 overtime win over Kansas State.

"Richard Young ran 24 times for 120 yards and 2 touchdowns against K-State," Moss said. "That is a young man finding his orbit."

Isaac Wilson has handled the throwing since late September, with 1,164 yards, 7 touchdowns and 7 interceptions. Joseph Williams is the go-to target with 732 receiving yards and 5 scores. On defense, Lamont Lester Jr. has 5 sacks, and Gideon Lampron leads the team with 81 tackles.

Skylar clicked the stopwatch. "Arizona State is 4-4. We score 29.5 and allow 29.5. Perfectly balanced, which I appreciate."

The Sun Devils have been up and down. They crushed Morgan State 70-7, lost 20-48 at Texas A&M, beat Kansas 24-17, lost 19-55 to Baylor, beat Hawai'i 30-12, lost 25-53 at Texas Tech, beat Kansas State 28-17 and lost 20-27 at BYU last week.

"Win, lose, win, lose," Moss said. "You're a sine wave."

"And this is a win week," Skylar said.

Cutter Boley leads the team with 1,360 throwing yards and 13 touchdowns, though Mikey Keene and Jake Fette have taken the most throws in the last three games. Reed Harris is the headliner with 804 receiving yards and 8 touchdowns. Cardae Mack leads the ground game with 523 yards, and Owen Long has 72 tackles.

Then there is the history, which is about as even as it gets. Over the eight meetings since 2016, each side has won four. Colorado won 27-24 in 2023. Arizona State won 42-17 last year.

"Forty-two to seventeen," Skylar said. "I timed the drive home. It felt short."

"That was a different team," Moss said. "We have Joseph Williams now."

I asked both of them about the weekend. Skylar said the family is flying in for Family Weekend, and the first stop is the tailgate. Moss said Boulder fans travel well to Tempe in November because nobody wants to miss a warm night.

Here is how I see it. Both defenses give up a lot, and both teams are capable of a big play on any snap. Colorado has real momentum, and Young gives the Buffaloes a ground game they did not have a month ago. But the Sun Devils at home, on a family night with the crowd behind them, have been a different team. Harris will make a couple of big catches, Mack will grind out the tough yards, and Arizona State pulls away late by a score.

Moss says momentum is conserved. Skylar says she has the stopwatch to prove otherwise.

My call: Arizona State Sun Devils 30, Colorado Buffaloes 24.
