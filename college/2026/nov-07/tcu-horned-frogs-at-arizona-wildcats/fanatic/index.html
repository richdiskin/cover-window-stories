TCU Horned Frogs at Arizona Wildcats

TCU Horned Frogs (4-4) at Arizona Wildcats (6-2)

10:15 PM ET Fri Nov 6 · Casino Del Sol Stadium · ESPN

ShareXFacebook

TCU Horned Frogs vs Arizona Wildcats prediction for Friday, November 6: downtown Tucson is throwing the Greek Festival in Jácome Plaza that weekend, and Inez Paloverde has a plan. She is taking Ryder Galloway to Papou's Cafe for Greek coffee and loukoumades, the little honey-soaked doughnuts, before the late kickoff. She says it is Tucson hospitality. Ryder says it is a sugar trap.

Ryder grew up around the Fort Worth Stockyards and now works in finance downtown. He still wears the boots. Inez plays piccolo in the Arizona band and swears it never really gets cold in the desert, even in November.

"Ryder," she says, "you're going to love it here. Warm night, sweet food, and a Wildcats win."

"I'll take two out of three," Ryder says. "And I'm not picky about which two."

Arizona has given Inez plenty to play for. The Wildcats are 6-2 and ranked No. 24. Noah Fifita has 2,481 passing yards, 19 touchdown throws and only four interceptions. Isaiah Mizell caught seven balls for 182 yards at Texas Tech last week. Arizona scores 34.9 points a game and beat Iowa State 39-3 two weeks ago.

"Noah throws the ball like I play piccolo," Inez says. "High, sharp and right on the note."

Ryder points to the loss. "And you still lost 26-34 in Lubbock. Fifita threw for 417 and you lost."

TCU does not win pretty, and Ryder doesn't care. The Frogs are 4-4, but they give up just 14.1 points a game. They beat Kansas 22-16 last week and Baylor 17-6 in October, and their four losses came by five, eight, seven and four.

"We count every point twice in Fort Worth," Ryder says. "Koron Hayward has five and a half sacks. Jamel Johnson has 50 tackles. Nobody runs away from us."

Jaden Craig has been steady, with 2,207 yards, 11 touchdown throws and six interceptions. He ran for 87 yards against Kansas and trusts Kari Ashley, the tight end, who has 38 catches for 560 yards.

"Kari Ashley is a tight end who runs like a calf at a rodeo," Ryder says. "You'll see."

Inez answers with defense of her own. Chase Kennedy has six sacks and Taye Brown has 47 tackles. "Your quarterback is going to meet Chase so many times Friday he'll want to sit at Yia Yia's House with the grandmas."

The festival has a spot called Yia Yia's House, and a Kids Zone too. Ryder says he might hide in the Kids Zone if the Wildcats get rolling early.

The history is short. The only recent meeting was in 2024, and TCU won it 49-28.

"Forty-nine," Ryder says. "On you."

"That was a different Arizona," Inez says. "This one is ranked."

The two teams have one more common thread, and both of them want to talk about it. Arizona went to Morgantown and beat West Virginia 52-31. TCU lost 20-24 to the same Mountaineers at home two weeks later.

"Fifty-two," Inez says, holding up her piccolo like a pointer. "You scored twenty."

"And BYU beat you by 11 in Provo," Ryder says. "BYU beat us by seven. Same Cougars."

Inez shrugs. Kedrick Reescano has seven rushing touchdowns, and she says he is the closer when the Wildcats get near the end zone. Ryder says the Frogs have closed doors on better.

Here is how it shakes out. The TCU defense hangs in, Craig makes some plays with his legs, and the Frogs keep it close into the second half. But Fifita finds Mizell for the big plays, Kennedy gets after Craig, and Arizona pulls away just enough in the fourth quarter to send the home crowd out happy.

Inez buys Ryder a second plate of loukoumades to soften the blow. Ryder eats them anyway and says Tucson is all right, for a desert.

My call: Arizona Wildcats 26, TCU Horned Frogs 17.
