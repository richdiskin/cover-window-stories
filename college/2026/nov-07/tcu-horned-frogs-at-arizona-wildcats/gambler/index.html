TCU vs Arizona prediction, spread and over/under for Friday night: I have Arizona Wildcats −6.5 and Under 46.5, with Arizona Wildcats 26, TCU Horned Frogs 16 as my final. Kickoff is 10:15 PM ET at Casino Del Sol Stadium, and downtown Tucson spends Friday at the Greek Festival in Jácome Plaza, with loukoumades at Papou's Cafe.

Inez Paloverde plays piccolo in the Arizona band and swears the desert keeps her warm all November. "Arizona is 6-2 and ranked 24th. Noah Fifita threw for 417 yards at Texas Tech. Isaiah Mizell caught 7 balls for 182. We beat Iowa State 39-3 two weeks ago."

Ryder Galloway grew up around the Fort Worth Stockyards and now works in finance, so he counts everything. "TCU allows 14.1 points a game. We beat Kansas 22-16 and Baylor 17-6. And the last time we played, 2024, we beat Arizona 49-28."

Here is why I lay it with Arizona. Noah Fifita has 19 touchdown throws and 4 interceptions, and he completed 33 of 45 in Lubbock. Isaiah Mizell caught 7 balls for 182 yards in that game, and Arizona hung 39 on Iowa State two weeks ago. The Wildcats score 34.9 a game. TCU's defense is the best group the Frogs bring to Tucson, but my 26-16 is a ten-point Arizona win, past the 6.5.

The risk is that TCU defense. The Frogs have allowed more than 21 points only once all year, 24 to West Virginia, and they have held six opponents to 17 or fewer. TCU allows 14.1 points a game. Jaden Craig has thrown for 2,207 yards, and Kari Ashley gives him a tight end to throw to underneath. If TCU gets stops, the 6.5 can look long. Chase Kennedy, with 6 sacks, can make Craig's night hard, too.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 44.5 and the number now sits at 46.5. TCU scores 23.2 a game. Most of the Frogs' recent games have stayed quiet, and the only recent one that did not was the 20-24 loss to West Virginia. My 26 and 16 make 42, Under 46.5.
