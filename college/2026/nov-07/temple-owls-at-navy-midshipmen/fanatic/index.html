Both 2-6, Temple and Navy Fight to Stop the Bleeding

Temple Owls (2-6) at Navy Midshipmen (2-6)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Navy-Marine Corps Memorial Stadium · CBSSN

ShareXFacebook

Temple Owls vs Navy Midshipmen prediction for Saturday, November 7: both teams walk into Navy-Marine Corps Memorial Stadium at 2-6, and Teddy Fiorella says that makes it the most honest game in America. Cmdr. (Ret.) Annabel Merriweather says it makes it a game where somebody gets to stop the bleeding, and she has a chart showing which way the current runs.

Here's the deal. Kickoff in Annapolis is 3:30 p.m. ET. Before that, the Navy Football Brotherhood sets up its tailgate at Gate 5 and Taylor Avenue at noon, and the Naval Academy classes of 2011, 2016 and 2021 are back for their 15th, 10th and 5th reunions. This is also the season the stadium is marking the 100th anniversary of Navy's 1926 national championship team.

"Nineteen twenty-six," Teddy says. "In Philly we were busy inventing everything else."

"In Annapolis we were busy winning a national title, Theodore," Annabel says. "Note it in the log."

The stadium got new food this year too, including cream of crab soup and coffee from Travelin' Tom's in Annapolis. And in the southwest corner of the stadium, fans can take photos with a Blue Angel and a Marine Corps Harrier.

"Cream of crab soup," Teddy says. "Fancy. Where's the cheesesteak?"

"In Philadelphia," Annabel says. "Where you left it."

Teddy is wearing a Temple hoodie and running shoes. He jogged the steps at the stadium twice before anybody else showed up. Annabel watched him from the parking lot with a coffee and said nothing, which is the most frightening thing she can do.

Now the football.

Teddy goes first, because he is louder. Ajani Sheppard has 1,245 yards with 8 touchdowns and 3 interceptions, and yes, all three interceptions came in the 9-17 loss at East Carolina last week. Keveun Mason is the workhorse with 564 rushing yards. Colin Chase leads the receivers with 330 yards. And then there's Curly Ordonez, the linebacker, who leads the team with 77 tackles and 2 sacks.

"Ordonez is a Philly guy in spirit," Teddy says. "He'll hit you, then help you up, then hit you again."

Annabel takes the floor. Jackson Gutierrez runs the Navy offense, and he does it on the ground. He has 979 rushing yards and 13 rushing touchdowns, plus 717 yards through the air on very few throws. Jordan Collins, the tight end, has 212 receiving yards on just 6 catches. Coleman Cauley leads the defense with 63 tackles, and MarcAnthony Parker has 4 sacks.

"Gutierrez is a destroyer escort," Annabel says. "Low profile. Hard to stop. Keeps coming."

"Your team gave up 49 to North Texas," Teddy says.

"Steady as she goes," Annabel says. "And your team lost to Charlotte."

Teddy does sit-ups on the spot. Out of spite.

The numbers say these two belong together. Temple scores 23.6 a game and gives up 26.6. Navy scores 27.6 and gives up 29.9. Over the last nine meetings, Navy won five and Temple won four. Last year Navy won 32-31.

"One point," Teddy says. "One point, Commander."

"A point is a point," Annabel says. "Ask anyone who has docked a ship."

Both teams are coming off a bad week. Temple lost at East Carolina. Navy lost 13-35 to Notre Dame. Navy also dropped close ones at UTSA, 34-38, and at UAB, 20-24.

Here's how it plays out on the Severn. Navy keeps the ball for long, grinding drives, and Gutierrez carries it over and over. Temple answers with Mason on the ground and a couple of big throws from Sheppard to Chase. Ordonez makes a big stop in the third quarter that makes Teddy run up and down his row like it's the art museum steps. But Navy's running game wears on Temple in the fourth quarter, and Cauley gets the stop the Midshipmen need. It's a one-score game until late.

Annabel rings an imaginary ship's bell and says "Anchors aweigh." Teddy says you gotta take a punch, takes the punch, and asks where the cream of crab soup stand is.

My call: Navy Midshipmen 30, Temple Owls 23.
