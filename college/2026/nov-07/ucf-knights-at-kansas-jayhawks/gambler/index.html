Kansas Lays 3 to UCF in Lawrence, Jayhawks Win It Late

UCF Knights (4-4) at Kansas Jayhawks (3-5)

3:45 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium · ESPN2

ShareXFacebook

UCF vs Kansas prediction: our pick is Kansas Jayhawks −3, we're on the Over 48.5, and the final we're calling is Kansas Jayhawks 29, UCF Knights 25. Kickoff is 3:45 PM ET Saturday at David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium in Lawrence.

Knox Brevard-Stiles designs roller coasters in Orlando and talks about football in terms of g-forces. Fitz Calloway is a Lawrence lifer who only found out it was football season because somebody moved his basketball schedule off the fridge.

"Three points," Knox says. "That's one small drop, then the big loop."

"Rock Chalk," Fitz says. "Wait, it's football season?"

Why Kansas: The Jayhawks score 29.1 a game and give up 23.8. UCF just took a 9-41 beating from Baylor, and Keyone Jenkins went 14 of 21 for 82 yards in that one. Kansas lost 16-22 at TCU, but Cole Ballard threw for 285 yards in his start. Isaiah Marshall has 1,506 yards and 11 touchdowns this season, with 7 interceptions. Nik McMillan has 466 receiving yards on 25 catches, a big-play guy. Kansas also beat UCF 51-22 in 2023 and 27-20 last year.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 48.5, with Kansas −2.5 on the side, and it is still 48.5. We're on the Over 48.5. UCF scores 29.8 a game. Jenkins has 1,181 yards and 11 touchdowns, Duke Watson has 597 rushing yards, and Josh Derry has 37 catches for 428 yards. Kansas gives up 23.8 and UCF gives up 25.2. Both offenses can get there.

The room agrees on most of it. The Formula lands on Kansas −3 without backing it hard and has it Kansas 29-25. Next Man Up lands on Kansas −3 without backing it hard and has it Kansas 30-26. In The Trenches likes Kansas −3. The Formula likes the Over. Next Man Up likes the Over too. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

UCF has been a coaster this year: 39-31 in overtime over BYU, then the Baylor game. Kansas has been up and down too, but the Jayhawks are better at home, and Saturday is their Salute to Service game. Trey Lathan has 55 tackles and Leroy Harris III has 3.5 sacks. UCF gives up 25.2 a game, and Kansas should find enough plays to win by a field goal or more. Dylan Edwards has 573 rushing yards to close it out late.

"That's a lot of loops," Knox says.

"Three points," Fitz says. "In basketball that's one shot."

Kansas Jayhawks 29, UCF Knights 25. Kansas Jayhawks cover the 3. Over 48.5.
