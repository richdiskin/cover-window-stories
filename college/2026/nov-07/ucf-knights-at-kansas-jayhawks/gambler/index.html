UCF at Kansas: Lawrence Wants the Yards to Start Counting for Something

UCF Knights (4-4) at Kansas Jayhawks (3-5)

3:45 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium · ESPN2

XFacebook

UCF at Kansas: KU -4; total 48.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

Lawrence, Nahzae Cox just caught five passes for 145 yards. Kansas still lost 22-16 to TCU. UCF arrives after a 41-9 loss to Baylor, so neither team is likely to mistake a nice individual stat for a complete Saturday.

November has made the road narrow for both teams. Kansas cornerback Roman Pearson said the Jayhawks want to make a bowl game and are taking the rest of the season one win at a time, per the Lawrence Journal-World. UCF is trying to snap a 12-game road skid, KansasCity.com reports, and has not won on the road in Scott Frost's second stint.

ESPN's AP preview lists Kansas at 3-5 and UCF at 4-4. Cox's production came with Cole Ballard throwing for 285 yards. Those are last game's facts, not a guaranteed starting-lineup announcement for this one.

Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and its total is pending too.

Watch whether Kansas can turn its passing yards into a final drive that ends with a celebration. UCF's Duke Watson has 597 rushing yards in the same report, so keep an eye on the Knights' ability to make Kansas defend the ground game before chasing receivers. There is a fan's kind of hope here: fewer explanations after moving the ball, more points while doing it. Lawrence would like to collect on that hope today.

The published Cover Writer card stays Kansas Jayhawks −3 and Over 48.5. My final is Kansas Jayhawks 29, UCF Knights 25. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

Why Kansas: The Jayhawks score 29.1 a game and give up 23.8. UCF just took a 9-41 beating from Baylor, and Keyone Jenkins went 14 of 21 for 82 yards in that one. Kansas lost 16-22 at TCU, but Cole Ballard threw for 285 yards in his start. Isaiah Marshall has 1,506 yards and 11 touchdowns this season, with 7 interceptions. Nik McMillan has 466 receiving yards on 25 catches, a big-play guy. Kansas also beat UCF 51-22 in 2023 and 27-20 last year.

We're on the Over 48.5. UCF scores 29.8 a game. Jenkins has 1,181 yards and 11 touchdowns, Duke Watson has 597 rushing yards, and Josh Derry has 37 catches for 428 yards. Kansas gives up 23.8 and UCF gives up 25.2. Both offenses can get there.

Cox's big receiving day is worth appreciating, but Kansas would gladly trade some of the conversation about yards for a finished possession. UCF has its own correction to make after Baylor. Watson's ground game can help the Knights keep the afternoon orderly; Lawrence gets to test whether the Jayhawks can interrupt it. Kansas needs Cox involved before the possession reaches desperation. If the Jayhawks can do that, Lawrence has a football reason to get loud beyond the memory of last week's yards.

Kansas Jayhawks 29, UCF Knights 25. Kansas Jayhawks cover the 3. Over 48.5.
