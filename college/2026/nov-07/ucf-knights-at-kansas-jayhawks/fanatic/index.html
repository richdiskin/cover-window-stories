Roller Coaster Brain Meets a Lawrence Lifer: UCF Visits Kansas

UCF Knights (4-4) at Kansas Jayhawks (3-5)

3:45 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium · ESPN2

ShareXFacebook

UCF Knights vs Kansas Jayhawks prediction for Saturday, November 7: Knox Brevard-Stiles designs roller coasters in Orlando and has flown to Lawrence with a clipboard full of g-force charts, and Fitz Calloway, a Lawrence lifer, has just been told by his wife that the Jayhawks play football on Saturday.

"Rock Chalk," Fitz says. "Wait, it's FOOTBALL season?"

"It has been since September, Fitz," Knox says. "Your team is 3-5."

"In basketball we'd still be undefeated," Fitz says.

Here's the deal. Saturday is Kansas' Salute to Service game and its "Beak the Stigma" game, and it's the fifth home game of the season at David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium. Kickoff is 3:45 p.m. ET. Kansas is 3-1 at home this year, and the last time the Jayhawks played there, they beat Baylor 25-24 on Homecoming on Oct. 24, coming back from 11 points down in the fourth quarter.

"Eleven points in the fourth quarter," Knox says. "That's a launch coaster. Zero to sixty."

"I watched the end of that one," Fitz says. "Somebody told me it was on."

Knox has his own coaster story. UCF beat BYU 39-31 in overtime on Oct. 24. Then the Knights went home against Baylor and lost 9-41. Up the lift hill, then straight down.

"That's not a ride," Fitz says. "That's a malfunction."

"It's a lesson in physics," Knox says. "What goes up comes down hard."

Fitz insists on giving Knox the full tour. He points out every concession stand, especially the one where he plans to spend most of the second quarter.

"You know there's a game going on during all this," Knox says.

"I've heard rumors," Fitz says. "Somebody said there's a scoreboard and everything."

"There is," Knox says. "And it counts by sevens, not twos."

Now the football.

Knox goes first, flipping through his clipboard. Keyone Jenkins has thrown for 1,181 yards and 11 touchdowns. Duke Watson leads the ground game with 597 yards. Josh Derry has 37 catches for 428 yards. UCF scores 29.8 a game. On defense, Tackett Curtis leads with 48 tackles and Isaiah Nixon has 2.5 sacks.

"Jenkins is a smooth operator," Knox says. "When he's on, it's a ten-car train with no wobble."

"Against Baylor he threw for 82 yards," Fitz says.

"Every coaster has a maintenance day," Knox says.

Fitz takes over, confused but loyal. Isaiah Marshall has 1,506 passing yards with 11 touchdowns. Cole Ballard threw for 285 at TCU last week, though Kansas lost 16-22. Dylan Edwards leads the backs with 573 yards. Nik McMillan has 466 receiving yards on just 25 catches, which Fitz says is like a guard who only shoots threes. Trey Lathan has 55 tackles. Leroy Harris III has 3.5 sacks.

"McMillan's a sharpshooter," Fitz says. "Catch, go, gone."

"That's a good ratio," Knox says. "I respect the engineering."

Kansas scores 29.1 a game and gives up 23.8. UCF gives up 25.2. Knox notes that these teams have met before. Kansas won 51-22 in 2023 and 27-20 last year.

"Two straight," Fitz says. "Is that good in football?"

"It's good in anything, Fitz," Knox sighs.

Here's how it plays out in Lawrence. The Salute to Service crowd fills the stands early, and Kansas comes out fast with Marshall finding McMillan deep. UCF answers, because Jenkins and Watson are too good to stay quiet. It's a back-and-forth game, a real wooden coaster, all rattle and noise. Edwards grinds out the clock in the fourth quarter, and Lathan makes the stop that ends UCF's last drive.

Knox folds up his clipboard and says it was a good ride. Fitz checks his phone to see when basketball starts.

My call: Kansas Jayhawks 29, UCF Knights 25.
