Cougars Head North: BYU Takes the Holy War to Salt Lake City

By Bryce Halvorsen-Tuilagi · BYU Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Oh my heck, Cougar Nation! I'm Bryce Halvorsen-Tuilagi, your BYU insider. I'm a mountain-biking trail guide in Provo, and I chart every play like I'm reading a trail map from the top of Y Mountain. I tell dad jokes so clean they squeak.

My promise to you is simple. Every number in this BYU report has a source. If I don't know something about BYU, I'll tell you. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I thank God for mountains, family and fall Saturdays.

The School in 60 Seconds
BYU started playing college football in 1922 and has won 23 conference championships and the 1984 national championship. The Cougars were an FBS independent from 2011 to 2022 and joined the Big 12 on July 1, 2023. Home is LaVell Edwards Stadium, named for the coach who won 19 conference titles and that 1984 national title. Wikipedia lists 62,073 seats and CFBD lists 63,470.

The last five seasons: 10-3, 8-5, 5-7, 11-2 and 12-2. Oh my heck.

The Coach and the Scheme
Kalani Sitake played fullback for BYU under LaVell Edwards and graduated in 2000. He became head coach in 2016 (CFBD lists Dec. 19, 2015, as his hire date). Through 2025 he had four 10-win seasons. He's the first Tongan head coach in college football, and he entered the Polynesian Football Hall of Fame in 2025.

The 8-game numbers: 32.4 points per game scored, 18.8 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Bear Bachmeier: 135 of 214, 1,750 yards, 12 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB LJ Martin: 344 yards on 44 carries, 3 touchdowns.
WR Legend Glasker: 32 catches, 585 yards, 4 touchdowns.
DE Tausili Akana: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Cade Uluave: a team-high 52 tackles.

The Conference Picture
BYU is 7-1 overall and 4-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have BYU 2nd in the Big 12.
The season so far: W 63-7 vs Utah Tech Trailblazers (Sept. 5), W 28-17 vs Arizona Wildcats (Sept. 12), W 41-23 at Colorado State Rams (Sept. 19), W 17-10 at TCU Horned Frogs (Oct. 3), W 24-10 vs Iowa State Cyclones (Oct. 9), W 28-24 vs Notre Dame Fighting Irish (Oct. 17), L 31-39 (OT) at UCF Knights (Oct. 24), W 27-20 vs Arizona State Sun Devils (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to No. 3 Utah (8-0, 5-0 Big 12) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at noon ET, at Rice-Eccles Stadium, Salt Lake City, Utah, on ABC. We come in ranked No. 7.
Last time out. We beat the Arizona State Sun Devils 27-20 at home on Oct. 31. Bear Bachmeier went 19 of 30 for 236 yards, with 1 touchdown, Sione Moa ran 8 times for 20 yards and 2 touchdowns and Legend Glasker caught 4 balls for 87 yards.
Utah beat the Cincinnati Bearcats 49-25 on the road on Oct. 31. Devon Dampier went 22 of 29 for 260 yards, with 3 touchdowns; Wayshawn Parker ran 7 times for 82 yards and 1 touchdown; Hunter Andrews caught 5 balls for 112 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Players to watch. Utah quarterback Devon Dampier is 158 of 227 for 1,876 yards, with 18 touchdowns and 7 interceptions. Wayshawn Parker leads their ground game with 726 yards on 109 carries and 9 scores. Braden Pegan is their top target: 39 catches for 505 yards. On defense, linebacker Trey Reynolds has 46 tackles and defensive tackle Karson Kaufusi has 3 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 7 regular-season meetings with Utah from 2016 through 2025. We won 3 of them, and Utah won the last one 24-21 in 2025.
Availability. No official availability report for BYU or Utah was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Aspen Mortensen, Utah's insider. Here's what I said:
"The story where BYU comes out on top is the last two years. Twenty-two to twenty-one in 2024. Twenty-four to twenty-one in 2025. I do not need a musical for that. I have the scores memorized and set to my own soundtrack."
"You scored 21 both of the last two years. Same number twice, both losses. Your team has a ceiling and it is painted crimson."
"Our secondary will be fine. We beat Notre Dame 28-24. We beat Arizona State 27-20 last week. Our only loss came in overtime at UCF, 39-31, and I still say that one was a road trip that went a little long."
And the last word from Aspen: "Deal. Bring a jacket. You will want something to hide the blue."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)

My call: Utah Utes 30, BYU Cougars 22.
