Heroes Day at Camp Randall: Wisconsin Hosts Rutgers

By Lars "Brat" Ellefson · Wisconsin Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hand me the curds. (Polka accordion: oom-pah, oom-pah.) Hiya, folks, I'm Lars Ellefson, but everybody calls me Brat. I was a fullback for the Badgers back in my day, and I still carry a cheese wedge to every game. Every single one. You'll notice I play a little polka before I share a stat. My doctor says it's good for my heart. When we win, I jump around, literally, and hand out cheese curds. When we lose, I eat a brat in thoughtful silence. Then I eat another one.

The School in 60 Seconds
(Oom-pah.) The Badgers have played in the Big Ten since it formed in 1896. Wisconsin is one of 26 programs to win 700 or more games. Home is Camp Randall Stadium, the fourth-oldest stadium in college football, opened in 1917 and listed at 80,321 seats. The last five seasons were 9-4, 7-6, 7-6, 5-7 and 4-8.

The Coach and the Scheme
Luke Fickell is the head coach, and CFBD dates his hire to Nov. 27, 2022. (Oom-pah.) His teams are built on defense, the way a good barn is built on a stone foundation. Spoken like a fullback.

Through 8 games this season, Wisconsin is scoring 23.6 points per game and allowing 17.1.

Players to Know
QB Colton Joseph: 104 of 187, 1,346 yards, 11 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
Colton Joseph on the ground: 371 yards on 91 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Tyrell Henry: 30 catches, 397 yards, 1 touchdown.
LB Sebastian Cheeks: 6 sacks, the team lead.
LB Cooper Catalano: a team-high 75 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Wisconsin is 5-3 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Wisconsin 5th in the Big Ten.
The season so far: W 36-9 vs Western Illinois Leathernecks (Sept. 12), W 54-10 vs Eastern Michigan Eagles (Sept. 19), W 24-20 at Penn State Nittany Lions (Sept. 26), W 31-3 vs Michigan State Spartans (Oct. 3), L 7-17 at UCLA Bruins (Oct. 17), W 24-7 vs USC Trojans (Oct. 24), L 0-30 at Iowa Hawkeyes (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Rutgers (2-6, 1-4 Big Ten) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 4:15 p.m. ET, at Camp Randall Stadium, Madison, Wisconsin, on BTN.
Last time out. We lost to the Iowa Hawkeyes 30-0 on the road on Oct. 31. Colton Joseph went 5 of 14 for 90 yards, Abu Sama III ran 6 times for 23 yards and Jaylon Domingeaux caught 6 balls for 86 yards.
Rutgers lost to the Michigan Wolverines 28-21 at home on Oct. 31. AJ Surace went 3 of 12 for 123 yards, with 1 touchdown; Antwan Raymond ran 11 times for 85 yards; Jourdin Houston caught 1 balls for 80 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Rutgers quarterback Dylan Lonergan is 74 of 127 for 1,066 yards, with 10 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. Antwan Raymond leads their ground game with 733 yards on 134 carries and 6 scores. KJ Duff is their top target: 45 catches for 774 yards. On defense, defensive back Chris Joines has 41 tackles and defensive lineman Sekou Kromah has 3 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 4 regular-season meetings with Rutgers from 2016 through 2025. We won 2 of them, and Rutgers won the last one 42-7 in 2024.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, Wisconsin or Rutgers.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Vinny Scolari, Rutgers's insider. Here's what I said:
"You get respect. You just don't get wins in Madison. Four meetings, four Wisconsin wins. 31-17, 52-3, 24-13, 42-7."
"Wisconsin is 5-3. We give up 17.1 a game. We won 24-20 at Penn State. We beat USC 24-7. We beat Michigan State 31-3."
"Zero to thirty. We don't discuss it."
And the last word from Vinny: "Here's my problem. Joseph threw for 66 at UCLA and 58 against USC and 90 at Iowa. You can't throw it? We'll put nine guys in the box and make you."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Heroes Day at Camp Randall, with the free Salute to Veterans Tailgate at Camp Randall Memorial Park showing replica gear from the Wisconsin Veterans Museum.

My call: Wisconsin Badgers 33, Rutgers Scarlet Knights 18.
