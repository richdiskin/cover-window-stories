Eagles at Home: Boston College Hosts Florida State on Military Appreciation Day

By Siobhan Dunleavy · Boston College Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
All rise, Eagles fans. I'm Siobhan Dunleavy, your Boston College insider, grad student and former debate champion. I argue every play like it's a moot-court case, and I always appoint myself the judge, which my co-hosts find wicked unfair. When we win, I bang my toy gavel and announce, "Objection overruled, we WON." When we lose, I file an appeal with the Court of Iced Coffee. The court has never ruled in my favor, and I keep filing anyway. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, argued on the merits. "Objection: sustained."

The School in 60 Seconds
Boston College plays at Alumni Stadium in Chestnut Hill, listed at 44,500.

The Coach and the Scheme
Bill O'Brien was named Boston College's 37th head coach on Feb. 9, 2024. His résumé is a long exhibit list. At Penn State in 2012 and 2013 he went 15-9 and won the Bear Bryant and Maxwell Club coach of the year awards in 2012. With the Houston Texans he went 52-48 and won four AFC South titles.

Through 8 games this season, Boston College is scoring 21.6 points per game and allowing 26.5.

Players to Know
QB Mason McKenzie: 116 of 202, 1,450 yards, 8 touchdowns, 7 interceptions.
Mason McKenzie on the ground: 638 yards on 132 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Javarius Green: 28 catches, 308 yards, 1 touchdown.
DE Demetrius Ballard: 4.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Anthony Palano: a team-high 69 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Boston College is 4-4 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have Boston College 9th in the ACC.
The season so far: L 15-34 at Cincinnati Bearcats (Sept. 5), W 28-21 vs Rutgers Scarlet Knights (Sept. 11), W 22-16 vs Maine Black Bears (Sept. 19), L 14-21 vs Virginia Tech Hokies (Sept. 26), L 16-25 at SMU Mustangs (Oct. 3), W 27-21 vs Pittsburgh Panthers (Oct. 17), W 49-27 at Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets (Oct. 24), L 2-47 at Duke Blue Devils (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Florida State (3-5, 1-4 ACC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at noon ET, at Alumni Stadium (Chestnut Hill, MA), Chestnut Hill, Massachusetts, on ACC Network.
Last time out. We lost to the Duke Blue Devils 47-2 on the road on Oct. 31. Mason McKenzie went 9 of 17 for 87 yards, Bo MacCormack III ran 9 times for 24 yards and Jackson Wade caught 1 balls for 36 yards.
Florida State lost to the Clemson Tigers 36-24 at home on Oct. 31. Ashton Daniels went 21 of 33 for 350 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Quintrevion Wisner ran 13 times for 43 yards; Micahi Danzy caught 4 balls for 108 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Florida State quarterback Ashton Daniels is 127 of 214 for 1,968 yards, with 14 touchdowns and 11 interceptions. Ousmane Kromah leads their ground game with 609 yards on 114 carries and 5 scores. Duce Robinson is their top target: 24 catches for 438 yards. On defense, linebacker Chris Jones has 56 tackles and defensive lineman Rylan Kennedy has 6 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 8 regular-season meetings with Florida State from 2016 through 2025. We won 4 of them, and Florida State won the last one 28-13 in 2024.
Availability. The public injury data for Florida State and Boston College is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Gianna Ravello, Florida State's insider. Here's what I said:
"History is what you reach for when your present is 3-5. Let me introduce a counterargument. You are 3-5."
"Duke was a bad day. Two weeks ago we went to Georgia Tech and won 49-27. Mason McKenzie runs this team on his own legs. He has 1,450 throwing yards and 638 more on the ground. He is our quarterback, our running back and our closing argument."
"Your whole cast has 11 interceptions. Daniels threw 3 last week against Clemson and you lost 36-24. A cast is only as good as the person who keeps dropping the script."
And the last word from Gianna: "My rebuttal. Nice speech. Florida State by eight. Then I buy you a chowder."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Veterans and Military Appreciation Day at Alumni Stadium, and Florida State is in Chestnut Hill for the first time since 2023.

My call: Florida State Seminoles 31, Boston College Eagles 23.
