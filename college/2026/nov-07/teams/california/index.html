Golden Bears Reset: California Gets a Week Off Before Virginia

By Quentin Oyelaran-Fitch · California Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Greetings, comrades of the Blue and Gold! I'm Quentin Oyelaran-Fitch, your Cal insider. I'm writing a philosophy dissertation on the ethics of the fourth-down decision, and I have opinions. When I see a bad play-call, I don't just complain. I make a picket sign. I have a closet full of them: "THIRD-AND-LONG IS A SOCIAL CONSTRUCT." "RUN IT ON SECOND DOWN, PLEASE (RESPECTFULLY)." When we win, I raise a fist and shout "Power to the play-call!" When we lose, I organize a peaceful, fully permitted protest against the punter. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, argued with conviction.

The School in 60 Seconds
Every Cal fan knows the greatest play in college football history, and we just call it The Play. In the 1982 Big Game against Stanford, the Bears returned the final kickoff through five laterals, and Kevin Moen scored as the Stanford band wandered onto the field. Cal won 25-20. Our home is California Memorial Stadium in Berkeley, listed at 62,467, with the hills right behind it. Cal now plays in the ACC. A cross-country revolution, if you will.

The Coach and the Scheme
Tosh Lupoi was named the 35th head coach in program history on Dec. 4, 2025, replacing Justin Wilcox.

The 9-game numbers: 21.9 points per game scored, 31.6 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Jaron-Keawe Sagapolutele: 178 of 264, 1,751 yards, 11 touchdowns, 6 interceptions.
RB Adam Mohammed: 363 yards on 82 carries, 3 touchdowns.
WR Chase Hendricks: 61 catches, 565 yards, 4 touchdowns.
LB JD McKinley: 3 sacks, the team lead.
LB AJ Tuitele: a team-high 52 tackles.

The Conference Picture
California is 3-6 overall and 2-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have California 12th in the ACC.
The season so far: L 24-45 vs UCLA Bruins (Sept. 5), W 21-18 at Syracuse Orange (Sept. 12), W 49-7 vs Wagner Seahawks (Sept. 19), L 10-24 vs Clemson Tigers (Sept. 25), L 31-39 at UNLV Rebels (Oct. 3), W 25-20 vs Virginia Tech Hokies (Oct. 10), L 7-41 vs Wake Forest Demon Deacons (Oct. 17), L 20-59 at SMU Mustangs (Oct. 24), L 10-31 at NC State Wolfpack (Oct. 31).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for California this week. We're off from Tuesday, Nov. 3, through Saturday, Nov. 7, so the rest of this California report is about where we stand and what's next.
Last time out. We lost to the NC State Wolfpack 31-10 on the road on Oct. 31. Jaron-Keawe Sagapolutele went 29 of 45 for 196 yards, with 1 touchdown. Anthony League ran 3 times for 29 yards. Chase Hendricks caught 7 balls for 67 yards and 1 touchdown.
Up next. We play the Virginia Cavaliers on the road on Saturday, Nov. 14, with the kickoff time still to be announced, at Scott Stadium in Charlottesville.
Rest up, Bears. Virginia is next, and Berkeley needs this team fully loaded for the stretch run.
