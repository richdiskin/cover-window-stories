Rainbow Warriors in El Paso: Hawai'i Returns to the Sun Bowl

By Kainoa Akana-Baird · Hawai'i Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Aloha, Rainbow Warriors fans! I'm Kainoa Akana-Baird, your Hawai'i insider. When I'm not on the show, I teach big-wave surfing on O'ahu. And when I'm on the show, I do time-zone math, because when your team flies farther than anybody in college football, somebody has to keep the clocks straight. When we win, I say "Shoots!" and throw a shaka. When we lose, I shrug: "Island time. We'll catch the next wave." Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, with aloha. "Island time, but on schedule."

The School in 60 Seconds
Home for us is the Clarence T.C. Ching Athletics Complex, listed at 15,194. Last season was a big wave. Hawai'i went 9-4, opened the season by beating Stanford, and won the Hawai'i Bowl over Cal after coming back from 21 points down, tying the biggest comeback in program history. It was our first bowl bid since 2021.

The Coach and the Scheme
Timmy Chang was named Hawai'i's 25th head coach on Jan. 22, 2022. Before that, he was a Hawai'i legend. As our quarterback from 2000 to 2004, he set the NCAA career records for passing yards (17,072) and total offense, and he won a program-record 29 games as a starter. He was also a two-time Hawai'i Bowl Most Outstanding Player and a finalist for the Johnny Unitas Award as a senior. Before all that he was a record-setting quarterback at Honolulu's Saint Louis School. Local boy. He coached at Nevada, Jackson State, Emory & Henry and SMU before coming home. In 2025 he was the AFCA Region 5 Coach of the Year.

Through 8 games this season, Hawai'i is scoring 20.8 points per game and allowing 24.5.

Players to Know
QB Micah Alejado: 202 of 337, 2,186 yards, 9 touchdowns, 6 interceptions.
RB DeVon Rice: 385 yards on 82 carries, 6 touchdowns.
WR Kainoa Carvalho: 35 catches, 399 yards.
LB Wynden Ho'ohuli: 2 sacks, the team lead.
LB Jamih Otis: a team-high 54 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Hawai'i is 2-6 overall and 1-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have Hawai'i 8th in the Mountain West.
The season so far: L 27-37 at Stanford Cardinal (Aug. 29), L 6-21 vs UNLV Rebels (Sept. 5), W 29-19 vs New Mexico State Aggies (Sept. 12), L 10-27 at Wyoming Cowboys (Sept. 26), L 16-20 vs San José State Spartans (Oct. 3), L 12-30 at Arizona State Sun Devils (Oct. 10), L 10-21 vs New Mexico Lobos (Oct. 17), W 56-21 at Northern Illinois Huskies (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to UTEP (1-7, 0-4 Mountain West) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 9:30 p.m. ET, at Sun Bowl, El Paso, Texas, on FS1.
Last time out. We beat the Northern Illinois Huskies 56-21 on the road on Oct. 24. Micah Alejado went 22 of 32 for 323 yards, with 3 touchdowns, DeVon Rice ran 12 times for 71 yards and 3 touchdowns and Audric Harris caught 2 balls for 90 yards.
UTEP lost to the North Dakota State Bison 31-7 on the road on Oct. 31. EJ Colson Jr. went 11 of 16 for 59 yards; Raymond Moore III ran 3 times for 67 yards and 1 touchdown; Jaden Smith caught 6 balls for 41 yards.
Players to watch. UTEP quarterback EJ Colson Jr. is 66 of 99 for 687 yards, with 7 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Raymond Moore III leads their ground game with 481 yards on 70 carries and 3 scores. Kam Thomas is their top target: 19 catches for 283 yards. On defense, linebacker Jayden Wilson has 47 tackles and defensive end Sterling Miles has 3.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD shows no regular-season meetings with UTEP from 2016 through 2025, so this is a fresh fight for our guys.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Hawai'i vs. UTEP. When Hawai'i or UTEP puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Ernesto "Neto" Galindo, UTEP's insider. Here's what I said:
"Twenty-two years. You've been waiting a while."
"Carvalho. Same first name. Same greatness."
And the last word from Neto: "Hawai'i hasn't been ready all year."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
This is our first trip to the Sun Bowl since 2004, when UTEP won 51-20, so the Rainbow Warriors have an old score to settle in El Paso.

My call: Hawai'i Rainbow Warriors 29, UTEP Miners 20.
