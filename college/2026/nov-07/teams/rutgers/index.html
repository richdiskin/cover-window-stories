Knights in Madison: Rutgers Takes On Camp Randall

By Vinny Scolari · Rutgers Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Yo, Rutgers! How you doin'? Welcome! Vinny Scolari here, your Rutgers insider, born and raised off Exit 9 of the New Jersey Turnpike. I give directions by Turnpike exit for everything, and I mean everything. Where's the stadium? Exit 9. How am I feeling after a loss? Somewhere around Exit 7A. I grade every Big Ten trench battle like a boardwalk arm-wrestle: who pushed in, who flinched, who walked away with the stuffed animal. I travel with this Rutgers team, and I live with it. When we win, I order a sub big enough for the whole studio. Six feet. Everybody eats. When we lose, I say "Fuhgeddaboudit". and then I can't. I really can't. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, Jersey-style. "Exit 9 never lies."

The School in 60 Seconds
Here's something every college football fan should know. The very first college football game was played right in New Brunswick on Nov. 6, 1869, and Rutgers beat Princeton 6-4. That's right: the birthplace of the whole sport is off Exit 9. Home now is SHI Stadium in Piscataway, listed at 52,454. The man running the show is Greg Schiano, who came back to coach Rutgers again in December 2019. The Scarlet Knights play in the Big Ten, and every week is a heavyweight fight.

The Coach and the Scheme
Schiano's second stint began with his return on Nov. 30, 2019.

Through 8 games this season, Rutgers is scoring 27.8 points per game and allowing 32.6.

Players to Know
QB Dylan Lonergan: 74 of 127, 1,066 yards, 10 touchdowns, 6 interceptions.
RB Antwan Raymond: 733 yards on 134 carries, 6 touchdowns.
WR KJ Duff: 45 catches, 774 yards, 9 touchdowns.
DL Sekou Kromah: 3 sacks, the team lead.
DB Chris Joines: a team-high 41 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Rutgers is 2-6 overall and 1-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have Rutgers 15th in the Big Ten.
The season so far: L 21-37 vs Massachusetts Minutemen (Sept. 3), L 21-28 at Boston College Eagles (Sept. 11), L 35-42 vs USC Trojans (Sept. 19), W 58-7 vs Howard Bison (Sept. 25), L 15-47 vs Indiana Hoosiers (Oct. 3), W 37-30 at Maryland Terrapins (Oct. 17), L 14-42 at Northwestern Wildcats (Oct. 24), L 21-28 vs Michigan Wolverines (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Wisconsin (5-3, 3-2 Big Ten) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 4:15 p.m. ET, at Camp Randall Stadium, Madison, Wisconsin, on BTN.
Last time out. We lost to the Michigan Wolverines 28-21 at home on Oct. 31. AJ Surace went 3 of 12 for 123 yards, with 1 touchdown, Antwan Raymond ran 11 times for 85 yards and Jourdin Houston caught 1 balls for 80 yards and 1 touchdown.
Wisconsin lost to the Iowa Hawkeyes 30-0 on the road on Oct. 31. Colton Joseph went 5 of 14 for 90 yards; Abu Sama III ran 6 times for 23 yards; Jaylon Domingeaux caught 6 balls for 86 yards.
Players to watch. Wisconsin quarterback Colton Joseph is 104 of 187 for 1,346 yards, with 11 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Colton Joseph leads their ground game with 371 yards on 91 carries and 4 scores. Tyrell Henry is their top target: 30 catches for 397 yards. On defense, linebacker Cooper Catalano has 75 tackles and linebacker Sebastian Cheeks has 6 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 4 regular-season meetings with Wisconsin from 2016 through 2025. We won 2 of them, and we won the last one 42-7 in 2024.
Availability. The public injury data for Wisconsin and Rutgers is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Lars "Brat" Ellefson, Wisconsin's insider. Here's what I said:
"Heroes Day is a good day. My uncle did twenty years. I'll be at the tailgate looking at the old helmets. Then I'll be in the stands looking at us get no respect."
"The Michigan game is the one. AJ Surace went 3 for 12 and we still almost had it. Jourdin Houston caught one ball and took it 80 yards for a score. That's Jersey. One shot."
And the last word from Brat: "We beat USC throwing for 58. That's the Wisconsin way. Run it, hit you, run it again."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We walk into Wisconsin's Heroes Day at Camp Randall, where veterans got a chance at free tickets from the state's Department of Veterans Affairs, so expect a full house.

My call: Wisconsin Badgers 33, Rutgers Scarlet Knights 18.
