Bearcats Head South: Cincinnati Takes On Houston

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hello, Bearcats, and welcome! I'm Lionel Abernathy, but everybody calls me Skyline, for reasons that will become obvious very quickly. I'm an engineering co-op kid, I chart everything, and I rank it all in Cincinnati chili ways. A three-way is respectable. A four-way is impressive. A five-way, with onions AND beans, is reserved for greatness. That goes for quarterbacks, defensive fronts and, yes, coaches' haircuts. When we win, I upgrade the team on air, three-way to five-way, no hesitation. When we lose, I downgrade to "plain spaghetti" and stare quietly into the distance. Every week I'll bring you the players, the coaches and the matchup, plus a food tangent that somehow loops back into a very sharp stat. I travel with this Cincinnati team, and I live with it. That's a five-way take.

The School in 60 Seconds
Cincinnati has played football on the Nippert Stadium site since 1901, which makes it the second-oldest playing site in college football. That's history with extra cheese. In 2021 the Bearcats became the first team from outside the Power Five to make the College Football Playoff, where they played Alabama in the Cotton Bowl. Now we're in the Big 12. Nippert is listed at 40,000 seats, and every one of them should be full.

The Coach and the Scheme
Scott Satterfield became the 43rd head coach in program history on Dec. 5, 2022.

The 8-game numbers: 34.1 points per game scored, 29.2 allowed.

Players to Know
QB JC French IV: 143 of 213, 1,773 yards, 13 touchdowns, 3 interceptions.
RB Cole Tabb: 752 yards on 79 carries, 10 touchdowns.
WR JV Gibson: 34 catches, 461 yards, 4 touchdowns.
Defender Patrick Bauer: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Jonathan Thompson: a team-high 63 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Cincinnati is 5-3 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have Cincinnati 8th in the Big 12.
The season so far: W 34-15 vs Boston College Eagles (Sept. 5), W 62-10 vs Western Carolina Catamounts (Sept. 12), W 35-31 vs Miami (OH) RedHawks (Sept. 19), W 31-26 vs Kansas State Wildcats (Sept. 26), L 7-34 at Arizona Wildcats (Oct. 3), L 42-45 at West Virginia Mountaineers (Oct. 17), W 37-24 vs Texas Tech Red Raiders (Oct. 24), L 25-49 vs Utah Utes (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to No. 17 Houston (6-2, 3-2 Big 12) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:30 p.m. ET, at TDECU Stadium, Houston, Texas, on Fox.
Last time out. We lost to the Utah Utes 49-25 at home on Oct. 31. JC French IV went 16 of 30 for 263 yards, Cole Tabb ran 14 times for 131 yards and 1 touchdown and JV Gibson caught 5 balls for 76 yards.
Houston lost to the Utah Utes 21-13 on the road on Oct. 24. Conner Weigman went 19 of 35 for 282 yards; Makhi Hughes ran 19 times for 52 yards and 1 touchdown; Amare Thomas caught 5 balls for 133 yards.
Players to watch. Houston quarterback Conner Weigman is 153 of 233 for 2,053 yards, with 20 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. Makhi Hughes leads their ground game with 516 yards on 117 carries and 4 scores. Amare Thomas is their top target: 41 catches for 620 yards. On defense, defensive back Kentrell Webb has 46 tackles and defensive lineman Ashton Porter has 3 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 6 regular-season meetings with Houston from 2016 through 2025. We won 3 of them, and Houston won the last one 34-0 in 2024.
Availability. No official availability report for Cincinnati or Houston was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Bearcats fans making the Houston trip have company in town: the 13th Annual Houston AfriFest runs Saturday from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. at the KBC Houston Event Center.

My call: Houston Cougars 37, Cincinnati Bearcats 24.
