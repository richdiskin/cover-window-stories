Homecoming at Floyd Stadium: Middle Tennessee Hosts WKU

By Opal Stonebriar · Middle Tennessee Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Well, hey there, Blue Raiders. Come in, sit down, and have a biscuit. I'm Opal Stonebriar, your Middle Tennessee insider. Folks call me the front-row grandma of Murfreesboro. I'm 34. It's a spiritual title.

Here's how this works. I will feed you, I will ride with these boys like they're my own, and if you say something about my team that isn't true, I will correct you fast and with a citation. I don't make up numbers, I don't make up injuries, and when I don't know something I'll say, "I don't know yet." Murfreesboro is a city that grew up fast, and so did our program. Come sit with us, and you'll leave a Blue Raider. I promise.

The School in 60 Seconds
Middle Tennessee plays FBS football in Conference USA, out of Murfreesboro, Tennessee. The Blue Raiders have played in 14 bowl games and seven I-AA playoffs. Home is Johnny "Red" Floyd Stadium, which opened in 1933. The sources disagree on its size. CFBD lists a capacity of 30,788, and Wikipedia lists 27,303. I'm noting both until I check the media guide.

The last five seasons have been a climb and then a dip: 7-6 in 2021, 8-5 in 2022, 4-8 in 2023, and 3-9 in both 2024 and 2025.

The Coach and the Scheme
Derek Mason was hired on Dec. 6, 2023, as the 15th head coach in program history. Before Murfreesboro, he was head coach at Vanderbilt from 2014 to 2020.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 23.9 points a game while giving up 29.6.

Players to Know
QB Roman Gagliano: 147 of 258, 1,587 yards, 12 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Antonio Martin: 586 yards on 112 carries, 2 touchdowns.
WR Cam'ron Lacy: 45 catches, 510 yards, 4 touchdowns.
LB Kaven Call: 1.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Korey Smith: a team-high 73 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Middle Tennessee is 4-4 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Middle Tennessee 3rd in the CUSA.
The season so far: W 38-14 vs Murray State Racers (Sept. 5), L 26-28 at Marshall Thundering Herd (Sept. 12), W 27-20 vs Nevada Wolf Pack (Sept. 19), L 13-23 at Jacksonville State Gamecocks (Sept. 26), L 0-55 at Kansas Jayhawks (Oct. 3), W 33-30 vs Delaware Blue Hens (Oct. 13), W 26-23 at Florida International Panthers (Oct. 20), L 28-44 vs Kennesaw State Owls (Oct. 28).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Western Kentucky (3-5, 2-2 Conference USA) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:30 p.m. ET, at Johnny "Red" Floyd Stadium, Murfreesboro, Tennessee, on ESPN+.
Last time out. We lost to the Kennesaw State Owls 44-28 at home on Oct. 28. Roman Gagliano went 21 of 43 for 183 yards, Antonio Martin ran 18 times for 106 yards and 1 touchdown and Cam'ron Lacy caught 4 balls for 73 yards.
Western Kentucky lost to the Delaware Blue Hens 35-21 at home on Oct. 27. Brock Glenn went 25 of 37 for 224 yards, with 1 touchdown; Brock Glenn ran 17 times for 129 yards; Moussa Barry caught 4 balls for 80 yards.
Players to watch. Western Kentucky quarterback Brock Glenn is 92 of 165 for 887 yards, with 6 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Brock Glenn leads their ground game with 451 yards on 91 carries and 5 scores. Jonathan Stafford Jr. is their top target: 46 catches for 433 yards. On defense, defensive back Jaylen Lewis has 62 tackles and linebacker Westen Ard has 2.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 10 regular-season meetings with Western Kentucky from 2016 through 2025. We won 6 of them, and we won the last one 42-26 in 2025.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, Middle Tennessee or Western Kentucky.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Thurman "Tops" Whitsitt, Western Kentucky's insider. Here's what I said:
"Thurman, the children will remember your manners. Mind them."
"Everybody needs to know that."
"Korey Smith is the deacon of that defense. He keeps everybody in their pew."
And the last word from Tops: "You gave up 44 to Kennesaw State last week."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Homecoming and the Hall of Fame Game, kids walk in the parade and form the high-five tunnel, and we lead 21-17-1 in games played in Murfreesboro.

My call: Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders 28, Western Kentucky Hilltoppers 27.
