Dodd After Dark: Georgia Tech Hosts Louisville Under the Lights

By Wendell Quist · Georgia Tech Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Greetings, Jackets fans. I'm Wendell Quist, your Georgia Tech insider. I'm an aerospace-engineering grad, and I model the trajectory of every punt, every throw and every kickoff. Everything is physics. I mean everything, including my relationship with the coaching staff, which I describe as a stable orbit. When we win, I calculate the exact trajectory of the winning kick and cheer in decimals. When we lose, I re-run the simulation 10,000 times looking for the bug. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, plotted to scale. "Graph paper doesn't lie."

The School in 60 Seconds
Georgia Tech claims national championships in 1917, 1928 and 1990. Home is Bobby Dodd Stadium at Hyundai Field, listed at 55,000, in the middle of Midtown Atlanta. Dimensionless, but large.

The Coach and the Scheme
Brent Key was hired on Nov. 29, 2022.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 24.8 points a game while giving up 30.8.

Players to Know
QB Alberto Mendoza: 169 of 240, 1,795 yards, 10 touchdowns, 6 interceptions.
RB Justice Haynes: 548 yards on 112 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Jordan Allen: 46 catches, 543 yards, 1 touchdown.
DE Noah Carter: 3 sacks, the team lead.
S Tae Harris: a team-high 50 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Georgia Tech is 2-6 overall and 1-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have Georgia Tech 16th in the ACC.
The season so far: L 13-14 vs Colorado Buffaloes (Sept. 3), L 24-45 vs Tennessee Volunteers (Sept. 12), W 44-11 vs Mercer Bears (Sept. 19), L 27-34 at Stanford Cardinal (Sept. 26), W 28-25 vs Duke Blue Devils (Oct. 10), L 24-34 at Virginia Tech Hokies (Oct. 17), L 27-49 vs Boston College Eagles (Oct. 24), L 11-34 at Pittsburgh Panthers (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Louisville (5-3, 4-2 ACC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:30 p.m. ET, at Bobby Dodd Stadium, Atlanta, Georgia, on ACC Network.
Last time out. We lost to the Pittsburgh Panthers 34-11 on the road on Oct. 31. Alberto Mendoza went 16 of 25 for 81 yards, Grady Adamson ran 6 times for 63 yards and 1 touchdown and Gavin Harris caught 3 balls for 41 yards.
Louisville beat the Stanford Cardinal 52-24 at home on Oct. 31. Lincoln Kienholz went 20 of 28 for 250 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Isaac Brown ran 9 times for 139 yards and 3 touchdowns; Tre Richardson caught 10 balls for 140 yards.
Players to watch. Louisville quarterback Lincoln Kienholz is 141 of 208 for 2,094 yards, with 12 touchdowns and 3 interceptions. Isaac Brown leads their ground game with 707 yards on 92 carries and 7 scores. Tre Richardson is their top target: 40 catches for 633 yards. On defense, linebacker T.J. Capers has 57 tackles and defensive lineman Tyler Thompson has 5.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 3 regular-season meetings with Louisville from 2016 through 2025. We won 2 of them, and we won the last one 31-19 in 2024.
Availability. No official availability report for Georgia Tech or Louisville was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Bianca Thornberry, Louisville's insider. Here's what I said:
"That's generous. Most guests don't score 52."
"Our side of it is harder. We're 2-6."
"Alberto Mendoza threw for 81 at Pitt. That's a bad trajectory. But he has 1,795 yards on the year. Justice Haynes has 548 rushing yards. Jordan Allen has 543 receiving."
And the last word from Bianca: "Every one of those was a coin flip. That's the part that makes me crazy. We could be 8-0."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Tech is running Dodd After Dark promotions for the 7:30 p.m. kickoff at Bobby Dodd Stadium at Hyundai Field, so the Flats will be loud and late.

My call: Louisville Cardinals 38, Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets 25.
