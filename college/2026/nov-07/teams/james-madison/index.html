Dukes in Hattiesburg: James Madison Takes On Southern Miss Thursday Night

By Lila Shenandoah · James Madison Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Purple reign, y'all! I'm Lila Shenandoah, your James Madison insider. I'm a former JMU cheer captain who somehow became the program's living encyclopedia. I travel with this team, I live with this team, and I turn every stat into a cheer. Yes, even penalties. "Give me a FLAG! Give me a FIVE! Give me. a moment to collect myself!"

Here's what you get from me. Volume, fire and receipts. I'm excited, I'm all-in, and every number I chant has a source behind it. Every opponent gets a respectful nod before we go to war, and I don't invent anything, ever. If I don't know, I'll say, "Hold that chant, I'm checking." The Shenandoah Valley shows up loud and proud for the Dukes. Give me a J, give me an M, give me a third-down stop.

The School in 60 Seconds
JMU first fielded a football team in 1972, and the Dukes play on campus at Bridgeforth Stadium in Harrisonburg, Virginia. CFBD lists it as built in 1975 with a capacity of 24,877. JMU won FCS national championships in 2004 and 2016 and finished as national runner-up in 2017 and 2019. The Dukes accepted an invitation to the FBS in 2021 and joined the Sun Belt. Famous Dukes include Charles Haley, who won five Super Bowl rings and is in both the College and Pro Football Halls of Fame.

The last five seasons are pure cheer material: 12-2, 8-3, 11-2, 9-4 and 12-2. Give me a W! Give me lots of them!

The Coach and the Scheme
Billy Napier is in his first season. CFBD lists his James Madison hire date as Dec. 6, 2025. He was head coach at Louisiana from 2018 to 2021, going 40-12 with three straight seasons of 10 or more wins, and then at Florida from 2022 to 2025. Before becoming a head coach, he was offensive coordinator and quarterbacks coach at Arizona State in 2017.

We've played 9 games, and we're putting up 39.1 points a game while giving up 18.9.

Players to Know
QB Camden Coleman: 145 of 237, 1,742 yards, 16 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB George Pettaway: 824 yards on 124 carries, 8 touchdowns.
WR Corey Scott: 27 catches, 386 yards, 5 touchdowns.
Defender Amar Thomas: 3 sacks, the team lead.
LB Nakian Jackson: a team-high 66 tackles.

The Conference Picture
James Madison is 7-2 overall and 4-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have James Madison 1st in the Sun Belt - East.
The season so far: W 20-13 vs Liberty Flames (Sept. 5), W 87-3 vs Wagner Seahawks (Sept. 12), W 26-13 at San Diego State Aztecs (Sept. 19), W 46-20 at Old Dominion Monarchs (Sept. 26), W 45-17 vs Marshall Thundering Herd (Oct. 3), W 27-24 at Georgia Southern Eagles (Oct. 10), W 49-22 vs Georgia State Panthers (Oct. 17), L 31-34 (OT) at App State Mountaineers (Oct. 22), L 21-24 (OT) vs Troy Trojans (Oct. 29).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Southern Miss (3-5, 2-2 Sun Belt) on Thursday, Nov. 5, at 8:00 p.m. ET, at M. M. Roberts Stadium, Hattiesburg, Mississippi, on ESPN2.
Last time out. We lost to the Troy Trojans 24-21 (OT) at home on Oct. 29. Camden Coleman went 14 of 25 for 175 yards, with 2 touchdowns, Seth Cromwell ran 23 times for 135 yards and 1 touchdown and Michael Scott caught 4 balls for 68 yards.
Southern Miss beat the UL Monroe Warhawks 35-21 on the road on Oct. 31. Landry Lyddy went 15 of 30 for 130 yards, with 1 touchdown; Steven Robinson ran 21 times for 151 yards and 1 touchdown; Mario Sanders caught 4 balls for 31 yards.
Players to watch. Southern Miss quarterback Landry Lyddy is 116 of 200 for 1,326 yards, with 8 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Steven Robinson leads their ground game with 728 yards on 142 carries and 9 scores. Mario Sanders is their top target: 44 catches for 600 yards. On defense, linebacker Mathis Haygood has 70 tackles and defensive lineman Jameer Lewis has 4 sacks.
The series. CFBD has one regular-season meeting with Southern Miss from 2016 through 2025, and Southern Miss won the last one 32-15 in 2024.
Availability. No official availability report for James Madison or Southern Miss was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Delphine "Dee" Broussard, Southern Miss's insider. Here's what I said:
"Dee, we're 7-2. We're first in the Sun Belt East. We beat Wagner 87-3. Our defense gives up 18.9 points a game. You want a photograph? Frame the scoreboard."
"Camden's fine. And George Pettaway is better than fine. Eight hundred twenty-four yards. Eight touchdowns. He runs like the Blue Ridge, all climb and no quit."
"If Jameer gets home twice. Nakian Jackson gets home three times. Sixty-six tackles, Dee. He's going to be in Lyddy's backfield so often your kids will think he's family."
And the last word from Dee: "One photograph. One. I don't hang a whole wall on one picture."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We get a Thursday night in Hattiesburg at M. M. Roberts Stadium, and Dukes fans who stay through Friday can catch Natasha, Pierre and the Great Comet of 1812 at the Mannoni Performing Arts Center.

My call: James Madison Dukes 36, Southern Miss Golden Eagles 23.
