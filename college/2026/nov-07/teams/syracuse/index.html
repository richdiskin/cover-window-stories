Orange in the Dome: Syracuse Takes On Clemson

By Nico Calabrese · Syracuse Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
That's a foot of snow of a take. Hey, how ya doin', I'm Nico Calabrese, born and raised in the Salt City, survivor of more lake-effect winters than I can count. I travel with the Orange and I live with the Orange, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines, with full upstate bluster. My scale for every play is feet of snow. A two-yard run? A dusting. A long touchdown? Twenty inches overnight, school's canceled. A turnover by us? That's the plow truck sliding into the ditch. When we win, I'll yell "That's a foot of snow of a WIN!" so loud they'll hear me in Utica. When we lose, I shrug: "Just a lake-effect squall." Then I grab a salt potato and get back to work.

The School in 60 Seconds
Syracuse plays in the ACC, and here's a fact I love: it's the only FBS school in New York in a Power Four conference. Home is the JMA Wireless Dome on campus, also known as "The Loud House", opened in 1980 and listed at 49,057 seats. The last five seasons: 5-7, 7-6, 6-7, a big 10-3 in 2024 and 3-9 last year. That 10-3 year? That was the Blizzard of '93, the one we still talk about.

The Coach and the Scheme
Fran Brown is the head coach; CFBD dates his hire to Nov. 28, 2023.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 31.4 points a game while giving up 26.4.

Players to Know
QB Steve Angeli: 98 of 172, 1,187 yards, 7 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Tylik Hill: 515 yards on 98 carries, 1 touchdown.
WR Umari Hatcher: 60 catches, 952 yards, 8 touchdowns.
LB Keyshawn Johnson: 6 sacks, the team lead.
LB Chris D'Appolonia: a team-high 57 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Syracuse is 4-4 overall and 2-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have Syracuse 12th in the ACC.
The season so far: W 66-3 vs New Hampshire Wildcats (Sept. 5), L 18-21 vs California Golden Bears (Sept. 12), L 13-27 at Pittsburgh Panthers (Sept. 17), W 42-41 (OT) at UConn Huskies (Oct. 3), L 25-28 at Virginia Cavaliers (Oct. 10), L 26-34 vs Louisville Cardinals (Oct. 17), W 31-30 (OT) at North Carolina Tar Heels (Oct. 24), W 30-27 vs SMU Mustangs (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Clemson (5-3, 3-2 ACC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at noon ET, at JMA Wireless Dome, Syracuse, New York, on ESPN2.
Last time out. We beat the SMU Mustangs 30-27 at home on Oct. 31. Steve Angeli went 21 of 34 for 200 yards, with 1 touchdown, Ahmad Miller ran 18 times for 56 yards and 1 touchdown and Umari Hatcher caught 6 balls for 65 yards.
Clemson beat the Florida State Seminoles 36-24 on the road on Oct. 31. Tait Reynolds went 18 of 32 for 189 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Tait Reynolds ran 14 times for 85 yards and 1 touchdown; Tyler Brown caught 5 balls for 74 yards.
Players to watch. Clemson quarterback Tait Reynolds is 138 of 212 for 1,285 yards, with 3 touchdowns and 3 interceptions. Gideon Davidson leads their ground game with 528 yards on 105 carries and 5 scores. Bryant Wesco Jr. is their top target: 38 catches for 411 yards. On defense, safety Corey Myrick has 63 tackles and defensive end Will Heldt has 7 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 9 regular-season meetings with Clemson from 2016 through 2025. We won 5 of them, and Clemson won the last one 34-21 in 2025.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Syracuse vs. Clemson. When Syracuse or Clemson puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Dixie-Lynn Pruitt, Clemson's insider. Here's what I said:
"Not the last one, though. We won the last one, 34-21, in 2025. You remember that one? I do. I have the program framed next to my snow shovel."
"Reynolds has 3 touchdown throws all season. Three. Umari Hatcher has 8 touchdown catches by himself. He has 60 catches for 952 yards. Hatcher has caught more touchdowns than your quarterback has thrown, and it is not close."
"We are 4-4 and we score 31.4 a game. You are 5-3 and you score 23.2. If points were snowfall, you would be the city that gets a dusting and closes the schools. We get four feet and go to work."
And the last word from Dixie-Lynn: "I have seen it too. It ends with my flag waving in your end zone."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
The CNY Veterans Parade steps off at noon Saturday at Chevy Court on the State Fairgrounds with more than 90 veterans and military organizations, and astronaut Jeanette Epps speaks at 10 a.m.

My call: Syracuse Orange 26, Clemson Tigers 25.
