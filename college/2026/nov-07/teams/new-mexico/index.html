Lobos in Reno: New Mexico Takes On Nevada Friday Night

By Paloma Begaye-Ortiz · New Mexico Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Red or green? Both. ¡Hola, Albuquerque! I'm Paloma Begaye-Ortiz, and when I'm not at a Lobos game, I'm floating a hot-air balloon over the Rio Grande at sunrise. Every October I fly in the Balloon Fiesta, and every Saturday I read Lobo football like wind currents: where it's drifting, where it's rising, and when it's about to soar. I travel with this New Mexico team and I live with it, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines, with some green-chile spice and a balloon metaphor for every drive. That offense floated like a balloon at dawn, up and up. When we win, I yell, "Red or green? BOTH!" and pump my fist. When we lose, I sigh, "Too much heat today."

The School in 60 Seconds
The New Mexico Lobos compete in the Mountain West. Our colors are cherry and silver. Home is University Stadium in Albuquerque, opened in 1960 and listed at 39,224 seats. The last five years started low: 3-9, 2-10, 4-8, then 5-7 and a big 9-4 in 2025. That's a balloon catching a thermal, y'all. Up and up.

The Coach and the Scheme
Jason Eck is the head coach; CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 16, 2024.

The 8-game numbers: 35.0 points per game scored, 11.9 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Toa Fa'avae: 58 of 101, 891 yards, 6 touchdowns, 7 interceptions.
RB Scottre Humphrey: 540 yards on 111 carries, 11 touchdowns.
TE Cade Keith: 22 catches, 295 yards, 3 touchdowns.
DE Darren Agu: 6 sacks, the team lead.
LB Mercury Swaim: a team-high 50 tackles.

The Conference Picture
New Mexico is 7-1 overall and 4-0 in league play. ESPN's standings have New Mexico 1st in the Mountain West.
The season so far: W 38-7 vs Central Michigan Chippewas (Sept. 5), W 70-7 vs Mercyhurst Lakers (Sept. 12), L 6-14 at Oklahoma Sooners (Sept. 19), W 42-18 at New Mexico State Aggies (Sept. 26), W 61-7 vs UTEP Miners (Oct. 3), W 21-10 at Hawai'i Rainbow Warriors (Oct. 17), W 21-16 vs North Dakota State Bison (Oct. 24), W 21-16 at San José State Spartans (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Nevada (3-5, 2-2 Mountain West) on Friday, Nov. 6, at 9:00 p.m. ET, at Mackay Stadium, Reno, Nevada, on CBSSN. We come in ranked No. 22.
Last time out. We beat the San José State Spartans 21-16 on the road on Oct. 31. Toa Fa'avae went 9 of 14 for 101 yards, with 1 touchdown, Kiefer Sibley ran 12 times for 102 yards and Cade Keith caught 2 balls for 30 yards and 1 touchdown.
Nevada lost to the UCLA Bruins 27-0 on the road on Oct. 31. AJ Bianco went 6 of 13 for 71 yards; Herschel Turner ran 11 times for 28 yards; Marshaun Brown caught 3 balls for 51 yards.
Players to watch. Nevada quarterback Carter Jones is 53 of 89 for 654 yards, with 3 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. Dominic Kelley leads their ground game with 588 yards on 118 carries and 5 scores. Gary Givens III is their top target: 19 catches for 305 yards. On defense, linebacker EJ Smith has 85 tackles and defender Dylan LaBarbera has 4.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 5 regular-season meetings with Nevada from 2016 through 2025. We won 1 of them, and Nevada won the last one 24-22 in 2025.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, New Mexico or Nevada.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Ridge Corbell, Nevada's insider. Here's what I said:
"We're 7-1, Ridge. Number 22 in the country. First in the Mountain West. We are flying so high you need binoculars."
"Mercury Swaim. Even his name is fast."
"Toa doesn't need to throw. We run. We hit. We win. Ask Hawai'i. Ask North Dakota State."
And the last word from Ridge: "Six points at Oklahoma. Your offense went for a balloon ride and forgot to come down."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We get a Friday night in Reno at Mackay Stadium, and the same night Reno Public Market has Margret's Funk Band playing free, so the Lobos fans who travel will have a soundtrack for the ride home.

My call: New Mexico Lobos 28, Nevada Wolf Pack 18.
