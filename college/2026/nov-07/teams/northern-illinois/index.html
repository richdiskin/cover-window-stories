Huskies Head West: Northern Illinois Visits San José

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hello, Huskies fans. I'm Nola Brandvik, your Northern Illinois insider. I studied agricultural science here in DeKalb, and I model the Huskies like a corn crop: planting, growing season, harvest. Nobody understands my measurements, but they trust them, and I appreciate that. I'm calm. Very calm. Illinois calm. When we win, I say, "Knee-high by the Fourth of July," which means a great win. When we lose, I stare at a photo of a cornfield for 30 seconds. It helps. Truly, it does. Try it sometime. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, harvested fresh.

The School in 60 Seconds
Northern Illinois plays at Huskie Stadium in DeKalb, listed at 23,595. This is our first season in the Mountain West as a football-only member. New field, new soil, same Huskies. The recent history is something to be proud of. Under Thomas Hammock, NIU won the 2021 MAC championship, won back-to-back bowl games and beat No. 5 Notre Dame in 2024, one of the biggest wins in school history. Hammock's teams earned three of what NIU calls "Boneyard Wins" over Power 4 opponents. That's the standard we're growing toward.

The Coach and the Scheme
Rob Harley is our interim head coach. He was the defensive coordinator and was promoted when Thomas Hammock resigned on Feb. 18, 2026, to return to the NFL.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 16.2 points a game while giving up 37.6.

Players to Know
QB Taron Dickens: 157 of 247, 1,617 yards, 8 touchdowns, 6 interceptions.
RB Telly Johnson Jr.: 574 yards on 103 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR DeAree Rogers: 64 catches, 633 yards, 5 touchdowns.
DB Avery Jones: 2 sacks, the team lead.
LB Jack Murray: a team-high 58 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Northern Illinois is 0-8 overall and 0-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have Northern Illinois 9th in the Mountain West.
The season so far: L 0-40 at Iowa Hawkeyes (Sept. 5), L 24-28 vs Illinois State Redbirds (Sept. 12), L 17-42 at Arizona Wildcats (Sept. 19), L 14-35 at Georgia State Panthers (Sept. 26), L 7-28 vs Air Force Falcons (Oct. 10), L 23-41 at Wyoming Cowboys (Oct. 17), L 21-56 vs Hawai'i Rainbow Warriors (Oct. 24), L 24-31 at UNLV Rebels (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to San José State (4-5, 2-3 Mountain West) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 4:00 p.m. ET, at CEFCU Stadium, San Jose, California, on MW+.
Last time out. We lost to the UNLV Rebels 31-24 on the road on Oct. 31. Taron Dickens went 19 of 37 for 253 yards, with 2 touchdowns, Elijah Porter ran 21 times for 113 yards and George Dimopoulos caught 7 balls for 127 yards and 1 touchdown.
San José State lost to the New Mexico Lobos 21-16 at home on Oct. 31. Luke Weaver went 16 of 23 for 190 yards; Jabari Bates ran 22 times for 63 yards; Carson Clark caught 8 balls for 91 yards.
Players to watch. San José State quarterback Luke Weaver is 154 of 242 for 1,810 yards, with 11 touchdowns and 8 interceptions. Jabari Bates leads their ground game with 649 yards on 153 carries and 1 score. Carson Clark is their top target: 55 catches for 642 yards. On defense, linebacker Dylan Lee has 78 tackles and defensive lineman Jayland McGlothen has 6 sacks.
The series. From 2016 through 2025, CFBD lists no regular-season games between us and San José State.
Availability. No official availability report for Northern Illinois or San José State was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Tessa Nakagawa-Ruiz, San José State's insider. Here's what I said:
"Out on the farm we'd call that ready for harvest."
And the last word from Tessa: "Some days you push to production and it breaks. Rollback, patch, try again."
