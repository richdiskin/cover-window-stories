Thursday Night in Boca: Florida Atlantic Hosts UTSA

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hey, Owls! I'm Kaia Brightwater, your Florida Atlantic insider. I used to play beach volleyball here in Boca Raton, and now I analyze offenses like swell reports. Choppy means three-and-outs. Clean means long, smooth drives. And a closeout? That's when the whole wave crashes at once, which is how a defense feels when we go tempo. The beachy talk is real, but so is the analysis underneath it, I promise. When we win, it's "a perfect swell day" and a slow-motion hair flip. When we lose, I sigh "flat water" and post a sunset anyway, because it's still Boca. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, sun and stats. "Surf's up on tempo."

The School in 60 Seconds
Florida Atlantic football is young, and it was built by a legend. Howard Schnellenberger founded the program from scratch and served as its first head coach. I love a program that's still writing its story.

The Coach and the Scheme
Zach Kittley was named the Hagerty Family Head Football Coach on Dec. 2, 2024.

Through 8 games this season, Florida Atlantic is scoring 37.8 points per game and allowing 35.8.

Players to Know
QB Caden Veltkamp: 227 of 316, 2,536 yards, 23 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Ethan Ervin: 369 yards on 91 carries, 2 touchdowns.
WR Easton Messer: 74 catches, 898 yards, 9 touchdowns.
LB Joseph Sipp Jr.: 2 sacks, the team lead.
LB Joseph Sipp Jr.: a team-high 57 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Florida Atlantic is 5-3 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Florida Atlantic 5th in the American.
The season so far: L 21-66 at Florida Gators (Sept. 5), W 38-30 vs Navy Midshipmen (Sept. 12), W 16-10 vs Florida International Panthers (Sept. 19), W 45-17 at UL Monroe Warhawks (Sept. 26), W 66-10 vs Texas Southern Tigers (Oct. 3), L 24-56 at Army Black Knights (Oct. 17), W 51-49 vs Rice Owls (Oct. 24), L 41-48 at North Texas Mean Green (Oct. 29).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host UTSA (7-1, 4-0 American) on Thursday, Nov. 5, at 7:30 p.m. ET, at Flagler Credit Union Stadium, Boca Raton, Florida, on ESPN.
Last time out. We lost to the North Texas Mean Green 48-41 on the road on Oct. 29. Caden Veltkamp went 37 of 56 for 452 yards, with 5 touchdowns, Leonard Farrow ran 14 times for 47 yards and Easton Messer caught 16 balls for 189 yards and 3 touchdowns.
UTSA beat the Tulane Green Wave 42-19 on the road on Oct. 24. Owen McCown went 16 of 23 for 173 yards; Will Henderson III ran 15 times for 102 yards and 1 touchdown; David Amador II caught 5 balls for 47 yards.
Players to watch. UTSA quarterback Owen McCown is 169 of 243 for 1,751 yards, with 10 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Will Henderson III leads their ground game with 664 yards on 109 carries and 5 scores. DJ Allen Jr. is their top target: 25 catches for 347 yards. On defense, linebacker Brandon Tucker has 56 tackles and linebacker Owen Pewee has 4 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 4 regular-season meetings with UTSA from 2016 through 2025. We won 2 of them, and we won the last one 38-24 in 2024.
Availability. No official availability report for Florida Atlantic or UTSA was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Marisol Ybarra, UTSA's insider. Here's what I said:
"Thirty-seven point eight a game. That's us. That's Caden Veltkamp. Two thousand five hundred thirty-six yards and 23 touchdown throws, and he's not done."
"Sixteen catches. Sixteen. Easton Messer is a wave that does not stop breaking. Seventy-four catches, 898 yards, nine touchdowns. Find me a Roadrunner who can keep up with that."
"You guys squeak. That's your whole personality. Squeak, squeak, beep beep."
And the last word from Marisol: "Two and two. Tonight breaks the tie."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Thursday, Nov. 5, Boca Raton's Night Market sets up in Sanborn Square from 6 to 9 p.m. with food, local artists, makers and music, and BocaConnect gives free rides.

My call: UTSA Roadrunners 38, Florida Atlantic Owls 32.
