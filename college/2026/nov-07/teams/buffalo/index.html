Bulls in Oxford: Buffalo Takes On Miami Tuesday Night

By Kendra Woolcott · Buffalo Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hey there, Bulls fans! I'm Kendra Woolcott, your Buffalo insider and a Western New York tailgate legend. (I don't jump through tables. I'm a legend for my wing sauce, not for furniture.) I love cold-weather football more than anything, and I start every take with a weather report. When we win, I celebrate in shorts, whatever the temperature. When we lose, I call it "just another lake-effect Saturday" and shovel off the desk. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, plus the forecast. "Bring a parka and a big blitz."

The School in 60 Seconds
Buffalo plays at Broadview Stadium in Amherst, listed at 30,270. In 2024, Pete Lembo's first season, the Bulls went 9-4 and beat Liberty 26-7 in the Bahamas Bowl. That's right: from snowbelt to the Bahamas.

The Coach and the Scheme
Pete Lembo was named head coach on Jan. 21, 2024.

Through 8 games this season, Buffalo is scoring 22.6 points per game and allowing 25.4.

Players to Know
QB Jason Wright: 92 of 185, 987 yards, 7 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Messiah Burch: 845 yards on 151 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Chance Morrow: 41 catches, 480 yards, 6 touchdowns.
Defender Sedrick Vessah: 2 sacks, the team lead.
LB SaVeon Brown: a team-high 67 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Buffalo is 5-3 overall and 3-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have Buffalo 3rd in the MAC.
The season so far: W 21-17 vs UAlbany Great Danes (Sept. 3), L 20-33 at Florida International Panthers (Sept. 12), L 13-55 at Penn State Nittany Lions (Sept. 19), W 31-28 vs Robert Morris Colonials (Sept. 26), L 17-20 vs Western Michigan Broncos (Oct. 3), W 23-8 at Toledo Rockets (Oct. 10), W 25-18 (OT) vs Massachusetts Minutemen (Oct. 17), W 31-24 vs Bowling Green Falcons (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Miami (OH) (5-3, 3-1 MAC) on Tuesday, Nov. 3, at 8:00 p.m. ET, at Yager Stadium, Oxford, Ohio, on ESPN.
Last time out. We beat the Bowling Green Falcons 31-24 at home on Oct. 24. Jason Wright went 13 of 21 for 127 yards, with 1 touchdown, Messiah Burch ran 33 times for 184 yards and 3 touchdowns and Clint Walker caught 2 balls for 40 yards and 1 touchdown.
Miami (OH) beat the Central Michigan Chippewas 28-27 on the road on Oct. 24. Caleb Heavner went 18 of 33 for 221 yards, with 1 touchdown; Caleb Heavner ran 15 times for 58 yards and 1 touchdown; Maleek Huggins caught 7 balls for 122 yards.
Players to watch. Miami (OH) quarterback David McComb is 65 of 103 for 1,121 yards, with 7 touchdowns and 1 interception. Rodney Nelson leads their ground game with 391 yards on 78 carries and 3 scores. Maleek Huggins is their top target: 24 catches for 431 yards. On defense, linebacker Malcolm McCain has 59 tackles and defensive lineman Roosevelt Andrews III has 4.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 9 regular-season meetings with Miami (OH) from 2016 through 2025. We won 2 of them, and we won the last one 37-20 in 2025.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, Buffalo or Miami (OH).
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Cordelia Van Doren, Miami (OH)'s insider. Here's what I said:
"Name one Oxford wing that's worth the drive. I'll wait. Weather report, by the way: whatever the sky does tonight, it's not lake effect, so it's not a problem."
"We win ugly and we like it. Ugly travels."
"Burch is a snowplow. You hear him before you see him, and then the road's clear."
And the last word from Cordelia: "Even legends lost once. and I can tell you which ones."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We get a Tuesday night in Oxford, and walking past the 11 bronze Cradle of Coaches statues at Yager Stadium's south gate is a reminder of how much football history lives in that town.

My call: Miami (OH) RedHawks 29, Buffalo Bulls 22.
