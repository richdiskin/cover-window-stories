Huskers Under the Friday Lights: Nebraska Visits Champaign

By Darla Rasmussen · Nebraska Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Go Big Red! I'm Darla Rasmussen, and I'm your Nebraska insider. I'm an ex-Husker volleyballer and farm girl who sells out every argument. Fair warning: I mention the sellout streak every segment. On the podcast, that segment is called Sellout Report. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate Nebraska players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no Nebraska fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Memorial Stadium in Lincoln, built in 1923 with a listed capacity of 85,458. The band is the Cornhusker Marching Band, and the fight song is "Hail Varsity." The program claims national titles from 1970, 1971, 1994, 1995 and 1997. The last five seasons went 3-9, 4-8, 5-7, 7-6 and 7-6, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about the sellout streak. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Matt Rhule leads the Cornhuskers, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Nov. 26, 2022. This is his fourth season in Lincoln.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 32.4 points a game while giving up 15.6.

Players to Know
QB Anthony Colandrea: 161 of 263, 1,825 yards, 15 touchdowns, 7 interceptions.
RB Jamal Rule: 463 yards on 73 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Kwazi Gilmer: 42 catches, 545 yards, 7 touchdowns.
DL Jahsear Whittington: 4.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Owen Chambliss: a team-high 53 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Nebraska is 5-3 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have Nebraska 11th in the Big Ten.
The season so far: W 49-21 vs Ohio Bobcats (Sept. 5), W 56-7 vs Bowling Green Falcons (Sept. 12), W 34-7 vs North Dakota Fighting Hawks (Sept. 19), W 31-13 at Michigan State Spartans (Sept. 26), W 48-23 vs Maryland Terrapins (Oct. 3), L 17-20 vs Indiana Hoosiers (Oct. 10), L 14-17 at Oregon Ducks (Oct. 17), L 10-17 vs Washington Huskies (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Illinois (5-3, 3-2 Big Ten) on Friday, Nov. 6, at 8:00 p.m. ET, at Gies Memorial Stadium, Champaign, Illinois, on Fox.
Last time out. We lost to the Washington Huskies 17-10 at home on Oct. 31. Anthony Colandrea went 24 of 44 for 255 yards, with 1 touchdown, Jamal Rule ran 13 times for 72 yards and Jacory Barney Jr. caught 6 balls for 65 yards.
Illinois beat the Maryland Terrapins 20-17 on the road on Oct. 31. Katin Houser went 18 of 32 for 223 yards, with 1 touchdown; Javari Barnett ran 6 times for 80 yards and 1 touchdown; Collin Dixon caught 6 balls for 103 yards.
Players to watch. Illinois quarterback Katin Houser is 140 of 216 for 1,663 yards, with 13 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Aidan Laughery leads their ground game with 560 yards on 89 carries and 2 scores. Collin Dixon is their top target: 60 catches for 781 yards. On defense, defensive back Matthew Bailey has 55 tackles and linebacker Ean Rhea has 2 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 9 regular-season meetings with Illinois from 2016 through 2025. We won 6 of them, and we won the last one 31-24 in 2024.
Availability. The public injury data for Illinois and Nebraska is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Margery Vandermolen, Illinois's insider. Here's what I said:
"Your corn is fine. Your defense is the problem. Twenty-four points a game, Margery. Ohio State hung 42 on you."
"Three close ones. We'll take that over getting run over. Our defense gives up 15.6 points a game. Owen Chambliss has 53 tackles. Jahsear Whittington has four and a half sacks. We just need Anthony to stop throwing to your guys."
"Kwazi catches everything. Even the ones Anthony throws to the wrong team."
And the last word from Margery: "A whisker counts. Ask Duke. They beat us 31-27 in September and nobody in Durham remembers the score. They just remember the win."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We walk into Illinois' Dads Weekend, with the Dads Weekend Tailgate at Grange Grove already sold out, so Gies Memorial Stadium will be packed with dads and kids yelling at us.

My call: Nebraska Cornhuskers 27, Illinois Fighting Illini 24.
