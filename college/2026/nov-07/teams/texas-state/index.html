Bobcats in Corvallis: Texas State Makes Its First Trip to Oregon State

By Yesenia Calderwood · Texas State Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Eat 'em up, Cats! Hey, y'all, I'm Yesenia Calderwood, and if you can't find me at a Bobcats game, I'm probably floating down the San Marcos River on an inner tube with a sun hat and a radio. I travel with the Bobcats and I live with the Bobcats, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines, sunny and laid-back but fiercely proud. San Marcos has a clear, cold river and a hot, rising football team. Grab a tube, grab a seat, and enjoy the ride with us. My motto for life and football: go with the flow, then run it up the middle. When we win, I'm floating on air. When we lose, I let the river carry it away and get ready for next week.

The School in 60 Seconds
Texas State football began in 1904 and has an overall winning record. The program has 14 conference titles, nine of them outright. As of 2026, the Bobcats play in the Pac-12. Home games are in San Marcos at UFCU Stadium, built in 1981 and seating 28,388. (Bobcat Stadium is the former name, still used in CFBD data.) Texas State is now the fourth-largest university in Texas. And the recent current is strong: 4-8, 4-8, then 8-5, 8-5 and 7-6. That's a river rising.

The Coach and the Scheme
G.J. Kinne is the head coach; CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 2, 2022. His offense is a flash flood, y'all.

Through 8 games this season, Texas State is scoring 38.1 points per game and allowing 32.0.

Players to Know
QB Brad Jackson: 156 of 232, 1,933 yards, 18 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Torrance Burgess Jr.: 922 yards on 129 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Chris Dawn Jr.: 41 catches, 467 yards, 5 touchdowns.
DL Tymere Jackson: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
DB Jayden Bradley: a team-high 56 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Texas State is 4-4 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Texas State 4th in the Pac-12.
The season so far: L 7-59 at Texas Longhorns (Sept. 5), L 26-31 vs UTSA Roadrunners (Sept. 12), W 49-35 vs North Texas Mean Green (Sept. 19), W 63-10 vs Incarnate Word Cardinals (Sept. 26), L 29-31 at San Diego State Aztecs (Oct. 3), W 66-27 vs Colorado State Rams (Oct. 15), W 20-17 vs Utah State Aggies (Oct. 24), L 45-46 at Boise State Broncos (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Oregon State (5-3, 3-1 Pac-12) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 10:30 p.m. ET, at Reser Stadium, Corvallis, Oregon, on CW.
Last time out. We lost to the Boise State Broncos 46-45 on the road on Oct. 31. Brad Jackson went 23 of 38 for 327 yards, with 4 touchdowns, Brad Jackson ran 9 times for 54 yards and 1 touchdown and Zechariah Sample caught 2 balls for 89 yards and 1 touchdown.
Oregon State lost to the Fresno State Bulldogs 38-22 on the road on Oct. 31. Braden Atkinson went 33 of 59 for 373 yards, with 1 touchdown; Cornell Hatcher Jr. ran 9 times for 53 yards and 1 touchdown; DeCorion Temple caught 6 balls for 97 yards.
Players to watch. Oregon State quarterback Braden Atkinson is 222 of 332 for 2,977 yards, with 25 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. AJ Newberry leads their ground game with 465 yards on 72 carries and 3 scores. Jesse Legree is their top target: 53 catches for 1,110 yards. On defense, linebacker Aiden Sullivan has 60 tackles and linebacker Takari Hickle has 6 sacks.
The series. From 2016 through 2025, CFBD lists no regular-season games between us and Oregon State.
Availability. No official availability report for Texas State or Oregon State was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Juniper Ostrowski, Oregon State's insider. Here's what I said:
"First meeting. We're making history."
"I'm from Texas. I'll be the judge."
"We lost by one at Boise. One point. On the blue turf."
And the last word from Juniper: "Sixty-six. We did it first."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
This is the first-ever meeting between Texas State and Oregon State, and we're flying into Corvallis for a night game at Reser Stadium.

My call: Oregon State Beavers 38, Texas State Bobcats 32.
