Sun Bowl Night: UTEP Hosts Hawai'i for the First Time Since 2004

By Ernesto "Neto" Galindo · UTEP Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
That's Hatch-hot! I'm Ernesto "Neto" Galindo, and I'm your UTEP insider. I'm a Sun Bowl ironworker's kid who grades effort like ore quality, and I measure effort in chile heat. On the podcast, that segment is called Chile Heat. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate UTEP players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no UTEP fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is the Sun Bowl in El Paso, built in 1963 with a listed capacity of 51,500. The fight song is "El Paso Fight," and the band is the Sound of the Southwest. The last five seasons went 7-6, 5-7, 3-9, 3-9 and 2-10, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about Paydirt Pete. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Scotty Walden leads the Miners, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 4, 2023.

Through 8 games this season, UTEP is scoring 14.1 points per game and allowing 36.5.

Players to Know
QB EJ Colson Jr.: 66 of 99, 687 yards, 7 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
QB Raymond Moore III: 481 yards on 70 carries, 3 touchdowns.
RB Kam Thomas: 19 catches, 283 yards, 1 touchdown.
DE Sterling Miles: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Jayden Wilson: a team-high 47 tackles.

The Conference Picture
UTEP is 1-7 overall and 0-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have UTEP 9th in the Mountain West.
The season so far: L 0-51 at Oklahoma Sooners (Sept. 4), W 51-10 vs Texas Southern Tigers (Sept. 12), L 17-52 at Michigan Wolverines (Sept. 19), L 7-33 vs Oregon State Beavers (Sept. 26), L 7-61 at New Mexico Lobos (Oct. 3), L 13-30 vs Nevada Wolf Pack (Oct. 10), L 11-24 vs San José State Spartans (Oct. 17), L 7-31 at North Dakota State Bison (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Hawai'i (2-6, 1-4 Mountain West) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 9:30 p.m. ET, at Sun Bowl, El Paso, Texas, on FS1.
Last time out. We lost to the North Dakota State Bison 31-7 on the road on Oct. 31. EJ Colson Jr. went 11 of 16 for 59 yards, Raymond Moore III ran 3 times for 67 yards and 1 touchdown and Jaden Smith caught 6 balls for 41 yards.
Hawai'i beat the Northern Illinois Huskies 56-21 on the road on Oct. 24. Micah Alejado went 22 of 32 for 323 yards, with 3 touchdowns; DeVon Rice ran 12 times for 71 yards and 3 touchdowns; Audric Harris caught 2 balls for 90 yards.
Players to watch. Hawai'i quarterback Micah Alejado is 202 of 337 for 2,186 yards, with 9 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. DeVon Rice leads their ground game with 385 yards on 82 carries and 6 scores. Kainoa Carvalho is their top target: 35 catches for 399 yards. On defense, linebacker Jamih Otis has 54 tackles and linebacker Wynden Ho'ohuli has 2 sacks.
The series. From 2016 through 2025, CFBD lists no regular-season games between us and Hawai'i.
Availability. No official availability report for UTEP or Hawai'i was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Kainoa Akana-Baird, Hawai'i's insider. Here's what I said:
"I've been waiting for a rematch. Now I get one."
"It's been tough. But we're home."
"Moore is the guy. Put the ball in his hands and let him go."
And the last word from Kainoa: "We lost close games. San José State by four. Stanford by ten. We're better than 2-6."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
UTEP hosts Hawai'i for the first time since Oct. 16, 2004, when the Miners won 51-20, and the Sun Bowl kickoff is 7:30 p.m. MDT.

My call: Hawai'i Rainbow Warriors 29, UTEP Miners 20.
