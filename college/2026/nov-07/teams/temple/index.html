Owls Head to Annapolis: Temple Takes On Navy

By Teddy Fiorella · Temple Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Yo, that's Temple Made! I'm Teddy Fiorella, and I'm your Temple insider. I'm a North Philly gym rat who grades effort like a Rocky montage. Fair warning: I defend Philly against every insult, including ones nobody made. On the podcast, that segment is called Philly Defense. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate Temple players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no Temple fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia, built in 2003 with a listed capacity of 69,879. The fight song is "Fight! Temple Fight!" and the band is the Temple University Diamond Marching Band. The last five seasons went 3-9, 3-9, 3-9, 3-9 and 5-7, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about Hooter the Owl. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
K.C. Keeler leads the Owls, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 1, 2024.

Through 8 games this season, Temple is scoring 23.6 points per game and allowing 26.6.

Players to Know
QB Ajani Sheppard: 85 of 140, 1,245 yards, 8 touchdowns, 3 interceptions.
RB Keveun Mason: 564 yards on 102 carries, 3 touchdowns.
WR Colin Chase: 20 catches, 330 yards, 2 touchdowns.
LB Curly Ordonez: 2 sacks, the team lead.
LB Curly Ordonez: a team-high 77 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Temple is 2-6 overall and 1-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have Temple 10th in the American.
The season so far: W 38-14 vs Rhode Island Rams (Sept. 5), L 9-27 vs Penn State Nittany Lions (Sept. 12), L 48-49 (OT) at Toledo Rockets (Sept. 19), L 17-21 vs Army Black Knights (Sept. 25), W 17-13 at South Florida Bulls (Oct. 3), L 20-34 vs UConn Huskies (Oct. 10), L 31-38 vs Charlotte 49ers (Oct. 17), L 9-17 at East Carolina Pirates (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Navy (2-6, 1-4 American) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:30 p.m. ET, at Navy-Marine Corps Memorial Stadium, Annapolis, Maryland, on CBSSN.
Last time out. We lost to the East Carolina Pirates 17-9 on the road on Oct. 31. Ajani Sheppard went 11 of 20 for 118 yards, Ajani Sheppard ran 15 times for 69 yards and JoJo Bermudez caught 3 balls for 48 yards.
Players to watch. Navy quarterback Jackson Gutierrez is 36 of 71 for 717 yards, with 7 touchdowns and 1 interception. Jackson Gutierrez leads their ground game with 979 yards on 178 carries and 13 scores. Jordan Collins is their top target: 6 catches for 212 yards. On defense, linebacker Coleman Cauley has 63 tackles and linebacker MarcAnthony Parker has 4 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 9 regular-season meetings with Navy from 2016 through 2025. We won 4 of them, and we won the last one 32-31 in 2025.
Availability. No official availability report for Temple or Navy was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Cmdr. (Ret.) Annabel Merriweather, Navy's insider. Here's what I said:
"Nineteen twenty-six. In Philly we were busy inventing everything else."
"Cream of crab soup. Fancy. Where's the cheesesteak?"
"Ordonez is a Philly guy in spirit. He'll hit you, then help you up, then hit you again."
And the last word from Annabel: "A point is a point. Ask anyone who has docked a ship."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Owls fans in Annapolis can try the new cream of crab soup and walking tacos at Navy-Marine Corps Memorial Stadium, where Navy is marking 100 years since its 1926 national championship team.

My call: Navy Midshipmen 30, Temple Owls 23.
