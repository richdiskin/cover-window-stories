Purple Reign: Washington Welcomes Penn State Back After 105 Years

By Evangeline "Vangie" Tollefsrud · Washington Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Bow Down to Washington! I'm Evangeline "Vangie" Tollefsrud, and I'm your Washington insider. I'm a Seattle native, I row crew, and I boat to every home game. On the podcast, my segment is called Sailgate Report. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate Washington players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no Washington fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Husky Stadium in Seattle, built in 1920 with a listed capacity of 70,138. The playing surface is Alaska Airlines Field, the band is the University of Washington Husky Marching Band, and the fight song is "Bow Down to Washington." The program claims national titles from 1960 and 1991. The last five seasons went 4-8, 11-2, 14-1, 6-7 and 9-4, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about sailgating on Lake Washington. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Jedd Fisch leads the Huskies in his third season, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Jan. 14, 2024.

Through 8 games this season, Washington is scoring 26.5 points per game and allowing 21.1.

Players to Know
QB Demond Williams Jr.: 123 of 187, 1,495 yards, 9 touchdowns, 3 interceptions.
RB Brian Bonner Jr.: 309 yards on 68 carries, 1 touchdown.
WR Chris Lawson: 44 catches, 638 yards, 3 touchdowns.
LB Jacob Manu: 2.5 sacks, the team lead.
S Alex McLaughlin: a team-high 56 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Washington is 5-3 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have Washington 11th in the Big Ten.
The season so far: W 24-10 vs Washington State Cougars (Sept. 6), W 16-14 vs Utah State Aggies (Sept. 12), W 48-16 vs Eastern Washington Eagles (Sept. 19), L 24-27 vs Minnesota Golden Gophers (Sept. 26), L 21-25 at USC Trojans (Oct. 3), L 24-41 vs Iowa Hawkeyes (Oct. 9), W 38-26 at Purdue Boilermakers (Oct. 16), W 17-10 at Nebraska Cornhuskers (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Penn State (4-4, 1-4 Big Ten) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:30 p.m. ET, at Husky Stadium, Seattle, Washington, on FS1.
Last time out. We beat the Nebraska Cornhuskers 17-10 on the road on Oct. 31. Elijah Brown went 19 of 30 for 136 yards, Demond Williams Jr. ran 4 times for 28 yards and Chris Lawson caught 12 balls for 155 yards and 1 touchdown.
Penn State beat the Purdue Boilermakers 31-17 at home on Oct. 31. Rocco Becht went 16 of 24 for 175 yards, with 1 touchdown; Cam Wallace ran 22 times for 135 yards and 1 touchdown; Benjamin Brahmer caught 4 balls for 58 yards.
Players to watch. Penn State quarterback Rocco Becht is 148 of 234 for 1,839 yards, with 13 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Carson Hansen leads their ground game with 374 yards on 89 carries and 5 scores. Chase Sowell is their top target: 34 catches for 510 yards. On defense, linebacker Tony Rojas has 57 tackles and linebacker Tony Rojas has 5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has one regular-season meeting with Penn State from 2016 through 2025, and we won the last one 35-6 in 2024.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Washington vs. Penn State. When Washington or Penn State puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Luann Kessler, Penn State's insider. Here's what I said:
"Luann, the blankets are for you. We're polite people. We figured you'd be cold."
"Penn State fans on a boat on my lake. I rowed that lake in the rain for four years. You people get a buffet."
"Lawson against Nebraska was a coxswain calling the stroke. Every catch right on the beat."
And the last word from Luann: "We have both been to the dark place."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Homecoming and the Purple Reign Game, opening with the Blanket Parade as former letterwinners welcome the team, and Penn State is making its first trip to Husky Stadium in 105 years.

My call: Washington Huskies 24, Penn State Nittany Lions 23.
