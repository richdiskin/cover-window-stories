Cards Under the Lights: Louisville Visits the Flats

By Bianca Thornberry · Louisville Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hold your hats! I'm Bianca Thornberry, and I'm your Louisville insider. I'm a Derby-party hostess who knows tempo stats as well as millinery. Fair warning: I rate every game's hat-worthiness. On the podcast, that segment is called Hat-Worthiness. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate Louisville players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no Louisville fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is L&N Federal Credit Union Stadium in Louisville, built in 1998 with a listed capacity of 60,000. The fight song is "Fight! UofL," and the band is the U of L Cardinal Marching Band, the Marching Cards. The last five seasons went 6-7, 8-5, 10-4, 9-4 and 9-4, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about the Cardinal Bird. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Jeff Brohm leads the Cardinals, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 7, 2022.

The 8-game numbers: 40.4 points per game scored, 27.0 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Lincoln Kienholz: 141 of 208, 2,094 yards, 12 touchdowns, 3 interceptions.
RB Isaac Brown: 707 yards on 92 carries, 7 touchdowns.
WR Tre Richardson: 40 catches, 633 yards, 2 touchdowns.
DL Tyler Thompson: 5.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB T.J. Capers: a team-high 57 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Louisville is 5-3 overall and 4-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Louisville 3rd in the ACC.
The season so far: W 59-13 vs Villanova Wildcats (Sept. 11), W 41-31 vs SMU Mustangs (Sept. 19), L 27-30 vs Wake Forest Demon Deacons (Sept. 26), L 28-31 at NC State Wolfpack (Oct. 3), W 44-20 vs Florida State Seminoles (Oct. 9), W 34-26 at Syracuse Orange (Oct. 17), W 52-24 vs Stanford Cardinal (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Georgia Tech (2-6, 1-4 ACC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:30 p.m. ET, at Bobby Dodd Stadium, Atlanta, Georgia, on ACC Network.
Last time out. We beat the Stanford Cardinal 52-24 at home on Oct. 31. Lincoln Kienholz went 20 of 28 for 250 yards, with 2 touchdowns, Isaac Brown ran 9 times for 139 yards and 3 touchdowns and Tre Richardson caught 10 balls for 140 yards.
Georgia Tech lost to the Pittsburgh Panthers 34-11 on the road on Oct. 31. Alberto Mendoza went 16 of 25 for 81 yards; Grady Adamson ran 6 times for 63 yards and 1 touchdown; Gavin Harris caught 3 balls for 41 yards.
Players to watch. Georgia Tech quarterback Alberto Mendoza is 169 of 240 for 1,795 yards, with 10 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. Justice Haynes leads their ground game with 548 yards on 112 carries and 4 scores. Jordan Allen is their top target: 46 catches for 543 yards. On defense, safety Tae Harris has 50 tackles and defensive end Noah Carter has 3 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 3 regular-season meetings with Georgia Tech from 2016 through 2025. We won 1 of them, and Georgia Tech won the last one 31-19 in 2024.
Availability. The public injury data for Georgia Tech and Louisville is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Wendell Quist, Georgia Tech's insider. Here's what I said:
"I love a night game. And I love fireworks. Georgia Tech is throwing a party, and Louisville is bringing the guests."
And the last word from Wendell: "Sixty-six. I think about it often."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We play under the lights at Bobby Dodd Stadium, where Tech is running its Dodd After Dark promotions, so expect a full night crowd on the Flats.

My call: Louisville Cardinals 38, Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets 25.
