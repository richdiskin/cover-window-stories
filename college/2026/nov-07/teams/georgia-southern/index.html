Homecoming at Paulson: Georgia Southern Hosts Marshall

By Royce Ogeechee · Georgia Southern Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hail Southern! I'm Royce Ogeechee, and I'm your Georgia Southern insider. I'm a Bulloch County farm kid who grew up hearing about the Eagles' FCS dynasty years from my granddad. On the podcast, my segment is called Creek Side Chat. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate Georgia Southern players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no Georgia Southern fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Allen E. Paulson Stadium in Statesboro, built in 1984 with a listed capacity of 25,000. The band is the Southern Pride Marching Band. The last five seasons went 3-9, 6-7, 6-7, 8-5 and 7-6, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about Beautiful Eagle Creek at Paulson Stadium. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Clay Helton leads the Eagles, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Nov. 2, 2021.

The 8-game numbers: 23.8 points per game scored, 20.5 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Max Johnson: 189 of 278, 1,924 yards, 11 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB David Mbadinga: 239 yards on 44 carries.
WR Alex Taylor: 34 catches, 435 yards, 3 touchdowns.
LB Anthony Bynum: 5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Brandon Tyson: a team-high 39 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Georgia Southern is 4-4 overall and 3-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have Georgia Southern 1st in the Sun Belt - East.
The season so far: W 31-0 vs Charleston Southern Buccaneers (Sept. 5), L 7-22 at Clemson Tigers (Sept. 12), L 27-31 at Jacksonville State Gamecocks (Sept. 19), L 28-42 vs Houston Cougars (Sept. 26), W 31-24 at Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (Oct. 3), L 24-27 vs James Madison Dukes (Oct. 10), W 23-15 at Old Dominion Monarchs (Oct. 15), W 19-3 vs App State Mountaineers (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Marshall (5-3, 2-2 Sun Belt) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:30 p.m. ET, at Allen E. Paulson Stadium, Statesboro, Georgia, on ESPN+.
Last time out. We beat the App State Mountaineers 19-3 at home on Oct. 31. Max Johnson went 36 of 47 for 327 yards, Weston Bryan ran 7 times for 42 yards and Alex Taylor caught 10 balls for 93 yards.
Marshall beat the Old Dominion Monarchs 36-23 on the road on Oct. 31. Carlos Del Rio-Wilson went 3 of 9 for 66 yards; Judah Price ran 15 times for 82 yards; Chris Culliver caught 2 balls for 67 yards.
Players to watch. Marshall quarterback Carlos Del Rio-Wilson is 93 of 149 for 1,107 yards, with 6 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Judah Price leads their ground game with 319 yards on 56 carries and 2 scores. Xayvion Turner-Bradshaw is their top target: 26 catches for 372 yards. On defense, defensive back Caleb Clark-Glover has 59 tackles and defender Kelvon McBride has 3 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 4 regular-season meetings with Marshall from 2016 through 2025. We won 2 of them, and Marshall won the last one 24-19 in 2025.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, Georgia Southern or Marshall.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Mercy Holderby, Marshall's insider. Here's what I said:
"So does Statesboro. Every single snap."
"Five points, one point, five points. Y'all keep making me nervous."
"Our defense held App to 3. Three. That's a peanut-sized number, and I know peanuts."
And the last word from Mercy: "September. That was September. We are a November team."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Homecoming and Military Appreciation Day, the GS Alumni and Friends Homecoming Tailgate runs from 12 to 3 p.m. at the Bishop Alumni Center, and the True Blue 5K is at M.C. Anderson Park.

My call: Georgia Southern Eagles 31, Marshall Thundering Herd 19.
