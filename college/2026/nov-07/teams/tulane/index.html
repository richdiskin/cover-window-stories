Green Wave at Home: Tulane Hosts Tulsa

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Roll Wave! Hey now, I'm Antoine Ducros-Labat, but all Uptown calls me Twan. I've lived within earshot of a brass band my whole life, and I believe every football game is a brass-band parade: you dance your way through it, no matter what. I travel with the Green Wave and I live with the Green Wave, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines, with a little music and a lot of food. You'll hear me say a big play "deserved a beignet." I mean that literally. I keep a bag of them in the booth. Uptown on game day, there's a brass band on every corner and a Green Wave flag on every porch. When we win, the parade rolls all night. When we lose, we still dance home. Roll Wave, and save me a beignet.

The School in 60 Seconds
The Green Wave represent Tulane University and compete in the American Conference. Home is Yulman Stadium on campus in Uptown New Orleans, opened in 2014 and listed at 30,000 seats. And the music lately has been loud: after 2-10 in 2021, Tulane went 12-2, 11-3, 9-5 and 11-3. That's four straight years of brass band, y'all.

The Coach and the Scheme
Will Hall is the head coach in his first season; CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 8, 2025.

The 8-game numbers: 22.5 points per game scored, 25.4 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Kadin Semonza: 64 of 119, 804 yards, 4 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB Johnnie Daniels: 493 yards on 79 carries, 6 touchdowns.
WR Destyn Hill: 32 catches, 390 yards, 2 touchdowns.
LB Makai Williams: 4 sacks, the team lead.
LB Dickson Agu: a team-high 78 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Tulane is 4-4 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Tulane 5th in the American.
The season so far: L 3-17 at Duke Blue Devils (Sept. 5), W 28-24 vs South Alabama Jaguars (Sept. 12), L 20-31 at Kansas State Wildcats (Sept. 19), W 24-21 vs Southern Miss Golden Eagles (Sept. 26), L 17-20 at Army Black Knights (Oct. 10), W 31-17 vs Memphis Tigers (Oct. 16), L 19-42 vs UTSA Roadrunners (Oct. 24), W 38-31 (OT) at Charlotte 49ers (Oct. 30).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Tulsa (4-4, 1-3 American) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at noon ET, at Yulman Stadium, New Orleans, Louisiana, on ESPNU.
Last time out. We beat the Charlotte 49ers 38-31 (OT) on the road on Oct. 30. Trace Johnson went 28 of 36 for 284 yards, with 1 touchdown, Johnnie Daniels ran 13 times for 99 yards and 2 touchdowns and Zycarl Lewis Jr. caught 3 balls for 55 yards.
Tulsa lost to the Army Black Knights 38-35 at home on Oct. 23. Baylor Hayes went 23 of 34 for 262 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Trequan Jones ran 5 times for 66 yards and 1 touchdown; Jimmy Calloway caught 10 balls for 88 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Tulsa quarterback Baylor Hayes is 122 of 195 for 1,343 yards, with 10 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. Damari Alston leads their ground game with 429 yards on 103 carries and 6 scores. Oran Singleton Jr. is their top target: 43 catches for 535 yards. On defense, linebacker Chris Thompson Jr. has 81 tackles and defensive lineman Kyran Duhon has 6 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 9 regular-season meetings with Tulsa from 2016 through 2025. We won 5 of them, and Tulsa won the last one 31-14 in 2025.
Availability. No official availability report for Tulane or Tulsa was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Wiley Pettibone, Tulsa's insider. Here's what I said:
"He also lost to Army at home. Thirty-eight to thirty-five. And that was the last game you played, two weeks ago. You have been sitting at home since October 23 thinking about it."
"That is the worst compliment I have ever heard a man give his own team."
"And your defense gives up 24.4 a game. You lost at Arkansas 34-6. You lost to North Texas in overtime, 45-44. You lost at Navy. You lost to Army. That is two service academies, Wiley. Your team is very respectful toward the military."
And the last word from Wiley: "I will not need to think. I will need another beignet."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Beignet Fest is Saturday at New Orleans City Park's Festival Grounds, supporting local autism programs, with live music from the Imagination Movers, Amanda Shaw and more.

My call: Tulane Green Wave 25, Tulsa Golden Hurricane 23.
