Buffs in the Desert: Colorado Heads to Tempe

By Moss Feldstein · Colorado Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hey, friends. I'm Moss Feldstein, your Colorado insider. I'm a physicist, a hacky-sack enthusiast and the guy running a quant model of the Buffs from a hammock under the Flatirons. I travel and live with this team, and I bring kombucha on every road trip. It's an acquired taste.

Here's my approach: chill vibes, sharp numbers. Every stat in this report is sourced, and when I compute one myself, I'll tell you. If I don't know something about Colorado, I'll say the data's not in yet. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I'm grateful to God for mountain sunsets and the chance to keep fighting. Ralphie's running.

The School in 60 Seconds
Colorado has played at Folsom Field in Boulder since 1924. CFBD lists 50,183 seats. The Buffs won the 1990 national championship, shared with Georgia Tech. They have two Heisman winners: Rashaan Salaam in 1994 and Travis Hunter in 2024. The all-time record is 735-556-36 through 2025.

Colorado was a charter Big 12 member, left for the Pac-12 after 2010 and returned to the Big 12 in 2024. The last five seasons swing wildly: 4-8, 1-11, 4-8, a radical 9-4 in 2024 and 3-9 in 2025. High variance. Physicists love that. Fans, less so.

The Coach and the Scheme
Deion Sanders, "Coach Prime," leads the program. CFBD records his hire date as Dec. 3, 2022. His résumé is unlike anyone's. He played 14 NFL seasons, won two Super Bowls and played nine Major League Baseball seasons. He's the only athlete to play in both a Super Bowl and a World Series. At Florida State he won the 1988 Jim Thorpe Award.

The 8-game numbers: 21.0 points per game scored, 29.4 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Isaac Wilson: 91 of 149, 1,164 yards, 7 touchdowns, 7 interceptions.
RB JaQuail Smith: 361 yards on 72 carries, 1 touchdown.
WR Joseph Williams: 51 catches, 732 yards, 5 touchdowns.
DL Lamont Lester Jr.: 5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Gideon ESPN Lampron: a team-high 81 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Colorado is 3-5 overall and 1-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have Colorado 14th in the Big 12.
The season so far: W 14-13 at Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets (Sept. 3), W 52-21 vs Weber State Wildcats (Sept. 12), L 7-41 at Northwestern Wildcats (Sept. 19), L 13-23 at Baylor Bears (Sept. 26), L 7-29 vs Texas Tech Red Raiders (Oct. 3), L 24-27 vs Utah Utes (Oct. 17), L 24-57 at Oklahoma State Cowboys (Oct. 24), W 27-24 (OT) vs Kansas State Wildcats (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Arizona State (4-4, 2-3 Big 12) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:30 p.m. ET, at Mountain America Stadium, Tempe, Arizona, on ESPN2.
Last time out. We beat the Kansas State Wildcats 27-24 (OT) at home on Oct. 31. Isaac Wilson went 14 of 21 for 185 yards, with 1 touchdown, Richard Young ran 24 times for 120 yards and 2 touchdowns and Joseph Williams caught 5 balls for 89 yards.
Arizona State lost to the BYU Cougars 27-20 on the road on Oct. 31. Jake Fette went 22 of 42 for 166 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Cardae Mack ran 23 times for 123 yards; Derek Eusebio caught 7 balls for 48 yards.
Players to watch. Arizona State quarterback Cutter Boley is 105 of 173 for 1,360 yards, with 13 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Cardae Mack leads their ground game with 523 yards on 78 carries and 6 scores. Reed Harris is their top target: 48 catches for 804 yards. On defense, linebacker Owen Long has 72 tackles and defensive lineman Jalen Thompson has 4 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 8 regular-season meetings with Arizona State from 2016 through 2025. We won 4 of them, and we won the last one 42-17 in 2025.
Availability. The public injury data for Arizona State and Colorado is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
