Night at Michie: Army Hosts Air Force for the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy

By Capt. (Ret.) Thaddeus Rourke · Army Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Attention on deck. I'm Captain Thaddeus Rourke, retired, your Army insider. I spent my career as a logistics officer, so I read every option play like a supply-chain plan: right item, right place, right time.

Standing orders for this column. Every fact is sourced, like a properly filed report. If I don't know something about Army, I'll log it as "status unknown" and move on. I celebrate these cadets as young leaders first, I respect every opponent, and I thank God daily for the men and women who serve. Discipline is a love letter to your teammates. Copy that, and beat Navy.

The School in 60 Seconds
Army football represents the United States Military Academy and plays at Michie Stadium in West Point, New York. Wikipedia lists capacity at 36,000 and CFBD at 38,000, so I'm flagging that as conflicting. The Black Knights play in the American Conference.

The honors roll is long. Army claims five national championships, including AP titles in 1944 and 1945. The academy has three Heisman winners: Doc Blanchard (1945), Glenn Davis (1946) and Pete Dawkins (1958). It counts 24 players and four coaches in the College Football Hall of Fame and 37 consensus All-Americans.

The last five seasons: 9-4, 6-6, 6-6, a magnificent 12-2 in 2024 and 7-6 in 2025. Mission accomplished more often than not.

The Coach and the Scheme
Jeff Monken has commanded since 2014, and CFBD records his hire date as Dec. 24, 2013. He was head coach at Georgia Southern from 2010 to 2013. Before that he worked under Paul Johnson as running backs coach and special teams coordinator at Georgia Southern, Navy and Georgia Tech. Yes, Navy. We've forgiven him.

Through 8 games this season, Army is scoring 33.6 points per game and allowing 25.5.

Players to Know
QB Cale Hellums: 43 of 76, 462 yards, 4 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
Cale Hellums on the ground: 874 yards on 172 carries, 17 touchdowns.
OL Brady Anderson: 10 catches, 163 yards.
LB Victor Afolabi: 3 sacks, the team lead.
LB Elijah Walton: a team-high 55 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Army is 5-3 overall and 4-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Army 4th in the American.
The season so far: W 59-3 vs Bryant Bulldogs (Sept. 5), L 24-28 vs South Florida Bulls (Sept. 12), W 21-17 at Temple Owls (Sept. 25), L 29-31 at Louisiana Tech Bulldogs (Oct. 3), W 20-17 vs Tulane Green Wave (Oct. 10), W 56-24 vs Florida Atlantic Owls (Oct. 17), W 38-35 at Tulsa Golden Hurricane (Oct. 23), L 22-49 at Memphis Tigers (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Air Force (6-2, 3-2 Mountain West) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:30 p.m. ET, at Michie Stadium, West Point, New York, on CBS.
Last time out. We lost to the Memphis Tigers 49-22 on the road on Oct. 31. Cale Hellums went 4 of 7 for 40 yards, with 1 touchdown, Godspower Nwawuihe ran 19 times for 100 yards and 1 touchdown and Zach Mundell caught 1 balls for 16 yards.
Air Force beat the UConn Huskies 35-26 at home on Oct. 31. Howard Fisher IV went 0 of 2 for 0 yards; Kemper Hodges ran 35 times for 200 yards and 4 touchdowns.
Players to watch. Air Force quarterback Liam Szarka is 19 of 38 for 296 yards, with 1 touchdown and 4 interceptions. Evan Medders leads their ground game with 490 yards on 73 carries and 6 scores. Simon Kibbee is their top target: 8 catches for 135 yards. On defense, linebacker Blake Fletcher has 79 tackles and defensive back Max Mustell has 3 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 7 regular-season meetings with Air Force from 2016 through 2025. We won 4 of them, and Air Force won the last one 20-17 in 2025.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, Army or Air Force.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Lt. Col. (Ret.) Marissa Okonkwo-Hale, Air Force's insider. Here's what I said:
"It's a good story. It ends 24-12."
"You lost 14-28 at Wyoming."
"Memphis was one bad night."
And the last word from Marissa: "The trophy has two academies on it already. We beat the other one."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Army's first night home game against Air Force since 2006, and the first night game ever played at Michie Stadium was against Air Force in 1984, a 24-12 win, with the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy at stake again.

My call: Army Black Knights 31, Air Force Falcons 25.
