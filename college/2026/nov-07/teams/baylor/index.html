Homecoming in Waco: Baylor Welcomes Iowa State

By Maggie Whitlock · Baylor Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hey, y'all! I'm Magnolia Whitlock, Maggie to my friends, and I'm your Baylor insider. I run an interior-design business in Waco, and I renovate roster weaknesses live on air. Shiplap for the secondary, new fixtures for the passing game, open concept for the run fits. I travel and live with this team, and I always pack a tape measure.

Here's my promise. I'm bubbly, but I don't fake the finishes. Every number here has a source, and when I do the math myself, I'll tell you. If I don't know something about Baylor, I'll say the permits haven't come through yet. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I give God thanks for this team and this town. Sic 'em.

The School in 60 Seconds
Baylor plays in the Big 12. After 64 seasons at off-campus Floyd Casey Stadium, the Bears moved into on-campus McLane Stadium in 2014. CFBD lists 45,140 seats. Talk about a glow-up.

The last five seasons have been a whole renovation show: a beautiful 12-2 in 2021, then 6-7, 3-9, 8-5 and 5-7.

The Coach and the Scheme
Baylor parted ways with seventh-year coach Dave Aranda after the Kansas game. CFBD lists his Baylor hire date as Jan. 22, 2020, and he's a defense-first designer. Joe Klanderman, the first-year defensive coordinator, is the interim head coach for the rest of the season. He won four national titles at North Dakota State and led the defense at Kansas State before coming to Waco. New project manager, same blueprints.

Through 8 games this season, Baylor is scoring 30.6 points per game and allowing 15.2.

Players to Know
QB DJ Lagway: 129 of 242, 1,671 yards, 13 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Dawson Pendergrass: 338 yards on 95 carries, 2 touchdowns.
WR Gavin Freeman: 63 catches, 792 yards, 3 touchdowns.
DE Garrick Ponder: 4 sacks, the team lead.
LB Travion Barnes: a team-high 57 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Baylor is 5-3 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Baylor 4th in the Big 12.
The season so far: W 44-3 vs Prairie View A&M Panthers (Sept. 12), W 36-19 vs Louisiana Tech Bulldogs (Sept. 19), W 23-13 vs Colorado Buffaloes (Sept. 26), W 55-19 at Arizona State Sun Devils (Oct. 3), L 6-17 vs TCU Horned Frogs (Oct. 17), L 24-25 at Kansas Jayhawks (Oct. 24), W 41-9 at UCF Knights (Oct. 30).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Iowa State (4-4, 2-3 Big 12) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at noon ET, at McLane Stadium, Waco, Texas.
Last time out. We beat the UCF Knights 41-9 on the road on Oct. 30. DJ Lagway went 16 of 25 for 319 yards, with 4 touchdowns, Ryelan Morris ran 11 times for 82 yards and Gavin Freeman caught 5 balls for 114 yards and 1 touchdown.
Iowa State beat the Oklahoma State Cowboys 51-49 at home on Oct. 31. Jaylen Raynor went 18 of 28 for 309 yards, with 3 touchdowns; Aiden Flora ran 29 times for 221 yards and 2 touchdowns; Jamal Polite Jr. caught 6 balls for 171 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Iowa State quarterback Jaylen Raynor is 111 of 186 for 1,416 yards, with 10 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Aiden Flora leads their ground game with 937 yards on 131 carries and 8 scores. Jamal Polite Jr. is their top target: 14 catches for 286 yards. On defense, linebacker Sullivan Schlimgen has 79 tackles and defensive end Isaac Terrell has 3 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 9 regular-season meetings with Iowa State from 2016 through 2025. We won 5 of them, and we won the last one 43-21 in 2024.
Availability. No official availability report for Baylor or Iowa State was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Twyla Hemmingsen, Iowa State's insider. Here's what I said:
"You also allowed 49 points. In my business we call that a house with a beautiful front door and no back wall. And the week before that, you went to Arizona and scored 3 points. Three. I have seen more points on a throw pillow."
"Nobody in Waco has had to tackle anybody. Our three losses came by one point to Auburn, 17-16, by eleven to TCU and by one at Kansas, 25-24. We have not been blown out once. You lost to Arizona by thirty-six."
"You can have the wallpaper. I am remodeling. Lagway has 1,671 yards, 13 touchdowns, only 4 interceptions. He is playing like a man who has finally found the right furniture for the room. Freeman is the accent piece that ties everything together."
And the last word from Twyla: "Fine. If we lose, I will take the farmers market bag home and frame it."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Baylor Homecoming at McLane Stadium, and the Waco Downtown Farmers Market sets up Saturday at 200 E. Bridge Street for anybody looking to start the day downtown.

My call: Baylor Bears 31, Iowa State Cyclones 21.
