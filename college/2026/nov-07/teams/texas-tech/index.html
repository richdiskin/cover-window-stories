Celebrate America in Lubbock: Texas Tech Hosts West Virginia

By Bodie Ratliff · Texas Tech Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Howdy. Bodie Ratliff. Texas Tech insider. I ride bareback at the rodeo, and in tumbleweed season I chart Air Raid concepts on the back of feed-store receipts. I travel with the team. I live with the team. People say I don't talk much. They're right. One word usually does it, and the wind fills in the rest. Somebody asks how the defense looked? "Mean." How was the trip? "Windy." When we win, I say "Guns up," real quiet, and then I grin longer than anybody's comfortable with. When we lose, I stare into the wind with my hat in my hand. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup from inside the program, West Texas plain. My motto: "Guns up, wind's up."

The School in 60 Seconds
Last season was the best in school history. Texas Tech went 12-2, crushed BYU 34-7 to win the Big 12 championship and made its first College Football Playoff. That was the program's first outright conference title since 1955. The run ended with a 23-0 loss to Oregon in the Orange Bowl. Home is Jones AT&T Stadium in Lubbock, listed at 60,454. Long story short. "Good."

The Coach and the Scheme
Joey McGuire was hired on Nov. 8, 2021.

The 8-game numbers: 35.6 points per game scored, 21.1 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Will Hammond: 139 of 205, 1,561 yards, 6 touchdowns, 6 interceptions.
RB J'Koby Williams: 794 yards on 100 carries, 6 touchdowns.
WR Kenny Johnson: 35 catches, 548 yards, 2 touchdowns.
DL A.J. Holmes Jr.: 7.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Austin Romaine: a team-high 66 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Texas Tech is 7-1 overall and 4-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have Texas Tech 2nd in the Big 12.
The season so far: W 33-10 vs Abilene Christian Wildcats (Sept. 5), W 35-24 at Oregon State Beavers (Sept. 12), W 28-26 vs Houston Cougars (Sept. 18), W 49-14 vs Sam Houston Bearkats (Sept. 26), W 29-7 at Colorado Buffaloes (Oct. 3), W 53-25 vs Arizona State Sun Devils (Oct. 17), L 24-37 at Cincinnati Bearcats (Oct. 24), W 34-26 vs Arizona Wildcats (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host West Virginia (5-3, 2-3 Big 12) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 9:30 p.m. ET, at Galaxy Stadium, Lubbock, Texas, on ESPN. We come in ranked No. 13.
Last time out. We beat the Arizona Wildcats 34-26 at home on Oct. 31. Thomas Castellanos went 11 of 24 for 144 yards, with 2 touchdowns, J'Koby Williams ran 14 times for 175 yards and 1 touchdown and Kenny Johnson caught 4 balls for 49 yards and 1 touchdown.
West Virginia beat the TCU Horned Frogs 24-20 on the road on Oct. 24. Michael Hawkins Jr. went 7 of 15 for 79 yards, with 1 touchdown; Cam Cook ran 18 times for 90 yards; Prince Strachan caught 2 balls for 24 yards.
Players to watch. West Virginia quarterback Michael Hawkins Jr. is 90 of 159 for 1,598 yards, with 11 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. Cam Cook leads their ground game with 923 yards on 186 carries and 7 scores. DJ Epps is their top target: 22 catches for 556 yards. On defense, linebacker Isaiah Patterson has 48 tackles and defender David Afogho has 2 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 10 regular-season meetings with West Virginia from 2016 through 2025. We won 5 of them, and West Virginia won the last one 49-0 in 2025.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, Texas Tech or West Virginia.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Jolene Buckhannon, West Virginia's insider. Here's what I said:
"You lost 31-52 to Arizona at home."
"Holmes is going to live in your backfield."
And the last word from Jolene: "I've moved on. That was a different team."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's the Celebrate America Game, with every branch of the military recognized, and Raider Alley, free and open to the public near the Engineering Key, has live music, food and the Goin' Band from Raiderland.

My call: Texas Tech Red Raiders 41, West Virginia Mountaineers 24.
