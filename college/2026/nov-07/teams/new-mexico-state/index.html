Homecoming in Las Cruces: New Mexico State Hosts Liberty

By Esperanza Lujan-Tafoya · New Mexico State Insider · Week 10

Meet Your Insider
¡Buenas noches, Aggies! I'm Esperanza Lujan-Tafoya, your New Mexico State insider. I'm a Las Cruces native, a chile farmer's daughter and an ag-science grad. That means I can tell you the soil pH of the practice field and how much Scoville heat a fourth-down stop packs. (A lot. A fourth-down stop is Christmas-style, red and green.)

Everything gets a chile grade from me. A routine first down? Mild. A 40-yard run? Hatch hot. But I don't heat up the facts. Every number I give you is real and sourced. Every opponent gets respect. If I don't know something about New Mexico State, I'll say so, and then I'll go find out. In the Mesilla Valley, fall smells like roasting chile and fresh-cut grass. Our Aggies play with that same heat, and the Organ Mountains watch every snap.

The School in 60 Seconds
New Mexico State plays FBS football in Conference USA. The road here was winding. The Aggies were independent in 2013, a football-only Sun Belt member from 2014 to 2017, and independent again from 2018. They announced in November 2021 that they would join Conference USA in all sports starting in 2023. Home is Aggie Memorial Stadium in Las Cruces, which CFBD lists as built in 1978 with a capacity of 28,853.

The recent highs are real, though. The Aggies went 10-5 in 2023. The last five seasons: 2-10, 7-6, 10-5, 3-9 and 4-8.

The Coach and the Scheme
Tony Sanchez is the head coach. CFBD lists his New Mexico State hire date as Dec. 23, 2023. He's an NMSU alum and a former Aggie wide receiver. He was head coach at UNLV from 2014 to 2019, and before that at Bishop Gorman High School in Las Vegas.

The 8-game numbers: 21.5 points per game scored, 25.2 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Trey Hedden: 65 of 124, 906 yards, 5 touchdowns, 6 interceptions.
RB James Jones: 889 yards on 139 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR TK King: 23 catches, 307 yards, 3 touchdowns.
DE Gabe Peterson: 5.5 sacks, the team lead.

The Conference Picture
New Mexico State is 3-5 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have New Mexico State 3rd in the CUSA.
The season so far: L 17-34 at Florida State Seminoles (Aug. 29), W 51-14 vs Mercyhurst Lakers (Sept. 5), L 19-29 at Hawai'i Rainbow Warriors (Sept. 12), L 18-42 vs New Mexico Lobos (Sept. 26), W 34-13 vs Western Kentucky Hilltoppers (Oct. 1), L 3-22 at Florida International Panthers (Oct. 7), W 30-24 at Sam Houston Bearkats (Oct. 21), L 0-24 vs Jacksonville State Gamecocks (Oct. 28).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Liberty (7-1, 4-0 Conference USA) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 8:00 p.m. ET, at Aggie Memorial Stadium, Las Cruces, New Mexico, on ESPNU.
Last time out. We lost to the Jacksonville State Gamecocks 24-0 at home on Oct. 28. Adam Damante went 6 of 19 for 29 yards, James Jones ran 20 times for 112 yards and Brodie Malone-Bradford caught 3 balls for 19 yards.
Liberty beat the Florida International Panthers 49-10 at home on Oct. 31. Deshawn Purdie went 16 of 19 for 227 yards, with 1 touchdown; Kanye Udoh ran 5 times for 31 yards and 1 touchdown; Makai Jackson caught 6 balls for 86 yards.
Players to watch. Liberty quarterback Deshawn Purdie is 96 of 152 for 1,453 yards, with 10 touchdowns and 1 interception. Kam Davis leads their ground game with 603 yards on 87 carries and 7 scores. Makai Jackson is their top target: 29 catches for 626 yards. On defense, linebacker Derrell Farrar has 43 tackles and defensive back Brenton Williams has 4 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 9 regular-season meetings with Liberty from 2016 through 2025. We won 6 of them, and we won the last one 30-27 in 2025.
Availability. No official availability report for New Mexico State or Liberty was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
