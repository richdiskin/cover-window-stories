By Abner Kettleworth · Massachusetts Insider · Week 10

Meet Your Insider
Greetings from the Pioneer Valley, friends. I'm Abner Kettleworth, your Massachusetts insider. I was an organic farmer in the Five College area until I started grinding game tape, and now I travel and live with this team. I still bring kale chips on the bus. Nobody eats them.

Fair warning: I make a Revolutionary War pun for every scheme. One if by run, two if by air. I'm also a stickler for the truth, which seems fitting for a Minuteman. Every number I give you carries a source. If I don't know something about Massachusetts, I'll tell you I'm still waiting on Paul Revere's report. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I thank God for every chilly autumn Friday. Shot heard 'round the film room.

The School in 60 Seconds
UMass began playing football in 1879. The Minutemen reached three FCS national championship games and won the title in 1998. They've played home games at Warren McGuirk Alumni Stadium since 1965, and CFBD lists its capacity at 17,000. UMass began moving up to FBS in 2011 and was an associate MAC member until leaving after the 2015 season. After years as an independent, the school announced in February 2024 that it would rejoin the MAC. CFBD lists UMass as a MAC member this season.

The last five seasons were a long winter at Valley Forge: 1-11, 1-11, 3-9, 2-10 and 0-12 in 2025.

The Coach and the Scheme
Joe Harasymiak leads the regiment. CFBD records his hire date as Dec. 4, 2024. In December 2015 he became the youngest head coach in Division I football when Maine hired him. He went 20-15 there in three seasons, and his 2018 team won the CAA and reached the FCS semifinals. He later was co-defensive coordinator and safeties coach at Minnesota, then defensive coordinator at Rutgers from 2022 to 2024.

Through 8 games this season, Massachusetts is scoring 26.5 points per game and allowing 21.1.

The Conference Picture
Massachusetts is 4-4 overall and 1-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have Massachusetts 10th in the MAC.
The season so far: W 37-21 at Rutgers Scarlet Knights (Sept. 3), W 37-10 vs Sacred Heart Pioneers (Sept. 12), W 36-14 vs Stonehill Skyhawks (Sept. 19), W 35-6 at Sacramento State Hornets (Sept. 26), L 14-38 vs Eastern Michigan Eagles (Oct. 3), L 19-20 vs Miami (OH) RedHawks (Oct. 10), L 18-25 (OT) at Buffalo Bulls (Oct. 17), L 16-35 at UConn Huskies (Oct. 23).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Ball State (3-5, 2-2 MAC) on Wednesday, Nov. 4, at 7:00 p.m. ET, at Warren McGuirk Alumni Stadium, Amherst, Massachusetts, on ESPNU.
Last time out. We lost to the UConn Huskies 35-16 on the road on Oct. 23. William Watson III went 22 of 38 for 205 yards, with 1 touchdown, Elijah Faulkner ran 6 times for 15 yards and Joseph Griffin Jr. caught 4 balls for 77 yards.
Ball State beat the Sacramento State Hornets 34-17 at home on Oct. 24. Keldric Luster went 14 of 27 for 234 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Jalen Bonds ran 17 times for 77 yards; Isaiah Thacker caught 1 balls for 65 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Ball State quarterback Keldric Luster is 133 of 211 for 1,300 yards, with 9 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. TJ Horton leads their ground game with 425 yards on 68 carries and 9 scores. Donovan Hamilton is their top target: 25 catches for 310 yards. On defense, linebacker Kaleb Stewart has 37 tackles and linebacker Kaleb Stewart has 2.5 sacks.
The series. We did not meet Ball State in the regular season from 2016 through 2025, per CFBD, so there is no recent history to go on.
Availability. No official availability report for Massachusetts or Ball State was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
