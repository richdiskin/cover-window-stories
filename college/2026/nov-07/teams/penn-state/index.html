Lions on Lake Washington: Penn State Sails Into Seattle

By Luann Kessler · Penn State Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
We Are... correct! I'm Luann Kessler, and I'm your Penn State insider. I'm an ex-Penn State volleyball setter who loves Creamery ice cream and linebackers equally. Fair warning: I rank every defense by ice cream flavor. On the podcast, that segment is called Creamery Rankings. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate Penn State players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no Penn State fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Beaver Stadium in University Park, built in 1960 with a listed capacity of 106,572. The playing surface is West Shore Home Field, the band is the Penn State Blue Band, and the fight song is "Fight On, State." The program claims national titles from 1982 and 1986. The last five seasons went 7-6, 11-2, 10-3, 13-3 and 7-6, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about the White Out. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Matt Campbell leads the Nittany Lions in his first season, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 8, 2025. He brought quarterback Rocco Becht with him from Iowa State.

The 8-game numbers: 29.0 points per game scored, 18.6 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Rocco Becht: 148 of 234, 1,839 yards, 13 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Carson Hansen: 374 yards on 89 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Chase Sowell: 34 catches, 510 yards, 3 touchdowns.
LB Tony Rojas: 5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Tony Rojas: a team-high 57 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Penn State is 4-4 overall and 1-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have Penn State 15th in the Big Ten.
The season so far: W 45-0 vs Marshall Thundering Herd (Sept. 5), W 27-9 at Temple Owls (Sept. 12), W 55-13 vs Buffalo Bulls (Sept. 19), L 20-24 vs Wisconsin Badgers (Sept. 26), L 13-34 at Northwestern Wildcats (Oct. 2), L 23-31 vs USC Trojans (Oct. 10), L 18-21 at Michigan Wolverines (Oct. 17), W 31-17 vs Purdue Boilermakers (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Washington (5-3, 2-3 Big Ten) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:30 p.m. ET, at Husky Stadium, Seattle, Washington, on FS1.
Last time out. We beat the Purdue Boilermakers 31-17 at home on Oct. 31. Rocco Becht went 16 of 24 for 175 yards, with 1 touchdown, Cam Wallace ran 22 times for 135 yards and 1 touchdown and Benjamin Brahmer caught 4 balls for 58 yards.
Washington beat the Nebraska Cornhuskers 17-10 on the road on Oct. 31. Elijah Brown went 19 of 30 for 136 yards; Demond Williams Jr. ran 4 times for 28 yards; Chris Lawson caught 12 balls for 155 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Washington quarterback Demond Williams Jr. is 123 of 187 for 1,495 yards, with 9 touchdowns and 3 interceptions. Brian Bonner Jr. leads their ground game with 309 yards on 68 carries and 1 score. Chris Lawson is their top target: 44 catches for 638 yards. On defense, safety Alex McLaughlin has 56 tackles and linebacker Jacob Manu has 2.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has one regular-season meeting with Washington from 2016 through 2025, and Washington won the last one 35-6 in 2024.
Availability. The public injury data for Washington and Penn State is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Evangeline "Vangie" Tollefsrud, Washington's insider. Here's what I said:
"We get a buffet and a trumpet section. Try to keep up."
"Seventeen to ten. Very rhythmic. Very low."
And the last word from Vangie: "That was in State College. Rain doesn't count when it's yours."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Penn State hasn't played at Husky Stadium since the final game of the 1921 season, and Nittany Lions fans on the sold-out Sailgate will cruise Lake Washington aboard the Spirit of Seattle before kickoff.

My call: Washington Huskies 24, Penn State Nittany Lions 23.
