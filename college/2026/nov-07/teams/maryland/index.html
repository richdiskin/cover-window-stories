Terps Crash Homecoming: Maryland Visits Purdue

By Abigail "Abby" Chesapeake · Maryland Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Crack it open: Old Bay on that stat! I'm Abigail "Abby" Chesapeake, and I'm your Maryland insider. I'm a D.C.-suburb lobbyist type who networks with every coordinator and cracks crabs on air. Fair warning: Old Bay is my universal answer to every problem. On the podcast, my segment is called Old Bay Index, and I season each stat to taste. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate Maryland players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no Maryland fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is SECU Stadium in College Park, built in 1950 with a listed capacity of 51,802. The band is the Mighty Sound of Maryland. The program claims a national title from 1953. The last five seasons went 7-6, 8-5, 8-5, 4-8 and 4-8, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about Testudo. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Maryland is under interim leadership. Mike Locksley is no longer the head coach, and co-defensive coordinator Aazaar Abdul-Rahim is the interim head coach. Abdul-Rahim is a Washington, D.C., native in his second stint with the Terps. We thank Coach Locksley for every Saturday he gave this program.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 26.9 points a game while giving up 31.6.

Players to Know
QB Malik Washington: 174 of 290, 1,805 yards, 9 touchdowns, 7 interceptions.
RB DeJuan Williams: 338 yards on 57 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Chris Durr Jr.: 58 catches, 638 yards, 4 touchdowns.
LB Zion Elee: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Daniel Wingate: a team-high 48 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Maryland is 2-6 overall and 0-5 in league play. ESPN's standings have Maryland 17th in the Big Ten.
The season so far: W 62-0 vs Hampton Pirates (Sept. 5), W 38-14 at UConn Huskies (Sept. 12), L 26-35 vs Virginia Tech Hokies (Sept. 19), L 3-54 vs UCLA Bruins (Sept. 26), L 23-48 at Nebraska Cornhuskers (Oct. 3), L 16-45 at Ohio State Buckeyes (Oct. 10), L 30-37 vs Rutgers Scarlet Knights (Oct. 17), L 17-20 vs Illinois Fighting Illini (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Purdue (3-5, 2-3 Big Ten) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 12:30 p.m. ET, at Ross-Ade Stadium, West Lafayette, Indiana, on BTN.
Last time out. We lost to the Illinois Fighting Illini 20-17 at home on Oct. 31. Malik Washington went 19 of 29 for 190 yards, DeJuan Williams ran 19 times for 57 yards and 1 touchdown and Chris Durr Jr. caught 6 balls for 58 yards.
Purdue lost to the Penn State Nittany Lions 31-17 on the road on Oct. 31. Ryan Browne went 10 of 29 for 142 yards, with 1 touchdown; Fame Ijeboi ran 11 times for 20 yards; Asaad Waseem caught 3 balls for 64 yards.
Players to watch. Purdue quarterback Ryan Browne is 155 of 250 for 1,964 yards, with 11 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Fame Ijeboi leads their ground game with 588 yards on 141 carries and 9 scores. Asaad Waseem is their top target: 41 catches for 652 yards. On defense, linebacker Jojo Hayden has 59 tackles and defensive lineman Curt Neal has 2.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 3 regular-season meetings with Purdue from 2016 through 2025. We won 1 of them, and we won the last one 31-29 in 2022.
Availability. The public injury data for Purdue and Maryland is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Barnaby Wolcott-Reese, Purdue's insider. Here's what I said:
"I am going to try it. Whether I love it depends on whether anybody brought a crab."
"That explains a lot about Indiana."
"He went 10 of 29 at Penn State last week."
And the last word from Barnaby: "I will not. Here is my prediction. Browne rebounds, Ijeboi scores twice, the defense holds just long enough, and Homecoming ends with a win. It is simple mechanics. Force applied, result achieved."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We walk into Purdue's Homecoming, with tailgates on Memorial Mall before the 12:30 kickoff, and a Maryland win would quiet the whole party.

My call: Purdue Boilermakers 35, Maryland Terrapins 26.
