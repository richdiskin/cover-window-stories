Flames in the Desert: Liberty Takes On New Mexico State

By Gideon Fairweather · Liberty Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Well, praise the Lord and hand me the program! I'm Gideon Fairweather, your Liberty insider. I grew up in Lynchburg, and I'm a youth pastor who coaches middle-school football on the side. That means I've given a locker-room speech about perseverance to eleven 12-year-olds who were mostly worried about Gatorade flavors. I'm ready for anything.

You'll hear a big laugh from me, and a lot of encouragement. When I look at a roster, I see a hundred-some young men who are each somebody's son, and I'll write about them that way. I'll also tell you the truth. Every number here is sourced. If we play poorly, I'll say so gently. If I don't know something about Liberty, I'll admit it. Lynchburg on game day feels like a family reunion with a marching band. Our guys play hard, play fair, and you can see the joy on their faces.

The School in 60 Seconds
Liberty University is a private Christian university in Lynchburg, Virginia. Its football program announced the move from FCS to FBS in July 2017, became a provisional FBS member in 2018, and became a full member with bowl eligibility in 2019. In 2020 the Flames entered the AP poll for the first time, at No. 25. They joined Conference USA for the 2023 season.

Home is Williams Stadium, which CFBD lists as built in 1989 with a capacity of 25,000. The last five seasons: 8-5, 8-5, a magnificent 13-1 in 2023, 8-4, and then a humbling 4-8 in 2025.

The Coach and the Scheme
Jamey Chadwell has led Liberty since the 2023 season. CFBD lists his Liberty hire date as Dec. 4, 2022. Before Lynchburg, he was head coach at North Greenville (2009-11), Delta State (2012), Charleston Southern (2013-16) and Coastal Carolina, first as interim in 2017 and then from 2019 to 2022. He played quarterback at East Tennessee State.

The 8-game numbers: 33.8 points per game scored, 13.5 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Deshawn Purdie: 96 of 152, 1,453 yards, 10 touchdowns, 1 interception.
RB Kam Davis: 603 yards on 87 carries, 7 touchdowns.
WR Makai Jackson: 29 catches, 626 yards, 5 touchdowns.
DB Brenton Williams: 4 sacks, the team lead.
LB Derrell Farrar: a team-high 43 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Liberty is 7-1 overall and 4-0 in league play. ESPN's standings have Liberty 1st in the CUSA.
The season so far: L 13-20 at James Madison Dukes (Sept. 5), W 24-23 vs Gardner-Webb Runnin' Bulldogs (Sept. 12), W 51-15 vs Ball State Cardinals (Sept. 19), W 34-17 at Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (Sept. 24), W 30-14 at Delaware Blue Hens (Oct. 2), W 35-3 vs Sam Houston Bearkats (Oct. 8), W 34-6 at Kennesaw State Owls (Oct. 22), W 49-10 vs Florida International Panthers (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to New Mexico State (3-5, 2-2 Conference USA) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 8:00 p.m. ET, at Aggie Memorial Stadium, Las Cruces, New Mexico, on ESPNU.
Last time out. We beat the Florida International Panthers 49-10 at home on Oct. 31. Deshawn Purdie went 16 of 19 for 227 yards, with 1 touchdown, Kanye Udoh ran 5 times for 31 yards and 1 touchdown and Makai Jackson caught 6 balls for 86 yards.
New Mexico State lost to the Jacksonville State Gamecocks 24-0 at home on Oct. 28. Adam Damante went 6 of 19 for 29 yards; James Jones ran 20 times for 112 yards; Brodie Malone-Bradford caught 3 balls for 19 yards.
Players to watch. New Mexico State quarterback Trey Hedden is 65 of 124 for 906 yards, with 5 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. James Jones leads their ground game with 889 yards on 139 carries and 4 scores. TK King is their top target: 23 catches for 307 yards. On defense, safety Nick Session has 58 tackles and defensive end Gabe Peterson has 5.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 9 regular-season meetings with New Mexico State from 2016 through 2025. We won 3 of them, and New Mexico State won the last one 30-27 in 2025.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Liberty vs. New Mexico State. When Liberty or New Mexico State puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Esperanza Lujan-Tafoya, New Mexico State's insider. Here's what I said:
"Homecoming. Who scheduled the Flames for Homecoming?"
"Two of nine. You got two."
And the last word from Esperanza: "James Jones ran for 112. Look at that instead."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We visit Las Cruces for New Mexico State's Homecoming, and we've won four straight in the series, including 49-35 in the 2023 CUSA Championship.

My call: Liberty Flames 31, New Mexico State Aggies 16.
