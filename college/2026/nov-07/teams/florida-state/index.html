Noles Head North: Florida State Returns to Chestnut Hill

By Gianna Ravello · Florida State Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Motion to reconsider: denied! I'm Gianna Ravello, and I'm your Florida State insider. I'm a Capitol lobbyist type who networks recruits and reads coverage like legislation. Fair warning: I turn every take into a bill on the floor. On the podcast, that segment is called Floor Debate. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate Florida State players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no Florida State fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Doak Campbell Stadium in Tallahassee, built in 1950 with a listed capacity of 67,277. The playing surface is Bobby Bowden Field, and the band is the Marching Chiefs. The program claims national titles from 1993, 1999 and 2013. The last five seasons went 5-7, 10-3, 13-1, 2-10 and 5-7, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about Osceola and Renegade. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Florida State fired Mike Norvell on Sunday, Nov. 1, the day after the 36-24 home loss to Clemson. Rick Stockstill, who was head coach at Middle Tennessee for 18 seasons, is the interim head coach, and offensive coordinator Tim Harris Jr. calls the plays. Norvell went 41-39 here, and his best team went 13-0 and won the ACC in 2023. We thank Coach Norvell for every Saturday he gave this program.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 30.6 points a game while giving up 28.9.

Players to Know
QB Ashton Daniels: 127 of 214, 1,968 yards, 14 touchdowns, 11 interceptions.
RB Ousmane Kromah: 609 yards on 114 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Duce Robinson: 24 catches, 438 yards, 4 touchdowns.
DL Rylan Kennedy: 6 sacks, the team lead.
LB Chris Jones: a team-high 56 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Florida State is 3-5 overall and 1-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have Florida State 16th in the ACC.
The season so far: W 34-17 vs New Mexico State Aggies (Aug. 29), L 24-27 vs SMU Mustangs (Sept. 7), L 36-50 at Alabama Crimson Tide (Sept. 19), W 34-7 vs Central Arkansas Bears (Sept. 26), W 38-7 vs Virginia Cavaliers (Oct. 3), L 20-44 at Louisville Cardinals (Oct. 9), L 35-43 (OT) at Miami Hurricanes (Oct. 17), L 24-36 vs Clemson Tigers (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Boston College (4-4, 2-3 ACC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at noon ET, at Alumni Stadium (Chestnut Hill, MA), Chestnut Hill, Massachusetts, on ACC Network.
Last time out. We lost to the Clemson Tigers 36-24 at home on Oct. 31. Ashton Daniels went 21 of 33 for 350 yards, with 2 touchdowns, Quintrevion Wisner ran 13 times for 43 yards and Micahi Danzy caught 4 balls for 108 yards and 1 touchdown.
Boston College lost to the Duke Blue Devils 47-2 on the road on Oct. 31. Mason McKenzie went 9 of 17 for 87 yards; Bo MacCormack III ran 9 times for 24 yards; Jackson Wade caught 1 balls for 36 yards.
Players to watch. Boston College quarterback Mason McKenzie is 116 of 202 for 1,450 yards, with 8 touchdowns and 7 interceptions. Mason McKenzie leads their ground game with 638 yards on 132 carries and 5 scores. Javarius Green is their top target: 28 catches for 308 yards. On defense, linebacker Anthony Palano has 69 tackles and defensive end Demetrius Ballard has 4.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 8 regular-season meetings with Boston College from 2016 through 2025. We won 4 of them, and we won the last one 28-13 in 2024.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Florida State vs. Boston College. When Florida State or Boston College puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Siobhan Dunleavy, Boston College's insider. Here's what I said:
"And you are 4-4 and last week you scored 2 points at Duke. Two. That is a safety, not a football game. I have seen more scoring at a church bake sale."
"Respectfully, if one man is your quarterback and your leading rusher, your team is a one-person debate club. Ashton Daniels has 1,968 throwing yards and 14 touchdowns. Ousmane Kromah has 609 rushing yards. Duce Robinson has 438 receiving. We have a whole cast."
"He also threw for 350 yards against Clemson. Micahi Danzy caught 4 balls for 108. We moved the ball all day. We just gave it away a few times. That is fixable. Two points is not fixable."
And the last word from Siobhan: "I accept the chowder. I reject the premise."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We head to Chestnut Hill for the first time since 2023, on BC's Veterans and Military Appreciation Day, and the Noles are 9-3 all-time at Alumni Stadium.

My call: Florida State Seminoles 31, Boston College Eagles 23.
