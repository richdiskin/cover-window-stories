The School in 60 Seconds
Kennesaw State football began play in 2015 as an FCS member of the Big South Conference. In 2022 the Owls moved to the ASUN's new FCS football league, and after that season they began the transition to FBS ahead of joining Conference USA in 2024. That's an entire program history in about a decade. Speedrun.

Home is Fifth Third Stadium, just outside the Kennesaw city limits. The game feed lists it as Walens Family Field at Fifth Third Stadium, and CFBD lists it as built in 2010 with a capacity of 11,040. The last five seasons: 11-2, 5-6, 3-6, 2-10, and then a big jump to 10-4 in 2025. Last year the Owls finished the regular season 9-3 and 7-1 in Conference USA.

The Coach and the Scheme
Jerry Mack is the head coach. CFBD lists his Kennesaw State hire date as Dec. 1, 2024, so this is his second season. He was head coach at North Carolina Central from 2014 to 2017, going 31-15, and was later offensive coordinator and associate head coach at Rice. He played wide receiver at Jackson State and Arkansas State.

Through 8 games this season, Kennesaw State is scoring 23.0 points per game and allowing 25.4.

The Conference Picture
Kennesaw State is 2-6 overall and 1-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have Kennesaw State 8th in the CUSA.
The season so far: W 47-0 vs West Georgia Wolves (Sept. 3), L 17-31 vs Georgia State Panthers (Sept. 12), L 9-42 at Tennessee Volunteers (Sept. 19), L 14-17 at Arkansas State Red Wolves (Sept. 26), L 26-27 vs Jacksonville State Gamecocks (Oct. 7), L 21-24 at Missouri State Bears (Oct. 14), L 6-34 vs Liberty Flames (Oct. 22), W 44-28 at Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders (Oct. 28).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Delaware (4-4, 2-2 Conference USA) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:00 p.m. ET, at Walens Family Field at Fifth Third Stadium, Kennesaw, Georgia, on ESPN+.
Last time out. We beat the Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders 44-28 on the road on Oct. 28. Rickie Collins went 16 of 19 for 206 yards, with 3 touchdowns, Boogie Woods III ran 12 times for 67 yards and 2 touchdowns and Dylan Gary caught 2 balls for 59 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Delaware beat the Western Kentucky Hilltoppers 35-21 on the road on Oct. 27. Nick Minicucci went 32 of 38 for 414 yards, with 4 touchdowns; Jo Silver ran 13 times for 49 yards; Sean Wilson caught 9 balls for 183 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Players to watch. Delaware quarterback Nick Minicucci is 111 of 170 for 1,477 yards, with 14 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Jo Silver leads their ground game with 377 yards on 83 carries and 2 scores. Sean Wilson is their top target: 40 catches for 591 yards. On defense, linebacker Gavin Moul has 53 tackles and linebacker Blake Matthews has 4.5 sacks.
The series. We did not meet Delaware in the regular season from 2016 through 2025, per CFBD, so there is no recent history to go on.
Availability. The public injury data for Delaware and Kennesaw State is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
