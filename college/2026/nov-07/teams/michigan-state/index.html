Green and White Under the Lights: Michigan State Storms the Big House

By Shay Kowalczyk · Michigan State Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hey, Spartans. I'm Shay Kowalczyk, your Michigan State insider. I caught softball in the East Lansing area for years, and I still frame every defensive call like a pitch sequence. Fastball inside, change-up away, and then you get 'em. I travel and live with this team. I also maintain that Ann Arbor is just a suburb with a stadium. That's not a stat. That's a lifestyle.

Here's my deal. I'm blunt and honest, and every number in this report has a source. If I don't know something about Michigan State, I'll say I'm still waiting on the sign from the dugout. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I give thanks to God for every chance to compete. Green and white, all day.

The School in 60 Seconds
Michigan State claims six national championships, including two from the major wire services in 1952 and 1965. The Spartans have won eleven conference titles, nine of them in the Big Ten. Home is Spartan Stadium on the main campus, regularly among the top 25 in NCAA attendance. CFBD lists it at 75,005 seats.

The last five seasons: a great 11-2 in 2021, then 5-7, 4-8, 5-7 and 4-8. We're in a slump at the plate. Every great hitter has one. What matters is the next at-bat.

The Coach and the Scheme
Pat Fitzgerald is in his first season as head coach. CFBD lists his Michigan State hire date as Dec. 1, 2025. He was head coach at Northwestern from 2006 to 2023. He took that job at 31, the youngest head coach in the Big Ten and FBS at the time. As a Northwestern linebacker, he won the Bronko Nagurski Trophy and Chuck Bednarik Award twice each. He's in the College Football Hall of Fame.

Through 8 games this season, Michigan State is scoring 19.9 points per game and allowing 25.8.

Players to Know
QB Alessio Milivojevic: 121 of 185, 1,263 yards, 7 touchdowns, 3 interceptions.
RB Jaziun Patterson: 327 yards on 76 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Chrishon McCray: 29 catches, 289 yards, 1 touchdown.
DL Eli Coenen: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
S Devin Vaught: a team-high 52 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Michigan State is 2-6 overall and 0-5 in league play. ESPN's standings have Michigan State 17th in the Big Ten.
The season so far: W 30-20 vs Toledo Rockets (Sept. 4), W 35-7 vs Eastern Michigan Eagles (Sept. 12), L 10-27 at Notre Dame Fighting Irish (Sept. 19), L 13-31 vs Nebraska Cornhuskers (Sept. 26), L 3-31 at Wisconsin Badgers (Oct. 3), L 37-38 (OT) vs Illinois Fighting Illini (Oct. 10), L 21-24 vs Northwestern Wildcats (Oct. 17), L 10-28 at UCLA Bruins (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Michigan (5-3, 2-3 Big Ten) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:30 p.m. ET, at Michigan Stadium, Ann Arbor, Michigan, on Peacock.
Last time out. We lost to the UCLA Bruins 28-10 on the road on Oct. 24. Leo Hannan went 9 of 15 for 67 yards, Kenneth Williams ran 17 times for 54 yards and Chrishon McCray caught 3 balls for 42 yards.
Michigan beat the Rutgers Scarlet Knights 28-21 on the road on Oct. 31. Tommy Carr went 22 of 27 for 266 yards, with 1 touchdown; Savion Hiter ran 20 times for 76 yards; JJ Buchanan caught 5 balls for 63 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Michigan quarterback Bryce Underwood is 72 of 124 for 924 yards, with 5 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. Jordan Marshall leads their ground game with 319 yards on 86 carries and 5 scores. JJ Buchanan is their top target: 38 catches for 611 yards. On defense, linebacker Troy Bowles has 57 tackles and defensive end John Henry Daley has 4 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 10 regular-season meetings with Michigan from 2016 through 2025. We won 6 of them, and we won the last one 31-20 in 2025.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, Michigan State or Michigan.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Gus Hartwell, Michigan's insider. Here's what I said:
"Blue Out. So a hundred thousand people dressed like a bruise."
"We are not a 2-6 team. We are a team that lost close games to people who got lucky."
"We don't talk about Madison."
And the last word from Gus: "Every few years the green gets lucky."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
This is the fifth consecutive night game in the series, Michigan is calling it a Blue Out, and the place is listed as sold out, so we're walking into the loudest building we'll see all year.

My call: Michigan Wolverines 26, Michigan State Spartans 15.
