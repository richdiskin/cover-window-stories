Rockets Out West: Toledo Visits Sacramento State for the First Time

By Rocco Szczepanski · Toledo Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
How ya doin', Rocket fans! I'm Rocco Szczepanski, your Toledo insider. That's Szczepanski. It's silent... nah, it's all pronounced, just fast. I come from a Jeep-plant family here in the Glass City, so I grade offenses the only way I know how: in horsepower. A sputtering three-and-out is a lawnmower engine. A long, physical drive is a big V8 rumbling down Front Street. When we win, I rev an imaginary engine and do a burnout gesture right there on set. When we lose, I say "engine trouble" and pop the hood on the depth chart. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, Northwest Ohio style. "That's 400 horsepower of run game."

The School in 60 Seconds
Toledo plays at the Glass Bowl, listed at 26,038, a great old stadium in a city built on hard work. The Rockets won the MAC championship in 2022.

The Coach and the Scheme
Mike Jacobs came from Mercer to replace Jason Candle and was named head coach on Dec. 10, 2025.

The 8-game numbers: 37.4 points per game scored, 25.4 allowed.

Players to Know
QB John Alan Richter: 173 of 259, 2,244 yards, 17 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB CJ Miller: 609 yards on 109 carries, 7 touchdowns.
WR Adjatay Dabbs: 40 catches, 592 yards, 7 touchdowns.
DE Andrew Zock: 8 sacks, the team lead.
LB Julian Fox: a team-high 65 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Toledo is 6-2 overall and 3-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have Toledo 3rd in the MAC.
The season so far: L 20-30 at Michigan State Spartans (Sept. 4), W 63-10 vs Central Connecticut Blue Devils (Sept. 12), W 49-48 (OT) vs Temple Owls (Sept. 19), W 41-16 vs San Diego State Aztecs (Sept. 26), W 39-24 at Ball State Cardinals (Oct. 3), L 8-23 vs Buffalo Bulls (Oct. 10), W 34-14 at Eastern Michigan Eagles (Oct. 17), W 45-38 vs Western Michigan Broncos (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Sacramento State (2-7, 1-5 MAC) on Wednesday, Nov. 4, at 7:00 p.m. ET, at Hornet Stadium, Sacramento, California, on CBSSN.
Last time out. We beat the Western Michigan Broncos 45-38 at home on Oct. 24. John Alan Richter went 29 of 41 for 391 yards, with 3 touchdowns, CJ Miller ran 33 times for 183 yards and 1 touchdown and Adjatay Dabbs caught 8 balls for 124 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Sacramento State beat the Kent State Golden Flashes 31-10 at home on Oct. 30. Jackson Sharman went 14 of 25 for 117 yards; Isaiah Ene ran 19 times for 107 yards and 2 touchdowns; Onterrio Smith Jr. caught 2 balls for 40 yards.
Players to watch. Sacramento State quarterback Jackson Sharman is 136 of 242 for 1,363 yards, with 8 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. Isaiah Ene leads their ground game with 410 yards on 77 carries and 3 scores. Matt Coleman is their top target: 41 catches for 492 yards. On defense, safety Boogsie Silvera has 71 tackles and defensive lineman Xavier Williams has 1.5 sacks.
The series. From 2016 through 2025, CFBD lists no regular-season games between us and Sacramento State.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, Toledo or Sacramento State.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Mateo Villaseñor, Sacramento State's insider. Here's what I said:
"Mateo, I grew up three streets from the Jeep plant. I grade offenses in horsepower. And the Toledo offense right now? That's four hundred horses and a racing stripe. You're cooking on a camp stove."
"Then there's Andrew Zock. Eight sacks. That's not a defensive end, that's a tow truck. He shows up and your quarterback goes for a ride."
And the last word from Mateo: "Seniors don't get tired on Senior Day."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
This is the first-ever meeting with Sacramento State, it's their Senior Day and last home game, and they'll be honoring 27 seniors, so we're expecting a fired-up crowd.

My call: Toledo Rockets 38, Sacramento State Hornets 18.
