Bears in Miami: Missouri State Crashes FIU's Homecoming

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Howdy from the Ozarks! I'm Clementine Bilyeu, your Missouri State insider. I'm a Springfield native and a float-trip guide, and I've been maroon since the day I was born. Mama swears my first word was "Bears." It was probably "boat," but I've chosen my story and I'm sticking with it.

I take a practical view of football, the same way I take a river. Read the current, respect the rocks, and keep everybody in the canoe. Missouri State is on a brand-new ride as an FBS program, and I'm thrilled to be your guide. I'll give you real numbers, honest takes, and kind words for every opponent. If I don't know something about Missouri State, I'll tell you, and then I'll paddle off and find out. Springfield is the heart of the Ozarks, and our Bears are taking the whole region along. Pack a snack. It's going to be a fun float.

The School in 60 Seconds
Missouri State University is in Springfield, Missouri, and its first football team took the field in 1909. Until 2005, the school was known as Southwest Missouri State. The Bears were a founding member of the Gateway Football Conference in 1985, which later became the Missouri Valley Football Conference.

Home is Plaster Sports Complex, which CFBD lists with a capacity of 17,337 and a 1941 construction year. The last five seasons: 8-4, 5-6, 4-7, 8-4 and 7-6.

The Coach and the Scheme
Casey Woods is in his first season as head coach. CFBD lists his Missouri State hire date as Dec. 19, 2025. He was previously offensive coordinator at SMU and played wide receiver at Tennessee.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 24.4 points a game while giving up 30.9.

Players to Know
QB Henry Belin IV: 85 of 126, 1,071 yards, 8 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Ramone Green Jr.: 272 yards on 67 carries, 2 touchdowns.
WR Isaiah Myers: 41 catches, 873 yards, 8 touchdowns.
DE Josh Lukusa: 2.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Jared Lloyd: a team-high 86 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Missouri State is 3-5 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Missouri State 3rd in the CUSA.
The season so far: L 0-50 at Texas A&M Aggies (Sept. 5), W 48-14 vs Lindenwood Lions (Sept. 12), L 24-30 vs Marshall Thundering Herd (Sept. 19), L 24-34 at SMU Mustangs (Sept. 26), L 13-34 at Western Kentucky Hilltoppers (Oct. 8), W 24-21 vs Kennesaw State Owls (Oct. 14), L 38-61 at Delaware Blue Hens (Oct. 20), W 24-3 vs Sam Houston Bearkats (Oct. 27).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Florida International (3-5, 1-3 Conference USA) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 4:00 p.m. ET, at Pitbull Stadium, Miami, Florida, on ESPN+.
Last time out. We beat the Sam Houston Bearkats 24-3 at home on Oct. 27. Henry Belin IV went 12 of 18 for 184 yards, with 2 touchdowns, Connor Lair ran 19 times for 75 yards and 1 touchdown and Jmariyae Robinson caught 7 balls for 102 yards and 1 touchdown.
Florida International lost to the Liberty Flames 49-10 on the road on Oct. 31. JJ Kohl went 21 of 28 for 164 yards, with 1 touchdown; Devonte Lyons ran 10 times for 52 yards; Kyle McNeal caught 6 balls for 56 yards.
Players to watch. Florida International quarterback JJ Kohl is 152 of 255 for 1,801 yards, with 11 touchdowns and 9 interceptions. Devonte Lyons leads their ground game with 435 yards on 94 carries and 1 score. Maguire Anderson is their top target: 41 catches for 596 yards. On defense, defensive back DeAndre Boykins has 45 tackles and defensive lineman DeeJay Holmes Jr. has 5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has one regular-season meeting with Florida International from 2016 through 2025, and Florida International won the last one 28-21 in 2025.
Availability. No official availability report for Missouri State or Florida International was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
