Senior Day at Hornet Stadium: Sacramento State Gets Its First Taste of MACtion

By Mateo Villaseñor · Sacramento State Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
¡Hola, Hornets! I'm Mateo Villaseñor, your Sacramento State insider. I'm a farm-to-fork chef in the capital city, and before that I played linebacker for the Hornets, so I explain defenses the only way I know: like recipes. A good defense is a good sauce. Balance, heat and nobody leaving early. I narrate every play like a cooking competition. When we win, it's "Plate it up, chef!" and a fist pump. When we lose, I say, "Back to the kitchen," and sharpen a knife. On paper. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, served fresh.

The School in 60 Seconds
Sacramento State joined the MAC as a football-only member on July 1, 2026, for a five-year term, moving up from FCS to FBS. Last year, in our final Big Sky season, we went 7-5 and 5-3 in the league. This is a brand-new menu. The Hornets took the spot left by Northern Illinois, which moved to the Mountain West. The rest of our sports play in the Big West. Home is Hornet Stadium in Sacramento, listed at 21,195. New dish, new kitchen, same hungry Hornets.

The Coach and the Scheme
Alonzo Carter was hired on Dec. 15, 2025, after Brennan Marion left for Colorado. Carter is a first-time head coach who spent eight seasons on Brent Brennan's staffs at San Jose State and Arizona, and he's a Northern California native who started out as a high school coach in the state.

The 9-game numbers: 17.0 points per game scored, 30.3 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Jackson Sharman: 136 of 242, 1,363 yards, 8 touchdowns, 6 interceptions.
RB Isaiah Ene: 410 yards on 77 carries, 3 touchdowns.
WR Matt Coleman: 41 catches, 492 yards, 5 touchdowns.
DL Xavier Williams: 1.5 sacks, the team lead.
S Boogsie Silvera: a team-high 71 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Sacramento State is 2-7 overall and 1-5 in league play. ESPN's standings have Sacramento State 12th in the MAC.
The season so far: L 17-28 at Eastern Michigan Eagles (Aug. 29), W 52-0 vs Mississippi Valley State Delta Devils (Sept. 5), L 3-49 at Fresno State Bulldogs (Sept. 12), L 10-31 vs North Dakota State Bison (Sept. 19), L 6-35 vs Massachusetts Minutemen (Sept. 26), L 10-38 at Bowling Green Falcons (Oct. 10), L 7-48 vs Ohio Bobcats (Oct. 17), L 17-34 at Ball State Cardinals (Oct. 24), W 31-10 vs Kent State Golden Flashes (Oct. 30).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Toledo (6-2, 3-1 MAC) on Wednesday, Nov. 4, at 7:00 p.m. ET, at Hornet Stadium, Sacramento, California, on CBSSN.
Last time out. We beat the Kent State Golden Flashes 31-10 at home on Oct. 30. Jackson Sharman went 14 of 25 for 117 yards, Isaiah Ene ran 19 times for 107 yards and 2 touchdowns and Onterrio Smith Jr. caught 2 balls for 40 yards.
Toledo beat the Western Michigan Broncos 45-38 at home on Oct. 24. John Alan Richter went 29 of 41 for 391 yards, with 3 touchdowns; CJ Miller ran 33 times for 183 yards and 1 touchdown; Adjatay Dabbs caught 8 balls for 124 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Players to watch. Toledo quarterback John Alan Richter is 173 of 259 for 2,244 yards, with 17 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. CJ Miller leads their ground game with 609 yards on 109 carries and 7 scores. Adjatay Dabbs is their top target: 40 catches for 592 yards. On defense, linebacker Julian Fox has 65 tackles and defensive end Andrew Zock has 8 sacks.
The series. CFBD shows no regular-season meetings with Toledo from 2016 through 2025, so this is a fresh fight for our guys.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Sacramento State vs. Toledo. When Sacramento State or Toledo puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Rocco Szczepanski, Toledo's insider. Here's what I said:
"You hear that, Rocco? Fireworks. My mother is coming. My uncle is coming. The guy who sells me tomatoes is coming. Nobody is leaving this kitchen hungry."
"A camp stove that just roasted Kent State 31-10. Five turnovers, Rocco. Five. Fata Puloka forced two fumbles. Miles Bailey jumped on two more. We held the ball for nearly 35 minutes. That is slow food. That is a braise."
"Silvera is my secret ingredient. You don't see him, you just taste him."
And the last word from Rocco: "And here's the thing about the schedule. We last played October 24. Rested. Tuned. You played Friday night and turn around five days later. I'm not saying that's unfair. I'm saying our engine has been sitting in the garage cooling."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's the last game at Hornet Stadium this season, Senior Day for 27 players, 916 Community and Native American Heritage Day, with post-game fireworks and our first taste of MACtion.

My call: Toledo Rockets 38, Sacramento State Hornets 18.
