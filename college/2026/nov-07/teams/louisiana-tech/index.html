Bulldogs in Troy: Louisiana Tech Crashes Homecoming

By Booker "Champ" Tullos · Louisiana Tech Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Well, that's just peachy. Howdy, Bulldog fans, I'm Booker Tullos, but folks around Ruston call me Champ. I grew up on a peach farm off the highway, played fullback for Tech, and to this day nothing in football makes me happier than a fourth-and-inches plunge into the end zone. I travel with this Louisiana Tech team and I live with it, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines in an easygoing north-Louisiana drawl, with peach puns baked right in like a cobbler. When we win, I holler "Peachy!" and do a fullback dive across the studio (the carpet has suffered). When we lose, I sigh "bruised peach," and then I go back and watch every snap. Sit a spell. And remember what my granddaddy said about peaches and football: the good ones take a whole season to ripen.

The School in 60 Seconds
Louisiana Tech first played in 1901 and has an all-time record of 654-508-37. The Bulldogs have won three Division II national championships and 25 conference titles, and played in 30 postseason games, 15 of them major college bowls. Since 1968, home has been Joe Aillet Stadium in Ruston, listed at 28,019 seats. After 13 seasons in Conference USA, Tech joined the Sun Belt on July 1, 2026, so this is our first fall in a new orchard. The recent climb: 3-9 three years running from 2021 to 2023, 5-8 in 2024 and 8-5 in 2025. That's a tree that's starting to bear fruit.

The Coach and the Scheme
Sonny Cumbie is the head coach, hired Nov. 30, 2021.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 32.6 points a game while giving up 20.9.

The season so far: W 80-6 vs Northwestern State Demons (Sept. 5), L 14-45 at LSU Tigers (Sept. 12), L 19-36 at Baylor Bears (Sept. 19), W 31-29 vs Army Black Knights (Oct. 3), W 32-31 vs Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns (Oct. 10), W 24-0 at UL Monroe Warhawks (Oct. 17), W 26-0 vs Old Dominion Monarchs (Oct. 24), W 35-20 at South Alabama Jaguars (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Troy (4-4, 2-2 Sun Belt) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 4:00 p.m. ET, at Veterans Memorial Stadium (AL), Troy, Alabama, on ESPN+.
Last time out. We beat the South Alabama Jaguars 35-20 on the road on Oct. 31. Blake Baker went 10 of 16 for 167 yards, with 3 touchdowns, Rayshun Hurt ran 6 times for 40 yards and Marcus Calwise Jr. caught 3 balls for 68 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Troy beat the James Madison Dukes 24-21 (OT) on the road on Oct. 29. Tucker Kilcrease went 15 of 31 for 179 yards; Jaheim Merriweather ran 13 times for 84 yards and 1 touchdown; Noreel White caught 3 balls for 64 yards.
Players to watch. Troy quarterback Goose Crowder is 102 of 183 for 1,162 yards, with 9 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Gavin Griffin leads their ground game with 436 yards on 98 carries and 3 scores. TK Keyes is their top target: 13 catches for 282 yards. On defense, linebacker Keno Jones has 65 tackles and defensive back Donnie Smith has 7 sacks.
The series. From 2016 through 2025, CFBD lists no regular-season games between us and Troy.
Availability. The public injury data for Troy and Louisiana Tech is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.

"Jackson is a wrecking ball. I'd have loved to block for a guy like that. Or better, not block against him."
