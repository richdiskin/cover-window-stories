Teal Homecoming: Coastal Carolina Hosts Old Dominion

By Marigold "Goldie" Seabrook · Coastal Carolina Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Teal deal, y'all! I'm Marigold "Goldie" Seabrook, and I'm your Coastal Carolina insider. I grew up in Conway in a shrimp-boat family, and I'm an English major who writes like the tide. On the podcast, my segment is called Low Tide Notes. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate Coastal Carolina players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no Coastal Carolina fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Brooks Stadium in Conway, built in 2003 with a listed capacity of 21,000. The playing surface is James C. Benton Field. The last five seasons went 11-2, 9-4, 8-5, 6-7 and 6-7, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about the teal turf. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Ryan Beard leads the Chanticleers, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 11, 2025.

Through 8 games this season, Coastal Carolina is scoring 26.8 points per game and allowing 25.1.

Players to Know
QB Deuce Bailey: 131 of 236, 1,935 yards, 19 touchdowns, 6 interceptions.
RB Dominic Lee-Knicely: 484 yards on 92 carries, 3 touchdowns.
WR Clayton Coppock Jr.: 20 catches, 350 yards, 5 touchdowns.
DE Ibrahim Diawara: 5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Marcellius Pulliam: a team-high 74 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Coastal Carolina is 3-5 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Coastal Carolina 3rd in the Sun Belt - East.
The season so far: L 24-31 at West Virginia Mountaineers (Sept. 5), W 45-7 vs Fordham Rams (Sept. 12), L 14-22 at Delaware Blue Hens (Sept. 19), L 17-34 vs Liberty Flames (Sept. 24), L 24-31 vs Georgia Southern Eagles (Oct. 3), W 27-21 at Marshall Thundering Herd (Oct. 10), L 32-35 vs App State Mountaineers (Oct. 16), W 31-20 at Georgia State Panthers (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Old Dominion (2-7, 1-5 Sun Belt) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 1:00 p.m. ET, at Brooks Stadium (SC), Conway, South Carolina, on ESPN+.
Last time out. We beat the Georgia State Panthers 31-20 on the road on Oct. 31. Deuce Bailey went 22 of 32 for 297 yards, with 3 touchdowns, Dominic Lee-Knicely ran 12 times for 32 yards and Clayton Coppock Jr. caught 6 balls for 100 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Old Dominion lost to the Marshall Thundering Herd 36-23 at home on Oct. 31. Jaxon Potter went 23 of 44 for 329 yards; Trey Hall ran 8 times for 41 yards; Josh Rodriguez caught 9 balls for 143 yards.
Players to watch. Old Dominion quarterback Quinn Henicle is 54 of 98 for 656 yards, with 4 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Devin Roche leads their ground game with 395 yards on 109 carries. Josh Rodriguez is their top target: 30 catches for 383 yards. On defense, safety Lucas Struck has 70 tackles and defensive lineman Javen Gibson has 5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 4 regular-season meetings with Old Dominion from 2016 through 2025. We won 2 of them, and we won the last one 47-7 in 2025.
Availability. No official availability report for Coastal Carolina or Old Dominion was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Homecoming Week, with the parade on Marina Drive on Friday, the Alumni TEALgate at Brooks Stadium from 10 a.m. to noon Saturday, and the SC UFO Festival in Conway the same day.

My call: Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 27, Old Dominion Monarchs 19.
