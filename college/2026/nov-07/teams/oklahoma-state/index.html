Pokes in the Little Apple: Oklahoma State Visits Manhattan

By Dusty Lockridge · Oklahoma State Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Well, howdy, Cowboy fans. I'm Dusty Lockridge, your Oklahoma State insider. I'm an ag-econ grad, I wear orange from my hat to my boots, and I travel and live with this team. I explain offense with rodeo timing. A quick slant is a two-second ride. A long touchdown drive is the full eight seconds, with a good score from the judges.

Here's my word as a cowboy. Every number I give you has a source. When I do math myself, I'll tell you. If I don't know something about Oklahoma State, I'll say so. No bull, so to speak. I celebrate our players as young men first, I respect every opponent, and I thank the good Lord for fall Saturdays in Stillwater. Ride 'em, Pokes.

The School in 60 Seconds
Oklahoma State football started in 1901 and plays at Boone Pickens Stadium in Stillwater. CFBD lists 55,509 seats. The Cowboys claim the 1945 national championship, awarded by the AFCA, plus eleven conference titles. They've played in 34 bowl games and gone 22-12, including five New Year's Six wins. Running back Barry Sanders won the Heisman in 1988. The program counts 66 All-Americans.

The last five seasons have been a bucking bronco: 12-2, 7-6, 10-4, then 3-9 and 1-11.

The Coach and the Scheme
Eric Morris is our first-year head coach. Wikipedia says he was introduced on Dec. 8, 2025, while CFBD lists his Oklahoma State hire date as Nov. 25, 2025. I'll flag that small difference. Before Stillwater he was head coach at Incarnate Word and North Texas, and offensive coordinator at Washington State.

The 8-game numbers: 41.1 points per game scored, 27.1 allowed.

The Conference Picture
Oklahoma State is 5-3 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Oklahoma State 4th in the Big 12.
The season so far: L 10-24 at Tulsa Golden Hurricane (Sept. 5), W 39-31 vs Oregon Ducks (Sept. 12), W 59-0 vs Murray State Racers (Sept. 19), W 41-24 at West Virginia Mountaineers (Sept. 26), W 42-28 vs UCF Knights (Oct. 10), L 32-35 at Houston Cougars (Oct. 17), W 57-24 vs Colorado Buffaloes (Oct. 24), L 49-51 at Iowa State Cyclones (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Kansas State (4-4, 1-4 Big 12) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 1:00 p.m. ET, at Bill Snyder Family Stadium, Manhattan, Kansas, on TNT, HBO Max.
Last time out. We lost to the Iowa State Cyclones 51-49 on the road on Oct. 31. Drew Mestemaker went 27 of 42 for 323 yards, with 5 touchdowns, Caleb Hawkins ran 25 times for 194 yards and 2 touchdowns and Justin Bowick caught 7 balls for 124 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Kansas State lost to the Colorado Buffaloes 27-24 (OT) on the road on Oct. 31. Blake Barnett went 18 of 28 for 224 yards, with 3 touchdowns; Rodney Fields Jr. ran 12 times for 65 yards; Josh Manning caught 6 balls for 73 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Kansas State quarterback Avery Johnson is 118 of 187 for 1,301 yards, with 10 touchdowns and 3 interceptions. Rodney Fields Jr. leads their ground game with 487 yards on 93 carries and 5 scores. Jaron Tibbs is their top target: 39 catches for 379 yards. On defense, safety Logan Bartley has 54 tackles and defensive end Jayden Bryant has 4 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 10 regular-season meetings with Kansas State from 2016 through 2025. We won 4 of them, and we won the last one 14-6 in 2025.
Availability. The public injury data for Kansas State and Oklahoma State is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Hattie Brandvold, Kansas State's insider. Here's what I said:
