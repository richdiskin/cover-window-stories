Faurot Field Fires Up: Missouri Hosts the Longhorns

By Delphine Arceneaux-Moss · Missouri Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Show me the tape! I'm Delphine Arceneaux-Moss, and I'm your Missouri insider. I'm a J-school style beat reporter who breaks news and blitz tendencies. Fair warning: I "break news" on obvious things, live, with a fake news ticker sound. On the podcast, that segment is called Breaking News. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate Missouri players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no Missouri fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Memorial Stadium in Columbia, built in 1927 with a listed capacity of 71,168. The playing surface is Faurot Field, the band is Marching Mizzou, and the fight song is "Every True Son, Fight Tiger." The program claims a national title from 1960. The last five seasons went 6-7, 6-7, 11-2, 10-3 and 8-5, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about Homecoming, which the NCAA recognizes as starting at Missouri in 1911. Breaking news: it's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Eli Drinkwitz leads the Tigers, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 9, 2019. He grew up in Alma, Ark.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 31.5 points a game while giving up 18.4.

Players to Know
QB Austin Simmons: 152 of 240, 1,952 yards, 16 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB Jamal Roberts: 1,005 yards on 190 carries, 9 touchdowns.
WR Cayden Lee: 53 catches, 756 yards, 5 touchdowns.
Defender Daeden Hopkins: 7 sacks, the team lead.
LB Nicholas Rodriguez: a team-high 84 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Missouri is 6-2 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Missouri 5th in the SEC.
The season so far: W 54-14 vs Arkansas-Pine Bluff Golden Lions (Sept. 3), W 38-21 at Kansas Jayhawks (Sept. 11), W 27-17 vs Troy Trojans (Sept. 19), L 24-31 at Mississippi State Bulldogs (Sept. 26), W 45-17 vs Florida Gators (Oct. 3), W 27-6 vs Texas A&M Aggies (Oct. 10), L 3-31 at Ole Miss Rebels (Oct. 17), W 34-10 at Arkansas Razorbacks (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host No. 10 Texas (6-2, 3-2 SEC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at noon ET, at Memorial Stadium, Columbia, Missouri, on ESPN. We come in ranked No. 19.
Last time out. We beat the Arkansas Razorbacks 34-10 on the road on Oct. 31. Austin Simmons went 20 of 31 for 179 yards, with 2 touchdowns, Jamal Roberts ran 23 times for 138 yards and 1 touchdown and Cayden Lee caught 6 balls for 78 yards.
Texas beat the Mississippi State Bulldogs 31-3 at home on Oct. 31. Arch Manning went 16 of 20 for 220 yards, with 3 touchdowns; Raleek Brown ran 8 times for 30 yards; Emmett Mosley V caught 4 balls for 98 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Texas quarterback Arch Manning is 140 of 216 for 1,727 yards, with 15 touchdowns and 7 interceptions. Raleek Brown leads their ground game with 452 yards on 92 carries and 5 scores. Ryan Wingo is their top target: 36 catches for 470 yards. On defense, defensive back Jelani McDonald has 63 tackles and defender Colin Simmons has 10.5 sacks.
The series. We did not meet Texas in the regular season from 2016 through 2025, per CFBD, so there is no recent history to go on.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, Missouri or Texas.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Caro Whitfield, Texas's insider. Here's what I said:
"You are 6-2 with two losses by three points each. Oklahoma 27, Texas 24. Florida 27, Texas 24. Same score twice. That is not a football team, that is a recurring dream."
"Speaking of the library, the Columbia Public Library is holding a Local Authors Open House Saturday. You should go. Ask somebody to write a happy ending for a team that keeps losing by three."
"Austin Simmons has 1,952 yards, 16 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. Two. Your guy has 7. If we are comparing who protects the football, I will take the man who treats it like a family recipe."
And the last word from Caro: "Two bags. One for me and one for the trophy case."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Saturday morning starts at the Columbia Farmers Market's winter market on West Ash Street from 9 to noon, then everybody heads to Faurot Field at Memorial Stadium.

My call: Texas Longhorns 25, Missouri Tigers 21.
