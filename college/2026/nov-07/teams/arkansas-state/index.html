The School in 60 Seconds
Arkansas State football was founded in 1911 and has played in the Sun Belt since 2001. The Red Wolves have claimed 12 conference championships, most recently in 2016, and one national championship at the NCAA College Division level in 1970. The school changed its mascot to the Red Wolves in 2008. Home is Centennial Bank Stadium in Jonesboro, opened in 1974 and listed at 30,382 seats. The last five seasons climbed like a sunrise over the timber: 2-10, 3-9, 6-7, 8-5 and 7-6.

The Coach and the Scheme
Butch Jones is the head coach; CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 12, 2020.

Through 8 games this season, Arkansas State is scoring 28.8 points per game and allowing 27.2.

The Conference Picture
Arkansas State is 4-4 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Arkansas State 2nd in the Sun Belt - West.
The season so far: L 24-42 at Memphis Tigers (Sept. 5), W 52-7 vs West Georgia Wolves (Sept. 12), L 7-31 at TCU Horned Frogs (Sept. 19), W 17-14 vs Kennesaw State Owls (Sept. 26), L 20-23 at Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns (Oct. 3), L 49-56 vs South Alabama Jaguars (Oct. 8), W 23-13 at Southern Miss Golden Eagles (Oct. 17), W 38-32 vs Georgia State Panthers (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
