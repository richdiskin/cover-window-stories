Reunion Weekend in Annapolis: Navy Hosts Temple

By Cmdr. (Ret.) Annabel Merriweather · Navy Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Good afternoon, and welcome aboard. I'm Commander Annabel Merriweather, retired, your Navy insider. I spent my career as a surface warfare officer, and I read an option play the way I read a nautical chart: depth, current and where the rocks are hiding. I speak in nautical terms, and I cannot be stopped. When we win, I say, "Anchors aweigh!" and ring a ship's bell. When we lose, I say, "Steady as she goes," and plot a new course. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, with crisp navigation.

The School in 60 Seconds
Navy plays at Navy-Marine Corps Memorial Stadium in Annapolis, listed at 34,000. Last season the Midshipmen went 11-2, won the Liberty Bowl 35-13 and finished No. 23 in the final AP poll. They also won the Commander-in-Chief's Trophy for the second straight season, beating Air Force 34-31 and Army 17-16. The win over Army came on a fourth-and-goal touchdown throw. Anchors aweigh. Navy has now beaten Army in back-to-back seasons, and in Annapolis that's the voyage everyone remembers.

The Coach and the Scheme
Brian Newberry was hired on Dec. 19, 2022. The scheme is the option, and it's the fleet's pride.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 27.6 points a game while giving up 29.9.

Players to Know
QB Jackson Gutierrez: 36 of 71, 717 yards, 7 touchdowns, 1 interception.
Jackson Gutierrez on the ground: 979 yards on 178 carries, 13 touchdowns.
TE Jordan Collins: 6 catches, 212 yards, 3 touchdowns.
LB MarcAnthony Parker: 4 sacks, the team lead.
LB Coleman Cauley: a team-high 63 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Navy is 2-6 overall and 1-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have Navy 13th in the American.
The season so far: W 42-15 vs Towson Tigers (Sept. 5), L 30-38 at Florida Atlantic Owls (Sept. 12), L 20-24 at UAB Blazers (Sept. 25), L 9-14 at Air Force Falcons (Oct. 3), W 34-26 vs Tulsa Golden Hurricane (Oct. 10), L 34-38 at UTSA Roadrunners (Oct. 17), L 39-49 vs North Texas Mean Green (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Temple (2-6, 1-3 American) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:30 p.m. ET, at Navy-Marine Corps Memorial Stadium, Annapolis, Maryland, on CBSSN.
Temple lost to the East Carolina Pirates 17-9 on the road on Oct. 31. Ajani Sheppard went 11 of 20 for 118 yards; Ajani Sheppard ran 15 times for 69 yards; JoJo Bermudez caught 3 balls for 48 yards.
Players to watch. Temple quarterback Ajani Sheppard is 85 of 140 for 1,245 yards, with 8 touchdowns and 3 interceptions. Keveun Mason leads their ground game with 564 yards on 102 carries and 3 scores. Colin Chase is their top target: 20 catches for 330 yards. On defense, linebacker Curly Ordonez has 77 tackles and linebacker Curly Ordonez has 2 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 9 regular-season meetings with Temple from 2016 through 2025. We won 5 of them, and Temple won the last one 32-31 in 2025.
Availability. No official availability report for Navy or Temple was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Teddy Fiorella, Temple's insider. Here's what I said:
"In Annapolis we were busy winning a national title, Theodore. Note it in the log."
"In Philadelphia. Where you left it."
"Gutierrez is a destroyer escort. Low profile. Hard to stop. Keeps coming."
And the last word from Teddy: "One point. One point, Commander."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
The Navy Football Brotherhood Tailgater sets up at Gate 5 and Taylor Avenue at noon, the classes of 2011, 2016 and 2021 are back for reunions, and the new stadium menu has cream of crab soup.

My call: Navy Midshipmen 30, Temple Owls 23.
