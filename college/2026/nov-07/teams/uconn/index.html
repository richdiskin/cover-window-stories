Huskies at the Rent: UConn Hosts North Carolina

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
What's up, Husky Nation. I'm Jo Ferrante, your UConn insider. I'm from the Hartford area. I played point guard in college and coached high school quarterbacks after that, so I treat football like a fast break and I know every Husky by number. I count possessions like a basketball coach, because every one matters.

Here's the deal. I'm brash and competitive, and I take "nobody watches UConn football" very personally. I also keep it honest. Every stat I give you has a source, and if I don't know something, I'll say so. Next play, next possession. I celebrate these players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I'm grateful to God for a Friday night under the lights. Pack the Rent.

The School in 60 Seconds
UConn first fielded a team in 1896 and played in Division I-AA until 1999. The Huskies began moving to Division I-A in 2000 and became a full member in 2002. They played in the Big East and then the American from 2004 through 2019. In 2019 the program went independent, since the current Big East doesn't sponsor football. CFBD lists UConn as an FBS independent this season.

Home is Pratt & Whitney Stadium in East Hartford. CFBD lists it as built in 2003 with 40,000 seats. The recent climb is real: 1-11 in 2021, 6-7 in 2022, 3-9 in 2023, then 9-4 in both 2024 and 2025. Back-to-back nine-win seasons. Say it louder for the people in the back.

The Coach and the Scheme
Jason Candle is the new head coach. CFBD records his hire date as Dec. 6, 2025. He was head coach at Toledo from 2016 to 2025 and an assistant there from 2009. Before that he coached at Mount Union from 2003 to 2008, and he played wide receiver at Mount Union and Geneva.

Through 8 games this season, UConn is scoring 34.4 points per game and allowing 25.2.

Players to Know
QB Kalieb Osborne: 129 of 215, 1,857 yards, 14 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB Cyncir Bowers: 571 yards on 84 carries, 7 touchdowns.
WR Shamar Porter: 16 catches, 308 yards, 3 touchdowns.
LB K'Von Sherman: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB John Lista: a team-high 58 tackles.

The Conference Picture
UConn is 4-4 overall and 0-0 in league play.
The season so far: W 56-7 vs Lafayette Leopards (Sept. 5), L 14-38 vs Maryland Terrapins (Sept. 12), W 48-20 at Southern Miss Golden Eagles (Sept. 19), L 21-24 at Miami (OH) RedHawks (Sept. 26), L 41-42 (OT) vs Syracuse Orange (Oct. 3), W 34-20 at Temple Owls (Oct. 10), W 35-16 vs Massachusetts Minutemen (Oct. 23), L 26-35 at Air Force Falcons (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host North Carolina (4-4, 2-3 ACC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at noon ET, at Pratt & Whitney Stadium, East Hartford, Connecticut, on CBSSN.
Last time out. We lost to the Air Force Falcons 35-26 on the road on Oct. 31. Kalieb Osborne went 17 of 33 for 263 yards, with 2 touchdowns, Kenji Christian ran 5 times for 68 yards and 1 touchdown and Alex Godavitarne caught 1 balls for 55 yards and 1 touchdown.
North Carolina lost to the Miami Hurricanes 38-35 at home on Oct. 31. Billy Edwards Jr. went 28 of 34 for 341 yards, with 4 touchdowns; Demon June ran 13 times for 44 yards and 1 touchdown; Jelani Thurman caught 5 balls for 70 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Players to watch. North Carolina quarterback Billy Edwards Jr. is 149 of 235 for 1,872 yards, with 15 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Benjamin Hall leads their ground game with 296 yards on 60 carries. Jordan Shipp is their top target: 45 catches for 587 yards. On defense, linebacker Derek McDonald has 82 tackles and defensive lineman Xavier Lewis has 7 sacks.
The series. We did not meet North Carolina in the regular season from 2016 through 2025, per CFBD, so there is no recent history to go on.
Availability. The public injury data for North Carolina and UConn is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Ainsley Pemberton, North Carolina's insider. Here's what I said:
"The sky is blue everywhere. You did not invent it. You just painted a ram on it. And here is the thing those kids do know. The last time these two teams played, in December of 2024, UConn won 27-14. Tell them about the sky after that."
"And you lost. Thirty-eight to thirty-five. You lost to Syracuse in overtime, 31-30. You lost to Notre Dame. You lost at Clemson. You are 4-4, Ainsley. Same as us. The only difference is that we have Kalieb Osborne and he has thrown 2 interceptions all year."
"We score 34.4 a game. You score 25.8. We went to Southern Miss and won 48-20. We went to Temple and won 34-20. We beat UMass 35-16. When this offense gets going, the scoreboard operator needs a nap."
And the last word from Ainsley: "Everybody loves me. It is the sky thing."

My call: North Carolina Tar Heels 30, UConn Huskies 24.
