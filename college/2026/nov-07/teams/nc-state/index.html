Red in Raleigh: NC State Hosts Duke on Military Appreciation Day

By Rutherford "Ruff" Pegram · NC State Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hey there, Pack! I'm Rutherford Pegram, but everybody calls me Ruff, and I'm your NC State insider. I'm a Wake County native and an engineer, and I grill at the same Carter-Finley lot every home game. I explain schemes with grill thermometers. A defense running at 225 degrees? That's low and slow. An offense at 450? That's a sear.

Here's my promise. I'm loud, I'm warm and I'm gloriously dramatic, but I don't cook the numbers. Every stat here has a source. If I don't know something about NC State, I'll tell you the thermometer's still reading. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I thank God for good friends, good food and good football. I howl once per segment, no more, no less. Wolfpack, howl!

The School in 60 Seconds
NC State plays in the ACC and was a founding member in 1953, after years in the Southern Conference. The Wolfpack has won seven conference championships and played in 34 bowl games, winning 17. Since 1966, home has been Carter-Finley Stadium, the largest college football stadium in North Carolina. CFBD lists it at 58,000 seats. In 2010 the school brought back a live mascot, a Tamaskan dog named Tuffy. Good boy.

The last five seasons have been steady heat: 9-3, 8-5, 9-4, 6-7 and 8-5. Four winning seasons in five years. The grill stays lit in Raleigh.

The Coach and the Scheme
Dave Doeren led NC State starting in 2013. CFBD records his hire date as Dec. 1, 2012. Before Raleigh he was head coach at Northern Illinois in 2011 and 2012. The school relieved Doeren of his duties after the loss at Stanford, and running backs coach and special teams coordinator Todd Goebbel will serve as interim head coach for the rest of the season. We thank Coach Doeren for every Saturday he gave this program.

The 8-game numbers: 32.1 points per game scored, 25.9 allowed.

Players to Know
QB CJ Bailey: 156 of 244, 1,955 yards, 13 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Jayden Scott: 358 yards on 62 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Keenan Jackson: 30 catches, 456 yards, 6 touchdowns.
LB Elijah Satchell: 4 sacks, the team lead.

The Conference Picture
NC State is 4-4 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have NC State 9th in the ACC.
The season so far: L 8-34 at Virginia Cavaliers (Aug. 29), W 73-0 vs Richmond Spiders (Sept. 11), L 31-35 at Vanderbilt Commodores (Sept. 19), W 41-31 vs App State Mountaineers (Sept. 26), W 31-28 vs Louisville Cardinals (Oct. 3), L 14-38 vs Wake Forest Demon Deacons (Oct. 10), L 28-31 at Stanford Cardinal (Oct. 23), W 31-10 vs California Golden Bears (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Duke (6-2, 3-2 ACC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 12:30 p.m. ET, at Carter-Finley Stadium, Raleigh, North Carolina, on CW.
Last time out. We beat the California Golden Bears 31-10 at home on Oct. 31. CJ Bailey went 13 of 25 for 192 yards, with 2 touchdowns, Davion Gause ran 19 times for 104 yards and 1 touchdown and Chance Robinson caught 4 balls for 76 yards and 1 touchdown.
Duke beat the Boston College Eagles 47-2 at home on Oct. 31. Walker Eget went 11 of 18 for 201 yards, with 1 touchdown; Nate Sheppard ran 20 times for 97 yards and 5 touchdowns; Jeremiah Hasley caught 3 balls for 55 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Duke quarterback Walker Eget is 133 of 215 for 1,641 yards, with 9 touchdowns and 3 interceptions. Nate Sheppard leads their ground game with 972 yards on 173 carries and 14 scores. Jonah Burton is their top target: 22 catches for 308 yards. On defense, linebacker Nick Morris Jr. has 45 tackles and defensive end Kevin O'Connor has 4.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 4 regular-season meetings with Duke from 2016 through 2025. We won 3 of them, and we won the last one 45-33 in 2025.
Availability. No official availability report for NC State or Duke was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Harrison Wexley-Moore IV, Duke's insider. Here's what I said:
"And a gentleman says thank you. Here is your thank you. NC State has a running back named Duke. Jayden Scott. Wears number 1. Everybody calls him Duke. So on Saturday, there will be exactly one Duke on that field worth watching, and he is ours."
"We did not name him. We just took the name. Like we take your lunch most years."
"Fair. But you went to Georgia Tech and lost 28-25. You lost to North Carolina 24-20. You beat Virginia 19-16 and needed every bit of it. On the road, your private club turns into a tent."
And the last word from Harrison: "For one afternoon. Then I go back to being right."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Military Appreciation at Carter-Finley, and downtown, the Raleigh Food Truck Rodeo fills Fayetteville Street Saturday with free admission.

My call: Duke Blue Devils 28, NC State Wolfpack 25.
