Owls in Denton: Rice Crashes North Texas Homecoming

By Odalys Chen-Moreau · Rice Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hello, Owl family! I'm Odalys Chen-Moreau, your Rice insider. I'm finishing a bioengineering PhD, and I model football like a clinical trial: control group, treatment group and a very long methods section. You should also know I'm basically a stats cop with a badge. If someone makes a sweeping claim off two games, I will walk over, flash the badge and say, "N equals too small." When we win, I shout "Statistically significant!" and do a dance worthy of any marching band. When we lose, I say "N is too small to panic"... and then I panic quietly in the lab. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, peer-reviewed.

The School in 60 Seconds
Rice Stadium is one of the most historic venues in American sports. On Sept. 12, 1962, President John F. Kennedy stood there and gave his speech recommitting the country to landing on the moon. On Jan. 13, 1974, the stadium hosted Super Bowl VIII, when Miami beat Minnesota 24-7. It's listed at 47,000 seats. Rice is a small private school playing big-time football in the American Athletic Conference, and that's a story worth telling every single week. Our hypothesis is that grit travels well.

The Coach and the Scheme
Scott Abell was named head coach on Nov. 26, 2024, after seven seasons at Davidson.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 21.0 points a game while giving up 31.8.

Players to Know

Jacurri Brown on the ground: 669 yards on 118 carries, 6 touchdowns.

DL Chibby Nwajuaku: 2 sacks, the team lead.

The Conference Picture
Rice is 1-7 overall and 0-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have Rice 14th in the American.
The season so far: W 31-3 vs Houston Christian Huskies (Sept. 5), L 0-52 at Notre Dame Fighting Irish (Sept. 12), L 21-28 vs Western Michigan Broncos (Sept. 19), L 24-38 at Fresno State Bulldogs (Sept. 26), L 14-16 vs UTSA Roadrunners (Oct. 3), L 12-28 at East Carolina Pirates (Oct. 10), L 17-38 vs Tulsa Golden Hurricane (Oct. 17), L 49-51 at Florida Atlantic Owls (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to North Texas (6-2, 4-0 American) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 4:00 p.m. ET, at DATCU Stadium, Denton, Texas, on ESPNU.
Last time out. We lost to the Florida Atlantic Owls 51-49 on the road on Oct. 24. Gael Ochoa went 2 of 5 for 80 yards, with 1 touchdown, Gael Ochoa ran 12 times for 259 yards and 4 touchdowns and Braylen Walker caught 1 balls for 71 yards and 1 touchdown.
North Texas beat the Florida Atlantic Owls 48-41 at home on Oct. 29. Tayven Jackson went 33 of 46 for 404 yards, with 4 touchdowns; Jahiem White ran 26 times for 193 yards and 2 touchdowns; Justin Stevenson caught 9 balls for 119 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. North Texas quarterback Tayven Jackson is 193 of 267 for 2,577 yards, with 22 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Jahiem White leads their ground game with 1,054 yards on 142 carries and 13 scores. Justin Stevenson is their top target: 50 catches for 781 yards. On defense, linebacker Zakye Barker has 62 tackles and defensive lineman Davion Rhodes has 4 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 8 regular-season meetings with North Texas from 2016 through 2025. We won 4 of them, and we won the last one 56-24 in 2025.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, Rice or North Texas.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Grady Mayhew-Ochoa, North Texas's insider. Here's what I said:
"Eighty years. Now that's a sample size I respect."
"Correlation is not causation. But I admit that's a lot of yards."
"statistically devastating."
And the last word from Grady: "Seven and one. That's a pretty steady groove."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We walk into North Texas' 80th annual Homecoming at DATCU Stadium, and the Mean Green have won 7 of their last 9 Homecoming games, so this is a tough house.

My call: North Texas Mean Green 42, Rice Owls 24.
