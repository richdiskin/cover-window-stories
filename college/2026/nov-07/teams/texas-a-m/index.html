Aggies Head East: Texas A&M Brings the Twelfth Man to Columbia

By Colt Wendeborn · Texas A&M Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
HOWDY! Howdy, and let me yell this: welcome, Aggies! I'm Colt Wendeborn, petroleum engineering graduate, and I report on football practice the way the Yell Leaders lead Midnight Yell, with my whole chest. I travel with this Texas A&M team, and I live with it. My one weakness is that I explain everything with an Aggie tradition, and then I explain the tradition, and then sometimes I explain the explanation. You'll learn a lot. Every week you'll get the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines, delivered with the straight-arrow earnestness of a man who irons his maroon. When we win, I yell. Just yell. Joyfully. When we lose, I go practice silent yell drills in a closet until I feel better. Gig 'em, and thank you for reading.

The School in 60 Seconds
Texas A&M competes in the SEC and claims three national titles and 18 conference championships. Home is Kyle Field, an outdoor stadium on campus that seats 102,733. CFBD dates it to 1927. (A tradition explained: the student section stands the entire game because the 12th Man is ready to go in. That's why I'm standing while I write this.) The last five seasons have run 8-4, 5-7, 7-6, 8-5 and then a big 11-2 in 2025, Mike Elko's second year.

The Coach and the Scheme
Elko was hired Nov. 27, 2023.

Through 8 games this season, Texas A&M is scoring 28.2 points per game and allowing 18.9.

Players to Know
QB Marcel Reed: 126 of 222, 1,390 yards, 8 touchdowns, 8 interceptions.
RB KJ Edwards: 360 yards on 55 carries, 2 touchdowns.
WR Isaiah Horton: 23 catches, 314 yards, 3 touchdowns.
DE Marco Jones: 2.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Ray Coney: a team-high 40 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Texas A&M is 4-4 overall and 1-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have Texas A&M 14th in the SEC.
The season so far: W 50-0 vs Missouri State Bears (Sept. 5), W 48-20 vs Arizona State Sun Devils (Sept. 12), L 21-31 vs Kentucky Wildcats (Sept. 19), L 6-35 at LSU Tigers (Sept. 26), W 34-7 vs Arkansas Razorbacks (Oct. 3), L 6-27 at Missouri Tigers (Oct. 10), W 41-0 vs The Citadel Bulldogs (Oct. 17), L 20-31 at Alabama Crimson Tide (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to No. 23 South Carolina (5-3, 3-3 SEC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:00 p.m. ET, at Williams-Brice Stadium, Columbia, South Carolina, on ESPN.
Last time out. We lost to the Alabama Crimson Tide 31-20 on the road on Oct. 24. Marcel Reed went 14 of 21 for 163 yards, with 1 touchdown, KJ Edwards ran 8 times for 43 yards and Mario Craver caught 6 balls for 45 yards and 1 touchdown.
South Carolina beat the Oklahoma Sooners 42-25 on the road on Oct. 31. LaNorris Sellers went 19 of 24 for 301 yards, with 3 touchdowns; LaNorris Sellers ran 13 times for 96 yards and 2 touchdowns; Mazeo Bennett Jr. caught 4 balls for 107 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. South Carolina quarterback LaNorris Sellers is 152 of 243 for 1,849 yards, with 16 touchdowns and 7 interceptions. LaNorris Sellers leads their ground game with 603 yards on 98 carries and 6 scores. Mazeo Bennett Jr. is their top target: 35 catches for 431 yards. On defense, linebacker Justin Okoronkwo has 62 tackles and defensive lineman Tomiwa Durojaiye has 4 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 10 regular-season meetings with South Carolina from 2016 through 2025. We won 3 of them, and South Carolina won the last one 31-30 in 2025.
Availability. No official availability report for Texas A&M or South Carolina was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Aggies who get to Columbia early can catch Palmetto Luna Arts' free 15th Day of the Dead Celebration on Boyd Plaza at the Columbia Museum of Art Saturday morning before we go to work.

My call: South Carolina Gamecocks 28, Texas A&M Aggies 26.
