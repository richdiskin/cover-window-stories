Hawkeyes in Evanston: Iowa Gets Its First Look at the New Ryan Field

By Orrin Kalvelage · Iowa Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hello, neighbors. I'm Orrin Kalvelage, your Iowa insider. I grew up on a farm near Iowa City and haven't missed a home opener in my life. I own one black-and-gold sweater, and I wear it with pride every single week. My wife says it needs retiring. I say it's got plenty of Saturdays left in it.

I'm gentle and I'm stubborn, and I'm wildly proud of defense and special teams. I believe a good punt is a love letter to your defense. Here's my pledge: every number in this report has a source. If I don't know something about Iowa, I'll tell you plainly. I celebrate our players as young men first, I respect every opponent, and I give thanks to God for honest work and honest football. Go Hawks.

The School in 60 Seconds
Iowa joined the conference that became the Big Ten in 1899 and played its first league season in 1900. The Hawkeyes play at Kinnick Stadium in Iowa City, capacity 69,250. They've won 13 conference championships, including 11 in the Big Ten. Iowa has been ranked No. 1 in the AP and Coaches polls 15 times.

The last five seasons are steady as a fence post: 10-4, 8-5, 10-4, 8-5 and 9-4. That's Iowa football. You know what you're getting, and what you're getting is tough.

The Coach and the Scheme
Kirk Ferentz is in his 28th season as head coach, the longest current tenure in FBS. He's led Iowa since 1999. He's the Big Ten's all-time leader in wins. Before Iowa he was head coach at Maine from 1990 to 1992 and an assistant with the Cleveland Browns and Baltimore Ravens. He played linebacker at UConn.

The 8-game numbers: 28.2 points per game scored, 12.5 allowed.

Players to Know

RB Kamari Moulton: 541 yards on 86 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Tony Diaz: 33 catches, 461 yards, 2 touchdowns.
DE Joseph Anderson: 4 sacks, the team lead.

The Conference Picture
Iowa is 6-2 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Iowa 5th in the Big Ten.
The season so far: W 40-0 vs Northern Illinois Huskies (Sept. 5), W 16-13 vs Iowa State Cyclones (Sept. 12), W 55-0 vs Northern Iowa Panthers (Sept. 19), W 20-19 at Michigan Wolverines (Sept. 26), L 14-31 vs Ohio State Buckeyes (Oct. 3), W 41-24 at Washington Huskies (Oct. 9), L 10-13 at Minnesota Golden Gophers (Oct. 24), W 30-0 vs Wisconsin Badgers (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Northwestern (6-2, 3-2 Big Ten) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:30 p.m. ET, at Ryan Field, Evanston, Illinois, on Fox. We come in ranked No. 21.
Last time out. We beat the Wisconsin Badgers 30-0 at home on Oct. 31. Hank Brown went 20 of 24 for 222 yards, with 2 touchdowns, Xavier Williams ran 20 times for 78 yards and Tony Diaz caught 4 balls for 75 yards and 1 touchdown.
Northwestern lost to the Oregon Ducks 34-26 on the road on Oct. 31. Aidan Chiles went 22 of 38 for 281 yards, with 1 touchdown; Aidan Chiles ran 14 times for 93 yards and 1 touchdown; Griffin Wilde caught 5 balls for 146 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Northwestern quarterback Aidan Chiles is out against Iowa; backup Ryan Boe is listed as available. Caleb Komolafe leads their ground game with 618 yards on 104 carries and 5 scores. Griffin Wilde is their top target: 44 catches for 761 yards. On defense, linebacker Braydon Brus has 67 tackles and linebacker Braydon Brus has 4 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 8 regular-season meetings with Northwestern from 2016 through 2025. We won 5 of them, and Northwestern won the last one 40-14 in 2024.
Availability. No official availability report for Iowa or Northwestern was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Ezra Lindqvist, Northwestern's insider. Here's what I said:
"Nice building. Shame about the football."
"We beat Michigan in their own house 20-19. We'll be fine in a stadium that still smells like paint."
"We scored 30 against Wisconsin and they scored zero."
And the last word from Ezra: "You can't block out 2020. It was one point."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We get our first look at the new 35,000-seat Ryan Field, and Northwestern is 5-0 at home this year, so we're walking into a building that hasn't lost.

My call: Northwestern Wildcats 24, Iowa Hawkeyes 21.
