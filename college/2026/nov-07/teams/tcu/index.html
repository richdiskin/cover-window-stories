Frogs Under the Desert Lights: TCU Visits Tucson

By Ryder Galloway · TCU Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Well, howdy, Frog fans, and welcome! I'm Ryder Galloway, your TCU insider. I'm a Stockyards cowboy who became a finance guy, so I wear boots with a quarter-zip and I explain defenses as cattle drives. You've got your point riders, your flank riders and your drag riders, and when everyone holds their spot, nobody gets loose. I travel with this TCU team, and I live with it. Fair warning: if something goes wrong, anything at all, I will probably blame Dallas. Flat tire? Dallas. Missed field goal? Dallas. When we win, it's "Yeehaw!" and my hat goes flying. When we lose, I mutter about Dallas some more. And the best sound in football is me hollering "Yeehaw, that's a turnover!" Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, Fort Worth style.

The School in 60 Seconds
TCU has a national title on the wall. The 1938 Horned Frogs went 11-0, beat Carnegie Tech 15-7 in the Sugar Bowl and won the national championship. Quarterback Davey O'Brien won the Heisman Trophy that year. The 2010 team went 13-0 and won the Rose Bowl, and in 2022 Sonny Dykes led TCU all the way to the College Football Playoff national championship game. Home is Amon G. Carter Stadium in Fort Worth, listed at 46,000. Not one bit of that history happened in Dallas. And the rivalry with Baylor has a name now, the Revivalry, and we just won it in Waco. Ten more minutes on that and I'd be throwing my hat again.

The Coach and the Scheme
Sonny Dykes was hired on Nov. 29, 2021, and in his first season he took the Frogs to the national title game.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 23.2 points a game while giving up 14.1.

Players to Know
QB Jaden Craig: 175 of 269, 2,207 yards, 11 touchdowns, 6 interceptions.
RB Jeremy Payne: 429 yards on 88 carries, 2 touchdowns.
TE Kari Ashley: 38 catches, 560 yards, 4 touchdowns.
LB Koron Hayward: 5.5 sacks, the team lead.
S Jamel Johnson: a team-high 50 tackles.

The Conference Picture
TCU is 4-4 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have TCU 8th in the Big 12.
The season so far: W 63-7 vs Grambling Tigers (Sept. 12), W 31-7 vs Arkansas State Red Wolves (Sept. 19), L 13-21 at UCF Knights (Sept. 26), L 10-17 vs BYU Cougars (Oct. 3), W 17-6 at Baylor Bears (Oct. 17), L 20-24 vs West Virginia Mountaineers (Oct. 24), W 22-16 vs Kansas Jayhawks (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to No. 24 Arizona (6-2, 3-2 Big 12) on Friday, Nov. 6, at 10:15 p.m. ET, at Casino Del Sol Stadium, Tucson, Arizona, on ESPN.
Last time out. We beat the Kansas Jayhawks 22-16 at home on Oct. 31. Jaden Craig went 13 of 29 for 141 yards, Jaden Craig ran 15 times for 87 yards and 1 touchdown and Kari Ashley caught 3 balls for 50 yards.
Arizona lost to the Texas Tech Red Raiders 34-26 on the road on Oct. 31. Noah Fifita went 33 of 45 for 417 yards, with 1 touchdown; Kedrick Reescano ran 9 times for 18 yards and 2 touchdowns; Isaiah Mizell caught 7 balls for 182 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Arizona quarterback Noah Fifita is 200 of 270 for 2,481 yards, with 19 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Kedrick Reescano leads their ground game with 321 yards on 89 carries and 7 scores. Isaiah Mizell is their top target: 34 catches for 675 yards. On defense, linebacker Taye Brown has 47 tackles and linebacker Chase Kennedy has 6 sacks.
The series. CFBD has one regular-season meeting with Arizona from 2016 through 2025, and Arizona won the last one 49-28 in 2024.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for TCU vs. Arizona. When TCU or Arizona puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Inez Paloverde, Arizona's insider. Here's what I said:
"I'll take two out of three. And I'm not picky about which two."
"And you still lost 26-34 in Lubbock. Fifita threw for 417 and you lost."
"We count every point twice in Fort Worth. Koron Hayward has five and a half sacks. Jamel Johnson has 50 tackles. Nobody runs away from us."
And the last word from Inez: "Fifty-two. You scored twenty."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We get a Friday night in Tucson, and the Tucson Greek Festival at Jácome Plaza is going that night, with Greek coffee and loukoumades at Papou's Cafe for any Frogs fans downtown.

My call: Arizona Wildcats 26, TCU Horned Frogs 16.
