Honor Night in Logan: Utah State Hosts Fresno State

By Hyrum Tolleson · Utah State Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Go Aggies! Well, hello there, neighbors. I'm Hyrum Tolleson, and I've spent most of my life on a dairy up in Cache Valley, which is a fancy way of saying I've been up before the sun since I was eight years old. I travel with the Aggies and I live with the Aggies, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines, slow and steady, the way you'd tell a story over the fence. I'm a patient man. It takes a lot to get me riled, and a lot less to get me laughing. Logan sits in a valley so striking it almost feels unfair. Our Aggies play with that small-town heart, and afterward, everybody goes for ice cream. I always order a double scoop of Aggie Ice Cream, win or lose. When we play well, that's udderly dominant.

The School in 60 Seconds
Utah State football was established in 1892, which makes it one of the oldest programs in the western United States. We play in the Pac-12 now. Home since 1968 has been Merlin Olsen Field at Maverik Stadium in Logan, listed at 25,513 seats. The field is named for Merlin Olsen, a Pro Football Hall of Famer who played here. The Aggies have won 13 conference championships across four different leagues, most recently in 2021, the year we went 11-3. Since then: 6-7, 6-7, 4-8 and 6-7. Steady folks. We'll get the herd back to the top.

The Coach and the Scheme
Bronco Mendenhall is the head coach; CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 6, 2024.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 16.9 points a game while giving up 21.9.

Players to Know
QB Grady Brosterhous: 99 of 178, 1,120 yards, 7 touchdowns, 6 interceptions.
RB Javen Jacobs: 491 yards on 98 carries, 3 touchdowns.
WR Eli Wood: 37 catches, 479 yards, 5 touchdowns.
LB Jordan Pendleton: 2.5 sacks, the team lead.
S Brevin Hamblin: a team-high 62 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Utah State is 3-5 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Utah State 4th in the Pac-12.
The season so far: L 17-29 vs Idaho State Bengals (Sept. 5), L 14-16 at Washington Huskies (Sept. 12), L 0-33 at Utah Utes (Sept. 19), W 21-10 vs Troy Trojans (Sept. 26), L 18-37 at Boise State Broncos (Oct. 3), W 17-16 vs Washington State Cougars (Oct. 9), L 17-20 at Texas State Bobcats (Oct. 24), W 31-14 vs Colorado State Rams (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Fresno State (5-3, 2-2 Pac-12) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 9:30 p.m. ET, at Maverik Stadium, Logan, Utah, on USA Net.
Last time out. We beat the Colorado State Rams 31-14 at home on Oct. 31. McCae Hillstead went 17 of 22 for 173 yards, with 1 touchdown, McCae Hillstead ran 11 times for 79 yards and 1 touchdown and Anthony Garcia caught 11 balls for 121 yards.
Fresno State beat the Oregon State Beavers 38-22 at home on Oct. 31. Jayden Mandal went 11 of 17 for 202 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Bryson Donelson ran 16 times for 108 yards and 1 touchdown; Josiah Freeman caught 6 balls for 88 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Players to watch. Fresno State quarterback Jayden Mandal is 134 of 212 for 1,595 yards, with 10 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. Bryson Donelson leads their ground game with 407 yards on 81 carries and 6 scores. Josiah Freeman is their top target: 38 catches for 533 yards. On defense, linebacker K'vion Thunderbird has 66 tackles and defensive lineman Dylan Hampsten has 5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 5 regular-season meetings with Fresno State from 2016 through 2025. We won 1 of them, and Fresno State won the last one 28-17 in 2025.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, Utah State or Fresno State.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Rosalind "Roz" Ayala, Fresno State's insider. Here's what I said:
"We fill it fine. Just wait until you hear it."
"We lost to Washington by two. On the road. We beat Washington State by one. We lost to Texas State by three. We play everybody close."
"We don't talk about that."
And the last word from Roz: "USC is USC. That was the first game."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Utah State is honoring all military and first responders at Maverik Stadium, and current USU students can claim a free ticket.

My call: Fresno State Bulldogs 21, Utah State Aggies 19.
