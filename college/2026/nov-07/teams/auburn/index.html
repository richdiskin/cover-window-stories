Navy on the Plains: Auburn Salutes the Troops and Squares Off with Arkansas

By Lacey Bellamy · Auburn Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Well, hey there, Auburn family! I'm Lacey Bellamy. I'm a proud former sorority president on the Plains, and I can recite every Iron Bowl down by heart. Ask me anything. I mean it. I'll even act them out, and in my reenactments, I always play the hero. Every single time. I'm Southern to the bone, and I'll have the sharpest take in the room, all wrapped in a nice Alabama drawl. When we win, I roll an imaginary oak in imaginary toilet paper and shriek "War Eagle!" When we lose, I sip my lemonade with great dignity and say "We'll be back" to absolutely everybody. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, plus a whole lot of War Eagle fight.

The School in 60 Seconds
Auburn has a national championship from 2010, the season Cam Newton led the Tigers. Home is Jordan-Hare Stadium, listed at 87,451. There is no louder place to spend a fall afternoon.

The Coach and the Scheme
Alex Golesh came to Auburn from South Florida. He was hired on Nov. 30, 2025.

Through 8 games this season, Auburn is scoring 24.1 points per game and allowing 26.8.

Players to Know
QB Byrum Brown: 159 of 247, 1,560 yards, 9 touchdowns, 7 interceptions.
Byrum Brown on the ground: 484 yards on 139 carries, 7 touchdowns.
WR Keshaun Singleton: 39 catches, 433 yards, 2 touchdowns.
LB Elijah Melendez: 4 sacks, the team lead.
LB Elijah Melendez: a team-high 63 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Auburn is 4-4 overall and 2-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have Auburn 12th in the SEC.
The season so far: W 43-8 vs Southern Miss Golden Eagles (Sept. 12), L 39-44 vs Florida Gators (Sept. 19), W 21-15 vs Vanderbilt Commodores (Sept. 26), L 14-24 at Tennessee Volunteers (Oct. 3), W 27-24 at Georgia Bulldogs (Oct. 17), L 17-38 vs LSU Tigers (Oct. 24), L 15-45 at Ole Miss Rebels (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Arkansas (3-5, 1-4 SEC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 12:45 p.m. ET, at Jordan-Hare Stadium, Auburn, Alabama, on SEC Network.
Last time out. We lost to the Ole Miss Rebels 45-15 on the road on Oct. 31. Byrum Brown went 25 of 38 for 195 yards, with 1 touchdown, Jeremiah Cobb ran 7 times for 35 yards and Chas Nimrod caught 4 balls for 62 yards and 1 touchdown.
Arkansas lost to the Missouri Tigers 34-10 at home on Oct. 31. KJ Jackson went 20 of 34 for 229 yards; Cam Settles ran 12 times for 38 yards; Antonio Jordan caught 7 balls for 106 yards.
Players to watch. Arkansas quarterback KJ Jackson is 151 of 249 for 1,673 yards, with 7 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Sutton Smith leads their ground game with 217 yards on 63 carries and 1 score. Chris Marshall is their top target: 43 catches for 633 yards. On defense, linebacker Ja'Quavion Smith has 53 tackles and defensive lineman Quincy Rhodes Jr. has 3 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 10 regular-season meetings with Arkansas from 2016 through 2025. We won 3 of them, and Arkansas won the last one 33-24 in 2025.
Availability. The public injury data for Arkansas and Auburn is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Bubba Fayette-Dorsey, Arkansas's insider. Here's what I said:
"You should care a little. Y'all scored 10 points against Missouri."
"And we beat y'all 33-24 last year. Eat. You're going to need your strength."
"We beat Georgia 27-24 in Athens. Nobody gets to tell me this team is done."
And the last word from Bubba: "KJ Jackson has thrown for 1,673 yards. Chris Marshall has 633 receiving yards. That is a real receiver. And we beat y'all 24-14 in 2024, right in front of everybody."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Saturday at Jordan-Hare is Military Appreciation day, and the school is asking everybody to wear navy, so the Plains will be a sea of it when Arkansas walks in.

My call: Auburn Tigers 29, Arkansas Razorbacks 19.
