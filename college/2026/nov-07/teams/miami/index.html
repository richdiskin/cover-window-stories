Canes in South Bend: Miami Takes On Notre Dame

By Javi "The Breeze" Castillo · Miami Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
¡Qué tal, Canes! Welcome! I'm Javi Castillo, but you can call me The Breeze. I played defensive back at The U back in the day, and now I scout in linen and sunglasses, rain or shine. I travel with this Miami team, and I live with it. My thing is bilingual hype one-liners that always rhyme. "Mensah lets it fly, adiós, goodbye!" I'm working on them. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, smooth as an ocean breeze. When we win, I throw up the "U" with my arms and shout "Swag!" When we lose, I put my sunglasses on indoors and say nothing. Very loudly. My motto never changes: "It's all about the U, baby."

The School in 60 Seconds
Miami owns five national championships: 1983, 1987, 1989, 1991 and 2001. Last season the Canes made it all the way to the national championship game, losing 27-21 to Indiana at Hard Rock Stadium on Jan. 19, 2026. Hard Rock is listed at 64,767. Sí, señor.

The Coach and the Scheme
Mario Cristobal was hired on Dec. 6, 2021.

Through 8 games this season, Miami is scoring 47.6 points per game and allowing 17.8.

Players to Know
QB Darian Mensah: 188 of 233, 2,425 yards, 25 touchdowns, 1 interception.
RB Javian Mallory: 495 yards on 71 carries, 7 touchdowns.
WR Malachi Toney: 73 catches, 1,027 yards, 13 touchdowns.
DL Marquise Lightfoot: 7 sacks, the team lead.
LB Mohamed Toure: a team-high 44 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Miami is 8-0 overall and 6-0 in league play. ESPN's standings have Miami 1st in the ACC.
The season so far: W 45-6 at Stanford Cardinal (Sept. 4), W 77-7 vs Florida A&M Rattlers (Sept. 10), W 33-20 at Wake Forest Demon Deacons (Sept. 18), W 52-3 vs Central Michigan Chippewas (Sept. 26), W 41-13 at Clemson Tigers (Oct. 3), W 43-35 (OT) vs Florida State Seminoles (Oct. 17), W 52-23 vs Pittsburgh Panthers (Oct. 24), W 38-35 at North Carolina Tar Heels (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to No. 4 Notre Dame (7-1, 0-0 FBS Independent) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:30 p.m. ET, at Notre Dame Stadium, Notre Dame, Indiana, on NBC. We come in ranked No. 1.
Last time out. We beat the North Carolina Tar Heels 38-35 on the road on Oct. 31. Darian Mensah went 22 of 29 for 274 yards, with 3 touchdowns, Javian Mallory ran 12 times for 51 yards and Malachi Toney caught 7 balls for 75 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Players to watch. Notre Dame quarterback CJ Carr is 148 of 222 for 1,849 yards, with 18 touchdowns and 1 interception. Nolan James Jr. leads their ground game with 519 yards on 90 carries and 8 scores. Jordan Faison is their top target: 38 catches for 497 yards. On defense, linebacker Drayk Bowen has 45 tackles and defensive lineman Boubacar Traore has 3.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 3 regular-season meetings with Notre Dame from 2016 through 2025. We won 0 of them, and Notre Dame won the last one 27-24 in 2025.
Availability. The public injury data for Notre Dame and Miami is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Declan Moriarty-Chen, Notre Dame's insider. Here's what I said:
"Oye, I love this place. The bagpipes, the band. Very classy. And then we beat you."
And the last word from Declan: "Florida State put 35 on you. North Carolina put 35 on you."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Canes fans in South Bend should catch the Victory March from the Basilica of the Sacred Heart at 5:15 p.m., because that's the moment Notre Dame Stadium starts to rumble.

My call: Notre Dame Fighting Irish 33, Miami Hurricanes 29.
