Blue Hens in Georgia: Delaware Visits Kennesaw State

By Henrietta "Hen" Lofland · Delaware Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Good evening, and please lower your voices; this is a library. Kidding. Shout all you like. I'm Henrietta Lofland, but my friends and the circulation desk call me Hen, and I'm your Delaware insider. I'm a Newark native, a librarian by day and a wing-T devotee by night. I have opinions about the buck sweep that I've been asked not to share at dinner parties.

Delaware is the First State, and our Blue Hens are writing a brand-new chapter at the FBS level. I'm here to annotate it carefully. You'll get footnotes, because every fact I give you will have a source. You'll get honesty, because if we stumble I'll say so, kindly. You'll also get a librarian's promise: if I don't know something, I'll go look it up before I say it. I brought a pen. A blue one, naturally.

The School in 60 Seconds
Delaware plays in Conference USA at the FBS level. Before that, the Blue Hens were an FCS power. They claim six national titles, in 1946, 1963, 1971, 1972, 1979 and 2003, and they reached the FCS title game again in 2007 and 2010. Delaware has produced quarterbacks Rich Gannon and Joe Flacco, among others.

Home is Tubby Raymond Field at Delaware Stadium in Newark. The stadium dates to 1952 per CFBD. Capacity is conflicting: CFBD lists 22,000, while Wikipedia says 18,500 seats. The last five seasons: 5-6, 8-5, 9-4, 9-2 and 7-6.

The Coach and the Scheme
Ryan Carty has led the program since the 2022 season. CFBD lists his Delaware hire date as Dec. 10, 2021.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 29.1 points a game while giving up 27.5.

Players to Know
QB Nick Minicucci: 111 of 170, 1,477 yards, 14 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Jo Silver: 377 yards on 83 carries, 2 touchdowns.
WR Sean Wilson: 40 catches, 591 yards, 5 touchdowns.
LB Blake Matthews: 4.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Gavin Moul: a team-high 53 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Delaware is 4-4 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Delaware 3rd in the CUSA.
The season so far: W 42-7 vs Merrimack Warriors (Sept. 3), L 26-35 at Vanderbilt Commodores (Sept. 12), W 22-14 vs Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (Sept. 19), L 3-42 at Virginia Cavaliers (Sept. 26), L 14-30 vs Liberty Flames (Oct. 2), L 30-33 at Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders (Oct. 13), W 61-38 vs Missouri State Bears (Oct. 20), W 35-21 at Western Kentucky Hilltoppers (Oct. 27).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Kennesaw State (2-6, 1-3 Conference USA) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:00 p.m. ET, at Walens Family Field at Fifth Third Stadium, Kennesaw, Georgia, on ESPN+.
Last time out. We beat the Western Kentucky Hilltoppers 35-21 on the road on Oct. 27. Nick Minicucci went 32 of 38 for 414 yards, with 4 touchdowns, Jo Silver ran 13 times for 49 yards and Sean Wilson caught 9 balls for 183 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Kennesaw State beat the Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders 44-28 on the road on Oct. 28. Rickie Collins went 16 of 19 for 206 yards, with 3 touchdowns; Boogie Woods III ran 12 times for 67 yards and 2 touchdowns; Dylan Gary caught 2 balls for 59 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Players to watch. Kennesaw State quarterback Rickie Collins is 160 of 229 for 1,632 yards, with 7 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. Boogie Woods III leads their ground game with 358 yards on 85 carries and 6 scores. Javon Rogers is their top target: 26 catches for 397 yards. On defense, linebacker Tywon Christopher has 62 tackles and defensive lineman Ugonna Nwoha has 5 sacks.
The series. We did not meet Kennesaw State in the regular season from 2016 through 2025, per CFBD, so there is no recent history to go on.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, Delaware or Kennesaw State.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We walk into Kennesaw State's Homecoming, and Taste of Kennesaw runs from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. with food and live music if Blue Hen fans need a pregame stop.

My call: Kennesaw State Owls 27, Delaware Blue Hens 26.
