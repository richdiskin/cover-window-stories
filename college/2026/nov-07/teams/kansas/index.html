Salute to Service in Lawrence: Kansas Hosts UCF

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hi, everybody. I'm Fitz Calloway, your Kansas insider. I'm a lifelong Lawrence native and a hoops-first guy who converted to football analytics out of spite. I travel and live with this team, and I convert every football stat into basketball points. A touchdown is two and a half three-pointers. I refuse to stop.

Here's my promise. I'm self-deprecating, but I'm not sloppy. Every number in this report has a source, and when I do the math myself, I'll say so. If I don't know something about Kansas, I'll admit it. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I thank God for this town, this team and this stubborn hope. I'm only here because of spite and the stats. Okay, also love. Rock Chalk.

The School in 60 Seconds
Kansas football began in 1890, which makes it one of the oldest programs in the country. Home is David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium, which opened in 1921 and is one of the oldest college football stadiums anywhere. Until 2014 it had a running track around the field. CFBD lists 47,000 seats.

The last five seasons: 2-10 in 2021, then 6-7, a joyful 9-4 in 2023, and 5-7 twice. That 9-4 season is a memory I cherish.

The Coach and the Scheme
Lance Leipold has been head coach since 2021. CFBD lists his Kansas hire date as April 30, 2021. At Wisconsin-Whitewater from 2007 to 2014, he won six Division III national championships. He reached 100 wins faster than any coach in NCAA history, in his 106th game. Then he coached Buffalo from 2015 to 2020.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 29.1 points a game while giving up 23.8.

Players to Know
QB Isaiah Marshall: 105 of 196, 1,506 yards, 11 touchdowns, 7 interceptions.
RB Dylan Edwards: 573 yards on 125 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Nik McMillan: 25 catches, 466 yards, 5 touchdowns.
DE Leroy Harris III: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Trey Lathan: a team-high 55 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Kansas is 3-5 overall and 1-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have Kansas 14th in the Big 12.
The season so far: W 51-6 vs Long Island University Sharks (Sept. 4), L 21-38 vs Missouri Tigers (Sept. 11), W 55-0 vs Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders (Oct. 3), L 24-41 at Utah Utes (Oct. 10), L 24-35 at Kansas State Wildcats (Oct. 17), W 25-24 vs Baylor Bears (Oct. 24), L 16-22 at TCU Horned Frogs (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host UCF (4-4, 2-3 Big 12) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:45 p.m. ET, at David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium, Lawrence, Kansas, on ESPN2.
Last time out. We lost to the TCU Horned Frogs 22-16 on the road on Oct. 31. Cole Ballard went 20 of 35 for 285 yards, with 1 touchdown, Keaton Kubecka ran 3 times for 58 yards and Nahzae Cox caught 5 balls for 145 yards.
UCF lost to the Baylor Bears 41-9 at home on Oct. 30. Keyone Jenkins went 14 of 21 for 82 yards; Rocco Marriott ran 2 times for 50 yards; Josh Derry caught 3 balls for 70 yards.
Players to watch. UCF quarterback Keyone Jenkins is 122 of 180 for 1,181 yards, with 11 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Duke Watson leads their ground game with 597 yards on 92 carries and 3 scores. Josh Derry is their top target: 37 catches for 428 yards. On defense, linebacker Tackett Curtis has 48 tackles and defender Isaiah Nixon has 2.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 2 regular-season meetings with UCF from 2016 through 2025. We won 1 of them, and UCF won the last one 27-20 in 2025.
Availability. No official availability report for Kansas or UCF was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Knox Brevard-Stiles, UCF's insider. Here's what I said:
"Rock Chalk. Wait, it's FOOTBALL season?"
"In basketball we'd still be undefeated."
"I watched the end of that one. Somebody told me it was on."
And the last word from Knox: "It's good in anything, Fitz."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Kansas' Salute to Service game and the fifth home game at David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium, where we're 3-1 this year, including the 25-24 Homecoming comeback over Baylor.

My call: Kansas Jayhawks 29, UCF Knights 25.
