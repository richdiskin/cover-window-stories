Hoosiers at 8-1: Indiana Rests Up for a Big Date With USC

By Mallory Penhallow · Indiana Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Well hello, Hoosier Nation, and welcome! I'm Mallory Penhallow, former Miss Monroe County runner-up, and these days I run a 40-tab efficiency model on everything this football team does. I travel with the team, I live with the numbers, and yes, the clicker is rhinestone. My pageant answer to every question is the same: "It's the explosives, people." Each week I give one opponent my honorary Miss Congeniality sash. It goes to whoever I roast the hardest, and I always keep it clean. When we win, you get the full pageant wave. When we lose, it's "grace in defeat," and then I quietly open tab 41.

The School in 60 Seconds
Before Curt Cignetti showed up, Indiana had the most losses of any program in college football, 713 of them over 130-plus years. It had won three bowl games in its whole history, and the last one came in 1991. Then 2025 happened. The Hoosiers went 16-0 and beat Miami 27-21 at Hard Rock Stadium on Jan. 19, 2026, to win their first national championship. The last team to win 16 games without a loss was Yale in 1894. Quarterback Fernando Mendoza won the Heisman, and he scored the deciding touchdown himself on a 12-yard run on fourth-and-4. Cignetti's quote still sounds like an acceptance speech to me: "We won the national championship at Indiana University. It can be done."

The Coach and the Scheme
Cignetti was hired on Nov. 30, 2023. Part of his rebuild came from bringing 13 transfers with him from his old school, James Madison.

The 9-game numbers: 32.4 points per game scored, 12.4 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Josh Hoover: 124 of 206, 1,860 yards, 19 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB Turbo Richard: 745 yards on 113 carries, 6 touchdowns.
WR Charlie Becker: 50 catches, 875 yards, 9 touchdowns.
DL Tobi Osunsanmi: 10 sacks, the team lead.
LB Isaiah Jones: a team-high 67 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Indiana is 8-1 overall and 5-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have Indiana 2nd in the Big Ten.
The season so far: W 52-16 vs North Texas Mean Green (Sept. 5), W 55-0 vs Howard Bison (Sept. 12), W 38-0 vs Western Kentucky Hilltoppers (Sept. 19), W 29-23 vs Northwestern Wildcats (Sept. 25), W 47-15 at Rutgers Scarlet Knights (Oct. 3), W 20-17 at Nebraska Cornhuskers (Oct. 10), L 15-19 vs Ohio State Buckeyes (Oct. 17), W 13-6 at Michigan Wolverines (Oct. 24), W 23-16 vs Minnesota Golden Gophers (Oct. 31).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for Indiana this week. We're off from Tuesday, Nov. 3, through Saturday, Nov. 7, so the rest of this Indiana report is about where we stand and what's next.
Last time out. We beat the Minnesota Golden Gophers 23-16 at home on Oct. 31. Grant Wilson went 9 of 17 for 156 yards, with 1 touchdown. Lee Beebe Jr. ran 15 times for 84 yards. Charlie Becker caught 8 balls for 149 yards and 1 touchdown.
Up next. We play the USC Trojans at home on Saturday, Nov. 14, with the kickoff time still to be announced, at Memorial Stadium (Bloomington, IN).
Hoosiers, enjoy the quiet for exactly one week, because USC comes to town next and that one matters.
