Bearkats Crash Homecoming: Sam Houston Visits Jacksonville

By Lemuel "Lem" Coxwell · Sam Houston Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Evenin', folks. Pull up a stump. I'm Lemuel Coxwell, but everybody from here to the Trinity River calls me Lem, and I'm your Sam Houston insider. I'm a Walker County native and a high-school history teacher, and I collect Texas folklore the way some folks collect bottle caps. My students say I can turn a fire drill into a three-part saga. They're not wrong.

Here's how I'll tell it. I'll be patient, because seasons are long and young men grow. I'll be proud, because Huntsville is a small Texas town with a big Texas name, and our Bearkats play like they're carrying it. Most of all, I'll be honest. A good storyteller never needs to stretch the truth. Every number I give you will be real. If I don't know something about Sam Houston, I'll say so plain, then go dig through the archives.

The School in 60 Seconds
Sam Houston State fielded its first football team in 1912. Home is Elliott T. Bowers Stadium in Huntsville, which seats 14,000, and CFBD lists it as built in 1986. In 2021 the Bearkats left the Southland Conference for the WAC's FCS football league, and in November 2021 they accepted an invitation to join Conference USA at the FBS level starting in 2023-24.

The last five seasons tell a tale with some hills in it: 11-1 in 2021, 5-4 in 2022, 3-9 in 2023, 10-3 in 2024 and 2-10 in 2025. Every good story has a hard chapter before a good one.

The Coach and the Scheme
Phil Longo was named Sam Houston's 16th head coach on Dec. 18, 2024, so this is his second season. Wikipedia describes him as a practitioner of the air raid offense. He was previously offensive coordinator at Wisconsin, and before that offensive coordinator and quarterbacks coach at North Carolina, Ole Miss and Sam Houston.

The 8-game numbers: 18.0 points per game scored, 25.1 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Landyn Locke: 147 of 236, 1,653 yards, 12 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Alton McCaskill: 232 yards on 59 carries, 1 touchdown.
WR Tim Burns Jr.: 18 catches, 392 yards, 3 touchdowns.
DE Josh Alexander-Felton: 4 sacks, the team lead.
LB Dean Ford: a team-high 47 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Sam Houston is 1-7 overall and 0-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have Sam Houston 10th in the CUSA.
The season so far: L 21-24 at Troy Trojans (Sept. 5), L 17-23 vs Tulsa Golden Hurricane (Sept. 12), W 59-0 vs Nicholls Colonels (Sept. 19), L 14-49 at Texas Tech Red Raiders (Sept. 26), L 3-35 at Liberty Flames (Oct. 8), L 3-16 vs Western Kentucky Hilltoppers (Oct. 14), L 24-30 vs New Mexico State Aggies (Oct. 21), L 3-24 at Missouri State Bears (Oct. 27).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Jacksonville State (6-2, 4-0 Conference USA) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:00 p.m. ET, at AmFirst Stadium, Jacksonville, Alabama, on CBSSN.
Last time out. We lost to the Missouri State Bears 24-3 on the road on Oct. 27. DJ Bailey went 20 of 35 for 258 yards, MJ Dowdy ran 8 times for 18 yards and Grady O'Neill caught 6 balls for 74 yards.
Jacksonville State beat the New Mexico State Aggies 24-0 on the road on Oct. 28. Caden Creel went 11 of 17 for 109 yards, with 1 touchdown; Caden Creel ran 21 times for 211 yards and 1 touchdown; Luke Gilbert caught 5 balls for 67 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Jacksonville State quarterback Caden Creel is 140 of 209 for 1,741 yards, with 13 touchdowns and 8 interceptions. Caden Creel leads their ground game with 713 yards on 126 carries and 5 scores. Deondre Johnson is their top target: 23 catches for 438 yards. On defense, safety Caleb Nix has 54 tackles and defensive lineman Tae Floyd has 2 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 3 regular-season meetings with Jacksonville State from 2016 through 2025. We won 1 of them, and Jacksonville State won the last one 21-11 in 2024.
Availability. No official availability report for Sam Houston or Jacksonville State was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Burl Ashcraft-Neeley, Jacksonville State's insider. Here's what I said:
"I'll be honest with you. It has been a long fall in Huntsville."
"Then the offense went cold. Three points at Liberty. Three against Western Kentucky. Three at Missouri State. I have told some tall tales in my classroom, and none of them were as hard to explain as that."
And the last word from Burl: "And we won the last three. 35-28, 21-11 and 29-27."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We walk into Jacksonville State's Homecoming, "Cocky Reigns Supreme," so the parade crowd will be in the stands at AmFirst Stadium.

My call: Jacksonville State Gamecocks 30, Sam Houston Bearkats 15.
