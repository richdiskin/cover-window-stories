Tide Rolls Into Baton Rouge: Alabama Takes On Death Valley

By Mary-Kathryn 'MK' Strickland · Alabama Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Roll Tide, and I mean that as a greeting. Mostly. I'm Mary-Kathryn Strickland, but everyone from the Quad to the press box calls me MK. I rode for the Tide equestrian team, I read blitz pickups the way some girls read Southern Living, and I can quote a legendary coaching maxim for every situation, perfectly, usually at dinner. My family has asked me to stop. I will not stop. I travel with this Alabama team and I live with it, and every week you'll get the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines with a little Tuscaloosa swagger. When we win, you'll hear "Roll Tide" in seventeen different tones, each more satisfied than the last. When we lose, I'll say "We will not speak of it," and I will not speak of it.

The School in 60 Seconds
Alabama claims 18 national championships, 13 of them wire-service titles from the poll era. Paul "Bear" Bryant won six from 1958 to 1982, and Nick Saban won six more between 2007 and 2023. The Tide's Heisman winners are Mark Ingram II (2009), Derrick Henry (2015), DeVonta Smith (2020) and Bryce Young (2021). Alabama has 30 SEC championships, an NCAA-record 79 bowl appearances and 46 bowl wins, also a record, plus a record 16 straight 10-win seasons from 2008 through 2023. Bryant-Denny Stadium seats 101,821 and opened in 1929. Coach Bryant has a maxim for this, and I'll save it for dinner.

The Coach and the Scheme
Kalen DeBoer was hired Jan. 12, 2024, and his first two Tide teams went 9-4 and 11-4.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 38.2 points a game while giving up 21.2.

Players to Know
QB Keelon Russell: 148 of 221, 2,057 yards, 15 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Daniel Hill: 332 yards on 71 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Lotzeir Brooks: 29 catches, 488 yards, 1 touchdown.
LB Yhonzae Pierre: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Caleb Woodson: a team-high 63 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Alabama is 6-2 overall and 4-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Alabama 3rd in the SEC.
The season so far: W 48-10 vs East Carolina Pirates (Sept. 5), W 45-17 at Kentucky Wildcats (Sept. 12), W 50-36 vs Florida State Seminoles (Sept. 19), W 49-18 vs South Carolina Gamecocks (Sept. 26), W 56-23 at Mississippi State Bulldogs (Oct. 3), L 17-20 vs Georgia Bulldogs (Oct. 10), L 10-26 at Tennessee Volunteers (Oct. 17), W 31-20 vs Texas A&M Aggies (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to No. 15 LSU (6-2, 3-2 SEC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:30 p.m. ET, at Tiger Stadium (LA), Baton Rouge, Louisiana, on ABC. We come in ranked No. 9.
Last time out. We beat the Texas A&M Aggies 31-20 at home on Oct. 24. Keelon Russell went 19 of 30 for 311 yards, with 3 touchdowns, Daniel Hill ran 9 times for 36 yards and Rico Scott caught 6 balls for 156 yards.
LSU beat the Auburn Tigers 38-17 on the road on Oct. 24. Sam Leavitt went 32 of 45 for 388 yards, with 3 touchdowns; Dilin Jones ran 21 times for 91 yards and 1 touchdown; Jackson Harris caught 9 balls for 178 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Players to watch. LSU quarterback Sam Leavitt is 180 of 256 for 2,173 yards, with 17 touchdowns and 8 interceptions. Dilin Jones leads their ground game with 657 yards on 149 carries and 6 scores. Jackson Harris is their top target: 36 catches for 634 yards. On defense, linebacker TJ Dottery has 58 tackles and defensive end Princewill Umanmielen has 8.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 10 regular-season meetings with LSU from 2016 through 2025. We won 5 of them, and LSU won the last one 20-9 in 2025.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Alabama vs. LSU. When Alabama or LSU puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We roll into Baton Rouge on a Saturday when LSU's Mike's Kids Club Tailgate fills the Fan Zone outside the PMAC, which means the next generation of Tiger fans will be screaming at us, too.

My call: LSU Tigers 31, Alabama Crimson Tide 27.
