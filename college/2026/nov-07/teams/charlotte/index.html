Niners in Birmingham: Charlotte Takes On UAB at Protective Stadium

By Priya Halloran · Charlotte Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Price that drive! I'm Priya Halloran, and I'm your Charlotte insider. I'm an uptown bank risk analyst who prices every drive like a loan, interest rate and all. On the podcast, that segment is called Drive Ledger. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate Charlotte players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no Charlotte fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Jerry Richardson Stadium in Charlotte, built in 2013 with a listed capacity of 15,300. The band is the Pride of Niner Nation Marching Band. The last five seasons went 5-7, 3-9, 3-9, 5-7 and 1-11, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about the 49ers name, from the 1946 founding. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Tim Albin leads the 49ers, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 8, 2024.

Through 8 games this season, Charlotte is scoring 20.6 points per game and allowing 40.2.

Players to Know
QB Conner Harrell: 74 of 108, 831 yards, 5 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB Henry Rutledge: 452 yards on 74 carries, 1 touchdown.
WR Jaden Barnes: 48 catches, 462 yards, 1 touchdown.
DL DJ Burgess: 2 sacks, the team lead.
LB Gavin Willis: a team-high 65 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Charlotte is 1-7 overall and 1-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have Charlotte 10th in the American.
The season so far: L 41-43 (OT) vs The Citadel Bulldogs (Sept. 5), L 9-41 at Ole Miss Rebels (Sept. 12), L 21-26 at App State Mountaineers (Sept. 19), L 7-34 vs Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns (Sept. 26), L 8-59 vs Memphis Tigers (Oct. 3), L 10-50 at North Texas Mean Green (Oct. 10), W 38-31 at Temple Owls (Oct. 17), L 31-38 (OT) vs Tulane Green Wave (Oct. 30).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to UAB (4-4, 2-2 American) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:30 p.m. ET, at Protective Stadium, Birmingham, Alabama, on ESPN+.
Last time out. We lost to the Tulane Green Wave 38-31 (OT) at home on Oct. 30. Conner Harrell went 23 of 36 for 239 yards, with 2 touchdowns, Henry Rutledge ran 22 times for 121 yards and Henry Rutledge caught 4 balls for 79 yards.
UAB lost to the South Florida Bulls 30-10 on the road on Oct. 31. Ryder Burton went 13 of 27 for 149 yards; Bam McReynolds ran 8 times for 22 yards; CJ Smith caught 2 balls for 53 yards.
Players to watch. UAB quarterback Ryder Burton is 104 of 194 for 1,333 yards, with 6 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Rod Robinson II leads their ground game with 450 yards on 108 carries and 1 score. CJ Smith is their top target: 21 catches for 403 yards. On defense, linebacker Muaaz Byard has 76 tackles and linebacker Marlin Dean has 7.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 4 regular-season meetings with UAB from 2016 through 2025. We won 0 of them, and UAB won the last one 29-27 in 2024.
Availability. The public injury data for UAB and Charlotte is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Ozella Birchfield, UAB's insider. Here's what I said:
"That's a beautiful tradition. I'm still wearing teal. I'll clap in teal."
"The first five games were a write-down. Since then we're a turnaround story. I'd hold."
"Your team lost 10-30 at South Florida last week."
And the last word from Ozella: "Two and two. Somebody breaks it Saturday."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We walk into UAB's 10th annual Children's Harbor Game, where the Blazers are 8-1 all-time and wear the names of 99 kids on their backs, so the building will be emotional and loud.

My call: UAB Blazers 33, Charlotte 49ers 22.
