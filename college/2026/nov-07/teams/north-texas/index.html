80th Homecoming in Denton: North Texas Hosts Rice

By Grady Mayhew-Ochoa · North Texas Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Mean Green, baby! I'm Grady Mayhew-Ochoa, your North Texas insider, coming at you from Denton, a town with more music going on per square block than just about anywhere in Texas. I'm a sports nerd and an indie-rock nerd, and I hear football the way I hear a record. Every offense has a tempo. Some are steady four-on-the-floor. Ours? Ours is in 7/8 time, and the defense keeps counting wrong. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, with liner notes.

The School in 60 Seconds
North Texas plays at DATCU Stadium in Denton, listed at 30,850. Eric Morris left for Oklahoma State, and Neal Brown took over. Mean Green, baby.

The Coach and the Scheme
Neal Brown was named the 21st head coach in North Texas history on Dec. 2, 2025. He had 10 seasons as an FBS head coach at Troy and West Virginia and went 72-51. At Troy he won the 2017 Sun Belt title and was the conference's Coach of the Year, and his 2016-18 teams went 31-8. His 2023 West Virginia team went 9-4. He's 5-1 in bowl games.

The 8-game numbers: 43.8 points per game scored, 31.9 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Tayven Jackson: 193 of 267, 2,577 yards, 22 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Jahiem White: 1,054 yards on 142 carries, 13 touchdowns.
WR Justin Stevenson: 50 catches, 781 yards, 8 touchdowns.
DL Davion Rhodes: 4 sacks, the team lead.
LB Zakye Barker: a team-high 62 tackles.

The Conference Picture
North Texas is 6-2 overall and 4-0 in league play. ESPN's standings have North Texas 1st in the American.
The season so far: L 16-52 at Indiana Hoosiers (Sept. 5), W 44-6 vs UNLV Rebels (Sept. 12), L 35-49 at Texas State Bobcats (Sept. 19), W 63-14 vs Houston Christian Huskies (Sept. 26), W 45-44 (OT) at Tulsa Golden Hurricane (Oct. 1), W 50-10 vs Charlotte 49ers (Oct. 10), W 49-39 at Navy Midshipmen (Oct. 24), W 48-41 vs Florida Atlantic Owls (Oct. 29).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Rice (1-7, 0-4 American) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 4:00 p.m. ET, at DATCU Stadium, Denton, Texas, on ESPNU.
Last time out. We beat the Florida Atlantic Owls 48-41 at home on Oct. 29. Tayven Jackson went 33 of 46 for 404 yards, with 4 touchdowns, Jahiem White ran 26 times for 193 yards and 2 touchdowns and Justin Stevenson caught 9 balls for 119 yards and 1 touchdown.
Rice lost to the Florida Atlantic Owls 51-49 on the road on Oct. 24. Gael Ochoa went 2 of 5 for 80 yards, with 1 touchdown; Gael Ochoa ran 12 times for 259 yards and 4 touchdowns; Braylen Walker caught 1 balls for 71 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Rice quarterback Jacurri Brown is 40 of 83 for 478 yards, with 3 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. Jacurri Brown leads their ground game with 669 yards on 118 carries and 6 scores. Jordan Clark is their top target: 20 catches for 257 yards. On defense, safety AJ Brown has 59 tackles and defensive lineman Chibby Nwajuaku has 2 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 8 regular-season meetings with Rice from 2016 through 2025. We won 4 of them, and Rice won the last one 56-24 in 2025.
Availability. The public injury data for Rice and North Texas is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Odalys Chen-Moreau, Rice's insider. Here's what I said:
"It's the Mean Green fight tempo. Very fast. You'll see."
"You hear that? She respects something."
"That's bebop. Four hundred yards. A drum solo every drive."
And the last word from Odalys: "Eight games. Still small. I'm writing it down anyway."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's North Texas' 80th annual Homecoming, a tradition held every season since 1946, and the Mean Green are 53-25-1 all-time on Homecoming.

My call: North Texas Mean Green 42, Rice Owls 24.
