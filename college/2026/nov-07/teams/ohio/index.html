Bobcats Under the Tuesday Lights: Ohio Visits Akron

By Penny Trautman · Ohio Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Brick by brick, friends. I'm Penny Trautman, and I live in Athens, Ohio, where the streets are brick, the hills are green, and the Bobcats run the football like they're laying Court Street, one sturdy block at a time. I'm a brick-street archivist by hobby (it's a real hobby; I have a binder), and I chart this run game the same way. I travel with this Ohio team and I live with it, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines with some hill-country wit and an unreasonable number of brick puns. When we win, I tap a brick for luck and grin. When we lose, I say "brick by brick" and start rebuilding by Monday. Welcome to the porch. One more thing: I'll always be honest with you, even when the mortar's cracked.

The School in 60 Seconds
Ohio is the senior member of the Mid-American Conference. The Bobcats have played home games at Peden Stadium since 1929, and CFBD lists it at 24,000 seats. The recent run has been one of the steadiest in the league: 10-4 in 2022, 10-3 in 2023, 11-3 in 2024 and 9-4 in 2025. That's four straight winning seasons stacked like good masonry.

The Coach and the Scheme
John Hauser is in his first season as head coach; CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 26, 2025.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 29.6 points a game while giving up 21.9.

Players to Know
QB Nick Poulos: 58 of 98, 781 yards, 6 touchdowns.
RB Victor Rosa: 364 yards on 77 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Riley Neer: 37 catches, 497 yards, 5 touchdowns.
LB Michael Molnar: 5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Michael Molnar: a team-high 70 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Ohio is 6-2 overall and 4-0 in league play. ESPN's standings have Ohio 1st in the MAC.
The season so far: L 21-49 at Nebraska Cornhuskers (Sept. 5), W 29-27 (OT) vs Jacksonville State Gamecocks (Sept. 12), L 36-41 at South Alabama Jaguars (Sept. 19), W 35-7 vs Stonehill Skyhawks (Sept. 26), W 13-10 at Kent State Golden Flashes (Oct. 3), W 31-14 vs Central Michigan Chippewas (Oct. 10), W 48-7 at Sacramento State Hornets (Oct. 17), W 24-20 vs Eastern Michigan Eagles (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Akron (1-7, 0-4 MAC) on Tuesday, Nov. 3, at 7:00 p.m. ET, at InfoCision Stadium, Akron, Ohio, on ESPN2.
Last time out. We beat the Eastern Michigan Eagles 24-20 at home on Oct. 24. Nick Poulos went 19 of 29 for 266 yards, with 1 touchdown, Victor Rosa ran 4 times for 40 yards and Dom Dorwart caught 4 balls for 125 yards and 1 touchdown.
Akron lost to the Kent State Golden Flashes 35-20 on the road on Oct. 24. Brayden Roggow went 20 of 34 for 275 yards; Jordan Gant ran 28 times for 162 yards and 2 touchdowns; Kyan Mason caught 7 balls for 129 yards.
Players to watch. Akron quarterback Brayden Roggow is 112 of 190 for 1,343 yards, with 7 touchdowns and 9 interceptions. Jordan Gant leads their ground game with 618 yards on 127 carries and 6 scores. Marcel Williams is their top target: 34 catches for 375 yards. On defense, linebacker Donelius Johnson has 71 tackles and defensive lineman Cyrus Durham has 2.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 9 regular-season meetings with Akron from 2016 through 2025. We won 3 of them, and Akron won the last one 30-10 in 2024.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Ohio vs. Akron. When Ohio or Akron puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Hollister Grubbs, Akron's insider. Here's what I said:
"I voted early, I ate early, and I'm sitting early. You know what this is, Zip? This is a Bobcats team that's 6-2 and 4-0 in the MAC. We drove up from the hills to keep building."
"Low on air. is a polite way to say you've lost seven of eight."
"Every one of those was a brick in the wall. Some bricks are pretty. Some are just heavy."
And the last word from Hollister: "If he hits Mason down the far hash. you'll be tapping that brick so hard it turns to gravel."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We get a Tuesday night at InfoCision Stadium, and Akron is calling it Taco Tuesday, so Bobcat fans should show up hungry and loud.

My call: Ohio Bobcats 32, Akron Zips 21.
