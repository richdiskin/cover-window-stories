All Roads Lead to Neyland: Tennessee Celebrates Homecoming Against Kentucky

By Earl 'Rocky Top' Haskew · Tennessee Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
(banjo: plink-a-plink-plink.) Cue the banjo. Howdy, Big Orange Country, I'm Earl Haskew, but everybody on Cumberland Avenue calls me Rocky Top. I was a long snapper for the Vols, which means I spent four years looking at the world upside down and backward, and honestly it gave me a great perspective on defensive fronts. I travel with this team and live with this team, and before every stat I play a little banjo intro. Sometimes the banjo's out of tune on purpose. You'll know when. Every week you'll get the players, the matchups, the history and a few checkerboard metaphors from a fella who loves the end zones at Neyland more than his own front porch. When we win, I play "Rocky Top" until somebody stops me, and nobody ever does. When we lose, I play it slow and sad. Pull up a rocking chair.

The School in 60 Seconds
(plink.) Tennessee started playing football in 1891. The Vols claim six national titles, including two from the wire-service polls in 1951 and 1998, and 16 conference championships. We rank fifth all-time in bowl appearances, 55 of them, with four Sugar Bowls, three Cotton Bowls, two Orange Bowls, a Fiesta and a Peach among the wins. Neyland Stadium is the cathedral, and the Vols have won 485 games there, the most any program has won in its current home. Capacity is listed at 101,915 by one source and 102,455 by CFBD, so I'll just say "a whole lot of orange" and show you both. Josh Heupel's teams have gone 11-2, 9-4, 10-3 and 8-5 since 2022.

The Coach and the Scheme
Heupel was hired Jan. 27, 2021.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 32.2 points a game while giving up 16.2.

Players to Know
QB George MacIntyre: 69 of 103, 728 yards, 2 touchdowns, 3 interceptions.
RB DeSean Bishop: 957 yards on 171 carries, 11 touchdowns.
WR Mike Matthews: 34 catches, 528 yards, 3 touchdowns.
DL Tyree Weathersby: 7 sacks, the team lead.
LB Amare Campbell: a team-high 52 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Tennessee is 6-2 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Tennessee 5th in the SEC.
The season so far: W 56-9 vs Furman Paladins (Sept. 5), W 45-24 at Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets (Sept. 12), W 42-9 vs Kennesaw State Owls (Sept. 19), L 17-20 vs Texas Longhorns (Sept. 26), W 24-14 vs Auburn Tigers (Oct. 3), W 24-17 at Arkansas Razorbacks (Oct. 10), W 26-10 vs Alabama Crimson Tide (Oct. 17), L 24-27 at South Carolina Gamecocks (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host No. 8 Kentucky (7-1, 5-1 SEC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 6:15 p.m. ET, at Neyland Stadium, Knoxville, Tennessee, on ESPN. We come in ranked No. 16.
Last time out. We lost to the South Carolina Gamecocks 27-24 on the road on Oct. 24. George MacIntyre went 16 of 23 for 136 yards, DeSean Bishop ran 32 times for 120 yards and 1 touchdown and Braylon Staley caught 6 balls for 56 yards.
Kentucky beat the Vanderbilt Commodores 34-17 at home on Oct. 24. Kenny Minchey went 19 of 26 for 203 yards, with 1 touchdown; Jovantae Barnes ran 15 times for 68 yards; Kenny Darby caught 4 balls for 71 yards.
Players to watch. Kentucky quarterback Kenny Minchey is 160 of 232 for 1,755 yards, with 14 touchdowns and 1 interception. CJ Baxter leads their ground game with 487 yards on 88 carries and 8 scores. Kenny Darby is their top target: 38 catches for 507 yards. On defense, linebacker Grant Godfrey has 72 tackles and defensive lineman Mi'Quise Humphrey-Grace has 5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 10 regular-season meetings with Kentucky from 2016 through 2025. We won 5 of them, and Kentucky won the last one 56-34 in 2025.
Availability. The public injury data for Kentucky and Tennessee is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Rhett Abernathy-Coyle, Kentucky's insider. Here's what I said:
"Paper doesn't play in Neyland."
"Tennessee is 6-2. We give up 16.2 a game. We held Alabama to 10 points at home. We lost to Texas by three and at South Carolina by three. That's it."
"George doesn't need to be a hero. Hand it to Bishop. Let the defense work. That's how we beat Alabama 26-10."
And the last word from Rhett: "Two overtime wins. You know what that tells me? We don't lose close games."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Homecoming, "All Roads Lead to Tennessee," with Monica Abbott as grand marshal, the parade rolling down Volunteer Boulevard to Neyland at 4 p.m. Friday, and the Vols in their Dark Mode alternates.

My call: Tennessee Volunteers 30, Kentucky Wildcats 23.
