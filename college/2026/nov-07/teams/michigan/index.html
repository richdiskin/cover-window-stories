Blue Out in the Big House: Michigan Hosts Michigan State Under the Lights

By Gus Hartwell · Michigan Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Greetings, Wolverines, and welcome. I am Augustin Hartwell, but everybody calls me Gus. You'll know me by the maize sweater, the engineering-school pin and the coffee mug that says VICTORS. I travel with this team, and I label my data with fight-song lyrics. "Champions of the West" is a perfectly good column header, and I expect Dr. Ingrid Solvang, our Big Ten expert, to accept it. My motto is "Hail to the spreadsheet." When we win, I belt out "Hail to the Victors," off-key and proud. When we lose: "Victors. valiant. later." Then I head to the library.

The School in 60 Seconds
Michigan played its first game on May 30, 1879, and it has more wins than any other program in college football. The Wolverines claim 12 national championships, most recently in 2023. That 2023 team went 15-0, won the program's 45th Big Ten title, and beat Washington 34-13 for the national title. Three Wolverines have won the Heisman: Tom Harmon (1940), Desmond Howard (1991) and Charles Woodson (1997). Michigan Stadium has been home since 1927 and set the stadium's attendance record of 115,109 against Notre Dame on Sept. 7, 2013. Official sources list two different capacities, 107,601 and 109,901, so we note both.

The Coach and the Scheme
Kyle Whittingham was named Michigan's 22nd head coach on Dec. 26, 2025, with a five-year contract that runs through 2030. He spent 21 seasons as Utah's head coach, went 177-88 there, and had eight seasons with 10 or more wins. That makes him the 22nd head coach in a program history spanning 146 years, and he took over after Michigan fired Sherrone Moore. The standard he inherits is enormous: Michigan has more all-time wins than any program in college football, and the record crowd at Michigan Stadium is 115,109, set against Notre Dame on Sept. 7, 2013.

Through 8 games this season, Michigan is scoring 21.2 points per game and allowing 16.4.

The Conference Picture
Michigan is 5-3 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have Michigan 11th in the Big Ten.
The season so far: W 13-12 vs Western Michigan Broncos (Sept. 5), W 17-10 vs Oklahoma Sooners (Sept. 12), W 52-17 vs UTEP Miners (Sept. 19), L 19-20 vs Iowa Hawkeyes (Sept. 26), L 14-20 at Minnesota Golden Gophers (Oct. 3), W 21-18 vs Penn State Nittany Lions (Oct. 17), L 6-13 (OT) vs Indiana Hoosiers (Oct. 24), W 28-21 at Rutgers Scarlet Knights (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Michigan State (2-6, 0-5 Big Ten) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:30 p.m. ET, at Michigan Stadium, Ann Arbor, Michigan, on Peacock.
Last time out. We beat the Rutgers Scarlet Knights 28-21 on the road on Oct. 31. Tommy Carr went 22 of 27 for 266 yards, with 1 touchdown, Savion Hiter ran 20 times for 76 yards and JJ Buchanan caught 5 balls for 63 yards and 1 touchdown.
Michigan State lost to the UCLA Bruins 28-10 on the road on Oct. 24. Leo Hannan went 9 of 15 for 67 yards; Kenneth Williams ran 17 times for 54 yards; Chrishon McCray caught 3 balls for 42 yards.
Players to watch. Michigan State quarterback Alessio Milivojevic is 121 of 185 for 1,263 yards, with 7 touchdowns and 3 interceptions. Jaziun Patterson leads their ground game with 327 yards on 76 carries and 5 scores. Chrishon McCray is their top target: 29 catches for 289 yards. On defense, safety Devin Vaught has 52 tackles and defensive lineman Eli Coenen has 3.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 10 regular-season meetings with Michigan State from 2016 through 2025. We won 4 of them, and Michigan State won the last one 31-20 in 2025.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Michigan vs. Michigan State. When Michigan or Michigan State puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Shay Kowalczyk, Michigan State's insider. Here's what I said:
"A hundred thousand people dressed like winners. Wear green if you want to be easy to find."
"You lost 3-31 at Wisconsin."
"Our defense held Penn State to 18. Iowa got 20. Indiana got 13 in overtime. Nobody runs on us."
And the last word from Shay: "Every few years the green shows up. That's the deal in this state."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's the Blue Out and Military Appreciation Game, with an extended Michigan Marching Band pregame, flags of the six military branches, American flags on all 16 flag poles and more than 150 Michigan Football alumni on the field.

My call: Michigan Wolverines 26, Michigan State Spartans 15.
