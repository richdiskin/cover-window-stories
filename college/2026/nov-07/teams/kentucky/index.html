Cats Crash Homecoming: Kentucky Takes On Rocky Top

By Rhett Abernathy-Coyle · Kentucky Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Pedigree tells. Good evening, friends, I'm Rhett Abernathy-Coyle. I grew up on a horse farm out past Keeneland, mucking stalls before school and watching yearlings run in the morning mist. I learned early that you can tell a great deal about a horse from its bloodlines, and I grade football players the same way a Derby breeder grades a foal. I travel with the Wildcats and I live with the Wildcats, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines, with a Southern gentleman's flourish. When we win, you'll hear my best Derby announcer: "And down the stretch they come!" When we lose, I'll say, "Even the best horses stumble," and adjust my hat. Now, pull up a chair, and let's talk bloodlines.

The School in 60 Seconds
The Wildcats represent the University of Kentucky in the SEC. Home is Kroger Field in Lexington, opened in 1973 and listed at 61,000 seats. The last five seasons: a fine 10-3 in 2021, then 7-6, 7-6, 4-8 and 5-7.

The Coach and the Scheme
Will Stein is our first-year head coach; CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 1, 2025.

The 8-game numbers: 32.9 points per game scored, 24.8 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Kenny Minchey: 160 of 232, 1,755 yards, 14 touchdowns, 1 interception.
RB CJ Baxter: 487 yards on 88 carries, 8 touchdowns.
WR Kenny Darby: 38 catches, 507 yards, 3 touchdowns.
DL Mi'Quise Humphrey-Grace: 5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Grant Godfrey: a team-high 72 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Kentucky is 7-1 overall and 5-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have Kentucky 1st in the SEC.
The season so far: W 45-13 vs Youngstown State Penguins (Sept. 5), L 17-45 vs Alabama Crimson Tide (Sept. 12), W 31-21 at Texas A&M Aggies (Sept. 19), W 45-21 vs South Alabama Jaguars (Sept. 26), W 35-34 (OT) at South Carolina Gamecocks (Oct. 3), W 35-34 (OT) vs LSU Tigers (Oct. 10), W 21-13 at Oklahoma Sooners (Oct. 17), W 34-17 vs Vanderbilt Commodores (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to No. 16 Tennessee (6-2, 3-2 SEC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 6:15 p.m. ET, at Neyland Stadium, Knoxville, Tennessee, on ESPN. We come in ranked No. 8.
Last time out. We beat the Vanderbilt Commodores 34-17 at home on Oct. 24. Kenny Minchey went 19 of 26 for 203 yards, with 1 touchdown, Jovantae Barnes ran 15 times for 68 yards and Kenny Darby caught 4 balls for 71 yards.
Tennessee lost to the South Carolina Gamecocks 27-24 on the road on Oct. 24. George MacIntyre went 16 of 23 for 136 yards; DeSean Bishop ran 32 times for 120 yards and 1 touchdown; Braylon Staley caught 6 balls for 56 yards.
Players to watch. Tennessee quarterback George MacIntyre is 69 of 103 for 728 yards, with 2 touchdowns and 3 interceptions. DeSean Bishop leads their ground game with 957 yards on 171 carries and 11 scores. Mike Matthews is their top target: 34 catches for 528 yards. On defense, linebacker Amare Campbell has 52 tackles and defensive lineman Tyree Weathersby has 7 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 10 regular-season meetings with Tennessee from 2016 through 2025. We won 5 of them, and we won the last one 56-34 in 2025.
Availability. No official availability report for Kentucky or Tennessee was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Earl 'Rocky Top' Haskew, Tennessee's insider. Here's what I said:
"Here's the thing nobody in Knoxville wants to hear. We're 7-1. We're the better team on paper."
"Kenny Minchey has thrown one interception all year. One. He has 1,755 yards and 14 touchdowns. He doesn't panic."
"What about the quarterback?"
And the last word from Rocky Top: "Everybody remembers the one they won."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We walk into Tennessee's Homecoming, with the Europa and the Bull fountain dyed orange and the Vols in Dark Mode alternates, and nothing would shut Neyland up faster than a Kentucky win.

My call: Tennessee Volunteers 30, Kentucky Wildcats 23.
