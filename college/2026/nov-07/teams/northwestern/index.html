Meet Your Insider
Hello, Wildcats, and welcome! I'm Ezra Lindqvist, your Northwestern insider. I'm a data journalist by training, which means I fact-check the coaches in real time. Politely. Mostly politely. I travel with this Northwestern team and I live with it, and I'm fully aware that I'm doing the "smartest kid in the Big Ten" bit. I footnote my own jokes¹, and then I footnote the footnotes². The School in 60 Seconds
Northwestern opened 2026 with two games at Martin Stadium, right on the shore of Lake Michigan, and then opened the brand-new Ryan Field against Penn State on Oct. 2. Northwestern won that one 34-13. Correction to my earlier draft: there was no earlier draft. The coach is David Braun, who became interim coach in 2023, was named the permanent head coach on Nov. 15 of that year and was the consensus Big Ten Coach of the Year. Footnote: that's a very good first season.

The Coach and the Scheme
Braun's start date in the coaching database is July 10, 2023.

Through 8 games this season, Northwestern is scoring 33.6 points per game and allowing 18.8.

The Conference Picture
Northwestern is 6-2 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Northwestern 5th in the Big Ten.
The season so far: W 34-18 vs South Dakota State Jackrabbits (Sept. 5), W 41-7 vs Colorado Buffaloes (Sept. 19), L 23-29 at Indiana Hoosiers (Sept. 25), W 34-13 vs Penn State Nittany Lions (Oct. 2), W 45-14 vs Ball State Cardinals (Oct. 10), W 24-21 at Michigan State Spartans (Oct. 17), W 42-14 vs Rutgers Scarlet Knights (Oct. 24), L 26-34 at Oregon Ducks (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host No. 21 Iowa (6-2, 3-2 Big Ten) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:30 p.m. ET, at Ryan Field, Evanston, Illinois, on Fox.
Last time out. We lost to the Oregon Ducks 34-26 on the road on Oct. 31. Aidan Chiles went 22 of 38 for 281 yards, with 1 touchdown, Aidan Chiles ran 14 times for 93 yards and 1 touchdown and Griffin Wilde caught 5 balls for 146 yards and 1 touchdown.
Iowa beat the Wisconsin Badgers 30-0 at home on Oct. 31. Hank Brown went 20 of 24 for 222 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Xavier Williams ran 20 times for 78 yards; Tony Diaz caught 4 balls for 75 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Iowa quarterback Hank Brown is 109 of 169 for 1,190 yards, with 8 touchdowns and 3 interceptions. Kamari Moulton leads their ground game with 541 yards on 86 carries and 4 scores. Tony Diaz is their top target: 33 catches for 461 yards. On defense, linebacker Cam Buffington has 70 tackles and defensive end Joseph Anderson has 4 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 8 regular-season meetings with Iowa from 2016 through 2025. We won 3 of them, and we won the last one 40-14 in 2024.
Availability. The public injury data for Iowa and Northwestern is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Orrin Kalvelage, Iowa's insider. Here's what I said:
"The football is 6-2. Same as yours. And we haven't lost at home."
"We scored 26 at Oregon. You'd be lucky to score 26 against a high school."
"Wisconsin scores zero against everybody."
And the last word from Orrin: "I can block out anything. I'm from Iowa. We're patient people."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
The new 35,000-seat Ryan Field opened last month after 2 2/3 years of construction, and we're 5-0 at home this season.

My call: Northwestern Wildcats 24, Iowa Hawkeyes 21.
