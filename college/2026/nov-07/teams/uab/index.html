Children's Harbor Game: UAB Hosts Charlotte With 99 Names on Its Back

By Ozella Birchfield · UAB Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Forged, not given! I'm Ozella Birchfield, and I'm your UAB insider. I'm a Birmingham native, a steel-family granddaughter and a community-theater actor. On the podcast, my segment is called Forge Report. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate UAB players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no UAB fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Protective Stadium in Birmingham, built in 2021 with a listed capacity of 45,000. The band is the UAB Marching Blazers. The last five seasons went 9-4, 7-6, 4-8, 3-9 and 4-8, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about Blaze the dragon. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Alex Mortensen leads the Blazers, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 5, 2025.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 21.6 points a game while giving up 25.2.

Players to Know
QB Ryder Burton: 104 of 194, 1,333 yards, 6 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Rod Robinson II: 450 yards on 108 carries, 1 touchdown.
WR CJ Smith: 21 catches, 403 yards, 1 touchdown.
LB Marlin Dean: 7.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Muaaz Byard: a team-high 76 tackles.

The Conference Picture
UAB is 4-4 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have UAB 5th in the American.
The season so far: L 23-42 at Illinois Fighting Illini (Sept. 3), W 26-20 vs UL Monroe Warhawks (Sept. 12), L 14-21 at Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns (Sept. 19), W 24-20 vs Navy Midshipmen (Sept. 25), W 33-14 vs Samford Bulldogs (Oct. 3), L 13-38 at Memphis Tigers (Oct. 10), W 30-17 (OT) vs East Carolina Pirates (Oct. 15), L 10-30 at South Florida Bulls (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Charlotte (1-7, 1-3 American) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:30 p.m. ET, at Protective Stadium, Birmingham, Alabama, on ESPN+.
Last time out. We lost to the South Florida Bulls 30-10 on the road on Oct. 31. Ryder Burton went 13 of 27 for 149 yards, Bam McReynolds ran 8 times for 22 yards and CJ Smith caught 2 balls for 53 yards.
Charlotte lost to the Tulane Green Wave 38-31 (OT) at home on Oct. 30. Conner Harrell went 23 of 36 for 239 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Henry Rutledge ran 22 times for 121 yards; Henry Rutledge caught 4 balls for 79 yards.
Players to watch. Charlotte quarterback Conner Harrell is 74 of 108 for 831 yards, with 5 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. Henry Rutledge leads their ground game with 452 yards on 74 carries and 1 score. Jaden Barnes is their top target: 48 catches for 462 yards. On defense, linebacker Gavin Willis has 65 tackles and defensive lineman DJ Burgess has 2 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 4 regular-season meetings with Charlotte from 2016 through 2025. We won 4 of them, and we won the last one 29-27 in 2024.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for UAB vs. Charlotte. When UAB or Charlotte puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Priya Halloran, Charlotte's insider. Here's what I said:
"I'm going to cry before kickoff. Then I'm going to yell. In that order. Every year."
"Marlin Dean is a blast furnace. You open the door, you get burned."
"And we beat Navy 24-20 in September. Furnaces have off days. The fire doesn't go out."
And the last word from Priya: "I know that figure. I put it in red. It has been in red since September."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Kids Takeover and the 10th annual Children's Harbor Game, and our players will wear gray jerseys carrying the names of 99 children served by Children's Harbor, so wear gray.

My call: UAB Blazers 33, Charlotte 49ers 22.
