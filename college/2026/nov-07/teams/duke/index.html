Blue Devils in Raleigh: Duke Takes the Short Ride to Carter-Finley

By Harrison Wexley-Moore IV · Duke Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Good evening. I'm Harrison Wexley-Moore IV, your Duke insider. I'm pre-law, pre-med and, frankly, pre-everything. Did I mention I also passed that exam? I did. Whichever one you're thinking of.

Here's my academic honor code for this column. Every fact has a citation, like any good thesis. If I don't know something about Duke, I'll mark it "incomplete" rather than invent an answer. Durham, class is in session.

The School in 60 Seconds
Duke plays in the ACC and has won 18 conference championships: eight in the ACC and ten in the Southern Conference. The program counts 53 All-Americans, ten ACC Players of the Year and three Pro Football Hall of Famers. Home is Wallace Wade Stadium in Durham, which CFBD lists as built in 1929 with 40,004 seats.

For decades the university de-emphasized football, starting around the mid-1960s. Then came a renaissance under David Cutcliffe from 2008 to 2021, including Duke's first Coastal Division title in 2013, clinched with a win over North Carolina. The last five seasons show the progress: 3-9 in 2021, then 9-4, 8-5, 9-4 and 9-5. Four straight winning seasons. I've written longer papers on less.

The Coach and the Scheme
Manny Diaz is our head coach. CFBD records his hire date as Dec. 7, 2023. He was head coach at Miami from 2019 to 2021 and defensive coordinator and linebackers coach at Penn State in 2022 and 2023. He's also coached at Mississippi State, Louisiana Tech, Texas, Middle Tennessee, NC State and Florida State. That's a doctoral program in defense.

Through 8 games this season, Duke is scoring 32.0 points per game and allowing 14.2.

Players to Know
QB Walker Eget: 133 of 215, 1,641 yards, 9 touchdowns, 3 interceptions.
RB Nate Sheppard: 972 yards on 173 carries, 14 touchdowns.
WR Jonah Burton: 22 catches, 308 yards, 1 touchdown.
DE Kevin O'Connor: 4.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Nick Morris Jr.: a team-high 45 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Duke is 6-2 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Duke 5th in the ACC.
The season so far: W 17-3 vs Tulane Green Wave (Sept. 5), W 31-27 at Illinois Fighting Illini (Sept. 12), W 35-7 vs Stanford Cardinal (Sept. 19), W 62-7 vs William & Mary Tribe (Sept. 26), L 25-28 at Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets (Oct. 10), L 20-24 vs North Carolina Tar Heels (Oct. 17), W 19-16 at Virginia Cavaliers (Oct. 23), W 47-2 vs Boston College Eagles (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to NC State (4-4, 2-3 ACC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 12:30 p.m. ET, at Carter-Finley Stadium, Raleigh, North Carolina, on CW.
Last time out. We beat the Boston College Eagles 47-2 at home on Oct. 31. Walker Eget went 11 of 18 for 201 yards, with 1 touchdown, Nate Sheppard ran 20 times for 97 yards and 5 touchdowns and Jeremiah Hasley caught 3 balls for 55 yards and 1 touchdown.
NC State beat the California Golden Bears 31-10 at home on Oct. 31. CJ Bailey went 13 of 25 for 192 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Davion Gause ran 19 times for 104 yards and 1 touchdown; Chance Robinson caught 4 balls for 76 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. NC State quarterback CJ Bailey is 156 of 244 for 1,955 yards, with 13 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Jayden Scott leads their ground game with 358 yards on 62 carries and 4 scores. Keenan Jackson is their top target: 30 catches for 456 yards. On defense, linebacker Popo Aguirre has 76 tackles and linebacker Elijah Satchell has 4 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 4 regular-season meetings with NC State from 2016 through 2025. We won 1 of them, and NC State won the last one 45-33 in 2025.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Duke vs. NC State. When Duke or NC State puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Rutherford "Ruff" Pegram, NC State's insider. Here's what I said:
"I have tolerated your brisket for six years. As a courtesy. A gentleman never refuses food from a neighbor, even one wearing that much red."
"That is the single most NC State thing I have ever heard. You could not beat us, so you named a running back after us."
"Most years? We won 45-33 last year. We won 29-19 the year before that. We won 24-3 in 2023. Three. You scored three points, Ruff. I have seen more points on the scoreboard at a tennis match."
And the last word from Ruff: "Afterward, you eat my brisket and say thank you, win or lose. It is Military Appreciation day. We can all be decent for one afternoon."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We make the short trip to Raleigh for NC State's Military Appreciation game, and the free Raleigh Food Truck Rodeo on Fayetteville Street will have the whole town eating before kickoff.

My call: Duke Blue Devils 28, NC State Wolfpack 25.
