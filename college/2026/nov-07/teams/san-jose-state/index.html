The School in 60 Seconds
San José State first played a regular season in 1898. Since then the Spartans have produced more than 90 All-Americans, won 18 conference championships and sent 139 players to the NFL, including Pro Football Hall of Famers Bill Walsh and Dick Vermeil. Home is CEFCU Stadium, built in 1933 as Spartan Stadium, listed at 30,456 seats. Regional rivals include Fresno State, Stanford, Hawai'i and San Diego State. We play in the Mountain West. The last five years went 5-7, 7-5, 7-6, 7-6 and 3-9.

The Coach and the Scheme
Ken Niumatalolo is the head coach, hired Jan. 21, 2024.

We've played 9 games, and we're putting up 19.9 points a game while giving up 22.3.

The Conference Picture
San José State is 4-5 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have San José State 7th in the Mountain West.
The season so far: L 26-42 at USC Trojans (Aug. 29), W 27-21 at Eastern Michigan Eagles (Sept. 4), W 30-20 vs Cal Poly Mustangs (Sept. 12), L 10-26 vs Fresno State Bulldogs (Sept. 19), W 20-16 at Hawai'i Rainbow Warriors (Oct. 3), L 13-16 (OT) vs Wyoming Cowboys (Oct. 9), W 24-11 at UTEP Miners (Oct. 17), L 13-28 at Nevada Wolf Pack (Oct. 24), L 16-21 vs New Mexico Lobos (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Northern Illinois (0-8, 0-4 Mountain West) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 4:00 p.m. ET, at CEFCU Stadium, San Jose, California, on MW+.
Last time out. We lost to the New Mexico Lobos 21-16 at home on Oct. 31. Luke Weaver went 16 of 23 for 190 yards, Jabari Bates ran 22 times for 63 yards and Carson Clark caught 8 balls for 91 yards.
Northern Illinois lost to the UNLV Rebels 31-24 on the road on Oct. 31. Taron Dickens went 19 of 37 for 253 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Elijah Porter ran 21 times for 113 yards; George Dimopoulos caught 7 balls for 127 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Northern Illinois quarterback Taron Dickens is 157 of 247 for 1,617 yards, with 8 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. Telly Johnson Jr. leads their ground game with 574 yards on 103 carries and 5 scores. DeAree Rogers is their top target: 64 catches for 633 yards. On defense, linebacker Jack Murray has 58 tackles and defensive back Avery Jones has 2 sacks.
The series. We did not meet Northern Illinois in the regular season from 2016 through 2025, per CFBD, so there is no recent history to go on.
Availability. The public injury data for Northern Illinois and San José State is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
