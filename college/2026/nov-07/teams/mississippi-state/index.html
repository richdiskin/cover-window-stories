No Place Like State: Mississippi State Celebrates Homecoming Against Vanderbilt

By Wade Pettus · Mississippi State Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Ring it if you mean it. (Ding.) Howdy, Bulldog people. I'm Wade Pettus, poultry-science graduate, and I chart Air Raid concepts on the back of feed-store receipts. I have a drawer full of them. It has never once been wrong about a third down. Every week I'll bring you the players, the matchup, the history and a little gentle Starkville deadpan. When we win, I ring the cowbell exactly once for every point we scored, no matter how long it takes. When we lose, the cowbell goes in a sock, out of respect. I believe football is best explained slowly, the way you'd explain a good hen house. I'm glad you're here.

The School in 60 Seconds
Mississippi State plays in the SEC and won the league championship in 1941, plus a division title in 1998. The Bulldogs have made 27 bowl appearances and produced 38 All-Americans, 171 All-SEC selections and 124 NFL players, 11 of them first-round draft choices. Our home, Davis Wade Stadium at Scott Field, is the second-oldest stadium in FBS. CFBD dates it to 1914 and lists capacity at 61,337. I wrote that on a receipt, too.

The Coach and the Scheme
Jeff Lebby was hired Nov. 26, 2023, and this is his third season. He came from Oklahoma, where he was offensive coordinator for Brent Venables's first two seasons, 2022 and 2023. His first two years in Starkville went 7-18. This offseason he brought back Zach Arnett as defensive coordinator, Arnett's second stint in that job here.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 26.6 points a game while giving up 28.1.

Players to Know
QB Kamario Taylor: 104 of 159, 1,460 yards, 17 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Fluff Bothwell: 563 yards on 108 carries, 3 touchdowns.
WR Anthony Evans III: 56 catches, 666 yards, 5 touchdowns.
DL Jaray Bledsoe: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Tyler Lockhart: a team-high 48 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Mississippi State is 4-4 overall and 2-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have Mississippi State 12th in the SEC.
The season so far: W 62-13 vs UL Monroe Warhawks (Sept. 5), W 38-13 at Minnesota Golden Gophers (Sept. 12), W 41-34 at South Carolina Gamecocks (Sept. 19), W 31-24 vs Missouri Tigers (Sept. 26), L 23-56 vs Alabama Crimson Tide (Oct. 3), L 0-31 at LSU Tigers (Oct. 17), L 15-23 vs Oklahoma Sooners (Oct. 24), L 3-31 at Texas Longhorns (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Vanderbilt (3-5, 0-5 SEC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 4:15 p.m. ET, at Davis Wade Stadium, Starkville, Mississippi, on SEC Network.
Last time out. We lost to the Texas Longhorns 31-3 on the road on Oct. 31. AJ Swann went 12 of 15 for 97 yards, Fluff Bothwell ran 17 times for 49 yards and Marquis Johnson caught 6 balls for 61 yards.
Vanderbilt lost to the Kentucky Wildcats 34-17 on the road on Oct. 24. Jared Curtis went 27 of 36 for 247 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Jamezell Lassiter ran 1 times for 6 yards; Junior Sherrill caught 8 balls for 78 yards.
Players to watch. Vanderbilt quarterback Jared Curtis is 128 of 197 for 1,489 yards, with 8 touchdowns and 8 interceptions. Sedrick Alexander leads their ground game with 274 yards on 64 carries and 5 scores. Junior Sherrill is their top target: 49 catches for 706 yards. On defense, safety CJ Heard has 60 tackles and linebacker Nick Rinaldi has 3.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 2 regular-season meetings with Vanderbilt from 2016 through 2025. We won 1 of them, and Vanderbilt won the last one 45-6 in 2021.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Mississippi State vs. Vanderbilt. When Mississippi State or Vanderbilt puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Penelope 'Pippa' Ashcroft, Vanderbilt's insider. Here's what I said:
"Now mine. We were 4-0. Beat UL Monroe 62-13, won at Minnesota, won at South Carolina 41-34, beat Missouri 31-24. Kamario Taylor was throwing it all over the yard."
And the last word from Pippa: "Your last two in this series were 24-17 and 45-6. I know. I looked it up so you wouldn't get to tell me."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Homecoming week, "There's No Place Like State," and the Diamond Dawg Classic at Dudy Noble Field on Friday, Nov. 6, brings back Will Clark and Rafael Palmeiro, so wear maroon.

My call: Mississippi State Bulldogs 32, Vanderbilt Commodores 23.
