Dads Weekend in Champaign: Illinois Hosts Nebraska Friday Night

By Margery Vandermolen · Illinois Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Good afternoon, class! Oh, sorry. Old habits. Welcome, Illini Nation. I'm Margery Vandermolen, your Illinois insider, and I explain football the way your best-loved teacher explained long division: patiently, cheerfully and with a lot of charts. I'm a prairie-state numbers whiz. I've crunched the numbers on just about everything, and the numbers always say the same thing: orange. You'll get corn jokes from me. You'll get chart jokes. Sometimes you'll get a corn chart, which is both. And every Saturday morning I'll ask you the same question: "Orange you glad it's Saturday?" When something wonderful happens on the field, there's only one thing to say: "Oskee-wow-wow!" Each week I'll give you the players, the coaches and the matchup, explained clearly enough that Grandma and the grandkids can both follow along.

The School in 60 Seconds
Here's your history lesson, and yes, it will be on the test. Illinois is a founding member of the Big Ten and claims five national championships: 1914, 1919, 1923, 1927 and 1951. The 1923 title team opened Memorial Stadium and introduced the world to Harold "Red" Grange, the Galloping Ghost. On Oct. 18, 1924, at the stadium's dedication game, Grange scored four touchdowns against Michigan in the first 12 minutes. He ran for a fifth and threw for a sixth in a 39-14 win. ESPN once chose the legendary No. 77 as the greatest player in college football history. The stadium honors University of Illinois students who died in World War I. Illinois also claims 15 Big Ten titles. High fives all around.

The Coach and the Scheme
Bret Bielema was named the 26th head coach in Illinois history on Dec. 19, 2020. He's an Illinois native. One of his early wins was a nine-overtime victory at Penn State in 2021, the longest game in college football history.

Through 8 games this season, Illinois is scoring 29.0 points per game and allowing 24.2.

Players to Know
QB Katin Houser: 140 of 216, 1,663 yards, 13 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Aidan Laughery: 560 yards on 89 carries, 2 touchdowns.
WR Collin Dixon: 60 catches, 781 yards, 10 touchdowns.
LB Ean Rhea: 2 sacks, the team lead.
DB Matthew Bailey: a team-high 55 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Illinois is 5-3 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Illinois 5th in the Big Ten.
The season so far: W 42-23 vs UAB Blazers (Sept. 3), L 27-31 vs Duke Blue Devils (Sept. 12), W 48-10 vs Southern Illinois Salukis (Sept. 19), L 19-42 at Ohio State Buckeyes (Sept. 26), L 17-24 vs Purdue Boilermakers (Oct. 3), W 38-37 (OT) at Michigan State Spartans (Oct. 10), W 21-10 vs Oregon Ducks (Oct. 24), W 20-17 at Maryland Terrapins (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Nebraska (5-3, 2-3 Big Ten) on Friday, Nov. 6, at 8:00 p.m. ET, at Gies Memorial Stadium, Champaign, Illinois, on Fox.
Last time out. We beat the Maryland Terrapins 20-17 on the road on Oct. 31. Katin Houser went 18 of 32 for 223 yards, with 1 touchdown, Javari Barnett ran 6 times for 80 yards and 1 touchdown and Collin Dixon caught 6 balls for 103 yards.
Nebraska lost to the Washington Huskies 17-10 at home on Oct. 31. Anthony Colandrea went 24 of 44 for 255 yards, with 1 touchdown; Jamal Rule ran 13 times for 72 yards; Jacory Barney Jr. caught 6 balls for 65 yards.
Players to watch. Nebraska quarterback Anthony Colandrea is 161 of 263 for 1,825 yards, with 15 touchdowns and 7 interceptions. Jamal Rule leads their ground game with 463 yards on 73 carries and 5 scores. Kwazi Gilmer is their top target: 42 catches for 545 yards. On defense, linebacker Owen Chambliss has 53 tackles and defensive lineman Jahsear Whittington has 4.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 9 regular-season meetings with Nebraska from 2016 through 2025. We won 3 of them, and Nebraska won the last one 31-24 in 2024.
Availability. The public injury data for Nebraska and Illinois is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Darla Rasmussen, Nebraska's insider. Here's what I said:
"Welcome to Illinois. Our corn is taller and our dads are louder."
"Collin Dixon is the best thing to come out of a cornfield since popcorn. And our dads have seen your team lose three in a row."
"Four of five. Ask your dad."
And the last word from Darla: "You sold out the tailgate. Doesn't mean you sold out the win."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Dads Weekend in Champaign, with the Illinois Dads Day Football Game, a sold-out Dads Weekend Tailgate at Grange Grove and a Dads Weekend 5K at the Arboretum on Saturday morning.

My call: Nebraska Cornhuskers 27, Illinois Fighting Illini 24.
