Commodores Crash the Party: Vanderbilt Visits Starkville for Homecoming

By Penelope 'Pippa' Ashcroft · Vanderbilt Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
That's a hit single, y'all. Hey there, I'm Penelope Ashcroft, but everyone on West End calls me Pippa. I study economics at Vanderbilt by day, and by night I write country songs on a beat-up guitar, and every single one of them is about a football game. I travel with the Commodores and I live with the Commodores, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines, with a little Music City wordplay and a chorus or two. Got an interception return in my heart and a third-down stop on my mind. That's my sound. When we win, I sing a big country chorus, key change and all. When we lose, I write a sad breakup ballad to the turnover that did us in. I've got a whole album of those this month.

The School in 60 Seconds
The Commodores represent Vanderbilt University in the SEC. Home is FirstBank Stadium, right on our Nashville campus, opened in 1922 and listed at 40,351 seats. Now here's a song with a happy ending: the last five seasons went 2-10, 5-7, 2-10, then 7-6 in 2024 and a 10-3 smash hit in 2025. Ten wins at Vanderbilt. Y'all, that one went platinum. This season is the follow-up album, and follow-up albums are hard.

The Coach and the Scheme
Clark Lea is the head coach; CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 14, 2020. He's the producer behind that 10-3 record.

Through 8 games this season, Vanderbilt is scoring 22.8 points per game and allowing 30.2.

Players to Know
QB Jared Curtis: 128 of 197, 1,489 yards, 8 touchdowns, 8 interceptions.
RB Sedrick Alexander: 274 yards on 64 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Junior Sherrill: 49 catches, 706 yards, 4 touchdowns.
LB Nick Rinaldi: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
S CJ Heard: a team-high 60 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Vanderbilt is 3-5 overall and 0-5 in league play. ESPN's standings have Vanderbilt 16th in the SEC.
The season so far: W 28-9 vs Austin Peay Governors (Sept. 5), W 35-26 vs Delaware Blue Hens (Sept. 12), W 35-31 vs NC State Wolfpack (Sept. 19), L 15-21 at Auburn Tigers (Sept. 26), L 14-38 at Georgia Bulldogs (Oct. 3), L 17-52 vs Ole Miss Rebels (Oct. 10), L 21-31 vs Arkansas Razorbacks (Oct. 17), L 17-34 at Kentucky Wildcats (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Mississippi State (4-4, 2-4 SEC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 4:15 p.m. ET, at Davis Wade Stadium, Starkville, Mississippi, on SEC Network.
Last time out. We lost to the Kentucky Wildcats 34-17 on the road on Oct. 24. Jared Curtis went 27 of 36 for 247 yards, with 2 touchdowns, Jamezell Lassiter ran 1 times for 6 yards and Junior Sherrill caught 8 balls for 78 yards.
Mississippi State lost to the Texas Longhorns 31-3 on the road on Oct. 31. AJ Swann went 12 of 15 for 97 yards; Fluff Bothwell ran 17 times for 49 yards; Marquis Johnson caught 6 balls for 61 yards.
Players to watch. Mississippi State quarterback Kamario Taylor is 104 of 159 for 1,460 yards, with 17 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Fluff Bothwell leads their ground game with 563 yards on 108 carries and 3 scores. Anthony Evans III is their top target: 56 catches for 666 yards. On defense, linebacker Tyler Lockhart has 48 tackles and defensive lineman Jaray Bledsoe has 3.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 2 regular-season meetings with Mississippi State from 2016 through 2025. We won 1 of them, and we won the last one 45-6 in 2021.
Availability. The public injury data for Mississippi State and Vanderbilt is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Wade Pettus, Mississippi State's insider. Here's what I said:
"Y'all have the theme down. No place like State. I'll give you that. It's the only place in the league where I can hear my own heartbeat over a cowbell."
"Chicken biscuits. You would bring up chickens."
"The Auburn game still hurts. Six points. Then Georgia, then the Ole Miss game I won't talk about. But look at Jared Curtis at Kentucky. Twenty-seven of thirty-six for 247 and two touchdowns. He is figuring it out."
And the last word from Wade: "Whoever it is, he gets to throw against a defense that gave up 52 to Ole Miss. That changes the whole night. The last month we played four ranked-looking giants. Vanderbilt is a different kind of Saturday."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We crash Mississippi State's Homecoming, and the week's festivities include the Diamond Dawg Classic at Dudy Noble Field on Friday with Will Clark and Rafael Palmeiro, so Davis Wade will be packed in maroon.

My call: Mississippi State Bulldogs 32, Vanderbilt Commodores 23.
