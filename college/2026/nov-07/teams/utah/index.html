Holy War at Rice-Eccles: Utah Defends Its House Against BYU

By Aspen Mortensen · Utah Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Send it, no fear, Ute fans! I'm Aspen Mortensen, your Utah insider. I used to race skis, and now I chart Utah's defensive speed like slalom gates. Who cut inside, who cleared the gate, who missed it. I'll be making avalanche puns. Consider yourself warned.

Here's my promise. Every number has a source. If I don't know something about Utah, I'll tell you plainly. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I thank God for the Wasatch, my family and fall football.

The School in 60 Seconds
Utah football began in 1892 and has played at the current site of Rice-Eccles Stadium since 1927. The Utes have won 27 conference championships in five conferences, and they have two BCS bowl wins: the 2005 Fiesta Bowl, 35-7 over Pittsburgh under Urban Meyer, and the 2009 Sugar Bowl under Kyle Whittingham. CFBD lists Rice-Eccles at 51,444 seats.

The last five seasons: 10-4, 10-4, 8-5, 5-7 and 11-2.

The Coach and the Scheme
Morgan Scalley is in his first season as head coach. He took over in December 2025. He played defensive back for Utah from 2001 to 2004, was on Whittingham's staff from 2007 and spent 10 seasons as defensive coordinator.

Through 8 games this season, Utah is scoring 38.9 points per game and allowing 15.9.

Players to Know
QB Devon Dampier: 158 of 227, 1,876 yards, 18 touchdowns, 7 interceptions.
RB Wayshawn Parker: 726 yards on 109 carries, 9 touchdowns.
WR Braden Pegan: 39 catches, 505 yards, 6 touchdowns.
DT Karson Kaufusi: 3 sacks, the team lead.
LB Trey Reynolds: a team-high 46 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Utah is 8-0 overall and 5-0 in league play. ESPN's standings have Utah 1st in the Big 12.
The season so far: W 66-14 vs Idaho Vandals (Sept. 3), W 43-10 vs Arkansas Razorbacks (Sept. 12), W 33-0 vs Utah State Aggies (Sept. 19), W 31-17 at Iowa State Cyclones (Sept. 26), W 41-24 vs Kansas Jayhawks (Oct. 10), W 27-24 at Colorado Buffaloes (Oct. 17), W 21-13 vs Houston Cougars (Oct. 24), W 49-25 at Cincinnati Bearcats (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host No. 7 BYU (7-1, 4-1 Big 12) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at noon ET, at Rice-Eccles Stadium, Salt Lake City, Utah, on ABC. We come in ranked No. 3.
Last time out. We beat the Cincinnati Bearcats 49-25 on the road on Oct. 31. Devon Dampier went 22 of 29 for 260 yards, with 3 touchdowns, Wayshawn Parker ran 7 times for 82 yards and 1 touchdown and Hunter Andrews caught 5 balls for 112 yards and 2 touchdowns.
BYU beat the Arizona State Sun Devils 27-20 at home on Oct. 31. Bear Bachmeier went 19 of 30 for 236 yards, with 1 touchdown; Sione Moa ran 8 times for 20 yards and 2 touchdowns; Legend Glasker caught 4 balls for 87 yards.
Players to watch. BYU quarterback Bear Bachmeier is 135 of 214 for 1,750 yards, with 12 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. LJ Martin leads their ground game with 344 yards on 44 carries and 3 scores. Legend Glasker is their top target: 32 catches for 585 yards. On defense, linebacker Cade Uluave has 52 tackles and defensive end Tausili Akana has 3.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 7 regular-season meetings with BYU from 2016 through 2025. We won 4 of them, and we won the last one 24-21 in 2025.
Availability. The public injury data for BYU and Utah is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Bryce Halvorsen-Tuilagi, BYU's insider. Here's what I said:
"Devon Dampier has 1,876 yards and 18 touchdowns. Last week at Cincinnati he went 22 of 29 for 260 and 3 scores. Hunter Andrews caught 5 balls for 112 yards and 2 touchdowns, and we won 49-25. That is not a ceiling. That is a ski lift."
"A little long. That is how you describe losing to UCF. We went to Colorado and won 27-24. We beat Arkansas 43-10. We held Utah State to zero. Zero. Your mountain has a letter on it, ours has a scoreboard."
"Braden Pegan has 505 receiving yards and 6 touchdowns. Glasker has 4. If you want to talk about legends, talk about touchdowns, because that is what people remember on the drive home."
And the last word from Bryce: "Fine. I will come up Friday, watch the show, and stay for the game. If Utah wins, I will buy dinner. If BYU wins, you have to hike the Y with me in November."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Friday night downtown, the Eccles Theater on Main Street hosts the concert premiere of Alex Boyé's The King of New York, and Disney On Ice is at the Delta Center all weekend, so Salt Lake City is busy before the Holy War.

My call: Utah Utes 30, BYU Cougars 22.
