Horns Head to Columbia: Texas Takes On Mizzou at Faurot Field

By Caro Whitfield · Texas Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
That's a premium asset. Well, hello, y'all, I'm Caroline Whitfield, Caro to my friends. I was a rodeo queen in a former life, I spent a few years in consulting after that, and now I bring both to the Forty Acres: big-hat polish and a very organized brain. I travel with the Longhorns and I live with the Longhorns, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines. I size up every opponent's roster in imaginary Caro Bucks, and I'll tell you exactly what each player is worth to me. I don't do rebuilds. I do acquisitions. When we win, I raise a single eyebrow and say, "As forecast." When we lose, I call an emergency board meeting with myself. Hypothetically, heads roll.

The School in 60 Seconds
The Texas Longhorns began playing in 1893 and compete in the SEC. We claim four consensus national championships: 1963, 1969, 1970 and 2005, and the program has been led by three Hall of Fame coaches. Home is DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium in Austin, opened in 1924 and listed at 100,119 seats. The last five seasons: 5-7, 8-5, then 12-2, 13-3 and 10-3. That is a blue-chip portfolio.

The Coach and the Scheme
Steve Sarkisian has been our head coach since CFBD's hire date of Jan. 2, 2021.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 30.8 points a game while giving up 17.1.

Players to Know
QB Arch Manning: 140 of 216, 1,727 yards, 15 touchdowns, 7 interceptions.
RB Raleek Brown: 452 yards on 92 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Ryan Wingo: 36 catches, 470 yards, 4 touchdowns.
Defender Colin Simmons: 10.5 sacks, the team lead.
DB Jelani McDonald: a team-high 63 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Texas is 6-2 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Texas 5th in the SEC.
The season so far: W 59-7 vs Texas State Bobcats (Sept. 5), W 24-23 vs Ohio State Buckeyes (Sept. 12), W 30-6 vs UTSA Roadrunners (Sept. 19), W 20-17 at Tennessee Volunteers (Sept. 26), L 24-27 vs Florida Gators (Oct. 17), W 34-27 vs Ole Miss Rebels (Oct. 24), W 31-3 vs Mississippi State Bulldogs (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to No. 19 Missouri (6-2, 3-2 SEC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at noon ET, at Memorial Stadium, Columbia, Missouri, on ESPN. We come in ranked No. 10.
Last time out. We beat the Mississippi State Bulldogs 31-3 at home on Oct. 31. Arch Manning went 16 of 20 for 220 yards, with 3 touchdowns, Raleek Brown ran 8 times for 30 yards and Emmett Mosley V caught 4 balls for 98 yards and 1 touchdown.
Missouri beat the Arkansas Razorbacks 34-10 on the road on Oct. 31. Austin Simmons went 20 of 31 for 179 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Jamal Roberts ran 23 times for 138 yards and 1 touchdown; Cayden Lee caught 6 balls for 78 yards.
Players to watch. Missouri quarterback Austin Simmons is 152 of 240 for 1,952 yards, with 16 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. Jamal Roberts leads their ground game with 1,005 yards on 190 carries and 9 scores. Cayden Lee is their top target: 53 catches for 756 yards. On defense, linebacker Nicholas Rodriguez has 84 tackles and defender Daeden Hopkins has 7 sacks.
The series. CFBD shows no regular-season meetings with Missouri from 2016 through 2025, so this is a fresh fight for our guys.
Availability. The public injury data for Missouri and Texas is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Delphine Arceneaux-Moss, Missouri's insider. Here's what I said:
"And you lost to Florida, too? Oh wait, no. You beat Florida 45-17. Then you went to Oxford and scored 3 points. Three. I have seen higher numbers on a thermometer in Austin in January."
"I would never ask the Aggies anything. That is one thing Texas and Missouri agree on."
"Close, yes. I will agree with close. Simmons gets to your quarterback when it matters, Manning finds Wingo for the play everybody talks about on Sunday, and the Saturday market crowd waves goodbye to the burnt orange as we drive south."
And the last word from Delphine: "You are buying souvenirs at the market before you leave, aren't you."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Longhorn fans who land in Columbia early can hit the Saturday winter Columbia Farmers Market from 9 to noon on West Ash Street, then find their seats at Faurot Field.

My call: Texas Longhorns 25, Missouri Tigers 21.
