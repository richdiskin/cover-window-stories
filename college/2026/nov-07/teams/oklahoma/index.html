Sooners in the Swamp: Oklahoma Brings the Fight to Gainesville

By Savannah Redbird-Lowe · Oklahoma Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Boomer! (You say Sooner.) Louder, Norman, I can't hear you over the Schooner wheels. I'm Savannah Redbird-Lowe, former Oklahoma rodeo princess, and I read a defense the way I used to read a barrel pattern: clean turn, tight pocket, no tipped barrels. "Sooner!" call-and-response at least once in every single report. That is my one rule, and I don't budge on it. Every week you'll get the players, the matchups, the storylines and the history, told straight with a little Plains-state sass. When we win, you'll hear me holler "Boomer!" and wait for you. When we lose, I'll lift my chin and tell you we'll roll the Schooner again. Saddle up, y'all.

The School in 60 Seconds
Oklahoma football started in 1895. The program claims seven national championships (1950, 1955, 1956, 1974, 1975, 1985 and 2000), 50 conference titles and seven Heisman Trophy winners. The Sooners also hold the Division I record for consecutive wins, 47 straight, and Oklahoma is the only program where four different coaches have won more than 100 games. These days we're an SEC program. Home is Gaylord Family Oklahoma Memorial Stadium in Norman, listed at 86,112 seats in the CFBD venue file. Last season the Sooners went 10-3. That is a lot of trophies to dust, and I do it in boots.

The Coach and the Scheme
Brent Venables is the head coach. The CFBD coaches file dates his hire to Dec. 5, 2021, and he has led the program since 2022.

The 8-game numbers: 22.0 points per game scored, 20.8 allowed.

Players to Know
QB John Mateer: 162 of 247, 1,835 yards, 13 touchdowns, 7 interceptions.
RB Lloyd Avant: 347 yards on 79 carries, 2 touchdowns.
WR Isaiah Sategna III: 45 catches, 591 yards, 3 touchdowns.
DL Adepoju Adebawore: 4 sacks, the team lead.
LB Owen Heinecke: a team-high 63 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Oklahoma is 4-4 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have Oklahoma 11th in the SEC.
The season so far: W 51-0 vs UTEP Miners (Sept. 4), L 10-17 at Michigan Wolverines (Sept. 12), W 14-6 vs New Mexico Lobos (Sept. 19), L 13-41 at Georgia Bulldogs (Sept. 26), L 13-21 vs Kentucky Wildcats (Oct. 17), W 23-15 at Mississippi State Bulldogs (Oct. 24), L 25-42 vs South Carolina Gamecocks (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to No. 25 Florida (5-3, 3-3 SEC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:45 p.m. ET, at Ben Hill Griffin Stadium, Gainesville, Florida, on SEC Network.
Last time out. We lost to the South Carolina Gamecocks 42-25 at home on Oct. 31. John Mateer went 26 of 43 for 244 yards, with 2 touchdowns, Tory Blaylock ran 6 times for 32 yards and Ivan Carreon caught 5 balls for 88 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Players to watch. Florida quarterback Aaron Philo is 110 of 156 for 1,367 yards, with 9 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Jadan Baugh leads their ground game with 801 yards on 152 carries and 13 scores. Dallas Wilson is their top target: 31 catches for 417 yards. On defense, linebacker Myles Graham has 55 tackles and linebacker Jayden Woods has 4.5 sacks.
The series. We did not meet Florida in the regular season from 2016 through 2025, per CFBD, so there is no recent history to go on.
Availability. No official availability report for Oklahoma or Florida was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Dax Marchetti, Florida's insider. Here's what I said:
"Fan Fest. Do they have barbecue?"
"That was seventeen years ago. Ours was more recent."
And the last word from Dax: "You gave up 42 to South Carolina."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We get the Swamp at night on Florida's Saluting Those Who Serve game, and the free Fan Fest outside the O'Connell Center will have the home folks loud long before we take the field.

My call: Florida Gators 28, Oklahoma Sooners 23.
