Monarchs in Conway: Old Dominion Crashes Coastal's Homecoming

By Winnie Pocklington · Old Dominion Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hear ye, hear ye! Let it be proclaimed from the harbor to the Hampton Roads Bridge-Tunnel that the Monarchs' royal court reporter has arrived. I am Winifred Pocklington, Winnie to my loyal subjects, and I treat every first down like a knighthood and every win like a coronation. I'm regal, I'm playful, and I'm salt-air tough, because Norfolk does that to a person. I travel with this Old Dominion team and I live with it, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines with a royal-court flourish and an honest heart. When we win, there will be trumpets (metaphorical, mostly). When we lose, the crown gets polished and the court reconvenes on Monday. Long live the Monarchs.

The School in 60 Seconds
Hear ye: Old Dominion's football story has two chapters. The first team, in 1930, was the William & Mary Norfolk Division Braves. The modern Monarchs were founded in 2009, played two seasons as an FCS independent, joined the Colonial Athletic Association in 2011, moved up to Conference USA in the FBS in 2014 and joined the Sun Belt in 2022. Home is S.B. Ballard Stadium in Norfolk, listed at 20,118 seats. Our five most recent seasons went 6-7, 3-9, 6-7, 5-7 and then a royal 10-3 in 2025. That last one deserved a parade down Hampton Boulevard. The crown, as they say, is heavy. We carry it anyway.

The Coach and the Scheme
Ricky Rahne was hired Dec. 7, 2019.

Through 9 games this season, Old Dominion is scoring 16.3 points per game and allowing 27.8.

Players to Know
QB Quinn Henicle: 54 of 98, 656 yards, 4 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Devin Roche: 395 yards on 109 carries.
WR Josh Rodriguez: 30 catches, 383 yards.
DL Javen Gibson: 5 sacks, the team lead.
S Lucas Struck: a team-high 70 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Old Dominion is 2-7 overall and 1-5 in league play. ESPN's standings have Old Dominion 7th in the Sun Belt - East.
The season so far: W 31-10 vs Norfolk State Spartans (Sept. 5), L 21-44 at Virginia Tech Hokies (Sept. 12), L 17-20 vs East Carolina Pirates (Sept. 19), L 20-46 vs James Madison Dukes (Sept. 26), L 10-42 at Georgia State Panthers (Oct. 3), W 10-3 at App State Mountaineers (Oct. 10), L 15-23 vs Georgia Southern Eagles (Oct. 15), L 0-26 at Louisiana Tech Bulldogs (Oct. 24), L 23-36 vs Marshall Thundering Herd (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Coastal Carolina (3-5, 2-2 Sun Belt) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 1:00 p.m. ET, at Brooks Stadium (SC), Conway, South Carolina, on ESPN+.
Last time out. We lost to the Marshall Thundering Herd 36-23 at home on Oct. 31. Jaxon Potter went 23 of 44 for 329 yards, Trey Hall ran 8 times for 41 yards and Josh Rodriguez caught 9 balls for 143 yards.
Coastal Carolina beat the Georgia State Panthers 31-20 on the road on Oct. 31. Deuce Bailey went 22 of 32 for 297 yards, with 3 touchdowns; Dominic Lee-Knicely ran 12 times for 32 yards; Clayton Coppock Jr. caught 6 balls for 100 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Players to watch. Coastal Carolina quarterback Deuce Bailey is 131 of 236 for 1,935 yards, with 19 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. Dominic Lee-Knicely leads their ground game with 484 yards on 92 carries and 3 scores. Clayton Coppock Jr. is their top target: 20 catches for 350 yards. On defense, linebacker Marcellius Pulliam has 74 tackles and defensive end Ibrahim Diawara has 5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 4 regular-season meetings with Coastal Carolina from 2016 through 2025. We won 2 of them, and Coastal Carolina won the last one 47-7 in 2025.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Old Dominion vs. Coastal Carolina. When Old Dominion or Coastal Carolina puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We walk into Coastal's Homecoming at Brooks Stadium, and Conway is also hosting the SC UFO Festival on Saturday, so the whole town will be out.

My call: Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 27, Old Dominion Monarchs 19.
