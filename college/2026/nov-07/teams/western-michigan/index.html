Broncos Reload: Western Michigan Rests Before Tuesday Night With Akron

By Corwin Dahlquist · Western Michigan Insider · Week 10

Meet Your Insider
Hi there, Bronco fans! I'm Corwin Dahlquist, your Western Michigan insider. I studied paper engineering here in Kalamazoo, a town with deep paper-mill roots, so yes, I can tell you the tensile strength of a playbook page. I'm an earnest Michigan nerd, and I size up every opponent the way you'd size up a Kalamazoo diner breakfast. A short stack is light work. A full stack means you'll need a nap afterward. When we win, I declare a full stack and do a big syrup-pour gesture. When we lose, I mourn "short stack today" and eat dry toast on air, because I believe in accountability. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, served hot.

The School in 60 Seconds
Last season was a full stack with whipped cream on top. Western Michigan won the 2025 MAC championship, only the fourth conference title in school history. Lance Taylor was named MAC Coach of the Year. Quarterback Broc Lowry was MAC Offensive Player of the Year, and defensive end Nadame Tucker was Defensive Player of the Year and won the Vern Smith Leadership Award as the league's top player. The 2025 team won seven conference games, tied for the second-most in school history behind the 8-0 run in 2016. The Broncos also won the Cannon Trophy in 2023 and 2025, and Michigan MAC Trophies in 2023, 2024 and 2025, and they finished the season in the Myrtle Beach Bowl against Kennesaw State. Home is Waldo Stadium in Kalamazoo, listed at 30,200.

The Coach and the Scheme
Lance Taylor was hired on Dec. 8, 2022, and in December 2025 he signed an extension through the 2030 season.

We've played 9 games, and we're putting up 26.2 points a game while giving up 19.2.

Players to Know

RB Jalen Buckley: 711 yards on 144 carries, 8 touchdowns.

DE Austin Alexander: 5 sacks, the team lead.

The Conference Picture
Western Michigan is 6-3 overall and 4-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have Western Michigan 2nd in the MAC.
The season so far: L 12-13 at Michigan Wolverines (Sept. 5), W 49-14 vs Monmouth Hawks (Sept. 12), W 28-21 at Rice Owls (Sept. 19), L 7-32 vs Boise State Broncos (Sept. 26), W 20-17 at Buffalo Bulls (Oct. 3), W 38-7 vs Kent State Golden Flashes (Oct. 10), W 21-14 at Central Michigan Chippewas (Oct. 17), L 38-45 at Toledo Rockets (Oct. 24), W 23-10 vs Bowling Green Falcons (Oct. 31).
