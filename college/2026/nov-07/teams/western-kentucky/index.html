Tops in Murfreesboro: Western Kentucky Puts the Streak on the Road

By Thurman "Tops" Whitsitt · Western Kentucky Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Go Tops! I'm Thurman "Tops" Whitsitt, and I'm your Western Kentucky insider. I'm a Warren County barber with three decades of Hilltopper stories, told one haircut at a time. On the podcast, my segment is called Barbershop. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate Western Kentucky players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no Western Kentucky fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Houchens Industries-L.T. Smith Stadium in Bowling Green, Ky., built in 1968 with a listed capacity of 22,113. The fight song is "Stand Up and Cheer!" and the band is the Big Red Marching Band. The last five seasons went 9-5, 9-5, 8-5, 8-6 and 9-4, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about Big Red. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Tyson Helton leads the Hilltoppers, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Nov. 27, 2018.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 21.2 points a game while giving up 31.1.

Players to Know
QB Brock Glenn: 92 of 165, 887 yards, 6 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
Brock Glenn on the ground: 451 yards on 91 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Jonathan Stafford Jr.: 46 catches, 433 yards, 1 touchdown.
LB Westen Ard: 2.5 sacks, the team lead.
DB Jaylen Lewis: a team-high 62 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Western Kentucky is 3-5 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Western Kentucky 3rd in the CUSA.
The season so far: L 14-49 at Nevada Wolf Pack (Sept. 5), L 20-70 at Georgia Bulldogs (Sept. 12), L 0-38 at Indiana Hoosiers (Sept. 19), W 52-7 vs Mercyhurst Lakers (Sept. 26), L 13-34 at New Mexico State Aggies (Oct. 1), W 34-13 vs Missouri State Bears (Oct. 8), W 16-3 at Sam Houston Bearkats (Oct. 14), L 21-35 vs Delaware Blue Hens (Oct. 27).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Middle Tennessee (4-4, 2-2 Conference USA) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:30 p.m. ET, at Johnny "Red" Floyd Stadium, Murfreesboro, Tennessee, on ESPN+.
Last time out. We lost to the Delaware Blue Hens 35-21 at home on Oct. 27. Brock Glenn went 25 of 37 for 224 yards, with 1 touchdown, Brock Glenn ran 17 times for 129 yards and Moussa Barry caught 4 balls for 80 yards.
Middle Tennessee lost to the Kennesaw State Owls 44-28 at home on Oct. 28. Roman Gagliano went 21 of 43 for 183 yards; Antonio Martin ran 18 times for 106 yards and 1 touchdown; Cam'ron Lacy caught 4 balls for 73 yards.
Players to watch. Middle Tennessee quarterback Roman Gagliano is 147 of 258 for 1,587 yards, with 12 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Antonio Martin leads their ground game with 586 yards on 112 carries and 2 scores. Cam'ron Lacy is their top target: 45 catches for 510 yards. On defense, linebacker Korey Smith has 73 tackles and linebacker Kaven Call has 1.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 10 regular-season meetings with Middle Tennessee from 2016 through 2025. We won 4 of them, and Middle Tennessee won the last one 42-26 in 2025.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, Western Kentucky or Middle Tennessee.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Opal Stonebriar, Middle Tennessee's insider. Here's what I said:
"I didn't need to know that."
"Seven straight. That's a lot of fresh haircuts, Opal."
"Glenn is a straight-razor quarterback. Smooth, quick, and you don't see him coming."
And the last word from Opal: "It's a homecoming. Different thing. More casseroles."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We've won 7 in a row in this series and lead it 39-35-1, but it's MTSU's Homecoming at Floyd Stadium, and they're 55-29-2 all-time on Homecoming.

My call: Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders 28, Western Kentucky Hilltoppers 27.
