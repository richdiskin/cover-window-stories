Knights in Lawrence: UCF Takes Its Shot at the Jayhawks

By Knox Brevard-Stiles · UCF Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Charge On, Knights! I'm Knox Brevard-Stiles, your UCF insider. I grew up in Orlando, I'm a theme-park ride engineer, and I swear the stands really move. I measure crowd noise with a ride-safety gauge, and I wear a knight's helmet after big wins. Please keep your hands and feet inside the stadium at all times.

My Ride Check: every cheer gets measured, every claim gets checked. Every number in this UCF report has a source. If I don't know something about UCF, I'll say so. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I thank God for Florida sunshine and fall football.

The School in 60 Seconds
UCF first fielded a varsity team in 1979 as an NCAA Division III program and reached Division I-A, now the FBS, in 1996. That made UCF the first program in NCAA history to play in all four divisions of college football. As a Division I-AA program, the Knights made the 1990 and 1993 playoffs. Today they play in the Big 12.

Home is the Acrisure Bounce House on UCF's main campus east of Orlando. Wikipedia lists 45,906 seats and CFBD lists 44,206. CFBD says it opened in 2007. The first game there was a 35-32 loss to No. 6 Texas on Sept. 15, 2007, and UCF led 24-23 early in the fourth quarter. Ride Check: the stands held up just fine that day.

The last five seasons: 9-4, 9-5, 6-7, 4-8 and 5-7.

The Coach and the Scheme
Scott Frost is back for a second run in Orlando. He coached UCF in 2016 and 2017, and his 2017 team went 13-0. Then he coached Nebraska from 2018 to 2022. CFBD lists his return as Dec. 7, 2024. As a player, he led Nebraska to the 1997 national championship. He has coached Heisman winner Marcus Mariota and Heisman candidate McKenzie Milton.

Through 8 games this season, UCF is scoring 29.8 points per game and allowing 25.2.

Players to Know

RB Duke Watson: 597 yards on 92 carries, 3 touchdowns.

Defender Isaiah Nixon: 2.5 sacks, the team lead.

The Conference Picture
UCF is 4-4 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have UCF 8th in the Big 12.
The season so far: W 73-6 vs Bethune-Cookman Wildcats (Sept. 3), L 7-12 at Pittsburgh Panthers (Sept. 12), W 44-30 vs Georgia State Panthers (Sept. 19), W 21-13 vs TCU Horned Frogs (Sept. 26), L 17-27 at Houston Cougars (Oct. 3), L 28-42 at Oklahoma State Cowboys (Oct. 10), W 39-31 (OT) vs BYU Cougars (Oct. 24), L 9-41 vs Baylor Bears (Oct. 30).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Kansas (3-5, 1-4 Big 12) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:45 p.m. ET, at David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium, Lawrence, Kansas, on ESPN2.
Last time out. We lost to the Baylor Bears 41-9 at home on Oct. 30. Keyone Jenkins went 14 of 21 for 82 yards, Rocco Marriott ran 2 times for 50 yards and Josh Derry caught 3 balls for 70 yards.
Kansas lost to the TCU Horned Frogs 22-16 on the road on Oct. 31. Cole Ballard went 20 of 35 for 285 yards, with 1 touchdown; Keaton Kubecka ran 3 times for 58 yards; Nahzae Cox caught 5 balls for 145 yards.
Players to watch. Kansas quarterback Isaiah Marshall is 105 of 196 for 1,506 yards, with 11 touchdowns and 7 interceptions. Dylan Edwards leads their ground game with 573 yards on 125 carries and 4 scores. Nik McMillan is their top target: 25 catches for 466 yards. On defense, linebacker Trey Lathan has 55 tackles and defensive end Leroy Harris III has 3.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 2 regular-season meetings with Kansas from 2016 through 2025. We won 1 of them, and we won the last one 27-20 in 2025.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, UCF or Kansas.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Fitz Calloway, Kansas's insider. Here's what I said:

"Eleven points in the fourth quarter. That's a launch coaster. Zero to sixty."
"It's a lesson in physics. What goes up comes down hard."
And the last word from Fitz: "Two straight. Is that good in football?"

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We visit Lawrence on the Jayhawks' Salute to Service day, and they're 3-1 at home after rallying from 11 points down to beat Baylor 25-24, so nobody here is taking this trip lightly.

My call: Kansas Jayhawks 29, UCF Knights 25.
