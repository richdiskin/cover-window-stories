Mountaineers on the High Plains: West Virginia Visits Lubbock

By Jolene Buckhannon · West Virginia Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Mornin', Mountaineers. Welcome. I'm Jolene Buckhannon. I used to captain the rifle team, so I count possessions the way I used to count rounds: every one matters and you don't waste any. I travel with this West Virginia team, and I live with it. I'm calm, flat, deadpan as a holler on a Sunday morning. until I'm not. I also keep a running pepperoni-roll tally of every fumble the other team coughs up. One roll per fumble, and I eat the tally afterward. When we win, I sing one verse of "Country Roads," drop the mic, then lift it right back up, because mics cost money. When we lose, I count to ten out loud, real slow, like I'm reloading. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, straight shooting. "Load up, I don't miss."

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Milan Puskar Stadium in Morgantown, listed at 60,000, and when that place sings "Country Roads," you feel it in your boots. The man in charge is a familiar one. Rich Rodriguez was named the 36th head coach in school history in December 2024, returning to the program he once led. His teams have always run the football, and this one sure does.

The Coach and the Scheme
Rodriguez was hired on Dec. 11, 2024.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 35.9 points a game while giving up 32.2.

Players to Know
QB Michael Hawkins Jr.: 90 of 159, 1,598 yards, 11 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB Cam Cook: 923 yards on 186 carries, 7 touchdowns.
WR DJ Epps: 22 catches, 556 yards, 5 touchdowns.
Defender David Afogho: 2 sacks, the team lead.
LB Isaiah Patterson: a team-high 48 tackles.

The Conference Picture
West Virginia is 5-3 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have West Virginia 8th in the Big 12.
The season so far: W 31-24 vs Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (Sept. 5), W 52-7 vs UT Martin Skyhawks (Sept. 12), L 24-41 vs Oklahoma State Cowboys (Sept. 26), L 42-45 at Iowa State Cyclones (Oct. 3), L 31-52 vs Arizona Wildcats (Oct. 10), W 45-42 vs Cincinnati Bearcats (Oct. 17), W 24-20 at TCU Horned Frogs (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to No. 13 Texas Tech (7-1, 4-1 Big 12) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 9:30 p.m. ET, at Galaxy Stadium, Lubbock, Texas, on ESPN.
Last time out. We beat the TCU Horned Frogs 24-20 on the road on Oct. 24. Michael Hawkins Jr. went 7 of 15 for 79 yards, with 1 touchdown, Cam Cook ran 18 times for 90 yards and Prince Strachan caught 2 balls for 24 yards.
Texas Tech beat the Arizona Wildcats 34-26 at home on Oct. 31. Thomas Castellanos went 11 of 24 for 144 yards, with 2 touchdowns; J'Koby Williams ran 14 times for 175 yards and 1 touchdown; Kenny Johnson caught 4 balls for 49 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Texas Tech quarterback Will Hammond is 139 of 205 for 1,561 yards, with 6 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. J'Koby Williams leads their ground game with 794 yards on 100 carries and 6 scores. Kenny Johnson is their top target: 35 catches for 548 yards. On defense, linebacker Austin Romaine has 66 tackles and defensive lineman A.J. Holmes Jr. has 7.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 10 regular-season meetings with Texas Tech from 2016 through 2025. We won 5 of them, and we won the last one 49-0 in 2025.
Availability. The public injury data for Texas Tech and West Virginia is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Bodie Ratliff, Texas Tech's insider. Here's what I said:
"Nice of you to have a stadium we can find. There's nothing else out here."
"Raider Alley. Do they have pepperoni rolls?"
"We beat TCU 24-20 on the road last week. We're hot."
And the last word from Bodie: "Forty-nine to nothing. Remember that one?"

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We play in Lubbock on Texas Tech's Celebrate America night, and Raider Alley near the Engineering Key will have the Goin' Band from Raiderland cranking before we get there.

My call: Texas Tech Red Raiders 41, West Virginia Mountaineers 24.
