Hokies in Dallas: Virginia Tech Takes On SMU Friday Night

By Ruthanne Dellinger · Virginia Tech Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hey, Hokie Nation! I'm Ruthanne Dellinger, your Virginia Tech insider. I grew up on a farm outside Blacksburg, and I was a drum major in the Corps of Cadets. I analyze defense like it's a lunch-pail job, because it is.

Here's my promise. I've got mountain-girl spunk, but I don't stretch the truth. Every number in this Virginia Tech report has a source. If I don't know something about Virginia Tech, I'll tell you straight. I celebrate our players as people first, I respect every opponent, and I thank God for country roads, crisp fall air and Hokie football. Exit light, enter stats.

The School in 60 Seconds
Virginia Tech has played football since 1892 and has won more than 700 games. The Hokies have appeared in 33 bowl games, including the 2000 BCS National Championship Game. They play in the ACC after previously competing in the Big East. Home is Lane Stadium in Blacksburg, with seating for more than 65,000. ESPN voted it No. 2 on its 2007 list of the "Top 20 Scariest Places to Play." CFBD lists 65,632 seats.

The last five seasons were a climb: 6-7, 3-8, 7-6, 6-7 and 3-9.

The Coach and the Scheme
James Franklin is in his first season as our head coach. CFBD lists his Virginia Tech hire date as Nov. 17, 2025. He coached Vanderbilt from 2011 to 2013 and Penn State from 2014 to 2025.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 35.0 points a game while giving up 20.5.

Players to Know
QB Ethan Grunkemeyer: 187 of 262, 2,140 yards, 17 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB Jeffrey Overton Jr.: 639 yards on 113 carries, 4 touchdowns.
WR Que'Sean Brown: 51 catches, 685 yards, 3 touchdowns.
DL Aycen Stevens: 8 sacks, the team lead.
LB Keon Wylie: a team-high 41 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Virginia Tech is 6-2 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Virginia Tech 5th in the ACC.
The season so far: W 73-3 vs VMI Keydets (Sept. 5), W 44-21 vs Old Dominion Monarchs (Sept. 12), W 35-26 at Maryland Terrapins (Sept. 19), W 21-14 at Boston College Eagles (Sept. 26), L 33-35 vs Pittsburgh Panthers (Oct. 2), L 20-25 at California Golden Bears (Oct. 10), W 34-24 vs Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets (Oct. 17), W 20-16 at Clemson Tigers (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to SMU (6-2, 4-2 ACC) on Friday, Nov. 6, at 7:00 p.m. ET, at Gerald J. Ford Stadium, Dallas, Texas, on ESPN.
Last time out. We beat the Clemson Tigers 20-16 on the road on Oct. 24. Ethan Grunkemeyer went 20 of 32 for 156 yards, with 2 touchdowns, Jeffrey Overton Jr. ran 10 times for 26 yards and Que'Sean Brown caught 5 balls for 69 yards.
SMU lost to the Syracuse Orange 30-27 on the road on Oct. 31. Kevin Jennings went 29 of 36 for 352 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Kendrick Raphael ran 14 times for 51 yards and 1 touchdown; Yannick Smith caught 5 balls for 95 yards.
Players to watch. SMU quarterback Kevin Jennings is 185 of 251 for 2,414 yards, with 21 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Kendrick Raphael leads their ground game with 692 yards on 135 carries and 9 scores. Yamir Knight is their top target: 44 catches for 523 yards. On defense, linebacker Alexander Kilgore has 41 tackles and defensive end Marques White has 5.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD shows no regular-season meetings with SMU from 2016 through 2025, so this is a fresh fight for our guys.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, Virginia Tech or SMU.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Tatum Delacroix-Price, SMU's insider. Here's what I said:
"Two interceptions. In eight games. Our quarterback treats the football like a family heirloom."
And the last word from Tatum: "Your running game went to sleep at Clemson. Jeffrey Overton Jr. had 10 carries for 26 yards. If you can't run on us, Ruthanne, you're throwing into Marques White all night, and he has five and a half sacks of his own."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We get a Friday night in Dallas, and SMU's Club Takeaway has the home crowd in blue at Gerald J. Ford Stadium, so Hokie fans need to bring the volume.

My call: SMU Mustangs 32, Virginia Tech Hokies 25.
