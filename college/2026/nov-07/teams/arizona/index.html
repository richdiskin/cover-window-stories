Friday Night in Tucson: Arizona Takes On TCU

By Inez Paloverde · Arizona Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hi, hi, hi, Wildcat family, welcome! I'm Inez Paloverde, your Arizona insider, and I am a desert-sunrise optimist. I talk fast, I love hard, and I will defend this team to the last saguaro. I love the desert and I want you to love it too, so be warned: there will be cactus metaphors. A good defense is a lot like a cactus. It's beautiful from a distance, and you do not want to touch it. And when people complain about the Tucson heat, I just smile and say, "It's a dry hype." Every week I'll bring you the players, the coaches and the matchup, plus all the heat I can carry. And I always end the same way: "Bear Down, friends."

The School in 60 Seconds
Let's start with those two words. "Bear Down" goes back to October 1926 and John "Button" Salmon, the student-body president and the Wildcats' quarterback. He was badly hurt in a car accident, and before he died he asked coach J.F. "Pop" McKale to tell the team to "bear down". It's been our rallying cry ever since. Home is Casino Del Sol Stadium in Tucson, listed at 50,782 seats. The program had a big 2025, going 9-4 and reaching the Holiday Bowl, where it lost to SMU 24-19.

The Coach and the Scheme
Brent Brennan was hired on Jan. 16, 2024. He went 4-8 his first season, then turned it around with nine wins in 2025.

Through 8 games this season, Arizona is scoring 34.9 points per game and allowing 18.9.

Players to Know
QB Noah Fifita: 200 of 270, 2,481 yards, 19 touchdowns, 4 interceptions.
RB Kedrick Reescano: 321 yards on 89 carries, 7 touchdowns.
WR Isaiah Mizell: 34 catches, 675 yards, 6 touchdowns.
LB Chase Kennedy: 6 sacks, the team lead.
LB Taye Brown: a team-high 47 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Arizona is 6-2 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Arizona 4th in the Big 12.
The season so far: W 35-7 vs Northern Arizona Lumberjacks (Sept. 5), L 17-28 at BYU Cougars (Sept. 12), W 42-17 vs Northern Illinois Huskies (Sept. 19), W 34-24 at Washington State Cougars (Sept. 26), W 34-7 vs Cincinnati Bearcats (Oct. 3), W 52-31 at West Virginia Mountaineers (Oct. 10), W 39-3 vs Iowa State Cyclones (Oct. 24), L 26-34 at Texas Tech Red Raiders (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host TCU (4-4, 2-3 Big 12) on Friday, Nov. 6, at 10:15 p.m. ET, at Casino Del Sol Stadium, Tucson, Arizona, on ESPN. We come in ranked No. 24.
Last time out. We lost to the Texas Tech Red Raiders 34-26 on the road on Oct. 31. Noah Fifita went 33 of 45 for 417 yards, with 1 touchdown, Kedrick Reescano ran 9 times for 18 yards and 2 touchdowns and Isaiah Mizell caught 7 balls for 182 yards and 1 touchdown.
TCU beat the Kansas Jayhawks 22-16 at home on Oct. 31. Jaden Craig went 13 of 29 for 141 yards; Jaden Craig ran 15 times for 87 yards and 1 touchdown; Kari Ashley caught 3 balls for 50 yards.
Players to watch. TCU quarterback Jaden Craig is 175 of 269 for 2,207 yards, with 11 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. Jeremy Payne leads their ground game with 429 yards on 88 carries and 2 scores. Kari Ashley is their top target: 38 catches for 560 yards. On defense, safety Jamel Johnson has 50 tackles and linebacker Koron Hayward has 5.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has one regular-season meeting with TCU from 2016 through 2025, and we won the last one 49-28 in 2024.
Availability. The public injury data for TCU and Arizona is empty this week, so I'm sticking to who has actually been on the field.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Ryder Galloway, TCU's insider. Here's what I said:
"Noah throws the ball like I play piccolo. High, sharp and right on the note."
"Your quarterback is going to meet Chase so many times Friday he'll want to sit at Yia Yia's House with the grandmas."
"That was a different Arizona. This one is ranked."
And the last word from Ryder: "And BYU beat you by 11 in Provo. BYU beat us by seven. Same Cougars."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
The Tucson Greek Festival runs Friday and Saturday at Jácome Plaza, with Papou's Cafe serving Greek coffee and loukoumades, so downtown Tucson is buzzing the same night we play TCU.

My call: Arizona Wildcats 26, TCU Horned Frogs 16.
