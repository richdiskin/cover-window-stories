Gold in the Cold: Minnesota Hosts UCLA

By Torvald "Tor" Engebretsen · Minnesota Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Well, hello there. I'm Torvald Engebretsen, Tor to my friends, and I'm your Minnesota insider. I grew up in Minneapolis ice-fishing with my grandpa, and I know the history of every rivalry trophy this program plays for. Minnesota has more trophies than I have winter coats, and I have a lot of winter coats.

I'm dry, I'm earnest and I'm polite, but get me started on rivalry lore and I'm unstoppable. Here's my promise: every number in this report has a source. If I don't know something about Minnesota, I'll tell you so. I celebrate our players as young people first, I respect every opponent, even the ones from south of the border, and I thank God for crisp autumn Saturdays. Cold enough for ya? Good.

The School in 60 Seconds
Minnesota started playing football in 1882 and has been in the Big Ten since the league began in 1896 as the Western Conference. The Golden Gophers claim seven national championships. Four of them, in 1936, 1940, 1941 and 1960, came from the major wire-service polls. Since 2009, home has been Huntington Bank Stadium in Minneapolis. CFBD lists 50,850 seats.

The last five seasons have been sturdy: 9-4, 9-4, 6-7, 8-5 and 8-5. Four winning seasons in five years. My grandpa would call that a good catch.

The Coach and the Scheme
P.J. Fleck has led Minnesota since 2017. CFBD lists his Minnesota hire date as Jan. 6, 2017. He coached Western Michigan from 2013 to 2016. He played receiver at Northern Illinois and spent time with the San Francisco 49ers. He was a receivers coach at Northern Illinois, Rutgers and the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. In 2019 he led the Gophers to one of their best seasons in half a century.

Through 8 games this season, Minnesota is scoring 25.2 points per game and allowing 20.5.

Players to Know
QB Drake Lindsey: 161 of 259, 1,592 yards, 10 touchdowns, 6 interceptions.
RB TJ Thomas: 379 yards on 63 carries, 1 touchdown.
WR Javon Tracy: 46 catches, 460 yards, 1 touchdown.
DL TJ Bush Jr.: 5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Maverick Baranowski: a team-high 60 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Minnesota is 5-3 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Minnesota 5th in the Big Ten.
The season so far: W 59-7 vs Eastern Illinois Panthers (Sept. 3), L 13-38 vs Mississippi State Bulldogs (Sept. 12), W 41-7 vs Akron Zips (Sept. 19), W 27-24 at Washington Huskies (Sept. 26), W 20-14 vs Michigan Wolverines (Oct. 3), L 13-41 at Purdue Boilermakers (Oct. 10), W 13-10 vs Iowa Hawkeyes (Oct. 24), L 16-23 (OT) at Indiana Hoosiers (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host No. 5 UCLA (8-0, 5-0 Big Ten) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at noon ET, at Huntington Bank Stadium, Minneapolis, Minnesota, on Fox.
Last time out. We lost to the Indiana Hoosiers 23-16 (OT) on the road on Oct. 31. Drake Lindsey went 20 of 37 for 120 yards, with 1 touchdown, TJ Thomas ran 6 times for 54 yards and Javon Tracy caught 7 balls for 40 yards.
UCLA beat the Nevada Wolf Pack 27-0 at home on Oct. 31. Ty Dieffenbach went 16 of 30 for 178 yards, with 1 touchdown; Wayne Knight ran 11 times for 53 yards; Jalen Saint Paul caught 3 balls for 52 yards.
Players to watch. UCLA quarterback Nico Iamaleava is 72 of 116 for 982 yards, with 4 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Wayne Knight leads their ground game with 680 yards on 110 carries and 12 scores. Semaj Morgan is their top target: 36 catches for 415 yards. On defense, linebacker Jalen Woods has 62 tackles and linebacker Sammy Omosigho has 5.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has one regular-season meeting with UCLA from 2016 through 2025, and UCLA won the last one 21-17 in 2024.
Availability. No official availability report for Minnesota or UCLA was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Kenzie Rhee, UCLA's insider. Here's what I said:
"Cut to the scoreboard in 2024. Minnesota 21, UCLA 17. I remember that one. I remember it the way I remember a walleye that was this big. Every year it gets a little bigger."
"Thank you. I practice it on the ice."
"That is the first honest thing you have said all day. Nothing to lose is the best way to play. We are 5-3, we have a home crowd, and we have Maverick Baranowski with 60 tackles waiting for Knight at the hole like a pike under the ice."
And the last word from Kenzie: "Final scene. Knight runs for a touchdown, the defense holds in the fourth quarter, and UCLA flies home 9-0 with snow on its shoes."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Saturday, The Museum of Russian Art opens its Painting Snow exhibition of forty paintings with free admission all day, and the Gophers men's hockey team has its Gold Out against Wisconsin.

My call: UCLA Bruins 25, Minnesota Golden Gophers 20.
