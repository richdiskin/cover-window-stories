Heroes Day in Boone: App State Hosts Georgia State

By Zeb Coldiron · App State Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Well, howdy. Come on up the mountain. I'm Zebulon Coldiron, but you can call me Zeb, and I'm your App State insider. I'm a fourth-generation Watauga County kid, and I learned to whittle and read a defense on the same front porch. My granddaddy could spot a blitz before the snap and carve a perfect duck in the same afternoon. I'm still working on the duck.

I don't hurry, and I don't holler (much). I treat every week like a hike: steady climbing, honest footing, then a view at the top. That means real numbers, plain talk, and a fair word for every visitor. If I don't know something about App State, I'll tell you, and then I'll go find out. Some programs get a stadium. We got a mountain with a stadium on it. Come up for the game, stay for the leaves, and bring your grandma a jacket.

The School in 60 Seconds
Appalachian State is in Boone, North Carolina, and has played FBS football in the Sun Belt since 2014. Before that, the Mountaineers were an FCS power from 1978 to 2013. They won three straight national championships from 2005 to 2007, the first FCS team to do it since the playoffs began in 1978. The program has won about 661 games and 22 conference championships. Armanti Edwards won the Walter Payton Award back to back, in 2008 and 2009, the first player to do so.

Home is Kidd Brewer Stadium, named for the coach whose 1937 team went unbeaten and unscored upon in the regular season, outscoring opponents 206-0. CFBD lists a capacity of 30,000. The last five seasons: 10-4, 6-6, 9-5, 5-6 and 5-8.

The Coach and the Scheme
Dowell Loggains is in his second season. CFBD lists his App State hire date as Dec. 7, 2024. He came to Boone from South Carolina, where he was offensive coordinator. Before that, he was an NFL offensive coordinator for the Titans, Bears, Dolphins and Jets. He played quarterback at Arkansas.

Through 8 games this season, App State is scoring 26.8 points per game and allowing 22.6.

Players to Know

RB Jaquari Lewis: 431 yards on 86 carries, 8 touchdowns.
WR Sam Pickett III: 36 catches, 560 yards, 4 touchdowns.
DL Caleb Sandstrom: 5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Colton Phares: a team-high 63 tackles.

The Conference Picture
App State is 5-3 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have App State 3rd in the Sun Belt - East.
The season so far: W 55-3 vs Maine Black Bears (Sept. 5), W 27-24 at East Carolina Pirates (Sept. 12), W 26-21 vs Charlotte 49ers (Sept. 19), L 31-41 at NC State Wolfpack (Sept. 26), L 3-10 vs Old Dominion Monarchs (Oct. 10), W 35-32 at Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (Oct. 16), W 34-31 (OT) vs James Madison Dukes (Oct. 22), L 3-19 at Georgia Southern Eagles (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Georgia State (4-4, 1-3 Sun Belt) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 2:30 p.m. ET, at Kidd Brewer Stadium, Boone, North Carolina, on ESPN+.
Last time out. We lost to the Georgia Southern Eagles 19-3 on the road on Oct. 31. Malachi Singleton went 15 of 28 for 215 yards, Jaquari Lewis ran 3 times for 11 yards and Sam Pickett III caught 4 balls for 86 yards.
Georgia State lost to the Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 31-20 at home on Oct. 31. Ayden Pereira went 20 of 37 for 200 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Qwantavius Wiggins ran 11 times for 96 yards; Grant Hollier caught 3 balls for 52 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Georgia State quarterback Cameran Brown is 110 of 166 for 1,356 yards, with 14 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Qwantavius Wiggins leads their ground game with 446 yards on 65 carries and 3 scores. Rodshaun Dorsey is their top target: 28 catches for 459 yards. On defense, linebacker Jaydon Hood has 49 tackles and linebacker Xavier Esqillion has 7 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 10 regular-season meetings with Georgia State from 2016 through 2025. We won 5 of them, and Georgia State won the last one 41-20 in 2025.
Availability. No official availability report for App State or Georgia State was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's the App State Heroes Day Game, and the Heroes Day Tailgate runs on the McKinney Alumni Center patio on Blowing Rock Road from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m.

My call: App State Mountaineers 30, Georgia State Panthers 27.
