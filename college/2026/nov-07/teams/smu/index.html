Friday Night on the Boulevard: SMU Hosts Virginia Tech

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Well, hello, Mustangs! I'm Tatum Delacroix-Price, your SMU insider. I run a little boutique in Highland Park. I know every handshake in this town, and I know every nickel blitz in our playbook. Now, fair warning: I grade the other team's sideline outfits on air, and I am never wrong. Polished, then savage. That's the Dallas way. When we win, I pop imaginary confetti and declare the Boulevard officially iconic. When we lose, I put on bigger sunglasses. Much bigger. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, with a little Dallas swagger. My motto: "Pony up or sit down."

The School in 60 Seconds
SMU has a Heisman Trophy winner: Doak Walker in 1948. Then came the Pony Express era, with Eric Dickerson and Craig James in the backfield. More recently, the Mustangs played in the 2024 ACC championship game and lost to Clemson 34-31 on a field goal at the end. Home is Gerald J. Ford Stadium in University Park, listed at 32,000, an intimate little jewel box of a stadium. And it's spotless.

The Coach and the Scheme
Rhett Lashlee was hired on Nov. 30, 2021.

The 8-game numbers: 35.2 points per game scored, 22.2 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Kevin Jennings: 185 of 251, 2,414 yards, 21 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Kendrick Raphael: 692 yards on 135 carries, 9 touchdowns.
WR Yamir Knight: 44 catches, 523 yards, 3 touchdowns.
DE Marques White: 5.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Alexander Kilgore: a team-high 41 tackles.

The Conference Picture
SMU is 6-2 overall and 4-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have SMU 3rd in the ACC.
The season so far: W 27-24 at Florida State Seminoles (Sept. 7), W 56-10 vs UC Davis Aggies (Sept. 12), L 31-41 at Louisville Cardinals (Sept. 19), W 34-24 vs Missouri State Bears (Sept. 26), W 25-16 vs Boston College Eagles (Oct. 3), W 23-13 vs Virginia Cavaliers (Oct. 17), W 59-20 vs California Golden Bears (Oct. 24), L 27-30 at Syracuse Orange (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Virginia Tech (6-2, 3-2 ACC) on Friday, Nov. 6, at 7:00 p.m. ET, at Gerald J. Ford Stadium, Dallas, Texas, on ESPN.
Last time out. We lost to the Syracuse Orange 30-27 on the road on Oct. 31. Kevin Jennings went 29 of 36 for 352 yards, with 2 touchdowns, Kendrick Raphael ran 14 times for 51 yards and 1 touchdown and Yannick Smith caught 5 balls for 95 yards.
Virginia Tech beat the Clemson Tigers 20-16 on the road on Oct. 24. Ethan Grunkemeyer went 20 of 32 for 156 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Jeffrey Overton Jr. ran 10 times for 26 yards; Que'Sean Brown caught 5 balls for 69 yards.
Players to watch. Virginia Tech quarterback Ethan Grunkemeyer is 187 of 262 for 2,140 yards, with 17 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. Jeffrey Overton Jr. leads their ground game with 639 yards on 113 carries and 4 scores. Que'Sean Brown is their top target: 51 catches for 685 yards. On defense, linebacker Keon Wylie has 41 tackles and defensive lineman Aycen Stevens has 8 sacks.
The series. We did not meet Virginia Tech in the regular season from 2016 through 2025, per CFBD, so there is no recent history to go on.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, SMU or Virginia Tech.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Friday is Arbor Day in University Park, where kids from Highland Park ISD help plant a tree in one of the city's parks, and that night it's Club Takeaway at Ford Stadium, so wear blue.

My call: SMU Mustangs 32, Virginia Tech Hokies 25.
