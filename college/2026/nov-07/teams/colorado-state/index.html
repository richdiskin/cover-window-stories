Rams at Canvas: Colorado State Hosts Boise State

By Ansel Rimrock · Colorado State Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Mountain air, thick defense. Hello, friends, I'm Ansel Rimrock. Most mornings you'll find me on a trail in the foothills west of town, and on fall Saturdays you'll find me with the Rams. I travel with this Colorado State team and I live with it, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines. I'm a calm guy, but don't mistake calm for quiet: I'm competitive right down to my trail shoes. I think football is just a long trail run with blocking. You pace yourself, you climb, you don't panic on the switchbacks, and you finish strong. Fort Collins on a fall Saturday is green and gold from the foothills to the stadium. Visiting teams breathe a little harder up here, and our kids love it. Ram tough, trail ready.

The School in 60 Seconds
The Colorado State Rams compete in the Pac-12. Our big rivalries are with Colorado, Wyoming and Air Force. We've played at Canvas Stadium since 2017; before that, the Rams spent 49 seasons, 1968 to 2016, at Sonny Lubick Field at Hughes Stadium. CFBD lists Canvas at 36,500 seats. The last five seasons were a steep climb: 3-9, 3-9, 5-7, a nice 8-5 in 2024 and then 2-10 in 2025. Every trail has a hard stretch. We're climbing out of one.

The Coach and the Scheme
Jim Mora was hired on Nov. 26, 2025, and this is his first season.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 35.5 points a game while giving up 42.4.

Players to Know
QB Hauss Hejny: 90 of 134, 1,138 yards, 14 touchdowns, 2 interceptions.
RB Mel Brown: 454 yards on 52 carries, 3 touchdowns.
WR RJ Vick: 29 catches, 425 yards, 4 touchdowns.
DL Brandon Kelley: 1.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Oumar Diomande: a team-high 76 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Colorado State is 3-5 overall and 1-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have Colorado State 7th in the Pac-12.
The season so far: W 35-13 vs Wyoming Cowboys (Sept. 5), W 58-24 vs Southern Utah Thunderbirds (Sept. 12), L 23-41 vs BYU Cougars (Sept. 19), L 45-59 at UTSA Roadrunners (Sept. 26), L 26-56 vs Oregon State Beavers (Oct. 3), L 27-66 at Texas State Bobcats (Oct. 15), W 56-49 vs San Diego State Aztecs (Oct. 24), L 14-31 at Utah State Aggies (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host No. 20 Boise State (7-1, 4-0 Pac-12) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 6:00 p.m. ET, at Canvas Stadium, Fort Collins, Colorado, on USA Net.
Last time out. We lost to the Utah State Aggies 31-14 on the road on Oct. 31. Hauss Hejny went 19 of 30 for 186 yards, with 1 touchdown, Durell Robinson ran 16 times for 66 yards and 1 touchdown and Terrence Smith Jr. caught 5 balls for 70 yards.
Boise State beat the Texas State Bobcats 46-45 at home on Oct. 31. Maddux Madsen went 24 of 33 for 236 yards, with 1 touchdown; Dylan Riley ran 25 times for 86 yards and 1 touchdown; Rasean Jones caught 9 balls for 117 yards and 3 touchdowns.
Players to watch. Boise State quarterback Maddux Madsen is 148 of 222 for 1,681 yards, with 15 touchdowns and 1 interception. Dylan Riley leads their ground game with 1,049 yards on 165 carries and 9 scores. Rasean Jones is their top target: 38 catches for 554 yards. On defense, linebacker Boen Phelps has 49 tackles and linebacker Jayden Virgin-Morgan has 3.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 9 regular-season meetings with Boise State from 2016 through 2025. We won 6 of them, and we won the last one 49-21 in 2025.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Colorado State vs. Boise State. When Colorado State or Boise State puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Thatcher Bluestem, Boise State's insider. Here's what I said:
"The one we won was 31-30 in 2023. I was there. I ran home."
"That San Diego State game. Hauss Hejny went 24 of 31 for 333 and 3 touchdowns. When he plays, we are a different team."
And the last word from Thatcher: "Maddux Madsen has thrown 1 interception all year. One. In eight games. He has 1,681 yards and 15 touchdowns. And Dylan Riley has 1,049 rushing yards."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's a Saturday at Canvas Stadium with gameday parking open from 10 a.m. to 10 p.m., and Boise State alumni are holding a Bronco Bash on College Avenue from 1 to 3:30, so expect some blue in Fort Collins.

My call: Boise State Broncos 44, Colorado State Rams 28.
