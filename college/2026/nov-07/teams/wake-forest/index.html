Deacons at 7-1: Wake Forest Hosts Merrimack in Winston-Salem

By Eloise Brightman-Rowe · Wake Forest Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Roll the Quad! I'm Eloise Brightman-Rowe, and I'm your Wake Forest insider. I'm a debate-team champ and Wake grad who rolls the Quad and roasts bad tackling, politely. On the podcast, that segment is called Debate Club. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate Wake Forest players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no Wake Forest fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium in Winston-Salem, built in 1968 with a listed capacity of 31,500. The fight song is "O' Here's to Wake Forest," and the band is the Spirit of the Old Gold & Black. The last five seasons went 11-3, 8-5, 4-8, 4-8 and 9-4, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about rolling the Quad. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Jake Dickert leads the Demon Deacons, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 18, 2024.

Through 8 games this season, Wake Forest is scoring 39.1 points per game and allowing 17.9.

Players to Know
QB Gio Lopez: 153 of 228, 2,176 yards, 18 touchdowns, 1 interception.
RB Ty Clark III: 376 yards on 78 carries, 7 touchdowns.
WR Carlos Hernandez: 57 catches, 856 yards, 5 touchdowns.
DL Langston Hardy: 5.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Vincent Firenze: a team-high 52 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Wake Forest is 7-1 overall and 5-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have Wake Forest 2nd in the ACC.
The season so far: W 38-16 vs Akron Zips (Sept. 3), W 38-36 at Purdue Boilermakers (Sept. 12), L 20-33 vs Miami Hurricanes (Sept. 18), W 30-27 at Louisville Cardinals (Sept. 26), W 57-3 vs Stanford Cardinal (Oct. 3), W 38-14 at NC State Wolfpack (Oct. 10), W 41-7 at California Golden Bears (Oct. 17), W 51-7 vs Virginia Cavaliers (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview
We host the Merrimack Warriors (4-5) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 3:30 p.m. ET, at Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium in Winston-Salem, on ACC Network. We come in ranked No. 14.
Last time out. We beat the Virginia Cavaliers 51-7 at home on Oct. 31. Gio Lopez went 13 of 21 for 229 yards, with 1 touchdown. Jamar Searcy ran 8 times for 78 yards and 1 touchdown. Carlos Hernandez caught 8 balls for 136 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Merrimack quarterback AJ Hairston Jr is 114 of 206 for 1,362 yards, with 7 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. Running back Matthias Latham has 623 yards on 121 carries, and receiver Austin Palmer has 25 catches for 417 yards. On defense, safety Danny Simms has 57 tackles and lineman Dwayne Green Jr has 3.5 sacks.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this one when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health.

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Saturday's kickoff in Winston-Salem is 3:30 p.m. ET at Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium, and with the Deacons at 7-1, the home crowd has every reason to show up loud.
