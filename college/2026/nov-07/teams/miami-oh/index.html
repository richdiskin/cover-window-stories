Tuesday Night at Yager: Miami (OH) Hosts Buffalo

By Cordelia Van Doren · Miami (OH) Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Good afternoon, RedHawks, and welcome. I'm Cordelia Van Doren, your Miami (OH) insider. I'm a brick-campus girl through and through, and I know every coach who has ever walked through Oxford. I travel with this team and I live with this team, usually in a cardigan. I do have one small habit. I ambush my co-hosts with coaching-tree trivia when they least expect it. When we win, I add a gold sticker to my Cradle of Coaches chart. When we lose, I remind everyone that even legends lost once, and then I tell you exactly which legends and when. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, with a little history pinned to the lapel. "Another coach from the Cradle, folks."

The School in 60 Seconds
Miami is the Cradle of Coaches, and that's not my phrase. The term was coined in 1959 by Bob Kurz. The honor roll includes Paul Brown, Weeb Ewbank, Ara Parseghian and Bo Schembechler. Pop quiz: name another campus that produced all four. I'll wait. In the modern era the RedHawks keep adding chapters. Under Chuck Martin, Miami won the 2019 MAC championship, beating Central Michigan 26-21 in the title game, and the 2023 title, beating No. 23 Toledo 23-14. Home is Yager Stadium in Oxford, listed at 24,286.

The Coach and the Scheme
Chuck Martin was hired on Dec. 3, 2013, which makes him one of the longest-serving coaches in the conference.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 27.0 points a game while giving up 25.8.

Players to Know
QB David McComb: 65 of 103, 1,121 yards, 7 touchdowns, 1 interception.
RB Rodney Nelson: 391 yards on 78 carries, 3 touchdowns.
WR Maleek Huggins: 24 catches, 431 yards, 1 touchdown.
DL Roosevelt Andrews III: 4.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Malcolm McCain: a team-high 59 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Miami (OH) is 5-3 overall and 3-1 in league play. ESPN's standings have Miami (OH) 3rd in the MAC.
The season so far: L 14-59 at Pittsburgh Panthers (Sept. 5), W 45-7 vs Holy Cross Crusaders (Sept. 12), L 31-35 at Cincinnati Bearcats (Sept. 19), W 24-21 vs UConn Huskies (Sept. 26), L 20-24 vs Bowling Green Falcons (Oct. 3), W 20-19 at Massachusetts Minutemen (Oct. 10), W 34-14 vs Akron Zips (Oct. 17), W 28-27 at Central Michigan Chippewas (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Buffalo (5-3, 3-1 MAC) on Tuesday, Nov. 3, at 8:00 p.m. ET, at Yager Stadium, Oxford, Ohio, on ESPN.
Last time out. We beat the Central Michigan Chippewas 28-27 on the road on Oct. 24. Caleb Heavner went 18 of 33 for 221 yards, with 1 touchdown, Caleb Heavner ran 15 times for 58 yards and 1 touchdown and Maleek Huggins caught 7 balls for 122 yards.
Buffalo beat the Bowling Green Falcons 31-24 at home on Oct. 24. Jason Wright went 13 of 21 for 127 yards, with 1 touchdown; Messiah Burch ran 33 times for 184 yards and 3 touchdowns; Clint Walker caught 2 balls for 40 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Buffalo quarterback Jason Wright is 92 of 185 for 987 yards, with 7 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Messiah Burch leads their ground game with 845 yards on 151 carries and 5 scores. Chance Morrow is their top target: 41 catches for 480 yards. On defense, linebacker SaVeon Brown has 67 tackles and defender Sedrick Vessah has 2 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 9 regular-season meetings with Buffalo from 2016 through 2025. We won 7 of them, and Buffalo won the last one 37-20 in 2025.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Miami (OH) vs. Buffalo. When Miami (OH) or Buffalo puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Kendra Woolcott, Buffalo's insider. Here's what I said:
"We win close and we like it. Close is a family tradition. Ask the statues."
"Our defense has met snowplows. Roosevelt Andrews III has 4.5 sacks, and Malcolm McCain has 59 tackles. We've been clearing roads in Oxford since before your stadium had a parking lot."
"Whoever throws it, Huggins catches it. That's not a scheme. That's manners."
And the last word from Kendra: "Just another lake-effect Tuesday."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
On Tuesday, Party at the Polls runs at the Bell Tower on East High Street from 11 a.m. to 7:30 p.m., and the 11 bronze statues of the Cradle of Coaches still stand at the south gate of Yager Stadium.

My call: Miami (OH) RedHawks 29, Buffalo Bulls 22.
