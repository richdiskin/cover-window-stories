Clones Crash Homecoming: Iowa State Visits Waco

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Good evening, Cyclone Nation, and here's your Saturday forecast! I'm Twyla Hemmingsen, your Iowa State insider, coming to you live from the Ames weather center. Okay, it's my kitchen, but it has a very nice map on the wall. I'm high-energy, a little nerdy and joyful about it, and I talk about football the way the TV meteorologist talks about a cold front: pressure systems, wind shifts, and a big red blob moving across the screen. Every week I'll bring you the players, the coaches and the matchup, plus a full radar sweep of the opponent. Expect conditions to change quickly. When things are going our way, you'll hear "Forecast: loud, with gusts." When it's really rolling: "Cyclone warning in effect." Umbrellas optional, joy mandatory.

The School in 60 Seconds
Our home is Jack Trice Stadium in Ames, which holds 61,500. It's named for Jack Trice, Iowa State's first Black athlete. The stadium got his name in 1997, and it is still the only major college football stadium named for a Black man. Every Cyclone fan should know his story. On the field, Iowa State went 8-4 last season. Then came the biggest offseason storm in the country. Coach Matt Campbell left for Penn State and took three-year starting QB Rocco Becht with him. More than 50 Cyclones entered the transfer portal, including 16 starters. That's a full barometric reset.

The Coach and the Scheme
Jimmy Rogers was named Iowa State's 34th head coach on Dec. 5, 2025, coming from Washington State on a six-year contract. Before that he went 27-3 and won an FCS national title at South Dakota State, and he brought in more than 45 transfers to rebuild this roster.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 29.0 points a game while giving up 27.2.

Players to Know
QB Jaylen Raynor: 111 of 186, 1,416 yards, 10 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Aiden Flora: 937 yards on 131 carries, 8 touchdowns.
WR Jamal Polite Jr.: 14 catches, 286 yards, 2 touchdowns.
DE Isaac Terrell: 3 sacks, the team lead.
LB Sullivan Schlimgen: a team-high 79 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Iowa State is 4-4 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have Iowa State 8th in the Big 12.
The season so far: W 38-10 vs Southeast Missouri State Redhawks (Sept. 5), L 13-16 at Iowa Hawkeyes (Sept. 12), W 55-7 vs Bowling Green Falcons (Sept. 19), L 17-31 vs Utah Utes (Sept. 26), W 45-42 vs West Virginia Mountaineers (Oct. 3), L 10-24 at BYU Cougars (Oct. 9), L 3-39 at Arizona Wildcats (Oct. 24), W 51-49 vs Oklahoma State Cowboys (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Baylor (5-3, 3-2 Big 12) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at noon ET, at McLane Stadium, Waco, Texas.
Last time out. We beat the Oklahoma State Cowboys 51-49 at home on Oct. 31. Jaylen Raynor went 18 of 28 for 309 yards, with 3 touchdowns, Aiden Flora ran 29 times for 221 yards and 2 touchdowns and Jamal Polite Jr. caught 6 balls for 171 yards and 1 touchdown.
Baylor beat the UCF Knights 41-9 on the road on Oct. 30. DJ Lagway went 16 of 25 for 319 yards, with 4 touchdowns; Ryelan Morris ran 11 times for 82 yards; Gavin Freeman caught 5 balls for 114 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Baylor quarterback DJ Lagway is 129 of 242 for 1,671 yards, with 13 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Dawson Pendergrass leads their ground game with 338 yards on 95 carries and 2 scores. Gavin Freeman is their top target: 63 catches for 792 yards. On defense, linebacker Travion Barnes has 57 tackles and defensive end Garrick Ponder has 4 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 9 regular-season meetings with Baylor from 2016 through 2025. We won 4 of them, and Baylor won the last one 43-21 in 2024.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Iowa State vs. Baylor. When Iowa State or Baylor puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Maggie Whitlock, Baylor's insider. Here's what I said:
"Arizona was a weather event. Every storm has a calm spot. We came out of it and scored 51."
"Freeman is very good. Sixty-three catches, 792 yards. I will grant you that the way a forecaster grants you a sunny afternoon, with an asterisk. But Flora has 937 rushing yards and 8 touchdowns, and nobody in Waco has had to tackle him yet."
"And in 2024 we beat you 43-21. Forty-three. That is not a weather event, that is climate."
And the last word from Maggie: "That is how you know it is good design. Homecoming, Twyla. Wear something warm and something green."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
We walk into Baylor's Homecoming at McLane Stadium, and Cyclone fans in early can hit the Waco Downtown Farmers Market on East Bridge Street on Saturday.

My call: Baylor Bears 31, Iowa State Cyclones 21.
