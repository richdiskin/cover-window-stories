Taco Tuesday MACtion: Akron Hosts Ohio Under the Lights

By Hollister Grubbs · Akron Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Greetings, Zips fans, and welcome! I'm Hollister Grubbs, but everybody calls me Zip. I'm a polymer-science grad from the Rubber City, and I measure football the only scientific way: in tire tread depth. Deep, grippy tread means a team that holds the road on third down. Bald tires mean a team sliding all over the place. I travel with this team and I live with this team, usually with a clipboard of formulas. When we win, I bounce like a superball: BOING! When we lose, I say, "Some tread wear today," and start scribbling equations to figure out why. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, vulcanized for durability. "That's some sticky tread."

The School in 60 Seconds
Akron plays at InfoCision Stadium, listed at 30,000, in a city that put the world on rubber tires. Our big rivalry is the Wagon Wheel game against Kent State. The trophy dates to 1946, and Kent State won the first nine games for it. Kent State won the 2025 meeting 42-35. Before that, we won the 2023 and 2024 meetings, 31-27 and 38-17.

The Coach and the Scheme
Joe Moorhead was hired on Dec. 4, 2021. Before Akron he was Mississippi State's head coach in 2018 and 2019, then Oregon's offensive coordinator and quarterbacks coach in 2020 and 2021.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 19.0 points a game while giving up 32.8.

Players to Know
QB Brayden Roggow: 112 of 190, 1,343 yards, 7 touchdowns, 9 interceptions.
RB Jordan Gant: 618 yards on 127 carries, 6 touchdowns.
WR Marcel Williams: 34 catches, 375 yards, 3 touchdowns.
DL Cyrus Durham: 2.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Donelius Johnson: a team-high 71 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Akron is 1-7 overall and 0-4 in league play. ESPN's standings have Akron 12th in the MAC.
The season so far: L 16-38 at Wake Forest Demon Deacons (Sept. 3), W 45-10 vs Robert Morris Colonials (Sept. 12), L 7-41 at Minnesota Golden Gophers (Sept. 19), L 10-38 vs UNLV Rebels (Sept. 26), L 17-41 at Central Michigan Chippewas (Oct. 3), L 23-25 vs Eastern Michigan Eagles (Oct. 10), L 14-34 at Miami (OH) RedHawks (Oct. 17), L 20-35 at Kent State Golden Flashes (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Ohio (6-2, 4-0 MAC) on Tuesday, Nov. 3, at 7:00 p.m. ET, at InfoCision Stadium, Akron, Ohio, on ESPN2.
Last time out. We lost to the Kent State Golden Flashes 35-20 on the road on Oct. 24. Brayden Roggow went 20 of 34 for 275 yards, Jordan Gant ran 28 times for 162 yards and 2 touchdowns and Kyan Mason caught 7 balls for 129 yards.
Ohio beat the Eastern Michigan Eagles 24-20 at home on Oct. 24. Nick Poulos went 19 of 29 for 266 yards, with 1 touchdown; Victor Rosa ran 4 times for 40 yards; Dom Dorwart caught 4 balls for 125 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Ohio quarterback Nick Poulos is 58 of 98 for 781 yards, with 6 touchdowns. Victor Rosa leads their ground game with 364 yards on 77 carries and 5 scores. Riley Neer is their top target: 37 catches for 497 yards. On defense, linebacker Michael Molnar has 70 tackles and linebacker Michael Molnar has 5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 9 regular-season meetings with Ohio from 2016 through 2025. We won 6 of them, and we won the last one 30-10 in 2024.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, Akron or Ohio.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Penny Trautman, Ohio's insider. Here's what I said:
"Penny, I have measured every tire in this lot. The wear pattern says one thing. Akron is 1-7, sure, but we played Eastern Michigan to 23-25. We're not flat. We're just low on air."
"And you lost to South Alabama 36-41. I keep notes. Rubber remembers."
"Fine. Now let me talk about Jordan Gant."
And the last word from Penny: "Brick remembers the other eight."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Tuesday night MACtion is back at InfoCision Stadium, gates open at 6, it's Taco Tuesday theme night, and earlier in the day the Akron Zoo has free admission for Election Day from 11 to 4.

My call: Ohio Bobcats 32, Akron Zips 21.
