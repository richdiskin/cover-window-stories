Homecoming in West Lafayette: Purdue Hosts Maryland

By Barnaby Wolcott-Reese · Purdue Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Boiler up! I'm Barnaby Wolcott-Reese, and I'm your Purdue insider. I'm a West Lafayette native and a mechanical engineer, and I built a model Boilermaker Special in my garage. On the podcast, my segment is called Blueprint. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate Purdue players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no Purdue fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Ross-Ade Stadium in West Lafayette, built in 1924 with a listed capacity of 57,282. The band is the Purdue All-American Marching Band, and the fight song is "Hail Purdue!" The last five seasons went 9-4, 8-6, 4-8, 1-11 and 2-10, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about the Boilermaker Special. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Barry Odom leads the Boilermakers in his second season, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 10, 2024.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 29.5 points a game while giving up 32.1.

Players to Know
QB Ryan Browne: 155 of 250, 1,964 yards, 11 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Fame Ijeboi: 588 yards on 141 carries, 9 touchdowns.
WR Asaad Waseem: 41 catches, 652 yards, 1 touchdown.
DL Curt Neal: 2.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Jojo Hayden: a team-high 59 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Purdue is 3-5 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have Purdue 11th in the Big Ten.
The season so far: W 44-19 vs Indiana State Sycamores (Sept. 4), L 36-38 (OT) vs Wake Forest Demon Deacons (Sept. 12), L 38-52 at UCLA Bruins (Sept. 19), L 10-49 vs Notre Dame Fighting Irish (Sept. 26), W 24-17 at Illinois Fighting Illini (Oct. 3), W 41-13 vs Minnesota Golden Gophers (Oct. 10), L 26-38 vs Washington Huskies (Oct. 16), L 17-31 at Penn State Nittany Lions (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Maryland (2-6, 0-5 Big Ten) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 12:30 p.m. ET, at Ross-Ade Stadium, West Lafayette, Indiana, on BTN.
Last time out. We lost to the Penn State Nittany Lions 31-17 on the road on Oct. 31. Ryan Browne went 10 of 29 for 142 yards, with 1 touchdown, Fame Ijeboi ran 11 times for 20 yards and Asaad Waseem caught 3 balls for 64 yards.
Maryland lost to the Illinois Fighting Illini 20-17 at home on Oct. 31. Malik Washington went 19 of 29 for 190 yards; DeJuan Williams ran 19 times for 57 yards and 1 touchdown; Chris Durr Jr. caught 6 balls for 58 yards.
Players to watch. Maryland quarterback Malik Washington is 174 of 290 for 1,805 yards, with 9 touchdowns and 7 interceptions. DeJuan Williams leads their ground game with 338 yards on 57 carries and 4 scores. Chris Durr Jr. is their top target: 58 catches for 638 yards. On defense, linebacker Daniel Wingate has 48 tackles and linebacker Zion Elee has 3.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 3 regular-season meetings with Maryland from 2016 through 2025. We won 2 of them, and Maryland won the last one 31-29 in 2022.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Purdue vs. Maryland. When Purdue or Maryland puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Abigail "Abby" Chesapeake, Maryland's insider. Here's what I said:
"There are no crabs in Indiana."
"What it explains is that we have other priorities. Like football. Ryan Browne has 1,964 yards and 11 touchdowns. He has torque. He has thrust. He is a precision instrument."
"Every engine has a rough cycle. You recalibrate. Six weeks before that he threw for 378 at UCLA. That is the real output."
And the last word from Abby: "Probably. But I will lose with Old Bay on my fingers, and you will win with pork on yours. We both know who had the better afternoon."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Homecoming, and the Purdue Ag and Vet Med Homecoming Tailgate sets up at the south end of Memorial Mall before the 12:30 kickoff with a catered meal from Shoup's Country Foods.

My call: Purdue Boilermakers 35, Maryland Terrapins 26.
