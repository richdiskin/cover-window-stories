Louisiana Salutes: The Ragin' Cajuns Host South Alabama

By Amélie "Mimi" Fontenot · Louisiana Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Laissez les bons stats rouler! Bonjour, mes amis, I'm Amélie Fontenot, but everybody from Lafayette to Breaux Bridge calls me Mimi. By weekday I'm an accountant. A touchdown? Magnifique. A turnover? Ça c'est bon (if it's ours). Every week you'll get the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines with a little accordion in the background. When we win, I two-step around the desk. When we lose, I say "Laissez les bons temps... later," and eat my boudin solemnly. Allons!

The School in 60 Seconds
The Ragin' Cajuns represent the University of Louisiana at Lafayette, and you may remember the old names, Southwestern Louisiana and USL. We play in the Sun Belt. Home since 1971 has been Cajun Field in Lafayette, listed at 41,426 seats. Our recent history has some magnifique years: 13-1 in 2021 and 10-4 in 2024, with 6-7 seasons in between and 6-7 again in 2025. And the gameday? Cher, the food alone is worth the drive. I've never met a tailgate that didn't have a pot of something on it.

The Coach and the Scheme
Michael Desormeaux was hired Dec. 5, 2021.

The 8-game numbers: 30.1 points per game scored, 21.2 allowed.

Players to Know
QB Lunch Winfield: 130 of 195, 1,452 yards, 6 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
Lunch Winfield on the ground: 470 yards on 110 carries, 9 touchdowns.
WR KeDarius Wade: 13 catches, 279 yards, 2 touchdowns.
LB Lester Jones: 2 sacks, the team lead.
LB Jake St. Andre: a team-high 60 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Louisiana is 5-3 overall and 2-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Louisiana 2nd in the Sun Belt - West.
The season so far: W 38-7 vs Lamar Cardinals (Sept. 5), L 30-49 at USC Trojans (Sept. 12), W 21-14 vs UAB Blazers (Sept. 19), W 34-7 at Charlotte 49ers (Sept. 26), W 23-20 vs Arkansas State Red Wolves (Oct. 3), L 31-32 at Louisiana Tech Bulldogs (Oct. 10), W 33-6 vs Troy Trojans (Oct. 17), L 31-35 at Southern Miss Golden Eagles (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host South Alabama (4-4, 2-2 Sun Belt) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 5:00 p.m. ET, at Our Lady of Lourdes Stadium, Lafayette, Louisiana, on ESPN+.
Last time out. We lost to the Southern Miss Golden Eagles 35-31 on the road on Oct. 24. Lunch Winfield went 22 of 41 for 299 yards, with 2 touchdowns, JJ Garner ran 7 times for 52 yards and 1 touchdown and Shelton Sampson Jr. caught 5 balls for 58 yards.
South Alabama lost to the Louisiana Tech Bulldogs 35-20 at home on Oct. 31. Jared Hollins went 14 of 31 for 192 yards, with 2 touchdowns; PJ Martin ran 12 times for 82 yards; Anthony Eager caught 7 balls for 102 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Players to watch. South Alabama quarterback Jared Hollins is 82 of 156 for 1,195 yards, with 9 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Keenan Phillips leads their ground game with 576 yards on 107 carries and 6 scores. Brendan Jenkins is their top target: 27 catches for 451 yards. On defense, safety Cole Blaylock has 46 tackles and linebacker Xavious Anderson Jr. has 3 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 10 regular-season meetings with South Alabama from 2016 through 2025. We won 3 of them, and South Alabama won the last one 31-22 in 2025.
Availability. ESPN had no injury data for this game when I checked, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health on either side, Louisiana or South Alabama.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Azalea Fairhope-Grice, South Alabama's insider. Here's what I said:
"Of course you do. One kick. Everybody in Mobile has it framed."
And the last word from Azalea: "Home is also where you lost to us twice."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Louisiana Salutes, honoring military members, veterans and first responders, and this is the 15th meeting with South Alabama.

My call: Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns 35, South Alabama Jaguars 26.
