Beat Week in Oxford: Ole Miss Takes On Georgia Twice

By Langston Fairbanks · Ole Miss Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hotty Toddy, and hand me the footnotes. Good evening, gentle readers. I am Langston Fairbanks, a man of seersucker and iced tea, a student of literature who has read every word Mr. Faulkner wrote and every snap the Rebels have played this season, often on the same afternoon in the Grove. I travel with this Ole Miss team and I live with it, and every week I will bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines. Be warned: my takes tend to run three paragraphs. I can't help it. Football, like a good novel, deserves a proper beginning, middle and end. But I always land on a hard number. When we win, I recite a celebratory monologue under the nearest chandelier. When we lose, I murmur, "The past is never dead," and sip my iced tea in silence.

The School in 60 Seconds
The Rebels represent the University of Mississippi, which everyone calls Ole Miss, and play in the SEC. The program was founded in 1893 and has won six SEC titles: 1947, 1954, 1955, 1960, 1962 and 1963. Ole Miss has been recognized as national champion three times by major selectors. Home is Vaught-Hemingway Stadium on campus, opened in 1915 and listed at 64,038 seats. And the recent chapters read like an epic: 10-3, 8-5, 11-2, 10-3, and last year a 13-2 season that AP describes as a playoff team. Since 2023, the Rebels are 25-1 at home.

The Coach and the Scheme
Pete Golding is the head coach in his first full season; CFBD dates his hire to Nov. 30, 2025.

Through 8 games this season, Ole Miss is scoring 37.1 points per game and allowing 24.0.

Players to Know
QB Trinidad Chambliss: 189 of 296, 2,189 yards, 18 touchdowns, 8 interceptions.
RB Kewan Lacy: 388 yards on 78 carries, 5 touchdowns.
WR Deuce Alexander: 48 catches, 669 yards, 5 touchdowns.
DE Blake Purchase: 4.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Keaton Thomas: a team-high 52 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Ole Miss is 6-2 overall and 4-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Ole Miss 3rd in the SEC.
The season so far: W 41-9 vs Charlotte 49ers (Sept. 12), W 32-24 vs LSU Tigers (Sept. 19), L 28-52 at Florida Gators (Sept. 26), W 52-17 at Vanderbilt Commodores (Oct. 10), W 31-3 vs Missouri Tigers (Oct. 17), L 27-34 at Texas Longhorns (Oct. 24), W 45-15 vs Auburn Tigers (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host No. 2 Georgia (7-1, 5-1 SEC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:30 p.m. ET, at Vaught-Hemingway Stadium, Oxford, Mississippi, on ABC. We come in ranked No. 12.
Last time out. We beat the Auburn Tigers 45-15 at home on Oct. 31. Trinidad Chambliss went 22 of 34 for 263 yards, with 2 touchdowns, Shekai Mills-Knight ran 16 times for 71 yards and 1 touchdown and Horatio Fields caught 6 balls for 73 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Georgia quarterback Gunner Stockton is 130 of 181 for 1,671 yards, with 16 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Dwight Phillips Jr. leads their ground game with 415 yards on 55 carries and 2 scores. Craig Dandridge is their top target: 20 catches for 349 yards. On defense, linebacker Raylen Wilson has 51 tackles and linebacker Raylen Wilson has 4 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 4 regular-season meetings with Georgia from 2016 through 2025. We won 4 of them, and we won the last one 43-35 in 2025.
Availability. No official availability report for Ole Miss or Georgia was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Thaddeus "Thad" Okafor-Brandt, Georgia's insider. Here's what I said:
"I already made my three gifts. School of Law, a scholarship and the band. That's three points."
And the last word from Thad: "Chambliss also has 8 interceptions. Three at Texas. Georgia's defense eats that up."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
This is Beat Week, a six-day fundraising fight from Nov. 2 to 7 between Ole Miss and Georgia, and every charitable gift of $5 or more to the university counts as one point.

My call: Georgia Bulldogs 31, Ole Miss Rebels 30.
