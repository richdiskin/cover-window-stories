Gold Out in Miami: FIU Celebrates Homecoming Against Missouri State

By Lourdes Ibarguen-Tate · Florida International Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
¡Hola, Panther people! I'm Lourdes Ibarguen-Tate, and I grew up in Sweetwater, five minutes from campus, at a kitchen table where my abuela taught me football with cafecito and dominoes. The dominoes were the players. The creamer cups were the defense. To this day I can't watch a fourth-down stop without craving a colada.

Here's what I am: the loudest, warmest, most bilingual hype machine FIU has ever had, and I will never, ever let anybody sleep on this program. Here's what I'm not: a person who makes things up. Every week I'll bring you the real numbers, the real schedule, and an honest, kind look at whoever we're playing. If I don't know something about Florida International, I'll say "no sé" and go find out. Miami has a lot of teams, but only one is right down the street from my abuela's house. ¡Vamos, Panthers!

The School in 60 Seconds
FIU plays in Conference USA at the FBS level, and home is Pitbull Stadium in Miami. The stadium opened in 1995, and CFBD lists its capacity at 20,000, which matches Wikipedia's figure. The program's trophy case includes a share of the 2010 Sun Belt championship and three bowl appearances.

Look at the last five seasons and you'll see why last year mattered so much to my family: 1-11 in 2021, then three straight 4-8 years, then 7-6 in 2025 and the first bowl trip since 2019. The Panthers also play Florida Atlantic every year in the Shula Bowl.

The Coach and the Scheme
Willie Simmons is in Year Two. CFBD lists his Florida International hire date as Dec. 7, 2024. Before FIU, he was head coach at Florida A&M from 2018 to 2023, winning a black college national championship and a SWAC title in 2023, and at Prairie View A&M from 2015 to 2017. He played quarterback at Clemson and The Citadel.

Through 8 games this season, Florida International is scoring 18.9 points per game and allowing 20.4.

Players to Know
QB JJ Kohl: 152 of 255, 1,801 yards, 11 touchdowns, 9 interceptions.
RB Devonte Lyons: 435 yards on 94 carries, 1 touchdown.
WR Maguire Anderson: 41 catches, 596 yards, 4 touchdowns.
DL DeeJay Holmes Jr.: 5 sacks, the team lead.
DB DeAndre Boykins: a team-high 45 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Florida International is 3-5 overall and 1-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have Florida International 8th in the CUSA.
The season so far: L 9-19 at South Florida Bulls (Sept. 5), W 33-20 vs Buffalo Bulls (Sept. 12), L 10-16 at Florida Atlantic Owls (Sept. 19), W 20-3 vs Long Island University Sharks (Sept. 26), W 22-3 vs New Mexico State Aggies (Oct. 7), L 24-27 at Jacksonville State Gamecocks (Oct. 13), L 23-26 vs Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders (Oct. 20), L 10-49 at Liberty Flames (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Missouri State (3-5, 2-2 Conference USA) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 4:00 p.m. ET, at Pitbull Stadium, Miami, Florida, on ESPN+.
Last time out. We lost to the Liberty Flames 49-10 on the road on Oct. 31. JJ Kohl went 21 of 28 for 164 yards, with 1 touchdown, Devonte Lyons ran 10 times for 52 yards and Kyle McNeal caught 6 balls for 56 yards.
Missouri State beat the Sam Houston Bearkats 24-3 at home on Oct. 27. Henry Belin IV went 12 of 18 for 184 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Connor Lair ran 19 times for 75 yards and 1 touchdown; Jmariyae Robinson caught 7 balls for 102 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Missouri State quarterback Henry Belin IV is 85 of 126 for 1,071 yards, with 8 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Ramone Green Jr. leads their ground game with 272 yards on 67 carries and 2 scores. Isaiah Myers is their top target: 41 catches for 873 yards. On defense, linebacker Jared Lloyd has 86 tackles and defensive end Josh Lukusa has 2.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has one regular-season meeting with Missouri State from 2016 through 2025, and we won the last one 28-21 in 2025.
Availability. No official availability report for Florida International or Missouri State was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Clementine Bilyeu, Missouri State's insider. Here's what I said:
"It's Homecoming, mija. And Military Appreciation. Everybody wears gold. Even the visitors, if they have manners."
"Enough to fill a section. Abuela keeps the list."
"Our defense is like Abuela's kitchen. You don't get through without permission."
And the last word from Clementine: "That's a muddy stretch of river."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's Homecoming and Military Appreciation at Pitbull Stadium, and the call is to wear gold.

My call: Florida International Panthers 26, Missouri State Bears 21.
