Broncos on the Road: Boise State Brings the Blue to Fort Collins

By Thatcher Bluestem · Boise State Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Paint it blue! Howdy, friends, I'm Thatcher Bluestem, and yes, my car is blue, my pancakes are blue and my opinions are the bluest you'll ever hear. I travel with the Broncos and I live with the Broncos, and every week I'll bring you the players, the matchups, the history and the storylines, loud and sunny and endlessly proud. Visitors always ask me if the field really looks that blue in person. Friend, it looks bluer. Bring sunglasses and an open mind. We earned every seat at the table, and I'll never take one of them for granted. Smurf Turf, baby, show some respect.

The School in 60 Seconds
Boise State was established in 1933 and now plays in the Pac-12. The Broncos have won 23 conference championships and six division titles in the Mountain West's Mountain Division. Home is Albertsons Stadium, on campus in Boise, listed at 36,387 seats, and yes, that's where the famous blue turf lives. The last five years: 7-5, 10-4, 8-6, a glorious 12-2 in 2024 and 9-5 in 2025, all in the Mountain West. Blue all the way down.

The Coach and the Scheme
Spencer Danielson is the head coach, and CFBD dates his hire to Dec. 3, 2023.

Through 8 games this season, Boise State is scoring 35.9 points per game and allowing 23.6.

Players to Know
QB Maddux Madsen: 148 of 222, 1,681 yards, 15 touchdowns, 1 interception.
RB Dylan Riley: 1,049 yards on 165 carries, 9 touchdowns.
WR Rasean Jones: 38 catches, 554 yards, 4 touchdowns.
LB Jayden Virgin-Morgan: 3.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Boen Phelps: a team-high 49 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Boise State is 7-1 overall and 4-0 in league play. ESPN's standings have Boise State 1st in the Pac-12.
The season so far: L 27-34 at Oregon Ducks (Sept. 5), W 38-20 vs Memphis Tigers (Sept. 12), W 38-24 vs South Dakota Coyotes (Sept. 19), W 32-7 at Western Michigan Broncos (Sept. 26), W 37-18 vs Utah State Aggies (Oct. 3), W 38-31 at Fresno State Bulldogs (Oct. 10), W 31-10 at Washington State Cougars (Oct. 24), W 46-45 vs Texas State Bobcats (Oct. 31).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We travel to Colorado State (3-5, 1-3 Pac-12) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 6:00 p.m. ET, at Canvas Stadium, Fort Collins, Colorado, on USA Net. We come in ranked No. 20.
Last time out. We beat the Texas State Bobcats 46-45 at home on Oct. 31. Maddux Madsen went 24 of 33 for 236 yards, with 1 touchdown, Dylan Riley ran 25 times for 86 yards and 1 touchdown and Rasean Jones caught 9 balls for 117 yards and 3 touchdowns.
Colorado State lost to the Utah State Aggies 31-14 on the road on Oct. 31. Hauss Hejny went 19 of 30 for 186 yards, with 1 touchdown; Durell Robinson ran 16 times for 66 yards and 1 touchdown; Terrence Smith Jr. caught 5 balls for 70 yards.
Players to watch. Colorado State quarterback Hauss Hejny is 90 of 134 for 1,138 yards, with 14 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. Mel Brown leads their ground game with 454 yards on 52 carries and 3 scores. RJ Vick is their top target: 29 catches for 425 yards. On defense, linebacker Oumar Diomande has 76 tackles and defensive lineman Brandon Kelley has 1.5 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 9 regular-season meetings with Colorado State from 2016 through 2025. We won 3 of them, and Colorado State won the last one 49-21 in 2025.
Availability. No official availability report for Boise State or Colorado State was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Ansel Rimrock, Colorado State's insider. Here's what I said:
"You know what I like about this trip? We've been coming here forever and we've won almost every time. Eight of nine since 2016."
And the last word from Ansel: "About that Texas State game."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
Bronco fans are holding a free Bronco Bash at 147 S. College Ave. in Fort Collins from 1 to 3:30 p.m. local time before kickoff, and the call is to wear blue.

My call: Boise State Broncos 44, Colorado State Rams 28.
