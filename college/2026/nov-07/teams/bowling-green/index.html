Falcons Regroup: Bowling Green Uses the Open Week Before Kent State

By Darcy Fenwick · Bowling Green Insider · Week 10

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Hey, Falcons fans! I'm Darcy Fenwick, your Bowling Green insider. I played goalie for the club hockey team, and I still watch everything like I'm in the crease. Defending the throw? That's goaltending. An interception returned for a touchdown? That's a shorty, the best goal in hockey. When we win, I bang my stick on the desk. When we lose, I send myself to the penalty box for two minutes of silence. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, with a little rink-rat attitude. "The goalie says no."

The School in 60 Seconds
Bowling Green plays at Doyt L. Perry Stadium, listed at 24,000. Our head coach is one of the most famous names in football: Eddie George, the 1995 Heisman Trophy winner at Ohio State. That season he ran for 1,927 yards and 24 touchdowns, then a school record, and he also won the Doak Walker, Maxwell and Walter Camp awards. He was NFL Rookie of the Year in 1996, made four Pro Bowls, and is in the College Football Hall of Fame. He even appeared on Broadway in "Chicago." That's a hat trick and then some. He's also one of only two NFL running backs, with Jim Brown, to rush for 10,000 yards without ever missing a start. Per the school, only Walter Payton started more consecutive regular-season games than his 130. Iron man. Goalie-approved.

The Coach and the Scheme
Eddie George was named Bowling Green's 21st head coach on March 9, 2025.

Through 9 games this season, Bowling Green is scoring 17.8 points per game and allowing 29.0.

Players to Know
QB Jay Kastantin: 150 of 225, 1,567 yards, 11 touchdowns, 8 interceptions.
RB Austyn Dendy: 371 yards on 104 carries, 4 touchdowns.
TE Jeremiah Scoby: 31 catches, 319 yards.
DE Myles Bradley: 9 sacks, the team lead.
LB Dorian Pringle: a team-high 86 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Bowling Green is 2-7 overall and 2-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have Bowling Green 8th in the MAC.
The season so far: L 13-20 vs Tarleton State Texans (Sept. 5), L 7-56 at Nebraska Cornhuskers (Sept. 12), L 7-55 at Iowa State Cyclones (Sept. 19), L 6-14 vs South Florida Bulls (Sept. 26), W 24-20 at Miami (OH) RedHawks (Oct. 3), W 38-10 vs Sacramento State Hornets (Oct. 10), L 31-32 vs Ball State Cardinals (Oct. 17), L 24-31 at Buffalo Bulls (Oct. 24), L 10-23 at Western Michigan Broncos (Oct. 31).

This Week: Bye Week
No game for Bowling Green this week. We're off from Tuesday, Nov. 3, through Saturday, Nov. 7, so the rest of this Bowling Green report is about where we stand and what's next.
Last time out. We lost to the Western Michigan Broncos 23-10 on the road on Oct. 31. Jay Kastantin went 20 of 31 for 149 yards, with 1 touchdown. Nakai Amachree ran 11 times for 61 yards. Eli Jacon-Duffy caught 4 balls for 41 yards.
Up next. We play the Kent State Golden Flashes at home on Tuesday, Nov. 10, 7:00 p.m. ET, at Doyt L. Perry Stadium in Bowling Green.
Use the week, Falcons. Kent State is coming on a Tuesday night, and we want fresh legs for it.
