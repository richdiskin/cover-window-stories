Night in the Swamp: Florida Salutes Those Who Serve and Welcomes Oklahoma

By Dax Marchetti · Florida Insider · Week 10

XFacebook

Meet Your Insider
That play had scales! I'm Dax Marchetti, and I'm your Florida insider. I'm a herpetology minor, and I compare every defense to a reptile species. Fair warning: I classify every opponent as a species, lovingly, and my reptile index is weirdly accurate. On the podcast, that segment is called Reptile Index. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate Florida players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, no Florida fan hears it from me.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Ben Hill Griffin Stadium in Gainesville, built in 1930 with a listed capacity of 88,548. The playing surface is Steve Spurrier–Florida Field, the band is the Pride of the Sunshine, and the fight song is "The Orange and Blue." The program claims national titles from 1996, 2006 and 2008. The last five seasons went 6-7, 6-7, 5-7, 8-5 and 4-8, from 2021 through 2025. And if you visit, ask somebody about the Swamp. It's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Jon Sumrall leads the Gators, and CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Nov. 30, 2025. It's his first season in Gainesville.

We've played 8 games, and we're putting up 35.6 points a game while giving up 28.1.

Players to Know
QB Aaron Philo: 110 of 156, 1,367 yards, 9 touchdowns, 5 interceptions.
RB Jadan Baugh: 801 yards on 152 carries, 13 touchdowns.
WR Dallas Wilson: 31 catches, 417 yards, 3 touchdowns.
LB Jayden Woods: 4.5 sacks, the team lead.
LB Myles Graham: a team-high 55 tackles.

The Conference Picture
Florida is 5-3 overall and 3-3 in league play. ESPN's standings have Florida 9th in the SEC.
The season so far: W 66-21 vs Florida Atlantic Owls (Sept. 5), W 52-3 vs Campbell Fighting Camels (Sept. 12), W 44-39 at Auburn Tigers (Sept. 19), W 52-28 vs Ole Miss Rebels (Sept. 26), L 17-45 at Missouri Tigers (Oct. 3), L 19-38 vs South Carolina Gamecocks (Oct. 10), W 27-24 at Texas Longhorns (Oct. 17).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We host Oklahoma (4-4, 2-3 SEC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:45 p.m. ET, at Ben Hill Griffin Stadium, Gainesville, Florida, on SEC Network. We come in ranked No. 25.
Oklahoma lost to the South Carolina Gamecocks 42-25 at home on Oct. 31. John Mateer went 26 of 43 for 244 yards, with 2 touchdowns; Tory Blaylock ran 6 times for 32 yards; Ivan Carreon caught 5 balls for 88 yards and 2 touchdowns.
Players to watch. Oklahoma quarterback John Mateer is 162 of 247 for 1,835 yards, with 13 touchdowns and 7 interceptions. Lloyd Avant leads their ground game with 347 yards on 79 carries and 2 scores. Isaiah Sategna III is their top target: 45 catches for 591 yards. On defense, linebacker Owen Heinecke has 63 tackles and defensive lineman Adepoju Adebawore has 4 sacks.
The series. CFBD shows no regular-season meetings with Oklahoma from 2016 through 2025, so this is a fresh fight for our guys.
Availability. No official availability report for Florida or Oklahoma was in the public data I checked, so nobody gets ruled in or out here.
From the preview booth. I shared the mic this week with Savannah Redbird-Lowe, Oklahoma's insider. Here's what I said:
"They have everything. Come early."
"We beat Texas too. 27-24. In Austin."
"Same score. Except ours was on their field."
And the last word from Savannah: "So did you. Almost. You gave up 38."

Local Flavor (unsponsored, not an ad)
It's the Saluting Those Who Serve game, honoring armed forces members, veterans and local first responders, and the free Fan Fest outside Gate 2 of the O'Connell Center opens 3 hours before kickoff.

My call: Florida Gators 28, Oklahoma Sooners 23.
