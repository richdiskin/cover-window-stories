Eagles Under the Dome: Eastern Michigan Meets Central Michigan in Detroit

ShareXFacebook

Meet Your Insider
Gray turf, bright future! Hi, I'm Imogen Tremblay. I studied education in Ypsilanti, I build spreadsheets for fun, and I have one tab, color-coded, devoted entirely to the "gray turf advantage." My model says it's worth 1.3 points. My heart says 13. I travel with this Eastern Michigan team and I live with it, and every week I'll walk you through the players, the matchups and the history, plus at least one gentle reminder that Ypsilanti is not Ann Arbor's little brother. We are Ann Arbor's cooler cousin with better parking. When we win, I'll show you a spreadsheet with one enormous green cell, and I will roar. When we lose, I'll update the model, grimly, and come back next week with more columns. Thanks for being here.

The School in 60 Seconds
Eastern Michigan has played football since 1891, missing only 1944. Before we were the Eagles, we were the Normalites (1899-1928) and the Hurons (1929-1990). We've had five undefeated seasons: 1906, 1925, 1927, 1943 and 1945, and the 1943 team didn't allow a point. Our golden age ran from 1925 through 1939 under Elton Rynearson, and our stadium carries his name. The field inside is Crosby Field at "The Factory," and yes, it's gray. CFBD lists Rynearson at 30,200 seats. We compete in the Mid-American Conference. Spreadsheet note: the all-time record is 490-622-47. Honesty is a column too.

The Coach and the Scheme
Chris Creighton was hired Dec. 11, 2013, which makes him a fixture in Ypsilanti. His teams went 7-6 and 9-4 in 2021 and 2022.

The 9-game numbers: 21.3 points per game scored, 25.7 allowed.

Players to Know

RB Amareon Blue: 405 yards on 79 carries, 3 touchdowns.

DL Quincy Byas: 5 sacks, the team lead.

The Conference Picture
Eastern Michigan is 4-5 overall and 3-2 in league play. ESPN's standings have Eastern Michigan 6th in the MAC.
The season so far: W 28-17 vs Sacramento State Hornets (Aug. 29), L 21-27 vs San José State Spartans (Sept. 4), L 7-35 at Michigan State Spartans (Sept. 12), L 10-54 at Wisconsin Badgers (Sept. 19), W 29-3 vs Lindenwood Lions (Sept. 26), W 38-14 at Massachusetts Minutemen (Oct. 3), W 25-23 at Akron Zips (Oct. 10), L 14-34 vs Toledo Rockets (Oct. 17), L 20-24 at Ohio Bobcats (Oct. 24).

This Week: Game Preview from Both Sides
We meet Central Michigan (3-5, 1-3 MAC) at a neutral site on Wednesday, Nov. 4, at 7:30 p.m. ET, at Ford Field, Detroit, MI — neutral site, on ESPN2.
Last time out. We lost to the Ohio Bobcats 24-20 on the road on Oct. 24. Brogan McCaughey went 27 of 39 for 197 yards, with 1 touchdown, Amareon Blue ran 11 times for 67 yards and Joey Mattord caught 7 balls for 55 yards and 1 touchdown.
Central Michigan lost to the Miami (OH) RedHawks 28-27 at home on Oct. 24. Jadyn Glasser went 18 of 31 for 221 yards, with 1 touchdown; Jayden Clerveaux ran 9 times for 32 yards; Langston Lewis caught 10 balls for 163 yards and 1 touchdown.
Players to watch. Central Michigan quarterback Angel Flores is 54 of 92 for 475 yards, with 2 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. Angel Flores leads their ground game with 618 yards on 128 carries and 4 scores. Langston Lewis is their top target: 24 catches for 379 yards. On defense, linebacker Mayson Hitchens has 62 tackles and defensive lineman Jaxon Cherry has 3 sacks.
The series. CFBD has 10 regular-season meetings with Central Michigan from 2016 through 2025. We won 9 of them, and we won the last one 24-13 in 2025.
Availability. Nothing official on injuries yet for Eastern Michigan vs. Central Michigan. When Eastern Michigan or Central Michigan puts out a real report, you'll hear it from me, not a rumor.
