Florida State Seminoles at Boston College Eagles

Florida State Seminoles (3-5) at Boston College Eagles (4-4)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Alumni Stadium (Chestnut Hill, MA) · ACC Network

ShareXFacebook

Florida State vs Boston College prediction, pick against the spread and over/under for Saturday at Alumni Stadium: Boston College has made it Veterans and Military Appreciation Day in Chestnut Hill, and Florida State is making its first trip there since 2023. Kickoff is noon ET. The Seminoles are 3-5. The Eagles are 4-4. Neither record is the reason anybody bought a ticket.

DraftKings opened Florida State −6.5 with the over/under at 51.5. It sits at Florida State −6.5 and 52.5. The side has not budged and the points have ticked up by one. My side is Florida State Seminoles −6.5, and my number is Over 52.5.

Laying almost a touchdown with a 3-5 team on the road needs a reason, so here it is. Florida State scores 30.6 a game. Boston College scores 21.6 and allows 26.5, and the Eagles just lost 2-47 at Duke, with Mason McKenzie going 9 of 17 for 87 yards. McKenzie is the whole show in Chestnut Hill: 1,450 throwing yards and 638 rushing yards. When he is held in check, there is no plan B.

Florida State's problem is giving the ball away. Ashton Daniels has 1,968 yards with 14 touchdowns and 11 interceptions, and in the 24-36 loss to Clemson last week he threw for 350 and was intercepted 3 times. Micahi Danzy caught 4 for 108 in that game. Ousmane Kromah has 609 rushing yards. The Seminoles also allow 28.9 a game, which is why I am on the Over too. This is a game where Florida State scores in the thirties and Boston College finds three or four drives against a defense that has given up 36 or more in four of its five losses.

The series: Boston College won 28-13 in 2024. Florida State won 31-29 in 2023. Florida State won six of the eight meetings since 2016.

The room is unanimous on the side for once. The Formula has Florida State 31-23 and takes Florida State −6.5 with the Over, which is exactly my score. Next Man Up has Florida State 30-23 and takes Florida State −6.5 and the Over. In The Trenches likes Florida State −6.5 off the trench matchup. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

When every card in the building agrees, somebody usually ends up embarrassed. I am willing to risk it, mostly because the alternative is trusting a team that scored 2 points last week.

Florida State Seminoles 31, Boston College Eagles 23 is my final. Daniels throws an interception or two and still gets to 31, Kromah wears down the Boston College front, and McKenzie keeps it respectable without keeping it close.

Florida State Seminoles 31, Boston College Eagles 23. Florida State Seminoles cover the 6.5. Over 52.5.
