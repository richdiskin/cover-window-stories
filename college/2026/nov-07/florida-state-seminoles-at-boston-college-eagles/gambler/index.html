Florida State at Boston College: Chestnut Hill Gets a Reset, Not a Quiet Afternoon

Florida State Seminoles (3-5) at Boston College Eagles (4-4)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Alumni Stadium (Chestnut Hill, MA) · ACC Network

ShareXFacebook

Florida State at Boston College: FSU -6; total 53.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

Chestnut Hill hosts two teams that would probably prefer not to replay last Saturday in the meeting room. Boston College lost 47-2 at Duke. Florida State lost 36-24 to Clemson. Those finals come from ESPN's AP preview, and neither one needs dramatic background music.

Bill O'Brien skipped the cheap shot after Florida State's coaching change. “Rick Stockstill is a great coach. I've known him for a long time,” O'Brien said, per Boston College Athletics. The Seminoles have something else to fix besides the headset: they have not won a true road game since Nov. 25, 2023, according to Sports Illustrated's Florida State site.

There is real news beyond the bruised pride: Florida State's official Nov. 1 announcement says Rick Stockstill is Florida State's interim head coach following a Nov. 1 leadership change. That makes this a first public response under new leadership, not a place to recycle an old coaching rumor as fact.

Follow The Money likes Boston College Eagles +5.5 and Over 53.5.

Ashton Daniels threw for 350 yards but three interceptions against Clemson, per ESPN. Boston College quarterback Mason McKenzie leads his team with 638 rushing yards in the same capsule. That is the contrast worth watching: can the Seminoles keep their passing production without donating possessions, and can BC's quarterback get its offense out of last week's ditch? An afternoon at Alumni Stadium beats another week of explaining the last score.

My Cover Writer call is Florida State Seminoles −6 and Over 53.5. My final is Florida State Seminoles 31, Boston College Eagles 23.

Daniels' production against Clemson is the reason I still expect Florida State to move the ball, not a promise that a coaching change fixes everything. His turnovers and the Seminoles' defensive problems leave McKenzie room to answer. I keep the eight-point road win, with real risk attached to an offense missing important help.

Laying almost a touchdown with a 3-5 team on the road needs a reason, so here it is. Florida State scores 30.6 a game. Boston College scores 21.6 and allows 26.5, and the Eagles just lost 2-47 at Duke, with Mason McKenzie going 9 of 17 for 87 yards. McKenzie is the whole show in Chestnut Hill: 1,450 throwing yards and 638 rushing yards. When he is held in check, there is no plan B.

Florida State's problem is giving the ball away. Ashton Daniels has 1,968 yards with 14 touchdowns and 11 interceptions, and in the 24-36 loss to Clemson last week he threw for 350 and was intercepted 3 times. Micahi Danzy caught 4 for 108 in that game. The ACC game-day report lists Ousmane Kromah and Duce Robinson out; the Seminoles need production from the players who are available. The Seminoles also allow 28.9 a game, which is why I am on the Over too. This is a game where Florida State scores in the thirties and Boston College finds three or four drives against a defense that has given up 36 or more in four of its five losses.

Florida State Seminoles 31, Boston College Eagles 23. Florida State Seminoles cover the 6. Over 53.5.
