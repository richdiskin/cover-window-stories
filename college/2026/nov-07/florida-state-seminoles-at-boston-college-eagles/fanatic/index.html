Florida State Returns to Chestnut Hill With Boston College Waiting

Florida State Seminoles (3-5) at Boston College Eagles (4-4)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Alumni Stadium (Chestnut Hill, MA) · ACC Network

ShareXFacebook

Florida State vs Boston College prediction for Saturday in Chestnut Hill, and if you have a free afternoon, these two will fill it. Gianna Ravello grew up in Tallahassee with a Seminole spear painted on her bedroom wall. Siobhan Dunleavy won a national debate title at Boston College and has not lost an argument at a dinner table since.

Siobhan: Saturday is Veterans and Military Appreciation Day at Alumni Stadium. Noon Eastern. It is a good day to be in Chestnut Hill and a good day to say thank you. It is also the first time Florida State has come up here since 2023, so I would like to welcome you back to New England, where November is a season and not a rumor.

Gianna: Thank you. I brought a coat. I also brought the history. Florida State is 16-6 all-time against Boston College. Nine and three at Alumni Stadium. We have won three straight road games in this series. I have been to your house before. I know where the good chair is.

Siobhan: History is what you reach for when your present is 3-5. Let me introduce a counterargument. You are 3-5.

Gianna: And you are 4-4 and last week you scored 2 points at Duke. Two. That is a safety, not a football game. I have seen more scoring at a church bake sale.

Siobhan: Duke was a bad day. Two weeks ago we went to Georgia Tech and won 49-27. Mason McKenzie runs this team on his own legs. He has 1,450 throwing yards and 638 more on the ground. He is our quarterback, our running back and our closing argument.

Gianna: Respectfully, if one man is your quarterback and your leading rusher, your team is a one-person debate club. Ashton Daniels has 1,968 throwing yards and 14 touchdowns. Ousmane Kromah has 609 rushing yards. Duce Robinson has 438 receiving. We have a whole cast.

Siobhan: Your whole cast has 11 interceptions. Daniels threw 3 last week against Clemson and you lost 36-24. A cast is only as good as the person who keeps dropping the script.

Gianna: He also threw for 350 yards against Clemson. Micahi Danzy caught 4 balls for 108. We moved the ball all day. We just gave it away a few times. That is fixable. Two points is not fixable.

Siobhan: Point of order. Anthony Palano has 69 tackles for us. Demetrius Ballard has 4.5 sacks. Our defense will be in Daniels' face, and a quarterback with 11 interceptions in a cold stadium in November is a quarterback who will give us gifts.

Gianna: Rylan Kennedy has 6 sacks for us. He is going to chase McKenzie around that field like a man trying to catch the last train out of Boston. And your offense scores 21.6 a game. Ours scores 30.6. The math is not a debate.

Siobhan: The math also says you allow 28.9 a game. That is not a defense, that is a revolving door with a logo on it. You lost to Louisville 44-20. You lost to Alabama 50-36. You gave up 43 to Miami in overtime.

Gianna: Those are good teams. Boston College is not Alabama. No disrespect. Alumni Stadium is a fine building and the people are very nice, and I am sure the veterans being honored on Saturday will see a good game. I am just telling you who wins it.

Siobhan: The last time Florida State came here, in 2023, you won 31-29 and needed every last snap. The year after, we went to Tallahassee and beat you 28-13. That one still stings, doesn't it?

Gianna: It stings like a cold wind off the reservoir. Which is to say, I will put on a scarf and move on. This is a different year. Kromah runs downhill, Daniels hits a couple of shots to Robinson and Danzy, and your crowd gets very quiet around the third quarter.

Siobhan: My closing argument. McKenzie makes plays with his legs, the defense gets two takeaways off Daniels, and Boston College keeps this a game into the fourth quarter. We honor the veterans, we sing loud, and we make you earn every yard of the trip.

Gianna: My rebuttal. Nice speech. Florida State by eight. Then I buy you a chowder.

Siobhan: I accept the chowder. I reject the premise.

My call: Florida State Seminoles 31, Boston College Eagles 23.
