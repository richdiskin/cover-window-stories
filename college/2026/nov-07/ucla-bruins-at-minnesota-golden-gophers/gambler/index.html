UCLA at Minnesota: Minneapolis Has Already Learned How to Celebrate

UCLA Bruins (8-0) at Minnesota Golden Gophers (5-3)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Huntington Bank Stadium · Fox

ShareXFacebook

UCLA at Minnesota: UCLA -3.5; total 38.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

Minneapolis has not been waiting politely for the famous visitors. AP reports that Minnesota fans stormed the field after trophy wins over Michigan and Iowa last month. Now unbeaten UCLA arrives, and the question is whether the Gophers can give their home crowd another reason to forget where the aisles are. Bruins coach Bob Chesney is not handing out November participation ribbons. “At 8-0, it means the standard's got to raise a little bit more,” he says in the AP preview. Minnesota quarterback Drake Lindsey answers a painful overtime loss at Indiana with this: “We're just going to keep being us and learn from this.” That is a better temperature reading than any anonymous message-board diagnosis. The Minnesota athletic department says Luke Drzewiecki made a 60-yard field goal at Indiana and has hit 26 straight dating to last season. When the kicker warms up, pay attention. UCLA counters with a defense AP credits with 26 takeaways. A long kick can rescue a stalled drive; a giveaway can erase one before the kicker even gets interested. It is a real new test, not just another undefeated graphic. The Gophers' home record is 11 wins in their last 12 in their official preview. Minneapolis has earned the right to expect a fight.

Minnesota's place has turned into a genuine problem for visitors. The Gophers have won seven straight Big Ten games at home, the team's official preview says, and Luke Drzewiecki has been named the Big Ten's Special Teams Player of the Week two weeks running. Unbeaten guests are welcome in Minneapolis. Easy afternoons are not.

The published Cover Writer card stays Minnesota Golden Gophers +6 and Over 39.5. My final is UCLA Bruins 25, Minnesota Golden Gophers 20. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and its total is pending too.

UCLA is unbeaten, and the defense is the reason. The Bruins allow 13.4 a game and score 33.5. Wayne Knight has 680 rushing yards and 12 touchdowns. But the quarterback picture has shifted. Nico Iamaleava leads the team with 982 throwing yards, and Ty Dieffenbach has had the most attempts in each of the last three games, including 16 of 30 for 178 in a 27-0 win over Nevada. The Bruins won 17-15 at Oregon and 17-7 over Wisconsin. They grind.

Minnesota plays in close games and does not panic. The Gophers lost 16-23 in overtime at Indiana last week, beat Iowa 13-10 and beat Michigan 20-14. Drake Lindsey has 1,592 yards with 10 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. Javon Tracy has 460 receiving yards. The Gophers score 25.2 and allow 20.5. They also beat UCLA 21-17 in 2024, the only meeting in the stretch I can check.

UCLA Bruins 25, Minnesota Golden Gophers 20. Minnesota Golden Gophers cover the 6. Over 39.5.
