Western Kentucky at Middle Tennessee: Murfreesboro Has a Running Quarterback to Corral

Western Kentucky Hilltoppers (3-5) at Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders (4-4)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Johnny "Red" Floyd Stadium · ESPN+

ShareXFacebook

Western Kentucky at Middle Tennessee: WKU -2.5; total 53.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

Murfreesboro gets Western Kentucky and Brock Glenn, who just ran for 129 yards in a loss. When your opponent's quarterback can do that and still leave disappointed, there is usually a fairly determined visitor coming down the road.

This is the 100 Miles of Hate, and Western Kentucky arrives chasing an eighth straight win in the series, per the Bowling Green Daily News. Tyson Helton keeps it respectful anyway, saying WKU has tremendous respect for a Middle Tennessee team having a good season. That is rivalry the right way: no cheap shots, just a road game that everybody in both towns circled.

ESPN's AP preview has Middle Tennessee at 4-4 after a 44-28 loss to Kennesaw State and WKU at 3-5 after losing 35-21 to Delaware. Middle Tennessee's Antonio Martin ran for 106 yards and a touchdown in its latest game. Glenn and Martin give both sets of fans somebody to point at when the other fellow starts talking too confidently.

Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and its total is pending too.

Watch the Blue Raiders when Glenn looks to escape: somebody has to tackle rather than simply wave at the problem. On the other side, Cam'ron Lacy has 510 receiving yards in the report. This one can matter deeply without a studio panel granting it importance. Murfreesboro gets the next chapter written the proper way, between the sidelines.

The published Cover Writer card stays Western Kentucky Hilltoppers +1.5 and Over 52.5. My final is Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders 28, Western Kentucky Hilltoppers 27. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

Why Western Kentucky with the points: WKU has won nine of the last ten meetings, including 42-26 last year. That is not a fluke. Middle Tennessee is coming off a 28-44 home loss to Kennesaw State in which Roman Gagliano went 21 of 43 with 2 interceptions. Brock Glenn is a real problem for a defense that gives up 29.6 a game. He has 887 yards through the air and leads WKU with 451 on the ground, and he ran 17 times for 129 yards against Delaware. In a game this close, the team with the quarterback who can run gets the hook.

We're on the Over 52.5. WKU gives up 31.1 a game and Middle Tennessee gives up 29.6. Gagliano has 1,587 yards and 12 touchdowns, and Cam'ron Lacy has 45 catches for 510 yards. Antonio Martin ran for 106 yards against Kennesaw. Jonathan Stafford Jr. has 46 catches for WKU. Both offenses should move the ball.

Middle Tennessee Blue Raiders 28, Western Kentucky Hilltoppers 27. Western Kentucky Hilltoppers cover the 1.5. Over 52.5.
