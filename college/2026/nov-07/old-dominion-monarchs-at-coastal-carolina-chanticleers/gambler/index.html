Old Dominion at Coastal Carolina: Conway Has a Passing Game Worth Finding on the Remote

Old Dominion Monarchs (2-7) at Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (3-5)

1:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Brooks Stadium (SC) · ESPN+

ShareXFacebook

Old Dominion at Coastal Carolina: CCU -6.5; total 45.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

Conway gets a Coastal Carolina offense with a fresh reason to let it fly. Deuce Bailey threw for 297 yards and three touchdowns in a 31-20 win over Georgia State, per ESPN's AP preview. Old Dominion is next, and the Monarchs bring a receiver who just had a serious Saturday of his own.

It is Homecoming and a Black Out at Brooks Stadium, with Coastal wearing black uniforms for the first time this season, per Coastal Athletics. Deuce Bailey leads the Sun Belt in passing yards per game and touchdown throws, and Old Dominion arrives trying to end a three-game skid.

Josh Rodriguez caught nine passes for 143 yards in Old Dominion's 36-23 loss to Marshall. Coastal's Clayton Coppock Jr. had six catches for 100 and two scores against Georgia State. That is a proper receiver argument: two guys who just put useful tape on the table, not two names picked out of a roster for decoration.

Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and its total is pending too.

Old Dominion is 2-7 and Coastal 3-5 in the ESPN preview. This is not a reason to wave the game off. It is a reason the next good afternoon matters to people who have kept showing up through the rough ones. Bailey also threw two interceptions last week. Watch whether he keeps the fireworks and loses the gifts. The home fans deserve the entertaining version without the extra anxiety.

The published Cover Writer card stays Coastal Carolina Chanticleers −7.5 and Over 44.5. My final is Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 27, Old Dominion Monarchs 19. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

Here is why I lay the points. Old Dominion scores 16.3 a game, and it was shut out 0-26 at Louisiana Tech two weeks ago. Three different quarterbacks have led the Monarchs in throws this year: Quinn Henicle, Ryan Huff and Jaxon Potter. Potter threw for 329 yards against Marshall, but 0 touchdowns and 3 interceptions. Coastal is coming off a 31-20 win at Georgia State, where Bailey threw 3 touchdowns and Clayton Coppock Jr. caught 2.

The risk is real. Coastal is 3-5 and gives up 25.1 a game. It lost 14-22 at Delaware and 17-34 to Liberty. Old Dominion also won 10-3 at App State, so this defense can steal a day.

Old Dominion allows 27.8 a game, and Coastal scores 26.8. The Monarchs gave up 36 to Marshall last week.

Bailey does not have to win a throwing contest against his own highlight reel. Taking the available completion would do plenty here. The danger for Coastal is letting an ordinary possession turn into a giveaway and asking its defense to do the whole job twice. Coppock's recent work gives the passing game somebody worth looking for without forcing the ball.

Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 27, Old Dominion Monarchs 19. Coastal Carolina Chanticleers cover the 7.5. Over 44.5.
