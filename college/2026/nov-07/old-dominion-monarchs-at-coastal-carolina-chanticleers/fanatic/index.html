Old Dominion Drives Into Coastal Carolina's Teal Homecoming

Old Dominion Monarchs (2-7) at Coastal Carolina Chanticleers (3-5)

1:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Brooks Stadium (SC) · ESPN+

ShareXFacebook

Old Dominion Monarchs vs Coastal Carolina Chanticleers prediction, Saturday in Conway: the Homecoming parade already rolled down Marina Drive on Friday night, and Goldie Pruitt has been in teal since Sunday.

Winnie Harcastle drove down from Norfolk anyway. He always does.

"Hear ye, hear ye," he announced at the alumni TEALgate outside Brooks Stadium, holding a paper plate like a royal decree. "Let it be known that the Monarchs won this game 47-7 last year."

Goldie did not even blink. She is a marine biology grad, and she explains defense like tide charts: what comes in, goes out. "Let it be known," she said, "that you are 2-7."

Winnie lowered the plate. It is true. Old Dominion beat Norfolk State in the opener and won 10-3 at App State in October, and the rest of the fall has been rough. The Monarchs lost 17-20 to East Carolina, 10-42 at Georgia State, 0-26 at Louisiana Tech and 23-36 to Marshall last week. They score 16.3 points a game and give up 27.8.

"Our quarterback room has range," Winnie said.

"Your quarterback room has a revolving door," Goldie said.

That is fair too. Quinn Henicle, Ryan Huff and Jaxon Potter have all led the Monarchs in throws in a game this season. Potter threw for 329 yards against Marshall, which sounds great until you remember the 3 interceptions. Henicle leads the team with 656 yards. Winnie likes to say the next one is always around the corner. Goldie likes to say that is the problem.

What Winnie does have is a defense with some bite. Lucas Struck has 70 tackles from the safety spot. Javen Gibson has 5 sacks. Old Dominion held App State to 3 points on the road, and Winnie brings that up roughly every eleven minutes. "Three points," he said. "In Boone. You could not do that."

Coastal could not. The Chanticleers lost 32-35 to App State at home in October, and that one still stings. Coastal is 3-5, and the losses have mostly been close: 24-31 at West Virginia, 24-31 to Georgia Southern, 32-35 to App. Goldie calls them low tides. They always come back in.

And they came back in last week. Coastal won 31-20 at Georgia State, Deuce Bailey threw for 297 yards and 3 touchdowns, and Clayton Coppock Jr. caught 6 balls for 100 yards and 2 scores. Bailey has 1,935 yards and 19 touchdowns on the season. Dominic Lee-Knicely has 484 rushing yards. Marcellius Pulliam has 74 tackles, and Ibrahim Diawara has 5 sacks. Goldie lists them in order, like a reef survey.

"Last year was last year," she said. "Your team scored 47 on us and I still came to Homecoming."

Winnie had no answer for that, which is rare for a town crier.

Here is how Goldie sees the afternoon. Bailey gets time, finds Coppock deep once or twice, and the home crowd gets loud early. Old Dominion hangs around because Struck and Gibson make some plays, and because Potter will hit a long one sooner or later. But the Monarchs do not score enough to keep up for four quarters. Coastal pulls away a little in the second half, the student section is loud, and the alumni stay late.

Winnie thinks his defense keeps it close. He might be right for a while. He usually is, for about a half.

The rest of the weekend is all Coastal. The Alumni Association brunch is Sunday, and Goldie already RSVP'd for both of them. Winnie says he will go if he gets to say a few words. Goldie says he can talk about the Chanticleers or he can talk about nothing.

Up the road, the South Carolina UFO Festival lands at the Conway Sports and Fitness Center on Saturday too. Winnie wants to know if anybody there has seen a Monarchs touchdown drive lately. Goldie tells him to keep watching the sky, because that's the only place a Monarchs drive is showing up Saturday.

My call: Coastal Carolina Chanticleers 27, Old Dominion Monarchs 19.
