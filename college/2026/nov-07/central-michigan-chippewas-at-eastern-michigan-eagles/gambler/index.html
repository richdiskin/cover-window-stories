Central Michigan vs Eastern Michigan prediction, pick against the spread and over/under for Wednesday night at Ford Field in Detroit: I'm on Central Michigan Chippewas +1.5 and Over 44.5, with a final of Central Michigan Chippewas 23, Eastern Michigan Eagles 22. Here is why I take the points. This is a coin flip on paper, and the Chippewas have the better recent history in the series, 6 wins to 4 in the last ten meetings, including 24-13 last year. Central Michigan just lost 27-28 to Miami (OH) with Jadyn Glasser throwing for 221 yards and Langston Lewis catching 10 for 163. A one-point game rewards the side getting a point and a half, and my final has the Chippewas winning outright by 1.

On the over/under, the market came down. DraftKings opened 46.5 and moved to 44.5, and that is the move I like going the other way. Eastern Michigan allows 25.7 a game and Central allows 25.9, and the last two meetings went 38-34 and 24-13. The 23-22 lands at 45, just over the number, which is the honest size of it.
