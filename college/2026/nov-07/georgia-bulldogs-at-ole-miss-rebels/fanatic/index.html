Georgia at Ole Miss: Oxford Has Plenty of Respect. The Tackling Will Be Less Polite

Georgia Bulldogs (7-1) at Ole Miss Rebels (6-2)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Vaught-Hemingway Stadium · ABC

XFacebook

Oxford has Georgia-Ole Miss, and the coaches have inconveniently refused to give us a cheap feud. Kirby Smart says Pete Golding has done “a tremendous job”; Golding calls Smart and his staff “the gold standard in college football,” according to AP. We will have to survive on the actual game.

Georgia's Quintavius Johnson has not forgotten Oxford. “Even going back to my freshman year, playing in Mississippi, getting the field stormed, it gives us a chip,” he said, per DawgNation. The Rebels have a sold-out house and have won 16 straight at home, per 247Sports, so one side's happiest memory is the other side's motivation.

That should not be difficult. Georgia is 7-1, Ole Miss 6-2. AP reports the teams traded major wins last season: Georgia won the regular-season meeting 43-35, then Ole Miss won the playoff quarterfinal 39-34. The rematch comes with a score each fan base can bring to the argument.

Asked how to defend Trinidad Chambliss, Smart replied, “How long do you have?” The real answer is less amusing for defenders: Smart says Chambliss makes excellent decisions down after down. Golding calls Georgia quarterback Gunner Stockton “an elite competitor and tough as nails.” Those are on-record assessments, not anonymous praise with a team logo attached.

Georgia brings a rushing attack whose Nate Frazier, Dwight Phillips Jr. and Bo Walker have combined for 19 touchdowns in AP's report. Ole Miss brings Chambliss and 18 touchdown passes. Watch whether Georgia can make the night methodical before the Rebels make it frantic. Oxford gets the kind of game where the next snap is more interesting than the last compliment.

My call: Georgia Bulldogs 31, Ole Miss Rebels 30.
