Baylor Lays 9.5 to Iowa State on Homecoming in Waco

Iowa State Cyclones (4-4) at Baylor Bears (5-3)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · McLane Stadium

ShareXFacebook

Iowa State vs Baylor prediction, pick against the spread and over/under for Saturday at McLane Stadium: this is Baylor Homecoming, the whole of Waco is wearing green and gold, and the Waco Downtown Farmers Market on Bridge Street will be open a few hours before the noon ET kickoff. Iowa State comes in at 4-4. Baylor is 5-3. The market has not moved an inch.

DraftKings opened Baylor −9.5 with the over/under at 50.5 and it is still sitting at Baylor −9.5 and 50.5. Nobody blinked. I am laying it. My side is Baylor Bears −9.5, and my number is Over 50.5.

The Baylor case is defense and a quarterback who just found his range. The Bears allow 15.2 points a game, and their three losses came 16-17 to Auburn, 6-17 to TCU and 24-25 at Kansas. Nobody has blown them out. DJ Lagway has 1,671 yards with 13 touchdowns and 4 interceptions, and last week at UCF he went 16 of 25 for 319 and 4 scores in a 41-9 win. Gavin Freeman has 63 catches for 792 yards and caught 5 for 114 in that game.

Iowa State is a weird team to price. It beat Oklahoma State 51-49 last week, with Jaylen Raynor throwing for 309 and 3 touchdowns, Aiden Flora carrying 29 times for 221, and Jamal Polite Jr. catching 6 for 171. A week earlier it lost 3-39 at Arizona. The Cyclones score 29.0 and allow 27.2. Flora has 937 yards and 8 touchdowns, and he is the reason I think Iowa State scores enough to push this over 50.5 even in a loss.

The series argues against me. Iowa State won 43-21 in 2024 and holds a 5-4 lead in the nine meetings since 2016. But that Baylor defense was not this one.

Here is the room. The Formula has Baylor 31-21 and takes Baylor −9.5 with the Over, the exact score I landed on. Next Man Up has Baylor 32-21 and likes Baylor −9.5 and the Over. In The Trenches likes Iowa State +9.5 off the trench matchup, and it is the lone voice in the room for the Cyclones. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

The Trenches crew thinks the Cyclones' front can keep this inside ten. I think the Trenches crew has never tried to block a homecoming crowd that has been tailgating since the farmers market opened.

Baylor Bears 31, Iowa State Cyclones 21 is my final. Lagway and Freeman hit two shots, the Baylor defense gets Raynor into long downs, and Flora gets his yards with the score already decided.

Baylor Bears 31, Iowa State Cyclones 21. Baylor Bears cover the 9.5. Over 50.5.
