Iowa State at Baylor: Waco Homecoming Meets the Man Who Just Ran for 221

Iowa State Cyclones (4-4) at Baylor Bears (5-3)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · McLane Stadium

XFacebook

Iowa State at Baylor: BAY -10; total 51.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

Waco has a homecoming guest who apparently forgot the instruction about taking it easy on the host. Iowa State arrives after a 51-49 win over Oklahoma State; Aiden Flora supplied 221 rushing yards. The Cyclones' official primer says Baylor is celebrating homecoming. That is an actual town-and-campus reason for the occasion, not an imagined scene from a parking lot.

Jaylen Raynor shows up wanting to cut it loose again, The Gazette reports, after his 309-yard, three-touchdown day against Oklahoma State. Iowa State's notes say he owns 10 career fourth-quarter game-winning drives, the most among active FBS quarterbacks. Waco answers with a Baylor front that has 34 sacks, tied for second nationally, and Jimmy Rogers called it one of the best defensive fronts his team has seen.

The same primer reports Joe Klanderman is Baylor's interim coach following Dave Aranda's Oct. 25 dismissal. His Bears just won 41-9 at UCF. Iowa State is 4-4, Baylor 5-3, and this is a meeting of a team trying to climb above even and a home team trying to keep an emphatic response going.

Flora needs 63 rushing yards to reach 1,000, according to Iowa State. Baylor is allowing 15.3 points a game in that report. Watch the first few runs: does the visiting back move the pile, or does Baylor make Jaylen Raynor do the rescuing? Gavin Freeman, coming off 114 receiving yards against UCF in ESPN's AP capsule, gives the Bears their own way to turn an ordinary possession into a problem. Homecoming deserves a football argument worth bringing home.

My published card stays Baylor Bears −9.5 and Over 50.5. My final is Baylor Bears 31, Iowa State Cyclones 21. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

The Baylor case is defense and a quarterback who just found his range. The Bears allow 15.2 points a game, and their three losses came 16-17 to Auburn, 6-17 to TCU and 24-25 at Kansas. Nobody has blown them out. DJ Lagway has 1,671 yards with 13 touchdowns and 4 interceptions, and last week at UCF he went 16 of 25 for 319 and 4 scores in a 41-9 win. Gavin Freeman has 63 catches for 792 yards and caught 5 for 114 in that game.

Iowa State is a weird team to price. It beat Oklahoma State 51-49 last week, with Jaylen Raynor throwing for 309 and 3 touchdowns, Aiden Flora carrying 29 times for 221, and Jamal Polite Jr. catching 6 for 171. A week earlier it lost 3-39 at Arizona. The Cyclones score 29.0 and allow 27.2. Flora has 937 yards and 8 touchdowns, and he is the reason I think Iowa State scores enough to push this over 50.5 even in a loss.

Baylor Bears 31, Iowa State Cyclones 21. Baylor Bears cover the 9.5. Over 50.5.
