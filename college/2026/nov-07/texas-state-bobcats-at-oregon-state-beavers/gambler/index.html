Texas State Takes 6.5 in Corvallis, Bobcats Keep It Within a Score

Texas State Bobcats (4-4) at Oregon State Beavers (5-3)

10:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Reser Stadium · CW

ShareXFacebook

Texas State vs Oregon State prediction: we're taking Texas State Bobcats +6.5, we're on the Under 70.5, and the final we're calling is Oregon State Beavers 38, Texas State Bobcats 32. Kickoff is 10:30 PM ET Saturday at Reser Stadium in Corvallis, and it is the first time these two programs have ever played.

Yesenia Calderwood has been in San Marcos for twenty years and says the Bobcats can score with anybody. Juniper Ostrowski has never missed a home game at Reser and says the Beavers have the best quarterback on the West Coast that nobody talks about.

"First meeting," Yesenia says. "Make a good first impression."

"We plan to," Juniper says. "On the scoreboard."

Why the Bobcats with the points: DraftKings opened Oregon State −8.5 and it has dropped to 6.5. Texas State scores 38.1 a game. Brad Jackson has 1,933 yards, 18 touchdowns and 5 interceptions, and he threw for 327 yards and 4 touchdowns in a 45-46 loss at Boise State last week. That's a team that can trade scores with anyone. Torrance Burgess Jr. has 922 rushing yards. Oregon State is good, but it lost 22-38 at Fresno State last week and 24-35 to Texas Tech. Six and a half is too many against a team that can keep pace.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 66.5 and it went up to 70.5. We're on the Under 70.5. Both teams score, but the market already moved it up from 66.5, and that is a lot to ask. Oregon State scored only 22 at Fresno State. This one comes up just short.

The room splits again. The Formula lands on Texas State +6.5 and has it 36-31. Next Man Up takes Oregon State −6.5 and has it 39-32. In The Trenches is split and takes no side. The Formula lands on the Under. Next Man Up lands on the Over. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Braden Atkinson is the reason Oregon State wins. He has 2,977 yards, 25 touchdowns and just 2 interceptions. Jesse Legree has 1,110 receiving yards and 14 touchdowns. That's a lot of firepower. But Texas State has its own, and we think the Bobcats stay within a score.

"Twenty-five touchdowns," Juniper says. "Two interceptions."

"Eighteen touchdowns," Yesenia says. "And we play defense in Texas."

Oregon State Beavers 38, Texas State Bobcats 32. Texas State Bobcats cover the 6.5. Under 70.5.
