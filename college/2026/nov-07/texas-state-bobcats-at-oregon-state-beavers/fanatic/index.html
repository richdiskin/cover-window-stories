First-Ever Meeting: Texas State Heads North to Face Oregon State

Texas State Bobcats (4-4) at Oregon State Beavers (5-3)

10:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Reser Stadium · CW

ShareXFacebook

Texas State vs Oregon State prediction for Saturday night in Corvallis: the Bobcats come up from San Marcos at 4-4, the Beavers are 5-3, and it is the first-ever meeting between these two programs.

Yesenia Calderwood has lived in San Marcos for most of her life and floats the river every summer. She brings a maroon foam finger to every road game and refuses to put it down. Juniper Ostrowski grew up in the Willamette Valley, has had the same seats at Reser Stadium for years, and wears a bright orange rain jacket no matter what the sky says.

"First meeting," Yesenia says. "We're making history."

"You're making a long flight home," Juniper says.

Corvallis is ready for the newcomers. Before kickoff, the OSU Alumni Association runs Tailgate Town at the CH2M HILL Alumni Center from 4:30 p.m. until kickoff at 7:30 p.m. Pacific, which is 10:30 p.m. ET. There's barbecue from Pacific Northwest pitmasters, indoor games, photo spots and appearances from OSU Cheer and the OSU Marching Band.

"Barbecue," Yesenia says. "In Oregon."

"Pacific Northwest pitmasters," Juniper says. "It's good."

"I'm from Texas," Yesenia says. "I'll be the judge."

Yesenia goes first. Texas State can score. The Bobcats put up 38.1 points a game. Brad Jackson has 1,933 yards, 18 touchdowns and 5 interceptions. Last week he threw for 327 yards and 4 touchdowns at Boise State, and Texas State lost 45-46. Torrance Burgess Jr. has 922 rushing yards. Chris Dawn Jr. leads the receivers with 467 yards. On defense, Jayden Bradley has 56 tackles, and Tymere Jackson has 3.5 sacks.

"We lost by one at Boise," Yesenia says. "One point. On the blue turf."

"You lost 7-59 at Texas," Juniper says.

"Everybody loses at Texas," Yesenia says.

"We didn't play Texas," Juniper says.

"Lucky you," Yesenia says.

Juniper takes her turn, and she has a lot to work with. Braden Atkinson is putting up huge numbers. He has 2,977 yards, 25 touchdowns and only 2 interceptions. Against Colorado State he went 31 of 34 for 427 yards and 5 touchdowns. Against San Diego State it was 409 yards and 5 more touchdowns. Jesse Legree has 1,110 receiving yards and 14 touchdowns. AJ Newberry leads the backs with 465 yards. On defense, Takari Hickle has 6 sacks and Aiden Sullivan has 60 tackles.

"Atkinson is the best quarterback nobody talks about," Juniper says.

"You lost 22-38 at Fresno State last week," Yesenia says.

"Fresno State is tough at home," Juniper says.

The Beavers scored 66 on San Diego State and 56 at Colorado State. Texas State scored 66 on Colorado State and 63 on Incarnate Word. These teams can both put up points in a hurry.

"Sixty-six," Juniper says. "We did it first."

"We did it too," Yesenia says. "So it doesn't count."

First meeting, so nobody has the script. This is the guess. It is a cool night at Reser. Oregon State gets the ball first, and Atkinson finds Legree deep for a touchdown on the opening drive. Texas State answers right away. Jackson hits Dawn for a big gain, and Burgess runs it in. That's how the whole first half goes. Score, answer, score, answer.

In the second half, Hickle gets to Jackson on a big third down, and Oregon State gets a field goal out of it. Atkinson finds Legree again early in the fourth quarter to stretch the lead. Texas State keeps fighting. Jackson throws a late touchdown to make it a one-score game, but the Beavers get the ball back and run out the clock.

Juniper says Corvallis made a good first impression. Yesenia says the Bobcats want a rematch someday, in San Marcos, where Juniper can leave the rain jacket at home.

My call: Oregon State Beavers 38, Texas State Bobcats 32.
