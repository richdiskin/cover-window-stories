Cincinnati Bearcats at Houston Cougars

Cincinnati Bearcats (5-3) at Houston Cougars (6-2)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · TDECU Stadium · Fox

ShareXFacebook

Cincinnati Bearcats vs Houston Cougars prediction for Saturday, November 7: Houston is hosting its 13th Annual AfriFest across town at The KBC Houston Event Center, with music, food and culture from 11 in the morning until 7 at night, and Marisol Treviño has already told everybody she is going.

Gus Bellweather flew down from Cincinnati with a Bearcats hoodie and a list.

"Five of the last six," he said, unfolding the list at Marisol's tailgate. "Thirty-four to nothing in 2024. Twenty-four to fourteen in 2023. Thirty-five to twenty in 2021. Shall I go on?"

Marisol is a Houston engineer, and she builds her arguments the way she builds bridges: load first, then style. "You can go on," she said. "Or you can look at what happened last Saturday."

Gus folded the list back up.

Last Saturday, Cincinnati lost 25-49 to Utah at home. Cole Tabb ran for 131 yards and a touchdown, but the defense could not stop anything. That has been the story of the Bearcats' fall. They are 5-3, they score 34.1 points a game and they give up 29.2. They lost 7-34 at Arizona and 42-45 at West Virginia. They also beat Kansas State 31-26 and Texas Tech 37-24, so they are dangerous on any given day.

"Cole Tabb," Gus said, recovering. "Seven hundred fifty-two yards. Ten touchdowns. JC French IV has 13 touchdown throws and 3 interceptions. We do not give the ball away."

That is true, and Marisol knows it. French is careful, and JV Gibson has 461 receiving yards. Jonathan Thompson has 63 tackles, and Patrick Bauer has 3.5 sacks.

Houston is 6-2, and Marisol thinks it is the best Cougars team she has seen in years. Conner Weigman has 2,053 yards, 20 touchdowns and just 2 interceptions. He threw 5 touchdowns in a 35-32 win over Oklahoma State and 4 more in a 35-16 win at Kansas State. Amare Thomas has 620 receiving yards. Makhi Hughes has 516 rushing yards. The defense gives up 21.0 points a game, and Kentrell Webb has 46 tackles from the back of it.

"You lost at Utah too," Gus said.

"Thirteen to twenty-one," Marisol said. "And then we had two weeks to think about it."

Houston has not played since Oct 24, so the Cougars come in rested. Marisol thinks a rested Weigman is the most dangerous player on the field. Gus says Tabb has fresher legs than anybody, because he runs so fast nobody touches him. Marisol says that is not how legs work.

Here is how it shakes out. Tabb breaks a long one early, and for a quarter Gus looks like a man about to add another entry to his list. But Houston settles in. Weigman starts finding Thomas on third downs, Hughes grinds out the tough yards, and the Cougars build a lead before halftime. Cincinnati keeps scoring, because it always does, but the defense cannot get off the field. Houston pulls away in the fourth quarter.

Gus will fly home with his list a little shorter. Marisol says she will mail him a new one. He says he will frame it next to the old one, because a Bearcats fan never throws away proof of the good years. She tells him the good years are a lot like Houston weather in November: pleasant while they last, and gone before you notice.

In the morning, she is taking him to AfriFest. The Nigerian American Multicultural Council hosts it every year, and Marisol says it is the best food in Houston for one day. Gus says he will try everything once. Marisol says he should start with the jollof rice and work from there.

My call: Houston Cougars 37, Cincinnati Bearcats 24.
