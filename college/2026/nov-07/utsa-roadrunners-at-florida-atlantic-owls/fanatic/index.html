UTSA Roadrunners at Florida Atlantic Owls

UTSA Roadrunners (7-1) at Florida Atlantic Owls (5-3)

7:30 PM ET Thu Nov 5 · Flagler Credit Union Stadium · ESPN

ShareXFacebook

UTSA Roadrunners vs Florida Atlantic Owls prediction for Thursday, November 5: it is Night Market night in downtown Boca Raton, and Kaia Brightwater has already walked Sanborn Square twice. Marisol Ybarra came in from San Antonio with a Fiesta medal on her cap and a 7-1 team behind her, and the two of them are settling this one before the Owls ever leave the tunnel.

The market runs from six to nine on Thursday evening, with local makers, music, sweet treats and a free BocaConnect shuttle hauling people downtown. Kaia knows every table. She knows the dark chocolate guy, the jam lady and the vanilla booth. She also knows Florida Atlantic football scores in bunches, and she wants Marisol to hear it twice.

"Thirty-seven point eight a game," Kaia says, holding up a jar of jam like a trophy. "That's us. That's Caden Veltkamp. Two thousand five hundred thirty-six yards and 23 touchdown throws, and he's not done."

Marisol nibbles a sample of chocolate and smiles. "Mija, I love a big offense. I married one. But UTSA is 4-0 in the American. We beat Tulane 42-19 on their own field. Owen McCown does not panic. He reads the field like a parade route. Left at the Alamo, right at the river, and he never drops the float."

Here is the thing Kaia will not say out loud: Florida Atlantic gives up 35.8 points a game. Army hung 56 on the Owls in October. North Texas went for 48 last week, even with Veltkamp throwing for 452 yards and five touchdowns and Easton Messer catching 16 balls for 189 yards.

"Sixteen catches," Kaia says. "Sixteen. Easton Messer is a wave that does not stop breaking. Seventy-four catches, 898 yards, nine touchdowns. Find me a Roadrunner who can keep up with that."

Marisol does not even look up. "Brandon Tucker has 56 tackles. Owen Pewee has four sacks. And we have Will Henderson III, who ran for 102 yards at Tulane and has 664 on the year. When we get the lead, Kaia, we hand the ball to Will and you watch your offense sit on the bench eating jam."

That is the whole fight. UTSA has won five in a row, and most of them were tight. Sixteen to fourteen at Rice. Thirty-eight to thirty-four over Navy. The Roadrunners do not blow people out on the road very often. They just keep running until the other side runs out of road.

"You guys squeak," Kaia says. "That's your whole personality. Squeak, squeak, beep beep."

"And you guys swim," Marisol says. "Beautifully. Right up until the fourth quarter, when you notice the tide went out."

Florida Atlantic has its own proof. The Owls beat Navy 38-30 in September with Veltkamp going 27 of 32 for 386 yards and five touchdowns. They beat Rice 51-49. When the Owls get going in their own building, the stadium gets loud and the ocean breeze feels like it is pushing the ball downfield.

The history is split right down the middle. Florida Atlantic won 40-26 in 2019 and 24-3 in 2020. UTSA won 36-10 in 2023 and 38-24 in 2024. Two apiece in the recent meetings, so nobody gets to brag at the jam table.

"Two and two," Marisol says. "Tonight breaks the tie."

"Tonight," Kaia says, "breaks your heart."

McCown is the steady hand. He has 1,751 yards with 10 touchdowns and only four interceptions, and he threw for 359 yards against Navy when the Roadrunners needed all of them. DJ Allen Jr. leads the receivers with 347 yards. Florida Atlantic needs Joseph Sipp Jr., who leads the Owls with 57 tackles and two sacks, to get into McCown's face early.

Here is how it shakes out. Veltkamp and Messer give the Boca crowd plenty to scream about, and the Owls keep the game within reach all night. UTSA answers every punch, Henderson grinds the fourth quarter, and McCown makes the late throw that matters. The Owls hang around right to the end. The Roadrunners just do what they have done for five straight games and finish.

Kaia buys one more square of dark chocolate on the way out of the square. Marisol buys the jam, because a Roadrunner always brings something home from the road.

My call: UTSA Roadrunners 38, Florida Atlantic Owls 32.
