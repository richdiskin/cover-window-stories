Florida Atlantic Takes 7 From UTSA on a Thursday Night Shootout

UTSA Roadrunners (7-1) at Florida Atlantic Owls (5-3)

7:30 PM ET Thu Nov 5 · Flagler Credit Union Stadium · ESPN

ShareXFacebook

UTSA vs Florida Atlantic prediction, spread and over/under for Thursday night: I have Florida Atlantic Owls +7 and Under 72.5, with UTSA Roadrunners 38, Florida Atlantic Owls 32 as my final. Kickoff is 7:30 PM ET at Flagler Credit Union Stadium, and downtown Boca Raton runs its monthly Night Market in Sanborn Square that same evening, chocolate and jam tables and all.

Kaia Brightwater reads this one like a swell report. "Caden Veltkamp threw for 452 yards and five scores at North Texas and we still lost 41-48. Easton Messer caught 16 balls that night. Our offense never goes flat. It just needs a defense that shows up for one set."

Marisol Ybarra has a Fiesta medal pinned to her cap. "UTSA is 7-1 and 4-0 in the American. Owen McCown has 10 touchdown throws and 4 interceptions. We won at Tulane 42-19. You don't win a parade by running fastest. You win it by never stopping."

Here is the case for the home side. Florida Atlantic scores 37.8 points a game, and only Florida and Army have run away from the Owls. UTSA has won five straight, but three of those wins came by 7 or fewer: 16-14 at Rice, 31-24 over South Florida and 38-34 over Navy. The Roadrunners close games. They rarely bury anyone on the road against a passing game this loud. My 38-32 is a six-point UTSA win, inside the 7.

On the over/under, the market already priced the fireworks. DraftKings opened it at 70.5 and it has climbed to 72.5. UTSA allows 26.0 a game, and Will Henderson III and a ground game with 664 yards from him alone can shorten the clock when the Roadrunners lead. My 38 and 32 add to 70, under the posted number by two and a half.

The worry for the Under is obvious. Florida Atlantic gives up 35.8 a game, and Messer has 898 receiving yards. If the first quarter turns into a track meet, the Under is in trouble early.

The room is split all over the map. The Formula has UTSA 37-29, takes UTSA Roadrunners −7, and likes the Under. Next Man Up has UTSA 40-34, sides with Florida Atlantic +7 and goes Over. In The Trenches sees the run games pointing at different teams, so no side, and it reads sides only with no score this week. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

The Formula: "Thirty-seven to twenty-nine. Eight points, Next Man Up. Pack a jam jar for the ride home."

Next Man Up: "Seventy-four combined, partner. Bring a flashlight for the scoreboard."

Kaia grabs a dark chocolate square at the Night Market before the walk to the stadium. Marisol takes two, one for each Roadrunner touchdown she is sure is coming.

UTSA Roadrunners 38, Florida Atlantic Owls 32. Florida Atlantic Owls cover the 7. Under 72.5.
