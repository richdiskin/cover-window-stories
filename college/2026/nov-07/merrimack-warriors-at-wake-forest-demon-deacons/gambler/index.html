No Number Yet, but Wake Forest Rolls Merrimack on Homecoming

Merrimack Warriors (4-5) at Wake Forest Demon Deacons (7-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium · ACC Network

ShareXFacebook

Merrimack vs Wake Forest prediction for Saturday: there is no line posted yet on this game. ESPN has no spread and no over/under up as of early Tuesday, so I have no side and no over/under call on it. What I do have is a final score, Wake Forest Demon Deacons 41, Merrimack Warriors 10, and the reasons behind it. Kickoff is 3:30 PM ET in Winston-Salem, and it is Homecoming, with Deacon fans asked to wear gold for the Gold Rush game.

Eloise Brightman-Rowe has the gold blazer out already. "No line? Fine. I don't need a market to tell me what a 51-7 win over Virginia looks like."

That is the first thing to know. Wake Forest is 7-1, No. 14, and it has won five in a row since a 20-33 loss to Miami on September 18. The last month has been lopsided: 57-3 over Stanford, 38-14 at NC State, 41-7 at California and 51-7 over Virginia. Across eight games the Deacs score 39.1 a game and give up 17.9.

The second thing is who is coming to town. Merrimack is an FCS team at 4-5, and this is the first meeting ever between these programs. The Warriors have won two in a row, 24-17 over New Hampshire and 19-14 over Wagner, so they are not arriving cold. But they score 18.0 a game and allow 27.1, and away from home they lost 7-42 at Delaware, 20-45 at Tarleton State, 24-41 at Yale and 21-27 at Dartmouth. Their one road win was 23-14 at Maine.

Why 41-10: Gio Lopez has 2,176 yards with 18 touchdown throws and 1 interception, and Carlos Hernandez has 856 receiving yards. That is an offense that scored 41 or more three times in its last four games. Merrimack's best hope is Matthias Latham, who has 623 rushing yards, and a slow afternoon that keeps Lopez off the field. Wake's defense held Stanford to 3 and California and Virginia to 7 apiece, and Langston Hardy has 5.5 sacks. AJ Hairston Jr has 7 touchdown throws and 6 interceptions on the year, so I don't see a lot of Warrior points. Ten feels right, and maybe generous. Wake Forest has also won its last 20 games against FCS opponents, going back to 2000.

The room has nothing posted on this one either. The Formula, Next Man Up and In The Trenches have no pick on Merrimack at Wake Forest, because there is no line for them to read. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

If a line goes up before Saturday, I will look at it then. Until then, this is a score and nothing more.

"Roll the Quad!" Eloise says. "And somebody hand the Warriors a gold scarf. It's Homecoming."

Wake Forest Demon Deacons 41, Merrimack Warriors 10. No line is posted yet, so there is no side and no over/under call.
