Merrimack at Wake Forest: Winston-Salem Has Homecoming, and Dickert Is Not Taking Attendance for Granted

Merrimack Warriors (4-5) at Wake Forest Demon Deacons (7-1)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Allegacy Federal Credit Union Stadium · ACC Network

ShareXFacebook

Winston-Salem gets Homecoming and Reunion Weekend, according to Wake Forest's official game center. Merrimack gets the visiting sideline. For the Deacons, this is a family gathering with a football responsibility attached, not a free Saturday with uniforms.

Wake Forest's Homecoming honors the 2006 ACC championship team and Jim Grobe, the coach who led the Deacs to that title, per Last Word on Sports. Jake Dickert is not sneering at the FCS visitor, either. He pointed to Merrimack's aggressive, well-disguised third-down defense and told his team to stay ahead of the chains.

Field Level Media quotes Jake Dickert calling it “a big standard week.” Wake is 7-1, Merrimack 4-5, and this is their first meeting, the report says. Merrimack coach Mike Gennetti says his players have embraced “the importance of finishing what we've started.” The visitors are entitled to bring their own purpose into the reunion.

Watch Merrimack's AJ Hairston after his two rushing touchdowns in a 19-14 win over Wagner. A crowded national schedule is not a reason to forget the people making their own homecoming memories here.

My call: Wake Forest Demon Deacons 41, Merrimack Warriors 10.
