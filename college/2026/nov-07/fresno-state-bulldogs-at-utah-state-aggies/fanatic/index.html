Fresno State Climbs the Cold Hill to Face Utah State in Logan

Fresno State Bulldogs (5-3) at Utah State Aggies (3-5)

9:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Maverik Stadium · USA Net

ShareXFacebook

Fresno State vs Utah State prediction, and Logan is going to be cold Saturday night: the Bulldogs roll in from the Central Valley at 5-3, the Aggies are 3-5 but just beat Colorado State, and the hill at Maverik Stadium won't care who brought a hoodie.

Roz Ayala has been going to Fresno State games since she was a kid, back when she sat on her dad's shoulders. She wears a red hoodie to every game, no matter how hot or cold it is. Hyrum Tolleson grew up in Cache Valley and walks to the stadium. He says anyone who needs a hoodie in November has never lived in Logan.

Saturday's game is about more than football. Utah State is honoring all military members and first responders. Kickoff is 9:30 p.m. ET, which is 7:30 p.m. in Logan, on Merlin Olsen Field at Maverik Stadium. Current Utah State students can get a free ticket, so the student section should be packed.

"Free tickets," Roz says. "That's the only way you can fill the place."

"We fill it fine," Hyrum says. "Just wait until you hear it."

Roz goes first. Fresno State's offense runs through Jayden Mandal. He has 1,595 throwing yards with 10 touchdowns and only 2 interceptions. Against Sacramento State he went 23 of 26 for 316 yards. Last week against Oregon State, he went 11 of 17 for 202 yards and 2 touchdowns in a 38-22 win. Bryson Donelson ran for 108 yards in that game and has 407 on the season. Josiah Freeman leads the receivers with 533 yards and caught 2 touchdowns last week. On defense, K'vion Thunderbird leads with 66 tackles, and Dylan Hampsten has 5 sacks.

"We beat Oregon State by 16," Roz says. "Oregon State scores a ton."

"You lost 0-39 at USC," Hyrum says.

"USC is USC," Roz says. "That was the first game."

Hyrum takes his turn, and he knows the Aggies have been up and down. Utah State has used two quarterbacks this year. Grady Brosterhous leads the team with 1,120 yards, 7 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. But McCae Hillstead took over last week and looked sharp. He went 17 of 22 for 173 yards in a 31-14 win over Colorado State. He also ran for 79 yards and a touchdown. Anthony Garcia was the hero of that game with 11 catches for 121 yards. Javen Jacobs leads the backs with 491 yards. Eli Wood leads the receivers with 479 yards and 5 touchdowns. On defense, Brevin Hamblin has 62 tackles, and Jordan Pendleton has 2.5 sacks.

"We lost to Washington by two," Hyrum says. "On the road. We beat Washington State by one. We lost to Texas State by three. We play everybody close."

"You lost to Idaho State," Roz says.

"We don't talk about that," Hyrum says.

History is pretty even. In the five meetings since 2016, Utah State won three and Fresno State won two. Utah State won 28-17 last year, and Hyrum has the program from that game framed in his basement. Fresno State won 37-32 in 2023 and 35-16 in 2020.

"Three to two," Hyrum says. "We're ahead."

"Not for long," Roz says.

Then they actually play it. It is cold and the wind is blowing off the mountains. Fresno State gets the ball first, and Mandal hits Freeman on a quick throw to get the Bulldogs moving. The drive stalls and they kick a field goal. Utah State answers with Hillstead. He runs for a first down and then finds Garcia for a big gain. The Aggies kick a field goal of their own.

The game stays tight. Neither team can run away with it. Donelson breaks a long run in the third quarter to give Fresno State the lead. Hillstead finds Wood in the end zone to get Utah State back within two. The Aggies get one more chance late, but Thunderbird makes a big stop, and Fresno State holds on.

Roz takes off her hoodie and waves it over her head in the cold. Hyrum says she will regret that in the morning.

My call: Fresno State Bulldogs 21, Utah State Aggies 19.
