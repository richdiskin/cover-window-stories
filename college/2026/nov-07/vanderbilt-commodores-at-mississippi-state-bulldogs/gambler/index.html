Vanderbilt at Mississippi State: Starkville Wants More Than Three Points This Time

Vanderbilt Commodores (3-5) at Mississippi State Bulldogs (4-4)

4:15 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Davis Wade Stadium · SEC Network

ShareXFacebook

Vanderbilt at Mississippi State: MSST -3; total 48.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

Starkville, the request is not complicated: a different afternoon from the 31-3 loss at Texas. Vanderbilt arrives after a 34-17 loss to Kentucky, according to ESPN's AP preview. Both teams can leave last week's frustration at the door, but only if they do something about it.

Two teams walk in looking for relief. Vanderbilt is trying to end a five-game skid, and Mississippi State is trying to end a four-game skid, per CBS Sports, with Homecoming in Starkville. The quarterback twist is real, too: former Vanderbilt quarterback AJ Swann is in the Bulldogs' room, with freshman Parker Puckett behind him.

Mississippi State's Anthony Evans III has 666 receiving yards; Vanderbilt's Junior Sherrill has 706. Sherrill caught eight for 78 in Vanderbilt's latest game, while State's Da'Marion Bothwell ran for 49 against Texas. Receivers can give this game a face beyond the records.

Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and its total is pending too.

The Bulldogs are 4-4, the Commodores 3-5. Watch whether State can keep possessions alive long enough for Evans to matter, and whether Jared Curtis can limit the giveaway that undoes Vanderbilt's next good drive. That is football business, not sourced gossip, and it needs no anonymous embellishment. Starkville gets a home chance to make the next conversation less painful.

The published Cover Writer card stays Mississippi State Bulldogs −3 and Over 48.5. My final is Mississippi State Bulldogs 32, Vanderbilt Commodores 23. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

Here is why I lay the three. Both teams are sliding, but Vanderbilt's slide has more holes in it. The Commodores have lost five in a row, giving up 21, 38, 52, 31 and 34 along the way, and Curtis has 8 interceptions to go with his 8 touchdown throws. Mississippi State's offense has gone quiet over the last month, with 0 at LSU and 3 at Texas, and Kamario Taylor has not been the Bulldog with the most throws in any of the last three games. Still, the Bulldogs scored 62, 38, 41 and 31 when they had their footing in September, and this is the softest defense they have seen since then.

The risk is plain. If the quarterback spot stays unsettled, a 15-23 day like the one against Oklahoma is on the table, and Vanderbilt has enough throwing game to sneak out of town.

Vanderbilt allows 30.2 and Mississippi State allows 28.1. The two meetings in this series since 2020 went 24-17 and 45-6.

Evans and Sherrill can make the game easier for their quarterbacks, but only if the offense gives each play a chance to develop. One reckless possession can erase several useful ones. Starkville does not need another reason to discuss last week's mistakes; it needs the next drive to reach a different ending.

Mississippi State Bulldogs 32, Vanderbilt Commodores 23. Mississippi State Bulldogs cover the 3. Over 48.5.
