Texas Lays 2.5 in Columbia and Outlasts No. 19 Missouri

Texas Longhorns (6-2) at Missouri Tigers (6-2)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Memorial Stadium · ESPN

ShareXFacebook

Texas vs Missouri prediction, pick against the spread and over/under for Saturday at Faurot Field: the Columbia Farmers Market runs its winter Saturday market on West Ash Street that morning, and by the time the farmers fold their tables, Texas and Missouri will be into the first quarter at noon ET. Two 6-2 teams, a No. 10 and a No. 19, and a market that has already moved.

DraftKings opened Texas −3.5 with the over/under at 49.5. It now sits at Texas −2.5 and 43.5. That is six points knocked off the over/under, which tells you the room expects a slog. I am going the other way on both halves of it. My side is Texas Longhorns −2.5, and my number on points is Over 43.5.

The case for Texas is the trouble it makes in the backfield. Colin Simmons has 10.5 sacks, and Missouri's Austin Simmons has thrown just 2 interceptions in 1,952 yards. A careful quarterback is still a quarterback who can be hurried. Arch Manning has 1,727 yards and 15 touchdowns, and his last outing was 16 of 20 for 220 and 3 scores in a 31-3 win over Mississippi State. The two Texas losses came 24-27 to Oklahoma and 24-27 to Florida, both one-possession games.

The case for Missouri is Jamal Roberts, who has 1,005 yards and 9 touchdowns and carried 23 times for 138 in the 34-10 win at Arkansas. Mizzou beat Florida 45-17, the same Florida team that beat Texas. Then again, Mizzou also lost 3-31 at Ole Miss, a team Texas beat 34-27. Commutative property does not cash tickets, but it does explain why the market keeps nudging toward the home team.

Texas scores 30.8 a game and allows 17.1. Missouri scores 31.5 and allows 18.4. Two offenses that clear 30 do not usually combine for 43, and that is the heart of my Over 43.5.

Now the room. The Formula has Texas 28-21 and likes Texas −2.5 and the Over, so it agrees with me on both. Next Man Up has Texas 22-21 and takes Missouri +2.5 with the Under, which is a one-point game and a vote for the farmers going home early. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. In The Trenches calls the trench matchup split and offers no side. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

The Next Man Up crew will tell you a one-point Texas win is the sharp read. I will tell you they built a 43-point game out of two offenses that each put up 30, and that any grower on West Ash Street can do better arithmetic than that.

Texas Longhorns 25, Missouri Tigers 21 is my final. Manning keeps the ball out of trouble, Simmons gets home twice, and Roberts gets his yards without getting Missouri all the way back.

Texas Longhorns 25, Missouri Tigers 21. Texas Longhorns cover the 2.5. Over 43.5.
