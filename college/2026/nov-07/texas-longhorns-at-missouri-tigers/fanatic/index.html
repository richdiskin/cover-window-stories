Two 6-2 Teams, One Faurot Field: Texas Barges Into Mizzou

Texas Longhorns (6-2) at Missouri Tigers (6-2)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Memorial Stadium · ESPN

ShareXFacebook

Texas vs Missouri prediction for Saturday at Faurot Field, and the two loudest people in Columbia have been at each other's throats over this one for weeks. Caroline Whitfield bleeds burnt orange and has never once lowered her voice in a stadium. Delphine Arceneaux-Moss has followed Mizzou from the press box and the student section and will tell you which one has better coffee.

Delphine: Saturday morning, the Columbia Farmers Market sets up its winter market at 1769 West Ash. Local growers, local bakers, local folks who have watched a lot of visiting teams leave town quiet. I want every Texas fan in Columbia to go. Buy something from a farmer. Learn something about patience, because you are going to need it at Faurot Field.

Caro: Patience is a word people use when they are about to lose. I will go to the market. I will buy whatever is in season. I will wear burnt orange in the produce aisle and nobody will say a thing, because we are 6-2 and ranked No. 10 and everybody in Columbia knows it.

Delphine: You are 6-2 with two losses by three points each. Oklahoma 27, Texas 24. Florida 27, Texas 24. Same score twice. That is not a football team, that is a recurring dream.

Caro: And you lost to Florida, too? Oh wait, no. You beat Florida 45-17. Then you went to Oxford and scored 3 points. Three. I have seen higher numbers on a thermometer in Austin in January.

Delphine: Ole Miss was a bad night. We answered it. We went to Arkansas and won 34-10, and Jamal Roberts carried it 23 times for 138 yards. He has 1,005 yards this year and 9 touchdowns. He runs like the field owes him money.

Caro: Roberts is a real back. I will give you that one, free of charge, and it's the last free thing you get from me. But you have not seen Colin Simmons up close. He has 10.5 sacks. Your quarterback is going to spend Saturday looking over his shoulder like somebody is following him home from the library.

Delphine: Speaking of the library, the Columbia Public Library is holding a Local Authors Open House Saturday. You should go. Ask somebody to write a happy ending for a team that keeps losing by three.

Caro: Clever. Here is my happy ending. Arch Manning has 1,727 yards and 15 touchdown throws. Last week against Mississippi State he went 16 of 20 for 220 yards and 3 touchdowns, and Texas won 31-3. Emmett Mosley caught 4 balls for 98 yards. That is a quarterback who is finding his rhythm in November, which is the month that matters.

Delphine: Austin Simmons has 1,952 yards, 16 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. Two. Your guy has 7. If we are comparing who protects the football, I will take the man who guards it like his truck keys.

Caro: Seven interceptions is a number I will own. He also has eight more touchdowns than interceptions. And Texas scores 30.8 a game while giving up 17.1. You score 31.5 and give up 18.4. Basically identical, except one of us has a defense that makes quarterbacks nervous.

Delphine: Daeden Hopkins has 7 sacks for us. Nicholas Rodriguez has 84 tackles. You think the Texas rush gets to show up and our front just watches? We beat Texas A&M 27-6. Ask the Aggies how much fun it was.

Caro: I would never ask the Aggies anything. That is one thing Texas and Missouri agree on.

Delphine: Finally, common ground. Here is my other point. Your team has to travel north for a game in November. You will get off the plane, feel the wind, and wonder why Columbia smells like woodsmoke and diesel.

Caro: It smells like a road win, and I am going to enjoy every bite of it. The Longhorns are bringing Ryan Wingo, who has 470 receiving yards, and Raleek Brown, who has 452 on the ground. Nobody in your secondary has seen speed like Wingo's since the last time a Texas team came through.

Delphine: Fine. Here is what happens. The market closes, the tailgates fill up, Faurot Field gets loud, and Roberts runs downhill for four quarters. Mizzou keeps it close all day.

Caro: Close, yes. I will agree with close. Simmons gets to your quarterback when it matters, Manning finds Wingo for the play everybody talks about on Sunday, and the Saturday market crowd waves goodbye to the burnt orange as we drive south.

Delphine: You are buying souvenirs at the market before you leave, aren't you.

Caro: Two bags. One for me and one for the trophy case.

My call: Texas Longhorns 25, Missouri Tigers 21.
