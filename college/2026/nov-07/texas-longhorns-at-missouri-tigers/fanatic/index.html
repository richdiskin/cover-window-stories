Texas at Missouri: Columbia Has Company, and Neither Side Can Afford the Goodbye

Texas Longhorns (6-2) at Missouri Tigers (6-2)

12:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Memorial Stadium · ESPN

ShareXFacebook

Columbia, clear some room for the Longhorns. Texas is visiting Missouri for the first time since 2011, and with both teams 6-2, this is no casual reunion. Somebody's November gets a whole lot heavier before dinner. The AP preview calls it a de facto playoff elimination game; that is an assessment, not an official elimination rule.

Eli Drinkwitz offered Ryan Wingo a scholarship in eighth grade and still has not gotten over losing him. “Ryan was one of those that broke my heart, honestly,” Drinkwitz said this week, per On3, before adding that he wishes the St. Louis kid all the best “except for Saturday.” Missouri has its own heart story, too: the Tigers wore gold DJ heart decals in October for freshman lineman Johnnie Jones after his heart transplant, the Columbia Daily Tribune reported.

Here is the Missouri heartbeat, straight from safety Santana Banner in that AP report: “Everyone's bought in, everybody is fighting for each other.” Texas coach Steve Sarkisian counters: “We're built for this type of stuff. We know how to do it.” Good. Bring both speeches to the trenches and let the pads edit them.

Jamal Roberts ran for 138 yards in Missouri's 34-10 win at Arkansas, putting him over 1,000 for the season, per AP. Missouri wants its runner setting the terms. Texas wants Arch Manning keeping drives alive, not spending the afternoon escaping trouble. The Longhorns have already allowed 24 sacks, also per AP. That is your watch-with-your-buddy argument: whose backfield gets to operate on its own schedule?

The latest injury news is better than Thursday's: Burnt Orange Nation reports Ryan Wingo, Kade Phillips and Graceson Littleton were upgraded to probable Friday; Colin Simmons and Cam Coleman remained questionable. Wingo is a St. Louis product returning to his home state, the report notes. Probable is not a final Saturday clearance. Columbia gets the noise; the rest of us get a reason to sit down early.

My call: Texas Longhorns 25, Missouri Tigers 21.
