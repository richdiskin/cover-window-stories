Iowa at Northwestern: Evanston Gets Iowa, and the Quiet Plays May Be the Loudest Ones

Iowa Hawkeyes (6-2) at Northwestern Wildcats (6-2)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Ryan Field · Fox

XFacebook

Evanston has a meeting of two 6-2 teams that deserves more than a glance between the famous games. Northwestern comes off a 34-26 loss at Oregon. Iowa just shut out Wisconsin 30-0. ESPN's AP preview gives both fan bases a reason to believe this Saturday matters on its own terms.

Northwestern's new Ryan Field comes with the Wildcat Wall, a standing student section built for roughly 2,000 people and built to be loud, per The Gazette. Iowa is ready to answer with a silent cadence, so stadium pride turns into a real football problem for the visitor.

Northwestern's Griffin Wilde caught five passes for 146 yards and a touchdown against Oregon. Iowa's Hank Brown went 20-for-24 for 222 yards and two scores against Wisconsin. With Chiles out and Ryan Boe available behind him, Northwestern has a different quarterback problem tonight: keeping Iowa from making the pocket feel like a phone booth.

Iowa allows 79 rushing yards a game in that report, while Northwestern's Caleb Komolafe has 618 rushing yards for the season. Follow the ordinary early-down gains. Against a defense like Iowa's, the difference between manageable and desperate can feel enormous before anything spectacular happens. Northwestern gets a home chance to show it can win the patient part of a big game.

My call: Northwestern Wildcats 24, Iowa Hawkeyes 21.
