Iowa at Northwestern: Evanston Gets Iowa, and the Quiet Plays May Be the Loudest Ones

Iowa Hawkeyes (6-2) at Northwestern Wildcats (6-2)

7:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Ryan Field · Fox

XFacebook

Iowa at Northwestern: IOWA -3; total 42.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

Evanston has a meeting of two 6-2 teams that deserves more than a glance between the famous games. Northwestern comes off a 34-26 loss at Oregon. Iowa just shut out Wisconsin 30-0. ESPN's AP preview gives both fan bases a reason to think this Saturday matters on its own terms.

Northwestern's new Ryan Field comes with the Wildcat Wall, a standing student section built for roughly 2,000 people and built to be loud, per The Gazette. Iowa is ready to answer with a silent cadence, so stadium pride turns into a real football problem for the visitor.

Northwestern's Griffin Wilde caught five passes for 146 yards and a touchdown against Oregon. Iowa's Hank Brown went 20-for-24 for 222 yards and two scores against Wisconsin. With Chiles out and Ryan Boe available behind him, Northwestern has a different quarterback problem tonight: keeping Iowa from making the pocket feel like a phone booth.

Iowa allows 79 rushing yards a game in that report, while Northwestern's Caleb Komolafe has 618 rushing yards for the season. Follow the ordinary early-down gains. Against a defense like Iowa's, the difference between manageable and desperate can feel enormous before anything spectacular happens. Northwestern gets a home chance to show it can win the patient part of a big game.

My published card stays Northwestern Wildcats −2.5 and Over 44.5. My final is Northwestern Wildcats 24, Iowa Hawkeyes 21. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

Aidan Chiles is out tonight. His 1,898 yards, 13 touchdowns and one interception stay in the record book, but they cannot take a snap against Iowa. Griffin Wilde has 761 receiving yards and went for 146 at Oregon. Northwestern scores 33.6 a game. Iowa allows 12.5, which is the best number on the field, but Iowa has played a softer path to get there. Northwestern lost 26-34 at Oregon and still threw for 281 yards. Northwestern now needs the rest of its offense to carry more of the load.

We're on the Over 44.5. Iowa scores 28.2 a game, and Hank Brown went 20 of 24 for 222 yards and 2 touchdowns last week. Northwestern scores in bunches at home. Both teams get into the 20s.

Komolafe's useful carries do not have to look spectacular to matter. Against Iowa, staying out of a desperate passing down is a substantial accomplishment. Brown's latest passing work is evidence that Iowa can finish a possession through the air when the opportunity comes. Northwestern cannot devote all its attention to the running game and hope the rest takes care of itself. Wilde supplies a matching problem for the Hawkeyes. Evanston gets two defenses with actual choices to make.

Northwestern Wildcats 24, Iowa Hawkeyes 21. Northwestern Wildcats cover the 2.5. Over 44.5.
