Purdue Lays 6.5 to Maryland on Homecoming in West Lafayette

Maryland Terrapins (2-6) at Purdue Boilermakers (3-5)

12:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Ross-Ade Stadium · BTN

ShareXFacebook

Maryland vs Purdue prediction, pick against the spread and over/under for Saturday at Ross-Ade Stadium: it is Purdue Homecoming, kickoff is 12:30 PM ET, and the College of Agriculture and Vet Med alumni will be tailgating on Memorial Mall before the game. Maryland is 2-6 and has lost every Big Ten game it has played. Purdue is 3-5. Somebody has to win, and I know who.

DraftKings opened Purdue −7 with the over/under at 57.5. It is now Purdue −6.5 and still 57.5. Half a point of drift toward Maryland, the total parked. My side is Purdue Boilermakers −6.5, and my number is Over 57.5.

Begin with Ryan Browne. He has 1,964 yards with 11 touchdowns and 5 interceptions, and before a rough 10 of 29 for 142 at Penn State last week he threw for 378 at UCLA and 329 against Wake Forest. Fame Ijeboi has 588 rushing yards and 9 touchdowns, and Asaad Waseem has 652 receiving yards. Purdue scores 29.5 a game, and at home it beat Minnesota 41-13.

Maryland's problem is that it stopped stopping people. The Terrapins allow 31.6 a game. They lost 3-54 to UCLA, 23-48 at Nebraska and 16-45 at Ohio State. Malik Washington has 1,805 yards and 9 touchdowns, but also 7 interceptions, and Chris Durr Jr. has 58 catches for 638 yards. The closest Big Ten game was last week, 17-20 against Illinois. Purdue went to Illinois and won 24-17.

Purdue's defense is not good either, allowing 32.1 a game. That is the case for Over 57.5. Two defenses giving up more than 31 a game, two quarterbacks who can throw, and a Homecoming crowd that wants points.

The series is close in recent memory. Purdue won 31-29 in 2022 and 40-14 in 2019. Maryland won 50-7 in 2016.

The cards agree more than usual. The Formula has Purdue 34-26 and takes Purdue −6.5 and likes the Over. Next Man Up has Purdue 36-26 and likes Purdue −6.5 with the Over. In The Trenches calls the trench matchup split and offers no side. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

When The Formula and Next Man Up agree on the side and the Over, I do not go looking for reasons to argue. Purdue at home against a team that has not won a Big Ten game is the cleanest spot on the board.

Purdue Boilermakers 35, Maryland Terrapins 26 is my final. Browne bounces back, Ijeboi scores twice, and Washington throws enough to push it over 57.5 in the fourth quarter.

Purdue Boilermakers 35, Maryland Terrapins 26. Purdue Boilermakers cover the 6.5. Over 57.5.
