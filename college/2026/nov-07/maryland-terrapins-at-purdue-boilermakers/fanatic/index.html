Maryland Crabs Meet Purdue Homecoming in West Lafayette

Maryland Terrapins (2-6) at Purdue Boilermakers (3-5)

12:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Ross-Ade Stadium · BTN

ShareXFacebook

Maryland vs Purdue prediction for Saturday in West Lafayette, from two people who should never have been seated together at a wedding, and were, and have been texting each other insults ever since. Abby Chesapeake is from Annapolis, has cracked crabs since she could hold a mallet, and judges every situation by whether it would be improved with Old Bay. Barnaby Wolcott-Reese is a Purdue engineer who builds scale replicas of the Boilermaker Special in his garage and cannot stop making engineering puns, even when begged.

Barnaby: Saturday is Homecoming at Ross-Ade. Kickoff at 12:30. And before that, the Ag and Vet Med alumni tailgate on the south end of Memorial Mall, ten to noon, catered by Shoup's Country Foods. Abby, it is a full Indiana table. Pork, corn, the works. You are going to love it.

Abby: I am going to try it. Whether I love it depends on whether anybody brought a crab.

Barnaby: There are no crabs in Indiana.

Abby: That explains a lot about Indiana.

Barnaby: What it explains is that we have other priorities. Like football. Ryan Browne has 1,964 yards and 11 touchdowns. He has torque. He has thrust. He is a precision instrument.

Abby: He went 10 of 29 at Penn State last week.

Barnaby: Every engine has a rough cycle. You recalibrate. Six weeks before that he threw for 378 at UCLA. That is the real output.

Abby: You lost that game 52-38.

Barnaby: And you lost to UCLA 54-3. If we are comparing our results against the Bruins, I am comfortable with mine.

Abby: That was a bad day. Malik Washington threw 4 interceptions. We do not talk about it in Maryland. It is like a crab with no meat in it. You crack it, you look, you set it aside, and you move on.

Barnaby: You have been setting aside a lot of crabs this year. Two wins and six losses. Zero and five in the Big Ten.

Abby: We beat UConn 38-14 on the road. Washington went 34 of 41 for 347. When he is right, he is right. And Chris Durr Jr. has 58 catches for 638 yards. He catches everything. He is a crab trap. Whatever comes near him, it is staying.

Barnaby: Asaad Waseem has 652 receiving yards for us. More yards. Fewer catches. That is called efficiency. That is an engineering term.

Abby: That is a word you made up to feel better about fewer catches.

Barnaby: Fame Ijeboi has 588 rushing yards and 9 touchdowns. He is a piston. Up and down the field, all afternoon, no rest. We beat Minnesota 41-13 at home. We beat Illinois 24-17 on the road. The same Illinois that beat you last week.

Abby: Illinois beat us 20-17. Three points. We were right there. That is the most encouraging loss I have had all year.

Barnaby: The most encouraging loss. That should be on a Maryland shirt.

Abby: Your defense gives up 32.1 a game. That should be on a Purdue shirt. Ours gives up 31.6. So we are better at defense, Barnaby. By half a point. Write it on the garage wall next to your train.

Barnaby: It is not a train. It is a replica of the Boilermaker Special. It runs. It has a horn. It is a working locomotive in miniature.

Abby: So it is a train.

Barnaby: It is an engineering achievement.

Abby: Does it carry crabs?

Barnaby: It could, theoretically. Load capacity is not the issue. The issue is that there are no crabs in Indiana.

Abby: There will be on Saturday. I am bringing a cooler.

Barnaby: Here is the history. Purdue won 31-29 in 2022. Purdue won 40-14 in 2019. Maryland won 50-7 in 2016, which I do not acknowledge because I was not yet emotionally invested.

Abby: Fifty to seven. Say it with me.

Barnaby: I will not. Here is my prediction. Browne rebounds, Ijeboi scores twice, the defense holds just long enough, and Homecoming ends with a win. It is simple mechanics. Force applied, result achieved.

Abby: Here is mine. Washington throws for plenty, Durr catches everything, and we make it close enough that you sweat through your engineering shirt.

Barnaby: You will lose.

Abby: Probably. But I will lose with Old Bay on my fingers, and you will win with pork on yours. We both know who had the better afternoon.

My call: Purdue Boilermakers 35, Maryland Terrapins 26.
