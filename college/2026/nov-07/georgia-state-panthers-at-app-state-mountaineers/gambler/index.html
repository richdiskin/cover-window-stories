Georgia State Takes 5.5 in Boone and Hangs With App State

Georgia State Panthers (4-4) at App State Mountaineers (5-3)

2:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Kidd Brewer Stadium · ESPN+

ShareXFacebook

Georgia State vs App State prediction, spread and over/under for Saturday: I have Georgia State Panthers +3.5 and Over 55.5, with App State Mountaineers 30, Georgia State Panthers 27 as my final. Kickoff is 2:30 PM ET in Boone, and it is the Heroes Day Game, with a Heroes Day Tailgate on the McKinney Alumni Center patio on Blowing Rock Road before kickoff.

Malik Odom talks about Georgia State like a man who has been waiting a decade. "Cameran Brown has 14 touchdown throws. We score 33.9 a game. Rodshaun Dorsey has 8 touchdown catches."

Zeb Whitlock, App State to the bone, pours coffee before he answers. "App State has won all 10 meetings since 2016, Malik. It was 41-20 last year. And Xavier Esqillion's 7 sacks don't scare Colton Phares."

Here is why I take the points. App State's offense has disappeared twice: 3-10 at home to Old Dominion and 3-19 at Georgia Southern last week. Malachi Singleton threw 2 interceptions in that one. Georgia State has lost three in a row, but it scored 32 at Arkansas State and 22 at James Madison, and it can put up points. Ayden Pereira has led the Panthers in throws the last two weeks after Brown led the first six. My 30-27 is a 3-point App State win, inside the 3.5. DraftKings opened App State −3.5, and it has not moved.

The risk is the history. Ten straight meetings, and App State won eight of them by double digits. Georgia State also gives up 26.6 a game, and Jaquari Lewis has 8 rushing touchdowns.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 57.5 and it is down to 55.5. Georgia State scores 33.9 a game and allows 26.6. App State scores 26.8 and allows 22.6. My 30 and 27 make 57, a point and a half over 55.5.

The room mostly sides with the road team. The Formula has App State 29-26 and takes Georgia State +3.5 with the Under. Next Man Up has App State 30-27, takes the Panthers +3.5, and likes the Over. In The Trenches takes Georgia State +3.5 as well, and it reads sides only with no score this week. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "Same score as the column, to the point. Somebody has been reading my notes."

The Formula: "Three points either way. The mountain does not care who copied."

Zeb will be at the Heroes Day Tailgate, thanking veterans and their families. Malik says he will join him there, since Georgia State fans can respect a good tailgate even in enemy country.

App State Mountaineers 30, Georgia State Panthers 27. Georgia State Panthers cover the 3.5. Over 55.5.
