Northern Illinois at San José State: San Jose Gets a Game That Means More Than the Records Look

Northern Illinois Huskies (0-8) at San José State Spartans (4-5)

4:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · CEFCU Stadium · MW+

XFacebook

San Jose hosts a Northern Illinois team still looking for its first win. That is an 0-8 record, not a license to stop caring. The Huskies just lost 31-24 to UNLV while San Jose State fell 21-16 to New Mexico, per ESPN's AP preview.

San José State makes it Parent and Family Weekend with a salute to military and first responders, per SJSU Athletics. This is only the second meeting ever between these programs. The first was in 1995, and Northern Illinois won it 18-17. Thirty-one years later, NIU is still looking for its first win of the season, and that kind of team keeps showing up anyway.

Northern Illinois got 113 rushing yards from Elijah Porter and 127 receiving yards from George Dimopoulos in that loss. San Jose State's Carson Clark caught eight for 91. Those players did enough to give fans a reason to come back, even when the result did not.

Watch Luke Weaver's decisions for the Spartans after his two interceptions against New Mexico. Watch whether Northern Illinois can keep Porter's ground game connected to the passing attack instead of chasing from behind. The home team is 4-5, and a good afternoon would move it back to even. San Jose gets the chance to keep that possibility from becoming another one-score explanation.

My call: San José State Spartans 30, Northern Illinois Huskies 18.
