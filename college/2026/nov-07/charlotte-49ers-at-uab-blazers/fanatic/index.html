Charlotte 49ers at UAB Blazers

Charlotte 49ers (1-7) at UAB Blazers (4-4)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Protective Stadium · ESPN+

ShareXFacebook

Charlotte 49ers vs UAB Blazers prediction for Saturday, November 7: Protective Stadium turns into Kids Takeover, the Blazers wear gray jerseys with 99 children's names on the back, and Ozella Birchfield has already ironed her gray sweater twice. Priya Halloran drove down from uptown Charlotte with a spreadsheet, a thermos and the calm face of a woman who has watched her team go 1-7.

Here's the deal. Saturday is the 10th annual Children's Harbor Game. Since the first one in 2017, UAB players have swapped their own names off the backs of their jerseys for the names of kids facing serious medical challenges, and each child gets the jersey worn in their honor after the game. More than 400 tickets and parking tickets were bought for the families. Fans are asked to wear gray. The Blazers are 8-1 in these games.

"I'm going to cry before kickoff," Ozella says. "Then I'm going to yell. In that order. Every year."

Priya nods. "That's a beautiful tradition. I'm still wearing teal. I'll clap in teal."

There's also a display in Blazer Village this week called "Derail the Niners." Priya has seen it.

"Derail," she says. "Clever jab. We already derailed ourselves in September. You can't derail a train that's in the shop."

"Forged, not given, honey," Ozella says. "Birmingham was a train town before it was anything. We know what a derailment looks like."

Kickoff is 3:30 p.m. ET, and before that the Blazer Walk brings the team through Uptown with the Marching Blazers. UAB is 4-0 at Protective this season, which Ozella will tell you about whether you ask or not.

Now the football.

Priya goes first, and she goes first because she has the better story to tell, even at 1-7. Charlotte's season turned when Conner Harrell took over at quarterback. He has 831 yards with 5 touchdowns and just 2 interceptions, and in his three games the 49ers won 38-31 at Temple and took Tulane to overtime before losing 31-38. Henry Rutledge leads the ground game with 452 yards, and he ran 22 times for 121 yards against Tulane. Jaden Barnes has 48 catches for 462 yards. Gavin Willis leads the defense with 65 tackles.

"The first five games were a write-down," Priya says. "Since then we're a turnaround story. I'd hold."

Ozella takes the floor. Ryder Burton has thrown for 1,333 yards. Rod Robinson II has 450 rushing yards. CJ Smith is the big-play guy with 21 catches for 403 yards, which is a lot of yards for not many catches. Then there's the defense. Marlin Dean has 7.5 sacks, and Muaaz Byard leads the team with 76 tackles.

"Marlin Dean is a blast furnace," Ozella says. "You open the door, you get burned."

"Your team lost 10-30 at South Florida last week," Priya says.

"And we beat Navy 24-20 in September," Ozella says. "Furnaces have off days. The fire doesn't go out."

Charlotte scores 20.6 a game and gives up 40.2. UAB scores 21.6 and allows 25.2. Ozella keeps reading the 40.2 out loud like it's a hymn.

"I know that figure," Priya says. "I put it in red. It has been in red since September."

The recent history is even. Going back to 2017, they've split four meetings, two apiece. Charlotte won the last one, 29-27 in 2024, and Priya remembers the Thanksgiving weekend drive home like it was a victory lap at Charlotte Motor Speedway.

"Two and two," Ozella says. "Somebody breaks it Saturday."

"Somebody always does," Priya says.

Here's how it plays out under the Birmingham sky. The building is gray and loud early, and the Blazers come out flying in those jerseys. Robinson gets UAB rolling on the ground, and Smith catches a long one over the top that makes Ozella stand on her chair. Charlotte stays in it longer than anybody in Uptown wants, with Harrell finding Barnes on third downs and Rutledge running hard. But Dean gets home a couple of times in the second half, and Byard is everywhere. UAB pulls away in the fourth quarter, and the kids on the jerseys get the afternoon they deserve.

Ozella hugs a stranger in gray. Priya opens a spreadsheet tab called "Write-Downs," adds one more row, and then shuts the laptop and claps for the kids anyway, in teal.

My call: UAB Blazers 33, Charlotte 49ers 22.
