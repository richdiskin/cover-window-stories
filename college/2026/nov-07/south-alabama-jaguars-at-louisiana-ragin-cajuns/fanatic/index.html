Louisiana Salutes Night: South Alabama Walks Into Cajun Country

South Alabama Jaguars (4-4) at Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns (5-3)

5:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Our Lady of Lourdes Stadium · ESPN+

ShareXFacebook

South Alabama vs Louisiana prediction for Louisiana Salutes night in Lafayette: the Ragin' Cajuns honor military members, veterans and first responders at Our Lady of Lourdes Stadium on Saturday, with a 5:00 PM ET kickoff, and South Alabama shows up as the one visitor in the league that seems to like it here.

The Jaguars have won their last two trips to Lafayette. They won 24-22 in 2024, and in 2022 they won 20-17 on a field goal at the very end. That is the part Mobile people bring up first. The part they bring up second, if you push, is that they are 2-5 all time at Cajun Field. Saturday is the 15th meeting between the two.

I got the two sides together on a phone call that ran long.

Azalea Fairhope-Grice builds Mardi Gras floats in Mobile and used to coach high school volleyball, which means she can spot a rotation breaking down from the cheap seats. Amélie Fontenot, Mimi to everyone, is an accountant in Lafayette who will tell you about zydeco whether you asked or not, and then she will show you the steps.

"I want to talk about 2022," Azalea said before I could ask anything.

"Of course you do," Mimi said. "One kick. Everybody in Mobile has it framed."

"We have two, actually. 2024 too. 24-22."

"And last year we won 31-22 and nobody in Mobile mentions it."

They are both right, and that is the fun of this one. Over the ten meetings from 2016 to 2025, Louisiana won eight and South Alabama won two. But the Jaguars' two came in the last four years, and both came in this building.

This South Alabama team is 4-4 and plays wide open. The Jaguars score 33.2 a game and allow 32.8. They beat Ohio 41-36, beat UL Monroe 52-35 and won a 56-49 shootout at Arkansas State. Then the month went sideways, with a 13-20 loss at Marshall and a 20-35 loss to Louisiana Tech last Saturday.

"The Marshall game was a volleyball match where nobody could serve," Azalea said. "Jared Hollins went 12 for 33. That is not him."

Hollins has 1,195 throwing yards with 9 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Keenan Phillips is the lead back with 576 yards and 6 scores, Brendan Jenkins leads the receivers at 451 yards, and Cole Blaylock leads the defense with 46 tackles. Against Louisiana Tech, Anthony Eager caught 7 balls for 102 yards and 2 touchdowns, so there are weapons.

Mimi waited politely and then went to work. "Lunch Winfield."

That is the whole Louisiana argument in two words. Winfield has thrown for 1,452 yards and leads the team on the ground with 470 yards and 9 rushing touchdowns. The Cajuns are 5-3, scoring 30.1 a game and allowing just 21.2, with Jake St. Andre at 60 tackles.

"We lost by one at Louisiana Tech, 31-32," Mimi said. "We lost 31-35 at Southern Miss. Close ones. At home we beat Troy 33-6 and Arkansas State 23-20. Home is home."

"Home is also where you lost to us twice," Azalea said.

Mimi did a little two-step in her kitchen. I could hear it over the phone.

What about the salute? Both of them liked it. Azalea's father was a firefighter in Mobile, and she said a night that honors first responders hits her harder than any rivalry. Mimi said Lafayette does these nights right, with the crowd on its feet for every service member they bring onto the field.

Here is how I see the game. Louisiana's defense is the better group on the field, and Winfield is the best player in it. At home, with the crowd up for Louisiana Salutes, the Cajuns should take control by the third quarter. But South Alabama scores too much to go away. Phillips will find room, Hollins will hit enough shots, and the Jaguars will keep it a two-score game into the fourth.

Azalea says she has seen floats lose a wheel and still finish the parade. Mimi says she will be dancing on the way out either way.

My call: Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns 35, South Alabama Jaguars 26.
