Akron Gets 13.5 on Taco Tuesday, but Ohio Takes It

Ohio Bobcats (6-2) at Akron Zips (1-7)

7:00 PM ET Tue Nov 3 · InfoCision Stadium · ESPN2

Ohio vs Akron prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: I'm on Akron Zips +13.5 and Over 51.5 at DraftKings, with my final at Ohio Bobcats 32, Akron Zips 21. Tuesday night MAC football lands at InfoCision Stadium at 7:00 PM ET on Election Day, and the Zips are throwing a Taco Tuesday theme night for a team that has won once all fall.

Penny Trautman drove up from the Athens brick streets with a thermos and a grin. "Ohio Bobcats are 6-2 and 4-0 in the MAC," she says. "Nick Poulos threw for 266 yards against Eastern Michigan, and Dom Dorwart caught four balls for 125. We lay our wins down one brick at a time."

Hollister "Zip" Grubbs has his tire gauge out at the tailgate. "We're 1-7, Penny, I know. But Jordan Gant ran 28 times for 162 yards and two scores at Kent State. Rubber that's been stretched that far snaps back."

Here is the case for the points. Ohio has won its last four by 3, 17, 41 and 4, and two of those were 13-10 at Kent State and 24-20 over Eastern Michigan. Victor Rosa leads the ground game with 364 yards, and this offense is happy to shorten the night. Akron loses big to good teams, but it played Eastern Michigan to 23-25. My final has Ohio by 11, inside the 13.5.

The worry is Brayden Roggow. He has 9 interceptions on the year, including 4 in the 14-34 loss at Miami (OH). A Bobcats defense led by Michael Molnar, with 5 sacks and 70 tackles, can turn mistakes into a runaway.

On the over/under, Ohio scores 29.6 a game and Akron gives up 32.8. The Zips score 19.0 against an Ohio defense allowing 21.9. The open at DraftKings was 49.5, and it has climbed to 51.5. My 32-21 adds up to 53.

At 13.5, all four cards with a side take Akron and nobody takes Ohio, and every card with an over/under pick says Over 51.5. The Formula has Ohio 31-21 and likes Akron Zips +13.5. Next Man Up has Ohio 33-22, takes Akron Zips +13.5, and likes the Over 51.5. In The Trenches likes Akron Zips +13.5, reads sides only and has no score this week. Follow The Money likes Akron Zips +13, with the consensus spread opened at +14, and likes the Over 51.5, since the consensus total opened 50.5 and is 51.5 now.

Next Man Up: "Thirty-three to twenty-two. Eleven points, Zip, and we get 13.5. Save me a taco."

The Formula: "Ten points. Still short of the 13.5."

Penny taps a brick she carried up from Athens for luck. Hollister checks the tread on his sneakers and heads for the taco table before the gates fill.

Ohio Bobcats 32, Akron Zips 21. Akron Zips cover the 13.5. Over 51.5.
