Taco Tuesday in Akron: Ohio Brings the Hot Sauce to the Zips

Ohio Bobcats (6-2) at Akron Zips (1-7)

7:00 PM ET Tue Nov 3 · InfoCision Stadium · ESPN2

XFacebook

Ohio Bobcats vs Akron Zips prediction for Tuesday, November 3: Taco Tuesday at InfoCision Stadium, and Penny Trautman of Athens has brought her own hot sauce. The gates open at 6:00 PM in Akron, daylight saving time just ended, and Hollister "Zip" Grubbs swears the extra hour of sleep has his Zips ready to bounce.

It is Election Day in the Rubber City, so the morning belongs to the polls and the Akron Zoo is open free from 11 to 4. By the time the sun goes down, though, the only vote anybody in the parking lot cares about is the one taken on the field under the lights.

"I voted early, I ate early, and I'm sitting early," Penny says, settling into the visitors' side with a brick wrapped in a green scarf. "You know what this is, Zip? This is a Bobcats team that's 6-2 and 4-0 in the MAC. We drove up from the hills to keep building."

Hollister has a notebook full of tread diagrams. "Penny, I have measured every tire in this lot. The wear pattern says one thing. Akron is 1-7, sure, but we played Eastern Michigan to 23-25. We're not flat. We're just low on air."

"Low on air," Penny says, "is a polite way to say you've lost seven of eight."

"And you lost to South Alabama 36-41," Hollister fires back. "I keep notes. Rubber remembers."

The Bobcats arrive with a defense that has stopped arguing with anybody. Michael Molnar sits at 70 tackles and 5 sacks, and he plays like a man who smelled the tacos and wants the opposing offense to miss dinner. Ohio has given up 21.9 points a game all season. Over the last four weeks, the Bobcats beat Kent State 13-10, Central Michigan 31-14, Sacramento State 48-7 and Eastern Michigan 24-20.

"Every one of those was a brick in the wall," Penny says. "Some bricks are pretty. Some are just heavy."

On offense, Nick Poulos has thrown for 781 yards with 6 touchdowns, and he was sharp in that last game against Eastern Michigan, 19 of 29 for 266 yards. Dom Dorwart caught four for 125 that day, and Riley Neer leads the team with 37 catches for 497 yards. Victor Rosa carries the ground game with 364 yards and 5 touchdowns.

"Rosa runs like Athens streets look," Penny says. "Bumpy, old and impossible to knock over."

Hollister nods slowly, then grins. "Fine. Now let me talk about Jordan Gant."

He has earned the floor. Gant ran 28 times for 162 yards and two touchdowns at Kent State, and he owns 618 yards on 127 carries this fall. When the Zips trust him, they look like a different team.

"Gant is vulcanized," Hollister says. "Heat-treated. Stronger under pressure. That's polymer science, Penny. Look it up."

Brayden Roggow is the bigger question. He has thrown for 1,343 yards and 7 touchdowns, but also 9 interceptions, and the four he threw at Miami (OH) still sting. Marcel Williams has 34 catches for 375 yards, and Kyan Mason went for 129 at Kent State. When Roggow keeps the ball out of trouble, Akron can move it.

"If he hands Molnar a gift, we're opening presents early," Penny says.

"If he hits Mason down the far hash," Hollister says, "you'll be tapping that brick so hard it turns to gravel."

Donelius Johnson anchors the Akron defense with 71 tackles, and Cyrus Durham has 2.5 sacks. They will need every one of them against a Bobcats team scoring 29.6 points a game.

The history is not kind to the home crowd. In nine meetings from 2016 to 2024, Ohio won 8 and Akron won 1, the 37-34 game in 2017. The 2024 meeting ended 30-10 for the Bobcats.

"Rubber remembers 2017," Hollister says, tapping his notebook. "BOING."

"Brick remembers the other eight," Penny says, and she offers him a taco anyway, because that's how folks from the hills do it.

Here is how it shakes out. Ohio's defense keeps Roggow honest, Poulos finds Neer and Dorwart for the big plays, and Rosa keeps the chains moving. Akron does not fold. Gant runs hard all night, the Zips hit a couple of shots, and the home crowd gets something to holler about in the fourth quarter. It just isn't enough to swing it.

Penny leaves with her brick. Hollister leaves with a fresh page of tread math and a promise that the Zips will bounce back. Both leave full of tacos.

My call: Ohio Bobcats 32, Akron Zips 21.
