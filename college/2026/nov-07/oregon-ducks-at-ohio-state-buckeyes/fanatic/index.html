Scarlet the Shoe: Ohio State Turns the Horseshoe Red for Oregon

Oregon Ducks (5-3) at Ohio State Buckeyes (6-2)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Ohio Stadium · CBS

ShareXFacebook

Oregon Ducks vs Ohio State Buckeyes prediction for Saturday, November 7: Ohio State has called this one Scarlet the Shoe, every fan in the Horseshoe is asked to wear scarlet, and Bud Kowalczyk has been laying out sweaters on his tailgate table since dawn.

Ingrid Halvorsen showed up in green. Of course she did.

"You know what today is," Bud said.

"I know what today is," Ingrid said. "It is the day my Ducks come into your house and remind you about USC."

Bud winced. He is a retired steelworker from Youngstown, and he explains football like a mill shift: you show up, you work, you go home tired. He did not enjoy last Saturday. Ohio State lost 18-30 at USC, and Luke Fahey led the Buckeyes in throws that day instead of Julian Sayin. Bud does not want to talk about why. Ingrid did not ask.

"We are 6-2," he said. "We give up 15.5 points a game. We lost by a point at Texas and we lost at USC. That is it."

That is it. Ohio State beat Illinois 42-19, Iowa 31-14, Maryland 45-16 and Indiana 19-15. Sayin has 2,007 yards, 19 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. Jeremiah Smith has 62 catches for 1,067 yards and 13 touchdowns. Bo Jackson has 637 rushing yards. Earl Little Jr. has 43 tackles, and Kenyatta Jackson Jr. has 3 sacks. The Buckeyes score 36.6 points a game.

"Jeremiah Smith," Bud said, "is the best player in America. Write that down."

Ingrid wrote nothing down. She is a software designer from Portland, and she thinks about football like a product launch: the version you ship matters more than the version you planned.

Oregon has shipped a few versions this year. Dante Moore led the Ducks in throws early, including 378 yards against Boise State. Dylan Raiola took over and threw for 289 in a 41-27 win at USC. Brock Thomas led them in throws against Nebraska. Now Raiola is back, and last week he went 22 for 31 for 336 yards and 3 touchdowns in a 34-26 win over Northwestern. Dakorien Moore caught 7 balls for 128 yards and 2 scores in that one.

"We beat USC by 14," Ingrid said. "You lost to USC by 12. Math."

Bud said football does not work that way. Ingrid said it worked fine for her.

Oregon is 5-3, and the losses have been close and frustrating: 31-39 at Oklahoma State, 15-17 to UCLA, 10-21 at Illinois. Jordon Davison has 439 rushing yards and 7 touchdowns. Matayo Uiagalelei has 6.5 sacks, and Koi Perich has 53 tackles. The Ducks give up 21.4 points a game.

Here is how it shakes out. Oregon comes out fast, Uiagalelei gets into the backfield early, and for a few minutes the Horseshoe is very quiet. But Ohio State settles in. Smith makes a catch that nobody else in the country makes, Bo Jackson pounds away in the third quarter, and the Buckeyes' defense keeps Raiola from finding Moore deep. Oregon scores enough to keep Ingrid hopeful into the fourth quarter. Ohio State pulls away late.

Bud will celebrate the way he always does, by hugging people he does not know. Ingrid will say the next meeting is in Eugene, and she will mean it as a warning.

Before kickoff, Bud holds out a scarlet sweater. Ingrid looks at it for a long time. She agrees to wear it for exactly one photo, for Scarlet the Shoe, and then the green comes right back on. Bud frames the photo anyway. He says it will hang in his garage next to his old ticket stubs, the ones he never lets anybody touch. Ingrid says she will send him a green frame for it. Bud says he would sooner wear the sweater to Eugene. She says that can be arranged.

My call: Ohio State Buckeyes 31, Oregon Ducks 21.
