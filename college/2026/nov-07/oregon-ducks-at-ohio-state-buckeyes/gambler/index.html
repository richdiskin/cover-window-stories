Ohio State Lays 7 and Handles Oregon in the Horseshoe

Oregon Ducks (5-3) at Ohio State Buckeyes (6-2)

3:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Ohio Stadium · CBS

ShareXFacebook

Oregon vs Ohio State prediction, spread and over/under for Saturday: I have Ohio State Buckeyes −7 and Over 48.5, with Ohio State Buckeyes 31, Oregon Ducks 21 as my final. Kickoff is 3:30 PM ET at Ohio Stadium, and it is a Scarlet the Shoe game, with every fan asked to wear scarlet.

Ingrid Halvorsen arrived from Eugene in green and yellow. "Dylan Raiola threw for 336 and 3 touchdowns against Northwestern. Matayo Uiagalelei has 6.5 sacks. We beat USC 41-27."

Bud Kowalczyk, Buckeyes to his core, points across the lot. "Ingrid, we give up 15.5 a game. Jeremiah Smith has 1,067 yards and 13 touchdowns. We're 6-2."

Here is why I lay the points. Ohio State's defense allows 15.5 a game, the best in this game, and it held Indiana to 15 and Iowa to 14. Oregon has scored 15 against UCLA, 17 against Nebraska and 10 at Illinois. The Ducks have also had three different quarterbacks lead them in throws: Dante Moore, Raiola and Brock Thomas. My 31-21 is a 10-point Ohio State win, past the 7. DraftKings opened Ohio State −6, and it has moved to 7.

The risk is Ohio State's last game. The Buckeyes lost 18-30 at USC, and Luke Fahey led them in throws that day, not Julian Sayin. I am not going to guess who throws it Saturday. Oregon also beat USC by 14, and it put 34 on Boise State.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 52.5, and it has dropped to 48.5. I think that went a little far. Ohio State scores 36.6 a game, and Oregon scores 33.2. Smith has 13 touchdown catches, and Jordon Davison has 7 rushing touchdowns for Oregon. My 31 and 21 make 52, three and a half over 48.5.

The room lines up with me, except up front. The Formula has Ohio State 32-22 and takes the Buckeyes −7 with the Over. Next Man Up has Ohio State 31-21, takes the Buckeyes −7, and likes the Over. In The Trenches takes Oregon +7, and it reads sides only with no score this week. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "Same score as the column. I'll take the credit."

The Formula: "One point apart on both ends. The Horseshoe usually decides these things."

Bud has a scarlet sweater ready for Ingrid, for the photo only. She says she will wear it for exactly one picture, and then the green comes back out.

Ohio State Buckeyes 31, Oregon Ducks 21. Ohio State Buckeyes cover the 7. Over 48.5.
