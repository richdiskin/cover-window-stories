Sam Houston Bearkats at Jacksonville State Gamecocks

Sam Houston Bearkats (1-7) at Jacksonville State Gamecocks (6-2)

7:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · AmFirst Stadium · CBSSN

ShareXFacebook

Sam Houston vs Jacksonville State prediction for Homecoming in Jacksonville, Alabama: the theme on campus is Cocky Reigns Supreme, the parade comes first, and then the Gamecocks take Burgess-Snow Field at AmFirst Stadium against Sam Houston at 7:00 PM ET.

Jax State calls itself The Friendliest Campus in the South, and Homecoming is when it tries hardest to prove it. There are tailgates and reunions all day, including the Golden Gamecocks Reunion for the old grads. During the game, the school will recognize its 2026 Alumni of the Year winners along with the Homecoming Queen, King and Court. Cocky, the mascot, gets top billing for once.

I brought in two people who know their regions.

Lem Coxwell teaches history in Walker County, Texas, and will tell you a tall tale about Sam Houston the man before he will tell you anything about Sam Houston the football team. Burl Ashcraft-Neeley cooks barbecue in Calhoun County, Alabama, and has opinions about pork shoulder that he shares freely.

"I'll be honest with you," Lem said. "It has been a long fall in Huntsville."

It has. The Bearkats are 1-7. They score 18.0 points a game and allow 25.1. The only win was 59-0 over Nicholls in September. But it hasn't been all blowouts. They lost 21-24 at Troy to open the year, 17-23 to Tulsa and 24-30 to New Mexico State.

"Then the offense went cold," Lem said. "Three points at Liberty. Three against Western Kentucky. Three at Missouri State. I have told some tall tales in my classroom, and none of them were as hard to explain as that."

Landyn Locke leads the throwing with 1,653 yards and 12 touchdowns, though DJ Bailey took the most throws at Missouri State and went 20 of 35 for 258. Tim Burns Jr. leads the receivers with 392 yards, and Alton McCaskill leads the ground game with 232. The defense has been the steady part. Dean Ford has 47 tackles and Josh Alexander-Felton has 4 sacks.

Burl had a rib in one hand and a list in the other. "We're 6-2. We give up 19.9 a game. And we just went to Las Cruces and shut out New Mexico State, 24-0."

Jacksonville State scores 26.9 a game and wins tight. After a 7-33 loss at North Dakota State and a 49-7 win over Eastern Kentucky, the Gamecocks lost 27-29 in overtime at Ohio. Then they won five straight, by scores of 31-27, 23-13, 27-26, 27-24 and 24-0.

"Caden Creel," Burl said. "That's the show. He has 1,741 throwing yards and 713 rushing. He's like a good smoker. Steady heat."

Deondre Johnson leads the receivers at 438 yards, and Caleb Nix leads the defense with 54 tackles.

Lem pointed out the history. "We beat them 42-7 in 2021."

"And we won the last three," Burl said. "35-28, 21-11 and 29-27."

"All close."

"All ours."

That back-and-forth is the game in a nutshell. Jax State wins, but it rarely runs away from anybody. Sam Houston's defense is good enough to keep things from getting silly, but the Bearkats have to find some points from somewhere.

I asked Burl about Homecoming. He said the parade goes through campus, the families set up early, and the smell of smoke hangs over the whole place by noon. Lem said he has never been, but he figures any town that names its stadium field after two people instead of one has a story worth hearing.

Here is how I see it. Jacksonville State on Homecoming, with Creel running and throwing, should control this from the second quarter on. The Gamecocks' defense is coming off a shutout, and the Bearkats have scored 3 points in three of their last four. But this Jax State team has not blown many people out, and Sam Houston's defense will make it work for every score. Creel gets his yards, the Gamecocks pull clear in the second half, and the Bearkats get a couple of late scores to make it look respectable.

Lem says he will have a new tall tale either way. Burl says the shoulder will be ready at halftime.

My call: Jacksonville State Gamecocks 30, Sam Houston Bearkats 15.
