Sam Houston at Jacksonville State: Jacksonville Has Caden Creel, and That Is Plenty to Start With

Sam Houston Bearkats (1-7) at Jacksonville State Gamecocks (6-2)

7:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · AmFirst Stadium · CBSSN

XFacebook

Sam Houston at Jacksonville State: JXST -16.5; total 45.5 (ESPN/DraftKings).

Jacksonville, Alabama, gets a quarterback who just ran for 211 yards. Caden Creel did that in Jacksonville State's 24-0 win over New Mexico State, per ESPN's AP preview. Sam Houston has the next opportunity to figure out what the last defense could not.

Last year's meeting ended on a 52-yard field goal by Garrison Rippa as time expired, a 29-27 Jax State win that left Sam Houston seconds short, per Sam Houston Athletics. Now it is Homecoming in Jacksonville, and linebacker Morven Joseph is back after missing all of last season. “It's just been a blessing,” he said, per the Calhoun Journal, adding that the hardest part was not being able to play alongside his teammates.

The Gamecocks are 6-2, the Bearkats 1-7 after losing 24-3 to Missouri State. Creel also threw two interceptions in that shutout win. The useful part of a good result is knowing it still contains things to correct.

Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and its total is pending too.

Sam Houston's D.J. Bailey threw for 258 yards without an interception against Missouri State, according to the same report. Watch whether those passing yards become sustained possessions rather than a long catalog of frustration. Jacksonville State can keep building a good season; the visitors can make one night feel better than the record. Creel's next open running lane will not ask which storyline you preferred.

The published Cover Writer card stays Sam Houston Bearkats +16.5 and Under 48.5. My final is Jacksonville State Gamecocks 30, Sam Houston Bearkats 15. These are the published selections at their original numbers, not replacement bets at the current market.

That 3 is the whole Under. Sam Houston has scored 3 points in three of its last four games, at Liberty, against Western Kentucky and at Missouri State, and it scores 18.0 a game for the year. Jacksonville State allows 19.9 a game and just pitched a shutout. The Gamecocks are not a run-up-the-score outfit either. Their wins came 31-27, 23-13, 27-26, 27-24 and 24-0 after the 49-7 opener against Eastern Kentucky.

That is also why I want the points. Jacksonville State has won close games all fall, and 16.5 asks it to win by three scores against a defense that has held Tulsa to 23, Western Kentucky to 16 and Missouri State to 24. Dean Ford has 47 tackles and Josh Alexander-Felton has 4 sacks. The recent meetings went 35-28, 21-11 and 29-27, all one or two scores.

The risk is that Sam Houston's offense is simply this broken. DJ Bailey took the most throws at Missouri State and went 20 of 35 for 258 without a touchdown. If that offense cannot finish drives, the Gamecocks can get to 30 and keep the Bearkats in single digits.

Jacksonville State Gamecocks 30, Sam Houston Bearkats 15. Sam Houston Bearkats cover the 16.5. Under 48.5.
