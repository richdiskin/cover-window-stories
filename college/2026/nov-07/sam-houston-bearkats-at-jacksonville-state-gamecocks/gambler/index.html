Sam Houston Takes 16.5 at Jacksonville State's Homecoming

Sam Houston Bearkats (1-7) at Jacksonville State Gamecocks (6-2)

7:00 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · AmFirst Stadium · CBSSN

ShareXFacebook

Sam Houston vs Jacksonville State prediction, pick against the spread and over/under for Homecoming at AmFirst Stadium: Jacksonville State is calling this weekend Cocky Reigns Supreme, with the parade rolling before a 7:00 PM ET kickoff, and the Gamecocks are laying 16.5 to a 1-7 Sam Houston team that has had trouble scoring at all. My side is Sam Houston Bearkats +16.5, my call on points is Under 48.5, and my score is Jacksonville State Gamecocks 30, Sam Houston Bearkats 15.

Lem Coxwell teaches history in Walker County and can tell you a Texas tall tale for every quarter. "We're 1-7, sure. But we lost 21-24 at Troy, 17-23 to Tulsa and 24-30 to New Mexico State. Landyn Locke has 1,653 yards and 12 touchdowns. And we beat these folks 42-7 in 2021."

Burl Ashcraft-Neeley runs a pit in Calhoun County and smokes a pork shoulder low and slow, which is how he likes his football too. "We're 6-2 and we went out to New Mexico State and won 24-0. Caden Creel has 1,741 throwing yards and 713 on the ground. Homecoming crowd. Pig on the smoker. Y'all scored 3 at Missouri State."

That 3 is the whole Under. Sam Houston has scored 3 points in three of its last four games, at Liberty, against Western Kentucky and at Missouri State, and it scores 18.0 a game for the year. Jacksonville State allows 19.9 a game and just pitched a shutout. The Gamecocks are not a run-up-the-score outfit either. Their wins came 31-27, 23-13, 27-26, 27-24 and 24-0 after the 49-7 opener against Eastern Kentucky.

That is also why I want the points. Jacksonville State has won close games all fall, and 16.5 asks it to win by three scores against a defense that has held Tulsa to 23, Western Kentucky to 16 and Missouri State to 24. Dean Ford has 47 tackles and Josh Alexander-Felton has 4 sacks. The recent meetings went 35-28, 21-11 and 29-27, all one or two scores.

The risk is that Sam Houston's offense is simply this broken. DJ Bailey took the most throws at Missouri State and went 20 of 35 for 258 without a touchdown. If that offense cannot finish drives, the Gamecocks can get to 30 and keep the Bearkats in single digits.

The room splits on the side and agrees on points. The Formula has Jacksonville State 29-16, takes Sam Houston +16.5 and likes the Under. Next Man Up has Jacksonville State 31-14, sides with the Gamecocks −16.5 and likes the Under. In The Trenches takes Jacksonville State −16.5 and likes it, and it reads sides only with no score this week. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Lem says the Bearkats have pulled off taller tales than this one. Burl says the shoulder comes off the smoker at halftime either way.

Jacksonville State Gamecocks 30, Sam Houston Bearkats 15. Sam Houston Bearkats cover the 16.5. Under 48.5.
