Rutgers Takes 17.5 at Camp Randall as Wisconsin Lays a Big Number

Rutgers Scarlet Knights (2-6) at Wisconsin Badgers (5-3)

4:15 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Camp Randall Stadium · BTN

ShareXFacebook

Rutgers vs Wisconsin prediction, pick against the spread and over/under for Heroes Day at Camp Randall: Wisconsin opens its doors to veterans at 4:15 PM ET in Madison, and the Badgers are laying a big number to a Rutgers team that just played Michigan to a touchdown. My side is Rutgers Scarlet Knights +17.5, my call on points is Over 45.5, and my score is Wisconsin Badgers 33, Rutgers Scarlet Knights 18.

Vinny Scolari drove in from Exit 9 and grades trenches the way he grades boardwalk arm-wrestlers, by who blinks first. "Seventeen and a half? We just lost 21-28 to Michigan. We won 37-30 at Maryland. KJ Duff has 774 receiving yards and 9 touchdowns. Nobody blinks first here."

Lars 'Brat' Ellefson played fullback for the Badgers and still wears the cheese wedge to every home game. "Wisconsin gives up 17.1 a game. We went to Happy Valley and won 24-20. We beat USC 24-7. And we've won the last four of these, 31-17, 52-3, 24-13 and 42-7."

Both of them have a point, and that is why I want the points. Wisconsin's offense has been a sometime thing. The Badgers were shut out 0-30 at Iowa and scored 7 at UCLA, and Colton Joseph has thrown for 66, 58 and 90 yards in three of his last four games. He leads the team in both throwing yards at 1,346 and rushing at 371. That is a defense-first team that wins 24-20 and 24-7, not one that wins by three scores every week.

Rutgers has warts. The Scarlet Knights give up 32.6 a game and lost 15-47 to Indiana and 14-42 at Northwestern. But they score 27.8, Antwan Raymond has 733 rushing yards, and Duff can flip a game on one catch. Against Michigan, AJ Surace hit only 3 of 12 throws and they still had a chance late.

On points, my 33 and 18 add to 51, past 45.5. Wisconsin's scoring defense is the reason the Over is not easy, but Rutgers has allowed 28 or more in seven of its eight games, and the Badgers have hit 31 or more three times.

The room is in agreement on the side. The Formula has Wisconsin 32-19, takes Rutgers +17.5 and likes the Over. Next Man Up has Wisconsin 34-17, takes Rutgers +17.5 and likes the Over. In The Trenches takes Rutgers +17.5 and likes it, and it reads sides only with no score this week. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Vinny says Jersey guys do not need three touchdowns of cushion, just three hours of stubborn. Brat says the cheese wedge has seen worse.

Wisconsin Badgers 33, Rutgers Scarlet Knights 18. Rutgers Scarlet Knights cover the 17.5. Over 45.5.
