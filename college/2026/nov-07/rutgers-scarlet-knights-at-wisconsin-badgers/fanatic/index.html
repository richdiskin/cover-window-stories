Heroes Day at Camp Randall: Rutgers Walks Into Wisconsin's House

Rutgers Scarlet Knights (2-6) at Wisconsin Badgers (5-3)

4:15 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Camp Randall Stadium · BTN

ShareXFacebook

Rutgers vs Wisconsin prediction for Heroes Day at Camp Randall: Saturday at 4:15 PM ET, Madison turns its home game into a salute to veterans, and Rutgers walks into the middle of it carrying a long trip and a short memory of last week.

Camp Randall carries that weight well. There is a free Salute to Veterans Tailgate at Camp Randall Memorial Park, with replica gear on loan from the Wisconsin Veterans Museum, and the new Camp Randall Veterans Memorial Plaza is open for people to walk through before kickoff.

I sat down with two guys who could not be more different if they tried.

Vinny Scolari is from off Exit 9 of the Turnpike. He grades blockers the way he grades arm-wrestling on the boardwalk, by watching who blinks. Lars Ellefson, who everybody calls Brat, played fullback for the Badgers and owns a foam cheese wedge older than some of the current roster.

"Heroes Day is a good day," Vinny said. "My uncle did twenty years. I'll be at the tailgate looking at the old helmets. Then I'll be in the stands looking at us get no respect."

Brat laughed. "You get respect. You just don't get wins in Madison. Four meetings, four Wisconsin wins. 31-17, 52-3, 24-13, 42-7."

"Fifty-two to three was 2021. Different guys."

That is true. This Rutgers team is 2-6, and it is a strange 2-6. The Scarlet Knights score 27.8 points a game and allow 32.6. They lost 21-37 to UMass, which hurts to type, and 21-28 at Boston College. Then they gave USC everything in a 35-42 loss, beat Howard 58-7, got run over 15-47 by Indiana, won 37-30 at Maryland, lost 14-42 at Northwestern and then played Michigan close in a 21-28 loss last Saturday.

"The Michigan game is the one," Vinny said. "AJ Surace went 3 for 12 and we still almost had it. Jourdin Houston caught one ball and took it 80 yards for a score. That's Jersey. One shot."

Dylan Lonergan leads the team with 1,066 throwing yards and 10 touchdowns, and Surace has taken the most throws in three of the last four games. Antwan Raymond is the workhorse with 733 rushing yards and 6 scores, and he ran for 85 against Michigan. KJ Duff is the guy you build the night around, with 774 receiving yards and 9 touchdowns. Sekou Kromah has 3 sacks and Chris Joines leads with 41 tackles.

Brat had his own list. "Wisconsin is 5-3. We give up 17.1 a game. We won 24-20 at Penn State. We beat USC 24-7. We beat Michigan State 31-3."

"And Iowa?" Vinny asked.

Brat looked at the cheese wedge for a while. "Zero to thirty. We don't discuss it."

The Badgers score 23.6 a game, which tells you everything about how they win. Colton Joseph is the whole offense. He has 1,346 throwing yards and 11 touchdowns, and he is also the leading rusher at 371 yards. Tyrell Henry is the top receiver at 397. The defense is the real show, with Cooper Catalano at 75 tackles and Sebastian Cheeks at 6 sacks.

"Here's my problem," Vinny said. "Joseph threw for 66 at UCLA and 58 against USC and 90 at Iowa. You can't throw it? We'll put nine guys in the box and make you."

"We beat USC throwing for 58," Brat said. "That's the Wisconsin way. Run it, hit you, run it again."

I asked them about the veterans plaza. Both of them got quiet. Brat said he walked through it on a weeknight and stayed longer than he planned. Vinny said his uncle would have liked the replica gear more than the football.

So here is how I see the game. Wisconsin's defense should win the day. Rutgers has allowed 28 or more in seven of eight games, and the Badgers at home on a big emotional Saturday will be ready. But the Wisconsin offense does not blow anyone out unless the other side hands it over. Raymond will move the chains some, Duff will get a couple of shots, and Rutgers will score enough to make the fourth quarter mean something.

Vinny promised to stay for the whole thing. Brat promised to lend him a cheese wedge if it gets cold.

My call: Wisconsin Badgers 33, Rutgers Scarlet Knights 18.
