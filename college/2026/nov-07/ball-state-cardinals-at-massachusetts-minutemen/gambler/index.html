UMass Lays 2.5 to Ball State on a Wednesday Night in Amherst

Ball State Cardinals (3-5) at Massachusetts Minutemen (4-4)

Ball State vs UMass prediction, pick against the spread and over/under for Wednesday night in Amherst: I'm on Massachusetts Minutemen −2.5 and Over 48.5, with a final of Massachusetts Minutemen 31, Ball State Cardinals 22. McGuirk Alumni Stadium opens its MAC slate on a Wednesday night built around Salute to Service and Faculty & Staff Appreciation, so the bleachers will be full of professors grading film instead of midterms.

Abner Kettleworth, the Pioneer Valley farmer who grinds tape between harvests, has the case for the hosts. "We dropped four in a row, 14-38 to Eastern Michigan, 19-20 to Miami (OH), 18-25 in overtime at Buffalo and 16-35 at UConn. Fine. But we still score 26.5 a game and allow 21.1. Ball State gives up 35.2. You don't need a roll call to see who brought more men."

Gus Ponder drove in from Muncie with his punt chart rolled up like a diploma. "We won two straight, Abner. Bowling Green 32-31 in overtime, then Sacramento State 34-17. TJ Horton has 425 yards and 9 touchdowns on the ground. Garfield hates Mondays. Good thing this is a Wednesday."

The side begins with the quarterbacks. William Watson III has thrown 8 touchdowns against 2 interceptions. Keldric Luster has 9 touchdowns but 6 interceptions, and the hosts bring a front with William DePaepe at 3 sacks and Timmy Hinspeter running down 72 tackles. Both teams played Sacramento State. UMass won 35-6 on the road. Ball State won 34-17 at home. That gap is the margin I want, and my final has UMass by 9, clear of the 2.5.

On the over/under, Ball State's defense is the reason. The Cardinals surrendered 56 to Ohio State, 51 to Liberty and 45 to Northwestern, and even the Kent State and Toledo nights went 26 and 39 against them. DraftKings opened 48.5, moved to 49.5 and is back at 48.5. My 31-22 lands at 53.

Most of the room is with me on the side: The Formula and Next Man Up back UMass −2.5, and In The Trenches takes Ball State +2.5. The Formula has Massachusetts 30-23 and likes both the Minutemen −2.5 and the Over. Next Man Up has Massachusetts 31-21 and likes the same two. In The Trenches reads sides only, has no score this week and likes Ball State Cardinals +2.5, so the ground game has Gus smiling. Follow The Money has no side pick and takes the Under 48.5. The consensus spread opened Massachusetts −3 and is −2.5 now, and the total opened 49.5 and is 48.5 now. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Next Man Up: "Thirty-one to twenty-one, Trenches. Bring a sweater to Amherst."

In The Trenches: "Bring a punt chart. Gus did."

Abner rings a tiny colonial bell as the veterans are honored before kickoff. Gus unrolls the chart, finds the Garfield doodle in the corner and grins anyway.

Massachusetts Minutemen 31, Ball State Cardinals 22. Massachusetts Minutemen cover the 2.5. Over 48.5.
