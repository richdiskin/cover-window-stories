West Virginia at Texas Tech: Lubbock Has Two Ground Games and No Reason to Go to Bed Early

West Virginia Mountaineers (5-3) at Texas Tech Red Raiders (7-1)

9:30 PM ET Sat Nov 7 · Galaxy Stadium · ESPN

XFacebook

Lubbock, do not let the passing-game conversation hide the running backs. Texas Tech's J'Koby Williams ran for 175 yards on 14 carries in a 34-26 win over Arizona. West Virginia's Cam Cook has 923 rushing yards for the season. ESPN's AP preview puts those names at the center of a very useful late-night argument.

Texas Tech has won 13 straight at home in Lubbock, per WVNews, which is the kind of house West Virginia has to walk into late at night.

Tech is 7-1 and West Virginia 5-3. The Mountaineers' latest game was a 24-20 win over TCU, with Michael Hawkins Jr. running for two touchdowns. Tech's Thomas Castellanos ran for 117 and a score against Arizona. Those are last-game performances, not unsupported confirmations of today's starting quarterbacks.

The Mountaineers produce 261.6 rushing yards; Tech allows 94.9 in the ESPN report. That is the confrontation to keep an eye on when the rest of the country starts looking for the remote's power button. One team wants to keep moving the pile, the other has made a habit of preventing it. Lubbock gets the final word on how loud that disagreement becomes.

My call: Texas Tech Red Raiders 41, West Virginia Mountaineers 24.
