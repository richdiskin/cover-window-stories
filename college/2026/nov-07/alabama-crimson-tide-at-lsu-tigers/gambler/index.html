Alabama Gets 5.5 in Death Valley, and the Tide Makes LSU Sweat

Alabama Crimson Tide (6-2) at LSU Tigers (6-2)

Alabama vs LSU prediction, spread and over/under for Saturday: I have Alabama Crimson Tide +5.5 and Over 52.5, with LSU Tigers 31, Alabama Crimson Tide 27 as my final. Kickoff is 3:30 PM ET at Tiger Stadium, and Mike's Kids Club is hosting a tailgate in the Fan Zone outside the PMAC, with food, games and STEM activities for the young ones.

Hollis Dunaway says "Roll Tide" the way some people say grace. "Alabama has won 8 of the last 10, Remy-Jean. Keelon Russell has 15 touchdown throws and 5 interceptions. We beat you 20-9 last year."

Remy-Jean Fontenot stirs a pot of jambalaya and smiles. "We give up 16.0 a game, Hollis. Princewill Umanmielen has 8.5 sacks. And Sam Leavitt threw for 388 at Auburn."

Here is why I take the points. Alabama scores 38.2 a game and has only lost to Georgia, 17-20, and at Tennessee, 10-26. Russell threw for 311 yards against Texas A&M and 365 against South Carolina. LSU lost 24-32 at Ole Miss and 34-35 in overtime at Kentucky, so the Tigers are beatable. My 31-27 is a 4-point LSU win, inside the 5.5. DraftKings opened LSU −4, and it has moved to 5.5, which gives me an extra point and a half.

The risk is pressure up front. Umanmielen has 8.5 sacks, and LSU's defense allowed 0 to Mississippi State and 6 to Texas A&M. Alabama scored 10 at Tennessee. If Russell is under pressure all afternoon, this can get away.

On the over/under, DraftKings opened at 54.5 and it is down to 52.5. LSU scores 40.1 a game, and Alabama scores 38.2. Leavitt has 17 touchdown throws, and Jackson Harris caught 9 balls for 178 yards at Auburn. My 31 and 27 make 58, five and a half over 52.5.

The room agrees across the board. The Formula has LSU 30-28 and takes Alabama +5.5 with the Over. Next Man Up has LSU 32-27, takes the Tide +5.5, and likes the Over. In The Trenches sees a split up front, so no side, and it reads sides only with no score this week. Follow The Money is still waiting on a consensus spread, so its side is pending, and it has no total pick. Letdown Watch has no score this week.

Remy-Jean is taking her nieces to the Mike's Kids Club tailgate before kickoff. Hollis says he will tag along and keep his Tide hat in his pocket until they get inside.

LSU Tigers 31, Alabama Crimson Tide 27. Alabama Crimson Tide cover the 5.5. Over 52.5.
