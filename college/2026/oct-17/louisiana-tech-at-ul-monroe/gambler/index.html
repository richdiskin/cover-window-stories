Louisiana Tech at UL Monroe

Louisiana Tech (3-2) at UL Monroe (0-5)

8:00 PM ET Sat Oct 17 · Malone Stadium, Monroe, LA · ESPN+

An in-state matchup brings Louisiana Tech (3-2, 1-0 Sun Belt) to Malone Stadium to face UL Monroe (0-5, 0-1) at 8 p.m. on ESPN+.

ULM insider Darnell Vidrine resets his optimism counter to 100 every week. He's sure the Warhawks are one play from greatness, citing Aidan Armenta's one interception all season and an open week to prepare. Louisiana Tech insider Booker "Champ" Tullos can't resist a peach pun. He's riding high after last week's 32-31 win over Louisiana, with Blake Baker starting and Jalen Mickens leading the receivers.

Trey Kukuk is questionable for Louisiana Tech. ESPN rates ULM's defense last in the country, and that's a big opportunity for Baker. Austin Carlisle and receiver Marcellus Jackson give the Warhawks some options. Mild weather sets the stage for an offensive game, and Louisiana Tech has bragging rights in the state to protect.

Our call: Louisiana Tech 38, UL Monroe 21.
