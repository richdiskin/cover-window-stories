Late-Night Liftoff: No. 14 Houston Brings a Four-Game Winning Run to Unbeaten No. 10 Utah

Houston (6-1) at Utah (6-0)

10:15 PM ET Sat Oct 24 · Rice-Eccles Stadium · ESPN

No. 14 Houston (6-1) at No. 10 Utah (6-0). Saturday, Oct. 24, 10:15 p.m. ET (8:15 p.m. Mountain), Rice-Eccles Stadium, Salt Lake City, ESPN.

Your hosts are Yolanda "Yo" Pacheco, a Houston fan who breaks down every game like a launch countdown, and Aspen Mortensen, a Utah fan and skier who reads defenses like slalom gates.

Yo: Houston, we have liftoff, Aspen. This is the biggest test of our season.

Aspen: Send it, no fear, Yo. Just know our gates are tight, and the mountain's steep at night.

Players to watch

Houston quarterback Conner Weigman has completed 134 of 198 passes for 1,771 yards, 20 touchdowns and two interceptions. Receiver Amare Thomas has 36 catches for 487 yards and six touchdowns. Running back Makhi Hughes has 464 yards. Kentrell Webb leads with 43 tackles, and Javion White has 2.5 sacks.

Utah quarterback Devon Dampier has 1,412 yards, 15 touchdowns and four interceptions. Running back Wayshawn Parker has 597 yards and seven touchdowns. Receiver Braden Pegan has 32 catches for 422 yards and six touchdowns. Trey Reynolds leads with 35 tackles.

Yo: Twenty touchdowns, two interceptions. That's a clean launch every time.

What happened last week

Houston beat No. 18 Oklahoma State 35-32, its fourth straight win and first over a ranked team this season. Weigman went 24 of 33 for 343 yards with five touchdown passes. Houston gained 505 yards. Thomas caught seven passes for 126 yards and two scores. Willie Fritz said of Weigman, "He just keeps getting a little bit better every single week".

Utah won 27-24 at Colorado. Dampier threw for 278 yards and three touchdowns, with two interceptions, and Pegan caught two touchdowns. "It's not always pretty, but we did enough," Morgan Scalley said.

Aspen: Not pretty, but we cleared the last gate. Six for six, and the longest active winning run in the country, Yo.

Yo: Four straight for us, and the last one came against a ranked team. The countdown is just getting started, Aspen.

Injuries and availability

The official Big 12 availability report for this game hasn't been published yet. No injury news has surfaced for either team.

What the coaches are up to

Morgan Scalley is in his first season as Utah's head coach. He was a close second in midseason Big 12 coach of the year voting. Utah's 6-0 start is its first since 2015. The Utes' 12-game winning run is the longest active one in FBS.

Willie Fritz is in his third season at Houston, hired Dec. 3, 2023. Houston's run defense allows 82.6 yards a game, and Utah's defense allows 14.8 points a game.

Player storylines

Utah has scored a touchdown on its opening drive in every game and has outscored opponents 75-3 in the first quarter. Houston needs a fast start of its own. Third downs are the gate to watch, and both teams like to run the ball behind stout fronts. Houston has converted 54 of 88, and Utah's defense has allowed only 25 of 81.

What's happening on campus

It's Utah's Homecoming game, under the lights at Rice-Eccles Stadium. This is the sixth meeting and the second as Big 12 members.

Anything else fun and true

Houston leads the series 5-0 and won the last meeting 17-14 on Oct. 26, 2024. Utah has never beaten Houston.

Aspen: Five and oh, Yo. Every ski run has a bottom, and we've been waiting at the lift for a long time.

Yo: Mission control says keep the countdown going, Aspen.
