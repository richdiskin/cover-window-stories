Michigan State at UCLA

Michigan State (2-5) at UCLA (6-0)

3:30 PM ET Sat Oct 24 · Rose Bowl · CBS

UCLA is 6-0 under a first-year coach, and it just won with a backup quarterback making his first start. Kenzie Rhee would greenlight that script. Shay Kowalczyk caught softball in East Lansing and says the Spartans just need one more strike.

Michigan State (2-5) at No. 9 UCLA (6-0), Saturday at 3:30 p.m. ET on CBS at the Rose Bowl. It's a Big Ten game and Homecoming in Pasadena, with UCLA honoring its 1966 Rose Bowl team, the one that beat Michigan State. DraftKings has UCLA Bruins −14 with a total of 46.5. My card: UCLA Bruins −14, Over 46.5, and a final of UCLA Bruins 32, Michigan State Spartans 17.

Kenzie speaks for UCLA, a Westwood film-school producer who storyboards every drive. "Wayne Knight has 11 touchdowns and a rushing score in all six games, darling. That's a franchise player. And our third-down defense is third-best in FBS."

Shay speaks for Michigan State, a catcher who frames every defensive call like a pitch sequence. "We led Northwestern 21-13 in the fourth and lost on a field goal with two seconds left. Before that, we lost by one in overtime to Illinois. Framed it. One more strike."

Why UCLA by more than two touchdowns? The Bruins take the ball away and then run it at you. UCLA is plus-10 in turnover margin, fifth in FBS, and runs for 231 yards a game. Michigan State's red-zone defense ranks 135th, so when the Bruins get close, they score. My final has UCLA by 15, a point past the number.

Nico Iamaleava missed the Wisconsin game, and UCLA hasn't announced his status, but Ty Dieffenbach went 22 of 33 for 264 yards in a 17-7 win.

The caveat is Shay's quote. The Spartans lose close, and Michigan State gives up just 36.3 penalty yards a game, seventh in FBS. UCLA gives up 83.2, 133rd. Flags are how a big favorite lets a backdoor cover happen.

Why the Over? Knight against a Michigan State run defense ranked 67th, plus a red-zone defense that doesn't stop anybody, means UCLA gets its points. Alessio Milivojevic has seven touchdown passes and will find room against a UCLA pass defense ranked 47th. My 32-17 adds up to 49, two and a half over 46.5.

Now the room. The Formula has UCLA 30-20 and likes Michigan State and the Over. Next Man Up has UCLA 33-15 and likes the Bruins. Letdown Watch has UCLA 32-17, my exact final, and the Over. In The Trenches likes UCLA too. Follow The Money skips the side and takes the Under.

Next Man Up opened: "Thirty-three to 15. The Bruins are unbeaten, at home, on Homecoming."

The Formula answered: "A team with 83.2 penalty yards a game laying two touchdowns. I'm watching the flags."

Letdown Watch added: "Thirty-two to 17. That clears the 14. I'll take the Bruins and my chances."

Follow The Money: "And I'll take the Under, because Dieffenbach's first start finished 17-7. Some scripts are short films."

Bottom of the card: an unbeaten home team that takes the ball away and finishes in the red zone, against a road defense that can't stop anybody near the goal line. Shay wants a new scene. Kenzie already wrote the ending. I'll lay the 14.

UCLA Bruins 32, Michigan State Spartans 17. UCLA Bruins cover the 14. Over 46.5.
