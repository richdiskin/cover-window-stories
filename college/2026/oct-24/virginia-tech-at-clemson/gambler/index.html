DraftKings has Clemson Tigers −2.5 with a total of 49.5. My card: Clemson Tigers −2.5, Over 49.5, and a final of Clemson Tigers 27, Virginia Tech Hokies 24.

Why the Over? Grunkemeyer and Brown can hit big plays, and Hauser turns stalled drives into points from anywhere. My 27-24 adds up to 51, a point and a half over 49.5.
