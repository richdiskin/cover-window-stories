What the coaches are up to

Brent Key was hired at Georgia Tech on Nov. 29, 2022. The win over No. 23 Duke on Oct. 10 made the Jackets 8-1 against ranked ACC opponents under Key. His defense is the project: it allows 179.0 rushing yards per game, 110th in FBS.

Bill O'Brien became Boston College's 37th head coach on Feb. 9, 2024. He went 15-9 at Penn State and won four AFC South titles with the Houston Texans. At Alabama, he coached Heisman winner Bryce Young. O'Brien also has Georgia Tech history, so this is a homecoming of sorts for him, too.

What's happening on campus

It's Homecoming on The Flats. Georgia Tech celebrated the 65th anniversary of the Ramblin' Reck at the Duke game. Georgia Tech has drawn nine straight crowds of more than 45,000 at Bobby Dodd, its longest such run since 2014-16. It also has six straight crowds of 50,000 or more, the longest since 1966-67. Volleyball makes it a big weekend too: Georgia Tech hosts No. 2 Pitt on Friday night and No. 5 SMU on Sunday at McCamish Pavilion.
