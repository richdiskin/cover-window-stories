Saturday Night in Austin: No. 7 Ole Miss's Perfect Red Zone Meets No. 19 Texas's Wall

Ole Miss (5-1) at Texas (4-2)

7:30 PM ET Sat Oct 24 · DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium · ABC

No. 7 Ole Miss (5-1, 3-1 SEC) at No. 19 Texas (4-2, 1-2 SEC). Saturday, Oct. 24, 7:30 p.m. ET (6:30 p.m. Central), DKR-Texas Memorial Stadium, Austin, ABC.

Your hosts are Langston Fairbanks, an Ole Miss fan in seersucker who reads Faulkner and Rebel game film in the same sitting, and Caro Whitfield, a Texas fan, former rodeo queen and ex-consultant who values players like assets.

Langston: Madam, you price your players like assets? I prefer to read them like poetry. Let us meet in the fourth quarter.

Caro: Poetry is lovely, darling. I prefer a balance sheet. See you in the fourth quarter.

Players to watch

Ole Miss quarterback Trinidad Chambliss has completed 142 of 210 passes for 1,676 yards, 14 touchdowns and four interceptions. Receiver Deuce Alexander has 35 catches for 523 yards and five touchdowns. Running back Kewan Lacy has 352 yards and five touchdowns. Linebacker Luke Ferrelli leads with 39 tackles, and Blake Purchase has 3.5 sacks.

Texas quarterback Arch Manning has nearly 1,300 yards, 11 touchdowns and five interceptions. Defensive end Colin Simmons has 10.5 sacks. Linebacker Rasheem Biles leads with 48 tackles and has two interceptions. Receiver Ryan Wingo has 26 catches for 345 yards. Running back Daylan "Hollywood" Smothers has 289 yards.

Caro: Ten and a half sacks. Darling, that is a premium asset. Right now he's an asset under repair, and I'm praying for a quick recovery.

What happened last week

Ole Miss beat No. 13 Missouri 31-3 on Homecoming. Chambliss threw three touchdowns and ran for another. Lacy ran 16 times for 122 yards, and Makhi Frazier added 101 yards. Coach Pete Golding said of Chambliss, "He wasn't perfect either, but I'm glad he's on our side".

Texas lost 27-24 to Florida, its second straight three-point loss. Manning went 14 of 20 for 173 yards and a touchdown.

Langston: Thirty-one to three. A short story, beautifully told.

Injuries and availability

Wednesday's first SEC availability report lists Texas defensive end Colin Simmons as doubtful after he hurt his ankle against Florida. Backup end Richard Wesley is out, and running back Hollywood Smothers, receiver Kaliq Lockett and lineman Jaydon Chatman are questionable. For Ole Miss, safeties Edwin Joseph and MJ Preston are among seven players out, linemen Terez Davis and Brycen Sanders and cornerback Patrick Broomfield are questionable, and Kewan Lacy is probable. Updated reports come Thursday and Friday evenings, with a final one 90 minutes before kickoff.

What the coaches are up to

Pete Golding is in his first full season at Ole Miss, hired Nov. 30, 2025. His offense has scored on every red-zone trip, first in FBS.

Steve Sarkisian has led Texas since 2021. His defense allows 272.5 yards a game, 12th in FBS.

Player storylines

Chambliss against Texas's 12th-ranked pass defense is the central conflict, and it gets harder for Texas if Simmons can't go. Manning should find room against an Ole Miss defense allowing 390 yards a game, which ranks 108th. The Rebels' third-down defense ranks 118th. Ole Miss scores fast, holding the ball only 26:59 a game.

What's happening on campus

It's an SEC night game on ABC in the middle of a three-game Texas homestand: Florida, Ole Miss, then Mississippi State on Oct. 31. The kickoff forecast is 85 degrees and partly cloudy. Langston calls it seersucker weather.

Anything else fun and true

This is the ninth meeting between the schools and their first as SEC members. Texas leads the series 6-2. Ole Miss is making its first trip to Austin since 2013. Texas's wins this season include a 24-23 victory over Ohio State. Ole Miss still has No. 1 Georgia ahead, and four of its last five games are at home.

Caro: Six and two, darling. That's a sturdy portfolio, and I don't sell in October.

Langston: Every great novel has a twist in the final chapter, Caro. Mr. Faulkner would want me to remind you that the past is never dead. It isn't even past.

The Cover Window
My call: Texas Longhorns 30, Ole Miss Rebels 24.
