"Save Percentage Says No": Darcy Fenwick Takes the Falcons to Buffalo

By Darcy Fenwick · Bowling Green Insider

Meet Your Insider
Hey, Falcons fans, and welcome to The Cover Window! I'm Darcy Fenwick, your Bowling Green insider. I played goalie for the club hockey team, and I still watch everything like I'm in the crease. Pass defense? That's save percentage. An interception returned for a touchdown? That's a shorty, the best goal in hockey. I travel with this team and I live with this team, and in a hockey town like this one, I fit right in. When we win, I bang my stick on the desk. When we lose, I send myself to the penalty box for two minutes of silence. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, with a little rink-rat attitude. "Save percentage says no."

The School in 60 Seconds
Bowling Green plays at Doyt L. Perry Stadium, listed at 24,000. Our head coach is one of the most famous names in football: Eddie George, the 1995 Heisman Trophy winner at Ohio State. That season he ran for 1,927 yards and 24 touchdowns, then a school record, and he also won the Doak Walker, Maxwell and Walter Camp awards. He was NFL Rookie of the Year in 1996, made four Pro Bowls, and is in the College Football Hall of Fame. He even starred on Broadway in "Chicago". That's a hat trick and then some. He's also one of only two NFL running backs, with Jim Brown, to rush for 10,000 yards without ever missing a start. Per the school, only Walter Payton started more consecutive regular-season games than his 130. Iron man. Goalie-approved.

The Coach and the Scheme
Eddie George was named Bowling Green's 21st head coach on March 9, 2025. At Tennessee State he took over a program averaging about three wins a season and went 9-3 in the 2024 regular season, with a share of the conference title and an FCS playoff berth. He was the Big South-OVC Coach of the Year. In 2026 the defense is our strength. We have 18 sacks, and opponents convert only 32% of third downs, 23rd in FBS. The pass defense allows 178 yards a game, 28th. Save percentage says no. But the run defense allows 209.6 yards a game (132nd), and the red-zone defense ranks 129th. On offense we run on about 57% of plays and score 18 points a game (129th). Time to shoot more. And we've got good blockers in front of the net: only 11 sacks allowed.

Players to Know
QB Jay Kastantin (No. 17): 1,201 yards, 7 TD, 4 INT, 66.5% completions. Against Ball State he went 17 of 24 for 215 yards and two TDs.
RB Austyn Dendy (No. 1): 332 yards and 4 TD on 94 carries. He scored against Ball State.
WR Brennan Ridley: two catches for 84 yards and a TD last week. WR Jeremiah Scoby: 26 catches, 269 yards.
LB Dorian Pringle (No. 3): 67 tackles, seventh in FBS, including 10 last week. Our best defenseman.

The Conference Picture
We're 2-5 overall but 2-1 in the MAC. That's what matters in the standings. The MAC wins: 24-20 at Miami (OH) and 38-10 over Sacramento State. Last week we lost to Ball State 32-31. One goal. Ouch. Non-conference was tough: Tarleton State 20-13, at Nebraska, at Iowa State and South Florida 14-6. A win in Buffalo keeps us right in the MAC race. Western Michigan is next.
