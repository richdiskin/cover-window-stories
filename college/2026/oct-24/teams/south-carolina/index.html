The School in 60 Seconds
South Carolina plays in the SEC and has since 1992. Before that, the Gamecocks were in the ACC from 1953 through 1970, winning the 1969 conference title, and then a major independent from 1971 through 1991. The independent years gave us George Rogers, our 1980 Heisman Trophy winner and the No. 1 overall selection in the 1981 draft. Jadeveon Clowney went No. 1 overall in 2014. We won the SEC East in 2010. Lou Holtz and Steve Spurrier are both in the College Football Hall of Fame, and Shane Beamer was the 2024 SEC coach of the year. Home is Williams-Brice Stadium, listed at 80,250 seats. When that stadium starts bouncing, you understand. The Gamecocks finished in the final AP top 25 in 1984 and 1987 as an independent, and have posted eight final top-25 finishes in the SEC era.

The Coach and the Scheme
Beamer was hired Dec. 6, 2020, and this is his sixth season. His teams went 9-4 in 2024 and 4-8 in 2025. The 2026 Gamecocks bring the noise on offense: 474.7 yards a game (13th in FBS), 232.3 rushing yards (10th) and 37.7 points (18th). CFBD has 1,394 rushing yards on 233 carries, just about 6 yards a carry. The defense is sturdy against the run, 103.7 yards allowed a game (25th), and leakier through the air, 237.2 passing yards allowed (107th). The red zone defense ranks 122nd. That's the one part of the show we're still rehearsing.

Players to Know

RB Matthew Fuller: 485 yards on 81 carries, 5 TD.

LB Justin Okoronkwo: a team-high 43 tackles. DE Anthony Addison: 3 sacks. DB Peyton Williams: an interception.
Freshman Julian Walker: two starts and a sack, per the school's injury rundown.

The Conference Picture
South Carolina is 3-3 and 1-3 in the SEC. The three SEC losses were by 7 to Mississippi State, by 31 at Alabama and by 1 in overtime to Kentucky, 35-34. The win at Florida, 38-19, came right before the bye. We're unranked. Next up is a trip to Oklahoma on Oct. 31. Knocking off the No. 11 team in the country at home would be one fine entrance into November.

This Week: No. 11 Tennessee (6-1) at South Carolina (3-3)
Saturday, Oct. 24 · 3:30 p.m. ET · Williams-Brice Stadium · ESPN.

Our offense vs. their defense: this is the main event. Tennessee allows 14.7 points (9th), 280.6 yards (15th) and 159.3 passing yards (8th). It has 29 sacks. Our run game, 10th in FBS, against their run defense, 42nd, is where I'd put the spotlight.
Their offense vs. our defense: Tennessee runs for 217.1 yards a game (19th) and ranks 6th in red zone scoring. We allow 103.7 rushing yards a game (25th), so that's strength against strength. Their quarterback spot is a work in progress, per AP.
Series: UT's game notes list the all-time series at Tennessee 29, South Carolina 11, with 2 ties. From 2016 through 2025 it's even, 4-4.
Availability: Beamer, after the Florida game: "Everybody that missed the game yesterday or left the game, like Judge Collier did, we all expect to be back against Tennessee. The only one that we would be most concerned about. is QuaySheed Scott." That group included DT Gabriel Brownlow-Dindy, Walker and center Carter Miller.
