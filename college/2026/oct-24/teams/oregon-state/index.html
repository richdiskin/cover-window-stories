"Measure twice, kick once": Juniper Ostrowski Takes the Open Week

Meet Your Insider
Measure twice, kick once! I'm Juniper Ostrowski, and I'm your Oregon State insider here at The Cover Window. The short version: I'm a forestry PhD who charts treetop weather and red-zone efficiency. I follow this team from the stands, every week, and I love it out loud. Fair warning about my habit: corrects everyone's tree species in metaphors. On the podcast, my segment is called Tree ID. The show card describes it like this: corrects everyone's tree metaphors. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, you won't hear it from me. This week the Beavers have an open date, so instead of a game preview you get the full tour: the school, the coach, the players, the league and what's next.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Reser Stadium in Corvallis, OR, built in 1953 with a listed capacity of 43,363. The fight song is "Hail to Old OSU", per Wikipedia. The band is the Oregon State University Marching Band, per Wikipedia. The last five seasons went 7-6, 10-3, 8-5, 5-7 and 2-10, from 2021 through 2025. The most frequent opponents from 2016 through 2025 tell you who matters around here: Washington State (3-8 in 11 meetings), California (5-5 in 10 meetings), Oregon (3-7 in 10 meetings), Washington (1-7 in 8 meetings). That's a list of games, not an official rivalry list. The 2026 recruiting class ranked No. 100 nationally in CFBD's team rankings. And if you visit, ask somebody about Benny the Beaver; it's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
JaMarcus Shephard leads the Beavers; CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Nov. 28, 2025. Through 7 games, Oregon State scores 42.7 points a game and allows 23.1. By our math from CFBD figures, the offense runs on about 42% of its plays, gaining 138.1 rushing yards and 371.3 passing yards a game. That's a team that wants to throw it around. On third down, Oregon State converts 46.5% of the time and opponents convert 38.3%. The defense has 17 sacks and 33 tackles for loss, and the turnover ledger reads 4 giveaways against 9 takeaways. Penalties cost 61.1 yards a game. The high-water mark so far was 66 points against San Diego State on Oct. 10, 2026, and 0 of the 7 games were decided by eight points or fewer. Over the season, Oregon State has outscored opponents by a combined 137 points. Every one of those numbers is a little homework for the open week.

Oregon State is 5-2 overall, 3-1 at home and 2-1 away. In Pac-12 play the Beavers are 3-0, which sorts them No. 1 of 8 by record. That's the top spot in the table, at least for now. Right around us: Boise State (2-0). The top of the table: Oregon State (3-0), Boise State (2-0), San Diego State (2-1). That order is a sort by record, not the official tiebreaker order. Every league game from here is a chance to move up, and the open week is when the plan for that climb gets built. An open date in late October is a chance to heal up and study the stretch run.
