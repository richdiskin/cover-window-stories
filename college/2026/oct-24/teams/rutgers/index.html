"Exit 9 Never Lies": Vinny Scolari Takes the Scarlet Knights to Evanston

By Vinny Scolari · Rutgers Insider

Meet Your Insider
Yo, Rutgers! How you doin'? Welcome to The Cover Window! Vinny Scolari here, your Rutgers insider, born and raised off Exit 9 of the New Jersey Turnpike. I give directions by Turnpike exit for everything, and I mean everything. Where's the stadium? Exit 9. How am I feeling after a loss? Somewhere around Exit 7A. I grade every Big Ten trench battle like a boardwalk arm-wrestle: who pushed in, who flinched, who walked away with the stuffed animal. I travel with this team and I live with this team. When we win, I order a sub big enough for the whole studio. Six feet. Everybody eats. When we lose, I say "Fuhgeddaboudit". and then I can't. I really can't. Every week you'll get the players, the coaches and the matchup, Jersey-style. "Exit 9 never lies."

The School in 60 Seconds
Here's something every college football fan should know. The very first college football game was played right in New Brunswick on Nov. 6, 1869, and Rutgers beat Princeton 6-4. That's right: the birthplace of the whole sport is off Exit 9. Home now is SHI Stadium in Piscataway, listed at 52,454. The man running the show is Greg Schiano, who came back to coach Rutgers again in December 2019. The Scarlet Knights play in the Big Ten, and every week is a heavyweight fight.

The Coach and the Scheme
Schiano's second stint began with his return on Nov. 30, 2019. The 2026 numbers say this offense can sling it: 265.8 passing yards a game (35th in FBS) and 16 passing touchdowns. Rutgers averages 31.2 points and 403.3 yards a game. And here's a stat I'll yell from the top of the Driscoll Bridge: Rutgers is the least penalized team in FBS, at 28.7 penalty yards a game. Discipline, baby. The defense, though, needs some fixing. It allows 31.8 points a game (119th) and 185.2 rushing yards (118th). Turnovers are a problem too, at minus-5 (122nd).

Players to Know
WR K.J. Duff: 42 catches, 741 yards and 9 TD. At Maryland he had 9 catches for 113 yards and two touchdowns, including a catch AP called incredible. The man's a Turnpike express lane.
QB Dylan Lonergan: 1,066 yards, 10 TD, 6 INT. He went 23 of 35 for 291 yards and three touchdowns at Maryland.
RB Antwan Raymond: 498 yards and 5 TD on 105 carries.
RB Clay Thevenin: 109 rushing yards and a touchdown at Maryland, plus a receiving TD.
I couldn't verify current defensive leaders from a free source, so I'm not guessing. Jersey guys are loud, not sloppy.

The Conference Picture
Rutgers is 2-4 overall and 1-2 in the Big Ten, 1-3 at home and 1-1 on the road. We're not ranked. The losses were to UMass, at Boston College, to USC (42-35) and to Indiana. We hung with USC until the end, losing by seven, and the Maryland comeback showed what this team's made of. On the ground we're at 3.78 yards a carry, and the protection has given up 14 sacks. If the big guys win their arm-wrestles, this offense can go the whole length of the Turnpike. Michigan comes to Piscataway next week, so this is a big two-week stretch.
