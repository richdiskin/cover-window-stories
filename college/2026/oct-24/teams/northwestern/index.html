"Footnote: I Was Right": Ezra Lindqvist Welcomes Rutgers to the New Ryan Field

By Ezra Lindqvist · Northwestern Insider

Meet Your Insider
Hello, Wildcats, and welcome to The Cover Window! I'm Ezra Lindqvist, your Northwestern insider. I'm a data journalist by training, which means I fact-check the coaches in real time. Politely. Mostly politely. I travel with this team and I live with this team, and I'm fully aware that I'm doing the "smartest kid in the Big Ten" bit. I footnote my own jokes¹, and then I footnote the footnotes². When we win, I publish a formal correction to everyone who doubted us. The School in 60 Seconds
The headline this season is home. Northwestern opened 2026 with two games at Martin Stadium, right on the shore of Lake Michigan, and then opened the brand-new Ryan Field against Penn State on Oct. 2. Northwestern won that one 34-13. Correction to my earlier draft: there was no earlier draft. Northwestern has won all four of its home games this season. The coach is David Braun, who became interim coach in 2023, was named the permanent head coach on Nov. 15 of that year and was the consensus Big Ten Coach of the Year. Footnote: that's a very good first season.

The Coach and the Scheme
Braun's start date in the coaching database is July 10, 2023. The 2026 numbers say Northwestern runs the ball and protects it. It runs on most of its plays, for 191.5 yards a game (39th in FBS), with 14 rushing touchdowns. It averages 33.5 points a game. And here's the headline stat: Northwestern has lost only two turnovers all season. Its plus-7 turnover margin ranks eighth in FBS. In the red zone the Wildcats rank fourth in FBS. The defense allows 17.0 points a game (26th), but 230.5 passing yards a game ranks 100th. Correction requested. Two more items for the record: the pass rush has 9 sacks, and the protection has allowed 14. Opponents average 4.27 yards a carry against us. I'm filing that under "areas for improvement," with a footnote noting how good everything else has been.

Players to Know
QB Aidan Chiles: 1,398 yards, 9 TD and 0 INT. Against Michigan State he went 18 of 27 for 226 yards and ran for a touchdown.
RB Caleb Komolafe: 401 yards and 4 TD on 75 carries. Against MSU: 67 rushing yards, a touchdown and four catches for 51 yards.
WR Griffin Wilde: 30 catches, 482 yards, 3 TD.
K Jackson Kleather: three field goals against Michigan State, including the 34-yard winner with two seconds left.
Matthew Smith: blocked a Michigan State field-goal try late.

The Conference Picture
Northwestern is 5-1 overall and 2-1 in the Big Ten, 4-0 at home and 1-1 on the road. The Wildcats are No. 24 in the AP poll and No. 23 in the Coaches Poll. The only loss was 29-23 at Indiana. Next week is a trip to Oregon, so footnote this: winning this week matters. Holding serve at home before going to Eugene puts Northwestern in good shape for the second half of the season. Data journalism suggests momentum is mostly a myth. Data journalism has never been to a Northwestern pep rally.
