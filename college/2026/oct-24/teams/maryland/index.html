Meet Your Insider
Crack it open: Old Bay on that stat! I'm Abigail 'Abby' Chesapeake, and I'm your Maryland insider here at The Cover Window. The short version: I'm a dC-suburb lobbyist type who networks with every coordinator and cracks crabs on air. I follow this team from the stands, every week, and I love it out loud. Fair warning about my habit: old Bay is her universal answer to every problem. On the podcast, my segment is called Old Bay Index. The show card describes it like this: seasons each stat to taste. My rules are simple. I keep it clean and kind, I celebrate players as people, I respect every opponent, and I never invent a stat, a quote or a fact. If I can't source it, you won't hear it from me. This week the Terrapins have an open date, so instead of a game preview you get the full tour: the school, the coach, the players, the league and what's next.

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is SECU Stadium in College Park, MD, built in 1950 with a listed capacity of 51,802. The fight song is "Maryland Victory Song; Maryland Fight Song (Played after scoring a touchdown)", per Wikipedia. The band is the Mighty Sound of Maryland, per Wikipedia. The program claims national titles from 1953, per Wikipedia. The last five seasons went 7-6, 8-5, 8-5, 4-8 and 4-8, from 2021 through 2025. The most frequent opponents from 2016 through 2025 tell you who matters around here: Indiana (4-6 in 10 meetings), Rutgers (6-4 in 10 meetings), Penn State (1-8 in 9 meetings), Michigan State (3-6 in 9 meetings). That's a list of games, not an official rivalry list. The 2026 recruiting class ranked No. 43 nationally in CFBD's team rankings. And if you visit, ask somebody about Testudo; it's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Mike Locksley leads the Terrapins; CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 4, 2018. Through 7 games, Maryland scores 28.3 points a game and allows 33.3. By our math from CFBD figures, the offense runs on about 43% of its plays, gaining 133.0 rushing yards and 244.7 passing yards a game. That's a team that wants to throw it around. On third down, Maryland converts 33.7% of the time and opponents convert 42.4%. The defense has 17 sacks and 36 tackles for loss, and the turnover ledger reads 7 giveaways against 10 takeaways. Penalties cost 63.6 yards a game. The high-water mark so far was 62 points against Hampton on Sept. 6, 2026, and 1 of the 7 games was decided by eight points or fewer. Over the season, opponents have outscored Maryland by a combined 35 points. Every one of those numbers is a little homework for the open week.

The Conference Picture
Maryland is 2-5 overall, 1-3 at home and 1-2 away. In Big Ten play the Terrapins are 0-4, which sorts them No. 17 of 18 by record. Ohio State sits on top at 4-0 in league games. Right around us: Penn State (0-4) and Michigan State (0-4). The top of the table: Ohio State (4-0), UCLA (4-0), Indiana (3-1). That order is a sort by record, not the official tiebreaker order. Every league game from here is a chance to move up, and the open week is when the plan for that climb gets built. An open date in late October is a chance to heal up and study the stretch run.
