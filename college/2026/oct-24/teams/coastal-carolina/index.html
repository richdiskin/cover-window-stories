Meet Your Insider

The School in 60 Seconds
Home is Brooks Stadium (SC) in Conway, SC, built in 2003 with a listed capacity of 21,000. The playing surface is James C. Benton Field, per Wikipedia. The last five seasons went 11-2, 9-4, 8-5, 6-7 and 6-7, from 2021 through 2025. The most frequent opponents from 2016 through 2025 tell you who matters around here: Georgia State (5-4 in 9 meetings), App State (5-4 in 9 meetings), Georgia Southern (4-5 in 9 meetings), Troy (3-4 in 7 meetings). That's a list of games, not an official rivalry list. The 2026 recruiting class ranked No. 118 nationally in CFBD's team rankings. And if you visit, ask somebody about the teal turf; it's the tradition I'd show you first.

The Coach and the Scheme
Ryan Beard leads the Chanticleers; CollegeFootballData dates the hire to Dec. 11, 2025. Through 7 games, Coastal Carolina scores 26.1 points a game and allows 25.9. By our math from CFBD figures, the offense runs on about 54% of its plays, gaining 147.6 rushing yards and 234.9 passing yards a game. That's a balanced menu. On third down, Coastal Carolina converts 36.5% of the time and opponents convert 44.6%. The defense has 15 sacks and 37 tackles for loss, and the turnover ledger reads 10 giveaways against 14 takeaways. Penalties cost 63.9 yards a game. The high-water mark so far was 45 points against Fordham on Sept. 12, 2026, and 5 of the 7 games were decided by eight points or fewer. Over the season, Coastal Carolina has outscored opponents by a combined 2 points. Every one of those numbers is a little homework for the open week.

The Conference Picture
Coastal Carolina is 2-5 overall, 1-3 at home and 1-2 away. In Sun Belt play the Chanticleers are 1-2, which sorts them No. 10 of 14 by record. James Madison sits on top at 4-0 in league games. Right around us: Arkansas State (1-2) and Old Dominion (1-3). The top of the table: James Madison (4-0), Louisiana Tech (2-0), South Alabama (2-0). That order is a sort by record, not the official tiebreaker order. Every league game from here is a chance to move up, and the open week is when the plan for that climb gets built. An open date in late October is a chance to heal up and study the stretch run.
