Blue Out Under the Lights: No. 12 Kentucky's Ball Hawks Host Vanderbilt

Vanderbilt (3-4) at Kentucky (6-1)

7:30 PM ET Sat Oct 24 · Kroger Field · SEC Network

Vanderbilt (3-4, 0-4 SEC) at No. 12 Kentucky (6-1, 4-1 SEC). Saturday, Oct. 24, 7:30 p.m. ET, Kroger Field, Lexington, SEC Network.

Your hosts are Pippa Ashcroft, a Vanderbilt economics student who writes a country song about every game, and Rhett Abernathy-Coyle, a Kentucky fan raised on a horse farm out past Keeneland who grades teams like bloodlines.

Pippa: You grade bloodlines, Rhett? Sugar, I grade hooks. And your team's got a catchy one this year. I'll give you that.

Rhett: A song about every game? Charming, Pippa. Write me one about a horse, and I'll come to the show.

Players to watch

Kentucky quarterback Kenny Minchey has completed 141 of 206 passes for 1,552 yards, 13 touchdowns and just one interception. Running back C.J. Baxter has 484 yards and eight touchdowns. Receiver Kenny Darby has 34 catches for 436 yards. Defensive back Jordan Castell has four interceptions, linebacker Grant Godfrey leads with 67 tackles and Mi'Quise Humphrey-Grace has four sacks.

Vanderbilt quarterback Jared Curtis has 1,242 yards, six touchdowns and seven interceptions. Receiver Junior Sherrill has 41 catches for 628 yards and four touchdowns. Running back Sedrick Alexander has 270 yards and five touchdowns. Safety CJ Heard leads with 51 tackles, and Nick Rinaldi has 3.5 sacks.

Pippa: Sherrill is a hit single, y'all. Eight catches, 99 yards and a score last week.

What happened last week

Kentucky won 21-13 at Oklahoma. Minchey went 21 of 28 for 158 yards and two touchdowns. Jason Patterson led the running backs with 38 yards on 12 carries.

Vanderbilt lost 31-21 to Arkansas, which snapped a 14-game SEC skid. Curtis threw for 274 yards, and Alexander and Makhilyn Young ran for touchdowns. Sherrill caught eight passes for 99 yards and a score.

Rhett: Five straight wins since Alabama. A thoroughbred that never breaks stride.

Injuries and availability

The official SEC availability report for this game hasn't been published yet. Baxter didn't lead Kentucky's backs at Oklahoma, so watch for his name when the report posts. No injury news has surfaced for Vanderbilt.

What the coaches are up to

Will Stein is in his first season at Kentucky, hired Dec. 1, 2025. His Wildcats are plus-11 in turnover margin, third in FBS, and 3-0 on the road. They won back-to-back one-point games at South Carolina and against LSU.

Clark Lea has led Vanderbilt since December 2020. His team went 10-3 last season. His defense came in highly touted but has struggled.

Player storylines

Ball security could decide it. Kentucky has taken the ball away 16 times, and Vanderbilt has thrown seven interceptions. Sherrill gets a shot at a Kentucky pass defense ranked 116th. Kentucky's weak spot is third downs, where it ranks last in FBS. Vanderbilt's run defense allowed 196 yards to Ole Miss and 170 to Arkansas.

What's happening on campus

Kentucky Athletics set this game as a Blue Out under the lights at Kroger Field. Kentucky's Homecoming already happened, against LSU on Oct. 10. Both teams have a week off after this game.

Anything else fun and true

Kentucky's only loss was 45-17 to Alabama. Vanderbilt opened the season 3-0, including a 35-31 win over NC State. Next, Kentucky visits No. 11 Tennessee on Nov. 7, and Vanderbilt goes to Mississippi State.

Rhett: And every great horse saves its kick for the stretch, Pippa.
