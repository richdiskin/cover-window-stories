Texas Tech at Cincinnati

Texas Tech (6-0) at Cincinnati (4-2)

12:00 PM ET Sat Oct 24 · Nippert Stadium · FS1

Texas Tech is 6-0, and its defense has 11 interceptions. Cincinnati has thrown none. Saturday at noon, one of those numbers is getting a new entry.

No. 8 Texas Tech (6-0) at Cincinnati (4-2), Saturday at 12:00 p.m. ET on FS1 at Nippert Stadium. It's a Big 12 game and Homecoming No. 100 at UC, with the parade heading down Clifton Avenue that morning. It's also Texas Tech's first trip ever to Nippert. DraftKings has Texas Tech Red Raiders −11.5 with a total of 56.5. My card: Cincinnati Bearcats +11.5, Over 56.5, and a final of Texas Tech Red Raiders 34, Cincinnati Bearcats 23.

Lionel "Skyline" Abernathy speaks for Cincinnati and ranks everything in chili ways. "JC French has 1,338 yards and nine touchdowns with zero interceptions. We've lost two turnovers all season, the fewest in FBS. That's a five-way, onions and beans."

Bodie Ratliff speaks for Texas Tech, a bareback rider who answers most questions in one word. Asked about the Red Raider defense, Bodie said: "Ours."

Bodie's not wrong. Texas Tech allows 2.87 yards a carry and 82.0 rushing yards a game, and its 11 interceptions rank second in the nation. Cincinnati runs for 5.93 a carry. Something gives, but Cincinnati doesn't have to win this game. The Bearcats have to stay inside 11.5, and I think they do.

Why Cincinnati with the 11.5? Texas Tech is the better team. The Red Raiders just beat Arizona State 53-25, with J'Koby Williams running for a career-high 165 yards. They've won four straight road games dating to last season and are chasing the fifth 7-0 start in school history. But Cincinnati is 0-2 on the road and 4-0 at home, and this is at home, on Homecoming No. 100. My final has Tech by 11.

And look at what Cincinnati just did. The Bearcats gained 622 yards at West Virginia, French threw for a career-high 389, and Cole Tabb scored three times. They lost 45-42 on a field goal at the horn, but that offense can score on anybody, and that's how a home underdog hangs around. The caveat is Tech's defense: if French throws his first interception of the year, the margin can grow in a hurry.

Why the Over? Cincinnati scored 42 last week. Texas Tech scored 53. My 34-23 adds up to 57, half a point over 56.5. The last meeting finished 44-41.

Now the room, and the boss is with the majority. The Formula has Tech 35-24, an 11-point game, so it takes Cincinnati +11.5. Next Man Up has Tech 34-25 and takes the Bearcats too. Letdown Watch is the lone voice with Texas Tech, at 35-21, and it calls the Under. Follow The Money and In The Trenches sit this one out.

The Formula opened: "Eleven points. The line is 11.5. That half point is Homecoming No. 100 doing its job."

Letdown Watch answered: "Thirty-five to 21. Tech's defense has picked off 11 passes. French hasn't met them yet."

Next Man Up: "And French hasn't thrown one. Irresistible force, immovable object. Take the points."

Bottom of the card: an unbeaten road team with a ball-hawking defense against a Cincinnati offense that can score with anybody. Guns up, says Bodie. Tech can win it. I'll take the 11.5 with Cincinnati.

Texas Tech Red Raiders 34, Cincinnati Bearcats 23. Cincinnati Bearcats cover the 11.5. Over 56.5.
