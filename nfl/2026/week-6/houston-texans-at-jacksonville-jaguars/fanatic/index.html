Houston Texans at Jacksonville Jaguars

Houston Texans (1-4) at Jacksonville Jaguars (4-1)

9:30 AM ET Sun Oct 18 · Wembley Stadium, London, England · NFL Net

The Houston Texans (1-4) and the Jacksonville Jaguars (4-1) meet at Wembley Stadium in London, a neutral site, with a 9:30 AM ET kickoff on Sunday, Oct 18, on NFL Network.

This AFC South meeting crosses an ocean. Jacksonville arrives 4-1 overall, 3-0 at home and 1-1 on the road, with 123 points scored and just 70 allowed in five games. That is the fewest points allowed by any team on this slate. Houston is 1-4, 0-3 at home and 1-1 on the road, and has scored 110 while allowing 112.

Houston's injuries are on offense and in the secondary. Running back David Montgomery and receiver Tank Dell are out, and so is safety Jalen Pitre. Cornerback Ja'Marcus Ingram and linebacker Henry To'oTo'o are questionable.

Jacksonville is close to full strength. Cornerback Montaric Brown is out, as is long snapper Ross Matiscik. Cornerback Jaylon Jones is questionable. Neither team has a starting quarterback on the injury report.

This looks much closer than the records.

The trench numbers do not settle it. The schedule offers no angle for either side, even with the trip to London.

The question is whether Houston's point differential, nearly even despite a 1-4 record, says more about this team than the standings do. For Jacksonville, the job is to keep doing what it has done all season: allow very little and let the offense do enough.

My call: Texans 24, Jaguars 23.
