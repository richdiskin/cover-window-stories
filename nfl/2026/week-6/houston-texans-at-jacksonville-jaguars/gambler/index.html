Houston Texans at Jacksonville Jaguars

Houston Texans (1-4) at Jacksonville Jaguars (4-1)

9:30 AM ET Sun Oct 18 · Wembley Stadium, London, England · NFL Net

The Houston Texans (1-4) face the Jacksonville Jaguars (4-1) at Wembley Stadium in London, kicking off at 9:30 AM ET Sunday, Oct 18, on NFL Network, and the number has not moved at all. Jacksonville opened at −2.5 on Oct 13 at 2:12 PM ET and is still −2.5 as of Oct 18 at 7:32 AM ET. The total opened at 42.5 (Oct 13 at 6:20 AM ET) and is still 42.5. The number sits a half-point below 3, so a Jaguars win by exactly a field goal clears it.

This is one of the few games with a published split. On the spread, bets split 50/50 and money 51/49 between Houston and Jacksonville, so tickets and dollars line up. On the total, a little more than half of the bets are on the Under but four of every five dollars are on the Over, so the tickets and the dollars point opposite ways.

The trench matchups point in opposite directions. No starting quarterback is on the injury report for either team.

The injuries that do exist fall mostly on Houston. Running back David Montgomery, receiver Tank Dell and safety Jalen Pitre are out. Jacksonville is without cornerback Montaric Brown and long snapper Ross Matiscik.

The records and the scoring tell different stories. Jacksonville is 4-1, 3-0 at home, and has scored 123 points while allowing only 70 in five games. Houston is 1-4, 1-1 on the road, but has scored 110 and allowed 112, nearly even. A near-even point differential behind a 1-4 record is the case for the Texans keeping it close.
