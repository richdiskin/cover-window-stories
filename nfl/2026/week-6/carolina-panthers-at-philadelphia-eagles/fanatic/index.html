Carolina Panthers at Philadelphia Eagles

Carolina Panthers (2-2) at Philadelphia Eagles (2-3)

1:00 PM ET Sun Oct 18 · Lincoln Financial Field, Philadelphia, PA · CBS

The Carolina Panthers (2-2) visit the Philadelphia Eagles (2-3) at Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia, kickoff 1:00 PM ET Sunday, Oct 18, on CBS.

Philadelphia plays without quarterback Jalen Hurts, out with a calf injury, and running back Saquon Barkley, out with a hamstring injury. The Eagles' list keeps going: guard Landon Dickerson, a starter, is out, along with running back Will Shipley, tight ends Grant Calcaterra and Eli Stowers, cornerback Jakorian Bennett, safety Michael Carter II and linebacker Jalyx Hunt. Linebacker A.J. Epenesa is doubtful and center Drew Kendall is questionable.

Carolina is healthier but not whole. Tackle Monroe Freeling is doubtful, tackle Taylor Moton and guard Damien Lewis are questionable, and running back AJ Dillon is out, with Trevor Etienne questionable. On defense, Jaelan Phillips and Tershawn Wharton are questionable.

The records are close. Carolina is 2-2, 1-1 on the road, with 121 points scored and 109 allowed. Philadelphia is 2-3 and has scored only 92 while allowing 112, with a 1-1 home record.

Carolina has clearly been the better team this season.

The schedule offers no angle for either side.

The story is simple and hard for Philadelphia: a rebuilt backfield, a missing guard and a depleted set of tight ends against a healthier Carolina roster. If the Panthers' tackles are limited, the Eagles' rush has its own opening.

My call: Panthers 22, Eagles 16.
