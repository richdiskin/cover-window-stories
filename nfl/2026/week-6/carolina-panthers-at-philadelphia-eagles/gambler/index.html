Carolina Panthers (2-2) at Philadelphia Eagles (2-3)

1:00 PM ET Sun Oct 18 · Lincoln Financial Field, Philadelphia, PA · CBS

The Carolina Panthers (2-2) visit the Philadelphia Eagles (2-3) at Lincoln Financial Field, kicking off at 1:00 PM ET Sunday, Oct 18, on CBS. It is the one 1:00 PM game where the market history does not tell a clean story. The current consensus, as of Oct 18 at 7:32 AM ET, has Carolina as a 3-point road favorite with a total of 39. With Philadelphia's starting quarterback ruled out, the opening number no longer tells the story. No bets-versus-money split was available.

The number sits exactly on 3. A Carolina win by a field goal pushes.

The quarterback news is the whole story. Jalen Hurts is out with a calf injury. Running back Saquon Barkley is also out, along with guard Landon Dickerson, a trench starter, running back Will Shipley and tight ends Grant Calcaterra and Eli Stowers. Carolina has injury questions too, mostly up front. Running back AJ Dillon is out, tackle Monroe Freeling is doubtful, and tackle Taylor Moton and guard Damien Lewis are questionable. If those blockers sit, the Eagles' defense has an opening.
