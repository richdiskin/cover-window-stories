Chicago Bears at Atlanta Falcons

Chicago Bears (3-2) at Atlanta Falcons (2-3)

1:00 PM ET Sun Oct 18 · Mercedes-Benz Stadium, Atlanta, GA · Fox

The Chicago Bears (3-2) visit the Atlanta Falcons (2-3) at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta, kickoff 1:00 PM ET Sunday, Oct 18, on Fox.

The records tell an odd story. Atlanta is 0-2 at home and 2-1 on the road. Chicago is 2-1 at home and 1-1 away. Both teams have scored 119 points in five games, but Chicago has allowed 96 and Atlanta 122.

Chicago's biggest question is at quarterback. Caleb Williams is listed questionable with a hamstring injury. The defense is short-handed in the middle: linebackers T.J. Edwards and Keyshaun Elliott are out, and so is defensive end Dayo Odeyingbo. Running back Kyle Monangai is out, and cornerbacks Kyler Gordon and Tyrique Stevenson are questionable.

Atlanta comes in healthier. Cornerback C.J. Henderson is out, and defensive end Za'Darius Smith is questionable with a neck injury.

The play-by-play numbers see this game very differently from the standings, with Atlanta well ahead of a Chicago team one game better in the standings.

The calendar adds nothing. Neither team is on a short week, coming off extra rest, or finishing a long road trip.

So the game turns on two things. Can Atlanta finally win in its own building after two home losses? And can a Chicago defense missing a starting end and two linebackers hold up against an Atlanta offense that has matched the Bears' 119 points, with Williams' status still open?

My call: Falcons 31, Bears 22.
