Dallas Cowboys at Green Bay Packers

Dallas Cowboys (2-3) at Green Bay Packers (3-2)

8:20 PM ET Sun Oct 18 · Lambeau Field, Green Bay, WI · NBC

The Dallas Cowboys (2-3) visit the Green Bay Packers (3-2) at Lambeau Field, kicking off at 8:20 PM ET Sunday, Oct 18, on NBC, and the side has held steady. Green Bay opened at −1.5 on Oct 13 at 12:31 PM ET and is still −1.5 as of Oct 18 at 7:32 AM ET. At 1.5, a one-point Packers win is the only win that falls short.

The total has climbed a point, from 52.5 (Oct 13 at 12:28 AM ET) to 53.5. No bets-versus-money split was available.

The trench numbers show where that could come from. Green Bay's rush gets home on about one in every 13 dropbacks, and Dallas allows only one in 33. Dallas gets a sack about once every 29 dropbacks against a Green Bay front allowing one in 19. On the ground, Dallas runs for 3.8 yards a carry against a Packers defense allowing 4.2, while Green Bay runs for only 3.4 against a Dallas defense allowing 5.3. None of those matchups is a clear advantage, but the Packers' run game has a softer matchup than its average suggests.

The injuries cut into Green Bay's defensive interior. Defensive tackles Javon Hargrave and Jonathan Ford are out and end Jordon Riley is doubtful; cornerback Benjamin St-Juste and receiver Skyy Moore are also out. Dallas is without tackle Drew Shelton and cornerback Cobie Durant, with cornerbacks Joey Porter Jr. and Ameer Speed and linebacker DeMarvion Overshown questionable. No starting quarterback is out.
