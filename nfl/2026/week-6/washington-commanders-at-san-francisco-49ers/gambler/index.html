Washington Commanders at San Francisco 49ers

Washington Commanders (1-3-1) at San Francisco 49ers (5-0)

8:15 PM ET Mon Oct 19 · Levi's Stadium, Santa Clara, CA · ESPN, ABC

The Washington Commanders (1-3-1) visit the San Francisco 49ers (5-0) at Levi's Stadium in Santa Clara on Monday, Oct 19, kicking off at 8:15 PM ET on ESPN and ABC, and the side has not moved. San Francisco opened at −7.5 on Oct 13 at 6:59 AM ET and is still −7.5 as of Oct 18 at 7:32 AM ET. The number sits a half-point past 7, so a 49ers win by exactly 7 lands on Washington's side.

The total has risen 1.5 points, from 50.5 (Oct 13 at 1:04 AM ET) to 52.

The ratings show the most lopsided matchup of the week.

The quarterback news is not a factor. Marcus Mariota is listed out for Washington, but he is the backup; Jayden Daniels practiced fully and starts.

The trench absences cut both ways. San Francisco is without defensive end Nick Bosa, and Washington is without defensive end Charles Omenihu; both are starters. Also out: receiver Deebo Samuel Sr., safety Marques Sigle and linebacker Garret Wallow for San Francisco, and running back Jacory Croskey-Merritt and linebacker Javontae Jean-Baptiste for Washington. Receivers Mike Evans, Christian Kirk and Stefon Diggs are all questionable.

San Francisco, 3-0 at home, has scored 154 points and allowed 93 in five games. Washington, 0-2 on the road, has scored 115 and allowed 149.
