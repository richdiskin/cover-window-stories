New Orleans Saints at New York Giants

New Orleans Saints (1-4) at New York Giants (3-1-1)

1:00 PM ET Sun Oct 18 · MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, NJ · Fox

The New Orleans Saints (1-4) visit the New York Giants (3-1-1) at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, kicking off at 1:00 PM ET Sunday, Oct 18, on Fox, and the number has barely moved. The Giants opened at +1.5 on Oct 13 at 4:56 AM ET and are still +1.5 as of Oct 18 at 7:32 AM ET, which makes New Orleans a 1.5-point road favorite. The total opened at 45.5 (Oct 13 at 6:59 AM ET) and is 46, a half-point tick. No bets-versus-money split was available.

At 1.5, the number sits below a field goal. In practice, the Giants clear it by winning outright or losing by one point.

The ratings see a coin flip.

The trench numbers favor the running games on both sides. New Orleans runs for 3.5 yards a carry against a Giants defense allowing 5.4; the Giants run for 3.9 against a Saints defense allowing 5.1. The Saints' rush gets a sack on about one in every 16 dropbacks against a Giants front allowing one in 14, and the Giants get one in 21 against a Saints front allowing one in 16. None of those matchups is a clear advantage, but two defenses that leak on the ground can shorten a game.

No starting quarterback is out. New Orleans is without linebackers Kaden Elliss and Anfernee Jennings, with Alvin Kamara and Chris Olave questionable. The Giants are without cornerback Colton Hood, safety Tyler Nubin and tight end Theo Johnson, with Abdul Carter and Andrew Thomas questionable.
