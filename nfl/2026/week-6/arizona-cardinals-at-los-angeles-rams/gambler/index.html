Arizona Cardinals (1-4) at Los Angeles Rams (2-3)

4:05 PM ET Sun Oct 18 · SoFi Stadium, Inglewood, CA · Fox

The Arizona Cardinals (1-4) visit the Los Angeles Rams (2-3) at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, kicking off at 4:05 PM ET Sunday, Oct 18, on Fox. The game opened with Los Angeles at −13 on Oct 13 at 1:35 AM ET. As of Oct 18 at 7:32 AM ET the consensus is Rams −12.5, a half-point move toward Arizona. The number is well past 7. A 13-point Rams win, which pushed at the opener, now clears it.

The total opened at 52.5 (Oct 13 at 1:38 AM ET) and has dropped a full point to 51.5. No bets-versus-money split was available for this game.

The trench numbers are closer than the ratings. Arizona runs for 3.6 yards a carry against a Rams defense allowing 4.6, and the Rams run for 4.8 against an Arizona defense allowing 4.6. The Rams get a sack about once every 19 dropbacks against an Arizona front that allows one in 16. None of those matchups is a clear advantage.

Injuries tilt toward Los Angeles. No starting quarterback is out. Arizona is without safeties Wydett Williams Jr. and Andrew Wingard and running back Jeremiyah Love, with safeties Budda Baker and Kitan Crawford questionable. The Rams list only safety Kam Curl and cornerback Trent McDuffie as questionable.
