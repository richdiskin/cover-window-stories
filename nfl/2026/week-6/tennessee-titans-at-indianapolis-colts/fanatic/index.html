Tennessee Titans at Indianapolis Colts

Tennessee Titans (0-5) at Indianapolis Colts (2-3)

1:00 PM ET Sun Oct 18 · Lucas Oil Stadium, Indianapolis, IN · Fox

The Tennessee Titans (0-5) visit the Indianapolis Colts (2-3) at Lucas Oil Stadium in Indianapolis, kickoff 1:00 PM ET Sunday, Oct 18, on Fox.

Tennessee is still looking for its first win. The Titans have scored 58 points in five games and allowed 109, and they are 0-2 on the road. Indianapolis has had the opposite problem: 124 points scored but 138 allowed, with a 1-1 home record.

The injury news is lighter than most this week. Tennessee is without defensive tackle Jordan Elliott, out for a personal matter, and safety Kevin Winston Jr., with running back Tyjae Spears and cornerback Joshua Williams questionable. Indianapolis is missing linebacker Jaylon Carlies and receiver Ashton Dulin, and tight end Tyler Warren is questionable with a groin injury.

Both teams have struggled this season, with Indianapolis a bit ahead of Tennessee.

The ground game is the matchup to watch. Tennessee averages 3.8 yards per carry, and the Colts' defense allows 5.3. Indianapolis also runs for 3.8, against a Titans defense that allows 3.8. In the passing game, Tennessee's rush gets home on about one in every 16 dropbacks against a Colts front that allows one in 23, while Indianapolis gets a sack about once every 17 dropbacks against a Tennessee front that allows one in 19. None of those matchups is a clear advantage.

The calendar adds nothing; both teams are on a normal week.

For Tennessee, this is a chance to run at a defense that has given up yards on the ground. For Indianapolis, the job is to stop giving up points to a team that has struggled to score them.

My call: Colts 24, Titans 20.
