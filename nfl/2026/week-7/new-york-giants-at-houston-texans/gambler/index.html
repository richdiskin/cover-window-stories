New York Giants at Houston Texans

New York Giants (3-2-1) at Houston Texans (2-4)

1:00 PM ET Sun Oct 25 · Reliant Stadium, Houston, TX · Fox

Nine and a half is a lot to lay with a 2-4 team, even one that has won two in a row. I'll take the points, and now I know who's taking the snaps.

New York Giants (3-2-1) at Houston Texans (2-4), Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on Fox at Reliant Stadium. The consensus had Houston −9.5 with a total of 43; it's now −10.5 and 42.5. My card: New York Giants +9.5, Over 43, and a final of Texans 26, Giants 18, the average of four independent scores.

Why the Giants? Start with the honest part: The Formula puts the Texans at −1.8 and the Giants at −9.6. Add 1.75 for home field and it's Texans by 9.6. That's right on top of the number, and my averaged final, Texans 26, Giants 18, is an eight-point game, a point and a half short of 9.5. When the number is this big and the average lands inside it, I want the side getting the points. Houston has won two straight, but the Texans have scored 144 and allowed 143. That's not a team that has been burying people.

Here's what In The Trenches saw, and why it likes the Giants +9.5: New York's run defense gives up 5.3 yards a carry, but Houston only runs for 3.6, 1.6 yards a carry below what the Giants allow. A team that can't run can't sit on a big lead, and nine and a half is a lot of points to protect for four quarters.

The quarterback question is settled. After Wednesday's practice, John Harbaugh named J.J. McCarthy the starter, with Jameis Winston, who threw two interceptions last week, as the backup. McCarthy came on late against the Saints and led three straight scoring drives, and Harbaugh called the switch the plan going forward. It's still his first start in this offense, and he's facing Will Anderson Jr. and Danielle Hunter behind a leaky Giants front. On the other side, Houston running back David Montgomery is out with a groin injury, per Friday's injury report.

Why the Over? The Formula's number is 46.9, and it likes the Over 43 outright. My final adds up to 44, a point clear. The Giants just put up 27 in a game they trailed 34-10, and Houston's last game produced 65 points.

Now, the room, and this one gets loud. The Formula has Texans 28, Giants 18, and its card shows Houston −9.5. Everyone else has the Giants inside the number: Next Man Up at Texans 25-19, Letdown Watch at Texans 26-19 and In The Trenches at Texans 24-17. Follow The Money makes no pick and isn't in my average, but its market split is Texans 26, Giants 17, a 43-point game.

In The Trenches started it: "Laying nine and a half with a team that can't run the ball? Bold."

The Formula fired back: "Bold is taking the Giants with a quarterback making his first start for them. Ten points, genius, and I still sleep better."

Follow The Money stayed out of it.

Bottom of the card: an average that lands inside the number, a Houston run game that can't put teams away, and a total that's too low for the way both offenses just played. Take the 9.5 and the Over.

Texans 26, Giants 18. Giants cover the 9.5. Over 43.
