Cleveland Browns at Tennessee Titans

Cleveland Browns (4-2) at Tennessee Titans (1-5)

1:00 PM ET Sun Oct 25 · Nissan Stadium, Nashville, TN · CBS

Here's a confession before the case: three of my four cards say the Titans keep this inside the hook, and I agree. I'll take the 1.5 with Tennessee.

Cleveland Browns (4-2) at Tennessee Titans (1-5), Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on CBS at Nissan Stadium. The consensus had Cleveland −1.5 with a total of 38.5; it's now −2 and 37.5. My card: Tennessee Titans +1.5, Under 38.5, and a final of Browns 19, Titans 18, the average of four independent scores.

Why take the points? Because when you add up the whole room, Cleveland wins by exactly 1.5, right on the number, and on the number I take the points. My final has the Browns by one. The Formula puts Tennessee at −9.4 and Cleveland at −7.2, gives the Titans 1.75 for home field and gets Browns by 0.5, so that card takes the points too. Only Letdown Watch, with the Browns winning 20-17, gets Cleveland past the hook. This is a 4-2 road team against a 1-5 home team, but the Browns needed a late Derrick Henry fumble to beat Baltimore 13-10.

Now the risk, and it's a real one. All four Browns victories came down to the final moments, and a team that keeps finding the ending can find a two-point one. Quinshon Judkins was a full participant by Friday. But Tennessee just won its first game, 26-20 in Indianapolis, with six sacks, three takeaways and a blocked punt for a touchdown. The Titans' defense is real. Cleveland's defense got healthier as the week went on: Schwesinger and Mason Graham carry no game status on Friday's injury report.

Why the Under? Because these offenses haven't earned 38.5. The Formula's number is 36.8, and my final adds up to 37, a point and a half under 38.5. Both teams run for just 3.8 yards a carry. I'll admit the risk: both teams just found late points, Cleveland with 10 in the fourth quarter and Tennessee with 20 after halftime, and one special-teams score can wreck a low total. But I'll take two offenses that need four quarters to get going.

Now the fun part, because the room split on the side. The Formula has Browns 19, Titans 18. Next Man Up and In The Trenches both have Browns 20, Titans 19. All three are one-point games. Letdown Watch is the lone voice at Browns 20, Titans 17, and that card pulls my average right up to the 1.5 and not an inch past it. Follow The Money makes no pick, and its market split, Browns 20, Titans 19, isn't part of my average.

The Formula jumped on Letdown Watch first: "You want to lay points on the road with a team that needed a last-second kick to beat a backup quarterback? Bold."

Letdown Watch was ready: "You've got the Browns winning too, by one. That's not a pick, that's a shrug."

Next Man Up tried to change the subject. Letdown Watch wasn't done: the Titans just threw a party on Broadway, and parties come with mornings after. Follow The Money just smiled.

Bottom of the card: I expect a low-scoring Browns win by one, with the Titans keeping it inside the hook and the game staying under 38.5. It's close, and I'm telling you it's close.

Browns 19, Titans 18. Titans cover the 1.5. Under 38.5.
