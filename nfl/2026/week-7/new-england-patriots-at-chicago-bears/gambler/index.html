New England Patriots at Chicago Bears

New England Patriots (4-2) at Chicago Bears (4-2)

8:15 PM ET Thu Oct 22 · Soldier Field, Chicago, IL · Prime Video

Thursday night, Soldier Field, a short week for both teams, and the number says Chicago by 3. I say the number is a field goal too generous to the home team.

Here's the setup: New England Patriots (4-2) at Chicago Bears (4-2), 8:15 p.m. ET on Prime Video. The consensus has Chicago −3 with a total of 50.5 (Oct 20, 8:52 AM ET). My card: New England Patriots +3, Under 50.5, and a final of Patriots 24, Bears 22.

Why the Patriots? Because the season-long numbers don't see two equal teams. The Formula: Net ratings Patriots 1.6, Bears −4.5 (nflverse play-by-play, Oct. 20, 2026). Drake Maye has thrown at least three touchdown passes in three straight games, and the Patriots have won three in a row.

Now, the honest caveat, because I don't sell sunshine: the final injury report is out, and it isn't kind to New England. Guards Alijah Vera-Tucker and Ben Brown are out, so Walter Rouse, making his first career start, and Matt Pryor step in, and right tackle Morgan Moses is questionable with a foot injury. Cornerback Carlton Davis III is out again too. Chicago's pass rush gets home often, and Kyler Gordon just posted three sacks and two forced fumbles in Atlanta. With that line held together with tape on Thursday, Maye will feel it. Chicago has its own holes: running back Kyle Monangai and linebacker Jack Sanborn are out and linebacker T.J. Edwards is doubtful, though right guard Jonah Jackson is off the report.

Why the Under? Because these defenses don't hand out points. New England has allowed 113 in six games, and Chicago 119. The Formula's number is 43.2, and my final is 46, four and a half points shy of the posted 50.5. Caleb Williams is back from a hamstring and was sharp last week, but the Bears also handed out a pile of penalty yards in Atlanta, and drives that stall on flags don't end in touchdowns.

Now, the room didn't agree, and you know I love it when the room doesn't agree.

Follow The Money has no pick. The Formula's score is Patriots 24, Bears 19.

Next Man Up has Patriots 24, Bears 23, and Letdown Watch has Patriots 23, Bears 22, so both keep New England inside the field goal. In The Trenches went its own way, Bears 25, Patriots 23, and spent the whole meeting staring at New England's banged-up offensive line.

Letdown Watch couldn't resist: "Bears by two? You climbed the whole ladder and stopped one rung short."

Bottom of the card: the Patriots don't need to win this game to cash +3. They just need to keep it inside a field goal, and I think they win it outright.

Patriots 24, Bears 22. Patriots cover the 3. Under 50.5.
