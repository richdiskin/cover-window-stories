Green Bay Packers at Detroit Lions

Green Bay Packers (4-2) at Detroit Lions (3-2)

4:25 PM ET Sun Oct 25 · Ford Field, Detroit, MI · Fox

A full field goal in a rivalry game, with a Packers defense that hasn't allowed more than 14 points in three weeks? Thank you, I'll take those.

Green Bay Packers (4-2) at Detroit Lions (3-2), Sunday at 4:25 p.m. ET on Fox at Ford Field. The consensus had Detroit −3 with a total of 53.5; it's now −3.5 and 55.5. My card: Green Bay Packers +3, Under 53.5, and a final of Lions 26, Packers 25.

Why Green Bay? The Formula: Net ratings Packers −2.0, Lions −5.0 (nflverse play-by-play, Oct. 20, 2026). The Packers getting a field goal, with the better season-long numbers and the hotter defense? That's a gift. Even my final, which has Detroit winning by a point, keeps this inside the field goal. Green Bay just held Dallas without a first-half point, intercepted Dak Prescott twice, and won 23-13 despite three dropped touchdown passes.

The caveat, and it's a big one: Green Bay's run game averages 3.2 yards a carry, and Detroit allows 4.4. That's a big reason three of my four cards have Detroit winning. If Jordan Love has to drop back 40 times into a pass rush that lives in the backfield, Aidan Hutchinson could take over. Detroit also comes in rested off the bye.

Why the Under? I'll be honest with you about this one, because Detroit can score. The Formula's own number is 52.2, more than a point below 53.5. My final is Lions 26, Packers 25, which adds up to 51, two and a half points short. Green Bay's defense hasn't allowed more than 14 points in three weeks, its last game finished 23-13, and a Packers run game averaging 3.2 yards a carry tends to stall drives. The danger is Detroit: it has scored 164 points in five games, and its last outing was a 45-point explosion in Arizona with seven straight scoring drives. Jahmyr Gibbs ran for 151 yards in that one.

Now the room, and it's a rumble. The Formula has Packers 27, Lions 25 and likes Green Bay +3. Next Man Up and In The Trenches both have Lions 26, Packers 24, Detroit winning but inside the number. Letdown Watch has Lions 28, Packers 24 and takes Detroit −3. Follow The Money has no pick.

The Formula started it: "Lions minus three? Against a defense that hasn't allowed more than 14 points in three weeks? Good luck with that."

Letdown Watch snapped back: "Four points, and I'm not apologizing. Green Bay can't run, and Hutchinson can't wait."

Next Man Up and In The Trenches, sitting on the same score, are quietly enjoying this.

Bottom of the card: the better season-long numbers and the hotter defense, getting a full field goal in a division game. Take the points, and trust that Green Bay defense to keep this one just under the number.

Lions 26, Packers 25. Packers cover the 3. Under 53.5.
