Miami Dolphins at New York Jets

Miami Dolphins (0-5) at New York Jets (2-4)

1:00 PM ET Sun Oct 25 · MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, NJ · CBS

Miami is 0-5. The Jets are 2-4. Nobody's buying tickets to this one for the fireworks, but I think there will be just enough of them. And yes, I'm taking the winless team.

Miami Dolphins (0-5) at New York Jets (2-4), Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on CBS at MetLife Stadium. The consensus has the Jets −4.5 with a total of 39.5 (Oct 20, 12:52 UTC). My card: Miami Dolphins +4.5, Over 39.5, and a final of Jets 23, Dolphins 19, the average of four independent scores.

Why take the points with an 0-5 team? Because I think the Jets win, just not by enough. I'll give The Formula its due: it puts the Jets at −2.8 and Miami at −8.4, adds 1.75 for home field and gets Jets by 7.3, and it likes the Jets. But it's one card of four now. Next Man Up has Jets 22, Dolphins 21, and Letdown Watch has Jets 21, Dolphins 20. Those are one-point games, and both like Miami with the points. Put all four together and it's Jets by four, inside the number. And the Jets just lost 26-19 in Foxborough, a game Aaron Glenn said his team handed New England two touchdowns in.

The matchup I'm counting on: Miami runs for 5.0 yards a carry and the Jets allow 4.3. If Ollie Gordon II gets going, the Dolphins can shorten the game and keep it close. And Miami is rested off the bye.

The honest caveat: Malik Willis threw two interceptions and lost a fumble in his last outing against Cincinnati, and he's facing a Jets front with David Bailey, who just stripped the ball loose in Foxborough. If Miami hands the ball over like that again, the Jets cover with room to spare.

Why the Over? The Formula's number is 39.7, just above the posted 39.5, but the other cards go higher, and Next Man Up and In The Trenches both have 43. My averaged final lands at Jets 23, Dolphins 19. That's 42, two and a half points over. The path is there: Geno Smith should have time against the quietest rush in this matchup, he threw for 252 yards last week, and Miami's run game can keep drives alive on the other side.

The room? Not so peaceful anymore. The Formula has Jets 24, Dolphins 16 and wants to lay it. In The Trenches has Jets 24, Dolphins 19. Next Man Up and Letdown Watch have Miami within a point. Follow The Money makes no pick and isn't in my average, and its market split is Jets 22, Dolphins 18.

The Formula went first: "You're taking the points with a team that has scored 70 in five games? That's 14 a game. Bring a calculator."

Next Man Up shot back: "And you're laying 4.5 with a team that just gift-wrapped two touchdowns for New England. Bring a mop."

Bottom of the card: a rested team with a run game, a Jets team that just handed a game away, and two offenses that should find just enough points. Take the 4.5 and the Over.

Jets 23, Dolphins 19. Dolphins cover the 4.5. Over 39.5.
