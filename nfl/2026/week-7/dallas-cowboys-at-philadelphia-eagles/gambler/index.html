Dallas Cowboys at Philadelphia Eagles

Dallas Cowboys (2-4) at Philadelphia Eagles (2-4)

8:15 PM ET Mon Oct 26 · Lincoln Financial Field, Philadelphia, PA · ESPN, ABC, Disney+

XFacebook

Dallas is laying 4.5 points on the road. On Monday night. In Philadelphia. Off a 2-4 start. I've seen braver things, but not many.

Dallas Cowboys (2-4) at Philadelphia Eagles (2-4), Monday, Oct. 26 at 8:15 p.m. ET on ESPN, ABC and Disney+ at Lincoln Financial Field. The consensus has Dallas −4.5 with a total of 44.5 (Oct 20, 8:52 AM ET). My card: Philadelphia Eagles +4.5, Over 44.5, and a final of Eagles 24, Cowboys 23.

Why the Eagles? Because once you strip away the names, these teams are dead even. The Formula: Net ratings Cowboys −5.7, Eagles −7.4 (nflverse play-by-play, Oct. 20, 2026). My final has Philadelphia winning by one, and you're still getting 4.5. The Formula likes the side, and so do I.

I know what you're thinking: no Jalen Hurts. He's on injured reserve with a blood clot in his calf, Saquon Barkley's hamstring is a question, and Tanner McKee struggled against Carolina. That's real, and the first official injury report doesn't come out until Thursday. But Dallas hasn't exactly earned a road number like this. The Cowboys just got shut out in the first half at Green Bay and threw two interceptions in scoring range.

The caveat: Philadelphia runs for just 3.8 yards a carry against a Dallas defense that allows 4.9, and that's the matchup that worries me most. Dallas also protects Dak Prescott well; he's rarely sacked.

Why the Over? The Formula's number is 46.1, and my final adds up to 47. Dallas has scored 151 and allowed 159 in six games. That's a team whose games tend to have some points in them.

Now the room. The Formula has Eagles 24, Cowboys 23. Letdown Watch has Eagles 25, Cowboys 21. Next Man Up has Cowboys 23, Eagles 22, and In The Trenches has Cowboys 26, Eagles 25. Split on the winner, but all four like Philadelphia +4.5. Follow The Money has no pick.

In The Trenches opened: "Philly can't run, and Dallas doesn't give up sacks. I've got the Cowboys winning, and I still want the 4.5. That's how big this number is."

The Formula: "First sensible thing you've said all season."

Next Man Up and Letdown Watch, parked right next to The Formula on the Eagles, pretended to check their phones.

Bottom of the card: two even teams, a road team laying more than it's earned, and a Monday night crowd that will be loud. Take the points and the Over.

Eagles 24, Cowboys 23. Eagles cover the 4.5. Over 44.5.
