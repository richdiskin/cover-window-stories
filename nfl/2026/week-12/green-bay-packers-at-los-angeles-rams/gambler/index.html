My Packers vs Rams prediction for Wednesday night starts with two teams that each had last Sunday off and come into SoFi Stadium at six wins and four losses. I'm laying the spread with Rams −2.5, going Over 44.5 for the over/under pick, and writing down Rams 26, Packers 20.

Thanksgiving week started early in Inglewood. On November 18, Hollywood Park and the Rams handed turkeys and grocery boxes to the first 2,500 cars in the Red Zone at SoFi, with captain Quentin Lake on hand. Teresa Ocampo was out there waving cars through, and she's carried that same energy into a Wednesday night home date with the Packers.

The Formula: Net ratings Los Angeles Rams 2.3, Green Bay Packers −3.7 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 24, 2026).

The case for Los Angeles is the last month. The Rams have won four straight, at Las Vegas, against the Chargers, at Washington and 34-6 at Arizona, and they've allowed 187 points in ten games, well short of Green Bay's 221. Matthew Stafford has 19 touchdown throws and Kyren Williams has 616 rushing yards with six touchdowns behind him. The Rams will do it without Puka Nacua, who is on injured reserve after knee surgery, according to the latest injury listings.

Gus Lindqvist has a real answer from Green Bay. Jordan Love has 20 touchdown throws against seven interceptions, and Christian Watson has 916 yards and seven scores. The Packers are 3-2 on the road, with wins at the Jets, Tampa Bay and New England. But the last time anyone saw them, Minnesota beat them 30-10 at Lambeau, and that sits with you for ten days.

Rest is even. Both teams have had 10 days since their last game, and only Green Bay has to cross the country. That's why this is a short number and not a big one, and why a field goal is about the right size.
