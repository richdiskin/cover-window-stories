Kansas City Chiefs at Buffalo Bills

Kansas City Chiefs (8-2) at Buffalo Bills (9-1)

8:20 PM ET Thu Nov 26 · Highmark Stadium, Orchard Park, NY · NBC

XFacebook

My Chiefs vs Bills prediction for Thanksgiving night is a game that goes to the final possession, and that's why I want the points. Chiefs +3 earns my spread pick, Over 45.5 handles the over/under, and Bills 25, Chiefs 23 is the score I'm standing behind.

Buffalo opens the day with the 131st YMCA Turkey Trot on a new loop course that starts and finishes at the Buffalo Convention Center, and it closes the day with the best game on the holiday slate. Marty Kowalczyk is running the race in the morning and sitting in Orchard Park at night.

The Formula: Net ratings Buffalo Bills 3.0, Kansas City Chiefs 4.4 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 24, 2026).

Buffalo is 9-1 and has won six straight since the Week 4 loss to New England. James Cook III has 1,060 rushing yards and 10 touchdowns, Greg Rousseau has 10 sacks, and the Bills have scored 313 points, most of anyone in this matchup. Josh Allen has 14 touchdown throws with nine interceptions, though, and that's the opening Kansas City needs.

Della Ruiz brings a Chiefs team that is 8-2 and has allowed just 163 points. Patrick Mahomes has 22 touchdown throws against four interceptions, Kenneth Walker III has 872 rushing yards, and George Karlaftis has eight sacks. Walker is ruled out with a groin injury, and Buffalo's Greg Rousseau was a full participant in practice and carries no game status on the latest injury report. A defense that good, with a quarterback who doesn't give the ball away, doesn't lose by more than a field goal very often.

Both teams are on four days of rest after Sunday wins, and only the Chiefs have to fly in.

The forecast calls for rain, and that's the only real worry for the Over. These are still two offenses built around quarterbacks who make plays late. Bills 25, Chiefs 23 adds up to 48, two and a half points clear of 45.5, and a two-point Buffalo win keeps Kansas City inside the 3 by a point. Tight, yes. That's this game.

Marty gets his win on the biggest night of the year in Orchard Park. Della gets the points, a soaked red jacket and a story about running a race she never signed up for.

Bills 25, Chiefs 23. Chiefs cover the 3. Over 45.5.
