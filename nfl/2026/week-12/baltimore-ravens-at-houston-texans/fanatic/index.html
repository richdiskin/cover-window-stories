Baltimore Ravens at Houston Texans

Baltimore Ravens (4-7) at Houston Texans (4-6)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 29 · Reliant Stadium, Houston, TX · CBS

XFacebook

Lupe Garza was downtown at nine o'clock Thanksgiving morning for the 77th H-E-B Thanksgiving Day Parade, the way her family has done since her abuela pushed her down the sidewalk in a stroller. She'll tell you it's the second-best thing that happens in this city in late November. The first, as of last week, is a Texans home win.

Desmond Hargrove flew in from Dundalk on Saturday and asked what took the Texans so long.

Desmond: Ten games, and you finally won at home?

Lupe: We beat the Colts by twenty-three. We're not apologizing for when it happened.

It's been a strange year for Lupe. The Texans lost their first four at Reliant, and she started to wonder if the building was haunted. Then Indianapolis came to town on a Thursday night and Houston finally exhaled. The Texans have four wins now, and three of them came away from home, in Tennessee, in London and in Los Angeles. She calls it the year of the road trip.

Desmond's year has been worse, and he knows it. The Ravens have lost six straight. Lamar Jackson is out, and Desmond still watches the highlights of September like old home movies. Baltimore was four wins and one loss once. Now it's four and seven, and Dundalk is in mourning.

Desmond: Derrick Henry has eleven rushing touchdowns. Eleven. He's thirty-two and running through people like it's still Tennessee.

Lupe: Will Anderson Jr. has eleven and a half sacks, and he's twenty-five.

That's the matchup both of them care about. Henry is the last thing in Baltimore that still scares people, and Desmond's whole plan is to hand him the ball thirty times and dare Houston to do something about it. Anderson is the Texans' best player, and Lupe's plan is for him to live in the backfield all afternoon.

C.J. Stroud is the other part of Lupe's case. He has 22 touchdown throws and only four interceptions, and she calls him "the calmest man in Harris County." Nico Collins has six touchdown catches and is the one Stroud looks for when he needs it. Desmond counters with Zay Flowers, who has 785 receiving yards and is quicker than anything on the Texans' secondary, and Roquan Smith, who has 91 tackles and never seems to stop.

Desmond: Roquan Smith is going to be in Stroud's face all day.

Lupe: Stroud's been in tougher spots. He survived the first half of this season.

The Ravens' slide has been a slow one. They lost close ones in Cleveland and to the Chargers. They lost big in Buffalo. Last Sunday they scored 33 in Carolina and still lost by two, which Desmond says is the most Ravens thing that has ever happened. He still thinks this team has a game like this in it, a day where the defense shows up and Henry carries everybody.

Lupe thinks the Texans are just getting started. Houston had ten days to get ready, while Baltimore is back on the road for a second straight week, and Lupe has been reminding Desmond about it since he landed.

Lupe: Rest matters. My abuela says so.

Desmond: Your abuela doesn't know Derrick Henry.

Lupe: Everybody in Texas knows Derrick Henry. He went to high school in Florida, but we've adopted him.

They'll eat leftover turkey tamales in the parking lot, which Desmond had never heard of until Saturday and now won't stop talking about. Lupe will wear her Battle Red. Desmond will wear purple and a look of grim determination.

Desmond: One win. That's all I want for Christmas.

Lupe: Ask Santa. He was in the parade.

My call: Texans 26, Ravens 20.
