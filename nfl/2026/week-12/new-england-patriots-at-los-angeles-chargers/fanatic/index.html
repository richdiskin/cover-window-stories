The Fanatic · NFL

New England Patriots at Los Angeles Chargers

New England Patriots (5-5) at Los Angeles Chargers (1-9)

4:05 PM ET Sun Nov 29 · SoFi Stadium, Inglewood, CA · CBS

XFacebook

Colleen Sheehan booked her hotel in Hollywood because it looked fun on the website. Then Benny Ishikawa sent her the road closures. The Hollywood Christmas Parade is on Sunday, Hollywood Boulevard between Orange and Highland is shut down all weekend, and Highland, Vine and Sunset close starting at four in the afternoon.

Benny: So you'll be fine getting to the game. Getting back to your hotel is your problem.

Colleen: I'm from Quincy. I've been stuck behind worse.

Benny lives in Torrance and has had Chargers season tickets since the team came up the freeway from San Diego. He's not having a good year. Nobody in powder blue is. The Chargers have one win in ten games, and they haven't won at home at all. Benny has been to all five home losses and stayed until the end of every one, which his wife calls loyalty and his brother calls a cry for help.

Benny: We beat Baltimore. On the road. That happened.

Colleen: That was two weeks ago.

Benny: And it was beautiful.

He's not wrong about the beautiful part. A win is a win when you've only got one. And last Sunday the Chargers scored plenty against the Jets and still lost by three, which Benny swears felt like progress.

Colleen's Patriots are sitting at five and five, and she is mostly at peace with it. Drake Maye broke his right fibula in the Detroit game and is listed out, and Colleen is trying very hard not to talk about it. She'll tell you the Patriots won four of five in one stretch this fall, including a blowout of Las Vegas, and she'll leave out the last two weeks, a home loss to Green Bay and a long afternoon against Detroit in Munich.

Colleen: The Munich thing doesn't count. That was basically a vacation.

Benny: You lost by twenty-five.

Colleen: On vacation.

The Patriots have had two weeks to forget about it, with a bye last weekend, and Colleen thinks the rest is exactly what they needed. Elijah Ponder has seven and a half sacks. Christian Elliss has been everywhere. DeMario Douglas has been the most reliable receiver on the roster.

Benny's case is shorter, and it starts with Justin Herbert getting through the concussion protocol. Omarion Hampton runs hard. Khalil Mack is still out there chasing quarterbacks at an age when most players are coaching high school.

Benny: All we need is one clean game. No turnovers. Herbert gets time. Hampton gets going. That's it.

Colleen: That's a lot of things.

Benny: It's three things.

Colleen: That's three more than you've had at home.

That one hurt, and she knew it. She bought him a churro at the stadium to make up for it, which he accepted without saying thank you.

Both of them expect points. The Chargers' defense has had trouble keeping people out of the end zone, and the Patriots have put up big numbers at times this year. Benny thinks Herbert is due for a big day in his own building. Colleen thinks her team has had too long to sit with the Munich loss to come out flat.

What they agree on is that it'll be close at the end, and that the walk back to the car will be miserable for one of them. Colleen is hoping she can get back to Hollywood before the parade traffic shuts everything down. Benny has offered to drive her, which is generous, and also means she has to listen to his talk-radio station the whole way.

Benny: Win or lose, I'm getting you to the parade.

Colleen: Win or lose, I'm wearing this jersey to the parade.

My call: Patriots 24, Chargers 21.
