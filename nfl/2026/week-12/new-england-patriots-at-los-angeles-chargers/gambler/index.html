New England Patriots at Los Angeles Chargers

New England Patriots (5-5) at Los Angeles Chargers (1-9)

4:05 PM ET Sun Nov 29 · SoFi Stadium, Inglewood, CA · CBS

XFacebook

My Patriots vs Chargers prediction is a New England road win against a Los Angeles team that hasn't won at home all year. Patriots +1.5 gets my spread pick, a low 35.5 sends my over/under call to the Over, and I've got it Patriots 24, Chargers 21.

The Hollywood Christmas Parade fills Hollywood Boulevard on Sunday, and Benny Ishikawa will spend the afternoon at SoFi hoping the Chargers give Los Angeles something else to celebrate. His team is 1-9 and 0-5 at home.

The Formula: Net ratings Los Angeles Chargers −8.3, New England Patriots −2.8 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 24, 2026).

New England is 5-5 and comes off a bye after losses to Green Bay and to Detroit in Munich. Drake Maye has 18 touchdown throws against 11 interceptions, but he is listed out with a fractured right fibula from the Munich loss, according to the latest injury listings, and Tommy DeVito also played quarterback in that game. Elijah Ponder has 7.5 sacks. The Patriots are 5-1 against AFC teams, and the long cross-country trip is softened by two weeks to prepare. New England scored 45 points against Las Vegas in a 45-17 win in Week 5.

The Chargers have scored only 168 points in ten games. Justin Herbert has eight touchdown throws and 11 interceptions and is listed questionable after entering the concussion protocol, according to the latest injury listings. Omarion Hampton has 563 rushing yards. Los Angeles has lost close games, 27-24 to the Jets last Sunday, and it has been blown out, 44-22 by Houston at home.

The room is unanimous. The Formula has Chargers 21, Patriots 24. Next Man Up has Chargers 21, Patriots 24. Letdown Watch has Chargers 20, Patriots 26. In The Trenches has Chargers 21, Patriots 23. All four take the Patriots +1.5 and Over 35.5. Follow The Money offers no pick this week.

The total is the number that jumps out. At 35.5, it is low for these two teams, and both quarterback situations explain why. Eight of the Chargers' ten games and eight of New England's ten finished with more than 35.5 points. The Chargers' defense has allowed 266 points, and the Patriots have scored 221. The forecast is 72 degrees, and SoFi has a roof.

Patriots 24, Chargers 21 adds up to 45, which clears 35.5 by nine and a half, and a three-point New England win is a point and a half more than the spread needs. If New England's offense looks like it did against Las Vegas, this could get away from Los Angeles early. If New England's offense stalls instead, a three-point game is still the most likely finish.

Colleen Sheehan gets the road win and a late drive back to Hollywood through parade closures. Benny gets one more Sunday of waiting for the home win that hasn't come.

Patriots 24, Chargers 21. Patriots cover the 1.5. Over 35.5.
