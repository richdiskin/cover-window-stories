Carolina Panthers at Tampa Bay Buccaneers

Carolina Panthers (5-5) at Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-7)

8:15 PM ET Mon Nov 30 · Raymond James Stadium, Tampa, FL · ESPN

XFacebook

There's an ice rink on the Hillsborough River right now. Winter Village is up at Curtis Hixon Waterfront Park, put on by the Lightning, and Eddie Valdés took his kids skating there on Saturday. Seventeen dollars a skater with rentals, seventy-five minutes on the ice, and Eddie spent most of it holding onto the wall. He's lived in Ybor City his whole life and has never claimed to be a winter person.

Tamika Rhodes drove down from Gastonia on Sunday, eight hours with one stop for gas, and Eddie made her go skating too.

Tamika: I didn't drive five hundred miles to fall on ice in Florida.

Eddie: You didn't fall.

Tamika: I came close four times.

Monday night is what she came for. Her Panthers have won two straight and sit at five and five, right in the thick of things. Last Sunday they beat Baltimore by two points, and Tamika says her voice still hasn't come all the way back.

Tamika: Bryce Young has eighteen touchdown throws. Chuba Hubbard has nine rushing touchdowns. Tetairoa McMillan catches everything.

Eddie: We beat you by thirty in your own building.

Tamika: That was October. Different team.

It was a long day for Carolina, and Eddie hasn't let her forget it. That was the high point of the Buccaneers' year, a stretch in the middle of the season when they won three of four and looked like themselves. Since then they've lost two straight, to Chicago by three and in Detroit, and Eddie is starting to feel the way he did in September when they lost the first four.

Eddie: Bucky Irving is the best thing about this team. Eight hundred and ninety-three yards. Eight touchdowns. He runs angry.

Tamika: Everybody on your team runs angry. You're three and seven.

Eddie: That's why.

The Bucs have been rough at home this year, with only one win at Raymond James, and that bothers Eddie more than anything. He grew up going to games at the old stadium with his dad. Home is supposed to mean something. Josiah Trotter has ninety tackles in the middle of the defense, and David Walker has five sacks. Eddie thinks the defense can get after Young if the crowd gets loud.

Tamika's defense doesn't have big names, but Princely Umanmielen has been getting home, and Devin Lloyd leads the team in tackles. She thinks the Panthers are better than they've been in years. She's been waiting a long time to say that.

Tamika: We're not the team you beat in October.

Eddie: You keep saying that.

Tamika: Because it keeps being true.

Both of them expect a close one. The Panthers have shown they can score, and the Bucs have shown they can run the ball when they need to. Eddie figures a home crowd on a Monday night gives Tampa Bay just enough to get it done. Tamika figures it'll come down to Young having the ball with a couple of minutes left and a chance to make something happen.

Eddie: If it's close at the end, I'm closing my eyes.

Tamika: You can't close your eyes during Monday Night Football.

Eddie: Watch me.

After the game they're going back to Winter Village. Eddie wants another shot at the rink. Tamika wants one of the hot chocolates she saw on the way in and the right to stand at the wall and watch.

Tamika: I'm not skating again.

Eddie: You'll skate.

My call: Buccaneers 24, Panthers 22.
