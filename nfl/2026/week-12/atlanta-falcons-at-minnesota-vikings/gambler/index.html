Atlanta Falcons at Minnesota Vikings

Atlanta Falcons (3-7) at Minnesota Vikings (7-3)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 29 · U.S. Bank Stadium, Minneapolis, MN · Fox

XFacebook

This Falcons vs Vikings prediction is about a Minnesota team that wins but doesn't run anybody off the field. My pick against the spread lands on Falcons +7, the over/under is Under 42.5, and the score is Vikings 22, Falcons 18.

Six blocks of Nicollet Mall close Friday night for the Shine On Minneapolis block party and the Peavey Plaza tree lighting, and Ingrid Haugen will be there. By Sunday she wants the Vikings to look like the team that started 6-0, not the one that lost 35-10 to San Francisco in Mexico City. Minnesota won its first six games before going 1-3 since.

The Formula: Net ratings Minnesota Vikings −5.3, Atlanta Falcons −3.6 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 24, 2026).

Minnesota is 7-3 and has allowed only 161 points, but it has scored just 186. That's the whole case for the points. The Vikings' wins this season came by scores like 9-3 at Chicago, 15-10 over Miami and 16-10 over Indianapolis. A team that wins like that rarely wins by more than a touchdown. Kirk Cousins started the Mexico City game and is the card's expected starter, with Carson Wentz also used in that one.

Terrence Ojo's Falcons are 3-7, but they come off a bye and have shown they can win on the road, 35-14 at Green Bay. Bijan Robinson has 951 rushing yards and Drake London has 801 receiving yards. Atlanta also beat San Francisco 29-20, so this is not a team that folds against good opponents.

The room mostly sides with Atlanta. The Formula has Vikings 20, Falcons 21. Next Man Up has Vikings 20, Falcons 18. In The Trenches has Vikings 20, Falcons 17. Those three take the Falcons +7 and the Under. Letdown Watch has Vikings 26, Falcons 17, lays the 7 with Minnesota and is on Over 42.5. Follow The Money has no pick.

The Under follows the Minnesota defense. Andrew Van Ginkel has 8.5 sacks and Blake Cashman has 85 tackles, and the Vikings' games have been low all year. Atlanta has scored 201 points in ten games. The game is indoors, so the 37-degree forecast doesn't matter. Vikings 22, Falcons 18 adds up to 40, two and a half points short of 42.5, and a four-point win leaves Atlanta three points inside the 7.

Ingrid gets her home win and a walk back down Nicollet. Terrence gets the points and a lesson about skyways.

Vikings 22, Falcons 18. Falcons cover the 7. Under 42.5.
