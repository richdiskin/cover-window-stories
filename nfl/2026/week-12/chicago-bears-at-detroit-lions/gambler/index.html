Chicago Bears at Detroit Lions

Chicago Bears (8-2) at Detroit Lions (8-2)

1:00 PM ET Thu Nov 26 · Ford Field, Detroit, MI · CBS

XFacebook

The Bears vs Lions prediction for Thanksgiving starts with a strange fact: Detroit is undefeated at home and still getting points. I'm picking Lions +1.5 on the spread, Under 55.5 goes down on the over/under, and I'm penciling in Lions 27, Bears 24.

Detroit gets its 100th America's Thanksgiving Parade on Woodward before kickoff, with Dan Gilbert as grand marshal and 33 floats. Darnell Whitfield will walk from the end of the route at Congress Street straight to Ford Field, where his Lions are 5-0 this season, 6-0 at home counting the Munich game that went down as a home date.

The Formula: Net ratings Detroit Lions 0.3, Chicago Bears −4.8 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 24, 2026).

That home record is the backbone of the side. Detroit is 8-2, has won six straight since the Week 4 loss at Carolina, and has scored 288 points. Jahmyr Gibbs has 1,098 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns. Jared Goff has 20 touchdown throws against four interceptions, and Amon-Ra St. Brown has eight touchdown catches. Getting 1.5 with that team in that building is the bargain of the holiday.

Kasia Nowicki will tell you Chicago is no fluke, and she's right. The Bears are 8-2 too, with five straight wins, including 34-17 at Seattle. Caleb Williams has 10 touchdown throws against six interceptions, and D'Andre Swift has 10 rushing touchdowns. Chicago has allowed 197 points, fewer than Detroit's 220. This is a close game. That's exactly why I want the points with the home team.

Both teams played Sunday and both have four days of rest.

The Under is about the size of the number, not the quality of the offenses. Every card score in the room lands well short of 55.5, and a short-week division game rarely gets to 56. Lions 27, Bears 24 adds up to 51, four and a half points short of 55.5, and any Detroit win covers the 1.5.

Darnell gets the hundredth parade and a home win to go with it. Kasia gets a long drive back to Berwyn and a team that's still very much in this race.

Lions 27, Bears 24. Lions cover the 1.5. Under 55.5.
