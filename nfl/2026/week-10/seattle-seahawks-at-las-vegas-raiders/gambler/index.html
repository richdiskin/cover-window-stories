Seattle Seahawks at Las Vegas Raiders

Seattle Seahawks (5-4) at Las Vegas Raiders (4-5)

4:05 PM ET Sun Nov 15 · Allegiant Stadium, Las Vegas, NV · CBS

Grand Prix crews have the Las Vegas Strip torn up all week, with Koval Lane closed from Saturday through Nov. 23, and the Raiders' run game has looked stuck in that kind of traffic all season. My Seahawks vs Raiders prediction for Week 10 is the Seattle Seahawks −3.5 against the spread, and my over/under call is Over 43.5.

Tuesday's Action Network consensus has Seattle laying 3.5 on the road, total 43.5.

The Formula: Net ratings Las Vegas Raiders −7.4, Seattle Seahawks 2.4 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 10, 2026).

Start on the ground, because that's where this one tilts. Las Vegas gains 3.1 yards a carry, and Seattle allows 3.6. Ashton Jeanty has the talent, but he hasn't had the room. Going the other way, the Raiders allow 5.3 a carry, which lets Seattle stay ahead of the chains no matter who's lined up in the backfield.

Mike Macdonald cleared up the quarterback plan on Monday. Sam Darnold will miss another week with quad and rib injuries, and Drew Lock, who started last week's 17-3 win against Arizona, is expected to start again through an oblique injury. I'm pricing this with the backup taking the snaps, and the Seahawks still look like the better team.

The records back it up. Seattle is 5-4 with 202 points scored and only 162 allowed. Las Vegas is 4-5 and 1-3 at home, with 238 scored and 268 allowed, and it's coming off a 38-17 loss at San Francisco. The Raiders traded Kirk Cousins to Minnesota at Tuesday's deadline, so No. 1 pick Fernando Mendoza makes his first NFL start, and Brock Bowers is the first place he'll look. Maxx Crosby can still wreck a game by himself. That doesn't fix a defense that has given up 268 points in nine games.

That number drives the Over. Seattle doesn't need a big day to put points on that defense, and Las Vegas has scored 238, and Bowers gives a rookie somewhere to go. Allegiant Stadium has a roof, so there's no wind to worry about.

The cards line up behind Seattle. All four with a score lay the 3.5: The Formula at Seahawks 28, Raiders 20, Letdown Watch at Seahawks 28, Raiders 21, Next Man Up at Seahawks 24, Raiders 18, and In The Trenches at Seahawks 23, Raiders 18. Two of the four are Over. Next Man Up and In The Trenches both see a grind. Follow The Money has no call on either question.

There's a coaching subplot too. Klint Kubiak called Seattle's offense in 2025, won Super Bowl LX, and now runs the Raiders. Familiarity cuts both ways, though, and Mike Macdonald has had a full week to change what Kubiak thinks he knows.

My card says Seattle Seahawks 26, Las Vegas Raiders 19. Seven points clears the 3.5, and 45 total points clears 43.5 by one and a half.

The race cars arrive next week. This week, Seattle gets through the traffic first.

Seahawks 26, Raiders 19. Seahawks cover the 3.5. Over 43.5.
