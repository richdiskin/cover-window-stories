Los Angeles Rams at Arizona Cardinals

Los Angeles Rams (5-4) at Arizona Cardinals (3-6)

4:05 PM ET Sun Nov 15 · State Farm Stadium, Glendale, AZ · CBS

XFacebook

Nine and a half points is a long walk across the State Farm Stadium lots, and those lots spent Friday and Saturday packed for Dino's Git Down, a free GM car show with more than 250 vendors. My Rams vs Cardinals prediction for Week 10 takes the Arizona Cardinals +9.5 against the spread, and my over/under call is Over 40.5.

The Rams are 9.5-point road favorites in the Action Network consensus as of Nov. 10, with the total sitting at 40.5.

The Formula: Net ratings Arizona Cardinals −6.5, Los Angeles Rams 2.3 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 10, 2026).

The Rams are the better team, and I'm not pretending otherwise. They're 5-4 and 3-1 on the road, with 214 points scored and 181 allowed, and they won 20-13 at Washington last week. Arizona is 3-6 with 178 scored and 255 allowed, and it hasn't won at home.

But Arizona already beat these Rams once, 18-16 in Los Angeles in Week 6. The Cardinals are 3-3 on the road. And their head coach, Mike LaFleur, was the Rams' offensive coordinator from 2023 through 2025. Nobody in that building is going to be surprised by what Sean McVay wants to do on first down.

The quarterback situation deserves a straight answer. Jacoby Brissett started Arizona's first eight games before he was benched in Week 8 and traded to Baltimore, and Gardner Minshew started last week's 17-3 loss at Seattle. Minshew went on injured reserve Tuesday with a season-ending arm injury, so rookie Carson Beck makes his first NFL start. Even with that, nine and a half in a division game is a big ask.

Now the total, which looks low to me. The Rams have scored 214 in nine games, and Arizona has given up 255. Los Angeles runs for 4.9 yards a carry against a defense that allows 4.3, so Kyren Williams should keep Matthew Stafford ahead of schedule, with Puka Nacua waiting downfield. Arizona doesn't need much from Trey McBride and Jeremiyah Love to push this past 40.5.

This is the rare game where the room doesn't argue. All four cards with a score take Arizona plus the points, and all four are on the Over. The Formula and Next Man Up both have Rams 27, Cardinals 19. Letdown Watch has Rams 26, Cardinals 19, and In The Trenches has Rams 26, Cardinals 20. Follow The Money has no position on either.

My card says Los Angeles Rams 27, Arizona Cardinals 19. That's an eight-point Rams win, inside the 9.5, and 46 points, five and a half clear of 40.5.

The car show in Glendale had one hard rule this weekend: no burnouts. The Rams win this game without leaving tire marks on the spread.

Rams 27, Cardinals 19. Cardinals cover the 9.5. Over 40.5.
