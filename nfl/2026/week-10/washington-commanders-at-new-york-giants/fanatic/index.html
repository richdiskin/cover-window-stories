Washington Commanders at New York Giants

Washington Commanders (2-5-1) at New York Giants (5-2-1)

8:15 PM ET Thu Nov 12 · MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, NJ · Prime Video

XFacebook

Washington Commanders vs New York Giants on Thursday night in Week 10 comes with a dress code: the Giants have asked every fan at MetLife Stadium to wear white for a full White Out, with the team in its Vintage White uniforms, a rally towel and a light-up band for every fan, and fireworks and a laser show for anyone in place by 7:45. Leon Drobny has been practicing towel waves since Monday, and Claire Benton packed nothing but burgundy and a folded copy of the Week 5 box score.

Claire doesn't do gimmicks. She does structure. She is the Commanders fan who runs every race in the District with a pace chart taped to her wrist, and she has been reading that box score all week like it owes her rent.

Claire: Twenty-seven to twenty-seven. Read it with me, Leon.

Leon: I framed it. Fourteen straight points in the last eleven minutes. That was my comeback, not your tie.

He has a point and she hates it. Washington led by fourteen in the fourth quarter at home five weeks ago, watched the Giants climb all the way back, and then let overtime run dry while sitting in field goal range. Nobody in Washington has explained that last part to Claire's satisfaction. Dan Quinn said after Sunday's 20-13 loss to the Rams that all three phases have to finally show up together, and Claire agreed out loud, alone, in her kitchen.

Leon, meanwhile, is in a good mood, which unsettles everyone who knows him. The Giants went into Philadelphia on Sunday and won 19-11, their first win in that building in thirteen years. Arvell Reese, the rookie linebacker taken fifth overall, had twelve tackles, three sacks and a forced fumble on a hit people are still replaying. Leon keeps a framed list of every upset this franchise ever pulled in January and February. He's thinking about starting a second frame for November.

Leon: Reese is twenty-one. Twenty-one! He's younger than my car.

Claire: Your car has a tape deck. Everybody's younger than your car.

What worries Claire isn't the kid. It's the short week. Washington is 2-5-1 and 0-3 on the road, and it's walking into a building that plans to flash thousands of light-up wristbands at its quarterback. Jayden Daniels was a full participant on Monday and Tuesday, even with a left elbow listed, and he's the whole reason Claire still drives up the Turnpike. When Daniels gets outside the pocket, everything Washington wants opens up. The Commanders run for 4.7 yards a carry, and this Giants defense allows 4.8. Claire wants handoffs, a moving clock and a full house sitting on its towels.

Leon has worries too, and he reads them off like a hospital chart. J.J. McCarthy hurt his hip in Philadelphia, but he was a full go in practice by Tuesday, and John Harbaugh said it looks like he'll play. Cam Skattebo sat out Sunday with a hamstring, and Harbaugh expects him to be full-go. Leon trusts Harbaugh. He has trusted exactly two head coaches in his life, and the other one beat New England twice in the Super Bowl.

Claire: You're 5-2-1, and only one of those wins came by double digits.

Leon: You're 2-5-1, and you've won two games. Period. Wait, don't write that down.

She already did. The Giants have scored 182 and allowed 172, the profile of a team that lives in one-score games and likes it there. Washington has scored 172 and allowed 202. Claire doesn't argue with arithmetic she can't fix, so she argues with the building instead.

Claire: All that white just makes burgundy easier to spot. Daniels will find me.

Leon: He'll find you during the laser show. That's when the whole building turns around and stares at you.

Claire will stand there anyway, arms folded, the only dark spot in her section, pace chart still on her wrist out of habit. Leon will wave his towel in her face after every third down. The tie still lives in both of their heads, and after Thursday only one of them gets to stop thinking about it.

My call: Giants 23, Commanders 22.
