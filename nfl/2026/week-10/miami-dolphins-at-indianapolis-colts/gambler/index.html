Miami Dolphins at Indianapolis Colts

Miami Dolphins (1-7) at Indianapolis Colts (2-7)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 15 · Lucas Oil Stadium, Indianapolis, IN · CBS

XFacebook

Indiana's favorite underdog movie turns forty, and Hoosiers gets a Sunday screening with a Q and A at the Palladium in Carmel while the Colts are playing. My Dolphins vs Colts prediction doesn't need a miracle: the pick is Colts 24, Dolphins 20, which hands Indianapolis −2.5 my spread vote and sends the over/under to the Over at 40.5.

Two bad teams, and I'm not dressing it up. Miami is 1-7 and winless at home, with 126 points scored and 200 allowed. Indianapolis is 2-7 and has lost five straight, with 184 scored and 249 allowed. The question isn't who's good. It's who's less broken in this building on this afternoon.

The Formula: Net ratings Indianapolis Colts −8.9, Miami Dolphins −8.5 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 10, 2026).

The Colts have the stronger offense and home field, and that's enough. They're 1-3 at home, not great, but Miami is 1-3 on the road, and its last game was a 19-9 loss in which it never reached the end zone against Detroit. The Colts have also won three straight in the series. A four-point Colts win lands a point and a half past the 2.5, and that's the score I'm defending.

The risk is Daniel Jones. He's thrown ten interceptions against six touchdowns and has been picked off in eight of nine games, including four straight. That's why this isn't a bigger number, and it's why Miami can hang around. Kyle McCord makes his first NFL start now that Malik Willis is on injured reserve, and Miami needs its run game to keep him in manageable downs.

The Over is about the run defenses. Miami runs for 4.5 yards a carry, and Indianapolis allows 5.3, the worst number on either side of this matchup. The Colts run for 4.2 against a Dolphins defense that allows 4.2. Indianapolis has allowed 249 points in nine games, and Jones's turnovers hand out short fields. Colts 24, Dolphins 20 adds up to 44, three and a half clear of 40.5.

Three cards lay the 2.5 with Indianapolis: Next Man Up has Colts 25, Dolphins 19, In The Trenches has Colts 24, Dolphins 20, and Letdown Watch has Colts 23, Dolphins 20. The Formula is the lone card on Miami with the points, at Colts 23, Dolphins 22. All four cards with a score are Over. Follow The Money has no pick on either, with the spread and total parked since Tuesday morning.

Lou Ferraro will skip the movie for the game and say he planned it that way. Carla Ruiz will watch Hoosiers on the flight home and admit the ending works. Sunday's ending is smaller: the home team by four, with plenty of points.

Colts 24, Dolphins 20. Colts cover the 2.5. Over 40.5.
