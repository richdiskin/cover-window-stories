Minnesota Vikings at Green Bay Packers

Minnesota Vikings (6-2) at Green Bay Packers (6-3)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 15 · Lambeau Field, Green Bay, WI · Fox

XFacebook

Green Bay spends Saturday riding the Packers Heritage Trail trolley out to old City Stadium, and my Vikings vs Packers prediction rides with the team that has the better defense and the worse quarterback. Minnesota with the 4.5-point spread is the side I want, the Under 42.5 is my over/under, and Packers 21, Vikings 17 is my pick for the final.

The quarterback situation is ugly, and I'm pricing it that way. Kyler Murray has a Grade 2 hamstring strain, and Carson Wentz went 8-of-25 with two interceptions and a lost fumble in Monday's loss to Buffalo before Max Brosmer finished the game. At Tuesday's deadline, Minnesota sent a third-round pick to Las Vegas to bring back Kirk Cousins. Kevin O'Connell said Wednesday it's still too early to commit to Cousins starting Sunday, so it's Cousins or Wentz at Lambeau, and I'm keeping the score where it is either way.

The Formula: Net ratings Green Bay Packers −2.3, Minnesota Vikings −6.2 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 10, 2026).

Here's why the Vikings still get the points. Minnesota has allowed 116 points in eight games, the stingiest number in this game by a mile, and it won the first meeting 39-22 in September. Green Bay is 6-3 and just scored forty-five in Foxborough behind Jordan Love, but it runs for only 3.3 yards a carry. Against a defense this good, the Packers have to throw for everything, and that tends to produce long drives that stall and field goals instead of touchdowns. A four-point Packers win is the real shape of that game.

The Under is the stronger half. Minnesota has scored only 146 points in eight games, and neither a quarterback who just walked back into the building nor the one who went 8-of-25 on Monday is going to fix that in five days. The Vikings run for 4.3 against a Packers defense that allows 4.1, so the plan is handoffs and a running clock. Kickoff is noon local time at Lambeau in mid-November. Packers 21, Vikings 17 is 38 points, four and a half under 42.5.

The room tilts hard toward Minnesota and the Under. Next Man Up has Packers 20, Vikings 17, Letdown Watch has Packers 22, Vikings 19, and In The Trenches has Packers 19, Vikings 16, and all three take the Vikings with the points. The Formula is the lone card laying the 4.5, at Packers 22, Vikings 17. All four cards with a score are Under. Follow The Money has no side pick and is the only Over after the total rose from 41.5 to 42.5.

Paul Lindgren will narrate the whole trolley ride. Erik Dahl will spend it refreshing his phone to find out who starts at quarterback. Neither one is going to enjoy watching this offense, but only one of them needs it to be pretty.

Packers 21, Vikings 17. Vikings cover the 4.5. Under 42.5.
