Jacksonville Jaguars at Tennessee Titans

Jacksonville Jaguars (6-2) at Tennessee Titans (1-7)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 15 · Nissan Stadium, Nashville, TN · Fox

XFacebook

The Jaguars vs Titans spread sits at 10.5, and the Fairgrounds Nashville is spending the weekend on Christmas Village, three expo halls of holiday shopping from Thursday to Sunday. My prediction is a Jacksonville win that doesn't get gift-wrapped: Jaguars 27, Titans 17, which puts my pick on Tennessee +10.5 and my over/under on the Under 46.5.

Jacksonville leads the league in point differential, and I'm not pretending otherwise. The Jaguars are 6-2 with 229 points scored and only 121 allowed. They beat the Colts by thirty-five, then went to Baltimore on a Thursday and won by twenty-three. On Monday they traded for safety Jessie Bates III and defensive tackles Davon Godchaux and Jordan Davis, and Liam Coen said Bates has a good chance to play.

The Formula: Net ratings Tennessee Titans −11.7, Jacksonville Jaguars 2.2 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 10, 2026).

So why take the points? Because 10.5 is a lot to lay on the road against a team coming off its bye. Tennessee is 1-7 and 0-4 at home, but Cam Ward's last game was the best of his career: 26 of 40 for more than three hundred yards, two touchdowns and no interceptions against Cincinnati. Jacksonville's three road games produced a 2-1 record, and a defense this good tends to win by squeezing, not by piling on. Jaguars 27, Titans 17 is a ten-point game, a half-point inside the number.

The ground game is where Tennessee's ceiling sits. The Titans run for 4.2 yards a carry, but Jacksonville allows only 3.4, which forces Ward to throw into a secondary that just added Bates. That's the Jaguars' path to a blowout, and it's real. The other side of it is that Jacksonville runs for 4.4 against a Titans defense that allows 4.3, so the Jaguars will happily hand off with a lead and drain the clock instead of chasing a fourth touchdown.

That clock is the Under. Tennessee has scored only 144 points in eight games. Jacksonville allows about fifteen a game. Jaguars 27, Titans 17 is 44, two and a half under 46.5, and a Jacksonville team running out the fourth quarter is the likeliest way this game ends.

The room splits two and two on the side. Next Man Up, at Jaguars 27, Titans 17, and In The Trenches, at Jaguars 28, Titans 19, take Tennessee with the points. The Formula, at Jaguars 28, Titans 16, and Letdown Watch, at Jaguars 27, Titans 16, lay the 10.5. The Formula, Next Man Up and Letdown Watch are Under; In The Trenches is the lone Over. Follow The Money has no pick on either, with the spread and total both sitting where they opened.

Ivy Corcoran has the series streak saved on her phone. Pete Varga has a wreath to hang and a quarterback he trusts. Jacksonville wins, and Tennessee keeps the door from slamming.

Jaguars 27, Titans 17. Titans cover the 10.5. Under 46.5.
