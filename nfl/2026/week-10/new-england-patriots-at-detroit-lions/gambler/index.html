New England Patriots at Detroit Lions

New England Patriots (5-4) at Detroit Lions (6-2)

9:30 AM ET Sun Nov 15 · FC Bayern Munich Stadium, Munich, Germany · Fox

Listen to the podcast›
XFacebook

Munich gets an American weekend, and the Patriots vs Lions spread at 2.5 asks whether Detroit's home-field magic packs into a suitcase. My prediction for Week 10 is Patriots 26, Lions 25, so New England with the points is my pick, and Under 51.5 is where I land on the over/under.

The Lions are the designated home team, and they brought a show: a Fan Fest on Odeonsplatz Friday and Saturday with Kevin Saunderson and Big Sean on stage. The Patriots answered with a Patriots Haus and all six Super Bowl rings on display. None of that travels onto the field on Sunday, and neither does Detroit's 4-0 home record. Kickoff is 3:30 in Munich, 9:30 in the morning on the East Coast, and the Lions practice at home Wednesday and Thursday before they fly out, which gives them about 48 hours on the ground.

The Formula: Net ratings Detroit Lions −2.5, New England Patriots −0.1 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 10, 2026).

New England is coming off the most points it has ever allowed at Gillette Stadium, a forty-five to twenty-seven loss to Green Bay in which Drake Maye threw three interceptions before halftime. It's also 5-4, it's scored 211 points, and Maye still finished that game with three touchdown throws. Detroit is 6-2 with four straight wins, but the last one was a 19-9 grinder in Miami in which Jared Goff was sacked three times. Take the home building away and these are two teams separated by a field goal at most.

The injuries are real on both sides. Mike Vrabel doesn't expect left guard Ben Brown to play, with Mike Onwenu and Alijah Vera-Tucker already on injured reserve, and Rhamondre Stevenson was headed for an MRI on his knee. Detroit lost Jack Campbell to injured reserve with a neck injury. Aidan Hutchinson leads the league in pressures, and Milton Williams leads a deep Patriots front against a short-handed Lions interior. Those cancel more than they tilt.

The Under is the cleaner half. Detroit has held four straight opponents to 21 points or fewer, the first time it's done that in a decade. The Lions run for 4.7 yards a carry against a defense that allows 4.0, and a patched-up Patriots front will want the ball on the ground too. A neutral-site game on a short body clock, with both teams wanting to run it, finishes at 51, a half-point shy of the posted 51.5.

The room is unanimous. The Formula has Patriots 26, Lions 23. Next Man Up has Lions 25, Patriots 24, and Letdown Watch and In The Trenches both land on Lions 26, Patriots 25. All four take New England plus the 2.5 and the Under. Follow The Money has no pick on either, with the spread parked at 2.5 and the total moving only half a point.

Glen Tolliver gets his rings photo and a one-point win. Martin Sorensen gets to keep telling everyone the window is open, just not this Sunday in Bavaria.

Patriots 26, Lions 25. Patriots cover the 2.5. Under 51.5.
