The Fanatic · NFL

San Francisco 49ers at Dallas Cowboys

San Francisco 49ers (7-1) at Dallas Cowboys (4-5)

4:25 PM ET Sun Nov 15 · AT&T Stadium, Arlington, TX · Fox

XFacebook

The 12 Days of Christmas gazebos at the Dallas Arboretum are in their final season, and Cole Garrison went Saturday to say goodbye with the last person he wanted beside him. Nora Haskell is in town for San Francisco 49ers vs Dallas Cowboys in Week 10, and between gazebos she kept reminding him that Brock Purdy and a 7-1 team are about to visit Dak Prescott at AT&T Stadium.

She made him walk past every one of the twelve.

Nora: "Twelve gazebos. You know what I count, Cole?"

Cole: "Rings."

Nora: "Rings."

Here's the problem for both of them. They have the same number. San Francisco has five Super Bowl titles. Dallas has five. Nora's last one came after the 1994 season. Cole's came a year later, in Super Bowl XXX. They've each spent three decades insisting their five weighs more.

Cole Garrison is the most honest Cowboys fan in North Texas, which isn't saying much around here. He knows the franchise is the most valuable brand in sports. He also knows the last ring came after the 1995 season, and he won't let the first fact excuse the second. His standard answer to anybody bragging about the stadium is four words: valuation isn't a trophy.

Nora is the 49ers fan who only counts rings. Bill Walsh set the standard for her: the West Coast offense, a 10-4 playoff mark, and five titles that started with one play. The NFC championships in 2012, 2019 and 2023 don't impress her, because none of them ended in a parade.

The play, of course, is The Catch. Nora brings it up every time these teams meet. The 1981 NFC title game, Joe Montana to Dwight Clark, 58 seconds left, and San Francisco won 28-27 against Dallas.

Cole: "You came all the way to Texas to talk about a game from 1981."

Nora: "I came all the way to Texas because you have a beautiful garden and a run defense that gives up 5.2 yards a carry."

That one landed. Christian McCaffrey and the 49ers run for 4.9 a carry, and Cole has spent the week trying not to picture it. His answer is the middle of his own defense.

Cole: "Quinnen Williams and Kenny Clark inside. If Dallas loses that fight, blame the coaching, not the talent."

Nora: "I'll blame whoever's standing there when McCaffrey runs by."

The records tell part of it. San Francisco is 7-1, 2-1 on the road, and coming off a 38-17 win against Las Vegas. The 49ers have allowed just 156 points in eight games, with Nick Bosa and Fred Warner setting the tone. Dallas is 4-5, only 1-3 at home, but it won 27-23 at Indianapolis last week and has scored 246 points. CeeDee Lamb and Jake Ferguson are not a quiet afternoon for anybody.

Cole: "Dak isn't what's costing us games. It's everything that isn't named Dak."

Nora: "So it's the other 52 guys."

Cole: "Some of them. Not Lamb."

The Dallas Zoo turned on more than two million lights on Friday night, and Cole has promised Nora a trip before she flies home, if she can keep her mouth shut about The Catch until they get there. She has made no promises.

Cole's honest read on Sunday: Dallas puts up a fight, Prescott keeps it interesting into the fourth quarter, and the 49ers have just a little more of everything up front. Nora's read is shorter. She counts rings, and on Sunday she'll be counting touchdowns.

My call: 49ers 30, Cowboys 23.
