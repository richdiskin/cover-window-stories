Cleveland Browns at Pittsburgh Steelers

Cleveland Browns (5-2) at Pittsburgh Steelers (5-2)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · Acrisure Stadium, Pittsburgh, PA · CBS

Two 5-2 teams, both unbeaten at home, both 2-2 on the road, and a 3.5-point number in a rivalry game with a total under 40. That half point past the field goal is the whole story, and I'm taking it with Cleveland.

Here's the setup: Cleveland Browns (5-2) at Pittsburgh Steelers (5-2), 1 p.m. Eastern on CBS from Acrisure Stadium, the first Sunday back on standard time. The consensus has Pittsburgh −3.5 with a total of 39.5 (as of Oct 27, 4:04 p.m. ET). My card: Cleveland Browns +3.5, Over 39.5, and a final of Steelers 22, Browns 19.

Why the Browns? Because these teams are almost the same team. The Formula puts Pittsburgh at −5.6 and Cleveland at −5.9, three tenths apart, and even with 1.75 for home field it comes out Steelers by only 2.1. In a game this tight, 3.5 is a gift. Pittsburgh has scored 153 and allowed 138 in seven games, a modest margin for a 5-2 team.

The rushes match up evenly too. Cleveland's rush gets a sack about once every thirteen dropbacks, and Pittsburgh's gets one about once every fourteen. Pittsburgh's quarterbacks go down about once every thirteen dropbacks. Nobody has a clean pocket in this game, and that tends to keep scores close.

Now the honest caveat: Cleveland's record is better than its scoreboard. The Browns have scored 133 and allowed 146, which makes 5-2 look a little lucky. Their quarterbacks have been sacked about once every ten dropbacks. And Pittsburgh runs it well, 5.0 yards a carry against a Cleveland defense allowing 4.6, while Cleveland gets 4.2 against a Steelers defense allowing 3.9. Deshaun Watson started Cleveland's last game at Tennessee, and Aaron Rodgers started Pittsburgh's last game at New Orleans. The Week 8 injury report isn't out yet, so those are the quarterbacks I'm working with. Cleveland is also on its second straight road trip.

Why the Over? This is the one total where the whole room agrees with me. The posted 39.5 is a very low number, the Formula's is 40.7, and my final adds up to 41. Pittsburgh's run game and two leaky pockets produce just enough short fields to clear it.

The room is unanimous on the Over and nearly so on the side. The Formula and In The Trenches both have Steelers 21, Browns 19. Next Man Up has Steelers 22, Browns 19, my exact score. Letdown Watch is the outlier at Steelers 23, Browns 18, the only opinion that lays the 3.5. Follow The Money stays quiet: the spread and total haven't moved since they opened.

Bottom of the card: two mirror-image teams, a Steelers win by a field goal, and a number half a point too big. I'll take it.

Steelers 22, Browns 19. Browns cover the 3.5. Over 39.5.
