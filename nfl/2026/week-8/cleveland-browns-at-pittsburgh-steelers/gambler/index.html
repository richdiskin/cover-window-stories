Cleveland Browns at Pittsburgh Steelers

Cleveland Browns (5-2) at Pittsburgh Steelers (5-2)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · Acrisure Stadium, Pittsburgh, PA · CBS

Browns vs Steelers prediction, pick against the spread and over/under for Week 8: Acrisure Stadium hosts Pitt and Georgia Tech at 7:30 Saturday night, then flips back for two 5-2 AFC North teams on Sunday afternoon. Both are unbeaten at home, the spread is 3.5 in a rivalry game with a total under 40, and that half point past the field goal is why I'm with Cleveland.

CBS has the 1 p.m. Eastern kickoff. The consensus has Pittsburgh −3.5 with a total of 39.5 as of Oct 28, 6:49 p.m. ET. My card: Cleveland Browns +3.5, Over 39.5, final Steelers 22, Browns 19.

Cleveland is the side because of that hook, and because these teams are almost the same team.

The Formula: Net ratings Pittsburgh Steelers −5.6, Cleveland Browns −5.9 (nflverse play-by-play, Oct 28, 2026).

In a game this tight, 3.5 is a gift. Pittsburgh has scored 153 and allowed 138 in seven games, a modest margin for a 5-2 team.

The rushes match up evenly too. Cleveland's rush gets a sack about once every thirteen dropbacks, and Pittsburgh's gets one about once every fourteen. Pittsburgh's quarterbacks go down about once every thirteen dropbacks. Nobody has a clean pocket in this game, and that tends to keep scores close.

Here's what keeps me honest: Cleveland's record is better than its scoreboard. The Browns have scored 133 and allowed 146, which makes 5-2 look a little lucky. Their quarterbacks have been sacked about once every ten dropbacks. And Pittsburgh runs it well, 5.0 yards a carry against a Cleveland defense allowing 4.6, while Cleveland gets 4.2 against a Steelers defense allowing 3.9. Deshaun Watson started Cleveland's last game at Tennessee, and Aaron Rodgers started Pittsburgh's last game at New Orleans. Neither Watson nor Rodgers appears on Wednesday's practice report. Pittsburgh's defense had a quiet practice: T.J. Watt, Cameron Heyward and Jalen Ramsey all sat out. Cleveland is also on its second straight road trip.

The Over is the cheaper call. This is the one total where the whole room agrees with me. The posted 39.5 is a very low number, the Formula's is about 40, and my final adds up to 41. Pittsburgh's run game and two leaky pockets produce just enough short fields to clear it.

The room is unanimous on the Over and nearly so on the side. The Formula and In The Trenches both have Steelers 21, Browns 19. Next Man Up has Steelers 22, Browns 19, my exact score. Letdown Watch is the outlier at Steelers 23, Browns 18, the only opinion that lays the 3.5. Follow The Money has no pick.

Acrisure will have hosted Pitt under the lights the night before, and on Sunday it gets two mirror-image teams, a Steelers win by a field goal, and a number half a point too big. I'll take it.

Steelers 22, Browns 19. Browns cover the 3.5. Over 39.5.
