Freiermuth Plunges In at the Gun, Steelers Lead Browns 20-6 at Halftime

Cleveland Browns (5-2) at Pittsburgh Steelers (5-2)

Halftime · Sunday, Nov 1, 2026, 2:57 PM ET

XFacebook

🏈 HALFTIME | Browns at Steelers | Acrisure Stadium

Steelers 20, Browns 6

🎙️ Ed Kowalski bleeds black and gold. Frank Haller drove in from Cleveland still counting eight titles. They called every snap of the first half.

⏱️ 1ST QUARTER

Frank: We got the ball first. Isaiah Bond brought the opening kick out to our 27. I felt eight championships deep.

Ed: Then Alex Highsmith and Keeanu Benton dropped Watson on the second snap.

Frank: And Watson got hit late on the next one. Roughing flag, 15 yards, and he had to sit for a few plays. Shedeur Sanders came in.

Ed: A few snaps later Harold Fannin Jr. got the ball knocked loose. Pittsburgh threw the red flag, the call got flipped, and we took it at the Cleveland 42.

Frank: And Chris Boswell missed from 57. Wide left. I'm saying it loud so the whole North Shore hears it.

Ed: Watson came right back. Patrick Queen and Cam Heyward got him and the ball popped free. Benton fell on it at the Cleveland 45.

Frank: Two giveaways before the quarter was five minutes old. That's not a football team, Ed. That's a charity drive.

Ed: Rico Dowdle ran for 15, then 9, then 9 more into the end zone. 7-0 Steelers at 7:57 of the 1st.

Frank: We went three-and-out. Nick Herbig sacked Watson. Corey Bojorquez boomed a 55-yard punt, so at least somebody in brown had a good day.

Ed: Then Jaylen Warren went up the gut for 51 yards. Thirteen plays, 86 yards, 7:12 off the clock. Chuck Noll football.

⏱️ 2ND QUARTER

Frank: And at our 2, the Browns said no. Three snaps, zero yards. Boswell had to settle for a 20-yarder. 10-0. I'm counting that as a win.

Frank: Then we woke up. Watson to Fannin. Watson to Fannin. A 25-yard grab down the right side. Andre Szmyt drilled a 32-yarder. 10-3.

Ed: Darnell Washington caught one for 24. Dowdle put it on the turf, and Troy Fautanu jumped on it like it owed him money.

Frank: Then Huntington sacked Rodgers for 11. Thank you, sir.

Ed: Boswell from 37. Good. 13-3 at 3:27.

Frank: Watson hit KC Concepcion for 24 and Denzel Boston for 12. T.J. Watt got Watson for 8, fine, but Szmyt nailed a 49-yarder. 13-6 with 45 seconds left. I was already planning my halftime victory lap.

Ed: Kaden Wetjen brought the kick back to our 31. Warren for 23. Rodgers to Pat Freiermuth for 28. Rodgers to Germie Bernard for 15, down to the 1. Spike with 6 seconds left.

Ed: Then a direct snap to Freiermuth. One yard. Touchdown with 0:01 on the clock. 20-6.

Frank: Six plays, 69 yards, 44 seconds. I need a minute. Maybe a long walk by the lake.

📊 FIRST-HALF NUMBERS

Steelers: 244 yards of offense, 160 on the ground, 8.9 a carry

Browns: 113 yards of offense

Warren: 10 carries, 102 yards

Fannin: 6 catches, 65 yards

Browns lost 2 fumbles and took 4 sacks

Szmyt: 2 for 2, long of 49

🗣️ HALFTIME TALK

Ed: Fourteen up, the run game rolling, the building loud. Noll would nod.

Frank: Nobody hands out trophies at halftime, Ed. Szmyt is perfect and nobody in gold can stop Fannin. Thirty minutes left.

Ed: Thirty minutes of Steelers football. Bring it.
