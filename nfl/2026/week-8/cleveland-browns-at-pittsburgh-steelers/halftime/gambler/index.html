🏈 HALFTIME | Browns at Steelers

Steelers 20, Browns 6

Our calls: Browns +3.5 and Over 39.5.

📉 SPREAD: Browns +3.5 is trailing the cover by 10.5. Down 14 at the half. Cleveland needs to win the second half by 11 or more.

📈 TOTAL: 26 points at the half against 39.5. The second half needs 14 to reach the Over. 13 or fewer and it stays Under.

😬 Opening series and I'm already sweating. Watson gets dropped by Highsmith and Benton, then takes a late hit on the next snap. Roughing flag, Watson out for a few plays, Shedeur Sanders in.

💀 Fannin catches a short one, Pittsburgh challenges, and it gets reversed to a fumble. Steelers ball at the Cleveland 42.

🙏 Boswell misses from 57, wide left. Breathe.

💀 Watson returns and gets strip-sacked by Queen and Heyward. Benton recovers at the Cleveland 45. Two lost fumbles before 10:30 of the 1st.

🏃 Dowdle goes 15, 9, then a 9-yard TD run. 7-0 Steelers at 7:57 of the 1st.

🥶 Browns three-and-out. Herbig sack.

🏃 Warren rips off 51. Thirteen plays, 86 yards, 7:12 of clock.

🛡️ Cleveland holds at its own 2. Three snaps, zero yards. Boswell 20-yard FG, 10-0 at 14:05 of the 2nd. That stand kept me alive.

✅ Browns answer: 10 plays, 61 yards. Fannin is the whole offense, including a 25-yard catch. Szmyt 32-yard FG, 10-3 at 8:43.

😮‍💨 Pittsburgh goes 45 yards. Washington for 24. Dowdle fumbles, Fautanu falls on it. Huntington sacks Rodgers for 11. Boswell 37-yard FG, 13-3 at 3:27.

✅ Browns go 44 yards. Concepcion for 24, Boston for 12, Watt sack for 8, then Szmyt from 49. 13-6 with 45 seconds left. At that point we're 3.5 short, 19 on the board, and I'm feeling human again.

🔪 Then the gut punch. 44 seconds, 6 plays, 69 yards. Warren 23, Freiermuth 28, Bernard 15, spike, then a direct snap to Freiermuth for 1 yard and the score with 0:01 left. 20-6. A 7-point swing at the gun.
