Cleveland Browns at Pittsburgh Steelers

Cleveland Browns (5-2) at Pittsburgh Steelers (5-2)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · Acrisure Stadium, Pittsburgh, PA · CBS

Cleveland Browns vs Pittsburgh Steelers is a 5-2 against 5-2 AFC North fight in Week 8, Deshaun Watson and Todd Monken against Aaron Rodgers and Mike McCarthy, 1 p.m. Eastern on CBS. Acrisure Stadium gets no rest for it: at 7:30 Saturday night the same field hosts Pitt against Georgia Tech, so the grounds crew gets one overnight to turn it back into a Steelers field before Frank Haller walks in from the North Shore to tell Ed Kowalski that Cleveland's eight titles still outrank six Lombardis.

Ed Kowalski measures every franchise by how long it keeps its people, and he likes that the building works double shifts.

Ed: Pitt on Saturday, the Steelers on Sunday, same field, same rivers. That's continuity, Frank. Chuck Noll coached here for more than two decades and never held another head coaching job.

Frank: You say that like it's a personality.

Frank will remind anyone in earshot that Cleveland was the gold standard before the Super Bowl existed, and he left a Halloween show by Mushroomhead, Cleveland's own masked metal band, at the Agora to make the drive.

Frank: Before nineteen sixty-five, Ed. Four AAFC championships in a row, then four NFL championships. We ran this league while your team waited on its first title.

Ed: Six Super Bowls. IX, X, XIII, XIV, XL, XLIII.

Frank: I'll give you six. I won't give anybody the Factory of Sadness.

Ed: The Immaculate Reception. Fourth down, twenty-two seconds left, down a point to the Raiders. And the Steel Curtain team in the bicentennial season started one and four, then posted five shutouts in its last nine games.

Frank: Still not eight championships.

Ed: Mel Blount, Terry Bradshaw, Alan Faneca, Dermontti Dawson. I can keep going until kickoff.

These teams are mirror images. Both are 3-0 at home and 2-2 on the road. Cleveland has scored 133 points and allowed 146; Pittsburgh has scored 153 and allowed 138. Watson started the Browns' last game, at Tennessee. Rodgers started the Steelers' last game, at New Orleans. Cleveland makes its second straight road trip.

Ed: You've allowed more than you scored and you're 5-2. That's a lucky penny.

Frank: Wins are wins. Nobody asks how.

Monken has Mike Rutenberg calling a 4-3 defense. McCarthy has Patrick Graham calling a 3-4. Pittsburgh runs for 5.0 yards a carry against a Cleveland defense allowing 4.6. Cleveland runs for 4.2 against a Steelers defense allowing only 3.9, which is why Frank keeps talking about the past.

The pocket is a mosh pit on both sides, the kind Mushroomhead would recognize. Cleveland's quarterbacks have been sacked about once every ten dropbacks, and Pittsburgh's rush gets there about once every fourteen. Pittsburgh's quarterbacks go down about once every thirteen dropbacks, and Cleveland's rush gets home about once every thirteen.

Ed: T.J. Watt and Cameron Heyward. Watson will hear footsteps in his sleep.

Frank: Jared Verse and Mason Graham will be visiting Rodgers. Denzel Ward will be waiting on anything thrown his way.

Ed: Patrick Queen in the middle. He likes this rivalry.

Frank: Everybody in Cleveland likes this rivalry. We just like it more when we win it.

Wednesday's official practice report hit both defenses. Watt, Heyward and Jalen Ramsey all sat out for Pittsburgh, Verse sat out for Cleveland, and nobody in this game has a game status yet. If Watt and Heyward are both cleared, Ed expects that 3.9 to hold. If not, Frank's 4.2 starts to look like a plan.

Frank: Last thing. McCarthy. How long does he get?

Ed: If he stays as long as Noll, I'll be very old and very happy.

The Pitt crowd will have left the place loud. Ed wants it louder by four o'clock, and Frank wants it quiet enough to hear the Browns celebrating.

My call: Steelers 22, Browns 19.
