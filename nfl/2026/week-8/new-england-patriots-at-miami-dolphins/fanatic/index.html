New England Patriots at Miami Dolphins

New England Patriots (4-3) at Miami Dolphins (1-5)

4:25 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · Hard Rock Stadium, Miami Gardens, FL · CBS

New England Patriots vs Miami Dolphins is Drake Maye and Mike Vrabel against Malik Willis and Jeff Hafley, an AFC East game in Week 8 at Hard Rock Stadium, 4:25 p.m. Eastern on CBS. Glen Tolliver flew south the morning after Salem's Haunted Happenings closed its month with a Halloween night finale and drone show, and he landed in a Miami still buzzing from Wynwood's HalloWYN weekend, where Carla Ruiz was waiting to argue that a rebuild beats a pile of old receipts.

Carla Ruiz picked him up and drove him past the Wynwood murals on purpose.

Carla: Every wall in that neighborhood used to be a warehouse, Glen. Somebody looked at a broken block and saw a plan. That's how I look at this roster.

Carla treats a broken roster the way a contractor treats a house that needs new floors. Glen treats championships as receipts for work already done, useful for nothing except proving it can be done again.

Glen: Six Super Bowls. XXXVI, XXXVIII, XXXIX, XLIX, LI, LIII. Receipts. They don't win next Sunday. And I keep Super Bowl LX, Seattle twenty-nine, us thirteen, in my wallet so I don't get nostalgic.

Carla: Painful wallet. Miami won Super Bowls VII and VIII back to back, and five AFC titles, and I'm proud of every one. But I don't throw good years after bad decisions.

Glen named John Hannah, Mike Haynes, Ty Law, Randy Moss and Curtis Martin. Carla said Miami's history is real and its present is a construction site, and she said it like a woman who has seen the permit.

New England is 4-3, 3-0 at home and 1-3 on the road, with 160 points scored and 143 allowed. Miami is 1-5, 0-2 at home and 1-3 away, with 98 scored and 157 allowed in six games. Maye started the Patriots' last game, at Chicago. Willis started the Dolphins' last game, at the Jets. New England arrives with ten days of rest.

Glen: Three and oh at home, one and three away. Process says fix the road.

Carla: Our one win came on the road, so home cooking hasn't helped us either.

Vrabel has Zak Kuhr calling a 3-4 defense and Josh McDaniels calling the offense. Hafley has Sean Duggan running a 4-3 and Bobby Slowik on offense. The best ground matchup belongs to Miami, 4.7 yards a carry against a Patriots defense allowing 4.1. New England runs for 4.4 against a Dolphins defense allowing 4.2.

Carla: Ollie Gordon II and Malik Washington. That's the first floor of the new house.

The pocket favors the visitors. Miami's quarterbacks have been sacked about once every eleven dropbacks, and New England's rush gets home about once every fourteen. Miami's rush has been the quietest in the game, a sack about once every twenty-nine and a half dropbacks, against New England blockers who allow one about once every seventeen.

Glen: Christian Gonzalez and Milton Williams will make Willis earn every yard.

Carla: Zach Sieler and Jordyn Brooks will visit Maye.

Glen: A.J. Brown and Hunter Henry will visit your secondary.

Carla: We have room for guests. The guest house is being rebuilt too.

Wednesday's official practice report had Maye and Willis both practicing fully, Brown limited, and nobody with a game status yet.

Glen: When does the rebuild end?

Carla: When the books balance and the scoreboard agrees. Not one day sooner.

Glen: Salem spent a whole month on one night. You've spent longer on one roster.

Carla: Salem had centuries of material. Give me a couple more drafts.

Wynwood was warehouses once. Carla says the Dolphins are a mural in progress, and Glen says he'd like to see the paint dry before Christmas.

My call: Patriots 26, Dolphins 20.
