New England Patriots (4-3) at Miami Dolphins (1-5)

New England has been perfect at home and shaky on the road, so I'll take Miami.

It's 4:25 p.m. Eastern on CBS from Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens. The consensus has New England −7 with a total of 44 as of Oct 28, 6:49 p.m. ET. My card: Miami Dolphins +7, Over 44, final Patriots 26, Dolphins 20.

Miami plus seven is a road-record argument, because New England's split is extreme. The Patriots are 3-0 at Gillette and 1-3 away from it.
