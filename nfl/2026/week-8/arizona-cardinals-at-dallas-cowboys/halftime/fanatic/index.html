Wide Right at the Gun: Cowboys Up 14-3, Cardinals Still Swinging

Arizona Cardinals (2-5) at Dallas Cowboys (3-4)

Halftime · Sunday, Nov 1, 2026, 2:57 PM ET

XFacebook

🏈 HALFTIME IN ARLINGTON

Cowboys 14, Cardinals 3

🔴 Neil Carver here, Cardinals lifer, already hoarse.

⭐ Cole Garrison here, Cowboys loyalist, already gloating.

🛑 Neil: Opening series, Arizona goes three-and-out. Fine. We were just stretching.

💪 Cole: Then we go for it on fourth-and-1 at our own 32. Bold. Very Texas.

😤 Neil: And Mack Wilson stuffs Javonte Williams for a loss of 2. Ball on the Dallas 30. Thank you, Cole.

🧱 Cole: Thank you for what happened next. Arizona marches to the 1. Fourth-and-goal. Jeremiyah Love up the middle. Caleb Downs and Kenny Clark say no. Zero yards.

😩 Neil: One yard, Cole. One yard is a hallway.

🦅 Cole: Then Shemar James grabs a Brissett throw, Rashan Gary in his face, and runs it back 33 yards to the Arizona 5.

🤯 Neil: And Budda Baker punches the ball out of Williams at the 2! We have it! We have it!

🙃 Cole: You do not have it. James Mitchell falls on it in the end zone. Touchdown, Dallas. Cowboys 7, Cardinals 0 after one.

🎭 Neil: Macbeth had better luck with the witches.

🚚 Cole: Second quarter, Dak takes us 85 yards in 12 plays. CeeDee for 27 down the left side. Dak finds Ryan Flournoy from 9 out. 14-0.

📣 Neil: Then we answer. Kendrick Bourne for 29. Chad Ryland from 37. Points on the board. 14-3.

⚡ Cole: Tyler Goodson brings the kickoff back 45 yards. We are rolling.

💥 Neil: Zaven Collins sacks Dak for minus 10. Dallas punts. Who is rolling now?

😬 Cole: Your crew, right into DeMarvion Overshown. He knocks the ball loose from Bam Knight, LT Overton recovers at the Arizona 47.

🦶 Neil: And Brandon Aubrey trots out for a 61-yarder with four seconds left...

🙌 Neil: Wide right! The desert lives!

😑 Cole: We still lead by 11.

📊 Halftime numbers:

Arizona 136 yards, Dallas 109.

Arizona 17:10 of possession, Dallas 12:50.

Arizona coughed it up twice: one interception, one lost fumble.

Dak 9 of 11 for 85 and a touchdown. Brissett 8 of 20 for 104 with an interception.

CeeDee Lamb 4 catches for 44.

🔥 Neil: We outgained you, we held the ball longer, and we still have Trey McBride and a whole second half.

🏟️ Cole: And we have the scoreboard. Same as I told you all week.

⏳ Thirty minutes to go. Nobody is done yelling.
