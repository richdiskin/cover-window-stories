Arizona Cardinals at Dallas Cowboys

Arizona Cardinals (2-5) at Dallas Cowboys (3-4)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · AT&T Stadium, Arlington, TX · Fox

Arizona is 2-5 and has allowed 208 points. The Cowboys are favored by a touchdown minus a half. And I'm still taking the Cardinals, because Dallas hasn't earned the right to lay that many to anybody.

Here's the setup: Arizona Cardinals (2-5) at Dallas Cowboys (3-4), 1 p.m. Eastern on Fox from AT&T Stadium, the first Sunday back on standard time. The consensus has Dallas −6.5 with a total of 54.5 (as of Oct 27, 4:04 p.m. ET). My card: Arizona Cardinals +6.5, Under 54.5, and a final of Cowboys 28, Cardinals 25.

Why the Cardinals? Because Dallas is 1-2 at home and 3-4 overall, with 189 points scored and 180 allowed. That's a losing team being treated like a contender in its own building. The Formula puts Dallas at −5.7 and Arizona at −8, adds 1.75 for home field, and comes out Cowboys by 4.1. Arizona has also been better away from home: 2-2 on the road and 0-3 in the desert.

The run defenses are the story. Both teams allow 4.9 yards a carry. Dallas runs for 4.1 and Arizona for 3.6, so neither offense is going to take full advantage, but neither defense is going to choke a game shut either. That keeps the back door open for the team getting points.

Now the honest caveat: Dallas protects its quarterback. The Cowboys have allowed a sack about once every forty dropbacks, and Arizona's rush, which gets home about once every fourteen, may not touch Dak Prescott all day. Prescott started the Philadelphia game, Jacoby Brissett started Arizona's game against Denver, and with the Week 8 injury report not yet out, those are the quarterbacks I'm going with. Arizona's own blockers allow a sack about once every nineteen dropbacks against a Dallas rush that gets there about once every twenty-two.

Why the Under? The posted 54.5 is a big number, and my final is 53. The Formula's number is 51.4. Both of these offenses have to go the long way, and Arizona's run game at 3.6 a carry doesn't produce quick scores. Dallas comes in on six days of rest, and Arizona flies in on a normal week. Neither side has a schedule reason to explode.

The room is unanimous on the side and split on the total. The Formula has Cowboys 28, Cardinals 24. Next Man Up has Cowboys 28, Cardinals 25, my exact score. Letdown Watch has Cowboys 28, Cardinals 22. In The Trenches has Cowboys 28, Cardinals 27 and goes Over. Every scoring opinion has Dallas winning by less than a touchdown. Follow The Money has nothing to add: the spread and total haven't moved since they opened.

Bottom of the card: I don't need Arizona to win. I need four room opinions and one stubborn set of numbers to be right that Dallas wins by less than seven. I'll take the 6.5.

Cowboys 28, Cardinals 25. Cardinals cover the 6.5. Under 54.5.
