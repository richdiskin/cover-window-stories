Arizona Cardinals at Dallas Cowboys

Arizona Cardinals (2-5) at Dallas Cowboys (3-4)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · AT&T Stadium, Arlington, TX · Fox

Arizona Cardinals vs Dallas Cowboys in Week 8 is Jacoby Brissett against Dak Prescott at AT&T Stadium, 1 p.m. Eastern on Fox. Neil Carver left Glendale just as State Farm Stadium, the Cardinals' own home, hosts a Harry Potter drone show with twelve hundred drones on Halloween weekend, and Cole Garrison spent his week a few blocks from the Cowboys' building, where the city of Arlington put on a free outdoor Macbeth at Levitt Pavilion, and the two of them have argued all week about which franchise is the tragedy.

Cole: Witches, a cursed king, everybody overreaching. Very local, Neil.

Neil: You're describing every NFC East December I can remember.

Neil measures every Cardinals team against the club that won the NFC title in the oh-eight season, the franchise's only Super Bowl trip, and he admits this one isn't there yet. Cole separates the Cowboys business from the Cowboys football team, and he will tell you, without being asked, that the business is winning.

Cole: Five Super Bowls. VI, XII, XXVII, XXVIII, XXX. I'm painfully aware the last one was XXX.

Neil: Two NFL championships from before the Super Bowl existed. I don't use them as proof of anything modern. I just enjoy that Jim Thorpe is on our Hall of Fame list, along with Night Train Lane and Charley Trippi.

Cole: Troy Aikman, Tony Dorsett, Michael Irvin, Bob Lilly. And Tom Landry, our first head coach, here for twenty-nine seasons, the man who built the 4-3 everybody copied.

The joke writes itself, and Neil enjoys it a little too much: Arizona sets up in a 4-3 base, and Dallas, the franchise that gave football the 4-3, now plays a 3-4.

Dallas is 3-4, 1-2 at home and 2-2 on the road, with 189 points scored and 180 allowed. Arizona is 2-5, 0-3 at home and 2-2 away, with 142 scored and 208 allowed. Prescott started the Cowboys' last game, at Philadelphia. Brissett started Arizona's last game, at home against Denver.

Neil: Both of our wins came on the road. We travel well.

Cole: Good, because the parking here is its own road trip.

Both run defenses have leaked, each allowing 4.9 yards a carry. Dallas runs for 4.1 and Arizona for 3.6, so neither offense has cashed in on the other's softness. The real separation is in the pocket. Dallas has been a fortress, giving up a sack about once every forty dropbacks, while Arizona's rush gets home about once every fourteen. Arizona's blockers allow one about once every nineteen dropbacks, and the Dallas rush arrives about once every twenty-two.

Neil: Josh Sweat and Budda Baker know how to find a way into a fortress. And Trey McBride is the best player on our roster. Watch the tight end, Cole.

Cole: CeeDee Lamb finds end zones. Quinnen Williams and Rashan Gary find quarterbacks. Brissett will meet them both.

Wednesday's official report gave Neil plenty to complain about. Arizona listed fifteen players: Josh Sweat, Budda Baker and Isaac Seumalo got rest days, Walter Nolen III, Zaven Collins, Mack Wilson Sr. and both right-side blockers, Jon Gaines II and Elijah Wilkinson, did not practice, and Jeremiyah Love and Michael Wilson were limited. Neither team has game statuses yet; George Pickens and Quinnen Williams were limited on Dallas's estimated report. Cole calls that load management. Neil calls it a start.

The coaching staffs are where Neil plants his flag. Mike LaFleur, Nathaniel Hackett and Nick Rallis are building something in the desert, and Neil will defend that to anyone.

Neil: What's your staff building, Cole?

Cole: Brian Schottenheimer and Christian Parker are building a football team. Jerry Jones is building a brand. One of those is going very well.

Neil: Which one?

Cole: Ask me in January. If we're still playing, both.

Neil: Twelve hundred drones above our stadium this weekend, and all I want is one McBride touchdown in the fourth quarter.

Cole: Keep the drones. We have the scoreboard you weren't supposed to stare at.

Macbeth closes with a man who trusted the wrong prophecy. Cole would like Neil to know that the scoreboard in Arlington is not a prophecy.

My call: Cowboys 28, Cardinals 25.
