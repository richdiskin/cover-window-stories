Atlanta Falcons at Tampa Bay Buccaneers

Atlanta Falcons (3-4) at Tampa Bay Buccaneers (2-5)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · Raymond James Stadium, Tampa, FL · Fox

Atlanta Falcons vs Tampa Bay Buccaneers is an NFC South game in Week 8, Michael Penix Jr. and Bijan Robinson against Baker Mayfield and a Bucs team still hunting its first home win, 1 p.m. Eastern on Fox at Raymond James Stadium. Tampa Bay has spent the week on baseball: Tropicana Field across the bay hosted World Series games against the Milwaukee Brewers, and Ben Williamson's twelfth-inning walk-off in Game 4 is still the first thing Sal Ferris brings up, even to Russ Amundsen, who drove down from Atlanta to remind him that division wins outlast October walk-offs.

Sal: Twelve innings, Russ. Somebody owned that moment. That's all I want from a football team. Somebody owns it.

Sal wants to know who is responsible for everything, and he will find out by Monday. Russ Amundsen runs every Falcons opinion through the division race, and he arrived from an Atlanta weekend that gave Mercedes-Benz Stadium, the Falcons' own building, to Georgia against Florida on Saturday.

Russ: Our stadium hosted the biggest rivalry in the Southeast, and I still drove down here, because a division win in Tampa counts double in my house.

Sal: Who owns 0-4 at home? That's my question. I've got a list of names, and I'm not done writing.

Russ: Before you fire anybody, you have two Super Bowls, XXXVII and LV. We have two NFC titles and no Lombardis.

Sal offered the defenses people still talk about: Lee Roy Selmon, the first great Buccaneer, then Derrick Brooks, Warren Sapp, John Lynch and Ronde Barber. Russ answered with Deion Sanders, Tony Gonzalez, Claude Humphrey and Morten Andersen, plus Brett Favre's very brief Atlanta stay. Sal admitted Steve Young passed through Tampa too, briefly, and asked that nobody discuss it.

Atlanta is 3-4, 1-3 at home and 2-1 on the road, with 171 points scored and 173 allowed. Tampa Bay is 2-5, 0-4 at home and 2-1 away, with 162 scored and 151 allowed. Penix started the Falcons' last game, at home against San Francisco. Mayfield started the Bucs' last game, at Carolina.

Sal: We've scored more than we've allowed, and we're 2-5. Explain it.

Russ: I can't. Ask whoever owns it.

Kevin Stefanski runs Atlanta, with Jeff Ulbrich calling a 3-4 defense. Todd Bowles runs Tampa Bay, also in a 3-4, with Zac Robinson calling the offense. Both teams can run. Atlanta gets 4.9 yards a carry against a Tampa Bay defense allowing only 3.8, and Tampa Bay gets 4.8 against an Atlanta defense allowing 4.1. Something gives on the ground, and it probably decides the afternoon.

The pocket has been rougher on the Tampa side. Bucs quarterbacks have been sacked about once every ten dropbacks, and Atlanta's rush gets home about once every sixteen and a half. Atlanta's quarterbacks go down about once every fourteen dropbacks, and Tampa Bay's rush arrives exactly once every sixteen.

Russ: Robinson, Drake London, Kyle Pitts. Your defense can't stop all three.

Sal: Vita Vea in the middle and Antoine Winfield Jr. behind him. Bucky Irving and Emeka Egbuka on the other side.

Russ: Jessie Bates III will be waiting for Baker.

Sal: Baker has seen Jessie Bates before. Baker sees everybody.

Wednesday's official practice report had Mayfield practicing fully and Egbuka limited, and nobody in this game carries a game status yet.

Russ: If we win, I check the division standings the second the clock hits zero.

Sal: If we lose at home again, I check who owns it the second the clock hits zero. And then I watch the baseball highlights again to feel better.

Tampa Bay found out this week that one swing in the twelfth can change a whole city's mood. Sal is hoping Raymond James gets its version, and Russ is hoping it goes home disappointed.

My call: Falcons 23, Buccaneers 22.
