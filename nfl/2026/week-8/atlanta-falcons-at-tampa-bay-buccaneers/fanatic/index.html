Atlanta Falcons at Tampa Bay Buccaneers

Atlanta Falcons (3-4) at Tampa Bay Buccaneers (2-5)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · Raymond James Stadium, Tampa, FL · Fox

Sunday at Raymond James Stadium in Tampa, 1 p.m. Eastern on Fox, the first Sunday back on standard time. It's an NFC South game. Atlanta arrives at 3-4. Tampa Bay is 2-5 and still looking for its first home win. Russ Amundsen runs every Falcons opinion through the division race and nothing else. Sal Ferris wants to know who's responsible for everything, and he'll find out by Monday.

Russ: Sal, check the division, not the polls. This one counts double in my house. A division win in Tampa is worth more than any win in October against anybody from the other conference.

Sal: Who owns this, Russ? That's my question. Who owns 0-4 at home? I've got a list of names, and I'm not done writing.

Russ: Before you start firing people, remember you have two Super Bowls. XXXVII and LV. We have two NFC titles and no Lombardis, and I'm not happy about it.

Sal: Lee Roy Selmon, Russ. The first great Buccaneer. Derrick Brooks. Warren Sapp. John Lynch. Ronde Barber. We built defenses down here that people still talk about.

Russ: Deion Sanders played for us. Tony Gonzalez. Claude Humphrey. Morten Andersen kicked for us. And Brett Favre, very briefly, which nobody in Green Bay wants to discuss.

Sal: Steve Young played here too. Very briefly. We don't discuss that either.

Russ: And don't tell me about luck, Sal. You refuse to accept luck as an excuse.

Sal: Correct. Luck doesn't own anything. People own things.

The season so far: Atlanta is 3-4, 1-3 at home and 2-1 on the road, with 171 points scored and 173 allowed. Tampa Bay is 2-5, 0-4 at home and 2-1 on the road, with 162 scored and 151 allowed. Michael Penix Jr. started the Falcons' last game, at home against San Francisco. Baker Mayfield started the Bucs' last game, at Carolina.

Sal: We've allowed fewer points than we've scored, Russ. At 2-5. Explain that to me.

Russ: I can't, Sal. That's a question for whoever owns it.

The matchup: Kevin Stefanski runs Atlanta, with Jeff Ulbrich calling a defense that sets up in a 3-4 base. Todd Bowles runs Tampa Bay, also in a 3-4 base, with Zac Robinson calling the offense. Both teams run the ball well. Atlanta gets 4.9 yards a carry against a Tampa Bay defense allowing only 3.8. Tampa Bay gets 4.8 against an Atlanta defense allowing 4.1.

In the pocket, Tampa Bay has had the rougher time. Bucs quarterbacks have been sacked about once every ten dropbacks, and Atlanta's rush gets home about once every sixteen and a half. Atlanta's quarterbacks go down about once every fourteen dropbacks, and Tampa Bay's rush gets there exactly once every sixteen.

Russ: Bijan Robinson, Sal. Drake London. Kyle Pitts. Your defense can't stop all three.

Sal: Vita Vea in the middle, Russ, and Antoine Winfield Jr. behind him. And Bucky Irving and Emeka Egbuka on the other side of the ball.

Russ: Jessie Bates III will be waiting for Baker.

Sal: Baker has seen Jessie Bates before. Baker sees everybody.

No injury talk, by house rule. The Week 8 report isn't out, and both men were asked to keep their guesses to themselves.

Russ: Last thing, Sal. If we win, I'm checking the division the second the clock hits zero.

Sal: If we lose at home again, I'm checking who owns it the second the clock hits zero.

Respect to both: an Atlanta team that has played its best football away from home, a Tampa Bay defense that hasn't let teams run on it, and a division Sunday on the Gulf Coast that should come down to the final possession.

My call: Falcons 23, Buccaneers 22.
