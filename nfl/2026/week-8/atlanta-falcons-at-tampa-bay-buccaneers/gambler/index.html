Atlanta Falcons at Tampa Bay Buccaneers

Atlanta Falcons (3-4) at Tampa Bay Buccaneers (2-5)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · Raymond James Stadium, Tampa, FL · Fox

Tampa Bay hasn't won a home game all season, and it's laying 2.5 to a division rival. I'll take the points, and I'll tell you right now that I think Atlanta wins the game.

Here's the setup: Atlanta Falcons (3-4) at Tampa Bay Buccaneers (2-5), 1 p.m. Eastern on Fox from Raymond James Stadium, the first Sunday back on standard time. The consensus has Tampa Bay −2.5 with a total of 45.5 (as of Oct 28, 6:49 p.m. ET). My card: Atlanta Falcons +2.5, Under 45.5, and a final of Falcons 23, Buccaneers 22.

Why the Falcons? Start with the building. Tampa Bay is 0-4 at home this year and 2-1 on the road, and Atlanta is 2-1 on the road. Whatever home field is supposed to be worth, it hasn't shown up in Tampa. The Formula puts Atlanta at −0.3 and Tampa Bay at −5.9, gives the Bucs 1.75 for home field, and still comes out Falcons by 3.8. That's an outright Atlanta win, and I'm getting points on top of it.

The run game backs it up. Atlanta runs for 4.9 yards a carry against a Tampa Bay defense allowing 3.8. That's the best run defense in this game, and the Falcons have been moving the ball on the ground anyway. Tampa Bay runs for 4.8 against an Atlanta defense allowing 4.1, so the Bucs can answer, but they'll be answering a team that controls the clock.

Now the honest caveat: this is a division game, and these teams are close. Atlanta has scored 171 and allowed 173 in seven games, and Tampa Bay has actually been better on the scoreboard at 162 scored and 151 allowed. Tampa Bay's quarterbacks have been sacked about once every ten dropbacks, but Atlanta's rush only gets there about once every sixteen and a half. Atlanta's quarterbacks go down about once every fourteen dropbacks, against a Tampa Bay rush that gets home exactly once every sixteen. Michael Penix Jr. started Atlanta's last game against San Francisco, and Baker Mayfield started Tampa Bay's last game at Carolina. On Wednesday's official injury report, Mayfield was a full participant, Penix isn't listed, and there are no game statuses yet. Emeka Egbuka was limited for Tampa Bay.

Why the Under? My final adds up to 45, half a point shy of the posted 45.5. Two teams that run the ball this well shorten a game, so I'll trust the clock over the Formula's 46.9.

The room is split almost down the middle, which is exactly what you'd expect in a 2.5 game. The Formula has Falcons 25, Buccaneers 22. Next Man Up has Buccaneers 24, Falcons 23, Letdown Watch has Buccaneers 23, Falcons 22, and In The Trenches has Buccaneers 21, Falcons 20. Every one of those has the game decided by three points or fewer, and every one of the four sides with Atlanta and the points. Follow The Money stays off the side, since the spread hasn't moved from 2.5, but it likes the Under: Action Network's consensus total opened at 46.5 and has dropped a full point.

Bottom of the card: a home team that hasn't won at home, a road team that runs the ball, and every scoring opinion inside a field goal. Give me the Falcons and the 2.5.

Falcons 23, Buccaneers 22. Falcons cover the 2.5. Under 45.5.
