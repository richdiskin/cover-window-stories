Halftime Sweat: Bucs 9, Falcons 0, Three Penix Interceptions and a Rally Needed

Atlanta Falcons (3-4) at Tampa Bay Buccaneers (2-5)

Halftime · Sunday, Nov 1, 2026, 2:57 PM ET

ShareXFacebook

21+ 🔞

🏈 HALFTIME
Tampa Bay 9, Atlanta 0

😬 We have Falcons +2.5 and Under 45.5. One of these has me sweating through my shirt.

⏱️ 1st quarter

❌ Atlanta opens with 10 plays, 35 yards. Nick Folk misses from 53, wide left. Free points gone.

🏴‍☠️ Bucky Irving rips off 24 on the first snap. Chase McLaughlin hits from 46. TB 3-0 (7:43 1st).

🚨 Very next snap, Michael Penix Jr. throws it to Alex Anzalone. A Drake London roughness flag hands Tampa the Atlanta 18.

🙏 Then Baker Mayfield fumbles, recovers, pitches to Irving, and Irving coughs it up at the 1. Jessie Bates III knocks it loose, out of the end zone, touchback. Points off the board.

🚨 Three snaps later Anzalone grabs his second. 12-yard return to the Atlanta 19.

🏴‍☠️ Tampa goes nowhere. McLaughlin from 34. TB 6-0 (4:02 1st).

🦅 Atlanta punts, Tampa punts. Breathe.

⏱️ 2nd quarter

🔥 Bijan Robinson opens with 26 yards to the Tampa 36.

🚨 Four snaps later Jacob Parrish picks Penix at the Tampa 17. Third giveaway.

🐢 Tampa grinds 13 plays, 60 yards, 8:29 of clock. Kenny Gainwell breaks a 20. Stalls at the Atlanta 23. McLaughlin from 42. TB 9-0 (4:04 2nd).

❌ Atlanta reaches the Tampa 36 and Robinson gets stopped cold on fourth down. Turnover on downs.

⏱️ Tampa runs it, Atlanta burns all three timeouts, Tampa punts with 41 seconds left. Penix fumbles the snap, falls on it, half over.

📊 First-half numbers
Total yards: TB 146, ATL 97
Throwing yards: TB 0, ATL 24
Mayfield: 0 of 2
Penix: 2 of 10, 24 yards, 3 INT
Irving: 19 carries, 107 yards
Robinson: 14 carries, 67 yards
Third downs: ATL 0-6, TB 2-7
Red zone: TB 0-2, three field goals total
Turnovers: ATL 3, TB 1
Possession: TB 16:23, ATL 13:37

💸 Falcons +2.5: trailing the cover by 6.5. Down 9, and we need Atlanta to lose by 2 or fewer, or win outright. One unanswered touchdown flips it.

✅ Under 45.5: 9 points at the half. The second half needs 36 or fewer to stay Under. 37 or more and it goes Over.

😤 Tampa has zero completions and still leads by nine. Penix, please stop feeding Anzalone. Bijan, please get the ball 25 more times.
