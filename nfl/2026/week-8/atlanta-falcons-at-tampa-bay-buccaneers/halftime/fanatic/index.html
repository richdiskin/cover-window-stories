Bucs Ball-Hawks Grab Three Takeaways, Lead Falcons 9-0 at the Half

Atlanta Falcons (3-4) at Tampa Bay Buccaneers (2-5)

Halftime · Sunday, Nov 1, 2026, 2:57 PM ET

ShareXFacebook

🏈 HALFTIME
Tampa Bay 9, Atlanta 0

🏴‍☠️ Sal Ferris (Bucs): Twelve innings across the bay. Thirty minutes here. And this time I know exactly who owns it. Alex Anzalone. Write it down.

🦅 Russ Amundsen (Falcons): I drove down from Atlanta for this. I want my gas money back.

⏱️ 1st quarter

🦅 Russ: We opened great. Ten plays, 35 yards, Bijan Robinson grinding, Jahan Dotson for 19. Then Nick Folk sent a 53-yard kick wide left.

🏴‍☠️ Sal: Bucky Irving answered on the very first snap. 24 yards up the middle. Chase McLaughlin from 46. Good. 3-0.

🏴‍☠️ Sal: Then Penix threw it straight to Anzalone on the very next snap. Drake London got flagged for roughness on the return. Bucs at the Atlanta 18.

🦅 Russ: And then your quarterback fumbled, scooped it back up, and pitched it to Irving, who ran to the 1 and fumbled it out of the end zone. Jessie Bates III knocked it loose. Touchback. That's division football, Sal.

🏴‍☠️ Sal: Three snaps later Anzalone did it AGAIN. Twelve-yard return to the Atlanta 19. McLaughlin from 34. 6-0.

🦅 Russ: Bijan kept chewing. Jake Bailey boomed a 55-yard punt. The Bucs picked up one first down and punted right back.

⏱️ 2nd quarter

🦅 Russ: Bijan opened the quarter with a 26-yard run to the Tampa 36. I was on my feet!

🏴‍☠️ Sal: Then Jacob Parrish grabbed Penix's throw at the Tampa 17. Three takeaways. Three!

🏴‍☠️ Sal: Then we just ran right at 'em. Thirteen plays, 60 yards, 8 minutes and 29 seconds. Irving, Irving, Irving, Kenny Gainwell for 20. McLaughlin from 42. 9-0.

🦅 Russ: We got to the Tampa 36 late. Bijan got stopped cold on fourth down. I'm still yelling.

🏴‍☠️ Sal: You burned all three timeouts, we punted, and the half ended on a fumbled snap that Penix fell on. I'll take it.

📊 The half
Bucky Irving: 19 carries, 107 yards
Alex Anzalone: 2 interceptions
Jacob Parrish: 1 interception
Chase McLaughlin: 3 for 3 on field goals
Bijan Robinson: 14 carries, 67 yards
Penix: 2 of 10, 24 yards, 3 interceptions
Atlanta: 0 for 6 on third down

🏴‍☠️ Sal: Mayfield hasn't completed a throw. Zero for two. We're up nine anyway. Somebody explain it.

🦅 Russ: Two trips inside our 20 and you got one field goal, Sal. Nine points is no big deal for a team with Bijan, London and Kyle Pitts. Thirty minutes left. Don't you dare check the baseball highlights yet.

🏴‍☠️ Sal: I'm not checking a single score until the clock hits zero.
