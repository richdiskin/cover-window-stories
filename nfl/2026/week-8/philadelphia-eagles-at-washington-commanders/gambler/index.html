Philadelphia Eagles at Washington Commanders

Philadelphia Eagles (2-5) at Washington Commanders (1-4-1)

8:20 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · Northwest Stadium, Landover, MD · NBC

XFacebook

Eagles vs Commanders prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: Washington spent Saturday running the fifty-first Marine Corps Marathon with the Key and Memorial bridges closed, and on Sunday night a 1-4-1 Commanders team with no road wins is laying 6. That's too many points for this record, even rested, even at home, so I'm taking the Eagles.

NBC has the 8:20 p.m. Eastern kickoff from Northwest Stadium in Landover. DraftKings has Washington −6 with a total of 40.5 as of Nov 1, 7:23 p.m. ET. My card: Philadelphia Eagles +6, Over 40.5, final Commanders 24, Eagles 21.

The Eagles side is about what Washington hasn't done, because Washington is 1-1-1 at home and has scored 132 points while allowing 172 in six games. That's not a team that wins by seven.

The Formula: Net ratings Washington Commanders −6.0, Philadelphia Eagles −8.0 (nflverse play-by-play, Oct 28, 2026).

Its card has Commanders 25, Eagles 22. I have it closer than that, a three-point game, and 6 gives me plenty of room.

Washington's run game is the strongest thing on the field: 4.8 yards a carry against a Philadelphia defense allowing 4.3. That's how the Commanders win. But Philadelphia's defense hasn't been gashed everywhere, and the Eagles run for 3.9 against a Washington defense allowing 4.2, enough to stay balanced.

The two things that could burn me: the schedule and the rush. Washington last played in Week 6 at San Francisco and comes in with thirteen days of rest. Philadelphia comes in on six days of rest after hosting Dallas. And the Eagles' rush has been nearly silent, getting a sack about once every thirty-one and a half dropbacks, while Philadelphia's own quarterbacks go down about once every fourteen. Washington allows a sack about once every fifteen and gets one about once every twenty-one. Tanner McKee started the Eagles' last game, and Jayden Daniels started Washington's last game. Jalen Hurts is on injured reserve, and Daniels is off Sunday's inactive list. Philadelphia is without DeVonta Smith, Dontayvion Wicks, center Jake Majors, Jonathan Jones and Jihaad Campbell. Washington is without Daron Payne, Isaac Yiadom, Fabian Moreau, Tanoa Togiai and backup quarterback Athan Kaliakmanis. Terry McLaurin is out for the season with a torn patellar tendon.

The Over is the easy half. This is the one where every scoring opinion agrees. The posted 40.5 is low for two defenses that have allowed 175 and 172 points. The Formula's number is well above that, and my final adds up to 45. Two leaky defenses on a Sunday night should clear it.

Every scoring opinion is on the Over and the Eagles. The Formula has Commanders 25, Eagles 22. Letdown Watch has Commanders 23, Eagles 21. In The Trenches has no pick: center Jake Majors and Daron Payne are inactive, and its season numbers were built with them on the field. Next Man Up has Commanders 22, Eagles 21. Follow The Money is the one dissent: it sides with Washington.

Washington moved thousands of marathon runners across closed bridges on schedule Saturday. Moving 6 points past a division rival is harder: two teams with three wins between them, every scoring opinion inside four points, and 6 on the board. Give me the Eagles.

Commanders 24, Eagles 21. Eagles cover the 6. Over 40.5.
