Philadelphia Eagles at Washington Commanders

Philadelphia Eagles (2-5) at Washington Commanders (1-4-1)

8:20 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · Northwest Stadium, Landover, MD · NBC

Sunday night at Northwest Stadium in Landover, 8:20 p.m. Eastern on NBC, the first Sunday back on standard time. It's an NFC East game. Philadelphia comes in at 2-5. Washington is 1-4-1 and coming off two weeks of rest. Ray Donnelly explains every Eagles result by watching the big men up front. Claire Benton believes coaching structure beats raw talent every time.

Ray: Watch the guards, Claire. That's where every Eagles game is won or lost. Super Bowl LIX, forty to twenty-two against Kansas City. The guards won that game, and nobody wrote it down but me.

Claire: Structure first, Ray. Joe Gibbs won three Super Bowls here, XVII, XXII and XXVI. That's what coaching does. Structure beats names on a jersey.

Ray: We have two Super Bowls too. LII and LIX. And three NFL championships before the merger.

Claire: Two NFL championships for us before the merger. Sammy Baugh. Cliff Battles. George Allen. Bobby Beathard.

Ray: Chuck Bednarik. Reggie White. Steve Van Buren. Pete Pihos. Bob Brown. Big, tough people who played up front.

Claire: Of course you'd say that.

Ray: Of course I would. And I won't take a quarterback opinion as a substitute for watching the guards. Ever.

Claire: And I won't pretend one quarterback can save a franchise without a structure around him.

The season so far: Philadelphia is 2-5, 1-3 at home and 1-2 on the road, with 127 points scored and 175 allowed. Washington is 1-4-1, 1-1-1 at home and 0-3 on the road, with 132 scored and 172 allowed in six games. Tanner McKee started the Eagles' last game, at home against Dallas. Jayden Daniels started the Commanders' last game, at San Francisco in Week 6.

Claire: Thirteen days of rest, Ray. Dan Quinn had two weeks to rebuild the structure.

Ray: Six days for us. My guards are tired. Your guards are rested. I'm already nervous.

The matchup: Both defenses set up in a 3-4 base. Nick Sirianni runs Philadelphia, with Vic Fangio on defense and Sean Mannion calling the offense. Dan Quinn runs Washington, with Daronte Jones on defense and David Blough calling the offense. Washington runs for 4.8 yards a carry against an Eagles defense allowing 4.3. Philadelphia runs for 3.9 against a Commanders defense allowing 4.2.

In the pocket, the Eagles' rush has been the quietest in the building, getting a sack about once every thirty-one and a half dropbacks. Philadelphia's quarterbacks go down about once every fourteen dropbacks. Washington's blockers allow a sack about once every fifteen, and its rush gets home about once every twenty-one.

Ray: Jalen Carter and Jordan Davis, Claire. They're due. They've been due for weeks.

Claire: Daron Payne and Frankie Luvu are due too, Ray. And Terry McLaurin is always due.

Ray: Saquon Barkley and DeVonta Smith. And Quinyon Mitchell will be waiting for anything near McLaurin.

Claire: Jacory Croskey-Merritt is on my depth chart. Say the name three times fast and he's already in the end zone.

No injury talk, and no guessing about who plays quarterback where. The Week 8 report isn't out, and both of them know the rule.

Ray: Last thing, Claire. If we win this, I'm giving the game ball to a guard.

Claire: If we win, Ray, I'm giving it to the play sheet.

Respect to both: two proud old franchises having hard years, a rested Washington team in its own building, and a Sunday night division game that should come down to who wins up front.

My call: Commanders 23, Eagles 22.
