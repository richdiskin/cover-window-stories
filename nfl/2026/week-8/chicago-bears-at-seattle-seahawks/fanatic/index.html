Chicago Bears at Seattle Seahawks

Chicago Bears (5-2) at Seattle Seahawks (4-3)

8:15 PM ET Mon Nov 2 · Lumen Field, Seattle, WA · ESPN

Chicago Bears vs Seattle Seahawks on Monday Night Football closes Week 8 at Lumen Field, Caleb Williams and Ben Johnson against Sam Darnold, Mike Macdonald and the defending Super Bowl champions, the night after Seattle Center finishes its two-day Día de Muertos festival. Walt Brenner, who reminds everyone that professional football started with the Bears, walked through the ofrendas on Sunday and announced that Chicago's ancestors could fill every altar in the building, while Kara Holm, who knows that defending a title is harder than winning one, told him altars are for remembering, not for winning in November.

Walt: Eight NFL championships before the merger, Kara. Bronko Nagurski. Red Grange. We were winning titles before most franchises had a mascot.

Kara: And we won the last one. Super Bowl LX, twenty-nine to thirteen against New England. Every team on our schedule has circled us since.

Walt left a Chicago weekend that started with Boo with SUE at the Field Museum, trick-or-treating around SUE the T. rex, and he wanted it known that his franchise has its own fossils.

Walt: Super Bowl XX. And I won't let anybody tell me that team is the whole story. We were here first.

Kara: XLVIII and LX for us. Steve Largent. Mike McCormack.

Walt: Devin Hester. Steve McMichael. Jared Allen.

Kara: You have more Hall of Famers. We have the most recent trophy.

Walt: For now.

Kara: Exactly. Champions get hunted. I'm not assuming we repeat. Nobody should.

Chicago is 5-2, 3-1 at home and 2-1 on the road, with 180 points scored and 143 allowed. Seattle is 4-3, 3-1 at home and 1-2 away, with 168 scored and only 125 allowed. Williams started the Bears' last game, at home against New England. Darnold started the Seahawks' last game, at home against Kansas City.

Walt: Eleven days of rest. Ben Johnson had time to draw up something special.

Kara: Macdonald had eight. He only needs one.

Johnson has Dennis Allen calling a 4-3 defense and Press Taylor running the offense. Macdonald has Aden Durde calling a 3-4 and Brian Fleury on offense. The toughest group here is Seattle's run defense, allowing only 3.5 yards a carry, and Chicago runs for 4.4 into it. Seattle runs for 4.0 against a Bears defense allowing 4.4.

Both rushes have been busy. Chicago's gets a sack about once every eleven dropbacks, and Seattle's blockers allow one exactly once every fifteen. Seattle's rush gets home about once every thirteen dropbacks, and Chicago's quarterbacks go down about once every fifteen and a half.

Walt: Grady Jarrett and T.J. Edwards will find Darnold, and Jaylon Johnson waits downfield.

Kara: DeMarcus Lawrence and Leonard Williams find Caleb first. Devon Witherspoon will be waiting on Rome Odunze.

Walt: Colston Loveland and D'Andre Swift. We have weapons.

Kara: Jaxon Smith-Njigba would like to meet your secondary.

Wednesday's official practice sheet carried no names at all from either club, which left a Monday night with nothing to hide behind.

Walt: What does a champion owe the rest of the league?

Kara: Nothing. The league thinks we owe it a rematch every week. That's what being hunted means.

Walt: Sunday your city honored everybody who came before. Monday it gets reminded who came first.

Kara: Then remember it from the visitors' side. The marigolds come down Monday morning. By kickoff this whole neighborhood is navy and action green.

My call: Seahawks 25, Bears 20.
