Chicago Bears at Seattle Seahawks

Chicago Bears (5-2) at Seattle Seahawks (4-3)

8:15 PM ET Mon Nov 2 · Lumen Field, Seattle, WA · ESPN

The defending champions are at home on Monday night, rested, and laying a field goal to a 5-2 team. This is a real game, but Seattle is the right side. I'm laying the 3.

Here's the setup: Chicago Bears (5-2) at Seattle Seahawks (4-3), Monday, Nov. 2, 8:15 p.m. Eastern on ESPN from Lumen Field. The consensus has Seattle −3 with a total of 46.5 (as of Oct 28, 6:49 p.m. ET). My card: Seattle Seahawks −3, Under 46.5, and a final of Seahawks 25, Bears 20.

Why the Seahawks? Because Seattle's record undersells it. The Seahawks have scored 168 and allowed only 125 in seven games, and they're 3-1 at home. The Formula puts Seattle at 5.1 and Chicago at −5.3, adds 1.75 for home field, and comes out Seahawks by 12.2. I'm not going that far, but a five-point Seattle win is a comfortable place to stand when the number is 3.

The Seattle run defense is the other reason. The Seahawks allow only 3.5 yards a carry, the stingiest number in this game. Seattle runs for 4.0 against a Chicago defense allowing 4.4.

Now the honest caveat: Chicago is good, and it can run. The Bears are 5-2, they've scored 180 points, and they run for 4.4 yards a carry, a real number against that Seattle front. Chicago's rush gets a sack about once every eleven dropbacks, and Seattle's protection allows one exactly once every fifteen. Seattle's rush gets home about once every thirteen dropbacks, and Chicago's quarterbacks go down about once every fifteen and a half. Caleb Williams started Chicago's last game against New England, and Sam Darnold started Seattle's last game against Kansas City. Neither team has a name on Wednesday's official injury report, so those are the quarterbacks. Chicago also comes in on eleven days of rest, three more than Seattle.

Why the Under? My final adds up to 45, and the Formula's number is 44. Seattle has allowed 125 points in seven games, and two rushes this active stall drives. The posted 46.5 is a point and a half too high.

The room agrees on the Under and splits on the side. The Formula has Seahawks 28, Bears 16, and Letdown Watch has Seahawks 25, Bears 20, both laying the 3. Next Man Up has Seahawks 23, Bears 22, and In The Trenches has Seahawks 22, Bears 20, both taking Chicago and the points. Follow The Money stays quiet: Action Network's consensus spread opened at 3.5 and slipped to 3, short of the full point it needs, and the total hasn't moved.

Bottom of the card: the champs at home, a run defense allowing 3.5 a carry, and a five-point final. I'll lay the field goal.

Seahawks 25, Bears 20. Seahawks cover the 3. Under 46.5.
