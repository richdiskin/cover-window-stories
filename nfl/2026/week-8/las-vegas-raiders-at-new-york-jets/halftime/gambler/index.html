🏈 RAIDERS AT JETS | HALFTIME SWEAT

Jets 23, Raiders 14

📌 Our card: Jets −1.5 and Under 45.5

✅ Jets −1.5: covering by 7.5 at the half

😬 Under 45.5: 37 points at the break, 8.5 from the number. The second half needs 8 or fewer to stay Under. 9 or more and it goes Over.

⏱️ FIRST QUARTER

Geno Smith opens with a 31-yard throw to Garrett Wilson. Braelon Allen adds runs of 15 and 15.

The Raiders hold at the 3. Jason Sanders hits from 21. Jets 3, Raiders 0. Four points left on the table, and for the Under I'll take it.

Kirk Cousins answers with 10 plays and 60 yards. Brock Bowers goes 23 to the 7, then catches the 12-yard touchdown. Raiders 7, Jets 3.

Wilson loses the ball on the next series, the Raiders recover, and replay flips it to an incomplete throw. No turnover. Deep breath.

Jets grind 12 plays and kick a 36-yarder. Raiders 7, Jets 6.

Cousins gets sacked for minus 9 by McDonald and the Raiders go three and out.

⏱️ SECOND QUARTER

Adonai Mitchell catches throws of 20 and 22, then Isaiah Williams scores from 18 down the middle. Jets 13, Raiders 7.

Then the gut punch for the Under. Dylan Laube takes the kickoff 95 yards. Seven points in 12 seconds with no offense on the field. Raiders 14, Jets 13. The Jets went from covering to trailing the cover by 2.5.

Jets answer: Allen for 24, then Geno to Mitchell from 12. Jets 20, Raiders 14.

The Raiders get to midfield and come up short on a direct snap to Heyward. Replay upholds it. Jets ball on downs at their 46.

Allen goes nine, six, eleven, nine, four and four. The Raiders burn all three timeouts. Maxx Crosby sacks Geno for minus 10, and the Jets kick a 39-yarder with 9 seconds left. Jets 23, Raiders 14. Cousins kneels.
