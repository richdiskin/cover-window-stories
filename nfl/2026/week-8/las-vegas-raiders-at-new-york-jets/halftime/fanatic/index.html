Braelon Allen Bulldozes Vegas, Jets Up 23-14 at the Half

🏈 RAIDERS AT JETS | HALFTIME

Jets 23, Raiders 14

🗽 Mike Lasker here. Everybody said this game didn't matter. Look at the scoreboard, Vic.

🏴‍☠️ Vic Moreno. It's halftime, Mike. Settle down. My guys flew across the country and they're still right here.

⏱️ FIRST QUARTER

Mike: First snap of the day, Geno Smith throws deep to Garrett Wilson for 31. Good afternoon, Meadowlands.

Mike: Braelon Allen runs for 15, then 15 more. Write that name down.

Vic: And my defense digs in at the 3. No touchdown. Jason Sanders kicks a 21-yarder. Jets 3, Raiders 0.

Vic: Then Kirk Cousins goes to work. Ten plays, 60 yards. Jalen Nailor for 12, twice. Brock Bowers for 23 to the 7.

Vic: Bowers again from 12 out. Touchdown. Raiders 7, Jets 3.

Mike: You took a delay of game at the 7 first. Just saying.

Mike: Next drive, Wilson loses the ball and you guys recover it. Then replay says incomplete. Ball stays with us. Super Bowl III energy.

Vic: Twelve plays and you settle for a 36-yard field goal. Raiders 7, Jets 6.

Mike: McDonald sacks Cousins for minus 9. Three and out to close the quarter.

⏱️ SECOND QUARTER

Mike: Adonai Mitchell for 20. Mitchell for 22. Then Isaiah Williams, 18 yards down the middle. Touchdown. Jets 13, Raiders 7.

Vic: Twelve seconds later. Dylan Laube catches the kickoff at the 5 and goes 95 yards. Touchdown. Raiders 14, Jets 13. Willie Brown is smiling somewhere.

Mike: Fine. That was a great return. Then Allen rips off 24 up the middle, and Geno hits Mitchell from 12 out. Touchdown. Jets 20, Raiders 14.

Vic: We got to midfield. A direct snap to Heyward came up short, replay upheld it, and the Jets took it back on downs at their 46.

Mike: And then Braelon Allen happened. Nine yards. Six. Eleven. Nine. Four. Four. Vic's guys burned all three timeouts trying to slow him down.

Vic: Maxx Crosby got Geno for minus 10 in the final minute. Crosby always shows up.

Mike: Sanders drills a 39-yarder with 9 seconds left. Three for three. Cousins takes a knee. Halftime.

📊 FIRST HALF NUMBERS

Yards: Jets 276, Raiders 97

Braelon Allen: 18 carries, 121 yards

Geno Smith: 11 of 17, 146 yards, 2 touchdowns

Adonai Mitchell: 5 catches, 78 yards, 1 touchdown

Kirk Cousins: 6 of 7, 79 yards, 1 touchdown

Brock Bowers: 3 catches, 40 yards, 1 touchdown

Dylan Laube: 3 kick returns, 144 yards, 1 touchdown

Turnovers: none for either team

🗽 Mike: The Jets had the ball for more than 19 minutes. Everybody said this game didn't matter. That's exactly why it does.

🏴‍☠️ Vic: Nine points is one good drive and one good return. We've packed the vans before. We can pack a comeback.

Mike: Thirty minutes to go. Super Bowl III. Look it up.
