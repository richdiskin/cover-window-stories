Las Vegas Raiders at New York Jets

Las Vegas Raiders (3-4) at New York Jets (2-5)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, NJ · Fox

Two teams with losing records, a short number, and a Raiders team flying across the country for a 1 p.m. kickoff. I'm laying the 1.5 with the Jets.

Here's the setup: Las Vegas Raiders (3-4) at New York Jets (2-5), 1 p.m. Eastern on Fox from MetLife Stadium, the first Sunday back on standard time. The consensus has the Jets −1.5 with a total of 45.5 (as of Oct 27, 4:04 p.m. ET). My card: New York Jets −1.5, Under 45.5, and a final of Jets 24, Raiders 21.

Why the Jets? Not because of the record. New York is 2-5 and has scored 137 points in seven games. It's because Las Vegas doesn't stop anybody: 197 points allowed in seven games, and a run defense giving up 5.2 yards a carry. The Formula puts the Jets at −2.6 and the Raiders at −8.1, adds 1.75 for home field, and comes out Jets by 7.3. That's a lot of daylight for a number this short.

The ground game is where I see it. The Jets run for 3.9 a carry, nothing special, but against 5.2 allowed, that's room. Las Vegas runs for only 3.2 a carry against a Jets defense allowing 4.3. If neither team can run on the other, it's the Raiders who are in more trouble, because their defense is the one leaking.

Now the honest caveat: the Jets can't protect. New York's quarterbacks have been sacked about once every eleven dropbacks, and the Raiders' rush gets home about once every fourteen and a half. Las Vegas, meanwhile, protects well, allowing a sack about once every twenty-eight dropbacks, against a Jets rush that gets there about once every twelve. Kirk Cousins started the Raiders' last game against the Rams, and Geno Smith started the Jets' last game against Miami. The Week 8 injury report isn't out yet, so I'm going with the quarterbacks who started last week.

Why the Under? Because neither team runs the ball well, and offenses that can't run stall out. The Formula's number is 47.6, a little above the posted 45.5, but my final is 45. The Jets have allowed only 150 in seven games, and their defense is the best group on either side of this field.

The room disagrees about everything, which is fair for a 1.5 game. The Formula has Jets 27, Raiders 20. Letdown Watch and In The Trenches both have Jets 22, Raiders 21, a one-point Jets win that doesn't get past 1.5. Next Man Up has Raiders 24, Jets 23. Follow The Money adds nothing: the spread and total haven't moved since they opened.

Bottom of the card: a cross-country early start for a road team that can't stop the run. I'll lay the short number with the home side.

Jets 24, Raiders 21. Jets cover the 1.5. Under 45.5.
