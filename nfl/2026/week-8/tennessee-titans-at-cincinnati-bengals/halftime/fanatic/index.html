Fourteen Straight: Bengals Grab a 14-13 Halftime Lead on the Titans

Tennessee Titans (1-6) at Cincinnati Bengals (4-2)

Halftime · Sunday, Nov 1, 2026, 2:57 PM ET

XFacebook

🏈 HALFTIME: Titans at Bengals, Paycor Stadium

Bengals 14, Titans 13

Pete Varga drove up from East Nashville. Laura Kessel never left her seat. Here's the first half, in order.

⚔️ Tennessee opens with 10 plays and 52 yards. Cam Ward keeps it alive on 4th and 1 with a 6-yard scramble, and Joey Slye drills a 46-yard field goal. Titans 3, Bengals 0.

Pete: "Fourth down on the opening drive. That's growth, Laura. Write it down."

🥶 Cincinnati goes three plays and out, and the punt travels 28 yards.

💨 Then Tony Pollard. No huddle, right tackle, 40 yards, touchdown. Titans 10, Bengals 0 with 7:55 left in the first.

Pete: "Forty yards, no huddle. That is Titans football, Laura."

🚨 Joe Burrow throws deep for Ja'Marr Chase, and Amani Hooker intercepts it at the Tennessee 9 and brings it back 22 yards.

Laura: "One throw. Every football question is a quarterback question, and he'll answer this one."

🎯 Tennessee grinds out 11 plays and 58 yards, with a 23-yard Ward throw to Elic Ayomanor. Slye hits from 29. Titans 13, Bengals 0 with 2:14 left in the first.

🐯 Cincinnati answers with 9 plays and 65 yards into the second quarter. Burrow goes deep middle to Chase, 30 yards, touchdown. Titans 13, Bengals 7 at 13:20.

Laura: "Burrow to Chase. I was quiet for four seconds. I'm loud now."

😱 Ward hits Gunnar Helm for 24 and Chimere Dike for 21 to reach the Cincinnati 27. Then a short throw to Wan'Dale Robinson gets knocked loose, and Josh Newton recovers for the Bengals at their 32.

Pete: "That's a learning moment. Growth hurts."

🧱 The Bengals march 10 plays to the Tennessee 5, helped by a 33-yard Tee Higgins catch. On 4th and 2, Jacob Martin sacks Burrow for a 15-yard loss. Turnover on downs. That's two sacks in the half for Martin.

Pete: "Your man throwing it? We found him. Twice."

👟 Tennessee goes three plays for 5 yards and punts it away with 2:19 left.

⏱️ Two-minute drill. Burrow hits Mike Gesicki for 28, then Higgins twice. Tennessee burns all three timeouts. An interference flag on the Titans moves the ball to the 1, and Burrow finds Jack Endries for a 1-yard touchdown with 20 seconds left. Bengals 14, Titans 13.

🎭 Last gasp: a Cincinnati face mask hands Tennessee 15 free yards, but Ward's throw falls incomplete and a 5-yard run ends the half at the Tennessee 46.

📊 Half numbers:
Tennessee 252 yards, Cincinnati 212
Pollard 9 carries, 67 yards, 1 touchdown
Ward 14 of 22, 166 yards
Burrow 15 of 20, 178 yards, 2 touchdowns, 1 interception
Higgins 5 catches, 71 yards

Laura: "Fourteen straight. The man throwing it decides the afternoon. Arithmetic, Pete."

Pete: "Thirty minutes left. My Ward touchdown drive is still out there, and I'm still cheerful in your parking lot."

No ending is written yet. Second half, here we go.
