Titans +10.5 Sitting Pretty, Under 46.5 Sweating at the Half

Tennessee Titans (1-6) at Cincinnati Bengals (4-2)

Halftime · Sunday, Nov 1, 2026, 2:57 PM ET

ShareXFacebook

🏈 HALFTIME: Titans at Bengals

Bengals 14, Titans 13

Our calls: Titans +10.5 and Under 46.5. Let's sweat it out.

✅ Spread: Tennessee trails by 1 with 10.5 in its pocket. We're covering by 9.5.

😬 Total: 27 points at the half against 46.5. The second half needs 19 or fewer to stay Under. Twenty or more and it goes Over.

Now the first half, in order, and my blood pressure with it.

1️⃣ Titans open with 10 plays, 52 yards. Cam Ward converts a 4th and 1 with a 6-yard scramble. Joey Slye, 46-yard field goal. 3-0 Titans.

2️⃣ Bengals three and out. Short punt, 28 yards.

3️⃣ Tony Pollard, 40 yards off right tackle, touchdown at 7:55 of the first. 10-0 Titans. I'm feeling great. That's the problem.

4️⃣ Joe Burrow throws deep for Ja'Marr Chase. Amani Hooker intercepts at the Tennessee 9 and runs it back 22.

5️⃣ Titans grind 11 plays, 58 yards. Slye from 29 at 2:14 of the first. 13-0 Titans. The Under people are getting nervous already.

6️⃣ Bengals go 9 plays, 65 yards. Burrow to Chase, 30 yards deep middle, touchdown at 13:20 of the second. 13-7.

7️⃣ Ward hits Gunnar Helm for 24 and Chimere Dike for 21 to reach the Cincinnati 27. Then Wan'Dale Robinson fumbles after a short catch and Josh Newton recovers for Cincinnati at its 32. Points off the board for Tennessee. Good for the Under. Terrible for my nerves.

8️⃣ Bengals drive 10 plays to the Tennessee 5, with a 33-yard Tee Higgins catch in there. 4th and 2: Jacob Martin sacks Burrow for minus 15. Turnover on downs. Points saved for the Under, and the cover stays comfy.

9️⃣ Titans three and out. Punt at 2:19.

🔟 Two-minute drill. Burrow to Mike Gesicki for 28, Higgins twice, Tennessee burns all three timeouts. An interference flag puts it at the 1, and Burrow finds Jack Endries for the 1-yard touchdown with 20 seconds left. 14-13 Bengals. Fourteen straight for Cincinnati.

⏹️ Tennessee gets 15 free yards on a Bengals face mask, then an incompletion and a 5-yard Ward run end the half.

🧾 The sweat:
Spread: we can lose by 10 and still cash. Cincinnati has momentum and the ball game's best throwing arm right now, so 9.5 of cushion feels nice but not safe.

Total: 27 already, with both offenses over 6 yards a play. Nineteen more is the ceiling. Two touchdowns and a field goal in the second half is 17 and we're fine. Three touchdowns is 21 and we're done.

Hold on tight.
