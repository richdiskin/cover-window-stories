Carolina Panthers at Green Bay Packers

Carolina Panthers (3-3) at Green Bay Packers (4-3)

8:15 PM ET Thu Oct 29 · Lambeau Field, Green Bay, WI · Prime Video

Carolina Panthers vs Green Bay Packers opens Week 8 on Thursday night at Lambeau Field after Green Bay spent Wednesday chasing a record high, with a clear, mid-fifties kickoff forecast that counts as a heat wave up here in late October. Packers fan Paul Lindgren calls the weather a courtesy to guests, and Panthers fan Mitch Kovac, who flew in braced for the frozen tundra, calls it a trap. Carolina comes in at 3-3, Green Bay at 4-3, both teams on a short week, 8:15 p.m. Eastern on Prime Video, the last kickoff before the clocks fall back. Paul is in shirtsleeves, guarding the Packers' ledger of championships like the wind might take it. Mitch showed up in a parka out of pure suspicion.

Mitch: Paul, I packed for the frozen tundra. You people sold me a brochure.

Paul: The tundra takes Thursdays off when company comes. Go stand by the Titletown stage before the game. Ha Ha Clinton-Dix and Jarrett Bush are the alumni up there tonight. Two guys who made a living taking the football away from quarterbacks.

Mitch: I'll shake their hands and show them my depth chart. They'll want to see how a roster gets built.

Paul: Built? Mitch, we have a ledger. Thirteen league championships. Eleven came before the merger, and four Super Bowls, the first two, then XXXI and XLV, and nobody in this county counts the overlap twice.

Mitch: You recite it like a grocery list you learned at your grandmother's knee.

Paul: I did learn it at her knee. She also taught me Herb Adderley, Jerry Kramer, Willie Davis and LeRoy Butler, in that order, before I could ride a bike.

Mitch: And I learned Julius Peppers. Drafted in Charlotte, raised in Charlotte, carried into the Hall of Fame. Carolina won the NFC South last season the same way, from the inside out. Dan Morgan built this roster front first, and Derrick Brown is the proof.

Paul: Lovely speech. Look at our season, though. Seven games, 144 points scored, 145 allowed. We're 2-1 at home. We have been walking a tightrope since September.

Mitch: A tightrope with a one-point hole in it. My guys have scored 159 and allowed 166 in six, and we're 2-1 on the road. We travel, Paul. We like your stadium.

The real argument tonight is up front, and the official injury report gave both of them ammunition. Green Bay will be without guard Travis Glover and center Sean Rhyan, the two men in the middle of Jordan Love's protection, plus linebacker Zaire Franklin. Receiver Matthew Golden is questionable. Carolina ruled out guards Damien Lewis and Torricelli Simpkins III, linebacker Jaelan Phillips and cornerback Akayleb Evans. Bryce Young was a full participant all week, and Love isn't on the report at all.

Mitch: Two of the people who snap and block for Love are out. Matt LaFleur and Adam Stenavich will be drawing protections on a napkin by halftime.

Paul: Napkins are for brats, Mitch. And two of your guards are out too. So are your linebacker and your corner. We can count that high in Brown County.

Mitch: Then count my sacrifices. Back home in NoDa, the neighbors are decorating houses for the NoDaWeen contest. Hodges Family Farm closes its pumpkin patch for the year on Saturday. I'm missing all of it to sit on your porch.

Paul: You're missing it for history. Both defenses play a 3-4 base front, Ejiro Evero's for you and Jonathan Gannon's for us. Love started at Detroit, Young started at home against Tampa Bay.

Mitch: Fine. Ask me the question you always ask.

Paul: Super Bowl trophies since your first season?

Mitch: None yet. Two NFC titles, a division banner from last year and a locker room of players we drafted ourselves. Ask again in February.

Paul: I'll write your answer in pencil. The ledger only takes ink for titles.

Mitch: Keep the pencil sharp. And lend me a sweatshirt for the fourth quarter. Even a heat wave in Green Bay goes cold after dark.

Paul hands Mitch a sweatshirt with a G on it. Mitch puts it on inside out, and they walk toward the gate in weather nobody in Brown County will admit was pleasant.

My call: Packers 24, Panthers 22.
