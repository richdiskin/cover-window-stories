Carolina Panthers at Green Bay Packers

Carolina Panthers (3-3) at Green Bay Packers (4-3)

8:15 PM ET Thu Oct 29 · Lambeau Field, Green Bay, WI · Prime Video

Thursday night at Lambeau Field, and the number says Green Bay by 3.5. I say that is a field goal and a hook of respect for a home team that has scored 144 points and allowed 145.

Here's the setup: Carolina Panthers (3-3) at Green Bay Packers (4-3), 8:15 p.m. Eastern on Prime Video, the last kickoff of the year on daylight time before the clocks fall back Sunday. The consensus has Green Bay −3.5 with a total of 48 (as of Oct 27, 4:04 p.m. ET). My card: Carolina Panthers +3.5, Under 48, and a final of Packers 24, Panthers 22.

Why the Panthers? Because Green Bay hasn't separated from anybody. Seven games, 144 scored, 145 allowed, a point differential of minus one. Carolina has scored 159 and allowed 166 in six games and is 2-1 on the road. The Formula puts Green Bay at −3.5 and Carolina at −1.5, and even after handing the Packers 1.75 points for Lambeau, it comes out Carolina by 0.3. That is a coin flip wearing a field goal and a hook.

The ground game is where it shows. Green Bay runs for 3.2 yards a carry against a Carolina defense that allows 4.9. That's 1.7 yards a carry sitting there for the taking, and the Packers haven't been able to take it from anybody. Carolina runs for 4.5 against a Green Bay defense allowing 4.1, so the Panthers can stay on schedule without anything cute.

Now the honest caveat, because I don't sell sunshine: protection. Carolina's quarterbacks have been sacked about once every fourteen dropbacks, and Green Bay's rush gets home about once every eighteen. Bryce Young started the Tampa Bay game and figures to face a busy night in a loud building on a short week. Green Bay's blockers have been steadier, giving up a sack about once every twenty dropbacks, and Carolina's rush gets there at about that same clip. The Week 8 injury report isn't out yet, so I'm not guessing at anybody's status.

Why the Under? Because this Green Bay offense lives at about three touchdowns a game, 144 points in seven. The Formula's number is 47.7, and my final is 46, two points shy of the posted 48. A run game stuck in the mud and two teams on four days of rest is not a recipe for fireworks. It's a recipe for punts and field goals.

The room mostly agrees with me. The Formula has Panthers 24, Packers 23, an outright Carolina win. In The Trenches has Packers 24, Panthers 23, and Next Man Up has Packers 23, Panthers 22, both well inside the number. Letdown Watch is the lone dissenter at Packers 25, Panthers 20, and it would happily lay the points with Green Bay. Follow The Money has nothing to chase: the spread opened at 3.5, the total opened at 48, and neither has budged.

Bottom of the card: Carolina doesn't need to win at Lambeau. It needs to keep a Green Bay offense that can't run the ball inside a field goal and a hook. That's the game I see, a one-score night decided late, with the Packers winning it and the Panthers getting the money.

Packers 24, Panthers 22. Panthers cover the 3.5. Under 48.
