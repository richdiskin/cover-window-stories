Carolina Panthers at Green Bay Packers

Carolina Panthers (3-3) at Green Bay Packers (4-3)

8:15 PM ET Thu Oct 29 · Lambeau Field, Green Bay, WI · Prime Video

Thursday night at Lambeau Field, and the number says Green Bay by 3.5. I say that is a field goal and a hook of respect for a home team that has scored 144 points and allowed 145.

Here's the setup: Carolina Panthers (3-3) at Green Bay Packers (4-3), 8:15 p.m. Eastern on Prime Video, the last kickoff of the year on daylight time before the clocks fall back Sunday. The consensus has Green Bay −3.5 with a total of 47.5 (as of Oct 28, 6:49 p.m. ET). My card: Carolina Panthers +3.5, Under 47.5, and a final of Packers 24, Panthers 22.

Why the Panthers? Because Green Bay hasn't separated from anybody. Seven games, 144 scored, 145 allowed, a point differential of minus one. Carolina has scored 159 and allowed 166 in six games and is 2-1 on the road. The Formula puts Green Bay at −3.5 and Carolina at −1.5, and even after handing the Packers 1.75 points for Lambeau, it comes out Carolina by 0.3. That is a coin flip wearing a field goal and a hook.

The injury report is where this game changed. Green Bay ruled out guard Travis Glover and center Sean Rhyan, so two of the men in the middle of Jordan Love's protection won't be there Thursday night. The Packers are also without linebacker Zaire Franklin, and receiver Matthew Golden is questionable. Losing two interior blockers on a short week is not how a team lays more than a field goal comfortably.

Now the honest caveat, because I don't sell sunshine: Carolina got hit too. The Panthers ruled out guard Damien Lewis, linebacker Jaelan Phillips and cornerback Akayleb Evans. Bryce Young was a full participant and started the Tampa Bay game, but he figures to face a busy night in a loud building on a short week with a patched-up front of his own, and his defense is missing a linebacker and a corner.

Why the Under? Because this Green Bay offense lives at about three touchdowns a game, 144 points in seven. The Formula's number is 47.7, and my final is 46, a point and a half shy of the posted 47.5. Two patched-up offensive fronts and two teams on four days of rest is not a recipe for fireworks. It's a recipe for punts and field goals.

The room mostly agrees with me on the side. The Formula has Panthers 25, Packers 24, an outright Carolina win, and Next Man Up has Packers 23, Panthers 22, well inside the number. Letdown Watch is the lone dissenter at Packers 25, Panthers 20, and it would happily lay the points with Green Bay. In The Trenches sits this one out, because three interior blockers are ruled out and its season numbers were built with them on the field. Follow The Money has nothing to chase: Action Network's consensus opened at Green Bay −3.5 and hasn't budged, and the total has slipped only from 48 to 47.5.

Bottom of the card: Carolina doesn't need to win at Lambeau. It needs to keep a Green Bay offense missing two interior blockers inside a field goal and a hook. That's the game I see, a one-score night decided late, with the Packers winning it and the Panthers getting the money.

Packers 24, Panthers 22. Panthers cover the 3.5. Under 47.5.
