The Fanatic · Halftime · NFL

Bills 16, Ravens 7 at the half: Bass boots three, Baltimore burns through three quarterbacks

Baltimore Ravens (4-3) at Buffalo Bills (5-1)

Halftime · Sunday, Nov 1, 2026, 2:57 PM ET

ShareXFacebook

🏈 HALFTIME IN ORCHARD PARK

Bills 16, Ravens 7

Owen Pratt (Ravens) and Ellen Marsh (Bills) walk you through the first half, snap by snap.

🟦 Q1, 15:00
Baltimore's opening kickoff sails out of bounds. Bills ball at their own 40.
Ellen: Free yards before anybody blinks. Thank you, Owen.

📈 Josh Allen hits Khalil Shakir for 26, then Shakir again for 18 down to the Baltimore 5.
Shakir looks like he scores, but replay puts him down at the 1.
Allen keeps it himself and powers in from a yard out.
Bills 7, Ravens 0 (10:10 Q1)
Ellen: Ten plays, 60 yards.

🚑 Baltimore's first snap: Lamar Jackson is hurt on the play.
Tyler Huntley comes in. Three and out.
Owen: Not how anybody in purple drew it up.

🦵 Tyler Bass from 56 yards. Good.
Bills 10, Ravens 0 (6:05 Q1)

🛑 Derrick Henry rips off 8, but Baltimore punts again.

🔥 Then the Ravens strike back. Broderick Washington Jr. steps in front of Allen at the Buffalo 17.
Henry bulls 12 yards to the 5, and after a Bills encroachment flag he plows in from 2.
Bills 10, Ravens 7 (3:18 Q1)
Owen: THAT is Baltimore football. Take the ball, hand it to the big man, go home.

🧱 Buffalo marches to the Baltimore 37, but a fourth-down throw to Keon Coleman falls incomplete on the first snap of Q2.
Owen: Stopped cold. Write it down, Ellen.

💥 Three plays later Cole Bishop sacks Huntley, the ball squirts loose, and Kaleb Elarms-Orr pounces at the Baltimore 20.
Ellen: Write THAT down, Owen. In ink.

🦵 The Bills grind nine plays toward the doorstep, Allen gets dropped for a loss of 3, and Bass knocks home a 22-yarder.
Bills 13, Ravens 7 (9:50 Q2)

🏃 Rasheen Ali answers with a 59-yard kickoff return to the Buffalo 35.
Joe Fagnano is now the Baltimore quarterback, the third one to take a snap today.
He hits Josh Cuevas for 15 and Ja'Kobi Lane for 13, all the way to the 7.
Then a sack shoves Baltimore back to the 16, two throws fall incomplete, and Tyler Loop's 34-yard try clanks off the left upright.
Owen: The upright. THE UPRIGHT. That pole owes me a crab cake.
Ellen: That pole is a season ticket holder.

🔁 Both teams trade three and outs.

🏃 James Cook bursts for 16, then 6, then 3. Ty Johnson grabs an 18-yarder to the Baltimore 39.
Right after the two-minute warning, Allen finds DJ Moore for 18, but Roquan Smith punches it free and T.J. Tampa recovers at the Baltimore 18.
Owen: Roquan Smith, you magnificent linebacker.

🎯 Baltimore can't cash it in. Greg Rousseau sacks Fagnano, Justice Hill grabs two catches, and then Davison Igbinosun snags a throw meant for Zay Flowers at midfield and races 17 yards to the Baltimore 33.
Ellen: Another Baltimore drive, smashed.

⏱️ A chop block call shoves Buffalo back 15. No matter.
With 20 seconds left, Allen tucks it and runs 14 yards to the 33.
Bass drills a 51-yarder with 9 seconds to go.
Bills 16, Ravens 7 (0:09 Q2)

📊 First-half numbers
Yards of offense: Bills 202, Ravens 52
Ravens sacked 4 times
Bills 3 for 4 on fourth down
Time of possession: Bills 19:30, Ravens 10:30
Cook: 14 carries, 54 yards
Shakir: 3 catches, 49 yards
Henry: 6 carries, 26 yards and the Ravens' only touchdown
Two turnovers apiece

Owen: Down nine with half a game to play. Baltimore has climbed bigger hills than this one.
Ellen: Bring your climbing gear. Bass is warmed up.
