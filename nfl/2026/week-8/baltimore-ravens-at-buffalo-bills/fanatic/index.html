Baltimore Ravens at Buffalo Bills

Baltimore Ravens (4-3) at Buffalo Bills (5-1)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · Highmark Stadium, Orchard Park, NY · CBS

Baltimore Ravens vs Buffalo Bills in Week 8 gets the first Sunday of standard time at Highmark Stadium, with Lamar Jackson practicing fully and Josh Allen rested off a bye. While Ellen Marsh spent the off week walking Science Stadium, the Buffalo Museum of Science exhibit built with the Bills, Sabres and Bandits, Owen Pratt was back in Baltimore circling the Maryland Science Center's Pumpkin Smash, where people fling their jack-o'-lanterns off the roof the morning after Halloween, and he arrived in Orchard Park sure his defense would do the same to Buffalo's January dreams.

Owen: I'll miss it for this. Somewhere on the Inner Harbor a pumpkin is falling off a roof without me, Ellen, and I'd like you to know what I gave up.

Ellen: You gave up a pumpkin. We gave up two weeks of football waiting for you. Buffalo is 5-1, 3-0 on the road, and Joe Brady had the whole staff in the building drawing up plays while your guys were icing their knees from Cincinnati.

Owen judges every Ravens team by its defense first, and he starts there now, because that is where Baltimore always starts. Super Bowl XXXV belonged to Ray Lewis in the middle and a defense nobody could score on. XLVII made it two Lombardi trophies. Ellen judges every Bills team by January and only January, and her ledger has its own weight: two straight AFL championships, then four straight Super Bowl trips with Thurman Thomas, Andre Reed and Bruce Smith.

Owen: And how did those four Sundays in late January go?

Ellen: You know exactly how they went. It's why five AFC East crowns in a row didn't get one hat out of me.

Owen: Ed Reed would have bought you the hat. Then intercepted it.

Here is what the season says. Buffalo has scored 197 points and allowed 171 in six games, and it has been better away from Orchard Park than at home, 2-1 here. Baltimore is 4-3, 3-1 on the road and 1-2 at home, with 169 scored and 167 allowed. Tyler Huntley started the Cincinnati game, but Jackson was a full participant Wednesday and is expected back. Allen started Buffalo's last game, in Week 6 at Las Vegas.

Both of these teams want to run, and both defenses have spent the fall refusing to let anybody do it. Buffalo gains 5.4 yards a carry; Baltimore allows 3.9. Baltimore gains 4.8; Buffalo also allows 3.9.

Owen: Derrick Henry in the fourth quarter. You've seen the film.

Ellen: James Cook in the first. In our house the game gets decided before you warm up.

The front sevens look alike on paper. Jesse Minter and Anthony Weaver run a 3-4 base for Baltimore with Roquan Smith in the middle of it. Jim Leonhard runs a 3-4 base for Buffalo. Same blueprint, different zip code, and Ellen will not stop saying so.

Where they separate is the pocket. Baltimore's quarterbacks have been sacked about once every twelve dropbacks, the stat that keeps Owen up at night. Buffalo's rush has been quieter, home about once every twenty-five. Buffalo's blockers give one up about once every fifteen, and Baltimore's rush gets there about once every sixteen.

Owen: Once every twelve. I'll say it out loud. The Ravens are supposed to hand those out.

Ellen: Greg Rousseau and Bradley Chubb would be glad to help you collect more.

Wednesday's practice report gave Owen good news and bad. Jackson was full. Roquan Smith, Kyle Hamilton and Calais Campbell all sat out, three of the men who make that defense what Owen describes in his sleep. No one carries an official game designation yet. Ellen noted that Buffalo's list is mostly limited practices. Owen noted that nobody asked.

Ellen: When Buffalo finally wins one, I'll celebrate for exactly one night. Then I'll ask about next year.

Owen: That's how Baltimore thinks every year. We just get to do the celebrating part first.

Ellen: Enjoy your pumpkin, Owen. Listen for the splat on the drive home.

Somewhere on the Inner Harbor the last pumpkin hits the pavement around kickoff, and Owen would trade every one of them for a fourth-quarter stop against Allen.

My call: Bills 29, Ravens 24.
