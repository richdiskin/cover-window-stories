Baltimore Ravens at Buffalo Bills

Baltimore Ravens (4-3) at Buffalo Bills (5-1)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · Highmark Stadium, Orchard Park, NY · CBS

Ravens vs Bills prediction, pick against the spread and over/under for Week 8: Baltimore's Maryland Science Center spends Sunday morning launching jack-o'-lanterns off its roof at the Pumpkin Smash, and by 1 p.m. the Ravens will find out whether their defense can do the same to Josh Allen in Orchard Park. Buffalo is off a bye, Lamar Jackson is back, and I'm laying the 3 anyway.

Kickoff is 1 p.m. Eastern on CBS from Highmark Stadium. The consensus has Buffalo −3 with a total of 54.5 as of Oct 28, 6:49 p.m. ET, and my card reads Buffalo Bills −3, Under 54.5, with a final of Bills 29, Ravens 24.

The Bills side starts with the calendar. Buffalo last played in Week 6 at Las Vegas and comes in off a bye. Baltimore played Cincinnati at home last Sunday and has a normal week. The Bills are 5-1 with 197 points scored in six games, and they have been better on the road (3-0) than at home (2-1). The Formula puts Buffalo at 2.2 and Baltimore at −2, and with 1.75 for home field it comes out Bills by 6. That's a six-point team getting asked to lay only 3.

The ground game agrees. Buffalo runs for 5.4 yards a carry against a Baltimore defense that allows 3.9, and Baltimore's protection has been the shakiest thing on the field: about one sack every twelve dropbacks. Tyler Huntley started the Cincinnati game for the Ravens, but he's not the quarterback I'm handicapping. Lamar Jackson was a full participant on Wednesday's official injury report and is expected back, and Josh Allen started Buffalo's last game before the bye. The bigger Baltimore worry is on defense: Roquan Smith, Kyle Hamilton and Calais Campbell all sat out Wednesday.

Baltimore can run too, and that is where this could go wrong. The Ravens get 4.8 a carry against a Buffalo defense that also allows 3.9, and they are 3-1 away from home. Buffalo's rush only gets home about once every twenty-five dropbacks, so Lamar Jackson won't be running for his life. If Baltimore controls the clock, this stays a field goal game.

As for the total, the Under, because the posted 54.5 already assumes both offenses hit their best day. My final is 53, a point and a half shy, and two teams that can run the ball shorten the game.

The room splits right down the middle on the side. The Formula has Buffalo by 6 and Letdown Watch has Bills 30, Ravens 21, and both lay the 3. Next Man Up has Bills 27, Ravens 25 and In The Trenches has Bills 28, Ravens 26, and both would take Baltimore with the points. On the total, three of the four sit below 54.5, and only The Formula goes over. Follow The Money finally has a read: Action Network's consensus total opened at 53.5 and climbed a full point to 54.5, so it sides with the Over.

Baltimore's pumpkins come off the Science Center roof a few hours before kickoff, and the Ravens' pocket looks like the next thing to fall. A rested home team running at 5.4 a carry against protection that gives up sacks is worth a field goal.

Bills 29, Ravens 24. Bills cover the 3. Under 54.5.
