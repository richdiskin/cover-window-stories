The Fanatic · Halftime · NFL

Vikings 7, Lions 6 at the half: four sacks, a touchdown taken away and a buzzer-beating Bates kick

Minnesota Vikings (6-0) at Detroit Lions (4-2)

Halftime · Sunday, Nov 1, 2026, 2:57 PM ET

ShareXFacebook

🏈 HALFTIME AT FORD FIELD

Vikings 7, Lions 6

Erik Dahl (Vikings, professional worrier) and Martin Sorensen (Lions, Honolulu blue to the bone) walk you through the first half.

🟣 Q1, first snap
Kyler Murray hits Ben Yurosek for 24 yards.
Erik: "For one second I felt joy. Then I remembered who I root for."

❌ Four plays later, Will Reichard's 57-yard try sails wide right.
Erik: "There it is. Feels like home."

🦁 Detroit gets a roughing-the-passer flag and a free 15.
Then Amon-Ra St. Brown draws a taunting flag and gives 15 right back.
Andrew Van Ginkel sacks Jared Goff. Eric Wilson sacks him again for 10. Punt.
Erik: "I told you Goff would see purple."
Martin: "He saw it twice. He doesn't have to enjoy it."

🟣 Jordan Mason rips off 10, then the Vikings stall. Punt.

🦁 Lions grind 12 plays, 57 yards.
Goff finds Jameson Williams deep middle for 46... and Williams taunts. Another 15 back.
Goff to St. Brown in the end zone is ruled a touchdown. Replay takes it away.
Jahmyr Gibbs runs for 12, Sam LaPorta catches one at the 4, then Gibbs gets dropped for a loss.
✅ Jake Bates, 25-yard field goal. Lions 3, Vikings 0 at 2:18 of the 1st.
Martin: "Two taunting flags, a touchdown erased, and we STILL lead. The window is open, Erik."

🟣 Vikings march 74 yards in 7 plays.
D.J. Reed is flagged for interference, 23 yards.
Murray goes deep right to T.J. Hockenson for 22, down to the Detroit 16.
Q2 opens with Murray to Aaron Jones for 15, all the way to the 1.
🎯 Murray to Justin Jefferson from 1 yard out. TOUCHDOWN at 14:25 of the 2nd. Reichard adds the kick.
Vikings 7, Lions 3.
Erik: "Jefferson said he'd like a word. That was the word."

🧤 Three plays after a muffed-but-saved kickoff, Goff throws for Williams and Pierre intercepts at the Minnesota 46.
Martin: "We don't discuss that one. Ever."

🔁 Then the punters take the wheel.
Brian O'Neill jumps early, Vikings three-and-out, punt pins Detroit at the 8.
Penei Sewell jumps early on back-to-back snaps. Lions punt.
Murray gets sacked for 9 by Gill-Howard. Punt.
Van Ginkel gets Goff again. Punt.

🟣 3:10 left. Jefferson catches two in a row, Murray scrambles 22 yards to midfield after the two-minute warning.
❌ Then Murray throws for Jordan Addison and D.J. Reed intercepts at the Detroit 38.
Erik: "Seven-game rumor. One-throw reality."

🦁 1:47 left, Lions take it.
Gibbs, St. Brown, Gibbs. Williams for 14. St. Brown for 6.
A Sewell holding flag wipes a play, then Gibbs bursts 19 yards to the Minnesota 19.
✅ Bates from 37 with 0:02 left. Good.
Murray takes a knee. Halftime.

📊 First half by the numbers
Lions 154 yards of offense, Vikings 130
Vikings defense: 4 sacks, Van Ginkel in on 3 of them
One interception each
Lions 0-for-2 in the red zone, 6 penalties for 72 yards
Jefferson: 4 catches, 25 yards, 1 touchdown

Martin: "Two field goals, a touchdown taken away, and we're down ONE. Second half, this building gets loud."
Erik: "Up one at the half. Still a rumor. Thirty minutes to go, and I trust none of them."
