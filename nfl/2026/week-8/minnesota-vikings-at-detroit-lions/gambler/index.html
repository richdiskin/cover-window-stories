Minnesota Vikings at Detroit Lions

Minnesota Vikings (6-0) at Detroit Lions (4-2)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · Ford Field, Detroit, MI · Fox

The only unbeaten team in this game is getting 3.5 points. Minnesota is 6-0, has allowed 74 points in six games, and the consensus still has Detroit laying more than a field goal. I'll take the points and the unbeaten team.

Here's the setup: Minnesota Vikings (6-0) at Detroit Lions (4-2), 1 p.m. Eastern on Fox from Ford Field, the first Sunday back on standard time. The consensus has Detroit −3.5 with a total of 46.5 (as of Oct 27, 4:04 p.m. ET). My card: Minnesota Vikings +3.5, Under 46.5, and a final of Lions 23, Vikings 22.

Why the Vikings? Because they haven't lost, and they haven't needed their building to win: 3-0 at home, 3-0 on the road. They've scored 125 and allowed 74. Detroit is 3-0 at home and has been the better offense, 184 points in six games, but the Lions have also allowed 165. A defense allowing about a dozen points a game against an offense that gives up its share is a recipe for a close game, and I'm getting 3.5 in a close game.

Minnesota's rush is the loudest number in this matchup. Minnesota gets a sack about once every nine dropbacks, and Detroit's protection gives one up about once every twenty-one. That's a real mismatch in Minnesota's favor, and it's how a road team keeps a game within a field goal.

Now the honest caveat: Minnesota's own protection is the shakiest thing on the field. The Vikings have been sacked about once every ten dropbacks, and Detroit's rush gets there about once every thirteen. Kyler Murray started Minnesota's last game against Indianapolis, and Jared Goff started Detroit's against Green Bay. The Week 8 injury report isn't out yet, so those are the quarterbacks I'm working from. And the honest truth about the run game is that it's even: Minnesota runs for 4.3 a carry, Detroit for 4.4, and both defenses allow 4.2.

Why the Under? Minnesota has allowed 74 points in six games. My final is 45, the Formula's number is 45.7, and the posted 46.5 asks for more than this Vikings defense has allowed to anybody lately. Two rushes getting home this often kill drives.

The room is about as split as it gets, which tells you how close this is. The Formula has Lions 25, Vikings 20, the only scoring opinion that lays the 3.5. In The Trenches has Lions 21, Vikings 20. Next Man Up has Vikings 23, Lions 22, and Letdown Watch has Vikings 25, Lions 24. Two of the four have Minnesota winning outright. Follow The Money is silent: the spread and total haven't moved since they opened.

Bottom of the card: I have Detroit by a point at home, which is respect for Ford Field. But a 6-0 team with a rush like this getting 3.5 is the number I want.

Lions 23, Vikings 22. Vikings cover the 3.5. Under 46.5.
