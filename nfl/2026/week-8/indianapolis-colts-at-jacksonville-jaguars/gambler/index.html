Indianapolis Colts at Jacksonville Jaguars

Indianapolis Colts (2-5) at Jacksonville Jaguars (4-2)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · EverBank Stadium, Jacksonville, FL · CBS

Colts vs Jaguars prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: downtown Jacksonville hands out candy at Treats at the Plaza on Saturday, and a rested Jaguars team gets to host a 2-5 Colts team on its second straight road trip the next afternoon. Everything here screams Jaguars at 9.5, and I'm still taking Indianapolis and the points.

CBS has it at 1 p.m. Eastern from EverBank Stadium. The consensus has Jacksonville −9.5 with a total of 46.5 as of Oct 28, 6:49 p.m. ET. My card: Indianapolis Colts +9.5, Over 46.5, final Jaguars 28, Colts 20.

The Colts side is a margin argument, because 9.5 is a lot to ask of an offense that has scored 154 points in seven games. Jacksonville's defense has been terrific, allowing just 104, but winning by double digits takes points, and the Jaguars score only a few more points a game than the 2-5 Colts.

The Formula: Net ratings Jacksonville Jaguars −0.3, Indianapolis Colts −6.5 (nflverse play-by-play, Oct 28, 2026).

Its card has Jaguars 27, Colts 19, a clear Jacksonville win that still falls short of 9.5.

The run game is the surprise. Indianapolis runs for 4.2 yards a carry against a Jacksonville defense allowing only 3.3, which is a tough wall, but the Colts can also hold the ball: Jacksonville runs for 4.3 against an Indianapolis defense allowing 5.1. If Jacksonville decides to grind, it burns clock, and clock is a friend to the team getting 9.5.

The part that could sink me is the schedule. Jacksonville is coming off a bye, and Indianapolis played at Minnesota last week and now travels again. Daniel Jones started the Minnesota game for the Colts, and Trevor Lawrence started Jacksonville's last game against Houston before the bye. Indianapolis quarterbacks have been sacked about once every fourteen dropbacks, while Jacksonville's rush gets home about once every twenty-three. Jacksonville allows a sack about once every nineteen, and the Colts get one about once every seventeen. Jones and Lawrence are both absent from the Wednesday practice report, which is the good news. Sauce Gardner and Tyler Warren were limited for Indianapolis.

The Over comes from the same Indianapolis run defense. My final adds up to 48, over the posted 46.5. The Formula's number is about 46, and I'm going higher because a Colts team chasing a lead has to throw, and a rested Jacksonville team should score its share in its own building.

Three of the four scores take the Colts. Next Man Up has Jaguars 27, Colts 22 and In The Trenches has Jaguars 28, Colts 21, both with Indianapolis inside the number and both going Over. The Formula has Jaguars 27, Colts 19, also inside, but it goes Under. Letdown Watch is the lone score on Jacksonville, Jaguars 28, Colts 18, on the rest advantage. Follow The Money is the one that talks this week, and it sides with Jacksonville too.

The Riverfront Plaza treats run out Saturday, and Jacksonville wins Sunday. I just don't think it wins by ten. I'll take the Colts and the 9.5.

Jaguars 28, Colts 20. Colts cover the 9.5. Over 46.5.
