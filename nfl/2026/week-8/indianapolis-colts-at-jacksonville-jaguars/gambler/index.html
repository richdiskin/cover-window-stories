Indianapolis Colts at Jacksonville Jaguars

Indianapolis Colts (2-5) at Jacksonville Jaguars (4-2)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · EverBank Stadium, Jacksonville, FL · CBS

Jacksonville is rested, at home, and laying 9.5. Indianapolis is on its second straight road trip with a 2-5 record. Everything about this screams Jaguars, and I'm still taking the Colts and the points.

Here's the setup: Indianapolis Colts (2-5) at Jacksonville Jaguars (4-2), 1 p.m. Eastern on CBS from EverBank Stadium, the first Sunday back on standard time. The consensus has Jacksonville −9.5 with a total of 46.5 (as of Oct 27, 4:04 p.m. ET). My card: Indianapolis Colts +9.5, Over 46.5, and a final of Jaguars 28, Colts 20.

Why the Colts? Because 9.5 is a lot to ask of an offense that has scored 154 points in six games. Jacksonville's defense has been terrific, allowing just 104, but winning by double digits takes points, and the Jaguars score about the same per game as the 2-5 Colts do. The Formula puts Jacksonville at −0.3 and Indianapolis at −6.5, adds 1.75 for home field, and comes out Jaguars by 7.9. That's a clear Jacksonville win that still falls short of 9.5.

The run game is the surprise. Indianapolis runs for 4.2 yards a carry against a Jacksonville defense allowing only 3.3, which is a tough wall, but the Colts can also hold the ball: Jacksonville runs for 4.3 against an Indianapolis defense allowing 5.1. If Jacksonville decides to grind, it burns clock, and clock is a friend to the team getting 9.5.

Now the honest caveat: the schedule. Jacksonville is coming off a bye, and Indianapolis played at Minnesota last week and now travels again. Daniel Jones started the Minnesota game for the Colts, and Trevor Lawrence started Jacksonville's last game against Houston before the bye. Indianapolis quarterbacks have been sacked about once every fourteen dropbacks, while Jacksonville's rush gets home about once every twenty-three. Jacksonville allows a sack about once every nineteen, and the Colts get one about once every seventeen. The Week 8 injury report isn't out yet, so I'm working from last week's starting quarterbacks.

Why the Over? My final adds up to 48, over the posted 46.5. The Formula's number is 45.4, and I'm going higher because a Colts team chasing a lead has to throw, and a rested Jacksonville team should score its share in its own building.

The room is split down the middle. Next Man Up has Jaguars 27, Colts 22 and In The Trenches has Jaguars 28, Colts 21, both with Indianapolis inside the number and both going Over. The Formula has Jaguars 27, Colts 19, also inside. Letdown Watch has Jaguars 28, Colts 18 on the rest advantage. Follow The Money is the one that talks this week, and it sides with Jacksonville.

Bottom of the card: Jacksonville wins. I just don't think it wins by ten. I'll take the Colts and the 9.5.

Jaguars 28, Colts 20. Colts cover the 9.5. Over 46.5.
