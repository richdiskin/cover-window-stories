Jaguars Roar to a 28-0 Halftime Lead as Lane Takes One 92 Yards

Indianapolis Colts (2-5) at Jacksonville Jaguars (4-2)

Halftime · Sunday, Nov 1, 2026, 2:57 PM ET

ShareXFacebook

🏈 HALFTIME IN DUVAL: Colts at Jaguars

Jaguars 28, Colts 0

Lou Ferraro flew down from Indianapolis with three pre-merger titles in his carry-on. Ivy Corcoran brought her splits. Here's how 30 minutes went.

🐴 Lou: First snap, Jonathan Taylor rips off 19 yards to the Jacksonville 48. I told you.

🐆 Ivy: And then you punted.

🐴 Lou: A beauty! Rigoberto Sanchez pinned you at your own 2. Downed right there by Dulin.

🐆 Ivy: So we went 98 yards. Twelve plays. 7:08 off the clock.

🐆 Ivy: Brenton Strange hauled in a 46-yard deep throw from Trevor Lawrence. Bhayshul Tuten ran it straight down your throat.

🐆 Ivy: Then Lawrence found Brian Thomas Jr. from 12 yards out. 7-0, 5:30 left in the first.

🐴 Lou: Your own guy jumped early right before that score. I saw it.

🐆 Ivy: We scored anyway, Lou.

🐴 Lou: Fine. Then Travon Walker sacked Jones, we punted, you punted, and my Colts drew a face mask to get going.

🐆 Ivy: Then two flags in a row on your side for jumping early. Back to back. Then another punt.

🐴 Lou: CJ Allen sacked Lawrence for minus 10 in the second quarter! The Horseshoe bites back!

🐆 Ivy: Then Taylor put it on the ground at our 49. Jacksonville fell on it.

🐆 Ivy: Four plays later, Lawrence hit Nate Boerkircher deep down the left side for 31. 14-0 with 7:14 to go in the half.

🐴 Lou: I'm not talking about that one.

🐆 Ivy: Another Colts punt. Then we went 80 yards in seven plays. Parker Washington for 16, Jakobi Meyers for 10.

🐆 Ivy: And Tuten burst through the right side for a 39-yard touchdown run. 21-0 at the 1:51 mark.

🐴 Lou: Then Jaylahn Tuimoloau came screaming in, sacked Lawrence and stripped him! Curry scooped it up at your 38 and rumbled to the 31!

🐴 Lou: Josh Downs for 5. Keenan Allen for 9. We're at the 17. Timeout. This is the comeback, Ivy.

🐆 Ivy: Rayuan Lane III picked off Jones at our 8.

🐆 Ivy: And ran 92 yards the other way. Touchdown. Ten seconds left. 28-0.

🐴 Lou: ...

🐆 Ivy: Yards of offense at the half: Jaguars 236, Colts 98. Tuten has 94 on the ground. Write that in ink.

🐴 Lou: We've got 30 minutes and a locker room full of grown men who are about to get yelled at. Colts fans don't quit. Ask Baltimore.

🐆 Ivy: Get yelled at all you want. We're coming back out hungry.

🔥 Second half is next. The final whistle hasn't blown yet.
