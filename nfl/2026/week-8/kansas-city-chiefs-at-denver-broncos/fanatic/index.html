Kansas City Chiefs at Denver Broncos

Kansas City Chiefs (5-1) at Denver Broncos (5-2)

4:25 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · Empower Field at Mile High, Denver, CO · CBS

Sunday at Empower Field at Mile High in Denver, 4:25 p.m. Eastern on CBS, the first Sunday back on standard time. It's an AFC West game. Kansas City comes in at 5-1 on its second straight road trip. Denver is 5-2 and unbeaten at home. Chet Morrell audits every Chiefs season against the Stram teams and the Reid and Mahomes run. Ruth Calder believes every era comes down to one question about the quarterback.

Chet: What's the audit say, Ruth? Four Super Bowls. IV, LIV, LVII and LVIII. Hank Stram won three AFL titles and Super Bowl IV. Andy Reid and Patrick Mahomes added three more.

Ruth: Where's he in the arc, Chet? That's my question about every quarterback. John Elway won his two titles late in his career, Super Bowls XXXII and XXXIII. That's the arc done right. We added a third in Super Bowl 50.

Chet: The audit also says we lost Super Bowl LIX to Philadelphia, forty to twenty-two. I keep that one on my desk so nobody thinks repeats are automatic.

Ruth: That's the healthiest thing a Chiefs person has ever said to me.

Chet: Derrick Thomas. Emmitt Thomas. Lamar Hunt.

Ruth: John Elway. Terrell Davis. Champ Bailey. Floyd Little.

Chet: Fine company.

Ruth: The best company.

The season so far: Kansas City is 5-1, 3-0 at home and 2-1 on the road, with 143 points scored and only 103 allowed in six games. Denver is 5-2, 3-0 at home and 2-2 on the road, with 144 scored and 141 allowed. Patrick Mahomes started the Chiefs' last game, at Seattle. Bo Nix started the Broncos' last game, at Arizona.

Ruth: Three and oh at home, Chet. Mile High is a tough place to visit.

Chet: We're three and oh at Arrowhead, Ruth. Somebody's perfect home record ends Sunday, and it isn't ours.

The matchup: Andy Reid runs Kansas City, with Steve Spagnuolo calling a defense that sets up in a 4-3 base and Eric Bieniemy calling the offense. Sean Payton runs Denver, with Vance Joseph calling a 3-4 and Davis Webb calling the offense. Kansas City runs for 5.6 yards a carry against a Denver defense allowing 4.7. Denver runs for 4.0 against a Chiefs defense allowing just 3.6.

In the pocket, Denver's rush is the loudest group on the field. The Broncos get a sack about once every thirteen dropbacks. Kansas City's blockers allow one about once every twenty-eight. Denver's blockers allow a sack about once every twenty-five dropbacks, and the Chiefs' rush gets there exactly once every twenty-seven.

Ruth: Nik Bonitto and Zach Allen, Chet. They'll be in Patrick's face all afternoon. And Pat Surtain II will be waiting downfield.

Chet: Chris Jones and George Karlaftis will be visiting Bo. And Travis Kelce, Rashee Rice and Kenneth Walker III will be visiting your secondary.

Ruth: Bo Nix, Chet. Where's he in the arc? I'll tell you when he's done. Not before.

Chet: That's the audit I respect.

No injury talk, by order of the editor. The Week 8 report isn't out, and we took their phones at the gate.

Chet: Last thing, Ruth. Does Mahomes get late-career titles like Elway?

Ruth: Ask me when his arc is done, Chet. I don't grade the middle of the story.

Respect to both: a Kansas City defense that has allowed only 103 points in six games, a Denver team that hasn't lost at home, and an old division rivalry in Denver that should go down to the wire.

My call: Chiefs 22, Broncos 21.
