Kansas City Chiefs at Denver Broncos

Kansas City Chiefs (5-1) at Denver Broncos (5-2)

4:25 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · Empower Field at Mile High, Denver, CO · CBS

Kansas City Chiefs vs Denver Broncos is Patrick Mahomes and Andy Reid against Bo Nix and Sean Payton, an AFC West game in Week 8 at Empower Field at Mile High, 4:25 p.m. Eastern on CBS. The stadium lots got their warmup on Thursday, when the Mile High Trunk or Treat filled them with decorated cars and kids in costume, and by Sunday those same lots belong to orange jerseys again, with Ruth Calder ready to tell Chet Morrell that the Chiefs' run of Lombardis means nothing at altitude.

Ruth Calder knows those lots well, and she asks one question about every era: where is the quarterback in his arc?

Ruth: John Elway won his two titles late, Super Bowls XXXII and XXXIII. That's the arc done right. Then we added Super Bowl 50.

Chet Morrell audits every Chiefs season against the Hank Stram teams and the Reid and Mahomes run, and he left Kansas City with the Nelson-Atkins Museum's Día de los Muertos celebration on the weekend calendar.

Chet: Four Super Bowls, IV, LIV, LVII and LVIII. Stram won three AFL titles and Super Bowl IV. Reid and Mahomes added three more. And the audit also lists Super Bowl LIX, forty to twenty-two, Philadelphia. I keep it on my desk so nobody thinks repeats are automatic.

Ruth: Healthiest thing a Chiefs person has ever said to me.

They traded Hall of Fame names like neighbors trading recipes: Derrick Thomas, Emmitt Thomas and Lamar Hunt from Chet; Elway, Terrell Davis, Champ Bailey and Floyd Little from Ruth.

Kansas City is 5-1, 3-0 at home and 2-1 on the road, with 143 points scored and only 103 allowed in six games. Denver is 5-2, 3-0 at home and 2-2 away, with 144 scored and 141 allowed. Mahomes started the Chiefs' last game, at Seattle. Nix started the Broncos' last game, at Arizona. This is Kansas City's second straight road trip.

Ruth: Three and oh at Mile High.

Chet: Three and oh at Arrowhead. Somebody's perfect home record ends Sunday, and it isn't ours.

Reid has Steve Spagnuolo calling a 4-3 defense and Eric Bieniemy calling the offense. Payton has Vance Joseph calling a 3-4 and Davis Webb running the offense. Kansas City runs for 5.6 yards a carry against a Denver defense allowing 4.7, the biggest mismatch in the game. Denver runs for 4.0 against a Chiefs defense allowing just 3.6.

Ruth would rather talk about the pocket, because that's where Denver is loudest. The Broncos get a sack about once every thirteen dropbacks, while Kansas City's blockers allow one about once every twenty-eight. Denver's blockers allow a sack about once every twenty-five dropbacks, and the Chiefs' rush gets there exactly once every twenty-seven.

Ruth: Nik Bonitto and Zach Allen will be in Mahomes' face all afternoon, and Pat Surtain II will be waiting downfield.

Chet: Chris Jones and George Karlaftis will visit Nix. Travis Kelce, Rashee Rice and Kenneth Walker III will visit your secondary.

Then Wednesday's official practice report took some air out of her. Mahomes practiced fully. Allen sat out for Denver, and Ruth changed the subject. Nobody has a game status yet.

Chet: If Allen can't go, the audit says we run it at 5.6 until you stop us.

Ruth: And if he can, your audit gets a footnote.

Chet: Does Mahomes get late-career titles like Elway?

Ruth: Ask me when his arc is done. I don't grade the middle of the story. Bo, either.

Chet: That's an audit I respect.

The trunks are long gone from those lots. Ruth wants the Chiefs' visit packed up with them, and Chet came to Denver to make sure they leave in red.

My call: Chiefs 22, Broncos 21.
