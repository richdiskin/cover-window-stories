Los Angeles Chargers at Los Angeles Rams

Los Angeles Chargers (0-6) at Los Angeles Rams (3-4)

4:05 PM ET Sun Nov 1 · SoFi Stadium, Inglewood, CA · Fox

Chargers vs Rams prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: Grand Park's Día de los Muertos closes Sunday afternoon with "The Power of One" as its theme, and the winless Chargers would settle for exactly one. They've scored 90 points in six games, they visit the team they share a building with, and I'm laying the 6.5 with the Rams.

The 4:05 p.m. Eastern kickoff is on Fox from SoFi Stadium in Inglewood. The consensus has the Rams −6.5 with a total of 45.5 as of Oct 28, 6:49 p.m. ET. My card: Los Angeles Rams −6.5, Under 45.5, final Rams 26, Chargers 18.

Laying it with the Rams comes down to the pocket.

The Formula: Net ratings Los Angeles Rams 4.4, Los Angeles Chargers −7.0 (nflverse play-by-play, Oct 28, 2026).

The Chargers are 0-3 at home and 0-3 on the road, and they've allowed 151 points while scoring 90. The Rams have scored 170 in seven games and are 2-1 away from home.

The ground game is where it shows up. The Rams run for 5.0 yards a carry against a Chargers defense allowing 4.3. The Chargers run for 4.2, right at what the Rams allow, so they don't have a clear path back into the game on the ground.

The pocket is the real mismatch. The Chargers' quarterbacks have been sacked about once every ten dropbacks. The Rams protect much better, allowing a sack about once every twenty-six dropbacks, against a Chargers rush that gets home about once every seventeen. Justin Herbert started the Chargers' last game, at Kansas City in Week 6, and Matthew Stafford started the Rams' last game at Las Vegas. Neither quarterback is on Wednesday's official injury report, so those are the starters. The Rams held center Coleman Shelton out of practice and had cornerback Trent McDuffie limited, the Chargers held out safety Derwin James Jr. and guard Kayode Awosika, and nobody has a game status yet.

The risk I'm swallowing: the Chargers are coming off a bye, and the Rams are 1-3 at their own address. A rested team with two weeks to fix things is the classic back-door danger, and the Rams have allowed 159 points, so they haven't been airtight.

The Under is the Chargers' offense. The Chargers have scored 90 points in six games, and that's the best argument for any Under all week. My final is 44, the Formula's number is about 43, and the posted 45.5 asks the Chargers to show up with an offense they haven't shown yet.

The room agrees on the winner and splits on the margin. The Formula has Rams 28, Chargers 15, Letdown Watch has Rams 27, Chargers 19, and In The Trenches has Rams 24, Chargers 17, all laying the 6.5. Next Man Up is the holdout at Rams 25, Chargers 20, and it would take the Chargers with the points. Follow The Money has no side pick and likes the Over 45.5. Letdown Watch's 27-19 clears 45.5 too, so that's two voices pulling the other way on the total.

Grand Park's theme asks for one. The Chargers may get there someday, but not here: a winless team with a leaky pocket against a Rams team that runs the ball and protects its quarterback. I'll lay it.

Rams 26, Chargers 18. Rams cover the 6.5. Under 45.5.
