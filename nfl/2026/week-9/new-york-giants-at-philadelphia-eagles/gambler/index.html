New York Giants at Philadelphia Eagles

New York Giants (4-2-1) at Philadelphia Eagles (2-6)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 8 · Lincoln Financial Field, Philadelphia, PA · Fox

ShareXFacebook

Giants vs Eagles prediction for Week 9 at Lincoln Financial Field: my pick against the spread is the Philadelphia Eagles +3.5, and my over/under call is the Over 39.5. The New York Giants are 4-2-1 and giving points in Philadelphia, while the 2-6 Eagles have been miserable and still play in a building that boils over by the second quarter.

Kickoff is 1 p.m. ET Sunday on Fox. The Action Network consensus as of Tuesday morning is Giants −3.5 with a total of 39.5. The day before, the Rocky Steps Challenge runs from 9 to 11 a.m. at the Philadelphia Museum of Art: 72 steps, top in under 12 seconds. That's the mood I'm playing. Philadelphia doesn't have to be good on Sunday. It has to be stubborn for three hours.

Start with the quarterbacks, because this one's strange. In each team's last game, the man under center wasn't the guy who's started most of the season. J.J. McCarthy played for New York instead of Jameis Winston, and Tanner McKee played for Philadelphia instead of Jalen Hurts. The Week 9 injury report isn't out, so I'm not guessing about anybody's health. Next Man Up lists McCarthy and McKee as the expected starters, and that's the game I'm handicapping.

The Giants have scored 163 and allowed 161. That's a coin flip wearing a 4-2-1 jersey, and they're 1-1-1 on the road. The Eagles have scored 137 and allowed 202, which is ugly, no argument. But John Harbaugh's team comes off a bye with an 85-mile trip, and its last game was also away from home. Three and a hook asks more of New York than it has shown away from MetLife.

My card is Philadelphia Eagles 21, New York Giants 22. New York wins by one and Philadelphia gets the cover. That's 43 total points. Neither defense has given me a reason to expect a shutout: the Giants allow 5.0 yards a carry and the Eagles allow 4.4. Nick Sirianni needs a couple of stops from Vic Fangio, not a miracle.

The room is unanimous on the side. The Formula, Next Man Up, Letdown Watch and In The Trenches all take Philadelphia +3.5. The Formula has Giants 23, Eagles 22. Next Man Up has Giants 21, Eagles 20. Letdown Watch has Giants 22, Eagles 21. In The Trenches has Giants 22, Eagles 20. Those same four are on the Over.

Follow The Money has no side pick and is on the Under 39.5.

That Under is the one dissent, and it deserves respect, because 39.5 is a low total and nobody in this matchup is lighting up a scoreboard. I still see two defenses that give ground and two offenses that can find 20. I'll take the home team with the points and the Over.

Giants 22, Eagles 21. Eagles cover the 3.5. Over 39.5.
