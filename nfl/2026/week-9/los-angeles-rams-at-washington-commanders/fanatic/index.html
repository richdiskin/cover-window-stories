Los Angeles Rams at Washington Commanders

Los Angeles Rams (4-4) at Washington Commanders (2-4-1)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 8 · Northwest Stadium, Landover, MD · Fox

XFacebook

The Fanatic Podcast

Episode posts before kickoff

Claire Benton was on Constitution Avenue at 8 a.m. Sunday for the National Veterans Parade 5K, and by 9 she had her race bib pinned to the dashboard and her car pointed at Landover. She posted her finishing time to the group chat before she hit the Beltway, and nobody asked.

Diane Kerr flew in from Los Angeles on Saturday and stayed on Claire's couch, which Claire now regrets.

Diane: You ran three miles before a football game.

Claire: I ran three miles so I could scream for four hours without guilt. That's called structure.

Structure is Claire's whole religion. She was raised on Joe Gibbs, three Super Bowl titles with three different quarterbacks, and she figures that's the only proof anybody needs. Build the machine, plug in the guy, win. She talks about the Hogs up front like they're family, and she'll bring up Sammy Baugh before you finish saying hello. To Claire, the building wins first and the big names show up second.

Diane has a different religion. She follows coaching trees, and she can draw the Sean McVay branch on the back of a parking stub from memory. Then she tells you where every assistant ended up and whether he was any good. This week she gets to rub one branch right in Claire's face.

Diane: Kliff Kingsbury. Called your offense two straight years. Now he's ours.

Claire: You can have him.

Diane: We did take him. That's the point. He knows every call Daniels likes when the pocket closes.

Claire didn't answer that one. She just stared out the window at the parade crowd breaking up near the Archives.

Washington is 2-4-1, and Claire won't pretend it's a good team. But the Commanders are 2-1-1 at home and haven't won on the road all year, and she figures this is a Northwest Stadium game, not a road game, so it counts. Jayden Daniels can turn a busted play into a first down with his legs, and Washington runs for 4.8 yards a carry. Claire likes that.

Diane likes her own ground game more. The Rams run for 5.0 a carry and give up 4.0, and Matthew Stafford has been doing this so long that he's seen every blitz Dan Quinn has ever drawn up. She brings up Puka Nacua the way some people bring up their kids.

Diane: Stafford on early downs with a clean pocket is a surgeon. You know what Nacua does after the catch.

Claire: I know what our crowd does when a surgeon has to work fast.

The Rams have history too, and Diane is happy to recite it. The Fearsome Foursome, Deacon Jones and Merlin Olsen chasing quarterbacks into the bleachers. The Greatest Show on Turf, Kurt Warner and Marshall Faulk running up scores in a dome. Two Super Bowl titles, XXXIV and LVI.

Claire: Your team has lived in three cities. Ours has lived in one zip code and suffered in it honestly.

Diane: Suffered honestly. Put that on a shirt.

Claire's real fear isn't Stafford or Nacua. It's the trip. She thinks the Rams fly across the country, start slow, and Washington gets a couple of early stops with the crowd going nuts. Diane's fear is simpler. Daniels gets loose once and turns one broken play into seven points, and the whole stadium decides it's the old days again.

Diane: Antonio Williams worries me more than you'd think.

Claire: Good. He should.

They stopped for gas off Route 50 and argued about whether Kingsbury would get booed. Claire said yes, loudly and for the entire first quarter. Diane said nobody in the upper deck would know which one he was. Claire said they'd know. They always know.

Claire has her bib in the window and her voice saved for Kingsbury. Diane has a Stafford jersey and no shame about wearing it in Landover. They'll share the parking spot, share the ride home, and not say one word to each other in the fourth quarter.

My call: Rams 26, Commanders 21.
