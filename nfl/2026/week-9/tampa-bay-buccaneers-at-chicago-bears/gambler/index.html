Tampa Bay Buccaneers at Chicago Bears

Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-5) at Chicago Bears (6-2)

8:20 PM ET Sun Nov 8 · Soldier Field, Chicago, IL · NBC

ShareXFacebook

Buccaneers vs Bears prediction, pick against the spread and over/under for Sunday night: Tampa Bay +6 and Under 46.5. Ringling Bros. has "The Greatest Show On Earth" at Allstate Arena in Rosemont through Sunday, and with respect to the circus, the show at Soldier Field is going to be a lot tighter than this number.

Kickoff is 8:20 p.m. Eastern at Soldier Field. The consensus has Chicago −6 with the over/under at 46.5 as of Nov 3, 12:15 p.m. ET, and neither number has moved. My card: Bears 24, Buccaneers 20.

Tampa Bay's 3-5 record is lying to you. The Buccaneers have scored 178 points and allowed 151. That's a team that has outscored its opponents and still sits at 3-5, and it just shut out Atlanta 16-0. Teams that keep games that close don't usually get run out of buildings. They're 2-1 on the road and 1-4 at home, which is backwards, and it helps here.

Chicago is the better team, and I'm not arguing otherwise. The Bears are 6-2, 3-1 at Soldier Field, with 214 scored and 160 allowed, and they just went to Seattle and won 34-17 on Monday night. Caleb Williams started that one, and Baker Mayfield started Tampa's win over Atlanta. The Week 9 injury report isn't out, so I'm not claiming any statuses. What I will say is the Bears come back on six days after a Monday road game, and a four-point win is about where I see this landing.

Three of the four handicappers with a score agree on the side. The Formula has Bears 23, Buccaneers 21. Next Man Up has Bears 25, Buccaneers 22. In The Trenches has Bears 23, Buccaneers 19. All three keep Tampa inside 6. Letdown Watch is the holdout at Bears 26, Buccaneers 19, giving Chicago the cover after Tampa travels 1,002 miles. Follow The Money has no pick because the market hasn't moved. That's three to one for the Bucs.

The Under follows the defenses. Tampa has allowed 151 points in eight games and Chicago has allowed 160. My final adds up to 44, two and a half below 46.5. The Formula comes in at 44, Letdown Watch at 45 and In The Trenches at 42, all Under. Next Man Up is the only one on the Over, at 47. That's three to one again.

The risk is Chicago's offense catching fire the way it did in Seattle. Thirty-four points on the road is a warning, and I hear it. But Tampa just held an NFL offense without a point for a full afternoon, and a defense that does that travels. A late-night game with two defenses that make you earn it is a game that stays inside a touchdown.

Bears 24, Buccaneers 20. Buccaneers cover the 6. Under 46.5.
