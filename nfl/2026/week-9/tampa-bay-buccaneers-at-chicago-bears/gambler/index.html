Tampa Bay Buccaneers at Chicago Bears

Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-5) at Chicago Bears (6-2)

8:20 PM ET Sun Nov 8 · Soldier Field, Chicago, IL · NBC

ShareXFacebook

Ringling Bros. wraps up its weekend run at Allstate Arena in Rosemont on Sunday, and the Chicago History Museum closes its "Aquí en Chicago" exhibit the same afternoon. My Buccaneers vs Bears pick is Tampa Bay plus the 6.5, and the Under 47.5, after the circus leaves town.

The market has nudged a little toward Chicago. The Bears opened at −6 on Tuesday and sit at −6.5 in the Wednesday evening consensus. The over/under went from 46.5 to 47.5. Neither move is big enough to change my read, and the half point helps the Tampa Bay side.

The Formula: Net ratings Chicago Bears −3.7, Tampa Bay Buccaneers −4.5 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 4, 2026).

That's less than a point between them, and 6.5 is a long way from that.

Chicago is 6-2 and 3-1 at home, with 214 points scored and 160 allowed. The Bears beat Seattle 34-17 on Monday night, so they get only six days before this one. Tampa Bay is 3-5 but 2-1 on the road, with 178 scored and 151 allowed. The Bucs' defense has kept them in a lot of games.

The run game favors Tampa Bay. The Bucs run for 5.1 yards a carry, and the Chicago defense gives up 4.3. Bucky Irving can carry the offense when Baker Mayfield needs help. Chicago runs for 4.3, and Tampa Bay allows only 3.8.

Caleb Williams has been playing well, and Luther Burden III gives him another target. But a short week after a big Monday win is a classic spot for a flat first half. I see Chicago winning, but by four, not by a touchdown.

The Under fits that kind of game. Tampa Bay wants to run the ball and keep Williams on the sideline. Chicago is coming off a short week. My final is 44, three and a half under the number.

Most of the room is on Tampa Bay. The Formula has it Bears 23, Bucs 21. Next Man Up is 25-22, and In The Trenches is 23-19, all three on the Bucs plus the points. Letdown Watch is the only card laying the 6.5, with a 26-19 final.

The total is lopsided in the room. The Formula, Next Man Up, Letdown Watch and In The Trenches are all on the Under. Follow The Money is the lone Over after the one-point rise.

Rosemont gets the circus. Soldier Field gets a Bears team on short rest, and Tampa Bay gets close enough to cover.

Bears 24, Buccaneers 20. Buccaneers cover the 6.5. Under 47.5.
