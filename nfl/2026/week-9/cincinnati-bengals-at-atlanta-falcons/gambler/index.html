Cincinnati Bengals at Atlanta Falcons

Cincinnati Bengals (5-2) at Atlanta Falcons (3-5)

9:30 AM ET Sun Nov 8 · Santiago Bernabéu, Madrid, Spain · NFL Net

A Lombardi Trophy sits inside the Casa de las Alhajas this week, next to a ring from every Super Bowl ever played, and the NFL House in Madrid is where my Bengals vs Falcons prediction starts. Sunday at the Bernabéu, I'm taking Atlanta plus the 3 and the Under 52.5.

The number hasn't moved an inch. The spread and the over/under both opened Cincinnati −3 and 52.5 on Tuesday, and both are still there in the Wednesday evening consensus. Nobody has found a reason to push it, which tells me the market is comfortable calling this a field-goal game. I think it's a little less than that.

The Formula: Net ratings Atlanta Falcons −2.2, Cincinnati Bengals −2.5 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 4, 2026).

The Formula sees a rounded tie, Falcons 24, Bengals 24.

That is about as close as it gets, and they're meeting in Madrid, not Atlanta. That's the heart of the case. Cincinnati is the better team on paper at 5-2, with 206 points scored and 158 allowed. Atlanta is 3-5 and has been outscored 189 to 171. But a close game and a three-point number leave the Falcons room to lose and still get paid.

The last week explains why the Bengals are laying it. Joe Burrow threw five touchdown passes in an overtime shootout win over Tennessee at home. Atlanta got shut out in Tampa, and Michael Penix Jr. threw four interceptions. That's the freshest picture in everybody's head, and it's exactly the kind of picture that props up a favorite for one more week.

Look at how Atlanta wins instead. Bijan Robinson and the Falcons run for 4.8 yards a carry, and Cincinnati's defense allows 5.0. The Bengals run for only 3.8 against an Atlanta defense that gives up 4.6, so Burrow is carrying the offense with Tee Higgins outside. If Atlanta keeps feeding Robinson, it shortens the game and keeps Burrow on the sideline.

That's also the case for the Under. Both teams have spent a week getting across an ocean, both had to adjust their body clocks, and the first quarter in an international game often looks like it. My final adds up to 48, four and a half points under the number.

The three desks split on the side and agree on the Under. The Formula takes Atlanta with its rounded 24-24 tie. Next Man Up also takes the points, showing Bengals 26, Falcons 23. Letdown Watch lays it with Cincinnati at Bengals 26, Falcons 22. In The Trenches has no score or pick. Follow The Money has no pick.

Two of the three desks take Atlanta plus the 3, and the final has the Bengals by two. That's where I land.

Manuel Turizo plays the halftime show, the crowd will be half curious and half confused, and Cincinnati escapes Madrid with a win that doesn't quite reach the number.

Our desks see it Bengals 25, Falcons 23. The pick: Falcons +3, Under 52.5.
