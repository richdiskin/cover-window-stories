Rob Gronkowski goes into the Patriots Hall of Fame on Saturday at Patriot Place Plaza, and the day after that party, my Packers vs Patriots pick has flipped back. The Sunday desk update makes New England minus the 3.5 the call, along with Under 46.

The math is different this time. My updated final is Patriots 24, Packers 20. The spread opened Patriots −2.5 on Tuesday morning and climbed to −3.5 in the consensus as of Wednesday evening, while the over/under dipped from 47 to 46. A four-point Patriots win clears the locked 3.5 by half a point. The price stays put; the updated score changes the side.

The Formula sees it Patriots 24, Packers 19, with New England clearing the 3.5.

This is still a small Patriots number, not a runaway. New England is 5-3 and 3-0 at home, with 184 points scored and 162 allowed. Green Bay is also 5-3, with 171 scored and 164 allowed, and 2-2 on the road. These are two teams separated by four points in the updated call, and I'm asking New England to do just enough at home.

Rest favors Green Bay. The Packers played last Thursday and get ten days to get ready. New England gets a normal week.

The health list cuts against New England more than the Packers. The final injury report lists A.J. Brown and TreVeyon Henderson out, Hunter Henry questionable and Christian Barmore without a game designation. Two important offensive players are definitely missing, and Henry's availability is still unresolved.

The ground game points toward the Under. Green Bay runs for just 3.1 yards a carry against a New England defense that allows 4.0. The Patriots run for 4.4 against a Packers defense that also allows 4.0. Neither side is going to bully the other, so drives stall and kickers get work. My final adds up to 44, two points under the number.

The three scoring desks tilt New England on the side and agree on the total. The Formula has Patriots 24, Packers 19, and Letdown Watch has Patriots 24, Packers 20; both lay the 3.5. Next Man Up takes Green Bay with Patriots 23, Packers 20. All three are Under. In The Trenches has no pick. Follow The Money takes New England and the Under after the moves off 2.5 and 47, but contributes no score.

Removing In The Trenches changes the average; the other three desk scores are unchanged. That average leaves New England four points ahead. Next Man Up is the dissenting side call. Brown and Henderson remain out, but no new point deduction for either player produced this flip. The updated margin is on New England's side of the locked spread, so that is the pick.

Gronk gets his night in Foxborough. Sunday is a grind between two teams with matching records, and New England does just enough to get beyond a field goal.

Our desks see it Patriots 24, Packers 20. The pick: Patriots −3.5, Under 46.
