Arizona Cardinals at Seattle Seahawks

Arizona Cardinals (3-5) at Seattle Seahawks (4-4)

4:25 PM ET Sun Nov 8 · Lumen Field, Seattle, WA · Fox

ShareXFacebook

Cardinals vs Seahawks prediction, pick against the spread and over/under: Arizona +10.5 and Over 42.5. Lumen Field hosts the Sounders and LAFC on Saturday afternoon and the Seahawks and Cardinals on Sunday, and I like the visitors to stay a lot closer than this number says.

Kickoff is 4:25 p.m. Eastern at Lumen Field. The consensus has Seattle −10.5 with the over/under at 42.5 as of Nov 3, 12:15 p.m. ET. My card: Seahawks 27, Cardinals 19.

Start with who's laying the points. Seattle is 4-4, 3-2 at home, with 185 scored and 159 allowed, and the Seahawks just got handled 34-17 by Chicago on Monday night. They turn around on six days. That's not a team I'm comfortable laying double digits with against anybody.

Arizona is 3-5, but the Cardinals have done their best work away from home, 3-2 on the road and 0-3 at home. They won 33-30 at Dallas last Sunday. Jacoby Brissett started that game, and Arizona also used Gardner Minshew at quarterback in it. The Week 9 injury report isn't out, so I'm not assigning a status to anybody.

My final is Seattle by eight, which leaves two and a half points of room under the 10.5. The case against me is Letdown Watch. Arizona flies 1,104 miles on its second straight road trip, and Letdown Watch still has Seahawks 29, Cardinals 18. The Formula agrees at 30-18. Those are real numbers. But Next Man Up has Seattle winning by seven, and In The Trenches has 23-18, and both keep Arizona inside the number.

Follow The Money adds the last piece on the side. The spread came down from Seattle −11.5 to −10.5 Tuesday morning, and Follow The Money is with Arizona.

The Over is where Follow The Money and I part ways. It went Under after the over/under dropped from 44.5 to 42.5, a two-point slide. My final adds up to 46. Arizona has allowed 238 points in eight games, close to 30 a game, and Sam Darnold with Jaxon Smith-Njigba should find room. Arizona just put up 33 in Dallas. The Formula's 48, Next Man Up's 47 and Letdown Watch's 47 all clear 42.5.

The room splits three to two on both questions. On the side, Next Man Up, In The Trenches and Follow The Money take Arizona, while The Formula and Letdown Watch lay the 10.5. On the over/under, The Formula, Next Man Up and Letdown Watch go Over, and Follow The Money and In The Trenches go Under.

The grounds crew gets one night to turn a soccer pitch back into a football field. Seattle gets six days to turn a 34-17 loss into a double-digit win. I like the crew's odds better.

Seahawks 27, Cardinals 19. Cardinals cover the 10.5. Over 42.5.
