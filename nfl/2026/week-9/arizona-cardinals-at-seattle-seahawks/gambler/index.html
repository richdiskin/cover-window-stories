Arizona Cardinals at Seattle Seahawks

Arizona Cardinals (3-5) at Seattle Seahawks (4-4)

4:25 PM ET Sun Nov 8 · Lumen Field, Seattle, WA · Fox

ShareXFacebook

The Seattle Sauna Festival fills the National Nordic Museum in Ballard on Saturday and Sunday, and the Sounders host LAFC on Saturday. My Cardinals vs Seahawks spread pick goes against the home crowd: Arizona plus the 8.5, with the Over 39.5.

This market has moved a lot since it opened. Seattle opened at −11.5 and is down to −8.5 in the Wednesday evening consensus, a three-point drop. The over/under fell even harder, from 44.5 to 39.5. Now we know why: both teams are changing quarterbacks.

The Formula: Net ratings Seattle Seahawks 3.1, Arizona Cardinals −6.8 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 4, 2026).

That gap is real, and the Formula likes Seattle to cover. But Seattle is coming off a Monday night loss to the Bears, 34-17, and gets only six days to recover. Arizona gets a normal week.

Seattle is 4-4 and 3-2 at home, with 185 points scored and 159 allowed. Arizona is 3-5, but all three wins have come on the road, where the Cardinals are 3-2. They've scored 175 and allowed 238, so the defense has been the problem.

The matchup on the ground cuts against Arizona, though. Arizona runs for 4.1 yards a carry against a Seattle defense that allows only 3.5, so the Cardinals will have to throw. Seattle runs for 4.0, and the Arizona defense gives up 4.5.

Seattle ruled out Sam Darnold on Wednesday with a quad injury. With Darnold out, the planned starter for Seattle is veteran quarterback Drew, the former Denver Broncos starter, but he is still working back from an elbow injury, and Jalen Milroe will be ready behind him. Arizona has named Gardner Minshew its starter, and ESPN's Adam Schefter reported Wednesday that the Cardinals are trading Jacoby Brissett to Baltimore, a deal not expected to be official for a few days. A quarterback whose throws are being managed all week is not one I lay 8.5 with. Trey McBride and Jaxon Smith-Njigba are the targets that matter. I think Seattle wins, but by five or so, not by nine.

The Over at 39.5 looks like an overreaction. The number fell five points on the quarterback news, but Arizona has allowed 238 points in eight games, and Seattle can still move the ball on this defense. My final comes to 43, three and a half clear of it.

The cards were built before Wednesday's quarterback news, so read them that way. They are split down the middle on the side. The Formula has Seahawks 30, Cardinals 18, and Letdown Watch has it 28-18, both laying the points. Next Man Up goes the other way at 27-20, and In The Trenches likes Arizona at 23-18. Follow The Money likes the Cardinals too, reading the three-point drop.

The total isn't close in the room. The Formula, Next Man Up, Letdown Watch and In The Trenches are all on the Over. Follow The Money is alone on the Under, following the five-point fall.

Ballard will be warm all weekend. Lumen Field should be loud on Sunday, but Arizona hangs around long enough to cash.

Seahawks 24, Cardinals 19. Cardinals cover the 8.5. Over 39.5.
