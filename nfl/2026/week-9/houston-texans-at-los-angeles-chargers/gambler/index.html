Houston Texans at Los Angeles Chargers

Houston Texans (2-5) at Los Angeles Chargers (0-7)

4:05 PM ET Sun Nov 8 · SoFi Stadium, Inglewood, CA · CBS

ShareXFacebook

Texans vs Chargers prediction for Week 9: my pick against the spread is the Los Angeles Chargers +4.5, and my over/under call is the Over 41. The Houston Texans are 2-5 and giving 4.5 on the road. The Chargers are 0-7. I know how that sounds. I'm taking the points anyway.

Kickoff is 4:05 p.m. ET, 1:05 p.m. in Inglewood, Sunday on CBS at SoFi Stadium. The Action Network consensus as of Tuesday morning is Texans −4.5 with a total of 41. That evening, the Clippers host the Pelicans down the road at Intuit Dome, so Inglewood gets a doubleheader. Chargers fans need the first half of it to go better than the season has.

Houston's road form is the reason this spread exists. The Texans are 0-4 at home and 2-1 away, with 165 points scored and 170 allowed. They're coming off a bye with a 1375-mile trip. DeMeco Ryans had two weeks to prepare, and that's the strongest argument for Houston.

Now the Chargers. Scoring 99 points in seven games is a brutal offense, and Jim Harbaugh knows it. They've allowed 175. Their losses include a Week 8 game against the Rams at SoFi that counted as a road game in their own stadium. Justin Herbert is the expected starter, per Next Man Up, and C.J. Stroud is Houston's.

The trenches keep this close. Houston runs for 3.6 yards a carry, and the Chargers allow 4.4. Los Angeles runs for 4.0 against a Houston defense allowing 3.9. Neither ground game should take over, and when nobody can run, nobody runs away with it.

My card is Los Angeles Chargers 19, Houston Texans 23. A four-point Texans win means Los Angeles Chargers +4.5 cashes by the hook, and 42 points is one clear of 41. With Mike McDaniel running the offense, the Chargers find enough to get to 19, and Houston's defense, coming in fresh, doesn't let them get any further.

Every card with a side is on the Chargers. The Formula has Texans 22, Chargers 21. Next Man Up has Texans 23, Chargers 19. Letdown Watch has Texans 24, Chargers 20. In The Trenches has Texans 20, Chargers 16. All four take Los Angeles +4.5. On the total, The Formula, Next Man Up and Letdown Watch are on the Over 41, and In The Trenches is on the Under. Follow The Money has no pick on the side or the total.

Somebody has to stop losing in this stadium eventually. Houston gets the win, and the Chargers keep it close enough to make the last drive matter.

Texans 23, Chargers 19. Chargers cover the 4.5. Over 41.
