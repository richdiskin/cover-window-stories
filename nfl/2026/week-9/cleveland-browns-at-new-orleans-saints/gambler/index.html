Cleveland Browns at New Orleans Saints

Cleveland Browns (5-3) at New Orleans Saints (2-5)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 8 · Caesars Superdome, New Orleans, LA · CBS

ShareXFacebook

Browns vs Saints prediction for Week 9: my pick against the spread is the Cleveland Browns +2.5, and on the over/under I'm taking the Over 43.5. The New Orleans Saints are 0-3 at the Caesars Superdome this season, and the number still asks Cleveland to walk into that building and lose by three or more.

Kickoff is 1 p.m. ET Sunday on CBS. The Action Network consensus as of Tuesday morning, Nov. 3, is Saints −2.5 with a total of 43.5. Uptown, the Oak Street Po-Boy Festival opens at 10 a.m. the same day, so a good chunk of the crowd will roll into the Dome stuffed and in no mood to watch a fourth loss in the Dome.

Cleveland is 5-3, but nobody should mistake this team for a bully. The Browns have scored 139 points and allowed 173. They win tight, ugly games, they're 3-0 at home and 2-3 away from it. That's exactly the kind of team I want getting points in a game that projects close. New Orleans is 2-5, has scored 162 and given up 194, and both of its wins came on the road.

The honest knock on Cleveland is the schedule. Letdown Watch flags it: the Browns are on 7 days of rest, flying 925 miles, and their last game was also on the road, at Pittsburgh. The Saints are coming off a bye and playing at home. Kellen Moore and Brandon Staley had extra time to get Tyler Shough and that defense ready for Deshaun Watson. That's why I have New Orleans winning. It's also why I don't have them winning by more than a point.

My card says New Orleans Saints 23, Cleveland Browns 22. Both defenses have leaked points all year, so I'm not expecting a rock fight. That's 45 points, clear of 43.5, and a one-point Saints win keeps the Browns safely inside the number.

The room agrees on the side. All four cards with a side are on Cleveland +2.5. The Formula has Browns 24, Saints 21. Next Man Up has Saints 22, Browns 21. Letdown Watch has Saints 23, Browns 21. In The Trenches has Saints 22, Browns 21. The total splits down the middle: The Formula and Letdown Watch are on the Over, while Next Man Up and In The Trenches are on the Under. Follow The Money sits this one out with no pick.

I don't need Cleveland to win. I need the Browns to keep doing what they've done all year, which is hang around and make the fourth quarter miserable for everybody. New Orleans finally gets a home win, and it doesn't get it by a field goal.

Saints 23, Browns 22. Browns cover the 2.5. Over 43.5.
