Cleveland Browns at New Orleans Saints

Cleveland Browns (5-3) at New Orleans Saints (2-5)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 8 · Caesars Superdome, New Orleans, LA · CBS

ShareXFacebook

New Orleans eats its way through the weekend, with Beignet Fest in City Park on Saturday and the Oak Street Po-Boy Festival running all day Sunday, and the Browns vs Saints over/under is the first thing on my plate. I'm on the Over 42.5, and I'm taking Cleveland plus the 3.

The total has dropped a full point since Tuesday, from 43.5 to 42.5 in the Wednesday evening consensus, and New Orleans went from −2.5 to −3. I think the market got the total wrong. Two bad defenses in a dome don't usually produce a rock fight.

The Formula: Net ratings New Orleans Saints −12.1, Cleveland Browns −6.9 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 4, 2026).

That's a five-point gap in Cleveland's favor, and the Saints are giving three at home. The home field in the Superdome hasn't been worth much this year. New Orleans is 2-5, 0-4 at home and 2-1 on the road, with 194 points allowed in seven games.

Cleveland is a strange 5-3. The Browns are 3-0 at home and 2-3 on the road, and they've scored only 139 points while allowing 173. That's a team winning close games with defense and luck, and it's why I can't call them a clear favorite on the road. But I don't need them to win. I need them to stay within a field goal.

Now the concern. Deshaun Watson is in the concussion protocol, and he did not practice Wednesday, along with defensive tackle Maliek Collins and linebacker Quincy Williams. For the Saints, Alvin Kamara sat out too. If Watson can't clear the protocol, Shedeur Sanders is in line to start. The card still lists Watson, so his status is the one to track before Sunday.

The Over case is the running game. Cleveland runs for 4.1 yards a carry, and the Saints allow 5.1. New Orleans runs for just 3.5, but the Browns allow 5.0. Defenses that leak on the ground give up long, slow drives that end in points, and Tyler Shough has Chris Olave to finish them. Harold Fannin Jr. gives whoever starts for Cleveland an easy target over the middle. My final adds up to 45, two and a half points over the number.

The room is in step on both questions. All four cards with a score are on Cleveland plus the 3 and the Over. The Formula has the Browns winning outright, Browns 24, Saints 21. Next Man Up and In The Trenches both land at Saints 22, Browns 21, and Letdown Watch has it Saints 23, Browns 21. Follow The Money is the only Under, following the drop from 43.5.

When four cards agree on a one or two-point game, or a Browns win, three points is plenty of cushion.

The po-boy lines on Oak Street will be long Sunday, the dome will be half full of nervous Saints fans, and New Orleans scrapes out a win that doesn't reach the number.

Saints 23, Browns 22. Browns cover the 3. Over 42.5.
