Buffalo Bills at Minnesota Vikings

Buffalo Bills (6-1) at Minnesota Vikings (6-1)

8:15 PM ET Mon Nov 9 · U.S. Bank Stadium, Minneapolis, MN · ESPN, ABC

XFacebook

The Fanatic Podcast

Episode posts before kickoff

Erik Dahl painted his face purple for the first time since college on Saturday. It wasn't even for the Vikings. He was at Huntington Bank Stadium watching the Gophers play UCLA on Salute to Service day, and he got carried away. By the second quarter he was leading his whole row in the ROW chant. By halftime his face paint was running down his neck.

Ellen Marsh, his sister-in-law from Buffalo, was sitting next to him. She had flown in early for the Monday game, and she spent the whole afternoon trying to pretend she didn't know him.

Ellen: You're a grown man.

Erik: I'm a grown man with a team that's 6-1.

Ellen: So am I. And mine actually scores.

That's been the fight all week. Erik's Vikings are 6-1 and unbeaten at home. Their only loss was a close one in Detroit, and Erik still hasn't forgiven the refs. He loves this defense. He says it's the best Vikings defense he's seen since the Purple People Eaters, and he says it like he was there for the Purple People Eaters, which he wasn't.

Ellen's Bills are 6-1 too, and they just crushed Baltimore. Ellen thinks Josh Allen is the best quarterback in football, and she won't hear any argument. She's been a Bills fan her whole life, through four straight Super Bowl losses in the early nineties, the long playoff drought after that, and all the years of coming close with Allen.

Erik: Four Super Bowls. Four losses.

Ellen: Four Super Bowls. How many trips has your team made since the seventies?

Erik had to think about it. Then he stopped thinking about it and changed the subject.

The truth is, both fan bases know heartbreak. The Vikings have been to four Super Bowls and lost them all too. Erik knows it. He just doesn't like hearing it from Ellen.

Erik's big hope is Carson Wentz, with Kyler Murray ruled out because of a hamstring strain. Murray came to Minnesota and has been everything Erik wanted. Now Erik needs Wentz to keep the offense moving. Erik loves Aaron Jones Sr. almost as much. He says Jones runs like a guy who's angry at the ground.

Ellen counters with James Cook III. Cook can turn any carry into a touchdown, and Ellen says he's the best running back the Bills have had since Thurman Thomas. Erik says Ellen says that about every Bills running back.

On Monday afternoon, before the game, they're going to the Dakota to catch the Blind Boys of Alabama. Ellen's husband bought the tickets months ago. Erik thinks it's a strange way to warm up for a football game. Ellen thinks it's the only way to keep Erik from painting his face again.

Then they'll walk to U.S. Bank Stadium. Erik has his Randy Moss jersey, the one he's had since high school. Ellen has her Allen jersey and a Bills knit hat with the pom-pom on top. She's planning to wear the hat indoors, just to make a point.

Erik: It's a dome. You don't need a hat.

Ellen: I'm from Buffalo. I always need a hat.

Erik wants the dome so loud that Allen can't hear himself think. Ellen says Allen doesn't need to think, he just needs the ball. Erik says that's the dumbest thing he's heard all week, then admits it might be true.

Thanksgiving is three weeks out, and they'll both be at the same table. One of them is going to spend the whole meal hearing about Monday night.

My call: Bills 24, Vikings 21.
