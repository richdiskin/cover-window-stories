Jacksonville Jaguars at Baltimore Ravens

Jacksonville Jaguars (5-2) at Baltimore Ravens (4-4)

8:15 PM ET Thu Nov 5 · M&T Bank Stadium, Baltimore, MD · Prime Video

ShareXFacebook

Jacksonville Jaguars vs Baltimore Ravens in Week 9 lands on a Thursday night when Baltimore is literally hanging DayGlo on the walls: MICA's Pinkard Gallery on Mount Royal Avenue opens Globe Posters for the People at 5 p.m., a show built around the old Baltimore print shop whose screaming fluorescent show posters sold concerts and fight nights to this whole town. Owen Pratt will be standing in that gallery about three hours before the 8:15 kickoff, looking for a poster loud enough to announce whoever plays quarterback, while Ivy Corcoran, up from Duval with her standings sheet folded in her jacket, asks him why he's shopping for art on the worst week of his football life.

Here's the worst week. Lamar Jackson hurt his right thumb on Baltimore's very first offensive snap in Orchard Park on Sunday, a 36-10 loss, and is headed for injured reserve. Tyler Huntley came in, hurt his toe, has already been ruled out for Thursday and is a candidate to go to IR. That leaves Joe Fagnano, the undrafted kid out of Connecticut, who finished the loss in Buffalo, is nursing an ankle injury and was limited in Monday's walk-through. On Tuesday Jesse Minter made it official: Fagnano starts Thursday. Baltimore had three different quarterbacks throw before halftime, something no NFL team had done since the Cowboys nine seasons ago.

Owen: Globe knew how to sell a night. Big block letters, fluorescent ink, one night only. I want one that says BALTIMORE in letters you can read from the upper deck.

Ivy: Put the record on it too. Baltimore 4-4, Jacksonville 5-2. That's the only print I need.

That's Ivy in one sentence. She doesn't argue with feelings, she argues with the standings, and right now the standings say Jacksonville sits on top of the AFC South. The Jaguars have scored 196 points and allowed 111 in seven games. Baltimore has scored 179 and allowed 203 in eight, and it is 1-2 at home.

Owen grew up reading box scores from the bottom up. Sacks, takeaways, then whoever was throwing. In his house, defense signs the checks, and he has two Lombardi Trophies to wave around when anybody doubts it. Super Bowl XXXV was a Ray Lewis production with Trent Dilfer handing off. A rookie quarterback in a jam is not the end of the world to Owen. It's the plan.

Owen: Minter isn't going to ask a backup to win it. He's going to keep handing it off until the clock starts begging, and he's going to let Kyle Hamilton and Roquan Smith turn Thursday night into a fistfight.

Ivy: Your front has been run through all fall. Two hundred and three, Owen. I didn't write that number on the poster. Your defense did.

He has no comeback for that, so he goes after the ground game, where Baltimore actually has a case. The Ravens run for 4.8 yards a carry. Jacksonville's defense, though, gives up only 3.4, one of the meanest run fronts in the league, and the Jaguars run for 4.6 against a Baltimore defense that allows 3.8. Thursday becomes a shoving match at the point of attack, and Ivy likes her side of that shove.

Ivy also gave something up for this trip. Back home, JJ Grey & Mofro are playing the Jax Amphitheater at the Greater Jacksonville Agricultural Fair on Thursday night, out at the fair's new grounds on Normandy Boulevard. She traded swamp funk and a fried-everything midway for a short week, a cold parking lot on Russell Street and a quarterback room held together with tape.

Owen: You'll be back for the fair. It runs eleven days. My season might not.

Ivy: That's the first true thing you've said all night.

Here's how it goes. Baltimore gets its carries, whoever plays quarterback makes a couple of throws that make the stadium stand up, and Hamilton and Smith keep it ugly enough that Owen still likes his chances in the fourth quarter. Then Trevor Lawrence finds one more drive than Baltimore can answer, and the Jaguars leave town with a road win and Ivy's standings sheet still folded neatly in her jacket.

Owen buys the poster anyway. He says it'll look better next January.

My call: Jaguars 25, Ravens 21.
