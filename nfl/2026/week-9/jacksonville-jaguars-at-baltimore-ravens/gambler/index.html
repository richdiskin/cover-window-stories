Jacksonville Jaguars at Baltimore Ravens

Jacksonville Jaguars (5-2) at Baltimore Ravens (4-4)

8:15 PM ET Thu Nov 5 · M&T Bank Stadium, Baltimore, MD · Prime Video

ShareXFacebook

Baltimore will spend Thursday evening staring at fluorescent posters, and my Jaguars vs Ravens prediction starts in that gallery. MICA's Pinkard Gallery on Mount Royal Avenue opens "Globe Posters for the People" from 5 to 7 p.m., a show about the old print shop whose DayGlo cards sold this town its concerts and fight nights, and my call against the 9.5 is just as loud: Ravens with the points, and the Over 39.5.

The news first, because it's official now. The NFL.com injury report released Wednesday lists Lamar Jackson (thumb) and Tyler Huntley (toe) as Out. Joe Fagnano is the expected starter, per coach Jesse Minter through ESPN, and Fagnano went 10-of-21 with an interception in relief during the loss in Buffalo. Nobody in Baltimore is pretending that's the same offense.

The number agrees with them. DraftKings via ESPN as of Thursday, 7:00 p.m. ET, has Jacksonville −9.5 with an over/under of 39.5. Wednesday evening's consensus was Jacksonville −10. The side came back half a point toward Baltimore, and the total held at 39.5. I still think the number priced Fagnano too hard.

The Formula: Net ratings Baltimore Ravens −5.5, Jacksonville Jaguars 0.7 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 4, 2026).

That's a Jaguars side, but it isn't a 10-point side. Jacksonville is 5-2 and has allowed just 111 points in seven games, and it's 4-1 at home. On the road it's 1-1. Both teams are on 4 days of rest, so nobody gets fresher legs on a short week.

What keeps Baltimore inside the number is Derrick Henry and the ground game. The Ravens run for 4.8 yards a carry. Jacksonville allows 3.4, so it won't be pretty, but a backup quarterback's best friend is a team that keeps handing off and keeps the clock moving. Jacksonville has health questions of its own: Bhayshul Tuten is questionable with an ankle. Baltimore's game-day inactives include corner Nate Wiggins, tackle Emery Jones Jr., defensive tackle Aeneas Peebles, receiver Elijah Sarratt and quarterback Kedon Slovis.

The total is the bigger call. Jacksonville has scored 196 points in seven games, and Baltimore's defense has given up 203 in eight. Short weeks get sloppy, sloppy games hand out short fields, and short fields turn into points. My final is 45, well clear of 39.5.

The room is with me on the side and the Over. The Formula, Next Man Up and Letdown Watch all have Baltimore plus the 9.5 and the Over. Next Man Up is the toughest on the Ravens at Jaguars 26, Ravens 19, and even that lands inside the number. In The Trenches has no pick: Baltimore tackle Emery Jones Jr. is out, and its season numbers were built with him on the field. Follow The Money has no call: the line moved half a point, short of a full point.

Jacksonville wins the game. Baltimore, with a backup quarterback and a building that still knows how to get loud, loses it by five.

Jaguars 25, Ravens 20. Ravens cover the 9.5. Over 39.5.
