Jacksonville Jaguars at Baltimore Ravens

Jacksonville Jaguars (5-2) at Baltimore Ravens (4-4)

8:15 PM ET Thu Nov 5 · M&T Bank Stadium, Baltimore, MD · Prime Video

ShareXFacebook

Jaguars vs Ravens prediction, pick, spread and over/under for Thursday Night Football in Week 9: Baltimore spends Thursday evening at MICA's Pinkard Gallery, where Globe Posters for the People opens at 5 p.m. to honor the city's loudest print shop, and the Ravens need that kind of volume for an undrafted rookie quarterback. Lamar Jackson's thumb is headed for injured reserve, Tyler Huntley is out, and I'm still taking the points.

Kickoff is 8:15 p.m. Eastern on Prime Video from M&T Bank Stadium. The consensus sits at Jacksonville Jaguars −9.5 with a total of 42.5 as of Nov 3, 9:47 a.m. ET. My card reads Baltimore Ravens +9.5 and Over 42.5, with a final of Jaguars 25, Ravens 21.

Start with the quarterback, because everything else follows. Jackson hurt his right thumb on Baltimore's first offensive snap in Buffalo. Huntley came in and hurt his toe, and he has been ruled out for Thursday. That puts Joe Fagnano, an undrafted rookie from UConn, in line to start on a sore ankle after a debut that included an interception and a lost fumble. Baltimore also signed Kedon Slovis off Green Bay's practice squad as insurance. That's a real drop, and I'm pricing it as one.

The number already did most of that work, though. Nine and a half is a big ask for a road team on four days' rest after a trip of nearly seven hundred miles, and Jacksonville has played only two road games. The Formula, built on eight weeks of play, projects Jacksonville by 3. Knock Baltimore down hard for the rookie and you still land short of 9.5.

The ground game is where Baltimore hangs around. The Ravens run for 4.8 yards a carry, and expect Jesse Minter to ride Derrick Henry and keep the rookie out of long downs. Jacksonville's run defense allows just 3.4, so this won't be easy, but Henry turns four-yard gains into a short game and a shorter clock. Kyle Hamilton and Roquan Smith give Baltimore enough on defense to keep Trevor Lawrence honest in the fourth quarter.

The Over looks wrong at first glance with a rookie under center, which is exactly why the total sits as low as 42.5. Baltimore has allowed 203 points in eight games. Jacksonville has scored 196 in seven and put up 42 on Indianapolis last week. Every short field off a rookie mistake becomes Jacksonville points, and Baltimore's offense has 179 points this season to prove it can answer. Jaguars 25, Ravens 21 is 46, comfortably past the number.

Four of the five handicappers back Baltimore and the Over. Follow The Money has no pick on either one because the consensus has barely budged since it opened early Tuesday.

Ivy Corcoran will tell you Jacksonville is 5-2 and Baltimore is 4-4, and she's right. Owen Pratt will tell you Baltimore wins ugly or not at all. I'll split the difference: the Jaguars win, and the poster doesn't get printed for a blowout.

Jaguars 25, Ravens 21. Ravens cover the 9.5. Over 42.5.
