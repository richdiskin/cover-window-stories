Denver Broncos at Carolina Panthers

Denver Broncos (6-2) at Carolina Panthers (3-4)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 8 · Bank of America Stadium, Charlotte, NC · CBS

ShareXFacebook

Broncos vs Panthers prediction for Week 9: my pick against the spread is the Carolina Panthers +3, and my over/under call is the Over 44. The Denver Broncos are 6-2, but they're 2-2 on the road, and Bank of America Stadium is where I want the points.

Kickoff is 1 p.m. ET Sunday in Charlotte. The consensus as of Tuesday morning is Broncos −3 with a total of 44. Two hours after kickoff, the Ally Tipoff fills the Spectrum Center uptown with South Carolina vs North Carolina and NC State vs Kentucky in women's college basketball. Charlotte gets a full day of sports, and the Panthers are the opening act.

Denver earned its respect. Sean Payton's team is 4-0 at home, and it has scored 172 points and allowed 165. That pair tells me something: the Broncos are winning games, not running people over. On the road they're 2-2. Bo Nix makes a 1357-mile trip on 7 days of rest. Carolina has had 10 days at home since its last game at Green Bay.

Carolina is 3-4 with 178 points scored and 193 allowed. That's a defense with problems and an offense that can keep up. Bryce Young and Dave Canales have this team scoring more than Denver this season in one fewer game. Denver's defense allows 4.6 yards a carry, and Carolina runs for 4.3. I expect the Panthers to stay on schedule and keep Nix on the sideline longer than he'd like.

My card is Carolina Panthers 22, Denver Broncos 24. Denver wins by two, and Carolina Panthers +3 gets home by a point. That's 46 total points, two clear of 44. Both teams have given up more than 20 a game on the season, and I don't see either defense suddenly turning into a wall.

Three of the four side cards are with me on Carolina +3. The Formula actually has the Panthers winning, 24-23. Next Man Up and In The Trenches both have Broncos 24, Panthers 22. Letdown Watch is the holdout, taking Denver −3 at Broncos 25, Panthers 20. All four are on the Over 44. Follow The Money has nothing to say here: no pick.

One history note, since nobody in Charlotte has forgotten it: Denver beat Carolina in Super Bowl 50, 24-10. Different rosters, same colors, same sore spot. Ejiro Evero runs Carolina's defense now, and Payton will test it early. I'll take the home team with the points and expect a game still in doubt in the fourth quarter.

Broncos 24, Panthers 22. Panthers cover the 3. Over 44.
