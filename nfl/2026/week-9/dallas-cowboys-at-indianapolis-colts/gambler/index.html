Dallas Cowboys at Indianapolis Colts

Dallas Cowboys (3-5) at Indianapolis Colts (2-6)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 8 · Lucas Oil Stadium, Indianapolis, IN · Fox

ShareXFacebook

Cinderella gets her company premiere at Clowes Hall this weekend, with the Indianapolis Ballet and the symphony in the pit, and the Cowboys vs Colts spread has its own glass-slipper feel. Indianapolis is 2-6, Dallas is laying 5.5 on the road, and I'm taking the Colts with the points plus the Under 51.5.

The line opened Dallas −6 on Tuesday and slipped to −5.5 in the Wednesday evening consensus, with the over/under holding at 51.5. That half point went toward the home team. I'd have taken the Colts at six, too.

The Formula: Net ratings Indianapolis Colts −8.2, Dallas Cowboys −7.1 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 4, 2026).

Both of these teams grade out poorly, and they grade out close together. That's a coin flip, not a game where Dallas should be laying more than five.

The records cover up how ugly the Cowboys have been. Dallas is 3-5 and has scored 219 points, but it has also allowed 213, and last Sunday it blew a two-touchdown fourth-quarter lead to Arizona. A team that can't close games has no business laying points on the road. Dallas is 2-2 away from home.

Indianapolis is coming off a mess of its own, a five-touchdown loss in Jacksonville. Daniel Jones starts again, and Jonathan Taylor is the reason the Colts can stay in this. Indianapolis runs for 4.2 yards a carry. The Dallas defense gives up 5.2, the kind of soft middle that lets a home team stay on schedule and keep Dak Prescott and CeeDee Lamb waiting.

Dallas runs for just 3.8 a carry, and the Colts defense also gives up 5.2, so both teams should be able to grind. That's what pushes me toward the Under. Two offenses that can hand it to their backs eat clock, and my final comes to 51, half a point below the number. It's a thin call on the total and I'll say so plainly.

The room is unanimous on the side. The Formula, Next Man Up, Letdown Watch and In The Trenches are all on Indianapolis plus the 5.5. Each of the four calls this a one-point game. The Formula has it Colts 26, Cowboys 25, and Letdown Watch has it Colts 25, Cowboys 24. In The Trenches is the only one with Dallas winning, by a single point. On the total, The Formula and Letdown Watch are Under, while Next Man Up and In The Trenches are Over. Follow The Money stays out after only a half-point drift.

When four cards agree a game is that close, laying more than five with a team that just gave away a fourth-quarter lead is a hard sell.

The Christmas Nights of Lights opens at the State Fairgrounds on Friday, the Colts give their crowd a reason to stay to the end, and Indianapolis wins a close one on its own field.

Colts 27, Cowboys 24. Colts cover the 5.5. Under 51.5.
