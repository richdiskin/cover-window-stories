Las Vegas Raiders at San Francisco 49ers

Las Vegas Raiders (4-4) at San Francisco 49ers (6-1)

4:25 PM ET Sun Nov 8 · Levi's Stadium, Santa Clara, CA · Fox

ShareXFacebook

San Jose State hosts Northern Illinois at CEFCU Stadium on Saturday, a short drive from Levi's Stadium, and the South Bay gets a full football weekend. My Raiders vs 49ers prediction lays the big number: San Francisco −10 and the Under 52.5.

The spread hasn't budged. The 49ers opened at −10 on Tuesday and are still −10 in the Wednesday evening consensus. The over/under ticked up a point, from 51.5 to 52.5, and I'm happy to take the higher number on the Under.

The Formula: Net ratings San Francisco 49ers 8.6, Las Vegas Raiders −8.0 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 4, 2026).

That's a big gap between these two teams.

There is a catch, and it's a big one. Christian McCaffrey, Trent Williams, Mike Evans and Deebo Samuel Sr. all missed Wednesday's practice, according to the NFL.com report. That's the lead back, the left tackle and two receivers. It's only Wednesday, but if even two of them sit, the 49ers won't look the same.

That's why I'm not going all the way to the Formula's 35-17. My final is 49ers by 11, enough to clear the 10 but not by much.

San Francisco is 6-1 and 4-0 at home, with 197 points scored and only 139 allowed. The 49ers are coming off a bye, so Brock Purdy and George Kittle have had extra time to get healthy and ready. Las Vegas is 4-4, but 3-1 on the road, with 221 scored and 230 allowed.

The run game tells the story. San Francisco runs for 4.9 yards a carry, and the Raiders give up 5.4. Las Vegas runs for only 3.2, and the 49ers allow 3.6. Kirk Cousins will have to throw, and Brock Bowers will be his main target.

On the total, I see the 49ers controlling the ball on the ground and keeping Cousins on the sideline. That shortens the game. The over/under went up a point, and I think it went the wrong way.

The cards split on the side. The Formula is the most aggressive, 49ers 35, Raiders 17. Letdown Watch is next at 30-18. Next Man Up likes the Raiders plus the points with a 28-21 final, and In The Trenches agrees on Las Vegas at 30-22.

The room is clearer on the total. The Formula, Next Man Up, Letdown Watch and In The Trenches all go Under. Follow The Money is the only voice on the Over after the one-point rise.

The Spartans and Huskies get the weekend started in San Jose. Sunday, the 49ers finish it.

49ers 31, Raiders 20. 49ers cover the 10. Under 52.5.
