Speedway Christmas opens Friday night at Charlotte Motor Speedway, and my Ravens vs Panthers prediction sees Baltimore staying within a field goal. I'm on the Ravens +2.5 for my spread pick and Over 43.5 for the over/under, with a Panthers 24, Ravens 22 final.

Mitch Kovac bought one vehicle ticket for the drive-through lights in Concord and plans to take Owen Pratt around the track twice, mostly so Owen can stop thinking about Monday night.

The Formula: Net ratings Carolina Panthers −3.6, Baltimore Ravens −4.5 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 17, 2026).

Baltimore has lost five straight, the last one 23-20 at home to a Chargers team that hadn't won all season, and Kyle Hamilton said afterward that the Ravens are disgracing the team's standards. That's the kind of locker room that either cracks or gets angry. The Ravens are 1-4 at home but 3-2 on the road, and Derrick Henry still has ten rushing touchdowns. Carolina is 1-3 at home and has allowed 236 points in nine games, so this isn't a spot where the Panthers have earned much benefit of the doubt.

Carolina's case is real. The Panthers beat Detroit 32-26 at home, won 25-14 in Philadelphia and just took a 20-17 game in New Orleans, and Tetairoa McMillan has 660 receiving yards. It's just not a team that has done enough at home to lay points against anybody.

The room splits on the side. The Formula has Panthers 26, Ravens 23 and Next Man Up has Panthers 24, Ravens 21, both laying the 2.5. Letdown Watch has Panthers 24, Ravens 22, and In The Trenches has Panthers 24, Ravens 23, and those two take Baltimore. Follow The Money has no side but takes the Under 43.5, since the total has dropped a point from 44.5 overnight. The four scoring cards are all on the Over.

I'm with the scoring cards on the total. Carolina has put up 219 points, Bryce Young has 16 touchdown throws, and Chuba Hubbard has 711 rushing yards. Baltimore has allowed 259 points, and the Ravens have scored 30 or more three times this season. Jacoby Brissett started Monday, and Zay Flowers, with 688 receiving yards, gives him a target he can rely on.

Panthers 24, Ravens 22 lands on 46, clear of 43.5, and a two-point game keeps Baltimore inside the 2.5. Hamilton's words should get some kind of response from Baltimore, even if it comes up a little short.

Panthers 24, Ravens 22. Ravens cover the 2.5. Over 43.5.
