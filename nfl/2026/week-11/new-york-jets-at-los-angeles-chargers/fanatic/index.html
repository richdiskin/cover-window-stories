New York Jets at Los Angeles Chargers

New York Jets (2-8) at Los Angeles Chargers (1-8)

4:05 PM ET Sun Nov 22 · SoFi Stadium, Inglewood, CA · Fox

XFacebook

Marty Szabo has been sitting in the driver's seat of a car he can't afford for eleven minutes, and the salesman has stopped pretending he's going to buy it.

The LA Auto Show opens Friday at the Los Angeles Convention Center downtown and runs through the 29th, and Marty goes every year. It's where he goes to feel hopeful. This year, for the first time since September, he doesn't need it.

The Chargers won on Monday night.

Marty: Say it again.

Mike Lasker: The Chargers won.

Marty: One more time.

Mike flew in from New Jersey for New York Jets vs Los Angeles Chargers in Week 11, and he is already tired of this game. The Chargers started 0-8. Then they went to Baltimore and won 23-20, and Marty has been floating around Los Angeles ever since like a man who found his lost wallet with everything still in it.

Mike would like to point out that the Jets have two wins, which is twice as many.

Marty: You've lost five in a row.

Mike: And you've won one in a row. I'm aware.

The Jets did win in Tennessee to open the season and beat Cleveland at home in October, and those are the two games Mike talks about when he has to talk about anything. Since then it's been New England, Miami, Las Vegas, Kansas City and Buffalo, and the 38-33 loss to the Raiders is the one that still keeps him awake. Geno Smith has 2,226 yards and 12 touchdown throws against only three interceptions. Garrett Wilson has 633 receiving yards. Braelon Allen has 638 on the ground. David Bailey has 7.5 sacks, and Jamien Sherwood has 86 tackles.

Mike: We're not as bad as the record.

Marty: Nobody is as bad as their record. That's what records are for.

The Chargers have been worse than their record, honestly, and Marty knows it. They're 0-5 at SoFi. They've scored 144 points in nine games. Justin Herbert has 1,720 yards and six touchdown throws, and the interceptions are a subject Marty will discuss only after a second coffee. Omarion Hampton has 474 yards and four touchdowns. Khalil Mack has three sacks. Daiyan Henley has 62 tackles, but a knee injury has him out Sunday, and Marty took that news personally.

But they won, and Marty has decided that changes everything.

They walk the floor at the Convention Center together on Friday. Mike stops at every truck. Marty stops at every convertible. That's the difference between New Jersey and Southern California in one sentence, and Mike says so.

Marty: You came 2,449 miles to make fun of my car taste?

Mike: I came 2,449 miles because I had a free weekend and nothing better to do.

Marty: Your team has nothing better to do either.

That one hurt, and Marty enjoyed it.

Sunday is a 1:05 kickoff out at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, and Marty has a plan. He'll get there early, sit in the same seat he sat in for every loss this season, and see if Monday was a turning point or a fluke. Mike will sit next to him and say nothing for most of the first half, which is how Jets fans watch road games.

Neither of them expects a blowout. Two teams with three wins between them don't usually produce one. What they expect is a long afternoon, a lot of field goals, and one of them driving home in a better mood than the other.

My call: Chargers 22, Jets 21.
