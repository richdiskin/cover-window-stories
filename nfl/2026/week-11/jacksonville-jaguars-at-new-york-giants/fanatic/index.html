Jacksonville Jaguars at New York Giants

Jacksonville Jaguars (7-2) at New York Giants (5-3-1)

4:25 PM ET Sun Nov 22 · MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, NJ · CBS

XFacebook

The first 25,000 people through the gates at MetLife Stadium on Sunday get a military knit beanie, and Leon Drobny intends to be one of the first through the gate. He has done the math on the parking lot. He has done the math on the tunnel. Leon has been a Giants fan long enough to know that free things go fast in New Jersey.

It's Salute to Service day, and Leon's father served, so this one matters to him more than most. He'll wear the beanie the whole game even if it's warm.

Ivy Corcoran thinks it might be. She flew up from Jacksonville for Jacksonville Jaguars vs New York Giants in Week 11 and checked the forecast on the plane: 60 degrees, light wind.

Ivy: That's not beanie weather.

Leon: It's beanie weather if they give you a beanie.

The Giants have had ten days to stew. They lost 24-21 to Washington on Thursday night, and Leon spent the long weekend replaying the fourth quarter in his head like a song he hates. Before that they won in Houston and in Philadelphia, and he was starting to say things out loud he usually keeps to himself. They're 5-3-1, with a tie in Washington back in October that Leon still describes as a loss with a better attitude.

J.J. McCarthy started the Thursday game. Cam Skattebo has 496 yards and four touchdowns and runs like every carry is personal. Malik Nabers has 58 catches for 692 yards, and Leon says he's the most fun Giants receiver since he was a kid. Arvell Reese has 81 tackles and 4.5 sacks, and Leon is already shopping for the jersey.

Ivy listens to all of it the way you listen to someone describe a vacation you've already taken.

Ivy: We've outscored people by 125.

Leon: I read it.

Ivy: Two hundred sixty-nine to 144.

Leon: I said I read it.

Jacksonville is 7-2. The Jaguars lost in Denver in September and to Houston at Wembley in October, and they've won every other game, most of them comfortably. They beat Indianapolis 42-7, won 33-10 in Baltimore and won 40-23 in Tennessee last Sunday. Trevor Lawrence has 2,284 yards and 21 touchdown throws against five interceptions, and Ivy says he finally looks like the guy the city was promised. Parker Washington has 645 receiving yards and six touchdowns. Bhayshul Tuten has 609 yards on the ground. Foyesade Oluokun has 68 tackles.

Leon: You've been on the road two weeks in a row.

Ivy: We like the road.

Leon: Nobody likes New Jersey.

That one got a laugh out of both of them, which is the way it usually goes with Leon and Ivy. They met years ago at a game in this same stadium and have been arguing about football since.

Sunday afternoon, they'll sit together in the upper level. Leon will wear his beanie and his Lawrence Taylor jersey. Ivy will keep her opinions to a respectful volume, at least during the Salute to Service moments. She has family who served too, and she'll stand for those with everybody else.

After that, it's football. Leon thinks the ten days of rest will matter. Ivy thinks the Jaguars are better than anyone the Giants have seen in a month. Both of them are probably right, and both of them know this one is going to be tighter than it looks on paper.

My call: Jaguars 25, Giants 20.
