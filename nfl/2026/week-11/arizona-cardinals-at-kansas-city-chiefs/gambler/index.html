Arizona Cardinals at Kansas City Chiefs

Arizona Cardinals (3-7) at Kansas City Chiefs (7-2)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 22 · Arrowhead Stadium, Kansas City, MO · CBS

Arrowhead's parking lots open four and a half hours before kickoff, which gives Chet Morrell plenty of time to explain why the Chiefs won't win by 16. That's my Cardinals vs Chiefs prediction in short: Arizona +15.5 is the pick against the spread, Over 40.5 is my over/under play, and Chiefs 28, Cardinals 18 is the score.

Neil Carver flew in from Phoenix, and Chet took him to see Holiday Reflections at Union Station, which opens Friday with the 30-foot Grand Hall tree moved to the west side. Neil spent most of the visit arguing that his team plays better away from home. He has a case, and the Cardinals travel more than a thousand miles for this one, which Neil insists is the kind of trip they like.

The Formula: Net ratings Kansas City Chiefs 3.7, Arizona Cardinals −6.9 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 17, 2026).

Kansas City is 7-2, 4-0 at Arrowhead, and has allowed only 156 points in nine games. That's not in dispute. What matters is that 15.5 points is a huge number in the NFL, and Arizona has been competitive on the road. The Cardinals are 3-3 away from home, with wins at the Chargers, at the Rams and in Dallas, and none of their six road games has been decided by more than 14.

Sunday morning update: as of Nov 22, 6:11 AM ET, DraftKings has Chiefs −15.5, total 40.5. This preview was written against Chiefs −16, total 41.5, so the spread moved from Chiefs −16 to Chiefs −15.5 and the total dropped from 41.5 to 40.5. The Cover Writer stays on Cardinals +15.5 with Chiefs 28, Cardinals 18, because Chiefs by 10 stays inside the 15.5. The Over holds, now 40.5, with 46 points. Official statuses (NFL.com Week 11 report, ESPN team updates, checked Nov 22, 6:11 AM ET): Cardinals QB Carson Beck was downgraded to out Saturday with back spasms and veteran Josh Johnson starts, per the Cardinals' website and NFL.com. Chiefs RB Kenneth Walker III, guard Trey Smith and tackle Jaylon Moore are out.

Follow The Money, which follows the Action Network consensus: the consensus spread opened Chiefs −16 and is Chiefs −15.5 now, and the consensus total opened 41.5 and is 40.5 now, so it has no side pick (no move of a full point) and takes the Under 40.5. The Formula: Chiefs 29, Cardinals 17 on net ratings. It stays on Cardinals +15.5 (Chiefs by 12 stays inside the 15.5) and stays on the Over, now 40.5 (46 points). Next Man Up: Chiefs 26, Cardinals 17 on who's playing. It stays on Cardinals +15.5 (Chiefs by 9 stays inside the 15.5) and stays on the Over, now 40.5 (43 points). Letdown Watch: Chiefs 28, Cardinals 16 on rest and travel. It stays on Cardinals +15.5 (Chiefs by 12 stays inside the 15.5) and stays on the Over, now 40.5 (44 points). In The Trenches: Chiefs 27, Cardinals 20 on the line of scrimmage. It stays on Cardinals +15.5 (Chiefs by 7 stays inside the 15.5) and stays on the Over, now 40.5 (47 points).

The risk on the Over is Arizona's offense, which managed three points at Seattle and six against the Rams over the last two weeks. Kansas City carries most of the load here. The Chiefs scored 35 on the Jets and 35 more at Atlanta, Patrick Mahomes has 19 touchdown throws, and Kenneth Walker III has 872 rushing yards, though a groin injury has him out Sunday. Trey McBride, with 78 catches, gives Arizona at least one reliable way to move the ball late.

Chiefs 28, Cardinals 18 puts 46 points on the board, comfortably past 40.5. A ten-point Kansas City win is a good day for Chet and still leaves the Cardinals five and a half points of cushion.

Kansas City has won by more than 16 three times this season. Arizona, for all its misery at home, hasn't lost a road game by that much.

Chiefs 28, Cardinals 18. Cardinals cover the 15.5. Over 40.5.
