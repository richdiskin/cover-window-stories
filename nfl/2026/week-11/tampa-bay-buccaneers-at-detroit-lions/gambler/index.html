Detroit lost Aidan Hutchinson for the season to a torn ACL during a sack celebration in Munich, and my Buccaneers vs Lions prediction starts there. Detroit −3.5 gets my spread pick and Over 47.5 gets my over/under pick, with Lions 26, Buccaneers 22 as the final.

Martin Sorensen took the news the way Detroit takes most November news, by going skating. The Rink at Campus Martius Park opens Saturday with an Ugly Holiday Sweater Party under the city's Christmas tree, and Sal Ferris, in from Tampa, has been handed a pirate-ship sweater for the occasion.

The Formula: Net ratings Detroit Lions −0.4, Tampa Bay Buccaneers −5.2 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 17, 2026).

Dan Campbell confirmed the injury on Tuesday and called it a gut punch, and he wasn't exaggerating. Hutchinson has six sacks this season and 49 since Detroit drafted him in 2022. Alim McNeill, with 6.5 sacks, now carries that load. The Lions are still 7-2 and 4-0 at Ford Field, and nobody here is saying they lose. The number has come down from 6.5 to 3.5 since Tuesday, which is about right for a defensive front suddenly missing its best player, against a Tampa Bay team that has scored 202 points and allowed 178. That's a better point differential than a 3-6 record suggests, and the Bucs are rested, coming off a bye after a 27-24 loss in Chicago.

The line moved 3 points, so the pick moves with it. The Over holds, now 47.5, with 48 points. Lions CB D.J. Reed and RB Sione Vaki are questionable.

The Over comes from Detroit's offense. The Lions have scored 257 points in nine games, Jared Goff has 18 touchdown throws, Jahmyr Gibbs has 1,000 rushing yards and 11 touchdowns, and Amon-Ra St. Brown has 755 receiving yards. Bucky Irving brings 825 rushing yards of his own for Tampa Bay. Lions 26, Buccaneers 22 is 48 points, enough to clear 47.5 by a half-point, and Detroit by four clears the 3.5 by a half-point.

Without Hutchinson, Detroit is still good enough to win. It just isn't the same team up front, and against a rested opponent that matters, which is why this is a half-point cover and not a comfortable one.

Lions 26, Buccaneers 22. Lions cover the 3.5. Over 47.5.
