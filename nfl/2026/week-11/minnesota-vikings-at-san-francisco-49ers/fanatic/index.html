Minnesota Vikings at San Francisco 49ers

Minnesota Vikings (7-2) at San Francisco 49ers (8-1)

8:20 PM ET Sun Nov 22 · Estadio Banorte, Mexico City, Mexico · NBC

XFacebook

Erik Dahl has never seen the Vikings play in Mexico City, for the simple reason that the Vikings never have. Sunday night at Estadio Banorte, that changes, and Erik has been practicing his Spanish on the plane, mostly the words for "where is my seat" and "that was holding."

Nora Haskell has done this before. She was in the building in November 2022, back when it was still called Estadio Azteca, when the 49ers beat Arizona 38-10, and she has been telling Erik about it since the schedule came out.

Nora: The noise is different here. It comes from everywhere.

Erik: It comes from everywhere in Minneapolis too.

Nora: Not like this.

Minnesota Vikings vs San Francisco 49ers in Week 11 is the first Sunday night game ever played outside the United States, and the NFL's first game in Mexico in four years. Tickets in the general sale went in about an hour back in September. Erik got his through a cousin and doesn't want to talk about what he owes the cousin now.

There is no public parking at the stadium, so they'll take the Tren Ligero to the Estadio Azteca station like everybody else, and the gates open at 3:30 for a 7:20 local kickoff. Nora has planned the afternoon down to the taco stand. Erik has planned it down to the jersey.

These two teams have history, and Erik brought the receipts. The all-time series is 25-25-1. The Vikings have won the last two meetings. Nora would like it noted that the six postseason meetings have gone San Francisco's way five times.

Erik: Regular season is all that counts tonight.

Nora: You're the one who brought up history.

The Vikings are 7-2 and have won with defense all year. They've allowed 126 points in nine games, the fewest of anyone Nora can name off the top of her head, and Andrew Van Ginkel has 8.5 sacks. Blake Cashman has 76 tackles. Kirk Cousins started the 30-10 win in Green Bay last Sunday, and Erik says that game was the most relaxed he's felt in years. Aaron Jones Sr. has 521 rushing yards, and Justin Jefferson has 467 receiving yards, which Erik considers a personal insult to Jefferson and wants explained.

The 49ers are 8-1. They opened the season in Melbourne, so a long trip isn't new, and last week they outlasted Dallas 46-40 in a game Nora watched standing up. Brock Purdy has 2,369 yards and 20 touchdown throws against five interceptions. Christian McCaffrey has ten rushing touchdowns, and George Kittle has 557 receiving yards. Fred Warner has 88 tackles and, as far as Nora is concerned, has never had a bad day in his life.

Nora: You know we're technically the home team.

Erik: In Mexico.

Nora: Technically.

Neither team is really at home. Both of them flew a long way to get here, both are coming off road wins, and both fan bases will be outnumbered by locals who just want to see football on a Sunday night in their own city. Nora loves that. Erik is starting to.

After the game, they'll ride the train back toward the city center and argue the whole way. Erik will want to talk about the defense. Nora will want to talk about Purdy. Somewhere around the second stop, they'll both admit it was worth the trip.

My call: 49ers 24, Vikings 19.
