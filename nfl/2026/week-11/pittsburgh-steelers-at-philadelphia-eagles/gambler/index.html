My Steelers vs Eagles prediction has Philadelphia losing an eighth straight game and still keeping it close. The pick is Eagles +4.5 on the spread and Over 38.5 on the over/under, with Steelers 22, Eagles 19 as the final.

Ray Donnelly spent Saturday at the Christmas Village preview at LOVE Park, walking Ed Kowalski past the German Christmas pyramid and up the Ferris wheel, and Ed spent most of the ride still talking about Cincinnati.

The Formula: Net ratings Philadelphia Eagles −8.8, Pittsburgh Steelers −7.6 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 17, 2026).

Pittsburgh is 6-3 and unbeaten at home, but 2-3 on the road, and the Steelers are coming off a 38-18 loss in Cincinnati on Sunday night. Philadelphia has had a bye to reset after seven straight losses. The Steelers get the usual seven days after that Sunday night trip, while the Eagles have had two weeks to prepare. Nobody is mistaking the Eagles for a good team at 2-7 with 148 points scored, but laying 4.5 on the road with a Pittsburgh team that just got run out of the building is asking a lot.

The Formula likes the Eagles enough to have them winning outright, 21-20. Letdown Watch has Steelers 22, Eagles 19, and In The Trenches has Steelers 22, Eagles 20, so three of the four scoring cards take Philadelphia with the points. Next Man Up is the lone card laying 4.5, at Steelers 22, Eagles 17. Follow The Money stays quiet on the side and the total. On the total, all four cards with a score have the Over 38.5.

That Over is built on how low 38.5 is. Aaron Rodgers has 15 touchdown throws, Jaylen Warren has 719 rushing yards, and Pittsburgh has scored 198 points in nine games. The Eagles don't need much to help, just a couple of scoring drives and a field goal, which even this offense has managed most weeks. The forecast calls for 61 degrees and light wind, so there's no weather reason to expect a slog.

T.J. Watt, with 7.5 sacks, is the biggest problem for whoever starts at quarterback for Philadelphia. That's why Pittsburgh still wins. Steelers 22, Eagles 19 lands on 41 points, enough to get past 38.5, and a three-point game sits inside 4.5.

An eighth straight loss will sting in South Philadelphia, but two weeks of preparation should keep it from turning lopsided.

Steelers 22, Eagles 19. Eagles cover the 4.5. Over 38.5.
