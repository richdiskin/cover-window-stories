Pittsburgh Steelers at Philadelphia Eagles

Pittsburgh Steelers (6-3) at Philadelphia Eagles (2-7)

1:00 PM ET Sun Nov 22 · Lincoln Financial Field, Philadelphia, PA · CBS

XFacebook

Somebody in Philadelphia put up a 65-foot Ferris wheel this week, and Ray Donnelly is just relieved that something in this city is going up.

The Christmas Village preview weekend opens Saturday at LOVE Park, City Hall's Courtyard and North Broad, and Ray has been planning his route like a parade marshal. Bratwurst first, then raclette, then the Churro Split, then one slow lap around the German Christmas pyramid, which is 30 feet tall and carries more than 3,000 lights. Ray has walked past it every year he can remember. This year he needs it more.

Ed Kowalski drove in from Pittsburgh for Pittsburgh Steelers vs Philadelphia Eagles in Week 11, and Ray met him at the Ferris wheel.

Ed: You look tired.

Ray: We've lost seven in a row, Ed. I look like a man who has lost seven in a row.

The Eagles started 2-0. Then came Chicago, the Rams, Jacksonville in London, Carolina, Dallas, Washington and the Giants, and Ray can name the worst moment of each one without being asked. Joe Milton III started the last one, a 19-11 home loss to the Giants, and the bye week since has been the quietest two weeks of Ray's football life. Philadelphia has scored 148 points in nine games, and the Eagles are 1-4 at the Linc.

So Ray is holding on to small things. Nolan Smith Jr. has five sacks. Zack Baun has 65 tackles and plays every snap like it insulted his family. Makai Lemon has 37 catches and keeps getting open. Saquon Barkley is still Saquon Barkley in Ray's head, whatever the season says.

Ed, to his credit, is not gloating. He just watched his team lose 38-18 in Cincinnati on Sunday night, and he hasn't fully recovered.

Ed: We were 6-2 going in. I was making plans.

Ray: What kind of plans?

Ed: The kind you don't say out loud in Pittsburgh.

The Steelers are 6-3 and 4-0 at home, but only 2-3 away. Aaron Rodgers has 2,014 yards and 15 touchdown throws against six interceptions. Jaylen Warren has 719 rushing yards. DK Metcalf has 432 receiving yards. T.J. Watt has 7.5 sacks, and Payton Wilson has 88 tackles, and Ed describes the two of them as the reason he still has hair. Mike McCarthy has already been asked about Cincinnati more times than Ed can stand.

They rode the Ferris wheel once. Ed held on with both hands. From the top you can see City Hall and the whole length of the holiday stalls, more than 120 vendors, and Ray pointed out every one he planned to eat at before Sunday.

Ed: You know they close this whole thing again on Monday.

Ray: For three days. Then it's back on Thanksgiving. Philadelphia always comes back, Ed.

Ed: Your team hasn't.

Ray let that one go. It was a fair shot.

The Linc will be loud for the first quarter no matter what, because it always is. Ray will bring the lucky scarf his late uncle gave him, and Ed has promised to keep his celebrating to a polite nod. What Ray wants is simple: one good afternoon, a defense that gets to Rodgers, and an end to the streak somewhere other than in his nightmares.

He'll probably have to wait another week. But it'll be close enough to keep him in his seat until the end.

My call: Steelers 22, Eagles 19.
