Las Vegas Raiders (4-6) at Denver Broncos (7-2)

Denver has won five straight, but my Raiders vs Broncos prediction has them coming the way they usually do, by a touchdown or less. Raiders +10.5 is the call against the spread, the Over 41.5 is my over/under pick, and Broncos 27, Raiders 19 is the final score.

Ruth Calder took Vic Moreno to the 110-foot Mile High Tree lighting on the Tivoli Quad on Friday night, and he spent most of it explaining why the Raiders can keep this one respectable.

The Formula: Net ratings Denver Broncos −2.5, Las Vegas Raiders −9.0 (nflverse play-by-play, Nov 17, 2026).

Denver is 7-2, 4-0 at home and coming off a bye, so there's no question who the better team is. The question is margin. Only one of Denver's seven wins came by more than a touchdown, a 30-16 game in Arizona. The rest were by seven or fewer, including a 17-14 win against Seattle and a 28-24 win against Kansas City. The Broncos have scored 198 points and allowed 186, which is not the profile of a team that blows people out.

Sunday morning update: as of Nov 22, 6:11 AM ET, DraftKings has Broncos −10.5, total 41.5. This preview was written against Broncos −10, total 40.5, so the spread moved from Broncos −10 to Broncos −10.5 and the total rose from 40.5 to 41.5. The Cover Writer stays on Raiders +10.5 with Broncos 27, Raiders 19, because Broncos by 8 stays inside the 10.5. The Over holds, now 41.5, with 46 points. Official statuses (NFL.com Week 11 report, ESPN team updates, checked Nov 22, 6:11 AM ET): Raiders TE Brock Bowers is questionable with an ankle injury and DT Tonka Hemingway is out; Broncos S Brandon Jones is questionable. No quarterback is on either team's report.

Follow The Money, which follows the Action Network consensus: the consensus spread opened Broncos −10 and is Broncos −10.5 now, and the consensus total opened 40.5 and is 41 now, so it has no side pick (no move of a full point) and has no total pick (no move of a full point). Next Man Up: Broncos 26, Raiders 19 on who's playing. It stays on Raiders +10.5 (Broncos by 7 stays inside the 10.5) and stays on the Over, now 41.5 (45 points). Letdown Watch: Broncos 30, Raiders 16 on rest and travel. It stays on Broncos −10.5 (Broncos by 14 clears the 10.5) and stays on the Over, now 41.5 (46 points). In The Trenches: Broncos 25, Raiders 19 on the line of scrimmage. It stays on Raiders +10.5 (Broncos by 6 stays inside the 10.5) and stays on the Over, now 41.5 (44 points).

The Raiders are 3-2 on the road, with wins at the Chargers, in New Orleans and at the Jets, and they've scored 251 points this season. Brock Bowers has 676 receiving yards and six touchdowns, and Ashton Jeanty gives them a runner who can keep the clock moving if they get a lead. Maxx Crosby, with five sacks, can make Nix uncomfortable. Fernando Mendoza started the 21-13 loss to Seattle. Las Vegas has also allowed 289 points, so Bo Nix, with 11 touchdown throws, should find the end zone too.

Broncos 27, Raiders 19 totals 46, well past 41.5. An eight-point Denver win is more comfortable than most of its wins have been this year, and it still leaves the Raiders two and a half points to spare.

Mile High in late November tends to belong to Denver, and it will again. Ten and a half points is simply too much to ask of an offense that wins with patience instead of fireworks.

Broncos 27, Raiders 19. Raiders cover the 10.5. Over 41.5.
