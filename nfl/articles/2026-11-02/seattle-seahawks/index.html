Seattle Seahawks vs Chicago Bears Week 8: No Encore Tonight 17-34

Final: Chicago Bears (6-2) 34, Seattle Seahawks (4-4) 17, Mon., Nov. 2, Lumen Field, Seattle. Next: vs Arizona Cardinals, Sun., Nov. 8, 4:25 PM ET, Lumen Field, Seattle

XFacebook

Chicago Bears 34, Seattle Seahawks 17, and the 12s walked out of Lumen Field into a long, quiet Monday night. Across town at Easy Street Records in West Seattle, Pearl Jam's Jeff Ament and his brother Barry were talking through Since Forever, their book of black-and-white photos from the band's early years. Classic Seattle sound. Down on Occidental, the champs could not find a note.

It started badly and never really turned. Chicago held the ball for 17 plays and 8:32 on its first drive and scored. Without left tackle Charles Cross, out with an ankle injury, Sam Darnold was under fire all night, and Jeffery Simmons sacked him on our first snap after that score. The run game, with Zach Charbonnet back from a torn ACL, Jadarian Price off injured reserve and Quenton Nelson making his Seattle debut, managed 81 yards.

There were sparks. Darnold found Jaxon Smith-Njigba for 43 yards and a touchdown with 30 seconds left in the half, but Jason Myers pushed the extra point wide left and we trailed 13-9. Down 20-9, Myers had a 33-yarder blocked by Austin Booker. Then DeMarcus Lawrence ripped the ball from Colston Loveland and fell on it, Darnold hit Cooper Kupp from 7, and Tory Horton caught the two-point try. It was 20-17, and the comeback was right there.

Chicago answered in six plays, with Kalif Raymond tearing off 39 yards before D'Andre Swift scored. James Lynch intercepted Darnold at our 22 and returned it to the 6, and Swift scored his third short touchdown. Our last drive died at the Chicago 7 on fourth down.

Darnold went 17 of 34 for 188 yards with two touchdowns, an interception and three sacks. We went 1 of 11 on third down. Smith-Njigba caught six for 118 and kept fighting. Ernest Jones IV made 13 tackles, Devon Witherspoon eight, and Michael Dickson boomed five punts for 259 yards. George Holani brought back six kicks for 144. Leonard Williams left with a stinger. That snapped a 30-game run of never losing by more than four points, and it's three losses in four games. At 4-4, Arizona comes to Lumen on Sunday. Get loud, get Cross back, get right.
