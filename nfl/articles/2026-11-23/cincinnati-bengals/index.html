Cincinnati Bengals vs Washington Commanders Week 11: Stevens Beats Us at the Gun, 20-22

Final: Washington Commanders (4-5-1) 22, Cincinnati Bengals (7-3) 20, Mon., Nov. 23, Northwest Stadium, Landover. Next: vs New Orleans Saints, Sun., Nov. 29, 1:00 PM ET, Paycor Stadium, Cincinnati

XFacebook

Drew Stevens kicked a 26-yard field goal with zero on the clock, the Washington Commanders beat the Cincinnati Bengals 22-20 in Landover, and our five-game winning streak is dead. Saturday night, David Sedaris brought his Too Many Jokes About Shadows tour to the Aronoff Center. Monday night the shadow was a Washington rookie we never found.

We scored 31 or more in every one of those five wins, and this one ended at 20. It was wet, it was ugly early, and Joe Burrow got sacked on the very first snap. Our first four drives produced three points, a 38-yard Evan McPherson field goal at the end of a 15-play grind.

Then Burrow found his rhythm. On third-and-11, Samaje Perine took a short throw 44 yards, and two plays later Tee Higgins hauled in a 31-yard touchdown down the left side with 1:55 left in the half. 10-0. Washington went 79 yards in 1:43, a Dax Hill interference flag put the ball at the 1, and Jacory Croskey-Merritt plowed in with 12 seconds left. 10-7 at the break.

Jayden Daniels opened the third with a 47-yard scramble, and Stevens tied it 10-10. Our answer was the best drive of the night: 66 yards in eight plays, Mike Gesicki grabbing balls of 19 and 9 yards in the middle of the field, and Burrow flipping a 4-yarder to Colbie Young for the touchdown. 17-10.

That was our last touchdown. Stevens kicked from 40 for 17-13, we went three-and-out, he made a 25-yarder for 17-16, we punted again, and with 2:26 left he hit from 41. Washington 19-17.

Burrow didn't blink. Higgins for 12, Gesicki for 32, and after a sack shoved us back to the 37, McPherson drilled a 56-yarder with 46 seconds left. 20-19. That's a grown-man kick.

And then the defense that sacked Daniels three times, all three by Boye Mafe, gave up 64 yards on two throws. Antonio Williams caught one for 22 down the middle and another for 42 to our 8, Daniels spiked it with five seconds left, and Stevens finished us. The rookie had nine catches for 122 yards, and nobody on our side could find him.

Burrow went 28 of 40 for 300 yards and two touchdowns with no interceptions. Ja'Marr Chase got 31 yards on five catches from nine targets. We ran 14 times for 31 yards while Washington ran for 150. Run like that and your kicker has to bail you out. Ours did. Theirs got the last word.

We're 7-3 and still on top of the AFC North. Next up: the New Orleans Saints at Paycor Stadium, Sunday, Nov. 29, 1:00 PM ET. Get home, get loud, Who Dey.
