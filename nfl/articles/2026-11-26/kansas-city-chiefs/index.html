Kansas City Chiefs vs Buffalo Bills Week 12: Beaten at the Gun, 10-13

Final: Buffalo Bills (10-1) 13, Kansas City Chiefs (8-3) 10, Thu., Nov. 26, Highmark Stadium, Orchard Park. Next: at Los Angeles Rams, Thu., Dec. 3, 8:15 PM ET, SoFi Stadium, Inglewood

XFacebook

The Buffalo Bills beat the Kansas City Chiefs 13-10 on a 57-yard Tyler Bass field goal as time ran out in Orchard Park. In Kansas City, the 97th Plaza Lighting Ceremony moved up so Kansas City could flip the switch and still make kickoff, and Royals catcher Carter Jensen lit up the Country Club Plaza at 6:25. Those lights stay on into January. My mood will take longer to come back on.

This was a defensive fight, and our defense deserved a win. Alohi Gilman intercepted Josh Allen in the end zone in the second quarter, and Nohl Williams intercepted him again at our 13 in the third. Nick Bolton was everywhere, with 14 tackles, a sack and two tackles for loss. Allen finished 10 of 21 for 81 yards.

We came alive late in the half. Patrick Mahomes found Travis Kelce for 24 and 21 on the two-minute drive, and Harrison Butker kicked a 26-yarder with two seconds left to make it 3-7. Jalen Royals brought the second-half kickoff back 37 yards, Emmett Johnson ripped off runs of 13 and 12, and Johnson finished the drive from the 1 for 10-7.

Then the drives died. Bass tied it from 54 early in the fourth. Our next trip reached the Buffalo 17 on a 19-yard Noah Gray catch, and then a holding flag wiped out a 10-yard Johnson run to the 7, a pre-snap flag backed us up again, and Butker pushed a 46-yarder wide right with 6:01 left.

The last two minutes hurt. A Rashee Rice catch got overturned on review, and Matt Araiza had to punt from deep in our end twice. On the second one, with 15 seconds left, Greg Dortch brought it back 17 yards to our 39. Bass hit from 57 with no time left.

Mahomes went 16 of 29 for 174 yards. Kelce caught four balls for 71. Johnson ran 17 times for 72 yards and the touchdown. We held James Cook III to 3.7 yards a carry and still lost on a kick.

We're 8-3, and it's a short week before the Los Angeles Rams at SoFi Stadium on Thursday, Dec. 3, at 8:15 PM ET. The Plaza lights shine through Jan. 10, and I'm driving down to see them Friday night anyway. They still look good. So does this defense.
