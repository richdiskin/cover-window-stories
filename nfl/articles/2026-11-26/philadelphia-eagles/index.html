Philadelphia Eagles vs Dallas Cowboys Week 12: Elliott From 59, 39-38

Final: Philadelphia Eagles (4-7) 39, Dallas Cowboys (5-7) 38, Thu., Nov. 26, AT&T Stadium, Arlington. Next: at Arizona Cardinals, Sun., Dec. 6, 4:05 PM ET, State Farm Stadium, Glendale

XFacebook

Philadelphia Eagles 39, Dallas Cowboys 38, and Jake Elliott won it from 59 yards with two seconds left in Arlington. In Philadelphia, the Thanksgiving Day Parade on the Benjamin Franklin Parkway, the oldest in the country going back to 1920, got called off Wednesday night because of a high wind warning. Philly lost its parade, so the Birds held one in Texas.

Nobody was planning a parade at halftime. Dallas led 28-10. Javonte Williams had two touchdown runs and a 12-yard touchdown catch with four seconds left in the half, and Brandon Aubrey made it 31-10 with a 59-yarder in the third. Our one bright spot was DeVonta Smith, who took a Joe Milton throw 50 yards for a score in the second quarter.

Smith did it again with 15 seconds left in the third, 45 yards for 17-31. Then Ty Robinson got a hand on a Bryan Anger punt, and Kenny Moore II scooped it at the Dallas 3 and carried it in. Dak Prescott answered with a 14-yard scramble for 38-24, and Britain Covey brought the kickoff back 55 yards. Three snaps later, Milton ran it in from 15 himself. The two-point try failed, and it was 30-38.

Milton gave one away when Cobie Durant intercepted him with 7:14 left, but the defense held, and Aubrey pushed a 40-yarder wide left at 3:27. Milton went 70 yards in four plays: Darius Cooper for 14, Elijah Moore for 23 and 9, and Makai Lemon for a 24-yard touchdown. A holding flag erased a good two-point try, and the second try fell incomplete. 36-38.

Then Dallas went for it on fourth-and-1 at our 39 with 55 seconds left, and Robinson buried Prescott for a 9-yard sack. We had the ball at our 48 with 50 seconds to play. Smith caught one for 9, a Dallas interference flag gave us 2 more, Smith grabbed another for 5, and Elliott walked out and hit from 59. Andrew Mukuba intercepted the last heave for good measure.

Smith caught 11 balls for 200 yards and two scores. Milton threw for 331 yards and three touchdowns and ran for another. Jeremiah Trotter Jr. made 12 tackles, and Robinson finished with a blocked punt and the sack that flipped the game. We scored 22 points in the fourth quarter.

That's two straight wins, and we're 4-7 with the Arizona Cardinals next at State Farm Stadium, Sunday, Dec. 6, at 4:05 PM ET. The Parkway can have its floats back next year. On Thursday, the parade ran through Arlington. Fly, Eagles, Fly.
