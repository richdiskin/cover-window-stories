Buffalo Bills vs Minnesota Vikings Week 9: Keep-Away Kings 23-6

Final: Buffalo Bills (7-1) 23, Minnesota Vikings (6-2) 6, Mon., Nov. 9, U.S. Bank Stadium, Minneapolis. Next: at New York Jets, Sun., Nov. 15, 1:00 PM ET, MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford

XFacebook

Buffalo Bills 23, Minnesota Vikings 6, and we walked into U.S. Bank Stadium on a Monday night and ground down the least-scored-on defense in football. Back home, the Sabres posted Monday that 27 veterans are coming to their practice Tuesday to meet the players and take home signed jerseys before Military Appreciation Night on Veterans Day. Good week to be from Buffalo. Better night to wear the charging buffalo.

It was not pretty early, and we don't need pretty. Josh Allen got sacked four times, and Byron Murphy Jr. ran down a deep heave in the end zone and brought it back 54 yards. Didn't matter. Greg Rousseau fell on a Carson Wentz throw that sailed backward, Tyler Bass hit from 39, and our defense kept slamming the door. Minnesota got four straight first-half drives beginning past its own 40 and never got deeper than our 48.

Then came the haymaker before the break. Tied 3-3, Allen marched us 80 yards in eight plays, rolled right, took a hard hit from Andrew Van Ginkel and dropped a 25-yard touchdown to tight end Keleki Latu in the back of the end zone with 44 seconds left. Will Reichard answered with a 53-yarder with five seconds to go, so it was 10-6 at halftime.

The second half was a Buffalo clinic in keep-away. Dorian Williams intercepted Wentz at our 11 on Minnesota's first series. We answered with 14 plays and 83 yards, with Ty Johnson hauling in a 36-yarder, and Bass made it 13-6 from 25. Terrel Bernard intercepted Wentz at our 34 on the next Vikings drive. Then we took 8:32 off the clock in the fourth quarter, 15 plays and 84 yards, and James Cook III bulled in from 5 for 20-6. Bass added a 52-yarder, his third make of the night and his 24th in a row, a franchise record. Minnesota's last gasp died on downs at our 13.

Allen went 21 of 31 for 234 yards and a touchdown, and that score tied Peyton Manning for the most touchdowns, throwing, running and catching, in a player's first 10 years with 322. He's halfway through year nine. Cook carried 31 times for 107 yards. Khalil Shakir caught four for 58. We held the ball for 42:32, and Minnesota went 0 for 8 on third down. Rousseau had a sack, three hits on the quarterback and that fumble recovery, Javon Solomon added a sack, and Bernard had six tackles and two breakups to go with his interception. We're 7-1, 4-0 on the road, and that's the best eight-game opening in Buffalo since 1993. The Jets are next at MetLife on Sunday. Go Bills.
