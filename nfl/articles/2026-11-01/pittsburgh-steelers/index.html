Pittsburgh Steelers vs Cleveland Browns Week 8: Steel Rolls 27-6

Final: Cleveland Browns (5-3) 6, Pittsburgh Steelers (6-2) 27, Sun., Nov. 1, Acrisure Stadium, Pittsburgh. Next: bye week, then at Cincinnati Bengals, Sun., Nov. 15, 8:20 PM ET, Paycor Stadium, Cincinnati

ShareXFacebook

Pittsburgh Steelers 27, Cleveland Browns 6. Sunday afternoon, along the Mon, the old Carrie Blast Furnaces lit up for Samhain, a fire festival running from 3 to 9 PM inside the old mill. Down at Acrisure Stadium, another old Pittsburgh furnace was running hot. That made 23 straight regular-season home wins over Cleveland, tying the NFL record.

It started like it always does against these guys. Cleveland went backward. Daylen Everette ripped the ball from Harold Fannin Jr. on the opening drive, and Pittsburgh won the challenge to get it. Chris Boswell missed from 57, so Patrick Queen and Cameron Heyward went and got it back, splitting a sack of Deshaun Watson as Queen jarred the ball loose and Keeanu Benton fell on it. Five plays later, Rico Dowdle ran 9 yards for a 7-0 lead. The Browns finished the first quarter with minus-1 yard.

Jaylen Warren set the tone on a third-and-13, bursting 51 yards up the middle, and Boswell added two field goals. Andre Szmyt kept Cleveland close with kicks from 32 and 49, and it was 13-6 with 45 seconds left in the half. Then Warren went 23 yards, Pat Freiermuth caught one for 28, Germie Bernard got us to the 1, and with no timeouts left Mike McCarthy skipped the chip shot. Direct snap to Freiermuth, touchdown, 20-6 with one second showing.

Early in the third, Heyward blew up Quinshon Judkins on a third-and-1. Next time we had it, Aaron Rodgers found Darnell Washington for 58 yards to the 1, and Warren punched it in for 27-6. Warren finished with 143 yards on 17 carries. Washington had a career-high 94 on three catches.

The defense did the rest. Eight sacks, the most since 2021. T.J. Watt had two, giving him 20.5 against Cleveland, the most by any player against the Browns in NFL history. Queen made 12 tackles with 1.5 sacks. Nick Herbig and Kevin Jobity Jr. got one each, Jobity's first in the league. Cleveland went 0 for 3 in the red zone and turned it over on downs twice.

Rodgers threw an interception to Denzel Ward and we lost a fumble, so it was not a clean sheet. It was still our fourth straight win. We are 6-2, alone in first in the AFC North, and 4-0 at home under McCarthy. After the bye, it is Cincinnati on a Sunday night. Same furnace. Different fuel.
