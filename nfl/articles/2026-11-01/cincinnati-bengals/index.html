Cincinnati Bengals vs Tennessee Titans Week 8: Burrow Ends It 43-36

Final (OT): Tennessee Titans (1-7) 36, Cincinnati Bengals (5-2) 43, Sun., Nov. 1, Paycor Stadium, Cincinnati. Next: at Atlanta Falcons, Sun., Nov. 8, 9:30 AM ET, Santiago Bernabéu, Madrid

XFacebook

Cincinnati Bengals 43, Tennessee Titans 36 in overtime. While the Cincinnati Zoo ran HallZOOween on Sunday afternoon, the crowd at Paycor Stadium got a Halloween scare of its own, and it started early.

Every question with this team is a quarterback question, and for one quarter the answer was ugly. Joe Burrow threw deep for Ja'Marr Chase and Amani Hooker intercepted it at the Tennessee 9. Tony Pollard went 40 yards for a touchdown. Joey Slye kicked two field goals. It was 13-0 Titans after the first quarter.

Then Burrow went to work. A holding call wiped out a Chase Brown touchdown, so on third-and-18 he put it on Chase from 30 yards out. DJ Turner forced a Wan'Dale Robinson fumble that Josh Newton recovered, and Burrow hit Jack Endries from 1 yard with 20 seconds left in the half for a 14-13 lead. Hooker picked Burrow off again on the first drive of the third quarter, but Boye Mafe answered with a strip-sack of Cam Ward, Dexter Lawrence II recovered, and on the next snap Tee Higgins caught a 17-yard touchdown.

It was never comfortable. Hooker intercepted Burrow once more, three in all. Tennessee tied it 21-21, Demetrius Knight Jr. and Lawrence dropped Pollard in the end zone for a safety, and the fourth quarter brought three lead changes and a tie. Mike Gesicki's 25-yard touchdown made it 29-28, his fifth straight game with a score. Ward answered with a touchdown and a two-point throw for 36-29.

So there it was, 3:46 left, down seven. Burrow drove us down and found Higgins from 18 yards with 1:17 to go, and Lawrence sacked Ward with 32 seconds left in regulation to force a punt. We got the ball first in overtime, and Burrow finished a five-play, 70-yard drive by scrambling nine yards into the end zone himself. The defense closed it with a fourth-down stop on the final Tennessee snap.

The numbers: Burrow 38 of 48 for 462 yards, five touchdown throws and the winning run. Higgins caught 10 for 155 and two scores, Chase 11 for 141, and the offense went 7 of 10 on third down. The honest part: three interceptions, a Brown fumble that led to a Titans score, and 440 yards allowed to a 1-7 team. That has to get cleaned up.

Three straight wins and 5-2 for the first time since 2021. Next stop is Madrid against Atlanta.
