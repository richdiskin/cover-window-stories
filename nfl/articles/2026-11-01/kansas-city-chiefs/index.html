Kansas City Chiefs vs Denver Broncos Week 8: One Yard Short 24-28

Final: Kansas City Chiefs (5-2) 24, Denver Broncos (6-2) 28, Sun., Nov. 1, Empower Field at Mile High, Denver. Next: vs New York Jets, Sun., Nov. 8, 1:00 PM ET, Arrowhead Stadium, Kansas City

XFacebook

Denver Broncos 28, Kansas City Chiefs 24, and I'm still staring at the ceiling. On Halloween night, the West Bottoms threw a free party on the Rock Island Bridge, music and costumes for all ages. Twenty-four hours later, we went up to Mile High looking to sweep the team we handled at Arrowhead in Week 1. Patrick Mahomes gave us every reason to believe.

Wil Lutz kicked two early field goals, but Mahomes hit Rashee Rice for an 11-yard score, and then came the throw of the day. Pinned at our own 5, Mahomes found rookie Emmett Johnson for a 73-yard touchdown and a 14-6 lead. Denver got stuffed at our 1 three straight times before Bo Nix hit Jaylen Waddle on fourth down, but Johnson's 38-yard catch set up Harrison Butker's 45-yarder. We led 17-13 at the half.

The third quarter was pure Andy Reid. Denver went three-and-out on its first three drives, the home crowd booed, and Mahomes marched us 63 yards in 11 plays, finishing with a 4-yard throw to Noah Gray. That's 24-13, and you could hear Mile High go quiet.

Then it slipped. Nix led an 80-yard, nine-play drive, found Nate Adkins for the score and hit Waddle on the two-point try. Our answer stalled when Alex Singleton sacked Mahomes, and Butker's 54-yard try sailed wide right with 2:36 left. One snap later Nix hit Jonah Coleman for 51 yards, and Coleman ran it in from the 5. Johnson ripped off a 44-yard kickoff return, Travis Kelce caught a 32-yarder, and Kenneth Walker III, the Super Bowl LX MVP with Seattle, got us to the 1 with 35 seconds left. An incompletion. Walker stuffed by JL Skinner. A false start. Malcolm Roach sacked Mahomes as time ran out.

Mahomes went 30-of-41 for 321 yards, three touchdowns and no interceptions. Johnson caught five for 128, Rice seven for 53, Kelce five for 67, and Walker ran 21 times for 69. Nick Bolton made nine tackles and George Karlaftis had a strip-sack. Eleven penalties for 105 yards is the stat I can't shake. That's two straight losses and second place in the AFC West. The Jets come to Arrowhead next Sunday. Chiefs Kingdom rides on.
