Minnesota Vikings vs Detroit Lions Week 8: Perfect Run Ends 19-15

Final: Minnesota Vikings (6-1) 15, Detroit Lions (5-2) 19, Sun., Nov. 1, Ford Field, Detroit. Next: vs. Buffalo Bills, Mon., Nov. 9, 8:15 PM ET, U.S. Bank Stadium, Minneapolis

ShareXFacebook

Detroit Lions 19, Minnesota Vikings 15. I spent six weeks telling everyone that 6-0 was a rumor, and on Sunday at Ford Field, Minnesota Vikings vs Detroit Lions proved me right in the worst way. Back home, the BareBones Halloween Extravaganza was set for its final night in Powderhorn Park, a puppet show about the cycles of living and dying. Fitting. The undefeated part of our season just finished its cycle.

For a half, the purple held. Detroit drew two taunting flags on its first two drives and lost a touchdown to replay. Kyler Murray lofted a 1-yard throw early in the second quarter, and Justin Jefferson caught it for a 7-3 lead. Andrew Van Ginkel was a menace, with a career-high 2.5 sacks. D.J. Reed's diving interception in front of Jordan Addison set up a Detroit field goal right before the half, and we still went in up 7-6.

Then the ground gave way. Detroit ran for 66 yards in the first half and 164 in the second. Jahmyr Gibbs went 30 yards for a touchdown late in the third. Murray answered with the drive of the day, an 8-yard touchdown to T.J. Hockenson and a two-point throw to Addison, and we led 15-12 with 8:44 left.

We held that lead for a little over four minutes. Detroit ran six straight times, Gibbs and Sione Vaki taking turns, and Jared Goff hit Jameson Williams from 7 yards with 4:35 left. Our offense went three-and-out, Kevin O'Connell chose to punt on fourth-and-5 from our 33, and we never saw the ball again.

Murray went 17 of 29 for 210 yards, two touchdowns and an interception, and he ran for 35. Addison caught three balls for 66, including a 51-yarder. Hockenson had four for 58, and Jefferson had five for 33. We ran for 81 yards and went 3 of 10 on third down, and Detroit held the ball for 35:43. Joshua Metellus made 13 tackles, Eric Wilson had 11 and a sack, and Byron Murphy Jr. and Blake Cashman made 10 apiece.

Our whole section went quiet when Jack Campbell was carted off in the fourth quarter. Some things matter more than a division game.

We are 6-1 and still first in the NFC North. The last loss before this one came on Nov. 30, 2025, at Seattle. Buffalo visits U.S. Bank Stadium on Monday night, Nov. 9, and we get an extra day to get ready. I will not panic in November. I will not celebrate in it, either.
