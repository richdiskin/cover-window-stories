Cleveland Browns vs Pittsburgh Steelers Week 8: Browns Fall 27-6

Final: Cleveland Browns (5-3) 6, Pittsburgh Steelers (6-2) 27, Sun., Nov. 1, Acrisure Stadium, Pittsburgh. Next: at New Orleans Saints, Sun., Nov. 8, 1:00 PM ET, Caesars Superdome, New Orleans

XFacebook

Pittsburgh Steelers 27, Cleveland Browns 6. All weekend, Jurassic Quest filled the Huntington Convention Center downtown with life-size dinosaurs and a costume contest for the kids. Sunday in Pittsburgh, our offense played like one of those dinosaurs: huge, slow, and getting knocked over by a meteor every few minutes. Eight sacks. Six points. Kids, look away.

The trouble started on the opening drive. Deshaun Watson got hurt on a roughing call, Shedeur Sanders came in, and Harold Fannin Jr. had the ball stripped at our 42. Pittsburgh won the challenge. Two drives later, Patrick Queen knocked it out of Watson's hands and Pittsburgh scored five plays later. We had minus-1 yard after one quarter.

Give the defense credit for keeping it a game. Carson Schwesinger made 10 tackles, and Adin Huntington sacked Aaron Rodgers on a third-and-goal to force a field goal. Andre Szmyt hit from 32 and 49, and it was 13-6 with 45 seconds left in the half.

Then the dam broke. Pittsburgh went 69 yards in six plays, and Pat Freiermuth took a direct snap in from the 1 with one second left. Early in the third, Darnell Washington rumbled 58 yards to the 1 and Jaylen Warren scored for 27-6. Warren ran for 143 yards. We ran for 34 as a team, our lowest of the season.

Watson still threw for 324 yards, his most in a game as a Brown, going 27 of 39. Fannin caught 10 for a career-high 154 yards, the second-most ever by a Browns tight end in one game. Denzel Ward intercepted a deep throw meant for DK Metcalf in the third quarter, his third of the season, tying his career high. Corey Bojorquez dropped three punts inside the 20.

But zero for 3 in the red zone, zero for 2 on fourth down, and no touchdowns for the first time all season. We are 5-3 and the season series is split. New Orleans is next on Nov. 8. Somebody find this offense a helmet before the next meteor hits.
