Los Angeles Chargers vs Los Angeles Rams Week 8: Film Room 9-24

Final: Los Angeles Chargers (0-7) 9, Los Angeles Rams (4-4) 24, Sun., Nov. 1, SoFi Stadium, Inglewood. Next: vs Houston Texans, Sun., Nov. 8, 4:05 PM ET, SoFi Stadium, Inglewood

XFacebook

Los Angeles Rams 24, Los Angeles Chargers 9, and I'm taking this one straight to the film room, because I refuse to file it under that tired old phrase. On Saturday, El Segundo, where this team goes to work every week, threw its Halloween Frolic on Main Street with bounce houses, games and music. On Sunday at SoFi Stadium, we held our roommates without a first down for five straight drives and still walked out 0-7.

Clue one came early. Justin Herbert's short throw got intercepted by Quentin Lake, who brought it back 45 yards to our 19, and Brandon McManus kicked a 30-yard field goal. Cameron Dicker answered from 34, 46 and 41 yards, and Mike McDaniel dialed up back-to-back flea-flickers in the second quarter that actually worked. The Rams had 24 yards on their first 15 plays. We led 9-3.

Clue two is where the case cracks. Matthew Stafford finally found Tutu Atwell for 40 yards, the Rams' first first down of the day, and Kyren Williams walked in from a yard out with 19 seconds left in the half. Herbert hit Ladd McConkey for 54 yards down to the 1 as the clock ran out. Nine points where there should have been 16, and a 10-9 deficit at the break.

The second half opened with Blake Corum carrying the Rams 55 yards on an eight-play drive that ended with his 23-yard touchdown run. Then Trent McDuffie intercepted Herbert and ran it back 26 yards to our 22, and two plays later Puka Nacua took a screen 18 yards for 24-9. Our last real shot ended at their 17 with 6:39 left, when Oronde Gadsden caught one a yard short on fourth-and-8. The Rams ran out the clock from there.

Herbert finished 24-of-32 for 257 yards with two interceptions, and he's thrown one in all seven games. McConkey caught four for 86, Gadsden five for 52 and Quentin Johnston four for 37. The run game is the evidence nobody can explain away: 25 carries, 64 yards, with Omarion Hampton at nine for 20 and Kimani Vidal at eight for 23. Tuli Tuipulotu had a sack, and Dicker went 3-for-3. We're the only winless team left, and Derwin James went on injured reserve Saturday. Houston visits next Sunday. Back to the tape.
