Tennessee Titans vs Cincinnati Bengals Week 8: So Close in OT 43-36

Final (OT): Tennessee Titans (1-7) 36, Cincinnati Bengals (5-2) 43, Sun., Nov. 1, Paycor Stadium, Cincinnati. Next: bye week, then Jacksonville Jaguars, Sun., Nov. 15, 1:00 PM ET, Nissan Stadium, Nashville

ShareXFacebook

Cincinnati Bengals 43, Tennessee Titans 36 in overtime. On Halloween night the Five Points corner of East Nashville filled up for All Hallows East, costumes and live music until 10. The next afternoon, the Titans walked into Paycor Stadium and gave Cincinnati a first quarter to remember for all the wrong reasons.

I grade this season on growth, and Sunday gave me a lot of it before it gave me the loss. Joey Slye hit from 46. Tony Pollard broke a 40-yard touchdown run. Amani Hooker read Joe Burrow deep for Ja'Marr Chase and intercepted it at our 9, and Slye added a 29-yarder. It was 13-0 after one quarter.

Hooker was not finished. He intercepted Burrow again on the opening drive of the third quarter and a third time at our 5 late in that quarter. That made him the first Titan with three interceptions in a game since Kevin Byard in 2016. Jacob Martin added three sacks, including a 15-yard loss on fourth-and-2 at our 5 before the half. Anthony Hill Jr. made 14 tackles.

Then there is Cam Ward. He went 26 of 40 for 304 yards, two touchdown throws and no interceptions, the first 300-yard game of his career. After a Boye Mafe strip-sack put us behind, Ward led an 80-yard answer, Pollard scored from the 1 and Ward ran in the two-point try himself for a 21-21 tie. When Alontae Taylor punched the ball away from Chase Brown, Ward hit Chimere Dike for an 11-yard score and a 28-23 lead. Down 29-28 late, he found Gunnar Helm for 25 on third down, then Wan'Dale Robinson for 15 and a touchdown, then Helm for two. 36-29 with 3:46 to go.

The honest part is the end. Burrow found Tee Higgins from 18 yards with 1:17 left. Cincinnati got the ball first in overtime and went 70 yards in five plays, Burrow running the last nine himself. Our answer died on fourth-and-3 at our 35 with a throw to Elic Ayomanor that fell incomplete. The defense gave up 510 yards, and a safety on Pollard in our own end zone cost two points we needed.

Pollard ran 21 times for 117 yards and two scores. Dike caught five for 71 and returned six kickoffs for 160. Tennessee is 1-7, which hurts. The kid looked like a franchise quarterback for four quarters, which counts.

The bye comes at the right time. Jacksonville visits Nissan Stadium on Nov. 15.
