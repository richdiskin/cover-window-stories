Detroit Lions vs Minnesota Vikings Week 8: Lions Hand Vikings First Loss, 19-15

Final: Minnesota Vikings (6-1) 15, Detroit Lions (5-2) 19, Sun., Nov. 1, Ford Field, Detroit. Next: at Miami Dolphins, Sun., Nov. 8, 1:00 PM ET, Hard Rock Stadium, Miami Gardens

XFacebook

Detroit Lions 19, Minnesota Vikings 15. On Sunday afternoon the Detroit Riverfront hosted its Día de los Muertos celebration at Robert C. Valade Park, with ballet folklórico, Mariachi Mexico de Detroit and a community ofrenda by the water. Up at Ford Field, Detroit Lions vs Minnesota Vikings turned into a four-quarter street fight, and the Vikings walked out with their first loss of the season.

It did not start clean. Amon-Ra St. Brown and Jameson Williams drew taunting flags on our first two drives, and a touchdown grab by St. Brown got overturned on review. Jake Bates bailed us out from 25 yards. Kyler Murray answered early in the second quarter with a 1-yard touchdown throw to Justin Jefferson, and Andrew Van Ginkel spent the half chasing Jared Goff around the pocket.

Then D.J. Reed dove in front of Jordan Addison for an interception late in the half, and Bates drilled a 37-yarder with two seconds left. Vikings 7, Lions 6 at the break, and our running game had barely cleared its throat.

The second half belonged to the backs. Jahmyr Gibbs ripped off a 30-yard touchdown run late in the third for a 12-7 lead. It was his 10th touchdown of the season, and only Gibbs and Barry Sanders have reached 10 in four straight seasons for this franchise. Murray fought back with an 8-yard touchdown to T.J. Hockenson and a two-point throw to Addison, and Minnesota led 15-12 with 8:44 to go.

So we ran it right at them. Gibbs for 17. Sione Vaki for 5, 12 and 10. Gibbs for 9, then 9 more. Goff found Williams in the back of the end zone from 7 yards with 4:35 left, the defense forced a three-and-out, and we ran out the clock.

We finished with 230 rushing yards, 164 of them after halftime. Gibbs had 153 on 23 carries and Vaki 65 on 11. Goff went 18 of 25 for 176 yards and his first interception of the season, and he has now thrown a touchdown in 29 straight games. The defense allowed 266 yards and 15 points, our fewest all season, and Minnesota went 3 of 10 on third down.

Nobody partied, though. Jack Campbell was carted off in the fourth quarter and taken to a hospital with a neck injury. Detroit is praying for him tonight.

Three straight wins, 5-2, unbeaten in the division, and Miami is next on Nov. 8. This window does not stay open forever. Today we kicked it wider.
