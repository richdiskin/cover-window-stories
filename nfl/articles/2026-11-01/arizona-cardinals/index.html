Arizona Cardinals vs Dallas Cowboys Week 8: Minshew Steals One 33-30

Final (OT): Arizona Cardinals (3-5) 33, Dallas Cowboys (3-5) 30, Sun., Nov. 1, AT&T Stadium, Arlington. Next: at Seattle Seahawks, Sun., Nov. 8, 4:25 PM ET, Lumen Field, Seattle

ShareXFacebook

Arizona Cardinals 33, Dallas Cowboys 30 in overtime. Two nights earlier, 1,200 drones lit up the sky over State Farm Stadium for the Harry Potter DroneArt Show. On Sunday in Arlington, the Cardinals pulled off a trick nobody in our building will forget, and for three quarters it looked like there would be no magic at all.

My measuring stick is still the 2008 team that reached the Super Bowl, and the first half came nowhere near it. Jeremiyah Love got stuffed on fourth-and-goal at the Dallas 1. Shemar James intercepted Jacoby Brissett and ran it back to our 5, and Dallas fell on its own fumble in the end zone for a touchdown. Dak Prescott hit Ryan Flournoy from 9 yards. Chad Ryland's 37-yarder made it 14-3 at the half.

Brissett was sacked four times and finished 10 of 23. Then Gardner Minshew came in, and the afternoon flipped. On his first drive, Tyler Allgeier went 63 yards up the middle for a touchdown. Prescott's scramble made it 24-10, but Allgeier ripped off 52 more and caught a 5-yard score from Minshew.

Dallas kicked its way to 30-24 with Brandon Aubrey field goals, and when a Rashan Gary roughing call kept a drive alive, Minshew found Trey McBride for a 14-yard touchdown. The onside kick failed. Down six with 65 seconds left, Minshew went 80 yards in eight plays, hitting Michael Wilson for 21 and 27, then for 13 and the tying touchdown with six seconds left. Ryland pushed the extra point wide left.

He got another shot. Both teams went three-and-out to start overtime, Josh Sweat sacking Prescott on third down. Wilson ran for 17, Allgeier pounded the middle, and Ryland drilled a 42-yarder to win it.

The numbers: Minshew 17 of 23 for 188 yards, three touchdown throws and no interceptions. Allgeier ran 11 times for 147 yards and caught five for 44. Wilson caught eight for 111, McBride seven for 72. We outgained Dallas 467-261 and ran for 202 yards.

The honest part: the first half was ugly, the injuries piled up until Allgeier was the only running back we had left, with Love and Bam Knight both out and receivers Kendrick Bourne and Reggie Virgil gone too, and this team is still 3-5. That 2008 bar is a long way off. Still, it is five straight wins over Dallas and 5-0 at this stadium. Seattle is next.
