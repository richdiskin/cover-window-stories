Washington Commanders vs Philadelphia Eagles Week 8: 27 Straight 27-10

Final: Washington Commanders (2-4-1) 27, Philadelphia Eagles (2-6) 10, Sun., Nov. 1, Northwest Stadium, Landover. Next: vs Los Angeles Rams, Sun., Nov. 8, 1:00 PM ET, Northwest Stadium, Landover

XFacebook

Washington Commanders 27, Philadelphia Eagles 10, and the whole DMV can dance tonight. The Washington Ballet opened its 50th season this weekend with Go-Go 50 at the Warner Theatre, ballet set to the heartbeat of D.C. On Sunday Night Football in Landover, our guys found the beat after a rough first quarter and never let Philadelphia score again.

That start was ugly. Drew Stevens missed from 55, Andrew Mukuba forced a Chig Okonkwo fumble that Zack Baun recovered, and Saquon Barkley took a direct snap in from 3. Philly led 10-0. Then Stevens buried a 56-yarder with 28 seconds left in the quarter, and the comeback had its first note.

Jayden Daniels went to work in the second quarter: 11 plays, 89 yards, Treylon Burks grabbing 21 along the way, and an 8-yard touchdown throw to Stefon Diggs to tie it 10-10. Daniels took a hard hit on the last drive of the half and got up slow, but he stayed in, Diggs grabbed a 20-yarder on that drive, and Stevens banked a 39-yarder for 13-10.

The defense did the rest. Dorance Armstrong stripped Tanner McKee late in the third, Charles Omenihu recovered, and Daniels dropped a 53-yard dime to Burks to set up Stevens from 25. Jake Elliott's 41-yard retry hit the left upright after a Philly illegal formation flag wiped out his make. Stevens hit from 42 for 19-10, Rasul Douglas intercepted McKee on fourth-and-25, and Rachaad White ripped off 33 yards before punching it in from 1. Austin Ekeler ran in the two-point try. Twenty-seven straight points.

Daniels went 16-of-29 for 217 yards and a touchdown with no interceptions, his first win as a starter since he beat the Chargers on Oct. 5, 2025. Burks caught three for 81, Antonio Williams five for 54, and White ran for 55. Stevens went 4-of-5 for 13 points. Five different guys sacked McKee: Armstrong, Omenihu, Shy Tuttle, K'Lavon Chaisson and Odafe Oweh. Nick Cross made nine tackles. We did it without Terry McLaurin, too. At 2-4-1 we're still in the NFC East hunt, and the Rams come to Northwest Stadium next Sunday. Hail to the Commanders.
