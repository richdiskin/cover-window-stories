Denver Broncos vs Kansas City Chiefs Week 8: Mile High Stand 28-24

Final: Kansas City Chiefs (5-2) 24, Denver Broncos (6-2) 28, Sun., Nov. 1, Empower Field at Mile High, Denver. Next: at Carolina Panthers, Sun., Nov. 8, 1:00 PM ET, Bank of America Stadium, Charlotte

XFacebook

Denver Broncos 28, Kansas City Chiefs 24, and we're sitting alone atop the AFC West. Across town, the Denver Coliseum spent Sunday on its Día de Muertos Festival, a community altar, folkloric dancing and music from 11 in the morning until 8 at night. At Empower Field at Mile High, 76,831 of us watched this team claw back from a grave of its own.

Wil Lutz opened with field goals from 39 and 45, but Patrick Mahomes hit Rashee Rice for a score and then rookie Emmett Johnson for 73 yards from their own 5. The second quarter's best moment was ours, though. Bo Nix and the offense got stuffed at the Kansas City 1 on three straight snaps, and on fourth down Nix found Jaylen Waddle for the touchdown. Harrison Butker's 45-yarder after a 38-yard Johnson catch made it 17-13 at the half.

The third quarter tested every one of us. Three straight three-and-outs, boos raining down, and Noah Gray's 4-yard touchdown catch made it 24-13. Mile High doesn't quit at 5,280 feet, and neither did Nix.

Nix took us 80 yards in nine plays, Marvin Mims Jr. ripped off a 14-yard run to the 4, and Nate Adkins caught the touchdown before Nix hit Waddle for two. Singleton sacked Mahomes on the next drive, Butker missed from 54, and Nix fired a strike to Jonah Coleman for 51 yards. Coleman punched it in from the 5 with 1:56 left. Then the stand. Kansas City reached our 1 with 35 seconds left. JL Skinner stuffed Kenneth Walker III, the Chiefs got flagged for a false start, and Malcolm Roach sacked Mahomes as time ran out.

Nix finished 20-of-32 for 222 yards, two touchdowns and no turnovers. Coleman ran 11 times for 41 and caught four for 63, and Courtland Sutton grabbed four for 53. Alex Singleton made 14 tackles, Pat Surtain II had eight and Zach Allen hit Mahomes three times. We went 3-for-3 in the red zone. Sean Payton is now unbeaten at home against Kansas City, and Carolina is next. Orange crush forever.
