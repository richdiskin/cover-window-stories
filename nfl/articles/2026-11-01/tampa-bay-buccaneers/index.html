Tampa Bay Buccaneers vs Atlanta Falcons Week 8: Mud Run 16-0

Final: Atlanta Falcons (3-5) 0, Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-5) 16, Sun., Nov. 1, Raymond James Stadium, Tampa. Next: at Chicago Bears, Sun., Nov. 8, 8:20 PM ET, Soldier Field, Chicago

XFacebook

Tampa Bay Buccaneers 16, Atlanta Falcons 0, and we own this one from the first handoff to the last. Busch Gardens closed out Howl-O-Scream on Saturday night, but the real scare in this town came Sunday afternoon in driving rain at Raymond James Stadium. It belonged to Atlanta. We're 3-5, and we've won three of four since that 0-4 hole.

The Falcons blinked first when Nick Folk pushed a 53-yarder wide left on their opening drive. Chase McLaughlin made them pay with field goals of 46 and 34 yards in the first quarter and 42 in the second, and he's now 19 for 19 this season. We should have had more. Alex Anzalone intercepted Michael Penix Jr., a roughness flag set us up at the Atlanta 18, and then Bucky Irving lost the ball at the 1 out of the end zone. That one's on us, and we own it.

Then the ground game took the deed to the place. Baker Mayfield, in his second game back from a fractured thumb, handed off 52 times. We ran it 56 times for 324 yards, a franchise record, topping the 283 we put up at Philadelphia in 2015. Irving carried 27 times for 170 yards and scored on a 4-yard run late in the third quarter for the 16-0 cushion. Kenny Gainwell added 140 on 25 carries, then ran it seven straight times at the end to close the door. Mayfield went 0-for-3 through the air. No team had gone a full game without a completion since Buffalo in 1974.

The defense owned the rest. Penix went 8-of-24 with four interceptions: two from Anzalone, one from Zyon McCollum and one from Jacob Parrish on fourth-and-6 at our 32. Parrish and Antoine Winfield Jr. each made seven tackles. Atlanta went 0-for-12 on third down. Jahan Dotson got loose for 76 yards in the fourth quarter, and we held at the 14 anyway on fourth-and-2.

Riley Dixon punted six times for a 44.8 average and kept the field tilted all day. The one gut punch was Yaya Diaby leaving with a knee injury in the first quarter. We sit a half-game behind Carolina in the NFC South, and next comes Chicago at Soldier Field on Sunday night. Bring the rain gear again.
