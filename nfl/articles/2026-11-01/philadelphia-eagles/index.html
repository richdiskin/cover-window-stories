Philadelphia Eagles vs Washington Commanders Week 8: Skid Hits Six 10-27

Final: Washington Commanders (2-4-1) 27, Philadelphia Eagles (2-6) 10, Sun., Nov. 1, Northwest Stadium, Landover. Next: vs New York Giants, Sun., Nov. 8, 1:00 PM ET, Lincoln Financial Field, Philadelphia

ShareXFacebook

Washington Commanders 27, Philadelphia Eagles 10, and I need a minute. On Saturday, folks back home climbed aboard the Rocky Halloween Tour in full costume, because no costume meant no tour. On Sunday Night Football in Landover, our Birds came out swinging like Rocky in round one, went up 10-0, then took 27 straight to the chin.

That first quarter was pure Philly. Jake Elliott hit from 40. Andrew Mukuba punched the ball out of Chig Okonkwo's hands, Zack Baun fell on it at their 26, and Tanner McKee dropped one in to Saquon Barkley for 24 yards down to the 2. Barkley took the direct snap and walked in from 3. Ten-nothing, Birds.

Then Washington found its feet. Drew Stevens drilled a 56-yarder, Jayden Daniels capped an 11-play, 89-yard drive with an 8-yard touchdown throw to Stefon Diggs, and Stevens hit from 39 with 19 seconds left in the half. Late in the third, Dorance Armstrong stripped McKee, Charles Omenihu recovered, Treylon Burks hauled in a 53-yarder, and Stevens made it 16-10.

Here's the one that hurts. McKee led a 15-play, 57-yard march that chewed up 7:35. Elliott made a 36-yarder, but Jordan Mailata was flagged for illegal formation, and the retry from 41 clanged off the left upright. Stevens answered from 42 for 19-10. On fourth-and-25, Rasul Douglas intercepted McKee, Rachaad White ripped off 33 yards and then scored from 1, and Austin Ekeler ran in the two-point try.

McKee, in his third start for the injured Jalen Hurts, went 23-of-34 for 212 yards and an interception and got sacked five times. Barkley had 30 yards on 13 carries. Makai Lemon caught six for 57 and Eli Stowers three for 53. Jeremiah Trotter Jr. made 10 tackles, Mukuba nine, and Jalen Carter and Nolan Smith Jr. each got a sack. Jaydon Blue returned six kicks for 144 yards. That's six straight losses and 2-6, a start this franchise hasn't seen since 1999. The Giants come to the Linc next Sunday. Rocky got knocked down too. Get up.
