New England Patriots vs Miami Dolphins Week 8: Rain Check 24-19

Final: New England Patriots (5-3) 24, Miami Dolphins (1-6) 19, Sun., Nov. 1, Hard Rock Stadium, Miami Gardens. Next: vs Green Bay Packers, Sun., Nov. 8, 4:25 PM ET, Gillette Stadium, Foxborough

XFacebook

New England Patriots 24, Miami Dolphins 19, and I'll take a soaked jersey every week if it ends like this. Salem closed out Haunted Happenings on Halloween night with a drone show over the North River. On Sunday our guys flew south into a downpour at Hard Rock Stadium and spent a half looking like they'd stayed up for it.

The first half was ugly. Andy Borregales opened with a 34-yard field goal, but Jacob Rodriguez punched the ball loose from Efton Chism and Miami turned it into three. Zane Gonzalez banked a 52-yarder off the left upright, Borregales missed from 40, and Malik Willis drove Miami 70 yards in 11 plays for Malik Washington's 1-yard touchdown catch with eight seconds left. We trailed 13-3 and got sacked twice by Chop Robinson along the way.

Then Drake Maye found his feet in the wet. Mike Vrabel threw the red flag on a ruled incompletion, and the replay gave Kyle Williams a 43-yard touchdown. Next came a 13-play, 73-yard grind, kept alive when replay upheld DeMario Douglas on fourth down, and Maye faked the pitch and ran it in from 2 for a 17-13 lead. Maye completed his first eight throws of the half.

Kevin Byard sealed the swing early in the fourth. Willis's throw caromed off DaQuan Jones, and Byard grabbed his first interception as a Patriot. One play later, Maye hit Douglas deep for a 48-yard touchdown and 24-13. Gonzalez missed from 43, rookie Kevin Coleman Jr. scored from 35 with 2:04 left, the two-point try failed, and Rhamondre Stevenson pulled in the onside kick. Kneel, kneel, ballgame.

Maye went 16-of-20 for 199 yards and two touchdowns, ran for another, and has accounted for at least three touchdowns in five straight games. Stevenson ran 19 times for 82, and Douglas caught three for 76. The defense sacked Willis five times, with Cory Durden and Gabe Jacas getting a sack and a half each, and Christian Elliss made nine tackles. We were down 13-3 and won without A.J. Brown and TreVeyon Henderson. Green Bay is next at Gillette. Six banners hang in Foxborough for a reason.
