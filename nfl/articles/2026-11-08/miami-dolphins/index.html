Miami Dolphins vs Detroit Lions Week 9: Red Zone Blues 19-9

Final: Detroit Lions (6-2) 19, Miami Dolphins (1-7) 9, Sun., Nov. 8, Hard Rock Stadium, Miami Gardens. Next: at Indianapolis Colts, Sun., Nov. 15, 1:00 PM ET, Lucas Oil Stadium, Indianapolis

XFacebook

Detroit Lions 19, Miami Dolphins 9, on a Sunday when the biggest finish in South Florida happened down at Homestead. Ryan Blaney won the NASCAR Cup Series Championship Race at Homestead-Miami Speedway with a move on the next-to-last lap, and Kyle Larson clinched his third Cup title. Up at Hard Rock Stadium, the Dolphins never found a late move of their own.

The day opened with a heartbreak that didn't count. On Detroit's first drive, Jordyn Brooks scooped up a Jared Goff fumble and ran 72 yards for a touchdown, and replay turned it into an incomplete throw. Josh Uche got home anyway, with a sack and a tip that rookie Chris Johnson intercepted. Zane Gonzalez's 34-yarder made it 3-3.

Jeff Hafley's defense did its job. Goff was sacked three times, by Uche, Jacob Rodriguez and Zeek Biggers, and Detroit finished with 270 yards. Brooks made 12 tackles. Gonzalez hit from 52 in the third quarter to make it 9-6, and for a while this felt like another one-score fight.

Then came the fake punt. Bradley Pinion threw 46 yards to Kaytron Allen early in the fourth to put us at the Detroit 18, the best call of the afternoon. Two sacks later, we settled for a 34-yard Gonzalez field goal. That was the whole day in one drive: zero for three in the red zone and no touchdowns.

Malik Willis went 13 of 25 for 128 yards with an interception, a Chuck Clark return that set up a Detroit field goal, and he was sacked three times. Ollie Gordon II carried 16 times for 55 yards. Caleb Douglas caught six balls for 70. We were flagged 11 times, and Gonzalez also clanked a 38-yarder off the right upright at the end of the first half.

The sore spot: Jahmyr Gibbs went in from the 8 on fourth down late in the third, and that was the only touchdown anybody scored. We had come into this game with four straight one-score finishes, and a blueprint gets judged by whether it finally wins one of those. Not yet. We're 1-7 and still winless at home.

Next Sunday we go to Indianapolis. Blaney found his move with a lap to go. We're still looking for ours.
