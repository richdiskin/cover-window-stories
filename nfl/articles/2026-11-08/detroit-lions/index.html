Detroit Lions vs Miami Dolphins Week 9: Bates Kicks, Lions Grind 19-9

Final: Detroit Lions (6-2) 19, Miami Dolphins (1-7) 9, Sun., Nov. 8, Hard Rock Stadium, Miami Gardens. Next: vs New England Patriots, Sun., Nov. 15, 9:30 AM ET, FC Bayern Munich Stadium, Munich

XFacebook

Detroit Lions 19, Miami Dolphins 9, and this one was for No. 46. Back home on Saturday, Arboretum Detroit handed out free trees to city residents who pitched in to plant and care for its nursery. On Sunday at Hard Rock Stadium, the Lions did the same kind of work: no fireworks, just dig, plant and repeat until Miami ran out of daylight.

The whole week was about Jack Campbell, who went on injured reserve after the neck injury he suffered against Minnesota. Our guys walked into the stadium wearing his No. 46, his jersey hung in his locker, and fans held up Campbell Strong signs. You could see it in how this team tackled.

The offense was not pretty. On our first drive, Jordyn Brooks returned a Jared Goff fumble 72 yards for a touchdown before replay turned it into an incomplete throw. Chris Johnson intercepted a tipped ball later in the first quarter, and Goff was sacked three times. But Jake Bates kept planting kicks: 28 yards, 40, 33, and finally a 43-yarder with 4:28 left for a 19-9 lead.

The one touchdown was worth the wait. Up 9-6 late in the third, Goff hit Jameson Williams for 31 yards, our longest play of the day, and Jahmyr Gibbs took it in from the 8 on fourth down for 16-6. That was his 11th scrimmage touchdown of the season. Earlier, Jack Fox faked a punt and threw 11 yards to Tay Martin, and we went four for four on fourth down.

The defense won it. Chuck Clark intercepted Malik Willis on Miami's first drive of the second half and returned it 34 yards, setting up a Bates kick. Derrick Moore had two sacks, Alim McNeill added one, and Miami went zero for three in the red zone. Willis finished 13 of 25 for 128 yards.

The film session: 270 yards for an offense that came in averaging 395.4 a game is not who we are. Gibbs needed 20 carries to get 74 yards. Amon-Ra St. Brown caught eight balls for 86, and Goff went 17 of 25 for 170 with no touchdown throws. We also gave up a 46-yard fake punt in the fourth that led to Miami's last field goal.

Still, we're 6-2 with four straight wins, and Dan Campbell now has 54 regular-season wins, second in franchise history. Next Sunday it's New England in Munich. Somebody pack Jack's jersey.
