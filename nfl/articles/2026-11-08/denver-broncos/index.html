Denver Broncos vs Carolina Panthers Week 9: Bonitto Seals It 26-21

Final: Denver Broncos (7-2) 26, Carolina Panthers (3-5) 21, Sun., Nov. 8, Bank of America Stadium, Charlotte. Next: Raiders, Sun., Nov. 22, 4:25 PM ET, Empower Field at Mile High, Denver

XFacebook

Denver Broncos 26, Carolina Panthers 21, and that is five straight wins. Saturday was free day and night at the museums for Denver Arts Week, and the Denver Museum of Nature & Science has Mythic Creatures: Dragons, Unicorns & Mermaids on display. Somebody should add a case for this team. We have now won 16 straight one-score games, and on Sunday in Charlotte we found a way again.

Bo Nix went right at a Carolina secondary playing without its top four cornerbacks, and the flags piled up. Courtland Sutton caught a 3-yard touchdown, and Wil Lutz kicked from 26 and 48 for a 13-7 halftime lead. Nix finished 25 of 37 for 237 yards and two touchdowns.

The game turned on a flag of our own. Carolina was up 14-13 after Darren Waller's 15-yard catch and had first-and-goal at our 1 late in the third. Bryce Young threw to the corner, it was ruled incomplete, and the Panthers ran another play. Then the officials ruled that Sean Payton had thrown his red challenge flag before that snap. On review, Devon Key had caught the ball off Tommy Tremble's hand. Interception, our ball at the 20.

From there we went 80 yards in 14 plays, and Jonah Coleman took it in from 9 to make it 19-14 with 10:17 left. The two-point throw fell incomplete. Next time out, Nix found Pat Bryant wide open down the seam for a 26-yard touchdown and a 26-14 lead.

We nearly handed it back. Young converted a fourth-and-18 with a 25-yard throw to John Metchie III and hit Tetairoa McMillan for a 4-yard score with 3:05 left. Then a Nix pitch got away from Coleman for an 18-yard loss and a punt. Enter Nik Bonitto. With 1:24 left he came around the corner, stripped Young and fell on the ball himself. Nix got the last first down on a third-down sneak.

The to-do list: Carolina outgained us by 41 yards, Nix threw an interception at the Carolina 5, and we ran 27 times for 82 yards. Ja'Quan McMillian had a sack and two tackles for loss, and Jahdae Barron made eight tackles. This team keeps winning close, and some week the close ones will turn. Not this week.

We are 7-2 and alone atop the AFC West. Bye week, then the Raiders at Mile High on Nov. 22. The museum can keep its dragons. We have Bonitto.
