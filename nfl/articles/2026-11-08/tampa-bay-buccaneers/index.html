Tampa Bay Buccaneers vs Chicago Bears Week 9: Late Rally Wasted 27-24

Final: Chicago Bears (7-2) 27, Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-6) 24, Sun., Nov. 8, Soldier Field, Chicago. Next (after a bye): at Detroit Lions, Sun., Nov. 22, 1:00 PM ET, Ford Field, Detroit

XFacebook

Chicago beat us 27-24. Back home, the 67th annual Gem, Mineral & Jewelry Show filled the Florida State Fairgrounds all weekend. At Soldier Field, our defense dug up four interceptions off Caleb Williams, real gems, and we still flew home with empty pockets.

I sat through the orange-uniform years, so two rings mean I've earned the right to be honest. We were down 14-0 before our offense got going. Williams hit Rome Odunze from 9, then Luther Burden III took a short throw 56 yards. Then Kenny Gainwell caught a 44-yarder, Cade Otton converted a fourth-and-2, and a delay of game on fourth-and-goal pushed us back before Chase McLaughlin kicked a 27-yard field goal.

Zyon McCollum was the reason we stayed alive. Tykee Smith intercepted Williams at our 3. After we got stuffed on fourth-and-1, McCollum intercepted Williams on the very next snap, and he got him again just before the half after a replay reversal. Emeka Egbuka caught a 21-yarder and McLaughlin kicked from 21 as time ran out. 17-6.

Baker Mayfield came out of the half swinging. Tez Johnson caught a 58-yarder to the 2, and Bucky Irving punched it in. Alex Anzalone then intercepted Williams, and two plays later Jaylon Johnson intercepted Mayfield and went 67 yards the other way. Instead of a chance to lead, we trailed 24-13.

Still, nobody in this group quits. McLaughlin hit from 35, and Rueben Bain Jr. knocked the ball loose from Burden with 3:48 left. Ifeatu Melifonwu recovered it at the Chicago 21. Irving ran in from 6, Mayfield powered in the two-point try himself, and it was 24-24 with 2:51 to go. Then Williams found Colston Loveland for 38 yards, and Cairo Santos hit a 38-yarder as time expired.

Irving ran 20 times for 97 yards and two scores. McLaughlin went 3 for 3. What sank us: Mayfield went 18 of 32 for 243 yards with two interceptions and four sacks, we went 2 of 12 on third down, and the pre-snap flags piled up. Five takeaways should win any game.

We're 3-6. After the bye, it's Detroit.
