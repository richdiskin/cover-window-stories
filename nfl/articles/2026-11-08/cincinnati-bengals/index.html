Cincinnati Bengals vs Atlanta Falcons Week 9: Matador Day 34-17

Final: Cincinnati Bengals (6-2) 34, Atlanta Falcons (3-6) 17, Sun., Nov. 8, Santiago Bernabéu, Madrid. Next: vs Pittsburgh Steelers, Sun., Nov. 15, 8:20 PM ET, Paycor Stadium, Cincinnati

XFacebook

Cincinnati Bengals 34, Atlanta Falcons 17, and Madrid got the full Who Dey show. All weekend a Bengals-themed Menina stood in Plaza de España, one of those public sculptures modeled on the girls in Velázquez's 1656 painting, dressed in our colors for the NFL Experience. On Sunday at the Santiago Bernabéu, Joe Burrow and Ja'Marr Chase painted the real masterpiece.

It did not open pretty. Bijan Robinson ran for 42 yards on Atlanta's first three carries, and Michael Penix Jr. finished an 80-yard drive with a 1-yard touchdown throw to Charlie Woerner. Our answer took five plays. Burrow lofted a deep ball to Chase for 47 yards and a 7-7 game, and Chase and Tee Higgins acted out a bullfight in the end zone, Chase swinging the invisible cape, Higgins charging with his hands up for horns.

After a Nick Folk field goal put Atlanta up 10-7, we scored on every remaining trip before halftime. Burrow ran it in from the 1 for 14-10. Higgins caught a 4-yarder to finish an 88-yard march for 21-10. With 57 seconds left, Burrow found Dohnte Meyers for 42 yards, and Evan McPherson drilled a 57-yard field goal with 24 seconds to go. It was 24-10 at the half, and Chase already had eight catches for 142 yards.

Robinson punched one in from the 1 in the third quarter to make it 24-17, and our guys didn't blink. Seven plays, 73 yards, and Burrow hit Higgins from 8 for 31-17. Folk pushed a 38-yarder wide left early in the fourth, McPherson hit from 43, and Barrett Carter intercepted Penix to slam the door.

Burrow went 24 of 29 for 271 yards with three touchdown throws, no interceptions and that rushing score. Chase finished with 10 catches for 155 yards. Higgins scored twice. We converted six of 10 third downs, never gave the ball away, and McPherson went 2 for 2 with four extra points.

The homework: Atlanta ran for 157 yards at 6.5 a carry, Robinson topped 100, and Penix scrambled for 50 more. That run defense has to get fixed before December. And when the stadium lights went dark on one of our snaps inside the final two minutes, the down had to be replayed, and that was the only snap all day we needed twice.

This is four straight wins, and 30 or more points in four straight games for the first time since 1997, tying a franchise record. It is also the first international win in team history, after a tie and a loss in London. We're 6-2. Pittsburgh comes to Paycor Stadium next Sunday night. Bring the stripes.
