Carolina Panthers vs Denver Broncos Week 9: Flagged at the 1 26-21

Final: Denver Broncos (7-2) 26, Carolina Panthers (3-5) 21, Sun., Nov. 8, Bank of America Stadium, Charlotte. Next: at Saints, Sun., Nov. 15, 1:00 PM ET, Caesars Superdome

XFacebook

Denver Broncos 26, Carolina Panthers 21. Up in Huntersville this weekend, the Carolina Renaissance Festival had knights riding in Tournament Jousting, where everybody knows the rules before the horses charge. At Bank of America Stadium on Sunday, we lost a game on a rule most of us had never seen used.

Bryce Young was good. He went 28 of 39 for 276 yards with three touchdown throws: 10 yards to Tommy Tremble before the half, 15 to Darren Waller for a 14-13 lead early in the third, and 4 to Tetairoa McMillan late. Waller caught eight for 90. Chuba Hubbard ran 16 times for 79 yards, including a 23-yard burst on fourth-and-1 that set up the first score.

Then came the third quarter. We drove 87 yards to first-and-goal at the Denver 1. Young threw to the corner for Tremble, and it was ruled incomplete. We ran the next play. Then the officials stopped everybody and said Sean Payton had thrown his red flag before that snap. On review, Devon Key caught the ball after it came off Tremble's hand. Interception. Instead of going up eight, we watched Denver drive 80 yards the other way for a Jonah Coleman touchdown.

Pat Bryant's 26-yard score made it 26-14. Young answered with a 25-yard strike to John Metchie III on fourth-and-18, then found McMillan with 3:05 left. The defense got the ball back when a Bo Nix pitch got away for an 18-yard loss. At the Denver 36 with 1:24 left, Nik Bonitto came around the corner, stripped Young and recovered it himself. Denver ran out the clock.

What beat us: we committed 13 penalties for 119 yards, Ryan Fitzgerald missed a field goal in the second quarter, and a secondary playing without its top four cornerbacks drew interference flags all afternoon. Corey Thornton, one of the corners called up this week, still intercepted Nix at our 5, and Patrick Jones II had a sack. We were 3 of 3 on fourth down and 3 of 10 on third.

Three straight losses, 3-5, and somehow still tied for first in the NFC South. Next is a trip to the Superdome. Keep Pounding.
