Buffalo Bills vs New York Jets Week 10: Cook Goes 74 and the Bills Hit 8-1, 31-12

Final: Buffalo Bills (8-1) 31, New York Jets (2-8) 12, Sun., Nov. 15, MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford. Next: vs Miami Dolphins, Sun., Nov. 22, 1:00 PM ET, Highmark Stadium, Orchard Park

XFacebook

Bills Mafia, we are 8-1! Buffalo 31, New York 12 on Sunday at MetLife Stadium, and it's our best start since 1991. Thirty-five years, people, and I'm jumping around my living room like it's the Rockpile. And from what I saw, at least half that stadium was wearing our colors. Road games, home crowd.

It wasn't pretty early. We led just 10-6 at halftime, and the Jets kept kicking field goals. But Josh Allen did what Josh Allen does. He punched in two 1-yard touchdowns, one in the first quarter and one in the third, and by the end of the day he had moved ahead of Peyton Manning for the most touchdowns by a player through his first 10 seasons. He's in year nine. Let that sink in.

Then Allen found DJ Moore for a 45-yard touchdown late in the third to make it 24-12. Moore caught four balls for 90 yards. Dalton Kincaid had a 54-yard catch.

And James Cook III? He ran 18 times for 157 yards and iced it with a 74-yard touchdown run with 4:42 left. 31-12. Gone. Nobody touched him. Khalil Shakir added 29 rushing yards on three carries.

The defense was nasty when it counted. TJ Parker had two sacks and three hits on the quarterback. Dorian Williams had nine tackles and a sack. Davison Igbinosun intercepted a deep throw near midfield in the third quarter. Kaleb Elarms-Orr recovered a fumble at our 21 with less than five minutes left, and Cook scored on the very next snap. Then we stopped them on fourth down with 1:54 left. Tommy Doman Jr. put all three of his punts inside the 20.

Joe Brady is 5-0 on the road in his first season, which only two other coaches in NFL history have done. That's five straight road wins, a bounce-back from a short week after our Monday night win at Minnesota, and an 8-1 record. Not bad at all.

Next up: the Bills come home to Highmark Stadium to host the Miami Dolphins, Sunday, Nov. 22, 1 PM ET. Go Bills!
