Los Angeles Rams vs Arizona Cardinals Week 10: 31 Straight Points in the Desert, 34-6

Final: Los Angeles Rams (6-4) 34, Arizona Cardinals (3-7) 6, Sun., Nov. 15, State Farm Stadium, Glendale. Next: vs Green Bay Packers, Wed., Nov. 25, 8:00 PM ET, SoFi Stadium, Inglewood

XFacebook

Whose house? Rams house, even in Glendale! Los Angeles 34, Arizona 6 on Sunday at State Farm Stadium, and that's four straight wins. We're 6-4, and this team keeps finding new heroes every single week.

I'll admit the first quarter had me nervous. Arizona kicked two 49-yard field goals and led 6-3 late into the second quarter. Matthew Stafford even got intercepted early in the second. Then the switch flipped. Stafford found Tyler Higbee in the back of the end zone from 5 yards with 1:06 left in the half. On Arizona's next drive, a throw bounced off a receiver's hands and Kamren Kinchens grabbed it and ran 33 yards to their 32. Three plays later, Xavier Smith caught a short throw, broke a few tackles and went 22 yards for his first NFL touchdown. 17-6 at the half.

We never looked back. Blake Corum powered in from a yard out in the third quarter. Wesley Bailey intercepted Carson Beck on the next drive, and Stafford hit Davis Allen for a 19-yard touchdown. 31-6. Brandon McManus added a 43-yarder on the first play of the fourth. That's 31 straight points to close it out.

Stafford finished 18 of 26 for 249 yards and three touchdowns, with a backup center snapping him the ball most of the day. Corum ran 17 times for 100 yards. Ronnie Rivers added 81 rushing yards and 62 on kickoff returns. Konata Mumpfield caught three balls for 81. We ran it 46 times for 202 yards and held the ball for 42:23. That's bully ball.

The defense was suffocating. Arizona had 123 yards and six first downs, and went 0 for 6 on third down. We sacked Beck five times. Byron Young had a sack and two tackles for loss, Braden Fiske had a sack, and Shaun Dolac had six tackles and half a sack. Ethan Evans punted exactly once all day.

Next up: the Rams come home to SoFi Stadium to host the Green Bay Packers, Wednesday, Nov. 25, 8 PM ET. Let's make it five, Rams House.
