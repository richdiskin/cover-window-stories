Detroit Lions vs New England Patriots Week 10: Amon-Ra Owns Munich, 35-10

Final: Detroit Lions (7-2) 35, New England Patriots (5-5) 10, Sun., Nov. 15, FC Bayern Munich Stadium, Munich. Next: vs Tampa Bay Buccaneers, Sun., Nov. 22, 1:00 PM ET, Ford Field, Detroit

XFacebook

Prost, Detroit! Lions 35, Patriots 10 on Sunday morning at FC Bayern Munich Stadium, and we are 7-2 with five straight wins. 70,225 people in Germany got a full dose of Lions football, and I watched it at 9:30 in the morning with a coffee in one hand and a Honolulu Blue scarf in the other.

This was Amon-Ra St. Brown's day. The man whose mother is from Leverkusen showed up in lederhosen, ran onto the field carrying a German flag, and then caught a 56-yard touchdown from Jared Goff in the first quarter. In the third, his teammates shoved him across the goal for a 28-yarder in a pile of Patriots, and it was 28-3. Four catches, 98 yards, two scores. Homecoming, Detroit style.

And Jahmyr Gibbs? Ridiculous. He ran 22 times for 153 yards and two touchdowns and caught seven balls for 99 more. He scored from 5 yards one play after a 50-yard catch-and-run down the right side, and he put it away with a 28-yard touchdown run in the fourth. 35-10. The team says he's the first player since Priest Holmes in 2002 with that kind of rushing, receiving and scoring day.

Goff finished 20 of 31 for 303 yards and three touchdowns, including a 12-yarder to Isaac TeSlaa that capped a 97-yard march. He did throw two interceptions, one in the end zone right before halftime, so we'll clean that up in Allen Park.

The defense smothered them. New England finished with 151 yards. DJ Wonnum had a sack and two tackles for loss. Roger McCreary intercepted a deep throw and broke up two more. Derrick Barnes made seven tackles. Aidan Hutchinson got the sack on their opening drive, and he got hurt on the play, so we're all praying for him tonight.

Jack Fox averaged 44 yards a punt, and Jake Bates was 5 for 5 on extra points. Five straight wins. This team is rolling.

Next up: the Lions are back at Ford Field to host the Tampa Bay Buccaneers, Sunday, Nov. 22, 1 PM ET. Danke, Munich. One Pride!
