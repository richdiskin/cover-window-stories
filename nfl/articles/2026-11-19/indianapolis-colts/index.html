Indianapolis Colts vs Houston Texans Week 11: Three Points, Three Kicks, One Long Night, 3-26

Final: Houston Texans (4-6) 26, Indianapolis Colts (3-8) 3, Thu., Nov. 19, Reliant Stadium, Houston. Next: vs New York Giants, Sun., Nov. 29, 1:00 PM ET, Lucas Oil Stadium, Indianapolis

XFacebook

Texans 26, Colts 3. I'd love to tell you it was closer than that, but I don't do fairy tales on Thursdays. The Indianapolis Colts are 3-8 after a night in Houston where the only stretch that worked for us was the first 10 minutes.

And those first 10 minutes were great! Daniel Jones moved us 44 yards on the opening drive, and Spencer Shrader knocked through a 34-yard field goal for a 3-0 lead. Then A.J. Haulcy intercepted C.J. Stroud on Houston's very first possession. I was out of my seat, already mapping a parade route down Meridian Street.

Then Shrader's 56-yard try drifted wide left, and Stroud hit Nico Collins for 42 yards and a touchdown three plays later. Kamari Lassiter intercepted Jones on the first play of the second quarter, Houston went 85 yards for a 14-3 lead, and Shrader's 62-yarder got blocked. 17-3 at the half. Three field goal tries, one make, and that was our whole scoring night.

The third quarter didn't help. Will Anderson Jr. sacked Jones in our own end zone for a safety, and a 13-yard Marlin Klein touchdown made it 26-3.

Let's be fair to our guys. We were missing both of our regular tackles, Bernhard Raimann and Jalen Travis, and both of our outside corners, Sauce Gardner and Charvarius Ward Sr. Jonathan Taylor had nowhere to run, finishing with 21 yards on 11 carries. Jones went 12 of 27 for 144 yards. We finished with 167 yards of offense and 38 rushing yards, both season lows, and it's the first time we failed to reach the end zone since a 24-6 loss to Detroit in November 2024.

Bright spots, because I'm a Colts fan and I always find them: CJ Allen made 16 tackles and played like a man possessed. Rigoberto Sanchez boomed three punts for a 49.0 average. Anthony Gould caught two balls for 48 yards, and Seth McGowan ran back three kickoffs for 117 yards before a hamstring injury in the second quarter.

Ten days to regroup. Next up: the New York Giants come to Lucas Oil Stadium on Sunday, Nov. 29, at 1:00 PM ET. Pack the place, Colts fans. Go Colts!
