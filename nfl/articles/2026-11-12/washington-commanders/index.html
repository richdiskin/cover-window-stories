Washington Commanders vs New York Giants Week 10: Diggs With 57 Seconds Left, 24-21

Final: Washington Commanders (3-5-1) 24, New York Giants (5-3-1) 21, Thu., Nov. 12, MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford. Next: vs Cincinnati Bengals, Mon., Nov. 23, 8:15 PM ET, Northwest Stadium, Landover

XFacebook

Hail to the Commanders, and somebody please check on my voice. Washington 24, New York 21 on Thursday night at MetLife Stadium, and we took this one in front of 80,450 people who wanted any other ending. We're 3-5-1, and that last minute felt like a whole season.

It was a slugfest for a half. Drew Stevens opened the scoring from 46 yards. The Giants answered with a 16-play march that ate 7:48 and ended with a Cam Skattebo plunge, but Jayden Daniels took us 67 yards in nine plays and flipped it to Ben Sinnott from a yard out with five seconds left in the half. 10-7, us.

Skattebo scored again from 13 early in the third to put them ahead 14-10. Daniels answered on third-and-1, hitting Antonio Williams for a 31-yard touchdown. 17-14. Then Frankie Luvu punched the ball loose from Malik Nabers inside our 20, and Jeremy Reaves fell on it. Reaves had nine tackles and was everywhere all night.

The fourth quarter tried to break my heart. We came up empty on fourth-and-1 at their 30. Isaiah Likely took a catch 66 yards to give New York a 21-17 lead with 8:13 left. Daniels got intercepted twice, once at their 2 by Jevon Holland and once on fourth down by Ar'Darius Washington. I sat down. I stood up. I sat down again.

Then their punt from our 41 went 12 yards and out of bounds, and we had it at our 29 with 1:46 to go. A 33-yard interference flag got us moving, and on third-and-4 Daniels found Stefon Diggs down the middle for a 32-yard touchdown with 57 seconds left. Three plays, 71 yards, 49 seconds. Rasul Douglas sealed it by intercepting J.J. McCarthy at our 11 with 34 seconds left.

Daniels finished 23 of 33 for 249 yards and three touchdowns. Diggs caught six for 83. Sonny Styles made 13 tackles, Odafe Oweh had a sack, and Tress Way averaged 49.5 yards on four punts. That tie with these same Giants back in October at Northwest Stadium? Consider it settled.

Next up: the Cincinnati Bengals at Northwest Stadium on Monday, Nov. 23, 8:15 PM ET. Rest up, fellas. HTTR.
