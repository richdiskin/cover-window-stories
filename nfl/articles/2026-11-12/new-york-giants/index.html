New York Giants vs Washington Commanders Week 10: 57 Seconds From a Win, 21-24

Final: Washington Commanders (3-5-1) 24, New York Giants (5-3-1) 21, Thu., Nov. 12, MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford. Next: vs Jacksonville Jaguars, Sun., Nov. 22, 4:25 PM ET, MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford

XFacebook

Fifty-seven seconds. That's how close we were. Washington 24, New York 21 on Thursday night at MetLife Stadium, and 80,450 of us walked out to the parking lots stunned. We're 5-3-1 and still sitting first in the NFC East, but this one is going to sting all weekend.

Cam Skattebo ran like a man who'd been insulted. He capped a 16-play, 72-yard drive with a 1-yard plunge in the second quarter and bounced in from 13 in the third for a 14-10 lead. Two touchdowns and 78 yards on 18 carries, and every one of them hurt somebody.

Isaiah Likely was the best player on the field. Nine catches, 127 yards, and on third-and-7 from our 34 he ran right through the middle of their defense for a 66-yard touchdown. 21-17 Giants with 8:13 left. J.J. McCarthy finished 23 of 36 for 293 yards.

And the secondary went to work. Jevon Holland intercepted Jayden Daniels at our 2, and Ar'Darius Washington took another one away on fourth down at their 46 with 2:19 to go. Arvell Reese made 13 tackles, Micah McFadden had 10, and Shelby Harris sacked Daniels for a 14-yard loss in the third quarter.

Then the last two minutes. Our punt from their 41 went just 12 yards. A 33-yard interference flag put Washington in business, and Stefon Diggs caught a 32-yard touchdown with 57 seconds left. We still had a chance, but McCarthy's deep throw for Malik Nabers was intercepted at their 11.

A few leftovers will keep me up tonight. Dominic Zvada missed a 33-yarder wide left in the first quarter, and Nabers lost a fumble inside their 20 in the third. Those are points left on the field in a three-point game.

Next: the Jacksonville Jaguars at MetLife on Sunday, Nov. 22, at 4:25 PM ET. First place is still ours. Let's go keep it. Go Big Blue.
