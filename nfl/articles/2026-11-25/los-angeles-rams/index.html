Los Angeles Rams vs Green Bay Packers Week 12: Corum's Birthday Bash, 26-13

Final: Los Angeles Rams (7-4) 26, Green Bay Packers (6-5) 13, Wed., Nov. 25, SoFi Stadium, Inglewood. Next: vs Kansas City Chiefs, Thu., Dec. 3, 8:15 PM ET, SoFi Stadium, Inglewood

XFacebook

Los Angeles Rams 26, Green Bay Packers 13, and Blake Corum spent his 26th birthday running through Green Bay 32 times in the NFL's first-ever Thanksgiving Eve game. We wore the Fearsome White uniforms for the first time, built to honor Deacon Jones, Merlin Olsen, Lamar Lundy and Rosey Grier. Grier, 94, was in the building, and he got a huge ovation when he was honored during a timeout.

The old Fearsome Foursome would have loved this front. Nate Landman forced a Tucker Kraft fumble on Green Bay's second drive, Quentin Lake fell on it, and Brandon McManus turned it into his second field goal. Byron Young got home on Jordan Love, and Jaylen McCollough and Emmanuel Forbes Jr. each intercepted him. Green Bay ran 14 times all night for 55 yards.

It was 13-13 at halftime, and nobody in Inglewood felt good about it. Kyren Williams had scored from 7 to finish a 79-yard drive, but Love answered with an 80-yarder of his own, and Trey Smack hit from 54 and 48. So Sean McVay handed it to Corum, and kept handing it to him.

The first drive of the second half went 14 plays and 64 yards and burned 8:02. Corum got stuffed at the 2, then at the 1, and finally plowed in from 1 yard out after a replay review for 20-13. He ran 25 times for 161 yards in the second half alone. Green Bay had the ball for 6:15 after halftime.

Matthew Stafford threw for 140 yards and didn't need a yard more. Konata Mumpfield caught four for 69. Corum ripped off 35 yards on third-and-1 to set up McManus from 40 for 23-13, then broke a 29-yarder to the Green Bay 15 on our last drive. McManus closed it from 30 with 27 seconds left. Four field goal tries, four makes, against the team he used to kick for.

One bad moment: Kamren Kinchens was ejected for the hit that knocked Christian Watson out of the game before halftime. On the bright side, McVay finally beat Matt LaFleur, his old offensive coordinator, and we snapped a five-game skid against the Packers.

Five straight wins, 7-4, and the Kansas City Chiefs come to SoFi Stadium on Thursday, Dec. 3, at 8:15 PM ET. Short turnaround, so somebody get Corum a cold tub and a slice of birthday cake. Whose house? Rams house.
