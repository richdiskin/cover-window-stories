Tennessee Titans vs Dallas Cowboys Week 11: 30 Points Wasted, 30-51

Final: Dallas Cowboys (5-6) 51, Tennessee Titans (1-9) 30, Sun., Nov. 22, AT&T Stadium, Arlington. Next: at Jacksonville Jaguars, Sun., Nov. 29, 1:00 PM ET, EverBank Stadium, Jacksonville

XFacebook

Dallas 51, Tennessee 30. We put up 30 points on the road and lost by 21, which tells you all you need to know about how our defense spent Sunday in Arlington. We're 1-9.

Cam Ward did his part. He went 21 of 30 for 248 yards with two touchdown throws and no interceptions, and he snuck one in from a yard out. His 59-yard strike to Carnell Tate in the third quarter cut it to 34-23 and had me off the couch. Tony Pollard ran 21 times for 110 yards and looked like the toughest guy on the field.

Calvin Ridley caught five for 68, and his 12-yard touchdown with 31 seconds left came on fourth-and-8, the second fourth-and-8 we converted on that drive. Chimere Dike caught six for 69 and returned six kickoffs for 127 yards. Joey Slye hit all three field goals, from 38, 48 and 28. The offense moved the ball. That's not where this one went wrong.

Here's where it went wrong. Dak Prescott went 28 of 33 for 325 yards and four touchdowns, and nobody laid a hand on him. Zero sacks. Dallas went 6 for 6 in the red zone. CeeDee Lamb caught 11 balls, George Pickens caught seven, and every time we scored in the third quarter, they answered right back.

And the one time we had a real chance to make it a game in the fourth, Ward got stripped by Quinnen Williams at the Dallas 20, and the Cowboys ran it all the way back to our 15. They scored three plays later. Ward got sacked five times on the day. Joshua Williams made 11 tackles and Cedric Gray had eight, but we never took the ball away once.

Back home, Cheekwood's Holiday LIGHTS opened Friday, more than a million lights along a one-mile walking path through the gardens. I'm heading out there this week. After three hours of watching our secondary chase Dallas receivers, a long, quiet walk in Nashville sounds like exactly what the doctor ordered.

Next: the Titans head to EverBank Stadium to face the Jacksonville Jaguars, Sunday, Nov. 29, at 1 PM ET. Keep Cam Ward upright, keep feeding Pollard, and find somebody who can get to the quarterback. Titan Up.
