Pittsburgh Steelers vs Philadelphia Eagles Week 11: Boswell Can't Do It All, 16-19

Final: Philadelphia Eagles (3-7) 19, Pittsburgh Steelers (6-4) 16, Sun., Nov. 22, Lincoln Financial Field, Philadelphia. Next: vs Denver Broncos, Fri., Nov. 27, 3:00 PM ET, Acrisure Stadium, Pittsburgh

XFacebook

Zambelli fireworks lit up the Allegheny on Saturday night, and on Sunday the Pittsburgh Steelers fizzled in South Philly, losing 19-16 to the Philadelphia Eagles. Light Up Night turned 65 this year and the Rachel Carson Bridge threw itself a 100th birthday party Downtown, but our offense stayed dark until the last five minutes. We're 6-4 with two straight losses, and I'm steaming.

We dug a 13-0 hole in the first quarter. Our opening drive died on fourth down at our own 40, which handed Philly a short field and a Jake Elliott field goal. He added another one, and then Saquon Barkley went 38 yards around the left end for a touchdown with 35 seconds left in the quarter.

Give the defense its due, because it kept us alive. Philadelphia finished with 214 yards. Alex Highsmith had two sacks, Jack Sawyer had one, Cameron Heyward broke up a throw, and Patrick Queen made a tackle for loss. Robert Spears-Jennings punched the ball loose on the second-half kickoff, Carson Bruener grabbed it at their 26, and that turned into three points.

Chris Boswell was perfect from 25, 40 and 52 yards. But you don't beat anybody living on field goals. We got inside their 20 twice and came out with zero touchdowns, converted five of 17 third downs, and Aaron Rodgers went down five times. Philly's kicker hit from 53 and 55 in the second half, and that 55-yarder with 4:19 left made it 19-9.

Then, finally, the offense showed up. Rodgers led a 13-play, 72-yard drive with a couple of scrambles to keep it moving and dropped a 20-yard touchdown deep left to Max Hurleman with 1:21 left. 19-16. We needed one stop after that and didn't get it. DeVonta Smith caught a 7-yard throw to move the chains, and Philly knelt out the clock.

Rodgers finished 30 of 44 for 227 yards. Hurleman was the bright spot with six catches for 61 yards and the touchdown, plus 68 yards on kick and punt returns. Pat Freiermuth caught five for 39, Roman Wilson had a 33-yard grab, and Cameron Johnston averaged 49.2 yards on six punts. We held the ball for 35:30 and still lost.

That one hurts, because a team that sacks the other quarterback three times and wins the turnover battle should walk out of there with a win.

Next up is Black Friday football at Acrisure Stadium against the Denver Broncos, Friday, Nov. 27, at 3:00 PM ET. Turkey sandwiches in the bag, Terrible Towels in the air. Let's get the taste of this one out of our mouths.
