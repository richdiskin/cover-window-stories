Chicago Bears vs New Orleans Saints Week 11: Safety First, 21-13

Final: Chicago Bears (8-2) 21, New Orleans Saints (3-7) 13, Sun., Nov. 22, Soldier Field, Chicago. Next: at Detroit Lions, Thu., Nov. 26, 1:00 PM ET, Ford Field, Detroit

XFacebook

Bears 21, Saints 13 at Soldier Field, and we're 8-2. Eight and two! Write it on the fridge. Then scribble a note next to it about the defense, because that's who won this one.

Caleb Williams was sharp when it counted, 15 of 23 for 181 yards and two touchdowns. After Cairo Santos opened the scoring from 41, Williams flipped a 3-yard score to D'Andre Swift, and later Zavion Thomas ripped off a 30-yard run that set up a 15-yard touchdown to Colston Loveland two plays later. That made it 16-0 in the second quarter. Santos pushed the extra point wide right, which I'll be grumbling about in my sleep.

Thomas was a live wire all day. He also caught two balls for 56 yards, including a 40-yarder on third-and-17 in the third that set Santos up for a 35-yard field goal and a 19-10 lead.

Now, the defense. Jeffery Simmons had three sacks. Three! Montez Sweat dropped Tyler Shough in the end zone for a safety early in the fourth quarter, and that made it 21-10. Cam Lewis and Clark Phillips III got sacks too, six in all. Dillon Thieneman intercepted Shough, Austin Booker fell on a fumble, and when the Saints got to our 10 in the fourth, we stopped them cold on fourth-and-2.

It wasn't perfect. Williams threw an interception, Swift coughed one up in the fourth, and we went 5 for 12 on third down. Chris Olave caught nine balls for 102 yards against us, and Travis Etienne Jr. broke a 46-yarder that set up their only touchdown. Charlie Smyth also banged one in from 56 at the end of the half, and I didn't love that.

The city had a big weekend before we even got to the lakefront. Navy Pier kicked off its Winter Fireworks on Saturday night, the first of a show every Saturday through Dec. 26, and on Sunday our defensive front brought the noise. Okay, a safety isn't fireworks. It sure felt like it from where I was standing.

Short week now. We head to Ford Field to face the Detroit Lions on Thanksgiving, Thursday, Nov. 26, at 1 PM ET, and both teams are 8-2. Eat early. Bear down.
