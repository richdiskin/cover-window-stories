Los Angeles Chargers vs New York Jets Week 11: Couldn't Get a Stop, 24-27

Final: New York Jets (3-8) 27, Los Angeles Chargers (1-9) 24, Sun., Nov. 22, SoFi Stadium, Inglewood. Next: vs New England Patriots, Sun., Nov. 29, 4:05 PM ET, SoFi Stadium, Inglewood

XFacebook

Six Jets possessions, five Jets scores, and one more home loss for the Los Angeles Chargers, 27-24 to the New York Jets at SoFi Stadium. On Saturday, the 20th Great Los Angeles Walk set off down the full length of Wilshire Boulevard from Pershing Square to the beach, and on Sunday the Jets strolled up and down our field like they'd signed up for it. We're 1-9, and 0-5 at home.

New York never punted. Not once. They went nine of 11 on third downs, and Geno Smith completed 17 of 21. We couldn't get off the field, and that's the whole story of the afternoon.

It stings because we fought. Down 17-3 in the second quarter, Justin Herbert drove us 72 yards in 12 plays and ran it in himself from 10 yards out on the last play of the half. 17-10. After their 18-play field goal drive made it 20-10, Herbert hit Ladd McConkey from 11 yards early in the fourth, and it was a three-point game.

McConkey caught both of Herbert's touchdowns and five balls on the day. Omarion Hampton ran 14 times for 89 yards, including a 36-yarder. Herbert finished 15 of 21 for 131 yards with no interceptions and ran for 32 more. But he got sacked six times, and that's a lot of drives going backward.

Down 27-17, we put together a 16-play, 63-yard march that took 7:48, with a challenge that flipped an incomplete throw to McConkey into a catch along the way, and Herbert found him from 6 yards out with 1:58 left. 27-24. We still had three timeouts. We used all of them, and the Jets ran out the clock anyway.

Cameron Dicker drilled a 52-yard field goal in the first quarter. Elijah Molden made eight tackles, Cam Hart had six, and Troy Dye got our only sack. We didn't cough the ball up once, and we still lost, because a defense has to force a punt at some point.

The New England Patriots come to SoFi Stadium next Sunday, Nov. 29, at 4:05 PM ET. One home win. That's all I'm asking for before Christmas. Bolt up.
