Monday Morning Quarterback: NFL weekend review, Nov 22, 2026

Monday, Nov. 23, 2026.

Part 1: Handicap Recap

Twelve Week 11 games are final, from Thursday night in Houston to Sunday night at Estadio Banorte in Mexico City. The people holding tickets got all of it: a Dolphins +16.5 that lost by half a point on a 28-yard field goal with 1:57 left, a Ravens +2.5 that survived by half a point after a Panthers field goal at 1:44, a Raiders cover that died on a pick-six with 1:52 to go, an Over in Kansas City that cashed by half a point thanks to a 44-yarder at the end of the first half, a backdoor touchdown with 8 seconds left in Detroit that cashed an Over, and a Sunday night Under that survived two Minnesota trips inside the San Francisco 20.

Bengals at Commanders tonight is the last Week 11 game, and it gets graded in Tuesday's edition.

Vikings 10, 49ers 35

Four cards had the 49ers −3.5, and Letdown Watch was alone on the Vikings +3.5. Every over/under pick on the board was the Under 46.5.

The side was settled before halftime. Brock Purdy hit George Kittle for touchdowns of 17 and 8 yards, Aaron Jones Sr. answered with an 11-yard run right after a roughing the passer flag on Nick Bosa, and Will Reichard's 48-yarder made it 49ers 14, Vikings 10. Then Purdy found Mike Evans from 4 yards out at 2:09 of the second, and on Minnesota's very next snap Ji'Ayir Brown picked off Kirk Cousins and ran it back 18 yards for a touchdown. 49ers 28, Vikings 10. Letdown Watch's Vikings +3.5 was down 18 at the break.

That left the Under as the only sweat in the building, and it got two scares. Christian McCaffrey's 6-yard touchdown at 13:26 of the fourth made it 49ers 35, Vikings 10, 45 points, a point and a half under the 46.5. Minnesota drove right back to the San Francisco 20, and on fourth-and-13 DeeJay Dallas got pushed out at the 10, 3 yards short. Carson Wentz got the Vikings to the 19 late, and on fourth-and-8 at 1:52 his pass for Gavin Bartholomew hit the turf. A field goal on either trip makes it 48 points. Three kneel-downs later, the Under had cashed by 1.5 on the card and by half a point at the closing 45.5.

Follow The Money passed on the side and the over/under.

Raiders 24, Broncos 38

Four cards took the Raiders +10.5, Letdown Watch laid it with Denver, and all five over/under pickers had the Over 41.5.

A 3-yard pass interference flag on Eric Stokes set up Adam Trautman's 1-yard touchdown catch, and Jonah Coleman scored from the 1. The Raiders had a 4-yard touchdown to Jalen Nailor wiped out by offensive pass interference on Cody White and settled for Matt Gay's 28-yarder, so it was 17-3 Denver. Then Fernando Mendoza hit Ashton Jeanty for a 68-yard touchdown at 2:45 of the second, and Mendoza found Ian Thomas from 19 yards out at 5:13 of the third. Broncos 17, Raiders 17, and the Raiders +10.5 tickets were feeling great.

Coleman scored from 4 yards out with 20 seconds left in the third and from 7 yards at 9:21 of the fourth. Broncos 31, Raiders 17, 48 points, and the Over 41.5 was home. The Raiders lost another Nailor touchdown to an illegal forward pass on Mendoza, then Jack Bech caught a 7-yard touchdown at 4:29. Broncos 31, Raiders 24. The Raiders +10.5 was covering by 3.5 with four and a half minutes to go.

Then came the two-minute warning. On third-and-5 at 1:52, Mendoza threw for Tre Tucker, and Ja'Quan McMillian jumped it and went 35 yards the other way for a touchdown. Broncos 38, Raiders 24. That pick-six flipped the side, and Letdown Watch's Broncos −10.5 cashed on it.

Follow The Money sat out the side and the over/under.

Jaguars 18, Giants 23

Four cards took the Giants +6, and Next Man Up laid it with Jacksonville. Everybody, Follow The Money included, had the Under 45.5.

New York led 20-6 at the half. A 37-yard pass interference flag on Montaric Brown set up Jameis Winston's 1-yard touchdown on fourth-and-goal, J.J. McCarthy hit Malik Nabers for an 8-yard touchdown, and Dominic Zvada kicked a 54-yarder with 11 seconds left in the half. Next Man Up's Jaguars −6 was down 14 at the break.

The Under got nervous late. Zvada's 43-yarder made it Giants 23, Jaguars 9 at 10:03 of the fourth, and Trevor Lawrence hit Brian Thomas Jr. for an 18-yard touchdown at 6:39. The two-point pass for Josh Cameron got picked off. Giants 23, Jaguars 15. Cam Little's 26-yarder at 3:40 made it Giants 23, Jaguars 18, 41 points, and a Jacksonville touchdown from there kills the Under. The Jaguars never touched the ball again. Cam Skattebo ran twice for 5 yards each, and McCarthy knelt three times.

Follow The Money stayed off the side.

Jets 27, Chargers 24

Geno Smith threw touchdowns to Breece Hall from 7 yards and Sterling Shepard from 8, and Justin Herbert ran in a 10-yarder on the last play of the half. Jets 17, Chargers 10, 27 points. Jason Sanders kicked a 24-yarder at 3:23 of the third. Jets 20, Chargers 10.

The Over got there early in the fourth. Ladd McConkey caught an 11-yard touchdown at 13:01, and Smith answered with a 16-yarder to Shepard at 9:46. Jets 27, Chargers 17, 44 points, and the Over 41.5 had cashed with almost 10 minutes left.

Then it got loud on the side. Los Angeles got a sack wiped out by an illegal use of hands flag on Azareye'h Thomas, got to the Jets 6, and McConkey caught a 6-yard touchdown at 1:58. Jets 27, Chargers 24. Letdown Watch's Chargers −3 needed the ball back and a touchdown. Los Angeles kicked deep, burned all three timeouts on Breece Hall runs, and Braelon Allen ran 15 yards on third-and-7 at 0:47 to end it. The Jets won outright.

Follow The Money had nothing on the side.

Dolphins 9, Bills 26

Tyler Bass kicked field goals of 43 and 54 yards, Josh Allen hit DJ Moore for a 4-yard touchdown, and James Cook III scored from the 1. Bills 20, Dolphins 3. Then the end of the half turned into a turnover party. Jacob Rodriguez picked off Allen, Daniel Carlson hit a 52-yarder, Zayne Anderson picked off Allen again, and Julius Wood picked him off a third time at the Miami 7 to end the half. Bills 20, Dolphins 6 at the half, 26 points.

Carlson's 27-yarder made it 20-9, Bass missed from 54, and Bass's 40-yarder made it Bills 23, Dolphins 9 at 14:56 of the fourth. The Dolphins +16.5 was covering by 2.5 with almost a full quarter to play.

Then came the sweat of the weekend. Buffalo had first-and-goal at the Miami 10 at 2:47. Allen threw incomplete for Moore twice, Khalil Shakir got nothing on third down, and the Bills sent out Bass for a 28-yarder at 1:57. Bills 26, Dolphins 9. A 17-point game, and the Dolphins +16.5 was losing by half a point.

Miami got one last shot, and any score covers. Ollie Gordon II converted a fourth-and-1, Jalen Tolbert caught a 17-yarder to the Buffalo 26, and Kyle McCord threw three straight incompletions from the 25, the last one for Caleb Douglas on fourth-and-9 at 0:25. Kyle Allen took a knee, and the Dolphins +16.5 lost by half a point.

Called it at both numbers: Follow The Money on the Under, stayed under by 12.5 points at the opening 47.5 and stayed under by 10 points at the closing 45.

Follow The Money took a pass on the side.

Ravens 33, Panthers 35

Every side on the board was the Ravens +3. The Formula and Next Man Up got there in Sunday's refresh, leaving the Panthers −2.5 for the Ravens +3, and that move is about to look smart. Five pickers had the Over 43.5, and Follow The Money took the Under.

The Over was over by halftime. Mike Green sacked Bryce Young for a safety, Jacoby Brissett hit Rashod Bateman for a 52-yard touchdown, Chuba Hubbard ran 18 yards for a score, and Devontez Walker took the next kickoff 86 yards to the house. Ravens 26, Panthers 22 at the half, 48 points. Follow The Money's Under was dead before the third quarter started.

The flag that mattered for the side came at 5:06 of the second. Tetairoa McMillan caught a 7-yard touchdown, Ryan Fitzgerald's extra point came off the board on an unnecessary roughness flag on Ethan Burke, and Carolina went for two and got it on a Hubbard run. Burke's flag handed Carolina a point it never had to earn.

Hubbard scored from the 1 in the third, Fitzgerald hit a 33-yarder, and the Panthers led 32-26. Derrick Henry ran 15 yards for a touchdown at 6:32 of the fourth. Ravens 33, Panthers 32. Carolina drove to the Baltimore 19, Young's third-down throw for McMillan fell incomplete, and Fitzgerald kicked a 37-yarder at 1:44. Panthers 35, Ravens 33. The Ravens +3 was covering by 1, and at the closing +2.5 by half a point. Brissett converted a fourth-and-1, Tyrel Dodson picked him off at 0:36, and Young knelt twice. The Panthers −2.5 that The Formula and Next Man Up dropped that morning would have lost by half a point.

Follow The Money kept its money off the side.

Buccaneers 21, Lions 31

Jake Bates missed a 53-yarder early, then Jared Goff hit Amon-Ra St. Brown for a 14-yard touchdown and Bates made a 56-yarder. Baker Mayfield found Ted Hurst III for a 23-yard touchdown with 4 seconds left in the half. Lions 10, Buccaneers 7.

The Buccaneers +3.5 got robbed early in the third. Bucky Irving went 80 yards for a touchdown, and a holding flag on Luke Goedeke wiped it out. Goff hit Jameson Williams for a 57-yard touchdown on Detroit's next drive, Irving punched one in from the 1 at 2:41, and it was Lions 17, Buccaneers 14 going to the fourth. Tampa was covering +3.5 by half a point, and the Under was alive at 31 points.

Then the flags turned on Tampa. A 15-yard face mask on Josiah Trotter moved Detroit inside the 20, and Jahmyr Gibbs scored from the 1 at 5:24. Baker Mayfield took sacks on third and fourth down, an unnecessary roughness flag on Vita Vea kept the next Detroit drive alive, and Gibbs scored from 4 yards out at 1:55. Lions 31, Buccaneers 14, 45 points.

Cardinals 7, Chiefs 34

The Cardinals +15.5 was gone by halftime. Patrick Mahomes hit Travis Kelce for touchdowns of 8 and 22 yards, Emmett Johnson ran one in from the 3, and Harrison Butker kicked a 40-yarder. Josh Johnson's 2-yard touchdown pass to Simi Fehoko was all Arizona had. Mahomes got flagged for intentional grounding on the last drive of the half, and Butker hit from 44 anyway as time ran out. Chiefs 27, Cardinals 7 at the half, 34 points.

That 44-yarder ended up deciding the over/under. Xavier Worthy's 18-yard touchdown at 14:54 of the fourth made it Chiefs 34, Cardinals 7, 41 points, half a point over the 40.5. Nobody scored again. Arizona's last real drive reached the Kansas City 27 before a 9-yard Tyler Allgeier run came back on a holding flag on Isaiah Adams, and it died on fourth down at the 30. The Over cashed by half a point, and Follow The Money's Under lost by half a point at the close.

Follow The Money passed on the side.

Steelers 16, Eagles 19

Five cards had the Eagles +3.5, Follow The Money included, and Next Man Up laid it with Pittsburgh. Five pickers had the Over 37.5, and Follow The Money took the Under. This game opened with the Eagles laying 5.5 and closed with them getting 3.5.

Jake Elliott kicked field goals of 41 and 29 yards, and Saquon Barkley ran 38 yards for a touchdown. Eagles 13, Steelers 0 after one quarter.

After that it turned into a kicking contest, which is exactly what the Over didn't need. Chris Boswell made three field goals, the last a 52-yarder after DK Metcalf got disqualified and backed Pittsburgh up 15 yards. Elliott made a 53-yarder in the third, and after a roughing the passer flag on T.J. Watt and a holding flag on Markel Bell he made a 55-yarder at 4:19 of the fourth. Eagles 19, Steelers 9, 28 points, and the Over 37.5 needed 10 more.

Aaron Rodgers drove Pittsburgh 72 yards, picked up a fourth-and-1 himself, and hit Max Hurleman for a 20-yard touchdown on third-and-10 at 1:21. Eagles 19, Steelers 16, 35 points. Next Man Up's Steelers −3.5 needed the ball back and a touchdown, and the Over needed 3 more points. Pittsburgh burned all three timeouts, Joe Milton found DeVonta Smith for 7 yards on third-and-6, and Milton knelt twice. The Over fell 2.5 short, and Follow The Money cashed the Eagles and the Under.

Called it at both numbers: Follow The Money on the Under, stayed under by 7.5 points at the opening 42.5 and stayed under by 2.5 points at the closing 37.5.

Saints 13, Bears 21

Four cards took the Saints +11.5, Letdown Watch laid it with Chicago, and all five over/under pickers had the Under 48.5. The Under was never in doubt. The side was.

Chicago led 16-0 after Caleb Williams hit D'Andre Swift for a 3-yard touchdown and Colston Loveland for a 15-yarder, and Cairo Santos missed the extra point on the second one. Hold on to that missed kick. Tyler Shough hit Alvin Kamara for a 1-yard touchdown on fourth-and-goal, and Charlie Smyth hit a 56-yarder on the last play of the half. Bears 16, Saints 10.

Santos made it 19-10 in the third, and Montez Sweat sacked Shough in the end zone for a safety at 12:55 of the fourth. Bears 21, Saints 10. With Santos's extra point, Chicago would have been up 12 and Letdown Watch's Bears −11.5 would have been covering. Without it, Letdown Watch needed one more score.

It never came. The Saints went for it on fourth-and-2 at the Chicago 10 and threw incomplete to Noah Fant at 8:51, and Smyth later hit a 51-yarder on fourth-and-19 at 1:10. Bears 21, Saints 13. Chicago recovered the onside kick and Caleb Williams knelt twice. The Saints +11.5 covered by 3.5, and the 34 points stayed under the closing 47.5 by 13.5.

Follow The Money stayed off the side and the over/under.

Titans 30, Cowboys 51

Four cards took the Titans +7.5, and Letdown Watch laid the points with Dallas. All five over/under pickers had the Under 54.5. That was the whole room on one ticket, and the ticket lost by 26.5.

Dallas scored touchdowns on its first four drives. Javonte Williams scored from the 1, KaVontae Turpin ran 22 yards for a touchdown, and Dak Prescott threw touchdowns to CeeDee Lamb from 8 and George Pickens from 10. Cowboys 27, Titans 9 at the half, 36 points. The Titans +7.5 was down 18.

The Under died in the third. A 38-yard pass interference flag on Caleb Downs set up Cam Ward's 1-yard touchdown run, Prescott hit Williams for an 11-yard score, and Ward hit Carnell Tate for a 59-yard touchdown at 7:19. Cowboys 34, Titans 23, 57 points, with a quarter and a half to play.

Prescott ran in from 2 yards after Dallas challenged and won, Brandon Aubrey hit a 49-yarder, and Quinnen Williams strip-sacked Ward with Markquese Bell taking it 70 yards to the Tennessee 15. Prescott found Brevyn Spann-Ford for a 2-yard touchdown. Cowboys 51, Titans 23. Calvin Ridley's 12-yard touchdown on fourth-and-8 with 31 seconds left made the final margin 21, and Letdown Watch's Cowboys −7.5 was never sweating.

Called it at both numbers: Letdown Watch on the Cowboys, covered by 14.5 points at the opening Cowboys −6.5 and covered by 13.5 points at the closing Cowboys −7.5.

Follow The Money had nothing on the side and the over/under.

Colts 3, Texans 26

C.J. Stroud hit Nico Collins for a 42-yard touchdown and Kayshon Boutte for a 24-yarder, and Ka'imi Fairbairn added a 27-yarder with 15 seconds left in the half. Indianapolis got a 34-yard Spencer Shrader field goal and nothing else. Shrader missed from 56, Logan Hall blocked his 62-yarder, and Kamari Lassiter picked off Daniel Jones. Texans 17, Colts 3 at the half, 20 points.

The Colts +12.5 was down 14, so one field goal would have put it back in front. Will Anderson Jr. sacked Jones in the end zone for a safety instead, at 10:22 of the third, and Stroud hit Marlin Klein for a 13-yard touchdown at 4:19. Texans 26, Colts 3. Nobody scored in the fourth, Stroud knelt it out, and the game finished with 29 points. Follow The Money cashed both of its tickets.

Called it at both numbers: Follow The Money on the Texans, covered by 17.5 points at the opening Texans −5.5 and covered by 10.5 points at the closing Texans −12.5; Follow The Money and In The Trenches on the Under, stayed under by 16.5 points at the opening 45.5 and stayed under by 15.5 points at the closing 44.5.

Part 2: Looking forward

Bengals at Commanders.

Kickoff is 8:15 PM ET tonight on ESPN at Northwest Stadium in Landover, Maryland. The Bengals come in 7-2, 4-1 on the road, and beat the Steelers 38-18 last time out. The Commanders are 3-5-1, 2-2-1 at home, and beat the Giants 24-21 last time out.

Today's number: DraftKings has the Bengals −6 with the over/under at 54.5 as of 2:49 AM ET Monday, and the Action Network consensus matches it. The card was built on Bengals −6.5 and 52.5. The spread is down half a point, and the over/under is up 2. Tonight's cards get their game-day line check before kickoff.

The card has the Commanders +6.5 and the Under 52.5, projecting Bengals 27, Commanders 23. The Formula has the Commanders +6.5 and the Under, projecting Bengals 27, Commanders 23. Next Man Up has the Commanders +6.5 and the Under, projecting Bengals 27, Commanders 23. Letdown Watch has the Commanders +6.5 and the Under, projecting Bengals 26, Commanders 22. Follow The Money has no pick on either one. In The Trenches has the Commanders +6.5 and the Under, projecting Bengals 28, Commanders 24.

Every pick on the board is the Commanders +6.5 and the Under 52.5. A Bengals win by 7 or more beats the whole room on the side, and a 53-point game beats it on the over/under.

Next Man Up's card names Jayden Daniels and Joe Burrow as the expected starters. ESPN lists Washington tackle Laremy Tunsil and running back Jacory Croskey-Merritt on injured reserve, with running back Rachaad White questionable. Cincinnati is without guard Dalton Risner, and wide receiver Andrei Iosivas is on injured reserve. Burrow has thrown for 2,795 yards with 27 touchdowns and 6 interceptions, Ja'Marr Chase has 878 receiving yards and 7 touchdowns, and Boye Mafe has 6.5 sacks. Daniels has thrown for 1,499 yards with 10 touchdowns and 2 interceptions and run for 280 more, Stefon Diggs has 386 receiving yards and 6 touchdowns, and Odafe Oweh has 5 sacks.
