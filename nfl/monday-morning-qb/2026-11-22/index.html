Part 1: Handicap Recap

Monday night is final, so all 13 Week 11 games are graded, from Thursday night in Houston to Monday night in Landover, with a Sunday night stop in Mexico City. Every side on the board Monday night was the Commanders +6, and those tickets were losing twice: down 10 late in the first half and down 7 in the third. Drew Stevens kicked five field goals, the last a 26-yarder as time ran out, and Washington won the game outright.

Every side is graded on the team picked and every over/under on the Over or Under called, once against the opening line and once against the closing line. A push counts as neither a hit nor a miss, overtime points count, and picks are graded as they stood at kickoff. Monday's game-day line refresh moved every Commanders ticket from 6.5 to 6 and every Under from 52.5 to 54.5, and put Follow The Money on the Over 54.5. Nothing changed after that. Monday night gets the full ride. The rest of the week gets a quick lap, with the plays that swung the tickets.

Bengals 20, Commanders 22

The first quarter was all punts. Boye Mafe sacked Jayden Daniels three times in the first half, and Cincinnati gave the ball away at its own 45 when Frankie Luvu stopped Joe Burrow for no gain on fourth-and-1. The Bengals broke through in the second. A 15-play, 71-yard drive ended in Evan McPherson's 38-yarder at 6:17. On the next Cincinnati drive, Burrow hit Samaje Perine for 44 yards on third-and-11 and Tee Higgins for a 31-yard touchdown at 1:55. Bengals 10, Commanders 0. The Commanders +6 was losing by 4, and the half was almost gone.

Then came the flag the Washington tickets were begging for. Daniels went 79 yards in 1:43, and a 32-yard throw to Chig Okonkwo got the Commanders to the Cincinnati 6. On second-and-goal from the 5 with 19 seconds left, his pass for Treylon Burks fell incomplete, and Dax Hill was flagged for pass interference. Ball at the 1. Jacory Croskey-Merritt went in on the next snap with 12 seconds left. Bengals 10, Commanders 7 at the half, 17 points. The +6 was covering by 3, and the Under had 37.5 points to play with.

Daniels opened the second half with a 47-yard scramble to the Cincinnati 14, and the drive went backward from there: a false start on Chris Paul, two incompletions, and a 36-yard Stevens field goal at 11:47. Bengals 10, Commanders 10. Cincinnati answered right away. Trey Amos was flagged for pass interference on Ja'Marr Chase, Mike Gesicki caught passes of 19 and 9 yards, and Burrow hit Colbie Young for a 4-yard touchdown at 7:04. Bengals 17, Commanders 10. The +6 was losing again, this time by 1. Stevens hit a 40-yarder at 2:36, and it was Bengals 17, Commanders 13, with the +6 back on top by 2.

The fourth quarter belonged to Stevens and the Under. Myles Murphy jumped offside on third-and-3 at 11:48, and Washington had first-and-goal at the 8. Daniels threw incomplete for Antonio Williams, scrambled for 1, threw incomplete for Stefon Diggs, and Stevens kicked a 25-yarder at 10:32. Bengals 17, Commanders 16, 33 points. On the next Washington drive, a holding flag on Josh Conerly Jr. made it first-and-20, Daniels hit Diggs for 29 yards on second-and-20, and the drive stalled at the 24. Stevens hit from 41 on fourth-and-1 at 2:26. Commanders 19, Bengals 17, 36 points.

Cincinnati had 2:26 to get the lead back, and it did. Gesicki caught a 32-yarder to the Washington 33, Nick Cross and Kain Medrano sacked Burrow on third-and-7, and McPherson drilled a 56-yarder on fourth-and-14 with 46 seconds left. Bengals 20, Commanders 19, 39 points. The Bengals had the lead, and every Commanders +6 ticket was still covering by 5. Follow The Money's Over needed 16 more points in 46 seconds. Daniels hit Antonio Williams for 22 and 42 yards, spiked it at the 8 with 5 seconds left, and Stevens kicked a 26-yarder as time ran out. Commanders 22, Bengals 20, 42 points.

The damage report. Washington won by 2, so the Commanders +6 covered by 8 on the card, by 8.5 at the closing +6.5 and by 3.5 at the opening +1.5. The 42 points stayed under the 54.5 by 12.5. Washington got inside the Cincinnati 20 four times and scored one touchdown, and three of Stevens's five field goals came on those trips. Turn those three kicks into touchdowns and the game lands on 54, still half a point under. Daniels threw for 289 yards and ran for 55, and Antonio Williams caught 9 passes for 122 yards. Burrow threw for 300 yards and two touchdowns, but Cincinnati ran 14 times for 31 yards. Every card that wrote a score had the Bengals winning by 4, and the Commanders won the game by 2.

Follow The Money sat out the side.

Vikings 10, 49ers 35

Letdown Watch was alone on the Vikings +3.5, and that ticket was finished before halftime. Brock Purdy hit Mike Evans for a 4-yard touchdown at 2:09 of the second, and on Minnesota's next snap Ji'Ayir Brown picked off Kirk Cousins and ran it back 18 yards for a touchdown. 49ers 28, Vikings 10. The Under 46.5 was the whole room's sweat. Christian McCaffrey's 6-yard touchdown at 13:26 of the fourth made it 49ers 35, Vikings 10, 45 points. Minnesota got to the San Francisco 20 and came up short on fourth-and-13, then got to the 19 and threw incomplete on fourth-and-8 at 1:52. A field goal on either trip beats the Under. It cashed by 1.5 on the card and by half a point at the closing 45.5.

Follow The Money stayed off the side and the over/under.

The Raiders lost a Jalen Nailor touchdown to an illegal forward pass on Fernando Mendoza, and Jack Bech caught a 7-yard touchdown at 4:29. Broncos 31, Raiders 24, and the Raiders +10.5 was covering by 3.5. Then on third-and-5 at 1:52, Ja'Quan McMillian jumped a throw for Tre Tucker and went 35 yards the other way. Broncos 38, Raiders 24. That pick-six flipped the side, and Letdown Watch cashed on it.

Follow The Money had nothing on the side and the over/under.

Follow The Money stayed out of the side.

Ravens 33, Panthers 35

Every side on the board was the Ravens +3, and The Formula and Next Man Up got there in Sunday's refresh by leaving the Panthers −2.5. The Over 43.5 was over at halftime, Ravens 26, Panthers 22, 48 points, so Follow The Money's Under was dead early. The side came down to a flag and a field goal. After Tetairoa McMillan's 7-yard touchdown at 5:06 of the second, an unnecessary roughness flag on Ethan Burke wiped out the extra point, and Carolina went for two and got it. Derrick Henry's 15-yard touchdown at 6:32 of the fourth made it Ravens 33, Panthers 32, and Ryan Fitzgerald's 37-yarder at 1:44 made it Panthers 35, Ravens 33. The Ravens +3 survived by 1, and by half a point at the closing +2.5. Tyrel Dodson picked off Jacoby Brissett at 0:36, and the Panthers −2.5 that The Formula and Next Man Up dropped would have lost by half a point.

Buffalo got first-and-goal at the Miami 10 at 2:47, threw incomplete twice, ran for nothing, and sent Bass out for a 28-yarder at 1:57. Bills 26, Dolphins 9, and the +16.5 was losing by half a point. Miami got to the Buffalo 25, where any score covers, and Kyle McCord threw three straight incompletions, the last on fourth-and-9 at 0:25. The Dolphins +16.5 lost by half a point, and Follow The Money's Under cashed.

Aaron Rodgers converted a fourth-and-1 himself and hit Max Hurleman for a 20-yard touchdown on third-and-10 at 1:21. Eagles 19, Steelers 16, 35 points. The Over fell 2.5 short, and Follow The Money cashed the Eagles and the Under.

Cardinals 7, Chiefs 34

Arizona's last real drive lost a 9-yard Tyler Allgeier run to a holding flag on Isaiah Adams and died on downs at the 30. The Over 40.5 cashed by half a point for five cards, and Follow The Money's Under lost by the same half point.
