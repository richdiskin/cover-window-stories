Part 1: Handicap Recap

Fourteen Week 9 games are final, from Thursday night in Baltimore to Sunday night in Chicago, with a Sunday morning stop in Madrid. The people holding tickets got all of it: a push, a walk-off field goal that settled two numbers at once, a kneel-down that saved an Under, an Under that survived by half a point, a fourth-down stop at the 2 that saved a cover by half a point, and an overtime touchdown that cashed every Over ticket on a game that was 16-16 after regulation.

Bills at Vikings tonight is the last Week 9 game, and it gets graded in Tuesday's edition.

Buccaneers 24, Bears 27

Chicago went up 14-0 in the first quarter on Caleb Williams touchdown throws of 9 yards to Rome Odunze and 56 yards to Luther Burden III. Then Williams started handing the ball back. An unnecessary roughness flag on Zavion Thomas pushed the Bears from the Tampa Bay 6 back to the 21, and Tykee Smith picked Williams off at the 3. Zyon McCollum intercepted him twice, the second one on a replay reversal of an incompletion. Cam Lewis picked off Baker Mayfield in between, and Chase McLaughlin's 21-yarder on the last play of the half made it Bears 17, Buccaneers 6.

Bucky Irving's 2-yard run at 11:37 of the third got Tampa Bay within 17-13. Alex Anzalone intercepted Williams at 4:38, and on the very next possession Jaylon Johnson picked off Mayfield and went 67 yards the other way. Bears 24, Buccaneers 13. The Buccaneers at the closing +6.5 were 4.5 short, and the Under crowd had 37 points on the board with a quarter to go.

McLaughlin hit from 35 at 11:24 of the fourth. Bears 24, Buccaneers 16, 40 points. At 7:19 the Bears sacked Mayfield back to the Tampa Bay 5 on third-and-8, and a face mask flag on Austin Booker took it off the board. Tampa Bay punted anyway after two more sacks. Then with 3:48 left Burden caught a short pass, Rueben Bain Jr. punched it out, and Ifeatu Melifonwu recovered at the Chicago 21. Irving scored from 6 yards out at 2:51, and Mayfield ran in the two-point try. 24-24. The Buccaneers were covering both numbers, the Under at the closing 47.5 was dead at 48 points, and the Under at the opening 48.5 was alive by half a point.

Then the Bears drove. Colston Loveland caught one for 38 yards to the Tampa Bay 16, Williams knelt twice, and Cairo Santos hit from 38 as time ran out. Bears 27, Buccaneers 24. Chicago played for the field goal instead of the end zone, and that is the only reason the Buccaneers at the opening +3.5 covered by half a point. The same three points put the game at 51 and took the last Under ticket in the building with them.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side.

Raiders 17, 49ers 38

San Francisco led 21-10 at the half. On the Raiders' first play of the third quarter Jaden Dugger intercepted Kirk Cousins and took it 35 yards for a touchdown. 28-10. Brock Bowers caught a 21-yard touchdown at 9:29 to make it 28-17, 45 points. Then Michael Mayer caught a 24-yarder down to the San Francisco 1, Upton Stout knocked it loose, and it went through the end zone for a touchback. Seven points that Under tickets never had to count.

Jordan Watkins caught a 37-yard touchdown from Brock Purdy with 36 seconds left in the third. 35-17, 52 points, and the Under at the closing 51.5 was gone. The card's 52.5 hung on by half a point into the fourth. Mayer caught a 3-yard touchdown at 12:05 that was nullified by his own offensive pass interference flag, and Renardo Green intercepted Cousins in the end zone on the next play. Under backers at 52.5 were thrilled for about seven minutes.

Eddy Pineiro's 35-yarder at 5:21 made it 38-17. Fifty-five points. Malik Mustapha picked off Cousins one more time and Mac Jones knelt it out. The 49ers covered by 12.5 at both numbers, and Follow The Money's Over cashed at both.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side; In The Trenches on the side or the over/under.

Cardinals 3, Seahawks 17

AJ Barner took a direct snap in from a yard out early in the second quarter. A 29-yard interference flag on Max Melton set up Barner's 7-yard touchdown catch with 15 seconds left in the half, and Jason Myers' 45-yarder at 3:50 of the third made it 17-3. That was the last point of the day. The Over at the closing 39.5 never had a pulse.

The side is where the drama lived. Arizona put together a 14-play, 76-yard drive in the fourth quarter. Josh Jobe intercepted Gardner Minshew II, and Jobe's own interference flag erased it. The Cardinals got to fourth-and-goal at the Seattle 2 at 4:39, and Minshew's throw for Trey McBride fell incomplete. A Cardinals touchdown there makes it 17-10, and every Seattle ticket at −13.5, −9.5 or −8.5 loses. Instead Seattle knelt it out.

No pick: In The Trenches on the side or the over/under.

Packers 45, Patriots 27

On New England's second snap Xavier McKinney intercepted Drake Maye, and Jordan Love scrambled 13 yards for a touchdown two plays later. Love hit Matthew Golden from 5 yards out, and it was 14-0 before eight minutes were gone. McKinney picked Maye off again in the second quarter, and the Packers led 24-14 at the half. Thirty-eight points at halftime, and the Under at 45 had a very long third quarter coming.

Love hit Savion Williams for a 10-yard touchdown at 10:51 to make it 45 points, and Trey Smack's 51-yarder at 5:13 pushed it past every number on the board. Then Love threw 39 yards to Christian Watson and ran in the two-point try himself. Packers 42, Patriots 14. The Under was finished before the fourth quarter started, and so were the Patriots tickets.

Called it at both numbers: Next Man Up on the Packers, covered by 19.5 points at the opening Packers +1.5 and covered by 21 points at the closing Packers +3.

No pick: In The Trenches on the side or the over/under.

Texans 44, Chargers 22

C.J. Stroud threw touchdowns of 17 yards to Xavier Hutchinson, 63 to Tank Dell and 12 to Kayshon Boutte in the first half, and Houston led 24-9. Nico Collins caught a 13-yarder in the third. When Stroud found Boutte again for 63 yards early in the fourth quarter it was 38-9, 47 points, and the Over had cashed at both numbers with almost a full quarter to spare.

Texans 44, Chargers 22. Houston by 22, the Chargers missed by 16.5 at the close, and 66 points went over the closing 40.5 by 25.5. In The Trenches had a long afternoon.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side or the over/under.

Jets 12, Chiefs 35

Jason Sanders kicked the Jets to four first-half field goals, from 23, 48, 49 and 54 yards, and Kenneth Walker III caught two touchdown passes from Patrick Mahomes. Then the half ended in a kicking clinic of the wrong kind. Sanders missed a 53-yarder wide left with 37 seconds left. Harrison Butker's 56-yarder hit the right upright, an offside flag on Malik McClain gave him another shot, and he missed that one from 51. Chiefs 14, Jets 12 at halftime.

Travis Kelce scored on fourth-and-goal from the 1 at 10:20 of the third, and Walker ran one in from 2 yards with 11 seconds left in the quarter. 28-12, 40 points. Emmett Johnson's 6-yard run at 3:35 of the fourth made it 35-12, 47 points, half a point under the closing 47.5.

From there every Under ticket needed nobody to score for three and a half minutes. The Jets went for it on fourth-and-9 at the Kansas City 37 and Geno Smith threw incomplete. The Chiefs ran Jaydn Ott three times and Justin Fields knelt at the New York 44. Under 47.5 cashed by half a point. The Jets at +9 lost by 14.

Called it at both numbers: Letdown Watch on the Chiefs, covered by 13.5 points at the opening Chiefs −9.5 and covered by 14 points at the closing Chiefs −9.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side; In The Trenches on the side or the over/under.

Cowboys 27, Colts 23

Dallas led 17-7 at the half and should have been up more in the third. CeeDee Lamb's 11-yard touchdown catch at 3:04 was reversed by replay, and the Cowboys settled for Brandon Aubrey's 28-yarder. That reversal took four points off the board, and Under tickets will want to send the replay booth a thank-you note.

Then the fourth quarter turned into a kicking contest. Spencer Shrader tied it 20-20 with a 59-yarder at 5:56. Aubrey missed from 50 wide left at 3:34. Shrader hit from 50 at 1:37, and the Colts led 23-20. Every Colts ticket on the board was cashing.

It lasted less than half a minute. Dak Prescott hit Ryan Flournoy for 46 yards to the Indianapolis 19, an illegal use of hands flag and an interference flag on Kapena Gushiken moved the ball to the 1, and Javonte Williams scored at 1:08. Cowboys 27, Colts 23. Caleb Downs intercepted Daniel Jones to end it. Dallas by 4: the Colts at the opening +1.5 lost by 2.5, the Colts at the closing +4 pushed, and the card's +5.5 cashed. Fifty points stayed under at both numbers.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side or the over/under.

Browns 22, Saints 23

This was 6-6 going into the fourth quarter, and Over tickets at 43.5 were reading the obituaries. Charlie Smyth's 48-yarder at 5:38 put the Saints up 9-6. On the very next snap Shedeur Sanders hit KC Concepcion for 64 yards and a touchdown, 13-9 Browns. Audric Estime's 2-yard run with 58 seconds left made it 16-13 Saints, and Andre Szmyt answered with a 51-yarder at 0:08. Regulation ended 16-16, 32 points, and the Over at the closing 43.5 needed 12 more in overtime.

It got 13. Tyler Shough hit Juwan Johnson for a 15-yard touchdown, Saints 23-16. Sanders came right back and found Harold Fannin Jr. for 39 yards and a touchdown with a minute left, and the Browns went for two and the win. The try to Denzel Boston fell incomplete. Saints 23, Browns 22.

So the Saints won and the Browns covered every number on the board, and the overtime touchdowns cashed the Over at the opening 41.5 and the closing 43.5. Next Man Up's original card, the Browns and the Over, cashed both pieces. The one it switched to lost both.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side; In The Trenches on the side or the over/under.

Broncos 26, Panthers 21

Carolina led 14-13 into the fourth. Jonah Coleman's 9-yard run at 10:17 put Denver ahead 19-14, and the two-point try failed. Bo Nix hit Pat Bryant for a 26-yard touchdown at 5:41. Broncos 26, Panthers 14, 40 points. Panthers +3 needed more than a touchdown, and the Over at the closing 44.5 needed one.

Bryce Young went for it on fourth-and-18 from the Denver 34 and hit John Metchie III for 25 yards, and a roughing the passer flag on Zach Allen moved it to the 4. Young found Tetairoa McMillan for the touchdown at 3:05. 26-21, 47 points. That cashed the Over at both numbers, for everybody.

The Panthers got the ball back down 5 with a chance to win it. Nik Bonitto strip-sacked Young at 1:24 and recovered it himself, and the replay held up. Broncos by 5. The Panthers at +3 missed by 2 at both numbers, and Letdown Watch took the side by itself.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side; In The Trenches on the side or the over/under.

Rams 20, Commanders 13

It was 13-13 after three quarters, and Puka Nacua's 7-yard touchdown at 12:51 of the fourth made it 20-13. Rams −4.5 needed that to hold, and Washington backers needed one touchdown.

Washington got the ball back in the final minute and moved it. On the final snap Jayden Daniels hit Chig Okonkwo for 20 yards to the Rams 11, Jaylen McCollough knocked it loose, Rachaad White scooped it up and lost it again, and Byron Young fell on it. Rams 20, Commanders 13. A Washington touchdown on that play beats the Rams −4.5 and −5.5. The Rams covered by 1.5 at the open and 2.5 at the close, and 33 points sailed under both numbers.

No pick: Follow The Money on the over/under.

Lions 19, Dolphins 9

Miami nearly had a 72-yard fumble return for a touchdown by Jordyn Brooks in the first quarter, and replay took it away. Zane Gonzalez's 38-yarder hit the right upright with 3 seconds left in the half. Jahmyr Gibbs went 8 yards for a touchdown on fourth-and-1 late in the third, and Detroit led 16-6.

The Dolphins had first-and-goal at the Detroit 7 early in the fourth. Alim McNeill sacked Malik Willis for 9 yards and Derrick Moore sacked him for 3 more, and Gonzalez kicked a 34-yarder. 16-9. A touchdown there makes it 16-13, with the Dolphins covering. Instead the Dolphins at +6.5 were missing by half a point, and then Jake Bates hit from 43 at 4:28 to make it 19-9. The Dolphins missed by 3.5. The Under cashed by a mile, 28 points against 50.5.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side or the over/under; In The Trenches on the side or the over/under.

Dominic Zvada kicked four field goals for the Giants, including a 49-yarder at 4:24 of the fourth that made it 19-8. Jake Elliott hit from 45 at 1:56 to make it 19-11, and Malik Nabers fell on the onside kick.

The Eagles still got one more shot from their own 5 with 1:32 left. Joe Milton got them only as far as their own 44, and on the last play of the game Darius Alexander sacked him. Ja'Marr Chase took one 47 yards to the end zone, Tee Higgins caught two touchdowns, and Evan McPherson hit a 57-yarder with 24 seconds left in the half. Bengals 24, Falcons 10 at the break, 34 points. Higgins's second touchdown, at 2:05 of the third, made it 31-17 and put 48 points on the board.

McPherson's 43-yarder at 4:36 made it 34-17, 51 points, and that killed the Under at the opening 49.5. Barrett Carter then intercepted Michael Penix Jr., and the Bengals drove to first-and-goal at the Atlanta 8 in the final minutes.

One more Bengals touchdown there makes it 58 points and sinks the Under at the closing 52.5 too. Joe Burrow took a knee at 0:39. Under tickets at 52.5 survived by 1.5 because Cincinnati decided it had enough, and because Folk's kick went left.

Part 2: Looking forward

Bills at Vikings.

Kickoff is 8:15 PM ET tonight on ESPN and ABC at U.S. Bank Stadium in Minneapolis. The Bills come in 6-1, 3-0 on the road, and beat the Ravens 36-10 last time out. The Vikings are 6-1, 3-0 at home, and lost to the Lions 19-15 last time out.

This game opened Bills −3 with the over/under at 48.5. As of 3:01 AM ET Monday the Action Network consensus was Bills −3.5 with the over/under at 43.5, and DraftKings had Bills −3 and 43.5. The card was built on Bills −3.5 and 45.5.

The card has the Vikings +3.5 and the Under 45.5, projecting Bills 24, Vikings 21. The Formula has the Bills −3.5 and the Over, projecting Bills 27, Vikings 19. Next Man Up has the Vikings +3.5 and the Under, projecting Bills 23, Vikings 22. Letdown Watch has the Vikings +3.5 and the Over, projecting Vikings 25, Bills 24. In The Trenches has the Vikings +3.5 and the Under, projecting Bills 22, Vikings 20. Follow The Money has no pick.

Four of the five pickers on the side have the Vikings, and the over/under splits three for the Under to two for the Over. Buffalo by exactly 3 is the finish to watch: a push for anybody holding Vikings +3 and a win at +3.5. And with the over/under down 5 points since the open, the Over crowd is already working uphill.

ESPN lists Vikings quarterback Kyler Murray out with a hamstring injury, along with wide receiver Jauan Jennings and running back Demond Claiborne. The card went up on Nov. 4, when Murray was listed as the expected starter. Buffalo has wide receivers Tyrell Shavers and Joshua Palmer out, and tackles Dion Dawkins and Alec Anderson questionable. Josh Allen has thrown for 1,793 yards and 11 touchdowns, James Cook III has 707 rushing yards and 7 touchdowns, and Greg Rousseau has 7 sacks. Justin Jefferson has 335 receiving yards and 4 touchdowns for Minnesota, and Andrew Van Ginkel has 6.5 sacks.
