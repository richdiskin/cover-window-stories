Monday Morning Quarterback: NFL weekend review, Nov 1, 2026

Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026.

XFacebook

Part 1: Handicap Recap

Monday night is final, so all 14 Week 8 games are graded, from Thursday night in Green Bay to Monday night in Seattle. The last sweat of the week went the wrong way early for most of the room, came back to life for about three minutes in the fourth quarter, and then got buried by a 39-yard run and three D'Andre Swift touchdowns.

Every side is graded on the team picked and every over/under on the Over or Under called, once against the opening line and once against the closing line. A push counts as neither a hit nor a miss, overtime points count, and a pick pulled before kickoff is not graded. Monday night gets the full ride. The rest of the week gets a quick lap, with the plays that swung the tickets.

Bears 34, Seahawks 17

The Seattle tickets were in trouble before the Seahawks ever touched the ball in Chicago territory. The Bears opened with a 17-play, 80-yard drive that ate 8:32, kept alive by Colston Loveland's 15-yard catch on 3rd-and-1, and Caleb Williams hit Luther Burden III from 1 yard out at 5:25 of the first. Jason Myers got Seattle on the board with a 27-yarder at 12:50 of the second, and Cairo Santos answered from 44 and 38. Bears 13, Seahawks 3, with 1:08 left in the half. Under tickets were sitting pretty at 16 points.

Then the last minute of the half. A 16-yard interference flag on Jaylon Johnson kept Seattle moving, and on 3rd-and-10 Sam Darnold hit Jaxon Smith-Njigba for a 43-yard touchdown with 30 seconds left. Myers pushed the extra point wide left. Bears 13, Seahawks 9 at the half, 22 points. Seattle backers were 4 points down and needed to win the second half by 8 at the close and 9 at the open. The Under had 24 points of room at the close and 25 at the open.

Chicago went three-and-out to start the third, and Montez Sweat sacked Darnold on Seattle's next series. Then the Bears went 69 yards in 10 plays, and Swift scored from 1 yard out at 5:38. Bears 20, Seahawks 9, 29 points, and Seahawks −3.5 was now 14.5 points on the wrong side.

The next drive is the one Seattle backers will be replaying all week. George Holani returned the kickoff 34 yards to the Seattle 41. A defensive holding flag on Kyler Gordon wiped out a Sweat sack. Tory Horton caught a 13-yarder to the Chicago 13. Then, on 2nd-and-8 from the 11 with 14 seconds left in the third, Darnold found Smith-Njigba down at the Chicago 1, and the flag came out on Smith-Njigba for offensive interference. Back to the 21. One incompletion, confirmed by replay, and a 6-yard catch by Zach Charbonnet later, Myers lined up a 33-yarder on 4th-and-12, and Austin Booker blocked it. Instead of first-and-goal at the 1, Seattle came away with nothing, and the Under crowd got a free 3 points of breathing room.

Seattle got the ball right back. Mike Morris sacked Williams for 10, and on 3rd-and-20 DeMarcus Lawrence punched the ball loose from Loveland and recovered it at the Chicago 25. Cam Lewis picked off Darnold in the end zone two snaps later, and a defensive holding flag on Chicago wiped it out. Darnold hit Cooper Kupp for a 7-yard touchdown at 11:46, then hit Horton for the two-point try. Bears 20, Seahawks 17, 37 points, and now everybody was sweating. The Seattle crowd needed to outscore Chicago by 7 the rest of the way at the close and by 8 at the open. The Under had 9 points of room at the close and 10 at the open, with 11:46 to play.

That room lasted 3:18. Zavion Thomas returned the kickoff 24 yards and Morris got flagged 15 more for unnecessary roughness. Chicago took a delay of game on third down, turning it into 3rd-and-9 at the Seattle 44, and Kalif Raymond ran 39 yards to the Seattle 5. Swift scored on the next snap at 8:28. Bears 27, Seahawks 17, 44 points. The side was gone. At the closing 46.5, any field goal now killed the Under. At the opening 47.5, the Under could survive a field goal but not a touchdown.

The Under at the open still had one prayer: keep Chicago out of the end zone. Seattle's Irv Charles got flagged for unnecessary roughness on the kickoff return, a holding flag on Tyrique Stevenson Sr. wiped out another Sweat sack, and on 3rd-and-7 James Lynch picked off Darnold and returned it 16 yards to the Seattle 6 at 6:01. Under backers needed a goal-line stand. They got an unnecessary roughness flag on Seattle's Nick Emmanwori instead, and even a Chicago delay of game from the 1 only backed the Bears up to the 5. Swift scored from there at 4:41, his third touchdown. Bears 34, Seahawks 17, 51 points. The Under was dead at both numbers.

Seattle's last drive got to the Chicago 7 behind a roughing the passer flag on Booker and a 14-yard Darnold scramble, and 4th-and-4 fell incomplete to Kupp at 2:21. Williams knelt it out. Swift finished with 27 carries for 69 yards and 3 touchdowns. Smith-Njigba caught 6 for 118 yards and the touchdown, and Seattle went 1 for 11 on third down and 1 for 4 in the red zone.

Called it at both numbers: Next Man Up on the Bears, covered by 21.5 points at the opening Bears +4.5 and covered by 20.5 points at the closing Bears +3.5.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side or the over/under; In The Trenches on the side or the over/under.

Panthers 19, Packers 27

Thursday night set the tone. Green Bay led 27-10 after Love's 4-yard touchdown to Josh Jacobs at 7:30 of the third, and Trey Smack's first extra point had already hit the left upright. Bryce Young hit Jalen Coker for 26 yards at 5:37 of the fourth, the two-point try failed, and the scoreboard froze at 46 points. The Under cashed at the closing 47.5 and lost at the opening 45.5 by half a point. One more missed kick, by either team, and the opening Under cashes.

Called it at both numbers: Letdown Watch on the Packers, covered by 1 point at the opening Packers −7 and covered by 4.5 points at the closing Packers −3.5.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side or the over/under; In The Trenches on the side or the over/under.

Vikings 15, Lions 19

Half a point, both ways. Murray hit T.J. Hockenson for an 8-yard touchdown at 8:44 of the fourth and Jordan Addison caught the two-point throw, 15-12 Vikings. Goff answered with a 7-yard touchdown to Jameson Williams at 4:35, and Detroit won by 4. Vikings +4.5 at the open cashed by half a point. Vikings +3.5 at the close lost by half a point.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side or the over/under.

Cardinals 33, Cowboys 30

Arizona had the points at every number and won the game outright, so the side was never the sweat. The Under was. Brandon Aubrey's 41-yarder at 1:09 made it 30-24, 54 points on the board, half a point under the closing 54.5. Minshew hit Michael Wilson for the tying touchdown with 6 seconds left, and Chad Ryland's 42-yarder won it in overtime.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side or the over/under.

Raiders 38, Jets 33

Seventy-one points buried every Under ticket by the third quarter, so the Jets side was the sweat. Down 38-33 with 27 seconds left, a Jets touchdown cashes Jets −1.5. An unnecessary roughness flag on Jeremy Chinn handed New York 15 free yards, and on the next snap Chinn picked off Geno Smith in the end zone.

Called it at both numbers: The Formula and Next Man Up on the Over, went over by 30.5 points at the opening 40.5 and went over by 24.5 points at the closing 46.5; Next Man Up, Letdown Watch and In The Trenches on the Raiders, covered by 6.5 points at the opening Raiders +1.5 and covered by 6.5 points at the closing Raiders +1.5.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side or the over/under.
