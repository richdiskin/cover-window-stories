Monday Morning Quarterback: NFL weekend review, Nov 15, 2026

Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026.

Part 1: Handicap Recap

Monday night is final, so all 14 Week 10 games are graded, from Thursday night at MetLife Stadium to Monday night in Baltimore, with a Sunday morning stop in Munich. The Chargers tickets never trailed. The Over crowd was the one sweating: every Over in the room sat at 36 points with 14:56 left and needed a Baltimore touchdown to get home. Derrick Henry delivered it from 1 yard out at 10:23.

Every side is graded on the team picked and every over/under on the Over or Under called, once against the opening line and once against the closing line. A push counts as neither a hit nor a miss, overtime points count, and picks are graded as they stood at kickoff. Monday's game-day line refresh moved every Chargers-Ravens side ticket from 5.5 to 4.5 and added Follow The Money on the Chargers, and nothing changed after that. Monday night gets the full ride. The rest of the week gets a quick lap, with the plays that swung the tickets.

Chargers 23, Ravens 20

The Chargers tickets were cashing from the first drive. On third-and-6 at the Baltimore 22, Justin Herbert scrambled up the middle for a 22-yard touchdown at 10:39 of the first. Later in the quarter Los Angeles challenged an incomplete deep ball to Tre' Harris, replay turned it into a 31-yard catch at the Baltimore 26, and Cameron Dicker hit from 38. Chargers 10, Ravens 0.

Derrick Henry scored from 2 yards at 12:33 of the second. Chargers 10, Ravens 7. Two snaps into Baltimore's next drive, Del'Shawn Phillips intercepted Brissett, and the Chargers kept the ball for 13 plays and 7:35. Omarion Hampton converted a fourth-and-1 at the Baltimore 39, a 15-yard unnecessary roughness flag on the Chargers pushed them right back, and they kept going anyway. Herbert scrambled to the 1 on third-and-goal, and Hampton went in on fourth-and-goal with 1:56 left in the half. Chargers 17, Ravens 7.

Baltimore answered with a 72-yard drive that had three flags on it and ended at the Chargers 8 with 3 seconds left, and Riley Patterson kicked a 26-yarder as the half ended. Chargers 17, Ravens 10 at the half, 27 points. The Chargers +4.5 was covering by 11.5, and the Over 38.5 on the card needed 12 more points in the second half.

The third quarter went field goal for field goal. Dalvin Tomlinson sacked Brissett at the 6 on third-and-3, the punt return set the Chargers up at the Baltimore 37, and Dicker hit from 34 at 9:03. Chargers 20, Ravens 10, 30 points. Baltimore stalled at the Chargers 17 after a no-gain run and two incompletions, and Patterson hit from 36 at 3:36. Chargers 20, Ravens 13, 33 points.

Then came the flag that kept the Over alive. On the first snap of the next Chargers drive, Herbert threw incomplete and Mike Green was called for roughing the passer, 15 yards. Hampton ran for 16 and 13 on the next two snaps, the drive stalled at the 17, and Dicker's 35-yarder at 14:56 of the fourth made it Chargers 23, Ravens 13, 36 points. The Over 38.5 needed 3 more points and the closing 39.5 needed 4.

Baltimore went 71 yards in 10 plays. Brissett hit Mark Andrews for 13 yards to the 4, and Henry scored from 1 yard on third-and-goal at 10:23. Chargers 23, Ravens 20, 43 points. Every Over ticket on the card cashed right there, by 4.5 at the card's 38.5 and by 3.5 at the closing 39.5. Anybody holding the Over at the opening 47.5 needed 5 more points and never saw them.

That left The Formula and Letdown Watch, the only Ravens tickets in the room, needing Baltimore to win by 5. The Ravens punted on fourth-and-9 from their own 42 at 4:11. They got one more look at 2:24, and on second-and-10 Brissett hit Zay Flowers for 15 yards, Tarheeb Still punched the ball out, and Elijah Molden recovered at the Chargers 46. Even a Baltimore touchdown on that drive would have won the game by 4, still half a point short of the 4.5. The Chargers ran it down, took a delay of game on fourth-and-2 with 18 seconds left, and punted for a touchback. Brissett's last throw, deep for Ja'Kobi Lane, fell incomplete.

The damage report. The Chargers won by 3, so the Chargers +4.5 covered by 7.5 and the Ravens −4.5 missed by 7.5, and at the opening Ravens −3.5 the margin was 6.5 both ways. Hampton ran 22 times for 105 yards, and Herbert threw for 129 and ran for 51. Brissett threw for 340 yards with no touchdowns, and Baltimore ran 15 times for 16 yards. Henry carried 10 times for 10 yards, and two of those carries were the Ravens' only touchdowns. Next Man Up projected Ravens 22, Chargers 21, the closest call in the room on the final margin, and In The Trenches projected 39 points and took the Over 38.5 by a single point of its own math.

Follow The Money passed on the over/under.

Steelers 18, Bengals 38

The Formula was alone on the Bengals, and the side was settled at halftime, Bengals 24, Steelers 3. The Under 49.5 was the sweat. Samaje Perine's 1-yard touchdown at 10:08 of the fourth made it Bengals 38, Steelers 11, 49 points, half a point to spare. Then Kris Jenkins Jr. was flagged for roughing the passer at 8:18, and Aaron Rodgers hit Pat Freiermuth for a 27-yard touchdown at 7:19. Bengals 38, Steelers 18, 56 points. Follow The Money's Over was the only over/under winner, and In The Trenches had switched from the Over to the Under on Sunday morning.

Called it at both numbers: The Formula on the Bengals, covered by 16.5 points at the opening Bengals −3.5 and covered by 15.5 points at the closing Bengals −4.5; Follow The Money on the Over, went over by 8.5 points at the opening 47.5 and went over by 6.5 points at the closing 49.5.

Follow The Money sat out the side.

49ers 46, Cowboys 40

Everybody but In The Trenches had the Under 55.5, and it was dead at 11:26 of the fourth, when Christian McCaffrey's 16-yard touchdown run made it 49ers 38, Cowboys 19, 57 points. The side went to the last snap. CeeDee Lamb caught a 1-yard touchdown at 0:43 and Dak Prescott ran in the two-point try. 49ers 46, Cowboys 40, so the 49ers −4.5 was covering by 1.5 and a Dallas field goal would have flipped it. Deebo Samuel Sr. fielded the onside kick, and the game ended on a string of laterals and a fumble recovered by Marques Sigle. In The Trenches' Over cashed by 30.5, and Next Man Up's Cowboys +4.5 missed by 1.5.

Follow The Money stayed off the side and the over/under.

Seahawks 21, Raiders 13

Five pickers had the Over 40.5, and it died on two empty Seattle trips in the fourth. Up 21-10, Seattle drove 14 plays and lost a sack-fumble at the Raiders' 12 at 12:17, recovered by Nakobe Dean, and Jason Myers missed a 44-yarder wide right at 7:05. Matt Gay's 43-yarder on fourth-and-20 at 2:04 made it Seahawks 21, Raiders 13, 34 points, and the Raiders +6.5 needed the ball back. A 15-yard scramble on second-and-11 at 1:55 ended that, and the Seahawks −6.5 covered by 1.5 through three kneel-downs. Next Man Up and In The Trenches had the Seahawks −3.5 and the Under 43.5 before Sunday's refresh, switched to the Raiders and the Over, and lost both. The original pair would have cashed both.

Rams 34, Cardinals 6

Every picker with a side had the Cardinals +9.5, and that was over at 5:55 of the third when Davis Allen's 19-yard touchdown made it Rams 31, Cardinals 6. The over/under fell 9 points from the open to the close, and every Over 38.5 sat at 37 points until Darius Robinson jumped for a neutral zone infraction on fourth-and-1 at the Arizona 35. Brandon McManus finished that drive with a 43-yarder at 14:55 of the fourth. Rams 34, Cardinals 6, 40 points. The Over 38.5 cashed by 1.5, and the same kick beat Follow The Money's Under by 1.5. The Rams kept the ball for 17 plays to end it, and the opening 47.5 never came into view.

Follow The Money had nothing on the side.

Chiefs 35, Falcons 13

Follow The Money took a pass on the side.

Texans 24, Browns 27

The flags ran this one. With Cleveland up 20-17, Emmanuel McNeil-Warren intercepted C.J. Stroud, a 37-yard pass interference call on Myles Harden wiped it out, and Stroud hit Dalton Schultz for an 11-yard touchdown at 9:17. Texans 24, Browns 20, and the Browns +4.5 was covering by half a point. Calen Bullock intercepted Deshaun Watson at 5:19, a 15-yard unnecessary roughness flag on Kamari Lassiter moved Cleveland into Houston territory, and Quinshon Judkins ran in from the 5 with 53 seconds left. Browns 27, Texans 24. Houston's fourth-and-10 conversion came back on a holding call, and the Browns +4.5 covered by 7.5. The Over had cashed back at 9:17, and In The Trenches, which left the Over for the Under on Sunday morning, missed by 10.5.

Follow The Money kept its money off the side.

Vikings 30, Packers 10

The Formula was alone on the Packers −2.5, and the Vikings led 17-0 at the half. The Under 42.5 was the sweat. Minnesota got to fourth-and-goal at the 2 with 37 points on the board and kicked a 20-yard field goal at 3:56. A touchdown there makes it 44 and beats the Under. Will Reichard's chip shot left it at 40, the Under cashed by 2.5 for five pickers, and Follow The Money's Over came up 2.5 short. The Vikings +2.5 covered by 22.5.

Jaguars 40, Titans 23

This line moved 8.5 points toward Jacksonville from the open to the close. The card's Under 46.5 survived Trevor Lawrence's 25-yard touchdown pass to Josh Cameron at 9:44 of the fourth by half a point, Jaguars 30, Titans 16, 46 points, and died one drive later on Tony Pollard's 32-yard touchdown catch at 8:21. Then the side got tense. Cam Little's 40-yarder at 4:31 made it Jaguars 33, Titans 23, and the Titans +10.5 was covering by half a point. Pollard was stopped on fourth-and-1 at 3:11, and Bhayshul Tuten ran in from 6 yards at 1:10. Jaguars 40, Titans 23. The Titans +10.5 missed by 6.5, The Formula and Letdown Watch cashed the Jaguars −10.5, and In The Trenches' Over cashed by 16.5.

Called it at both numbers: The Formula and Letdown Watch on the Jaguars, covered by 14.5 points at the opening Jaguars −2.5 and covered by 6 points at the closing Jaguars −11; In The Trenches on the Over, went over by 17.5 points at the opening 45.5 and went over by 17.5 points at the closing 45.5.

Follow The Money passed on the side and the over/under.

Dolphins 22, Colts 24

Three pickers left the Colts −2.5 for the Dolphins +4.5 on Sunday morning, and the switch paid. Spencer Shrader's 51-yarder made it Colts 24, Dolphins 16, 40 points, and the Over 37.5 on the card was home. The opening 47.5 was another story, and it died on three Miami misses: Zane Gonzalez's missed extra point after Chris Johnson's 59-yard pick-six, the incomplete two-point pass after Kyle McCord's 3-yard touchdown run at 14:56 of the fourth, and Gonzalez's miss from 51 at 9:54. Those 6 points that never happened left the game at 46, 1.5 short of the open. The Colts won by 2, the Dolphins +4.5 covered by 2.5, and Next Man Up and Follow The Money lost the Colts −4.5 by 2.5.

Panthers 20, Saints 17

The favorite flipped between the open and the close, so the Panthers ticket was +1.5 or −1.5 depending on when it was bought. Charlie Smyth's 55-yarder at 0:41 tied it 17-17, and the Panthers at the closing −1.5 were losing by 1.5. Carolina had 41 seconds. Kool-Aid McKinstry was flagged for 30 yards of pass interference, and Ryan Fitzgerald hit a 54-yarder as time ran out. Panthers 20, Saints 17. The Panthers covered at both numbers, Letdown Watch's Saints −1.5 missed by 4.5, and 37 points paid the Under for Next Man Up and Letdown Watch by 11.5 at the close.

Follow The Money sat out the side and the over/under.

Bills 31, Jets 12

Everybody but Letdown Watch had the Jets +8.5, and the Jets were within 17-12 with 4:21 left in the third. Josh Allen hit DJ Moore for a 45-yard touchdown 39 seconds later. Then the Jets drove to the Buffalo 23, Garrett Wilson fumbled at the 19, Kaleb Elarms-Orr recovered at 4:59, and James Cook III ran 74 yards for a touchdown on the next play. Bills 31, Jets 12, 43 points. The Jets +8.5 missed by 10.5, Letdown Watch's Bills −8.5 covered, and all five Under tickets survived a last Jets drive that died on fourth-and-11 at 1:54.

Follow The Money had nothing on the side and the over/under.

Patriots 10, Lions 35

Called it at both numbers: The Formula, Next Man Up and Follow The Money on the Under, stayed under by 3.5 points at the opening 48.5 and stayed under by 5.5 points at the closing 50.5.

Follow The Money passed on the side.

Commanders 24, Giants 21

The over/under fell 7 points from the open to the close. Late in the fourth the Giants led 21-17, Letdown Watch's Giants −2.5 was covering, and Follow The Money's Under 41.5 was alive. Then Jordan Stout punted 12 yards out of bounds at 1:54, Deonte Banks was flagged for 33 yards of pass interference, and Jayden Daniels hit Stefon Diggs for a 32-yard touchdown with 57 seconds left. Commanders 24, Giants 21, 45 points. The Commanders +2.5 covered by 5.5 for four pickers, Letdown Watch missed by 5.5, and the Over cashed by 3.5 at the card's 41.5 but fell 2.5 short of the opening 47.5.

Follow The Money stayed off the side.
