Part 1: Handicap Recap

Thirteen Week 10 games are final, from Thursday night at MetLife Stadium to Sunday night in Cincinnati, with a Sunday morning stop in Munich. The people holding tickets got all of it: a 54-yard field goal as time ran out against the Saints, an Under that died by half a point on a 43-yard touchdown with 4:18 left, a Titans cover that held until 1:10 to go, a field goal on fourth-and-goal at the 2 that saved an Under, a 12-yard punt that set up a Thursday night winner, and a Sunday night Under that was alive by half a point with 10 minutes left.

Chargers at Ravens tonight is the last Week 10 game, and it gets graded in Tuesday's edition.

Steelers 18, Bengals 38

Cincinnati built its lead in the first half. Evan McPherson kicked a 24-yarder, a 26-yard pass interference flag on Asante Samuel Jr. set up Joe Burrow's 3-yard touchdown to Drew Sample, and Burrow found Chase Brown from 2 yards out at 5:28 of the second quarter. Chris Boswell's 45-yarder got Pittsburgh on the board, and Burrow hit Dohnte Meyers for a 15-yard touchdown just before the half. Bengals 24, Steelers 3. The Steelers tickets were down 21 at the break.

The third quarter took the last bit of hope off the side. Ja'Marr Chase caught a pass at the Pittsburgh 4 and fumbled, Jack Endries fell on it for Cincinnati, and Brown caught a 4-yard touchdown pass on the next snap. Bengals 31, Steelers 3.

That left the Under as the only live ticket for most of the room. Aaron Rodgers hit DK Metcalf for a 22-yard touchdown on fourth-and-4 at 12:16 of the fourth and found Metcalf again for two points. Bengals 31, Steelers 11, 42 points. Brown looked like he scored from the 5, replay put him down at the 1, and Samaje Perine walked in from there at 10:08. Bengals 38, Steelers 11, 49 points, half a point under the 49.5 with 10 minutes to go.

The Under didn't survive the next Pittsburgh drive. Kris Jenkins Jr. was flagged for roughing the passer at 8:18, Rodgers recovered a Zach Frazier fumble on the very next snap, and Rodgers hit Pat Freiermuth for a 27-yard touchdown at 7:19. Bengals 38, Steelers 18, 56 points. Follow The Money's Over cashed, and the Under lost by 6.5 at the close. Barrett Carter thought he had a pick-six off Mason Rudolph at 3:34, and replay took it back, which saved nobody anything.

Called it at both numbers: The Formula on the Bengals, covered by 16.5 points at the opening Bengals −3.5 and covered by 15.5 points at the closing Bengals −4.5; Follow The Money on the Over, went over by 8.5 points at the opening 47.5 and went over by 6.5 points at the closing 49.5.

Follow The Money passed on the side.

49ers 46, Cowboys 40

Dak Prescott threw touchdowns of 35 and 39 yards to George Pickens, Brock Purdy hit Deebo Samuel Sr. for an 11-yard touchdown, and Christian McCaffrey took a short pass 74 yards to the end zone at 2:26 of the second. Brandon Aubrey missed the extra point after the second Pickens touchdown. 49ers 17, Cowboys 16 at the half, 33 points.

Kaelon Black ran 25 yards for a touchdown early in the third, Aubrey hit from 52, and Samuel went 70 yards on a run at 6:59. Malik Mustapha intercepted Prescott on fourth down at the San Francisco 10. McCaffrey's 16-yard touchdown run at 11:26 of the fourth made it 49ers 38, Cowboys 19, 57 points, and the Under 55.5 was gone with more than 11 minutes left.

Then it got loud on the side. Pickens caught a 5-yard touchdown at 9:08, and the two-point pass to him fell incomplete. Ryan Flournoy caught an 11-yard touchdown on fourth-and-6 at 3:42. 49ers 38, Cowboys 32. San Francisco answered in two plays: McCaffrey caught a 66-yard touchdown at 3:23 and then caught the two-point pass. 49ers 46, Cowboys 32.

Dallas wasn't done. Prescott converted a fourth-and-1, a 14-yard pass interference flag on Ephesians Prysock put the ball at the 1, and CeeDee Lamb caught a 1-yard touchdown at 0:43. Prescott ran in the two-point try. 49ers 46, Cowboys 40, and the 49ers −4.5 was covering by 1.5. A Dallas field goal would have flipped it. Deebo Samuel Sr. fielded the onside kick, the 49ers punted Dallas back to its own 9, and the last play turned into a string of laterals that ended with Marques Sigle recovering a fumble. The 49ers −4.5 survived, and 86 points beat the Under by 30.5 at the close.

Seahawks 21, Raiders 13

The Raiders scored first after Quay Walker recovered a sack-fumble on Seattle's second snap, and Fernando Mendoza ran 10 yards for the touchdown. Jaxon Smith-Njigba had a 29-yard touchdown wiped out by his own offensive pass interference, and Cooper Kupp caught a 34-yard touchdown on third-and-15 later in the same drive. Matt Gay's 34-yarder made it Raiders 10, Seahawks 7, and that was the half.

Jadarian Price ran 12 yards for a touchdown at 9:28 of the third. A Mendoza fumble was overturned on replay, and on the next Seattle possession Smith-Njigba got his touchdown back with a 71-yarder at 7:10. Seahawks 21, Raiders 10, 31 points.

That's where the Over died slowly. Seattle drove 14 plays and gave it away on a sack-fumble at the Raiders' 12, recovered by Nakobe Dean at 12:17 of the fourth. Jason Myers missed a 44-yarder wide right at 7:05. A touchdown on the first of those drives and a field goal on the second would have made it 41 points and cashed the Over 40.5.

Gay kicked a 43-yarder on fourth-and-20 at 2:04. Seahawks 21, Raiders 13. The Raiders +6.5 still needed the ball back, and Seattle's quarterback scrambled 15 yards on second-and-11 at 1:55 to end that idea. Three kneel-downs later, the Seahawks −6.5 had covered by 1.5, the game had 34 points, and Follow The Money's Under cashed by 6.5 at the close.

Browns 27, Texans 24. Houston converted a fourth-and-10, but a holding call on Febechi Nwaiwu took it back, and the fourth-and-20 pass fell incomplete. The Under cashed by 2.5 at the close, and Follow The Money's Over came up 2.5 short. The Vikings +2.5 covered by 22.5, and The Formula's Packers −2.5 missed by the same 22.5.
