Part 1: Handicap Recap

Cowboys 38, Eagles 21

The first quarter was a track meet, which the Over loved. Brandon Aubrey opened it with a 55-yard field goal. Quinnen Williams jumped offside with a neutral zone infraction, and on the next snap Saquon Barkley ran 8 yards for a touchdown. Dak Prescott hit CeeDee Lamb for 9 yards and a 10-7 lead. Then another Dallas flag broke the Eagles' way. On 4th-and-1 at the Philadelphia 44, Tanner McKee fumbled the snap and Williams recovered it, and Williams' own offside flag erased the whole play. Later in the drive, on 3rd-and-7 at the Dallas 9, LT Overton was flagged for roughing the passer on an incompletion. Dameon Pierce scored from 5 yards on the next snap. Eagles 14-10, and the Eagles +4.5 was covering by 8.5.

Then came the flag that saved every Eagles ticket in the second quarter. Tyler Goodson took the ensuing kickoff 91 yards for a touchdown, and a holding flag on Dallas' Justin Barron brought it all back. Instead of a Dallas lead, it was still Eagles 14-10. Dallas then went 81 yards in 14 plays anyway, converting a 4th-and-1 at the Philadelphia 44 on a Javonte Williams run, and Prescott hit George Pickens for a 21-yard touchdown. Before the half, Prescott found Lamb for 30 yards down to the Philadelphia 9 with 19 seconds left. Two incompletions later Aubrey kicked a 27-yarder on the final play of the half. Halftime: Cowboys 20, Eagles 14. The Eagles +4.5 was losing by 1.5, and the Over had 34 and needed 11 more.

The third quarter opened with the drive Eagles bettors will tell their grandkids about: 17 plays, 84 yards and 9:16 off the clock. McKee converted a 4th-and-1 at the Dallas 39 with a 15-yard pass to Makai Lemon. Then, on 3rd-and-goal from the 4, he hit Lemon for the touchdown. Eagles 21-20. The Eagles +4.5 was covering by 5.5, and the Over had 41 and needed four.

Here is the swing of the night. Dallas faced 2nd-and-1 at the Philadelphia 18 with 2:17 left in the third. Prescott lost the ball, and Riq Woolen scooped it up and ran 73 yards for a touchdown. Replay called it an incomplete forward pass and took the whole thing back. That would have put the Eagles up 27-20 before the extra point, with the Eagles +4.5 covering by 11.5. Instead, two snaps later, Prescott hit Lamb for a 16-yard touchdown and then ran in the two-point conversion himself. Cowboys 28-21. The Over cashed right there, with 49 points and more than a quarter to play. The side was still alive, because one Eagles field goal would have put them inside the 4.5.

The fourth quarter took that away, and it started with a flag. Cooper DeJean was called for a 17-yard pass interference on the first snap of the next Dallas drive, Javonte Williams ripped off 34 yards two plays later, and Aubrey made a 24-yarder on 4th-and-5 for a 31-21 lead. Now the Eagles +4.5 needed a touchdown. Philadelphia got to the Dallas 32, and DeMarvion Overshown intercepted McKee at the 30 with 7:55 left. Philadelphia got one more look after a Dallas delay of game on 4th-and-1 forced a punt. Quinnen Williams sacked McKee for a loss of 8, and McKee's 4th-and-9 throw to Hollywood Brown fell incomplete. Javonte Williams finished it with a 2-yard touchdown at 2:21. He ran 25 times for 155 yards. Cowboys 38-21.

No pick: Follow The Money on the side or the total.
